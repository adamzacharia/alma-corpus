## Science

- Fields: nearby_galaxies, astrochemistry
- Question: Where do the ionization front, the H2 dissociation front and the C/CO transition lie in the low-metallicity N13 PDR in the SMC, how large is the CO-dark H2 layer between them, and do plane-parallel constant-pressure or constant-density PDR models reproduce the observed separations?  
  > "Compared to our plane-parallel PDR models, we find that a constant pressure model matches the observed structure better than a constant density one." (abstract); "This study represents the first resolved benchmark for low metallicity PDRs." (abstract)
- Target **N13 PDR, Small Magellanic Cloud**: coordinates R.A. 00h 45m 26.760s, Decl. -73 deg 22' 55.66''  
  > "We investigate a PDR in the N13 region of the Small Magellanic Cloud (SMC) located at R.A.: $00^{\rm h} 45^{\rm m} 26.760s$" (2.1 Target); "The SMC sits at a distance of 62 kpc (cite: Scowcroft16)" (2.1 Target); z/distance 62 kpc (SMC distance); metallicity Z = 0.2 Zsun

## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA 12m+ACA data to cubes and moment maps**: PHANGS-ALMA pipeline v1.0 images calibrated 12m and ACA data; cubes convolved to circular Gaussian beams; moment maps built with 'broad' signal masking  
  > "to image the calibrated data from the 12m array and ACA and generate cubes and moment maps" (2.3 ALMA); "We convolve all cubes to have circular Gaussian beams, but do not convolve to matched spatial resolution." (2.3 ALMA)
   - software: PHANGS-ALMA pipeline
   - parameter: Final beams 0.270'' (CO 2-1), 0.331'' (CO 3-2), 0.276'' ([CI] 1-0)  
  > "The final resolution of the cubes are: 0.270$''$ for $^{12}$CO J=2$-$1, 0.331$''$ for CO 3$-$2, and 0.276$''$ for \[CI\] 1$-$0." (2.3 ALMA)
   - parameter: Highest resolution data used, not matched  
  > "For our goal of identifying the layers of the PDR, the highest resolution version of the data is ideal." (2.3 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Reduce JWST NIRSpec cubes and fix their astrometry**: JWST pipeline Detector1/Spec2/Spec3 with 1/f noise correction, drizzled cubes with 0.05'' pixels; JWST astrometry corrected via HST aligned to Gaia DR3 using centroids of stars  
  > "We then ran the Spec2 and Spec3 pipeline stages and created a drizzled cube with 0.05$''$ pixels for each grating." (2.2 JWST NIRSpec Integral Field Spectroscopy); "Using the *Photutils Centroids* Python package (cite: photutils), we measured the centroid positions of these stars in the HST F475W image" (2.2.1 Astrometric Alignment)
   - software: JWST pipeline, Photutils
   - parameter: JWST pipeline version 1.16.1.dev14, CRDS jwst_1293.pmap  
  > "(JWST pipeline version 1.16.1.dev14) using CRDS `jwst_1293.pmap`" (2.2 JWST NIRSpec Integral Field Spectroscopy)
   - parameter: Gaia-HST offset RA -0.174'' +/- 0.039'', Dec 0.158'' +/- 0.010'' (1000 stars)  
  > "We find a Gaia-HST offset in RA of $-0.174\ensuremath{''} \pm 0.039\ensuremath{''}$ and in Dec of $0.158\ensuremath{''} \pm 0.010\ensuremath{''}$" (2.2.1 Astrometric Alignment)
   - parameter: HST-JWST offset RA -0.444'' +/- 0.022'', Dec -0.197'' +/- 0.011'' (4 stars)  
  > "We find an HST-JWST offset in RA of $-0.444\ensuremath{''} \pm 0.022\ensuremath{''}$ and in Dec of $-0.197\ensuremath{''} \pm 0.011\ensuremath{''}$" (2.2.1 Astrometric Alignment)
   - assumption: ALMA astrometry is reliable because of extragalactic radio phase calibrators  
  > "The astrometry of the ALMA observations is well understood due to the nature of interferometric measurements with extragalactic radio sources as phase calibrators." (2.2.1 Astrometric Alignment)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CO 2-1 moment-0 map compared with the dust lane in HST image after correction  
  > "The alignment between the CO emission and the dust lane in the HST image matched well, giving us confidence that the JWST-HST-ALMA alignment was robust." (2.2.1 Astrometric Alignment)
3. **Make integrated line maps from JWST cubes**: PAHFIT for H recombination lines and 3.3 micron PAH; for faint H2 2.12 micron, local continuum regions, 1-d polynomial fit, subtraction and integration under the line; applied to all spaxels  
  > "we defined continuum regions around each line, fit a 1-d polynomial, and then subtracted the fitted continuum before integrating under the line" (2.2.2 Integrated Line Maps from JWST); "We fit emission lines and PAH features in each spaxel and created maps of the integrated feature strengths." (2.2.2 Integrated Line Maps from JWST)
   - software: PAHFIT
   - fitting: Local 1-d polynomial continuum for H2 2.12 micron; PAHFIT model for H I lines and PAH (priors not stated)  
  > "This model works well for PAH features and for bright and/or blended emission lines." (2.2.2 Integrated Line Maps from JWST)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Extract radial profiles perpendicular to the PDR**: Slice chosen by visual inspection in CARTA perpendicular to the H2 emission; intensities sampled every 0.01'' with cubic griddata interpolation; parallel slices offset by the pixel scale for uncertainties  
  > "We selected the end coordinates of a perpendicular slice by visual inspection using the CARTA software package (cite: CARTA)." (3.1 Radial Profile Analysis); "cubic interpolation method to measure the intensities at each point of the slice" (3.1 Radial Profile Analysis); "The parallel dashed blue lines represent additional slices, offset by the pixel scale, used to estimate uncertainties in the peak spatial placement." (3.1 Radial Profile Analysis)
   - software: CARTA, scipy griddata
   - parameter: Step size 0.01'', oversampling JWST and ALMA resolution elements  
  > "with a step size of 0.01$''$, which oversamples the resolution element of JWST and ALMA" (3.1 Radial Profile Analysis)
   - parameter: Slice start 0:45:26.6275, -73:22:56.1209; end 0:45:26.9911, -73:22:54.8568  
  > "Start Coordinates | $0:45:26.6275$ | $-73:22:56.1209$" (Table 1 (Radial Slice Locations))
   - assumption: Edge-on, plane-parallel PDR geometry so peaks along the slice map to depth  
  > "Edge-on geometry maximizes the angular separation of the layers to avoid blending" (2.1 Target)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Additional slices around the main slice give peak-position scatter  
  > "The errors on the peak measurements come from the standard deviation of peak calculations on each of the 5 slices" (Table 1 notes)
5. **Locate IF, DF and C/CO peaks and measure separations**: IF from peak/decline of HI Pa-alpha, DF from first peak of H2 2.12 micron, C/CO from the average of the CO 2-1 and CO 3-2 peak positions; CO and [CI] separations given as upper limits; systematic error from astrometric alignment added in quadrature  
  > "We take location of the C/CO transition to be the average of the ${}^{12}$CO J=2$-$1 and ${}^{12}$CO J=3$-$2." (4.2 Distances Between PDR Boundaries); "where we take the error on the mean for each and add them in quadrature" (5 Discussion)
   - parameter: Profiles normalised to the peak of each line over the whole profile for plotting  
  > "we overplot and normalize the radial profiles to the peak value of each emission line over the whole profile" (4.2 Distances Between PDR Boundaries)
   - assumption: CO emissivity peak lies very close to, but not exactly at, the model C/CO boundary  
  > "We note that the location of the peak in CO emission is very close, but is not exactly located, at the C/CO boundary determined from C and CO modeled abundances" (4.1 Observed Structure of N13)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: H2 1-0 S(1)/2-1 S(1) ratio of 2.17 at the first peak confirms FUV pumping and a DF identification  
  > "we measure a 1-0 S(1)/2-1 S(1) ratio of 2.17" (4.1 Observed Structure of N13)
6. **Compare convolved PDR model emissivity peaks with observed spacings**: Plane-parallel steady-state SMC PDR model at G0 ~1e3; volume emissivity profiles convolved with Gaussian kernels to each line's resolution; model DF peak shifted to the observed DF; constant-pressure and constant-density cases compared to observed DF-to-CO and DF-to-[CI] spacings  
  > "We align model predicted peak locations with our observations rather than doing a formal fit of the models." (3 Methods); "we shift the peak in the models to match the observed peak to compare the spacing of the boundaries" (5.1 Comparison to PDR Models)
   - software: in-house PDR model (code not named)
   - parameter: Constant pressure P_th/k ~7.6e6 K cm-3; constant density n ~3.9e4 cm-3  
  > "our PDR model gives a density of $n\sim 3.9\times 10^4$ cm$^{-3}$ at the cloud edge" (3.2 PDR Models)
   - parameter: G0 ~1e3 in Habing units at 1.8 pc from the star  
  > "This yields $G_0 \sim 10^3$ in units of the Habing field" (3.2 PDR Models)
   - parameter: Cosmic-ray ionization 3.3e-17 s-1 per H; metals 1/5 Galactic; A_V/N_H 5.35e-23 cm2  
  > "$A_V/N_{\rm H}= 5.35\times10^{-23}$ cm$^{2}$ from (cite: Gordon2024)" (3.2 PDR Models)
   - assumption: Steady-state chemistry, thermal equilibrium, fixed FUV spectral shape, homogeneous cosmic-ray flux  
  > "The abundances of the atomic and molecular species, as a function of depth into the cloud, are found from steady-state chemical balance" (3.2 PDR Models)
   - assumption: Distance of 1.8 pc from the star to the PDR boundary gives an upper limit on G0  
  > "This assumes the minimum distance between the PDR and star and gives an upper limit to" (footnote 5)
   - fitting: No formal fit; pressure adjusted within uncertainties to match the observed DF-to-[CI]/CO peak spacing  
  > "A formal fitting procedure is not warranted given the limited comparison (DF to C and DF to CO peak locations), and the large number of additional model parameters." (3 Methods)
   - validation: Best-fit pressure within uncertainties of the estimated H II region thermal pressure; constant-density match would need a pressure 7 times higher than observed  
  > "The best-fit pressure is only $\sim 35\%$ higher than (and consistent within the uncertainties of)" (5.1 Comparison to PDR Models); "Matching the constant density peak separations would require a pressure 7 times higher than what is observed in the HII region, making it physically unrealistic." (3.2 PDR Models)
7. **Test inclination effects**: Scale model layer separations by sin(i) for inclinations 0-90 deg and find inclination that matches observed separations  
  > "scaling the separations of different layers by $\sin(i)$ for 0–90$^\circ$ inclinations $i$ between the line-of-sight and the PDR surface" (5.3 Inclination and Geometry Effects)
   - assumption: Near edge-on geometry from HST imaging  
  > "inconsistent with the visual appearance in HST data showing an approximately edge-on geometry" (5.3 Inclination and Geometry Effects)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Constant-density model needs unrealistic ~80 deg inclination  
  > "requires an unrealistic inclination of $\sim$ 80$\ensuremath{^\circ}$ to match the observed separations" (5.3 Inclination and Geometry Effects)
8. **Test for sub-resolution clumps with CO peak temperature**: Radial profile of CO 2-1 peak temperature map from the PHANGS pipeline compared with emission-weighted gas temperature of the constant-pressure model  
  > "We compare the peak temperature radial slice to the emission-weighted gas temperature" (5.4 Clumpy PDRs)
   - software: PHANGS-ALMA pipeline
   - assumption: A peak temperature well below model gas temperature would indicate a low filling factor from unresolved clumps  
  > "this may indicate that the CO emission is not filling the entire beam, likely due to a low filling factor caused by unresolved clumps" (5.4 Clumpy PDRs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Observed ~34 K peak is only ~1 K above model prediction  
  > "only $\sim 1 K$ more than the model predictions for the line" (5.4 Clumpy PDRs)
9. **Compare CO-dark H2 column with a Galactic PDR**: Use the constant-pressure model DF-to-C/CO separation to get CO-dark H2 column density and compare with a Galactic PDR at the same G0 and pressure  
  > "We find that a Galactic PDR with the same incident FUV field and thermal pressure would have a CO-dark gas column density" (5.5 CO-dark H2)
   - assumption: Model depth not tied to a specific cloud model, so no unique N(H2) or X_CO  
  > "Therefore we cannot calculate a precise value of $X_{\rm{CO}}$." (5.5 CO-dark H2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CO 2-1, CO 3-2 and [CI] 1-0 peaks are co-spatial at the resolution of the data, so only upper limits on their separation are given  
  > "Because the separations between the ${}^{12}$CO J=2$-$1, ${}^{12}$CO J=3$-$2, and \[CI\] 1$-$0 layers are unresolved, we quote an upper limit on their spacing" (4.1 Observed Structure of N13)
- H2 2.12 micron shows two peaks interpreted as two dissociation fronts; the analysis uses the first  
  > "reveal two distinct intensity peaks that we interpret as two separate DFs" (4.1 Observed Structure of N13)
- [CI] 1-0 gives no improvement over CO as a tracer of CO-dark H2 in N13  
  > "there are no significant improvements in using \[CI\] 1$-$0 over CO to trace CO-dark H$_2$" (5.5 CO-dark H2)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: N13 chosen for its apparent edge-on PDR geometry in H-alpha HST imaging, which maximizes angular separation of the layers  
  > "We selected the region based on the appearance of an edge-on geometry for the PDR in narrowband H$\alpha$ observations from Hubble (cite: Smidge)." (2.1 Target)
- Why this method: Peak-location comparison via emissivities instead of a formal fit, because only two spacings are compared and many model parameters exist  
  > "A formal fitting procedure is not warranted given the limited comparison (DF to C and DF to CO peak locations), and the large number of additional model parameters." (3 Methods)
- Alternative: Constant-density model rejected because matching the separations needs a pressure 7 times higher than in the H II region, or an unrealistic ~80 deg inclination  
  > "Matching the constant density peak separations would require a pressure 7 times higher than what is observed in the HII region, making it physically unrealistic." (3.2 PDR Models)
- Alternative: Clumpy PDR structure considered, not fully excluded  
  > "we cannot dismiss the possibility of a clumpy structure based solely on the observed spacing of the \[CI\] and CO species" (5.4 Clumpy PDRs)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The second 12m configuration for Band 8 [CI] has not been observed, giving lower than planned S/N  
  > "The lack of 12m data yields lower than planned S/N, but does not affect the observed peak location which is the focus of this paper." (footnote 2)
- Limitation (authors): Model intensities depend on viewing angle and optical depth; only emissivities are compared  
  > "but the absolute line intensities depend on the viewing angle" (4.2 Distances Between PDR Boundaries)
- Limitation (authors): No unique H2 column density or X_CO can be derived because the model depth is not tied to a molecular cloud model  
  > "The plane-parallel model depth extends past the observed emission peaks but the total depth is not tied to a specific molecular cloud model" (5.5 CO-dark H2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single slice and one DF peak (the first of two) define the main number; results depend on this choice and on peak identification by eye
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model pressure is tuned to the observed DF-to-CO spacing, so the 'match' is partly by construction; only two spacings compared, no formal fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO peak is not exactly at the model C/CO boundary and CO/[CI] are unresolved at 0.27-0.33'' beams
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stated uncertainty on the Pa-alpha peak differs between text (0.013 pc) and table (0.018 pc); stat error described as from 'four adjacent slices' in one place and '5 slices' in another
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO-dark column factor of 5 is model-derived, not measured
