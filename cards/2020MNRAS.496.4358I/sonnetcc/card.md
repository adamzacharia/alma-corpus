## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image Band 3 data**: Automatic ALMA Science Pipeline calibration, tclean imaging of each channel with natural weighting; continuum maps from line-free channels; continuum-free cubes  
  > "The data were automatically calibrated using the ALMA Science Pipeline (part of the Common Astronomy Software Applications package – CASA)" (2 ALMA observations and data reduction); "Continuum maps were also created, using channels devoid of line emission" (2 ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, ALMA Science Pipeline, tclean
   - parameter: Natural weighting; beam 3.0 x 2.2 arcsec^2 at PA 91 deg  
  > "Natural weighting was used to maximise sensitivity" (2 ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Search cubes for line emitters and extract spectra**: Searched from continuum positions, then the full cubes; spectrum extracted from the spatial pixel with brightest line emission  
  > "we expanded our line search to the full cubes. For each source, the final spectrum was extracted from the spatial pixel with the brightest line emission." (2 ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Line emitters confirmed by faint IRAC counterparts; D' tentative at 8 sigma in the zeroth moment map  
  > "This confirms the reality of these line emitters, if it were ever in doubt" (3 Results)
3. **Measure continuum and line properties**: Continuum positions, peak and total flux densities; Gaussian fits to CO(4-3) for flux, FWHM and velocity offset from z=4.002  
  > "Properties of Gaussian fits to the three CO(4–3) line emitters that are associated with the protocluster" (Table 2)
   - parameter: Systemic redshift z=4.002  
  > "taken here to be $z=4.002$" (Table 2)
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits; sampler/priors not stated  
  > "Properties of Gaussian fits to the three CO(4–3) line emitters" (Table 2)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Identify Spitzer IRAC counterparts**: Overlay ALMA continuum and moment-0 contours on IRAC 3.6 um images; IRAC data combined with MOPEX standard pipeline  
  > "The data were combined with MOPEX using the standard pipeline." (3 Results)
   - software: MOPEX
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate molecular gas masses**: Two methods: CO(4-3) luminosity with r41 and alpha_CO, and 3 mm dust continuum extrapolated to rest-frame 850 um to give dust then gas mass  
  > "We estimate the molecular gas mass in the components related to the DRC using two methods, via the 3-mm continuum and the CO(4–3) line emission." (4.1 Gas mass estimates)
   - parameter: r41 = 0.41, alpha_CO = 3.5, delta_GDR = 135, T_MW = 25 K, kappa_850 = 0.065 m^2/kg, T_d = 38 K, beta = 1.8  
  > "Gas masses from CO assume $r_{41} = 0.41$ (cite: ivison11; bothwell13; cw13) and $\alpha_{\rm CO}=3.5$." (Table 3); "Gas masses from dust assume $\delta_{\rm GDR}=135$, mass-weighted dust temperature, $T_{\rm MW}=25$ K" (Table 3)
   - assumption: CMB dimming not applied; maximum correction of 1.5x for dust quantities, negligible for CO(4-3) from warm dense gas  
  > "We suggest a maximum correction of $1.5\times$ for the dust properties $L_{850}$ and $M_{\rm gas}{\rm (dust)}$" (Table 3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CO-derived and dust-derived masses compared; CO masses higher by factors of 2, 1.14 and >1.8  
  > "the masses derived from CO(4–3) are higher on average than those using 3-mm dust continuum (by factors of 2, 1.14 and $>1.8$)" (4.1 Gas mass estimates)
6. **Estimate SFRs and depletion times**: SFR from L'CO(4-3) using log SFR = -9.94 + 1.08 log L' + 1.2; depletion times from gas mass over SFR  
  > "$\rm{log\, SFR = -9.94 +1.08\,log(L^{\prime}_{\rm CO(4-3)})+1.2}$" (4.2 SFRs)
   - assumption: SFR sensitive to the assumed IMF  
  > "where SFR is sensitive to the assumed IMF, as always" (4.2 SFRs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Interpret protocluster mass and timing**: Compare line-of-sight velocity offsets of new members with core members; estimate infall timescale for halos at ~100 arcsec  
  > "they should start to interact with the core on a timescale of $\approx 1$ Gyr given the distance they need to travel" (4.3 Protocluster mass)
   - parameter: Assumed infall velocity of order 1,000 km/s  
  > "if we assume an infall velocity of order 1,000 km s$^{-1}$" (4.3 Protocluster mass)
   - assumption: Lambda-CDM with Omega_m=0.3, Omega_Lambda=0.7, H0=70 km/s/Mpc; 1 arcsec = 7.0 kpc at z=4.0  
  > "we adopt a $\Lambda$-CDM cosmology with $\Omega_{\rmn m} = 0.3$, $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.7$ and $H_0 = 70$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Three new CO(4-3) members (C, D, E) of the DRC at z=4.002 found, plus a tentative companion D'; F and G show lines at other redshifts; H is spurious  
  > "We find new members of DRC in three SMG fields; in two fields, the SMGs are shown to lie along the line of sight towards DRC; one SMG is spurious." (abstract)
- Most newly confirmed members are not the brightest continuum emitters in their fields  
  > "the majority of newly confirmed members of the DRC protocluster are not the brightest 3-mm continuum emitters in their respective field" (4.4 On the timing puzzle)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Search for CO(4-3) towards the six dusty starbursts closest to DRC to test membership  
  > "by searching for the $^{12}$CO(4–3) emission line[^7] towards the six dusty starbursts closest to DRC, components C–H, to test whether they belong to the same structure" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: D' as a fast outflow from D, or both triggered by a fly-by: outflow argued against because emission is compact and does not extend back to D  
  > "The emission is relatively compact and does not extend back to D, which argues against the outflow hypothesis." (3.1 Component D)
- Alternative: Mass of several 1e14 Msun virialised system at 100 arcsec scale judged unlikely  
  > "This seems unlikely, however." (4.3 Protocluster mass)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Individual dust temperatures cannot be estimated for these sources  
  > "We do not have sufficient data to estimate dust temperatures individually for these sources" (4.1 Gas mass estimates)
- Limitation (authors): Dynamical mass extension to C, D, E is far more uncertain  
  > "is far more uncertain" (4.3 Protocluster mass)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas masses depend on assumed alpha_CO, r41, gas-to-dust ratio and dust temperature, and are uncorrected for CMB effects
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spurious/line-of-sight conclusions rest on single lines whose identity (for F and G) is unknown
- Caveat (inferred, ours): D' is only a tentative 8 sigma moment-0 detection
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source selection (six LABOCA sources only) limits any statement about the protocluster's full extent
