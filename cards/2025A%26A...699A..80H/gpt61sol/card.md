## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine calibrated observations**: Concatenate calibrated datasets into one measurement set; retain separate fields and SPWs for combination during imaging.  
  > "We combined the calibrated datasets into a single measurement set (*ms*) used to create the images and data cubes, using the task `concat`." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "it ensures that all individual field and spectral windows (SPWs) are kept separately in the concatenated *ms*." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - software: CASA, concat
   - parameter: CASA 6.5.2; default freqtol and dirtol.  
  > "This task and the following procedure are performed using version 6.5.2 of CASA software." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "For the concatenation process, we opted for the default values for the frequency (`freqtol`) and direction (`dirtol`) tolerances." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify emission and subtract continuum**: Inspect an initial tapered mfs image; identify line ranges in sideband cubes; form line-excluded continuum images; subtract detected central continuum.  
  > "a $uv$-taper to 1 arcsec to identify the presence of detections manually." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "Pure continuum images were then created by avoiding the frequency ranges where the line was identified." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "In the case of continuum emission detected at the position of the central CRISTAL galaxy, we then subtract the continuum using `uvcontsub` and `fitorder` = 0." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: Initial mfs: Natural weighting, 1 arcsec taper; sideband cubes: Natural weighting, 40 km s−1.  
  > "create an initial *mfs* using natural weighting and a $uv$-taper to 1 arcsec" (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "Then, we created data cubes using natural weighting and a channel width of 40 km s$^{-1}$ for each of the sidebands." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - fitting: uvcontsub fitorder = 0.  
  > "we then subtract the continuum using `uvcontsub` and `fitorder` = 0." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Create spectral and matched-beam products**: Image continuum-subtracted data at several channel widths and weightings; generate additional circularized continuum and line products.  
  > "Subsequently, we generated spectral cubes with resolutions of FWHM/5, 20 km s$^{-1}$, and 10 km s$^{-1}$." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "We then selected the maximum value of BMAJ in those products and used it to circularize the beams for the continuum and spectral cubes." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean, auto-multithresh
   - parameter: Velocity coverage −1000 to +1000 km s−1 relative to ALPINE [C ii] frequency.  
  > "All these cubes cover a velocity range from $-1000$ km s$^{-1}$ to $+1000$ km s$^{-1}$" (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "relative to the reference frequency of the \[C ii\] emission as defined by the source in the ALPINE catalog (cite: rhc_lefevre20)." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - parameter: Natural; Briggs robust = 0.5; Natural with 1″ taper.  
  > "The cubes were created using three different weighting schemes: $(i)$ natural weighting, $(ii)$ Briggs weighting with `robust` = 0.5, and $(iii)$ natural weighting combined with a $uv$-taper of 1$''$." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - parameter: auto-multithresh thresholds 2, 4.5, 2, 0.0; nsigma = 1; σ = 1.4826 × MAD.  
  > "`Sidelobethreshold` = 2, `Noisethreshold` = 4.5, `Lownoisethreshold` = 2, and `Minbeamfrac` = 0.0." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "the cleaning was performed down to 1$\sigma$ by selecting `nsigma` = 1" (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "$\sigma = 1.4826\times {\rm MAD}$ with MAD being the median absolute deviation" (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - assumption: JvM correction not applied; impact expected to be minimal after deep cleaning.  
  > "the products presented here are not corrected for the “JvM effect” (cite: rhc_jorsater95; rhc_czekala21)." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "the impact of this effect is expected to be minimal because the data were cleaned deeply" (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Few 4.5σ noise peaks; cleaning them did not change the background-noise distribution.  
  > "We checked that the number of 4.5 noise peaks in the cubes was low and that the background noise distribution did not change by cleaning them." (Combination, calibration, and imaging)
4. **Measure gas intensity and velocity structure**: Generate moments 0, 1 and 2 using FWTM integration and a second dilated-mask method; apply the smoothed-cube mask to the original cube.  
  > "We generated moment maps for integrated intensity, intensity-weighted velocity, and velocity dispersion (moments 0, 1, and 2)." (Moment maps); "In the first version we integrated the line emission over a frequency range defined by the full width at tenth maximum (FWTM)." (Moment maps); "we then fed to casa task `immoments` to obtain the intensity, velocity, and velocity dispersion maps from the original cube." (Moment maps)
   - software: Python, CASA, pickCellSize, immoments
   - parameter: Natural cube, 20 km s−1 channels; smoothing σ = 100 km s−1 and spatial σ = 10 pix; mask threshold 2 × rms measured in signal-free regions.  
  > "Starting from the naturally-weighted cube binned in 20 km s$^{-1}$ channels" (Moment maps); "we convolved with a $\sigma=100$ km s$^{-1}$ Gaussian kernel along the velocity axis, and a $\sigma = 10$ pix - 2D Gaussian kernel in the spatial axes" (Moment maps); "Finally, we split cells above and below a $2\times {\rm rms}$ threshold into a 3D mask" (Moment maps)
   - parameter: Beam-dependent pixel size from pickCellSize, npix = 7.  
  > "the pixel size in arcseconds depends on the beam size of the cube, and it was determined using the CASA extension tool `pickCellSize` with `npix`=7" (Moment maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CRISTAL-01c detection confirmed through its NIRCam stellar counterpart.  
  > "we confirmed as a real detection after identifying its stellar counterpart in NIRCam imaging of the field." (Moment maps)
5. **Measure global linewidths**: Fit global [C ii] spectra with a single Gaussian for reported FWHM; identify systems needing a second component.  
  > "Based on a single Gaussian fit, we measure the FWHM of the \[C ii\] line." (Linewidths and fluxes); "The global \[C ii\] spectra of all CRISTAL galaxies can be well fitted by a single Gaussian component, except four: CRISTAL-02, -04, -06 and -22." (Linewidths and fluxes)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian for tabulated linewidths; broad plus narrow components for CRISTAL-02; double Gaussian for CRISTAL-22.  
  > "Based on a single Gaussian fit, we measure the FWHM of the \[C ii\] line." (Linewidths and fluxes); "The \[C ii\] line profile in CRISTAL-02 is best modeled by a double Gaussian fit including a broad and a narrow component" (Multi-wavelength view of individual CRISTAL galaxies); "exhibiting a broad \[C ii\] line profile that requires fitting with a double Gaussian profile." (Multi-wavelength view of individual CRISTAL galaxies)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Measure line and continuum fluxes**: Compare circular-aperture curves of growth, elliptical Gaussian fits and threshold integration; adopt aperture photometry for Table 1.  
  > "we used circular apertures of increasing size to measure the integrated flux and construct a flux curve-of-growth." (Linewidths and fluxes); "Using the CASA tool *imfit*, we measured the flux fitting a two-dimensional elliptical Gaussian" (Linewidths and fluxes); "based on the aperture photometry method." (Linewidths and fluxes)
   - software: astropy photutils, CASA, imfit
   - parameter: Natural-weighted integrated intensity maps; aperture radius at curve flattening or companion-induced increase.  
  > "To measure the \[C ii\] and continuum fluxes, we use the Natural-weighted integrated intensity maps" (Linewidths and fluxes); "We identified the radius where the curve-of-growth flattens out or where an increase in flux due to the presence of a companion galaxy is detected." (Linewidths and fluxes)
   - parameter: Threshold method: 2σ on integrated map, restricted to Gaussian fitting box.  
  > "we restricted our flux measurements to the area within the fitting box defined for the two-dimensional Gaussian fit method." (Linewidths and fluxes); "For our measurements, we used a $S/N$ threshold of 2$\sigma$ on the integrated map." (Linewidths and fluxes)
   - fitting: Elliptical Gaussian with source-dependent fitting box; minimize companion contribution; Condon flux-error formalism.  
  > "choosing a fitting box with a variable size depending on the source size." (Linewidths and fluxes); "For interacting systems, we defined the fitting box to minimize any additional flux contribution from the companion galaxy." (Linewidths and fluxes); "The error in the flux measurement was calculated following the formalism described by (cite: rhc_condon97)." (Linewidths and fluxes)
   - validation: Methods generally agree within 10%; four interacting components have approximately 20% higher threshold fluxes; ALPINE and visibility-plane comparisons also performed.  
  > "The \[C ii\] and continuum flux measurements obtained using the three methods are consistent within 10%, with the exception of CRISTAL-04b, -07a, -07b, and -09b." (Linewidths and fluxes); "The CRISTAL fluxes are consistent with those measured from the low-angular resolution ALPINE data reported by (cite: rhc_bethermin20), with discrepancies within $\sim15\%$" (Linewidths and fluxes); "agree on average within a $\sim10\%$ and $\sim20\%$ with the fluxes measured in (cite: rhc_ikeda25) and (cite: rhc_mitsuhashi24)." (Linewidths and fluxes)
7. **Prepare ancillary stellar imaging**: Retrieve, calibrate, resample, align and mosaic HST images; use DJA PRIMER-COSMOS mosaics or process other JWST data with artifact removal and corrected astrometry.  
  > "The pipeline automatically retrieves, calibrates, and re-samples the individual raw exposures that overlap with the target coordinates for each filter." (HST); "For the CRISTAL galaxies in the PRIMER-COSMOS field, we used the data products from the Dawn JWST Archive (DJA) Mosaic release v7 (cite: rhc_heintz25)" (JWST/NIRCam); "For the rest of the CRISTAL systems, we processed the data using a modified version of the official JWST pipeline (version 1.10.0, pmap 1075)." (JWST/NIRCam)
   - software: grizli, JWST pipeline, Dawn JWST Archive
   - parameter: JWST mosaic pixel scale 0.03″; default square kernel; pixel fraction 1.0.  
  > "The final mosaic was drizzled to a pixel scale of $0.03\ensuremath{''}$ with the default square kernel and pixel fraction of 1.0." (JWST/NIRCam)
   - parameter: JWST astrometry: 3D-HST aligned to Gaia DR3 as absolute reference.  
  > "we first use the 3D-HST catalog and align it to Gaia DR3, then use the astrometry-corrected 3D-HST catalog as the absolute reference catalog for the JWST pipeline" (JWST/NIRCam)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: HST alignment uncertainty about 0.1″.  
  > "leading to an uncertainty of about 0.1$''$." (HST)
8. **Adopt stellar and dust properties**: Use literature CIGALE stellar masses/SFRs, FIR luminosities and dust continuum sizes; use published stellar-clump SED properties.  
  > "The stellar masses and star formation rates were derived using the CIGALE code (cite: rhc_boquien19), and the details for these calculations are described in (cite: rhc_mitsuhashi24)." (Final CRISTAL sample); "FIR luminosities of CRISTAL galaxies are drawn from (cite: rhc_mitsuhashi24)." (The \[C ii\]/FIR ratio in CRISTAL galaxies and systems across redshift); "The physical properties of the stellar clumps are derived based on the modeling of the stellar light SED presented in (cite: rhc_lines24)." (CRISTAL-13: Burst of star formation and offset with \[C ii\] line emission)
   - software: CIGALE
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF normalization.  
  > "All stellar masses and SFRs are normalized to a (cite: rhc_chabrier03) initial mass function (IMF)." (Introduction)
   - fitting: CIGALE SED fitting; details in Mitsuhashi et al.  
  > "The stellar masses and star formation rates were derived using the CIGALE code (cite: rhc_boquien19), and the details for these calculations are described in (cite: rhc_mitsuhashi24)." (Final CRISTAL sample)
   - validation: Comparison with MAGPHYS and ALPINE LePhare estimates: mean difference approximately 0.1 dex.  
  > "by (cite: rhc_li24) using MAGPHYS (including JWST/NIRCam data)" (Final CRISTAL sample); "those in the ALPINE database (cite: rhc_faisst20) based on the LePhare SED fitting code (cite: rhc_arnouts99; rhc_ilbert06)" (Final CRISTAL sample); "with a mean difference of only $\sim0.1$ dex between the estimates." (Final CRISTAL sample)
9. **Compare gas, dust and stellar morphology**: Compare integrated [C ii], velocity fields, dust continuum and HST/JWST composites at consistent physical scales; identify companions, disturbances, tails and offsets.  
  > "each panel shows maps of: \[C ii\] integrated intensity (moment 0), \[C ii\] velocity field (moment 1), ALMA Band 7 dust continuum, composite HST/WFC3 imaging, and composite JWST/NIRCam imaging of the stellar light." (ALMA, HST, and JWST View of the CRISTAL Galaxies); "To ensure consistent comparison, all maps are scaled to the same physical size." (CRISTAL Family Portrait)
   - assumption: Flat universe: ΩM = 0.3, ΩΛ = 0.7, H0 = 70 km s−1 Mpc−1.

- Gas morphologies and kinematics include disks, mergers, disturbed systems and tails; [C ii] often extends beyond stellar light.  
  > "The survey reveals diverse morphologies and kinematics, including rotating disks, merging systems, \[C ii\] emission tails from potential interactions, and clumpy star formation." ## Reasoning

- Why this data: Known [C ii] detections supply reliable redshifts and fluxes; redshift selection places [C ii] in Band 7; combined configurations target resolved and diffuse emission.  
  > "the need of pre-selecting galaxies with a reliable redshift determination and a measured \[C ii\] line flux that allows us to plan follow-up spatially-resolved observations." (Sample selection); "was set by the requirement to observe the \[C ii\] transition in Band 7." (Sample selection); "while at the same time continue to be sensitive to large-scale ($\sim5\ensuremath{''}$ or $\sim15$ kpc), more diffuse \[C ii\] line and dust emission" (Observations)
- Why this method: Automasking chosen to match manual cleaning; dilated masks recover narrow companions and emission outside the main-target FWTM range.  
  > "This value choice is intended to match similar results when using manual cleaning." (Combination, calibration, and imaging); "The dilated-mask maps are particularly valuable for identifying systems within the \[C ii\] cubes that exhibit line emission outside the frequency range used to generate the FWTM moment maps" (Moment maps); "They are also effective in detecting companion systems with a central frequency within the FWTM frequency range but with significantly narrower \[C ii\] line profiles." (Moment maps)
- Alternative: Three flux estimators compared; aperture photometry adopted.  
  > "*Aperture photometry:*" (Linewidths and fluxes); "*Two-dimensional Gaussian fit:*" (Linewidths and fluxes); "based on the aperture photometry method." (Linewidths and fluxes)
- Alternative: CRISTAL-10 deficit mechanisms include non-PDR FIR from high ionization parameter or AGN, and optically thick/self-absorbed [C ii]; cause remains unclear.  
  > "significant non-PDR contributions to the FIR emission due to a high ionization parameter $U$" (CRISTAL-10: Arp 220 conditions at $z\approx5$?); "optically thick or self-absorbed \[C ii\] line emission due to the high FIR optical depth" (CRISTAL-10: Arp 220 conditions at $z\approx5$?); "the primary cause of the observed \[C ii\] deficit remains unclear" (CRISTAL-10: Arp 220 conditions at $z\approx5$?)
- Alternative: CRISTAL-13 eastern offset may involve dust obscuration; western suppression is also consistent with low metallicity and dust content.  
  > "One possibility is that this offset is caused by dust obscuration" (CRISTAL-13: Burst of star formation and offset with \[C ii\] line emission); "in regions with low gas metallicity and dust content, UV and optical light from young stars escapes without significant reprocessing, and the \[C ii\] is suppressed." (CRISTAL-13: Burst of star formation and offset with \[C ii\] line emission); "This scenario is also consistent with our observations of the western component of CRISTAL-13."   > "detect ($\gtrsim4\sigma$) and spatially resolve in \[C ii\] line emission each CRISTAL galaxy with at least $\sim$4 independent beams within one \[C ii\] effective radius."
