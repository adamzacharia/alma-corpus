## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image visibilities**: Standard calibration scripts, CASA cleaning with natural weighting; continuum subtracted using uvcontsub; manual cleaning of cubes  
  > "Raw ALMA visibility data were calibrated using standard calibration scripts specifically created for each Execution Block." (3 ALMA Observations and Reductions)
   - software: CASA, plotms
   - parameter: 10.16 km/s channels, 0.12" pixels, natural weighting  
  > "The spatial binning was 0.12$''$  square per pixel" (3 ALMA Observations and Reductions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Phases and amplitudes checked with plotms  
  > "Checks were made for irregularities in the phases and amplitudes of the visibility data as a function of time and channel (frequency), using *plotms* software." (3 ALMA Observations and Reductions)
2. **Make CO moment maps and spectra**: Masked-moment technique with smoothed cube and threshold at 1.5 times rms  
  > "showing the emission above a level of 1.5 times the rms noise" (4 Results)
   - software: makeplots IDL, CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure CO fluxes and continuum fluxes/upper limits**: Aperture photometry with aperture incremented until no additional flux; continuum 3sigma upper limit = 3 x sqrt(Nbeams) x Noise_rms  
  > "as a 3 sigma upper limit of (3 $\times$ sqrt(Nbeams) $\times$ Noise$_{rms}$)" (4 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: 6 percent flux calibration error added in quadrature  
  > "Errors account for random errors and 6 percent calibration source errors added in quadrature" (4 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive molecular gas masses and gas-to-dust ratios**: L_CO from Solomon & vanden Bout eq. 3; M_mol = 1.36 alpha_co L21 / r21; compared to MAGPHYS dust masses  
  > "M_{mol}=1.36 \alpha_{co} {\rm{L21}}(r_{21})^{-1}" (4.1 CO)
   - assumption: r21=1.0±0.5; XCO=3e20 (alpha_co=4.8); factor 1.36 for helium; optically thin molecular gas  
  > "We assume a CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor X$_{CO}=3\times 10^{20} \pm 1\times 10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$ (from Young et al. 2011)." (4.1 CO)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compare ALMA continuum to H-ATLAS SED extrapolation**: Modified blackbody with beta=2 fixed, normalised to H-ATLAS 250 micron flux, extrapolated to 1.3 mm  
  > "A conservative approach was taken by fixing $\beta=2$ in the modified black body to avoid overestimating fluxes at 1.3mm." (4.2 Continuum)
   - assumption: Dust may follow molecular gas distribution; Sersic index 1 for surface-brightness test  
  > "Assuming a sersic index of 1 for the surface brightness profile" (4.2 Continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Extended rotating CO disks in three ETGs; continuum mostly undetected, likely resolved out or surface brightness limited  
  > "There is a lack of associated, extended continuum emission in these ALMA observations, most likely because it is resolved out or surface brightness limited" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Select the dustiest ETGs with sizes matched to ALMA dynamic range  
  > "leading to predicted sizes of gas and dust distributions that were expected to match well with the spatial dynamical range available with ALMA" (2 Sample Selection)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Tapering GAMA64646 data gave no continuum detection; collisional heating of dust is an untested alternative  
  > "however, this also led to no continuum detection in the cleaned image" (4.2 Continuum)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Mmol has extra 33% (XCO) and 50% (r21) scale uncertainties; assumes optically thin gas  
  > "A further 33 percent uncertainty in M$_{mol}$ comes from uncertainty in X$_{CO}$ and 50 percent uncertainty from CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) ratio." (4.1 CO)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample of five; CO extents estimated by eye
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LAS comparable to or smaller than CO extents, so flux recovery is uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Offset CO line identification as CO(2-1) uncertain
