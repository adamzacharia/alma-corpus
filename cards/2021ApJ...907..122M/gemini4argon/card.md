## Analysis recipe

1. **Search for emission lines in dirty cubes and assess completeness**: Bin non-primary-beam-corrected dirty cubes to 80, 140, 300, and 750 km s^-1; subtract continuum using iterative 2-σ clipped channel average per pixel; divide each channel by its full-field rms to create SNR cubes; search for peaks with SNR > 6.2 and velocity-integrated SNR > 8; test completeness by injecting 1.2 x 10^7 simulated Gaussian lines into pure noise cubes.  
  > "To provide sensitivity to a wide range of line widths, we bin these data cubes to four different velocity resolutions (80, 140, 300 and 750 km s _[−]_ [1], see Cooke et al. 2018)." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS); "In the binned, continuum-subtracted SNR cubes, we detect six line emitters with peak SNR _>_ 6 _._ 2." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS); "We have checked the completeness of our line search by simulating 1 _._ 2 _×_ 10 [7] Gaussian-like emission line with the range of the FWHMs of 80–2000 km s _[−]_ [1] and luminosities of 10 [7] _[.]_ [5] –10 [10] _[.]_ [1] _L_ _⊙_" (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS)
   - software: CASA 5.6.1
   - parameter: Velocity bin widths: 80, 140, 300, 750 km s^-1  
  > "To provide sensitivity to a wide range of line widths, we bin these data cubes to four different velocity resolutions (80, 140, 300 and 750 km s _[−]_ [1], see Cooke et al. 2018)." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS)
   - parameter: Peak SNR threshold > 6.2; velocity-integrated SNR > 8 within ±0.5 FWHM  
  > "In the binned, continuum-subtracted SNR cubes, we detect six line emitters with peak SNR _>_ 6 _._ 2." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS); "We measure the SNR of the emission lines from velocity integrated maps between _ν_ obs _−_ 0 _._ 5 _×_ FWHM and _ν_ obs + 0 _._ 5 _×_ FWHM at the peak pixels. ## Reasoning

- Why this data: Wide-field ALMA Band 7 snapshot follow-up of a complete SCUBA-2 sample (AS2COSMOS) covers 1.6 deg^2 (2.2 x 10^6 cMpc^3), enabling statistical study of rare z > 4 SMGs via the bright [C II] 157.74 µm line.  
  > "To understand the formation process of _z_ _>_ 4 SMGs in the cosmological context, we need to study their statistical properties based on wide-field spectroscopic survey of _z_ _>_ 4 SMGs." (1. INTRODUCTION); "The [C ii] line is suitable for spectroscopic identifications and studies of gas dynamics of _z_ _>_ 4 SMGs at submillimeter wavelengths (e.g., Iono et al. 2006; De Breuck et al. 2014; Jones et al. 2017)." (1. INTRODUCTION)
- Why this method: Searching in non-primary-beam-corrected dirty cubes provides uniform spatial noise; iterative 2-σ clipped channel averaging avoids oversubtraction compared to linear fits; dirty cubes are used for flux measurements because the line SNRs are not high enough to require cleaning.  
  > "We start from the dirty cubes without primary beam correction, which have uniform noise levels across each field." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS); "We check that the 2- _σ_ clipped average is more stable than a linear fit without oversubtraction." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS); "As the SNRs of our detected lines are not high enough to require cleaning, we measure the line properties using the dirty, primary-beam-corrected flux data cubes." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS)
- Alternative: Linear fit and median for continuum estimation (2-σ clipped average chosen as more stable than linear fit without oversubtraction; median agreed within 10%)  
  > "We check that the 2- _σ_ clipped average is more stable than a linear fit without oversubtraction. We also confirm that continuum levels estimated from a median agree with those from 2- _σ_ clipped average within 10%" (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS)
- Alternative: Identification of AS2COS0034.1/34.2 emission lines as low/mid/high-J CO or z ~ 9 [O III] (rejected based on S_870µm/S_3GHz flux ratios and observed-frame equivalent widths)  
  > "Thus AS2COS0034.1/34.2 are unlikely to be low- or mid- _J_ CO line emitters at _z_ _≪_ 3 _._ 0." (3.1. Line identification); "Thus AS2COS0034.1/34.2 are unlikely to be high- _J_ CO line or [O iii] emitters." (3.1. Line identification)
- Alternative: Strong lensing interpretation of AS2COS0034.1/34.2 as multiple images of a single galaxy (rejected due to different velocity peaks and different line EWs)  
  > "We note that different velocity peaks and different EW of the emission lines (see Figure 2 and Table 1) suggest that these two sources are not strongly-lensed multiple images of a single galaxy." (3.2. Gravitational lensing)
- How they decided it was enough: Adopted peak SNR > 6.2 threshold because zero negative peaks exceed -6.2σ in the 184 inverted cubes; injection of 1.2 x 10^7 simulated lines showed ~100% completeness above L_[C II] = 3 x 10^9 L_sun.  
  > "We adopt this 6 _._ 2- _σ_ threshold on the grounds that no false detection appear in any of the inverted data cubes above this significance. This means that there is no negative peak exceeding _−_ 6 _._ 2 _σ_ in any of the 184 SNR cubes we searched." (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS); "From this analysis we find that the completeness is almost unity above _L_ [C ii] = 3 _×_ 10 [9] _L_ _⊙_" (3. ANALYSIS AND RESULTS)
- Limitation (authors): Excluded AS2COS0002.1 because its emission line falls at the edge of the spectral window, preventing measurement of total line flux or velocity width.  
  > "line flux or line velocity width of the emission line as it falls at the edge of the spectral window" (3. Analysis and results)
- Limitation (authors): Only lower limits can be placed on the rotation velocities and halo masses for AS2COS0001.2 and AS2COS0034.2 due to large inclination uncertainties.  
  > "We note that we can only place lower limits on the halo masses for AS2COS0001.2 and 34.2 due to the larger uncertainties of their inclination measurements." (3.4. Dark matter halo mass)
- Limitation (authors): Applicability of the flat rotation curve assumption (V_rot equal to halo circular velocity) to SMGs is uncertain.  
  > "Since it is not clear that the assumption of the flat rotation curve is applicable to our SMGs, we check the consistency with different methods for the halo mass calculation." (3.4. Dark matter halo mass)
- Limitation (authors): Common halo masses estimated with an NFW profile are lower limits because line-of-sight separations and transverse velocities are ignored.  
  > "Since we ignore the line-of-sight separations and the velocities in the transverse direction, the estimated halo masses with NFW profile are lower limits." (3.4. Dark matter halo mass)
- Limitation (authors): The [C II] number density derived from the full sample of five SMGs is only a lower limit because the parent sample is continuum-selected (S_850µm >= 6.2 mJy) and misses fainter-continuum [C II] emitters; if AS2COS0034.1/34.2 are not [C II] emitters, the lower limit drops to 40% (2/5).  
  > "As our continuum survey is complete to the level of 6 _._ 2 mJy, the number density of the full sample of five SMGs gives only a lower limit due to the missing fainter-continuum [C ii] emitters in our survey volume." (4.1. Nature of high-redshift SMGs); "We note that if AS2COS0034.1/34.2 are not [C ii] emitters, then the number density corresponding to our quoted lower limit should be reduced to 40% (2/5)." (4.1. Nature of high-redshift SMGs)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Two of the five analyzed sources (AS2COS0034.1 and AS2COS0034.2) lack independent spectroscopic confirmation from a second transition and remain candidate [C II] emitters.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): At 0.8'' (~5 kpc) resolution, velocity gradients in merging or interacting systems can mimic rotating disks, and beam smearing can bias deconvolved axial ratios and inferred inclinations.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Equating the [C II] disk rotation velocity at radii of 2-3 kpc directly to the virial circular velocity of the dark matter halo (r_vir = 70-110 kpc) without full dynamical modeling (e.g., baryon contribution vs. dark matter) can overestimate halo masses.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Duty cycle and cosmic SFRD estimates rely on small-number statistics (2 to 5 sources) drawn from a single overdense structure, making them susceptible to cosmic variance across the narrow redshift slices probed by the Band 7 tuning.
