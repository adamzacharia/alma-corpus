## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the line cubes**: Calibration by ALMA/NAASC staff, self-calibration on continuum, uvconsub, CLEAN with Briggs weighting  
  > "After continuum subtraction with the CASA `u`vconsub function, the data were CLEANed (cite: hogbom1974) using a $3 \sigma$ noise threshold and Briggs weighting" (2.3 Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5 (0 for V4046 Sgr)  
  > "Briggs weighting with a robustness parameter of 0.5 for all the lines." (2.3 Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Apply Keplerian mask and extract moment maps, spectra, radial profiles, and integrated fluxes**: Keplerian mask from beam, velocity resolution, stellar mass and disk geometry; fluxes integrated to R_sigma (~33% of peak CS) and R_max  
  > "the Keplerian mask selects the disk area in which the line emits within each channel, given the beam size, velocity resolution, stellar mass, and disk geometry, and assuming that the disk is flat." (2.3 Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Disk inclination, PA, stellar mass from Table 1  
  > "The disk and star parameters used to build the Keplerian masks are listed Table [\[tab:disk-params\]](#tab:disk-params)." (2.3 Data reduction)
   - assumption: Flat disk; mask padded to include flaring layers  
  > "However we pad the mask sufficiently to avoid this and have validated it against CO emission" (2.3 Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared against CO emission (Pegues et al. in prep.)  
  > "have validated it against CO emission (Pegues et al. in prep.)" (2.3 Data reduction)
3. **Rotational diagram fits for CS and H2CS column density and Tex**: LTE, optically thin rotational diagram with optical depth correction; MCMC posterior of Tex and Ntot from disk-integrated fluxes  
  > "We then used the likelihood function $\mathcal{L}(N_u,\ensuremath{T_{{\rm ex}}})$ described in appendix [7](#app:Nu_SnuDv) with the Python implementation `emcee`" (3.3.1 Rotational diagram analysis)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: CS fluxes integrated to 150 au (MWC 480) and 200 au (LkCa 15)  
  > "we use the disk flux densities $S_\nu \Delta v$ integrated out to 150 au and 200 au in the MWC 480 and LkCa 15 disks respectively" (7 Building rotational diagrams)
   - assumption: LTE (Tex = Tkin) and optically thin lines; beam filling factor of unity  
  > "First, these calculations assumed a beam filling factor of unity." (3.3.2 Isotopologue ratio analysis)
   - assumption: H2CS ortho-to-para ratio of 3  
  > "assuming the statistical ortho-to-para ratio of 3" (3.4 H2CS in MWC 480 and LkCa 15)
   - fitting: emcee affine-invariant ensemble MCMC; CS priors Tex U(3,300) K, Ntot U(1e7,1e20) cm-2; H2CS priors Tex U(10,100) K, Ntot U(1e7,1e20)  
  > "$$\begin{eqnarray}
    \ensuremath{T_{{\rm ex}}}(\rm{K}) = \mathcal{U}(3,300)\\
    N_{\rm {tot}} (\cc) = \mathcal{U}(\dix{7},\dix{20}).
\end{eqnarray}$$" (3.3.1 Rotational diagram analysis)
   - validation: Compared with least-squares fit; optical depths computed to check thin assumption  
  > "Random draws and results from the posterior distributions are depicted in gray in Fig. [5](#fig:CS-rot-dia), along with the least square fit results, shown in blue." (3.3.1 Rotational diagram analysis)
4. **Isotopologue ratio analysis of opacity (LkCa 15)**: Brightness temperature ratios of 13CS and C34S to 12CS with uv-tapered 12CS, assuming LISM isotope ratios; derive Tex, tau and Ntot  
  > "The ratio of the two isotopologues $^{12}$CS and $^{13}$CS can be expressed as:" (3.3.2 Isotopologue ratio analysis)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 68±15 and 32S/34S = 24.4±5.0; same Tex and emitting volume  
  > "i.e. that their has been no isotopic fractionation, $R\simeq 68\pm15$." (3.3.2 Isotopologue ratio analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Results cross-checked between 13CS and C34S methods and rotational diagram  
  > "similar to the rotational diagram result." (3.3.2 Isotopologue ratio analysis)
5. **Estimate CS column densities for full disk sample**: Adopt Tex=25±5 K (average of MWC 480 and LkCa 15) and convert single-line fluxes to Ntot  
  > "We adopt the average of the two, 25 K, as the typical disk-averaged CS rotational temperature, and an uncertainty of $\pm5$ K." (3.3.3 CS column density estimates for the disk sample)
   - assumption: Tex = 25 K for disks with a single CS line  
  > "we use the constraints on the CS rotational temperature in the LkCa 15 and MWC 480 disks and apply them to the disk sample." (3.3.3 CS column density estimates for the disk sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Disk chemistry modeling and comparison to observed column densities**: Nautilus v1.1 three-phase gas-grain 1+1D models with parametric physical structure, 0D cloud initial conditions, sulfur abundance 8e-8, 1 Myr  
  > "We ran the disk chemistry models for one million years." (4.2 Chemical modeling)
   - software: Nautilus
   - parameter: Disk parameters in Table 3 (e.g. Md=0.18 and 0.03 Msun, Rc=100 au)  
  > "| Disk mass: $M_{\rm{d}}$ ($M_\odot$) | 0.18 | 0.03 |" (Table 3)
   - assumption: Dust temperature equals gas temperature; dust-to-mass ratio 0.01; 0.1 micron grains; low S abundance 8e-8  
  > "The dust temperature is assumed equal to that of the gas." (4.1.1 Temperature profile); "A dust-to-mass ratio of 0.01 is assumed, with spherical grains of $0.1~\mu$m single radius size." (4.2 Chemical modeling)
   - fitting: No fitting; models not tuned  
  > "We note that the purpose of these models is not to quantitatively fit observations" (4.2 Chemical modeling)
   - validation: Model column densities, temperatures and profiles compared with observations  
  > "Across the disks, the model predictions are generally within a factor of a few for both MWC 480 and LkCa 15" (4.3 Model results)

## Qualitative findings

- CS radial profile centrally peaked in 5/6 disks; MWC 480 shows central dip; V4046 Sgr has a CS ring at ~100 au at the dust continuum edge  
  > "the CS radial emission profile is centrally peaked in 5/6 disks; the only exception is the disk around Herbig Ae star MWC 480" (3.1 CS fluxes and emission morphologies)
- First detections in disks of 13CS, C34S and H2CS lines  
  > "We also present the first detection in PPDs of $^{13}$CS $6-5$ (LkCa 15 and MWC 480), C$^{34}$S $6-5$ (LkCa 15)" (abstract)
- Model underpredicts H2CS and mispredicts relative abundance in LkCa 15 vs MWC 480  
  > "Furthermore the model predicts that more H$_2$CS should be present toward LkCa 15 compared to MWC 480, which is opposite to what we observed." (4.3 Model results)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Rotational diagram used to jointly derive Tex and column density from two CS lines; LTE justified by critical densities  
  > "we can simultaneously determine the excitation temperature and column density using a rotational diagram analysis" (3.3 CS column densities and excitation temperatures)
- Alternative: Isotopologue-ratio analysis (optically thick CS) gave discordant column densities; rotational diagram adopted for model comparisons  
  > "In the meantime we adopt the results from the rotational diagram analysis" (3.3.2 Isotopologue ratio analysis)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Low resolution and S/N cannot distinguish optically thick CS from isotopic enhancement or beam dilution; LTE and filling factor assumptions uncertain  
  > "Additional lines and higher spatial resolution observations are needed to distinguish between these scenarios." (abstract)
- Limitation (authors): Models simplistic: no X-rays, grain growth, LkCa 15 inner cavity; not tuned  
  > "we do not include LkCa15’s wide inner cavity (cite: alencar2018) in the LkCa disk structure." (5.1 Model predictions vs observations)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only two CS lines in two disks constrain Tex; sample-wide Tex is assumed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uncertainties from flux integration radius and Keplerian mask not propagated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Isotopic ratios assumed from LISM
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model depends on an assumed low sulfur abundance chosen by hand
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam dilution unaddressed quantitatively
