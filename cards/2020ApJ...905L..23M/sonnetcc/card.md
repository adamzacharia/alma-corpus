## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA continuum**: ALMA pipeline (CASA 5.4.0) plus manual scripts; tclean MFS imaging in CASA 5.6.1 with Briggs robust=0.5, nterms=2 (Bands 3, 4) or 1 (Bands 5, 6), no multiscale  
  > "Calibrated data were imaged using the task TCLEAN in CASA version 5.6.1." (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction and Imaging); "weighting with ROBUST=0.5" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction and Imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Band 6 continuum built from 3 dedicated continuum spws plus line-free part of the fourth spw  
  > "we imaged the 3 dedicated continuum spectral windows and the line-free portion of the fourth spectral window" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image 12CO(2-1) cube and make moment 0 map**: tclean in cube mode with multiscale clean; moment 0 by integrating over velocity range with line emission  
  > "The moment 0 map was then created by integrating the spectral cube at each pixel limited to the portion of velocity space associated with line emission." (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction and Imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: multiscale clean for scales ~1 and 3 times synthesized beam FWHM  
  > "to search for structures with scales $\approx$1 and 3 times the FWHM of the synthesized beam" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Beam-match and measure flux densities at all frequencies**: Convolve all images to the 3 GHz beam (imsmooth), fit an elliptical Gaussian with imfit inside an aperture of radius equal to the beam major FWHM; total flux = geometric mean of peak and integrated flux; 3 sigma upper limits for non-detections  
  > "the CASA task IMFIT was used to fit an elliptical Gaussian to the emission within a circular aperture having a radius equal to the FWHM of the synthesized beam major axis" (2.3 Photometry); "we take the total flux density" (2.3 Photometry)
   - software: CASA, imsmooth, imfit
   - parameter: Aperture radius 1.85'' (beam-matched 3 GHz beam major axis)  
  > "of the beam matched images (i.e., 1.85" (2.3 Photometry)
   - parameter: Source treated as unresolved if fitted major axis is not at least 2 sigma larger than beam major axis  
  > "be at least $2\sigma_{\phi_{\rm M}}$ larger than the FWHM of the synthesized beam major axis" (2.3 Photometry)
   - assumption: Geometric mean of peak and integrated flux chosen because their uncertainties are anti-correlated  
  > "the uncertainties on the peak brightness and integrated flux density from the elliptical Gaussian fitting are anti-correlated (cite: jc97)" (2.3 Photometry)
   - fitting: Elliptical Gaussian fit with IMFIT  
  > "the CASA task IMFIT was used to fit an elliptical Gaussian to the emission" (2.3 Photometry)
   - validation: 1.5 GHz IMFIT detection rejected after inspecting the map and treated as a noise feature; 203 GHz kept because the fit converged and a source appears at the location  
  > "it is clear from that map (see Figure [1](#fig:radpan)) that there is nothing co-spatial with NGC 4725 B and that a nearby noise feature is being fit." (2.3 Photometry)
4. **Fit radio-to-millimeter SED with free-free, synchrotron, AME and dust model**: Parametric model of free-free, free-free-absorbed synchrotron power law, log-normal AME and modified blackbody dust; MCMC sampling; default model fixes A_d = 0 (six parameters)  
  > "We employ a simple parametric model to fit the NGC 4725 B spectrum presented in Figure" (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum); "we consider this our default model and quote parameter constraints from a six parameter fit fixing $A_d = 0$" (3.2 Model Fits)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: T_e fixed at 10^4 K  
  > "Given the limited number of data points and the relative insensitivity of the spectrum to $T_e$, we fix $T_e = 10^4$ K." (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum)
   - parameter: alpha = -0.83 fixed for synchrotron  
  > "We therefore adopt $\alpha = -0.83$" (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum)
   - parameter: beta = 1.55 and T_d = 19.6 K fixed for dust  
  > "we fix $\beta = 1.55$ and $T_d = 19.6$ K" (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum)
   - parameter: Seven sampled parameters: EM, D, A_s, A_AME, W_AME, nu_p, A_d  
  > "Markov Chain Monte Carlo software (cite: emcee) to sample the seven parameters" (3.2 Model Fits)
   - assumption: Pure free-free absorption of synchrotron, AME and dust; optically thin synchrotron power law  
  > "We model the optically-thin synchrotron emission as a power law and the absorption as pure free-free absorption" (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC with broad uninformative priors: EM, D, A_s, A_AME, A_d positive; 10 < nu_p/GHz < 50; 0 < W_AME < 1; likelihood not stated  
  > "We use broad, uninformative priors, requiring only that $EM$, $D$, $A_s$, $A_{\rm AME}$, and $A_d$ be positive, that $10 < \nu_p/{\rm GHz} < 50$, and that $0 < W_{\rm AME} < 1$." (3.2 Model Fits)
   - validation: Chi-squared of the A_d = 0 fit compared with the seven-parameter alternative; 1.5 GHz point excluded from fits  
  > "$\chi^2 = 9.0$ for a six parameter fit to eleven data points" (3.2 Model Fits)
5. **Derive CO luminosity and molecular gas mass**: Integrate CO(2-1) line luminosity of nearest CO feature; convert with a CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) ratio of ~0.8 and alpha_CO ~4.3; 3 sigma upper limit at the position of NGC 4725 B  
  > "(cite: svb05; bwl13), the total nearby molecular gas reservoir is measured to be" (3.3 Associated Molecular Gas); "the corresponding upper limit on the molecular gas mass is" (3.3 Associated Molecular Gas)
   - assumption: CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) brightness temperature ratio ~0.8; alpha_CO ~4.3 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "brightness temperature ratio of $\sim$0.8 (cite: heracles09; lzk18)" (3.3 Associated Molecular Gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Infer cluster properties and star formation efficiency**: Use free-free fraction at 14 GHz to get Q(H0), convert to number of O7V stars and stellar mass with a Kroupa IMF, then SFR, gas depletion time and free-fall efficiency  
  > "Assuming that $\approx 50$% of the 14 GHz flux density arises from free-free emission" (4.1.1 Cluster Properties); "This implies a gas depletion time of $t_{\rm dep} \gtrsim 113$ Myr." (4.1.2 Molecular Gas Properties)
   - assumption: Nearby CO complex mass (1.2e6 Msun) taken as the mass of the cloud NGC 4725 B formed from; 100 pc gas layer thickness  
  > "Assuming a constant 100 pc thickness of the molecular gas layer for this CO complex" (4.1.2 Molecular Gas Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with LEGUS cluster masses and the Whitmore et al. YMC classification  
  > "NGC 4725 B is classified as a stage 3" (4.1.1 Cluster Properties)
7. **Compare AME to millimeter dust emission with Galactic values**: Compute tau_353 limit from the 353 GHz dust flux upper limit and compare the AME-to-tau_353 ratio with the Galactic typical value; also fit only the 203 and 221 GHz data with a modified blackbody  
  > "Fitting just the 203 and 221 GHz ALMA observations with the modified blackbody dust model, we derive a 95% upper limit of 520" (4.1.3 Physical Constraints on AME)
   - assumption: Dust temperature 20 K and 1.85'' beam  
  > "For an assumed dust temperature of 20 K and 1.85" (4.1.3 Physical Constraints on AME)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- NGC 4725 B is detected in the continuum at all four ALMA frequencies, marginally at 203 GHz, but molecular gas is not detected at its exact location  
  > "NGC 4725 B is detected in the continuum at all frequencies, although the detection at 203 GHz is marginal." (abstract); "While molecular gas is not detected at the exact location of NGC 4725 B" (abstract)
- There is no indication of millimeter dust emission associated with NGC 4725 B at the ALMA sensitivity

- Why this method: A simple parametric model was used because of limited data points  
  > "We employ a simple parametric model to fit the NGC 4725 B spectrum presented in Figure" (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum)
- Alternative: Fit with significant thermal dust emission (A_d free) gives a poorer chi-squared and is retained as an alternate scenario  
  > "the quality of the fit is poorer even with the addition of the extra fitting parameter" (3.2 Model Fits)
- Alternative: 1.5 GHz IMFIT detection rejected as a noise feature; newer VLA 15 GHz data and 2013 March 33 GHz data excluded in favor of the 14 GHz archival data  
  > "Consequently, we consider this as a non-detection" (2.3 Photometry)
- Alternative: Background gigahertz-peaked spectrum radio galaxy considered implausible  
  > "Thus, such a scenario seems implausible." (4.2 A Background Source?)
- How they decided it was enough: The model without millimeter dust emission is the maximum likelihood solution and is adopted as default  
  > "The model without millimeter dust emission is the maximum likelihood solution" (3.2 Model Fits)
- Limitation (authors): The AME functional form is only valid near the peak, not the tails  
  > "is unlikely to accurately represent the low or high frequency tails." (3.1 Parametric Models of the Radio-to-Millimeter Spectrum)
- Limitation (authors): ALMA interferometric data are insensitive to dust emission on scales larger than about 6 arcsec  
  > "it may be present only on much larger spatial scales that are invisible to the ALMA interferometric observations" (4.1.4 Lack of Millimeter Dust Continuum)
- Limitation (authors): More observations are needed to constrain the spectral shape above 30 GHz  
  > "More observations are needed to better constrain the shape of the spectrum, particularly at frequencies above 30 GHz." (3.2 Model Fits)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Few free-free and dust parameters are fixed (T_e, beta, T_d, alpha) so conclusions depend on those assumptions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only eleven flux densities constrain a six or seven parameter model, giving bimodal posteriors
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux calibration uncertainties of the ALMA and VLA data are not described in the SED fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam-matching to the 3 GHz beam of ~1.85'' discards the higher-resolution information at ALMA bands
- Caveat (inferred, ours): AME-to-dust ratio relies on an extrapolated 353 GHz limit from sparse data
