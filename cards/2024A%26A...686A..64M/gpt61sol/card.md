## Analysis recipe

1. **Select the mass-complete star-forming sample**: Select G13 galaxies by redshift, stellar mass, UVJ classification and uniform-noise GOODS-ALMA coverage; define an undetected subset excluding GG22 sources and neighbors.  
  > "We, moreover, restrict the sample to SFGs as indicated by the $UVJ$ diagram (cite: Whitaker2011), which allows us to classify galaxies in quiescent or star-forming according to their rest-frame colors." (Sample selection); "This coverage is defined as the area where the noise is uniform across the map, excluding the edges of the outermost pointing, where there is no pointing overlap." (Sample selection); "We will refer to the subsample obtained when excluding these galaxies as the undetected data-set hereafter." (Sample selection)
   - software: Python, photutils
   - parameter: 1.0 ≤ z ≤ 3.0; 10^8–10^11 M⊙; 5,530 objects  
  > "Our final sample is thus composed of 5,530 star-forming objects located at $1.0\leq z\leq3.0$, with stellar masses ranging 10$^{8-11}$ M$_\odot$." (Sample selection)
   - parameter: Exclude sources within 5″ of GG22 objects for the undetected data-set  
  > "We select those galaxies closer than 5$''$ to any object included in GG22." (Sample selection); "We will refer to the subsample obtained when excluding these galaxies as the undetected data-set hereafter." (Sample selection)
   - parameter: Check individual undetected sources with r = 0.8″ aperture photometry  
  > "We measured the photometry of each of these objects using the aperture photometry method provided by `photutils` from `Python`, selecting an aperture radius $r=0.8\ensuremath{''}$." (Sample selection)
   - assumption: Chabrier2003 IMF  
  > "All M$_\star$ and SFR estimations refer to a (cite: Chabrier2003) initial mass function (IMF)." (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Test inclusion of 14 objects slightly above 10^11 M⊙; gas fractions change by about a hundredth.  
  > "If we include these 14 galaxies in the highest mass bin considered in this work, the f$_{\mathrm{gas}}$ barely change, showing only a variation of the order of a hundredth." (Sample selection)
2. **Characterize the sample and comparison populations**: Compare redshift, stellar mass, apparent optical colors and ΔMS after applying common mass and redshift cuts.  
  > "All these comparison samples are cut to only include galaxies within the same M$_\star$ and redshift intervals that we are considering in this work" (Properties of the sample and comparison with other catalogs); "In the fourth panel, we show the difference with respect to the MS for each galaxy in these samples, $\Delta$MS" (Properties of the sample and comparison with other catalogs)
   - parameter: M23 main sequence below 10^10 M⊙; B19 above  
  > "We use M23 to calculate the $\Delta$MS of galaxies with M$_\star$/M$_\odot<10^{10}$ and B19 for sources with higher stellar masses, given that M23 focuses on the low-mass end of the MS." (Properties of the sample and comparison with other catalogs)
   - parameter: Additional color comparison in Δz = 0.5 bins at M⋆ > 10^10 M⊙  
  > "We thus decided to cut it in 0.5 redshift bins and select galaxies with $>10^{10}$ M$_\odot$, which allows a direct comparison with the other catalogs." (Comparison remarks)
   - fitting: Use the published M23 and B19 main-sequence fits  
  > "we include the M23 fit, defined for $1.5<z<2.0$, up to $10^{10}$ M$_\odot$, and the (cite: Barro2019) MS fit, B19 hereafter, above $10^{10}$ M$_\odot$." (Properties of the sample and comparison with other catalogs)
   - validation: Control for mixed redshifts and stellar masses in the color comparison  
  > "the latter results concerning the blue optical colors of our galaxies can be a consequence of mixing different redshifts and M$_\star$ when producing the color-color diagram." (Comparison remarks); "We thus decided to cut it in 0.5 redshift bins and select galaxies with $>10^{10}$ M$_\odot$, which allows a direct comparison with the other catalogs." (Comparison remarks)
3. **Register and stack the ALMA cutouts**: Correct G13 centroids for the HST–ALMA offset, extract low-resolution mosaic cutouts and median-stack by redshift and stellar mass; also compute mean stacks.  
  > "After defining the bins, we stack 50$\times$50 arcsec$^2$ (1,000$\times$1,000 pixel$^2$) cutouts within the low-resolution ALMA mosaic, centered at each source and using the coordinates of the centroids provided by G13." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements); "Before the stacking, we corrected these centroids for a known offset between the HST and ALMA data" (Stacking analysis and flux measurements); "We opted for median stacking galaxies instead of mean stacking them." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
   - parameter: Four redshift bins: 1.0–1.5, 1.5–2.0, 2.0–2.5, 2.5–3.0  
  > "4 redshift bins: $1.0\leq z<1.5$, $1.5\leq z<2.0$, $2.0\leq z<2.5$, and $2.5\leq z\leq3.0$" (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
   - parameter: Three log M⋆/M⊙ bins: [8,9), [9,10), [10,11]  
  > "3 M$_\star$ bins, 8$\leq$log M$_\star/$M$_\odot<$9, 9$\leq$log M$_\star/$M$_\odot<$10, 10$\leq$log M$_\star/$M$_\odot \leq$11" (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
   - parameter: 50 × 50 arcsec²; 1,000 × 1,000 pixels  
  > "we stack 50$\times$50 arcsec$^2$ (1,000$\times$1,000 pixel$^2$) cutouts within the low-resolution ALMA mosaic" (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
   - parameter: δR.A. = (0.011 ± 0.08)/3600 deg; δdecl. = (−0.26 ± 0.10)/3600 deg  
  > "We apply the correction from (cite: Whitaker2019), that corresponds to $\delta$R.A.(deg) = (0.011$\pm$0.08)/3600 and $\delta$decl.(deg) = ($-0.26\pm0.10)/3600$." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
   - assumption: Estimate required stack sizes using σ ∝ 1/√N and literature gas scaling relations  
  > "The estimation is based on the depth of the observations and the previous knowledge about the gas reservoirs in galaxies as given by the scaling relations derived in other works" (Stacking analysis and flux measurements); "we quantify the relation $\sigma$ $\;\propto 1/\sqrt{N}$ (with $\sigma$ being the resulting noise in the stacked map and $N$ the number of objects)" (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Stacked ALMA centroids agree with G13 within 0.06″; mean-stack results also calculated.  
  > "We checked that the centroids computed using the stacked emission in ALMA are compatible with those provided by G13, based on the HST imaging, within $0.06\ensuremath{''}$." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements); "Despite our choice, we computed the fluxes and further physical parameters from both, mean stacking and median stacking measurements." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
4. **Measure continuum fluxes and upper limits**: Measure r = 0.8″ aperture fluxes and correct with the dirty beam; for SNR > 3 remeasure at r = 1″; otherwise report three times the fitted sky standard deviation.

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Low-resolution images favor detections and flux measurements over resolving source extent.  
  > "This choice is motivated by our interest in detections and flux measurements, as opposed to resolving the extent of the sources." (Data)
- Why this method: Median stacking targets the bulk SFG population without bright-source bias and improves intermediate-mass SNR relative to mean stacking.  
  > "This choice is motivated by our aim to provide an estimate of the gas reservoirs of the bulk of the SFG population, not biased towards bright sources." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements); "the use of mean stacking reports lower SNR in 3 of the 4 redshift bins." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
- Alternative: Mean stacking computed as a comparison; median stacking adopted.  
  > "We opted for median stacking galaxies instead of mean stacking them." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements); "Despite our choice, we computed the fluxes and further physical parameters from both, mean stacking and median stacking measurements." (Stacking analysis and flux measurements)
