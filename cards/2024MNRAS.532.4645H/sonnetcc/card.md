## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract ALMA CO(2-1) and 1.3 mm continuum maps**: Archive Band 6 products visualised and turned into a flux map with CARTA, 2 sigma cut  
  > "We processed the data using the [CARTA](https://cartavis.org) software package and generated a flux map with a $2\sigma$ cut." (3.2 ALMA spectroscopy)
   - software: CARTA
   - parameter: rest frequencies 230.538 GHz (CO 2-1) and 232.538 GHz (1.33 mm continuum)  
  > "in rest frequencies of 230.538 GHz and 232.538 GHz corresponding to the CO (2-1) line and the 1.33 mm continuum emission respectively" (3.2 ALMA spectroscopy)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Reduce JWST/MIRI images with modified saturation handling**: JWST pipeline with the first-frame correction turned off for saturated pixels to gain a viable group for ramp fitting  
  > "by turning off the first frame correction for the saturated pixels, we gained an additional viable group" (2.1 Saturation)
   - software: JWST pipeline
   - parameter: JWST pipeline python package version 10.2, CRDS jwst_1097.pmap  
  > "processed using the JWST pipeline python package version 10.2 with CRDS reference files" (2 JWST Observations & Data reduction)
   - parameter: N_ints = 50, four dithers, 358 s per filter  
  > "A relatively large number of integrations ($N_{\rm ints} = 50$) and four dithered exposures yielded a final exposure time of 358 seconds in each filter." (2 JWST Observations & Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared X and Y profiles of the F1500W image between default and modified pipeline  
  > "Here, we compare the default pipeline with our modified approach." (2.1 Saturation)
3. **Register JWST astrometry to the ALMA continuum peak**: Align the target centroid of the images with the nuclear position taken as the peak of the ALMA 1.3 mm continuum, since tweak_registration fails when the galaxy fills the FOV  
  > "we corrected the astrometry by aligning the target centroid of our images with the AGN’s nuclear coordinates derived from ALMA" (2.2 Astrometry)
   - software: DAOStarFinder (default method that failed)
   - parameter: nuclear position RA = 07h 16m 31.26s, DEC = -29d 19' 28.85'' (ICRS)  
  > "we derive a nuclear position of $\rm RA = 07^h\, 16^m\, 31.26s$ and $\rm DEC = -29\degree \, 19'\, 28.85''$ (ICRS)" (2.2 Astrometry)
   - assumption: ALMA 1.3 mm continuum peak emerges from very close to the SMBH  
  > "By setting the nuclear position to be the peak in the ALMA 1.3 mm continuum (which emerges from very close to the SMBH)" (2.2 Astrometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Subtract the nuclear point source**: WebbPSF models per dither and band, drizzled with the same parameters as the science images, subtracted from the images  
  > "The individual PSF frames were drizzled into a single PSF model image for each band using the same image reconstruction parameters as the corresponding science images." (2.3 Point spread function)
   - software: WebbPSF
   - parameter: Point source accounts for 75% of the nuclear F560W flux and 57-61% in F1000W, F1500W, F1800W  
  > "The point source accounts for 75% of the F560W flux at the nucleus, 57-61% of the flux in the F1000W, F1500W and F1800W filters, and 35% of the flux in the F2100W filter." (2.3 Point spread function)
   - assumption: Time-dependent wavefront models and appropriate source SEDs  
  > "`WEBBPSF` allows the use of optimised PSFs including time-dependent wavefront distortion models and appropriate source spectral energy distributions (SEDs)" (2.3 Point spread function)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Check JWST flux calibration against Spitzer/IRS**: Convolve JWST images with the Spitzer PSF and perform aperture photometry within the Spitzer apertures  
  > "we convolved the JWST images with the Spitzer PSF[^2] to simulate Spitzer’s optical blurring effects." (2.5 Flux calibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: JWST photometry in good agreement with Spitzer/IRS low- and high-resolution SEDs  
  > "is in good agreement with the SED of the Spitzer/IRS from both low-resolution (navy) and high-resolution (turquoise) spectra" (2.5 Flux calibration)
6. **Measure region SEDs and estimate dust temperature**: Fixed-size aperture photometry of PSF-subtracted images in regions defined on the F1000W image; blackbody temperature from the five-band SED  
  > "We then examine and compare the MIR spectral energy distributions of these regions, measured in fixed sized apertures, to explore their properties." (4.2 MIR regions of interest)
   - parameter: Apertures larger than the F2100W PSF FWHM = 0.67 arcsec  
  > "we select apertures with diameters bigger than the PSF FWHM of the longest wavelength filter" (4.2 MIR regions of interest)
   - assumption: Simple black body  
  > "Assuming a simple black body, we estimate temperatures within the range $\sim 100-120\, \rm K$ for the regions in the extended emission." (4.2 MIR regions of interest)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Authors say values are approximate, based on five photometric points  
  > "These values serve as an approximation (based on five photometric points), and a more advanced fitting method is required to accurately estimate the temperature." (4.2 MIR regions of interest)
7. **Estimate and remove emission-line contamination in F1000W**: Scale SINFONI line maps of comparable ionisation potential (Br gamma for [S IV], H2 1-0 S(1) for H2 0-0 S(3)) to Spitzer/IRS line fluxes, ratio against the PSF-subtracted F1000W image, then subtract the boosted [S IV] map  
  > "First, we leverage the Spitzer/IRS SH spectrum (when possible, otherwise low spectral resolution) to estimate the total flux due to each line." (4.4 Emission line contamination); "Second, we use these estimates to scale a SINFONI line map of comparable ionisation potential (IP)" (4.4 Emission line contamination)
   - software: LINEFIT
   - parameter: Single-Gaussian fit of [S IV] in Spitzer/IRS SH gives total flux 7.564e-13 erg/s/cm2  
  > "yielding a total flux of $f_{\rm SIV}= 7.564 \times 10^{-13}\, \rm  erg/s/cm{^2}$" (4.4.1 [S IV] mock map)
   - parameter: [S IV] flux boosted by 4% for H2  
  > "we boosted the \[S IV\] flux by $4\%$ to account for the $\rm H_{2}$ contribution" (4.4.3 Pure dust continuum morphology)
   - assumption: Little to no star-formation contribution in Br gamma; extinction very low and ignored  
  > "we assume that there is little to no SF contribution in this line" (4.4.1 [S IV] mock map); "extinction, which is very low for this object and thus has no impact on the measurements made" (4.4 Emission line contamination)
   - fitting: Single-component Gaussian per spaxel (SINFONI, LINEFIT); single Gaussian for [S IV] in Spitzer/IRS  
  > "This single-component Gaussian fitting procedure was performed for each spaxel of the data cube." (3.1 VLT/SINFONI spectroscopy)
   - validation: Method validated against NGC 5728 (details in prep)  
  > "Our method has been validated against another nearby Seyfert from the GATOS survey, NGC 5728" (4.4 Emission line contamination)
8. **Test dust heating scenarios (AGN illumination, torus wind, jet shocks)**: Analytic estimates: Habing-field scaling G0 from L_bol and distance, equilibrium grain temperature T_d(a, G0) after Tielens, with shock radiative flux from Dopita and Sutherland; evaluated for NW2 at D ~ 100 pc and grain sizes 0.005-0.25 um (ISM) or 0.075-1 um (wind)  
  > "Assuming classical ISM dust grains within the range of $a= 0.005-0.25\, \rm \mu m$ (cite: DraineLee1984)" (5.2 Dust illuminated in the NLR); "Using Eqs. 1 & 2, but taking the larger grain sizes expected in the wind" (5.3 Dust originating from the torus)
   - parameter: L_bol,AGN = 5.5e42 erg/s from bolometric correction to L(2-10 keV)  
  > "we applied a bolometric correction according to Eq. 3 in (cite: 2020A&A...636A..73D) to the hard X-ray luminosity" (5.2 Dust illuminated in the NLR)
   - parameter: Shock v_s = 250 km/s, n = 4350 cm^-3, G0 = 653 F_T  
  > "we can express $F_{\rm T}$ in terms of the Habing field, so that $G_{0} = 653\, F_{T}$" (5.4 Dust heated by shocks)
   - assumption: AGN SED peaks in the UV like the Habing field; post-shock gas strongly radiative; shocks uniformly distributed  
  > "An important assumption here is that the intrinsic AGN SED that heats the dust peaks strongly in the UV, roughly like the local Habing field." (5.2 Dust illuminated in the NLR); "An important assumption here is that the post-shock gas is strongly radiative, radiating away all its thermalised energy efficiently." (5.4 Dust heated by shocks)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared predicted temperatures with the ~120 K measured in NW2  
  > "local shock heating is more likely to achieve the temperature of $\sim 120 \,\rm K$ that we find in the North-Western extended dust emission" (5.4 Dust heated by shocks)

## Qualitative findings

- The MIR polar structure is co-linear with the radio jet and perpendicular to the ALMA CO(2-1) gas lane that feeds the nucleus  
  > "This polar structure is co-linear with a radio jet and lies perpendicular to a molecular gas lane that feeds and obscures the nucleus." (abstract)
- Part of the MIR morphological match to ionised gas is emission-line contamination within the filter bands  
  > "We demonstrate that part of this spatial correspondence is due to contamination within the JWST filter bands from strong emission lines." (abstract)
- Regions D1 and D2, beyond the extended emission along a similar PA, have flat SEDs and are interpreted as star-forming regions  
  > "For this reason, we suggest that these features are star-forming regions." (4.2 MIR regions of interest)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Source selected for prior evidence of polar MIR emission; ALMA continuum chosen as a high-precision astrometric anchor  
  > "ESO 428-G14 was selected as it shows prior evidence of polar MIR emission from ground-based observations (cite: Asmus+16)." (1 Introduction); "leveraging its high-resolution and precision as a reliable astrometric reference" (2.2 Astrometry)
- Why this method: Line contamination had to be estimated indirectly because no JWST MIRI/MRS spectroscopy exists  
  > "The absence of any high-resolution JWST spectroscopy for ESO 428-G14, such as from the MIRI Mid-Infrared Spectrograph (MIRI/MRS), complicates this analysis" (4.4 Emission line contamination)
- Alternative: tweak_registration star-finding astrometry rejected because the galaxy fills the FOV  
  > "Given that the galaxy fills all of the field of view (FOV) in our images, this method proves ineffective." (2.2 Astrometry)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Temperature estimates rest on five photometric bands and a simple black body  
  > "These values serve as an approximation (based on five photometric points), and a more advanced fitting method is required to accurately estimate the temperature." (4.2 MIR regions of interest)
- Limitation (authors): Gas and dust structures cannot be cleanly separated without MIR spectroscopy  
  > "While it is challenging to disentangle gas and pure dust structures in the absence of MIR spectroscopy" (5.1 Dust beyond the torus)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Shock-heating temperature differs between text (154 K) and Table 1 (152 K)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mock line maps assume the Br gamma / H2 1-0 S(1) morphology matches [S IV] / H2 0-0 S(3); ionisation-potential differences could bias the dust-only image
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Analytic heating estimates are order-of-magnitude (single NW2 region, assumed grain sizes, no radiative transfer)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA data play only an astrometric and contextual role; CO and continuum processing details are not given
