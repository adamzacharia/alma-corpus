## Analysis recipe

1. **reduce MIRI-MRS spectrum and build continuum**: JDISCS reduction with empirical spectral response function, 1D extraction, then iterative smoothing continuum outside 12-16 micron; spline fit simultaneous with gas inside 12-16 micron  
  > "we use the JDISCS pipeline to replace the default fringe/throughput calibration with an empirical spectral response function derived from an asteroid calibrator" (2.1 JWST/MIRI-MRS); "The continuum is determined by iteratively smoothing the spectrum over fixed wavelength intervals to trace the underlying dust emission and interpolating between continuum-dominated points to construct a smooth baseline." (2.1 JWST/MIRI-MRS)
   - software: JDISCS pipeline, JWST Calibration software
   - parameter: 1D extraction with a wavelength-dependent aperture for each sub-band  
  > "The 1D spectrum is extracted with a wavelength-dependent aperture" (2.1 JWST/MIRI-MRS)
   - parameter: Interval excluded from continuum fit (CH4 absorption) 7.55-7.75 micron  
  > "we excluded specific intervals from the fit, namely, 7.55-7.75" (2.1 JWST/MIRI-MRS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **fit emission slabs (H2O, OH)**: LTE slab models in two intervals (15.5-18.8 and 18.8-27 micron), compared to continuum-subtracted spectrum with Gaussian likelihood; models extincted rather than data deredden  
  > "the slab models are computed in two MIRI/MRS wavelength intervals (15.5-18.8 and 18.8-27" (3.1 Emission); "Rather than correcting the observation for extinction, we extinct the models before comparing them with the observed spectrum." (3.1 Emission)
   - software: spectools_ir, emcee (affine-invariant MCMC sampler), HITRAN
   - parameter: 100 walkers, 10^4 steps; posterior medians with 16th-84th percentile intervals  
  > "We run the MCMC fitting routine using 100 walkers and" (3.1 Emission); "Quoted uncertainties are the central 68% credible intervals (16th-84th percentiles)." (3.1 Emission)
   - parameter: Flux uncertainties 2.5, 5 and 8 mJy in 15.5-20, 20-22, 22-27 micron  
  > "we adopt uncertainties of 2.5, 5, and 8 mJy for the 15.5–20, 20–22, and 22–27" (3.1 Emission)
   - assumption: Absolute extinction law of McClure (2009) with A_V=16 (from manara2015)  
  > "we use the absolute extinction law from (cite: McClure2009)" (3.1 Emission)
   - assumption: Intrinsic line broadening from thermal motions  
  > "The intrinsic line broadening of the slab models is assumed to arise from thermal motions" (3.1 Emission)
   - fitting: Affine-invariant MCMC ensemble sampler; Gaussian likelihood summed over two intervals; uniform priors plus a Gaussian prior (200 K, sigma 50 K) on the cold water temperature  
  > "We adopt a Gaussian likelihood, evaluated as the sum over the two spectral intervals" (3.1 Emission); "To stabilize the decomposition of multiple water emission components, we impose an additional Gaussian prior on the excitation temperature of the cold water component" (MCMC modeling details)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **fit absorption slabs with covering fraction**: LTE absorption slabs with a free covering fraction f_c; for 12-16 micron the continuum is a third-degree spline with eight free node fluxes fitted simultaneously with the gas  
  > "Because the spatial distribution of the absorbing gas relative to the continuum source is generally unknown, we allow the absorber to cover only a fraction of the continuum." (3.1 Absorption); "We select eight equally distributed wavelength points between 12–16" (3.1 Absorption)
   - software: spectools_ir, emcee (affine-invariant MCMC sampler), HITRAN
   - parameter: 100 walkers, 10^4 steps; posterior medians and 68% intervals  
  > "the fitting routines for absorption implement an MCMC algorithm to efficiently explore the parameter space, using 100 walkers and" (3.1 Absorption)
   - parameter: Fourth-degree spline gives the same parameters  
  > "We also note that using a fourth degree spline results in the same retrieved parameters." (3.1 Absorption)
   - assumption: Absorbing species coupled: 13CCH2 temperature and covering fraction within 10% of C2H2 (co-spatial)  
  > "we include a condition in our prior that enforces the covering fraction and temperature to be within 10% of the values for" (3.1 Absorption)
   - fitting: MCMC with uniform priors and physically motivated constraints on temperature, column density, covering fraction and spline coefficients  
  > "Uniform priors and physically motivated constraints are applied to all absorption model parameters" (3.1 Absorption)
   - validation: Degeneracy between N and f_c tested by showing three models with similar depth but different P, Q, R branch strengths  
  > "demonstrating that the degeneracy between $N_{\rm col}$ and $f_c$ is not global when multiple lines are considered" (3.1 Absorption)
4. **derive CO absorption from Keck/NIRSPEC**: Stack unblended 12CO lines, fit emission and absorption Gaussians, build empirical optical-depth spectrum, then fit LTE CO slab with f_c=1 using MCMC and MAP values  
  > "we first construct an empirical, pure-absorption spectrum and then fit it with LTE CO slab models." (3.2 Keck/NIRSPEC high-resolution CO analysis); "The retained per-line segments were combined into an inverse-variance-weighted stacked profile" (3.2 Keck/NIRSPEC high-resolution CO analysis)
   - software: HITRAN, emcee (affine-invariant MCMC sampler)
   - parameter: Absorption Gaussian FWHM 29.65 km/s used as convolution kernel  
  > "We adopted the observed FWHM of the absorption Gaussian (29.65 km s$^{-1}$" (3.2 Keck/NIRSPEC high-resolution CO analysis)
   - assumption: v=2-1 profile dominated by Keplerian rotation so only v=1-0 absorption used for column density  
  > "we restrict our subsequent analysis and column density determinations to the absorption observed in the" (3.2 Keck/NIRSPEC high-resolution CO analysis)
   - fitting: MAP temperature and column density, uncertainties from the 16th-84th percentile range of marginalised posteriors  
  > "the maximum-a-posteriori (MAP) temperature and column density" (3.2 Keck/NIRSPEC high-resolution CO analysis)
   - validation: MAP versus median choice affects CO column density by a factor of about 2, stated not to change conclusions  
  > "We emphasize that this result would not affect our overall conclusions" (MCMC modeling details)
5. **measure line velocity shifts**: Select unblended lines, shift to source frame, normalise, interpolate to a common velocity grid and take the median to make stacked profiles per species  
  > "We then compute the median of these normalized profiles to construct stacked line profiles for each species, which are used to compare their characteristic line velocity centroids." (4.3 Line shifts)
   - assumption: Systemic radial velocity -7.9 km/s  
  > "shift the spectrum to the source rest frame using a systemic radial velocity of" (4.3 Line shifts)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with JDISCS wavelength-calibration velocity precision from banzatti2024  
  > "Previous velocity resolution estimates reached with the JDISCS wavelength calibration reported standard deviations of 4.4 km/s at" (4.3 Line shifts)
6. **image extended MIRI line emission**: Subtract central point source using a PSF measured in 6 adjacent velocity channels, scale to the central peak, then make zeroth-moment maps  
  > "the central point source is subtracted by a point-spread function (PSF) measured in 6 velocity channels adjacent to the line." (2.1 JWST/MIRI-MRS); "The 0th moment line map is calculated by integrating over the full lines to" (2.1 JWST/MIRI-MRS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **model the SED for evolutionary stage**: Fit observed SED to a grid of pre-computed YSO radiative transfer models with sedfitter, disk required, envelope and ambient ISM optional; then select models near literature Teff, inclination, luminosity and A_V  
  > "For our fitting, we consider only the models that have a disk, but allow the ambient ISM and envelope to be optional." (SED Modeling)
   - software: sedfitter
   - parameter: 80% confidence on the Herschel upper limit  
  > "we allow for an 80% confidence on the upper limit" (SED Modeling)
   - assumption: Herschel flux extracted ~1.5 arcsec off-source treated as an upper limit on envelope emission  
  > "we take this to be an upper limit on the surrounding envelope emission" (SED Modeling)
   - fitting: chi-squared ranking of models by sedfitter  
  > "the remaining models are ranked by reduced" (SED Modeling)
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **compare column densities with IRS 46 and GV Tau N**: Compare maximum literature column densities, associated temperatures and the full range of literature ratios with the ISO-Oph 37 results  
  > "the column density ratio panel displays the full range of ratios permitted by the literature by combining the minimum and maximum reported column densities for each species" (4.1 Absorption)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Caveat that heterogeneous literature data make the comparison not strictly one-to-one  
  > "the comparison is not strictly one-to-one" (4.1 Absorption)

## Qualitative findings

- No detectable ice absorption features; only silicate absorption at 10 micron  
  > "with no detectable ice absorption features" (abstract)
- OH emission is prompt OH from H2O photodissociation, out of LTE, so no LTE columns derived  
  > "Because these lines are demonstrably out of LTE, we do not derive LTE column densities for OH" (4.2 Emission)
- Extended MIRI emission shows a slow wide-angle wind nested with a faster collimated jet  
  > "supports a nested morphology in which a fast, collimated jet is embedded within a slower, wider-angle wind" (4.4 Extended emission)
- SED does not distinguish a disk-only from an envelope-plus-disk configuration  
  > "the observed SED does not favor the absence or presence of an envelope, highlighting the evolutionary ambiguity of this source" (SED Modeling)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Keck/NIRSPEC added because MIRI-MRS cannot resolve CO profiles and only covers the CO band tail  
  > "we complement the JWST data with high-resolution" (3.2 Keck/NIRSPEC high-resolution CO analysis)
- Why this method: Covering fraction introduced because the spatial distribution of absorbing gas relative to the continuum is unknown; simultaneous spline continuum fit used to allow a pseudo-continuum in the 12-16 micron range  
  > "The continuum and molecular absorption are therefore fit simultaneously, allowing the fit to accommodate a pseudo-continuum if present." (3.1 Absorption)
- Alternative: Hot H2O emission slab (~900 K) dropped because it contributed negligibly  
  > "However, the hot water component contributed negligibly to the model flux and is therefore excluded from the final fit." (3.1 Emission)
- Alternative: UV-driven CO destruction scenario disfavoured by high shielding columns and the carbon-enhancement result  
  > "This result then favors the carbon enhancement scenario due to the sublimation of refractory carbon grains." (5.3 Presence and origin of excess carbon)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Comparison with IRS 46 and GV Tau N is not one-to-one because of heterogeneous data and models  
  > "As a result, the comparison is not strictly one-to-one, and systematic differences in wavelength coverage and modeling assumptions" (4.1 Absorption)
- Limitation (authors): Data cannot test the streamer scenario  
  > "However, the current data do not provide sufficient spatial or kinematic constraints to assess this scenario" (5.2 Evolutionary stage)
- Limitation (authors): Velocity-shift stacking for one species has only 3 lines and possible blending  
  > "As noted in Appendix" (4.3 Line shifts)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA data are used only as context from earlier papers, so no ALMA measurement here can be reproduced
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Species names are lost in the supplied text, so which molecule each slab number belongs to cannot be checked
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Table CO column (18.34) and text CO column (18.07) differ because of MAP versus median choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Key algorithms are in prep or submitted papers
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Covering fraction and column density are partly degenerate so hydrocarbon ratios mixing f_c=0.5 and f_c=1 fits are uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Literature columns from heterogeneous instruments are compared directly
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No quantitative chemical model supports the carbon-grain sublimation interpretation
