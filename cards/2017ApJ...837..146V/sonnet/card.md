## Analysis recipe

1. **Make continuum map and measure flux**: Average channels >=0.75 GHz from [C II]; taper to 1.9 arcsec for total flux; aperture photometry  
  > "created by averaging all channels at least 0.75 GHz away from the expected [C II ] emission line." (3.1. FIR Continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tapered peak consistent with aperture photometry  
  > "A similar value was derived when performing aperture photometry (see Figure 1)." (3.1. FIR Continuum)
2. **Measure [C II] line**: Tapered cube to 1.9 arcsec, extract peak-pixel spectrum, fit Gaussian+constant  
  > "we tapered the data cube to a beam of 1 [′′] . 9. We show the spectrum from the peak pixel in this cube, together with a Gaussian fit" (3.2. [C II ] Emission Line)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: Gaussian+constant  
  > "The red line is a Gaussian+constant fit to the spectrum." (Figure 2 caption)
   - validation: Compared with earlier PdBI flux  
  > "is consistent with the value of F [CII] = 1.03±0.14Jy km s [−][1]" (3.2. [C II ] Emission Line)
3. **Size and structure**: 2D Gaussian fits to continuum map and to [C II] map averaged over 600 km/s; aperture photometry; red/blue maps  
  > "we fitted a 2D Gaussian to the continuum map using the CASA task “imfit”." (3.4. Size and Structure of the Emission Regions)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian (imfit)  
  > "we fitted a 2D Gaussian to the continuum map using the CASA task “imfit”." (3.4. Size and Structure of the Emission Regions)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Upper limits on CO and [C I]**: uvcontsub, average over 400 km/s, 3 sigma limits corrected for line wings  
  > "we averaged the channels where we expected the CO(7-6) and [C I ] lines, based on the [C II ] redshift, over 400 km s [−][1]" (3.3. Limits on CO and [C I ] Emission)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Heating source and luminosities**: Intrinsic line fluxes corrected for CMB contrast; compare [C II]/[C I] with PDR/XDR models  
  > "F in = F obs /{1−B ν [T CMB (z = 7.0851)]/B ν [T ex ]}" (4.1. Origin of the Heating Radiation)
   - assumption: T_gas = T_dust; Td = 47, 41 or 30 K; constant dust temperature  
  > "In this paper we further assume thermody" (4.1. Origin of the Heating Radiation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **FIR luminosity, SFR, dust and gas mass**: Optically thin modified blackbody, FIR 42.5-122.5 um, TIR 8-1000 um, SFR from Murphy+2011; dust mass with kappa from Dunne+2000; gas mass from CO limit  
  > "integrating the dust SED from 42.5 µm to 122.5 µm, and taking the CMB into account" (4.2. Infrared Luminosity and Star-Formation Rates)
   - assumption: beta=1.6 or 1.95; alpha_CO = 0.8 Msun/(K km/s pc2); L'CO(1-0)=L'CO(2-1)  
  > "applying a conversion factor of α = M gas /L [′] CO(1−0) [=] [0][.][8][ M] [⊙] [(K km s] [−][1] [ pc] [2] [)] [−][1]" (4.3. Dust and Gas Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: [C II] SFR consistent with FIR SFR  
  > "Within the large uncertainties, this value is consistent with the one derived from the FIR continuum" (4.2. Infrared Luminosity and Star-Formation Rates)
7. **Dynamical mass**: Virial theorem Mdyn = 3R sigma^2/2G, R=4.3 kpc, sigma from tapered spectrum  
  > "M dyn = 3Rσ [2] /2G, with R the radius of the line emitting region, σ the velocity dispersion of the gas and G the gravitational constant." (4.4. Dynamical Mass Estimate)
   - assumption: No ordered rotation, so virial rather than disk model  
  > "From Figure 3 it is clear that in J1120+0641 there is no evidence for rotation on scales of ≳1 kpc. Instead, we here use the virial theorem to estimate a dynamical mass" (4.4. Dynamical Mass Estimate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Quasar is the only source detected in the ~25 arcsec ALMA field.  
  > "Within the field of view mapped by ALMA (∼25 [′′] ) the quasar is the only detected source." (5. SUMMARY)
- [C II] shows no ordered motion on kpc scales.  
  > "It therefore appears that the gas traced by [C II ] emission does not show rotation on scales of ≳1 kpc." (3.4. Size and Structure of the Emission Regions)

## Claim tuples

- H: Is the quasar host compact at 1 kpc resolution? | E: 2D Gaussian fits to continuum and [C II] maps | R: ~80% of flux in region of 1-1.5 kpc diameter | F: Host is very compact, with a ~20% extended component | **supported**
- H: Is the gas heated by star formation or the AGN? | E: [C II]/[C I] limit compared with PDR/XDR models | R: ratio >8.7 versus XDR maximum ~6 | F: Star formation dominates the heating | **supported**
- H: Does the [C II] gas show ordered rotation? | E: Red/blue channel maps | R: Red and blue peaks coincide with the continuum | F: No ordered motion, so a virial dynamical mass limit is used: 4.3e10 Msun, ~20x the black hole mass | **supported**
