## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and measure flux/size**: CASA CLEAN continuum images from line-free ranges, natural weighting; imfit to get fluxes and sizes  
  > "CASA *imfit* task was utilised to fit continuum images and to estimate the fluxes and the errors." (3 Observation and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: natural weighting; 50 km/s velocity bins  
  > "‘‘Natural’’ weight was adopted to create CLEAN images with spectral velocity bins of 50 km s$^{-1}$." (3 Observation and data reduction)
   - fitting: Gaussian fitting with imfit  
  > "CASA *imfit* task was utilised to fit continuum images and to estimate the fluxes and the errors." (3 Observation and data reduction)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract [CII] line flux**: Continuum-removed spectrum; Gaussian fit for velocity offset; integrate over +-125 km/s; imfit shows point source  
  > "The \[C ii\] 158 $\mu$m spectrum was integrated over $\pm$ 125 km s$^{-1}$ velocity width to estimate the emission line flux." (3 Observation and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: Gaussian line fit  
  > "The velocity offset between \[C ii\] 158$\mu$m and optical emission lines is 155 km s$^{-1}$ measured by a Gaussian fitting." (3 Observation and data reduction)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **SED fitting for SFR, dust and stellar properties**: MAGPHYS high-z version energy-balance SED fit to UV-FIR photometry; photometry beyond ~500 um and radio excluded  
  > "In this work, the photometries at the wavelength longer than $\sim$500$\mu$m were not used in order to avoid the possible contamination from the long-lived afterglows" (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - software: MAGPHYS
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; Bruzual & Charlot models; Charlot & Fall attenuation  
  > "The IMF is adopted from (cite: Chabrier2003)." (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - fitting: MAGPHYS best-fit templates  
  > "The best fit SED models are shown in Fig. [3](#fig3)." (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - validation: Compared with previous SFR/stellar mass/dust estimates  
  > "our measurement is consistent with the SFR$_{\rm IR}$ by (cite: Hunt2014) within the observational error." (5.1.2 GRB 080207 host)
4. **Derive LFIR and surface densities**: LFIR from LTIR via log ratio 0.24; Sigma = L/(pi r^2) with r = deconvolved FWHM/2 of band 8 major axis  
  > "The $\Sigma_{\rm TIR}$ and $\Sigma_{\rm FIR}$ were calculated as $L_{\rm TIR}$/($\pi r_{\rm IR}^{2}$) and $L_{\rm FIR}$/($\pi r_{\rm IR}^{2}$), respectively" (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - assumption: LFIR integrated over rest-frame 40-120 um  
  > "Here $L_{\rm FIR}$ is defined as a luminosity integrated between rest-frame 40 and 120 $\mu$m." (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compare with main sequence and [CII] deficit samples**: Place hosts on SFR-M* main sequence (Whitaker2014) and in L[CII]/LFIR vs LFIR, Sigma_FIR, LCO/LFIR planes; check afterglow contamination by extrapolating radio flux  
  > "the SFRs of the GRB 070521 and 080207 host galaxies derived from our analysis place them on the main sequence within observational uncertainties." (5.2 Main sequence)
   - assumption: Afterglow spectral slopes alpha=-1/2 to 1/3 (Sari1998)  
  > "assuming that the radio flux originates from the afterglow with typical spectral slopes, $\alpha$=$-$1/2 to 1/3, (cite: Sari1998)" (5.1.1 GRB 070521 host)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Both hosts lie on the z~2 main sequence; radio SFRs likely contaminated by afterglows.  
  > "Our SFRs are significantly lower than that derived from radio observations. This is probably due to the contamination of radio fluxes from the long-lived afterglows." (5.2 Main sequence)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: FIR is free from dust extinction and radio afterglow contamination  
  > "FIR emission is free from dust extinction and possible radio contamination from long-lived afterglows of GRBs." (abstract)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: AGN contribution as origin of the deficit and the radio excess considered but ambiguous  
  > "Therefore, the existence of the AGN is ambiguous from the BPT diagram." (5.1.1 GRB 070521 host)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Dust property uncertainties for 070521 host underestimated because only one FIR photometric point  
  > "uncertainties of dust properties of the GRB 070521 host, i.e., dust mass and dust temperature errors, are probably underestimated by MAGPHYS." (4 SED Modelling and results)
- Limitation (authors): [CII] detection only S/N~4; possible ALMA observational bias  
  > "the \[C ii\] deficit of the GRB 080207 host might be the observational bias toward the lower $L_{\rm [CII]}$/$L_{\rm FIR}$ ratio caused by the deep ALMA observation." (5.3.4 Observational bias)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): [CII] detection is marginal (S/N~4), so the deficit rests on a low-significance line
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only two hosts; the claim of a new GRB host property is weakly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single FIR point for 070521 makes MAGPHYS SFR error asymmetric and unreliable
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous comparison samples
