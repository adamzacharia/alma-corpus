## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, image and continuum-subtract the data**: CASA default pipeline calibration; natural-weighted tclean cubes (LSB and USB, 40 km/s channels, 0.1 arcsec pixels) cleaned to ~1 sigma inside two masks; MFS continuum from line-free channels; uvcontsub constant continuum fit; primary-beam correction; no self-calibration  
  > "We performed data reduction using the default calibration pipeline with the Common Astronomy Software Applications" (Observations and data reduction); "We obtained continuum images by combining the line-free channels from all spectral windows in multifrequency synthesis mode." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: cube channel width 40 km/s, LSB and USB cubes per setup  
  > "we produce two image-frequency cubes for the lower-side band (LSB) and the upper-side band (USB) respectively, with a channel width of $40\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$" (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: pixel size 0.1 arcsec  
  > "Therefore, we obtained cubes and continuum images with a pixel size of $0.1\si{\arcsecond}$." (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: line-free width 300 km/s adopted where no line is detected  
  > "we selected line-free channels by adopting a line width of $300\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ and source redshift based on the" (Observations and data reduction)
   - assumption: continuum constant with frequency within each ~4 GHz sideband (zeroth-order polynomial)  
  > "the continuum emission is well-described by a zeroth-order polynomial within the uncertainties" (Footnote 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract single-pixel spectra, fit lines and continuum, derive line luminosities**: Take the brightest continuum pixel of each band, extract the beam-integrated spectrum from the full cube, fit a constant plus Gaussian with emcee; luminosities from the Carilli and Walter relations  
  > "we identified the brightest pixel in the continuum map of each ALMA band and we extracted the (beam-)integrated spectra of the quasars and the companion galaxies from the full data cubes" (Line and continuum measurements); "We then fit each spectrum as a constant representing the continuum" (Line and continuum measurements)
   - software: emcee, CASA
   - parameter: best values and uncertainties from the 50th, 16th, 84th percentiles of the posteriors  
  > "we computed the best values and uncertainties from the 50th, 16th and 84th percentile of the distributions, respectively" (Line and continuum measurements)
   - parameter: L_line[Lsun] = 1.04e-3 S dv nu_obs D_L^2  
  > "L_{\rm line}\,[L_{\astrosun}]= 1.04\times10^{-3} S {\Delta \varv}\,\nu_{\rm obs}D_L^2" (Line and continuum measurements)
   - parameter: significance sigma = sqrt(dv FWHM) x <RMS>; 3 sigma upper limits assume FWHM = 300 km/s  
  > "$\sigma = \sqrt{\Delta \varv\,{\rm FWHM}} \times \left\langle{\rm RMS}\right \rangle$" (Line and continuum measurements); "we report $3\sigma$ upper limits assuming ${\rm FWHM} = 300\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$" (Line and continuum measurements)
   - assumption: sources unresolved or at best marginally resolved, so the peak pixel equals the integrated flux and no aperture correction is needed  
  > "Therefore, the targeted sources are (at best) marginally resolved in the observations." (Line and continuum measurements); "This allowed us to safely perform a single-pixel analysis of the data." (Line and continuum measurements)
   - fitting: emcee ensemble MCMC; free parameters are continuum level and Gaussian parameters; prior ranges not stated  
  > "and we obtained the posterior probability distributions of the free parameters" (Line and continuum measurements)
   - validation: Fits repeated with varied priors and starting points; all fits repeated at 80 km/s resolution (consistent within ~1 sigma); tapered highest-resolution data to the lowest resolution gave consistent fluxes  
  > "we performed different spectral fits by accurately varying the imposing priors and starting points in the MCMC fitting procedure" (Line and continuum measurements); "we performed all the fits to the data at a lower spectral resolution (${80\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}}$) and we verified that all derived parameters are consistent within ${\sim1\sigma}$" (Line and continuum measurements); "we tapered the highest spatial resolution data to the lowest one by using the option" (Line and continuum measurements)
3. **Fit dust SEDs and derive L_FIR, L_TIR and SFR_IR**: Optically thin modified blackbody with CMB contrast (da Cunha+2013) fitted to the continuum fluxes plus Decarli+2017 band 6 data; integrate over 40-400 um (FIR) and 8-1000 um (TIR); SFR_IR = 1.49e-10 L_TIR/Lsun  
  > "we modeled the continuum data with a modified black body taking into account the effect of the cosmic microwave background radiation" (Dust properties: FIR continuum); "we estimated the total (TIR, $8-1000\,{\rm \mu m}$, (cite: Sanders+2003)) and FIR ($40-400\,{\rm \mu m}$, (cite: Helou+1988)) rest-frame luminosities" (Dust properties: FIR continuum); "${\rm SFR_{IR}}/(M_{\astrosun}\,{\rm yr^{-1}}) = 1.49\times 10^{-10}L_{\rm TIR}/L_{\astrosun}$" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: box priors log Mdust > 0 and beta in (1, 2.5)  
  > "We limited the parameter space by imposing box-like priors defined as $\log M_{\rm dust}[M_{\astrosun}] > 0$, and $\beta\in(1,2.5)$." (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - parameter: Gaussian prior on T_dust, mean 47 K, sigma 5 K  
  > "we employed a relative tight Gaussian prior on $T_{\rm dust}$ with mean and standard deviation equal to $47\,{\rm K}$ and $5\,{\rm K}$, respectively" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - parameter: companions refit with T_dust prior centred on 35 K  
  > "we performed an additional fit centering the Gaussian dust temperature prior on $T_{\rm dust}=35\,{\rm K}$" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - parameter: nu_0 = 352.7 GHz for k_d(nu) = k_0 (nu/nu_0)^beta  
  > "where $\nu_0 = 352.7$ GHz, and $\beta$ is the dust spectral emissivity index" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - assumption: optically thin dust emission  
  > "by assuming optically thin dust emission (cite: Beelen+2006)" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - assumption: T_CMB ~19 K at z=6; the CMB heating correction to T_dust(z) is ignored (<2%)  
  > "is $T_{\rm CMB}\approx 19\, {\rm K}$ at $z=6$" (Dust properties: FIR continuum); "Therefore, we ignore this correction." (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - assumption: IR luminosity dominated by star formation; Kroupa IMF  
  > "The initial mass function (IMF) implicitly assumed in this $L_{\rm TIR}$ to ${\rm SFR}$ conversion is a Kroupa IMF" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
   - assumption: LCDM with H0 = 69.3 km/s/Mpc, Omega_m = 0.287  
  > "Throughout the paper we assume a standard $\Lambda\rm{CDM}$ cosmology with $H_0=69.3\,\si{km\,s^{-1}Mpc^{-1}}$, $\Omega_{m}=0.287$" (Introduction)

  > "In conclusion, in both the PJ231-20 and PJ308-21 quasars the AGN activity is mainly revealed through high-$J$ CO line emission" (Summary and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Observations designed not to resolve each source but to separate companions from quasars while maximizing S/N per beam  
  > "All the observations were designed not to spatially resolve the emission of each source, but with high enough angular resolution to resolve the companions from the quasars." (Observations and data reduction)
- Why this method: Single-pixel spectral analysis is justified because sources are unresolved and it maximizes S/N and limits quasar-companion blending; CLOUDY chosen because it treats all studied species simultaneously  
  > "This allowed us to safely perform a single-pixel analysis of the data." (Line and continuum measurements); "as it is highly customizable and allows the simultaneous treatment of all the species studied in this work" (CLOUDY models of photo- and X-ray dissociation regions)
- Alternative: Single XDR component for the PJ231-20 QSO CO SLED rejected as roughly consistent but requiring an extreme F_X at the grid boundary  
  > "but it points to an extreme value of the X-ray radiation field intensity of $F_{\rm X} \sim 10^2\,{\rm erg\,s^{-1}\,cm^{-2}}$, corresponding to the upper boundary of the parameter space." (CO SLED models)
- Alternative: Lower dust temperature scenario (35 K) considered for companions because they lack AGN heating  
  > "Therefore, in the case of the companion galaxies, we also considered a lower-temperature scenario." (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
- Alternative: H2O SLED models with N_H = 1e23 cm^-2 rejected in favour of N_H = 1e24  
  > "The observed H$_2$O line ratios of PJ231-20 QSO cannot be satisfyingly reproduced by any models with $N_{\rm H}=10^{23}\,{\rm cm^{-2}}$" (H2O SLED models)
- How they decided it was enough: Cleaning stopped at ~1 sigma residual; low-S/N lines accepted as detected when fits with varied priors and starting points give self-consistent solutions  
  > "We therefore assessed that the line is detected (although at low significance) when the derived best-fit models in all the cases, point to a self-consistent solution." (Line and continuum measurements)
- Limitation (authors): Composite CO SLED fit has more free parameters than data points, so best-fit values are degenerate  
  > "the number of free parameters is greater than data points, thus producing degeneracies in best-fit parameters." (CO SLED models)
- Limitation (authors): T_dust cannot be constrained by the dust fit and follows the prior  
  > "confirming that $T_{\rm dust}$ cannot be constrained with the fit." (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
- Limitation (authors): Poor SED coverage gives only tentative dust constraints for PJ308-21  
  > "we found only tentative constraints on $M_{\rm dust}$ and $\beta$ due to the poor coverage of the dust spectral energy distributions (SEDs)" (Dust properties: FIR continuum)
- Limitation (authors): CO SLED errors are statistical only; the gas-to-dust ratio systematic is ignored  
  > "ignoring any systematic uncertainties associated with the assumption of a gas-to-dust ratio by which we estimated the CO(1–0) luminosity in this work" (CO SLED models)
- Limitation (authors): CLOUDY models are simple single-cloud models that cannot give a realistic picture of the ISM  
  > "these simple models cannot provide a realistic picture of the ISM in galaxies" (CLOUDY models of photo- and X-ray dissociation regions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-pixel photometry of beam-integrated spectra with no aperture correction may bias fluxes where quasar and companion blend at ~1-1.8 arcsec resolution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust SED results depend on a tight T_dust prior and on the unstated k_0; L_TIR, SFR and CO(1-0) normalization inherit this
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO SLED normalization uses a dust-derived L_CO(1-0) with an assumed gas-to-dust ratio, making the PDR/XDR conclusion model dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Several detections are at 2-4.5 sigma and CLOUDY single-slab models are compared to unresolved multi-phase emission
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No CLOUDY scripts or fitting code are provided, limiting independent reproduction of the model grids
