## Analysis recipe

1. **Make continuum images and line cubes**: Average manually selected line-free channels for continuum tclean imaging; subtract continuum with uvcontsub and clean line cubes with tclean.  
  > "For continuum data, we manually selected line-free channels," (Observations); "the continuum image was obtained using the $tclean$ task by averaging line-free channels with a Briggs’s robust weighting of 0.5 to the visibilities." (Observations); "For spectral line, the line cube was cleaned using the $tclean$ task after removing the continuum emission using the $uvcontsub$ task, with a Briggs robust weighting of 0.5." (Observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub, auto-multithresh
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "with a Briggs robust weighting of 0.5." (Observations)
   - parameter: auto-multithresh standard values for 7 m data  
  > "We use the auto-multithresh algorithm with parameters set to the standard values for 7 m data, as specified in the user guides" (Observations)
   - parameter: Band 6 image: 81″ × 81″; Band 7 image: 62″ × 62″  
  > "The image sizes are $81^{\prime \prime} \times 81^{\prime \prime}$ and $62^{\prime \prime} \times 62^{\prime \prime}$ for Band 6 and Band 7, respectively." (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Measure continuum RMS in an emission-free region  
  > "The RMS noise of the continuum image is measured in an emission-free region," (Observations)
2. **Identify cores and measure continuum properties**: Dendrogram identification on uncorrected continuum images; CASA imfit on primary-beam-corrected images; retain cores identified in both bands.  
  > "on the continuum image without primary beam correction to identify dense cores." (Core Identification); "To accurately derive the physical parameters of these cores, we make use of the $imfit$ function in CASA on the continuum image after primary beam correction." (Core Identification); "Because of the different image sizes and sensitivities at two different bands, we only select the cores identified at both two bands." (Core Identification)
   - software: Dendrogram, CASA, imfit
   - parameter: min_value = 6σ; min_delta = 3σ; min_npix = pixels within the beam area  
  > "Because we focus on the cores with high mass, the *min_value* is set to be 6$\sigma$, where $\sigma$ is the RMS noise of the continuum image." (Core Identification); "The *min_delta* is set to be 3$\sigma$ and the *min_npix* equals to the number of pixels within the beam area." (Core Identification)
   - fitting: CASA imfit on primary-beam-corrected continuum images  
  > "To accurately derive the physical parameters of these cores, we make use of the $imfit$ function in CASA on the continuum image after primary beam correction." (Core Identification)
   - validation: Compare core positions and deconvolved sizes between bands  
  > "Comparing the observed properties at different bands, we find that the center of the cores has little difference but the deconvolved core size at Band 6 is always larger than that at Band 7." (Core Identification)
3. **Derive dust-based gas masses**: Mgas = η Fν d² / [Bν(Td) κν], using integrated Band 7 flux and average literature-map dust temperatures; repeat at 15 K and 10 K.  
  > "M_{\text {gas }}=\eta \frac{F_{\nu} d^{2}}{B_{\nu}\left(T_{d}\right) \kappa_{\nu}}" (Mass Estimation); "For the cloud cores located on this dust temperature map, we calculated the average dust temperature within the cloud cores to determine their mass." (Mass Estimation); "In addition, we also calculate the results at 10 K as the upper limits for the core masses," (Mass Estimation)
   - parameter: Integrated Band 7 flux  
  > "The gas masses of the cores can be derived from the integrated flux at Band 7 because of its higher spatial resolution." (Mass Estimation)
   - parameter: Average temperatures from Jiao_2022 where covered; otherwise Lombardi_2014 at 36″  
  > "(cite: Jiao_2022) obtained high-resolution dust temperature map ($\sim~ 10^{\prime\prime}$) of Orion A using the J-comb algorithm which combines the high and low-resolution images linearly." (Mass Estimation); "For cloud cores not covered by this dust temperature map, we applied the average dust temperature from the dust temperature map using the sophisticated SED model fitting at the angular resolution of $36^{\prime \prime}$ (cite: Lombardi_2014)." (Mass Estimation)
   - parameter: Alternative dust temperatures: 15 K and 10 K  
  > "Considering the possible overestimation in dust temperature, we calculate the core masses at 15 K, a typical dust temperature for IRDCs." (Mass Estimation); "In addition, we also calculate the results at 10 K as the upper limits for the core masses," (Mass Estimation)
   - assumption: Single dust temperature; optically thin dust emission  
  > "Assuming a single dust temperature and optically thin dust emission," (Mass Estimation)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust ratio η = 100  
  > "where $\eta=100$ is the gas-to-dust ratio," (Mass Estimation)
   - assumption: Dust opacity 1.37 cm² g−1 at 1.0 mm for volume density 10^6 cm−3  
  > "We adopt the value of 1.37 $\mathrm{cm^{2} \ g^{-1}}$ from (cite: OH_94) for dust opacity at 1.0 mm with a volume density of $10^6$ $\mathrm{cm}^{-3}$." (Mass Estimation)
   - assumption: Distance 414 pc  
  > "414 pc distance" (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Recalculate masses at lower temperatures to assess possible overestimation of the adopted dust temperatures  
  > "Considering the possible overestimation in dust temperature, we calculate the core masses at 15 K, a typical dust temperature for IRDCs." (Mass Estimation)
4. **Search for outflows**: Inspect blueshifted and redshifted CO (2-1) and SiO (5-4) emission and channel maps for paired linear bipolar structures.  
  > "In this study, we employed CO (2-1) and SiO (5-4) to identify outflow signatures in our cores." (Outflow Signatures); "Although the CO emission shows complex patterns, the bipolar outflows could be identified as pairs of linear emission structures." (Outflow Signatures); "We examined the blueshifted and redshifted components of CO and SiO emission surrounding the cores and visually inspected the corresponding channel maps." (Outflow Signatures)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual inspection of corresponding channel maps  
  > "We examined the blueshifted and redshifted components of CO and SiO emission surrounding the cores and visually inspected the corresponding channel maps." (Outflow Signatures)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Systematically search one well-studied GMC and resolve the internal structure of its selected massive starless-core candidates  
  > "In this work, we set out to carry out a systematic search for high-mass prestellar cores in a single giant molecular cloud (GMC), the Orion GMC." (Our Strategy); "This strategy is more efficient than previous searches in targeted regions" (Our Strategy); "We then use ALMA to resolve these candidate HMPCs to investigate their internal structure." (Our Strategy)
- Why this method: Use Band 7 integrated flux for higher spatial resolution; use a 6σ core threshold to focus on high-mass cores; test lower temperatures against possible temperature overestimation  
  > "The gas masses of the cores can be derived from the integrated flux at Band 7 because of its higher spatial resolution." (Mass Estimation); "Because we focus on the cores with high mass, the *min_value* is set to be 6$\sigma$, where $\sigma$ is the RMS noise of the continuum image." (Core Identification); "Considering the possible overestimation in dust temperature, we calculate the core masses at 15 K, a typical dust temperature for IRDCs." (Mass Estimation)
- Alternative: Recalculate masses at 15 K and 10 K because environmental heating could bias map temperatures upward  
  > "indicating that the average dust temperature may be affected by the surrounding environments." (Mass Estimation); "Considering the possible overestimation in dust temperature, we calculate the core masses at 15 K, a typical dust temperature for IRDCs." (Mass Estimation); "In addition, we also calculate the results at 10 K as the upper limits for the core masses," (Mass Estimation)
- Alternative: Rare outflow detections can reflect undetectable protostellar outflows or observational limitations  
  > "previous studies have found that some protostars don’t have detectable outflows (e.g., 38% in the ASHES survey; (cite: Li_2022))" (Deuterium Fraction); "our ACA observations might miss some potential outflows due to limited resolution, sensitivity, and image size" (Deuterium Fraction)
- Alternative: Scarcity of high-mass prestellar-core candidates could indicate a very short phase rather than nonexistence  
  > "The scarcity of HMPC candidates either challenges the existence of high-mass prestellar cores or indicates a very short lifetime for this phase." (Discussion)
- How they decided it was enough: Require core detections in both bands and at least five consecutive significant channels for N2H+/N2D+ spectra  
  > "Because of the different image sizes and sensitivities at two different bands, we only select the cores identified at both two bands." (Deuterium Fraction)

  > "our sources are located in the regions with strong star-forming activities, indicating that the average dust temperature may be affected by the surrounding environments." (Mass Estimation)
- Limitation (authors): ACA resolution, sensitivity and image size can hide potential outflows  
  > "our ACA observations might miss some potential outflows due to limited resolution, sensitivity, and image size" (Deuterium Fraction)
