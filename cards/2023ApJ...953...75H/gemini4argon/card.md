## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce ALMA data and create spectral cubes and continuum images**: Calibrate and image Band 3, 4, and 7 data using CASA tclean with natural weighting and 100 km s^-1 channel width.  
  > "We use the `CASA` package (cite: 2022PASP..134k4501C) to reduce the data and perform imaging. A spectral cube is created using the `tclean` task with natural weighting and a 100 km s$^{-1}$ channel width." (ALMA data)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting; channel width = 100 km s^-1; minimum PB response = 20%  
  > "A spectral cube is created using the `tclean` task with natural weighting and a 100 km s$^{-1}$ channel width." (ALMA data); "The resulting band 3 spectral cube has a $\sim1.5\ensuremath{'}$ arcmin diameter field of view (FOV) with a minimum primary beam (PB) response of 20% of the field center." (ALMA data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Blind search for emission line sources and measure line and continuum fluxes**: Smooth Band 3 cube with 300 km s^-1 FWHM Gaussian filter, mask central SMG, detect positive and negative peaks above S/N = 1.5 with DAOStarFinder, evaluate purity to set S/N > 5.2 threshold, fit 1D Gaussian to spectra and 2D Gaussian to moment-0 maps.  
  > "Inspired by (cite: 2016ApJ...833...67W), we smooth the band 3 cube with a 300 km s$^{-1}$ FWHM Gaussian filter and use the `DAOStarFinder` routine in the `photutils` package to detect all positive and negative peaks" (Source detection and line measurements); "For each source, we fit a single Gaussian profile to the 1D spectrum to derive the observed line frequency and FWHM (the bottom row in Figure [3](#fig:fig3))." (Source detection and line measurements); "The velocity-integrated fluxes are measured by fitting a 2D Gaussian profile to the zeroth-moment map, which sums along the spectral axis over a velocity range $\pm$FWHM from the line center." (Source detection and line measurements)
   - software: photutils
   - parameter: Gaussian filter FWHM = 300 km s^-1; detection threshold S/N > 5.2 (purity = 83%, a = 4.72, b = 0.71); moment-0 integration range = +/- FWHM from line center  
  > "and determine the free parameters $a=4.72$ and $b=0.71$, which gives a purity of 83% at S/N=5.2. Using this as a detection threshold, we find four sources with S/N$>5.2$ (Figure [2](#fig:fig2))." (Source detection and line measurements)
   - fitting: Error function fit to purity vs S/N; 1D Gaussian fit to spectrum; 2D Gaussian fit to moment-0 map  
  > "For each source, we fit a single Gaussian profile to the 1D spectrum to derive the observed line frequency and FWHM (the bottom row in Figure [3](#fig:fig3))." (Source detection and line measurements)
   - validation: Tested filter FWHM values from 100 to 500 km s^-1 in 100 km s^-1 steps; checked negative peak distribution (none above S/N = 5.2).

## Reasoning

- Why this data: An unbiased blind CO line search in a deep ALMA Band 3 spectral scan with ~29 GHz bandwidth and rich multiwavelength coverage in the SSA22 field avoids preselection biases toward MS or starburst galaxies.  
  > "An unbiased CO survey of galaxies in various stages of star formation is needed to investigate the evolution of gas content before and after galaxy quenching." (Introduction); "The SSA22 field has rich multiwavelength data coverage." (Optical and NIR data)
- Why this method: Two-component dust attenuation and nonparametric SFH in SED modeling account for complex dust geometry and dust heating by old stars, giving conservative SFR estimates; PSF photometry and 2D Sersic modeling overcome source blending.  
  > "As shown in Figure [3](#fig:fig3), the aperture flux of source 4 is contaminated by two blue neighboring sources. At this moment, we simply assume the two blue sources are foreground galaxies and subtract their contributions using 2D image modeling." (Optical and NIR data); "Flux densities are derived from PSF photometry to overcome source blending issues." (Infrared data); "Since the dust attenuations of old and young stars are modeled separately and both low and high attenuation values are allowed for the two stellar populations, the error range of SFR$_\mathrm{SED}$ serves as a conservative estimate of the SFR levels." (Stellar Mass and SFR from SED Modeling)
- Alternative: SMG-like CO conversion factor (alpha_CO = 1.36) and excitation ratios (r_21 = 0.84, r_31 = 0.52) vs normal SFG conversions: rejected in favor of normal SFG conversions based on the L_dust - L'_CO(1-0) relation for galaxy 1.  
  > "The position of galaxy 1 with respect to the $L_\mathrm{dust}-L^{'}_{\mathrm{CO(1-0)}}$ relation also disfavors a starburst nature of galaxy 1." (Molecular Gas Mass); "In the following discussion, we continue using $M_\mathrm{mol}$ from normal SFG conversions." (Molecular Gas Mass)
- Alternative: UV+IR SFR indicator vs SED-fitted SFR: rejected UV+IR SFR as primary indicator because dust heating by old stars overestimates SFR in low-sSFR galaxies like galaxy 4.  
  > "The difference between the two SFR methods increases with decreasing sSFR, because of the contribution of dust heating from old stars (cite: 2014MNRAS.445.1598H; 2019ApJ...877..140L), and this behavior is also found when using the `MAGPHYS` code (cite: 2019ApJ...882...65M)." (Stellar Mass and SFR from SED Modeling); "Therefore, we continue to choose SFR$_\mathrm{SED}$ as our SFR indicator." (Stellar Mass and SFR from SED Modeling)
- Alternative: Aperture photometry centered on the nearby NIR object for galaxy 3: tested and found photometric redshift 0.63 (+0.07/-0.06) still incompatible with any CO line redshift solution.  
  > "Since the flux is extracted at the CO position, we check the results by changing the position of the photometry aperture to the center of the nearby NIR object, then the photometric redshift of $0.63^{+0.07}_{-0.06}$ still does not overlap with any solution." (Line Identification)
- How they decided it was enough: Set line detection threshold at S/N = 5.2 where no negative peaks are found and detection purity reaches 83%; tested filter widths from 100 to 500 km s^-1 and found no additional detections.  
  > "No negative peak is found above S/N$=5.2$." (Source detection and line measurements); "and determine the free parameters $a=4.72$ and $b=0.71$, which gives a purity of 83% at S/N=5.2. Using this as a detection threshold, we find four sources with S/N$>5.2$ (Figure [2](#fig:fig2))." (Source detection and line measurements); "We have also tested other filter FWHM values from 100 to 500 km s$^{-1}$ with 100 km s$^{-1}$ steps but do not find any new detection." (Source detection and line measurements)
- Limitation (authors): Source 3 has only one line detection in the 29 GHz Band 3 bandwidth and an incompatible NIR photometric redshift, leaving its redshift unconfirmed between z_CO(2-1) = 1.512 and z_CO(3-2) = 2.767.  
  > "Given only one line detection within the $\sim29$ GHz bandwidth, possible solutions will be $z_{\mathrm{CO(2-1)}}=1.512$ or $z_\mathrm{CO(3-2)}=2.767$, but these need to be verified by detecting other spectral lines in future observations (cite: 2014ApJ...781L..39T; 2021ApJ...917...94M)." (Line Identification)
- Limitation (authors): Molecular gas mass estimates suffer from substantial uncertainties in alpha_CO and CO excitation ratios.  
  > "We note that molecular gas mass estimates suffer from substantial uncertainties in $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}$ and excitation." (Molecular Gas Mass)
- Limitation (authors): Dust-based M_mol is poorly constrained due to low S/N (<2) in all Herschel SPIRE bands and uncertainties in dust SED fitting.  
  > "The dust-based $M_\mathrm{mol}$ is the least robust here, because of low S/N ($<2$) in all Herschel bands and the uncertainty in the dust SED fitting (cite: 2016A&A...587A..73B)." (Molecular Gas Mass)
- Limitation (authors): Identification of galaxy 4 as quiescent is tentative because FIR, ALMA continuum, and JVLA 3 GHz upper limits are not deep enough to rule out obscured star formation up to ~220-294 M_sun yr^-1, and optical/NIR spectroscopy is lacking.  
  > "We caution that the current identification of galaxy 4 as a QG is only tentative. The assertion of the low SFR is based on very faint rest-frame UV fluxes and the obscured SFR of galaxy 4 is left not well constrained." (Caveats of Galaxy 4's SFR Estimate); "Obviously, current FIR and radio data are not deep enough to constrain galaxy 4’s SFR." (Caveats of Galaxy 4's SFR Estimate)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-line CO identifications for galaxies 2 and 4 rely entirely on photometric redshifts without secondary spectral line confirmations.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Applying a normal SFG CO-to-H2 conversion factor (alpha_CO = 3.6) and excitation ratio (r_21 = 0.76) to galaxy 4 may not be valid if it is genuinely a massive quiescent galaxy.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The low random alignment probability (1.18e-3, 3.2 sigma) of the two blue clumps around galaxy 4 leaves open the possibility that they are physically associated at z = 1.146 in an interacting system.
