## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and lines**: Continuum from line-free channels with uniform weighting and multiscale clean, phase self-cal (120 s); lines with natural weighting and multiscale clean after continuum subtraction  
  > "For the continuum imaging, uniform weighting of the visibility data and multi-scale clean with multiscale=\[0,4,20\] and cell=’0.02arcsec’ were adopted, and phase-only self-calibration was applied." (2 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: multiscale [0,4,20] continuum, [0,5,25] lines; cell 0.02''  
  > "Natural weighting and multi-scale clean with multiscale=\[0,5,25\] and cell=’0.02arcsec’ were adopted to produce the line images." (2 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Pipeline calibration independently checked; second self-cal iteration gave no improvement  
  > "We also independently checked the calibrated visibility data and confirmed that the pipeline calibration was fine." (2 ALMA Observations)
2. **Source positions, sizes and brightness temperatures**: 2D Gaussian fits for centroids and deconvolved sizes; Rayleigh-Jeans conversion to T_B  
  > "From the 2-dimensional Gaussian fittings, the centroid positions of the 0.9-mm dust-continuum emission are" (3.1)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Disk PA 160 deg, inclination 60 deg, coplanar CBD and CSDs  
  > "Throughout this paper, disk position and inclination angles of 160$\ensuremath{^\circ}$ and 60$\ensuremath{^\circ}$, and the co-planar configuration of the CBD and CSDs, are assumed." (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with JVLA 7-mm sizes and orbital motion history  
  > "The relative position in 2017 is indeed consistent with the global trend of the orbital motion starting from 1983" (3.1)
3. **Spectral index map and CBD mass**: Alpha from 0.9 and 1.3 mm maps convolved to common beam, pixels >10 sigma; mass M=S d^2/(kappa B(T))  
  > "The spectral-index map is made within the pixels where the image intensities at both wavelengths are above 10$\sigma$." (3.1)
   - parameter: T_d = 30-100 K; S_0.9mm = 1.40 Jy  
  > "With this mass opacity and $T_d$ = 30 – 100 K, the flux density originated from the CBD ($S_{\rm 0.9mm}$ = 1.40 Jy) gives the disk mass of 0.015 – 0.060 $M_{\odot}$." (3.1)
   - assumption: gas-to-dust 100; kappa_250um=0.1 cm2/g, beta=1.0 giving kappa_0.9mm=0.028; d=140 pc  
  > "$\kappa_{\rm 0.9mm}$ is calculated to be 0.028 cm$^{2}$ g$^{-1}$" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative Ossenkopf & Henning opacity gives ~60% higher mass  
  > "the mass estimate of the CBD becomes $\sim$60$\%$ higher" (3.1)
4. **Kinematics from channel maps, P-V diagrams and peak T_B maps**: Velocity channel maps, P-V cuts along major (PA 160) and minor (PA 70) axes with Keplerian curves, peak brightness temperature maps (moment 8)  
  > "Figure [8](#fig:c18opv) shows the Position-Velocity (P-V) diagrams of the C$^{18}$O emission along the major (P.A. = 160$\ensuremath{^\circ}$) and minor axes (P.A. = 70$\ensuremath{^\circ}$)" (3.3.2)
   - software: CASA, Miriad
   - parameter: v_sys = 6.4 km/s  
  > "the systemic velocity of $v_{\rm sys}$ = 6.4 km s$^{-1}$ is adopted" (3.3)
   - assumption: Binary mass 0.5 Msun, equal masses  
  > "on the assumption of equal protostellar masses" (3.3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Keplerian curves of 0.5 and 1.0 Msun  
  > "the emission ridges appear to be consistent with the Keplerian rotation curve from a central stellar mass of 0.5 $M_{\odot}$" (3.3.2)
5. **Compare with hydrodynamic simulation**: SFUMATO isothermal simulation (T=100 K) tailored to IRS 5, LTE radiative transfer with fitted T(r) and C18O abundance, CASA observing simulation, same imaging  
  > "Then CASA observing simulations were performed to create the simulated visibility data for the model images with the same antenna configuration" (4.1)
   - software: SFUMATO, CASA
   - parameter: X(C18O)=5.1e-7; T(r)=max[62 K (r_N/100au)^-0.5, 48 K (r_S/100au)^-0.5]; snapshot at 58.7 orbital periods  
  > "the adopted value is $X$(C$^{18}$O) = 5.1 $\times$ 10$^{-7}$" (4.1)
   - assumption: LTE  
  > "constructed on the assumption of the LTE condition" (4.1)
   - fitting: Abundance adjusted by eye to match intensities; no formal fit  
  > "The C$^{18}$O abundance was adjusted to approximately match the observed and model C$^{18}$O (3–2) intensities" (4.1)
   - validation: Qualitative comparison of continuum images, channel maps and P-V diagrams  
  > "Such features in the model appear to be consistent with the observed spiral arms" (4.2)

## Qualitative findings

- CBD is resolved into two spiral arms connected to Source N and Source S; m=1 mode less obvious than in L1551 NE.  
  > "The CBD is resolved into two spiral arms, one connecting to the CSD around the northern binary source, Source N, and the other to Source S." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Simulations tailored to IRS 5 used to interpret features, as for L1551 NE  
  > "To discuss the nature of these observed features, we performed hydrodynamical numerical simulations" (4.1)
- Alternative: Second self-calibration iteration (30 s) and amplitude+phase self-cal rejected as no improvement  
  > "did not improve the image quality and the noise, and thus we adopt the continuum image after the first self-calibration" (2 ALMA Observations)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Model CBD larger than observed; magnetic field not included; high-velocity blueshifted components near Source N not reproduced; infall not identified observationally  
  > "On the other hand, the model velocity channel maps do not reproduce the observed highly blueshifted components to the north and south of Source N." (5 Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Simulation comparison is qualitative with hand-tuned parameters and no quantitative goodness of fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Temperature and FU Ori interpretation based on lower-limit brightness temperatures
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass estimate depends on assumed opacity and temperature range
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-epoch single-config data; spatial filtering affects systemic-velocity channels
