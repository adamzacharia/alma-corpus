## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate the visibilities**: ALMA pipeline calibration in CASA, manual flagging, then three phase-only and one amplitude-and-phase self-calibration rounds on the QSO continuum  
  > "We used the standard ALMA pipeline in the Common Astronomy Software Application package (cite: casa22; Hunter23) to carry out the initial data editing and calibration of the visibility dataset." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis); "The self-calibration process involved three rounds of continuum imaging and phase-only self-calibration, followed by one round of amplitude-and-phase self-calibration and imaging," (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - software: CASA, gaincalR
   - parameter: Self-cal continuum imaging with Briggs weighting, robust = -0.5  
  > "The continuum imaging in the self-calibration procedure used Briggs weighting (cite: Briggs95) with a robust value of $-0.5$." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - parameter: Antenna-based gains measured with gaincalR  
  > "In the self-calibration procedure, the antenna-based gains were measured using the routine gaincalR (cite: Chowdhury20)." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - assumption: The bright mm QSO PKS B1228-113 in the field allows self-calibration of the target field  
  > "PKS B1228-113 was chosen as the pointing centre as it is bright at mm wavelengths, allowing self-calibration of the target field." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Self-calibration stopped when no further improvement in image or image/visibility residuals  
  > "after which no further improvement was seen in either the image or the image/visibility residuals upon further self-calibration" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
2. **Make continuum-subtracted CO(3-2) cubes and choose the final cube**: Apply self-cal gains to the full-resolution CO visibilities, subtract continuum, and make tclean cubes at several velocity resolutions and robust values; pick robust +1 at 35 km/s for modelling  
  > "We next applied the antenna-based gains obtained from the self-calibration procedure to the CO(3–2) visibility dataset at the original spectral resolution, and subtracted out the continuum emission from the calibrated visibilities." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis); "We then used the task tclean to make spectral cubes from the residual visibilities, using a range of velocity resolutions (25, 35, 50 km s$^{-1}$) and values of the robust parameter (-1, -0.5, 0, +0.5, +1)." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Clean threshold 0.5 sigma (per-channel RMS); square mask ~2.5'' x 2.5''  
  > "For all cubes, the CO(3–2) line emission was cleaned down to a threshold of 0.5$\sigma$, where $\sigma$ is the per-channel RMS noise." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis); "For cleaning, we used a square mask of size $~2.5'' \times 2.5''$, enclosing the CO emission for all frequency planes." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - parameter: Final cube: robust +1, 35 km/s channels  
  > "We chose a robust value of +1 and a velocity resolution of 35 km s$^{-1}$ for the final spectral cube used for the kinematic modelling" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Choice balances per-channel sensitivity, S/N and angular resolution (Table 1)  
  > "based on a balance between the per-channel sensitivity and signal-to-noise ratio, and the angular resolution" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
3. **Measure integrated CO(3-2) flux and size**: Single-component 2-D Gaussian fit with imfit to the velocity-integrated image  
  > "This was obtained using the casa task imfit to fit a single-component 2-D Gaussian model to the image of Figure" (Results)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: Velocity range -350 to +525 km/s for the integrated image  
  > "integrated over the velocity range of $-350$ km s$^{-1}$ to $+525$ km s$^{-1}$" (Results)
   - fitting: Single-component 2-D Gaussian via imfit  
  > "This was obtained using the casa task imfit to fit a single-component 2-D Gaussian model to the image of Figure" (Results)
   - validation: Fit residuals consistent with noise; flux matches the original low-resolution spectrum so no emission is resolved out  
  > "this model was found to yield an acceptable fit, with residuals consistent with noise." (Results); "indicating that no emission is being resolved out in our high-resolution ALMA cube." (Results)
4. **Derive velocity field and position-velocity slice**: Fit a Gaussian to the spectrum at each pixel with qubefit and use the mean as velocity; blank below 3 sigma integrated flux; take a PV slice along the major axis from the kinematic model  
  > "which was obtained by fitting a Gaussian function to the spectrum at each pixel of the cube, and then using the mean value of the function at each pixel, via the fitting package qubefit (cite: Neeleman21)" (Results); "the image has been blanked at spatial locations where the integrated CO(3–2) flux density has $<3\sigma$ significance." (Results)
   - software: qubefit
   - fitting: Per-pixel Gaussian line fit  
  > "which was obtained by fitting a Gaussian function to the spectrum at each pixel of the cube" (Results)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Fit a rotating thin-disk kinematic model**: Bayesian MCMC fit with qubefit of an infinitesimally thin exponential disk with constant rotation velocity and dispersion, convolved with the synthesized beam and compared to the cube  
  > "We used the Bayesian Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)-based Python fitting package qubefit (cite: Neeleman21) to model the kinematics of the CO(3–2) emission." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g); "The fitting routine convolves a user-defined model with the synthesized beam of the observations and then compares the resultant model cube to the actual spectral cube, using a defined likelihood function." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
   - software: qubefit
   - parameter: Nine free parameters: centre coordinates, redshift, inclination, PA, v_rot, sigma_v, scale length r_d, peak intensity I_0  
  > "the rotation velocity ($v_{rot}$), the velocity dispersion ($\sigma_{v}$), the disk scale length ($r_d$), and the peak intensity of the CO(3–2) emission ($I_0$)." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
   - parameter: 400,000 MCMC chains  
  > "Finally, we used 400,000 MCMC chains to estimate the probability distribution functions of the nine parameters of the model." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
   - assumption: Exponential intensity profile I(r) = I_0 exp(-r/r_d), infinitesimally thin disk  
  > "We assumed a rotating infinitesimally-thin exponential disk model for DLA-B1228g, in which the intensity $I$ of the CO emission at a radius $r$ is given by $I(r) = I_0 e^{-r/{r_d}}$," (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
   - assumption: Rotation velocity and velocity dispersion constant across the disk  
  > "For simplicity, we assume that both the rotational velocity and the velocity dispersion are constant across the disk." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
   - fitting: MCMC maximising a likelihood between model cube and data cube; priors not stated; errors inflated for reduced chi-square above 1  
  > "It then maximizes the likelihood function, following an MCMC approach in order to estimate the model parameters and their uncertainties." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g); "the errors have been increased appropriately to account for the fact that the reduced chi-square of the fit is slightly higher than unity" (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
   - validation: Reduced chi-square 1.2; no residual CO emission at or above 3 sigma in the data-minus-model map  
  > "The reduced chi-square of the fit is 1.2, consistent with a good fit." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g); "no residual CO(3–2) emission is detected at a statistically significant ($\geq3\sigma$) level" (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
6. **Derive the dynamical mass**: Spherical-mass formula M_dyn = v^2 R / G with v_circ ~ v_rot, evaluated within R = 2 R_e, with R_e = 1.678 r_d  
  > "For a spherically symmetric mass distribution, the dynamical mass enclosed within a radius $R$ is given by" (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g); "Finally, following (cite: Neeleman21), we estimate the dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g within a radius equal to twice the effective radius ($R_e$) of the CO(3–2) emission." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
   - parameter: R_e = 3.02 +/- 0.17 kpc; R = 2R_e = 6.04 +/- 0.34 kpc  
  > "implying an effective radius of $R_e = 3.02 \pm 0.17$ kpc." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g); "The radius that we will use to estimate the dynamical mass is then $R = 2R_e = 6.04 \pm 0.34$ kpc." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
   - assumption: Velocity dispersion term neglected: v_circ ~ v_rot = 328 +/- 7 km/s (rotation-dominated)  
  > "we will neglect the velocity dispersion in the above expression, and will assume $v_{circ} \approx v_{rot} = 328 \pm 7$ km s$^{-1}$." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
   - assumption: Spherical mass distribution; may overestimate by ~20% if mass is disk-like  
  > "this estimate would be higher than the true dynamical mass, by $\approx 20$% (cite: Neeleman21)." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the CO(1-0) molecular gas mass of ~1e11 Msun to assess baryon dominance  
  > "We thus find that the central regions of DLA-B1228g are baryon-dominated" (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
7. **Measure HST stellar emission and SFR limit**: Combine HST WFC3 exposures with AstroDrizzle, align to Gaia DR3 with TweakReg, measure F160W flux in a Kron elliptical aperture, derive an SFR upper limit from the F105W non-detection  
  > "The individual images were combined with AstroDrizzle (cite: hack20) to a scale of $0.06\ensuremath{''}$ pixel$^{-1}$, and the images astronomically aligned to GAIA DR3 (cite: GAIADR3final) with TweakReg," (HST observations and Data Analysis); "The source has an F160W AB magnitude of $24.5 \pm0.1$, measured over an elliptical aperture with the Kron radius (cite: Kron80)." (Results)
   - software: AstroDrizzle, TweakReg

## Qualitative findings

- CO(3-2) velocity field and PV slice are consistent with a rotating disk; kinematic model fits with no significant residuals  
  > "The velocity field appears consistent with that of a rotating disk, as also suggested by the double-horned profile seen in the high-resolution ALMA spectrum." (Results)
- No continuum emission detected at the location of DLA-B1228g  
  > "No continuum emission is detected at the location of DLA-B1228g in the $0.29\ensuremath{''} \times 0.22\ensuremath{''}$ resolution continuum image." (Results)
- F160W shows clumpy extended emission with no disk-like morphology  
  > "appears clumpy in nature, with no evidence of a disk-like morphology." (Results)
- Low-ionization metal absorption lies redward of the velocity expected from a thin HI disk, so a simple thin disk does not explain the absorption  
  > "A simple thin disk model thus fails to explain the observed low-ionization metal line absorption in the $z \approx 2.1929$ DLA." (The z ≈ 2.1929 DLA: Disk or CGM?)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: DLA field previously detected in CO(3-2) at low resolution as part of a high-metallicity DLA sample; PKS B1228-113 chosen as pointing centre because it is bright at mm wavelengths and allows self-calibration  
  > "This DLA field was originally searched for CO(3–2) emission by (cite: Neeleman18), as part of a sample of high-metallicity DLAs (cite: Kanekar20), and yielded a detection of strong CO(3–2) emission at low angular resolution" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis); "PKS B1228-113 was chosen as the pointing centre as it is bright at mm wavelengths, allowing self-calibration of the target field." (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
- Why this method: Simple axisymmetric exponential disk with constant v_rot and sigma_v because the CO S/N is modest and this model already fits  
  > "In passing, we note that we used a simple axisymmetric exponential disk model, with a constant rotational velocity and velocity dispersion, for the kinematic modelling as the signal-to-noise ratio of the detected CO(3–2) emission is not very high." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
- Alternative: Cubes made at 25, 35, 50 km/s and robust -1 to +1; robust +1 at 35 km/s chosen for modelling (Table 1)  
  > "using a range of velocity resolutions (25, 35, 50 km s$^{-1}$) and values of the robust parameter (-1, -0.5, 0, +0.5, +1)" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
- Alternative: Major-merger scenario considered and judged very unlikely  
  > "Overall, it appears very unlikely that DLA-B1228g is a major merger system" (Can the CO Kinematics be explained by a Major Merger?)
- Alternative: More complex kinematic models not tried since the simple model already fits  
  > "Given that this model already provides a good fit, more complicated models (with more parameters) are also likely to yield good fits to the observed CO(3–2) emission." (Kinematic modelling of DLA-B1228g)
- How they decided it was enough: Self-calibration stopped when no further improvement in image or residuals; final cube chosen on sensitivity/S/N versus resolution  
  > "after which no further improvement was seen in either the image or the image/visibility residuals upon further self-calibration" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis); "based on a balance between the per-channel sensitivity and signal-to-noise ratio, and the angular resolution" (ALMA Observations and Data Analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Intermediate angular resolution and sensitivity, only 2-3 beams across the CO region, so a merger can mimic a disk  
  > "we note that the current ALMA CO(3–2) image has intermediate angular resolution and sensitivity, with only $2-3$ synthesized beams across the CO-emitting region." (Results)
- Limitation (authors): Dynamical mass error includes only the fit error; spherical assumption dominates the uncertainty  
  > "We emphasize that the quoted error only includes the error from the fit." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
- Limitation (authors): No stellar mass estimate  
  > "We do not currently have an estimate of the stellar mass of DLA-B1228g." (The dynamical mass of DLA-B1228g)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Constant v_rot and sigma_v with a thin disk and only 2-3 beams across the source: inclination, v_rot and sigma_v are degenerate and the quoted errors are likely underestimated.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): qubefit priors, convergence and the meaning of '400,000 MCMC chains' are not given, so the stated uncertainties are hard to check.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The dynamical mass assumes sphericity and neglects sigma_v; the baryon-dominance claim relies on a CO(1-0) mass with an assumed alpha_CO and no stellar or HI mass.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The disk-versus-merger argument is qualitative; no merger model or comparison with simulations is fitted.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): HI disk size of >~160 kpc is an extrapolation from the local mass-size relation with an assumed HI mass.
