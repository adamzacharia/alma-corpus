## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate archival ALMA Band 6 data**: Retrieve data from the ALMA Science Archive and calibrate with the archive calibration or pipeline scripts in CASA  
  > "We retrieve these data from the ALMA Science Archive[^5]." (2.3 ALMA); "using the calibration script or pipeline script in the archive" (2.3 ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: CASA 3.4 (Cycle 0), 4.3.1 (Cycle 1), 4.7.0 (Cycle 3)  
  > "version 3.4, 4.3.1, and 4.7.0, respectively, using the calibration script or pipeline script in the archive." (2.3 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Combine Himiko Cycle 0 and Cycle 1 visibilities**: concatenate with CASA concat  
  > "we combine the Cycles 0 and 1 data using the CASA task `CONCAT`" (2.3 ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Total on-source time 6.4 h  
  > "to obtain high-sensitivity data with a total on-source time of 6.4 hours" (2.3 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Image continuum and [CII] cubes**: tclean in CASA 6.6.4 with natural weighting; continuum from spectral windows without [CII]; uv-tapered [CII] cube for flux and morphology  
  > "Using the CASA task `TCLEAN`, we generate both continuum images and" (2.3 ALMA); "We image from the calibrated visibility using CASA version 6.6.4." (2.3 ALMA)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting, 0.1" pixel, 50 km/s channel  
  > "with a natural weighting, a pixel scale of $0.1\ensuremath{''}$, and a spectral channel bin of $50~\mathrm{km~s^{-1}}$" (2.3 ALMA)
   - parameter: uv-taper Gaussian width 0.7"  
  > "with a Gaussian width of $0.7\ensuremath{''}$ to optimize the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N)." (2.3 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Continuum sensitivities consistent with previous studies  
  > "which is consistent with previous studies (cite: carniani18; matthee17)" (2.3 ALMA)
4. **Measure [CII] flux and luminosity**: Moment 0 maps, sum regions >3 sigma to get total spectrum, sum spectral bins for flux, convert to L[CII]  
  > "We create moment 0 maps around the \[C ii\]158$\ensuremath{\mu}m$ emission line and sum up data from regions of $>3\sigma$ to obtain the total spectrum of Himiko and CR7." (3.3.2 ALMA); "\[C ii\] flux measurements are conducted by summing up the spectral bins because the line shapes deviate from a Gaussian profile." (3.3.2 ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Noise from 1000 random circular apertures with same size as flux apertures  
  > "We measure the noise by placing 1000 random circular apertures around the objects" (3.3.2 ALMA)
   - assumption: D_L = 65862.5 Mpc at z=6.60 for luminosity conversion  
  > "$D_\mathrm{L}$ is the luminosity distance in Mpc ($D_\mathrm{L}=65862.5~\mathrm{Mpc}$ at $z=6.60$)" (Analysis, ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Summed fluxes largely consistent with least-squares Gaussian fit  
  > "However, these flux values are largely consistent with the results obtained by least-squares fitting of a Gaussian." (3.3.2 ALMA)
5. **Derive dust continuum upper limits and IR luminosity**: 3 sigma upper limit from noise of continuum maps; L_IR from limit assuming modified blackbody  
  > "We only put the $3\sigma$ upper limit for the dust continuum estimated from the noise of the continuum maps." (3.3.2 ALMA)
   - parameter: L_IR over 8-1000 um; T_dust 30-50 K and beta_IR 1.5-2.0 tested  
  > "but the trends of $L_\mathrm{[\textsc{C\,ii}]}/L_\mathrm{IR}$ or $L_\mathrm{IR}$ for Himiko and CR7 still hold if we adopt other $T_\mathrm{dust}$ or $\beta_\mathrm{IR}$ values ($30~\mathrm{K}<T_\mathrm{dust}<50~\mathrm{K}$ or $1.5<\beta_\mathrm{IR}<2.0$)" (4.5 [CII] and IR luminosity)
   - assumption: T_dust = 40 K, beta_IR = 1.5  
  > "assuming the dust temperature of $T_\mathrm{dust}=40~\mathrm{K}$ and the dust emissivity index of $\beta_\mathrm{IR}=1.5$." (4.5 [CII] and IR luminosity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Trends insensitive to T_dust and beta_IR within the tested ranges  
  > "The IR luminosity depends on the $T_\mathrm{dust}$ and $\beta_\mathrm{IR}$" (4.5 [CII] and IR luminosity)
6. **Derive dust mass upper limits**: Optically thin dust mass with CMB correction using the 3 sigma flux limit  
  > "In this equation, $B_\nu(T_\mathrm{CMB}(z))$ is used to correct for the cosmic microwave background (CMB) effects (e.g., (cite: ota14))." (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - parameter: rest wavelength 158 um  
  > "with the rest-frame wavelength of $\lambda_\mathrm{rest}=158~\mathrm{\ensuremath{\mu}m}$" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - assumption: Dust mass absorption coefficient from Dunne et al. 2000, kappa = 0.77 (850 um/lambda_rest)^beta cm2/g  
  > "We assume $\kappa_\mathrm{d}(\nu_\mathrm{rest})=0.77(850~\mathrm{\ensuremath{\mu}m}/\lambda_\mathrm{rest})^{\beta_\mathrm{IR}}~\mathrm{cm^2~g^{-1}}$ (cite: dunne00)" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - assumption: beta_IR = 1.5, T_dust = 40 K (also 35 and 45 K tabulated)  
  > "assuming $\beta_\mathrm{IR}=1.5$ and $T_\mathrm{dust}=40~\mathrm{K}$" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Estimate molecular/total gas mass and metal mass from L[CII]**: H2 mass from the Zanella et al. [CII] relation, total gas mass with metallicity-dependent factor xi, metal mass from gas and dust mass  
  > "The molecular H$_2$ gas mass $M_\mathrm{H_2}$ is estimated using the following relation presented in (cite: zanella18):" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems); "M_\mathrm{metal}= f_Z \times M_\mathrm{gas} + M_\mathrm{dust}" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - parameter: xi from 1.33 (zero metallicity) to 1.39 (solar)  
  > "Depending on the metallicity, $\xi$ varies from 1.33 (zero metallicity) to 1.39 (solar metallicity)." (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - assumption: Atomic HI gas mass ignored  
  > "In this paper, we ignore atmic H i gas mass because the atomic gas mass estimate has large uncertainties" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - assumption: Solar 12+log(O/H)=8.69 and metal mass fraction 0.0134; primordial He fraction 0.2485  
  > "The factor of 27.36 comes from the assumption of $12+\log{(\mathrm{O/H})_\odot}=8.69$ and a solar metal mass fraction of 0.0134 (cite: asplund09)." (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Molecular gas mass uncertainty ~0.3 dex acknowledged  
  > "We note that the estimate of molecular gas mass also has uncertainty ($\sim0.3$ dex" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
8. **Fit SED of clumps with NIRCam photometry**: Prospector SED fitting with FSPS, nonparametric SFH, Calzetti attenuation, Madau IGM, NIRCam photometry (PSF matched, 0.4" apertures); ALMA excluded  
  > "We perform a spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting with Prospector (version 1.2.0, (cite: leja17; johnson21)), utilizing NIRCam photometry to characterize the stellar population of these objects." (3.2 SED Modeling); "We do not include the ALMA photometry because the ALMA data have a lower resolution" (3.2 SED Modeling)
   - software: Prospector, FSPS, emcee, SEP, Photutils
   - parameter: log M* in 6-11 (top-hat prior), tau_V in 0-2 (log-uniform), Z=0.2 Zsun  
  > "We allow varying total stellar mass ($M_*$) and optical depth in the $V$ band ($\tau_V$) to be in the range of $6<\log{(M_*/M_\odot)}<11$ and $0<\tau_V<2$ with top-hat priors and log-uniform priors, respectively." (3.2 SED Modeling); "We set the metallicity to $Z=0.2Z_\odot$" (3.2 SED Modeling)
   - parameter: Circular 0.4" diameter apertures (0.5" for CR7-A), no aperture correction  
  > "We extract the photometry of each clump in a circular aperture with a $0.4\ensuremath{''}$-diameter, except for CR7-A" (3.1 NIRCam Photometry)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF, Calzetti attenuation, Madau IGM, no AGN component  
  > "We apply the (cite: chabrier03) initial mass function, the Calzetti dust attenuation law ((cite: calzetti00)), and the (cite: madau95) model of intergalactic medium attenuation." (3.2 SED Modeling); "We do not include AGN models in the SED fitting" (3.2 SED Modeling)
   - fitting: MCMC with emcee  
  > "We apply the Markov Chain Monte Carlo method using the `emcee` ((cite: Foreman-Mackey13)) to estimate the best-fit parameters and their uncertainties." (3.2 SED Modeling)
   - validation: Stellar masses compared with Marconcini et al. within 2 sigma  
  > "These results align well with the stellar mass estimates of CR7 derived from NIRSpec IFU spectra by (cite: marconcini24b) within 2$\sigma$." (4.2 Spectral energy distribution)
9. **Measure emission-line fluxes and direct-method metallicity**: Least-squares Gaussian fits to NIRSpec spectra, T_e([OIII]) from 4363/5007 with PyNeb, O abundance from PyNeb  
  > "We perform least-square Gaussian fitting of emission lines to measure spatially integrated emission line fluxes using the `scipy.optimize` package (cite: virtanen20)." (3.3.1 JWST NIRSpec); "using the Pyneb package `getIonAbundance`." (4.3 Emission line diagnostics)
   - software: SciPy, PyNeb
   - parameter: Up to three Gaussians for G395H; number chosen by Delta AIC > 20  
  > "We conduct the fitting with up to three Gaussian components for the high-resolution G395H data ($R\sim2700$) that show complex emission line profiles." (3.3.1 JWST NIRSpec)
   - assumption: n_e = 100 cm^-3 (PRISM cannot resolve [OII] doublet)  
  > "We assume an electron number density of $n_e=100~\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$ because we do not resolve the \[O ii\]$\lambda3726, 3729$ lines in the PRISM spectra ($R\sim100$)." (4.3 Emission line diagnostics)
   - fitting: Least-squares Gaussian fitting with AIC model selection  
  > "The optimal number of components is determined using the Akaike Information Criterion (AIC; (cite: akaike74))" (3.3.1 JWST NIRSpec)
   - validation: R3-based metallicities generally agree with direct method  
  > "which generally agrees with those measured by the direct method" (4.3 Emission line diagnostics)
10. **Estimate black hole mass of Himiko-B from broad Halpha**: Single-epoch H-alpha relation of Reines et al. 2013 equation 5 with epsilon scaling factor  
  > "We estimate the black hole mass ($M_\mathrm{BH}$) following the equation 5 of (cite: reines13)" (4.4 Signature of AGN)
   - parameter: epsilon = 1.075 (range 0.75-1.4 for uncertainty)  
  > "We adopt $\epsilon=1.075$ following (cite: reines15)." (4.4 Signature of AGN)
   - assumption: No dust-extinction correction for the broad line  
  > "We do not apply dust-extinction corrections to the black hole mass and bolometric luminosity values" (4.4 Signature of AGN)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Mass unchanged when the aperture is reduced to 0.2-0.3 arcsec; 0.5 dex systematic uncertainty  
  > "The black hole mass estimations have a systematic uncertainty of 0.5 dex (cite: reines15)." (4.4 Signature of AGN)

## Qualitative findings

- Neither Himiko nor CR7 shows a galaxy-wide disk-like velocity gradient, disfavoring a single rotating disk and motivating a merger scenario  
  > "This disfavors a single rotating disk and motivates a merger scenario for the origin of the clumps" (4.1 Morphology and Dynamics)
- [CII] emission of CR7 is spatially resolved with two peaks near CR7-A and -B  
  > "we find two flux peaks near the position of CR7-A and -B" (4.1 Morphology and Dynamics)
- Himiko and CR7 are dust-poor, consistent with the lack of 1 mm continuum detection  
  > "consistent with no 1-mm continuum detection corresponding to the dust mass limits of $M_\mathrm{dust}\lesssim 9\times10^6 M_\odot$" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Public JWST data plus archival ALMA Band 6 to probe stellar, ionized and cold gas/dust content of two bright Lya objects  
  > "We also include archival ALMA Band 6 data to examine their cold gas and dust characteristics." (Introduction)
- Why this method: A uv-tapered [CII] cube was used to optimize S/N for measuring the spatial distribution and flux  
  > "We use the uv-tapered line cube to measure the spatial distributions and fluxes of \[C ii\]$158\ensuremath{\mu}m$ of Himiko and CR7." (2.3 ALMA)
- Alternative: [CII] fluxes obtained by summing spectral bins rather than Gaussian fitting; Gaussian fit gives consistent results  
  > "However, these flux values are largely consistent with the results obtained by least-squares fitting of a Gaussian." (3.3.2 ALMA)
- Alternative: ALMA photometry excluded from SED fitting due to lower resolution and upper-limit-only continuum  
  > "We do not include the ALMA photometry because the ALMA data have a lower resolution" (3.2 SED Modeling)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only statistical errors are shown  
  > "Throughout this paper, we only show statistical errors." (2.3 ALMA)
- Limitation (authors): Deeper dust continuum observations are needed to constrain dust-to-gas and dust-to-metal ratios  
  > "We need deeper dust continuum observations aimed at Himiko and CR7 to constrain these properties." (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
- Limitation (authors): Higher-resolution ALMA data needed for the Himiko [CII] distribution and kinematics  
  > "Additional high-resolution ALMA data are necessary to comprehend the \[C ii\] distribution of Himiko" (5.5 Physical interpretations of the [CII] offset)
- Limitation (authors): Gas mass estimates have large uncertainty  
  > "The gas mass estimate has a large uncertainty" (5.6 Comparisons with other merger systems)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The [CII] and Lya offset in Himiko is inferred from a ~0.9 arcsec ALMA beam and ~0.75 arcsec Subaru PSF versus JWST resolution, with ALMA astrometric uncertainty comparable to the 0.3 arcsec offset
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust-mass limits depend on assumed T_dust (40 K, no CMB-dependent temperature evolution explored beyond 35-45 K), beta and kappa
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas masses rely on a low-redshift-calibrated [CII]-H2 conversion and ignore HI, with ~0.3 dex scatter
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only the Cycle 1 Himiko data are recalibrated with Help Desk support; the older CASA versions may limit exact reproduction of flux scales
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mean metallicity and stellar masses from aperture photometry without aperture corrections may bias clump comparisons
