## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce Chandra data and obtain AGN-subtracted soft-band image**: Reprocess 22 ACIS-S observations with acis_process_events (apply_cti=yes, VFAINT) and merge with reproject_obs; use the AGN-subtracted images from Tozzi et al. 2022b  
  > "we run the task `acis_process_events` with the parameter `apply_cti=yes` to flag background events, most likely associated with cosmic rays" (2.1 Chandra X-ray data); "The 22 level-2 event files are eventually merged together with the tool `reproject_obs`, using the reference coordinates of Obsid 21483." (2.1 Chandra X-ray data)
   - software: CIAO, CALDB 4.9.3
   - parameter: Diffuse emission within 2 arcsec of the AGN is unconstrained; central surface brightness parametrized by n_d in the range 1-6, preferred n_d=4  
  > "We adopted values in the range of 1-6, where $n_d$=1 corresponds to a constant surface brightness; namely, $n_d \sim 4$ to a developed cool core and $n_d \sim 6$ to an extreme cool core." (2.1 Chandra X-ray data)
   - parameter: Soft band 0.5-2 keV, hard band 2-7 keV  
  > "The soft (0.5-2 keV) and hard (2-7 keV) band images" (2.1 Chandra X-ray data)
   - assumption: Chandra PSF not deconvolved because HEW ~0.5 arcsec is smaller than the 2 arcsec annuli  
  > "We do not deconvolve the *Chandra* PSF when computing the surface brightness profile, since the half energy width is $\sim 0.5$ arcsec" (2.1 Chandra X-ray data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure azimuthally averaged X-ray surface brightness profile**: Count photons in annuli centered on the AGN, subtract rescaled background, apply vignetting correction and an energy conversion factor (Eq. 1)  
  > "we sample the soft and hard band background from a source-free annulus with inner and outer radius of 16 and 29.5 arcsec, respectively" (3 Morphology of X-ray emission); "we calculate the vignetting correction in the soft and hard bands, using the monochromatic exposure maps at 1.5 and 4.5 keV, respectively" (3 Morphology of X-ray emission)
   - software: XSPEC v.12.12.0
   - parameter: Seven annuli with outer radii 2, 4, 7, 10, 13, 17, 21 arcsec  
  > "we extracted the surface brightness profile from seven annuli with outer radius of 2, 4, 7, 10, 13, 17, and 21 arcsec centered on the AGN for the soft and hard images" (4.1 Azimuthally averaged profile)
   - parameter: ECF ~1.20e-11 (soft) and ~1.24e-11 (hard) erg/s/cm2/cts  
  > "is equal to $\sim 1.20\times10^{-11}$ erg/s/cm$^{2}$/cts in the soft band and $\sim 1.24\times10^{-11}$ erg/s/cm$^{2}$/cts in the hard band" (3 Morphology of X-ray emission)
   - parameter: Only soft band used afterwards, data beyond 17 arcsec neglected  
  > "we work on the soft band image only and we also neglect the data beyond 17 arcsec in the soft band" (4.1 Azimuthally averaged profile)
   - assumption: Thermal spectrum kT=2 keV and Galactic N_H=3.18e20 cm^-2 for the conversion factor  
  > "assuming a thermal spectrum with $kT=2$ keV and a Galactic absorption of $N_{H}^{Gal}=3.18 \times 10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$" (3 Morphology of X-ray emission)
   - assumption: All diffuse emission outside jet and AGN regions is proto-ICM thermal emission  
  > "in this work we assume that all the diffuse emission outside the jet and AGN regions is associated with the proto-ICM" (3 Morphology of X-ray emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Hard-band null profile after the same AGN subtraction suggests no significant residuals  
  > "the same subtraction procedure results in a null profile in the hard-band, suggesting that the AGN subtraction left no significant residuals" (4.1 Azimuthally averaged profile)
3. **Test azimuthal symmetry of the X-ray emission**: Surface brightness in 8 wedges (45 deg), 12 wedges (30 deg) and 4 quadrants out to 17 arcsec, excluding the central 2 arcsec and jet regions, fit with a constant  
  > "we considered eight different wedges with a step of 45 degrees" (4.2 Azimuthal fluctuations); "we considered four quadrants (0-90, 90-180. 180-270, 270-360 degrees)" (4.2 Azimuthal fluctuations)
   - parameter: Wedges extend to 17 arcsec, excluding central 2 arcsec and jet regions  
  > "extending up to a distance of 17 arcsec from the central AGN and excluding the central 2 arcsec and the jet regions" (4.2 Azimuthal fluctuations)
   - fitting: Constant surface brightness vs azimuth; reduced chi2 1.1 (7 dof) for 8 wedges, 1.18 (11 dof) for 12 wedges  
  > "we find a reduced value, $\chi^{2}=1.1,$ with 7 d.o.f." (4.2 Azimuthal fluctuations); "we obtained a reduced $\chi^{2}=1.18$ for 11 d.o.f." (4.2 Azimuthal fluctuations)
   - validation: Repeated with different wedge width; SW quadrant drop below 2 sigma  
  > "We see a drop in the south-west quadrant in at least two bins, but with a statistical significance lower than 2 $\sigma$." (4.2 Azimuthal fluctuations)
4. **Measure the X-ray centroid offset**: Fit the AGN-subtracted soft-band image with a single 2D beta model plus background in Sherpa, masking a 3 arcsec circle on the AGN  
  > "we fit the surface brightness distribution with a single two-dimensional (2D) beta model plus background in order to find the X-ray centroid of the emission" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis); "removing a circle of 3 arcsec centered on the AGN" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)
   - software: Sherpa, CIAO
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the SZ centroid offset: same direction  
  > "This shift is along the same direction of the SZ effect offset (6.2 $\pm$ 1.3 arcsec) measured with ALMA." (8 Conclusions)
5. **Reconstruct SZ pressure profile from ALMA+ACA visibilities**: Bayesian forward modelling of uv-plane visibilities with a line-of-sight integrated gNFW pressure model fitted jointly with a radio-galaxy point-component model; centroid fixed to the X-ray centroid; models combined with Bayesian Model Averaging  
  > "the SZ distribution is obtained as the line-of-sight integral of a generalized Navarro-Frenk-White (NFW, (cite: 2007Nagai)) pressure distribution, with scaling parameters and radial slopes fixed to match specific formulations from the literature" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis); "we inferred the SZ component jointly with the radio galaxy model, defined by the optimal, ordered set of point-like components, as in (cite: 2023DiMascolo)" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: uv upper threshold 65 klambda  
  > "We also consistently imposed an upper threshold of $65~\mathrm{k\lambda}$ to the analysed visibilities to make the extended radio galaxy unresolved in the direction orthogonal to the jet axis." (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)
   - parameter: SZ centroid fixed to X-ray centroid so that only the mass term (M500) is free per pressure model  
  > "we did, however, fix the centroid position of the SZ model component to match the one derived by modeling the soft-band X-ray surface brightness distribution" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis); "In such a way, the parameter set for each parameter reduces to the mass term only." (Pressure models)
   - assumption: Eight literature gNFW pressure profiles considered (A10 UP/MD, M14 UP/NCC, L15 REF/8.0/8.5, G17 EXT)  
  > "the self-similar extended pressure model by (cite: 2017Gupta)" (Pressure models)
   - fitting: Nested sampling with dynesty; priors and likelihood details not stated  
  > "The posterior exploration was performed using the nested sampling algorithm (cite: 2004Skilling; 2022Ashton), as implemented in the `dynesty` package (cite: dynesty; 2022Koposov)." (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)
   - validation: Model parameters statistically consistent with Di Mascolo et al. 2023; BMA profile used as reference with model variance propagated  
  > "the parameters for each of the reconstructed models are found to be statistically consistent with the ones reported in (cite: 2023DiMascolo)" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis); "we propagate the variance between the various pressure models in the marginalized posterior distribution" (5 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)
6. **Fit beta model to surface brightness and deproject to electron density**: Fit S(r) with beta fixed to 2/3 and center on the AGN for each n_d, then deproject using the relation between the global X-ray spectrum normalization and n_e(r) integrated over a 12 arcsec sphere  
  > "to characterize the Spiderweb proto-ICM we fix $\beta=2/3$" (5.1 ICM density profile); "we calculated the values of $n_{e0}$ deprojecting the $\beta$-model, using Equations 3 and 2 and using the relation between the normalization of the global X-ray spectrum measured in (cite: 2022bTozzi) and the electron density profile $n_e(r)$" (5.1 ICM density profile)
   - software: python curve_fit
   - parameter: Beta model center fixed to AGN coordinates  
  > "The center of the $\beta$ model is fixed to the AGN coordinates" (5.1 ICM density profile)
   - parameter: Spectral extraction region of 12 arcsec used for the volume integral  
  > "the volume $dV$ is simply the spherical volume included in the spectral extraction region of 12 arcsec" (5.1 ICM density profile)
   - assumption: beta fixed because r_c and beta are degenerate with limited radial range  
  > "due to the well known degeneracy between $r_c$ and $\beta$, these two parameters may be simultaneously constrained only when the surface brightness can be probed over a broad range of radii" (5.1 ICM density profile)
   - fitting: Least squares with curve_fit; errors by fixing alternately r_c and S_0 at best fit  
  > "Best-fit values and error bars are computed using the python function `curve_fit`." (Footnote 2); "Error bars (at 1$\sigma$ confidence level) on the best-fit parameters were obtained by fixing alternatively the $r_c$ and $S_0$ parameters to their best-fit values" (5.1 ICM density profile)
   - validation: Best-fit profiles affected by n_d at a level lower than 5%  
  > "the best-fit profiles are affected overall by $n_d$ at a level lower than $5$%" (5.1 ICM density profile)
7. **Derive the temperature profile from SZ pressure and X-ray density**: Marginalize the pressure model realizations within each X-ray annulus, then divide by the binned electron density: kT_e = P_e/n_e  
  > "we post-processed the pressure profiles to estimate the $P_e(r)$ by marginalizing the values of the analytic realizations within each annulus" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles); "the average electron temperature within a given annulus is finally computed as the ratio $k_{B}T_{e}(r)=P_{e}(r)/n_e(r)$" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles)
   - assumption: Ideal gas law; relativistic SZ correction negligible (<~1.5%); X-ray spectral temperature not used  
  > "When simply assuming the ideal gas law" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles); "we do not make use of the information provided by the X-ray spectral analysis" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the X-ray spectral kT=2.0 keV of Tozzi et al. 2022b; SZ-derived value lower by a factor of ~3, discussed as metallicity, non-thermal contamination or SZ filtering systematics  
  > "this value is lower by a factor of $\sim 3$ with respect to that obtained in (cite: 2022bTozzi) with the X-ray spectral analysis" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles)
8. **Compute entropy and cooling time profiles**: Pseudo-entropy K = kT/n_e^(2/3) = P_e/n_e^(5/3); t_cool from bremsstrahlung plus line cooling function  
  > "If we assume the perfect gas law and consider a monatomic gas, the specific pseudo-entropy is given by:" (5.3 Entropy and cooling time profile); "We assumed the cooling function of (cite: 1993Sutherland), with a metallicity of $Z=0.3 \, Z_{\odot}$ in units of (cite: 1989Anders)." (5.3 Entropy and cooling time profile)
   - parameter: Cool-core threshold 30-50 keV cm^2  
  > "found to be between 30 and 50 keV cm$^{2}$ by (cite: 2009Cavagnolo)" (5.3 Entropy and cooling time profile)
   - assumption: Metallicity Z=0.3 Zsun for cooling function  
  > "with a metallicity of $Z=0.3 \, Z_{\odot}$ in units of (cite: 1989Anders)" (5.3 Entropy and cooling time profile)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **Derive hydrostatic total mass, ICM mass and baryon fraction**: Hydrostatic equilibrium total mass from kT(r) and gas density slope; ICM mass by integrating gas density over volume; baryon fraction = M_ICM/M_tot  
  > "the total mass profile under the assumption of hydrostatic equilibrium" (5.4 Total and ICM mass profile); "we can estimate the baryon fraction as the ratio of the ICM mass to the total cluster or protocluster mass" (6.3 Considering a possible high baryon fraction)
   - parameter: mean molecular weight mu = 0.6  
  > "$\mu$ is the mean molecular weight of the ICM, which is set to 0.6" (5.4 Total and ICM mass profile)
   - assumption: Hydrostatic equilibrium  
  > "the total mass profile under the assumption of hydrostatic equilibrium" (5.4 Total and ICM mass profile)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Tozzi et al. 2022b: M_ICM consistent, M_tot lower by a factor of ~2 due to temperature difference  
  > "This discrepancy is entirely due to the difference between the X-ray spectral temperature derived in (cite: 2022bTozzi) and the temperature measured in this work." (5.4 Total and ICM mass profile)
10. **Estimate the AGN feedback power from the putative cavity**: Enthalpy-based buoyancy estimate, requiring v_rise ~ v_exp for a lower limit on L_m, plus sound-crossing, buoyant-rise and refill timescales  
  > "Requiring $v_{\rm rise} \approx v_{\rm exp}$ and solving for $t$ yields a lower limit on the mechanical power" (6.1 Estimate of the feedback energy budget); "Using the parameters we measured for the halo of the Spiderweb Galaxy, we obtained timescales of 80, 100, and 70 Myr" (6.1 Estimate of the feedback energy budget)
   - parameter: R=60 kpc, r_b=11 kpc, P(R)=5e-11 erg cm^-3, gamma=4/3, C_d=0.75, v_c=700 km/s  
  > "Adopting $R=60\,{\rm kpc}$, $r_b=11\,{\rm kpc}$, $P(R)=5\times 10^{-11}\,{\rm erg\,cm^{-3}}$, $\gamma=4/3$, $C_d=0.75$, and $v_c=700\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$" (6.1 Estimate of the feedback energy budget)
   - parameter: Sound speed with gamma_g=5/3, kT=2 keV, mu=0.6  
  > "$\gamma_g=5/3$, $kT=2\,{\rm keV}$, and $\mu=0.6$" (6.1 Estimate of the feedback energy budget)
   - assumption: Pressure inside the bubbles comparable to ICM pressure (subsonic expansion)  
  > "this estimate relies on the assumption that pressure inside the bubbles is comparable to the ICM pressure" (6.1 Estimate of the feedback energy budget)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Two approaches (v_rise=v_exp and Birzan et al. timescales) combined into a 1e43-1e44 erg/s range  
  > "we conclude that the AGN mechanical power is in the range of $\sim 10^{43}-10^{44}\,{\rm erg\,s^{-1}}$" (6.1 Estimate of the feedback energy budget)
11. **Estimate classical mass deposition rate of a cooling flow**: Steady-state isobaric cooling flow: soft-band X-ray luminosity of the central ~3 arcsec as function of n_d converted to MDR via dL_x = (5/2) Mdot k_B dT / (mu m_p)  
  > "we calculated the X-ray luminosity in the soft band for the central $\sim 3$ arcsec (in which the cool core resides) as a function of the parameter, $n_{d}$" (6.2 The Spiderweb: A possible cool-core cluster in the making)
   - assumption: Classical isobaric cooling flow, known to overestimate MDR limits in massive clusters by 10 to 100 times  
  > "which is known to overestimate the actual MDR upper limits inferred in massive clusters from X-ray spectral analysis by10 to 100 times" (6.2 The Spiderweb: A possible cool-core cluster in the making)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with IR SFR of 1390 +/- 150 and 1150 (+1150/-580) Msun/yr; described only as a consistency argument  
  > "Despite the fact that this is just a consistency argument and there are no additional proofs for the presence of a cooling flow in the ICM" (6.2 The Spiderweb: A possible cool-core cluster in the making)

## Qualitative findings

- Temperature gradient indicating a strong cool core at z>2, derived for the first time by combining independent SZ pressure and X-ray density  
  > "we derived, for the first time, the temperature profile in the ICM of a $z>2$ protocluster" (abstract)
- X-ray and SZ centroids both offset to the east/along the same direction, tentatively a cavity or a dynamically young halo  
  > "we observe a marginal agreement between the bulk distribution of the proto-ICM inferred from the SZ signal and from the X-ray diffuse emission beyond 3 arcsec from the AGN" (4 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Combine independent SZ pressure and X-ray density to get a temperature profile that X-ray spectroscopy cannot provide in annuli  
  > "The inherent sensitivity limitation of the available X-ray data makes the measurement of a spatially resolved temperature profile almost impossible." (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles)
- Why this method: Restrict to uv < 65 klambda to make the radio galaxy unresolved perpendicular to the jet, simplifying the model  
  > "This drastically reduces the morphological components required to model the emission, in turn, simplifying the overall modeling procedure." (2.2 ALMA+ACA data)
- Alternative: Non-thermal inverse-Compton contribution to the diffuse X-ray emission cannot be excluded but is not supported by any observed feature  
  > "Overall, we recognize that we cannot definitively exclude an IC contribution to the X-ray diffuse emission; however, this scenario is not supported by any observed feature." (3 Morphology of X-ray emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Diffuse emission within 2 arcsec of the AGN is not observationally constrained  
  > "the surface brightness of the diffuse component within a radius of 2 arcsec is not observationally constrained and was treated as a free parameter" (2.1 Chandra X-ray data)
- Limitation (authors): SZ normalisation may be underestimated because of interferometric filtering of large scales  
  > "the normalization of the SZ signal may be underestimated compared to the actual pressure distribution due to the impact of the large-scale interferometric filtering" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles)
- Limitation (authors): Low significance of SZ and X-ray signals limits detailed morphological comparison  
  > "The low significance of both the SZ signal and the X-ray emission from the proto-ICM inevitably limits the possibility of performing a detailed morphological comparison." (4 SZ and X-ray combined analysis)
- Limitation (authors): ICM metallicity practically unconstrained  
  > "we also note that the ICM metallicity is practically unconstrained" (5.2 Pressure and temperature profiles)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Temperature comes from a ratio of two independently modelled quantities with different systematics; error propagation is not described
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beta fixed at 2/3 and central n_d chosen by hand (n_d=4) drive the inner density and hence the cool-core strength
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SZ result depends on the radio-galaxy model and the 65 klambda cut inherited from the previous paper
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cavity interpretation rests on a sub-2-sigma depression
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spherical symmetry assumed despite the reported 2-6 arcsec offsets between centroids
