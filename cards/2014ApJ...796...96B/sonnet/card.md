## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image ALMA continuum**: CASA calibration, manual flux setting for gain calibrator, combined natural-weighted continuum map  
  > "We performed data calibration and imaging using the Common Astronomy Software Application (CASA)." (2.1 Atacama Large Millimeter Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: natural weighting, no primary beam correction  
  > "The resulting combined continuum map (natural weighting without primary beam correction)" (2.1 Atacama Large Millimeter Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **measure ALMA flux limit**: flux density at source location -4+/-11 uJy; 3 sigma upper limit  
  > "(with a flux density at the source location of $-4\pm 11$ $\mu$Jy), leading an upper limit of $F_\nu(222\,{\rm
  GHz})\lesssim 33$ $\mu$Jy" (2.1 Atacama Large Millimeter Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **derive IR luminosity and obscured SFR limits**: compare to template SEDs and modified blackbody  
  > "the ALMA non-detection corresponds to an upper limit on the integrated IR luminosity of $L_{\rm IR}(8-1000\, {\rm \mu m})\lesssim (2-5)\times 10^{10}$ L$_\odot$" (3 Host Galaxy Properties)
   - assumption: T_dust 30-50 K, beta 1.5-2; lower T bound from CMB T(z=8.23)~25 K  
  > "the lower bound on the blackbody temperature is set by the cosmic microwave background temperature $T_{\rm CMB}(z=8.23)\approx
25$ K (cite: dgw+13)" (3 Host Galaxy Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-checked against Rieke et al. 2009 templates  
  > "agrees well with the comparison to the (cite: raw+09) templates" (3 Host Galaxy Properties)
4. **limit stellar mass from HST and Spitzer**: BC03 models with constant SFR  
  > "for a stellar population age of $10-100$ Myr (typical of GRB host galaxies; (cite: sgl09; lb10)) the limit on the host stellar mass is $M_*\lesssim (1-5)\times 10^7$ M$_\odot$" (3 Host Galaxy Properties)
   - assumption: Salpeter IMF, 0.2 Zsun, no dust, age 10-100 Myr  
  > "Assuming a stellar population age of $10-100$ Myr, constant star formation rate, Salpeter IMF, $Z=0.2$ Z$_\odot$, and $A_V^{\rm
    host}=0$ mag." (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: GRB hosts with spectroscopic redshifts and ISM metallicities are a good sample for obscured SF studies at high z  
  > "Thus, spectroscopically-confirmed GRB host galaxies at high redshift provide an attractive sample for multi-wavelength follow-up observations." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Limits are weaker if SEDs resemble low-metallicity dwarfs like I Zw 18 (SFR limit relaxes to <~100 Msun/yr); asteroid flux models are uncertain  
  > "scaling the SED of the low metallicity dwarf galaxy I Zw 18 relaxes the star formation rate upper limit to $\lesssim
100$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$" (Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Limits depend strongly on assumed SED template and dust temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Natural weighting and no beam-correction details are minimal
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only a non-detection, so little constraint on typical dwarf-like hosts
