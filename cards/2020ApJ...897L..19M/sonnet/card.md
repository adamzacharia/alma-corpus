## Analysis recipe

1. **Image cubes**: tclean with Briggs weighting at 15 km/s resolution  
  > "with a velocity resolution of 15 km s$^{-1}$, cell size of 0.2$''$, image size of 200 pixel" (3 Morita Array Observation)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5, 0.2'' cells, 200 px  
  > "Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5" (3 Morita Array Observation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure CO(4-3) flux and [C I] upper limit**: Integrate primary-beam-corrected spectrum over FoV; 3 sigma upper limit for [C I] using box/beam scaling  
  > "We obtain a $3\sigma$ upper limit for the [C I] (1–0) velocity integrated intensity using" (3 Morita Array Observation)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: FWHM=96 km/s assumed for [C I]; N_box=3990; N_beam=247  
  > "$N_{\rm box} = 3990$ (the number of pixel where CO (4–3) is detected inside the three sigma contour of Figure [2](#fig:mom0)(b))" (3 Morita Array Observation)
   - assumption: [C I] line shape same as CO(4-3)  
  > "assuming the same value with CO (4–3) line shape" (3 Morita Array Observation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 30% systematic flux uncertainty adopted  
  > "we adopt 30 % as a conservative systematic uncertainly error" (3 Morita Array Observation)
3. **Compute luminosities**: L (Lsun) and L' (K km/s pc^2) equations from Solomon & Vanden Bout  
  > "$\nu_{\rm obs}=\nu_{\rm rest}/(1+z)$ is sky frequency for the observing line emission in GHz (cite: Solomon_2005)" (3 Morita Array Observation)
   - parameter: D_L=70.8 Mpc, z=0.01581  
  > "the corresponding luminosity distance is $D_L=70.8$ Mpc" (2 NGC 6052)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **PDR modeling of density**: Compare L[CI]/L_CO(4-3) and L[CI]/L_TIR upper limits to PDRT tracks of constant n_H and U_uv  
  > "The red solid and blue dashed lines indicate the tracks for constant $n_{\rm H}$ and $U_{\rm uv}$, respectively." (4.1 Photodissociation Regions)
   - software: PDRT
   - assumption: Milky Way metallicity; plane-parallel PDR  
  > "we assume the Milky Way metallicity for PDR modeling" (2 NGC 6052)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SPIRE/FTS U/LIRG sample  
  > "together with the data for nearby U/LIRGs observed by SPIRE/FTS (cite: Kame_2016), and the upper limits we obtain for NGC 6052." (Discussion, Photodissociation Regions)
5. **Molecular gas mass comparison**: M_H2 = alpha*L' for CO(1-0) and [C I] upper limit  
  > "where $M_{\rm H2}^{\rm line} = \alpha_{\rm line} L'_{\rm line}$" (4.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=0.8, alpha_[CI]=7.3 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "assuming a CO (1–0) to H$_2$ conversion factor $\alpha_{\rm CO}$=0.8" (4.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Jiao et al. global L'[CI]-L'CO relation  
  > "located an order of magnitude below the global relation obtained in previous surveys" (4.2 Molecular gas mass)
