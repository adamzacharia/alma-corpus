## Analysis recipe

1. **Image the ALMA continuum in Bands 7 and 4**: Run the default pipeline script ScriptForPi.py, then tclean to make the continuum image in each band  
  > "We utilized the default pipeline ScriptForPi.py and performed tclean using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (cite: 2022PASP..134k4501C) to obtain the continuum image in each band." (ALMA mm/sub-mm data and Herschel far-IR data)
   - software: CASA, tclean, ScriptForPi.py
   - parameter: Briggs weighting, robust = 2.0, cleaned to 3 sigma  
  > "The cleaning was done to 3 $\sigma$ with the default natural weighting parameters of WEIGHTING = “BRIGGS” and ROBUST = 2.0." (ALMA mm/sub-mm data and Herschel far-IR data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Reduce JWST NIRCam and MIRI images**: Rerun JWST calibration stages 1 and 2 from uncal products with custom fixes, then mosaic  
  > "We started from the Stage 1 “uncal.fits” products retrieved from the Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST)" (JWST near-to-mid IR Data)
   - software: JWST calibration pipeline, image1overf, JHAT
   - parameter: JWST pipeline 1.10.2 (jwst_1089.pmap) for NIRCam, 1.12.5 (jwst_1183.pmap) for MIRI  
  > "Our further process used the JWST pipeline version 1.10.2 in the context of `jwst_1089.pmap` for the NIRCam data and version 1.12.5 in the context of `jwst_1183.pmap` for the MIRI data." (JWST near-to-mid IR Data)
   - parameter: Mosaic pixel scale 0.06 arcsec  
  > "the individual exposures in each band were then stacked into one mosaic with a pixel scale of 0.06$''$" (JWST near-to-mid IR Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Model and subtract the foreground lens galaxy**: Galfit models of the lens in 10 x 10 arcsec cutouts; four-component model in NIRCam bands  
  > "we utilized the Galfit software (cite: Peng2002; Peng2010) to model this foreground galaxy." (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
   - software: Galfit, WebbPSF
   - parameter: Sersic disk, log-tanh spiral and truncated Sersic ring (plus a central point source) in NIRCam bands  
  > "a Sérsic profile for the disk component, a logarithmic-hyperbolic tangent spiral (log-tanh) profile for the barred spiral structure, a truncated Sérsic profile for the ring structure" (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
   - parameter: MIRI PSFs from WebbPSF  
  > "For the MIRI F770W and F1800W images, we directly used the WebbPSF to obtain their PSFs." (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residual images inspected; counter image recovered in the residuals  
  > "In addition to the cleaned arc, the counter image of the arc is now also recovered in the residual images." (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
4. **Measure JWST photometry of the arc**: PSF-match NIRCam to F444W, then dual-image SExtractor photometry on the lens-subtracted residuals  
  > "Matched-aperture photometry was then done by using SExtractor in the dual-image mode, where the F444W image was set as the detection image." (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
   - software: SExtractor, pypher, astroRMS
   - parameter: MAG_ISO colors, normalised to total using MAG_AUTO minus MAG_ISO in F444W  
  > "We took the isophotal magnitudes (“MAG_ISO”) to measure the colors and corrected the normalization of the SED to total magnitudes based on the difference between “MAG_AUTO” and “MAG_ISO” measured in the F444W band." (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
   - parameter: F090W 2-sigma limits of 28.43 mag (r=0.2 arcsec aperture) and 26.98 mag (MAG_ISO aperture)  
  > "One was the average 2 $\sigma$ depth of the image over the entire field as measured within an $r=$ 0.2$''$ aperture, which is 28.43 mag" (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images); "which is 26.98 mag and likely very conservative" (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
   - assumption: 10% of the flux added in quadrature to errors for imperfect lens removal  
  > "Considering that the removal of the foreground galaxy is not perfect, we added (in quadrature) 10% of the fluxes to the errors." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Measure ALMA fluxes of the arc and counter image**: Sum pixels within polygon apertures in CASA; Band 7 main and counter images separated, Band 4 blended  
  > "the photometry in the ALMA images was done using CASA with polygon apertures" (Photometry in the ALMA images); "Basically, this was to sum up the pixels within the polygons." (Photometry in the ALMA images)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Error = aperture pixel rms plus flux calibration uncertainty in quadrature  
  > "The flux error was estimated to include the rms of the pixel values within the aperture and the uncertainty of the flux calibration (cite: 2014Msngr.155...19F), which were added in quadrature." (Photometry in the ALMA images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Band 4 peak flux is lower than the polygon flux, taken as evidence that the source is resolved  
  > "which is significantly lower than that measured in the polygon. This supports that the Band 4 image is resolved." (Photometry in the ALMA images)
6. **Deblend Herschel SPIRE photometry**: Fit SPIRE PSFs with Galfit at the VLA positions of COSBO-7 and two neighbors simultaneously  
  > "to the maps using Galfit at the VLA positions of COSBO-7, H1 and H2 simultaneously" (Photometry in the Herschel SPIRE maps)
   - software: Galfit
   - parameter: Beam solid angles 469.35, 831.27, 1804.31 arcsec^2 for 250, 350, 500 um  
  > "we used the beam solid angles of 469.35, 831.27 and 1804.31 arcsec$^2$ for 250, 350 and 500 $\mu$m, respectively" (Photometry in the Herschel SPIRE maps)
   - parameter: H2 dropped in the 500 um fit because Galfit crashed otherwise  
  > "Therefore, we discarded H2 when de-blending in this band." (Photometry in the Herschel SPIRE maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Fit the JWST SED for photometric redshift and stellar properties**: Four SED tools (LePhare, EAZY-py, Bagpipes, CIGALE) fitted to the arc photometry for two F090W limits  
  > "we utilized four different tools, namely, LePhare (cite: Arnouts1999; Ilbert2006), the python version of EAZY (cite: Brammer2008), Bagpipes (cite: Carnall2018), and CIGALE" (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - software: LePhare, EAZY, Bagpipes, CIGALE
   - parameter: Redshift 0 to 10, no prior  
  > "The redshift was allowed to vary from 0 to 10, and no prior was applied." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - parameter: CIGALE: age > 10 Myr, AGN fraction 0, 0 <= Z/Zsun <= 2.5  
  > "The age of the stellar population was limited to $>10$ Myr, and the AGN contribution was set to 0." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry); "The metallicity was allowed to vary between $0 \le Z_{*}/Z_{\odot} \le 2.5$." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - parameter: F090W limit entered as flux and error for EAZY, CIGALE and Bagpipes  
  > "we set the flux density and its error in F090W to the magnitude limit quoted in" (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - assumption: BC03 models with Chabrier IMF and solar metallicity (LePhare)  
  > "The fitting by LePhare used the population synthesis models of (cite: Bruzual2003) with solar metallicity and the initial mass function (IMF) of (cite: Chabrier2003)." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - assumption: Calzetti dust law, E(B-V) from 0 to 1.0 mag  
  > "we adopted the Calzetti’s dust extinction law (cite: Calzetti1994; Calzetti2001) with $E(B-V)$ ranging from 0 to 1.0 mag" (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - fitting: LePhare rejects solutions that violate the F090W limit; other tools use the limit as a data point  
  > "LePhare allows the use of magnitude limits, which we enabled so that any solutions violating the F090W limit were rejected." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
   - validation: Refit with z <= 6; those solutions have significantly worse chi2  
  > "These solutions, however, all have significantly worse $\chi^2$" (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
8. **Fit the far-IR-to-mm SED with a modified blackbody**: Single-temperature MBB fit to Herschel, ALMA (two ALMA 870 um images combined) and MAMBO-2 photometry, for beta = 1.5, 2.0, 2.5  
  > "the fit was done using three different choices of $\beta=1.5$, 2.0 and 2.5" (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED); "The combined flux density is $S_{870}=10.55\pm1.06$ mJy." (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
   - software: cmcirsed
   - parameter: lambda_0 = 100 um  
  > "We set $\lambda_0=100$ $\mu$m." (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
   - parameter: L_FIR integrated over rest-frame 60-1000 um  
  > "are the far-IR luminosity integrated from 60 to 1000 $\mu$m in the rest-frame" (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
   - assumption: Redshift fixed to z = 7.455  
  > "We fixed the redshift to $z=7.455$ (see Appendix A)." (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
   - assumption: L_FIR treated as L_IR, Chabrier IMF in the SFR conversion  
  > "for a Chabrier IMF, and ignoring the difference between" (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeated at fixed z=4 as a check; temperature drops at lower redshift  
  > "Due to the degeneracy of dust temperature and redshift, the high temperature obtained in Section 4.3 is lowered in this case because of the adopted lower redshift." (Alternative z_ph estimate by limiting z <= 6)
9. **Reconstruct the source plane with a lens model**: SIE lens plus Sersic source fitted to the residual F444W image with PICS, optimizer then emcee  
  > "we re-constructed its image in the source plane by modeling the residual F444W image where the foreground lens is subtracted" (Lens modeling); "The mass model of the lens was a Singular Isothermal Ellipsoid (cite: Kormann1994)" (Lens modeling)
   - software: PICS, emcee, SciPy
   - parameter: Initial best fit with scipy optimize.minimize, then emcee posterior sampling  
  > "We first found the best-fit results using the `optimize.minimize` function in SCIPY" (Lens modeling)
   - parameter: Dusty-region magnification mu_d from the 3x3 pixel mean of the magnification map at the ALMA 870 um peak  
  > "take the average value of a $3\times 3$ pixel region around this position as $\mu_d$" (Lens modeling)
   - assumption: Centre of mass aligned with centre of light; modeling done in angular scales without assuming redshifts  
  > "Assuming that the center of mass aligns with the center of light" (Lens modeling); "The above modeling procedure was implemented on angular scales without assuming redshifts of the lens and the source." (Lens modeling)
   - fitting: Chi-square-like likelihood with weight map and mask; priors not stated  
  > "To fit the data, we designed the likelihood below:" (Lens modeling)
   - validation: Model counter-image flux ratio ~12.1 compared with measured ~11.7  
  > "The flux ratio between the arc and its counter-image is $\sim$12.1 in our model, which is consistent with the measured ratio of $\sim$11.7" (Lens modeling)
10. **Extract and interpret the ALMA Band 3 line**: Spectrum extracted with the CASA viewer in a 4 arcsec diameter aperture at the Band 4 image position; line attributed to CO(7-6)  
  > "We extracted the spectrum using the CASA task viewer." (ALMA Band 3 Line Detection); "with a circular aperture of 4$''$ in diameter" (ALMA Band 3 Line Detection)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Channel width 7.812 MHz, pixel 0.25 arcsec

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA 870 um is the only far-IR/mm image with enough resolution to separate the two lensed images  
  > "In this regime, the ALMA 870 $\mu$m image is the only one of a high enough resolution that separates the two lensed images of COSBO-7" (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
- Why this method: Four SED tools used because results depend on software and models  
  > "It is well known that SED fitting results depend on the fitting software as well as the models that it employs" (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
- Alternative: SED fits restricted to z <= 6 rejected as significantly worse in chi2  
  > "To explore such low-$z$ possibilities, we repeated the process described in Section 4.2 but with the difference of setting an upper limit of $z\leq 6$." (Alternative z_ph estimate by limiting z <= 6)
- Alternative: AzTEC 1.1 mm, SCUBA-2 450 um and SCUBA-2 850 um measurements left out of the far-IR-to-mm SED  
  > "the AzTEC 1.1 mm result is of low S/N" (Fitting of the far-IR-to-mm SED)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Low-redshift solutions cannot be excluded by SED fitting  
  > "Of course, we cannot rule out the low-redshift possibility due to the nature of SED fitting." (Fitting of the SED based on JWST photometry)
- Limitation (authors): Single Band 3 line is ambiguous (CO 6-5, 5-4, 4-3 alternatives)  
  > "However, such a single line is not sufficient to unambiguously determine the redshift because it could also be due to other lines such as CO (6-5)" (ALMA Band 3 Line Detection)
- Limitation (authors): No accurate photometry of the counter image  
  > "We did not attempt to obtain accurate photometry of the counter image because it is much fainter and is severely affected by the noise due to the image subtraction." (Photometry in the JWST NIRCam and MIRI images)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Redshift rests on photometry plus one ambiguous line; the MBB fit fixes z=7.455 and the dust temperature depends on it
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dusty-region magnification mu_d comes from the ALMA peak position mapped through a model fitted to F444W only, so astrometric offsets between ALMA and JWST propagate
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar SFR spans two dex across SED tools, so the starburst conclusion for the disk depends on template choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SPIRE 250 um flux is 16.58 mJy in the text but 15.58 mJy in the photometry table
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Lens-model uncertainties and priors are not given in enough detail to judge the 'disk' morphology claim
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Band 4 and Band 3 beams are 1.3-1.6 arcsec, so ALMA photometry of the counter image is blended there
