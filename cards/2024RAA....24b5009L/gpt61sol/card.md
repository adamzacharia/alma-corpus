## Science

- Fields: star_formation, astrochemistry, milky_way_ism
- Question: Investigate star formation within protoclusters down to 1000 au, including fragmentation, filamentary feeding, dense-core evolution and feedback.  
  > "The primary goal of the QUARKS survey was to investigate the star formation process within protoclusters down to a scale of 1000 au in an unbiased and statistical manner." (Summary); "to explore how (hub-)filamentary structures feed individual protostars within protoclusters" (Introduction); "to investigate the physical and chemical evolution of dense cores, and (4) to study various feedback mechanisms within protoclusters." (Introduction)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Higher-resolution follow-up of ATOMS dense kernels to resolve protocluster structures and gas transport; broad spectral coverage samples different gas components.  
  > "A higher-resolution ($\sim$1000 au) survey would enable to systematically establish whether the hierarchical structures remain common down to a protocluster scale" (Introduction); "to provide for theoretical studies the observational constraints onto the detailed process of gas mass transport down to the protocluster scale." (Introduction); "The spectral windows cover transitions of species such as CO, SO, N$_2$D$^+$, SiO, H$_{30}\alpha$, and many complex organic molecules, tracing gas components with varying temperatures and spatial extents." (Summary)
- Why this method: Combine arrays for compact and extended emission; use multi-scale multi-frequency synthesis for multiple configurations and SPWs; self-calibrate strong sources to improve dynamic range.
