## Analysis recipe

1. **Produce continuum maps and line cubes**: Average line-free channels; concatenate and jointly clean 12 m + 7 m data; also make 7 m-only continuum maps; use YCLEAN for line cubes  
  > "Continuum images were produced by averaging line-free channels." (Observations and Data Reduction); "After subtracting continuum emission, we combined the 12 m array data with the 7 m array data using the CASA task $\mathtt{concat}$, and then they were cleaned together." (Observations and Data Reduction); "For molecular lines, we used the automatic cleaning algorithm for imaging data cubes, YCLEAN (cite: Contreas_yclean_18; Contreras18) to CLEAN each spectral window with custom-made masks." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, concat, TCLEAN, YCLEAN
   - parameter: Continuum bandwidth ~3.7 GHz  
  > "The effective bandwidth for continuum emission was $\sim$3.7 GHz." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Continuum robust 0.5; MULTISCALE scales 0, 5, 15, 25 pixels  
  > "We used $\mathtt{TCLEAN}$ with Brigg’s robust weighting of 0.5 to the visibilities and an imaging option of MULTISCALE with scales of 0, 5, 15, and 25 times the pixel size" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Line robust 2.0; 512 × 512 pixels; 0.2 arcsec pixels  
  > "We adopted a Briggs's robust weighting of 2.0 (natural weighting) to improve the signal-to-noise (S/N) ratio." (Observations and Data Reduction); "All images have 512 $\times$ 512 pixels with a pixel size of 0.2$''$" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Adopt the continuum core sample**: Use Morii23 dendrogram cores and masses; reject faint and FOV-edge structures  
  > "Using the dendrogram technique (cite: Rosolowsky08), (cite: Morii23) identified cores using the 1.3 mm continuum images." (Core Sample); "They have excluded cores with a flux density smaller than 3.5$\sigma$. Additionally, they eliminated cores at the edge of FOV." (Core Sample); "For the analysis carried out in this work, we have adopted the core masses from (cite: Morii23)." (Core Sample)
   - parameter: F_min = 2.5 sigma; delta = 1.0 sigma  
  > "They set a minimum value, $F_\mathrm{min}$, as 2.5$\sigma$, a minimum significance to separate them, $\delta$, as 1.0$\sigma$" (Core Sample)
   - parameter: S_min = half-pixel numbers of the beam area  
  > "the minimum number of pixels to be contained in the smallest individual structure, $S_\mathrm{min}$, as the half-pixel numbers of the beam area." (Core Sample)
   - parameter: 839 leaf structures from 39 clumps  
  > "The total number of identified $\mathtt{leaf}$ structures is 839 from 39 clumps" (Core Sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Additional flux-density constraint to exclude suspicious structures  
  > "Since these parameters are optimistic values, they applied the additional constraint to the flux density to exclude suspicious structures." (Core Sample)
3. **Classify evolutionary stages**: Outflow cores from outflows; warm cores from warm emission without outflows; prestellar candidates from neither detection  
  > "We judged cores associated with outflows as outflow cores, and any detection of warm line emission without outflow detections as warm cores." (Classification); "In turn, cores without any detections of outflow and warm gas tracers are classified as prestellar core candidates." (Classification)
   - parameter: Outflow tracers: CO J = 2–1 and SiO J = 5–4  
  > "molecular outflows traced by CO $J$ = 2–1 and SiO $J$ = 5–4" (Classification)
   - parameter: Warm tracers: H2CO, CH3OH and HC3N; upper-state energy >45 K  
  > "H$_2$CO $J_\mathrm{K_a,K_c}$= 3$_{2,2}-2_{2,1}$, H$_2$CO $J_\mathrm{K_a,K_c}$= 3$_{2,1}-2_{2,0}$, CH$_3$OH $J_\mathrm{K}$= 4$_{2}-3_{1}$, and HC$_3$N $J=24-23$), which all have the upper state energy higher than 45 K." (Classification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with pilot ASHES classification  
  > "This is consistent with the classification of the pilot ASHES survey (cite: Li23)." (Classification)
4. **Measure core gas velocity dispersions**: Fit core-averaged N2D+ and DCO+ spectra from combined 12 m + 7 m data  
  > "We fitted the core-averaged spectrum of the N$_2$D$^+$ and DCO $^+$ taken from the 12 and 7 m arrays combined data with a single Gaussian." (Classification)
   - parameter: Detection S/N >3; smooth marginal ~3 sigma spectra over two native channels before fitting  
  > "We judged if the emission is detected with signal-to-noise larger than 3." (Classification); "the core-averaged spectrum is spectrally smoothed over two native channels, prior to Gaussian fittings, if it shows marginal $\sim$3$\sigma$ confidence in the native spectral resolution." (Classification)
   - parameter: Generally adopt DCO+ when both lines are detected; use either detected line otherwise  
  > "if both lines are detected in a core, we generally use $\sigma_{\rm obs}$ measured from DCO$^+$, except for a few cases." (Classification); "We adopt the $\sigma_{\rm obs}$ value if either line is detected." (Classification)
   - assumption: Both lines trace the same physical location: dense gas  
  > "Following the discussion in (cite: Li22), both lines trace the same physical location (dense gas)" (Classification)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian  
  > "We fitted the core-averaged spectrum of the N$_2$D$^+$ and DCO $^+$ taken from the 12 and 7 m arrays combined data with a single Gaussian." (Classification)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Classify gravitational stability**: Calculate alpha = M_vir/M_core with M_vir = 5 sigma_tot^2 R/(a beta G); classify bound, unbound and line non-detection cores  
  > "we calculated the virial parameter ($\alpha$), the ratio of the virial mass ($M_{\rm vir}$) to core mass ($M_{\rm core}$)." ## Qualitative findings

- Early core formation is regulated by thermal Jeans fragmentation rather than turbulent fragmentation  
  > "we conclude that core formation found in the ASHES sample (and likely in IRDCs in general) is regulated by thermal Jeans fragmentation rather than turbulent fragmentation." (Thermal versus Turbulent Jeans Fragmentation)
- Core properties favor clump fragmentation over sub-clump fragmentation  
  > "Overall, the observed core properties favor the fragmentation from clumps rather than sub-clumps." (Hierarchical fragmentation)
- Simultaneous sub-clump and core formation is a possible picture  
  > "A possible picture is that sub-clumps and cores are simultaneously formed from clumps, and core properties are determined from clump properties." (Early Fragmentation picture)
- Higher surface density accompanies higher fragmentation level  
  > "the higher the clump surface density, the more likely it is to have a larger number of cores." (Fragmentation Level and Clump Properties)
- Increasing core mass dynamic range with protostellar fraction is interpreted as clump-fed accretion  
  > "if we take the fraction of protostellar cores as an indicator of cluster evolution, the dynamic range of mass increases with cluster evolution." (Fragmentation Diversity); "This correlation can be interpreted as clump-fed accretion onto cores." (Fragmentation Diversity)
- No clear correlation between mass dynamic range and fragmentation pattern  
  > "we find no clear correlation between the dynamic range in mass and the fragmentation pattern; both aligned fragmentation and spread fragmentation show large and small mass dispersion." (Fragmentation Diversity)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Large early-stage IRDC sample with mosaicked high-resolution, high-sensitivity coverage  
  > "This is the largest sample of cores embedded in IRDCs and is thought to be the best sample for a statistical study of cores in the very early phase of evolution to date." (Introduction); "We have the largest sample in IRDCs thanks to the mosaicked high spatial resolution and high sensitivity ALMA observations." (Thermal versus Turbulent Jeans Fragmentation)
- Why this method: MULTISCALE for extended emission; natural weighting for line S/N; matched areas and mass threshold to reduce distance and sensitivity effects  
  > "an imaging option of MULTISCALE with scales of 0, 5, 15, and 25 times the pixel size, considering the extended components of IRDCs." (Observations and Data Reduction); "We adopted a Briggs's robust weighting of 2.0 (natural weighting) to improve the signal-to-noise (S/N) ratio." (Observations and Data Reduction); "we count the number of bound cores within the same physical area for all clumps and impose a mass threshold to reduce the effect of having different distances and sensitivities for each clump." (Fragmentation Level and Clump Properties)
- Alternative: Turbulent Jeans fragmentation rejected for early ASHES core formation  
  > "The core mass and core separation are explained by thermal Jeans fragmentation ruling out turbulent Jeans fragmentation at the very early stages of high-mass star formation." (Conclusions)
- Alternative: Step-by-step clump-to-sub-clump-to-core fragmentation considered unlikely  
  > "This implies that a step-by-step fragmentation (clump to sub-clump to core) is unlikely." (Hierarchical fragmentation)
- Alternative: Super-Jeans cores may grow through environmental gas accretion or reflect magnetic suppression of fragmentation  
  > "Those cores may have grown in mass by acquiring gas from the surrounding environment." (Early Fragmentation picture); "Alternatively, the magnetic field may play a role in suppressing fragmentation as suggested by theoretical studies (cite: Hennebelle08; Commercon11)." (Early Fragmentation picture)
- Alternative: Distance co-dependence checked using fixed-area clump properties  
  > "To confirm that these correlations do not result from the co-dependence on the distance" (Fragmentation Level and Clump Properties); "using flux within the same physical area (r=0.45 pc)." (Fragmentation Level and Clump Properties)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only 492 of 839 cores have observed velocity dispersions  
  > "As a result, we obtained $\sigma_{\rm obs}$ information of 492 cores among 839 cores." (Classification)
- Limitation (authors): Virial interpretation ignores magnetic fields and external pressure  
  > "if we ignore additional support of magnetic fields or external pressure." (Classification)
- Limitation (authors): Detailed per-core virial results deferred to a following paper  
  > "The detailed results for each core’s virial analysis will be presented in a following paper." (Classification)
- Limitation (authors): Clump-to-sub-clump distributions have insufficient statistics for a clear peak  
  > "We find no clear peak in the distributions due to the small number of statistics." (Hierarchical fragmentation)
- Limitation (authors): Simultaneous formation interpretation may apply only at very early stages  
  > "this simultaneous formation of sub-clumps and cores, in which the core properties are not determined by sub-clumps, may only be valid at the very early stages of high-mass star formation traced in the ASHES survey." (Hierarchical fragmentation)
- Limitation (authors): Extreme core masses can strongly affect dynamic-range measures  
  > "The x-axis of the top two panels is derived from the maximum and minimum core masses and can be significantly affected by a single peculiar object if exists." (Fragmentation Diversity)
- Limitation (authors): Q cannot identify aligned fragmentation  
  > "this parameter is unable to identify aligned fragmentation." (Fragmentation Diversity)
- Limitation (authors): Origin of fragmentation-pattern diversity remains unclear; magnetic-field and large-scale dynamics information needed  
  > "The origin of such variation in the fragmentation pattern is not yet clear from the current data" (Fragmentation Diversity); "further information on the magnetic field or clump-scale properties such as large-scale gas dynamics seems to be necessary." (Fragmentation Diversity)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A statistical projection correction does not recover individual three-dimensional separations.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Bound-core selection may favor cores with detectable deuterated-line emission.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Snapshot correlations support accretion and simultaneous formation interpretations without directly measuring their time sequence.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam-convolved sub-clump sizes propagate into densities and Jeans comparisons.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The printed Q normalization has inconsistent edge-count summation limits and needs implementation clarification.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The prose clump thermal Jeans mass range differs from the displayed table, which includes 1.0 M_sun and has a maximum of 5.3 M_sun.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust-temperature, distance, opacity and virial assumptions can shift the comparisons; propagated systematic uncertainties are not specified.
