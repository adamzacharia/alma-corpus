## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra and fit line parameters**: Spectra extracted from 0.42" circular region at continuum center; Gaussian fits to lines for Tb, FWHM, VLSR, integrated intensity  
  > "Line parameters are measured by fitting a Gaussian profile to observed lines." (3.1 Spectra)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Detection criterion 2.5 sigma and velocity coincidence with CO clouds  
  > "The detection criteria adopted here are the 2.5$\sigma$ significance level and the velocity coincidence with the systemic velocity of nearby CO clouds (cite: Won11)." (3.1 Spectra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Line IDs with CDMS and JPL  
  > "Spectral lines are identified with the aid of the Cologne Database for Molecular Spectroscopy" (3.1 Spectra)
2. **Rotation diagram analysis**: LTE optically thin rotation diagrams; two-temperature fits split at Eu=100 K for CH3OH and SO2  
  > "We use the transitions with $E_{u}$ $<$100 K to fit the lower-temperature component and the transitions with $E_{u}$ $>$100 K to fit the higher-temperature component" (4.1)
   - assumption: Optically thin, LTE; SO from SO(3_3-2_3) with Trot=50 K  
  > "We here assume an optically thin condition and the local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)." (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Non-LTE RADEX comparison, n(H2)=3e6 cm-3, Tbg 2.73 K  
  > "The calculated non-LTE column densities are reasonably consistent with the LTE estimations." (4.2)
3. **Column densities of other molecules**: Solve rotation diagram equation for N with assumed Trot (50 K for extended/SO/CS/CCH/CN, 100 K for compact)  
  > "For other molecules that are concentrated at the hot core, we assume $T_{\mathrm{rot}}$ = 100 K" (4.2)
   - software: RADEX
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive H2 column density**: Optically thin dust continuum at 870 and 1200 um; SED fits (Robitaille, Zhang & Tan); 9.7 um silicate depth; average adopted  
  > "we use $N_{\mathrm{H_2}}$ = (5.6 $\pm$ 0.6) $\times$ 10$^{23}$ cm$^{-2}$ as a representative value" (4.3.4)
   - parameter: Td = 20, 60, 150 K tested; 60 K adopted  
  > "We estimate $N_{\mathrm{H_2}}$ for three different dust temperatures, 20 K, 60 K, and 150 K" (4.3.1)
   - assumption: Dust-to-gas mass ratio 0.0027 (0.008 scaled by LMC Z~1/3); NH2/AV = 2.8e21; distance = LMC  
  > "We use the dust-to-gas mass ratio of 0.0027 for the LMC, which is obtained by scaling the Galactic value of 0.008" (4.3.1)
   - fitting: SED: chi2 cutoff (chi2-chi2best)/Ndata<=3 median; best five Zhang&Tan models averaged  
  > "We select the fit results which have $(\chi^2 - \chi^2_{best})/N_{data}$ $\leqq$ 3" (4.3.2)
   - validation: Consistency among methods  
  > "consistent $N_{\mathrm{H_2}}$ values are obtained by different methods" (4.3.4)
5. **Fractional abundances and comparison with Galactic/LMC hot cores**: N(X)/NH2; metallicity-scaled by factor 3 for LMC; compare with literature  
  > "the abundances of LMC hot cores are multiplied by three to correct for the metallicity difference" (5.2)
   - assumption: 12C/13C=49  
  > "assuming $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C = 49 (cite: Wan09)" (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Sulfur isotope ratios**: Ratios of column densities of SO, SO2, CS isotopologues; weighted mean  
  > "The weight is the inverse of the squared error value." (Table 6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Astrochemical simulations**: Gas-grain model with cold (1e5 yr, AV 1-5 mag), warm-up (5e4 yr, to 150 K, 6e6 cm-3, AV 100) and post-warm-up (1e5 yr) stages; LMC vs Galactic  
  > "The warm-up stage continues up to $t_h = 5 \times 10^4$ years in our model." (Appendix)
   - assumption: LMC: Z=1/3, gas-to-dust 300, chi_uv=3  
  > "Gas to dust ratio | 100 (MW), 300 (LMC)" (Table 10)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Rotating envelope traced by SO2 and 34SO, east red-shifted and west blue-shifted, velocity separation 2-3 km/s  
  > "the east side of the core is red-shifted and the west side is blue-shifted, and the velocity separation is about 2–3 km s$^{-1}$" (5.5)
- CCH and CN trace an outflow cavity (PDR-like)  
  > "The present characteristic distributions of CCH and CN presumably trace the PDR-like outflow cavity structure" (5.7)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Several independent NH2 methods used to check reliability  
  > "A column density of molecular hydrogen ($N_{\mathrm{H_2}}$) is estimated by several methods to check their reliability." (4.3)
- Alternative: SO rotation diagram rejected as unreliable due to optically thick lines; used SO(3_3-2_3) instead  
  > "Note that the temperature and column density derived from the rotation diagram of SO would not be reliable" (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Infall not detected because no optically thick tracer; NH2 includes low-temperature components  
  > "an infall motion is not seen in the present data, because we do not cover a fully optically thick molecular tracer in this work" (5.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single pointing with 0.4" beam; beam dilution and LTE assumptions affect Trot and N
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Assumed Trot for many species and Td=60 K drive abundance uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Simulations are a toy model with limited parameter exploration
