## Science

- Fields: star_formation, astrochemistry
- Question: Does the forming O-type star AFGL 4176 host a Keplerian-like disk, as traced by 1.2 mm continuum and CH3CN J=13-12 emission?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure mm1 continuum and derive mass**: Gaussian fit to mm1; mass and column density from Hildebrand (1983) equations  
  > "Performing a gaussian fit to mm1, we determined a peak flux of 37$\pm$2 mJy beam$^{-1}$" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - parameter: Td=190 K, gas-to-dust 154, kappa(1.21 mm)=0.24 cm2/g  
  > "assuming 190 K (the average temperature of the disk derived from CASSIS LTE line modeling below)" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - assumption: Dust temperature 190 K from LTE modeling average  
  > "assuming 190 K (the average temperature of the disk derived from CASSIS LTE line modeling below)" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Pixel-by-pixel LTE modeling of CH3CN/CH3(13)CN K ladders**: LTE model of each pixel spectrum giving Tex, vLSR, FWHM, N  
  > "The modeling was carried out using CASSIS and the JPL molecular spectroscopy database, using the Markov Chain Monte Carlo $\chi^2$ minimization method and assuming Local Thermodynamic Equilibrium (LTE)." (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: Initial ranges Tex=50-350 K, vLSR=-57 to -47 km/s, FWHM=0.5-10 km/s, N=1e14-1e17 cm-2; source size 0.53''  
  > "initial parameter ranges $T_{\rm ex}$ = 50 – 350 K, v$_{_{\rm LSR}}$ = -57 – -47 km s$^{-1}$" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 60; CH3CN abundance 1e-8  
  > "We assume a \[$^{12}$C/$^{13}$C\] abundance ratio of 60" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - fitting: MCMC chi2 minimization  
  > "using the Markov Chain Monte Carlo $\chi^2$ minimization method" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Moment maps and PV diagrams**: First/second moment maps of K=3; PV cuts of K=2,4,6,8 along PA=61.5 deg, width 1''  
  > "along a cut centered on the continuum peak position of mm1, with width=1$''$ and PA=61.5$^{\circ}$" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Qualitative comparison with PV diagrams of other disks  
  > "The PV diagrams of the K=2,4 and 6 lines exhibit the “butterfly” shape expected from a Keplerian-like rotation curve" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
4. **Radiative transfer disk model comparison**: Grid of gas and dust models fit to continuum and CH3CN K=2-8 profiles along major/minor axes and integrated spectra, convolved to the beam  
  > "we fit the profiles of the continuum and CH$_3$CN J=13–12 K=2-8 emission collapsed along the major and minor axes, as well as the integrated spectra for the lines." (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - software: MOLLIE, HYPERION
   - parameter: Disk radius 2000 AU, gas mass 12 Msun, surface density r^-1.5, inclination 30 deg, O7 star 25 Msun, infall 4.6e-4 Msun/yr  
  > "total gas mass 12 M$_{\odot}$, with a surface density decreasing as $r^{-1.5}$ and an inclination of 30$^{\circ}$" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - assumption: Draine dust with R_V=5.5; CH3CN abundance 1e-8 at >100 K, 5e-9 at 90-100 K, 1e-10 below 90 K  
  > "The abundance of CH$_3$CN relative to H$_2$ is taken to be 10$^{-8}$ at $>$100 K, dropping to 5$\times$10$^{-9}$ for 90 K $<$ T $<$ 100 K" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - fitting: Best fit from a discrete grid of disk masses  
  > "is the best fit from a discrete set of disk masses" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
   - validation: Model Gaussian major FWHM 880 AU matches observed 870 AU  
  > "a Gaussian fit to the continuum model image gives a major FWHM of 880 AU, in good agreement with the fit to the observations (FWHM=870 AU)" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)

## Qualitative findings

- 12CO outflow lobes are roughly perpendicular to mm1 major axis  
  > "The outflow lobes are orientated roughly NW-SE, perpendicular to the PA of the source mm1" (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
- Blue-asymmetry in low-K PV diagrams, likely radiative transfer or geometry effect  
  > "the PV-diagrams of the lower-K lines shown in Fig. [4](#PVfig) are asymmetrical about the systemic velocity, with the blue-shifted emission being brighter." (3 RESULTS and DISCUSSION)
