## Analysis recipe

1. **select radio-dominated AGN**: Extract VLA and ALMA sources at 5x rms with PyBDSM, cross-match to photo-z catalogs, build FIR/radio correlation vs redshift, select >=0.7 dex radio excess  
  > "We extract VLA and ALMA sources down to $5\times$ the local rms noise using PyBDSM" (2 Sample Selection)
   - software: PyBDSM
   - parameter: 0.7 dex threshold (~3x correlation dispersion); 0.75'' match radius in COSMOS  
  > "the 0.7-dex threshold being $\approx3$ times the dispersion of the far-infrared/radio correlation" (2 Sample Selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **measure positions and sizes**: 2D Gaussian fitting of VLA and ALMA emission, all parameters free; resolved if deconvolved size >0 in both axes  
  > "measured with 2D Gaussian fitting using the AIPS task `JMFIT`; all Gaussian parameters were free." (2 Sample Selection)
   - software: AIPS JMFIT
   - parameter: positional errors add fit error and phase-calibrator accuracy (2 mas VLA HUDF, 10 mas VLA COSMOS, <=1 mas ALMA) in quadrature  
  > "We add these two uncertainties in quadrature for all positional uncertainties considered." (2 Sample Selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: COSMOS VLA astrometry checked against 81 Gaia DR1 matches (median offset 11, 8 mas)  
  > "This results in 81 matches." (2 Sample Selection)
3. **derive dust mass, gas mass and gas fraction**: Same approach as Rujopakarn16 with Li & Draine dust opacity  
  > "We use a (cite: LiDraine01) dust mass absorption coefficient and a gas-to-dust ratio of 100" (3.2)
   - parameter: T_dust varied 20-30 K for uncertainties  
  > "The uncertainties are estimated by deriving masses for $T_{\rm dust}$ ranging over $20-30$ K" (3.2)
   - assumption: T_dust = 25 K; gas-to-dust 100  
  > "assuming a $T_{\rm dust}$ of 25 K (cite: Scoville17)" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **estimate SFR and AGN contamination**: Fit Rieke09 IR templates at rest >40 um (ALMA, Herschel) to get L_IR; convert with Kennicutt98 (Chabrier IMF); check AGN contribution with Lyu & Rieke templates normalized to IRAC 5.8/8.0 um  
  > "we use only observations at rest-frame wavelengths longward of 40 $\mu$m for the fit" (3.2)
   - assumption: Maximal AGN scenario: all 5.8-8.0 um emission from AGN  
  > "assumes that all of the $5.8 - 8.0$ $\mu$m emission is of AGN origin" (3.2)
   - fitting: template fitting; details not stated  
  > "We estimate the SFR of these galaxies by fitting libraries of infrared SED templates of star-forming galaxies to the far-infrared observations to estimate the infrared luminosity" (Dust mass, gas fraction, and star formation rate)
   - validation: Max AGN contribution to L_IR ~2-11%; radio-implied SFR comparison shows 2-15% of radio from SF  
  > "we find that the maximum AGN contribution to $L_{\rm IR}$ is $\simeq 2-11\%$" (3.2)
