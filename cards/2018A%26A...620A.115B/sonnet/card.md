## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image cubes**: Pipeline calibration, manual flagging, CLEAN with natural weighting, channel rebinning  
  > "The data were imaged using the CLEAN algorithm, and a natural weighting scheme was chosen to maximize the point-source sensitivity." (2.2)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 3 sigma clean threshold; rebinning 4/6/8  
  > "Because SPT0125-47 has several 5 $\sigma$ detections, we only needed a rebinning by a factor of four." (2.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra at point-source position**: Fit 2D Gaussian to MFS image of all channels, extract spectrum at centroid  
  > "We fitted a two-dimensional Gaussian model and found the width of the Gaussian to be consistent with that of the synthesized beam." (2.2)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Dense-gas emission not much more extended than continuum  
  > "we implicitly assume that the size of the regions emitting the dense-gas lines is not much more extended than the continuum" (2.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit lines and baseline simultaneously**: Gaussian lines plus first-order baseline fitted simultaneously; bootstrap of reshuffled residuals for uncertainties; 3-sigma upper limits = best-fit flux + 3 std  
  > "All the lines ($^{13}$CO(4-3), HCN(5-4), HCO$^{+}$(5-4), HNC(5-4), and CN(4-3)) and the continuum baseline are fitted simultaneously." (2.3)
   - parameter: FWHM 200-1000 km/s; velocity offset up to 200 km/s; positivity prior on fluxes; 10 000 bootstraps  
  > "allowed the full width at half maximum (FWHM) to vary between 200 and 1000 km/s" (2.3); "We repeated this 10 000 times." (2.3)
   - assumption: Same FWHM and velocity offset for all lines in low-S/N sources  
  > "We thus assume in our fit that these two properties are similar for all the lines." (2.3)
   - fitting: Gaussian profiles, bootstrap errors; Pearson neighbour-channel correlation <0.2 in residuals  
  > "found that the correlation is lower than 0.2 for all our five residual spectra" (2.3)
   - validation: Compared with moment-zero fluxes for SPT0125-47 and SPT0551-50: compatible at 1 sigma  
  > "The values derived by the two methods are compatible at 1 $\sigma$ for all the lines." (8)
4. **Derive unbiased sample means and line ratios**: Combine bootstrap PDFs by random draws (10 000) to get mean luminosity; ratios of mean fluxes weighted by luminosity distance squared; 16th/84th percentiles  
  > "We performed this operation 10 000 times to obtain the PDF of the average luminosity of the sample." (2.5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 500 simulated spectra; max bias 6.3%  
  > "The maximal difference between these two quantities is 6.3 %." (9)
5. **Deblend HNC(5-4) and CN(4-3)**: Joint fit with common width and velocity offset and positivity prior on both, then mean ratio  
  > "We fitted simultaneously all the lines in the spectrum including HNC(5-4) and CN(4-3) and forced all the lines to have the same width and velocity offset" (2.6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simulation recovers blended ratios  
  > "The ratios including blended lines are also well recovered." (9)
6. **Compare with local LIR-L'HCN relation and line ratios**: Convert 5-4 to 4-3 with Mills2013 ratios, correct for magnification, compare with Zhang2014 relation, HCO+/HCN, HNC/HCN, HCN/[CI], HCN/CO, 12CO/13CO  
  > "We also corrected our sources for the magnification based on (cite: Spilker2016)." (3.1)
   - assumption: Local 5-4/4-3 ratio applies; differential lensing negligible; Planck2015 cosmology, Chabrier IMF  
  > "We thus neglected this effect in this paper." (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Sample variance 0.28/sqrt(5)=0.125 dex considered  
  > "we thus expect a 1-$\sigma$ sample variance for five objects of 0.28 / $\sqrt{5}$ = 0.125 dex" (3.1)

## Qualitative findings

- HCN(5-4), HCO+(5-4) and 13CO(4-3) detected >5 sigma in SPT0125-47; tentative ~3 sigma in SPT0551-50  
  > "We detected three $>$5 $\sigma$ lines in SPT0125-47." (2.4)
- Dense-gas fraction proportional to star formation efficiency, consistent with the local sample  
  > "The relation linking our local reference sample and the SPT sources suggests that DGF $\propto$ SFE" (4.2)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Moment-zero maps unreliable because uv continuum subtraction was too sensitive; simultaneous fit used instead  
  > "These problems motivated our choice to use our own method introduced in Sect. [2.3](#sect:line_ext) to derive line fluxes." (8)
- Alternative: Standard stacking and averaging of individual ratios rejected  
  > "We tried to compute first the PDFs of the line flux ratios of individual sources and then average them, but this failed to get meaningful results." (2.5)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Low S/N; conversion 5-4 to 4-3 uncertain; differential lensing neglected  
  > "It is therefore not possible to draw any firm conclusions, especially considering the uncertainties involved in converting our HCN(5-4) fluxes to HCN(4-3)." (3.1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only five sources, mostly non-detections
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No lens model for SPT0551-50
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Local 5-4/4-3 ratio applied to high-z
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Point-source assumption with 2-3 arcsec beams
