## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA Band 6 data**: CASA pipeline calibration; tclean imaging per source with natural weighting, continuum by MFS and continuum-subtracted CO cubes  
  > "conducted with the `tclean` task with per-source optimization." (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations, data reduction and analysis)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting, untapered  
  > "we use a natural weighting scheme to maximize the recovery of large faint structures" (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations, data reduction and analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked that a 2 arcsec taper only slightly raises continuum fluxes  
  > "having verified that a heavy taper ($2^{\prime\prime}$) only slightly raises the measured continuum fluxes" (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations, data reduction and analysis)
2. **Extract continuum and CO fluxes**: Kron aperture photometry on regions above 2 x rms, with de-blending for close sources; detection at >3 sigma; stack the undetected  
  > "Fluxes are extracted using `Photutils` (cite: larry_bradley_2024_13989456) Kron apertures, selecting regions above $2\times \rm rms$" (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations, data reduction and analysis)
   - software: Photutils
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive stellar properties and dust masses by SED fitting**: CIGALE with BC03, flexible delayed+quenched SFH, CF00 attenuation, DL07 dust, Dn4000 constraint  
  > "we use the $D_{\rm n}4000$ option in CIGALE and iterate until measured and SED-derived $D_{\rm n}4000$ agree within $\pm 0.1$" (A.1 SED fitting methodology)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Umax=1e6, beta=2, gamma=0.01, 0.47<qPAH<4.6  
  > "We fix the radiation parameter $U_{\rm max}=10^6$, emission slope ($\beta=2$), and sample different intensities $U_{\rm min}$." (A.1 SED fitting methodology)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF  
  > "stellar population synthesis with a (cite: chabrier03) initial mass function (IMF)" (A.1 SED fitting methodology)
   - fitting: Bayesian probability-weighted average values from CIGALE  
  > "we adopt Bayesian (probability-weighted average) values" (A.1 SED fitting methodology)
   - validation: Mock-object tests; compared with MBB and Scoville methods; non-parametric SFH test  
  > "We use the "mock object" functionality within `CIGALE` to construct a synthetic catalogue" (A.2 Inspecting SED fitting systematics)
4. **Derive molecular gas mass and delta_DGR**: M_H2 from velocity-integrated CO(3-2) flux with R31 and alpha_CO; delta_DGR = Mdust/MH2; limits for non-detections  
  > "We assume a line ratio of $R_{31} = L^{\prime}_{\rm CO(3-2)} / L^{\prime}_{\rm CO(1-0)} = 0.5$" (2.3 Physical properties)
   - assumption: R31 = 0.5; alpha_CO = 4.36 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "a constant conversion factor $\alpha_{\rm CO} = 4.36 \:\rm M_{\odot}(\rm K\:km\:s^{-1} pc^{-2})$" (2.3 Physical properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared Mdust, fdust and delta_DGR across DL07, MBB (21 K), and Scoville calibration; consistent within ~0.2 dex  
  > "all methods yield values generally consistent within $\sim$0.2 dex" (B Comparison of different methods for deriving M_dust)
5. **Compare dust fraction vs age with exponential decline models and SIMBA tracks**: Exponential decline models A exp(-t/tau) with tau=0.7 and 2 Gyr; compare delta_DGR with SIMBA fast/slow quenching tracks  
  > "adopting short ($\tau_{\rm dust}\sim 0.7\:\rm Gyr$) and long ($\tau_{\rm dust}\sim 2 \: \rm Gyr$) removal timescales" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- No clear correlation between f_H2 and delta_DGR across stellar mass; dust is an unreliable tracer of H2 in QGs  
  > "(1) Dust is an unreliable tracer of $\rm H_2$ mass in QGs" (3.2)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Direct, independent dust and gas measurements in individual QGs are needed to disentangle quenching from ISM evolution  
  > "Disentangling the impact of quenching pathways from ISM evolution in QGs requires direct, independent measurements of both dust and gas masses" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Tapered images rejected to avoid contamination and keep flux extraction homogeneous  
  > "We opted for untapered images to avoid contamination from nearby bright sources" (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations, data reduction and analysis)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): delta_DGR concerns H2 only, ignoring atomic HI  
  > "We note that $\delta_{\rm DGR}$ in this study concerns $\rm H_2$ gas, ignoring atomic HI." (3 Direct probe of delta_DGR in old QGs)
- Limitation (authors): Mdust uncertain due to assumed Tdust and unknown AGN presence  
  > "uncertainties remain due to the assumed $T_{\rm dust}$ and limited knowledge about the AGN-presence in our sources" (B Comparison of different methods for deriving M_dust)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single constant alpha_CO=4.36 and R31=0.5 adopted for all QGs; delta_DGR scales directly with these
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Half the sample lacks detections in at least one tracer; limits drive some conclusions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mdust for sources without IR data depends on SED priors
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 17 galaxies at z~0.4, so generalisation to high z is uncertain
