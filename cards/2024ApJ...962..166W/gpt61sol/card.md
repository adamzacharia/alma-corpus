## Analysis recipe

1. **Constrain HD 32297 absorption column**: Gaussian unresolved R(0) equivalent-width upper limit; convert to state column and LTE total column; cross-check by summing R(0)-R(2)  
  > "We use the non-detection of the CO R(0) absorption line in the R$\sim$60,000 iSHELL spectrum to place an upper limit on the circumstellar CO column density of the HD 32297 debris disk." (iSHELL Observations); "This 1$\sigma$ limit of the R(0) line is 0.055 in units of normalized flux and is used to compute the 3$\sigma$ upper limit on the equivalent width and column density." (iSHELL Observations); "We then used Equation 1 to calculate the 3$\sigma$ column density upper limit for all three CO lines and summed all three upper limits together to get a total CO column density upper limit." (iSHELL Observations)
   - software: Spextool
   - parameter: Noise from 50 points, Δλ=0.0007 μm, centered on R(0) near 4.66 μm  
  > "the standard deviation (1$\sigma$) of the 50 data points ($\Delta\lambda=0.0007$ $\text{\textmu}$m) centered on the expected location of the R(0) CO line ($\sim$4.66 $\text{\textmu}$m)" (iSHELL Observations)
   - parameter: Gaussian FWHM 0.0003 μm from unsaturated telluric lines  
  > "We use the average FWHM (0.0003 $\mu$m) of the unsaturated telluric lines as the width of the CO lines" (iSHELL Observations)
   - assumption: LTE at 30 K for the adopted limit  
  > "Assuming that the CO is in LTE, the total column density of CO is given by" (iSHELL Observations); "We use a temperature of 30 K for the column density upper limit calculation" (iSHELL Observations)
   - assumption: CO lines spectrally unresolved  
  > "since we expect the CO lines to be spectrally unresolved." (iSHELL Observations)
   - fitting: Assumed Gaussian line profile with depth set by local 1σ noise  
  > "We assume a Gaussian line profile with a depth equal to the standard deviation (1$\sigma$)" (iSHELL Observations)
   - validation: Non-LTE sum gives 7×10^15 cm^-2, similar to LTE 6×10^15 cm^-2  
  > "This is similar to the column density upper limit inferred from assuming the gas is in LTE (6$\times10^{15}$ cm$^{-2}$), so the method for calculating the column density upper limit for HD 32297 does not affect the following analysis." (iSHELL Observations)
2. **Measure scattered-light vertical extent**: Rotate GPI image; average west-side vertical cuts; compare FWHM with archival HD 6307 PSF  
  > "We then took vertical cuts of the disk at each column of pixels from 0.25 to 0.7 arcseconds from the central star on the west side of the disk" (GPI observations); "Because the individual vertical cuts are low S/N, we averaged all the vertical cuts from the west side of the disk to get a higher S/N vertical profile." (GPI observations); "We estimated the profile of the GPI PSF at H-band by collapsing the IFS data cube along the wavelength axis and then taking a line cut through the PSF center." (GPI observations)
   - software: GPI Data Reduction Pipeline
   - parameter: Rotation 96 degrees; west-side cuts 0.25-0.7 arcseconds  
  > "we first rotated the image by 96 degrees" (GPI observations); "We then took vertical cuts of the disk at each column of pixels from 0.25 to 0.7 arcseconds from the central star on the west side of the disk" (GPI observations)
   - parameter: Cuts not aligned to the disk spine before averaging  
  > "The vertical cuts are not shifted to be aligned with the disk spine before averaging because we are not able to fit for the spine of the disk with the low S/N individual vertical cuts." (GPI observations)
   - assumption: Vertical cuts tend to trace the outer disk radius  
  > "all the vertical cuts will tend to trace the vertical structure at the outer disk radius." (GPI observations)
   - assumption: Distance 152 parsecs; outer radius 115 au  
  > "At a distance of 152 parsecs (cite: GAIA)" (GPI observations); "Assuming a disk outer radius of 115 au" (GPI observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Disk and PSF FWHM agree within uncertainties; disk unresolved vertically  
  > "The FWHM of the PSF of GPI at H-band is within the uncertainties of the FWHM of average of the vertical cuts of the disk." (GPI observations)
3. **Measure HD 110058 gas radii**: Construct horizontal-axis 12CO PV diagram; fit diagonal lines to its outer edges  
  > "We construct a PV diagram across the horizontal axis of the disk." (Appendix A); "Diagonal lines on the PV diagram represent different orbital radii in the plane of the disk and we fit diagonal lines to the outer edge of the PV diagram to determine the disk radii." (Appendix A)
   - parameter: 5σ edge threshold; exclude offsets without emission above 5σ  
  > "This is done by finding, at every offset position, the velocity where the flux drops below a 5$\sigma$ threshold, like was done in (cite: seifried16)." (Appendix A); "If there is no flux value above the 5$\sigma$ threshold for a given offset position, we do not include that offset position in the fitting." (Appendix A)
   - assumption: Stellar mass 1.84 M_sun; inclination 85.5 degrees from (cite: Hales2022)  
  > "We use a stellar mass of 1.84 M$_{\odot}$ and an inclination of 85.5 degrees." (Appendix A); "The inclination and stellar mass is derived from simultaneously fitting the $^{12}$CO J=3-2 and J=2-1 and $^{12}$CO J=3-2 lines with a radiative transfer model in (cite: Hales2022)." (Appendix A)
   - fitting: Diagonal-line fits to PV edges  
  > "we fit diagonal lines to the outer edge of the PV diagram to determine the disk radii." (Appendix A)
   - validation: PV diagram displayed with fitted radii and literature-parameter Keplerian orbit  
  > "The PV diagram with the best fit radii (black lines) is shown in Figure" (Appendix A); "The red curve shows a Keplerian orbit with the stellar mass and disk inclination reported by (cite: Hales2022)." (Appendix A)
4. **Infer CO scale heights**: Normalize R^-3/2 surface density to ALMA CO mass; numerically integrate sightline column versus T_H; match absorption columns or upper limit; calculate H(R)

## Qualitative findings

- CO and millimeter dust scale heights agree within uncertainties in all four systems  
  > "We find that for each of the four systems, the estimated CO scale height and the dust scale height are consistent within the uncertainties" (Trends between millimeter/micron dust and CO scale heights)
- Estimated CO scale height is independent of assumed mean molecular weight; T_H is not  
  > "Thus, the estimated scale height as a function of radius does not depend on our assumption of $\mu$" (Estimated CO Scale Height); "However, the value of $T_H$ that we estimate does depend on the value of $\mu$ we assume." (Estimated CO Scale Height)
- No comparable trend established for micron-sized dust  
  > "We do not see the same trend as with the millimeter and estimated CO scale heights, however, three of the scattered light scale heights are upper limits, so it is difficult to identify any trends." (Trends between millimeter/micron dust and CO scale heights)
- HD 32297 vertical structure is consistent with a dynamically cold system  
  > "Thus, our findings on the vertical structure of HD 32297 are consistent with the GPI H-band image that suggested HD 32297 is a dynamically “cold" system." (Origin of the Disk Scale Heights)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Edge-on CO-rich disks permit absorption columns and millimeter emission masses to constrain vertical structure  
  > "By combining column density measurements from absorption line spectroscopy and CO mass measurements from emission at millimeter wavelengths, we can constrain the vertical distribution of the gas provided that the geometry of the disk is known" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Hydrostatic assumption enables literature comparison; optically thin isotopologue masses reduce optical-depth bias; unresolved GPI profile motivates an upper limit  
  > "We choose to assume hydrostatic equilibrium to be able to compare with previous studies in the literature that also assume hydrostatic equilibrium for gas in debris disks" (CO Column Density Estimation); "To overcome this, we used CO masses for HD 131488 and HD 32297 that are derived from optically thin C$^{18}$O emission from (cite: 131488_alma; moor19)." (CO Column Density Estimation); "Since the disk is unresolved vertically, forward modeling would not provide a better constraint on the scale height and would similarly just give an upper limit." (GPI observations)
- Alternative: Non-LTE sum of low-state columns gives a similar HD 32297 upper limit  
  > "We also estimate the column density upper limit of CO without assuming that the gas is in LTE, since this assumption may be incorrect." (iSHELL Observations)
- Alternative: Tested μ=14 M_H, 2.35 M_H and CO molecular mass  
  > "We test three different values of mean molecular weight ($\mu$) in estimating the CO scale heights and $T_H$ values of these systems." (Estimated CO Scale Height); "We also tested other values of mean molecular weight of $\mu=2.35$ $M_H$, corresponding to primordial gas in a protoplanetary disk (cite: kimura16), as well as $\mu$ equal to the mass of the CO molecule." (CO Column Density Estimation)
- Alternative: No scattered-light forward modeling of HD 131488 because the vertical profile is unresolved  
  > "Because we do not find that the disk vertical profile for HD 131488 is spatially resolved, we do not forward model the disk to measure the vertical profile" (GPI observations)
- Alternative: Use single-Gaussian β Pic dust height despite a better double-Gaussian fit in the source work  
  > "(cite: beta_pic_dust) found that a double Gaussian is a better fit to the vertical structure of the $\beta$ Pic disk than a single Gaussian profile" (ALMA Continuum)
- Alternative: Weak mixing and photodissociation can produce compact CO without a shared dust origin  
  > "Thus, the similarity we find between the estimated CO scale height and the millimeter dust scale height might not be due to a similar origin as the dust." (Is scale height an indicator of secondary origin gas?)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Hydrostatic equilibrium may fail, invalidating inferred scale heights  
  > "If the gas in these disks is not in hydrostatic equilibrium, then our scale height estimates may not be accurate, which is a potential limitation to this method." (CO Column Density Estimation)
- Limitation (authors): HD 131488 inclination-height degeneracy allows an unresolved millimeter dust height  
  > "However, this might not actually reflect the true scale height of the disk because the visibility modeling found that the scale height is still consistent with an upper limit, depending on the inclination." (ALMA Continuum)
- Limitation (authors): Inclination uncertainties prevent definitive thermal-versus-dynamical conclusions for HD 110058 and HD 131488  
  > "For HD 110058 and HD 131488, the CO excitation temperature is within the uncertainties of the estimated value of $T_H$ because the uncertainty in disk inclination creates a large uncertainty in $T_H$" (Estimated CO Scale Height)
- Limitation (authors): Correlation is not definitively significant at 5%; HD 131488 dust height may be an upper limit  
  > "the distribution of $p$-values makes it so we are unable to definitively conclude that the correlation is statistically significant at the $5\%$ level." (Trends between millimeter/micron dust and CO scale heights); "We also call this a potential correlation because the scale height of the HD 131488 millimeter dust is consistent with an upper limit from the forward modeling." (Trends between millimeter/micron dust and CO scale heights)
- Limitation (authors): Estimated gas-height errors of 20-60% prevent establishing different gas and dust vertical distributions  
  > "We found that the scale height of the dust and are consistent with each other due to the 20-60$\%$ error bars for the estimated scale height." (Origin of the CO in these systems)
- Limitation (authors): Three scattered-light heights are upper limits  
  > "three of the scattered light scale heights are upper limits, so it is difficult to identify any trends." (Trends between millimeter/micron dust and CO scale heights)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Four systems provide limited leverage for a population correlation; Monte Carlo sampling does not increase the number of independent systems.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 0.0025 Monte Carlo standard error is distinct from uncertainty in the physical correlation.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gaussian sampling needs an explicit treatment of asymmetric errors, nonnegative heights and upper limits.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The conditional HD 131488 dust height should not be treated as an unconditional resolved measurement.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Appendix A reports HD 110058 R_out=80±10 AU, while the disk-parameter table gives 80±12 au.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): HD 32297 relies on private-communication dust geometry and height.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The acknowledgments print 2015.1.01243 without a suffix; it is preserved without correction.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 2022.1.00338.L is a future program mentioned for improved observations, not a dataset analyzed here.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The method's solution branch and the interpretation of a column-density upper limit require explicit numerical verification.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Different sections give different HD 32297 scattered-light inclinations.
