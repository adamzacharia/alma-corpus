## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure HCN/CO at common resolution**: Convolve HCN and CO(1-0) to common physical resolution; integrate HCN with CO-based PPV mask; ratio of integrated intensities  
  > "At this common resolution, we measure HCN/CO, the ratio of the HCN(1-0) to CO(1-0) integrated intensities." (2.1 HCN (1-0) to CO (1-0) Ratio)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 650 pc (ALMA), 2770 pc (EMPIRE)  
  > "This is 650 pc for ALMA and 2770 pc for EMPIRE." (2.1 HCN (1-0) to CO (1-0) Ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared ALMA and EMPIRE HCN/CO and different CO(1-0) sources for NGC 4321  
  > "The ALMA and EMPIRE results agree within $0.06$ dex." (2.5 Additional Checks)
2. **Compute intensity-weighted cloud-scale CO(2-1) intensity per HCN beam**: Convolve CO(2-1) at 120 pc, calculate <I> = (I^2 * Omega)/(I * Omega) with Gaussian kernel  
  > "we calculate the intensity-weighted average $I_{\rm CO(2-1)}$ within each larger HCN beam." (2.2 Average Cloud-Scale CO Intensity in each HCN Beam)
   - parameter: 120 pc resolution; PAWS CO(1-0) scaled by 0.7 for NGC 5194  
  > "We convolve all four CO (2-1) maps to share this common physical resolution." (2.2 Average Cloud-Scale CO Intensity in each HCN Beam)
   - assumption: alpha_CO(2-1) ~ 6.2 Msun pc-2 (K km/s)-1 (Galactic, with CO(2-1)/(1-0)=0.7)  
  > "the Galactic CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor is $\alpha_{\rm CO (2-1)} \approx 6.2$ M$_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$." (2.4 Interpretation in Terms of Physical Quantities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Monte Carlo with 100 noise realizations gives ~2 K km/s mean error  
  > "We repeat this process 100 times, and calculate the standard deviation in $\left< I_{\rm CO(2-1)} \right>$ over all realizations." (2.2 Average Cloud-Scale CO Intensity in each HCN Beam)
3. **Bin HCN/CO by <I_CO(2-1)>**: Average integrated HCN divided by integrated CO in bins of <I_CO(2-1)>  
  > "we measure the average HCN/CO in bins of $\left< I_{CO (2-1)} \right>$." (2.3 Binned Relation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Spectral stacking agrees with mask-and-average within ~10%  
  > "the spectral stacking yields the same results as our mask-and-average approach within ${\sim}10$%, with no systematic offset." (2.3 Binned Relation)
4. **Quantify correlation and fit power law**: Spearman rank correlation and bisector linear fit in log-log  
  > "Table [2](#tab:results) reports power law fits (fit via the bisector method)" (3.1 Observed Correlation Between HCN/CO and Cloud-Scale Surface Density)
   - assumption: alpha_CO(1-0)=4.35, alpha_HCN~14 for f_dense; f_dense ~ 2.3 HCN/CO  
  > "we assume $\alpha_{\rm CO (1-0)} = 4.35$ M$_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$ (cite: BOLATTO13)" (2.4 Interpretation in Terms of Physical Quantities)
   - fitting: bisector linear fit to log data  
  > "Table [2](#tab:results) reports power law fits (fit via the bisector method) that offer a reasonable description of each sample." (Observed Correlation Between HCN/CO and Cloud-Scale Surface Density)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compare with simple density distribution models**: Compute mass fraction above high vs low density threshold for power-law, top-hat and lognormal PDFs as mean density varies  
  > "Dividing the mass above the high density threshold by the mass above the low density threshold we construct a model “dense gas fraction”" (3.3 Expectations From Simple Models)
   - assumption: mean surface density traces mean volume density  
  > "(3) the mean surface density of a molecular cloud traces its mean volume density." (3.3 Expectations From Simple Models)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Intensity weighting preserves high-resolution information and down-weights empty regions  
  > "it preserves the high resolution information and down-weights empty regions." (2.2 Average Cloud-Scale CO Intensity in each HCN Beam)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): HCN-to-dense-gas conversion and effective critical density remain uncertain  
  > "Both the density of gas traced by HCN (1-0) and the conversion from HCN emission to a dense gas mass remain uncertain." (2.4 Interpretation in Terms of Physical Quantities)
- Limitation (authors): Distances uncertain by 10-30%  
  > "The distances to our targets are uncertain by $\sim 10{-}30\%$." (2.2 Average Cloud-Scale CO Intensity in each HCN Beam)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only five galaxies; central alpha_CO effects confound offsets
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Binning hides cloud-to-cloud scatter
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Different CO(1-0) sources add ~0.1-0.25 dex systematics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fit uncertainties likely underestimated since bins are not independent
