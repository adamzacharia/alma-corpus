## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure continuum sources**: Gaussian fitting within 3σ contour after primary beam correction; flux integrated in 3σ region  
  > "Total flux densities of the sources were measured by integrating the flux in the regions enclosed by the $3\sigma$ contours after primary beam correction." (3.1 1.3 mm continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Optically thin, Tdust=20 K, kappa(850um)=0.035, beta=1, gas-to-dust 100 (mass)  
  > "by assuming optical thinness, a dust temperature $T_{\rm dust}=20$ K, opacity coefficient $\kappa(850\ \ensuremath{\mu}m)=0.035\ {\rm cm}^{2}~{\rm g}^{-1}$" (3.1 1.3 mm continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Bolometric and submm luminosity from SED**: Trapezoidal integration of SED, not fitting  
  > "directly calculated to be $T_{\rm bol}\lesssim$30 K and $L_{\rm bol}\lesssim 2.6\ L_{\ensuremath{\odot}}$ by the trapezoidal integration, rather than fitting" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Systemic velocities from C18O profiles**: Gaussian fit to spectra above 3σ  
  > "The two profiles were fitted with a Gaussian function using emission above $3\sigma$ levels" (3.4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Outflow inclination and bending from knot momenta**: Ridge trace, PV diagram along it, momentum along Hubble-law lines; solve l_b/l_r=sin i_b/sin i_r and p_b/p_r=cos i_b/cos i_r  
  > "These are summarized as $l_b/l_r=\sin i_b/\sin i_r$ and $p_b/p_r=\cos i_b/\cos i_r$." (4.1)
   - parameter: velocity range 15<|V-Vsys|<50 km/s, Vsys=7.9 km/s; 1 beam cut width  
  > "the integrated velocity range is $15\ {\rm km~s^{-1}}<|V-V_{\rm sys}|<50\ {\rm km~s^{-1}}$, where $V_{\rm sys}=7.9\ {\rm km~s^{-1}}$" (4.1)
   - assumption: LTE, Tex=30 K, optically thin, X(12CO)=2.7e-4, same intrinsic momentum in all four flows  
  > "This calculation assumes the local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) condition, the constant excitation temperature 30 K" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Continuum visibility modeling**: Subtract simobserve visibilities of CLEAN components of one source; fit uniform disk (SMM4A) and double Gaussian (SMM4B) to amplitude profiles within ±10 deg of major axis  
  > "we fit the visibility amplitude profile with the Fourier transform of a uniform (boxcar) disk" (4.2)
   - software: CASA simobserve
   - fitting: Profile fitting; sampler/priors not stated  
  > "we fit the visibility amplitude profile with the Fourier transform of a uniform (boxcar) disk in order to focus on the remarkable geometry, sharp edge." (Continuum Visibility)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **C18O abundance**: Solve radiative transfer per channel for tau_gas using continuum-based N(H2); sum column; ratio  
  > "The fractional abundance of C$^{18}$O relative to H$_2$, $X$(C$^{18}$O) is then derived from the ratio of the two column densities." (4.3)
   - assumption: LTE, gas T = dust T = 20 K, kappa(850um)=0.035, beta=1, gas-to-dust 100, mean molecular weight 2.37  
  > "In addition, the mean molecular weight 2.37 is adopted to change the mass column density of gas to the number column density of $H_{2}$ molecules." (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested 15-40 K and optically thin case  
  > "15-40 K provides $X({\rm C}^{18}{\rm O})=0.3$-$3\times 10^{-8}$ at the SMM4B position." (4.3)

## Qualitative findings

- SO traces outflow cavity walls and SMM4B vicinity; C18O traces infalling/rotating envelope around SMM4B; C18O absorbed at SMM4A  
  > "The SO emission traces shocked regions, such as cavity walls of outflows and the vicinity of SMM4B." (abstract)

## Claim tuples

- H: SMM4A and SMM4B differ in evolutionary phase | E: Compared continuum visibilities, outflows, SO, C18O abundance and SED | R: SMM4A has large disk-like structure, 18 K brightness, fan outflow; SMM4B compact, collimated outflow, depleted C18O | F: SMM4A more evolved than SMM4B (and SMM11 youngest) | **supported**
- H: SMM4B outflow bends in 3D | E: Hubble-law knot momenta and lengths of blue and red lobes | R: i_b~36, i_r~70, alpha~40 deg | F: outflow intrinsically bent | **supported**
- H: C18O is depleted at SMM4B | E: Radiative transfer with continuum-derived N(H2) | R: abundance <~1e-8 | F: ~50 times below ISM | **supported**
