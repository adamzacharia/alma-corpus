## Analysis recipe

1. **Self-calibrate and image**: Align execution blocks, self-calibrate continuum, apply to full data, subtract continuum, tclean  
  > "The solutions were then applied to the non-averaged dataset." (2 Observation)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5, multiscale [0,5,15,25]  
  > "multiscale deconvolver with scales of \[0, 5, 15, 25\] pixels" (2 Observation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: robust chosen by peak S/N comparison  
  > "after testing different values (0.5, 1.0, and 2.0) for all three lines" (2 Observation)
2. **Moment maps and spectra**: Integrate ±20 km/s about vsys; extract spectra from two-beam elliptical aperture  
  > "we spectrally integrated over $\pm20$ km s$^{-1}$ with respect to the systemic velocity" (3 Analysis & results)
   - software: bettermoments
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Confirm detections**: Matched filter with Keplerian disk (Rout 10 au) and SO2 stacking  
  > "a simple Keplerian-rotating disk model with a small outer radius of 10 au" (3 Analysis & results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: H2S response ~6-7 sigma; SO2 <5 sigma, stacked 3.6 sigma  
  > "While the H$_2$S line yields $\sim6$–$7\sigma$ response, the two SO$_2$ lines show responses with $<5\sigma$." (3 Analysis & results)
4. **Fit line profiles**: Thin Keplerian disk, LTE, thermal broadening; free R, T, N per species plus common vsys (9 params)  
  > "We left three parameters free for each molecular species: emitting radius $R$, gas temperature $T$, and column density $N$." (3 Analysis & results)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: 100 walkers, 15,000 steps, 2,000 burn-in  
  > "We used 100 walkers to sample in 15,000 steps, of which initial 2,000 steps are discarded as burn-in." (3 Analysis & results)
   - assumption: H2S ortho-to-para ratio 3; LTE; SO2 radius same as SO; M*=2.0 Msun; i=46.7 deg  
  > "we assumed a statistical ortho-to-para ratio of 3 to calculate total H$_2$S column density" (3 Analysis & results)
   - fitting: likelihood exp(-chi2/2); uniform priors U(1,40) au R, U(20,500) K T, U(14,20) logN, U(0,11) km/s vsys; lower limits from 95% HDI  
  > "We used uniform priors, i.e., $\mathcal{U}(1,40)$ for $R$ in au, $\mathcal{U}(20,500)$ for $T$ in K" (3 Analysis & results)
   - validation: Model reproduces H2S and SO profiles; vsys consistent with literature  
  > "The systemic velocity $v_\mathrm{sys}$ derived here is consistent with the known literature value" (3 Analysis & results)
5. **Column density ratios and water-relative abundances**: Ratios from posterior samples with T>300 K; reanalyze archival water data for 3 sigma upper limits and compute abundances relative to water  
  > "we tentatively derived the column density ratios of SO and SO$_2$ relative to H$_2$S for the posterior MCMC samples with $T>300$ K to be 0.13 and 0.46" (4.2.1)
   - parameter: line width 40 km/s; point source; H2O/H2(18)O=557; HDO/H2O=2e-3; H2O abundance 1e-4  
  > "H$_2$$^{16}$O/H$_2$$^{18}$O $=557$ (cite: Wilson1999) and HDO/H$_2$O $=2\times10^{-3}$" (4.2.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Compact unresolved H2S and SO emission with ~40 km/s line width at disk center  
  > "We detect unresolved, compact emission of H$_2$S and SO (and tentatively SO$_2$) at the disk center with a broad line width of $\sim40$ km s$^{-1}$" (abstract)
- CS and SO radial profiles anti-correlated  
  > "showcasing a clear anti-correlation in radial distributions" (4.2.1)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Emission is unresolved so line profiles used to infer emitting radius  
  > "the line profiles still provide information about the emitting radii under the assumption that the emitting gas follows Keplerian rotation" (3 Analysis & results)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): T and N degenerate; mostly optically thick; only lower limits; low S/N  
  > "gas temperature $T$ and column densities $N$ are highly degenerate, and the solutions are mostly in the optically thick regime" (3 Analysis & results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Weak detections (S/N ~4-7); SO2 only tentative
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-transition fits, strong T–N degeneracy
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Assumed SO2 radius equals SO
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Thermal-only line width and thin-disk geometry
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Water upper limits from different data with assumed emitting size
