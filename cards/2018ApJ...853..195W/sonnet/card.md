## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA data**: Manual (J0102) or pipeline (J0546) calibration in CASA; natural-weighted clean continuum images and 50 MHz line cubes  
  > "Continuum images and data cubes centered on the CO and \[C I\] lines were produced using the standard deconvolution task in CASA, `clean`." (2.5.2 Calibration and imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ALMA positions compared with HST counterparts (offset 0.34 arcsec for CO, 0.26 arcsec for continuum)  
  > "we find offsets of $0\ensuremath{''}{}34$ (six sources)." (2.5.3 Astrometry)
2. **Detect line sources and measure fluxes**: SoFiA search of cubes, validated against optical counterparts; fluxes via IMFIT on moment-zero maps; upper limits 3x rms for 300 km/s width  
  > "We use a SNR threshold of 5.0 and smooth over 3 channels and 3 spatial pixels with Gaussian kernels." (3.2 Millimeter line detections)
   - software: SoFiA, CASA IMFIT
   - parameter: SNR threshold 5.0; fiducial line width 300 km/s  
  > "fiducial line width of $\Delta v = 300~\rm km~s^{-1}$" (3.2 Millimeter line detections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Searched negative (-1x) cubes to estimate false detections  
  > "we roughly estimate the number of false detections by extracting “sources” from versions of each data cube that have been multiplied by $-1$." (3.2 Millimeter line detections)
3. **Derive gas masses**: M_mol from CO(4-3) via Galactic X_CO and r41=0.4; M_ISM from continuum via dust-to-gas scaling law  
  > "We use a dust-to-gas scaling law (equation 8.26) from (cite: 2013seg..book..491S) to convert the ALMA continuum flux density into a total gas mass" (3.1 Dust continuum sources)
   - parameter: beta = 1.8; r_4,1 = 0.4; 0.3 dex added uncertainty on M_mol  
  > "$\beta = 1.8$ parameterizes the dust absorption coefficient $\kappa \propto \nu^{-\beta}$" (3.1 Dust continuum sources)
   - assumption: Galactic X_CO = 2e20 cm^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "assuming a Galactic $X_{\rm CO} = 2\times10^{20} \rm~cm^{-2}~(K~km~s^{-1})^{-1}$ conversion factor" (3.1 Dust continuum sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit IR SEDs for L_IR and SFR**: Fit 160 um PACS flux to Kirkpatrick+15 templates by chi2 with free normalization; emcee for normalization uncertainty; SFR = 1.65 Msun/yr x L_IR/1e10 Lsun  
  > "In order to find the best fitting template, $\chi^2$ is minimized while the overall SED normalization is allowed to vary as a free parameter." (3.3 Far-infrared continuum sources)
   - software: emcee
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; L_TIR(3-1100um) ~ 1.1 L_IR(8-1000um)  
  > "we approximate $L_{\rm TIR}(3-1100~\ensuremath{\mu}m) \approx 1.1\times L_{\rm IR}(8-1000~\ensuremath{\mu}m)$" (3.3 Far-infrared continuum sources)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC on normalization; posterior checked as normal; sigma taken as std  
  > "For each galaxy, we verify that the samples follow a normal distribution." (3.3 Far-infrared continuum sources)
   - validation: Compared with Chary & Elbaz templates and ALMA continuum predictions; PACS negative-map fidelity test  
  > "Only a single 4 $\sigma$ detection is found in the negative J0438 100 $\mu$m map" (3.3 Far-infrared continuum sources)
5. **Stack non-detections**: Bootstrap stacking of PACS, ALMA continuum, ATCA and ALMA spectra at member positions with 4 MAD outlier cut; compare to blank-sky  
  > "We use bootstrapping (with replacement) to calculate stacking uncertainties or confidence intervals." (4.1 Herschel/PACS continuum stacking)
   - parameter: 5000 bootstrap resamplings for PACS  
  > "(with 5000 resampling iterations)" (4.1 Herschel/PACS continuum stacking)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Blank-sky stacks and p-values  
  > "In every case, $p > 0.1$" (4.1 Herschel/PACS continuum stacking)
6. **Radio AGN diagnosis and evolution trends**: q_IR from L_IR and 1.4 GHz luminosity (alpha=0.8), threshold 1.8; power-law/linear fits with emcee for IRBG fraction and sSFR vs z  
  > "a separator of $q_{\rm IR} \approx 1.8$ is sometimes used to differentiate between star-forming galaxies and AGNs" (5.5 The FIR-radio correlation and AGN fraction)
   - software: emcee
   - assumption: radio spectral index alpha = 0.8  
  > "assuming $S_\nu \propto \nu^{-\alpha}$ with spectral index $\alpha = 0.8$" (3.4 Radio-bright sources)
   - fitting: chi2 MAP power-law then emcee sampling of index; 68% binomial intervals  
  > "We sample the marginalized distribution of the power-law index parameter using `emcee`." (5.4 Redshift evolution of infrared-bright galaxies)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- No ALMA continuum or line stacked detections; dust-continuum detections only in J0546.  
  > "We do not detect significant stacked ALMA dust continuum or spectral line emission" (abstract)
- IRBG fraction and sSFR increase with redshift; SFR-radius trend vanishes after normalizing by stellar mass.  
  > "The fraction of infrared-bright galaxies (IRBGs; $\log (L_{\rm IR}/L_\ensuremath{\odot}) > 10.6$) per cluster and average sSFR rise significantly with redshift." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Stellar mass from 3.6 um rather than SED fitting because optical coverage is incomplete and would bias with radius  
  > "We choose not to employ optical and NIR spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting because our optical wavelength observations do not sufficiently cover all cluster galaxies" (2.2.3 Spitzer Space Telescope)
- Alternative: Chary & Elbaz templates rejected because they underestimate cold dust (0.3 dex lower L_IR)  
  > "$L_{\rm IR}$ derived using (cite: 2001ApJ...556..562C) templates is systematically 0.3 dex lower" (3.3 Far-infrared continuum sources)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Optical selection of members may bias the star-forming census; small samples of CO detections.  
  > "we have grounds for concern that our optical selection induces a general bias in our search for dust-obscured galaxies" (5.3 Biases from optical selection of cluster members)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Galactic X_CO and fixed r41=0.4 dominate mass uncertainty; alternatives change masses by factors of several
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-band (160 um) SED fits give large L_IR and q_IR uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small number statistics (6 CO detections; IRBG counts)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Some [C I] and CO detections are marginal
