## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract [C II] spectra of SMG-A/B/C**: Spectra extracted from the deep C-6 data cube with circular apertures (1.0'' for SMG-A and SMG-B, 0.3'' for SMG-C to avoid quasar contamination); SMG-A/B seen in the moment zero map  
  > "The \[C II\] spectra of galaxy SMG-A and SMG-B were extracted from the deep C-6 data cube with a $1.0''$ diameter aperture centered at" (ALMA observation and [C II] emitters); "To avoid contamination from the flux of the quasar, we extracted the spectra for the SMG-C with a smaller aperture" (ALMA observation and [C II] emitters)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: aperture diameter 1.0'' (SMG-A, SMG-B); 0.3'' (SMG-C)  
  > "with a smaller aperture ($0.3''$ diameter aperture ) centered at (09h10m54.552s, –04d14m06.994s)" (ALMA observation and [C II] emitters)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Select LAE candidates from HSC narrow-band imaging**: Colour and S/N cuts on forced-catalogue CModel colours and 1.5'' aperture S/N, plus pipeline flag cuts and visual inspection of stacked images  
  > "The signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is measured in a $1.5''$ diameter aperture while the color was measured using the CModel magnitude following previous works (cite: Shibuya18)." (Subaru/HSC observation and LAE selection); "all sources satisfying these criteria were then visually inspected in the stacked images to further reject moving objects, satellite trails, uncorrected hot pixels, and other artifacts." (Subaru/HSC observation and LAE selection)
   - software: hscPipe
   - parameter: SNR(NB926) >= 5  
  > "SNR(NB926)\ge5" (Subaru/HSC observation and LAE selection)
   - parameter: z - NB926 >= 1.0  
  > "z-NB926\ge1.0" (Subaru/HSC observation and LAE selection)
   - parameter: SNR(i) < 3  
  > "SNR(i)<3" (Subaru/HSC observation and LAE selection)
   - parameter: 87 candidates pass; uniform selection within 40' of the quasar  
  > "In total, 87 LAE candidates pass our selection." (Subaru/HSC observation and LAE selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Spectroscopic follow-up of a subset: about 60% have a redshift and 92% of those are at z~6.6  
  > "We can only measure redshift for $\sim$60% of the LAE candidates but of these 92% are indeed at the intended redshift $z\sim6.6$" (Optical spectroscopy)
3. **Confirm LAEs spectroscopically**: Magellan/IMACS (one mask, 1'' slits, 300-line grism) and Keck/DEIMOS (three masks, 830G grating) multi-slit spectroscopy  
  > "We used the IMACS f/2 camera with 300-line red-blazed grism and designed a dedicated multi-slit mask" (Optical spectroscopy); "In total, the IMACS and DEIMOS observations convincingly confirmed 12 bright LAEs at $z\sim6.6$ and only one low-$z$ interloper at $z=0.85$." (Optical spectroscopy)
   - software: COSMOS2, PypeIt
   - parameter: IMACS: 11x1200 s (first night) + 6x1200 s (second night)  
  > "We obtained 11$\times$1200s exposures on the first night and 6$\times$1200s exposures on the second night." (Optical spectroscopy)
   - parameter: DEIMOS: 6 hours on mask1, 3 hours each on mask2 and mask3  
  > "We spent 6-hours of time on-source for mask1 and 3-hours each on the other two masks." (Optical spectroscopy)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure the LAE overdensity**: Compare the LAE surface density in the central ~14x30 arcmin^2 region with the outskirts of the HSC field (r>50 cMpc from the quasar)  
  > "On average, the density in the central region (24 LAEs within 420 arcmin$^2$) is $4.3^{+1.1}_{-0.9}$" (Overdensity of galaxies); "(50 LAEs within 3740 arcmin$^2$)" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - parameter: overdensity definition delta = n/nbar - 1  
  > "$\delta=\frac{n}{\bar{n}}-1=3.3^{+1.1}_{-0.9}$" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - assumption: Outskirts of the field (r>50 cMpc) represent the background density  
  > "We choose to only use the region with $r>50$ cMpc (cite: Jiang18) from the central quasar as a comparison field" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Background LAE densities in the HSC field compared with other z~6.6 LAE surveys; similar except near the depth limit  
  > "except at the very faint end close to the survey depth limit" (Overdensity of galaxies)
5. **Cumulative overdensity profiles of LAEs and [C II] emitters**: Overdensity measured in cylindrical volumes with logarithmically spaced radial bins; velocity bin ±1000 km/s for [C II] emitters and ±2250 km/s (NB926 width) for LAEs; compared with cross-correlation-function and protocluster profiles  
  > "We measure the overdensity profile in a set of cylindrical volumes with logarithmically spaced radial bins" (Overdensity of galaxies); "$\Delta v=\pm2250~{\rm km~s^{-1}}$ (the width of NB926 filter)" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - parameter: background [C II] number from cumulative number density with L_[CII] > 10^8.2 Lsun; background LAEs measured at r>50 cMpc  
  > "while the background number of LAEs is directly measured from the outskirts ($r>50$ cMpc ) of our HSC observations" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - parameter: quasar-LAE cross-correlation length r0QG = 7.7 h^-1 Mpc (gamma=1.8), derived from r0QQ and r0GG  
  > "$r_0^{\rm QG}=\sqrt{r_0^{\rm QQ}, r_0^{\rm GG}}=7.7~h^{-1}~{\rm Mpc}$ ($\gamma=1.8$)" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - assumption: Fixed slope gamma=1.8 for all comparison profiles  
  > "we used $\gamma=1.8$ and $r_0^{\rm QG}=20~h^{-1}~{\rm Mpc}$ to be consistent with $\gamma$ of other profiles" (Overdensity of galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Estimate present-day protocluster mass**: M_z=0 = (1+delta_m) rho_bar V, with delta_m obtained from 1 + b delta_m = C(1+delta) and C the redshift-space-distortion correction  
  > "$M_{z=0}=(1+\delta_m)\bar{\rho}V$" (The mass of the protocluster); "We thus can determine $\delta_m$ through $1+b\delta_m = C(1+\delta)$" (The mass of the protocluster)
   - parameter: volume from ~14x30 arcmin^2 (~35x74 cMpc^2) box and line-of-sight depth 36.9 cMpc  
  > "a line-of-sight depth of $36.9~ {\rm cMpc}$ which is derived from the redshift difference of the highest-$z$ LAE and lowest-$z$ LAE in the spectroscopic sample" (The mass of the protocluster)
   - parameter: mean matter density 3.88e10 Msun per cMpc^3  
  > "$\bar{\rho}=3.88\times10^{10}M_\odot~{\rm cMpc^{-3}}$ is the mean matter density of the Universe" (The mass of the protocluster)
   - assumption: Structure collapses into a cluster at z=0; Chiang13 formula extrapolated to z~6.6 and higher masses; LAE bias b=4.54±0.63  
  > "Extrapolating the (cite: Chiang13) formula to $z\sim6.6$ and higher masses, we derive $C=0.84$, $\delta_m=0.57$ and the present-day mass" (The mass of the protocluster)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Volume described as conservative; compared with Coma  
  > "We note that the volume is conservative because the boundary does not cover the whole structure" (The mass of the protocluster)
7. **Constrain quasar UV lifetime from the double-peaked LAE-11**: Measure Lya peak separation of LAE-11, infer escape fraction with an empirical formula, and compute light-travel time delay and a lower limit on quasar lifetime from the transverse separation  
  > "Using Equation (4), we estimate the time delay to be $\Delta t=1.6\times10^6$ yr." (A double peaked LAE in the quasar vicinity); "we estimate the lower limit of quasar UV lifetime using $t_q > \frac{D_\perp}{c}=8.1\times10^5$ yr" (A double peaked LAE in the quasar vicinity)
   - parameter: D_parallel = 0.18 pMpc, D_perp = 0.25 pMpc  
  > "$D_\parallel=0.18$ pMpc, and $D_\perp=0.25$ pMpc, respectively" (A double peaked LAE in the quasar vicinity)
   - assumption: Line-of-sight separation cannot be distinguished from peculiar velocity, so only the transverse separation sets the lower limit  
  > "we cannot distinguish the line-of-sight separation from the peculiar velocity of the galaxy around the quasar" (A double peaked LAE in the quasar vicinity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- All three [C II] emitters are undetected in Subaru narrow-band and z-band imaging, suggesting they are dustier than the narrow-band selected LAEs  
  > "which indicates that these \[C II\] emitters are likely dustier than the narrow-band selected LAEs" (ALMA observation and [C II] emitters)
- Overdensity far exceeds the expectation from the quasar-galaxy cross-correlation function  
  > "the overdensity in the J0910–0414 field is much higher than that expected from the quasar–galaxy cross-correlation function" (Overdensity of galaxies)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The quasar's Lya at the quasar redshift falls in the Subaru/HSC NB926 filter, and ALMA was used to find obscured galaxies  
  > "the redshifted Ly$\alpha$ line of galaxies at the quasar redshift is well placed in a narrow band filter, NB926, on Subaru/HSC" (Observations and data analysis); "To identify obscured galaxies in the quasar field" (ALMA observation and [C II] emitters)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Volume used for the mass is conservative and the line-of-sight depth is a lower limit  
  > "We note that the volume is conservative because the boundary does not cover the whole structure and the line-of-sight depth is a lower limit" (The mass of the protocluster)
- Limitation (authors): Many LAE candidates are not confirmed or ruled out and need deeper spectroscopy  
  > "Future deeper spectroscopic observations are needed to confirm the nature of these LAE candidates." (Optical spectroscopy)
- Limitation (authors): Non-uniform observations of other quasar fields make direct comparison difficult  
  > "the current non-uniform observations make it challenging to directly compare their physical properties and evolutionary phases" (Comparison with other galaxy overdensities traced by $z\sim6$ quasars)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA reduction, imaging and line-fitting details are deferred to an in-prep paper, so the [C II] luminosities cannot be checked from this text
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass estimate depends on extrapolating the Chiang13 formula to z~6.6 and masses beyond its calibration, and on one bias value from Ouchi18
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Overdensity of the central region depends on the choice of a ~14x30 arcmin^2 box drawn after seeing the LAE distribution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The lifetime limit uses a single galaxy and ignores peculiar velocity and Lya redshift uncertainty
