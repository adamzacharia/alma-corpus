## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and line cubes**: Concatenate 12+7 m, tclean multiscale with Briggs 0.5; lines cleaned with yclean and feathered with TP  
  > "The 12 and 7 m array datasets were concatenated and cleaned together using the CASA `tclean` algorithm with a Briggs weighting of 0.5." (3 Observations)
   - software: CASA, yclean
   - parameter: multiscale 0,3,10,30 pixels; pixel 0.16"  
  > "scale values of 0, 3, 10 and 30 times the image pixel size (0.16")" (3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify cores and measure fluxes**: Dendrogram on 267 GHz continuum; compare with 2-D Gaussian fit in CASA  
  > "Cores are defined and their fluxes extracted by using the dendrogram technique (cite: Rosolowsky-2008)" (4.1 Dust Structure)
   - software: astrodendro, CASA
   - parameter: threshold 10 x rms, step 1 x rms, min pixels = beam area / pixel size  
  > "we used a threshold of 10 $\times$ rms, a step of 1 $\times$ rms, and a minimum number of pixels equals to the beam’s area divided by the pixel size." (footnote 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gaussian fit flux 21.9 mJy vs dendrogram 26.0 mJy (~15% different)  
  > "both comparable to the values obtained with dendrograms ($\sim$15% different)" (4.1 Dust Structure)
3. **Derive core masses and densities**: Optically thin dust mass equation with Planck function  
  > "The mass of the cores is calculated by using the following expression" (4.1 Dust Structure)
   - parameter: T = 14 K; kappa_1.1mm = 1.13 cm2/g; beta = 1.7  
  > "we obtain $\kappa_{1.1 mm}$ = 1.13." (4.1 Dust Structure)
   - assumption: gas-to-dust ratio 100; mu_H2 = 2.8; spherical cores  
  > "A gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100 is assumed in this work." (4.1 Dust Structure); "assuming a spherical core and using the molecular mass per hydrogen molecule ($\mu_{\rm H_2}$) of 2.8" (4.1 Dust Structure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Linewidths, Mach number and virial parameter**: Gaussian fits to N2D+ and DCO+ spectra; sigma_nt from sigma_v^2 = sigma_th^2 + sigma_nt^2; virial mass with density index n = 1.8  
  > "We fitted a Gaussian profile to each spectrum across the ALMA1 core." (5.1.1 Virial Equilibrium)
   - parameter: T = 14 K; n = 1.8  
  > "Here we adopt a value of $n=1.8$" (5.1.1 Virial Equilibrium)
   - assumption: 50% mass uncertainty  
  > "assuming a 50% uncertainty in the mass value" (5.1.1 Virial Equilibrium)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Infall velocity and accretion rate**: Fit Hill5 model to average HCO+ spectrum with PySpecKit/lmfit; Mdot = 4 pi R^2 rho v_in  
  > "we fitted the average spectrum of the HCO$^+$ emission detected across ALMA1 using Hill5." (5.1.2)
   - software: PySpecKit, lmfit
   - parameter: initial guesses: tau 0.1-30, vlsr -89 to -86.6, vin 0.1-4, sigma 0.3-0.9 km/s, Tpeak 2-30 K  
  > "As initial guesses of the fit we used $\tau$ ranging from 0.1 to 30" (5.1.2)
   - assumption: Hill5 may underestimate infall velocity, so rate is a lower limit  
  > "which is an lower limit if we consider that the model can underestimate the infall velocity of the core." (5.1.2)
   - fitting: lmfit least squares with confidence levels from parameter-space exploration  
  > "The uncertainties in the fitting are given by `lmfit` functions that explicitly explore the parameter space and determine confidence levels." (5.1.2)
   - validation: Compared with singular isothermal sphere rate 2.5e-5 Msun/yr  
  > "This simple model gives an accretion rate of $(2.5\pm0.4)\times 10^{-5}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$" (5.1.2)
6. **Jeans mass, free-fall time and growth**: Thermal Jeans mass and free-fall time formulae; extrapolate constant accretion rate  
  > "For ALMA1 the core free fall time is $t_{ff}=\sqrt{3\pi/(32G\rho)}=3.3\times10^4$ yr." (5.2)
   - assumption: constant accretion rate over the free-fall time  
  > "(assuming a constant accretion rate over the whole time)" (5.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- HCO+ is blue-asymmetric self-absorbed while optically thin N2D+/DCO+ peak at the dip; no outflows detected, so ALMA1 is prestellar  
  > "We detect no high-velocity gas that would indicate molecular outflows originated from ALMA1, and thus confirm its prestellar nature." (4.2 Line Emission: Infall Signatures)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Cycle 2 267 GHz data used for dust analysis because of better sensitivity  
  > "For the dust analysis we used only the Cycle 2 data at 267 GHz as it has better sensitivity." (3 Observations)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Rotation and outflows as explanation of line asymmetries rejected: no rotation asymmetry pattern or velocity gradients  
  > "Furthermore, no velocity gradients are detected in the optically thin tracers that could reveal signs of rotation." (4.2 Line Emission: Infall Signatures)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only 10% of single-dish flux recovered; temperature from clump scale  
  > "Indeed only 10% of the flux observed by the single dish was detected by the 12 and 7 m ALMA arrays combined." (4.1 Dust Structure); "Given that the temperature has been determined on scales much larger than the cores" (4.1 Dust Structure)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single uniform 14 K temperature drives mass and virial results
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Hill5 on a single averaged spectrum; HNC does not show infall
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Magnetic support not constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distance and 50% mass error dominate alpha_vir
