## Analysis recipe

1. **Smooth ALMA emission-line cubes, generate masks and moment maps, and measure integrated line fluxes**: Hanning smooth cubes over 3 channels, create Boolean masks with Smooth & Search, generate moment maps with Makemask, build S/N map accounting for channel dependencies, and sum pixels with S/N > 3 for total flux  
  > "To further boost the S/N, the cubes were Hanning smoothed over three channels using the `Smoothspec` task, giving a velocity resolution of 30 km s$^{-1}$ and the rms noise ($\sigma_{\rm cube}$) listed in Table [\[tab:cubes\]](#tab:cubes)." (ALMA observations); "Subsequently, moment maps were obtained with the `Makemask` task, considering the signal inside a Boolean mask that was created with the `Smooth & Search` task." (ALMA observations); "Total line fluxes are measured summing pixels with $\rm{S/N>3}$ in the moment-zero maps; uncertainties are estimated repeating the sum for pixels with $\rm{S/N>2}$ and $\rm{S/N>4}$." (ALMA observations)
   - software: 3DBAROLO (version 1.6)
   - parameter: Hanning smoothing over 3 channels to 30 km s^-1 resolution  
  > "To further boost the S/N, the cubes were Hanning smoothed over three channels using the `Smoothspec` task, giving a velocity resolution of 30 km s$^{-1}$ and the rms noise ($\sigma_{\rm cube}$) listed in Table [\[tab:cubes\]](#tab:cubes)." (ALMA observations)
   - parameter: Smooth & Search parameters: Factor=1.5, Snrcut=4, Growthcut=3, Minchannels=3  
  > "To build such a mask, we use the task `Smooth & Search` with parameters `Factor=1.5` (factor for spatial smoothing), `Snrcut=4` (primary S/N threshold), `Growthcut=3` (secondary S/N threshold when growing the initial mask), and `Minchannels=3` (minimum number of channels for a detection to be accepted, considering that the data have been Hanning smoothed over 3 channels)." (Nonparametric 3D fitting)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux uncertainties estimated by repeating pixel sums for S/N > 2 and S/N > 4  
  > "Total line fluxes are measured summing pixels with $\rm{S/N>3}$ in the moment-zero maps; uncertainties are estimated repeating the sum for pixels with $\rm{S/N>2}$ and $\rm{S/N>4}$." (ALMA observations)
2. **Prepare archival SINFONI H-alpha cube for zC-400569**: Add WCS, convert spectral axis to velocity, rotate 30 deg counterclockwise to align with north, resample to 0.05'' pixels, subtract continuum using first-order polynomial fit to line-free channels, and extract H-alpha sub-cube  
  > "We first add the WCS coordinate system to the cube and convert the third axis unit from $\mu$m to velocity assuming the H$\alpha$ redshift of 2.2405 (cite: Forster2018)." (SINFONI data); "To align the cube with the north direction, we perform a rotation of 30 degrees counterclockwise at the kinematic center of the galaxy and resample the data with a pixel size of 0.05$''$, the same as its original value." (SINFONI data); "To estimate the continuum flux and subtract it from the SINFONI cube, we fit a first order polynomial to the line-free channels, excluding spectral regions with H$\alpha$ and \[N II\] emission." (SINFONI data)
   - parameter: Pixel size = 0.05'', PSF FWHM ~ 0.32'', LSF FWHM = 87 km s^-1 (sigma_inst ~ 37 km s^-1)  
  > "The full-width half-maximum (FWHM) of the point spread function (PSF) of the SINFONI cube is $\sim$0.32$''$, while the FWHM of the line spread function is $\Delta_{\rm V}\simeq87$ $\mathrm{km}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ ($\sigma_{\rm inst}
\simeq \Delta_{\rm V}/2.35 \simeq 37$ $\mathrm{km}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$) around the H$\alpha$ line (cite: Forster2018)." (SINFONI data)
   - assumption: H-alpha redshift = 2.2405  
  > "We first add the WCS coordinate system to the cube and convert the third axis unit from $\mu$m to velocity assuming the H$\alpha$ redshift of 2.2405 (cite: Forster2018)." (SINFONI data)
   - fitting: First-order polynomial fit to line-free channels for continuum subtraction  
  > "To estimate the continuum flux and subtract it from the SINFONI cube, we fit a first order polynomial to the line-free channels, excluding spectral regions with H$\alpha$ and \[N II\] emission." (SINFONI data)
   - validation: Checked astrometry against HST images; verified [NII] line flux ratios match theoretical value after continuum subtraction; tested higher-order polynomials  
  > "Finally, we checked the astrometry by overlaying the moment-zero map from the SINFONI cube with images from the Hubble Space Telescope (HST), which are discussed in Sect. [2.3](#sec:HST)." (SINFONI data); "After continuum subtraction, we checked that the flux ratios of the two \[N II\] lines are consistent with the theoretical value within the errors." (SINFONI data)
3. **Extract stellar surface brightness profiles and decompose into morphological components**: Fit elliptical isophotes to HST WFC3 F160W images to derive azimuthally averaged surface brightness profiles, then fit multicomponent galaxy models (de Vaucouleurs bulge + exponential disk, plus Sersic n=0.2 lens for zC-488879)  
  > "In short, after frame cleaning and sky determination, ARCHANGEL fits elliptical isophotes to the images and derives azimuthally averaged surface brightness profiles (see previous references for details)." (HST data); "For zC-400569, the surface brightness profile is well fitted by a classic bulge plus disk model, in which the bulge is described by a (cite: DeVauc1948) profile and the disk by an exponential profile (cite: Freeman1970)." (HST data); "Thus, we add a lens-like component using a (cite: Sersic1963) profile with $n=0.2$ and two further free parameters: $R_{\rm e,\,lens}$ and $\mu_{\rm e,\,lens}$." (HST data)
   - software: ARCHANGEL, SciPy (PYTHON)

## Qualitative findings

## Reasoning

- Why this data: zC-400569 has some of the highest quality SINFONI AO H-alpha data in the SINS survey, and its proximity on the sky to zC-488879 allowed simultaneous high-resolution ALMA targeting of multiple CO lines in both galaxies within a single field of view.  
  > "This galaxy is one of the best observed object from the SINS IFU survey (cite: Forster2009; Forster2018; Genzel2017)." (Introduction); "Luckily, next to zC-400569, there is another main-sequence galaxy at $z=1.47$ (COSMOS 0488879 or COSMOS2015 0301356) so we set up our ALMA observations to simultaneously target the CO($2-1$) and CO($3-2$) lines of this second object (see Fig. [1](#fig:TwoBirds))." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Nonparametric 3D tilted-ring fitting directly models beam smearing on 3D line profiles without imposing a predefined functional form on the rotation curve or gas density profile.  
  > "In addition to getting observations with the highest possible spatial resolution, the best approach to account for beam-smearing effects is modeling the three-dimensional (3D) emission-line datacube in a nonparametric fashion (cite: Corbelli1997; Sicking1997; Swaters1999; Fraternali2001; Fraternali2002; Gentile2003; Gentile2004; Gentile2007; Jozsa2007; Swaters2009; Lelli2010; Lelli2012a; Lelli2012b; Lelli2014; DiTeodoro2015)." (Introduction); "We model the gas kinematics in a nonparametric fashion without a-priori assumption on the gas density profile, rotation curve shape, and gravitational potential." (Nonparametric 3D fitting)
- Alternative: Higher-order polynomials for SINFONI continuum subtraction were tested and rejected because they did not significantly improve the subtraction.  
  > "We explored the use of higher-order polynomials but they did not significantly improved the continuum subtraction." (SINFONI data)
- Alternative: HST WFC3 F110W surface brightness profiles were derived but rejected for mass modeling due to larger errors compared to F160W.  
  > "The profiles from the F110W filter show a similar shape but they have larger errors, so they will not be used in the rest of this paper." (HST data)
- Alternative: Classic two-component bulge+disk model was rejected for zC-488879 because of profile curvature at 1'' < R < 2'', leading to the addition of a Sersic lens component.  
  > "For zC-400569, the same model does not provide satisfactory results because the surface brightness profile displays a curvature at $1''<R<2''$ that is reminiscent of “lenses” and “barlenses” in disk galaxies at $z\simeq0$ (cite: Laurikainen2011; Athanassoula2015)." (HST data)
- Alternative: Kinematic modeling of the [CI](1-0) cube was rejected due to low sensitivity, asymmetry, and a systemic velocity offset.  
  > "We were not able to perform a detailed kinematic analysis of the \[C I\] data, so we use them solely as a total H$_2$ mass tracer in Sect. [5.3](#sec:onlybaryons)." (The galaxy zC-400569 at $z\simeq2.4$)
- Alternative: Parametric 3D kinematic models (such as DYSMAL) were rejected in favor of nonparametric 3D tilted-ring fitting because parametric models make implicit assumptions about beam smearing and impose smooth rotation curve shapes.  
  > "Parametric 3D models assume predefined functions for the rotation-curve shape and the gas density profile, so they necessarily make implicit assumptions on the intrinsic strength of beam-smearing effects." (Nonparametric 3D fitting)
