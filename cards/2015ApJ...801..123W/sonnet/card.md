## Analysis recipe

1. **Re-reduce data to recover edge channels**: Re-reduced with CASA keeping 118 of 128 channels per baseband instead of 106  
  > "In summary our reduced product contains 118 of the original 128 channels per baseband, compared with 106 channels in the default reduction." (Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked noise does not increase in extra channels  
  > "We checked that the noise does not increase in these extra channels, except for the very first and last channels" (Observations)
2. **Fit [C II] line profile**: Single Gaussian plus flat continuum fitted with MCMC; extra 10% in quadrature on flux and FWHM for missing channels  
  > "A single Gaussian plus flat continuum model was fit to the available data using a Markov-Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)." (Results); "We add in quadrature an extra 10% uncertainty in both the line flux and FWHM due to the missing channels." (Results)
   - assumption: Symmetric line model  
  > "This is because a symmetric line model is used" (Results)
   - fitting: MCMC; priors not stated  
  > "A single Gaussian plus flat continuum model was fit to the available data using a Markov-Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)." (Results)
   - validation: Continuum level of fit consistent with continuum from line-free basebands (J2229+1457)  
  > "The continuum level of the fit is again consistent with the independent continuum level determined from the three line-free basebands." (Results)
3. **Measure source sizes**: 2D Gaussian fits to line and continuum maps  
  > "We used the CASA IMFIT task to fit 2D gaussian models to these maps." (Results)
   - software: CASA IMFIT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Convert continuum and [C II] to L_FIR and SFR**: Greybody T_d=47 K, beta=1.6; SFR=1.5e-10 L_FIR; SFR=1.0e-7 L_[CII]  
  > "As in we adopt a greybody spectrum with dust temperature, $T_{\rm d} =47$ K and emissivity index, $\beta=1.6$." (Results)
   - assumption: All dust heated by stars, not quasar; Chabrier IMF  
  > "We note that this assumes that all the dust contributing to the 1.2 mm continuum is heated by hot stars and not by the quasar." (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compare L_FIR evolution with redshift**: Mean L_FIR of four CFHQS quasars (J2329 set to 2sigma limit 60 uJy) compared with Herschel-based low-z samples at L_Bol ~7e12 Lsun  
  > "We combine the four values of far-infrared luminosity derived from these measurements assuming that J2329-0301 has a flux equal to its $2\sigma$ upper limit of $60\,\mu$Jy." (4.1)
   - assumption: L_FIR=1.43 nuL_nu(100um) and 1.5 nuL_nu(60um) conversions  
  > "We adopt $L_{\rm FIR}= 1.43\,\nu L_{\nu} (100\,\mu{\rm m})$ (cite: Chary:2001)" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Fit L_FIR-L_[CII] relation at z>5**: Single power-law fit to 12 z>5 sources with MCMC including observational uncertainties  
  > "We fit the 12 $z>5$ sources with a single power-law model of the dependence of $L_{\rm FIR}$ on $L_{\rm [CII]}$ incorporating the observational (but not systematic) uncertainties using a MCMC procedure." (4.2)
   - fitting: MCMC, power law  
  > "We fit the 12 $z>5$ sources with a single power-law model of the dependence of $L_{\rm FIR}$ on $L_{\rm [CII]}$ incorporating the observational (but not systematic) uncertainties using a MCMC procedure." (Discussion, The [C ii] – far-IR luminosity relation)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Derive sigma and M_dyn**: FWHM20 = 1.5 FWHM; v_cir=0.75 FWHM/sin i with i=arccos(a_min/a_maj); M_dyn=1.16e5 v_cir^2 D Msun, D=1.5x deconvolved FWHM  
  > "The circular velocity is therefore $v_{\rm cir}=0.75 \,$FWHM$_{\rm [CII]} / \sin i$." (4.3); "$M_{\rm dyn} \approx 1.16\times 10^5\, v_{\rm cir}^2 \,D \,M_\odot$" (4.3)
   - parameter: i=55 deg assumed when unresolved (J2229+1457, J1148, J1120)  
  > "An inclination of $i=55^{\circ}$ is assumed as this is the median inclination angle for the resolved sources in this paper and (cite: Wang:2013)." (4.3)
   - assumption: [C II] gas in an inclined disk; BH mass uncertainty +0.3 dex (0.45 dex if Eddington-based)  
  > "The \[C ii\] emitting gas is assumed to be in an inclined disk" (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared J1148 M_dyn with CO-based literature value  
  > "For comparison (cite: Walter:2004) determined a dynamical mass from CO emission in this quasar of $5.5\times 10^{10}\,M_\odot$" (4.3)

## Qualitative findings

- z=6 CFHQS quasars lie on the local M_BH-sigma relation on average but with much larger scatter.  
  > "whilst there is little mean shift between the $z=0$ and $z\approx 6$ data, there is a much larger scatter in the data at $z\approx 6$" (4.3)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Increase sample of ~1e8 Msun BH quasars to test host properties free of the sigma bias  
  > "these quasars are not subject to the bias in the $M_{\rm BH} - \sigma$ relation discussed previously because of their moderate black hole masses" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Low L_FIR could be due to low dust content rather than low SFR; judged not main factor  
  > "However, two lines of evidence point towards this not being the main factor for our quasar sample." (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Dynamical masses highly uncertain (unknown gas structure, marginal resolution, missed extended gas); no formal errors  
  > "We note that there is considerable uncertainty on these values due to the unknown spatial and velocity structure of the gas" (4.3)
- Limitation (authors): Missing red-wing data of J0055+0146 may bias size and inclination  
  > "We note that the missing data in the red wing of the \[C ii\] line may cause a bias in the size and inclination" (Results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tiny sample (4 CFHQS, ~10 total) and heterogeneous literature data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-temperature greybody and SFR conversions dominate systematics, not propagated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Assumed inclinations and unresolved sizes drive sigma/M_dyn
- Caveat (inferred, ours): J2229+1457 continuum is only 2 sigma
