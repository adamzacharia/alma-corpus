## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure ALMA upper limits for RMC 14**: Check for ALMA observations of the sample; report 3 sigma upper limits for RMC 14 in Bands 3 and 6  
  > "We checked for ALMA observations of the sample." (2.2 Archival data); "RMC 14 is not detected, with $3~\sigma$ upper limits of $1.41\,\rm mJy$ at $93\,\rm GHz$ and $6.0\,\rm mJy$ at $220\,\rm GHz$." (2.2 Archival data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Aperture photometry on Spitzer MIPS24/MIPS70 images**: Aperture photometry for sources lacking or with poor catalogue measurements  
  > "We performed aperture photometry for all sources that were either lacking measurements or are extended in the images." (3.1 Photometry from Spitzer MIPS24/MIPS70)
   - software: photutils
   - parameter: Flux errors only account for the background uncertainty; 3 sigma upper limits for non-detections  
  > "For non-detected sources, we provide $3\sigma$ upper limits." (3.1 Photometry from Spitzer MIPS24/MIPS70)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with catalogue values  
  > "the results are consistent with the published values" (3.1 Photometry from Spitzer MIPS24/MIPS70)
3. **Photometry and upper limits from Herschel PACS**: Procedure equivalent to Gaussian fitting with local peaks searched in beam-sized boxes  
  > "To maximise the signal-to-noise ratio, we adopted a procedure equivalent to Gaussian fitting." (3.2 Photometry and upper limits from Herschel)
   - parameter: 1 sigma uncertainty = background standard deviation plus 10% flux calibration, summed in quadrature  
  > "The $1\,\sigma$ total uncertainty is determined by summing in quadrature the standard deviation of the background and the flux calibration uncertainty of 10%." (3.2 Photometry from Herschel)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Classify the sample by infrared SED**: Four SED classes based on free-free excess, mid- to far-infrared dust excess, hot dust, or photosphere only  
  > "We identify four classes based on the individual infrared spectral energy distributions (SED):" (4 The LBV SED classification scheme)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature classifications and the [8.0] vs [8.0]-[24] colour-magnitude diagram  
  > "In Fig. [5](#fig:cmd-LMC) we present the \[8.0\] versus \[8.0\]-\[24\] colour-magnitude diagram (CMD) of stars in the LMC" (4 The LBV SED classification scheme)
5. **Grey-body dust masses of individual stars**: Grey-body fit of the infrared SED for LHA 120-S 119 and for RMC 71, HD 269216, RMC 81, RMC 40; beta fixed at 1.3 for three sources  
  > "we thus fit the infrared SED with a greybody to derive the characteristic temperature, dust mass and $\beta$ parameter" (5.1 Individual stars); "We fit these data by fixing the parameter $\beta$=1.3" (5.1 Individual stars)
   - parameter: kappa(850 um) = 1.7 cm^2 g^-1  
  > "an absorption coefficient value $\kappa_{\rm 850\mu m}$ of $\rm 1.7\, cm^{2}\, g^{-1}$" (5.1 Individual stars)
   - assumption: Optically thin dust, so radiative transfer effects are negligible  
  > "For such sources we can assume that radiative transfer effects are negligible." (5.1 Individual stars)
   - assumption: LMC distance 49.97 kpc for all sources  
  > "We assume a distance of $49.97\,\rm kpc$ for the LMC (cite: 2013Pietrzynski) for all sources." (5.1 Individual stars)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: RMC 71 grey-body mass compared with the radiative-transfer literature value  
  > "we also fit the data of RMC 71 with a grey-body and find a dust mass which agrees with the literature value" (5.1 Individual stars)
6. **Stack images of 18 LMC LBVs**: Co-add cutouts of SAGE, HERITAGE and WISE images at known coordinates, then fit a 2-D Gaussian to measure aggregate flux  
  > "The stacking analysis adopted here consists in co-adding the images, at a given wavelength, of individual sources with known coordinates, in order to reduce the noise by averaging, resulting in an aggregate flux density measurement." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC); "We then determined the aggregate flux density of the combined source by performing a 2-D Gaussian fitting in each image." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
   - software: Scipy Optimize, photutils
   - parameter: Bands stacked: WISE W3, MIPS24, MIPS70, PACS100, PACS160, SPIRE250; IRAC from catalogue sums  
  > "Images of the following bands were stacked: WISE W3, MIPS24, MIPS70, PACS100, PACS160, and SPIRE250." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
   - assumption: No deblending used since confusion is dominated by diffuse ISM emission  
  > "Here, we do not adopt any deblending method described by the authors, as the confusion in our case is dominated by diffuse ISM emission, of which a-priori positional information is difficult to obtain." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
   - fitting: Non-linear least squares; Gaussian widths fixed to the beam for MIPS70, PACS100, PACS160 and free for WISE W3 and MIPS24  
  > "for minimising the fitting function residuals" (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
   - validation: Per-filter correction factor from individual test sources compared with catalogue values  
  > "we corrected the values by a factor determined in each filter by comparing our own estimates of individual test sources with catalogue measurements" (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
7. **Fit the stacked SED for the integrated dust mass**: Power law for winds and photosphere plus a grey body for the dust, mass from the best-fit flux at 850 micron  
  > "with a power law representing the sum of stellar winds and photospheres" (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC); "The dust mass of the aggregate source is computed by assuming the flux density extracted from the best fit at $\rm 850\,\mu$m." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
   - parameter: Two absorption coefficients: kappa(850) of Sodroski et al. 1987 and the Gordon et al. 2014 kappa_160 = 9.6  
  > "Results are given for two different opacity values, as in (cite: 1987Sodroski) (SD87) and (cite: 2014Gordon) (GD14)." (4.2 Total dust mass in the LMC)
   - assumption: Systematic kappa uncertainty of about 25% not propagated  
  > "The uncertainty of about $25\%$ in $\kappa_{\lambda}$ due to the systematic error is not taken into account." (4.2 Total dust mass in the LMC)
   - fitting: Grey-body range set by chi-squared twice the minimum; lower limits at 70 and 250 micron used as constraints  
  > "The grey shaded region represents all the grey-body components that fit the data producing a $\chi^2$ which is twice the minimum value of the best fit." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Stack extreme AGB stars for comparison**: Same stacking on the Riebel et al. catalogue; 1342 extreme AGBs after removing ten sources; two-component fit  
  > "The final sample of extreme AGB stars from (cite: 2012Riebel) that we stacked comprises 1342 objects." (5.3 Stack analysis of other evolved stars in the LMC)
   - assumption: Photosphere black body of fixed 3000 K; grey body with beta = 0; extinction with R_V = 3.1; optically thin dust (lower limit on mass)  
  > "We fitted the data with two components: a black body of fixed temperature (3000 K) describing the stellar photosphere, and a grey body with dust spectral index $\beta=0$." (5.3 Stack analysis of other evolved stars in the LMC); "The dust mass derived from this fitting assumes that the dust is optically thin (at the wavelength at which $\kappa$ is defined), and thus must be considered a lower limit." (5.3 Stack analysis of other evolved stars in the LMC)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 24 micron stacked photometry consistent with Riebel et al. stacked photometry  
  > "we compared our photometry extracted from the stack image with the stacked photometry (cite: 2012Riebel) and found a consistent value" (5.3 Stack analysis of other evolved stars in the LMC)
9. **Estimate total LBV dust yield over the LMC lifetime**: Two cases: constant number of LBVs with an assumed LBV phase duration; and a star formation history plus IMF integral with an LBV fraction f_LBV,MS  
  > "The simplest estimate we can make of the total dust mass produced by LBVs in the LMC over all time is by assuming that the number of LBVs has on average been the same as today." (6.2.1 Simple case: Constant number of LBVs over time); "We assume that the LBV population closely followed the fluctuations in the star formation history (SFH) of the LMC." (6.2.2 Accounting for star formation history and initial mass function)
   - parameter: IMF slope -1.9 (Case 1) or Salpeter -2.35 (Case 2)  
  > "Assuming the slope $\gamma=-1.9$ from (cite: 2018Schneider) (Case 1) and $\gamma=-2.35$ from Salpeter (Case 2)" (6.2.2 Accounting for star formation history and initial mass function)
   - assumption: LBV fraction of massive main-sequence stars between about 30% and 100%  
  > "it seems plausible that $f_{\rm LBV,MS}$ ranges between about $30\%$" (6.2.2 Accounting for star formation history and initial mass function)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Four infrared SED classes of Magellanic LBVs; Class 4 sources can be removed from the LBV census  
  > "We find four classes of sources:" (abstract)
- The LBV stack integrated dust mass is two orders of magnitude larger than that of the 1342 extreme AGB stars  
  > "This is two orders of magnitude larger than the value inferred from stacking 1342 extreme-asymptotic giant branch (AGB) stars." (abstract)
- Individual LBV dust masses do not correlate with stellar parameters  
  > "The dust mass of individual LBVs does not correlate with the stellar parameters, possibly suggesting that the dust production mechanism is independent of the initial stellar mass or that the stars have different evolutionary histories." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Checked whether archival ALMA data exist for the sample sources  
  > "We checked for ALMA observations of the sample." (2.2 Archival data)
- Why this method: Stacking was used because individual sources are mostly undetected at 70 micron and longer due to confusion  
  > "Given the low detection rates of individual sources at $70\,\mu\rm m$ and longer, largely due to confusion, we have also carried out a stacking analysis to provide a statistical measure of total dust content." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
- Alternative: No deblending method was used for the stacks because confusion is dominated by diffuse ISM emission  
  > "Here, we do not adopt any deblending method described by the authors, as the confusion in our case is dominated by diffuse ISM emission, of which a-priori positional information is difficult to obtain." (5.2 Stack analysis of LBVs in the LMC)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Dust masses may be lower limits because cooler dust may be missed  
  > "Additionally, the dust masses estimated here could be a lower limit, as cooler dust may have escaped the observations." (6.2 Total dust mass in the LMC)
- Limitation (authors): Systematic uncertainty of about 25% in kappa is not propagated into the dust mass  
  > "The uncertainty of about $25\%$ in $\kappa_{\lambda}$ due to the systematic error is not taken into account." (6.2 Total dust mass in the LMC)
- Limitation (authors): The fraction of massive stars that become LBVs is unconstrained and dominates the yield uncertainty  
  > "A major uncertainty in this relation lies in the fraction of massive main-sequence stars that will become LBV stars" (6.2.2 Accounting for star formation history and initial mass function)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA is a minor part: only upper limits for one SMC source, with no processing details
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stack flux correction factors and SED fit parameters are shown only in figures
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust yield depends strongly on assumed LBV phase duration and LBV fraction, so the 1e4 to 1e5 Msun range is weakly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stack dominated by a few sources, notably RMC 143, and confusion-limited at far-infrared wavelengths
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single grey body with assumed kappa and beta may miss cold dust
