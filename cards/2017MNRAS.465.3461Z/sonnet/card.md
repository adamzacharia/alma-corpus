## Analysis recipe

1. **Identify clouds**: Dendrograms on Halpha and CO data cubes to extract flux, radius, velocity dispersion  
  > "to extract the basic properties of the clouds (flux, radius, and velocity dispersion): the HII regions from the GH$\alpha$FaS data-cubes, and the molecular clouds from the ALMA data-cubes." (3 Properties of clouds)
   - software: astrodendro
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Bootstrapping errors following Rosolowsky & Leroy 2006  
  > "We followed the procedure (the bootstrapping method) explained in (cite: 2006PASP..118..590R) to derive the errors in the measured parameters." (3.2.3 Uncertainties)
2. **Derive HII region properties**: SFR from Halpha luminosity (Kennicutt 1998), electron density, ionized mass, virial parameter  
  > "$\rm{SFR}(\rm{M_{\odot}/yr})=7.9\cdot10^{-42}L_{\rm{H\alpha}}(\rm{erg/s})$" (3.1.1)
   - assumption: E(B-V)=0.4 constant attenuation correction; spherical uniform-density HII regions  
  > "we correct using the average value found for $E(B-V)=0.4$ in the HII regions identified in the Antennae galaxies" (3.1.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive molecular cloud properties**: CO luminosity from Solomon et al. 1992, mass via alpha_CO, surface density and virial parameter  
  > "$M_{\mathrm{H_2}}=\alpha_{\mathrm{CO}}\thinspace L_{\mathrm{CO}},$" (3.2.2)
   - parameter: alpha_CO = 4.8 Msun (K km/s pc2)^-1, multiplied by 1.8 for CO(3-2) in Arp 186 and 236; factor 1.36 for helium  
  > "We adopt the value of $\alpha_{CO}=4.8\rm{M_{\odot}(K\thinspace km\thinspace s^{-1})^{-1}}$" (3.2.2)
   - assumption: Antennae average I_CO(1-0)/I_CO(3-2) ~ 1.8  
  > "the average ratio is $\frac{I_{CO}(1-0)}{I_{CO}(3-2)}\simeq1.8$" (3.2.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit scaling relations**: Radius-error-weighted linear fits of log relations, Pearson coefficient and reduced chi2; envelope regions chosen by minimum sigma per luminosity bin  
  > "We fit separately radius error weighted linear fits to the $\log(\Sigma_{\rm{SFR}})=a\log\rm{SFR}+b$ relation" (4.1)
   - fitting: weighted linear least squares by radius error; 95% confidence bands  
  > "We fit separately radius error weighted linear fits to the $\log(\Sigma_{\rm{SFR}})=a\log\rm{SFR}+b$ relation for the HII regions in the LIRGS, the Antennae, and for those in the non-interacting galaxy sample." (Results, Σ_SFR and Σ_mol)
   - validation: Pearson correlation coefficient and chi2_red  
  > "We estimate that the observed points are correlated using the Pearson Correlation Coefficient, and that the linear relation fits well enough through $\chi^2_{\rm{red}}$." (4.1)

## Qualitative findings

- Clouds with lower virial parameter have higher Sigma_SFR and Sigma_mol, i.e. are gravitationally dominated rather than pressure confined  
  > "Since the virial parameter decreases with the surface densities of both the star formation rate and the molecular gas, we claim that the clouds presented here are gravitationally dominated" (abstract)
