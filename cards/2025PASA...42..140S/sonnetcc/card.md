## Analysis recipe

1. **reduce UVIT FUV images**: CCDLab reduction of Level 1 data: drift correction from VIS images, flat-field, distortion correction, pattern noise reduction, Gaia DR3 astrometry  
  > "We used the CCDLab software package (cite: 2017PASP..129k5002P) to reduce the Level 1 data, with drift correction incorporated based on the VIS images" (3.1 UVIT Data)
   - software: CCDLab
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **identify FUV star-forming regions and remove AGN-affected ones**: ProFound watershed deblending with dilation photometry; regions with >= 6 pixels, skycut 3; photometric error <= 0.1; Gaia stars removed; regions overlapping BPT AGN-ionised HII regions (Shapely ellipse/circle intersection) excluded  
  > "A $\emph{skycut}$ of 3 which corresponds to a detection threshold of 3$\sigma$ (cite: 2018MNRAS.476.3137R) was applied to identify star-forming regions." (3.3 Identification of star formation sites); "A region was classified as AGN-affected if all three flags had a value of 3" (3.3 Identification of star formation sites)
   - software: ProFound, Shapely, Gaia DR3
   - parameter: minimum 6 pixels per region  
  > "the identified regions should encompass at least six pixels" (3.3 Identification of star formation sites)
   - parameter: 551 -> 147 (NGC 1365), 603 -> 108 (NGC 1433), 1801 -> 691 (NGC 1566) regions after cuts  
  > "the number of regions is reduced to 147 in NGC 1365, 108 in NGC 1433, and 691 in NGC 1566" (3.3 Identification of star formation sites)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **correct FUV magnitudes for extinction**: Balmer decrement from MUSE HII regions matched to FUV regions (mean ratio; 10 nearest HII regions if none overlap) gives A(Halpha), converted to A(FUV) with Cardelli coefficients; plus Milky Way A_V  
  > "In cases where no H II regions overlapped, the algorithm used the 10 closest H II regions to compute these values." (3.4 Extinction correction using MUSE Data); "The obtained A(H${\alpha}$) values were then converted to A$_{FUV}$ (extinction in the FUV wavelength) using the coefficients provided by (cite: 1989ApJ...345..245C)." (3.4 Extinction correction using MUSE Data)
   - software: Topcat
   - parameter: K_Halpha = 2.53, K_Hbeta = 3.61  
  > "where K$_{{H{\alpha}}}$ = 2.53 and K$_{{H{\beta}}}$ = 3.61 are the extinction coefficients for the Galactic extinction curve" (3.4 Extinction correction using MUSE Data)
   - assumption: intrinsic Balmer ratio 2.86 (case B, Te = 10,000 K, ne = 100 cm^-3)  
  > "we adopted an intrinsic Balmer ratio of 2.86 for case B recombination at an electron temperature T$_e$ = 10,000 K and a density n$_e$ = 100 cm$^{-3}$" (3.4 Extinction correction using MUSE Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **derive Sigma_SFR per region**: FUV SFR calibration, divided by physical region area and multiplied by cos(i)  
  > "\log(SFR_{FUV} \, (M_{\odot} \, \mathrm{yr}^{-1})) = 2.78 - 0.4 \, mag_{FUV} + 2 \log(D)" (3.5 Estimation of Sigma_SFR); "calculated by dividing the estimated SFR by its corresponding area and then multiplying by *cos(i)*" (3.5 Estimation of Sigma_SFR)
   - software: ProFound
   - parameter: area from the number of pixels containing 100% of the flux per region  
  > "The ProFound package identifies the total number of pixels containing 100$\%$ of the flux for each region." (3.5 Estimation of Sigma_SFR)
   - assumption: distances 19.57, 18.63, 17.69 Mpc; inclinations 55.4, 28.6, 29.5 deg  
  > "| NGC 1365 | 03 33 36.371 | -36 08 25.447 | 19.57 | 55.4 |" (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **derive Sigma_H2 per region**: UV regions overlaid on CO(2-1) moment0 maps with ProFound to extract line-integrated intensity; keep regions with S/N > 3; convert with R21 and metallicity-dependent alpha_CO and cos(i)  
  > "We selected only those regions where the CO(2-1) signal-to-noise ratio (*S/N*) $>$ 3" (3.6 Calculation of Sigma_H2); "This overlay process is facilitated by ProFound, allowing us to extract the line-integrated CO(2-1) intensity." (3.6 Calculation of Sigma_H2)
   - software: ProFound
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.35 (Z/Zsun)^-1.6 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1, with region mean Z from 12+log(O/H) via S calibration  
  > "\alpha_{\rm CO} = 4.35 \times \left(\frac{Z}{Z_\odot}\right)^{-1.6}" (3.6 Calculation of Sigma_H2); "These values were derived using the S calibration method (cite: 2016MNRAS.457.3678P)" (3.6 Calculation of Sigma_H2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **fit the resolved K-S relation**: Orthogonal distance regression of Sigma_SFR vs Sigma_H2 per galaxy, with Pearson and Spearman coefficients  
  > "We fitted the $\Sigma_{SFR}$ versus $\Sigma_{H_2}$ relation using an orthogonal distance regression (cite: 2020NatMe..17..261V; 2020A&A...634A..24K) algorithm." (4.1 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt Relation)
   - software: ODR (via cited SciPy/Virtanen and Kroupa works)
   - fitting: Orthogonal distance regression, slope N and intercept A; priors/likelihood not stated  
  > "The fitting parameters are summarized in Table [\[table:fit\]](#table:fit)." (4.1 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt Relation)
   - validation: Compared with literature slopes and with Pesa et al. (cite: 2021A&A...650A.134P) values for the same galaxies  
  > "For NGC 1566, they reported a strong rK-S relation with a near-linear slope of 1.04 $\pm$ 0.02, which is steeper than our slope value of 0.71 $\pm$ 0.04" (5 Discussion)
7. **compute SFE and its dependence on Sigma_H2 and bar location**: SFE = Sigma_SFR / Sigma_H2 per region; Pearson/Spearman correlations of SFE vs Sigma_H2; bar radius from IRAC 3.6 um elliptical isophote analysis; median SFE inside bars  
  > "We investigated the relationship of SFE with $\Sigma_{H_2}$ and $\Sigma_{SFR}$ to discern their respective contributions to SFE in these galaxies." (5.2 Star formation Efficiency in the sample); "The radial extent of the bar component in each galaxy was determined using isophotal parameters such as position angle and ellipticity." (5.3 SFE within the bar regions)
   - software: Python (elliptical isophote fitting)
   - assumption: SFE compared at fixed Sigma_H2 between bar and non-bar regions  
  > "we conducted an analysis incorporating $\Sigma_{H_2}$ as a covariate" (5.3 SFE within the bar regions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared bar SFE with literature medians  
  > "reporting a median SFE of 0.43 Gyr$^{-1}$ within the bars" (5 Discussion)

## Qualitative findings

- No single rK-S relation: each galaxy has its own slope and correlation strength at 120-132 pc  
  > "each galaxy exhibits its own unique SF relation, characterised by distinct power-law indices (N) and correlation coefficients" (5 Discussion)
- At fixed Sigma_H2, SFE shows no significant difference between bar and non-bar regions; Sigma_H2 drives the SFE trend  
  > "at a fixed $\Sigma_{H_2}$, the SFE does not show a statistically significant difference between bar and non-bar regions" (5.3 SFE within the bar regions)
- ALMA images are said to show small AGN outflows in the bar regions (no quantitative analysis given)  
  > "Analysis of ALMA images of our sample galaxies reveals small outflows of gas from the AGN in the bar regions." (5 Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Galaxies chosen for availability of both UVIT and ALMA observations, plus MUSE Halpha/Hbeta for extinction  
  > "These galaxies were selected primarily due to the availability of both UVIT and ALMA observations." (The Sample)
- Why this method: FUV used as a direct tracer of recent star formation; images not convolved to a common resolution to exploit UVIT's higher resolution  
  > "in order to leverage UVIT’s higher resolution, the images were not convolved to a common resolution in this study" (3.2 ALMA 12CO(2-1) Data)
- Alternative: Constant Milky Way alpha_CO = 4.35 rejected in favour of a metallicity-dependent alpha_CO  
  > "However, this factor is known to strongly depend on gas metallicity" (3.6 Calculation of Sigma_H2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): FUV traces SF over ~100 Myr, giving extended, diffuse regions and heterogeneous sizes; an intrinsic limitation of UV-based studies  
  > "These factors contribute to the heterogeneous sizes of the detected regions and represent an intrinsic limitation of UV-based studies" (4.1 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt Relation)
- Limitation (authors): Individual contributions of bar and AGN could not be separated; no non-barred comparison sample  
  > "although we could not fully separate their individual influences in our analysis" (Conclusion); "Although our sample lacks non-barred galaxies for direct comparison" (5.3 SFE within the bar regions)
- Limitation (authors): Slope depends on CO-to-H2 conversion factor, resolution and fitting method  
  > "the slope of the rK-S relation can vary significantly depending on the CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor used, the resolution, and the specific fitting methodology employed" (4.1 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt Relation)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only three galaxies; the weak NGC 1365/1433 slopes from ~100-150 regions are attributed to small number statistics without a test (e.g. subsampling NGC 1566)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Regions are selected from FUV peaks, so regions without CO detection (S/N<3) are dropped, which biases the slope and flattens the relation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Slope is sensitive to the metallicity-dependent alpha_CO (exponent -1.6) and an unspecified R21, so Sigma_H2 and the SFE-Sigma_H2 anti-correlation may partly be built in by the shared Sigma_H2 denominator
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Regions are not the same physical size or scale across galaxies (120-132 pc nominal, but areas vary), and no resolution-matched comparison was made
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Claims of AGN outflows in ALMA images are not backed by any shown analysis
