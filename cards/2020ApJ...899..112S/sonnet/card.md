## Analysis recipe

1. **Search for outflow emission and set upper limits**: Continuum-subtracted cubes; combine channels -1000 to -500 and +500 to +1000 km/s; measure flux in 10'' aperture; 3σ limit from source-free areas  
  > "we place a $3\,\sigma$ upper limit on high-velocity emission from the intensity map generated by combining the velcity channels between $-1000$ to $-500\, \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ and $+500$ to $+1000\, \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$" (2.1 Upper Limits on Outflow Flux)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: R21 = 0.62 and alpha_CO = 0.8 for outflow mass  
  > "We adopt $R_{21}=0.62$ (cite: Shangguan2019ApJ) and $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}=0.8$" (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure CO sizes**: Fit 2D Gaussian to line-core visibilities  
  > "We then fit the visibility data with the CASA task `uvmodelfit` using a two-dimensional Gaussian model." (2.2 Sizes of CO-emitting Region)
   - software: CASA uvmodelfit
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simulations with simobserve show robust sizes for >1''; consistent within 30% with high-resolution ALMA radii for six quasars  
  > "the CO radii constrained from the ACA data are consistent within 30% of the half-light radii measured by J. Molina et al. (in preparation)" (2.2 Sizes of CO-emitting Region)
3. **Derive gas masses, stellar masses and correlations**: Kendall tau with censored data (cenken), Linmix and LTS_PLANEFIT fits, partial correlation  
  > "We use the generalized Kendall’s $\tau$ calculated with the `cenken` function from the NADA package of `R`" (3.3 Molecular Gas and AGN Fueling)
   - software: R NADA, Linmix, LTS_PLANEFIT
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 3.1  
  > "assuming a CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor of $\alpha_\mathrm{CO} = 3.1$" (Sample and Data Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
