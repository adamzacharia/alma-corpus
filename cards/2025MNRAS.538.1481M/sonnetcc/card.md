## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image the line cubes**: Modified calibration pipeline in CASA, self-calibration on line-free continuum, continuum subtraction, 12m+7m merged tclean cubes with primary beam correction  
  > "After merging the 12m and 7m configurations, continuum subtracted line cubes were cleaned using the task $tclean$." (Observations); "For each configuration, line-free continuum emission was used for self-calibration." (Observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5 for lines, -2.0 for bright continuum  
  > "We adopted Briggs robustness parameter of 0.5 for molecular lines, while the parameter is -2.0 for the bright continuum to obtain the highest angular resolution." (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Map emission and identify components**: Moment 0 maps and velocity channel maps of H2CO, HDCO and D2CO; components labelled A, B, W and I-VII by velocity range  
  > "shows moment 0 maps, i.e., the spatial distribution of the lines emission integrated over the emitting velocity range" (Results); "Table [\[flows\]](#flows) lists these structures, and the velocity ranges over which they are detected in the channel maps" (Streamers and Outflow Cavities in H2CO emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CS(5-4) outflow cavity walls and earlier SO, C18O and CH3OH maps of the same structures  
  > "The association with the outflow cavity is confirmed by kinematics: the northern side is blue-shifted, while the southern side is red-shifted, in agreement with CS emission." (Streamers and Outflow Cavities in H2CO emission)
3. **Extract spectra at representative positions**: Spectra extracted at the continuum peaks of A, B, W and at positions in the SE outflow cavity (VI) and southern streamer (VII)  
  > "extracted in five selected positions representative of the compact and extended components identified towards the VLA1623-2417 cluster" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - parameter: For A and B, intensities integrated over blue- and red-shifted ranges to avoid absorption at systemic velocity  
  > "To properly measure the line intensities from the spectra extracted at the position of the continuum peaks of A and B and avoid the effect of absorption at systemic velocity" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive excitation temperature from HDCO line ratio**: Intensity ratio of HDCO 4(1,4)-3(1,3) (38 K) and 4(2,2)-3(2,1) (63 K) gives Tex per component  
  > "we used the intensity ratio of the two HDCO lines to determine the excitation temperature ($T_{\rm ex}$) of the gas in the different components." (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - assumption: LTE and optically thin emission  
  > "Local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and optically thin emission have been assumed." (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tex upper limits for streamers because the higher-excitation HDCO line is not detected  
  > "is an upper limit, due to the non-detection of the higher excitation HDCO line" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
5. **Derive column densities of H2CO, HDCO and D2CO**: LTE optically thin column densities using the HDCO-derived Tex for all three species  
  > "we assumed LTE and optically thin emission and adopted the excitation temperature ($T_{\rm ex}$) estimated from the HDCO lines." (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - assumption: The three species probe the same component  
  > "This is based on the assumption that the three species probe the same component" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Similarity of line profiles at each position supports common emitting component  
  > "an hypothesis reinforced by the similarity of the extracted line profiles at each position" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
6. **Compute abundance ratios and D/H**: D/H = 0.5 x N(HDCO)/N(H2CO) and D/H = sqrt(N(D2CO)/N(H2CO))  
  > "D/H = 0.5 $\times$ $N_{\rm HDCO}$/$N_{\rm H_2CO}$, and D/H = $\sqrt{N_{\rm D_2CO}/N_{\rm H_2CO}}$" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with D/H from earlier interferometric studies (PILS, FAUST) in a literature comparison figure  
  > "The range of D/H values found in the envelope, outflow cavities, and infalling streamer of VLA1623 A agrees with earlier findings." (Comparison with previous results on H2CO deuteration)
7. **Make intensity ratio maps and test origin of extended D2CO**: HDCO/H2CO and D2CO/H2CO intensity ratio maps over velocity ranges of components V, VI, VII; D2CO moment 1 and 2 maps used to argue for photodesorption in a PDR rather than shocks  
  > "We show the intensity ratio maps of HDCO/H$_2$CO and D$_2$CO/H$_2$CO" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers); "The extended D$_2$CO emission shows a narrow velocity dispersion between 0.3 and 1.2 km s$^{-1}$." (On the origin of D2CO extended emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Ratio maps compared with the table values  
  > "The distribution of the ratio maps is consistent with that found in Table" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)

## Qualitative findings

- H2CO traces the inner envelopes of VLA1623 A, B and W, the outflow cavities and several streamers; HDCO and D2CO are detected towards A, the cavities and one streamer, not towards B and W  
  > "The HDCO and D$_2$CO emissions are observed towards VLA1623A, in its outflow cavities, and in one of the streamers." (abstract)
- D2CO traces a flattened envelope where H2CO and HDCO are fainter, interpreted as grain formation in the prestellar phase and photodesorption by two nearby B stars  
  > "This may be due to D$_2$CO formation on dust grains in the cold prestellar phase, and subsequent photodesorption caused by the enhanced UV flux from two nearby B stars." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Interferometric data needed to avoid beam dilution of single-dish deuteration estimates  
  > "Interferometric observations are therefore mandatory to minimise beam dilution and disentangle the inner region (less than 100 au) around protostars from the other physical components" (Introduction)
- Alternative: Association of streamer II with the southern outflow cavity rejected because the streamer is blue-shifted while the southern cavity side is red-shifted  
  > "this is excluded by the fact that the accretion streamer is blue-shifted while the southern cavity side is red-shifted" (Streamers and Outflow Cavities in H2CO emission)
- Alternative: Shock release of D2CO disfavoured because shocks would produce broad line profiles  
  > "This is not consistent with the release of D$_2$CO molecules in shocks, which would produce large line profiles." (On the origin of D2CO extended emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): D/H in the streamers is not accurate because only an upper limit on Tex (lower limit on column densities) is available  
  > "the estimate of D/H in the streamers is not accurate because we only have an upper limit of the excitation temperature, hence a lower limit of the column densities of the three species" (Deuteration in the inner envelope, in the outflow cavities, and in the streamers)
- Limitation (authors): Structure IV lies near the half-power point of the primary beam, so no conclusion on its origin  
  > "therefore no reliable conclusion on its origin can be drawn" (Streamers and Outflow Cavities in H2CO emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tex from two HDCO lines with Eup of 38 and 63 K has large uncertainties (e.g. 125 +/- 60 K) and is applied to H2CO and D2CO under an LTE, optically thin, co-spatial assumption
- Caveat (inferred, ours): H2CO 3(0,3)-2(0,2) may be optically thick or absorbed towards A and B, which would bias N(H2CO) and the D/H ratios
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Streamer D/H values rely on Tex upper limits, and the D2CO 4(2,3)-3(2,2) line is only marginally detected (2-3 sigma)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The PDR photodesorption explanation for extended D2CO is argued qualitatively without chemical modelling
