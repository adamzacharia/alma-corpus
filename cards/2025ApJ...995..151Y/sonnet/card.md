## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO(1-0)**: ALMA scripts calibration, TCLEAN imaging, IMPBCOR, 3 sigma masking on 6'' smoothed map  
  > "The calibrated data were then imaged using the CASA task `TCLEAN` with Briggs weighting (robust = 0.5)" (2.2 ALMA CO Data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; 2 km/s channels  
  > "| NGC 1035 | 2.93$\times$2.06 | 2 | 182.6 | 38 | 1229 |" (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Convolve to common 7.5'' resolution**: Convolve CO cubes and 3.6um images to 7.5'' Gaussian PSF  
  > "we convolved the CO data cubes and the 3.6 $\ensuremath{\mu}m$ images to the common 7.5$\ensuremath{''}$ Gaussian PSF" (3.1 Radial Distributions)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive surface density maps**: Sigma_H2 from I_CO; Sigma_SFR from NUV+12um; Sigma_star from 3.4/3.6um  
  > "The H$_2$ surface mass density was derived from the intensity of CO ($I_{\rm CO}$) using the standard Galactic CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor" (3.1 Radial Distributions)
   - parameter: cos i correction; helium factor 1.36  
  > "where the factor of 1.36 accounts for the helium correction." (3.1 Radial Distributions)
   - assumption: X_CO = 2e20 cm^-2 [K km/s]^-1; alpha_CO = 4.35  
  > "The adopted conversion factor $X_{\rm CO}$ corresponds to $\alpha_{\rm CO} = 4.35\ M_\odot\ \rm pc^{-2}\ [K \,\mbox{km~s$^{-1}$}]^{-1}$, including the helium correction." (3.1 Radial Distributions)
   - assumption: Constant mass-to-light ratio 0.35 Msun/Lsun  
  > "assuming a constant mass-to-light ratio of $\Upsilon_\star^{3.4}= 0.35\ \mbox{$M_\odot$}L_{\odot}^{-1}$" (3.1 Radial Distributions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Radial profiles**: Concentric elliptical annuli of 7.5'' width; error bars are standard deviation in annulus  
  > "We used the GIPSY task `ELLINT` to obtain the profiles measured in concentric elliptical annuli with a fixed radial width of 7.5$\ensuremath{''}$" (3.1 Radial Distributions)
   - software: GIPSY
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Fit molecular SFL and SFE**: Least-squares power-law fit of Sigma_SFR vs Sigma_H2 per galaxy; SFE = Sigma_SFR/Sigma_H2  
  > "we performed least-squares fits for each galaxy" (3.2 Molecular SFL)
   - fitting: Least squares, no priors  
  > "we performed least-squares fits for each galaxy" (3.2 Molecular SFL)
   - validation: Tested with 1 sigma CO points and radially varying alpha_CO  
  > "we included additional data points down to the $1\sigma$ level of the CO intensity" (3.2 Molecular SFL)
6. **Compute Toomre Q_tot**: Two-component thick-disk Q from gas and stars  
  > "The total $Q$ parameter ($Q_{\rm tot}$) is calculated using the approximation given by (cite: 2011MNRAS.416.1191R)" (3.3 Gravitational Instability)
   - parameter: sigma_g = 5 km/s; sigma_star,r = 30 km/s  
  > "We adopt $\sigma_g = 5$ km s$^{-1}$, a representative value for dwarf galaxies" (3.3 Gravitational Instability)
   - assumption: Q_g from H2 only; sigma_z/sigma_r = 0.6 stars, 1 gas  
  > "we compute $Q_{\rm g}$ using only $\Sigma_{\rm H_2}$" (3.3 Gravitational Instability)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Depletion times are shorter than in massive spirals, likely due to constant Galactic alpha_CO  
  > "these short timescales are likely a consequence of adopting the standard Galactic conversion factor" (Summary and Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-resolution, sensitive ALMA CO overcomes limits of earlier single-dish studies  
  > "To better understand the nature of star formation in dwarf galaxies, it is essential to employ observational data with high resolution and sensitivity." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Constant Galactic alpha_CO adds systematic uncertainty; no resolved metallicity  
  > "We note that the adopted $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ introduces an additional systematic uncertainty." (3.2 Molecular SFL)
- Limitation (authors): Two galaxies have slopes from only two CO data points  
  > "the slope estimates rely on only two central data points where CO is significantly detected" (3.2 Molecular SFL)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample of six galaxies, not strictly dwarfs (log M* up to 9.7)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Slopes from few radial bins with scatter-based errors
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Constant sigma_g and sigma_star assumed; H I neglected in Q
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Constant alpha_CO for low-metallicity systems
