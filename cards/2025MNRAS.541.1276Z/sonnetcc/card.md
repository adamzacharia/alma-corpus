## Analysis recipe

1. **identify CO structures on Moment 0 maps**: astrodendro dendrogram on strictly masked PHANGS-ALMA CO (2-1) Moment 0 maps; leaves retained  
  > "Using the *astrodendro* package [^5], there are three major input parameters for the dendrogram algorithm" (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: min_npix = 1 beam area; no additional parameters set  
  > "we only require the smallest area of the identified structure be larger than 1 beam area. We do not set additional parameters in the algorithm" (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: visual correspondence between identified structures and background intensity maps  
  > "the CO structures identified by the dendrogram algorithm exhibit a strong correspondence with the background intensity maps" (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
2. **derive cloud mass and effective radius**: M_cloud = alpha_CO x I_CO x A; ellipse from rms sizes enlarged by factor 2; R_eff = sqrt(2a x 2b) x D  
  > "Then the effective physical radius of an ellipse is $R_{\rm eff}$ =$\sqrt{2a \times 2b}*D$, where $D$ is the distance of the galaxy." (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: alpha_CO(2-1) = 6.7 Msun pc^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "where $\alpha^{2-1}_{\rm CO} \approx 6.7" (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
   - assumption: unified CO-to-H2 conversion factor for all five galaxies  
  > "we use an unified CO$-$to$-$H$_{2}$ conversion factor" (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **extract spectra and classify velocity components**: remove galaxy rotation with KinMS dynamical models, extract average spectrum per structure, fit with GAUSSPY+; 67 structures with poor line profiles eliminated; 1712 leaves fitted  
  > "we removed the bulk motion due to galaxy rotation by creating gas dynamical models using the Kinematic Molecular Simulation (KinMS) package" (3.1.1 Velocity component); "According to the averaged spectra, 67 structures with poor line profiles were eliminated." (3.1.1 Velocity component)
   - software: KinMS, GAUSSPY+
   - fitting: automated Gaussian decomposition with GAUSSPY+, procedure inherited from earlier work  
  > "using the fully automated Gaussian decomposer `GAUSSPY+` algorithm" (3.1.1 Velocity component)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **match clusters and associations to clouds**: assign clusters or associations whose centres lie within the cloud effective radius; use 64 pc association scale; take V-band over NUV where overlapping; sum independent association masses; exclude clusters inside associations when summing total stellar mass  
  > "If the distances between the center coordinates of the associations and a molecular cloud are less than the effective radius of the cloud, we distribute these associations to the cloud." (3.2.1 Stellar associations); "To avoid redundant calculations of mass, the clusters that fall within the associations should be excluded." (3.2.3 Total stellar populations)
   - parameter: drop clusters/associations with mass less than mass error; age cut 20 Myr  
  > "an age cut of 20 Myr is imposed to eliminate chance superpositions between older stellar populations and molecular clouds" (2.1 Star cluster and stellar association catalogs)
   - assumption: typical GMC survival timescale 10-30 Myr  
  > "Considering that the typical survival timescale of GMCs is 10$-$30 Myr" (2.1 Star cluster and stellar association catalogs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: human-classified cluster catalogs used to verify the ML-catalog results  
  > "while also employing the human-classified cluster catalogs to verify the results" (2.1 Star cluster and stellar association catalogs)
5. **estimate embedded stellar mass from 21 micron**: M_*,tot,emb = Sigma_SFR x A x t_age, with Sigma_SFR from 21 micron intensity (WISE 22 micron prescription) and t_age from the 21 micron to CO peak separation  
  > "the JWST 21 $\mu$m emission was used to estimate the total mass of the embedded stellar populations in a cloud" (3.3 Embedded stellar populations)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: 21 micron dendrogram: min_npix = 1 beam area, min_value = 3 sigma_rms, min_delta = 3 sigma_rms  
  > "where $\sigma_{\rm rms}$ is the background intensity" (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
   - assumption: embedded phase duration 5.1 Myr from NGC 628 assumed valid for five galaxies  
  > "We assume this timescale is also applicable to the five galaxies in this work." (3.3 Embedded stellar populations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: method gives masses comparable to SED fitting, as compared in earlier work  
  > "This method gives quite comparable masses with those obtained from the SED fitting" (3.3 Embedded stellar populations)
6. **compute cloud-scale SFE**: SFE = M_*,tot/(M_cloud + M_*,tot + M_I); M_I from HII-region Halpha flux and electron density (Egorov2023 eqs 5-6), found negligible, so SFE = M_*,tot/(M_cloud + M_*,tot)  
  > "Therefore, when a reasonable estimate of the electron density is available, $M_{\rm I}$ can be neglected relative to $M_{\rm cloud}$." (3.4 Star formation efficiency)
   - assumption: typical electron density about 1 cm^-3 from the equation (likely underestimate, up to about 100 cm^-3)  
  > "For our samples, the typical value of the calculated electron density is only" (3.4 Star formation efficiency)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: median SFE compared to literature values  
  > "which is comparable with the values listed in (cite: Kim2022-516; Chevance2023-534)" (3.4 Star formation efficiency)
7. **fit correlations between cloud and stellar-population parameters**: linear fits in log-log space giving slope k, intercept b, uncertainties and Pearson r, tabulated for 11 relations  
  > "summarizes all the fitted correlations between the physical parameters of molecular clouds and their internal stellar populations" (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Pearson correlation coefficient r reported  
  > "$r$ is the Pearson correlation coefficient." (Figure 5 caption)
8. **model M_cluster,max-M_cloud with ICIMF theory**: embedded cluster mass function xi_ecl = k_ecl M^-beta, mass conservation, optimal sampling normalization, solving for M_ecl,max as function of M_*,tot = SFE x M_cloud  
  > "Here, we adopt the optimal sampling." (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
   - parameter: M_ecl,min = 5 Msun; M_ecl,up ~ 1e9 Msun; SFE = 1.4%; beta = 1.82 from fitted cluster mass function  
  > "Thus, we take $M_{\mathrm{ecl,min}}=5 M_{\odot}$" (3.5 The ICIMF theory); "As shown in Fig.[11](#fit), when fixing SFE=1.4%, a smaller $\beta$ can provide a better fit." (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
   - assumption: all stars in a cloud form in a population of embedded clusters  
  > "We start by assuming all the stars in a cloud form in a population of embedded clusters with the mass function" (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
   - fitting: model compared to observed relation by eye with beta and SFE varied; no sampler or likelihood stated  
  > "Since we will use the model to fit the $M_{\rm cluster, max}$-$M_{\rm cloud}$ relation, taking the slope fitted in Fig.[10](#cmf)(a) is more appropriate." (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Embedded stellar population mass is systematically smaller than the exposed population, suggesting a short embedded phase  
  > "found that the embedded stellar population mass is systematically smaller than that of the exposed stellar population. This suggests a short duration of the embedded phase." (Conclusion)
- SFE tends to decrease as cloud mass increases  
  > "There is a trend that the SFE decreases as the cloud mass increases." (Conclusion)
- Ionized gas mass M_I is negligible relative to M_cloud  
  > "The mass of the ionized gas ($M_{\rm I}$) in each cloud can be neglected relative to $M_{\rm cloud}$." (Conclusion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: high-resolution ALMA CO, JWST and HST allow connecting individual clouds with their stellar populations  
  > "The combination of these high-resolution observations provides an unprecedented look at the connection between individual clouds and the stellar populations" (Introduction)
- Why this method: strictly masked Moment 0 maps chosen for reliable structures; minimal dendrogram parameters to reduce dependence on settings  
  > "We do not set additional parameters in the algorithm to minimize the dependence of the identification on parameter settings." (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
- Alternative: broadly masked Moment 0 maps rejected as noisier with possible false positives  
  > "they tend to be noisier and may contain false positives" (3.1 Identification of molecular clouds)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): unified alpha_CO for all galaxies; per-cloud correction left for future work  
  > "it is important to correct $\alpha^{2-1}_{\rm CO}$ for each molecular cloud to accurately estimate the mass of the cloud. This will be left for future work." (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
- Limitation (authors): same SFE and beta used for all clouds gives only a rough fit  
  > "using the same parameter settings for all molecular clouds can only result in a rough fit." (3.5 The ICIMF theory)
- Limitation (authors): embedded populations may not be observed in PHANGS-HST  
  > "The embedded stellar populations in molecular clouds may not be observed in the PHANGS-HST survey" (3.3 Embedded stellar populations)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Correlation r values are modest (0.35-0.71) and fit method/uncertainty treatment is not described
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cloud scale is about 70-120 pc, so structures are blends of clouds and complexes rather than single GMCs
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Embedded phase duration of 5.1 Myr from NGC 628 is applied to other galaxies without testing
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cluster-to-cloud matching by centre distance within R_eff can mis-assign projected clusters
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model comparison to data is qualitative; no statistical fit of beta and SFE
