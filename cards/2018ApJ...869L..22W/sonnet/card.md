## Analysis recipe

1. **Image [OIII]88 cube and continuum**: CASA natural-weighted imaging; uv-plane continuum subtraction with line-free channels; continuum map from line-free channels  
  > "For all analysis of the [O]$_{88}$ line data, the continuum has been subtracted in the *uv*–plane using line–free channels." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure line and continuum at peak positions**: Gaussian fits to peak-extracted spectra; 2D Gaussian fits to integrated maps (unresolved); [CII] re-measured at peak  
  > "2D–Gaussian fitting shows that the [O]$_{88}$ line emission is unresolved at our resolution like the continuum emission." (3.2 [O]88 line emission)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits  
  > "From Gaussian fitting to the line." (Table 1)
   - validation: Beam sizes of [OIII] and [CII] data nearly identical, so no beam matching  
  > "Therefore, no additional beam matching was required." (2 Observations)
3. **Derive dust temperature, IR/FIR luminosity and SFR**: Modified blackbody fit to 88 and 158 um points, CMB effect included; SFR from L_IR  
  > "fitting the 88 $\mu$m and 158 $\mu$m data points (assuming they are optically thin) with a modified blackbody, taking the effect of the cosmic microwave background into account" (3.1 Continuum emission)
   - assumption: optically thin dust emission; Kennicutt & Evans 2012 SFR calibration  
  > "The resulting L$_{\rm IR}$–based star formation rates (cite: kennicutt12)" (3.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: [OIII]-based SFR compared with FIR-based  
  > "consistent with the FIR–based estimates (Sec. 3.1)" (3.2 [O]88 line emission)
4. **Compare line/FIR ratios to local LIRGs**: Plot L[OIII]/L_FIR, L[CII]/L_FIR and L[OIII]/L[CII] vs S88/S158 and T_dust against Díaz-Santos+17  
  > "we plot the [O]$_{88}$/FIR luminosity ratios vs. the continuum flux density ratios (S$_{88 \mu m}$/S$_{158 \mu m}$)" (3.3 Line/FIR luminosity ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Minimum ionized gas mass and ionizing photon budget**: M(O++) from L[OIII]; Q0 following Ferkinhoff; expected Q0 from constant SFR over 5 Myr, Chabrier IMF, blackbody stars  
  > "we infer the total stellar mass created in the last 5 Myr, and distribute it over different stellar mass bins based on a (cite: chabrier03) Initial Mass Function (IMF)." (4.3 Minimum number of ionizing photons and stars)
   - assumption: O/H=5.9e-4, most oxygen doubly ionized, gas-to-dust ~100, constant SFR  
  > "assuming a standard gas–to–dust ratio of $\sim$100" (4.2 Minimum ionized gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Kroupa IMF alternative gives similar Q0  
  > "this number changes to $Q_{\rm 0, exp}=2.3\times10^{55}$ s$^{-1}$ for a (cite: kroupa01) IMF" (4.3 Minimum number of ionizing photons and stars)

## Qualitative findings

- [OIII]88 and [CII] share positions and line widths, tracing the same potential; both sources unresolved.  
  > "which indicates that they trace the same gravitational potential on galactic scales" (4.1 Origin of the [C] and [O]88 emission)
