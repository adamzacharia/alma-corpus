## Analysis recipe

1. **Correlate and calibrate the EVN maser data**: SFXC correlation, then AIPS standard spectral-line reduction with Effelsberg as reference; fringe fitting on the strongest maser channel of the first epoch; phase referencing  
  > "The data reduction was carried out with the Astronomical Image Processing System (AIPS) developed by the National Radio Astronomy Observatory (NRAO) with standard procedures for spectral line observations." (2 Observations and data reduction); "The Effelsberg antenna was set as the reference." (2 Observations and data reduction); "fringe fitting (task FRING) was performed on the strongest maser channel from the first-epoch observations (cite: b09)" (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - software: AIPS, SFXC
   - parameter: 2-second integration time in the SFXC correlator  
  > "The SFXC software correlator in JIVE was used with a 2-second integration time (cite: k15)." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: 1024 channels in 2 MHz bandwidth giving 0.1 km/s resolution  
  > "Selection of 1024 channels in the 2 MHz bandwidth resulted in a 0.1 km s$^{-1}$ resolution." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: Phase referencing loop of 5 min: maser target 3.25 min, phase calibrator 1.75 min  
  > "The phase-referencing mode was used, repeating a loop of 5 min between the maser target (3.25 min) and a phase calibrator from the VLBA Calibrator List (1.75 min)." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: 3C345 scans used for bandpass and instrumental phases  
  > "The calibrator 3C345 was observed during a few 15-min scans at each run; these scans were used to calibrate the bandpass and instrumental phases." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - assumption: Fringe fitting on a maser channel removes annual parallax and Galactic rotation, leaving relative motions  
  > "This allowed us to exclude motions due to annual parallax and Galactic rotation and also provided the highest possible S/N to get the most accurate measurements of the relative positions." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit maser spot positions in channel maps**: Two-dimensional Gaussian fits to compact maser emission in each channel map with JMFIT or SAD  
  > "we used the AIPS tasks JMFIT or SAD (depending on the number of spots per source) fitting two-dimensional Gaussian models to the compact maser emission." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - software: AIPS (JMFIT, SAD)
   - parameter: Detection threshold 7 sigma_rms per channel map; typical 1 sigma_rms of 4 or 6 mJy  
  > "we searched for maser emission above a threshold of 7$\sigma_{\rm rms}$ in each channel map" (2 Observations and data reduction); "The rms noise level (1$\sigma_{\rm rms}$) in line-free channels was typically 4 or 6 mJy for each source" (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: Natural weighting for the E2 synthesised beams  
  > "The synthesised beams, obtained with natural weightings in E2, are also listed in Table" (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit per spot; formal errors typically below 0.1 mas  
  > "The formal fitting errors were typically less than 0.1 mas per spot." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Proper motions of cloudlets (method 1)**: Group spots into cloudlets, identify persistent cloudlets by linear motion relative to a bright, spectrally stable feature, fit linear displacements over three epochs, then subtract the mean motion of persistent cloudlets to define the rest frame  
  > "Relative proper motions were derived via linear fits of cloudlet displacements over the three epochs." (2.1 Proper motions measurements); "iii) Last, we subtracted the average proper motion of persistent cloudlets from the proper motion of each cloudlet." (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - parameter: Cloudlet = at least three (rarely two) spots in consecutive channels within half the synthesised beam  
  > "a maser cloudlet is defined as a combination of at least three (in rare cases two) maser spots appearing at consecutive spectral channels and coinciding in position within half the synthesised beam" (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - parameter: Conversion of mas/yr to km/s uses source distances, e.g. 4.18 kpc for G23.207-00.377  
  > "which at a distance of 4.18 kpc (cite: r19) correspond to linear velocities from 1.2 to 11.6 km s$^{-1}$." (3 Results)
   - assumption: Co-spatial spots at the same LSR velocity at different epochs trace the same gas volume  
  > "We assumed in our studies that co-spatial maser spots at the same LSR velocity identified at different epochs trace the same volume of gas." (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - fitting: Linear fits of cloudlet displacements over three epochs, per RA and Dec  
  > "In method 1, we derived the proper motions via linear fits of cloudlet displacements over three epochs." (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - validation: Error cones from the linear-fit uncertainties; consistency with method 2 and the pair separation analysis  
  > "We drew ‘error cones’ for each proper motion vector, as derived from the uncertainty of the linear fits in each direction (RA and Dec)." (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
4. **Proper motions from best-fit ellipses (method 2)**: Select spots visible at all three epochs, fit flux-weighted ellipses per epoch, align ellipse centres to epoch 1, build averaged proper motion vectors from displacements between pairs of spots within each group  
  > "i) selection of maser spots that were visible at all three epochs; ii) fitting the flux-weighted ellipses to the selected maser spots at each epoch using the code by (cite: f99);" (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - software: ellipse-fitting code by Fitzgibbon et al. 1999
   - parameter: Applied to the sources with more than 100 spots: G23.207-00.377, G23.389+00.185 (and G23.657-00.127 in the earlier paper)  
  > "For two targets with a significant number of spots ($>$100), namely G23.207$-$00.377 and G23.389$+$00.185" (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - assumption: Methanol emission traces an ellipse whose centre coincides with the MYSO position  
  > "Assuming that the methanol emission traces an ellipse and that the centre of the ellipse coincides with the MYSO position enabled us to remove any bulk motion of the ring in the plane of the sky" (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
   - fitting: Flux-weighted ellipse fit per epoch (Table 1 gives centre, semi-axes, PA)  
  > "Parameters of flux-weighted ellipses fitted to the maser cloudlets that appeared in all three epochs." (Table 1)
   - validation: Results consistent with method 1 after excluding the blueshifted spots to the SW of the ellipse fit  
  > "we obtained results consistent with method 1 after excluding the blueshifted spots to the SW of the ellipse fit." (3 Results)
5. **Separation between spot groups over epochs**: Histograms of separations between pairs of spots from distinct regions at each epoch, and of the change in separation between epochs (2004 or 2007 vs 2013, and vs 2015); mean and median reported  
  > "we calculated the separations between pairs of spots from distinct regions at each epoch." (4.1 Proper motions: Expansion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Used as an analysis independent of the two proper-motion methods  
  > "In order to quantify the internal motion of maser spots in each source in a manner independent from the methods described above" (4.1 Proper motions: Expansion)
6. **Decompose proper motions into radial and tangential components**: Radial and tangential components of the method 1 proper motions relative to the ring centre; mean radial motion per source  
  > "We calculated the radial and tangential components of the proper motions" (3 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Fit a preferential plane of gas motion to 3D velocities**: Search for the plane containing the 3D velocity vectors by minimising the average ratio Vz/Vt; compare plane inclination with the inclination from the ellipse axis ratio  
  > "we searched for the best plane containing the 3D velocity vectors by minimising the average ratio V$_z$/V$_t$" (4.4 Modelling a plane of gas motion)
   - parameter: Plane described by i_model and PA_model; systemic velocities from NH3 literature values  
  > "The plane is identified with two angles: i$_{\rm model}$, the angle between the perpendicular to the plane and the line of sight" (4.4 Modelling a plane of gas motion)
   - assumption: Planar motion hypothesis as for Cepheus A HW2; inclination of the ring from the ratio of minor to major axes  
  > "The inclination, i, is based on the ratio of minor to major axes in the same convention as i$_{\rm model}$" (4.4 Modelling a plane of gas motion)
   - fitting: Minimisation of the mean Vz/Vt; optimiser and parameter grid not stated  
  > "we searched for the best plane containing the 3D velocity vectors by minimising the average ratio V$_z$/V$_t$" (4.4 Modelling a plane of gas motion)
   - validation: Compared with the plane of the best ellipse fit; agreement within 20-40 deg for four sources only  
  > "We note the agreement (within 20-40$^\circ$) between the inclinations of the best plane from the above model and the plane of the ellipse" (4.4 Modelling a plane of gas motion)
8. **Compare maser positions with archival ALMA thermal tracers**: Inspect archival ALMAGal Band 6 C18O and CH3CN emission relative to the maser position for three sources; no processing is described  
  > "Methanol masers in G23.389$+$00.185 overlap with the centres of continuum emission of C$^{18}$O and CH${_3}$CN emission" (3.2 Infrared and radio counterparts)
   - parameter: Band 6, 216.8 to 244.14 GHz  
  > "The observations covered the frequency range from 216.8 GHz to 244.14 GHz (band 6)" (3.2 Infrared and radio counterparts)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Overall ring morphology of the maser emission stayed stable over 8 to 10.5 years while individual cloudlet intensities varied between epochs  
  > "The overall morphology of the maser emission has remained stable, although the intensities of individual maser cloudlets varied from epoch to epoch, suggesting internal instabilities." (Conclusions)
- Archival ALMAGal data show G23.389+00.185 masers coincide with C18O and CH3CN emission peaks, interpreted as a hot molecular core; offsets for the other two sources  
  > "proving that the local gas is warm and that the methanol masers arise from a hot molecular core (due to the nearby presence of CH${_3}$CN emission)" (3.2 Infrared and radio counterparts)
- Maser-spot distribution alone cannot decide whether the rings trace discs; multi-frequency, complementary-resolution observations are needed  
  > "Our studies also show that the maser spot distribution alone does not allow for a direct interpretation of the ongoing scenario" (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Maser proper motions give the most direct measurements of gas kinematics near MYSOs; archival ALMA data used to place masers relative to thermal tracers  
  > "Such observations provide the most direct measurements of the gas kinematics near MYSOs" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Relative motions are the aim, so fringe fitting was done on a maser channel; ellipse centre assumed to coincide with the MYSO to remove bulk ring motion  
  > "As we aimed for detailed internal motion studies of masers and relied on the relative motions, fringe fitting (task FRING) was performed on the strongest maser channel" (2 Observations and data reduction); "Assuming that the methanol emission traces an ellipse and that the centre of the ellipse coincides with the MYSO position enabled us to remove any bulk motion of the ring in the plane of the sky" (2.1 Proper motions measurements)
- Alternative: Infall (as claimed for Cep A and AFGL 5142) is disfavoured in favour of expansion  
  > "the proper motions are consistent with expansion rather than infall" (4.1 Proper motions: Expansion)
- Alternative: For G23.657-00.127 two scenarios were proposed: maser ring tied to an outflow or wide-angle wind at the base of a jet  
  > "in G23.657$-$00.127 we proposed two scenarios: a maser ring related to an outflow or a wide-angle wind at the base of a protostellar jet" (4.3 Origin of the methanol rings)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ALMA angular resolution is diverse and poor for G31.581+00.077, so the offset may be marginal; detailed analysis of the ALMA data is postponed  
  > "Moreover, the angular resolution of the data is diverse. It is especially poor for G31.581$+$00.077, and the offset might be marginal." (3.2 Infrared and radio counterparts); "A detailed analysis of the association between thermal and maser lines at high angular resolution goes beyond the scope of this study" (3.2 Infrared and radio counterparts)
- Limitation (authors): Few spots and a shorter baseline make the expansion in G28.817 and G31.047 less robust  
  > "only a small number of spots was available, and the time baseline of the observations was shorter, so the results are less robust and need to be confirmed with future observations." (4.1 Proper motions: Expansion)
- Limitation (authors): No complementary data to locate the YSO, disc or jet orientation for the maser rings  
  > "Due to the lack of complementary data for our sample of methanol maser rings, we were not able to infer the YSO position and the associated disc nor the jet orientation" (4.4 Modelling a plane of gas motion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA plays a minor, qualitative role (offsets from archival maps) with no processing details, so ALMA results cannot be reproduced from this paper
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cloudlet grouping and 'approximately linear' selection are subjective; number of cloudlets is small in three sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Proper-motion uncertainties are formal linear-fit errors; systematic astrometric error is not discussed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ellipse-centre assumption (centre = MYSO) is untested without a driving-source position
