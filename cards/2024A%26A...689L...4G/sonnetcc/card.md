## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure ALMA line profiles and integrated flux**: Velocity-resolved [CI] 609 um and C18O 3-2 profiles binned to 0.4 km/s; Gaussian profile fits to derive centroid, component separation and integrated flux  
  > "A two-Gaussian fit to the two \[C i\] 609 $\mu$m components shows a velocity centroid difference of only 2.3 $\pm$ 0.4 km s$^{-1}$" (3 Results); "line integrated flux is 605 mJy km s$^{-1}$" (On the location of d203-506)
   - parameter: Common velocity resolution of 0.4 km/s  
  > "We binned all spectra to a common velocity resolution of 0.4 km s$^{-1}$." (2 ALMA and JWST observations of d203-506)
   - assumption: Source is not spatially resolved at 0.52'' x 0.38'', so the [CI] profile is roughly Gaussian  
  > "Since our observation does not spatially resolve d203-506, the resulting line profile is more or less Gaussian." (3 Results)
   - fitting: Two-Gaussian fit to the [CI] 609 um profile (sampler, priors, likelihood not stated)  
  > "A two-Gaussian fit to the two \[C i\] 609 $\mu$m components" (3 Results)
   - validation: [CI] profile compared with HCO+ 4-3 convolved to the same resolution  
  > "it is similar to that of HCO$^+$ 4–3 (convolved to the same angular resolution)" (3 Results)
2. **Extract NIRSpec ON-OFF spectrum and NIR carbon line intensities**: ON spectrum from an elliptical aperture and OFF spectrum from a nearby circular aperture; intrinsic spectrum = ON - f*OFF; baseline subtraction and Gaussian line fitting  
  > "which we derived after baseline subtraction and Gaussian line fitting of the ON – $f$$\cdot$ OFF spectrum." (Extraction of NIR spectra); "The OFF spectrum was obtained in a circular aperture of radius $r$ = 0.365$''$" (Extraction of NIR spectra)
   - parameter: Scaling factor f ~ 0.95-1  
  > "We find $f\simeq 0.95-1$, similar to (cite: Rogers24) for other disks." (Extraction of NIR spectra)
   - assumption: f is set so that no He I recombination line emission comes from d203-506 itself  
  > "$f$ is the scaling factor needed to have no He i recombination line emission from d203-506 itself" (Extraction of NIR spectra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: f range gives ~5 % uncertainty on NIR carbon line intensities  
  > "This range provides a $\sim$5 % uncertainty in the line intensities of the NIR carbon lines" (Extraction of NIR spectra)
3. **Compare NIR C0 line ratios with recombination theory**: Observed I(0.984)/I(1.069) and I(1.069)/I(1.176) ratios compared with Case A and Case B recombination predictions  
  > "In any case, the absolute intensities of the NIR C$^0$ lines observed in d203-506 are stronger" (Recombination theory applied to the observed NIR carbon lines)
   - parameter: Foreground extinction 1.5 mag with R_V = 5.5 law applied to the upper ratio  
  > "includes a foreground extinction correction of 1.5 mag (cite: Peeters24) adopting the $R_V$ = 5.5 extinction law of (cite: Gordon23)" (Recombination theory applied to the observed NIR carbon lines)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive N(C0) and gas-phase carbon abundance from [CI] 609 um with PDR models**: Compare observed [CI] 609 um intensity with a grid of Meudon PDR models (varying n_H, G0 = 2e4), adopting n_H ~ 1e7 cm^-3, to constrain N(C0) and x_C  
  > "fit the observed $I$(\[C i\] 609 $\mu$m) with $N$(C$^0$) $\simeq$ 2$\times$10$^{17}$ cm$^{-2}$ and $x_{\rm C}$ $\simeq$ 1.4 $\times$ 10$^{-4}$, with an uncertainty of less than a factor of 2." (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - software: Meudon PDR code
   - parameter: G0 = 2e4 for the grid; n_H = n(H) + 2 n(H2)  
  > "and $G_0$ = 2 $\times$ 10$^4$" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - parameter: Dust effective FUV absorption cross section 7e-22 cm2/H at 1000 A  
  > "providing an effective absorption cross section of 7$\times$10$^{-22}$ cm$^{2}$ H$^{-1}$ at 1000 Å" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - assumption: 1D stationary slab of constant density; wind dynamics neglected  
  > "allows a precise determination of the H/H$_2$ and C$^+$/C/CO transition layers in the slab, but neglects the wind dynamics (cite: Haworth20)" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - assumption: [CI] 609 um optically thin, collisionally excited and thermalized so intensity scales with N(C0)  
  > "collisionally excited, and thermalized ($T_{\rm ex}$ = $T_{\rm k}$)" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation); "In this regime, $I$(\[C i\] 609 $\mu$m) is proportional to $N$(C$^0$) irrespective of the physical conditions." (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - assumption: Contribution from the inner disk close to the star assumed negligible and beam-diluted  
  > "We implicitly assumed that the contribution to the \[C i\] 609 $\mu$m intensity from the inner disk close to the central star (cite: Gressel20) is negligible and beam-diluted." (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - fitting: Model-grid comparison by eye against observed intensity (no formal sampler or likelihood stated)  
  > "Models with $n_{\rm H}$ $\simeq$ 10$^7$ cm$^{-3}$ fit the observed" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
   - validation: Models with x_C = 1.4e-4 compared with the Trapezium sight-line carbon abundance; I([CI]609) varies by only a factor of 3 for n_H = 1e6 to 1e8 cm^-3  
  > "the predicted line intensity $I$(\[C i\] 609 $\mu$m) only changes by a factor of 3" (Variations in I([CI] 609 um) and NIR carbon lines)
5. **Model NIR C0 lines with FUV pumping to constrain G0**: Added C0 FUV pumping and C+ recombination cascades to the Meudon code and compared predicted NIR line intensities with observations  
  > "We specifically included 401 levels of C$^0$ (electronic states and their fine-structure levels), with electronic configurations up to 29$d$ (at $\sim$11.25 eV) and involving a total of 1572 radiative transitions." (Inclusion of FUV-pumping for C0 levels)
   - software: Meudon PDR code
   - parameter: Reference model n_H = 1e7 cm^-3, G0 ~ (1-2)e4, x_C = 1.4e-4  
  > "with $G_0$ $\simeq$ (1–2)$\times$10$^4$, $n_{\rm H}$ = 10$^7$ cm$^{-3}$, and $x_{\rm C}$ = 1.4$\times$10$^{-4}$" (Table 1 notes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Models with pumping match NIR intensities; without pumping predicted intensities are up to two orders of magnitude lower  
  > "Without this radiative excitation mechanism, the predicted NIR line intensities would be up to two orders of magnitude lower than observed" (Inclusion of FUV-pumping for C0 levels)
6. **Estimate water freeze-out depth with an analytical toy model**: Equate rate of O atoms sticking on grains with photo-desorption rate of water as a function of G0 for n_H = 1e7 cm^-3 and Tk = Td  
  > "This analytical determination of the H$_2$O freeze out depth implies equating the rate at which undepleted gas-phase O atoms stick on grains" (Little H2O freeze-out in the outer disk)
   - parameter: n_H = 1e7 cm^-3, T_k = T_d  
  > "for a typical gas density in the outer disk and inner wind, $n_{\rm H}$ = 10$^7$ cm$^{-3}$, and $T_{\rm k}$ = $T_{\rm d}$" (Little H2O freeze-out in the outer disk)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- [CI] 609 um shows a roughly Gaussian profile rather than the double peak of an edge-on rotating disk, tracing the outer disk and photo-evaporative wind, while C18O 3-2 shows a double peak from the Keplerian disk  
  > "The \[C i\] 609 $\mu$m line does not show the characteristic double-peak profile of an edge-on rotating disk." (3 Results); "In contrast, C$^{18}$O 3–2 shows a clear double-peak profile, which traces deeper layers" (3 Results)
- NIR [CI] 0.9853 um emission is extended and similar to FUV-pumped H2 emission, tracing the atomic-to-molecular transition  
  > "The \[C i\] 0.9853 $\mu$m emission is similar to that of H$_{2}^{*}$" (3 Results)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: [CI] 609 um is a powerful tracer of external photo-processing that ALMA can observe at subarcsecond and sub-km/s resolution  
  > "which can be observed by the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) at subarcsecond and sub-km s$^{-1}$ resolutions" (Introduction)
- Why this method: The Meudon PDR treatment of FUV penetration, H2 excitation, thermal balance and chemistry locates the H/H2 and C+/C/CO transitions precisely  
  > "allows a precise determination of the H/H$_2$ and C$^+$/C/CO transition layers in the slab" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation)
- Alternative: Recombination-only excitation of NIR C0 lines rejected: C+ + e- recombination adds only <~5 % and cannot reproduce observed intensities and ratios  
  > "we find that C$^+$ + e$^-$ recombinations only increase the intensity of the observed NIR carbon lines by $\lesssim$ 5 %." (Inclusion of FUV-pumping for C0 levels)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Models are 1D constant-density slabs that neglect wind dynamics  
  > "but neglects the wind dynamics (cite: Haworth20)" (4 Analysis: Thermochemistry and line excitation); "Therefore, as a first approximation, we adopt constant density models." (Variations in I([CI] 609 um) and NIR carbon lines)
- Limitation (authors): Higher angular resolution is needed to map abundances radially and vertically  
  > "Still, even higher angular resolution will be needed to map radial and vertical chemical abundances in Orion’s disks." (Discussion: Photo-processing in d203-506)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): x_C relies on an adopted n_H ~ 1e7 cm^-3 from H2 and on a single-slab model for an unresolved, beam-averaged [CI] line
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA calibration and imaging are not documented here, so the 605 mJy km/s flux cannot be independently checked
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No formal fit or posterior for x_C; uncertainty 'less than a factor of 2' is not derived
- Caveat (inferred, ours): [CI] flux may include contribution from the wind and foreground or background Orion Bar gas that is not modelled
