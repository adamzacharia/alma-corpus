## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, continuum-subtract and image line cubes and continuum**: Automated ALMA imaging pipeline continuum subtraction (NGC 6921 manual); Briggs-weighted imaging; cubes smoothed to ~33 km/s; continuum image from line-free channels  
  > "Continuum-subtracted visibilities were created using the automated ALMA imaging pipeline (cite: Hunter2023)." (2.2 New ALMA observations); "1-millimeter continuum images were also created from the spectral line-free channels." (2.2 New ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA 5.1.1-5, ALMA imaging pipeline
   - parameter: Briggs weighting, robust = 0.5  
  > "Data were imaged with Briggs weighting (robust = 0.5; (cite: Briggs1995))" (2.2 New ALMA observations)
   - parameter: Cubes smoothed to ~33 km/s channels, about four times the pipeline products  
  > "approximately four times that of the observatory-provided pipeline products" (2.2 New ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure 1-mm continuum peak positions, flux densities and sizes**: Two-dimensional Gaussian fit to the continuum map with CASA IMFIT; two components for NGC 7130  
  > "We measure continuum properties via a two-dimensional Gaussian fit to the continuum map" (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Fit region contains all continuum emission >3 sigma  
  > "The fit was performed over a region that contained all continuum emission $>3\sigma$." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
   - parameter: NGC 7130 two-component fit with peak positions fixed to observed peaks  
  > "The peak positions of these two components were fixed to the observed continuum peaks to ensure they were tracing a physical phenomenon." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
   - assumption: 1-mm continuum peak taken as the AGN position (and spectral extraction position)  
  > "We assume the peak of the 1-mm continuum to be the AGN position where HCN-vib emission is most likely to be detected." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit; uncertainties per Condon 1997  
  > "Uncertainty analysis of two-dimensional Gaussian-fitting method can be found in (cite: Condon1997)." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
   - validation: Free peak positions gave residual maps with excess flux at both peaks (poorer fit), so positions were fixed  
  > "Setting them as free parameters, in contrast, resulted in residual maps with excess flux at both peaks, indicating a poorer fit." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
3. **Make moment 0 maps and measure HCN and HCO+ (3-2) line fluxes, FWHM and surface brightness at the continuum peak**: Beam-sized aperture spectra at the continuum peak; Gaussian FWHM via specutils; flux over twice the FWHM; luminosity from Solomon and Vanden Bout formula divided by aperture area  
  > "We measure the fluxes of the HCN (3-2) and HCO$^+$ (3-2) spectral lines at the 1-mm continuum peak by extracting spectra with a beam-sized aperture" (3.2 Dense gas tracers); "We measure spectral line surface densities $\Sigma_{\text{line}}$ by dividing the line luminosity by the area of the beam-sized aperture" (3.2 Dense gas tracers)
   - software: Spectral-Cube, Astropy specutils
   - parameter: Moment 0 integrated over all channels with >3 sigma line emission  
  > "We generate total intensity (moment 0) maps of the two spectral lines by integrating over all channels with" (3.2 Dense gas tracers)
   - parameter: Flux integrated over a velocity range of twice the Gaussian FWHM  
  > "so the fluxes were extracted over a velocity range with a size of twice the Gaussian FWHM" (3.2 Dense gas tracers)
   - parameter: Flux uncertainty from spectrum RMS plus an assumed 10% Band 6 flux uncertainty  
  > "and an assumed 10% flux uncertainty for ALMA Band 6" (Table 4 notes)
   - assumption: Rest frequencies from Splatalogue; D_L and z from Armus et al. 2009  
  > "These rest frequencies were obtained from the astronomical spectroscopy database, Splatalogue." (2.2 New ALMA observations)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to the line profile for FWHM  
  > "This was measured via a Gaussian fit to the line profile." (Table 4 notes)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Place 3-sigma upper limits on HCN-vib luminosity and surface brightness**: Boxcar line profile of width equal to the HCN (3-2) FWHM on central beam spectrum, converted with the luminosity equation and divided by beam area  
  > "we place 3$\sigma$ upper limits on the HCN-vib emission by assuming a boxcar line profile with a width equal to the FWHM of the HCN (3-2) line detection" (3.3 Limits on HCN-vib emission)
   - parameter: Example NGC 4922N: mean 1-sigma noise 0.25 mJy over FWHM 148 km/s gives <0.11 Jy km/s  
  > "the NGC 4922N spectrum has a mean 1$\sigma$ noise level of 0.25 mJy." (3.3 Limits on HCN-vib emission)
   - assumption: CON criterion Sigma_HCN-vib >= 1 Lsun/pc^2  
  > "fall below the 1 $L_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ threshold for CONs at $\sim$100 pc-scales" (3.3 Limits on HCN-vib emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Searched regions surrounding the continuum peak; HCN-vib also undetected  
  > "We also explore the regions surrounding the continuum peak, but HCN-vib emission is undetected." (3.3 Limits on HCN-vib emission)
5. **Search for non-circular motions: moment 1/2 maps and position-velocity diagrams**: Moment 1 and 2 maps masked at moment 0 >= 3 sigma; HCN (3-2) PV slices along the major axis through the continuum peak  
  > "We first analyze the nuclear kinematics of the four observed CT AGN and search for non-circular motions." (3.4.1 Nuclear kinematics); "PV slices were centered on the 1-mm continuum peak" (3.4.1 Nuclear kinematics)
   - software: Spectral-Cube
   - parameter: PV slice width about one beam  
  > "an approximately beam-sized slice width" (3.4.1 Nuclear kinematics)
   - parameter: Systemic velocity from the peak of the HCN (3-2) line in extracted spectra  
  > "Dashed red lines denote the systemic velocity determined from the peak of the HCN (3-2) line in the extracted spectra" (3.4.1 Nuclear kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Map HCN/HCO+ (3-2) luminosity ratio and extract spectra at HCN-enhanced regions for outflow wings**: Ratio of HCN and HCO+ moment 0 maps masked where HCN is below 3 sigma; beam-sized spectra at elevated-ratio positions  
  > "We create this Figure by taking the ratio of the HCN (3-2) and HCO$^{+}$ (3-2) total intensity maps after masking out the regions where HCN" (3.4.2 HCN/HCO+ line ratio)
   - software: Spectral-Cube
   - parameter: NGC 6921 omitted (HCO+ unresolved, low S/N)  
  > "NGC 6921 is again omitted from this analysis because the HCO$^+$ (3-2) emission was not spatially resolved and had a low signal-to-noise." (3.4.2 HCN/HCO+ line ratio)
   - assumption: Elevated HCN/HCO+ can be driven by mechanical heating from outflows  
  > "which can be driven by mechanical heating from outflows" (3.4.2 HCN/HCO+ line ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Compare CT AGN HCN-vib and 1-mm surface brightness with CONs and derive CON detection rate**: Re-measure HCN-vib upper limits with continuum-sized apertures, plot against S_1mm/Omega with literature CONs and U/LIRGs, compute detection-rate interval  
  > "we compare our complete sample of nine CT AGN to other local U/LIRGs (including CONs) by plotting" (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?); "These values were calculated using spectra extracted with a continuum-sized aperture" (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?)
   - parameter: 1-sigma confidence interval by beta distribution quantile technique  
  > "the 1-$\sigma$ confidence intervals were estimated using the beta distribution quantile technique" (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?)
   - assumption: Both continuum peaks of NGC 7130 plotted since the AGN position is unknown (10 points for 9 AGN)  
  > "resulting in 10 plotted values for our complete sample of nine CT AGN" (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?)
   - fitting: Binomial population with beta-distribution quantiles  
  > "which assigns a probability density function to an assumed binomial population" (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?)
   - validation: Also tested the L_HCN-vib/L_IR > 1e-8 criterion  
  > "We find that the CT AGN are not CONs with respect to both the" (4.1.1 Impact of Spatial Resolution)
8. **Test the effect of spatial resolution on the HCN-vib non-detections**: Treat the HCN-vib emission as a point source with fixed luminosity (3-sigma or 1-sigma limit) and solve for the aperture radius at which surface brightness reaches 1 Lsun/pc^2  
  > "approximating the HCN-vib emission region as a single-point source for each CT AGN." (4.1.1 Impact of Spatial Resolution)
   - assumption: Line luminosity constant; only aperture radius changes  
  > "this approximation assumes that the line luminosity remains constant, and only the radius of the aperture is adjusted" (4.1.1 Impact of Spatial Resolution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Radii below 5 pc are inconsistent with the CON criterion definition  
  > "smaller radii are inconsistent with the observed properties of CONs." (4.1.1 Impact of Spatial Resolution)
9. **Assess outflow detectability and census literature outflows in the nine CT AGN**: Scale line luminosities of known CON HCN outflows to the survey sensitivity; tally literature molecular outflows and ages  
  > "we confirm that our sensitivities are sufficient to detect similar outflows in our observed CT sample, if they are present." (4.2 Evolutionary link); "Of the nine hard X-ray-confirmed CT AGN, we find that at least 44% (4/9) host molecular outflows." (4.2 Evolutionary link)
   - assumption: HCN (3-2)/HCN (1-0) luminosity ratio of 0.5  
  > "The latter calculation assumes an HCN (3-2) to HCN (1-0) luminosity ratio of 0.5 (cite: Aalto2015b; Gorski2024)." (4.2 Evolutionary link)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- HCN (3-2) and HCO+ (3-2) are detected in all four new sources, but HCN-vib is undetected at the continuum peak and surrounding regions  
  > "We detect HCN (3-2) and HCO$^{+}$ (3-2) emission in all of the four observed CT AGN." (3.2 Dense gas tracers); "Bright HCN-vib emission is undetected at the continuum peak of any of the four observed CT AGN" (3.3 Limits on HCN-vib emission)
- No obvious non-circular motion or high-velocity outflow signatures in PV diagrams or HCN-enhanced regions  
  > "In fact, obvious signs of non-circular motion are not seen in any of the position-velocity diagrams." (3.4.1 Nuclear kinematics)
- NGC 7130 continuum is double-peaked; the western peak is brighter at 1 mm, so the AGN position is unresolved and both peaks are reported  
  > "we find that the western peak is brighter in the 1-mm continuum" (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
- CT AGN lack significant self-absorption features, unlike CONs (except NGC 7674 which is double-peaked)  
  > "The observed CT AGN also lack significant self-absorption features in the HCO$^+$ (3-2) and HCN (3-2) line profiles" (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: New targets chosen for ALMA visibility and lack of prior HCN-vib observations  
  > "These objects were selected due to their visibility to ALMA (dec. $<$ 40 deg) and lack of prior HCN-vib observations." (2.1 Sample)
- Why this method: HCN-vib emission is used as the tracer of CON-like infrared cores  
  > "Bright HCN-vib emission, therefore, indicates the presence of a CON-like infrared core, making it a crucial tool in identifying and characterizing CONs." (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: L_HCN-vib/L_IR > 1e-8 criterion considered for completeness but Sigma_HCN-vib adopted as primary because the ratio can be biased for extended IR emission or multiple nuclei  
  > "suggested that this criterion may be biased against galaxies with a spatially-extended infrared emission region or multiple nuclei." (2.1 Sample)
- Alternative: Free peak positions in the NGC 7130 two-component Gaussian fit rejected as a poorer fit  
  > "Setting them as free parameters, in contrast, resulted in residual maps with excess flux at both peaks, indicating a poorer fit." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
- Alternative: NGC 7130 AGN position left undecided; results given for both continuum peaks  
  > "Since further observations are necessary to constrain the AGN position, we present results for both continuum peaks in this paper." (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
- How they decided it was enough: Sensitivity judged sufficient by showing known CON outflows would be detected at 3-28 sigma  
  > "we confirm that our sensitivities are sufficient to detect similar outflows in our observed CT sample, if they are present." (4.2 Evolutionary link)
- Limitation (authors): In-band mm spectral index poorly constrained due to low sensitivity and limited bandwidth  
  > "the low sensitivity and the limited bandwidth of the observed spectral windows results in spectral index uncertainties as high as" (3.1 1-millimeter continuum)
- Limitation (authors): IRAS 25/60 micron comparison cannot be made fairly because of sample selection bias  
  > "but we cannot fully explore this property due to a sample selection bias." (4.1 Are CT AGN also CONs?)
- Limitation (authors): Heavily Compton-thick AGN (NH > 1e25) are largely absent, so CONs as an extreme CT subset are not excluded  
  > "Thus, our study does not eliminate the possibility that CONs are an extreme subset of CT AGN" (4.3 Sample selection bias - Heavily-CT AGN)
- Limitation (authors): Origin of the molecular outflows (AGN vs starburst) is unclear, e.g. NGC 6240  
  > "We acknowledge that it is unclear whether all of these molecular outflows are powered by an AGN." (Footnote 2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only nine sources, with just four new ALMA targets; the 0 (+17/-0)% detection rate is a weak upper bound
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous spatial resolutions and HCN-vib measurement methods across literature sources (continuum-sized vs beam-sized apertures; NGC 1068 limit at ~15 pc)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Boxcar line assumption sets the upper limit; real HCN-vib width may differ and the 3-sigma limits depend on the continuum subtraction quality
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow non-detection relies on 33 km/s-smoothed HCN (3-2) cubes and moment/PV inspection only, with no quantitative outflow-wing fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): AGN position for NGC 7130 unresolved, so the continuum surface brightness comparison depends on the peak chosen
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Evolutionary-sequence conclusions rest on literature outflow census and timescale arguments that are qualitative
