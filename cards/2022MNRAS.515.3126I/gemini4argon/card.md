## Analysis recipe

1. **Create dust continuum images from calibrated datacubes**: Image calibrated datacubes with TCLEAN and collapse to continuum maps by excluding channels within 2 x FWHM around any detected [C II] or [O III] emission line.  
  > "The datacubes were then imaged using the TCLEAN task with automasking to clean down to $2\sigma$ with a cell pixel size of $0.15\ensuremath{''}$ and natural weighting." (ALMA Data Reduction and Dust Continuum Images); "The dust continuum maps were created using the calibrated datacubes by excluding the frequency range that covers $\rm 2 \times FWHM$ around a detected emission line, [C ii] or [O iii], of the targeted source in the phase centre." (ALMA Data Reduction and Dust Continuum Images)
   - software: CASA, TCLEAN
   - parameter: cell size = 0.15 arcsec, natural weighting, automasking threshold = 2 sigma, line exclusion = 2 x FWHM  
  > "The datacubes were then imaged using the TCLEAN task with automasking to clean down to $2\sigma$ with a cell pixel size of $0.15\ensuremath{''}$ and natural weighting." (ALMA Data Reduction and Dust Continuum Images); "excluding the frequency range that covers $\rm 2 \times FWHM$ around a detected emission line, [C ii] or [O iii]" (ALMA Data Reduction and Dust Continuum Images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify dust continuum sources and determine purity threshold**: Run PyBDSF on non-primary-beam-corrected science images (within r = 1.5 arcsec of phase centre) and negative (-1 x science) images (over full primary beam, scaled to r = 1.5 arcsec area) across a grid of iSNR and pSNR to select thresholds giving 95% purity.  
  > "We used `PyBDSF` (cite: Moha15) to detect dust emission in each ALMA dust continuum image without any primary beam correction applied." (Identifying Dust Emission); "We employed the ranges of $1.0 \leq {\rm iSNR} \leq 3.0$ and $2.0 \leq {\rm pSNR} \leq 5.0$, divided into steps of 0.1 for both of the parameters, but excluded the cases with ${\rm iSNR} > {\rm pSNR}$." (Identifying Dust Emission); "To maximise the number of reliable detections, we adopted the detection thresholds of (pSNR, iSNR) $= (3.3, 2.0)$, where the purity reaches $95\%$, for identifying the dust continuum of the REBELS targets." (Identifying Dust Emission)
   - software: PyBDSF
   - parameter: pSNR = 3.3, iSNR = 2.0, search radius = 1.5 arcsec, minimum island size = 1/3 of beam area pixels  
  > "The minimum allowed island size was set to one-third of the number of pixels in the beam area (the default setting of `PyBDSF`)." (Identifying Dust Emission); "Thus, we limit the search area for the positive sources in a circular area of $1.5\ensuremath{''}$ radius centred in the pointing field." (Identifying Dust Emission); "we adopted the detection thresholds of (pSNR, iSNR) $= (3.3, 2.0)$, where the purity reaches $95\%$" (Identifying Dust Emission)
   - fitting: Multiple Gaussian decomposition of islands in PyBDSF  
  > "`PyBDSF` identifies “islands” of adjacent emission and then these “islands” are decomposed by fitting multiple Gaussian functions to find individual sources and their peaks." (Identifying Dust Emission)
   - validation: Evaluated purity p = (N_pos - N_neg) / N_pos using negative images, reaching 95% purity at pSNR = 3.3, iSNR = 2.0.  
  > "These numbers were used to evaluate the “purity” ($p$) defined as $$p = \frac{N_{\rm pos} - N_{\rm neg}}{N_{\rm pos}}$$ to indicate the reliability of the detections with a certain set of iSNR and pSNR." (Identifying Dust Emission)
3. **Measure dust continuum flux densities and upper limits**: Fit 2D Gaussian components using CASA imfit in a 3x3 arcsec box around PyBDSF positions; test whether sources are resolved via Monte Carlo point-source injection simulations; adopt integrated flux for resolved sources (REBELS-25) and peak flux for unresolved sources; compute 3 sigma upper limits for non-detections from dirty image RMS multiplied by 3 and by a 1.33 flux boosting factor.  
  > "We employed the `imfit` task in `CASA` to fit one or more Gaussian components in the dust continuum images." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "the reported flux for REBELS-25 is the `imfit` integrated flux, but the `imfit` peak fluxes for the rest of the sources." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "To derive the $3\sigma$ upper limit, we used the RMS noise in each dirty image and scaled it up by a factor of three. Then we multiplied this by a flux boosting correction factor of 1.33 at $S/N=3$" (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements)
   - software: CASA, PyBDSF
   - parameter: Fitting region = 3 x 3 arcsec (1.5 x 1.8 arcsec for REBELS-40); 20,000 mock point sources (100 simulations per image x 5 sources); flux boosting factor = 1.33 at S/N = 3  
  > "We limited the fitting region to $3\ensuremath{''} \times 3\ensuremath{''}$ centred on the source position found by `PyBDSF`" (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "Except for REBELS-40 (Super8-1), where a smaller region ($1.5\ensuremath{''} \times 1.8\ensuremath{''}$) was used" (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "Then we multiplied this by a flux boosting correction factor of 1.33 at $S/N=3$ from the same set of simulations described above" (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fitting in CASA imfit with free peak intensity, peak position, major and minor axis, and position angle (simultaneous two-component fit for REBELS-12 and REBELS-19)  
  > "The fitting parameters were peak intensity, peak position, major and minor axis, and position angle. For REBELS-12 and REBELS-19, there are two sources detected by `PyBDSF` within our detection radius ($1.5\ensuremath{''}$, see §3.1). In these cases, we provided initial estimates of the fitting parameters to fit the two sources simultaneously." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements)
   - validation: Inspected residual images of each source to confirm adequate fits; used 20,000 mock point source simulations to establish 95% confidence threshold for resolved sources (S_int/S_peak > 1.25 at S/N ~ 8).  
  > "We investigated the residual image of each source to confirm the fits were adequate." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "At $S/N \sim 8$ ($S/N \sim 4$), the probability that a source is robustly resolved with a 95% confidence level requires a ratio of $S_{\rm int}/S_{\rm peak} > 1.25$ ($S_{\rm int}/S_{\rm peak} \sim 2$)" (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements)
4. **Apply CMB correction and calculate total infrared luminosities (L_IR) and SFRs**: Correct continuum fluxes for the CMB effect (~4-18%), then convert rest-frame 158 um or 88 um monochromatic luminosity to total L_IR (8-1000 um) using median conversion factors from Sommovigo et al. (2022), and compute SFR_UV and SFR_IR.  
  > "Finally, we considered the effect of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) following the prescription of (cite: daCu13b)." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "The conversion factor is derived in §3.3.1 in (cite: Somm22) as: $$L_{\rm IR} = 14(^{+8}_{-5}) \, \nu L_{\nu, 158\mu{\rm m}}.$$" (Total Infrared Luminosity); "For these cases, the conversion factor, $L_{\rm IR} = 8(^{+1}_{-4}) \, \nu L_{\nu, 88\mu{\rm m}}$, was derived from the same SED with the median $T_{\rm d}$ of the the sample that has both the rest-frame 158$\mu$m continuum and [C ii] detections." (Total Infrared Luminosity)
   - parameter: L_IR = 14 (+8/-5) nu L_nu,158um; L_IR = 8 (+1/-4) nu L_nu,88um; SFR_UV = 7.1e-29 L_nu; SFR_IR = 1.2e-10 L_IR  
  > "The conversion factor is derived in §3.3.1 in (cite: Somm22) as: $$L_{\rm IR} = 14(^{+8}_{-5}) \, \nu L_{\nu, 158\mu{\rm m}}.$$" (Total Infrared Luminosity); "For these cases, the conversion factor, $L_{\rm IR} = 8(^{+1}_{-4}) \, \nu L_{\nu, 88\mu{\rm m}}$, was derived from the same SED" (Total Infrared Luminosity); "We adopted a prescription of ${\rm SFR_{UV}} = 7.1\times10^{-29}L_\nu$ [$\rm M_\odot\,yr^{-1}\,(erg\,s^{-1}\,Hz^{-1})^{-1}$] and ${\rm SFR_{IR}} = 1.2 \times 10^{-10}L_{\rm IR}$ [$\rm M_\odot\,yr^{-1}\,L_\odot^{-1}$] (cite: Bouw21b)." (Galaxies with Dust Emission at z ~ 7)
   - assumption: Flat LambdaCDM cosmology with H = 70 km s^-1 Mpc^-1, Omega_M = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7; Chabrier (2003) IMF  
  > "We adopt a flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $H=70\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, $\Omega_{\rm M}=0.3$ and $\Omega_{\rm \Lambda}=0.7$. All magnitudes are expressed in the AB system (cite: Oke83). For star formation rates (SFRs) and stellar masses ($M_*$), we adopt a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: Chab03)." (Introduction)
   - assumption: Single-temperature modified blackbody dust SED with dust emissivity spectral index beta = 2.0 and median dust temperature T_d = 46 K

## Qualitative findings

- Almost all dust continuum detected sources have UV spectral slopes beta_UV > -1.8, tending to be redder than non-detections, though two-sample KS tests for M_UV, beta_UV, A_V, and M_* are not statistically significant (p-value > 0.05).  
  > "Almost all of the observed sources that are detected with dust continuum have $\beta_{\rm UV} > -1.8$, but there are two exceptions, REBELS-10 and 13." (Physical properties of the dust-detected galaxies at z ~ 7); "None of these relations are statistically significant ($p$-value $> 0.05$) with the two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov test to reject the null-hypothesis that the distributions of the dust continuum detected and undetected samples are identical." (Physical properties of the dust-detected galaxies at z ~ 7)
- 17 of the 18 dust-detected galaxies are classified as LIRGs (3-8 x 10^11 L_sun) and one (REBELS-25) is a ULIRG (1.5 x 10^12 L_sun).  
  > "We estimate that all of the sources have an infrared (IR) luminosity ($L_{\rm IR}$) in a range of $3-8 \times 10^{11}\,{\rm L_\odot}$, except for one with $L_{\rm IR} = 1.5^{+0.8}_{-0.5} \times 10^{12}\, \,{\rm L_{\odot}}$." (Abstract); "They are in the class of luminous infrared galaxies (LIRGs) with $10^{11} < L_{\rm IR}/{\rm L_{\odot}} < 10^{12}$, except for REBELS-25 which is instead classified as an ultra luminous infrared galaxy (ULIRG" (Infrared properties of the dust-detected galaxies at z ~ 7)
- Only REBELS-25 is spatially resolved in its single dust continuum component at ~1.2-1.6 arcsec resolution, while REBELS-12 and REBELS-19 are resolved into two unresolved dust components.  
  > "All of the $S_{\rm int}/S_{\rm peak}$ ratios of the REBELS sources indicate that they are unresolved with a 95% probability, except for REBELS-25 which is the brightest source in our sample. Note that both REBELS-12 and 19 are resolved into two components, but their individual components are not resolved." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements)
- No statistically significant correlation is observed between the rest-UV to far-IR spatial offset and L_IR, IRX, beta_UV, or the molecular index I_m.  
  > "However, with current limited spatial resolution imaging, no clear trend has been seen in $L_{\rm IR}$, IRX, $\beta_{\rm UV}$, and $I_m$ against the UV-IR separation." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Compact array configurations (C43-1 and C43-2) and spectral scans in Bands 5, 6, and 7 were chosen to maximise spectral scan efficiency and sensitivity for simultaneous line and continuum detection across 40 UV-luminous galaxies selected over ~7 deg^2; pilot visibilities were combined for 6 overlapping targets to maximise continuum sensitivity.  
  > "To maximise the spectral scan efficiency, for the targets with $z_{\rm phot} < 8.5$ we have observed the [C ii] line with Band 5 or 6. For the targets at higher redshift, the [O iii] line has been observed with Band 7." (The REBELS program); "We therefore include the visibility measurement sets from the pilot observations for those six sources when creating the dust continuum maps to maximise sensitivity." (The REBELS program)
- Why this method: CASA imfit was used for flux measurements after PyBDSF detection because an a priori specified number of Gaussians can be fitted for complex morphologies; 2D Gaussian fitting with fixed 1-pixel width was used for UV-dust offset peaks to avoid being biased to a clump or noise peak.  
  > "This is because there are some galaxies with complex morphology, for which `imfit` is preferred as an apriori specified number of Gaussians can be fitted." (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "We determine the peaks of the rest-UV and dust continuum emission by a 2D Gaussian fit to avoid being biased to a clump or accidentally picking up a noise peak." (Spatial Offset between rest-UV and IR)
- Alternative: Using lower-redshift star-forming galaxy SED templates (Bethermin et al. 2017; Schreiber et al. 2018) or a modified blackbody with beta = 1.6 and T_d = 54 K (Bouwens et al. 2020) for L_IR conversion instead of the Sommovigo et al. (2022) model.  
  > "For a simple comparison, if we instead adopted the SED templates constructed with star-forming galaxies at lower redshifts (cite: Beth17; Schr18a), we would obtain a slightly lower conversion factor ($\sim 11$) for $L_{\nu, 158\mu{\rm m}}$." (Total Infrared Luminosity); "Furthermore, if we used a modified black body SED with $\beta=1.6$ and $T_{\rm d}$$=54$ K, which assumed a dust temperature evolution extrapolated to $z=7$ (cite: Bouw20), then this conversion factor, $11 \pm 3$, would also be consistent within the uncertainty of the conversion factor used in the current work." (Total Infrared Luminosity)
- Alternative: Adopting non-parametric star formation histories in BEAGLE SED fitting instead of a constant SFH.  
  > "In the paper, we adopt the constant SFH to facilitate comparisons to earlier studies. Note that non-parametric treatments of SFH for galaxies during the epoch of reionisation could lead to a higher stellar mass. We refer to (cite: Topp22) for the adoption of non-parametric SFHs of the REBELS galaxies and comparisons to the constant SFH values." (Galaxies with Dust Emission at z ~ 7)
- How they decided it was enough: Adopted PyBDSF thresholds of (pSNR, iSNR) = (3.3, 2.0) where detection purity reaches 95% within r = 1.5 arcsec, and required 95% confidence from 20,000 Monte Carlo mock sources to classify a source as spatially resolved.  
  > "To maximise the number of reliable detections, we adopted the detection thresholds of (pSNR, iSNR) $= (3.3, 2.0)$, where the purity reaches $95\%$, for identifying the dust continuum of the REBELS targets." (Identifying Dust Emission); "At $S/N \sim 8$ ($S/N \sim 4$), the probability that a source is robustly resolved with a 95% confidence level requires a ratio of $S_{\rm int}/S_{\rm peak} > 1.25$ ($S_{\rm int}/S_{\rm peak} \sim 2$)" (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements)
- Limitation (authors): Observations for six REBELS targets (REBELS-04, 06, 11, 16, 24, and 37) were not yet completed at the time of analysis.  
  > "There are still six targets whose observations are to be completed (REBELS-04, 06, 11, 16, 24, and 37)." (The REBELS program)
- Limitation (authors): Single-band continuum measurements lack direct dust temperature constraints, and the assumed median T_d = 46 K SED may not apply to z >= 8 targets observed at rest-frame 88 um.  
  > "Because we currently do not have a robust dust temperature measurement," (Dust Continuum Flux Measurements); "This suggests that the dust properties of the [C ii] detected galaxies at $z \sim 7$ and the 88$\mu$m targets at $z \gtrsim 8$ may be different." (Total Infrared Luminosity)
- Limitation (authors): Limited rest-frame optical/NIR coverage leads to large uncertainties in stellar mass estimates.  
  > "The limited rest-frame optical/NIR coverage we have of targets results in large uncertainties on estimates of the stellar mass (cite: Topp22)." (Physical properties of the dust-detected galaxies at z ~ 7)
- Limitation (authors): Coarse spatial resolution (~1.2-1.6 arcsec, or ~6.3 kpc at z = 7) and astrometric uncertainties (~0.2-0.5 arcsec for ALMA at SNR 5-3) limit the ability to resolve dust morphology, confirm whether secondary dust components in REBELS-12 and 19 belong to the target galaxies, and confirm UV-IR spatial offsets.  
  > "Based on the beam size at $\sim1$mm, we find that the expected astrometric uncertainty of the ALMA observations is $\sim 0.2\ensuremath{''}$ and $\sim 0.5\ensuremath{''}$ at the signal-to-noise ratios (SNR) of 5 and 3, respectively, for our continuum detections." (Astrometry Correction of the NIR images); "For the rest of the unresolved REBELS galaxies, unfortunately the limited spatial resolution makes it hard to discuss their morphology in further detail." (Dust Morphology); "To confirm the speculated two different populations in single galaxies (or a merging system) during the epoch of reionisation, higher spatial resolution observations are needed." (Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Applying a single median dust temperature (T_d = 46 K) and a single L_IR conversion factor across the entire sample introduces substantial systematic uncertainty in L_IR and SFR_IR, especially for the 88 um targets at z > 7.7.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Many of the reported UV-dust spatial offsets (~0.1-0.4 arcsec) are comparable to the expected ALMA positional uncertainty (~0.2-0.5 arcsec at SNR 3-5) inside a 1.2-1.6 arcsec beam, making individual small offsets tentative.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): For REBELS-12 and REBELS-19, summing the flux of two spatially separated continuum components when the secondary component lacks a spectroscopic redshift or optical counterpart may overestimate the target galaxy's L_IR if the secondary component is a foreground interloper.
