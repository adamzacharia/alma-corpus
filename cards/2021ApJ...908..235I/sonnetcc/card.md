## Science

- Fields: agn_and_quasars, high_z_galaxies
- Question: Is the highest-redshift red quasar (z = 6.72) in a merger-driven to outflow-driven transition: what are its FIR continuum and extended [C II] properties, and is the extended [C II] structure companion/merging galaxies or a cold AGN-driven outflow?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Image the [C II] cube and FIR continuum**: tclean with natural weighting; first clean cube including continuum to 3 sigma to find the line; integrate line-free channels into continuum map; subtract continuum in the uv plane with a first-order polynomial; average channels to 100 km/s  
  > "All images were reconstructed with the `tclean` task using natural weighting to maximize the sensitivity." (ALMA Observations); "which was subtracted in the $uv$ plane by using the first-order polynomial before making the final line cube." (ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA 5.6, tclean
   - parameter: velocity resolution 100 km/s, channel rms 0.13 mJy/beam  
  > "We averaged several channels to obtain a velocity resolution of 100 km s$^{-1}$, which resulted in a 1$\sigma$ channel sensitivity of 0.13 mJy beam$^{-1}$" (ALMA Observations)
   - parameter: continuum map 1 sigma = 17.6 uJy/beam  
  > "(1$\sigma$ = 17.6 $\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$)" (ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure the continuum flux density and source size**: 2'' diameter circular aperture centred on the continuum peak for flux density; 2-D elliptical Gaussian fits with imfit for sizes  
  > "we measure the continuum flux density with a $2\ensuremath{''}$ diameter circular aperture placed at this peak position" (FIR continuum properties); "we fit a 2-dimensional elliptical Gaussian to it" (Footnote 2)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: aperture diameter 2'' at the continuum peak  
  > "we measure the continuum flux density with a $2\ensuremath{''}$ diameter circular aperture placed at this peak position" (FIR continuum properties)
   - fitting: 2-D elliptical Gaussian (imfit); no further fit settings stated  
  > "we fit a 2-dimensional elliptical Gaussian to it" (Footnote 2)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive L_FIR, L_TIR, SFR_TIR and dust mass**: optically-thin modified black body at fixed T_d and beta, with CMB correction; SFR from L_TIR calibration; dust mass from a mass absorption coefficient  
  > "by assuming an optically-thin modified black body with dust temperature $T_d = 47$ K and emissivity index $\beta = 1.6$" (FIR continuum properties)
   - parameter: T_d = 47 K, beta = 1.6; L_FIR over 42.5-122.5 um, L_TIR over 8-1000 um  
  > "We determine the FIR luminosity ($L_{\rm FIR}$; 42.5–122.5 $\ensuremath{\mu}m$) and the total IR luminosity ($L_{\rm TIR}$; 8–1000 $\ensuremath{\mu}m$)" (FIR continuum properties)
   - assumption: T_d = 35 K (local star-forming galaxies) would make luminosities ~2x smaller  
  > "If we adopt $T_d = 35$ K, typical for local star-forming galaxies (cite: 2012ApJS..203....9U), these values become $\sim 2\times$ smaller." (FIR continuum properties)
   - assumption: SFR_TIR is an upper limit since all FIR emission is assumed to come from star formation (AGN contribution ignored)  
  > "as we have assumed that the FIR emission is due entirely to star formation" (FIR continuum properties)
   - assumption: kappa_nu = 0.4 (nu/250 GHz)^beta cm^2 g^-1  
  > "by adopting a rest-frame mass absorption coefficient of $\kappa_\nu = 0.4~(\nu/250{\rm GHz})^\beta$ cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$ (cite: 2004A&A...425..109A)" (FIR continuum properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit the area-integrated [C II] spectrum**: single Gaussian then double Gaussian fit (core + wing) to the spectrum integrated within a 1.5'' radius of the nucleus where [C II] is detected above 3 sigma  
  > "area-integrated spectrum measured over the region within $1\ensuremath{''}.5$ radius of the nucleus" ([C II] line emission); "We first fit the spectrum with a single Gaussian function" ([C II] line emission)
   - parameter: chi2/d.o.f. computed over +-1200 km/s  
  > "(estimated over $\pm$1200 km s$^{-1}$ range)" ([C II] line emission)
   - assumption: core component attributed to the host galaxy and used for the systemic redshift  
  > "We naively attribute the core component in the double Gaussian fit to the host galaxy itself" ([C II] line emission)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits compared by chi2/d.o.f.; single 37.6/22, double 21.1/19; sampler/priors not stated  
  > "we found $\chi^2$/d.o.f. = 37.6/22" ([C II] line emission); "This works fairly well with a returned $\chi^2$/d.o.f. = 21.1/19." ([C II] line emission)
   - validation: double Gaussian preferred at >99.9% confidence from the chi2 difference  
  > "With this difference in $\chi^2$, this model is preferred at $>99.9\%$ confidence." ([C II] line emission)
5. **Derive [C II] luminosity, SFR and equivalent width**: [C II] SFR from the core L_[CII] with a literature calibration; rest-frame EW from the [C II] core flux and the 1.2 mm continuum flux density  
  > "giving $108 \pm 7~M_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$ with the (cite: 2011MNRAS.416.2712D) calibration, which has $\sim 0.3$ dex dispersion." ([C II] line emission)
   - assumption: SFR_[CII] is an upper limit because part of [C II] may come from the quasar narrow line region  
  > "Again, this would be an upper limit" ([C II] line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Characterize extended [C II] at positions A-C**: spectra at three off-centre positions (A, B, C) give line centre, FWHM, flux and L_[CII]; extended structure judged real from several contiguous velocity channels  
  > "The extended structure that is directly connected to the continuum-bright core region is real, as we see positive signals from several contiguous velocity channels measured there" ([C II] line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: extended emission seen in several contiguous channels over -500 to +500 km/s  
  > "The extended structure that is directly connected to the continuum-bright core region is real" ([C II] line emission)
7. **Compare L_FIR with L_Bol of z>~6 quasars**: L_Bol from 1450 A luminosity with a bolometric correction of 4.4; L_FIR derived with the same assumptions; linear fit of log L_FIR versus log L_Bol excluding upper limits; Spearman correlation  
  > "after excluding the objects with upper limits on $L_{\rm FIR}$" (Comparison to the other $z \gtrsim 6$ quasars); "A Spearman correlation coefficient for this $\log L_{\rm Bol}$–$\log L_{\rm FIR}$ data is 0.56 ($p$-value = $2.0 \times 10^{-4}$)." (Comparison to the other $z \gtrsim 6$ quasars)
   - parameter: bolometric correction factor 4.4  
  > "we adopted the bolometric correction factor 4.4 from 1450 Å luminosity" (Comparison to the other $z \gtrsim 6$ quasars)
   - assumption: J1205-0000 compared after its dust extinction is corrected  
  > "once its dust extinction is corrected" (Comparison to the other $z \gtrsim 6$ quasars)
   - fitting: linear fit log L_FIR = (0.64 +- 0.03) log L_Bol + (3.55 +- 0.45); fitting algorithm not stated  
  > "(0.64 \pm 0.03) \times \log \left( \frac{L_{\rm Bol}}{L_\odot} \right) + (3.55 \pm 0.45)." (Comparison to the other $z \gtrsim 6$ quasars)
   - validation: described as only a marginal correlation  
  > "Although it is still a marginal correlation" (Comparison to the other $z \gtrsim 6$ quasars)
8. **Estimate outflow mass, rate, kinetic power and momentum load for blobs A-C**: Mdot_out = M_out v_out / R_out with v_out = |dv| + FWHM/2; M_out from L_[CII] under the high-density limit (lower limit); kinetic power and momentum load from the standard formulae  
  > "We define the outflow rate as $\dot{M}_{\rm out} = M_{\rm out} v_{\rm out}/R_{\rm out}$" (Cold neutral outflows?); "For $v_{\rm out}$, we adopt $|\Delta v| + {\rm FWHM}/2$" (Cold neutral outflows?)
   - parameter: R_out = 3.5, 6.4, 5.1 kpc for blobs A-C  
  > "as 3.5, 6.4, and 5.1 kpc for the blobs A–C, respectively." (Cold neutral outflows?)
   - parameter: X_C+ = 1.4e-4, T_ex = 200 K, n_crit ~3e3 cm^-3  
  > "we adopt $X_{\rm C^+} = 1.4 \times 10^{-4}$ and $T_{\rm ex} = 200$ K" (Cold neutral outflows?); "the line critical density ($\sim 3 \times 10^3$ cm$^{-3}$)" (Cold neutral outflows?)
   - assumption: high density limit n >> n_crit, giving a lower limit on M_out  
  > "we here assume the high density limit ($n \gg n_{\rm crit}$) to provide a" (Cold neutral outflows?)
   - assumption: 70% of [C II] arises from PDRs (factor 0.7)  
  > "The factor 0.7 in the first parenthesis indicates a typical fraction of" (Cold neutral outflows?)
   - assumption: projection correction averages to unity over all angles; entire extended structure assumed to be outflow  
  > "the average of this factor over all angles is unity." (Cold neutral outflows?); "by assuming that the extended structure is formed fully due to cold outflows." (Cold neutral outflows?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: varying T_ex in [100, 1000] K changes M_out by only ~20%  
  > "varying $T_{\rm ex} \in [100, 1000]$ K changes the inferred $M_{\rm out}$ by only $\sim 20\%$." (Cold neutral outflows?)
9. **Constrain star formation in potential companions**: 3 sigma upper limit on SFR_IR at the extended [C II] regions from undetected FIR continuum, with T_d = 35 K; compare with HSC y-band depth  
  > "An inferred $3\sigma$ upper limit on SFR$_{\rm IR}$ by assuming a modest $T_d$ of 35 K (cite: 2012ApJS..203....9U) is only $\sim 10~M_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$." (Companion/merging galaxies?)
   - assumption: T_d = 35 K  
  > "by assuming a modest $T_d$ of 35 K" (Companion/merging galaxies?)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Red quasars at z>6 have not been studied in their host galaxies, and previous z>~6 studies are biased to blue quasars  
  > "In this regard, one intriguing population of quasars, whose host galaxies have not been studied at all at $z > 6$, are the so-called" (Introduction); "previous $z \gtrsim 6$ studies are biased toward the unobscured (= blue) quasar population." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Double Gaussian adopted because the single Gaussian deviates clearly from the observed profile  
  > "but the observed profile clearly deviates from this model" ([C II] line emission)
- Alternative: Single Gaussian fit to the area-integrated spectrum rejected in favour of a double Gaussian  
  > "but the observed profile clearly deviates from this model" ([C II] line emission)
- Alternative: Companion/merging galaxy and cold outflow scenarios both retained; cannot be distinguished with current data  
  > "it is not possible to distinguish these two scenarios with the current dataset." (Nature of the extended [C II] emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The nature of the extended structure cannot be identified with current data  
  > "Although it is not practical to identify the nature of this extended structure" (abstract)
- Limitation (authors): Outflow mass is a lower limit and rates measured at only three positions  
  > "As our computation provided a lower limit on $M_{\rm out}$, and we measured rates only at the three selected positions" (Cold neutral outflows?)
- Limitation (authors): Extended [C II] in negative velocity channels needs deeper observations to confirm  
  > "but deeper observations are required to confirm its existence" ([C II] line emission)
- Limitation (authors): SFR estimates are upper limits because AGN contamination is neglected  
  > "these would be regarded as upper limits as we neglect a contamination from this intrinsically luminous quasar itself." (Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single 71-minute execution at ~0.8'' resolution; no kinematic modelling, so merger versus outflow is untested beyond qualitative comparison.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): L_FIR, SFR and dust mass rest on a fixed T_d = 47 K and beta = 1.6 (factor ~2 sensitivity to T_d); no SED constraint from multiple bands.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow rates depend on projection (tan alpha averaged to 1), assumed T_ex and X_C+, and treat all extended emission as outflow.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Positions A-C and R_out were selected by eye from a figure; no error budget for that choice.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): L_Bol-L_FIR fit uses a heterogeneous literature sample with unspecified fit method; correlation described by authors as marginal.
