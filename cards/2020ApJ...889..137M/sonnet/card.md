## Analysis recipe

1. **Select BBG candidates by color**: SExtractor on [3.6] image, K-[3.6] > 1.75 and K-[3.6] > 2.4([3.6]-[4.5])+1.05, then remove detections in X-ray, FIR, radio and optical  
  > "K - [3.6] > 1.75, \label{eq:BBGcriteria1}\\ K - [3.6] > 2.4 ([3.6] - [4.5]) + 1.05." (3.4 Expected colors)
   - software: SExtractor, IRAF
   - parameter: Effective area 0.41 deg2; 23 color-selected, 6 remaining after multi-band selection  
  > "We identified 23 objects satisfying the BBG color criteria, which are referred to as the color-selected sample in the following sections." (4.1 NIR Color Selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Completeness via artificial sources  
  > "We estimated the completeness of our source extraction by detecting artificial sources randomly embedded in the [3.6]-band image." (4.1 NIR Color Selection)
2. **ALMA Band 7 continuum photometry to reject dusty contaminants**: CASA pipeline calibration, clean continuum, imfit with 2D Gaussians in 2 arcsec apertures at [3.6] positions; upper limits for point sources  
  > "Photometry for the 12 targets was performed on the dust continuum images using CASA task `imfit` that fits the observed data within $2\ensuremath{''}$ diameter apertures" (5 Follow-up ALMA Band 7 observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 3 of 6 candidates undetected  
  > "Three among the six BBG candidates (eight out of the 12 targets in total) were detected in the continuum images." (5 Follow-up ALMA Band 7 observations)
3. **SED fitting with galaxy, AGN and XzLBG templates**: chi2 minimization with upper-limit term, analytic scaling, 1000 MC runs (300 for AGN/XzLBG)  
  > "As a result of 1,000 MC runs, we found that the BBG models at $5 \lesssim z \lesssim 8$ are significantly favored for all of the three BBG candidates." (6.2.1 Fitting)
   - software: PANHIT
   - parameter: 15 bands from F814W to ALMA Band 7  
  > "The resultant number of the bands is 15: $F814W, g, r, i, z, y, Y, J, H, K_s, [3.6], [4.5], [5.8], [8.0]$, and ALMA Band 7." (6.2 BBG candidates without ALMA detection)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF, Calzetti law, zero escape fraction, forbidden region A_V > max(4 SFR^0.3, 3.5), tau_SFH >= 30 Myr  
  > "we define the forbidden region in the $A_V$–SFR plane as $A_V > \rm{max}(4 \times SFR^{0.3}, 3.5)$" (6.1 Fitting Method)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo perturbation of fluxes; MC-best distribution gives confidence intervals  
  > "The distribution of the best-fit solutions in these MC realizations defines the probability of the fitting solutions as well as the confidence intervals around the solutions." (6.1 Fitting Method)
   - validation: Tested SMC/MW laws and composite galaxy+TORUS templates  
  > "We confirmed that the above results do not significantly change if we change the dust attenuation law to the SMC or MW law." (6.2.1 Fitting)
4. **Stellar mass density and progenitor SFRD**: n_BBG corrected for completeness; SMD from best-fit masses; SFRD = <SFR> x n_prog using 10^6 combinations of MC-best models  
  > "We made $10^6$ combinations of three MC-best BBG models from the randomly selected 100 MC realizations for each object" (7.3 Cosmic star-formation activity)
   - parameter: T_BBG = T_age - T_SF - 0.2 Gyr, T_SF = 2 tau_SFH  
  > "Assuming the BBG observable time duration as $T_{\rm BBG} = T_{\rm age} - T_{\rm SF} - 0.2\,{\rm Gyr}$ with $T_{\rm SF} = 2 \times \tau_{\rm SFH}$" (7.3 Cosmic star-formation activity)
   - assumption: All three candidates are real BBGs at 4.8<=z<=7.8  
  > "Assuming all of the three BBG candidates without ALMA detections to be passive galaxies at $4.8 \leq z \leq 7.8$" (7.2 Stellar mass density)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-check with rho_* / <T_BBG>  
  > "$\rho_{\rm SFR} \approx (2.4 \times 10^4\,M_{\odot}\,{\rm Mpc}^{-3}) / (0.56\,{\rm Gyr}) = 4.3 \times 10^{-5}\,M_{\odot}\,{\rm yr}^{-1}\,{\rm Mpc}^{-3}$" (7.3 Cosmic star-formation activity)

## Qualitative findings

- The three ALMA-detected candidates are dusty ULIRGs at z>~4  
  > "We conclude that the three BBG candidates with ALMA detections are actually ultra luminous infrared galaxies with $L_{\rm IR} > 10^{12}\,L_{\odot}$" (Appendix 10)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA offers the depth to break the degeneracy between passive and dusty red galaxies  
  > "ALMA may be the only instrument that can offer critical data to resolve the degeneracy between passive and dusty red galaxies" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: z~0 type-2 TORUS AGN and z>~17 LBG solutions judged unlikely but not excluded  
  > "Such an AGN with a very low-mass BH hosted by a relatively low mass galaxy that contributes little to the whole SED (“low-mass naked” AGN) seems unlikely" (7.1.1 AGNs at z~0)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): SFRD is a lower limit; contamination by AGN not excluded; spectroscopic confirmation needed  
  > "direct evidence of the BBG, i.e., spectroscopic confirmation of the Balmer break by coming *James Webb Space Telescope* ($JWST$; (cite: Gardner+06)), is needed." (8 Conclusion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only three candidates, so number density and SFRD have large Poisson uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Photometric redshifts and T_SF are template dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Possible marginal HSC i/z detections of BBG_22 and BBG_29
