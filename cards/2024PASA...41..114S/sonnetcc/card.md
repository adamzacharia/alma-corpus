## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce HST WFC3 imaging and grism data and fit redshift and emission lines**: Process with grizli (association, flat-fielding, astrometric alignment to Gaia DR3, drizzling, contamination modelling) then fit 2D G102+G141 grism spectra  
  > "then use grizli to fit the 2D G102+G141 grism data." (New hst wfc3 Observations)
   - software: grizli, sep (Source Extractor wrapper)
   - parameter: direct-image drizzle pixel scale 0.06 x 0.06 arcsec  
  > "drizzling of the direct imaging (with $0.06\times 0.06\,$ arcsec pixels) and grism visits" (New hst wfc3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure host aperture photometry in HST, HSC and HAWKI K**: photutils aperture photometry, 0.7 arcsec radius, local background from 3-5 arcsec annulus, aperture corrections from the WFC3 handbook  
  > "with a $0.7\,$arcsec radius aperture and a local background determined from a $3-5\,$arcsec annuli centred on the grizli reported position." (New hst wfc3 Observations)
   - software: photutils, astropy
   - parameter: 5% systematic uncertainty added in quadrature to the measurement uncertainty  
  > "A $5\%$ systematic uncertainty was added to the measurement uncertainty" (New hst wfc3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: F098M and F105W photometry compared with S22  
  > "The F098M and F105W photometry is consistent within 10% of that reported in S22" (Imaging)
3. **Image the LBA data and measure component fluxes and separation**: CASA imaging with Briggs weighting, uv taper and multi-frequency synthesis; component flux densities fitted with a MIRIAD task  
  > "with Briggs weighting of $R=0.5$, a uvtaper of $5000\,$k$\lambda$ and the multi-frequency synthesis option" (lba Observations); "The flux densities were measured with the" (LBA Results)
   - software: CASA, MIRIAD, AIPS
   - parameter: Briggs R = 0.5, uv taper 5000 klambda  
  > "with Briggs weighting of $R=0.5$, a uvtaper of $5000\,$k$\lambda$" (lba Observations)
   - assumption: Flux scale bootstrapped assuming the LBA-scale calibrator flux is 90% of the ATCA value  
  > "whose flux on lba scales was assumed to be 90% of that measured in a near-simultaneous atca observation" (lba Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: flux-scale error estimate supported by calibrators with near-simultaneous ATCA observations  
  > "this error estimate is supported by flux densities derived for calibrators in the observation that also had near-simultaneous atca observations" (lba Observations)
4. **Fit the host galaxy SED for stellar mass, age, SFR**: BEAGLE with delayed exponential star formation history and Calzetti dust attenuation, broad priors  
  > "Within beagle we use a delayed exponential sf history, a (cite: Calzetti:97) dust attenuation, and broad priors." (SED)
   - software: BEAGLE
   - parameter: five free parameters: age, stellar mass, current SFR (100 Myr average), metallicity, extinction  
  > "we allow only the following five parameters to vary: timescale of sf" (SED)
   - assumption: nebular ionisation parameter and metallicity set equal to those of the stars; dust to metal mass ratio 0.3  
  > "The ionisation parameter and metallicity of the nebular regions is set equal to that of the stars." (SED); "The dust to metal mass ratio is left as default value of 0.3." (SED)
   - fitting: median of the PDF as best fit with 68% intervals; 95% upper value used as a 2 sigma limit for SFR  
  > "beagle provides PDFs from which we take the median value as the best fit and the 68% confidence intervals for the uncertainties." (SED)
   - validation: including WISE limits did not change the results  
  > "made no appreciable difference to the host galaxy properties nor their uncertainties." (SED)
5. **Fit the Sersic profile to the F140W image**: PetroFit Sersic fit of the F140W cutout, PSF-convolved, then R80 from R_eff and Sersic index  
  > "We use the F140W image to fit a (cite: Sersic:68) profile to the host galaxy using" (Morphology); "The Sérsic model was convolved with the F140W PSF" (Morphology)
   - software: PetroFit
   - parameter: constant rms image equal to the local off-source rms  
  > "the rms image was created at a constant value equal to that of the locally determined off-source rms" (Morphology)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Model jet power, age and gas mass with RAiSE**: Parameter inversion on a RAiSE grid of synthetic images and SEDs, compared with NVSS flux density and LBA jet extent by maximum likelihood  
  > "These synthetic images at 1.4 GHz are compared to the following observable values using a maximum likelihood approach: NVSS flux density, and the frequency-invariant extent of the jets from the LBA image." (Modelling the Radio Jet)
   - software: RAiSE
   - parameter: three free parameters: jet power, age and total gas mass; other parameters at RAiSE defaults  
  > "We assume the default values for other parameters in raise" (Modelling the Radio Jet); "we therefore constrain the total gas mass (the sum of the molecular, neutral and ionised gas, i.e. the sum of Baryonic matter minus the stars) as a third free parameter (in addition to the jet power and age)." (Modelling the Radio Jet)
   - parameter: jet spine bulk Lorentz factor 5  
  > "the bulk flow in the jet spine (Lorentz factor of $\gamma_\text{j} =5$)" (Modelling the Radio Jet)
   - assumption: ISM follows the Sersic distribution of the stars, with Monte Carlo realisations of R_eff and profile  
  > "In doing this we are assuming that the ISM has the same distribution in the galaxy as the stars." (Modelling the Radio Jet)
   - assumption: Lower frequencies excluded because RAiSE lacks free-free absorption  
  > "We do not include lower frequencies in the modelling as RAiSE currently does not include free-free absorption" (Modelling the Radio Jet)
   - fitting: maximum likelihood over the 3D grid, giving PDFs with a 'fast' and a 'slow' solution  
  > "These synthetic images at 1.4 GHz are compared to the following observable values using a maximum likelihood approach" (Modelling the Radio Jet)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Compare [NeV]/[NeIII] and [OII]/[NeIII] ratios with SDSS and shock models**: Compare line ratios with SDSS/BOSS galaxies, LLAGN models and MAPPINGS III radiative shock models  
  > "we compare the line ratios to those predicted from the MAPPINGS III library of fast radiative shock models" (Origin of Ionising Field)
   - software: MAPPINGS III models
   - parameter: SDSS selection: [OIII] and [NeIII] at >= 2.5 sigma, [NeII] at >= 2 sigma  
  > "We restrict this comparison to galaxies with \[OIII\] and \[NeIII\] detected at $\ge 2.5\sigma$ and \[NeII\] detected at $\ge 2\sigma$." (Origin of Ionising Field)
   - assumption: no reddening correction applied to line ratios  
  > "We applied no reddening correction as all three lines are close in wavelength and hence their ratios are minimally affected by dust absorption." (Origin of Ionising Field)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Constrain black hole mass and Eddington ratio from jet power and bolometric luminosity**: Blandford-Znajek analytic jet-power relations (thin disk and ADAF) combined with the [NeV]-based bolometric luminosity  
  > "We use analytical solutions to the (cite: Blandford:77), hereafter BZ, model to express the jet power as function of black hole mass" (Black Hole Mass and Eddington Accretion)
   - parameter: maximum Eddington accretion rate 0.03 in the ADAF case  
  > "we can very tentatively constrain the black hole mass assuming a maximum Eddington accretion of $\dot{m}\le 0.03$" (Black Hole Mass and Eddington Accretion)
   - assumption: [NeV] bolometric luminosity approximates the true value  
  > "Assuming that the \[NeV\] bolometric luminosity is an approximation of the true value" (Black Hole Mass and Eddington Accretion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: WISE upper limit on bolometric luminosity used as a cross-check  
  > "the upper limit from the *WISE* non-detection, $<1.7\times 10^{12}\,$L$_\odot$, is more robust and equally well supports the conclusions above" (Black Hole Mass and Eddington Accretion)
9. **Limit the CO(4-3) luminosity and molecular gas fraction from the ALMA spectral scan**: Non-detection of CO(4-3) at 86.4 GHz in the 85-115 GHz ALMA scan converted to L'CO and molecular gas mass  
  > "we infer a limit to the CO luminosity of $L'_{\rm CO}< 10^{10}\,$K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$." (Host Galaxy Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Earlier millimetre and near-infrared spectroscopy failed to fix the redshift, so new HST grism and LBA data were obtained  
  > "failed to conclusively determine its redshift, necessitating the current study." (Introduction)
- Why this method: HST orbits were paired in two orientations to avoid contamination of the host spectrum by the nearby source  
  > "chosen to avoid contamination of the host galaxy spectrum by the nearby source" (New hst wfc3 Observations)
- Alternative: z~8 (Lyman-alpha) photometric solution from S22 rejected in favour of z~3 (MgII) after the new grism data  
  > "found two photometric redshift solutions at $z\approx 3$ and $z\approx 8$ consistent with the faint $1.15\,\mu$m line being MgII or Lyman-$\alpha$, respectively." (Introduction)
- Alternative: A triple power-law radio spectrum with turnover below 70 MHz gave no better fit than a double power law  
  > "was found not to provide a better model fit, despite the evident curvature across this frequency range." (Radio Data)
- Alternative: Of the fast and slow RAiSE jet solutions, the slow one is preferred  
  > "Of the two solutions, we believe the ‘slow’ jet to be more likely." (Modelling the Radio Jet)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_

  > "The SFR is poorly constrained with the 68% confidence interval spanning three orders of magnitude." (SED)
