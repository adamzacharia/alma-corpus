## Analysis recipe

1. **Flag line channels and build continuum data**: Make ~8 MHz cubes per base band, flag channels with localized emission >=4 sigma, ignore them in imaging, combine all four SPWs into one continuum image  
  > "Then channels with localized emission $4\sigma$ or more above the continuum of the channel were flagged." (Data Reduction and Calibration); "All four SPWs were then combined to make a single, aggregate continuum image." (Data Reduction and Calibration)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: channel width ~8 MHz; 4 sigma flagging threshold  
  > "For flagging spectral lines, an image cube with a channel width of $\sim8$ MHz" (Data Reduction and Calibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Clean full-resolution 12m images and subtract the QSO**: tclean at full resolution with robust 0.5, manually masked sources cleaned to image RMS; subsequent analysis uses clean residual images with QSO and strong positive emission removed  
  > "All subsequent analysis was performed upon the clean residual images, which have effectively had the central QSO (and any strong, positive emission) subtracted." (Data Reduction and Calibration)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; pixel 0.4 arcsec; 60x60 arcsec field  
  > "We cleaned using a robustness of $0.5$." (Data Reduction and Calibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Make tapered images and handle high-SNR source**: Gaussian uv tapers to ~6 and ~3 arcsec; Q1228+3128 (307 sigma) self-calibrated but excluded as dynamic-range limited  
  > "Q1228+3128 was therefore excluded from the analyses." (Data Reduction and Calibration); "We applied Gaussian tapers to the (u,v)-data during imaging to improve surface brightness measurements of extended signals." (Data Reduction and Calibration)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: taper resolutions ~6 arcsec and ~3 arcsec  
  > "We made tapered images for each source at $\sim 6^{''}$ and $\sim 3^{''}$ resolutions." (Data Reduction and Calibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Self-calibration judged insufficient to reach muJy-level dynamic range  
  > "However, even with self-calibration we determined the observation to not have enough dynamic range to detect the" (Data Reduction and Calibration)
4. **Stack residual images**: Weight each residual image by its MAD, apply clean masks, sum in Jy/beam; produce 6 and 3 arcsec stacks of nine targets  
  > "We stacked the source subtracted ALMA images by first measuring the median absolute deviation (MAD) of each residual image and applying weighting to each residual image based on the MAD." (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal); "All the residuals were then stacked by summation in units of Jy/beam." (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal)
   - parameter: masked regions excluded from stack  
  > "masked regions were not included in the stack." (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Convert stacked flux to Compton y**: Interpret stacked continuum decrement as thermal SZE and convert to Compton parameter using the Sazonov & Sunyaev formulation  
  > "We interpret the stacked continuum flux observations as SZE signal by converting to Compton parameter" (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal)
   - assumption: pure thermal SZE; kSZE neglected  
  > "For stacked QSO environments we use a pure thermal SZE model to determine the Compton parameters." (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal); "Our stacking analysis will not be sensitive to the non-spherically symmetric kSZE." (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Build and simulate halo and feedback models**: Integrate gNFW pressure through a 300^3-cell cube (0.4 arcsec cells) for four halo masses and build spherical wind bubble models; run through CASA simpredict and image with the same parameters as the data  
  > "We treat the halo as a cube divided into cells; the cube is $300^3$ cells." (3.1 gNFW Models); "These simulated visibities are then imaged using the same parameters as the QSOs" (3.3 Simulating Observations)
   - software: CASA, simpredict
   - parameter: halo masses 1e12, 3e12, 1e13, 3e13 Msun; outflow ages 3e7 and 1e8 yr; wind powers 1e11 to 1e13 Lsun  
  > "for halo masses of $1 \times 10^{12}M_{\odot}$ , $3 \times 10^{12}M_{\odot}$, $1 \times 10^{13}M_{\odot}$, and $3 \times 10^{13}M_{\odot}$." (3.1 gNFW Models)
   - assumption: average redshift z = 2.34; wind bubble beta = 0.8828, bias b_Q ~ 13, overdensity 180  
  > "at our average redshift of $z=2.34$" (3.1 gNFW Models)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simulator reproduces the primary beam and sidelobe effects of the observations  
  > "These imaged models now have the same primary beam and side lobe effects as the observations." (3.3 Simulating Observations)
7. **Radial profile comparison with models**: Measure mean y and RMS in radial bins of the stacks (6 arcsec stack for gNFW, 3 arcsec stack for winds), excluding r < ~3 arcsec, and compare to simulated models via chi-square and p-value  
  > "We measured the mean Compton parameter and RMS in each bin of the stack." (3.4 Radial Profile Analysis); "The central pixels of the image (out to $r\sim 3^{''}$) were not utilized in the radial profile analysis as that region is mostly masked out." (3.4 Radial Profile Analysis)
   - parameter: gNFW: ~6.7 arcsec wide bins on the ~6 arcsec stack; wind: ~5.3 arcsec wide bins on the ~3 arcsec stack  
  > "The beam of the smoothed ALMA image is $\sim 6^{''}$ while we used $\sim 6.7^{''}$ wide bins." (3.4 Radial Profile Analysis); "We analyzed this stack in Compton-$y$ as a radial profile using bins with a width of $\sim 5.3^{''}$." (3.4 Radial Profile Analysis)
   - assumption: halo concentration of 1 in all gNFW models  
  > "All models use a concentration of 1." (Table 2)
   - fitting: chi-square goodness of fit and p-value per model (4 d.o.f. for halo, 5 d.o.f. for winds); no sampler or priors  
  > "We quantify the relationship between the stacked QSO observations and the simulated gNFW models by calculating the $\chi^2$ and p-value" (4.1 Halo Mass)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- No strongly peaked central decrement indicative of a gNFW profile or feedback wind bubble is seen in the stack  
  > "We do not see evidence of a strongly peaked central decrement that would be indicative of a gNFW profile or feedback wind bubble." (Conclusions)
- Strong jet-mode feedback may be occurring, suppressing the SZE from halo and thermal winds  
  > "thus jet feedback may be occurring in these objects." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Ultraluminous quasars with extended Lya nebulae allow a rough halo mass estimate; ALMA Band 4 gives sensitive low-surface-brightness data  
  > "which allow a rough estimate of the typical halo mass in the sample to be made based on the gas dynamics" (Introduction)
- Why this method: ALMA interferometry allows subtraction of discrete sources while stacking builds signal to noise  
  > "Using ALMA allows us to take advantage of the capability to subtract emission from discrete sources, both in the field and associated with the quasar" (Introduction)
- Alternative: Q1228+3128 excluded from stack after self-calibration failed to reach needed dynamic range  
  > "Q1228+3128 was therefore excluded from the analyses." (Data Reduction and Calibration)
- Alternative: ALMA 7m array data not used because of much higher noise  
  > "Observations of these sources were also made with the ALMA 7m array but at a much higher noise level, so we do not utilize them in our analysis here." (Observations)
- How they decided it was enough: Best-fit chi-square similar to zero signal, so no further fitting  
  > "Further fitting of halo and feedback models is therefore not merited by the data available in this survey." (Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): Halo mass estimate from Lya gas dynamics is uncertain  
  > "uncertain due to radiative transfer effects and possible non-gravitational motions in the Ly" (Introduction)
- Limitation (authors): tSZE cannot be separated from kSZE without deep observations at other frequencies  
  > "It is also not possible to separate the tSZE from any kSZE contribution without further very deep observations at other frequencies." (Introduction)
- Limitation (authors): Stack insensitive to non-spherically symmetric kSZE  
  > "Our stacking analysis will not be sensitive to the non-spherically symmetric kSZE." (2.2 Stacked SZE Signal)
- Limitation (authors): Best-fitting model chi-square similar to zero signal  
  > "The $\chi^2$ of our best fitting models is similar to zero signal." (Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Manual masking of sources and removal of the QSO via clean may bias residual y at small radii and the central bins are dropped
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Radial bins are wider than the beam so neighboring bins are partly correlated, yet chi-square treats them as independent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The reported peak y (1.7 +/- 1.1)e-6 is about 1.5 sigma, so the 'measurement' is not a detection
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Quoted 90% confidence from a p-value of 0.09 is a loose threshold
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model comparison depends on assumed concentration of 1 and fixed outflow ages
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust emission from the QSO hosts could contaminate the Band 4 decrement and is not modeled
