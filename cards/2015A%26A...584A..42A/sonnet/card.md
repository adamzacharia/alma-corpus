2. **Measure line fluxes and source sizes**: 2D Gaussian fits to integrated intensity maps with IMFIT  
  > "Calculated from two-dimensional Gaussian fits to the integrated intensity emission using the AIPS task IMFIT which deconvolves the clean beam from the fitted component size." (Table 2)
   - software: AIPS IMFIT
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian  
  > "Calculated from two-dimensional Gaussian fits to the integrated intensity emission using the AIPS task IMFIT which deconvolves the clean beam from the fitted component size." (Table 1 footnote (Results))
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Test self-absorption via HCN-VIB/HCN ratio and other lines**: Channel-by-channel R_vib; for I_u<I_l require R_vib<0.5; compare with CH2NH, H2S, HOC+  
  > "Thus, for $I_u < I_l$ to hold, the *HCN-VIB/HCN line intensity ratio, ${\cal R}_{\rm vib}$,* should be $<$0.5." (5.1.1 Evidence from the HCN-VIB line)
   - assumption: 1e line as bright as 1f for thermal excitation; HCN and HCN-VIB from same gas  
  > "Note, that here we are assuming that all of the HCN emission is emerging from the same gas that produces the HCN-VIB line." (5.1.1 Evidence from the HCN-VIB line)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Estimate lower-limit 14 micron luminosity and surface brightness**: Blackbody sphere L=4 pi r^2 sigma T^4 with r=theta/2 and T_B(14um)=100 K  
  > "We assume a nuclear spherical "cloud" of dust for which we adopt a simple blackbody where $L$=4$\pi r^2 \sigma T^4$" (5.3 HCN-VIB lines as probes)
   - assumption: T_B(14um)=100 K lower limit  
  > "The high filling factor of hot, optically thick gas and dust suggests that we can use the minimum 14$\mu$m brightness temperature of 100 K to estimate a *lower limit* to the mid-infared luminosity." (Discussion, HCN-VIB lines as probes of extreme mid-IR surface brightness and luminosity)

## Qualitative findings

- HCN and HCO+ lines are double-peaked, self- and continuum-absorbed; HCN-VIB is more compact and peaks at systemic velocity  
  > "For all four galaxies, the HCN-VIB emission is significantly more compact (by factors of at least 2-3) than that of HCN and HCO$^+$" (4.2)

## Claim tuples

- H: HCN-VIB emission is detectable and compact in LIRG/ULIRG nuclei | E: PdBI and ALMA imaging of four galaxies | R: HCN-VIB detected in all four, FWHM <37-136 pc upper limits | F: Buried compact mid-IR-bright nuclei | **supported**
- H: HCN and HCO+ double peaks are caused by self/continuum absorption | E: R_vib, comparison with CH2NH, H2S, HOC+ | R: R_vib>1 in IC860 and Arp220W; other lines single-peaked | F: HCN and HCO+ fail to trace nuclei at r<100 pc | **supported**
- H: HCN-VIB strength relates to outflow strength | E: Compare L(HCN-VIB)/L(FIR) with outflow properties across eight galaxies | R: fast-outflow galaxies show fainter HCN-VIB | F: HCN-VIB may peak before onset of strong feedback (tentative) | **partially**
