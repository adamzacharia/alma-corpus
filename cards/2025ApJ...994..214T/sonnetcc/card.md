## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the ALMA data**: ALMA pipeline calibration in CASA; pipeline continuum images used; line cubes resampled to 0.4 km/s with cvel2 and imaged with tclean (Briggs, robust 0.5 for most sources, multiscale and robust 0.0-1.0 for extended sources)  
  > "All data calibration was performed by the ALMA pipeline in CASA (cite: CASA_2022; McMullin2007_casa)." (Observing Details and Data Products); "We image the resampled data using `tclean` with Briggs weighting, a robust value of 0.5, and no multiscale clean for most sources." (Observing Details and Data Products)
   - software: CASA, cvel2, tclean
   - parameter: velocity resolution 0.4 km/s  
  > "we resample the line cubes to 0.4 km s$^{-1}$ velocity resolution using the `cvel2` task." (Observing Details and Data Products)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect lines and continuum and characterise morphology**: CO detection = >5 sigma in at least two consecutive channels; continuum detection at 3 sigma; 2D Gaussian imfit to moment 0 and continuum images; compare continuum and CO position angles to test for outflow contamination  
  > "For the CO line cubes, we define “detection” to mean emission $>$5$\sigma_{i}$ in at least two consecutive channels, where $\sigma_{i}$ is the rms in channel $i$." (Observing Details and Data Products); "We use the `imfit` task in CASA to fit 2-dimensional Gaussians to the $^{12}$CO moment 0 maps, and to the 870 $\mu$m continuum data." (Disks vs Bipolar Outflows)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: continuum detection threshold 3 sigma (~0.30-0.50 mJy/beam)  
  > "with a detection limit of 3$\sigma$ ($\sim$0.30-0.50 mJy beam$^{-1}$ in most cases)" (Observing Details and Data Products)
   - assumption: ~90 degree misalignment of continuum and CO major axes is the outflow signature  
  > "The canonical morphological indicator of an outflow is a $\sim$90$^{\circ}$ misalignment between the observed disk major axes in continuum and line emission." (Disks vs Bipolar Outflows)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Position angles agree within uncertainties for the six resolved sources, taken as evidence that CO traces disk gas  
  > "The position angles of the continuum and moment-0 line emission agree within uncertainties for all six sources." (Disks vs Bipolar Outflows)
3. **Derive stellar luminosities from SED fits**: Fit optical-infrared photometry to BT-Settl models over a grid in A_V and theta=R*/d with Gaia distances; L* from integrated best-fit SED  
  > "We fit our OIR photometry data to the BT Settl models over our own grid in A$_V$ and $\theta$" (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting); "L$_{\star}$ is derived by integrating the best-fit model SED over frequency space and then multiplying by 4$\pi$R$_{\star}^2$." (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting)
   - parameter: log(g) 3.0-4.5; A_V 0 to 4.5 in steps of 0.1  
  > "We allow log(*g*) to vary between 3.0 and 4.5" (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting); "The allowed A$_V$ values range from 0 to 4.5 in steps of 0.1" (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting)
   - assumption: Solar metallicity, no alpha enhancement; extinction law of Wang & Chen 2019; extra 10% flux uncertainty for variability  
  > "We assume Solar metallicity values with no $\alpha$ enhancement." (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting); "an additional 10% uncertainty to account for potential variability in these low-mass pre-main sequence stars" (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting)
   - fitting: chi^2 minimisation; 1-sigma from Delta chi^2 = 3.52675 for three parameters  
  > "We determine the best-fit model by minimizing $\chi^2$." (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting); "For a 3-parameter fit, the interval corresponding to a 1$\sigma$ uncertainty is $\Delta\chi^2$ $=$ 3.52675." (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting)
   - validation: Down-weighting 2MASS data for IR-excess sources gave the same L* within uncertainties  
  > "We found that the downweighted fits produced the same L$_{\star}$ values as the standard fits within uncertainties." (Additional Figures for SED and pdspy Fitting)
4. **Fit Keplerian disk models to the CO visibilities with MCMC**: pdspy MCMC fit in visibility space of an exponentially tapered, hydrostatic, vertically isothermal flared disk (15 free parameters) with RADMC-3D models, galario for Fourier transform and emcee sampler  
  > "We use the open-source, python-based `pdspy` package to perform Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) fitting of disk models to our data in visibility space ($u,v$)." (Disk Model Fitting); "We use `radmc3d` (cite: Dullemond2012_radmc3d) to generate the disk models, `galario` (cite: Tazzari2018_galario) to transform the model data from image space to Fourier space, and the `emcee` package (cite: Foreman-Mackey2013_emcee) for the MCMC fitting." (Disk Model Fitting)
   - software: pdspy, radmc3d, galario, emcee
   - parameter: 200 walkers; minimum burn-in 1000 steps; convergence evaluated from 2500 steps  
  > "In this work, we use 200 walkers for all fits." (Fitting Procedure); "We use a minimum burn-in interval of 1000 steps" (Fitting Procedure); "At 2500 steps, we begin evaluating the log(M$_{\star}$) parameter for convergence." (Fitting Procedure)
   - parameter: Position offsets restricted to initial imfit guess +-0.25 arcsec; V_LSR +-1.2 km/s (+-2.4 km/s for low S/N)  
  > "We restrict the parameter range to this initial guess $\pm$0.25 arcsec" (V$_{LSR}$, RA$_{off}$, and Dec$_{off}$); "we expand the parameter range to V$_{LSR}$$\pm$2.4 km s$^{-1}$ ($\pm$6 channels)" (V$_{LSR}$, RA$_{off}$, and Dec$_{off}$)
   - parameter: Temperature exponent q range expanded from [0,1] to [0,2] for sources piling up at q = 1  
  > "we expand the parameter range to $q$ $=$ \[0,2\]" (Modifying Parameter Ranges and Restarting Fits)
   - assumption: Fixed CO/H2 = 1e-4 and 13CO/H2 = 10^-5.3; mean molecular weight 2.37; hydrostatic equilibrium; vertically isothermal disk  
  > "We adopt a CO/H$_2$ abundance of 10$^{-4}$ (cite: Allen1973; Encrenaz1975; Dickman1978)." (The Model); "$\mu$ = 2.37 is the mean molecular weight of the gas assuming solar metallicity" (The Model); "The disk flaring and scale-height parameters are calculated assuming hydrostatic equilibrium." (The Model)
   - assumption: Pure Keplerian rotation (with pressure effects through the flared model); the CO traces disk gas, not outflows  
  > "These findings together are consistent with the interpretation that the CO is tracing disk gas and not outflows." (Disks vs Bipolar Outflows)
   - fitting: emcee, 200 walkers, uniform priors except distance (Gaussian in parallax) and stellar mass (walkers seeded from a Chabrier IMF); chi^2 likelihood against the visibilities; fits stopped when log(M*) converged  
  > "We assume uniform priors for all parameters except source distance and stellar mass." (Fitting Procedure); "For distance, we use a Gaussian distribution in parallax, where the distribution is centered on the *Gaia* parallax value and has a width of 2$\log$(2)$\times\sigma_{Gaia}$." (Fitting Procedure); "For stellar mass, the initial distribution of walkers is set using the Charbrier IMF." (Fitting Procedure)
   - validation: Convergence when the slope of mean and median log(M*) over the last 1000 steps is <1% of the value, after a chi^2 cut keeping at least 101 walkers; trace plots inspected; 12CO, 13CO and joint fits compared  
  > "If the magnitude of the slope over the last 1000 steps is less than 1% of the value of the parameter itself, we consider the slope to be converged." (Stopping Criteria); "We require that both median and mean be converged in order to stop the fitting." (Stopping Criteria); "our results for the $^{12}$CO, $^{13}$CO, and $^{12}$CO$+^{13}$CO fits all agree within uncertainties" (Results)
5. **Choose best-fit values and uncertainties from the chains**: Report the minimum-chi^2 fit with uncertainties from the min/max parameter values within Delta chi^2 = 15.975 over the last 1000 steps, instead of median and standard deviation  
  > "We also determine the fit with the lowest $\chi^2$ value, and extract the parameter values used for that fit." (Comparing Methods of Deriving Parameter Values and Uncertainties); "We calculate the lower- and upper-bound uncertainties as the minimum and maximum parameter values across all fits with $\chi^2$ $\leq$ $\chi^2_{min}$ $+$ $\Delta \chi^2$ within the last 1000 steps." (Comparing Methods of Deriving Parameter Values and Uncertainties)
   - software: pdspy
   - parameter: Delta chi^2 = 15.975 (1 sigma, 15 parameters)  
  > "For a model with 15 free parameters, a value of $\Delta\chi^2$ $=$ 15.975 corresponds to a 1$\sigma$ uncertainty (cite: NumericalRecipesBook)." (Comparing Methods of Deriving Parameter Values and Uncertainties)
   - fitting: J15562477-2225552 reported as an upper limit from the 99.73% point of the CDF of walker values over the last 1000 steps  
  > "For this source, we report the mass as an upper limit: M$_{\star}$ $\leq$ 1.45M$_{\odot}$." (J15562477-2225552)
   - validation: Median and minimum-chi^2 values agree within uncertainties; standard-deviation errors are 2-3x smaller than Delta chi^2 errors  
  > "We find that the minimum $\chi^{2}$- and median-derived parameter values typically agree within uncertainties" (Comparing Methods of Deriving Parameter Values and Uncertainties)
6. **Derive isochronal masses from five evolutionary model sets**: Draw normal distributions in L* and Teff, interpolate through each track set with pdspy.stars, and take the median and scaled MAD of the mass distribution  
  > "To derive isochronal masses, we generate normal distributions in luminosity and temperature for each source." (Stellar Evolutionary Models Considered); "In the following analysis, we use the median and scaled MAD of each distribution as mass and uncertainty, respectively, for each model." (Stellar Evolutionary Models Considered)
   - software: pdspy.stars
   - parameter: Model sets: BHAC15, PARSEC v1.1, PARSEC v1.2S, Feiden (2016) non-magnetic and magnetic  
  > "five sets of evolutionary models: the BHAC15 tracks of" (Stellar Evolutionary Models Considered)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Compare dynamical and isochronal masses**: Orthogonal distance regression of M_iso vs M_dyn, mass-ratio statistics, Spearman correlation of ratio with M_dyn, and two-sample KS tests, for the full sample and for M_dyn <= 1 Msun  
  > "We fit a line to the data in each panel using the `scipy.odr` package, which performs Orthogonal Distance Regression (ODR) fitting." (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses); "In order to more fully quantify the similarity of the isochrone-derived and dynamical masses, we employ the 2-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) test." (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses)
   - software: scipy.odr
   - parameter: Significance threshold p < 6.3e-5 (4 sigma) for Spearman correlation  
  > "We consider a correlation to be significant if it has a p-value less than 6.3$\times$10$^{-5}$ (corresponding to $\geq$ 4$\sigma$ significance assuming normally-distributed data)." (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses)
   - parameter: Mass-limited sample M_dyn <= 1.0 Msun (excludes five sources)  
  > "once for the full sample, and once for only those sources with M$_{dyn}$ $\leq$ 1.0 M$_{\odot}$" (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses)
   - fitting: ODR line fit accounting for uncertainties in both axes  
  > "The ODR method accounts for uncertainties in both the dependent (M$_{iso}$) and independent (M$_{dyn}$) variables." (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses)
   - validation: Results robust to full vs mass-limited sample and to excluding binaries  
  > "We find that, in practice, our analysis is not meaningfully impacted by using the mass-limited sample versus the full sample." (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses)
8. **Derive disk dust masses and the Mdust-Mstar relation**: Optically thin dust mass from the 870 um flux density, Gaia distance, opacity and dust temperature; ODR fit of log(Mdisk) vs log(Mstar) for dynamical and BHAC15 masses  
  > "where $S_{\nu}$ is our measured continuum flux density at 870 $\mu$m, $d$ is the *Gaia*-derived distance to each source, $\kappa_{\nu}$ is the dust opacity, and $T_d$ is the dust temperature." (M$_{disk}$ vs. M$_{\star}$); "We derive a log(M$_{disk}$)$-$log(M$_{\star}$) relation using Orthogonal Distance Regression (ODR) fitting for both our dynamical masses and the BHAC15 M$_{\star}$ values." (M$_{disk}$ vs. M$_{\star}$)
   - software: CASA imfit, scipy.odr
   - parameter: kappa = 2.7 cm^2/g at 345 GHz; T_dust = 20 K or 25 K (L*/Lsun)^0.25  
  > "We assume a dust opacity of $\kappa_{\nu}$ $=$ 2.7 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$ at 345 GHz" (M$_{disk}$ vs. M$_{\star}$); "We derive these masses under two different assumptions for dust temperature: T$_{dust}$ $=$ 20 K, and T$_{dust}$ $=$ 25 K $\times$ (L$_{\star}$/L$_{\odot}$)$^{0.25}$" (M$_{disk}$ vs. M$_{\star}$)
   - assumption: Optically thin dust emission at 870 um with the stated opacity and the two T_dust prescriptions (gas-to-dust ratio not applied; dust masses only)

## Qualitative findings

- 12CO emission was detected for all 25 targets and 13CO for 6 of 25; continuum for 24 of 25  
  > "We detect $^{12}$CO emission associated with all 25 targets, and $^{13}$CO emission associated with 6/25 targets." (Observing Details and Data Products)
- Magnetic Feiden (2016) tracks agree with dynamical masses for M <= 1 Msun; BHAC15, PARSEC v1.1 and non-magnetic Feiden underestimate by >~25%  
  > "The magnetic models of (cite: Feiden2016) are in very good agreement with our dynamical results for M$_{\star}$ $\leq$1 M$_{\odot}$." (Summary)
- Magnetic models do not overestimate masses for the lowest-mass sources, in contrast to Braun et al. 2021  
  > "We find no indication that the magnetic models of (cite: Feiden2016) have a tendency to overestimate mass for the lowest-mass sources" (Summary)
- Mdisk-Mstar relation is steeper than linear for all mass and temperature choices; slope with dynamical masses is slightly shallower than with BHAC15 but within uncertainties  
  > "Regardless of the method used, we derive a relationship between log(M$_{disk}$) and log(M$_{\star}$) that is steeper than linear within the uncertainties of the ODR fit." (M$_{disk}$ vs. M$_{\star}$)
- J16035767-2031055, J16142029-1906481 and J16075796-2040087 return masses at odds with the literature, possibly from winds or outflows not in the model  
  > "The most likely explanation for these sources is that the exponentially-tapered flared disk model is not capturing some additional phenomenon traced in the CO data, such as disk winds and/or outflows." (J16035767-2031055 and J16142029-1906481)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Follow-up Cycle 7 observations of the CO-detected Upper Sco targets of spectral type K5 or later, at the resolution and sensitivity needed to detect faint CO disks  
  > "We conducted follow-up observations in ALMA Cycle 7 of the CO-detected (cite: Barenfeld2016) targets with spectral type K5 or later." (The Sample)
- Why this method: Fit models directly to the visibilities with a flexible flared disk, following Sheehan et al. 2019, rather than assuming a thin uniform disk  
  > "We use the open-source, python-based `pdspy` package to perform Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) fitting of disk models to our data in visibility space ($u,v$)." (Disk Model Fitting); "Our approach includes dynamical modeling of both spatial and spectral components of the gas lines and does not assume a uniform, thin disk structure" (Comparison with Literature Results)
- Alternative: Median and standard deviation of walker positions as best-fit and error rejected because the standard-deviation errors are 2-3 times smaller than Delta chi^2 errors  
  > "In this work, we report the parameter values from the minimum-$\chi^2$ fit, and determine upper- and lower-bound uncertainties using $\Delta\chi^2$." (Comparing Methods of Deriving Parameter Values and Uncertainties)
- Alternative: CO tracing disk winds or outflows instead of rotation considered and found unsupported for the six resolved sources  
  > "While we cannot rule out dust-gas disk misalignment for the remaining 18 sources, we find no evidence for this in our sample" (Disks vs Bipolar Outflows)
- Alternative: Starspots as an alternative explanation for isochronal mass offsets noted but not separable from global magnetic inhibition  
  > "Sufficient data do not yet exist to robustly distinguish between the starspot and global-magnetic inhibition possibilities." (The Potential Impact of Starspots)
- How they decided it was enough: Fits stopped when log(M*) mean and median slopes over the last 1000 steps were below 1% of the value (minimum 2500 steps)  
  > "If the magnitude of the slope over the last 1000 steps is less than 1% of the value of the parameter itself, we consider the slope to be converged." (Stopping Criteria)
- Limitation (authors): No usable 12CO fit for J16113134-1838259 B and no line fit for J16154416-1921171  
  > "We were unable to fit the $^{12}$CO data for J16113134-1838259 B and any of the line data for J16154416-1921171." (Results)
- Limitation (authors): Inclination is bimodal for nearly all sources; clockwise vs counterclockwise rotation is not distinguished  
  > "This indicates that we are not differentiating between clockwise and counterclockwise rotation for any targets." (Results)
- Limitation (authors): Model may miss winds, outflows or internal structure for some sources, leading to anomalously high masses  
  > "The most likely explanation for these sources is that the exponentially-tapered flared disk model is not capturing some additional phenomenon traced in the CO data, such as disk winds and/or outflows." (J16035767-2031055 and J16142029-1906481)
- Limitation (authors): Sparse coverage above 1 Msun and many known or candidate binaries among the high-mass sources  
  > "However, we also have very sparse data coverage above M$_{dyn}$ $=$ 1 M$_{\odot}$" (Comparing Dynamical and Isochronal Masses)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text and tables disagree on some numbers: maximum mass uncertainty is +2.0/-4.0 in Results but +4.0/-2.0 in Table 4 and Summary; minimum mass errors are +0.05/-0.07 vs +0.07/-0.05; the J15562477 upper limit is 1.45 Msun in Appendix 8 but <=1.9 in Table 4
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed CO/H2 = 1e-4 with no CO depletion or freeze-out and the vertically isothermal assumption can bias M* and the geometry; the effect is not tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Masses of 4.0 (+4.0/-2.0) and 1.32 Msun for two unresolved sources conflict with Teff and L*, so the pdspy model may fail for some disks while being used in the sample statistics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 1 Msun mass cut and the exclusion of J16154416-1921171 and J16113134-1838259 A are post hoc choices; sample is CO-selected and small (23)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Isochronal masses depend on Teff from spectral types (+-1 subclass) and L* from a new SED fit; starspots and binarity could shift the isochronal side
- Caveat (inferred, ours): KS tests and Spearman p-values assume normally distributed data and are applied to n of about 18-23
