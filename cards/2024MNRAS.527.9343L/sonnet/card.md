## Analysis recipe

1. **Select maser candidate sample**: Apply criteria on SoI angular radius, HST dust disc regularity and declination to maser parent sample  
  > "SMBH SoI angular radius $\theta_{\rm SoI} \equiv R_{\rm SoI} / D >0.01\ensuremath{''}$, where the SMBH SoI physical radius $R_{\rm SoI} \equiv{\rm G}M_\mathrm{BH}/\sigma_\star^2$" (2.1 Candidate selection)
   - assumption: M_BH ∝ D when rescaling masses to updated distances  
  > "$M_{\rm BH}\propto D$ for all dynamical mass measurements, where $D$ is the galaxy distance" (2.1 Candidate selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Calibrate and image ALMA CO(2-1) cubes and continuum**: Pipeline calibration; uvcontsub; tclean multiscale with interactive masks; primary beam correction  
  > "The datacubes were then cleaned to a depth of $1.5$ times the root-mean-square (RMS) noise of the line-free channels using the task `tclean` with the `MultiScale` algorithm" (3.1 ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 2.0, 10 km/s channels  
  > "we binned the channels to $10$ km s$^{-1}$ and used Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of $2.0$" (3.1 ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Varying imaging parameters does not significantly change any result  
  > "We note that varying these parameters does not significantly change any result of this paper." (3.1 ALMA observations)
3. **Make moment maps, spectra and PVDs**: Smoothed-cube mask (1.5 RMS threshold), hole/island filling, applied to unsmoothed PB-corrected cube; PVD with 2" mock slit along kinematic major axis  
  > "Each PVD was generated by adopting a $2\ensuremath{''}$-wide mock slit along the kinematic major axis" (3.1 ALMA observations)
   - software: scikit-image, pafit, CARTA
   - parameter: Fill holes < 2 beams; remove islands < 1 beam  
  > "(i) filling ‘holes’ of unselected pixels with areas smaller than two synthesised beams and (ii) removing ‘islands’ of selected pixels with areas smaller than one synthesised beam" (3.1 ALMA observations)
   - assumption: No covariance between adjacent channels  
  > "we safely assume no covariance between adjacent channels." (Footnote 9)
   - fitting: pafit fit to first-moment map for kinematic PA (visual for NGC 3393)  
  > "determined from a fit to the first-moment map using the `pafit` software" (3.1 ALMA observations)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive integrated fluxes and molecular gas masses**: Sum masked spectra; uncertainty from RMS of masked sums over line-free channels; mass from CO(2-1) flux with R21=1 and alpha_CO=4.3  
  > "assuming a CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) line ratio $R_{21}$ of unity (in brightness temperature units) and a CO-to-molecule conversion factor $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}=4.3$" (3.2 JCMT observations)
   - assumption: R21 = 1; alpha_CO = 4.3 Msun pc-2 (K km/s)-1 including heavy elements; adopted distances  
  > "A CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) line ratio of unity (in brightness temperature units) and a CO-to-molecule conversion factor of $4.3$ M$_{\odot}$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$ are assumed" (Table 3 footnote)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with JCMT spectra  
  > "our JCMT CO(2-1) spectrum (integrated flux density $186\pm16$ Jy km s$^{-1}$, corresponding to an integrated molecular gas mass of $(6.0\pm0.5)\times10^{9}$ M$_\odot$) is in good agreement" (3.5 NGC 5765B molecular gas)
5. **Measure 230-GHz continuum sources and spectral indices**: 2D Gaussian fits with imfit; power-law SED fit with VLA fluxes for NGC 3393  
  > "again derived using 2D Gaussian fits carried out with the `CASA` task `imfit`" (3.4 NGC 3393 continuum)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: S ∝ nu^alpha power law  
  > "we fit power laws of the form $S\propto\nu^\alpha$ to the spectral energy distributions" (3.4 NGC 3393 continuum)
   - validation: Resolution not matched to VLA and no 15 sigma cut; possible bias noted  
  > "We note however that we have neither matched our resolution to that of the VLA observations nor applied a $15$ $\sigma$ cut" (3.4 NGC 3393 continuum)
6. **Reduce JCMT spectra and compare with ALMA**: ORAC-DR in Starlink, rebin to 10 km/s, linear baseline, S=15.6 T_A*/eta_A with eta_A=0.52  
  > "The spectra were then converted to a flux density scale $S_\mathrm{v}=15.6\,T_\mathrm{A}^\star/\eta_\mathrm{A}$" (3.2 JCMT observations)
   - software: Starlink, ORAC-DR
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Estimate observing time needed for SMBH mass measurement**: Take central peak pixel, assume homogeneous gas, target beam ~ theta_SoI, S/N=5 per beam in 10 km/s channels, use ALMA OT  
  > "Requiring a signal-to-noise ratio $S/N=5$ per synthesised beam and $10$ km s$^{-1}$ channels, we estimate using the ALMA Observing Tool (OT)" (4 Potential for SMBH Mass Measurements)
   - software: ALMA Observing Tool
   - assumption: Homogeneous molecular gas within the central beam  
  > "assume a homogeneous molecular gas distribution within it" (4 Potential for SMBH Mass Measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- All three galaxies show disturbed or non-axisymmetric molecular gas; of nine maser galaxies with resolved CO, all but NGC 2273 show irregularities  
  > "almost all have morphological irregularities and/or kinematic disturbances and/or inflows/outflows, the only exception being NGC 2273" (5 Links between disc properties and maser emission)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Maser SMBH masses are the most accurate, so cross-checking with CO dynamics is desirable  
  > "Given its increasing popularity, cross-checking CO and maser (as well as other methods) SMBH dynamical measurements is highly desirable." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Circinus rejected for further study because its CO velocity field is already known to be disturbed  
  > "This galaxy is therefore not suited to cold gas dynamical modelling to derive a SMBH mass, and it is not considered further in this paper." (2.1 Candidate selection)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Fluxes and masses for NGC 1194 and NGC 3393 are lower limits due to FoV; uncertainties exclude systematics  
  > "likely do not cover the full molecular gas extent of NGC 1194 and NGC 3393." (Table 3 footnote)
- Limitation (authors): Spectral indices may be biased since resolution not matched to VLA  
  > "These could lead to a bias in the spectral indices estimated." (3.4 NGC 3393 continuum)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample of three galaxies; maser-disturbance correlation is anecdotal and has no control sample
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Observing-time estimates rely on a homogeneous-gas assumption and peak-pixel brightness
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass uncertainties exclude alpha_CO, R21 and distance systematics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): JCMT pointing offsets make NGC 3393 JCMT comparisons unreliable
