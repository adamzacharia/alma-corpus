## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and homogenize cubes**: clean with Briggs weighting, same parameters for both sets, convolved to common beam  
  > "We set the same `clean` parameters for the analysis of both datasets to create uniform images over all the frequency ranges." (Observations and Analyses)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; 5.0 km/s resolution; beam 0.45"x0.3"  
  > "With the velocity resolution of 5.0 km s$^{-1}$" (Observations and Analyses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make continuum map and identify clumps**: Average line-free-channel continuum maps from four frequency ranges, weighted by spectral width; identify eight peaks  
  > "Then we take an average weighted according to the widths of the included spectral ranges in the four continuum maps" (Observations and Analyses)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Continuum map has ~30% uncertainty versus spectrum-based continuum  
  > "the continuum map created on the image may contain $\sim 30$% uncertainty compared to the continuum determined on the spectra" (Observations and Analyses)
3. **Extract continuum-subtracted spectra and identify lines**: Per-clump continuum from mean of line-free channels; lines identified by eye with Splatalogue (CDMS, JPL) and Galactic line surveys  
  > "we conduct the best identification by eye that could be done with the current data." (3.2 Drastic difference of spectral features)
   - software: Splatalogue
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure line integrated intensities**: Integrate spatially over the beam at continuum peaks and spectrally over per-clump velocity ranges; no Gaussian fitting  
  > "We do not fit the lines with Gaussian profiles, since their unsymmetrical profiles and the existence of a lot of their neighboring lines prevent the precise Gaussian fitting." (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - assumption: Lines treated as point sources at continuum peaks  
  > "we spatially integrate the lines over the beam as point sources at the continuum peak positions" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Errors are rms only from S/N  
  > "the rms errors of the integrated intensities shown in Table [\[tab1\]](#tab1) arise only from S/N of the lines" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
5. **Derive dust masses**: M_dust = S_nu D^2 / (kappa_nu B_nu(T_dust))  
  > "M_{\mathrm{dust}} = \frac{S_\nu D^2}{\kappa_\nu B_\nu(T_{\mathrm{dust}})}" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - parameter: nu=353 GHz, D=3.5 Mpc, kappa=0.0865 m2/kg  
  > "$\nu = 353$ GHz (weighted averaged value of the whole frequency range analyzed), $D = 3.5$ Mpc" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - assumption: T_dust = 25 K; all continuum is dust (free-free up to 20%)  
  > "The contribution could account for up to 20% of 353 GHz continuum flux density" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Mass changes by factor 20 for T_dust 10-100 K; relative comparison more reliable  
  > "$M_{\mathrm{dust}}$ decreases by a factor of 20 when $T_{\mathrm{dust}}$ varies from 10 K to 100 K" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
6. **Count O5V stars from H26alpha**: Convert H26a flux to Hbeta (Hummer & Storey), then Lyman continuum photons (Case B), divide by N_L per O5V star  
  > "The numbers of O5V stars in individual clumps are obtained by dividing the Lyman continuum photon fluxes by the Lyman continuum photon flux per single O5V star ($N_L$)." (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - parameter: log N_L = 49.26  
  > "We adopt $\log{N_L} = 49.26$ given by (cite: Martins+05)." (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - assumption: Single O5V population, all photons absorbed, ne=1e3, Te=1e4 K, Case B  
  > "We assume all the ionizing photons arise from a single stellar population (O5V)" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Counts drop to one-third with log N_L = 49.71  
  > "The number of O5V stars decrease to one-third of the values shown in Table [\[tab1\]](#tab1) if we adopt the theoretical value $\log{N_L} = 49.71$" (3.3 Line profiles and emission parameters)
7. **Line-to-continuum ratios and rotation diagram**: Ratio of line integrated intensity to continuum integrated over same velocity range; rotation diagram of three SO2 transitions in Clumps 1, 2, 6  
  > "We detect three transitions of SO$_2$ in Clumps 1, 2, and 6" (4.2 The localization of hot molecular gases)
   - assumption: Three transitions share similar velocity profiles and source sizes  
  > "All of the three transitions have similar velocity profiles and source sizes." (4.2 The localization of hot molecular gases)
   - fitting: Rotation diagram (Blake+87) linear fit  
  > "we perform the rotation diagram analysis (cite: Blake+87) for SO$_2$ to estimate molecular gas temperature." (4.2 The localization of hot molecular gases and chemically rich environment)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Eight dusty ~10 pc clumps are in two parallel ridges; spectra vary drastically between clumps; Clump 1 is line-confusion limited with 36+ lines of 19 molecules.  
  > "their line spectra vary drastically from clump to clump although they are separated by only $\sim 10$ pc" (abstract)
