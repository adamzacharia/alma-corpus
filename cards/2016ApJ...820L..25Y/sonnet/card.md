## Science

- Fields: protoplanetary_disks, star_formation
- Question: Do the millimeter continuum gaps in the HL Tau disk have counterparts in the HCO+ (1-0) gas, and what do the gas gap widths and depths imply for their origin?  
  > "we report the radial intensity profiles and reconstructed intensity map of the HCO$^+$ (1–0) emission in the disk around HL Tau at a high angular resolution of $\sim$0.07$''$, revealing the counterparts of the continuum gaps in the gas component." (1 Introduction)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Image HCO+ and CO cubes**: Fourier transform of calibrated visibilities in CASA, no CLEAN, HCO+ continuum-subtracted  
  > "Our HCO$^+$ and CO image cubes were generated by Fourier-transforming their calibrated visibility data" (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust=-1, 0.4 km/s, no taper  
  > "with the Briggs robustness parameter of $-1$ and without tapering" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Azimuthally averaged radial HCO+ intensity profile**: Deprojected with i=47 deg, PA=138 deg; bins of half beam (~0.035''); pixels within ±45 deg of major axis; integrate 2-12 km/s excluding absorption and ±1 sigma channels  
  > "only the pixels within $\pm$45$^\circ$ of the major axis were included in azimuthal averaging" (3.1 HCO+ (1-0) Intensity Profile)
   - parameter: uncertainty = sqrt(N_int) x sigma x dv  
  > "The uncertainty of the integrated intensity at each radial bin was estimated as $\sqrt{N_{\rm int}} \times \sigma \times dv$" (3.1 HCO+ (1-0) Intensity Profile)
   - assumption: axisymmetric disk for map reconstruction  
  > "assuming the intensity distribution is axisymmetric" (3.1 HCO+ (1-0) Intensity Profile)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: gap hints seen on both sides of the disk  
  > "the hints of the gaps at $\sim$30 AU and $\sim$70 AU are seen on the both sides of the disk" (3.1 HCO+ (1-0) Intensity Profile)
3. **Derive continuum optical depth and HCO+ column density**: T from CO brightness temperature without continuum subtraction; tau_3mm from radiative transfer; correct HCO+ for continuum optical depth; column density from Mangum & Shirley formula  
  > "The temperature profile ($T$) is adopted to be the radial profile of the brightness temperature of the CO emission without continuum subtraction" (3.2 HCO+ Column Density Profile)
   - assumption: dust and gas thermally coupled with the same temperature; HCO+ thermalized; CO+continuum optically thick  
  > "we assume that the dust grains and the gas are thermally well coupled and have the same temperature, and that the HCO$^+$ gas is thermalized" (3.2 HCO+ Column Density Profile)
   - fitting: uncertainties propagated through equations  
  > "its 1$\sigma$ uncertainty was estimated by propagating the uncertainties in $T$ and $T_{\rm b,3mm}$" (3.2 HCO+ Column Density Profile)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit gap widths and depths**: Power law fit near each gap, subtract, Gaussian fit to gap for FWHM and central column density; contrast vs power law  
  > "we first fitted a power-law function to the column densities only at the radial bins near the gap" (3.3 HCO+ Gaps)
   - parameter: 10,000 Monte Carlo iterations  
  > "We repeated this fitting process for a 10,000 times" (3.3 HCO+ Gaps)
   - assumption: HCO+ abundance constant across gaps  
  > "Assuming the HCO$^+$ abundance does not vary across the gaps" (3.3 HCO+ Gaps)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo resampling of radii and densities; Gaussian fits to parameter distributions give best-fit and 1 sigma  
  > "the centers and standard deviations of the fitted Gaussian functions were adopted as the best-fit values and the 1$\sigma$ uncertainties" (3.3 HCO+ Gaps)
   - validation: 10,000 iterations sufficient to converge  
  > "the 10,000 iterations is sufficient to converge" (3.3 HCO+ Gaps)
5. **Estimate planet masses from gap depth**: Kanagawa15 gap formula using derived temperature and depths  
  > "lead to planet masses of $\sim$0.8 $M_J$ at $\sim$28 AU and $\sim$2.1 $M_J$ at $\sim$69 AU" (4 Discussion)
   - parameter: alpha = 0.001  
  > "assuming the viscosity $\alpha$ of 0.001" (4 Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- The central HCO+ cavity is likely due to high optical depth of the 3 mm continuum, not gas depletion.  
  > "the presence of the central cavity is likely due to the high optical depth of the 3 mm continuum emission and not the depletion of the HCO$^+$ gas." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Azimuthal averaging to raise S/N because no clear detection above 3 sigma in the cube  
  > "we performed azimuthal averaging on the image cube to enhance the S/N ratio" (3.1 HCO+ (1-0) Intensity Profile)
- Alternative: CO snow line origin of the outer gap is possible but needs detailed calculations; less likely for the inner gap.  
  > "Thus, this scenario is less possible to interpret the presence of the inner HCO$^+$ gap." (4 Discussion)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Column density biased by poorly constrained dust and gas temperature profiles.  
  > "the overall HCO$^+$ column density profile remains biased by the not as well constrained radial temperature profiles of the dust and the gas." (3.1 HCO+ (1-0) Intensity Profile)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas temperature from CO brightness temperature may differ from dust midplane temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Absorption by foreground cloud affects the integrated intensity
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gap widths are comparable to the beam, so uncertainties are large
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No cleaning of low-S/N cubes
