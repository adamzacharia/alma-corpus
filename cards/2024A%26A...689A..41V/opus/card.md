## Analysis recipe

1. **Remove compact source / line contamination**: Remove line-contaminated half spw; fit point sources (Dirac delta, first-order polynomial spectrum) with Band 3+4; point source search with uv-cut 11 kλ  
  > "Unresolved, compact sources are modeled using point-like (Dirac delta) emission models with a first-order polynomial to describe their spectral behavior." (Interloper and cluster member removal); "we ran a point source search with our forward modeling routine using both the ALMA+ACA Band 3 and ALMA Band 4 observations with a lower *uv*-cut of 11 k$\lambda$ to exclude extended emission." (Mitigating point-source contamination in the ACA and ALMA observations)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: lower uv-cut 11 kλ  
  > "with a lower *uv*-cut of 11 k$\lambda$ to exclude extended emission." (Mitigating point-source contamination in the ACA and ALMA observations)
   - fitting: Point source with RA, Dec, Amplitude, spectral slope and calibration parameters; uniform/Gaussian priors (Table priors)  
  > "namely: RA, Dec, Amplitude, spectral slope, and the three calibration uncertainty parameters of the observations." (Mitigating point-source contamination in the ACA and ALMA observations)
   - validation: No other significant detection in posterior; BCG point source rejected at 4.1σ  
  > "Through the Bayes factor, we can reject the presence of a point source at the location of the BCG in the ALMA+ACA data by $4.1\sigma$." (The theoretical formalism)
2. **Single-component pressure-profile fit to ALMA+ACA visibilities**: Forward model gNFW (theoretical) or A10-type (empirical) pressure profile, integrate to Compton y, convert to surface brightness, Fourier transform and interpolate to uv points; Gaussian likelihood on unbinned visibilities per pointing with primary beam  
  > "We then apply the ALMA and ACA transfer function to the model to account for the missing baselines and compute the likelihood of the resulting model on the unbinned visibilities assuming Gaussian properties for noise statistics" (Methodology)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: Shape parameters frozen to A10/M14 classes for empirical runs; unfrozen progressively for gNFW runs  
  > "we unfreeze the shape parameters one by one, thus systematically increasing the prior volume" (The theoretical formalism)
   - assumption: Non-relativistic SZ (relativistic corrections neglected)  
  > "we have neglected the temperature-dependent relativistic corrections of the thermal SZ in this work." (The thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect)
   - assumption: Planck cosmology, 1″ = 7.855 kpc  
  > "Here $1\ensuremath{''} = 7.855$ kpc for XLSSC 122 ($z=1.98$)." (Introduction)
   - fitting: Static nested sampling (dynesty); flat priors except Gaussian calibration and redshift priors; stop at Δln B = 0.01; Bayes factor vs null model  
  > "The implementation uses the static nested sampling method implemented in the `dynesty` package" (Visibility-based modeling of the ICM); "Sampling of the posterior distribution continues until the log difference of the Bayesian evidence is less than an arbitrary threshold, in our case set to $\Delta\ln\mathcal{B}=0.01$." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - validation: Residuals in visibility plane; uv-radial binned comparison  
  > "As the residuals indicate a good agreement between the observations and the modeled surface brightness, the base assumption that the bulk of the ICM is composed of a single pressure profile thus holds for this $z\sim2$ cluster." (The theoretical formalism)
3. **Joint ALMA+ACA+ACT fit**: Add ACT 100/150 GHz map likelihood (Fourier-space, empirical signal+noise covariance) linearly to interferometric log-likelihood  
  > "We treat the ACT and ACA+ALMA observations as independent when computing the final posterior distribution of the model parameters. This is done by adding the log-likelihoods linearly." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: covariances from 8 adjacent sky patches  
  > "Both the signal and noise covariances are estimated from $8$ patches of the sky adjacent to the field containing XLSSC 122." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - assumption: No covariance between ACT and ALMA+ACA; stationary ACT covariances  
  > "Hence, we assume no covariance between the ACA+ALMA observations and the ACT maps." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - fitting: Nested sampling, Bayes factor converted to σ via σ = sgn(ΔlnZ)√(2|ΔlnZ|)  
  > "In this work, we normalize the Bayesian evidence by the evidence of a null model to compute the Bayes factor $\mathcal{Z}$." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - validation: Unphysical runs (e>0.9, β at prior edge) discarded  
  > "Hence, these models are not reported in Table [\[table:sigle_ACA_ALMA_ACT\]](#table:sigle_ACA_ALMA_ACT)." (ALMA+ACA+ACT joint likelihood modeling)
4. **Model-based image reconstruction and two-component fits**: Subtract best gNFW model from visibilities, add imaged residuals to likelihood-weighted model smoothed to 5″; then fit two gNFW components (A10-UPP shapes) with ordered declination prior  
  > "we add the imaged residuals (shown on the right panel of Fig. [1](#fig:best_image)) to the likelihood-weighted model, which is smoothed with the synthesized beam ($5\ensuremath{''}$) and attenuated by the primary beam" (“Cleaned” image reconstruction); "we used an ordered prior on the declinations of the centroids of the two SZ components which enforces that one model component has a declination that is always higher than the other." (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
   - software: dynesty
   - fitting: Nested sampling; comparison of evidence with single-component models  
  > "The latter translates to a Bayesian evidence difference of 6.6 with respect to the best single elliptical gNFW model, indicating a tentative $3.6\sigma$ detection of the second component" (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
   - validation: Marginalized model compared with cleaned image contours  
  > "By marginalizing over the posterior distribution, we get a smoother model which closely resembles the “cleaned” image" (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
5. **Mass estimates (SZ, X-ray, dynamical)**: Evidence-weighted average of empirical ALMA+ACA+ACT masses; X-ray M-T relation; velocity dispersion via biweight scale and Munari scaling, converted with colossus  
  > "Our SZ mass is calculated by taking the evidence-weighted average of the mass estimates from the empirical formalism runs utilizing ALMA+ACA+ACT observations" (Mass estimates); "In total, we use the spectroscopic redshifts of 32 cluster members that fall within $r_{200,\mathrm{c}}$." (Mass estimates)
   - software: colossus
   - parameter: Diemer & Joyce concentration for M200→M500  
  > "To convert the halo mass to the $M_{500,\mathrm{c}}$ definition we use the concentration parameter definition of (cite: 2019ApJ...871..168D)" (Mass estimates)
   - assumption: Local A10 Y–M scaling  
  > "as robust scalings derived from high$-z$ clusters do not exist." (Mass estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **SZ/X-ray pseudo-temperature map**: Ratio of SZ flux to square root of X-ray surface brightness at ≈5″ resolution  
  > "we estimated temperature differences in XLSSC 122 by taking the ratio of the SZ flux over the square root of the X-ray surface brightness." (Thermodynamical implications)
   - assumption: constant temperature along the line of sight  
  > "(under the assumption of constant temperature along the line of sight)" (Thermodynamical implications)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Flattened inner pressure profile; classified as non-cool-core vs local or relaxed vs distant clusters  
  > "we find that XLSSC 122 can be classified as a non cool-core when compared to local observations or as relaxed when compared to distant clusters." (Morphological implications)
- Excess SZ flux in the south without X-ray emission; pseudo-temperature ~3x higher in the south  
  > "indicates that XLSSC 122 is far from isothermal, with a pseudo temperature increase of $\sim3\times$ in the south, indicative of a disturbance." (Thermodynamical implications)
- SZ mass lower than dynamical mass by ~2.5x  
  > "We find lower SZ-derived mass estimates than what is expected from the velocity-dispersion measurement ($\sim\times2.5$)." (Mass estimates)
- Better SZ/X-ray agreement than CARMA results  
  > "In contrast to the previous works, we find better agreement between the SZ effect signal and the X-ray emission as well as the cluster member distribution." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ACT adds total-flux / large-scale constraints missing from interferometric data; Band 4 constrains dust contamination  
  > "By adding single-dish ACT observations, we get an additional constraint on the total flux of XLSSC 122." (ALMA+ACA+ACT joint likelihood modeling); "to get better spectral constraints on the contaminating emission from dusty galaxies." (The Atacama Large Millimeter/Submillimeter Array)
- Why this method: uv-plane modeling avoids deconvolution issues; clean assumptions invalid for SZ  
  > "Modeling in the *uv*-plane avoids issues related to the deconvolution of interferometric data" (Visibility-based modeling of the ICM)
- Alternative: BCG continuum emission as cause of flat γ – tested and rejected  
  > "We find that the amplitude of any point source at the location of the BCG is consistent with noise." (The theoretical formalism)
- Alternative: Chandra data rejected for diffuse ICM study due to low counts
