## Analysis recipe

1. **Forward-model the SZ decrement in ALMA+ACA visibilities with a parametric gNFW pressure profile**: Parametric pressure model mapped on 3D grid, integrated to Compton y, converted to surface brightness per band, Fourier transformed, ALMA/ACA transfer function applied, Gaussian likelihood on unbinned visibilities; each mosaic pointing modeled individually with primary-beam correction  
  > "We Fourier transform the surface brightness map to the visibility space, which is the native space of the ALMA and ACA data." (Methodology); "we model each pointing individually and correct for the primary beam attenuation per field" (Visibility-based modeling of the ICM)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: gNFW shape with P0, r_s, eccentricity, position angle, centroid, alpha, beta, gamma; redshift Gaussian 1.977-1.979; ALMA and ACA flux-scale parameters marginalized  
  > "we mainly use flat priors except for the calibration and redshift uncertainty parameters which we marginalize over" (Visibility-based modeling of the ICM)
   - assumption: Non-relativistic thermal SZ; temperature-dependent relativistic corrections neglected (electron temperature below 5 keV)  
  > "we have neglected the temperature-dependent relativistic corrections of the thermal SZ in this work" (The thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect)
   - assumption: Shape parameters frozen to A10 or M14 averages in the empirical-formalism runs  
  > "When we use the formalism of (cite: Arnaud2010), we will freeze the shape parameters" (Parametric descriptions of the ICM)
   - fitting: Static nested sampling (dynesty) with flat priors (Table 1), Gaussian likelihood; stopping when change in ln evidence is below 0.01; Bayes factor vs null model converted to sigma  
  > "Sampling of the posterior distribution continues until the log difference of the Bayesian evidence is less than an arbitrary threshold, in our case set to" (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations); "we normalize the Bayesian evidence by the evidence of a null model to compute the Bayes factor" (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - validation: Residuals between data and model in the visibility plane checked; single-component assumption judged adequate  
  > "As the residuals indicate a good agreement between the observations and the modeled surface brightness" (Single component ICM modeling)
2. **Remove compact-source contamination (cluster-member lines and background point sources)**: Compact sources modeled as Dirac-delta point sources with first-order polynomial spectrum jointly with the SZ model; bright-line windows trimmed; a joint point-source search run with a lower uv-cut  
  > "Unresolved, compact sources are modeled using point-like (Dirac delta) emission models with a first-order polynomial to describe their spectral behavior." (Interloper and cluster member removal); "we ran a point source search with our forward modeling routine using both the ALMA+ACA Band 3 and ALMA Band 4 observations" (Interloper and cluster member removal)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: Band 4 data used to model a 21 sigma point source at the bright-line location, subtracted before SZ modeling  
  > "we find a $21\sigma$ point source continuum detection in the ALMA Band 4 observations" (Mitigating point-source contamination)
   - assumption: Point source at the BCG location tested by freezing a point component at the HST-based position  
  > "we reran the best-fitting gNFW model" (Single component ICM modeling)
   - fitting: Same nested sampling; point-source amplitude and spectral slope fitted  
  > "We modeled (including the Band 4 data) both the amplitude and spectral slope of the central galaxy." (Single component ICM modeling)
   - validation: BCG point source rejected by the Bayes factor at 4.1 sigma; gNFW parameters unchanged  
  > "Through the Bayes factor, we can reject the presence of a point source at the location of the BCG in the ALMA+ACA data by" (Single component ICM modeling)
3. **Add ACT large-scale constraint via joint likelihood with ALMA+ACA**: Same pressure model propagated to ACT 100 and 150 GHz maps; log-likelihoods of ALMA+ACA and ACT summed (no cross-covariance assumed); ACT covariance from interleaved splits and sky patches  
  > "combine them by adding the log-likelihoods linearly" (Methodology); "Hence, we assume no covariance between the ACA+ALMA observations and the ACT maps." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: ACT point-source sensitivity integrated over beam: 0.5 mJy (100 GHz) and 0.8 mJy (150 GHz)  
  > "with a point-source sensitivity integrated over the beam of 0.5 mJy and 0.8 mJy, respectively" (The Atacama Cosmology Telescope)
   - assumption: Signal and noise covariances stationary, so ACT likelihood computed in Fourier space with diagonal covariance  
  > "We assume that both the signal and noise covariances are stationary" (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - fitting: Joint nested sampling over ALMA+ACA and ACT likelihoods; evidence-based model comparison  
  > "We treat the ACT and ACA+ALMA observations as independent when computing the final posterior distribution of the model parameters." (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
   - validation: Eccentricity and mass compared across runs with and without ACT; centroid stated as unaffected by ACT  
  > "the centroid of the SZ effect is unaffected by the ACT-observation and driven by the ALMA+ACA observations" (ALMA+ACA+ACT joint likelihood modeling)
4. **Fit empirical (A10 and M14 class) pressure templates and derive halo mass and classification**: Shape parameters fixed to A10 or M14 class averages (UPP, MD, CC); centroid, eccentricity, position angle and log M500c free; mass linked to amplitude and scale radius  
  > "we freed the centroid, eccentricity, position angle, and mass of the system" (The empirical formalism)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: log M500c prior log-uniform 10^13.4 to 10^14.6 Msun  
  > "Log-Uniform" (Priors)
   - assumption: Self-similar P500 scaling and c500-M500 relation from Arnaud2010 with local REXCESS-based M500-YX  
  > "through the self-similarity principle, the local $M_{500,\mathrm{c}}-Y_{\rm X}$ relationship based on the REXCESS sample" (Parametric descriptions of the ICM)
   - fitting: Nested sampling with flat priors on centroid, eccentricity and PA; evidence used to rank A10-UPP, A10-MD, A10-CC, M14-UPP, M14-MD, M14-CC  
  > "we can say that XLSSC 122 is similar to local morphologically disturbed clusters (A10-MD)" (ALMA+ACA+ACT joint likelihood modeling)
   - validation: Classification distinguished only at about 3 sigma (Delta ln Z >= 4.5); A10-CC disfavored  
  > "we can distinguish them from the other pressure profile templates" (Conclusions)
5. **Test for a second (infalling group or filament) pressure component and produce a clean-like reconstruction**: Custom reconstruction: best gNFW model from forward modeling subtracted from visibilities; residual image added back to likelihood-weighted model; two-component gNFW fits with ordered declination prior on centroids, spherical and elliptical, with and without ACT  
  > "we ran a two-component model to confirm via" (Two component ICM modeling); "we used an ordered prior on the declinations of the centroids of the two SZ components" (Two component ICM modeling)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: Shape parameters frozen to A10-UPP values; theoretical formalism used for two-component models  
  > "The shape parameters of the gNFW are frozen to the A10-UPP values" (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
   - assumption: Secondary component modeled as a highly eccentric gNFW as a proxy for the filament; eccentricity of about 0.8 for the smaller component  
  > "imply an eccentricity for the smaller component of" (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
   - fitting: Nested sampling; marginalized likelihood-weighted average of samples used for the two-component models  
  > "we weighted each sample of the nested sampling routine with its likelihood and computed the weighted average" (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
   - validation: Evidence difference vs single-component models (6.6 for ALMA+ACA elliptical; 3.0 with ACT)  
  > "The latter translates to a Bayesian evidence difference of 6.6 with respect to the best single elliptical gNFW model" (Modeling asymmetric pressure distributions)
6. **Derive halo masses from SZ, X-ray and dynamics and compare**: SZ mass = evidence-weighted average of empirical ALMA+ACA+ACT runs, Y500 converted with Arnaud2010 local scalings; X-ray mass from Mantz2018 radial profile with f_gas 0.125 and TX-M500 relation; dynamical mass from velocity dispersion of 32 members within r200c  
  > "Our SZ mass is calculated by taking the evidence-weighted average of the mass estimates from the empirical formalism runs utilizing ALMA+ACA+ACT observations" (Mass estimates); "we use the spectroscopic redshifts of 32 cluster members that fall within" (Mass estimates)
   - software: colossus
   - parameter: Biweight scale of redshift-offset velocities; sigma_200c = 1014 +/- 169 km/s  
  > "estimating the biweight scale (cite: 1990AJ....100...32B) and standard error over these velocities" (Mass estimates)
   - parameter: BCG (lowest magnitude) used as reference for peculiar velocities  
  > "All peculiar velocities were estimated with respect to the BCG, which was selected as the galaxy with the lowest magnitude." (Mass estimates)
   - assumption: Redshift uncertainty of members not propagated to dynamical mass; X-ray f_gas assumed 0.125  
  > "the redshift uncertainty was not given by (cite: Willis2020)" (Mass estimates)
   - fitting: Analytic scaling relations; uncertainty propagated from velocity-dispersion statistics only  
  > "Here, we only propagated the statistical error on the velocity dispersion to the dynamical mass uncertainty" (Mass estimates)
   - validation: SZ mass compared to X-ray and dynamical masses; SZ lower than dynamical by about 2.5 times  
  > "We find lower SZ-derived mass estimates than what is expected from the velocity-dispersion measurement" (Mass estimates)

## Qualitative findings

- SZ flux excess in the south where no X-ray emission is detected

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA and ACA Band 3 data give high resolution; ACT adds large scales that the interferometers miss  
  > "In this paper, we treat this issue by combining archival SZ observations" (Introduction)
- Why this method: uv-plane forward modeling avoids deconvolution artifacts and uses the exact visibility sampling  
  > "Modeling in the uv-plane avoids issues related to the deconvolution of interferometric data" (Visibility-based modeling of the ICM)
- Alternative: Point source at the BCG rejected (4.1 sigma) as the cause of low gamma; positive continuum from central galaxies considered as alternative explanation for low gamma  
  > "However, another plausible explanation for low $\gamma$-values would be that the positive continuum emission of the central galaxies balances the negative surface brightness of the SZ decrement." (Single component ICM modeling)
- Alternative: Unphysical two-component gNFW runs with unfrozen beta and alpha excluded from the table (eccentricity above 0.9, beta below 2)  
  > "Hence, these models are not reported in Table" (ALMA+ACA+ACT joint likelihood modeling)
- How they decided it was enough: Nested sampling stopped when evidence changes by less than 0.01 in ln; model choice by Bayes factor  
  > "Sampling of the posterior distribution continues until the log difference of the Bayesian evidence is less than an arbitrary threshold, in our case set to" (Joint likelihood modeling of the ICM via ALMA, ACA, and ACT observations)
- Limitation (authors): Lack of short baselines in ALMA+ACA explains variation in mass estimates and inability to choose among templates  
  > "the lack of short baselines in the ALMA+ACA observations explains why the mass estimates vary between models" (Empirical formalism)
- Limitation (authors): Gaussian filament model is not ideal for the asymmetric structure  
  > "We are aware that this specific model is not ideal for modeling filamentary-like structures" (Discussion)
- Limitation (authors): Overall mass accuracy may be affected by hydrostatic mass bias common to SZ masses  
  > "the overall accuracy could still be affected by the hydrostatic mass biases common to SZ mass estimates" (Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Second-component result depends on an eccentric gNFW proxy for a filament; significance is tentative and model-dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Priors and posteriors for many runs are in supplementary material not in the main text, limiting checks
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Frozen A10/M14 shape parameters and dependence on the 0.125 gas fraction and TX-based X-ray mass add systematic uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Custom uv-plane reconstruction and ACT covariance estimate are not independently validated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-epoch archival data with a short Band 4 track and only 0.42 h ALMA on-source time
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux calibration uncertainty of about 5% not propagated explicitly in the quoted masses
