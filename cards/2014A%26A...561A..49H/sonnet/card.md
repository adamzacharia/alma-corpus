## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA Band 7 continuum and CO(3-2) limit**: Reduced and imaged in CASA; full 7.5 GHz bandwidth used for continuum since CO(3-2) undetected  
  > "As no emission was detected in the CO(3-2) line, in the end, the full 7.5 GHz bandwidth per polarization was used to obtain a continuum measurement" (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: CO line width equals HI width (~50 km/s), area 1 arcsec^2, CO(3-2)/CO(1-0)=0.6  
  > "Assuming the CO line width in SBS 0335$-$052 is the same as that for H ($\sim$50 kms$^{-1}$), we obtain a 3$\sigma$ upper limit on the CO(3-2) flux of 1.58 K km s$^{-1}$." (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Remove free-free contribution at 870 um**: Extrapolate 1.3 cm flux and Bralpha-based free-free; average and subtract from total Band 7 flux  
  > "Taking the average of these, 0.41 mJy, we attribute $\sim$0.06 mJy of the total ALMA Band 7 flux (0.47 mJy) to cool dust in SBS 0335$-$052." (2.2 Free-free emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Two independent free-free estimates agree (0.40 and 0.42 mJy)  
  > "From ${\rm Br}\alpha$, we obtain a similar ALMA Band 7 flux, 0.42 $\pm$ 0.05 mJy." (2.2 Free-free emission)
3. **Reduce Herschel PACS photometry**: HIPE v10.0 reduction, aperture photometry r=10", aperture correction, correlated-noise correction; scanamorphos cross-check  
  > "We performed photometry in apertures between radius 10$''$ and 14$''$, and found that the flux remains stable to within a few percent in the largest apertures" (2.3 Herschel observations)
   - software: HIPE, scanamorphos
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Remy-Ruyer et al. 2013 and scanamorphos reduction  
  > "Our photometry is slightly higher than, but compatible within the errors to that reported by (cite: remy13)." (2.3 Herschel observations)
4. **Fit SED with DUSTY to get dust mass**: Two-component model (stellar heating function plus DUSTY shell), 7D MCMC, three dust grain types fitted separately  
  > "We find the best fits and the range of model parameter values compatible with the data via exploration of the seven-dimensional (7D) parameter space with an efficient Markov Chain Monte Carlo code" (3 SED)
   - software: DUSTY, PyMC
   - assumption: Galactic A_V=0.155 mag with Cardelli et al. curve; metallicity prior Gaussian around 0.0347 Zsun  
  > "photometry was corrected for Galactic extinction assuming A$_V$ = 0.155 mag" (3 SED)
   - fitting: MCMC, Gaussian likelihood, uniform priors except truncated Gaussian metallicity prior  
  > "We calculate the likelihood of each model assuming Gaussian flux errors on the measurements." (3 SED)
   - validation: Single-T modified blackbody (beta=2, T 5-100 K) and 870 um optical depth dust mass check  
  > "We performed two “sanity checks” for the derived dust mass" (3.1 SED-fitting results)
5. **Fit I Zw 18 SED**: Same DUSTY procedure with Herschel/Spitzer photometry  
  > "the results from fitting it with the same *DUSTY* algorithm used for SBS 0335$-$052." (4 Comparison with I Zw 18)
   - software: DUSTY, HIPE, scanamorphos, MOPEX
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: MBB check  
  > "we performed a single-temperature MBB fit of the SED" (4.3 Fitting the SED of I Zw 18)
6. **Dust-to-gas ratios and alpha_CO limits**: Use HI column densities in dust region; gas scaling relations (Daddi 2010) for missing molecular gas; CO upper limits to bound alpha_CO  
  > "Because $\Sigma_{\rm H2}$ = $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ $I_{\rm CO(1-0)}$, we need first to convert the CO(3-2) limit to one on CO(1-0)." (5.2 Constraints on molecular gas conversion factors)
   - assumption: CO(3-2)/CO(1-0) 0.6-0.7  
  > "found an error-weighted mean CO(3-2)/CO(1-0) flux ratio of 0.6$\pm$0.06" (5.2 Constraints on molecular gas conversion factors)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: DUSTY used because SED is warm and optically thick, making single-T MBB unreliable  
  > "A third reason for the difference in dust masses is that the dust in SBS 0335$-$052 is not optically thin at short wavelengths" (3.1 SED-fitting results)
- Alternative: Single-temperature MBB gives 10x lower dust mass and misses warm and cool dust  
  > "However, Fig. [\[fig:MBB\]](#fig:MBB) shows that the single-$T$ MBB fit misses a large fraction of the warmer dust, and does not pass through the ALMA point" (SED-fitting results)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Diffuse emission at scales >3" may be missed; dust detection marginal  
  > "We could be missing some diffuse low-surface brightness emission in SBS 0335$-$052 because of the angular-size limitations of the extended ALMA configuration." (2.1 ALMA observations)
- Limitation (authors): Missing molecular gas estimates highly uncertain  
  > "these estimates of missing molecular gas for both galaxies are highly uncertain" (5.1 Dust-to-gas ratios)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust mass rests on a marginal (1 sigma) dust-only 870 um flux after free-free subtraction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): DUSTY spherical geometry and grain choice drive the result; degeneracies not fully explored
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Free-free extrapolation uncertainties dominate the ALMA constraint
