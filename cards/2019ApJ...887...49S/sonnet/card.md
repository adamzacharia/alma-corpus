## Analysis recipe

1. **Prepare Halpha maps**: Sky subtraction, seeing matching, Gaia astrometry, iterative R-band continuum subtraction, [NII]/transmission correction, Galactic extinction correction  
  > "iterate this process until successive continuum estimates differ by less than 1%." (2.2.3)
   - software: DAOPHOT APER/GETPSF (IDL)
   - parameter: [NII]/Halpha = 0.3 from NGC 0628 MUSE template  
  > "The NGC 0628 template spectrum has an intrinsic [Nii]/Hα ratio of 0.3." (2.2.4)
   - assumption: No internal extinction correction  
  > "Our final Hα line flux images trace emission from H II regions without any correction for attenuation." (2.2.6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals consistent with noise after sky subtraction  
  > "We repeated the above analysis and confirm that the residuals appear consistent with noise." (2.2.1)
2. **Remove DIG to isolate HII regions**: Two-stage unsharp masking (300 pc then 750 pc Gaussian FWHM) with 0.33 scaled subtraction  
  > "The scaling factor, which is 0.33, reflects that this is an initial estimate and that we do not want to oversubtract at this stage." (2.2.5)
   - software: Hiiphot (comparison)
   - parameter: kernels 300 pc and 750 pc FWHM; gain 0.33  
  > "We smooth our Hii region subtracted image with a Gaussian kernel that has FWHM 750 pc." (2.2.5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Three authors inspected masks; agreement with Hiiphot and spectroscopic DIG separation in a few targets  
  > "Three of the authors independently inspected the resulting masks to confirm that they indeed isolate the bright knots in each of our Hα maps" (2.2.5)
3. **Threshold CO and convolve**: CO clipped at 12.6 Msun/pc2 at 140 pc; convolve to 140 pc–1.5 kpc; blank faintest 2% of flux  
  > "We clip the CO images at our best matching resolution of 140 pc using a physical threshold of 12.6 M ⊙ pc −2 (accounting for galaxy inclination)" (3. METHODOLOGY)
   - parameter: 2% flux clip; tested 1-5%  
  > "We tested that other reasonable rejection criteria (between 1 and 5%) do not affect our conclusions." (3.2)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.35 Msun pc-2 (K km/s)-1, R21 = 0.7  
  > "assuming a standard Galactic conversion factor of α CO = 4.35 M ⊙ pc −2 (K km s −1 ) −1 (Bolatto et al. 2013)" (2.1 CO Images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Varied CO threshold (10-15 Msun/pc2; 3σ/5σ), DIG parameters by 25%, mask S/N by ±1σ; min/max adopted as uncertainty  
  > "We adopt the minimum and maximum values of the pixel fractions obtained from this suite of tests as an indication in the uncertainty of our measurements." (3.3)
4. **Classify sightlines and measure fractions**: Classify pixels as CO only, Halpha only, overlap, empty; compute fractions by number and flux per scale  
  > "we classify each pixel into one of four categories" (3.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeated excluding central 1 kpc; repeated at 90 pc  
  > "we define the center as the region within 1 kpc of the galaxy reference position" (A)
5. **Infer relative gas cycling timescale**: t_gas = t_Halpha × (f_CO-only + f_overlap)/(f_Halpha-only + f_overlap)  
  > "Here we apply a simple version of this approach to molecular gas that is visible above a fixed surface density threshold, treating individual pixels as discrete starforming units." (5.2)
   - parameter: t_Halpha = 5-10 Myr  
  > "Typical estimates for the visibility time of Hα are 5 − 10 Myr" (5.2.1)
   - assumption: Steady-state cycling  
  > "Under the assumption of simple steady-state cycling" (5.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement within ~2 with Chevance et al. (subm.) for most galaxies  
  > "our estimates also agree within a factor of ∼ 2 with cloud timescale estimates obtained by Chevance et al. (subm.)" (5.2.1)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Simple, reproducible, threshold-based approach to avoid uncertain CO-to-H2 and Halpha-to-SFR conversions  
  > "We made this choice exactly to minimize the bias due to uncertain conversions between CO and H 2 or Hα and a local star formation rate." (5.5)
- Alternative: Fixed surface brightness cuts for DIG separation were rejected as less satisfactory  
  > "We experimented with fixed surface brightness cuts to separate the Hα emission into the DIG and the H II regions, but found the resulting masks to be much less satisfactory." (2.2.5)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Fixed CO threshold gives variable completeness, biasing cycling time low; DIG removal and no extinction correction  
  > "A bias against recovering all of the CO emission present affects the inferred f scale, driving the apparent cycling time to artificially low values." (5.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Results depend on arbitrary thresholds and DIG filter tuning
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample of 8 massive galaxies, mixed CO(2-1) and CO(1-0), mixed Halpha data quality
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Steady-state timescale interpretation is simplistic
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uses a single NGC 0628 spectrum for [NII] correction of all galaxies
