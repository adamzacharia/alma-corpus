## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image ACA + 12-m data cubes**: ACA and 12-m data calibrated separately, continuum subtracted with line-free channels, combined with concat, imaged with Briggs weighting  
  > "For data reduction, the ACA and 12-m array data were calibrated separately, with continuum subtraction performed using line-free channels." (Observations); "The two datasets were then combined using the concat task, and imaging was carried out with Briggs weighting (robust = 0.5)." (Observations)
   - software: CASA 5.6
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "imaging was carried out with Briggs weighting (robust = 0.5)." (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ALMA SiO images recovered about 60% of the IRAM 30m flux after matching resolution  
  > "the ALMA SiO images recovered about 60% of the total flux measured with the IRAM 30m" (Observations)
2. **make integrated-intensity and moment maps**: smooth SiO spatially to 6 arcsec and spectrally to 0.6 km/s, apply BTS moment masking, integrate SiO over -20 to 120 km/s and H13CO+ over 40 to 50 km/s  
  > "The SiO data were spatially smoothed to a resolution of 6$^{\prime\prime}$, and the spectral resolution was degraded from 0.21 to 0.6 km s$^{-1}$." (Results); "integrated over the velocity ranges of -20 – 120 km s$^{-1}$ and 40 – 50 km s$^{-1}$, respectively" (Results)
   - software: BTS procedure
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CH3OH and CS maps used to confirm that the arc structures are real  
  > "To confirm that these structures are real, in Figure [5](#fig5) we also present the integrated intensity map of another shock tracer" (Discussion)
3. **extract and fit SiO and H13CO+ spectra at 20 positions**: average spectra over 20 rectangular regions fitted with Gaussians using pyspeckit; classify broad/narrow SiO by comparison with the broadest H13CO+ width (2.3 km/s); compare weak positions with 3 emission-free circles  
  > "These parameters were obtained by fitting the molecular line profiles with Gaussian functions using $\textit{pyspeckit}$." (Results); "we compared these spectra with those from the three circular regions with no detectable SiO emission" (Results)
   - software: pyspeckit
   - parameter: rms estimated as standard deviation over 50 km/s of line-free channels  
  > "The rms noise level of each spectrum was estimated as the standard deviation over a velocity range of 50 km s$^{-1}$ in line-free channels." (Results)
   - fitting: Gaussian line profiles via pyspeckit; priors/initial guesses not stated  
  > "We obtained the central velocity (${\upsilon}_{\rm LSR}$, km s$^{-1}$), linewidth ($\Delta {\upsilon}$ km s$^{-1}$), and peak intensity ($F$ mJy beam$^{-1}$) from $\textit{pyspeckit}$." (Details of the spectral fitting)
   - validation: SiO peak intensities at weak positions compared with 3 sigma noise in non-emission regions  
  > "Except for position 12, the peak intensities of the SiO spectra at the other positions are higher than the 3$\sigma$ noise level measured in the non–emission regions." (Results)
4. **identify outflows and measure lobe extents**: blue/red-shifted SiO emission compared with 8 and 24 um sources and MM cores; lobe extent from maximum separation between driving protostar and 3 sigma SiO contour  
  > "The extent of the outflow lobes is estimated by measuring the maximum spatial separation between the putative outflow-driving protostars (cyan stars) and the 3$\sigma$ contour levels measured in the SiO emission" (Identified outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **derive SiO column density and abundance**: LTE optically thin column density from integrated T_MB; abundance from extinction-derived H2 column density  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and that the SiO emission is optically thin (cite: jimenez2010parsec), the column density of SiO can be calculated as follows:" (Column density and abundance)
   - parameter: S mu^2 = 19.2, nu = 86.847 GHz, E_u/k = 6.25 K  
  > "The line strength ($S\mu^{2}$) is 19.2 for the SiO (2-1) line, and the SiO line frequency ($\nu$) is 86.847 GHz. The upper-level energy $E_u/k$ is 6.25 K." (Column density and abundance)
   - parameter: Tex = 9 K narrow, 50 K broad  
  > "We utilized an excitation temperature ($T_\textup{ex}$) of 9 K for narrow components, as estimated for Cloud H (cite: jimenez2010parsec), and 50 K for the broad component, as estimated for the shocked gas in molecular outflows (cite: 2005Jimnez)." (Column density and abundance)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin SiO  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and that the SiO emission is optically thin (cite: jimenez2010parsec)" (Column density and abundance)
   - assumption: 3 sigma (0.021 g cm-2) mass surface density adopted as upper limit at low-density positions  
  > "we adopt 3$\sigma$ as an upper limit." (Column density and abundance)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Narrow-SiO column changes by less than a factor 1.3 for Tex = 15 K instead of 9 K  
  > "the derived column densities of the narrow SiO change by less than a factor of 1.3 when assuming an excitation temperature of 15 K instead of 9 K" (Column density and abundance)
6. **derive outflow mass, momentum, energy and dynamical parameters**: N(SiO) converted to N(H2) with an assumed SiO abundance; M_out, P, E, t_dy, Mdot, F_SiO, L_SiO from lobe areas and velocity offsets relative to the systemic velocity  
  > "By converting the $N_\textup{SiO}$ to the H$_2$ column density ($N{_\mathrm{H_{2}}}$), we estimate the masses of the blue and red outflow lobes ($M_\textup{b/r}$), from which the total outflow mass ($M_\textup{out}$) is derived." (Identified outflow)
   - parameter: V_sys = 45.5 km/s  
  > "with respect to the systemic velocity of Cloud H (45.5 km $^{-1}$)" (Identified outflow)
   - assumption: SiO abundance ~1e-8; inclination 57.3 deg; jet mass-loss to accretion ratio 0.3  
  > "We adopt a typical SiO abundance ($\chi_\textup{SiO}$) of $\sim$10$^{-8}$, consistent with values reported for mid-infrared-dark sources by (cite: Sanchez-Monge2013A&A)." (Identified outflow); "we assume an average inclination angle of $\theta$ = 57.3$^{\circ}$ (cite: Beuther2002A&A)." (Identified outflow)
   - assumption: For position 7 the red lobe is assumed to have the same N_r and A_r as the blue lobe  
  > "For position 7, we assume that the red lobe has the same $N_\mathrm{r}$ and $A_\mathrm{r}$ as the blue lobe" (Identified outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with typical high-mass protostellar outflow parameters  
  > "These outflow parameters are consistent with those typically found in high-mass protostellar sources (cite: Beuther2002A&A)." (Identified outflow)
7. **compare shock tracers and radio continuum to test SNR / HII region / cloud-cloud collision scenarios**: CS and CH3OH moment maps in velocity channels overlaid on SiO; MeerKAT 1.3 GHz, GLOSTAR 5.8 GHz, GMRT 610 MHz, FUGIN 13CO and SCUBA-2 images compared qualitatively with arc morphology; flux and spectral index of the southern shell  
  > "Furthermore, the observed SiO, CS, and CH$_3$OH arc structures follow the outline of the 610 $\mathrm{MHz}$ emission, which may further support the interaction between the SNR G35.6-0.4 and Cloud H." (Interaction with Supernova Remnants); "The inferred spectral index is $\alpha$$\sim$1, which suggests that this spherical shell is likely associated with an optically thick HII region." (Interaction with Supernova Remnants)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Interpretation explicitly stated as not confirmable  
  > "Therefore, at this stage it is not possible to undoubtedly confirm this scenario." (Interaction with Supernova Remnants)

## Qualitative findings

- Broad SiO with high-velocity wings at positions 3, 4, 19, 20 (and 7) traces outflows, three outflows identified  
  > "From ALMA SiO observations, we have identified three outflows within the cloud." (Conclusions)
- Two arc-like (inner and outer) narrow SiO structures, also in CS and CH3OH, lie away from the star-forming filament and lack 8 and 24 um sources  
  > "Notably, both arcs lack associated 8 $\upmu$m and 24 $\upmu$m sources." (Results)
- Arc SiO abundances are about a factor 3 lower than in outflows, read as a gentle interaction with swept-up SNR material  
  > "The fact that the associated SiO abundances are factors of at least 3 lower than those detected in outflows, supports a scenario of a gentle interaction with the expanding swept-up material from the SNR." (Interaction with Supernova Remnants)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Earlier IRAM 30m data lacked angular resolution to tell large-scale shocks from embedded protostars  
  > "However, due to the lack of high-angular resolution observations, the origin of the SiO emission remains uncertain" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Moment masking was applied to improve the noise and make detections more robust  
  > "to improve the noise level and ensure a more robust detection (cite: Clarke2018MNRAS)." (Results)
- Alternative: Global gravitational collapse / cloud-cloud collision considered as an alternative formation scenario, though it does not explain the 610 MHz coincidence  
  > "Taken together, these findings provide an alternative scenario in which both the observed arc-like structures and the formation of Cloud H may be the result of a collision between giant molecular clouds." (Global gravitational collapse and cloud-cloud collisions)
- Alternative: A widespread low-mass protostar population judged unlikely  
  > "the probability of finding such a low-mass protostellar population outside the main filament of Cloud H seems rather unlikely." (A widespread population of low-mass protostars?)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): SiO images suffer from missing flux, so abundances are lower limits  
  > "However, note that these values should be considered as lower limits since the SiO images suffer from missing flux" (Column density and abundance)
- Limitation (authors): Outer-arc SiO is weak and near the map edge, so uncertainties are larger  
  > "We note that the SiO spectra at the positions in the outer arc are weak and located near the edge of the map, and therefore have larger uncertainties." (Results)
- Limitation (authors): Resolution too low to resolve outflow cavity substructure  
  > "the current resolution is insufficient to resolve the cavity substructures within the SiO emission." (Identified outflow)
- Limitation (authors): Limited westward 610 MHz coverage leaves overlap with the SNR uncertain; SNR scenario not confirmable  
  > "the actual overlap between the radio emission from SNR G35.6–0.4 and Cloud H remains uncertain." (Interaction with Supernova Remnants)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Narrow-SiO columns assume Tex = 9 K taken from single-dish work; no radiative transfer or multi-line check, and the broad/narrow Tex split is imposed by hand
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 60% flux recovery means abundance contrasts between arcs and outflows (factor ~3) are lower limits that may change with missing-flux correction, and the contrast is the main argument for a gentle shock
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow parameters use an assumed SiO abundance of 1e-8 and a single mean inclination; several positions have no identified driving source
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The SNR link rests on morphological match with 610 MHz contours with partial coverage; no kinematic or chemical test of the shock
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Printed outflow formulas contain apparent typos (V_maxb twice in t_dy; t undefined), and L_SiO lists three values for five positions
