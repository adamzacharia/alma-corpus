## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra at offset positions**: Spectra extracted at 0.6 arcsec (A) and 0.5 arcsec (B) offsets from continuum peaks  
  > "Two positions, offset by 0$''$6 and 0$''$5 from the peak continuum positions, have been investigated towards IRAS16293A and IRAS16293B, respectively." (Observations & spectroscopic data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit LTE synthetic spectra**: Large chi2 grid of Gaussian-profile LTE synthetic spectra over Tex, Ntot, Vpeak, FWHM, compared to extracted spectra  
  > "A large $\chi^2$ grid of spectral models was run in order to constrain the excitation temperature, $T_\mathrm{ex}$, the column density, $N_\mathrm{tot}$" (Results & Analysis)
   - parameter: Ntot 1e15-1e18 cm-2; Tex 100-300 K; source A FWHM 2.0-2.6, Vpeak 0.6-1.0 km/s; source B FWHM 0.8-1.2, Vpeak 2.5-2.9 km/s  
  > "The grid covered column density values from $1\times10^{15}$ to $1\times10^{18}$ cm$^{-2}$ and excitation temperatures from 100 to 300 K." (Results & Analysis)
   - parameter: Tex of CHD2OCHO fixed to that of main species; 260 optically thick CH3OCHO lines excluded  
  > "Therefore, we used the same excitation temperature as for the main species towards both sources, respectively." (Results & Analysis); "A significant number of lines of the main isotopologue (i.e. 260 lines) were found to be optically thick and excluded in the subsequent fits." (Results & Analysis)
   - assumption: LTE; source size 0.5 arcsec  
  > "the gas of the inner region is assumed to be in Local Thermodynamic Equilibrium (cite: Jorgensen-2016)" (Results & Analysis); "In all models, the same source size $\theta_\mathrm{s}$ = 0.5$''$ was adopted." (Results & Analysis)
   - fitting: chi2 grid minimisation, lines weighted by spectral resolution and blending risk  
  > "Each line is weighted with the spectral resolution and a factor corresponding to the risk of blending effect with other species." (Results & Analysis)
   - validation: Searched for anti-coincidences between synthetic and observed spectra; none found  
  > "Anti-coincidences between the synthetic and observed spectrum were searched, but none were found, suggesting that these excitation temperatures provide a good fit." (Results & Analysis)
3. **Correct for continuum and derive D/H ratios**: Continuum background correction factor applied to column densities; abundance ratios converted to D/H with statistical factors (binomial)  
  > "At the 0.6$''$ offset position towards the A source, the continuum flux is 0.55 Jy beam$^{-1}$, corresponding to a brightness temperature of 30 K, and a correction factor of 1.27 at 115 K." (Results & Analysis)
   - parameter: CHD2OCHO/CH3OCHO = 3 (D/H)^2; CH2DOCHO/CH3OCHO = 3 (D/H)  
  > "CHD$_2$OCHO / CH$_3$OCHO | = | 3 (D/H)$^2$" (D/H ratio vs abundance ratio)
   - assumption: Independent probability of D at each site  
  > "The probability of having a D atom in a particular site in a chemical group is independent with respect to the other potential sites." (D/H ratio vs abundance ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Map spatial distribution**: VINE maps with integration ranges 3.2 km/s (A) and 1.2 km/s (B) centred on corrected peak velocity from methanol 337.519 GHz line  
  > "The integration ranges are 3.2 and 1.2 km s$^{-1}$ centered on the corrected peak velocity towards the sources A and B, respectively." (Results & Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compare with chemical model**: Compare observed ratios with Taquet et al. 2014 GRAINOBLE model at early and late times for R<50 AU  
  > "at two different times in the model labelled "early" and "late" corresponding to $\sim$2$\ \times10^{4}$ yrs after the formation of the central protostar" (Astronomical Implications)
   - software: GRAINOBLE, DUSTY
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- First detection of CHD2OCHO in the ISM towards both sources; the D/H of CHD2OCHO is 2-3 times that of CH2DOCHO, not reproduced by the model  
  > "The D/H ratio of CHD$_2$OCHO is found to be 2–3 times higher than the D/H ratio of CH$_2$DOCHO for both sources" (abstract)

## Claim tuples

- H: Doubly-deuterated methyl formate is detectable in IRAS16293 A and B | E: LTE chi2 spectral fitting of PILS spectra | R: Lines above 3 sigma found, 18 unblended towards B and 2 unblended towards A | F: First detection of CHD2OCHO in the ISM | **supported**
- H: D/H of CHD2OCHO is similar to that of CH2DOCHO as predicted by chemistry | E: Compared derived D/H ratios and the Taquet et al. 2014 model | R: Observed CHD2OCHO D/H higher by factor 2-3; model gives similar ratios | F: Model lacks a mechanism enhancing D/H, e.g. H-D substitution | **partially**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High sensitivity of PILS enables a search for multiply-deuterated complex organics  
  > "The high sensitivity of the dataset enables a search to be undertaken for multiply-deuterated variants of these more complex COMs" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: H-D substitution on the methyl group vs inheritance from precursors CHD2OH/CH2DOH; unresolved  
  > "Further experiments and modelling work are needed to test this." (Astronomical Implications)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only two sources explored; link between methanol and methyl formate deuteration unclear  
  > "However, the ratios observed in IRAS16293 explore only the chemistry of two sources." (Astronomical Implications)
- Limitation (authors): Tex of CHD2OCHO cannot be constrained due to low Eu dispersion  
  > "means that the excitation temperature cannot be constrained properly" (Results & Analysis)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tex fixed to the main species may bias column densities of deuterated forms
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line blending on source A with fixed source size 0.5 arcsec
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uncertainties from the chi2 grid are not defined
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model comparison is qualitative with a single model
