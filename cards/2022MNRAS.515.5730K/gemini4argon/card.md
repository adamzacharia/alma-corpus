- Question: What is the extent of star-formation activity and the relative evolutionary stages of clumps in the molecular cloud associated with the southern HII region RCW 42?

  > "We have identified two cores within clump C1 (Fig. [6](#cont1)), using the algorithm Clumpfind." (Millimetre continuum emission towards C1); "We have estimated the masses of the cores assuming that the emission is optically thin at this frequency." (Millimetre continuum emission towards C1)
   - software: Clumpfind
   - parameter: threshold = 0.15 Jy/beam, contour spacing = 7 mJy/beam  
  > "A threshold of 0.15 Jy/beam and a contour spacing of 7 mJy/beam have been used to identify the dusty cores." (Millimetre continuum emission towards C1)
   - assumption: optically thin emission at 1.4 mm, dust temperature = 38 K, gas-to-dust ratio = 100, opacity per cloud mass = 0.3 cm^2/g for beta = 2 (or gas-to-dust ratio = 150 and beta = 1.8)  
  > "To obtain these estimates, we have considered the gas-to-dust ratio of 100, and opacity per cloud mass of 0.3 cm$^2$/g, using the relation given by (cite: 1983QJRAS..24..267H) for $\beta=2$." (Millimetre continuum emission towards C1); "The temperature of 38 K is adopted from the dust temperature map." (Millimetre continuum emission towards C1); "Using the opacity relation from (cite: 2013IAUS..292...19T) for $\beta=1.8$ with gas-to-dust ratio of 150, we estimate the dust masses to be 250 and 260 M$_\odot$, respectively." ## Qualitative findings

- All five dust clumps (C1-C5) in RCW 42 are in an active or evolved stage of star formation and lie in the envelope-clearing phase on the mass-luminosity diagram.  
  > "The clumps are investigated for their evolutionary stages based on association with various star-formation tracers, and we find that all the clumps are in active/evolved stage." (Abstract); "In addition, the positions of these clumps in the M-L diagram indicate that all these clumps are in the envelope clearing phase." (Evolutionary Stages of Clumps)
- An extended green object, EGO G274.0649-01.1460, is identified towards I-09230 in clump C3 and is associated with elongated radio emission indicative of an ionized jet/outflow.  
  > "We refer to this source as EGO G274.0649–01.1460 in this work." (Emission from warm dust); "It has radio emission with elongated morphology that is associated with extended green object EGO G274.0649–01.1460, suggesting the presence of high mass protostellar outflow(s) within C3." (Evolutionary Stages of Clumps)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA archive data were used to examine high-resolution cold dust emission towards the peak of the molecular cloud (I-09227 / C1), while GMRT, Herschel, Spitzer, and 2MASS probed the ionized gas, molecular cloud dust, and young stellar objects across multiple scales.  
  > "The high resolution cold dust emission towards the peak of the molecular cloud is examined using data from ALMA archive." (Atacama Large Millimetre/submillimeter Array (ALMA)); "In the present work, we examine this region at radio, infrared and submillimeter wavelengths." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: ALMA spectral line cubes were not used because the noise is very high in the 120s snapshot observations.
