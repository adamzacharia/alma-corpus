## Analysis recipe

1. **Identify slim filaments in SiO 5-4 and test for dust continuum**: Visual inspection of SiO 5-4 integrated intensity maps against criteria; check continuum and low-resolution SiO maps  
  > "the filaments should have spatially continuous SiO 5–4 integrated line emission with a signal-to-noise ratio greater than 3, a coherent velocity structure in the position-velocity diagram, and an aspect ratio greater than 10" (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
   - parameter: SiO integrated emission S/N > 3, coherent PV velocity, aspect ratio > 10  
  > "signal-to-noise ratio greater than 3, a coherent velocity structure in the position-velocity diagram, and an aspect ratio greater than 10" (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
   - parameter: Continuum non-detection limit 5 sigma = 0.2 mJy/beam  
  > "these filaments are not detected in the dust emission at the 5$\sigma$ level of 0.2 mJy beam$^{-1}$" (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Low-resolution ALMA SiO images also show the filaments, arguing against spatial filtering  
  > "similar filamentary emissions were also found in the low-resolution ALMA SiO images, suggesting that the slim filaments are unlikely to be a result of spatial filtering" (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
2. **Extract skeletons and measure filament widths**: FilFinder skeletons on SiO 5-4 integrated intensity; intensity profiles perpendicular to the skeleton every 5 pixels; Gaussian fit to the mean radial profile; beam deconvolution of the FWHM  
  > "we extract intensity profiles perpendicular to the skeletons at intervals of every 5 pixels (about one beam size) and perform Gaussian fits to the mean radial profile" (3.2.1 Widths); "The resulting skeletons are depicted as red lines in" (Skeletons identified by FilFinder)
   - software: FilFinder
   - parameter: Global threshold: integrated intensities below 3 sigma_area excluded from the mask  
  > "global threshold - integrated intensities below 3$\sigma_{\rm area}$ are excluded from the mask" (Skeletons identified by FilFinder)
   - parameter: Size threshold: minimum area of 125 pixel^2 (~5 beam sizes)  
  > "filaments must have a minimum area of 125 pixel$^{2}$ ($\sim$ 5 times the beam size)" (Skeletons identified by FilFinder)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian A(r) = A0 exp(-(r-mu)^2/(2 sigma_G^2)) fit to the mean radial profile  
  > "perform Gaussian fits to the mean radial profile" (3.2.1 Widths)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive velocity dispersions from averaged SiO spectra**: Gaussian fits to averaged SiO 5-4 spectra in the four regions, then removal of channel width and thermal terms  
  > "We perform Gaussian fits to the averaged SiO 5–4 spectra toward the four regions" (Gaussian fits to the SiO 5–4 spectra in four regions)
   - parameter: Gas temperature ~70 K  
  > "The typical gas temperature is approximately 70 K adopted from ." (3.2.2 Velocity dispersions)
   - assumption: Mean molecular weight per free particle 2.37; SiO molecular weight 44  
  > "$\mu_{p}$ = 2.37 is the mean molecular weight per free particle (cite: 2008A&A...487..993K)" (3.2.2 Velocity dispersions)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to averaged spectra (sampler, priors not stated)  
  > "We perform Gaussian fits to the averaged SiO 5–4 spectra toward the four regions" (Gaussian fits to the SiO 5–4 spectra in four regions)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Check velocity coherence with position-velocity diagrams**: PV diagrams along the filament major axes; test for velocity gradients typical of outflows  
  > "their PV diagrams display consistent velocities along their major axes" (3.2.3 Velocity structures); "the slim filaments do not show apparent velocity gradients along the major axes that are typically observed in outflows" (3.2.3 Velocity structures)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive column densities and relative abundances**: LTE, optically thin column densities at SiO peak positions for nine molecules; abundances normalised to CH3OH; only lower limits as H2 column is unconstrained  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) conditions and optically thin line emission, we estimated the column densities of the nine molecules" (3.3 Relative Abundances of the Molecules and Comparison to Outflows); "We normalized the abundances of the nine molecules with respect to that of CH$_3$OH" (3.3 Relative Abundances of the Molecules and Comparison to Outflows)
   - parameter: Excitation temperature 70 K  
  > "The excitation temperature is adopted to be 70 K following ." (Calculation of Molecular Column Densities)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin, Rayleigh-Jeans, negligible background  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) conditions, optically thin line emission, Rayleigh-Jeans approximation, and negligible background" (Calculation of Molecular Column Densities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compare relative abundances with outflows and dense cores**: Two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test of relative abundances against CMZ outflows, the IRAS 20126+4104 outflow, and CMZ dense cores  
  > "We performed a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test to compare the relative abundances in the slim filaments and outflows in the CMZ." (3.3 Relative Abundances of the Molecules and Comparison to Outflows)
   - parameter: Significance threshold p < 0.05 for different distributions  
  > "a small *p*-value (typically $<$ 0.05) indicates that the two distributions under consideration are significantly different" (3.3 Relative Abundances of the Molecules and Comparison to Outflows)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Estimate turbulent, thermal, gravitational, magnetic and external pressures**: P_turb = rho sigma_v^2 with density from the continuum-limit column density over the filament width; compare with thermal, gravity, magnetic and external pressure  
  > "The turbulent pressure can be estimated using $P_{\rm turb}$ = $\rho\sigma_{v}^{2}$." (4.1 A new class of filaments); "Assuming that the thickness along the line of sight is the same as the slim filament width of 0.024 pc" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - parameter: Mean sigma_v = 4.4 km/s  
  > "We adopt a mean $\sigma_{v}$ value of 4.4 km s$^{-1}$" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - parameter: Continuum limit 5 sigma = 0.2 mJy/beam at 226 GHz  
  > "Considering the dust emission at the 5$\sigma$ level of 0.2 mJy beam$^{-1}$" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust mass ratio 100  
  > "where $\eta$ = 100 is the assumed gas-to-dust mass ratio" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - assumption: T_dust = 20 K  
  > "dust temperature $T_{\rm dust}$ = 20 K following (cite: kauffmann2017a)" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - assumption: Dust opacity 0.817 cm2/g (beta = 1.5)  
  > "We adopt $\kappa_{\nu}$ = 0.817 cm$^{2}$g$^{-1}$" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - assumption: mu_H2 = 2.8  
  > "$\mu_{\rm H_{2}}$ = 2.8 is the mean molecular weight of the interstellar medium" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Estimate dissipation timescale and SiO replenishment budget**: Dissipation time from width and velocity dispersion; CMZ SiO mass from APEX SiO 5-4 (LTE, optically thin); compare depletion rate with per-filament replenishment rate to get the number and surface density of filaments  
  > "With the filament width of 0.024 pc and the typical velocity dispersion of 4.5 km s$^{-1}$, the dissipation timescale of the filaments is estimated to be $\sim$5.2 $\times$ 10$^{3}$ years" (4.1 A new class of filaments); "To maintain a balance between the replenishment and depletion, about 8000 filaments would be required over the CMZ" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - parameter: Excitation temperature 70 K; velocity range -50 to 120 km/s; area 325 x 56 pc^2  
  > "This estimate covers an area of 325 $\times$ 56 pc$^{2}$ in the CMZ" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin SiO 5-4, Tex = 70 K  
  > "Under the assumptions of LTE conditions, optically thin line emission, and an excitation temperature of 70 K, we estimate a SiO mass of 1.3 $M_{\odot}$ in the CMZ" (4.1 A new class of filaments)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Slim filaments are also seen in SO, H2CO, HNCO, HC3N and CH3OH, and marginally in CH3CN, H2(13)CO and c-C3H2  
  > "In some cases, the filaments are even marginally detected in the emission of CH$_3$CN 12$_{0/1}$–11$_{0/1}$, H$_2$$^{13}$CO 3$_{1,2}$–2$_{1,1}$, and c-C$_3$H$_2$ 6$_{1,6}$–5$_{0,5}$" (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
- Class I CH3OH masers, believed collisionally pumped, are found in all four regions with slim filaments  
  > "Class I CH$_3$OH masers are believed to be collisionally pumped (cite: 2014MNRAS.439.2584V), and are found in all the four regions with slim filaments" (4.2 Possible origin of the slim filaments)
- Magnetic fields are nearly perpendicular to the main filament skeletons  
  > "We find that magnetic fields are nearly perpendicular to the main filament skeletons." (4.2 Possible origin of the slim filaments)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: SiO 5-4 was used for identification because it traces shock activity  
  > "we focused on the SiO 5–4 transition that has been suggested to primarily trace shock activities" (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
- Alternative: Outflow origin rejected because the filaments lack along-axis velocity gradients  
  > "Therefore, it is unlikely that they are outflows." (3.2.3 Velocity structures)
- Alternative: Two explanations for the missing dust emission under shocks: dust destruction, or initially diffuse dust with sputtered Si and missing flux  
  > "(i) the shocks have destroyed most, if not all, of the dust grains, releasing Si into the gas phase; or (ii) the initial dust emission is diffuse, and a shock wave can efficiently sputter Si atoms and form SiO." (4.2 Possible origin of the slim filaments)
- Alternative: Widespread CH3OH masers in the CMZ may come from cosmic-ray photodesorption, but here are attributed to shocks because their velocities match the SiO  
  > "the masers in these regions are likely generated by shock activities, as their velocities are consistent with the $V_{\rm lsr}$ of the SiO emissions observed in the filaments" (4.2 Possible origin of the slim filaments)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The sample is not complete; only the most robust candidates were reported  
  > "We note that the identified slim filaments are by no means a complete sample of such objects in the surveyed clouds." (2.1 Identification of the slim filaments)
- Limitation (authors): Without dust emission the H2 column density is unconstrained, so abundances are lower limits  
  > "Consequently, we could only provide lower limits for molecular abundances and relative abundances between the molecules." (3.3 Relative Abundances of the Molecules and Comparison to Outflows)
- Limitation (authors): The nature and origin of the filaments need further multi-wavelength work  
  > "Future multi-wavelength investigations that better constrain excitation conditions and chemistry will help confirm the nature of these filaments." (4.2 Possible origin of the slim filaments)
- Limitation (authors): Dust emission could be undetectable because of missing flux  
  > "the dust emission remains undetectable by ALMA due to missing flux" (4.2 Possible origin of the slim filaments)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Filament selection is by visual inspection of one line and is not reproducible without the authors' masks.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The density, and hence the pressure, uses the continuum 5 sigma upper limit and a line-of-sight depth equal to the width, so the turbulent pressure is an upper limit and the geometry is assumed.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): One Tex of 70 K and one gas temperature are applied to all molecules and positions, and optically thin LTE is assumed for SiO and others.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The KS comparison uses few molecules and mixes lower limits and non-detections; sample sizes are small.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The shock origin rests on indirect clues; magnetic-field orientation is at 19.6 arcsec, far coarser than the 0.026 pc filaments.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The SiO budget extrapolates a 10-filament sample in 15 pc^2 to the whole CMZ and uses a SiO mass from single-dish data.
