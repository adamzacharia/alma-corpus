## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit laboratory rotational spectra and generate spectroscopic line lists and partition functions**: Least-squares fitting of measured submillimeter lines (150–530 GHz) using the Reduced Axis System (RAS) formalism and Watson's S-reduction Hamiltonian; partition functions computed from SPCAT rotational partition functions and harmonic vibrational approximations.  
  > "The analysis of the spectra were carried out using the RAS formalism and Watson’s S-reduction Hamiltonian implemented in the SPFIT code." (abstract); "The rotational partition function was obtained directly at various temperatures from SPCAT (Pickett 1991) with J up to 90, and K a up to 40." (3.3 Partition function)
   - software: SPFIT, SPCAT
   - parameter: J up to 90 and Ka up to 40 for Q_rot; four lowest vibrational excited states below 870 cm^-1 for Q_vib  
  > "The rotational partition function was obtained directly at various temperatures from SPCAT (Pickett 1991) with J up to 90, and K a up to 40." (3.3 Partition function); "The four lowest vibrational excited states levels were considered." (3.3 Partition function)
   - assumption: Some octic distortion constants (L_K, l_2, l_3) for 13C isotopologues fixed to main isotopologue values; dipole moments taken from ab initio calculations (µ_a = 2.32 D, µ_b = 1.31 D, µ_c = 1.23 D)  
  > "their standard deviation were kept fixed at the main isotopologue values." (3.1 13C isotopologues); "It is also the case of 2hydroxyacetonitrile: µ a = 2.32 D, µ b = 1.31 D, µ c = 1.23 D. These ab initio values are from Dalbouha et al. (2017)." (3 ANALYSIS OF THE ROTATIONAL SPECTRA)
   - fitting: Least-squares global fit of 3403 lines (45 parameters, rms 26.3 kHz) for HO13CH2CN, 3235 lines (44 parameters, rms 28.7 kHz) for HOCH213CN, and 3190 lines (44 parameters, rms 26.6 kHz) for DOCH2CN  
  > "In total, 3403 distinct lines of the HO 13 CH 2 CN isotopologue were assigned with maximum values for J and K a of 67 and 25, respectively. The standard deviation of the fit is 26.3 kHz and 45 parameters were determined." (3.1 13 C isotopologues); "For HOCH 13 2 [CN isotopologue, the dataset is com-] posed of 3235 lines with maximum values for J and K a of 63 and 26, respectively. The standard deviation of the fit is 28.7 kHz and 44 parameters were determined." (3.1 13 C isotopologues); "A total of 3190 distinct lines of the DOCH 2 CN were assigned with maximum values for J and K a of 60 and 21, respectively." (3.2 Deuterated isotopologue: DOCH 2 CN)
   - validation: Comparison of experimental rotational constants A, B, C and tunneling splitting E with ab initio calculations and main isotopologue parameters  
  > "We also compare in Table 2 the rotational constants A, B, and C, and energy difference between tunneling sublevels E from our study with the ab initio calculations." (3.1 13 C isotopologues)
2. **Search for isotopologues and derive column density upper limits in Serpens SMM1-a**: Extract single-beam spectrum towards the SMM1-a continuum peak, search for lines above 3 S/N, and compute LTE synthetic spectra in line-free regions to set upper limits on column density.  
  > "Analysis of an extracted spectrum towards the SMM1-a continuum peak in a single beam with the CASSIS 3 line analysis software" (4.1 SMM1-a); "To identify the isotopologues, their lines are searched within a 3 S/N detection. Upon identification, a synthetic spectrum is fitted to these lines, assuming Local Thermodynamic Equilibrium (LTE) conditions." (4.1 SMM1-a); "If no lines are detected, the upper limit column density of an isotopologue is determined in line-free spectral regions where a transition of said isotopologue is located." (4.1 SMM1-a)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: T_ex = 260 K, ΔV = 2.5 km s^-1, V_LSR = 6.8 km s^-1, 3 S/N detection threshold  
  > "To identify the isotopologues, their lines are searched within a 3 S/N detection." (4.1 SMM1-a); "Other synthetic spectrum parameters are adopted from the main isotopologue HOCH 2 CN detection towards SMM1-a and are fixed at T ex = 260 K, ∆ V = 2.5 km.s −1 , and V LSR = 6.8 km.s −1 ." (Figure 1 caption)
   - assumption: Local Thermodynamic Equilibrium (LTE); T_ex, ΔV, and V_LSR fixed to main isotopologue values from Ligterink et al. (2021)

  > "Due to the issues in determining line free spectral regions, a conservative estimate of the upper limit column density is given." ## Qualitative findings

- Substituting 12C with 13C in 2-hydroxyacetonitrile has very limited influence on the tunneling Hamiltonian parameters, whereas deuteration of the hydroxyl group (DOCH2CN) strongly alters the tunneling splitting E.  
  > "From the analysis of Table 1, it can be clearly seen that 12 C to 13 C isotopic substitution has a very limited influence on tunneling Hamiltonian parameters." (3.1 13 C isotopologues); "The deuteration of the hydroxyl group hydrogen implies a significant variation of the mass of the tunneling atom which has a direct influence on the tunneling probability" (3.2 Deuterated isotopologue: DOCH 2 CN)
- None of the three isotopologues (HO13CH2CN, HOCH213CN, DOCH2CN) are detected in existing ALMA observations towards Serpens SMM1-a or IRAS16293 B.  
  > "Only non-detections could be reported in this work toward SMM1-a and IRAS16293 B with no significant constraints on the 13 C/ 12 C and D/H ratios of this molecule." (5 CONCLUSION)
- Serpens SMM1-a is a better candidate target than IRAS16293 B for future follow-up ALMA observations targeting the strongest transitions of 2-hydroxyacetonitrile isotopologues.  
  > "Given the lower constraints obtained for IRAS16293 B and the small spectral range covered by the observations of SMM1-a so far, it appears that SMM1-a would be a better target for follow-up observations." (4.2 IRAS16293 B)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Serpens SMM1-a and IRAS16293 B are the only two astronomical sources where the main isotopologue of 2-hydroxyacetonitrile has been detected so far.  
  > "Later, a second detection of hydroxyacetonitrile was reported towards Serpens SMM1-a (Ligterink et al. 2021). These two detections are thus far the only observations of this molecule." (1 INTRODUCTION); "The main isotopologue of hydroxyacetonitrile has been detected towards Serpens SMM1-a (hereafter SMM1-a) at high abundance (Ligterink et al. 2021), making this a suitable target to search for its isotopologues DOCH 2 CN, HO 13 CH 2 CN and HOCH 13 2 [CN." (4.1 SMM1-a)
- Why this method: The Reduced Axis System (RAS) approach in SPFIT/SPCAT is particularly suitable for molecules exhibiting double-minimum large amplitude motion due to OH-group torsion.  
  > "This method is particulary suitable for double minimum LAM cases, we employed it successfully in previous studies (Motiyenko et al. 2010; Smirnov et al. 2013; Motiyenko et al. 2015) and in particular with the hydroxyacetonitrile normal species (Margulès et al. 2017)." (3 ANALYSIS OF THE ROTATIONAL SPECTRA)
- Alternative: Analyzing the trans rotamer of hydroxyacetonitrile, which was omitted because it lies 1.41 kcal mol^-1 (710 K) higher in energy than the gauche rotamer.  
  > "As for the parent isotopic species, we only analyzed the spectra of the gauche rotamer, which is the most stable one. The trans rotamer of hydroxyacetonitrile is calculated to be 1.41 kcal.mol −1 (493 cm −1 /710 K) higher in energy (Dalbouha et al. 2017)." (3 ANALYSIS OF THE ROTATIONAL SPECTRA)
- Alternative: Using the parent or 13C tunneling and Coriolis parameters as starting values for DOCH2CN, which was rejected because deuteration of the hydroxyl group strongly alters the tunneling dynamics.  
  > "Therefore, for DOCH 2 CN isotopic species, as starting set of tunneling and Coriolis coupling parameters, we could not use the ones determined for the parent or 13 C species." (3.2 Deuterated isotopologue: DOCH 2 CN)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The 1.2" synthetic beam in the SMM1-a observations is insufficient to resolve the hot corino.  
  > "A synthetic beam of approximately 1.2” was employed, which is insufficient to resolve the hot corino." (4.1 SMM1-a)
- Limitation (authors): Line-richness in SMM1-a makes it difficult to find completely line-free spectral regions, requiring conservative column density upper limits.  
  > "Due to the line-richness of the spectrum, it is difficult to find completely line free spectral regions." (4.1 SMM1-a); "Due to the issues in determining line free spectral regions, a conservative estimate of the upper limit column density is given." (4.1 SMM1-a)
- Limitation (authors): The spectral windows of the SMM1-a observations miss most of the strongest transitions of the three isotopologues.  
  > "Part of the reason that the isotopologues of HOCH 2 CN are not detected lies in the fact that the spectral windows of the SMM1-a observations miss the most of the strongest transitions of these species." (4.1 SMM1-a)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Upper limits on column densities are determined visually in crowded spectra without a formal statistical fitting procedure or rms noise quote per window.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam dilution / source size assumption for SMM1-a is not explicitly stated in this paper, requiring the reader to consult Ligterink et al. (2021).
