## Analysis recipe

1. **Image cubes**: Pipeline calibration, natural-weighted minimal cleaning, continuum-subtracted, primary-beam-corrected cubes  
  > "We performed minimal cleaning with natural weighting, generating continuum-subtracted and primary-beam-corrected maps" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 0.3″ pixels, 100 km/s channels  
  > "We chose 0.$^{\prime\prime}$3 pixels and a spectral resolution of 100 ${\rm km}\,{\rm s}^{-1}$." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Blind CO line search**: Blind search of PB-corrected cubes, peak S/N ≳5  
  > "requiring a peak S/N $\gtrsim5$, and resulting in a final catalog of 11 CO detections over all 3 clusters" (2.3 CO 2–1 Detections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Counterparts confirmed with HST imaging, spectroscopy and photometry  
  > "allows for unambiguous counterpart identification of the ALMA detections" (2.3 CO 2–1 Detections)
3. **Measure CO fluxes**: Collapse to integrated-intensity map, 2D Gaussian fit, extract spectrum in 4σ region, fit Gaussian (double Gaussian for one pair) to the profile  
  > "We then perform a two-dimensional Gaussian fit on their respective map, and use the best-fit major and minor FWHM to create a $4\sigma$ region for spectral profile extraction" (2.3 CO 2–1 Detections)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile; rms errors from line-free channels  
  > "determining rms errors from the line-free channels for each source" (2.3 CO 2–1 Detections)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive gas masses**: L'CO via Solomon05 Eq. 3; Mgas = alpha_CO L'(2-1)/r21  
  > "To estimate the total molecular gas mass we use $M_{\rm gas} = \alpha_{\rm CO}(L^{\prime}_{(2-1)}/r_{21})$." (2.3 CO 2–1 Detections)
   - assumption: r21=0.77, alpha_CO=4.36  
  > "We assume sub-thermalized emission with $r_{21}=0.77$" (2.3 CO 2–1 Detections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Infrared SFRs**: Aperture photometry on MIPS, simultaneous stacking for SPIRE, Bayesian fit of Chary & Elbaz templates, LIR converted with Kennicutt98  
  > "Assuming flat priors on both the amplitude and template, we calculate the weighted mean over the posterior to determine the infrared luminosity and its uncertainty." (2.4 Infrared Star Formation Rates)
   - parameter: 105 templates × 10^4 amplitudes (0–100)  
  > "each scaled by $10^4$ amplitudes, ranging from 0 to 100" (2.4 Infrared Star Formation Rates)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF  
  > "Stellar masses and star formation rates (SFRs) are based on a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: Chabrier03)." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: chi2 over template/amplitude grid, flat priors  
  > "we compute the $\chi^2$ value from the observed infrared fluxes" (2.4 Infrared Star Formation Rates)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compare with field scaling relations**: Sum chi2 vs Genzel15 tracks at each galaxy's mass; KS test against field samples above detection limit; include 3σ upper limits for 7 non-detections  
  > "We quantify this offset by summing the individual $\chi^2$ values from each data point compared to model track scaling relation at the cluster galaxy’s given stellar mass." (3.1 Gas Properties Scaled to the Star-Forming Main Sequence)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Selection-effect test with upper limits  
  > "the offset significance for the gas fraction only drops to $\sim4\sigma$" (3.1 Gas Properties Scaled to the Star-Forming Main Sequence)

## Qualitative findings

- Cluster gas fraction rises with redshift, mimicking the field; suggestive of steeper evolution but caution due to heterogeneous samples  
  > "The gas fraction in cluster galaxies mimics the strong evolution in the field." (3.2 Evolution of the Gas Fraction in Clusters)
