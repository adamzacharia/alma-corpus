## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and lines**: CASA CLEAN with Briggs weighting, varied robust; continuum from line-free channels; primary beam correction  
  > "We used Briggs weighting with different robust parameters in order to obtain the best compromise between spatial resolution and signal-to-noise ratio" (3 ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive dust disk mass**: Optically thin M = S D^2 / (B(Tdust) kappa), Tdust from stellar luminosity, Ossenkopf & Henning opacities  
  > "Assuming the ALMA continuum emission comes from thermal dust emission, and considering optically thin emission, we derive the total dust mass from (cite: Hildebrand1983)" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - parameter: Tdust 15 K (R=1.1 Rsun) or 20 K (R=2 Rsun); kappa 0.85 (1.3 mm), 1.8 (0.89 mm) cm2/g  
  > "obtained values of $\kappa$= 0.85 cm${^2}$g${^{-1}}$ and 1.8 cm${^2}$g${^{-1}}$, respectively." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - assumption: Distance 155 pc; optically thin thermal dust  
  > "$D$ is the distance to the source (155 pc)" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Ansdell fluxes rescaled to 155 pc  
  > "our results are compatible with theirs within the errors." (4.1 Continuum emission)
3. **Outflow geometry and mass**: Lobe length, opening and position angles measured by hand on CO(3-2) 3σ contour; mass from optically thin channel-by-channel CO(3-2), Tex ~26.4 K, no inclination correction; then dynamical quantities from formulas  
  > "which were measured by hand for both lobes." (4.3 Geometrical and dynamical properties); "We therefore derived an excitation temperature (T$\mathrm{_{ex}}$) of $\sim$26.4 K from this spectrum." (4.3 Geometrical and dynamical properties)
   - parameter: vmax ~6 km/s; Rlobe 1.8 arcsec  
  > "| Velocity | v$\mathrm{_{max}}$ | 6 km/s |" (Table 3)
   - assumption: Material near plane of sky (limb brightening); emission near source excluded so mass is lower limit  
  > "In our calculations we did not apply an outflow inclination correction because the brighter emission of the CO(3-2) at the borders of the cavity suggest a limb-brightening effect" (4.3 Geometrical and dynamical properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Uncertainties (Tex, opacity, extent) estimated to raise mass by <factor 2  
  > "may result in an increase in outflow mass by less than a factor 2." (4.3 Geometrical and dynamical properties)
4. **Spectral index**: alpha from fluxes at 328 and 225 GHz with propagated error  
  > "where F is the flux density at frequencies $\nu_{1}= 328$ GHz and $\nu_{2}= 225$ GHz." (4.4 Spectral index)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **SED grid fitting**: Grid of MCFOST radiative transfer models with 8 free parameters, chi2 for two near-IR datasets  
  > "we chose to run a grid of models to obtain a general idea of the system parameters." (5.2 SED fitting)
   - software: MCFOST
   - parameter: Teff fixed 3200 K, d 155 pc; stellar radius 1.1, 1.5, 2, 2.5 Rsun  
  > "The stellar temperature was fixed to 3200 K (cite: Alcala17-1)" (5.2 SED fitting)
   - assumption: No dusty envelope included  
  > "Par-Lup3-4 may still be surrounded by a dusty envelope, which we did not consider in our models" (5.2 SED fitting)
   - fitting: Grid search with chi2 (no MCMC)  
  > "$\chi^2$ values were then computed for each model." (5.2 SED fitting)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- First detection of the base of a bipolar molecular outflow in a VLM star, aligned with the optical jet; plus a perpendicular stream to the northeast of unknown origin  
  > "Our observations reveal for the first time the base of a bipolar molecular outflow in a very low-mass star" (abstract)
- 13CO velocity map suggests rotation perpendicular to outflow, Keplerian nature not confirmable  
  > "The spatial and spectral resolution as well as the sensitivity of our data do not allow us to confirm the Keplerian nature of this rotation." (4.2.3 13CO(3-2))

## Claim tuples

- H: Par-Lup3-4 drives a molecular outflow consistent with its optical jet | E: CO(2-1), CO(3-2) mapping with ALMA | R: Arc-like blue/red lobes at base of compact bipolar outflow, same orientation as jet | F: A bipolar molecular outflow base is detected | **supported**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Grid rather than MCMC for SED because of limited long-wavelength data  
  > "Because the amount of data available at long wavelengths for Par-Lup3-4 is limited, we chose to run a grid of models" (5.2 SED fitting)
- Alternative: Four origins for the NE stream: second outflow, cloud contamination, other source, infalling stream; not distinguishable  
  > "The current (sub)mm and optical observations do not allow us to distiguish between the four scenarios." (6.1 Nature of the extended gas emission to the northeast)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Outflow mass and lengths are lower limits; extended flux filtered and sensitivity limited  
  > "Our observations place lower limits on these outflow quantities." (7 Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): SED fit highly uncertain and discrepant with ALMA dust mass by factor ~2 to ~27  
  > "We note that there is a factor of $\sim$2 to $\sim$27 in difference between the dust disk mass obtained from fitting the SED" (5.2 SED fitting)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Hand-measured outflow geometry and no inclination correction make dynamical quantities order-of-magnitude
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cloud contamination near systemic velocity affects mass integration
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Binary claim rests on a degenerate SED fit and a radius assumption
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Temperature adopted from luminosity scaling uncertain for an edge-on source
