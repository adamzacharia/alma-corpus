## Analysis recipe

1. **Image HCN 3-2 and map integrated intensity**: Imaged HCN 3-2 cube (20 km/s channels, Briggs robust 0.5) and made an integrated-intensity (moment 0) map; HCN is in absorption in the inner 50 mas and in emission farther out, extending along the minor axis  
  > "In the inner $50$ mas ($14$ pc) towards the nucleus, HCN is observed in absorption, while it is observed in emission farther out from the nucleus." (3.4 HCN minor axis emission); "where Briggs weighting was used, adopting a robust parameter of 0.5." (2.2 ALMA observations)
   - parameter: Channel width 20 km/s (18 MHz), rms 0.26 mJy/beam  
  > "The resulting data have a sensitivity of 0.26 mJy per beam in a 20 km s$^{-1}$ (18 MHz) channel width." (2.2 ALMA observations)
   - assumption: Optical velocity convention in the barycentric frame; systemic velocity 3897 km/s  
  > "Throughout this paper the optical velocity convention is used with respect to the barycentric reference frame." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Position-velocity diagram along the minor axis and by-eye ellipse**: p-v diagram of a strong feature along the minor axis; low-velocity feature outlined by a skewed, shifted ellipse fitted by eye; separate high-velocity feature >200 km/s out to ~600 km/s  
  > "We analyzed the velocity structure of the minor axis emission using a position-velocity diagram (p-v diagram) of a strong feature along the minor axis." (3.4 HCN minor axis emission); "The ellipse, that was fitted by eye" (3.4 HCN minor axis emission)
   - parameter: Ellipse width 120 km/s, height 120 mas, shift 40 km/s, skew 0.5 km/s/mas  
  > "(width: $120$ km s$^{-1}$, height: $120$ mas, shift: $40$ km s$^{-1}$, skew: $0.5$ km s$^{-1}$  mas$^{-1}$)" (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - assumption: Ellipse in p-v space interpreted as a rotating, wide-angle, hollow outflow (front and back sides), following Tabone et al. 2020  
  > "We propose that this feature traces a rotating, wide-angle, and hollow, outflowing structure." (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - fitting: Ellipse fitted by eye (no sampler or likelihood)  
  > "The ellipse, that was fitted by eye" (3.4 HCN minor axis emission)
   - validation: Rotating nuclear-disk alternative ruled out because the elliptical structure persists in minor-axis p-v slices beyond the HCN disk radius  
  > "the elliptical structure is present also in minor axis p-v slices $>200$ mas, beyond the HCN disk radius of $\sim 150$ mas" (4.1 HCN outflow)
3. **Integrate and measure the high-velocity HCN emission**: Integrated-intensity image of the high-velocity emission and integrated fluxes of red and blue wings; blue flux flagged as possibly contaminated by a CH2NH line  
  > "integrated intensity image of the high-velocity ($\pm 250$ km s$^{-1}$ $-$ $\pm 750$ km s$^{-1}$) emission" (3.4 HCN minor axis emission); "We retrieved an integrated total flux of $12.7$ Jy km s$^{-1}$ for the redshifted high-velocity emission" (3.4 HCN minor axis emission)
   - parameter: High-velocity range +/-250 to +/-750 km/s relative to systemic  
  > "integrated intensity image of the high-velocity ($\pm 250$ km s$^{-1}$ $-$ $\pm 750$ km s$^{-1}$) emission" (3.4 HCN minor axis emission)
   - parameter: Outflow position angle PA_HCN = 105 deg  
  > "For the position angle of the collimated outflow we find $\mathrm{PA_{HCN}}=105^o$." (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the CO 6-5 outflow of Falstad et al. 2018 (same PA, lower maximum velocity 400 vs ~600 km/s)  
  > "Interestingly, this is the same as the position angle that (cite: falstad:18) find for the outflow traced by CO $J=6\to 5$ emission." (4.1 HCN outflow)
4. **Estimate HCN outflow mass and kinematic budget**: L(HCN 3-2) converted to HCN 1-0 luminosity with a 0.3 ratio, then to dense gas mass with the Gao & Solomon relation, using the red outflow only; mass-loss rate, momentum, energy computed with adopted velocity and size  
  > "Accounting only for the red outflow ($12.7$ Jy km s$^{-1}$, $250-500$ km s$^{-1}$), this procedure yields a collimated outflow mass of $M_{\mathrm{HCN}}=6.2\times 10^7\ M_{\odot}$." (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - parameter: Adopted M_HCN = 1-6e7 Msun  
  > "we adopt $M_{\mathrm{HCN}}=1-6\times 10^7\ M_{\odot}$ in order to compute the outflow kinematics from our mass estimates" (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - parameter: Outflow velocity ~250 km/s and size 30 pc  
  > "we put conservative estimates of $\sim 250$ km s$^{-1}$ and $30$ pc for the outflow velocity and size" (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - assumption: HCN 3-2/1-0 temperature ratio of 0.3  
  > "we assumed a HCN $\frac{J=3\to2}{J=1\to0}$ temperature ratio of $0.3$ (cite: krips:08)" (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - assumption: Conversion valid for gravitationally bound gas, mass overestimated if clumps are non self-gravitating (factor ~5 reduction considered)  
  > "We note that the conversion relation is derived for gravitationally bound gas, so the mass will be overestimated if the dense gas is in non self-gravitating clumps." (4.1 HCN outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared momentum and energy fluxes with the ULIRG outflow sample of Gonzalez-Alfonso et al. 2017  
  > "Compared to the sample of ULIRG outflows that (cite: gonzalez:17) analyzed" (4.1 HCN outflow)
5. **Estimate OH megamaser outflow mass and kinematics (e-MERLIN)**: Defined the OHM region as spectrally integrated intensity above 0.3 Jy/beam km/s (0.12 arcsec^2), used N_OH ~ 1e17 cm^-2 and x_OH = 2e-6 to get the mass, then rate, momentum and energy with v = 50 km/s and size 30 pc  
  > "We define the OHM emission region as the one where the spectrally integrated intensity exceeds $0.3\ \mathrm{Jy/beam}$ km s$^{-1}$, which has an angular area of $0.12 \ \mathrm{arcsec}^2$." (4.2 OHM outflow); "we compute the OHM outflow mass as $M_{\mathrm{OHM}} = 9 \times 10^6\ \mathrm{M_{\odot}}$" (4.2 OHM outflow)
   - parameter: Outflow velocity 50 km/s, size 30 pc  
  > "Adopting an outflow velocity of 50 km s$^{-1}$ and a size of 30 pc" (4.2 OHM outflow)
   - assumption: N_OH ~ 1e17 cm^-2, x_OH = 2e-6 relative to H2, mean particle mass 2.3 m_H  
  > "and an average particle mass of $\mu = 2.3 \ \mathrm{m_H}$" (4.2 OHM outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Test outflow launching and fate (radiation pressure, escape velocity, fountain)**: Compared momentum fluxes with L_IR/c, balanced radiative outward force against gravity on OHM clumps, integrated the equations numerically, and compared outflow velocities with sqrt(2) v_circ and 3 v_circ escape thresholds  
  > "after numerical integration of the above equations, adopting the cited typical values for the OHM clumps that occur in the outflow as we observe it, we found that after $\sim 4\times 10^5$ years" (4.3 Outflow launching)
   - parameter: Critical momentum flux pdot_crit = 2.1e11 Lsun/c (r/30 pc)(N/3e22 cm^-2)(v_circ/140 km/s)^2  
  > "is $\dot{p}_{\mathrm{crit}} = 2.1 \times 10^{11} L_{\odot}/c \left(\frac{r}{30 \ \mathrm{pc}}\right)" (4.3 Outflow launching)
   - assumption: Optical depth ~1 at 10 micron for the OHM clumps; typical clump values (r = 30 pc, N = 3e22 cm^-2, v_circ = 140 km/s)  
  > "with optical depths $\tau_{\mathrm{OHM}}\sim 1$ at $10 \, \mathrm{\mu m}$" (4.3 Outflow launching)

## Qualitative findings

- HCN 3-2 is in absorption in the inner 50 mas (14 pc) and in emission farther out, extending along the minor axis  
  > "In the inner $50$ mas ($14$ pc) towards the nucleus, HCN is observed in absorption, while it is observed in emission farther out from the nucleus." (3.4 HCN minor axis emission)
- OH megamaser emission lies along the minor axis ~0.2'' (~57 pc) from the 18 cm continuum peak and is undetected towards the nucleus and along the major axis  
  > "Emission towards the nucleus, as well as along the major axis, is undetected." (3.2 OH megamaser (OHM))
- H2CO megamaser traces the nuclear disk along the major axis  
  > "H$_2$CO emission is observed concentrated along the major axis" (3.4 H2CO megamaser)
- Fast collimated HCN outflow exceeds the escape velocity of the nuclear region and likely escapes it, while the OHM outflow does not  
  > "These outflow velocities are comfortably larger than $\sqrt{2}v_{\mathrm{circ}}\approx 200$ km s$^{-1}$, suggesting that this outflow will escape the nuclear region." (4.3 Outflow launching)
- HCN outflow momentum flux requires a boost beyond radiation pressure (jet or energy-driven AGN wind or MHD launching proposed)  
  > "The collimated HCN outflow requires a driving mechanism beyond radiation, due to the high momentum requirements." (4.3 Outflow launching)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: OHM disk-excitation scenario rejected because maximal emission is expected along the major axis, opposite to what is observed  
  > "We rule out that the OHM is excited in the disk, as in this scenario maximal emission is expected towards the disk major axis" (4.2 OHM outflow)
- Alternative: Blue side of the elliptical p-v structure as the rotating nuclear disk, ruled out because the structure persists beyond the HCN disk radius  
  > "We considered the possibility that the blue side of the elliptical structure represents the rotating nuclear disk, but ruled out this scenario" (4.1 HCN outflow)
- Alternative: OHM excited in the nuclear disk, ruled out  
  > "We rule out that the OHM is excited in the disk" (4.2 OHM outflow)
