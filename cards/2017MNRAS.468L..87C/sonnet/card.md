## Analysis recipe

1. **Recalibrate and image ALMA data**: Standard CASA calibration, flagging, clean with natural weighting  
  > "The final images were then created using the clean algorithm with natural weighting." (2.1 ALMA band 6 data)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum fluxes of S1, S2, region S**: Fit 2D ellipses constrained by beam shape for each SSC, masking the other; circular aperture of 0.5 arcsec radius for region S  
  > "We extract fluxes for the two SSCs by fitting 2D ellipses constrained by the shape of the ALMA beam." (2.1 ALMA band 6 data); "with a circular aperture of radius 0.5 arcsec covering S1 and S2" (2.1 ALMA band 6 data)
   - fitting: 2D ellipse fit; uncertainty = rms per beam x sqrt(N_beams)  
  > "Uncertainties are given as the rms per beam times $\sqrt{N_{\rm beams}}$." (2.1 ALMA band 6 data)
   - validation: Sum of Gaussians agrees with total within errors  
  > "The sum of the Gaussians agrees with the total within errors, indicating no significant extended emission." (2.1 ALMA band 6 data)
3. **CO(2-1) luminosity upper limit and alpha_CO lower limits**: 3-sigma L_CO limit assuming 50 km/s line width; convert to CO(1-0); assume depletion times of 2 Gyr, 0.2 Gyr, 20 Myr with SSC-derived SFRs to bound alpha_CO  
  > "we derive a conservative 3-$\sigma$ $L_{\rm CO(2-1)}$ limit of $3.6\times10^4$ K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$." (3 Star formation and molecular reservoir)
   - parameter: line width 50 km/s; solid angle 0.044 arcsec^2  
  > "Our solid angle is $0.044$ arcsec$^2$." (3 Star formation and molecular reservoir)
   - assumption: CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) = 0.8; tau_dep = 2 Gyr, 0.2 Gyr, 20 Myr  
  > "We assume three different molecular gas depletion times: 2 Gyr" (3 Star formation and molecular reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with previous CO limits and Glover & Clark extinction simulations  
  > "Following their study, our results are compatible with mean visual extinctions of 0.1-1 mag." (3 Star formation and molecular reservoir)
4. **SED analysis and cold dust mass**: Subtract free-free from Johnson 2009 S1/S2 models; fit modified black-body to ALMA excess in addition to PACS MBB (59 K, beta=2); F-test for dust excess in S1  
  > "In addition to the MBB fitted to the PACS observations (59 K, $\beta=2$), we require a second dust component" (4 Spectral energy distribution)
   - parameter: T_dust <= 14 K upper limit from PACS 160 um  
  > "The PACS 160 $\mu$m measurement puts an upper limit on the dust temperature of $T_{\rm dust} \le 14$ K" (4 Spectral energy distribution)
   - assumption: kappa160 = 1.6 m2/kg, beta = 1.7  
  > "we assume common values of $\kappa_{160}=1.6$ m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ and $\beta=1.7$ (cite: galliano-2011)" (4 Spectral energy distribution)
   - fitting: Chi2 comparison and F-test  
  > "an F-test indicates that the probability of this dust component is high ($>$98 per cent)" (4 Spectral energy distribution)
   - validation: Tested with MBB and more realistic dust optics with temperature distribution  
  > "This is valid when employing modified black-body spectra as well as more realistic dust optical properties and a distribution of temperatures (cite: galliano-2011)." (4 Spectral energy distribution)

## Qualitative findings

- Continuum is resolved into two compact knots S1 and S2; S1 has an mm excess over free-free consistent with a cold dust component  
  > "Therefore in the following we consider an additional dust component that is most visible in S1 and not S2." (4 Spectral energy distribution)
- CO not detected even at 50 pc resolution, implying large CO-dark gas  
  > "The most surprising finding is that, even at our 50 pc resolution, CO is not detected." (3 Star formation and molecular reservoir)
