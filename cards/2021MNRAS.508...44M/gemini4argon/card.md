2. **Determine central engine spin-down and ejecta parameters from SN optical light curves**: Model optical light curves of the SLSNe to extract dipole magnetic field B_*, initial spin period P_i, and ejecta mass M_ej.  
  > "The evolution of nascent PWNe is mainly governed by $B_*$, $P_i$ and $M_{\rm ej}$, which are determined through modeling SN optical light curves." ## Qualitative findings

- No significant radio/mm emission was detected from SN 2015bn, SN 2016ard, or SN 2017egm at the supernova positions; emission was detected from the host galaxy of SN 2017egm at a 13 arcsec offset.  
  > "No significant emission was found." (ALMA and NOEMA observations); "Although the 100 GHz image had a $\sim1\sigma$ fluctuation, no significant emission was found." (ALMA and NOEMA observations); "Emission was detected from the host galaxy at an offset of $13^{\prime\prime}$ from the SN position, consistent with the location of a known star-forming region (cite: 2017ApJ...845L...8N)." (ALMA and NOEMA observations)
- A Crab-motivated PWN model has ~2-3 sigma tensions with the 230 GHz ALMA non-detections for SN 2015bn and SN 2017egm (even for P_i = 1 ms), and models with P_i = P_max are ruled out for SN 2016ard and SN 2017egm.  
  > "With $P_i=1$ ms, the flux predictions for SN 2016ard and SN 2017egm are allowed by the data, while the models with $P_i=P_{\rm max}$ are ruled out." (Model implications); "On the other hand, we find that the model fluxes of SN 2015bn and SN 2017egm have $\sim(2-3)\sigma$ tensions with the non-detections at 230 GHz even for $P_i=1$ ms." (Model implications)
- High-magnetization and low-magnetization PWN models reduce predicted synchrotron radio/mm fluxes well below the ALMA and NOEMA upper limits and can be tested with future MeV-GeV gamma-ray telescopes.  
  > "We find that the ALMA data disfavors a PWN model motivated by the Crab nebula for SN 2015bn and SN 2017egm, and argue that this tension can be resolved if the nebular magnetization is very high or very low. Such models can be tested by future MeV-GeV gamma-ray telescopes such as AMEGO." (Abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-frequency radio/mm observations (90-230 GHz) at ages of 1-3 years probe nascent PWNe when the ejecta becomes transparent at ~100 GHz, whereas GHz-band detectability is often limited by strong synchrotron self-absorption and free-free absorption; SN 2015bn, SN 2016ard, and SN 2017egm were selected as the most promising nearby/bright targets.  
  > "We demonstrate the power of such high-frequency radio data, given that the detectability at the GHz band is often limited by strong absorption." (Introduction); "In reality, non-thermal emission escaping from the nebula is further degraded by free-free absorption in the SN ejecta, and the nebula typically becomes transparent at the 100 GHz band $\sim$1–10 years after the explosion (cite: MKM16; OKM18; MMB+18)." (Overview); "Among these 10 SLSNe, we found just two objects, SN 2015bn and SN 2016ard, to be promising mm targets for ALMA. Subsequently, the nearby SN 2017egm was discovered and added to our sample, with observations by ALMA and NOEMA conducted." (ALMA and NOEMA observations)
- Why this method: Time-dependent numerical kinetic equations coupled with ejecta dynamics and absorption processes accurately capture electromagnetic cascades, relic pairs, and fast-cooling synchrotron emission that simpler steady-state or fixed-index treatments miss.  
  > "We estimate $f_{\rm dep,\rm sd}$ as in (cite: KMB+16), but the treatment is improved by considering arbitrary $\gamma_b$ and $q_1<2$ rather than assuming $q_1=2$." (Theoretical Model); "Note that different from (cite: MKK+15) and (cite: MKM16), we take into account both electromagnetic cascades and contributions from relic pairs that are injected at $t<t_{\rm sd}$." (Theoretical Model)
- Alternative: Off-axis jet emission and circumstellar material (CSM) interaction-powered scenarios are noted as alternative mechanisms capable of producing high-frequency radio emission in SLSNe.  
  > "SLSNe may be accompanied by jets, and the resulting off-axis jet emission may produce variable radio emission (cite: EMO+21; HTM+21)." (Summary and discussion); "An alternative mechanism is the interaction-powered scenario, in which particles are accelerated at the shocks between the SN ejecta and dense circumstellar material (cite: SBN+16)." (Summary and discussion)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Present millimeter limits are not sufficient to cover the full parameter space allowed by optical data.  
  > "The present millimetre limits (cite: LOK+19; EMO+21) are not sufficient to cover the relevant parameter space allowed by optical data, so further observations at the mm band are necessary to critically test the pulsar/magnetar-driven scenario." (Summary and discussion)
- Limitation (authors): Large degeneracy in optical light-curve model parameters (B, P_i, M_ej) and uncertainty in nebular magnetization and ejecta clumpiness/asymmetry affecting free-free absorption.  
  > "However, there is a large degeneracy in model parameters, and detecting high-energy signals is relevant for revealing not only the central engine but also particle acceleration at hidden environments (cite: MMZ09; KPO13; MKK+15)."
