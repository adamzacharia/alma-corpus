## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA data**: CASA pipeline calibration; natural-weighted continuum images; line cubes at 1 km/s  
  > "We calibrated the data using the CASA 4.2.2 pipeline." (3 Observations and analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Manual calibration gave consistent results  
  > "Manual calibration with CASA 4.5.2 yielded consistent results." (3 Observations and analysis)
2. **Extract line profiles and fit expansion/stellar velocities**: Spectra from area ~twice the beam, converted to temperature scale; fitted with CLASS Shell profile  
  > "Line profiles were extracted from an area typically twice the beam-size and converted to a temperature scale within CASA." (3 Observations and analysis)
   - software: CASA, CLASS/GILDAS
   - parameter: H fixed to -1  
  > "$H$ was fixed to $-1$ in the fitting." (3 Observations and analysis)
   - assumption: OH/IR star velocities fixed to OH values due to low S/N  
  > "were fixed to the values determined from OH observations (Goldman et al. 2016) because of the poor signal-to-noise ratio" (3 Observations and analysis)
   - fitting: Shell profile fit; v_exp = half the full width at zero intensity  
  > "the expansion velocity v$_{\rm exp}$ is taken as half that value" (3 Observations and analysis)
   - validation: For IRAS 05506 H left free gave H = -1.02 +/- 0.02  
  > "we also fitted a profile allowing this parameter to vary as well and found $H= -1.02 \pm 0.02$." (3 Observations and analysis)
3. **Derive dust-driven-wind MLR and gas-to-dust ratio**: Modified MoD with DUSTY density type = 3; r_gd chosen so predicted v_exp equals observed  
  > "One can then compare this predicted expansion velocity to the observed value and determine the gas-to-dust ratio that makes them equal" (4.1 Predictions by dust-driven wind theory)
   - software: DUSTY, MoD
   - parameter: Fitted: luminosity, dust optical depth, dust temperature at inner radius  
  > "The fitted parameters are luminosity, dust optical depth, and dust temperature at the inner radius." (4.1 Predictions by dust-driven wind theory)
   - assumption: Reference r_gd = 200; distance (50 kpc) assumed  
  > "The next column lists the MLR and expansion velocity for a gas-to-dust ratio of 200" (4.1 Predictions by dust-driven wind theory)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: DUSTY MLR inherent uncertainty ~30%; luminosities agree with GS16  
  > "The luminosities agree well with the values determined by GS16" (4.1 Predictions by dust-driven wind theory)
4. **Compare with Galactic C-stars**: Same MoD density type 3 fits to Galactic extreme C-stars; compare v_exp, L, MLR  
  > "The SED and mid-IR spectra (*ISO*/SWS or *IRAS*/LRS) are fitted using the modified MoD code using the “density type = 3” mode" (4.2 A Galactic comparison sample)
   - software: MoD, DUSTY
   - assumption: Luminosities from PL relation of Groenewegen & Whitelock (1996)  
  > "Luminosities have been determined using the $PL$ relation of Groenewegen & Whitelock (1996)" (4.2 A Galactic comparison sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate CO-based gas MLR**: Ramstedt & Olofsson (2008) formula with f_CO from COLIBRI  
  > "The distance, $D$, is set to 50 kpc, and the averaged beam size, $\theta$, is 1.43$''$." (4.5 Gas MLRs)
   - software: COLIBRI
   - parameter: J=2-1: s_J=1.3e-11, a_J=0.82, b_J=0.46, c_J=0.59  
  > "$a_{\rm J}= 0.82$, $b_{\rm J}= 0.46$, $c_{\rm J} = 0.59$" (4.5 Gas MLRs)
   - assumption: f_CO = 4.5e-4 for C-stars (2.4 Msun); 2.0e-4 for OH/IR stars (factor 2 uncertain)  
  > "$f_{\rm CO}$ is roughly constant at $4.5 \cdot 10^{-4}$" (4.5 Gas MLRs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with dust-based MLRs  
  > "These values systematically fall below those based on consistent modelling of the dust." (4.5 Gas MLRs)

## Qualitative findings

- All four C-stars detected in CO 2-1; only one of two OH/IR stars detected (marginally)  
  > "All 4 C stars are detected, and one of the two OH/IR stars." (3 Observations and analysis)
- CO-based gas MLRs fall below dust-based MLRs, by factor 4-5 for C-stars and >20 for OH/IR stars  
  > "For the OH/IR stars the discrepancy is a factor $>$ 20, for the C-stars it is a factor of $4-5$" (4.5 Gas MLRs)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Maximum baseline chosen so emission stays unresolved given expected CO photodissociation radius  
  > "The maximum baseline (and thus the size of the beam) was chosen to match the expected CO photodissociation radius based on the formula of Mamon et al. (1988) so that the emission region remains unresolved." (3 Observations and analysis)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Small sample and non-trivial comparison with Galactic stars due to distance uncertainties  
  > "It must be stressed, however, that our sample is small and finding a suitable sample of comparison stars in the Galaxy is challenging." (5 Summary and conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only four C-star detections; statistics weak
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas-to-dust ratios degenerate with assumed distance and grain density
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Galactic comparison distances heterogeneous
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detailed CO radiative transfer deferred, so the MLR discrepancy is not resolved
