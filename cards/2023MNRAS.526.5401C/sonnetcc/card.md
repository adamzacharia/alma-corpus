## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and average visibilities**: Provided ALMA pipeline script in CASA, then time and channel averaging for continuum imaging  
  > "The raw data were calibrated with the provided ALMA pipeline script in CASA version 5.1.2-4 (cite: CASA)." (2.2 Observation Details)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 30 s averaging and two channels per spectral window  
  > "To reduce the data volume the visibilities were averaged in 30 second intervals and down to two channels per spectral window for the continuum imaging." (2.2 Observation Details)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make continuum images and inspect for extended emission**: Naturally weighted CLEAN images; additional 1 and 2 arcsec uv tapers inspected visually  
  > "shows naturally weighted images of the observational sample generated with the CLEAN algorithm in CASA" (2.3 Initial image analysis); "The sample was also visually inspected with 1 and 2 arcsec" (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure point-source flux at each Gaia DR3 stellar position**: Point-source model fit to visibilities with CASA uvmodelfit at the Gaia DR3 location; offsets fixed except for detections and near-detections  
  > "We do not allow the offset parameters to vary in these fits to avoid fitting to nearby non-stellar point sources except in the cases of detections and near detections" (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Stars separated by less than the beam are listed as one with the mean of two uvmodelfit fluxes  
  > "Systems where the on-sky separation is less than the beam size are listed as one with the mean flux of the two" (Table 3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: uvmodelfit fluxes consistent with fluxes measured directly from the images  
  > "are consistent with fluxes measured directly from the images." (2.3 Initial image analysis)
4. **Estimate stellar photosphere flux and derive mm excess**: SED fitting of model atmospheres to photometry (Yelverton19 method) to predict 880 um stellar flux; subtract from measured flux to get excess; 3 sigma criterion for detection  
  > "Stellar fluxes are estimated by fitting model atmospheres to photometry as outlined in (cite: Yelverton19)" (2.3 Initial image analysis); "We note two of our observations come close to our 3$\sigma$ criterion for detection." (6 Conclusion)
   - software: PHOENIX, multinest
   - fitting: multinest fit of PHOENIX photosphere plus blackbody disc models to photometry; priors not stated  
  > "this method uses synthetic photometry of PHOENIX (cite: 2013A&A...553A...6H) and blackbody disc models, and" (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Check detections for stellar flares**: Split observations into individual scans, re-image and check flux variance over time  
  > "The observations were split into their individual scans and re-imaged to check for variance of the flux along the time baseline of the observations." (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No evidence for flaring was found  
  > "No evidence for flaring was found." (2.3 Initial image analysis)
6. **Search for 12CO J=3-2 emission**: Continuum-subtracted CLEAN images made with uvcontsub, searched at the stellar location and near the expected stellar radial velocity  
  > "also checked in these observations by producing CLEAN continuum-subtracted images with the" (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No CO emission found in any observation  
  > "No CO emission was found in any observation." (2.3 Initial image analysis)
7. **Stack non-detections for a mean upper limit**: Stack images of 21 non-detections with the star within 0.5 arcsec of the phase centre; read flux at centre and RMS  
  > "A stacked image was also made from the non-detections in which the star is expected to lay within 0.5 arcsec of the phase centre." (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Identify serendipitous background sources**: Search primary-beam-corrected CLEAN images within 10 arcsec of phase centre for sources of at least 5 sigma; compare to galaxy number counts  
  > "Serendipitous sources within 10 arcsec of the phase centre whose flux reached at least 5$\sigma$ were identified in the primary beam-corrected CLEAN images" (2.3 Initial image analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Detected counts consistent with Popping20 galaxy number-count model  
  > "The expected number of background sources is $12^{+4}_{-10}$, consistent with our detections." (2.3 Initial image analysis)
9. **Constrain disc fractional luminosity and temperature for detections**: Fit SED/dust models to get fractional luminosity-temperature distribution, then restrict by disc radius (resolved radius or beam-size upper limit) converted to a mm grain temperature  
  > "These models must be compatible with the SED of the disc but do not take into account resolution effects or radial information derived from the image of the disc." (3.3 GSC 07396-00759)
   - software: multinest
   - parameter: GJ 2006 A radius limit 34 au gives T lower limit 25 K; AT Mic A radius limit 10 au gives T lower limit 40 K; GSC 07396-00759 radius 70.2 au gives T of 20 K  
  > "we can place a lower limit on the mm grain temperature of 25 K" (3.3 GJ 2006 A); "we can place a lower limit on the mm grain temperature of 40 K" (3.3 AT Mic A); "with a resolved radius of 70.2 au the mm dust grains would have a temperature of 20 K" (3.3 GSC 07396-00759)
   - assumption: Unresolved discs: beam semi-major axis sets an upper limit on disc radius  
  > "The beam size of the observation sets an upper limit on the radius of the disc" (3.3 GJ 2006 A)
   - fitting: Model distributions from the Yelverton19 SED fitting; sampler and priors not stated here  
  > "More details on the SED fitting procedure can be found in (cite: Yelverton19)." (3.3 GSC 07396-00759)
   - validation: _not stated_
10. **Compute detection rate and completeness-adjusted rate**: Count detections in the 36-star sample; for each detection compute completeness (fraction of observations that would have detected that disc flux), divide and sum; binomial small-number uncertainties; test with one million synthetic detection sets  
  > "if that disc flux were present for each observation, what fraction of the observations would have significantly detected it?" (5.1 Survey sensitivity and detection fraction); "To investigate these effects we generated one million sets of four synthetic debris disc detections" (5.1 Survey sensitivity and detection fraction)
   - parameter: Synthetic discs: radius uniform 10-100 au, fractional luminosity log-uniform 1e-7 to 1e-3, host luminosity drawn from sample without replacement  
  > "Within each set each disc had a radius selected randomly from between 10 and 100 au with linearly spaced probability and a fractional luminosity selected randomly from between $10^{-3}$ and $10^{-7}$ with logarithimically spaced probability." (5.1 Survey sensitivity and detection fraction)
   - assumption: Large assumptions about the underlying M-dwarf disc population in the synthetic test  
  > "This process has made large assumptions about the underlying M-dwarf disc population" (5.1 Survey sensitivity and detection fraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Synthetic completeness-adjusted rate 29.9 +12.3/-8.9 % not significantly above observed; implies observed uncertainties underestimated  
  > "While the synthetic rate is not significantly larger than the observed fraction, its greater uncertainty does imply that the uncertainties on the observed completeness adjusted detection rate are indeed likely underestimated." (5.1 Survey sensitivity and detection fraction)

## Qualitative findings

- None of the three new or confirmed disc hosts shows stellar flaring or 12CO J=3-2 emission  
  > "We confirm that none of these stars show evidence of stellar flaring and none of the discs show evidence of $^{12}$CO J=3-2 emission." (6 Conclusion)
- TYC 7443-1102-1 field is contaminated by two background sources; no disc is claimed  
  > "We identify the observation of TYC 7443-1102-1 as severely contaminated by two of these background galaxies." (6 Conclusion)
- HD 155555 C near-detection judged likely noise; AT Mic B excess not significant  
  > "we rule the flux measurement to likely be the result of noise." (3.2 HD 155555 C)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: BPMG chosen for youth to maximise disc detections; single Band 7 observation because about ten detections expected  
  > "For this reason, if a survey were to be optimised to recover as many disc detections as possible, a sample of young stars should be selected." (1 Introduction); "We anticipated of order ten detections (i.e. many non-detections), so did not aim to also obtain spectral information by observing with more than one band." (2.2 Observation Details)
- Why this method: Configurations chosen by stellar distance to keep a similar physical-scale sensitivity and avoid resolving out emission  
  > "The observations were spread across configurations C43-1, C43-2, and C43-3 depending on stellar distance to retain sensitivity to a similar physical scale and avoid resolving out disc emission." (2.2 Observation Details)
- Alternative: TYC 7443-1102-1 sub-mm sources considered as a debris disc but rejected as background galaxies; Herschel PACS excess also attributed to contamination  
  > "Therefore, we conclude that these mm-wave sources are most likely not associated with the star and constitute background galaxies." (3.2 TYC 7443-1102-1)
- Alternative: HD 155555 C 2-3 sigma flux considered as excess but judged noise  
  > "we rule the flux measurement to likely be the result of noise." (3.2 HD 155555 C)
- How they decided it was enough: Sample judged sufficient despite later growth of known BPMG membership  
  > "While observing more targets always provides better statistics, our sample is sufficient for our purposes here." (2.1 Observation Sample)
- Limitation (authors): Completeness-adjusted rate is dominated by AT Mic A and its uncertainties are likely underestimated  
  > "the uncertainties on the completeness adjusted detection rate are likely underestimated" (5.1 Survey sensitivity and detection fraction)
- Limitation (authors): No far-IR photometry so SED and temperature are poorly constrained  
  > "With a lack of far-IR photometry it is difficult to constrain an SED and model temperature" (3.3 GJ 2006 A)
- Limitation (authors): Single-band data give no spectral information  
  > "so did not aim to also obtain spectral information by observing with more than one band." (2.2 Observation Details)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detection rate rests on 4 detections, one of which (AU Mic) was not observed in this survey
- Caveat (inferred, ours): AT Mic A 0.13 arcsec offset from the stellar position and unreliable Gaia parallaxes weaken the disc identification
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abstract and Conclusion quote -5.5 for the completeness-adjusted uncertainty while the Discussion gives -5.3
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample was selected in 2017 and is incomplete compared with the later-enlarged BPMG membership
