## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce JWST MIRI/MRS data and extract spectra**: Standard JWST pipeline with extended fringe flats and residual fringe correction; spectra extracted from cube with growing aperture  
  > "we used the standard pipeline set-up, with extended fringe flats and residual fringe correction both on the detector and spectrum level, to reduce the data." (Data reduction)
   - software: JWST calibration pipeline
   - parameter: Pipeline version 1.15.1, pmap 1252  
  > "all data were reduced using version 1.15.1 of the standard" (Data reduction); "and pmap 1252." (Data reduction)
   - parameter: Extraction aperture 2xFWHM centred on source, annulus for background  
  > "The spectra are extracted from the cube using a growing circular aperture of 2$\times$FWHM" (Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux differences versus earlier GW Lup spectrum less than 1% at short wavelengths and under 3% at the longest  
  > "When comparing the difference in flux between the spectrum of GW Lup here and in (cite: ref:23GrDiTa), this ranges from less than a percent at the shorter wavelengths, to under 3% in the longest." (Data reduction)
2. **Subtract continuum from JWST spectra**: Iterative Savitzky-Golay filtering of emission lines, then IRSQR baseline estimate  
  > "the ‘iterative reweighted spline quantile regression’ (IRSQR) method from" (Data reduction)
   - software: PyBaselines
   - parameter: Savitzky-Golay filter applied iteratively until no outliers remain; negative spikes removed afterwards  
  > "until no more outliers were identified. Negative spikes were removed afterwards." (Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure line fluxes and normalise**: Slab fits of other molecules subtracted to limit blending; continuum-subtracted flux integrated over wavelength windows; H2O warm/cold individual lines selected  
  > "The flux of the continuum-subtracted spectrum is integrated in these wavelengths to determine the line flux." (Line fluxes); "We note that the slab fits were used solely for the purpose of removing other molecular spectral features; we do not derive any excitation properties from the slab fits." (Line fluxes)
   - parameter: Warm H2O lines 6000 <= Eup < 8000 K; cold lines Eup < 4000 K; 13 transitions in total  
  > "Following (cite: ref:23BaPoCa), we included a range of isolated warm lines (upper energies, $E_{\rm{up}}$, of $6000 \leq E_{\rm{up}} < 8000$ K) and isolated cold lines ($E_{\rm{up}} < 4000$ K)." (Line fluxes); "with a total of 13 individual transitions." (Line fluxes)
   - parameter: Carbon-species integration windows 14.936-15.014, 13.837-14.075, 13.61-13.751 um (species labels not recoverable from text)  
  > "14.936–15.014" (Table 2); "13.837-–14.075" (Table 2); "13.61–13.751" (Table 2)
   - parameter: Noise measured as standard deviation on the featureless spectrum around 15.9 um after slab subtraction  
  > "The noise is measured on the spectra around 15.9 $\mu$m directly after subtracting the rough slab fits" (Data reduction)
   - assumption: Fluxes rescaled to 140 pc; spectra divided by L_acc^0.6  
  > "when not considering line ratios, the fluxes are rescaled to a consistent distance of 140 pc" (Line fluxes); "scaled to a distance of 140 pc and divided by $L_\text{acc}^{0.6}$" (Spectra of disc sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Prepare ALMA visibilities (centre, self-calibrate, image)**: IMFIT for BP Tau geometry and phase offsets, FIXVIS to shift phase centres, self-calibration for three sources, Briggs imaging  
  > "For BP Tau, we estimated updated values using the CASA-task IMFIT by fitting an elliptical Gaussian." (ALMA observations); "and corrected for these offsets using the CASA-task FIXVIS." (ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Phase-only self-calibration, solint 'inf' then '30s', for CI Tau, DR Tau, IQ Tau  
  > "Therefore, we performed two rounds of phase-only self-calibration (solution intervals of ‘inf’ and ‘30s’) for these sources." (ALMA observations)
   - parameter: Briggs robust +0.5, threshold 1.5 x rms  
  > "We have imaged (down to a threshold of 1.5$\times$ the root-mean square) the dust continuum of all our sources using the ‘Briggs’ weighting scheme with a robust parameter of +0.5." (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Fit the visibilities with a set of Gaussians (radial brightness profile)**: Deprojected visibilities fitted with a sum of Gaussians modulated by cosines via Hankel transform, binned visibilities, MCMC  
  > "The model brightness distribution consists of a set of Gaussian functions" (Visibility fitting); "We bin the visibilities to speed up the fitting process, using the implementation of the FRANKENSTEIN-code (cite: JenningsEA20)" (Visibility fitting)
   - software: emcee, frankenstein
   - parameter: uv range limits: 2000 klambda (BP Tau, DL Tau, DR Tau, Sz 98, V1094 Sco), 3000 klambda (CI Tau, GW Lup), 1500 klambda (IQ Tau)  
  > "For BP Tau, DL Tau, DR Tau, Sz 98, and V1094 Sco we limit the visibilities to $\leq$2000 k$\lambda$, whereas we limit the visibilities of CI Tau and GW Lup to $\leq$3000 k$\lambda$." (Visibility fitting); "Finally, the visibilities of IQ Tau are limited to $\leq$1500 k$\lambda$." (Visibility fitting)
   - parameter: Binned visibility widths of 10-20 klambda  
  > "the binned visibilities have widths of 1-2$\times$10$^{4}$ $\lambda$ or 10-20 k$\lambda$." (Visibility fitting)
   - parameter: Only continuum spectral windows for DR Tau, GW Lup, Sz 98 (lower uncertainties) and DL Tau; all spectral windows (lines masked) for the others  
  > "In the cases of DR Tau, GW Lup, and Sz 98, we found that the uncertainties of the visibilities of the continuum spectral windows were significantly lower than those when including all spectral windows." (Visibility fitting)
   - parameter: Minimum number of additional Gaussians giving a good fit to visible features  
  > "For our sample, we report the minimum of additional Gaussian functions that results in a good fit to all the visible features in the visibilities." (Visibility fitting)
   - parameter: MCMC iterations: 2500 (round 1), 10,000 (round 2), final 5000 used for posterior medians  
  > "in the first round, we used 2500 iterations to explore the prior space, identifying the best-fit parameters." (Visibility fitting); "In the second round, we used 10,000 iterations to explore a confined parameter space around these best-fit parameters." (Visibility fitting); "From this second round, we use a final 5000 iterations to identify the median values of the posterior distributions." (Visibility fitting)
   - assumption: Literature inclinations and position angles (BP Tau from IMFIT)  
  > "we use the literature values while fitting the visibilities" (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC; medians with 16th and 84th percentile uncertainties; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "We fit the visibilities using the Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) implementation of the EMCEE-package (cite: emcee)." (Visibility fitting); "The lower and upper uncertainties are, respectively, taken to be the 16$^\textnormal{th}$ and 84$^\textnormal{th}$ percentiles." (Visibility fitting)
   - validation: Convolving the visibility-fit profile with a beam-like Gaussian reproduces the image-plane profile  
  > "Inferring a model profile by convolving the visibility fit with a Gaussian beam, tailored after the ALMA beam, results in a similar profile as that of the ALMA image plane." (Radial profiles and snowlines)
6. **Derive gap, ring and outer disc radii from the fitted profile**: Gaps and rings as local minima and maxima of the radial profile; gap depth as ratio to the adjacent ring; disc radii from curve of growth (68, 90, 95%)  
  > "Gaps and rings are identified as local minima and maxima in the radial profile in the visibility plane" (ALMA); "the gap depth is defined as the ratio of the gap intensity" (Characterising the millimetre continuum emission); "We also determine the continuum disc radius ($R_\textnormal{disc}$) using a curve-of-growth method" (Characterising the millimetre continuum emission)
   - parameter: R_gap reported as the innermost gap; outer dust radius encloses 90% of mm emission  
  > "We only document the distance of the gap located closest to the star in Table [\[tab:sample\]](#tab:sample) under $R_\text{gap}$" (ALMA); "the value that encompasses 90% of the millimetre emission is assumed (cite: ref:23MiKaBi)" (ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with earlier fits of the same discs; new or weak substructures flagged for caution  
  > "As these substructures are not identified by (cite: ref:22JeBoTa), we propose to treat these potential substructures with caution." (Comparison with previous works)
7. **Estimate snowline locations**: Midplane temperature power law from stellar plus accretion luminosity with fixed flaring angle, evaluated at species freeze-out temperatures  
  > "These are calculated as in (cite: ref:18LoPiHe), using" (Snowline estimates)
   - parameter: Freeze-out temperature ranges per species from Table 4 (values lost in text)  
  > "Freeze-out temperatures of selected species used to estimate snowline locations." (Table 4 caption)
   - assumption: Flaring angle 0.05  
  > "assuming a flaring angle of $\phi_{\text{inc}}=0.05$ (cite: ref:04DuDo)" (Snowline estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Snowlines not computed for SY Cha and PDS 70 because simple power laws no longer hold  
  > "since simple power laws no longer hold for these discs where the inner disc is very small and/or weak." (Snowline estimates)
8. **Correlate line fluxes with outer dust disc radius**: Pearson correlation coefficient and p value, excluding SY Cha and PDS 70  
  > "we use the Pearson correlation coefficient (PCC) and corresponding p value from" (Correlation coefficient and p value); "When calculating the PCC, we do not include SY Cha and PDS 70, as these are outliers in terms of disc structure." (Correlation coefficient and p value)
   - software: scipy
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Authors caution that the sample is too small for conclusive results  
  > "However, we note that these quantities must be used with some caution, as the sample size is too small to give conclusive results." (Correlation coefficient and p value)
9. **Compare each disc with a DR Tau spectral template and the H2O column-density line pair**: Rescale spectra to 140 pc and DR Tau to each disc via warm H2O line strength; compare cold-line quadruplet; compare H2O line-pair ratio with slab-model ratios  
  > "we followed the approach in (cite: ref:23BaPoCa), and rescaled all the spectra to a consistent distance of 140 pc" (DR Tau as a spectral template); "Similarly to (cite: ref:sz98), the line ratios are compared to the ratio in slab models of varying temperatures to find what column density the line ratio corresponds to." (Line fluxes)
   - assumption: DR Tau used as template as it is well studied and relatively well characterised  
  > "As it is well studied and its emission relatively well characterised, we used DR Tau as a template to compare to the emission of the other discs." (DR Tau as a spectral template)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- A dust gap does not necessarily result in weak inner-disc H2O emission  
  > "We find that the presence of a dust gap does not necessarily result in weak emission." (abstract)
- Relative lack of cold H2O goes with elevated carbon-species emission  
  > "Furthermore, the relative lack of colder -emission seems to go hand in hand with elevated emission from carbon-bearing species." (abstract)
- New ALMA substructures found: a gap at 16 au in V1094 Sco and a small inner cavity in BP Tau  
  > "reporting a new gap at 16 au for V1094 Sco, and a small inner cavity in BP Tau." (ALMA)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Highest-resolution public ALMA data to see substructure close to the star  
  > "As we are interested in the substructures closest to the host star" (ALMA observations); "we have retrieved all the highest available spatial resolution ALMA observations publicly available for our sources" (ALMA observations)
- Why this method: Consistent re-analysis and visibility-plane fitting reveals structure not visible in images  
  > "Therefore, to ensure all radii and substructures are retrieved in a consistent manner for all discs, the analysis has been redone here using the highest resolution archival data available." (ALMA); "we have fitted the visibilities to infer potentially ‘hidden’ substructures in the discs that cannot be retrieved from the cleaned images." (Visibility fitting)
- Alternative: Dust transport is not the only explanation for differing emission strength; gas-to-dust ratio or dust opacity changes could also enhance emission  
  > "Furthermore, radial transport of dust is not the only possible explanation for differences in emitting strength of species between discs." (Introduction)
- How they decided it was enough: Minimum number of extra Gaussians that fits all visible features in the visibilities  
  > "For our sample, we report the minimum of additional Gaussian functions that results in a good fit to all the visible features in the visibilities." (Visibility fitting)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample limits statistical conclusions  
  > "However, we note that these quantities must be used with some caution, as the sample size is too small to give conclusive results." (Correlation coefficient and p value)
- Limitation (authors): Resolution differences between targets may change apparent gap depth  
  > "Some substructures that appear to be much deeper in one disc compared to the other, may simply be a result of a resolution difference." (Gap depth and resolution)
- Limitation (authors): Ages are highly uncertain  
  > "Ages are generally very uncertain and may be based on different estimation methods." (Sample)
- Limitation (authors): Lowest-resolution ALMA data give larger residuals; higher resolution would improve fits  
  > "Higher resolution observations will likely provide better fits and cleaner residuals." (Characterising the millimetre continuum emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only ten discs with heterogeneous ALMA resolution (0.03 to 0.16 arcsec); gap depths are not comparable across targets
- Caveat (inferred, ours): PDS 70 and SY Cha have no new ALMA fit, yet are discussed as a group
- Caveat (inferred, ours): MCMC priors and likelihood are not given, so uncertainty on gap positions cannot be reproduced
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No statistical test of the claimed cold-H2O versus carbon dichotomy beyond visual colour-coding
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Correlation values with R_dust are only in figures, not tabulated
