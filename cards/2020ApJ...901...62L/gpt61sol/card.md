(Archival data and their analysis)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust parameter 0.5; beam 1.65″ × 1.14″; pixels 0.2″ × 0.2″  
  > "with Briggs robust parameter 0.5, achieving a beam size of 1$\,.\!\!^{\prime\prime}$65$\times$1$\,.\!\!^{\prime\prime}$14 and setting a pixel size of 0$\,.\!\!^{\prime\prime}$2$\times$0$\,.\!\!^{\prime\prime}$2." (Archival data and their analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Separate continuum and line emission**: Produce continuum and continuum-subtracted line cube with STATCONT; extract the three para-H2CS line cubes  
  > "From the corrected image, a continuum image and a continuum subtracted line cube were simultaneously produced using the python-based tool STATCONT[^2] (cite: Sanchez2018Statcont)." (Archival data and their analysis); "were then extracted from the continuum subtracted line cube." (Archival data and their analysis)
   - software: STATCONT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Para-H2CS morphology consistent with continuum emission  
  > "the morphology of the H$_2$CS triplet is consistent with the continuum emission" (Spatial distribution of H$_2$CS)
3. **Map averaged integrated-intensity ratios**: Compute R = 0.5×[(7_25–6_24 + 7_26–6_25)/(7_07–6_06)] from velocity-integrated intensities; use equal-weight regional averages  
  > "the averaged ratio para-H$_2$CS 0.5$\times[(7_{25}-6_{24}+7_{26}-6_{25})/7_{07}-6_{06}$\] between para-H$_2$CS $7_{25}-6_{24}/7_{07}-6_{06}$ and $7_{26}-6_{25}/7_{07}-6_{06}$ is used in this work." (H$_2$CS line ratios); "Averages were calculated, taking each line ratio value with the same weight." (H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - parameter: Signal-to-noise ratio ~30 threshold for the ratio maps  
  > "detected above a signal-to-noise ratio of $\sim$30 and derived from velocity integrated intensities in the Orion KL region" (H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - assumption: The two higher-excitation lines have similar upper-state energies and line profiles  
  > "have almost identical upper state energies above the ground state ($E_{\rm u}/k\,=\,98.8$ K)" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios); "similar observed line profiles." (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - fitting: Integrated line intensity fits; fitting model not stated  
  > "The uncertainties estimated here are exclusively based on the errors of the integrated line intensity fits" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Assess spatial filtering**: Compare ALMA triplet fluxes with APEX 12-m data and estimate the possible ratio overestimate  
  > "compared to the single-dish APEX 12-m data (beam size $\sim$26$''$, (cite: Brinkmann2020))" (H$_2$CS line ratios); "the fluxes recovered by ALMA data are $\sim$70%, $\sim$90%, and $\sim$90% for para-H$_2$CS $7_{07}-6_{06}$, $7_{25}-6_{24}$, and $7_{26}-6_{25}$, respectively." (H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - parameter: Maximum recoverable scale ~8.7″  
  > "Since the maximum recoverable scale is $\sim$8$\,.\!\!^{\prime\prime}$7" (H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - assumption: Missing fluxes ~30%, ~10%, ~10% for 7_07–6_06, 7_25–6_24, 7_26–6_25  
  > "the missing flux densities are assumed to be $\sim$30%, $\sim$10%, and $\sim$10% for para-H$_2$CS $7_{07}-6_{06}$, $7_{25}-6_{24}$, and $7_{26}-6_{25}$, respectively." (H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Nearly identical compact structures suggest ratios are weakly influenced by filtered emission  
  > "The para-H$_2$CS triplet traces compact emission regions, and exhibits nearly identical structures in Orion KL" (H$_2$CS line ratios); "the line ratios of para-H$_2$CS may only be weakly influenced by filtered out emission." (H$_2$CS line ratios)
5. **Infer kinetic temperatures**: Use RADEX non-LTE simulations to convert averaged para-H2CS ratios into kinetic temperatures  
  > "The kinetic temperatures are derived from the measured averaged velocity-integrated intensity ratios of para-H$_2$CS $7_{26}-6_{25}/7_{07}-6_{06}$ and $7_{25}-6_{24}/7_{07}-6_{06}$ with RADEX non-LTE modeling" (Conclusions)
   - software: RADEX, Leiden Atomic and Molecular Database
   - parameter: Line width Δv = 5 km s^-1 from these data  
  > "an adopted line width $\Delta v$ = 5 km s$^{-1}$ from our data" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - parameter: Collision rates derived by (cite: Wiesenfeld2013)  
  > "coupled with collision rates from the Leiden Atomic and Molecular Database[^4] derived by (cite: Wiesenfeld2013)" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - assumption: Optically thin triplet; n_H2 = 10^7 cm^-3; N_p-H2CS = 5×10^14 cm^-2  
  > "Under the assumption of optically thin conditions for the para-H$_2$CS (7–6) triplet, $n_{\rm H_2}=10^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$, and $N_{\rm p-H_2CS}=5\times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - assumption: Background temperature 2.73 K  
  > "a 2.73 K background temperature" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - assumption: Regional temperatures converted from equal-weight mean line ratios  
  > "The average values of gas kinetic temperatures are directly derived from the averaged values of the para-H$_2$CS (7–6) line ratios (taking each line ratio value with the same weight)" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - fitting: Temperature uncertainty: (dT_k/dR)×1/(S/N)×sqrt(0.5+R^2); derivative calculated numerically  
  > "Uncertainties of the gas temperature can be estimated from formula ($\mathrm{d}T_{\rm k}/\mathrm{d}{R})\times 1/(S/N)\times\sqrt{0.5+{R}^{2}}$" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios); "which can be calculated numerically." (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
   - validation: Explore density and column-density dependence; compare temperatures from the two ratios; check modeled optical depths  
  > "nearly independent of the H$_2$ volume density ($n_{\rm H_2}$) and para-H$_2$CS column density ($N_{\rm p-H_2CS}$) at $n_{\rm H_2}\,\gtrsim\,10^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ and $N_{\rm p-H_2CS}\,\lesssim\,6\times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ per km s$^{-1}$." (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios); "The resulting gas kinetic temperatures derived from para-H$_2$CS $7_{25}-6_{24}$/$7_{07}-6_{06}$ and $7_{26}-6_{25}$/$7_{07}-6_{06}$ are similar at $n_{\rm H_2} = 10^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ and $N_{\rm p-H_2CS} = 5\times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$" (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios); "The optical depths for $N_{\rm p-H_2CS}=5\times10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ are less than 0.3 for para-H$_2$CS $7_{07}-6_{06}$ and less than 0.09 for para-H$_2$CS $7_{25}-6_{24}$ and $7_{26}-6_{25}$ when the gas temperature is greater than 43 K." (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
6. **Assess alternative thermometers**: Compare literature temperatures and tracer limitations; estimate NH3 (6,6) opacity with RADEX  
  > "we made an estimation of NH$_3$ (6,6) opacity $\tau_{(\rm NH_{3}(6,6))}$ \> 1 by running RADEX" (Gas temperatures); "Various limitations, e.g. opacity, sensitivity, and poorly constrained correlations between excitation and kinetic temperatures will affect the results." (Gas temperatures)

## Qualitative findings

- Dense gas traced by para-H2CS is closely associated with 1.2 mm dust emission  
  > "dense gas traced by para-H$_2$CS 7–6 is closely associated with the dust traced at 1.2 mm in the Orion KL region." (Spatial distribution of H$_2$CS)

  > "Our resulting $T_{\rm kin}$ values reach maxima near the supposed location of the explosive event and become lower farther away from it" (Gas temperatures)
- Explosion-driven shocks probably dominate heating outside the western compact ridge  
  > "with the notable exception of the western region of the compact ridge, the high temperatures of the dense gas in Orion KL are probably caused by shocks from the explosive event" (Abstract)
- Western compact-ridge heating is likely caused by internal massive star formation  
  > "the higher temperatures in the western compact ridge are likely caused by internal massive star formation activity." (Conclusions)
- IRc4 and IRc7 may enhance temperatures only in tiny confined regions  
  > "Furthermore, the kinetic temperature may also be enhanced by IRc4 and IRc7, but only in tiny, spatially highly confined regions." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Nearby-frequency H2CS transitions can be observed simultaneously, minimizing resolution, flux-calibration and pointing differences  
  > "these transitions can be easily covered within a single spectral band and be observed simultaneously." (H$_2$CS as a temperature probe); "This minimizes observational uncertainties, such as differences in spatial resolution, uncertainties in absolute flux calibration, and deviating pointing errors." (H$_2$CS as a temperature probe)
- Why this method: H2CS inter-ladder ratios probe kinetic temperature; at the adopted conditions their temperature calibration is nearly independent of density and column density  
  > "the relative populations of the $K_{\rm a}$ ladders of H$_2$CS are governed by collisions (cite: Mangum1993; Wooten2009; Oya2016; Van2020)." (H$_2$CS as a temperature probe); "nearly independent of the H$_2$ volume density ($n_{\rm H_2}$) and para-H$_2$CS column density ($N_{\rm p-H_2CS}$) at $n_{\rm H_2}\,\gtrsim\,10^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ and $N_{\rm p-H_2CS}\,\lesssim\,6\times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ per km s$^{-1}$." (Kinetic temperatures from H$_2$CS line ratios)
- Alternative: Src I outflow considered less important for heating the northern and southwestern elongated ridge because it is slow  
  > "However, this outflow is quite slow ($<$20 km s$^{-1}$), suggesting that the outflow may not play a significant role in heating the dense gas in the northern edge and southwestern corner of the elongated ridge region." (Impact of the explosive outflow)
- Alternative: NH3 traces lower-density gas and NH3 (6,6) may suffer saturation  
  > "it is a lower density gas tracer and is not exclusively probing the very dense gas with $\sim$10$^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ and even higher values in the Orion KL region." (Gas temperatures); "NH$_3$ (6,6), where the hyperfine satellite lines were not covered, is probably affected by line saturation." (Gas temperatures)
- Alternative: SO2 rotation temperatures need not equal kinetic temperatures  
  > "SO$_2$ is generally not thought to be a gas kinetic temperature tracer and the rotation temperatures derived may differ from the gas kinetic temperatures." (Gas temperatures)
- Alternative: Para-H2CO J=3–2 and 4–3 ratios insensitive above 150 K  
  > "line ratios of para-H$_2$CO $J$ = 3–2 and 4–3 are insensitive to detect temperatures in excess of 150 K (cite: Mangum1993; Immer2016; Tang2018b)." (Gas temperatures)
