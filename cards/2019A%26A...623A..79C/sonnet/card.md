## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO(3-2) cubes and continuum**: Calibrated, imaged and cleaned with CASA, then analysed with GILDAS; Briggs weighting robust 0.5; masked clean; continuum subtracted  
  > "The data were calibrated, imaged, and cleaned with the CASA software (versions 4.5.3 to 4.7.2; (cite: McMullin2007)) and the analysis was then finalized with the GILDAS software" (3 Observations)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - parameter: 10 km/s channels, 0.01'' pixels  
  > "have 60 channels of 10 km/s width" (3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Missing flux compared with cycle 0 for NGC 1433 and 1566 (~25% missing)  
  > "we are missing $\sim$ 25% of the total flux, on large angular scales" (3 Observations)
2. **Locate AGN position from continuum**: Adopt pixel of maximum continuum emission as nucleus  
  > "the center position adopted is the pixel of maximum continuum emission, determined with an error equal to the TM1 beam" (4.1 Continuum emission and AGN position)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure torus size, flux and H2 mass**: Ellipse fitting in CO(3-2) cube; L'CO from Solomon & Vanden Bout; M(H2)=4.36 L'CO R13  
  > "We have identified in the CO(3-2) data cube these components through ellipse-fitting with the adopted center as the continuum point source" (4.2 Masses of possible tori)
   - assumption: XCO = 2e20 cm-2 (K km/s)-1, R13 = 2  
  > "assuming the standard CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor of X$_{CO}$ = 2 10$^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$(K km/s)$^{-1}$" (4.2 Masses of possible tori)
   - fitting: Uncertainties from extrema of possible fits using moments 0 and 1  
  > "The uncertainties on the torus size are determined from the extrema of the possible fits, taking into account both moments zero and one" (4.2 Masses of possible tori)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Build stellar potential and prior BH mass from M-sigma**: S4G 3.6um galfit components with M/L=0.5; Kormendy & Ho M-sigma relation for first BH mass estimates  
  > "We adopt a mass-to-light ratio of M/L = 0.5 M$_\odot$ /L$_\odot$ for this 3.6 $\mu$m band" (4.4.1 Model of the gravitational potential)
   - software: galfit
   - assumption: Dark matter negligible within 100 pc  
  > "The potential of the dark matter halo is negligible inside radii of 100 pc." (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Model rotation curves compared to larger-scale Halpha/HI/CO curves  
  > "The quality of the potentials was checked by comparing at larger scales the modeled rotation curve with previous observations." (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)
5. **Fit BH mass from gas dynamics**: Particle disk simulations projected into model cubes normalized to observed moment 0; vary BH mass and torus inclination (about a dozen values each); minimize weighted least squares over PV diagram, moments and cube  
  > "The criterion is then to minimize the quantity: $\sum\limits_{pix} w (F_{obs}-F_{mod})^2 / \sum\limits_{pix} w F_{obs}^2$" (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)
   - parameter: 1 million particles, Toomre Q=1, nuclear region 120 pc diameter  
  > "We used typically one million particles to ensure sufficient statistics." (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)
   - assumption: Axisymmetric disks only; bar/spiral perturbations neglected  
  > "It should be noted that we simulate only axisymmetric disks." (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)
   - fitting: Least-squares grid exploration; best fit a compromise between PV diagram and velocity dispersion map  
  > "The best fit must therefore be a compromise between the PV diagram and the velocity dispersion map." (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)
   - validation: Method tested on NGC 1672; no-BH model compared  
  > "We tested the methodology on the most regular PV diagram obtained, that of NGC 1672" (4.4.2 Fitting the PV diagrams)

## Qualitative findings

- Six of seven galaxies host a decoupled nuclear molecular disk/torus; NGC 1433 does not  
  > "For 6 out of 7 galaxies, we indeed detect a nuclear disk, decoupled in morphology and dynamics from the main disk." (5 Discussion and summary)
- Tori are rarely aligned with the main disk  
  > "the gas in the molecular tori is rarely aligned with the main disk, but present different inclination and position angles" (5.1.1 Orientation of the tori)
- Galaxies tend to follow the pseudo-bulge M-sigma relation  
  > "they have the tendency to follow the relation obtained for pseudo-bulge galaxies" (4.4.3 Summary of results on black hole masses)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: CO(3-2) is the best compromise between spatial resolution, sensitivity and field of view  
  > "It appears as the best compromise between spatial resolution, sensitivity, and field of view." (3 Observations)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Non-circular motions in barred galaxies not modelled; torus decoupling and missing flux add uncertainty  
  > "We postpone to further work the simulation of the non-circular motions in these barred galaxies, including both small and large scale." (5.3 Black hole masses)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fits are a grid exploration with no formal likelihood or posterior
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Assumed XCO and R13 are uncertain for AGN nuclei
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Modeling of only axisymmetric disks in barred galaxies
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Missing flux from lack of short spacings
- Caveat (inferred, ours): M/L fixed at 0.5
