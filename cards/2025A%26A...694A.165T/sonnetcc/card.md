## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce, align and merge the Chandra observations**: Per-ObsID reduction with CIAO, flare filtering, astrometric alignment with wcs_match to the deepest ObsID, GAIA correction, and merge with reproject_obs  
  > "Data reduction was carried out for each individual Chandra ObsID using CIAO 4.15 and the Chandra Calibration Database (CALDB-4.10.2) installed on Python 3.10." (Data Reduction, Alignment and Merge of the Observations)
   - software: CIAO, wavdetect, Python
   - parameter: Event grades 0, 2, 3, 4, 6 retained  
  > "The event files were then filtered for standard event grades of 0, 2, 3, 4, and 6." (Data Reduction, Alignment and Merge of the Observations)
   - parameter: Reference ObsID for alignment: 25728 (deepest)  
  > "using as reference the deepest ObsID 25728" (Data Reduction, Alignment and Merge of the Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect X-ray sources in the MUSE field region**: wavdetect on the merged data in soft, broad and hard bands; catalogs combined into a master list  
  > "We used `wavdetect` to produce a complete catalog of Chandra X-ray sources in this specific region, minimizing false detections and missed detections." (X-ray sources detection)
   - software: wavdetect, CIAO
   - parameter: False-positive probability threshold 1e-5 (falsesrc=-1, sigthresh=1e-5)  
  > "setting the false-positive probability threshold to $10^{-5}$" (X-ray sources detection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Expected number of spurious detections within 6 arcmin radius is about 17  
  > "we expected $\approx 17$ detections of background fluctuations within 6 arcmin radius from the aim point" (X-ray sources detection)
3. **Derive flux conversion factors and sky coverage**: Simulate spectra with fake_pha in Sherpa for ARFs/RMFs at the aim point to get count-rate to flux conversion factors; build sky-coverage curves with an S/N>2 completeness criterion  
  > "we adopted a completeness criterion of $S/N>2$, considering the observational solid angle within which ideal X-ray sources are detected above a specific flux." (Sky Coverage and Number of Counts)
   - software: Sherpa, CIAO
   - parameter: Power-law photon index Gamma between 1.2 and 1.9  
  > "The spectral emission is assumed to follow a power-law distribution with a photon index ($\Gamma$) ranging between $1.2$ and $1.9$" (Sky Coverage and Number of Counts)
   - parameter: One hundred simulated high-S/N spectra per ObsID  
  > "to simulate one hundred high-S/N spectra of an X-ray source positioned at the aim point" (Sky Coverage and Number of Counts)
   - assumption: Flux limits defined at minimum solid angle 2e-3 deg^2 (one tenth of the full FoV)  
  > "defined as those observed within a minimum solid angle $\Omega_{\rm min} = 2 \times 10^{-3} ~\rm deg^2$ equivalent to one tenth of the full FoV" (Sky Coverage and Number of Counts)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Number counts compared with the AGN+Galaxy model prediction  
  > "The black lines are the prediction from the AGN+Galaxy model developed in (cite: Gilli07)" (Sky Coverage and Number of Counts)
4. **Cross-match X-ray sources with MUSE and ALMA protocluster members**: Positional association between the Chandra catalog and spectroscopic members, with the protocluster redshift set from the ALMA CO(4-3) QSO redshift  
  > "The X-ray emission is associated with a specific galaxy if the centroid offset is less than 0.5 arcseconds." (Cross-Matching Chandra Data with Multi-Wavelength Datasets)
   - parameter: Match radius 0.5 arcsec  
  > "if the centroid offset is less than 0.5 arcseconds" (Cross-Matching Chandra Data with Multi-Wavelength Datasets)
   - parameter: Velocity window +-1000 km/s of the QSO redshift  
  > "All six X-ray detections are associated with galaxies that were spectroscopically-selected with MUSE and ALMA, within a velocity range of" (Cross-Matching Chandra Data with Multi-Wavelength Datasets)
   - assumption: Protocluster redshift equals that of the central QSO CTS G18.01 from CO(4-3)  
  > "The redshift of the protocluster is set to that of the QSO CTS G18.01" (Cross-Matching Chandra Data with Multi-Wavelength Datasets)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Fit X-ray spectra of the six protocluster AGN to get N_H, Gamma and L(2-10 keV)**: XSPEC fit of an absorbed power law with C-statistic; unabsorbed 2-10 keV luminosity from the clumin convolution model  
  > "We adopted a simple model `tbabs*(zphabs*pow)` consisting on a power-law convolved with a Galactic and intrinsic absorption." (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster)
   - software: XSPEC, CIAO
   - parameter: Galactic N_H = 1.18e20 cm^-2  
  > "The Galactic absorption `tbabs` is set to $N_{H,Gal} = 1.18 \times 10^{20}~\rm cm^{-2}$ according with the NASA HEASARC tool" (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster)
   - parameter: Gamma fixed to 1.8 for sources with fewer than 100 net counts  
  > "For the rest of the sources, we assumed an intrinsic power-law slope $\Gamma = 1.8$" (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster)
   - assumption: Fe K-alpha line at 6.4 keV tried for ID1 and ID2 but removed as poorly constrained  
  > "In all cases, we observed that the iron K-$\alpha$ line is poorly constrained and, therefore, it was removed from the spectral model." (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster)
   - fitting: XSPEC C-statistic fits, 90% confidence uncertainties  
  > "For the spectral fitting analysis we used `XSPEC`, by setting a C-statistic (cite: Arnaud96)." (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster)
   - validation: All six sources exceed log L(2-10 keV) = 43.5, taken to classify them as AGN  
  > "We observe that all X-ray protocluster members exhibit $\rm \log(L_{2-10 ~keV}/erg~s^{-1}) > 43.5$" (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster)
6. **Compute the cumulative hard X-ray luminosity function and AGN overdensity vs the field**: 1/Vmax space density of AGN with L>L_X in a 16 cMpc^2, +-1000 km/s volume (V16); overdensity delta16 = (phi_prot - phi_field)/phi_field using the Gilli07 field HXLF  
  > "using the $\rm 1/V_{max}$ method (cite: Schmidt68; Avni80; Vito14)" (AGN Overdensity in Protoclusters: An Excess of the Brightest AGNs in MQN01)
   - parameter: V16: A_max = 16 cMpc^2, delta v_max = 1000 km/s; centered on the most luminous QSO (QSO excluded)  
  > "this volume has a cross-sectional area of $A_{max} = 16~\rm cMpc^2$, corresponding to the MUSE FOV, and a velocity range of $\Delta v_{max} = 1000~\rm km~s^{-1}$" (AGN Overdensity in Protoclusters: An Excess of the Brightest AGNs in MQN01)
   - assumption: No incompleteness correction applied  
  > "To be conservative we did not apply the incompleteness correction as done in (cite: Vito18)." (AGN Overdensity in Protoclusters: An Excess of the Brightest AGNs in MQN01)
   - fitting: Uncertainties from iterations varying AGN number (Poisson) and luminosities (Gaussian within 90% limits)  
  > "we performed iterations of this calculation by randomly varying both the number of detected X-ray AGNs according to the Poisson statistics" (AGN Overdensity in Protoclusters: An Excess of the Brightest AGNs in MQN01)
   - validation: Compared with the Spiderweb and SSA22 HXLF in the same volume; Poisson intervals from Gehrels86  
  > "According to (cite: Gehrels86), the Poissonian intervals corresponding to a 1$\sigma$ Gaussian confidence level for these numbers of AGNs are" (AGN Spatial Distribution within Protoclusters)
7. **Measure the AGN fraction as a function of stellar-mass cut**: f_AGN = fraction of spectroscopic member galaxies above a M* cut that host an X-ray AGN, compared with Spiderweb (three M*/SFR estimates); QSOs excluded  
  > "we defined $f_{AGN}$ as a function of the stellar mass cut ($M_{*}^{cut}$) of the galaxy sample used to estimate it." (AGN Fraction in MQN01: Ubiquity of Accreting SMBHs in Massive Galaxies)
   - software: statsmodels
   - parameter: 68% binomial errors with Jeffreys Bayesian intervals  
  > "To estimate the 68$\%$ binomial error for $f_{AGN}$, we employed Jeffrey’s Bayesian statistics using the Python tool `statsmodels`." (AGN Fraction in MQN01: Ubiquity of Accreting SMBHs in Massive Galaxies)
   - assumption: Both protoclusters assumed to have nearly complete selections of massive star-forming galaxies  
  > "assuming that both protoclusters have nearly complete selections of massive star-forming galaxies" (AGN Fraction in MQN01: Ubiquity of Accreting SMBHs in Massive Galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeated for star-forming galaxies only (within 0.3 dex of the main sequence); conclusions largely unchanged  
  > "The results and conclusions from this analysis are largely unchanged." (AGN Fraction in MQN01: Ubiquity of Accreting SMBHs in Massive Galaxies)
8. **Estimate specific BH accretion rates and their trend with distance from the center**: lambda_sBHAR = k_bol L_X / (1.3e38 x 0.002 x M*); PDFs by Gaussian-kernel KDE with bootstrap resampling of L_X and M*; Pearson and Spearman correlation with projected distance from the central QSO  
  > "we adopted the non-parametric Kernel Density Estimation (KDE) method (cite: Moon95)" (Impact of the Environment on the SMBH accretion rates)
   - software: anderson_ksamp
   - parameter: k_bol = 25  
  > "we adopted an average value $k_\text{bol}=25$, as used in (cite: Aird18)" (Impact of the Environment on the SMBH accretion rates)
   - parameter: Bootstrap: vary L_X and M* within 1 sigma  
  > "randomly varying the parameters $L_X$ and $M_*$ within their respective 1$\sigma$ uncertainties" (Impact of the Environment on the SMBH accretion rates)
   - assumption: M_BH = 0.002 x M*  
  > "the assumption that $M_{BH}$ is equal to $0.002 \times M_*$ according to the relationship found by (cite: Haring04)" (Impact of the Environment on the SMBH accretion rates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the field sBHAR distributions of Aird18; authors caution about sample size  
  > "However, this comparison should be taken with caution as it could be affected by several factors" (Impact of the Environment on the SMBH accretion rates)

## Qualitative findings

- AGN velocity distribution in MQN01 is more compact than in SSA22 and Spiderweb; the radial AGN and galaxy profiles are statistically indistinguishable  
  > "The AGN velocity distribution in MQN01 appears being more compact compared to those in SSA22 and Spiderweb" (AGN Spatial Distribution within Protoclusters); "The results were inconclusive: in over 89$\%$ of the cases, we could not reject the hypothesis that the two samples originate from the same distribution" (AGN Spatial Distribution within Protoclusters)
- The presence of the protocluster does not show up in the X-ray number counts  
  > "We noticed that the presence of a protocluster does not impact the number counts." (Sky Coverage and Number of Counts)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Spectroscopic ALMA and MUSE members provide the protocluster galaxy sample against which Chandra X-ray sources are matched  
  > "Our analysis of X-ray Chandra data will leverage on the catalog of protocluster members spectroscopically selected through ALMA and MUSE observations." (Ancillary data)
- Why this method: X-rays are used because they penetrate obscuring gas around AGN in dense environments  
  > "X-ray observations are crucial for probing AGNs in these environments, as X-ray photons can penetrate high column density gas." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Fe K-alpha line tried then removed from the spectral model; incompleteness correction not applied to be conservative  
  > "In all cases, we observed that the iron K-$\alpha$ line is poorly constrained and, therefore, it was removed from the spectral model." (Spectroscopic Analysis of X-ray Sources in the MQN01 Protocluster); "To be conservative we did not apply the incompleteness correction as done in (cite: Vito18)." (AGN Overdensity in Protoclusters: An Excess of the Brightest AGNs in MQN01)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Different member selections and spectroscopic coverage across the three protoclusters make the comparison uncertain  
  > "prevent the possibility to use consistent physical method to derive comparable volumes, and imply that the comparison should be taken with some caution." (Comparative Analyses: AGN population in MQN01, SSA22 and Spiderweb protoclusters)
- Limitation (authors): Small number statistics (Poisson uncertainties) limit conclusions on environmental effects  
  > "In the analyses presented in this paper, Poissonian uncertainties provide a challenge for the determination of the role of environmental factors and galaxy properties on SMBH accretion and growth." (Summary and Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA plays a minor role (redshifts and member identification); the headline results rest on Chandra and on unpublished MUSE member and SED catalogs
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only six AGN in a volume chosen to maximise the MQN01 count, and for SSA22 and Spiderweb the volume was centered to maximise AGN, so overdensities may be biased high
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison with other protoclusters mixes heterogeneous selections and M*/SFR methods
- Caveat (inferred, ours): delta16 uses a single field HXLF (Gilli07) with no incompleteness correction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): MQN01 centered on a QSO by construction, so overdensity and radial trends may reflect that choice
