## Analysis recipe

1. **Image line cube and continuum**: Clean with Briggs weighting; line from one SPW, continuum from the other three; uv-taper version also made  
  > "The CO (6-5) line data cube was generated using the data in the SPW covering the sky frequency range of 680.975 to 682.975 GHz." (2 Observation)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; outertaper = 0.4"  
  > "Briggs weighting (with the parameter “robust” set to 0.5)" (2 Observation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Moment maps and total fluxes**: Moment 0 integrated 3971-4157 km/s; moments 1 and 2 with 4 sigma_ch threshold on uv-tapered image  
  > "We set the threshold at 4-$\sigma_{\rm ch}$, where $\sigma_{\rm ch}$ is the r.m.s noise per frequency channel," (3.1 CO (6-5) Line Emission)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Radio velocity convention nu_obs = nu_rest(1 - V/c)  
  > "$\nu_{\rm obs} = \nu_{\rm rest}\,(1 - V/c)$" (3.1 CO (6-5) Line Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Herschel total flux  
  > "Therefore the clump regions in Fig. 1a together account for $\sim$32% of the total CO (6-5) flux of the galaxy." (3.1 CO (6-5) Line Emission)
3. **AGN upper limits**: 3-sigma upper limits at AGN position (radio peak) for CO and continuum; SED comparison  
  > "then the 3-$\sigma$ flux upper limit is equal to 3 $\times$ (1.2 Jy  km s$^{-1}$) = 3.6 Jy  km s$^{-1}$" (4.1 The Central AGN)
   - assumption: AGN emission confined to area smaller than beam  
  > "We assume the AGN-related ${\mathrm{CO}}\,(6\text{--}5)$ emission is confined to an area smaller than our ALMA beam size" (4.1 The Central AGN)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with AGN SED template  
  > "This plot suggests that the ALMA data points are consistent with what is expected from the typical infrared SED for AGNs like NGC 5135." (4.1 The Central AGN)
4. **Clump parameters**: 2D Gaussian fit to clump maps; CLUMPFIND variant for blends; deconvolve beam; extract spectra in elliptical apertures  
  > "These were derived from a 2d Gaussian fit to the clump intensity map." (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
   - software: CASA, CLUMPFIND
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian  
  > "These were derived from a 2d Gaussian fit to the clump intensity map." (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Virial and molecular gas masses**: M_vir = 5 dV^2 R / G; M_mol from 435 um flux via Scoville et al. 2016 and from CO flux with alpha_CO  
  > "the Virial mass ($M_{vir}$) is estimated as" (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
   - parameter: dV deconvolved with 13.5 km/s channel; 10 km/s rotation removed; flux x1.731 for total  
  > "Thus, we multiply the flux density by 1.731 to estimate the total flux." (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
   - assumption: T_d = 25 K  
  > "assuming a dust temperature of the 25 K" (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8; CO(6-5)/CO(1-0) ~ 2  
  > "The ${\mathrm{CO}}\,(6\text{--}5)$ /${\mathrm{CO}}\,(1\text{--}0)$ is about 2" (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Dust-derived M_mol consistent with CO-derived  
  > "The molecular gas mass derived this way is consistent with the one derived from the dust continuum as shown in Column (12) of Table 2." (4.2 Properties of Molecular Gas Clumps)
6. **Compare CO/continuum ratio with [FeII]/Br-gamma and other-wavelength images**: Plot R_CO/cont vs [FeII]/Br-gamma for clumps; overlay on PAH, Pa-alpha, HST images  
  > "we show a plot of the \[Fe ii\]-to-Br-$\gamma$ line ratio versus the CO (6-5)-to-continuum flux density ratio for all the clumps listed in Table 2" (4.3.1 SN-powered Shock Heating Scenario)
   - assumption: HST images aligned by matching adopted AGN position with brightest point  
  > "The *HST* images aligned with our CO (6-5) data by matching our adopted AGN position with the brightest point in each optical image." (4.3.2 Bar-Induced Shock Heating Scenario)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 9 gives high-resolution CO(6-5) and dust continuum to test Herschel results down to GMC scales  
  > "it is now possible to obtain high-resolution mid-$J$ CO line and dust continuum images of the nuclei of nearby LIRGs" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Bar-induced shock heating considered as alternative, but cannot explain IC 5179 which lacks a strong bar  
  > "it is unclear how this bar-induced shock heating scenario for the CO (6-5) emission can be made to explain the similar variation in the CO (6-5)-to-continuum flux ratio seen in IC 5179" (4.3.2 Bar-Induced Shock Heating Scenario)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): AGN continuum non-detection may reflect lack of sensitivity  
  > "the non-detection in continuum emission may simply reflect the lack of sensitivity in our observation." (5 Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Correlation of R_CO/cont with [FeII]/Br-gamma uses only ~6 clumps and is not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature fixed at 25 K though likely warmer
- Caveat (inferred, ours): AGN position uncertain by 0.3-0.5 arcsec, comparable to several beams
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Short 21-minute observation; resolved-out flux uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Alternative heating (PDR, bar shocks, cosmic rays) not ruled out quantitatively
