## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image SMA and ALMA data to common resolution**: Calibrate with Uranus, correct 0.8" shift, subtract continuum, uv-taper all data to 4.2"x2.1"; merge SMA 3-2 data with Sakamoto data  
  > "We hence consider this effect as a systematic instrumental and calibrational artifact and correct the data for this $\sim$0.8$''$ shift." (2.1)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared integrated fluxes with CSO and SEST single-dish fluxes  
  > "Hence, the SMA observations did not resolve out significant emission from the $^{12}$CO(J=3–2)  and $^{12}$CO(J=6–5)  lines" (2.4)
2. **Fit multi-Gaussian line profiles and derive line ratios**: Up to three Gaussian components fitted to spectra at peaks and integrated profiles; centers and widths fixed from 3-2 and 2-1 fits; intensities converted to K for brightness temperature ratios  
  > "We fitted a multiple-component (up to three) Gaussian to all line profiles." (3.2.1)
   - fitting: Gaussian centres/FWHM fixed from lower transitions  
  > "The so found values have been averaged and subsequently fixed for each fit." (Table 3 notes)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive dust temperature and opacity from continuum SED**: Flux-ratio equation with chi2 fit to 220, 350, 686 GHz peak fluxes  
  > "Using the flux densities of the emission at 220 GHz[^4], 350 GHz and 686 GHz, this allows us to derive the opacities and dust temperatures in the different peaks" (Temperature, opacity and mass of the dust)
   - parameter: Td 2.5-150 K; tau220 0.02-1  
  > "varied the dust temperatures in the range of 2.5 K up to 150 K and opacities at 220 GHz between 0.02 and 1" (Temperature, opacity and mass of the dust)
   - assumption: beta=1.8; free-free/synchrotron <5% at 220 GHz  
  > "Contributions from the free-free/synchrotron emission can be neglected here as they are less than 5%" (footnote 4)
   - fitting: chi2 grid  
  > "this allows us to derive the opacities and dust temperatures in the different peaks, as shown in Fig.[3](#fig2.5) along with the $\chi^2$-fits." (Temperature, opacity and mass of the dust)
   - validation: beta 1.5 and 2.1 also tested; 1.8 best  
  > "but the best fit solutions were found for a value of 1.8" (footnote 5)
4. **Estimate dust masses and gas-to-dust ratio**: Mdust = S D^2/(kappa B(nu,Td)) with kappa158um 0.6-3 m2/g  
  > "Assuming $\beta$=1.8 and $\kappa_{158\mu\rm
      m}\simeq$0.6-3 m$^2$/g" (Temperature, opacity and mass of the dust)
   - assumption: D=3.5 Mpc, Td=25 K for global disk  
  > "for a dust temperature of T$_{\rm d}$=25 K" (Temperature, opacity and mass of the dust)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Remy-Ruyer 2014 gas-to-dust values  
  > "The gas-to-dust mass ratios are consistent with the values ($\sim$100-1000) usually found in active galaxies with metallicities around solar (cite: remy2014)." (Temperature, opacity and mass of the dust)
5. **LVG excitation modelling of CO ladder**: RADEX grid with reduced chi2 minimization; one- and two-temperature models for disk, east and west shells  
  > "We conducted an LVG analysis with the RADEX code (cite: vdt07) applying a reduced $\chi^2$ minimization method" (LVG Analysis)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: Tkin 10-400 K (10 K steps), nH2 1e3-1e8, NCO 1e14-1e19 (0.1 dex), dv=100 km/s, uniform sphere; acceptable chi2_red<=1.5; cold component <=150 K  
  > "All reduced $\chi^2_{\rm red}$ results for which the modelled line ratios are within at least $\sim$1-1.5$\sigma$ of the observed line ratios (i.e., $\chi^2_{\rm red}\leq1.5$) were considered as acceptable solutions." (LVG Analysis)
   - fitting: grid chi2  
  > "applying a reduced $\chi^2$ minimization method to fit the basic excitation conditions of the molecular gas in the western and eastern shells and the (averaged) disk." (LVG Analysis)
   - validation: Compared with Rosenberg 2014 and Hailey-Dunsheath 2008  
  > "This is in agreement with previous findings on the CO line SED of NGC 253 by different groups based on single-dish and/or satellite ( *Herschel*) observations (cite: ros14; hail08)." (LVG Analysis)

## Qualitative findings

- 12CO(6-5) follows the distribution of lower-J CO along the disk with little ratio variation; only marginal emission at the shells  
  > "Only marginal $^{12}$CO(J=6–5)  emission is detected in the vicinity of the expanding shells at the eastern and western edges of the disk." (abstract)
