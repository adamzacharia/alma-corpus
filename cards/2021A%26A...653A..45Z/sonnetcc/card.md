## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure source sizes from ALMA 213 GHz continuum**: Effective radius from 2D Gaussians fitted to continuum maps; H2(13)CO contours compared with continuum  
  > "We derived the effective radius of the sources based on the 2D Gaussians fitted to the continuum maps." (3.1 Line spectra of deuterated H$_2$CO species)
   - parameter: Source sizes 3” - 5.8”; 6” kept where no ALMA detection  
  > "The derived source sizes are between 3” and 5.8”." (3.1 Line spectra of deuterated H$_2$CO species)
   - assumption: 6” source size for sources without ALMA data or detection  
  > "We kept the assumption of 6” source size for these five objects in our calculations." (3.1 Line spectra of deuterated H$_2$CO species)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Reduce and smooth the APEX and IRAM spectra**: Reduction and analysis with GILDAS; spectra smoothed to 0.5 km/s  
  > "However, for the data analysis, we smoothed the spectra to a uniform velocity resolution of 0.5 km/s." (2.3 APEX SEPIA observations); "The data were reduced and analysed with the Gildas software" (2.3 APEX SEPIA observations)
   - software: GILDAS
   - parameter: Main beam efficiency 0.68 (APEX), 0.66 (IRAM-30m); rms ~0.01 K at 0.5 km/s  
  > "converted to main beam brightness temperature using $\eta_\mathrm{MB}$ of 0.68" (2.3 APEX SEPIA observations); "where a main beam efficiency, $\eta_\mathrm{MB}$ of 0.66 was used" (2.2 IRAM-30m H$_2$CO observations); "At this velocity resolution, we reached an rms of $\sim$ 0.01 K" (2.3 APEX SEPIA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive excitation temperatures and column densities with LTE fits**: MADCUBA SLIM synthetic LTE spectra compared with data via AUTOFIT; Tex from four APEX HDCO transitions, applied to H2(13)CO, HDCO and D2CO; H2CO lines fitted independently for bright sources and HMSCs; 3 sigma upper limits for non-detections  
  > "We derived the excitation temperature of the gas, T$_\mathrm{ex}$, using the four APEX HDCO transitions" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios); "We used the 3$\sigma$ noise levels to provide the upper limits to the intensity of the non-detected lines." (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - software: MADCUBA, SLIM, MADCUBA-AUTOFIT
   - parameter: Average Tex ~32 K with standard deviation 17 K, used to fit H2(13)CO, HDCO and D2CO  
  > "The derived average temperature is $\sim$32 K with a standard deviation of 17 K." (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - parameter: Source size 3.0-6.0 arcsec  
  > "We adopted a source size of $3.\!^{\prime\prime}0$ - $6.\!^{\prime\prime}0$ according to the continuum sizes measured with ALMA" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - assumption: LTE  
  > "we used the MADCUBA software that assumes LTE conditions" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - fitting: Levenberg-Marquardt nonlinear least-squares (MADCUBA-AUTOFIT)  
  > "The autofit tool uses the Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm to provide the best nonlinear least-squared fit (cite: Martin2019)." (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Convert H2(13)CO to H2CO column density**: H2CO column density from H2(13)CO column density using the 12C/13C ratio as a function of Galactocentric distance, to minimize optical depth effects  
  > "We have estimated the H$_2$CO column densities from the H$_2^{13}$CO column density using the $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C ratio" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - parameter: 12C/13C values 37.3-60.4 listed per source in Table 1  
  > "derived by (cite: Milam2005) as a function of Galactocentric distance to minimize the optical depth’s effect" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - assumption: 12C/13C from Milam et al. relation  
  > "derived by (cite: Milam2005) as a function of Galactocentric distance" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compute deuteration ratios and the F parameter**: Source-averaged column density ratios HDCO/H2CO, D2CO/H2CO, D2CO/HDCO; F = [HDCO/H2CO]^2 / [D2CO/H2CO]; compared with grain-surface and gas-phase models and literature  
  > "The presented column density ratios are source-averaged values." (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios); "F=\frac{[\mathrm{HDCO}/\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{CO}]^2}{[\mathrm{D}_2\mathrm{CO}/\mathrm{H_2CO}]}." (4.1 Grain-surface deuteration of H$_2$CO)
   - assumption: Line emission more compact than the beam, so envelope emission does not significantly bias source-averaged ratios  
  > "we see from the interferometric images that the emission of the lines is more compact than the beam" (3.2 Molecular column densities and column density ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA follow-up used to measure the size of the H2CO emission at ~6 arcsec scales and give deuteration fraction estimates  
  > "High-resolution follow-up observations of eight objects in ALMA Band 6 were performed to reveal the size of the H$_2$CO emission" (abstract)
- Why this method: Interferometric images needed to separate HDCO and D2CO emission from small dense cores  
  > "more observations carried out with interferometers (i.e. at high-angular resolution) are needed to separate the HDCO and D$_2$CO emission originating from the small and dense cores" (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Only clear detections used in the calculations; tentative detections of D2CO (G75-core, I19035-VLA1) treated as upper limits  
  > "We use only the clear detections in our calculations, and consider the rest as upper limits." (3.1 Line spectra of deuterated H$_2$CO species)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): HMSC upper limits are too high to constrain the HMSC-HMPO trend; more sensitive observations needed  
  > "The calculated upper limits for the HMSC sources are high, so the trend between HMSC and HMPO phases cannot be constrained." (abstract)
- Limitation (authors): The two transitional sources cannot separate HMSC and HMPO contributions  
  > "we cannot disentangle the contribution of the HMSC cores from the HMPO ones" (4.1 Grain-surface deuteration of H$_2$CO)
- Limitation (authors): AFGL5142 and IRAS05358+3543 have no ALMA data, so a 6 arcsec size was assumed  
  > "AFGL5142 and IRAS05358+3543 have no ALMA data" (3.1 Line spectra of deuterated H$_2$CO species)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Main results come from 34 arcsec APEX beams with an assumed 3-6 arcsec source size; ratios depend on this filling assumption and on the single-Tex LTE model
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA data play only a minor role and no ALMA reduction detail is given, so ALMA-derived sizes cannot be reproduced
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Per-source results are tabulated with multiple velocity components and large scatter (e.g. 18517+0437), weakening the evolutionary-trend claim
- Caveat (inferred, ours): HMSC sample has only upper limits, so the earliest-stage behaviour is unconstrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Excitation temperature choice (HDCO versus H2CO lines) changes by factor 2-3 for bright sources
