## Analysis recipe

1. **Self-calibrate archival EBs and build continuum-subtracted datasets**: Pseudo-continuum from line-flagged data, per-EB phase-only self-cal, EB alignment, iterative phase-only self-cal (short then combined baselines), amplitude rescaling, two amplitude+phase cycles, apply to full data, bin 30 s, uvcontsub  
  > "Self-calibration was performed iteratively, first on the short-baseline observations and then on the combined short- and long-baseline EBs" (Self-calibration); "time-binned the self-calibrated measurement sets by 30 s to reduce the data volume" (Self-calibration)
   - software: CASA, tclean, statwt, gaincal, uvcontsub
   - parameter: Pseudo-continuum made by flagging +/-15 km/s around 12CO, 13CO, C18O, DCN, SO, H2CO lines  
  > "we created pseudo-continuum datasets flagging channels within $\pm15$ km s$^{-1}$ of the systematic velocity" (Self-calibration)
   - parameter: Imaging of pseudo-continuum: multiscale, briggs robust=0.5, smallscalebias=0.6, cycleniter=300, gain=0.3  
  > "(`smallscalebias=0.6`), `cycleniter=300`, `gain=0.3`, and a `briggs` weighting scheme with `robust=0.5`" (Self-calibration)
   - parameter: First phase-only round: infinite solint, antennas with <3 baselines and SNR<4 rejected  
  > "rejecting those gain solutions corresponding to antennas with $<3$ baselines and ${\rm SNR}<4$" (Self-calibration)
   - parameter: Amplitude+phase cycles: infinite solint, SNR<5 solutions flagged, amplitude gains >20% flagged  
  > "Amplitude gains $>20\%$ were also flagged" (Self-calibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Peak SNR improvement after each step and ratio images between EBs  
  > "For CQ Tau, the peak SNR increased from 112.77 to 151.32 ($\approx34\%$) after self-calibration of the short-baseline data" (Self-calibration)
2. **Image the fiducial continuum**: tclean multiscale continuum imaging from the final self-calibrated, 30 s-averaged data  
  > "The continuum images were reconstructed from the measurement sets obtained after the last step of phase and amplitude self-calibration, time-averaged by 30 s." (Imaging and summary plots)
   - software: CASA, tclean, imstat
   - parameter: robust=0.0 (CQ Tau), robust=1.0 (MWC 758)  
  > "`robust=0.0` for CQ Tau and `robust=1.0` for MWC 758" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - parameter: gain=0.02  
  > "and `gain=0.02` (to minimize any artifacts in the continuum cavities)" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: RMS compared with expected thermal noise from apparentsens  
  > "Their root mean square (RMS) noise is within 24.3% and 91.6% of the expected thermal noise" (Data and self-calibration)
3. **Image SO line cubes**: tclean cube imaging of continuum-subtracted data, multiscale, natural weighting with uv-taper, Keplerian or continuum mask  
  > "For SO, the continuum-subtracted measurement sets were imaged using `tclean`" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - software: CASA, tclean, keplerian_mask
   - parameter: CQ Tau: 23 channels of 0.7 km/s; MWC 758: 7 channels of 1.4 km/s  
  > "23 channels of 0.7 km s$^{-1}$ (corresponding to absolute velocities from $-1.3$ to 13.9 km s$^{-1}$ in the LSRK reference frame) for CQ Tau" (Imaging and summary plots); "7 channels of 1.4 km s$^{-1}$ (corresponding to absolute velocities from 1.7 to 10.1 km s$^{-1}$ in the LSRK reference frame) for MWC 758" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - parameter: gain=0.01, 1 sigma threshold  
  > "a conservative `gain=0.01`" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - parameter: natural weighting with Gaussian uv-taper 0.05 arcsec (CQ Tau) and 0.25 arcsec (MWC 758)  
  > "a Gaussian $uv$-taper with $0.05\ensuremath{''}$ (CQ Tau) and $0.25\ensuremath{''}$ (MWC 758) full width at half maximum" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Different weighting, robust and uv-taper choices were tested; continuum and Keplerian masks compared  
  > "Different weighting schemes, `robust` parameters, and Gaussian $uv$-tapers were tested." (Imaging and summary plots)
4. **Detect SO and measure line fluxes by shift-and-stack**: gofish extraction of spectra from circular apertures, shifted to a common line centre assuming Keplerian rotation, stacked, and integrated  
  > "These line intensities were measured by integrating the shifted and stacked spectra extracted from a circular mask with a radius of $0.33\ensuremath{''}$ (CQ Tau) and $0.80\ensuremath{''}$ (MWC 758)." (SO emission)
   - software: gofish
   - parameter: Aperture radius set where the cumulative SO emission flattens: 0.33 arcsec (CQ Tau), 0.80 arcsec (MWC 758)  
  > "These apertures correspond to the disk radius where the cumulative distribution of the SO emission" (SO emission)
   - parameter: Cubes over >200 km/s velocity range to estimate background noise in line-free channels  
  > "across a much wider velocity range ($\gtrsim200$ km s$^{-1}$), essential to accurately determine the background noise in line free channels" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - assumption: Keplerian velocity field used to shift spectra  
  > "under the assumption of a Keplerian velocity field, corrects for the known disk rotation by shifting such spectra to a common line center" (Imaging and summary plots)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux uncertainty from standard deviation of integrals in equally wide line-free spectral ranges  
  > "The flux uncertainties, instead, were estimated as the standard deviation of the fluxes measured by integrating the shifted and stacked spectra in non-overlapping spectral ranges" (SO emission)
5. **Make SO moment maps**: bettermoments integrated intensity, peak intensity and velocity maps without channel or threshold clipping  
  > "generated using `bettermoments` (cite: TeagueForeman-Mackey2018), without applying any channel or threshold clipping" (SO emission)
   - software: bettermoments
   - parameter: No channel or noise clipping applied  
  > "Because of the moderate detection significance of SO, we did not apply any channel or noise clipping." (Imaging and summary plots)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No spatial noise correlations seen in moment or channel maps for any weighting scheme  
  > "No clear signs of spacial noise correlations can be seen in any of the moment maps, nor in any channel map, for all weighting schemes." (Imaging and summary plots)
6. **Convert literature SO fluxes to a common 6_5-5_4 flux at 140 pc**: LTE optically thin flux ratio (T = 30, 50, 70 K) and optically thick Rayleigh-Jeans ratio  
  > "In the optically thin regime, assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and a constant excitation temperature ($T=30$, 50, and 70 K)" (Flux conversion between SO transitions)
   - parameter: Rescale fluxes to a common distance of 140 pc  
  > "and rescaled the fluxes to a common distance of 140 pc" (Comparison with the other SO-bearing Class II disks)
   - assumption: Optically thick case: same excitation temperature and emitting area for both transitions  
  > "Assuming the same excitation temperature and emitting area for both transitions" (Flux conversion between SO transitions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Different excitation temperature assumptions do not qualitatively change results  
  > "We note that different assumptions on the excitation temperature do not qualitatively change our results" (Comparison with the other SO-bearing Class II disks)
7. **Test correlations of SO flux with stellar and disk properties**: Pearson correlation coefficient and p-values between SO flux and stellar/disk parameters  
  > "We adopted the Pearson Correlation Coefficient (PCC) and the corresponding $p$-values to quantitatively assess how the SO fluxes correlate with other parameters" (Correlation test)
   - parameter: Significance threshold p<0.05  
  > "While no statistically significant ($p<0.05$) correlations are found with stellar mass, luminosity, accretion rate, and disk mass" (Correlation test)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- In CQ Tau SO forms a ring at the continuum cavity edge, qualitatively Keplerian  
  > "In the former, the SO emission comes in the shape of a ring, arises from the edge of the continuum cavity, and is qualitatively consistent, at the currently available spectral resolution, with being in Keplerian rotation." (abstract)
- In MWC 758 SO is mostly inside the cavity but also extends to the ring, with less clear morphology and kinematics  
  > "the SO emission also extends to the continuum ring(s) and its morphology and kinematics are less clear" (abstract)
- Six of nine SO-bearing Class II disks are around Herbig stars with large inner cavities, with SO inside or near the cavity edge  
  > "Moreover, their detected SO emission is consistently located inside or near the edge of these cavities." (Comparison with the other SO-bearing Class II disks)
- Tentative anti-correlation of SO luminosity with stellar age; no other correlations  
  > "we identified a tentative anti-correlation between SO luminosity and individual stellar age" (Comparison with the other SO-bearing Class II disks)
