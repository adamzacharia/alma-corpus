## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate the visibilities**: CASA pipeline 6.2.1-7 with extra Tsys and spectral-normalisation routines; self-calibration on line-free channels  
  > "Data were calibrated employing the CASA pipeline 6.2.1-7 ((cite: CASA_Team_22)), including additional routines to correct for system temperature and spectral normalisation issues[^3]" (Observations and data reduction); "and self-calibrated using line-free channels" (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Absolute flux calibration uncertainty 10%  
  > "The absolute flux calibration uncertainty is 10%." (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Self-calibration improved continuum dynamic range by factors of 3 to 10  
  > "Self-calibration improved the dynamic range of the continuum maps by factors of between 3 and 10 depending on the data set." (Observations and data reduction)
2. **Image continuum and line cubes**: Two-step tclean deconvolution: unmasked pass at ~4.5x dirty noise, then auto-multithresh cycle seeded with that mask  
  > "An initial deconvolution was performed with a relatively low threshold ($\sim$4.5 times the dirty noise) and without masking the emission." (Details on data reduction); "a second deconvolution cycle was performed using the mask generated in step-$i$ as starting point for the AUTO-MULTITHRESH option in TCLEAN." (Details on data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Hogbom deconvolver, Briggs weighting, robust = 0.5  
  > "The final continuum-subtracted line-cubes were cleaned (Hogbom deconvolver) with BRIGGS weighting scheme with ROBUST $=0.5$" (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: Primary beam correction applied to all images  
  > "The primary beam correction was applied to all the images." (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: The two-step procedure is said to ensure all channels, including those with strong spatial filtering, are properly masked  
  > "This two-step procedure ensures that all channels, including those with strong spatial filtering, have been properly masked." (Details on data reduction)
3. **Fit compact continuum sources at 1.3 mm**: CASA 2D Gaussian fit giving peak positions, peak intensity and integrated flux density  
  > "We used the 2D Gaussian fit algorithm provided by CASA ((cite: CASA_Team_22)) to analyse the 1.3 mm continuum maps" (Source properties at 1.3 mm); "The fitting algorithm provided the positions of the continuum peaks of each source, the intensity of the continuum peaks" (Source properties at 1.3 mm)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Integrated flux over a circular region enclosing the entire 3 sigma continuum emission  
  > "over a circular region encompassing the entire 3$\sigma$ continuum emission" (Source properties at 1.3 mm)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian (details not stated)  
  > "We used the 2D Gaussian fit algorithm provided by CASA ((cite: CASA_Team_22)) to analyse the 1.3 mm continuum maps" (Source properties at 1.3 mm)
   - validation: Fitted peak positions match the coordinates reported by Lindberg et al. (2014)  
  > "matches the coordinates reported by (cite: Lindberg14)" (Continuum emission)
4. **Derive N(H2) and mass of the dusty cavity walls**: Optically thin dust emission at 1.3 mm converted to H2 column density and mass, following Sabatini et al. 2023  
  > "we estimate the H$_2$ column density and mass of the cavity walls, assuming optically thin dust emission" (Continuum emission)
   - parameter: Dust temperature 30 K  
  > "a dust temperature of 30 K (cite: Perotti23)" (Continuum emission)
   - parameter: Gas-to-dust ratio 100  
  > "a standard gas-to-dust ratio of 100" (Continuum emission)
   - parameter: kappa(1.3 mm) = 0.9 cm2 g-1  
  > "a dust opacity of $\kappa_{\rm 1.3mm}$= 0.9 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$ (cite: Ossenkopf94)" (Continuum emission)
   - assumption: Optically thin dust emission  
  > "assuming optically thin dust emission" (Continuum emission)
   - assumption: Distance 130 pc  
  > "at a resolution of $\sim$0.4$''$ ($\sim$52 au at the CrA distance of 130 pc; (cite: Lindberg14))" (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Error budget: 10% flux calibration, <15% dust temperature, ~30% opacity, giving overall uncertainty <~35%  
  > "the overall uncertainty for $N$(H$_2$) and the mass of each cavity wall is $\lesssim$35%" (Continuum emission)
5. **Lower limit on the dust spectral index of the cavity walls**: _not stated_
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Map line emission and extract spectra at three positions on the arc**: Moment 0 maps over [0,+12] km/s; spectra at positions A, B, C (each nine beams in area) fitted with two Gaussian components  
  > "the integrated intensity maps (moment 0 maps) of the brightest lines over the velocity range \[0, $+12$\] km s$^{-1}$" (Line emission); "Each region has an equivalent area of nine ALMA beams." (Physical properties of the molecular arc); "We used the Python Spectroscopic Toolkit (PySpecKit; (cite: Ginsburg22)) to fit the spectra assuming two Gaussian components." (Channel maps, velocity structures, and spectra)
   - software: PySpecKit, CASA
   - parameter: Velocity structures masked at the 3 sigma level  
  > "The velocity structures defined by the three molecular tracers are masked at the 3$\sigma$ level." (Channel maps, velocity structures, and spectra)
   - fitting: Two Gaussian components (PySpecKit)  
  > "We used the Python Spectroscopic Toolkit (PySpecKit; (cite: Ginsburg22)) to fit the spectra assuming two Gaussian components." (Channel maps, velocity structures, and spectra)
   - validation: Lines detected at >3 sigma, except two cases given as 3 sigma upper limits  
  > "The targeted lines are detected with a signal-to-noise ratio of $>$3$\sigma$." (Channel maps, velocity structures, and spectra)
7. **Rotational diagrams of CH3OH for N_tot and T_rot**: LTE, optically thin rotational diagrams of CH3OH at positions A, B, C using the blueshifted arc component  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and optically thin emission, we constructed rotational diagrams (RDs) to quantify the column density, $N_{\rm tot}$, and the rotational temperature, $T_{\rm rot}$, of CH$_3$OH" (Physical properties of the molecular arc); "The RD analysis is obtained using the line intensity integrated on the blueshifted arc component." (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - parameter: Error on integrated intensities ~20%  
  > "Taking into account the calibration uncertainty and the rms of the lines spectra, the errors on the integrated intensities is $\sim$20%." (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - assumption: LTE and optically thin emission  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and optically thin emission" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - assumption: Methanol A/E ratio equal to unity  
  > "It is assumed that the methanol A/E ratio is equal to unity, which is a good approximation for temperatures of $> 20$ K (cite: Wirstrom11; Carney19)" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Critical densities lie below the H2 density of SMM 1A, supporting LTE  
  > "which supports the assumption of LTE conditions" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
8. **Non-LTE LVG analysis of CH3OH at region A**: Large velocity gradient analysis with the grelvg code at region A  
  > "In region ‘A’, where methanol peaks, we performed a non-LTE large velocity gradient (LVG) analysis using the `grelvg` code (cite: Ceccarelli03)." (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - software: grelvg
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: LVG T_rot and N_tot agree with the rotational-diagram values; opacities tau < 0.5  
  > "The resulting $T_{\rm rot, LVG}\sim$70-90 K and $N^{\rm CH_3OH}_{\rm tot, LVG}$$\sim$2-6$\times$10$^{15}$ cm$^{-2}$ are in agreement with those derived from the RDs." (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
9. **H2CO and SiO column densities and abundance ratios**: H2CO N_tot from the CH3OH T_rot range; SiO N_tot assuming 50-150 K at the SiO peak (B); abundance ratios formed from the column densities  
  > "Assuming the same range of $T_{\rm rot}$ as that obtained for CH$_3$OH, we derive $N_{\rm tot}^{\rm H_2CO}$ $\simeq$ 0.8–2$\times$10$^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$." (Physical properties of the molecular arc); "assuming a temperature range of 50-150 K (cite: Podio21), obtaining $N_{\rm tot}^{\rm SiO}$ = 1-3 $\times$ 10$^{13}$ cm$^{-2}$ towards the SiO emitting peak (B)" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - assumption: SiO excitation temperature range 50-150 K  
  > "assuming a temperature range of 50-150 K (cite: Podio21)" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Column densities and abundance ratios compared with values in shocked regions around low-mass protostars  
  > "are in agreement with the values derived in shocked regions around low-mass protostars" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)

## Qualitative findings

- The CH3OH arc is confined within two elongated continuum structures that form a cone emerging from IRS7B  
  > "The arc is located at the edge of two elongated continuum structures that define a cone emerging from IRS7B." (abstract)
- The molecular arc is most likely the signature of a bow shock driven by IRS7B  
  > "we conclude that the observed molecular arc is most likely the signature of a bow-shock driven by IRS7B" (Physical origin of the molecular arc in CrA)
- First imaging of symmetric dust cavity walls opened by a jet at 50 au scale in a low-mass star-forming system  
  > "To our knowledge, this is the first imaging of symmetric cavity walls of dust opened by a jet (on scales of 50 au) in a low-mass star-forming system." (First imaging of symmetric dusty cavity walls)
- A fifth continuum source, FAUST-5, is detected for the first time SE of IRS7B, with no compact line counterpart (possible extragalactic origin)  
  > "No compact counterpart has been identified in the molecular lines in FAUST, suggesting a potential extragalactic origin." (Continuum emission)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: FAUST is designed to characterise the chemical diversity of young solar-like protostars down to ~50 au  
  > "is designed to characterise the chemical diversity of young solar-like protostars down to a spatial resolution of $\sim$50 au" (Introduction)
- Why this method: LTE rotational diagrams justified because the critical densities are below the H2 densities of SMM 1A  
  > "which supports the assumption of LTE conditions" (Physical properties of the molecular arc)
- Alternative: External irradiation by the Herbig Ae/Be star R CrA (Lindberg and Jorgensen 2012) was considered but disfavoured because no major illumination source lies in the direction implied by the arc morphology  
  > "The detection of methanol in the arc-structure in CrA can be associated with either ($i$) external irradiation (cite: Cuadrado17) or ($ii$) shocked regions (cite: Ceccarelli23)." (Physical origin of the molecular arc in CrA); "Remarkably, no major illumination source is found in either direction" (Physical origin of the molecular arc in CrA)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Higher-sensitivity, higher-resolution shock-tracer observations (SiO, CO) are needed to reveal the IRS7B jet  
  > "Additional higher-sensitivity and higher-resolution observations of shock tracers, such as SiO and CO, are needed to reveal the IRS7B jet." (Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): A more accurate spectral index needs more sensitive observations at longer wavelengths  
  > "a more accurate estimate of $\alpha$ will be made possible with more sensitive observations at longer wavelengths" (Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): p-H2CO emission is likely contaminated by envelope emission at redshifted and near-systemic velocities  
  > "the p-H$_2$CO emission is extended over a large portion of the FoV, and therefore it is likely contaminated by envelope emission" (Line emission)
- Limitation (authors): Part of the arc falls outside the field of view in the CH3OH 5(1,4)-4(1,3) line  
  > "In the CH$_3$OH-A (5$_{1,4}$-4$_{1,3}$) line, part of the arc structure falls outside the FoV" (Line emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The derivation of the dust spectral index lower limit (alpha > 1.4) is not described: which images, beam matching, apertures and noise treatment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cavity-wall mass uses a single T_dust = 30 K and kappa = 0.9 cm2 g-1 although the gas Trot reaches 50-90 K and the same walls are argued to host large grains, which would change kappa
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Possible free-free or jet contamination of the continuum at 3 mm is not discussed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Narrow CH3OH and H2CO line widths (<1.2 km/s) were used to support external heating, so a shock origin rests mostly on SiO at one position and the radio-jet alignment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotational diagrams combine Band 3 and Band 6 lines observed at different resolution and field of view; beam dilution and filling factor are not discussed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text inconsistencies: SiO described as on the eastern wall in the abstract but on the SW side in the text; JVLA jet at 6 cm in the abstract versus 3.5 cm in the Discussion
