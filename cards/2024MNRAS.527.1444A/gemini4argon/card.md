## Analysis recipe

1. **Determine SNR expansion centre and radio/X-ray radii**: Apply Minkowski tensor analysis tool BANANA to 5500 MHz, 9000 MHz, and Chandra broadband images, smoothing the line density map with a 40-pixel diameter circle. Extract 16 radial profiles in 22.5 deg segments using miriad cgslice and measure radius at the 3-sigma contour cutoff.  
  > "We used the Minkowski tensor analysis tool BANANA [9] (Collischon et al. 2021) to determine the centre of expansion." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation); "To estimate the radio continuum radius of E0102, we used the miriad task cgslice to plot 16 equispaced radial profiles in 22.5 [◦] segments around the remnant at 5500 MHz." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation); "We identified the cutoff as the point where each profile intersects the outer contour line (3 _𝜎_ ATCA image contour or 200 _𝜇_ Jy beam [−][1] at 5500 MHz)." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation)
   - software: BANANA, miriad (cgslice)
   - parameter: 40-pixel smoothing circle; 16 radial profiles of 30 arcsec length every 22.5 deg; 3-sigma cutoff (200 uJy/beam at 5500 MHz; 6e-7 counts/pixel/s for Chandra)  
  > "density map using a circle with a diameter of 40 pixels. We then took the centre position of the pixel where the line density was the highest." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation); "Each profile is 30 arcsec in length (Fig. 3)." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation); "A: Radial profiles overlaid on 3 _𝜎_ _Chandra_ broadband X-ray image contour (6 × 10 [−][7] counts pixel [−][1] s [−][1] )." (Figure 4 caption)
   - assumption: Distance to SMC of 60 kpc  
  > "This is ∼1.5 arcsec (∼0.4 pc at the distance of 60 kpc) north-west from the previous estimation" (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation)
   - fitting: Mean and 1-sigma uncertainty across the three images/profile sectors  
  > "From this, we calculated the mean centre position and the 1 _𝜎_ uncertainty of the mean." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation)
   - validation: Compared derived expansion centre with HST proper motion centre from Banovetz et al. (2021)  
  > "Therefore, our calculated position agrees well with previous estimates, within uncertainties." (3.1.1 Centre and radius calculation)
2. **Measure integrated radio continuum flux densities (including ALMA 108 GHz) and fit spectral index**: Extract integrated flux density from radio continuum images (MWA, ASKAP, ATCA, ALMA) by summing pixel brightnesses above 5-sigma local background noise within the SNR area (excluding unrelated point sources) using miriad imfit and Findlay (1966) eq. 24. Fit a power-law weighted least-squares model to flux density vs. frequency (excluding ALMA due to missing short spacings), and create a resolved spectral index map from 2100, 5500, and 9000 MHz ATCA images.  
  > "We used the miriad (Sault et al. 1995) task imfit to extract a total integrated flux density from all available radio continuum observations of SNR E0102 listed in Table 2 [10] ." (3.2 Spectral index); "We then estimated the sum of all brightnesses above 5 _𝜎_ of each individual pixel within that area and converted it to SNR integrated flux density following Findlay (1966, eq. 24)." (3.2 Spectral index); "We produced a spectral index map for E0102 using 2100, 5500 and 9000 MHz images (Fig. 7)." (3.2.1 Spectral index image)
   - software: miriad (imfit, regrid, convol, maths), aegean
   - parameter: 5-sigma pixel brightness threshold; regridded to 0.3 x 0.3 arcsec^2 pixel size and smoothed to 2.7 x 2.6 arcsec^2 resolution for spectral index map  
  > "We then estimated the sum of all brightnesses above 5 _𝜎_ of each individual pixel within that area" (3.2 Spectral index); "To do this, the images were re-gridded to the finest image pixel size (0 _._ 3 × 0 _._ 3 arcsec [2] ) using the miriad task regrid. These were smoothed to the lowest data resolution (2 _._ 7 × 2 _._ 6 arcsec [2] ) using the miriad task convol." (3.2.1 Spectral index image)
   - assumption: Pure power-law radio spectrum S_nu propto nu^alpha; flux density errors < 10%; ALMA 108 GHz flux excluded from fit due to missing short spacings  
  > "The radio spectrum of an SNR can often be described as a pure power-law of frequency: _𝑆_ _𝜈_ ∝ _𝜈_ _[𝛼]_" (3.2 Spectral index); "We did not use ALMA flux in the fit as it is affected from missing short spacings." (3.2 Spectral index)
   - fitting: Power-law weighted least-squares fit  
  > "The best power-law weighted least-squares fit is shown (thick black line), with the spatially integrated spectral index ⟨ _𝛼_ ⟩ = −0 _._ 61±0 _._ 01 [11]" (3.2 Spectral index)
   - validation: Cross-checked integrated flux densities with aegean  
  > "For cross-checking and consistency, we used aegean (Hancock et al. 2018) and found no significant difference in integrated flux density estimates." (3.2 Spectral index)
3. **Calculate fractional polarisation, rotation measure, and magnetic field strength**: Compute fractional polarisation maps at 5500 and 9000 MHz (5x5 arcsec^2 beam) using miriad impol. Convolve Q and U maps to 4 arcsec resolution and compute RM and intrinsic polarisation angles where polarised S/N >= 10-sigma using rmcalc. Estimate equipartition magnetic field using Urošević et al. (2018) and Arbutina et al. (2012) models, and derive line-of-sight and total shell magnetic field from RM gradient fit and depolarisation in the north-east shell.  
  > "We used the miriad task impol to produce polarisation maps." (3.3 Polarisation); "To calculate the _𝑅𝑀_ and intrinsic polarisation angle maps we used the program rmcalc from the DRAO export software package (Higgs et al. 1997)." (3.4 Rotation measure); "Using equation 3, with _𝑅𝑀_ = 2 × 531 rad m [−][2] = 1062 rad m [−][2], _𝑑𝑙_ = 5.3 pc, and electron density _𝑛_ _𝑒_ = 14.8 cm [−][3] for the post-shock gas (Vogt et al. 2017), the magnetic field strength of the uniform component inside the shell parallel to the line-of-sight is _𝐵_ ∥ = 16.7 _𝜇_ G." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
   - software: miriad (impol), DRAO export software package (rmcalc), eqp calculator
   - parameter: 10-sigma S/N threshold in polarised intensity for RM; equipartition inputs: alpha = 0.61, theta = 0.36 arcmin, kappa = 0, S_5500MHz = 0.131 Jy, f = 0.84; RM equation inputs: dl = 5.3 pc, n_e = 14.8 cm^-3, foreground MW RM = +23 rad m^-2  
  > "We only calculated _𝑅𝑀_ where the signal-to-noise ratio in the polarised intensity maps was at least 10 _𝜎_ at both frequencies." (3.4 Rotation measure); "We use: _𝛼_ = 0 _._ 61, _𝜃_ = 0.36 arcmin, _𝜅_ = 0, _𝑆_ 5500 MHz = 0.131 Jy, and f = 0.84." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102); "Using equation 3, with _𝑅𝑀_ = 2 × 531 rad m [−][2] = 1062 rad m [−][2], _𝑑𝑙_ = 5.3 pc, and electron density _𝑛_ _𝑒_ = 14.8 cm [−][3] for the post-shock gas (Vogt et al. 2017)" (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
   - assumption: Faraday-thin intermixed synchrotron emitting and Faraday rotating plasma (factor of 2 in RM); intrinsic synchrotron polarisation of 71%; compression ratio of 3.7 (combined compression and enhancement factor 4.8)  
  > "If all the parameters, as synchrotron emissivity, magnetic field strength and angle, and electron density are approximately constant along the line of sight through the SNR shell and the polarised emission is Faraday thin, the _𝑅𝑀_ seen by a background source is twice the value observed (Burn 1966)." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102); "We can assume the intrinsic percentage polarisation of the SNR’s synchrotron emission through the spectral index to be 71 per cent (Le Roux 1961)." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
   - fitting: Linear fit to the RM gradient along the north-east ridge  
  > "We fitted the gradient with a linear function, also shown in Fig. 10." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
   - validation: Compared RM-derived magnetic field with equipartition magnetic field models  
  > "To achieve the magnetic field strengths estimated through equipartition, we require an angle of 38 [◦] between the plane of the sky and the ambient magnetic field for ∼ 65 _𝜇_ G [16]" (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
4. **Examine HI and ALMA CO morphology around E0102**: Construct velocity channel maps, integrated intensity map (160.1-168.9 km/s), and position-velocity diagram of ASKAP HI data, and construct a peak temperature map of ALMA 12CO (J=2-1) line emission overlaid with ATCA 5500 MHz contours.  
  > "The E0102 SNR region was mapped in Hi using ASKAP from Pingel et al. (2022); these velocity channel maps are shown in Fig. 11, with our 5500 MHz image overlaid." (3.6 Hi/CO morphology); "Fig. 13 shows the peak temperature map of the 12 CO (J = 2–1) emission line obtained with ALMA (Tokuda et al. 2021)." (3.6 Hi/CO morphology)
   - parameter: HI velocity channel integration every 3.9 km/s from 188.5 to 153.3 km/s; HI integrated map over 160.1-168.9 km/s; PV slice Dec -72d01m42.9s to -72d02m02.1s  
  > "Each panel shows the Hi intensity distribution integrated every 3.9 km s [−][1] in a velocity range from 188.5 to 153.3 km s [−][1] ." (Figure 11 caption); "(a) Hi integrated velocity intensity map towards E0102, where the integration velocity range is from 160.1 to 168.9 km s [−][1] ." (Figure 12 caption); "(b) Position–velocity diagram of Hi with Declination −72 [◦] 01 [′] 42 [′′] _._ 9 to −72 [◦] 02 [′] 02 [′′] _._ 1." (Figure 12 caption)

## Qualitative findings

- No 12CO (J=1-0) or dense 12CO (J=2-1) clumps are detected towards the radio continuum shell of E0102.  
  > "We do not detect CO emission towards 1E 0102.2–7219." (abstract); "Spectral line emission 12 CO (J = 1–0), covered by one of the spectral windows in this dataset, is not detected." (2.6 ALMA observations); "Although several CO clumps are detected within the field of view shown in Fig. 11, we find no dense CO clumps towards the radio continuum shell of E0102, suggesting it is interacting with only the Hi clouds." (3.6 Hi/CO morphology)
- A bridge-like structure at 5500 MHz and a central horizontal bar-like feature at 9000 MHz are detected in the ATCA images.  
  > "The 5500 MHz image reveals a bridge-like structure, seen for the first time in a radio image." (abstract); "Another interesting feature that we detect exclusively in our 9000 MHz image is a horizontal bridge or bar-like feature in the central region with a measured flux density of 4.3 mJy." (3.1.4 Central region)
- Radio emission extends beyond the X-ray emission, with [Fe XIV] emission sandwiched between the radio forward shock and X-ray reverse shock.  
  > "as it most likely traces the forward shock while X-ray emission could be a tracer of reverse shocked gas (Gaetz et al. 2000)." (3.1.2 Radio vs. X-ray vs. optical); "Interestingly, we note that the [Fe xiv] emission is sandwiched between radio and X-ray emission (see Fig. 5, right)." (3.1.2 Radio vs. X-ray vs. optical)
- An HI cloud at ~160-180 km/s and a cavity-like structure (~3 times the SNR diameter) at 160.1-168.9 km/s are detected towards E0102.  
  > "We detect an Hi cloud towards this remnant at the velocity range of ∼160–180 km s [−][1] and a cavity-like structure at the velocity of 163.7–167.6 km s [−][1] ." (abstract); "We found a cavity-like structure of Hi, whose size is roughly three times larger than the diameter of the SNR shell." (3.6 Hi/CO morphology)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Complementary ATCA array configurations (long, mid, short baselines) were used to achieve good uv coverage to image the full extent of E0102; ACA + total power 12CO (J=2-1) was used because spatial filtering is significant in 12-m array data and ACA covers a wider region comparable to the HI dataset.  
  > "Complementary array configurations (Table 1) achieved good _𝑢𝑣_ coverage, including long (6A, 6C, and 6D), mid (1.5C), and short (EW367) baselines, essential to imaging the full extent of E0102." (2.1 ATCA observations); "Since spatial filtering may be significant (see section 3.3 in the ALMA Cycle 8 Technical Handbook of Cortes et al. 2020), the line emission [12] CO (J = 2–1) was obtained with the Atacama Compact Array (ACA, a.k.a. Morita Array) comprising the 7-m ALMA antennas and 12 m total power array" (2.6 ALMA observations)
- Why this method: Briggs robust = -2 (uniform weighting) and Gaussian uv tapering were chosen after experimenting to best image diffuse emission; electron-only equipartition model (kappa = 0) was used as Urošević et al. (2018) showed it is superior to kappa != 0.  
  > "To best image the diffuse emission emanating from the SNR, we experimented with a range of various values for weighting and tapering. We found Briggs weighting robust parameter of –2 (uniform weighting) the most optimal choice." (2.1 ATCA observations); "Their " _𝜅_ = 0" model assume cosmic rays composed of electrons only. Urošević et al. (2018) showed the latter type of equipartition is superior to the former." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
- Alternative: Extended configuration ALMA 3 mm continuum observations were available in the archive but excluded because no continuum was detected at higher resolution.  
  > "were available in the archive but since no continuum was detected at higher resolution, they were not included." (2.6 ALMA observations)
- Alternative: ALMA 108000 MHz flux density was excluded from the spectral index power-law fit because it is affected by missing short spacings.  
  > "We did not use ALMA flux in the fit as it is affected from missing short spacings." (3.2 Spectral index)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Limited sensitivity at 9000 MHz prevents conclusively deducing the true nature of the central horizontal bar-like feature.  
  > "We are unable to conclusively deduce its true nature due to the limited sensitivity of our images at 9000 MHz." (3.1.4 Central region)
- Limitation (authors): Internal SMC rotation measure contribution is unclear due to lack of background polarised sources near E0102.  
  > "Contributions from inside the SMC are not clear as the latest study of the SMC’s internal magnetic field structure (Livingston et al. 2022) did not have any samples in the area around E0102." (3.4 Rotation measure)
- Limitation (authors): Uncertainty in the RM-derived magnetic field strength is undetermined because the post-shock electron density uncertainty is unquantified; equipartition is only an order-of-magnitude analytical estimate.  
  > "This approach is a purely analytical method, described as an order of magnitude estimate because of errors in determination of distance, angular diameter, spectral index, a filling factor, and flux density" (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102); "The uncertainty in magnetic field strength remains undetermined, primarily due to it being a combination of the uncertainties associated with the _RM_ and electron density, with the electron density’s uncertainty being unquantified." (3.5 Magnetic fields in E0102)
- Limitation (authors): Higher angular resolution HI observations are needed to draw definitive conclusions about the wind-blown bubble scenario.  
  > "However, more data are required to draw definitive conclusions regarding this scenario, in particular, further Hi observations at higher angular resolutions." (3.6 Hi/CO morphology)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA 3 mm continuum RMS is printed as '31 um' in Section 2.6, an obvious typographical error for 31 uJy/beam.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA 108 GHz image has a maximum recoverable scale of ~16 arcsec whereas the SNR diameter is ~43 arcsec, causing severe spatial filtering of the 9 mJy flux measurement.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotation measure is derived from only two frequencies (5500 and 9000 MHz), leaving potential n-pi ambiguity and non-linear Faraday depth effects unaddressed.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): In Section 3.1.1, an average X-ray radius of 20.5 arcsec at 60 kpc corresponds to 6.0 pc, but is miscalculated as 5.1 pc in the text.
