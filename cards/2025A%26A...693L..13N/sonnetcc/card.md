## Analysis recipe

1. **homogenise HCN and CO maps and integrate intensities**: Convolve supporting data to the HCN resolution, sample on a half-beam hexagonal grid and integrate HCN and CO over a velocity window set by the CO emission  
  > "We sampled all maps with a half-beam-spaced hexagonal grid and computed the integrated intensities of the HCN and CO lines by integrating over a velocity range determined by the velocity extent of the CO emission." (Data and methods)
   - software: PyStructure
   - parameter: Velocity mask from 4-sigma CO peaks expanded into adjacent 2-sigma channels  
  > "The velocity integration mask was built from 4-sigma CO peaks and expanded into adjacent 2-sigma channels." (Data and methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Datasets stated to be well matched in sensitivity and resolution; ALMOND and EMPIRE compared in Appendix 5  
  > "The two datasets are well matched in terms of sensitivity and resolution" (Data and methods)
2. **derive SFR, stellar mass surface density and dynamical equilibrium pressure**: SFR from FUV+22 micron z0MGS maps; Sigma_star from Spitzer 3.6 micron; P_DE from the weight of the ISM in the galaxy potential  
  > "following the methodology of the original ALMOND paper (cite: Neumann2023a), which used a combination of infrared" (Star formation rate); "by calculating the weight of the interstellar medium in the galaxy potential via" (Dynamical equilibrium pressure)
   - parameter: sigma_gas,z = 15 km/s  
  > "$\sigma_{\rm gas, z}=\SI{15}{\km\per\s}$ (cite: Sun2018)" (Dynamical equilibrium pressure)
   - assumption: Stellar mass-to-light ratio 0.6 Msun/Lsun at 3.6 micron  
  > "adopted a mass-to-light ratio of $\Upsilon_\star = 0.6$ M$_\odot$ L$_\odot^{-1}$" (Stellar mass)
   - assumption: Typical disc flattening ratio to get stellar scale heights from scale lengths (value not stated)  
  > "assuming a typical disc flattening ratio (cite: Sun2020b; Sun2022)" (Dynamical equilibrium pressure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **convert CO and HCN to molecular and dense gas quantities**: CO(2-1) scaled to CO(1-0) with a Sigma_SFR-dependent R21; Sigma_mol from variable alpha_CO; fixed alpha_CO and alpha_HCN for reference conversions  
  > "with minimum $R_{21}$ of 0.35 and maximum 1.0." (Conversion factors); "which accounts for variations with metallicity" (Conversion factors)
   - parameter: R21 = 0.65 (Sigma_SFR / 1.8e-2 Msun/yr/kpc^2)^0.125, clipped to 0.35-1.0  
  > "\SI{1.8e-2}{M\ensuremath{_\odot}\per\year\per\square\kilo\parsec}}\right)^{0.125}" (Conversion factors)
   - parameter: alpha_CO^var = alpha_CO^fix (Z/Zsun)^-1.5 (max(Sigma_star,100)/100)^-0.25  
  > "\left(\dfrac{Z}{\SI{}{\zsun}}\right)^{-1.5}" (Conversion factors)
   - assumption: alpha_CO^fix = 4.35 Msun pc^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "adopting $\ensuremath{\alpha_{\text{CO}}}^{\rm fix}=\SI{4.35}{M\ensuremath{_\odot}\per\square\parsec}\,(\SI{}{\kkms})^{-1}$ (cite: Bolatto2013)" (Conversion factors, molecular gas surface density, and dense gas fraction)
   - assumption: alpha_HCN = 15 Msun pc^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "$\ensuremath{\alpha_{\text{HCN}}}=\SI{15}{M\ensuremath{_\odot}\per\square\parsec}\,(\SI{}{\kkms})^{-1}$ (cite: Schinnerer2024)" (Conversion factors, molecular gas surface density, and dense gas fraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **stack HCN and CO spectra in environment bins**: Per-galaxy spectral stacking of HCN and CO in logarithmic bins of Sigma_star, Sigma_mol and P_DE, with 3-sigma upper limits where HCN is undetected  
  > "For bins in which the stacks do not yield 3-sigma HCN detections, we estimated upper limits for HCN/CO and lower limits for SFR/HCN." (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions); "We stack in logarithmic bins for each galaxy individually, selecting ten bins from a fixed minimum" (Spectral stacking of HCN and CO)
   - software: PyStacker
   - parameter: Ten bins per galaxy from fixed minima Sigma_star = 30, Sigma_mol = 5 Msun/pc^2, P_DE = 1e4 K/cm^3 up to each galaxy maximum  
  > "$\ensuremath{\Sigma_{\star}}=\SI{3e1}{M\ensuremath{_\odot}\per\square\parsec}$, $\ensuremath{\Sigma_{\text{mol}}}=\SI{5e0}{M\ensuremath{_\odot}\per\square\parsec}$, $\ensuremath{P_{\text{DE}}}=\SI{1e4}{\kB\kelvin\per\cubic\cm}$" (Spectral stacking of HCN and CO)
   - parameter: Centre-versus-disc stacks exclude the centres and use nine bins  
  > "we exclude the centres from the stacking and stacks across the remaining sightlines adopting nine bins" (Spectral stacking of HCN and CO)
   - assumption: CO reference velocity (higher S/N than HCN) sets the stacking velocity for each position  
  > "We used the CO data, which have a much higher signal-to-noise ratio than the HCN, to determine the local mean reference velocity for the stacks (cite: Neumann2023b)." (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **fit scaling relations of HCN/CO and SFR/HCN against environment**: Linear fit in log-log space, log10 Y = b + m (log10 X - x0), to all stacks of all galaxies including limits  
  > "We fitted the combined set of stacks (including upper limits) for all galaxies using a linear function of the form" (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions)
   - software: LinMix
   - parameter: x0 = {2.4, 1.4, 5.0} for Sigma_star, Sigma_mol, P_DE  
  > "$x_0=\{2.4, 1.4, 5.0\}$ is a value close to the median $X$ value" (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions)
   - fitting: LinMix linear regression with measurement uncertainties and 3-sigma limits; sampler and priors not stated  
  > "The fitting was performed with the linear regression tool" (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions); "which takes measurement uncertainties and 3-sigma upper (lower) limits on HCN/CO (SFR/HCN) into account (cite: Neumann2023a)" (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions)
   - validation: Pearson coefficients and scatter reported; ALMOND-only versus EMPIRE-only fits compared  
  > "denotes the Pearson correlation coefficient; all $p$-values are much less than $0.01$." (Table 1, notes)
6. **compile Gao-Solomon relation across literature**: Place ALMOND and EMPIRE in a 31-survey literature compilation; compute median SFR/HCN, SFE_dense, depletion time and epsilon_ff for subsamples  
  > "We placed ALMOND and EMPIRE in the context of a literature compilation that comprises 31 HCN surveys spanning from the Milky Way to the high-redshift universe." (Gao–Solomon relation)
   - parameter: Dense gas freefall time 0.8 Myr for n_H2 about 3e3 cm^-3  
  > "$\ensuremath{t_\mathrm{ff}^\mathrm{dense}}=\SI{0.8}{\mega\year}$, assuming that HCN traces gas above a density of $\ensuremath{n_\mathrm{\ensuremath{\text{H}_2}}^\mathrm{dense}}\approx \SI{3e3}{\per\cubic\centi\metre}$ (cite: Jones2023; Bemis2024)" (Gao–Solomon relation)
   - assumption: Constant C_IR = 1.48e-10 Msun/yr/Lsun to convert SFR to L_IR  
  > "$\ensuremath{C_\mathrm{IR}}=\SI{1.48e-10}{M\ensuremath{_\odot}\per\year\perL\ensuremath{_\odot}}$ (cite: Murphy2011)" (Footnote 6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Median SFR/HCN compared with earlier compilations  
  > "which is consistent with previous literature compilations (cite: Jimenez-Donaire2019; Beslic2024)" (Gao–Solomon relation)
7. **compare galaxy centres with discs and AGN with non-AGN centres**: Centre defined as the central beam-size aperture; medians and percentiles of HCN/CO and SFR/HCN in centre, disc, AGN and non-AGN centres  
  > "For this exercise, we considered the central kiloparsec-scale, beam-size aperture as the centre and refer to the remaining galaxy parts as the disc." (Dense gas ratios in galaxy centres)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **check consistency of ALMOND and EMPIRE HCN**: Compare spectra, voxel-by-voxel brightness temperatures and integrated intensities of the three overlapping galaxies after homogenising resolution  
  > "The integrated line intensities yield consistent values within their uncertainties." (EMPIRE versus ALMOND)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistency of overlapping galaxies; ALMOND used where both exist  
  > "we employ the ALMOND data for the three galaxies NGC 628, NGC 2903, NGC 4321, due to the slightly better angular resolution and sensitivity of the ALMOND survey." (EMPIRE versus ALMOND)

## Qualitative findings

- Even in dense gas, star formation is an extremely inefficient process  
  > "This demonstrates that even in the dense gas, star formation appears to be an extremely inefficient process." (Gao–Solomon relation)
- Spectroscopic HCN/CO trends match cloud-scale CO imaging trends, so cloud properties vary with galactic environment  
  > "The fact that spectroscopic (presented here) and CO imaging results show similar trends as a function of galactic environment provides strong evidence that the physical properties of molecular clouds vary as a function of the galactic environment." (Dense gas relations with environmental conditions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMOND plus EMPIRE give the largest resolved galaxy dataset of dense gas tracers, filling the gap between cloud and integrated galaxy scales  
  > "ALMOND and EMPIRE form the largest resolved galaxy dataset, filling in the large gap in spatial scale, SFR, and" (Gao–Solomon relation)
- Why this method: Fixed alpha_HCN and alpha_CO used because the environmental dependence of the HCN conversion factor is unclear  
  > "we did this because the environmental dependence of the HCN-to-dense gas conversion factor" (Conversion factors, molecular gas surface density, and dense gas fraction)
- Alternative: EMPIRE Spitzer/Herschel-based SFR not used; FUV+22 micron SFR applied to all galaxies for homogeneity  
  > "here we adopted the same methodology across EMPIRE and ALMOND" (Star formation rate)
- Alternative: EMPIRE data not used for the three galaxies observed by both surveys; ALMOND used instead  
  > "To avoid duplicates, we employed the ALMOND data for these galaxies." (Galaxy sample)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): SFR/HCN to SFE_dense conversion is highly uncertain because alpha_HCN is uncertain  
  > "SFR/HCN can be interpreted, with significant uncertainty due to the uncertain conversion factor, as the rate per unit mass at which dense molecular gas converts into stars." (Gao–Solomon relation)
- Limitation (authors): HCN may not trace dense gas robustly in galaxy centres  
  > "a similarly likely explanation is that HCN might not be a robust tracer of dense gas in galaxy centres" (Dense gas ratios in galaxy centres)
- Limitation (authors): Kiloparsec resolution limits interpretation of centre-disc deviations  
  > "remain speculative due to the coarse, kiloparsec-scale resolution of the ALMOND and EMPIRE observations" (Dense gas ratios in galaxy centres)
- Limitation (authors): P_DE analysis limited to 26 galaxies with HI data  
  > "These observations were available for 26 galaxies of our sample" (Dynamical equilibrium pressure)
