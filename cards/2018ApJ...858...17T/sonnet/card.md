## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image**: Pipeline/manual calibration; CLEAN cubes at 10 and 3 km/s with thresholds ~1.5 and ~4 times rms  
  > "we used in this paper two thresholds to produce maps that will be used to detect the potential signals." (4.1 New Cycle 3-4 observations of NGC 5846 and NGC 4636)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: thresholds 0.5/1.5 mJy (NGC 5846, 4636), 1.6/4 mJy (NGC 5044)  
  > "increasing the threshold of the cleaning algorithm to 1.5 mJy for NGC 5846 and NGC 4636 and to 4 mJy for NGC 5044" (5 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Explored several velocity resolutions, weightings and thresholds  
  > "several velocity resolutions, Briggs weightings and threshold values were explored to determine the optimum setup" (4.1 New Cycle 3-4 observations of NGC 5846 and NGC 4636)
2. **Detect CO clouds**: Image detection (pixels >5 sigma, clustered) on 10 km/s cubes; spectral detection on 3 km/s cubes after noise whitening and Gaussian kernel convolution; require both; final cuts sigma>=6 km/s and S/N>=6  
  > "We produced a combined list of cloud candidates from these 4 clouds candidate lists by requiring at least 1 detection in the image and 1 detection in the spectrum." (5 Results)
   - parameter: kernels sigma = 9, 18, 30, 45, 60 km/s  
  > "Gaussian kernels of various width ($\sigma$ = 9, 18, 30, 45, 60 ${\rm km\,s^{-1}}$)" (5 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with earlier NGC 5044 list; S/N 5 gives same candidates  
  > "a signal-to-noise of 5 return the same cloud candidate for our data." (5 Results)
3. **Measure cloud spectra, sizes and fluxes**: Gaussian fits to spectra for velocity and dispersion; imfit elliptical Gaussian for sizes deconvolved from beam  
  > "we used the *imfit* function from the CASA package to calculate their size." (6 Analysis)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits; elliptical Gaussian imfit  
  > "The velocity and velocity dispersion has been obtained by fitting a Gaussian on the spectra of each clouds." (6 Analysis)
   - validation: Residuals consistent with noise  
  > "our measured residuals are consistent with pure noise" (6 Analysis)
4. **Derive molecular masses**: Bolatto13 formula with CO(2-1)->(1-0) conversion  
  > "Throughout the paper we use the *reference* conversion factor $X_{CO}=2\times 10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$" (6 Analysis)
   - assumption: X_CO = 2e20; T21/T10 = 0.8 (flux ratio 3.2)  
  > "we assume that the CO(2-1) to CO(1-0) temperature brightness ratio is 0.8 as found by (cite: braine92)" (6 Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Virial parameters, surface and volume density for resolved clouds**: alpha = 5 sigma^2 R/(GM) with r_c = sqrt(sigma_maj sigma_min)  
  > "average radius ($r_c$ = $\sqrt{\sigma_\mathrm{maj}\sigma_\mathrm{min}}$)" (6.2 Kinematics interpretation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Continuum SED analysis**: Fit FIR modified blackbody (beta=1.5) and compare to radio to estimate continuum origin at 230 GHz  
  > "The fit of a FIR modified black body spectra (emissivity $\beta$=1.5)" (Continuum)
   - assumption: radio fluxes corrected assuming uniform brightness  
  > "the fluxes were corrected assuming uniform brightness when the scale at which they were measured differed greatly from ALMA PSF." (Continuum)
   - fitting: modified blackbody; sampler not stated  
  > "The fit of a FIR modified black body spectra (emissivity $\beta$=1.5) returns a temperature of 31.8 $\pm$ 0.9, 30.0$\pm$1.1, 30.7$\pm$1.2 K" (Continuum)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- ACA data show no diffuse CO detection at current sensitivity  
  > "do not exhibit any detection of a CO diffuse component at the sensitivity level achieved by current exposures" (abstract)
- Some CO clouds coincide with HST dust features  
  > "Some of the molecular clouds are associated with dust features as revealed by HST dust extinction maps" (abstract)

## Reasoning

  > "We increased the signal-to-noise ratio criteria to 6 in order to remove potential false detections" (5 Results)

  > "we decided not to include them in our analysis because often their extent in the velocity domain was very limited" (5 Results)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_

  > "it is expected that the presented molecular masses are overestimated." (Conclusions)
