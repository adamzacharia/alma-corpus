## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce and image the ACA Cycle 10 spectral scans**: CASA pipeline calibration; interactive tclean with natural weighting; cubes with fixed elliptical mask, binned to about 100 km/s; no-continuum-subtracted cubes first, then continuum measured from line-free channels  
  > "We created continuum images using the interactive TCLEAN task within CASA with natural weighting." (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations); "We subsequently measured the continuum and extracted it from line-free regions adjacent to detected emission lines." (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Cube mask 16″ x 10″ elliptical; velocity binning about 100 km/s  
  > "Each spectral cube was imaged separately and cleaned using a fixed elliptical mask of" (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations); "During the data reduction steps, the cubes were further binned to velocity resolutions of" (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual inspection to remove data with irregular amplitude or phase values  
  > "including visual inspections to identify and remove data with irregular amplitude or phase values" (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations)
2. **Detect lines, measure integrated fluxes and fix the redshift**: Two S/N>5 lines at 97 and 145.5 GHz identified as CO(4-3) and CO(6-5); integrated fluxes from the full line-width at zero intensity; ambiguous CO ladder solutions (z = 1.38, 3.75, 6.1, 8.5) resolved with photometric redshift, dust temperature, SFR and LMT non-detections  
  > "In this case, the lines could correspond to redshifts of 1.38, 3.75, 6.1, or 8.5." (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation); "given the non-Gaussian shape of the line profiles, we calculated the full line-width at zero intensity (FWZI)" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS/CLASS
   - parameter: CO(4-3) flux integrated over about 550 km/s; APEX CO(9-8) forced measurement over 550 km/s; 75% error on ALMA CO(9-8) estimate  
  > "we performed a forced measurement by integrating the spectrum over a velocity width of 550 km s$^{-1}$" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation); "we assign a conservative 75% error to this value" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
   - assumption: Low-redshift z = 1.38 solution discarded because it conflicts with the photometric redshift z_phot > 3  
  > "we discard this low redshift solution due to its incompatibility with the photometric redshift" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Independent LMT/RSR detection of the 97 GHz line and non-detection of CO(5-4) and CO(7-6) disfavour higher-z solutions; agreement with Tamura et al. (2025) redshift  
  > "In this case, the non-detection disfavours the higher-z solutions." (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation); "is in excellent agreement with the redshift recently reported by (cite: Tamura2025)" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
3. **Model the gravitational lens and derive the magnification**: uv-plane lens modelling of B6 and B7 continuum independently; SIE lens and Sersic source; average of the two magnifications adopted  
  > "We modelled the lens as a spherical isothermal ellipsoid (SIE) and the source as a Sérsic profile (cite: Sersic1963)." (5 Lens modelling and magnification); "The model parameters were optimised by fitting only the continuum emission in B6 and B7 independently, using the non-linear sampler Dynesty." (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
   - software: PyAutoLens, Dynesty
   - parameter: B6: only spectral windows 0 and 1 used because of emission lines in the others  
  > "for B6, we used only spectral windows 0 and 1 due to the presence of emission lines in the other spectral windows" (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
   - parameter: Lens centre free with Gaussian prior between the two images and standard deviation 1″  
  > "a Gaussian prior centred between the two multiple images and a standard deviation of 1″" (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
   - assumption: Lens redshift unconstrained; magnification depends mainly on image geometry  
  > "The magnification factor, which is primarily dependent on the image geometry, remains largely unaffected by the unconstrained lens redshift." (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
   - fitting: Dynesty nested sampler; Gaussian prior on lens centre (other priors not stated)  
  > "using the non-linear sampler Dynesty" (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
   - validation: Lens position consistent with MUSE centroid; magnifications agree with Tamura et al. (2025)  
  > "The lens position recovered by the parametric model is consistent with the centroid of the MUSE detection." (5 Lens modelling and magnification); "Our derived magnification factors agree with those recently reported by (cite: Tamura2025)" (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
4. **Fit the FIR dust SED with a modified blackbody**: Bayesian greybody fit with MERCURIUS (PyMultiNest nested sampling), optically thin and self-consistent opacity scenarios, magnification-corrected  
  > "We first fitted J154506 using the Bayesian FIR SED PYMULTINEST fitting code MERCURIUS (cite: Witstok2022), which employs a nested sampling algorithm (cite: Buchner2014) to fit a greybody spectrum to FIR photometry." (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
   - software: MERCURIUS, PyMultiNest
   - parameter: Rest-frame wavelengths 10 to 10^3 micron fitted  
  > "For fitting, we considered rest-frame FIR wavelengths from 10 to" (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
   - parameter: T_dust gamma prior (shape a=1.5); beta_dust Gaussian prior centred 1.8, sigma 0.25  
  > "Specifically, we used a default gamma distribution with a shape parameter (a=1.5)" (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties); "we imposed a Gaussian prior centred at 1.8 with a standard deviation of 0.25." (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
   - assumption: kappa = 8.94 cm2/g at 158 micron, appropriate for SN-ejected dust after reverse shock destruction  
  > "This value of $\kappa$ is appropriate for dust grains ejected by supernovae (SNe) after reverse shock destruction (cite: Hirashita2014)." (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
   - fitting: Nested sampling (MERCURIUS/PyMultiNest) with gamma prior on T_dust and Gaussian prior on beta  
  > "we imposed a Gaussian prior centred at 1.8 with a standard deviation of 0.25." (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
   - validation: Posteriors shown; TUNER fit compared with MERCURIUS  
  > "The well-constrained TUNER model fits the observed data comparably to the best-fit MERCURIUS model" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
5. **Model dust and CO SED jointly with non-LTE radiative transfer**: TUNER non-LTE LVG radiative transfer with lognormal density PDF fitted to CO line SED and dust continuum with MCMC  
  > "non-local thermodynamic equilibrium (non-LTE) radiative transfer calculations model the CO line intensities and dust continuum using a large velocity gradient approximation" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
   - software: TUNER, emcee
   - parameter: Magnification fixed to give intrinsic properties  
  > "is fixed in the model to therefore provide the intrinsic source properties" (Simultaneous modelling of the observed continuum and emission lines)
   - parameter: Gas-to-dust mass ratio fixed at about 100 after an initial free fit  
  > "The initial model fits, obtained with a free molecular gas-to-dust mass ratio (GDMR), yielded a median GDMR of $100$, which we subsequently fixed" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
   - assumption: [CO]/[H2] fixed to a fiducial Milky Way value (log = -4.0)  
  > "to a fiducial Milky Way value" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
   - fitting: emcee with 100 walkers and 50 autocorrelation times, uniform priors  
  > "using 100 walkers and 50 autocorrelation times and uniform priors." (Simultaneous modelling of the observed continuum and emission lines)
   - validation: Narrow marginalised posteriors; consistency with MERCURIUS  
  > "the narrowness of the marginalised posterior distributions of the parameters" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
6. **Derive [CII]-based SFR and test AGN contribution**: [CII] luminosity from the APEX median spectrum corrected for magnification, SFR from the de Looze high-z relation; AGN fraction constrained with the MIPS 24 micron upper limit  
  > "Using the high-redshift relations from (cite: deLooze2014), we derive an SFR of 840" (6.3 [CII] as a tracer of cold ISM); "be constrained using the upper limit on the Multiband Imaging Photometer for Spitzer (MIPS)" (6.4 Possible AGN contribution)
   - assumption: Foreground extinction from Lupus-I at 24 micron negligible  
  > "we assume that foreground extinction from the Lupus-I molecular cloud at 24" (6.4 Possible AGN contribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SFR from [CII] consistent with SFR from L_IR; low Tk/Td supports star-formation heating  
  > "This [CII]-derived SFR is remarkably consistent with that obtained from the median total infrared luminosity" (6.3 [CII] as a tracer of cold ISM)

## Qualitative findings

- J154506 is confirmed as a strongly lensed extragalactic source at z = 3.7515, consistent with the arc-shaped continuum structure  
  > "consistent with the arc-shaped structure in ALMA continuum images" (7 Summary and conclusions)
- CO line SED peaks near CO(5-4) with a sharp turnover, unlike QSOs and the most extreme starbursts  
  > "The CO line SED peaks near the CO(5-4) transition, exhibiting a sharp turnover" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
- Derived CO conversion factor is significantly higher than the commonly assumed ULIRG value  
  > "the derived conversion factor is significantly higher than the commonly assumed ULIRG value" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
- No [CII] deficit is seen: [CII] emission acts as an efficient cooling channel  
  > "this suggests that the [CII] emission serves as an efficient cooling channel" (6.3 [CII] as a tracer of cold ISM)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ACA Band 3 and 4 spectral scans were taken to confirm the redshift; unaffected by dust extinction  
  > "were conducted to spectroscopically confirm the redshift of J154506" (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations)
- Why this method: Spectral setups that maximise the chance of detecting two lines are efficient for redshift confirmation; uv-plane lens modelling avoids CLEAN biases  
  > "Similar spectral setups, which maximise the likelihood of detecting two emission lines, have proven very efficient" (3.2 ALMA Cycle 10 Atacama Compact Array observations); "This uv-plane modelling approach consistently outperforms sky-plane modelling for simulated ALMA observations by avoiding biases and spurious structures introduced by the CLEAN process (cite: Maresca2025)." (5 Lens modelling and magnification)
- Alternative: Low-redshift z = 1.38 solution (CO(2-1)/CO(3-2)) rejected: conflicts with z_phot > 3, implies cold T = 15 K and SFR_IR below 100 Msun/yr; higher-z solutions disfavoured by LMT non-detections  
  > "Moreover, the derived dust temperature from FIR fitting at $z=1.38$, T$=$15 K is low and unlikely for galaxies at that redshift" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation); "In this case, the non-detection disfavours the higher-z solutions." (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
- Alternative: Optically thin dust assumption considered but judged likely unrealistic for a dust-obscured system; self-consistent opacity model also used  
  > "adopting an optically thin assumption" (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
- Alternative: Unified UV-to-FIR energy-balanced SED fitting judged unfeasible because of Lupus-I extinction and foreground contamination  
  > "These factors render a unified rest-frame UV-to-FIR energy-balanced SED fitting unfeasible" (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Stellar mass and other SED-derived properties highly uncertain because of Lupus-I extinction  
  > "makes SED-derived properties, such as the stellar mass, highly uncertain." (4 Spectroscopic redshift confirmation)
- Limitation (authors): Lens redshift not confirmed; MUSE and FORS2 data too poor  
  > "The low quality of this dataset precluded spectroscopic confirmation of the lens and prevented further detailed analysis." (3.5 MUSE and FORS2 observations)
- Limitation (authors): Dust mass strongly depends on the assumed kappa and optical depth  
  > "Dust mass estimates are highly dependent on the assumed" (6.1 Dust emission and FIR-derived properties)
- Limitation (authors): Unresolved data and fixed GDMR and [CO]/[H2] prevent tight gas-mass constraints  
  > "prevent a more tightly constrained molecular gas mass" (6.2 Radiative transfer model results)
- Limitation (authors): A minor AGN contribution cannot be ruled out  
  > "However, we acknowledge that a minor AGN contribution cannot be definitively ruled out." (6.4 Possible AGN contribution)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line detections beyond CO(4-3) and CO(6-5) (H2O+, HCN, [CI]) are S/N 2-3 and rest on ACA beams of 6-20 arcsec with Galactic confusion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Magnification from two independent uv-plane fits differs by a factor 1.6, and the lens redshift is unknown, so all intrinsic quantities carry unquantified systematic error
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature differs (30 vs 48 vs 38 K) between models, so L_IR, dust mass and alpha_CO depend strongly on model choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): TUNER fixes GDMR=100 and [CO]/[H2], so alpha_CO and gas mass are largely prior-driven
- Caveat (inferred, ours): [CII] detection is S/N 2-3 per spectrum and S/N>4 in the median only; Lupus-I extinction and APEX calibration not discussed in detail
