## Science

- Fields: high_z_galaxies
- Question: How do the sizes, spatial distributions and kinematics of dust, CO gas, H II regions and stars compare in the z=2.12 SMG ALESS67.1, and what does this imply for alpha_CO, IRX-beta and the Schmidt-Kennicutt relation?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Image Band 7 continuum and measure flux and size**: Natural-weight dirty image, clean a 1'' circle to 2 sigma; uvmodelfit Gaussian fit to the uv data, cross-checked with imfit and sextractor  
  > "we first use the uvmodelfit algorithm to model the *uv*–data. We find that the best bit (reduced $\chi^2$=0.5) Gaussian profile has an intensity of 3.7$\pm$0.2 mJy" (3.1 ALMA 870 μm continuum)
   - software: CASA uvmodelfit, imfit, sextractor
   - fitting: Gaussian profile, chi-square fit in uv plane  
  > "the best bit (reduced $\chi^2$=0.5) Gaussian profile" (3.1 ALMA 870 μm continuum)
   - validation: Consistent with imfit and sextractor on cleaned image  
  > "We obtain consistent results if we instead using the imfit algorithm or sextractor on the cleaned image." (3.1 ALMA 870 μm continuum)
2. **Extract CO(3-2) spectrum and line properties**: Iterative procedure: weight-averaged map over best-guess width, curve-of-growth aperture, clean to 2 sigma, Gaussian fit, update FWHM until convergence  
  > "Next the extracted spectrum is fitted with a Gaussian profile and the frequency width used for obtaining the averaged map is updated to be the FWHM of the spectral fit." (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: line flux contained within 2'' circular radius  
  > "all the line flux is contained within a circular radius of 2$''$" (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit; errors from fit and Monte Carlo injection of model profiles in random cube regions  
  > "We create fake spectra by injecting model profile into spectra extracted from randomly selected regions of the data cube" (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - validation: Fit errors consistent with Monte Carlo errors  
  > "The errors are estimates from the fit, and they are consistent with the errors derived from a Monte Carlo simulation." (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
3. **CO luminosity and brightness temperature ratio**: Solomon & Vanden Bout relation for L'CO; ratio with ATCA CO(1-0)  
  > "$L^\prime_{\rm CO}=3.25\times10^7 S_{\rm CO}\Delta v\nu_{\rm obs}^{-2}D_{\rm L}^2(1+z)^{-3}$" (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - assumption: Planck cosmology H0=67.77, OmegaM=0.31, OmegaL=0.69  
  > "H$_0 =$ 67.77 km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_M =$ 0.31, and $\Omega_\Lambda =$ 0.69" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure CO half-light size**: imfit on averaged map with bias correction from 36000 injected models, plus uv-plane chi-square Gaussian fit; uv-based result adopted  
  > "We therefore adopt the *uv*-based measurement for the $^{12}$CO size." (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - software: imfit
   - parameter: 36000 injected models, peak SNR 1-10, radius up to 2''  
  > "In total we inject 36000 models with the peak SNR, major axis, minor axis, and positional angle all randomized" (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - fitting: chi-square Gaussian fit to averaged visibilities vs uv distance  
  > "perform $\chi^2$ fitting assuming a Gaussian profile" (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
   - validation: Image-plane and uv results agree; injection tests for bias (3%) and scatter (20%)  
  > "We find a 3% upward bias in median (0.028$\pm$0.007) and a 20% scatter." (3.2 ALMA 12CO J=3–2)
5. **Fit SINFONI lines and measure integrated Halpha properties and size**: Chi-square fit of Halpha+[NII]+[SII] Gaussians with linear continuum over 1.9-2.2 um; AICc model selection; curve-of-growth aperture of 1.8'' radius  
  > "The spectra are fit with four Gaussian models, in which all include a linear continuum component with the slope and normalization allowed free." (3.3 SINFONI spectra)
   - parameter: [NII] doublet ratio fixed to 3; skylines masked; significance Delta chi2>25 ([SII] additional >9)  
  > "a fit with line components is considered significant if the fit, compared to a simple continuum-only model, has a lower AICc and provides a $\chi^2$ improvement of $\Delta\chi^2>25$" (3.3 SINFONI spectra)
   - fitting: Minimum chi-square with Monte Carlo errors  
  > "The errors are derived using Monte Carlo simulations similar to those used for measuring the errors of the CO emission." (3.3 SINFONI spectra)
   - validation: AICc model comparison  
  > "The model selection is determined based on the Akaike information criterion." (3.3 SINFONI spectra)
6. **2D kinematic modelling and kinemetry**: Adaptive binning (1,3x3,5x5 spaxels); arctan rotation curve fit with emcee; kinemetry K_asym  
  > "we run emcee, a Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) ensemble sampler (cite: Foreman-Mackey:2013aa), to explore the parameter space and derive uncertainties." (3.3.2 Two-dimensional kinematics)
   - software: emcee, kinemetry code (Krajnovic et al. 2006)
   - parameter: Fit v_asym sin(i) as a single parameter; centre and r_t within SINFONI FoV; PA 0-180 deg  
  > "Because $v_{asym}$ and $i$ are essentially degenerate for our data quality we treat $v_{asym}{\rm sin}(i)$ as a single parameter." (3.3.2 Two-dimensional kinematics)
   - fitting: Maximum likelihood with emcee; centre perturbed within MCMC errors for kinemetry  
  > "We fit the 2D model to the measured data based on maximum likelihood" (3.3.2 Two-dimensional kinematics)
   - validation: Reduced chi2=14.8 indicates poor disk fit; K_asym compared with Shapiro et al. criterion  
  > "The reduced $\chi^2_\nu=14.8$, indicating a relatively poor fit to the data and suggesting ALESS67.1 is not a pure rotating disk." (3.3.2 Two-dimensional kinematics)
7. **Derive alpha_CO dynamically and via gas-to-dust ratio**: Mdyn from turbulence-corrected vc at r_e; subtract stellar mass and 15% dark matter to get gas mass; divide by L'CO(1-0). Cross-check with gas-to-dust ratio from metallicity (N2 calibration)  
  > "the gas mass is derived by subtracting stellar and dark-matter mass from the dynamical mass" (4.2 alpha_CO)
   - parameter: dark matter fraction 15%; <sin^2 i>=2/3; median sigma 154±5 km/s  
  > "so we adopt 15%" (4.2 alpha_CO)
   - assumption: Gas and stars share same half-light radius; constant velocity dispersion  
  > "we assume the gas and stars have the same r$_e$ given this is what we find" (4.2 alpha_CO)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gas-to-dust method gives alpha_CO=1.4±0.7, consistent with dynamical estimate  
  > "we calculate a gas mass of $(5.5\pm0.6)\times 10^{10} M_\odot$, and therefore a $\alpha_{\rm CO,GDR}=1.4\pm0.7$" (4.2 alpha_CO)
8. **Resolved Schmidt-Kennicutt analysis**: 0.5'' concentric rings centred on 870um peak; maps convolved to CO beam; SFR from L_IR and Halpha (Kennicutt 1998), alpha_CO=0.8  
  > "we construct concentric rings with a width of 0$\ensuremath{''}$5 ($\sim$4 kpc) for each ring and centered at the peak of the 870 $\mu$m continuum." (4.5 The Schmidt-Kennicutt relationship)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; alpha_CO=0.8; Halpha not extinction corrected  
  > "We assume a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: Chabrier:2003aa) and adopt 0.8 for $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ in order to make comparisons with other studies." (4.5 The Schmidt-Kennicutt relationship)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Halpha and 870um dust emission are spatially offset (0.4''), with H-alpha ~3x more extended  
  > "The sky separation between the peaks of the H$\alpha$ and 870 $\mu$m continuum is 0$\ensuremath{''}$4" (3.3.2 Two-dimensional kinematics)
- CO and Halpha kinematics are in broad agreement  
  > "In general we find that the kinematics of both HII regions as traced by the optical emission lines and molecular gas as traced by $^{12}$CO(J=3-2) are in broad agreement." (4.1 Kinematics – CO and Halpha)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALESS67.1 is covered by all necessary follow-up observations and is representative of the ALESS sample  
  > "ALESS67.1 is one of the few SMGs so far that is covered by all the necessary follow-up observations, and it is representative of the ALESS sample" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Rotation-dominated criterion (vmax-vmin)/2sigma=0.4 suggested a disk, but detailed modelling used instead  
  > "However, the availability of the 2D velocity map allows us to conduct detail kinematic modelling to more reliably differentiate rotating systems from mergers." (3.3.2 Two-dimensional kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): CO detection is only ~3 sigma peak SNR so 2D CO kinematics and central detail are limited  
  > "Our $^{12}$CO(J=3-2) detection does not have high enough SNR to produce 2D kinematic maps like H$\alpha$" (4.1 Kinematics – CO and Halpha)
- Limitation (authors): ALESS67.2 nature inconclusive  
  > "so the nature of ALESS67.2 remains inconclusive" (3.1 ALMA 870 μm continuum)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single object, so generality of size contrast is uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SINFONI astrometry uncertainty (0.2-0.3'') comparable to dust-Halpha offset
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO from dynamical method relies on assumed dark matter fraction, inclination average and point-mass approximation despite merger evidence
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sigma_SFR and Sigma_H2 rings depend on assumed alpha_CO=0.8 and uncorrected Halpha
