## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and separate nuclear/extended components**: Image continuum with natural and Briggs weighting, model and subtract point source in uv plane, re-image residual; reduce point-source flux until extended profile is monotonic  
  > "We model and subtract the central point sources in the $uv$ plane prior to re-imaging the visibilities and isolating the extended continua." (4.1 Continuum Surface Brightness Modeling)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Over-subtraction checked by requiring roughly monotonically decreasing central profile  
  > "until the extended emission in the image plane has a roughly monotonically decreasing central profile" (4.1 Continuum Surface Brightness Modeling)
2. **Continuum spectral indices**: Image each continuum spw separately, subtract nucleus in uv plane, fit power law S_nu ~ nu^alpha at ~236 GHz  
  > "modeling the unresolved and (where detected) resolved continuum emission as a power law ($S_\nu\propto\nu^\alpha$)." (4.2 Continuum Spectral Fitting)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Line profile fitting and kinematic maps**: Gauss-Hermite fits per Voronoi-binned spectrum; uncertainties via 500 Monte Carlo resamplings  
  > "this process is repeated 500 times for each binned spectrum, and we set the formal uncertainties of each coefficient to the standard deviation of all its resampled fit values." (3.1.1 Line Profile Fitting)
   - software: Voronoi binning
   - parameter: |h3,h4|<0.35; |h5,h6|<0.25 for NGC 1332 and NGC 3258  
  > "We fix limits of $|h_3,h_4|<0.35$ (and $|h_5,h_6|<0.25$ when fitting the NGC 1332 and NGC 3258 emission)" (3.1.1 Line Profile Fitting)
   - fitting: Gauss-Hermite least-squares fit with noise-resampling errors  
  > "we set the formal uncertainties of each coefficient to the standard deviation of all its resampled fit values." (Line Profile Fitting)
   - validation: Re-imaged NGC 1380 and NGC 6861 into 4 km/s channels; widths agree within ~1 km/s  
  > "line profile fits to these more finely sampled data cubes show line widths that agree to within $\sim1$ km s$^{-1}$" (3.1.1 Line Profile Fitting)
4. **Gas masses and column densities**: M_H2 = alpha_CO L'_CO with L'_CO from integrated flux; R21 ~0.7, alpha_CO = 3.1, helium factor 0.36  
  > "using $\alpha_{\rm CO}=3.1$ $M_\ensuremath{\odot}$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$ as the extragalactic mass-to-luminosity ratio" (3.1.2 Surface Brightness and Mass Profiles)
   - assumption: R21 ~0.7; alpha_CO = 3.1; f_He = 0.36; disk thickness h ~0.1 R_d  
  > "Helium is included in the total gas mass as $M_{\rm gas}=M_{\rm H_2}(1+f_{\rm He})$ where $f_{\rm He}=0.36$" (3.1.2 Surface Brightness and Mass Profiles)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with ATLAS3D masses after adjusting alpha_CO  
  > "After adjusting for different $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ values, the ATLAS$^{\rm 3D}$ galaxies with interferometric data have a median H$_2$ mass that is four times larger" (3.1.2 Surface Brightness and Mass Profiles)
5. **Kinemetry, inclination and enclosed mass**: Kinemetry on v_LOS maps; i = arccos(q_min); v_c ~ k1/sin i; enclosed mass within 2b or 5b  
  > "we approximate the inclination $i$ by $\cos^{-1}q_{\rm min}$, where $q_{\rm min}$ is the minimum observed axis ratio." (3.1.3 Kinematic Properties)
   - software: kinemetry
   - assumption: thin disks  
  > "Assuming these disks are thin, we approximate the inclination $i$ by $\cos^{-1}q_{\rm min}$" (3.1.3 Kinematic Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Kinematic inclinations agree within a few percent with dust-disk cos^-1(b/a)  
  > "These kinematic inclination angles correlate well (to within a few percent) with those estimated from dust disk morphologies" (3.1.3 Kinematic Properties)
6. **Toomre Q stability**: Q = c_s kappa/(pi G Sigma) with c_s replaced by minimum observed sigma_LOS and v_c from k1/sin i  
  > "We therefore replace $c_s$ with the minimum observed $\sigma_{\rm LOS}$ for each disk." (3.1.4 Molecular Gas Stability)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Stability persists with uncertainties in k1, Sigma, i (2 deg) and sigma (1 km/s)  
  > "this formal stability measure persists after we include the uncertainties in $k_1$ and $\Sigma_{\rm gas}$" (3.1.4 Molecular Gas Stability)

## Qualitative findings

- CO emission coincides with dust and shows dynamically cold regular rotation; four galaxies show central velocity upturns  
  > "All CO emission is coincident with the dust and is in dynamically cold rotation." (abstract)
- Extended continuum of CO-faint, radio-bright galaxies aligns with jets; CO-bright disks show thermal dust spectral slopes  
  > "The extended continua of the CO-bright disks are coincident with optically thick dust absorption and have spectral slopes that are consistent with thermal dust emission." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Two-step process: moderate-resolution survey to find high-velocity central gas, then deeper higher-resolution follow-up for BH masses  
  > "First, we obtain sufficiently high resolution observations to detect high-velocity gas originating from within the BH sphere of influence." (1 Introduction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Gas masses subject to large systematic uncertainty from unknown alpha_CO in ETGs  
  > "Given the large scatter in $\alpha_{\rm CO}$, the derived gas masses are subject to potentially large systematic uncertainties." (3.1.2 Surface Brightness and Mass Profiles)
- Limitation (authors): Beam smearing contributes to observed line widths  
  > "portions of the lowest observed $\sigma_{\rm LOS}$ values are still the result of beam smearing." (3.1.1 Line Profile Fitting)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Assumed alpha_CO and R21 from late-type galaxies may not apply
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Toomre Q uses beam-smeared dispersion and approximate v_c
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Extended continuum depends on point-source subtraction choices
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spectral indices rely on narrow frequency baseline
