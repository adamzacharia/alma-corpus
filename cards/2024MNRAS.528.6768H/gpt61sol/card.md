## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure CO luminosities and molecular masses**: Smooth cubes to construct binary masks; sum masked original cubes; calculate L_CO and apply a constant Galactic conversion factor  
  > "The original datacubes multiplied by these masks are what we use to calculate the total CO(1-0) luminosities" (ALMaQUEST); "$\rm S_{CO}$ is the total CO intensity obtained by summing over the masked cube" (ALMaQUEST)
   - parameter: Gaussian smoothing: 1.5 × Bmaj_pix spatially; 4 channels spectrally  
  > "Gaussian kernels with widths of $\rm 1.5 \times {Bmaj}_{pix}$ spaxels spatially" (ALMaQUEST); "and 4 channels spectrally." (ALMaQUEST)
   - parameter: Mask threshold: standard deviation of original cube  
  > "Values lower than the standard deviation of the original datacube are set to 0 and all those above are set to 1." (ALMaQUEST)
   - parameter: L_CO = 3.25 × 10^7 × S_CO Δv × ν_sys^-2 × D_L^2 × (1+z)^-3  
  > "\rm L_{CO} = 3.25 \times 10^7 \times S_{CO} \Delta v \times {\upnu_{sys}}^{-2}\times {D_{L}}^2\times {(1 + z)}^{-3}\ ," (ALMaQUEST)
   - assumption: α_CO = 4.3 M_⊙ (K km s^-1 pc^2)^-1, including heavier elements/molecules  
  > "the constant Galactic conversion factor $\rm \upalpha _{CO} = 4.3~M_\odot\ {(K~km~s^{-1}~pc^2)}^{-1}$" (ALMaQUEST); "which includes a correction for the presence of heavier elements/molecules" (ALMaQUEST)
   - assumption: ΛCDM: H0 = 70 km s^-1 Mpc^-1, Ω_m0 = 0.3, Ω_Λ = 0.7  
  > "Throughout this paper we adopt a standard $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $\rm H_0=70\ $$${\rm km~s}^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\rm \Omega _{m_0}=0.3$, $\rm \Omega _{\Lambda}=0.7$" (Introduction)
   - assumption: Redshifts from NASA-Sloan Atlas  
  > "$\rm z$ is the redshift of the object (obtained from the NASA-Sloan Atlas catalogue; NSA)." (ALMaQUEST)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Near-solar metallicities support the constant Galactic conversion factor  
  > "All galaxies in the sample have near-solar metallicities" (ALMaQUEST)
2. **Construct resolved optical SFR maps**: Extinction-correct and mask MaNGA Pipe3D Hα maps; convert Hα luminosity to SFR  
  > "We calculate star-formation rates (SFRs) from the extinction corrected, masked H$\upalpha$ maps using the relation given in (cite: kennicutt98)" (MaNGA data products)
   - software: Pipe3D
   - parameter: S/N >2 in Hα, Hβ, [OIII] λ5007 and [NII] λ6584  
  > "Spaxels are required to have $\rm S/N>2$ in H$\upalpha$, H$\upbeta$, \[OIII\]$\uplambda$5007 and \[NII\]$\uplambda$6584 maps." (MaNGA data products)
   - parameter: Star-forming BPT classification; Hα EW >6 Å; log Σ_M* >7  
  > "Each spaxel must fall in the star-forming portion of the Baldwin, Phillips and Terlevich (BPT) diagram (cite: baldwin81) using the (cite: kauffmann03) classification." (MaNGA data products); "Spaxels require a H$\upalpha$ equivalent width (EW) \> 6 Å." (MaNGA data products); "each spaxel must have $\log \rm \Sigma _{M_*} > 7$ (masking anomalously small values)." (MaNGA data products)
   - parameter: SFR [M_⊙ yr^-1] = 7.9 × 10^-42 L(Hα) [ergs s^-1]  
  > "\rm SFR~[M_\odot~{yr}^{-1}] = 7.9 \times {10}^{-42}~L (H \upalpha)~[ergs~s^{-1}]\ ," (MaNGA data products)
   - assumption: Intrinsic Hα/Hβ = 2.86; Milky Way extinction curve  
  > "These are corrected for internal extinction by adopting an intrinsic H$\upalpha$/H$\upbeta$ = 2.86 and a Milky Way extinction curve (cite: cardelli89)." (MaNGA data products)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF  
  > "and a (cite: chabrier03) IMF." (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare resolved-disc SFE patterns with independent global-SFR results  
  > "finding a comparable result by an independent method does add credence to our interpretation of SFE suppression of the “barred + radial flow” subset galaxies." (Do radial bar-driven flows affect the SFEs of barred galaxies?)
3. **Assemble global SFRs and stellar masses**: Use GSWLC-X2 SFRs for 41 objects, WISE-derived SFRs for 5, and NSA Sersic stellar masses  
  > "GSWLC-X2 covers 41 of the 46 objects we use from ALMaQUEST" (GSWLC); "For the remaining 5 objects from ALMaQUEST that are not included in the GSWLC-X2 catalogue, we use SFRs derived only from the WISE 22-micron photometry in the AllWISE database (cite: wright10; cutri14)." (WISE); "We use the parameter `nsa_sersic_mass` from the `drpall-v3_1_1.fits` file released with DR17 of SDSS." (NSA)
   - parameter: SFR_W4 [M_⊙ y^-1] = 7.5 × 10^-10 (L_W4 − 0.044 L_W1) [L_⊙]  
  > "\rm {SFR}_{W4}~[M_\odot~y^{-1}] = 7.5 \times 10^{-10} \times (L_{W4} - 0.044~L_{W1})~[L_\odot]\ ," (WISE)
   - assumption: SDSS redshifts for W1 and W4 luminosities  
  > "using SDSS redshifts to calculate the luminosities of the W1 and W4 bands" (WISE)
   - fitting: GSWLC-X2 uses joint UV+optical+mid-infrared SED fitting  
  > "We use SFRs from GSWLC-X2, which uses joint UV+optical+mid-infrared (IR) spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting, using Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer (WISE) 22-micron photometry" (GSWLC)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Classify optical morphology**: Determine edge-on status from GZ2 or NSA inclination; use GZ2/HyperLEDA agreement for bars and inspect SDSS RGB images for disagreements or missing classifications  
  > "For each galaxy in the sample, we first use GZ2 to determine whether the galaxy is edge-on (cite: willett13)." (Morphological sub-samples); "If GZ2 and HL both agree that the galaxy has a bar, it is added to our barred sub-sample." (Morphological sub-samples); "If there is a disagreement between the catalogues (or it is only present in one of the catalogues), we visually inspect the SDSS red-green-blue (RGB) image of the object and decide whether or not a bar is present." (Morphological sub-samples)
   - parameter: t02_edgeon_a05_no_count and t03_bar_a06_bar_count thresholds ≥10 instead of ≥20  
  > "We require a lower count thresholds in the `t02_edgeon_a05_no_count` and `t03_bar_a06_bar_count` parameters than the recommendation (i.e. we use $\geq 10$ instead of $\geq 20$)" (Morphological sub-samples)
   - parameter: If t02_edgeon_a05_no_count <10, edge-on inclination cut >70°  
  > "If the count number is \<10 for the `t02_edgeon_a05_no_count`, we instead use an inclination cut of $>70^\circ$ (where inclinations are taken from the NSA catalogue) to determine if a galaxy is edge-on." (Morphological sub-samples)
   - parameter: t03_bar_a06_bar_debiased ≥0.2  
  > "We also use the lower limit for `t03_bar_a06_bar_debiased` of $\geq 0.2$" (Morphological sub-samples)
   - assumption: Edge-on features are insufficiently visible to establish bar presence  
  > "We define three morphological sub-samples; barred, un-barred and edge-on (where edge-on galaxies are too highly inclined to establish whether a bar is present)." (Morphological sub-samples)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual assessment supports classifications using the lower count threshold  
  > "However, after a visual assessment, we still find these classifications reliable with the lower count threshold." (Morphological sub-samples)
5. **Select circular kinematic models**: Fit six surface-brightness profiles with 3D KINMS cubes; select the lowest-BIC converged model  
  > "For each object in ALMaQUEST, we fit six different surface brightness profiles: an exponential disc, an exponential disc + central hole, a Gaussian ring, two exponential discs, an exponential disc + a Gaussian ring and two Gaussian rings" (Kinematic models); "The best-fit model is the one that produces the lowest BIC, whereby more complex surface brightness models are penalised for larger parameter spaces" (Kinematic models)
   - software: KINMS, KinMS_fitter, GASTIMATOR
   - parameter: Disc peak brightness and scale width; ring peak brightness, mean radius and width; x0, y0, V_sys, PA, inclination, total flux, V_max, R_turn and σ_gas  
  > "Each exponential disc has two free parameters describing the peak surface brightness ($F_{\rm exp,\ peak}$) and scale width ($R_{\rm exp}$)" (Kinematic models); "each Gaussian ring has three free parameters describing the peak surface brightness ($F_{\rm gauss,\ peak}$), the mean radius of the ring ($R_{\rm gauss}$) and the width of the ring ($\sigma _{\rm gauss}$)." (Kinematic models); "The total number of parameters used in each model varies depending on the surface brightness model used, with 10 being the minimum and 14 the maximum." (Kinematic models)
   - parameter: First component peak flux fixed to 1; second component varies relative to it  
  > "in one-component profiles the peak flux is fixed to 1 and in two-component profiles the peak flux of the first component in fixed to 1, while the other is left to vary relative to the first." (Kinematic models)
   - parameter: BIC = k log(n) − 2 log(L_hat); n is signal spaxels in the cube  
  > "\rm BIC \equiv k \log(n)-2 \log (\widehat{L})\ ," (Kinematic models); "$n$ is the number of spaxels in the datacube with signal" (Kinematic models)
   - assumption: Arctan circular velocity curve: V_rot(r) = (2 V_max/π) arctan(r/R_turn)  
  > "V_{\rm rot} (r) = \frac{2 V_{\rm max}}{\pi} \arctan{\left(\frac{r}{R_{\rm turn}}\right)}\ ," (Kinematic models)
   - assumption: Spatially constant gas velocity dispersion  
  > "The gas is also given a spatially-constant velocity dispersion ($\sigma _{\rm gas}$) in each model." (Kinematic models)
   - fitting: Initial least-squares minimisation; adaptive-step MCMC Gibbs sampler; uniform priors with physically motivated boundaries; 100,000 steps, 25 workers  
  > "implements a Python Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Gibbs sampler with adaptive stepping" (Kinematic models); "Before fitting each model with MCMC, we fit an initial model using a least-squares minimisation." (Kinematic models); "The MCMC is set with uniform priors with physically-motivated boundaries and run using 100,000 steps with 25 workers." (Kinematic models)
   - validation: Reject parameters whose 16th–84th percentile uncertainty width exceeds half the explored range; converged best-fit model found for every object  
  > "We reject models that have fit parameters with uncertainties (defined as the width between the $\rm 16^{th}$ and $\rm 84^{th}$ percentiles of the probability distribution function) larger than half the size of the parameter range explored" (Kinematic models); "We successfully find a converged best-fit model for each object in the sample." (Kinematic models)
6. **Detect radial bar-driven flows**: Add radial_barflow to the selected surface-brightness model; refit with MCMC and compare BIC with the no-flow model  
  > "We model radial motions induced by a central bar by using the `radial`$\_$`barflow` function of the `KinMS`$\_$`fitter` wrapper" (Kinematic models); "For each galaxy, we re-fit our best-fit model with radial bar-flows using MCMC, using the same priors and set-up as described previously (with additional uniform priors for the radial flow parameters)." (Kinematic models)
   - software: KINMS, KinMS_fitter, radial_barflow, GASTIMATOR
   - parameter: Four extra parameters: R_b, φ_b, mean radial velocity V_r and mean transverse velocity V_t; 14–18 free parameters total  
  > "a bar that extends from the galaxy’s centre to a radius $R_{\rm b}$, with a phase $\phi_{\rm b}$" (Kinematic models); "the gas has constant radial and transverse velocities ($\overline{V}_{\rm r}$, $\overline{V}_{\rm t}$) within the bar’s radius." (Kinematic models); "At minimum, therefore, each model has 14 free parameters and at maximum 18 free parameters." (Kinematic models)
   - parameter: Either V_t or V_r >2 × channel width; 2 × Δ_channel ≈22 km s^-1  
  > "We also require either $\overline{V}_{\rm t}$ or $\overline{V}_{\rm r}$ to be larger than $2 \times$ the channel width" (Kinematic models); "where $\rm 2 \times \Delta _{channel} \approx 22~km~s^{-1}$" (Kinematic models)
   - parameter: Converged flow model and BIC_with_flow < BIC_without_flow  
  > "If the model does not converge (based on the width of each parameter’s uncertainty), we classify the object as not having a radial bar-driven flow." (Kinematic models); "if $\rm{BIC}_{w/\ radial\ flow}\ < BIC_{w/o\ radial\ flow}$, the object is classed as having radial bar-driven flows" (Kinematic models)

## Software

- **KINMS** : Generate simulated interferometric datacubes and model molecular gas kinematics  
  > "KINMS allows us to create simple simulated interferometric datacubes by defining arbitrary surface brightness and velocity profiles." (Kinematic models)
- **KinMS_fitter** : KINMS fitting wrapper; define surface-brightness and velocity profiles and interface with GASTIMATOR  
  > "We also use the `KinMS_fitter`[^5] wrapper of KINMS, which acts as a front end for the most common fitting tasks, and provides a simple interface for defining surface brightness and velocity profiles." (Kinematic models); "It also provides an interface to the GASTIMATOR package" (Kinematic models)
- **GASTIMATOR** : Adaptive-step Python MCMC Gibbs sampler fitting mock interferometric cubes to original cubes  
  > "implements a Python Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Gibbs sampler with adaptive stepping" (Kinematic models); "to fit the mock interferometric datacubes generated by KinMS, with predefined surface brightness and velocity profiles, to the original datacubes." (Kinematic models)
- **radial_barflow** : KinMS_fitter function modelling radial motions induced by a central bar  
  > "We model radial motions induced by a central bar by using the `radial`$\_$`barflow` function of the `KinMS`$\_$`fitter` wrapper" (Kinematic models)
- **Pipe3D** : Source of MaNGA DR17 optical emission-line fluxes and stellar-mass surface density maps  
  > "We use optical emission line fluxes from the MaNGA Pipe3D pipeline derived from Data Release 17 (cite: sanchez16; sanchez18; sanchez22)." (MaNGA data products); "Using stellar-mass surface density maps ($\rm \Sigma _{M_*}$) from the Pipe3D pipeline" (MaNGA data products)
- **ALMA science archive** : Access to all ALMaQUEST data products used  
  > "All ALMaQUEST data products used in this work are available in the ALMA science archive" (Data availability)
- **SDSS Science Archive Server (SAS)** : Public access to MaNGA IFS and stellar mass maps  
  > "MaNGA IFS and stellar mass maps are also publicly available in the SDSS Science Archive Server" (Data availability)

## Qualitative findings

- Barred galaxies are more likely to host kinematics resembling radial bar-driven flows  
  > "We can infer from this that barred galaxies are statistically more likely to possess non-circular molecular gas kinematics that resembles radial bar-driven flows" (Barred galaxies)
- Multi-component profiles may indicate resonant structures  
  > "A multi-component surface brightness model can indicate the presence of a resonance system" (Barred galaxies)
- Barred galaxies with detected flows appear SFE-suppressed globally and in their discs  
  > "Conversely, we find these galaxies appear to have suppressed SFEs both globally and in their discs." (Summary & conclusions)
- Barred flow galaxies have concentrated gas without depleted central reservoirs  
  > "Galaxies in our “barred + radial flow” subset have consistently concentrated molecular gas distributions compared to those of the other subsets" (Summary & conclusions); "The “barred + radial flow” subset galaxies, despite having suppressed SFEs, do not appear to have depleted central molecular gas reservoirs" (Do radial bar-driven flows affect the SFEs of barred galaxies?)
- Inflow is inferred; the bisymmetric model cannot distinguish inflows from outflows  
  > "it is not possible to distinguish between gas inflows and outflows in the plane of the discs using our bisymmetric radial bar-driven flow model" (Barred galaxies); "we are inclined to infer that we are observing radial bar-driven inflows as opposed to outflows." (Barred galaxies)
- Shear-driven stabilization is proposed as a quenching mechanism  
  > "We speculate that dynamical effects, such as shear, driven by the radial motions of gas may act to quench the centralised gas reservoirs." (Summary & conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Match ALMA and MaNGA angular resolutions  
  > "to be comparable to the angular resolution of the optical integral-field spectroscopic data of the MaNGA survey." (ALMaQUEST)
- Why this method: Simplified constant-velocity bar-flow model reduces computation and enables statistical flow identification across the sample  
  > "This reduces the computational time" (Kinematic models); "allows us to identify the presence of radial flows statistically over a sample of galaxies." (Kinematic models)
- Alternative: Pure inclination cuts for edge-on classification can hide visibly discernible bars; GZ2 preferred when counts suffice  
  > "Applying an arbitrary inclination cut led to some galaxies being classified as edge-on when their features were still discernible (e.g. a central bar was visible)." (Morphological sub-samples)
- Alternative: Compare six single-/double-component brightness profiles and models with/without radial flows  
  > "For each object in ALMaQUEST, we fit six different surface brightness profiles" (Kinematic models); "if $\rm{BIC}_{w/\ radial\ flow}\ < BIC_{w/o\ radial\ flow}$, the object is classed as having radial bar-driven flows" (Kinematic models)
- Alternative: Gas exhaustion disfavoured as the explanation of suppressed SFE  
  > "Exhaustion of the central molecular gas reservoirs is, therefore, not the reason for the SFR suppression observed in this subset." (Summary & conclusions)
- Alternative: Outflows cannot be excluded kinematically; inflows are favoured by contextual evidence  
  > "it is not possible to distinguish between gas inflows and outflows in the plane of the discs using our bisymmetric radial bar-driven flow model" (Barred galaxies); "we are inclined to infer that we are observing radial bar-driven inflows as opposed to outflows." (Barred galaxies)
- Alternative: Flow model may capture shocks along molecular ridges  
  > "It is, however, possible that our radial bar-driven flow model is capturing shocks induced along molecular ridges" (Barred galaxies)
- Alternative: Weak bars, structural asymmetries, minor mergers or interactions may explain some un-barred flow detections  
  > "the un-barred sub-sample is likely to contain a fraction of weakly barred galaxies." (Un-barred galaxies); "Our un-barred sub-sample likely contains galaxies with a range of structural asymmetries" (Un-barred galaxies); "It is also possible that the un-barred sub-sample (as well as the other morphological sub-samples) contains galaxies under-going minor mergers or interactions" (Un-barred galaxies)
- How they decided it was enough: Integration chosen for S/N >3 in >50% of spaxels having MaNGA Hα S/N >3  
  > "to ensure a signal-to-noise ratio greater than 3 for more than 50% of the spatial pixels (spaxels) where the MaNGA H$\upalpha$ signal-to-noise ratio is also greater than 3." (ALMaQUEST)
- Limitation (authors): Small morphological and flow subsets limit SFE comparisons  
  > "We note that these subsets are small, particularly the “barred + no radial flow” subset, so our analysis is once again limited by the small number of galaxies available in this work." (Effect of radial bar-driven flows on the global SFEs of barred galaxies)
- Limitation (authors): Central resolved SFR analysis is limited by AGN contamination and low S/N  
  > "spatially-resolved analyses of SFRs in ALMaQUEST are limited by AGN contamination and/or low S/N in the central regions of some of the galaxies in the sample." (Effect of radial bar-driven flows on the global SFEs of barred galaxies)
- Limitation (authors): SDSS feature visibility depends on redshift and galaxy size  
  > "our classification procedure is contingent upon the visibility of features in SDSS imaging, which is dependent on both the redshift of galaxies and their size." (Morphological sub-samples)
- Limitation (authors): Weak bars are under-detected in GZ2  
  > "(cite: geron21; geron23) and (cite: walmsley22) also note that weak bars are under-detected in GZ2, with the barred sub-sample preferring strong bars." (Un-barred galaxies)
- Limitation (authors): Radial motions are easier to detect at higher inclinations  
  > "radial motions are more easily identified in galaxies with higher inclinations, as the component of the motions projected along the line of sight will be higher." (Statistical presence of radial bar-driven flows)
- Limitation (authors): Simplified bisymmetric flow model cannot determine inflow versus outflow  
  > "it is not possible to distinguish between gas inflows and outflows in the plane of the discs using our bisymmetric radial bar-driven flow model" (Barred galaxies)
- Limitation (authors): S/N affects flow detection and preferred brightness models  
  > "The S/N of the datacubes, therefore, do affect the detection of radial bar-driven flows and the best-fit model." (SNR degradation)
- Limitation (authors): Larger samples with comparable CO resolution and sensitivity are required  
  > "To assess this result further, a larger sample of galaxies with CO data of comparable resolution and sensitivity to the data of ALMaQUEST would be required." (Do radial bar-driven flows affect the SFEs of barred galaxies?)
