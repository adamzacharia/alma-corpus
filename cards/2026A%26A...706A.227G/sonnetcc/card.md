## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure size and flux of the 667 um nuclear continuum**: Fit the continuum image with a point source, a Gaussian and a point source + Gaussian (pG) convolved with the beam, minimizing chi^2  
  > "These models were convolved with the beam and their parameters varied to minimize the $\chi^2$ obtained from the comparison with the observed image." (Appendix: ALMA observations and models for the 667 um continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Synthesized beam 75 x 67 mas^2, rms 0.20 mJy/beam  
  > "The synthesized beam full-width half-maximum (FWHM) for the combined continuum image is 75$\times$67 mas$^2$" (Appendix: ALMA observations and models for the 667 um continuum)
   - fitting: Beam-convolved model images compared with the observed image, parameters varied to minimize chi^2  
  > "their parameters varied to minimize the $\chi^2$ obtained from the comparison with the observed image" (Appendix: ALMA observations and models for the 667 um continuum)
   - validation: Point-source-only and Gaussian-only fits leave significant residuals; the pG model shows no evident residuals  
  > "Instead, the fit is much better for the pG model, which shows no evident residuals" (Appendix: ALMA observations and models for the 667 um continuum)
2. **Constrain compact source radius, brightness temperature and optical depth**: Use the deconvolved size limit of the unresolved component and the Gaussian-disk equivalence to set R, then solve the 667 um optical depth equation for a dust temperature  
  > "Conservatively using the equivalence between a Gaussian source and a disk of uniform brightness (cite: sak08)" (2.1 ALMA observations of the 667 um continuum and model)
   - parameter: Upper limit FWHM <= 21 mas = 34 pc gives R <~ 27 pc  
  > "an upper limit for the nuclear source radius is $R\lesssim27$ pc, yielding a brightness of $T_{\mathrm{B}}\gtrsim190$ K" (2.1 ALMA observations of the 667 um continuum and model)
   - assumption: 667 um emission entirely from dust; T_d = 500 K for the hot component, 123 K for the warm component  
  > "The $667\,\mu$m emission is entirely attributed to dust" (2.1 ALMA observations of the 667 um continuum and model); "for an adopted $T_d=123$ K" (2.1 ALMA observations of the 667 um continuum and model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit the hot component (H_C) SED with a greenhouse model**: Fit a grid of greenhouse AGN models (N_H, L_unatt) to mid-IR 2.6-11 um continuum points plus the 667 um H_C flux, with a foreground cold-dust layer; fitted radius R_h and foreground tau at 6 um  
  > "A set of mid-IR $2.6-11$ $\mu$m continuum points unaffected by ice, a:C-H, or gas-phase bands, together with the flux density at 667 $\mu$m of the $H_C$, are used to find the best-fit model." (3.1 The continuum model)
   - parameter: Grid: log N_H = 24.0-25.6, log L_unatt = 11.0-12.6, steps of 0.2  
  > "both with logarithmic steps of $0.2$" (3.1 The continuum model)
   - assumption: Flat density profiles; inner radius 0.15 x R_out; dust absorption with emissivity index beta = 1.85 beyond 20 um; ices and a:C-H absorption not included  
  > "For simplicity, flat density profiles are used." (3.1 The continuum model); "the adopted inner radius of the source is $0.15\times R_{\mathrm{out}}$" (4.3 Uncertainties in the inferred parameters); "we use an emissivity index similar to that of the GC curve ($\beta=1.85$) all the way to millimeter wavelengths" (Appendix: The absorption coefficient of dust)
   - assumption: Dust-to-gas calibration: mu x DGR x k_670um = 2.2e-2 cm^2/g and tau_6um/N_H = 1.3e-23 cm^2  
  > "At $6$ $\mu$m we obtain $\tau_{6\mu m}/N_{\mathrm{H}}=1.3\times10^{-23}$ cm$^{2}$" (Appendix: The absorption coefficient of dust)
   - fitting: Best fit selected on the model grid; fitting algorithm not stated  
  > "The fitted parameters are, besides the properties of the greenhouse model ($N_{\mathrm{H}}$ and $L_{\mathrm{unatt}}$), its radius $R_h$" (3.1 The continuum model)
   - validation: Sizes of H_C and W_C from the SED agree with the ALMA 667 um unresolved and resolved components  
  > "are consistent with the ALMA 667 $\mu$m measurements for the unresolved and resolved components" (3.1 The continuum model)
4. **Fit the warm (W_C) and cold (C_C) components**: Fit a foreground-extincted graybody (+ 230 K blackbody) to JWST 11.5-26 um points and the resolved 667 um flux; add a 45 K graybody for the remaining far-IR emission  
  > "the $W_C$ is fitted by using *JWST* continuum points at $11.5-26$ $\mu$m together with the flux density at 667 $\mu$m of the resolved component" (3.1 The continuum model); "a graybody "cold" ($T_d=45$ K) component ($C_C$) is added to the model" (3.1 The continuum model)
   - parameter: W_C fitted parameters: T_d, intrinsic tau at 6 um, source radius R_w; same foreground tau as H_C  
  > "The fitted parameters for the $W_C$ are $T_d$, the intrinsic optical depth evaluated at 6 $\mu$m ($\tau_{\mathrm{6\mu m}}^{\mathrm{int}}$), and the source radius $R_w$." (3.1 The continuum model)
   - assumption: C_C also includes the part of the 667 um emission resolved out by ALMA  
  > "The $C_C$ also includes the fraction of the 667 $\mu$m emission that is resolved out by ALMA." (3.1 The continuum model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Far-IR absorption lines (Herschel/PACS) used to check the W_C continuum level (step 7)  
  > "indicating that the modeled $65-120$ $\mu$m continuum level generated by the $W_C$ is consistent with the available far-IR spectroscopic data" (4 Discussion and conclusions)
5. **Model the H2O nu2 band with non-LTE radiative transfer on the H_C T_d profile**: Use the T_d profile of the best-fit H_C model, vary X_H2O and the velocity field, and compare with the continuum-normalized JWST spectrum including a wavelength-dependent covering factor f_hot  
  > "Using the $T_d$ profile obtained from our best-fit continuum model for the $H_C$" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band); "we have defined a $\lambda-$dependent covering factor for the $H_C$" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band)
   - parameter: Photosphere outflow velocity rising linearly from 75 to 170 km/s plus an H2O shell at 150-400 km/s  
  > "an outflow velocity increasing linearly from 75 to 170 km s$^{-1}$" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band); "an additional H$_2$O shell flowing with velocities of $150-400$ km s$^{-1}$ was placed in front of the nucleus" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band)
   - parameter: Turbulent velocity 90 km/s  
  > "In combination with a turbulent velocity of $90$ km s$^{-1}$, this velocity field can approximately match the observed profiles." (Appendix: Details of the H2O band)
   - assumption: Uniform H2O abundance; dust and gas uniformly mixed; a hidden 230 K blackbody component dilutes the absorption  
  > "Dust and gas are uniformly mixed within the nucleus" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band); "a blackbody of 230 K, attenuated in the same way as the $H_C$ and $W_C$" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band)
   - fitting: Grid over X_H2O and velocity field compared with the observed spectrum; algorithm and statistic not stated  
  > "by varying only the H$_2$O abundance relative to H$_2$ ($X_{\mathrm{H_2O}}$, assumed uniform) and the gas velocity field" (3.2 The 6 um photosphere traced by the H2O nu2=1-0 band)
   - validation: Comparison of individual H2O line profiles with the model; the model overpredicts some low- and high-excitation lines  
  > "The model also overpredicts the absorption in some high excitation lines" (Appendix: Details of the H2O band)
6. **Check the W_C continuum with far-IR molecular absorption**: Model OH, 18OH, H2O, CH, CH+ and H2O+ absorption in the PACS spectra using the W_C (and H_C for OH 79 um) with abundances tuned to match troughs  
  > "An OH abundance of $\approx5\times10^{-6}$, similar to the value found in the nuclear region of NGC 4418 (cite: gon12) is required" (Appendix: The far-IR molecular absorption)
   - parameter: W_C tau_100um ~ 10, T_d = 123 K, R_w = 71 pc  
  > "warm ($T_d=123$ K, Table [\[tab\]](#tab)), and has a size ($R_w=71$ pc) larger than the $H_C$" (Appendix: The far-IR molecular absorption)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Reasonable match to the far-IR lines implies the W_C far-IR continuum is accurate to ~20%  
  > "the continuum level of the $W_C$ in the far-IR predicted by our model is accurate within $\sim20$%" (Appendix: The far-IR molecular absorption)
7. **Fit HCN, C2H2, CH4 and CO2 bands with the H_C model**: Apply the H_C model with f_hot to the 7-8 um bands and the 14 um and CO2 bands, deriving abundances relative to H2O  
  > "We have used our model for the $H_C$, which dominates the continuum at these wavelengths, to fit these bands" (Appendix: The HCN, C2H2, CH4, and CO2 bands)
   - assumption: Foreground CO2 ice with H2O:CO2 = 10:1 added for the CO2 nu3 P branch  
  > "we added foreground CO$_2$ ice with $\mathrm{H_2O:CO_2=10:1}$ (cite: ehr97; roc22)" (Appendix: The HCN, C2H2, CH4, and CO2 bands)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent results for the 7.5 and 14 um bands with a common H_C model; CO2 fit validates the H_C continuum  
  > "This match validates our fit to the $H_C$ continuum." (Appendix: The HCN, C2H2, CH4, and CO2 bands)
8. **Derive luminosity surface density, starburst test and outflow energetics**: Compute Sigma_bol, compare with starburst limits, estimate mass, momentum and energy outflow rates, and test radiation pressure and a hot bubble  
  > "the mass outflow rate associated with the $H_C$ is estimated as $\dot{M}=\mu M_{\mathrm{H_2}} v/R=2.9\times10^3$ $M_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$" (4.4 Outflow energetics)
   - parameter: v = 160 km/s, mu = 1.36, L_AGN = 1e12 Lsun  
  > "With the values of $M_{\mathrm{H_2}}$ and $R$ from Table [\[tab\]](#tab) and a velocity of $160$ km s$^{-1}$" (4.4 Outflow energetics); "where we have adopted $L_{\mathrm{AGN}}=10^{12}\,L_{\mathrm{\odot}}$" (4.4 Outflow energetics)
   - assumption: Entire nuclear source is expanding at 160 km/s although only the mid-IR photosphere is probed  
  > "Here we have assumed that the entire nuclear source is expanding, although we can only probe the kinematics of its mid-IR photosphere." (4.4 Outflow energetics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Hot-bubble shock velocity of 170 km/s at 13.2 pc compared with the observed blueshift  
  > "a shock velocity driven into the ambient gas of $170$ km s$^{-1}$ at 13.2 pc is obtained" (4.4 Outflow energetics)

## Qualitative findings

- The H_C is an extremely compact, optically thick, hot nucleus whose luminosity surface density is about 1 dex above other CONs, favoring an AGN at quasar level over a starburst  
  > "is $\sim1$ dex higher than the values found in other CONs with comparable sizes and gas mass surface densities" (4.3 A CON hidding a quasar, surrounded by a starburst)
- The H2O lines are blueshifted by about 160 km/s relative to emission lines, interpreted as expansion of the nuclear surface  
  > "both sources show the H$_2$O lines blueshifted by $\sim160$ km s$^{-1}$ relative to the emission lines" (2.2 JWST observations of the mid-IR continuum and molecular bands)
- The hot bubble scenario is favored over radiation pressure as the origin of the outflow because it links kinematics and chemistry  
  > "The strength of linking kinematics and chemistry may favor this scenario over radiation pressure as the origin of the observed outflow." (4.4 Outflow energetics)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Combine ALMA, JWST and Herschel to model the nucleus continuum from mid-IR to submm  
  > "observations of the eastern (E) nucleus of IRAS 07251$-$0248 to model its continuum emission from the mid-IR to submillimeter (submm) wavelengths" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Dust trapping raises T_d inside the nucleus, so the luminosity cannot be read directly from the millimeter continuum; greenhouse models and molecular absorption probe the photosphere  
  > "Even the luminosity arising from these nuclei is difficult to establish from millimeter continuum and lines observed with high-resolution, because trapping of dust emitted photons increases the dust temperature ($T_d$) within the nucleus (cite: gon19)" (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Point-source-only and Gaussian-only continuum fits are rejected in favor of the point source + Gaussian model  
  > "Appendix [5](#cont667) shows that the point-source and the Gaussian fits can be disregarded." (2.1 ALMA observations of the 667 um continuum and model)
- Alternative: W_C as a spherical shell surrounding the H_C (as the foreground extinction layer) is slightly disfavored  
  > "is slightly disfavored because the high optical depth of the $W_C$" (4 Discussion and conclusions)
- Alternative: A pure starburst origin of the H_C luminosity is rejected after listing several drawbacks  
  > "Nevertheless, the above pure-starburst scenario faces serious drawbacks in the case of the $H_C$ in IRAS 07251$-$0248." (4.3 A CON hidding a quasar, surrounded by a starburst)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): N_H2 and M_H2 of the H_C could be lower limits because of the adopted inner radius  
  > "could still be lower limits, because the adopted inner radius of the source is $0.15\times R_{\mathrm{out}}$" (4.2 Uncertainties in the inferred parameters)
- Limitation (authors): Part of the 667 um emission is resolved out by ALMA  
  > "a fraction of the total 667 $\mu$m emission from the merger is resolved out by ALMA" (2.1 ALMA observations of the 667 um continuum and model)
- Limitation (authors): Intrinsic luminosities depend on geometry and isotropy assumptions for optically thick components  
  > "The actual scenario is most likely intermediate between the spherical and flat morphologies" (4.2 Uncertainties in the inferred parameters)
- Limitation (authors): Absolute flux accuracy of Band 8 is about 20%  
  > "For Band 8, the absolute flux accuracy is $\sim$20% (ALMA Technical Handbook)." (Appendix: ALMA observations and models for the 667 um continuum)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Component parameters come from a custom greenhouse model grid and by-eye SED fitting with no stated fitting algorithm or posterior, so quoted uncertainties are hard to interpret
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Several conclusions (AGN dominance, outflow driver) rest on geometry assumptions for optically thick components and an extrapolated starburst simulation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The H_C properties depend on an assumed inner radius ratio and flat density profile; N_H2 and mass may be lower limits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A 230 K hidden component is added ad hoc to fix H2O line depths and its origin is uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA continuum is a single band; the temperature of the compact component is assumed (500 K) rather than measured
