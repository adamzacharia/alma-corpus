## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract per-channel spectra**: Fit a point source to calibrated visibilities in each channel at the fixed continuum position; fit uncertainty is channel noise  
  > "We fit a point source to the calibrated visibilities of each channel of each source, using the uncertainty from this fitting procedure as an estimate of the noise in each channel." (2 Data and Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Continuum removal, redshift scaling and flux normalization**: Remove first-order polynomial, scale to z=3 by (D_L ratio)^2 (1+z_c)/(1+z_s), normalize to S_1.4mm=15 mJy using modified blackbody SED fits  
  > "The ALMA spectra are then multiplied by the factor that converts the fitted 1.4 mm (rest-frame 350 $\mu\rm{m}$) flux density to a common $\ensuremath{S_\mathrm{1.4\,mm}}= 15$ mJy," (3 Stacking Methods)
   - parameter: common z = 3.0; S_1.4mm = 15 mJy  
  > "We then re-scale all spectra to a common redshift, $z_\mathrm{common} = 3.0$." (3 Stacking Methods)
   - assumption: single-temperature modified blackbody, beta=2, lambda_0=100 um  
  > "single-temperature modified blackbody, with $\beta=2$ and $\lambda_0$, the wavelength at which the dust opacity is unity, fixed to 100 $\mu\rm{m}$(cite: draine06)." (3 Stacking Methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Line ratios agree within ~15% for different normalizations  
  > "with the majority of average line ratios agreeing to within 15% regardless of normalization choice." (3 Stacking Methods)
3. **Build template and line-centred stacked spectra**: Inverse-variance weighted average on a 500 km/s grid (240-780 GHz) and in 600 km/s channels centred on each target line; detection if central channel S/N>3  
  > "We classify a line as detected if the line flux in the 0 km/s channel has $S/N > 3$." (3 Stacking Methods)
   - parameter: 500 km/s grid; 600 km/s line channels  
  > "The grid is constructed with 500 km/s spacing" (3 Stacking Methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Monte Carlo of 3000 channel shufflings at line-free 513 GHz shows Gaussian noise; <0.3 false detections expected  
  > "we expect $<0.3$ false detections in the $\sim$100 line frequencies we search." (3 Stacking Methods)
4. **Excitation modelling of 12CO/13CO and dense-gas tracers**: RADEX grid models (expanding sphere) fit to line ratios with 15% added uncertainty; priors tau<100 and virialized K_vir  
  > "line optical depths are constrained to be $\tau < 100$" (5 Results)
   - software: RADEX, LAMDA
   - parameter: N_CO/dv=1e15-1e19, Tkin=12-300 K, nH2=10-1e6, 12CO/13CO=10-1e4; TCMB~11 K  
  > "Our grid of models spans $N_\mathrm{CO} / \Delta v = 10^{15}-10^{19}$ $\rm{cm}^{-2}(\rm{km}/\rm{s})^{-1}$, $\ensuremath{T_\mathrm{kin}}= 12-300$ K" (5 Results)
   - assumption: single homogeneous gas component; NGC253 abundance ratios for dense tracers  
  > "we assume the relative abundance ratios of CN, HCN, HNC, and $\rm{HCO}^+$ are the same as in the local starburst galaxy NGC253" (5 Results)
   - fitting: Grid chi-square with posterior distributions; minimum chi2=10.4 for 3 dof  
  > "We find that no single model fits all seven available line ratios well (minimum $\chi^2 = 10.4$ for three degrees of freedom)." (5 Results)
   - validation: Appendix test recovering representative conditions from mock SLEDs of 22 sources  
  > "conditions representative of the inputs are recovered despite the variety of conditions used to generate the input spectra." (Appendix B)
5. **Test mid-IR pumping**: Split sample at S_21um = 2 mJy and compare averaged line ratios with 1000 random half-subsets  
  > "we also repeat the above procedure for 1000 random subsets of the sample, each containing half the total number of sources." (5.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Both groups consistent with random splits  
  > "both groups are consistent with randomly dividing the sample in half, suggesting that vibrational mode pumping is insignificant in the average SPT DSFG." (5.3)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Scale to common z and 1.4 mm flux to give a representative average of line strengths, at small cost in noise  
  > "it provides a more representative average of the wide distribution of line strengths seen in individual sources at a small cost in the final noise level." (3 Stacking Methods)
- Alternative: CMB temperature effects, outlier domination, mid-IR pumping and differential magnification considered as alternatives to pure collisional excitation; CMB and pumping rejected  
  > "This is unlikely to offer a satisfactory explanation for our observed line ratios." (5.3)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single-component modelling of a heterogeneous sample; no single model fits (chi2 min 10.4 for 3 dof); degeneracies remain; differential magnification not accounted  
  > "This likely reflects the simplicity of the modeling procedure, which represents a heterogeneous collection of galaxies as a single, homogeneous gas component of simple geometry." (4.1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Weighted average dominated by few sources in some lines (up to ~65% weight)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line luminosities only relative to a fiducial source; absolute values depend on normalization
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Few detected lines at S/N just above 3; many trials
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 12CO(4-3) deficit drives bimodal CO solution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Differential lensing ignored
