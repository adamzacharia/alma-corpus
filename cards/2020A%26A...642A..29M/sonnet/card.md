## Analysis recipe

1. **LTE modelling of methanol, isotopologs and methanethiol toward Sgr B2(N1S)**: Fit LTE synthetic spectra with Weeds; population diagrams used to set temperatures  
  > "We modeled the spectra of Sgr B2(N1S) and Sgr B2(N2) with the software Weeds (cite: Maret11) under the assumption of local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)" (4.1 Observations)
   - software: Weeds
   - parameter: Five parameters per species: size, column density, temperature, line width, velocity offset  
  > "Each species was modeled with a set of five parameters: size of the emitting region ($\theta_{\rm s}$), column density ($N$), temperature ($T_{\rm rot}$), line width ($\Delta V$), and velocity offset ($V_{\rm off}$)" (4.1 Observations)
   - parameter: Source size 2" assumed; T = 230 K for methanol, 250 K for methanethiol  
  > "We adopted a temperature of 230 K to model the emission of methanol and its isotopologs." (4.3 Comparison to other molecules)
   - assumption: LTE; Vsys = 62 km/s (N1S), 74 km/s (N2)  
  > "$V_{\rm sys}=62$ km s$^{-1}$ for Sgr B2(N1S) and $V_{\rm sys}= 74$ km s$^{-1}$ for Sgr B2(N2)" (4.1 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gaussian fit to integrated intensity maps gives mean size ~2.2" consistent with 2" assumption  
  > "A two-dimensional Gaussian fit to the integrated intensity maps of the least contaminated transitions of methanethiol yields a mean size of $\sim$2.2$\ensuremath{''}$" (4.3 Comparison to other molecules)
2. **Derive thioformamide column density upper limits**: Compare ReMoCA spectra to LTE spectra of thioformamide using formamide's parameters, column density as only free parameter  
  > "keeping only the column density of thioformamide as a free parameter. We found no evidence for emission of thioformamide toward either source." (4.2 Nondetection of thioformamide)
   - software: Weeds
   - assumption: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Thioformamide not detected; at least ~700 (N1S) and ~900 (N2) times less abundant than formamide, versus methanethiol ~40 and 120 times less than methanol.  
  > "thioformamide is at least $\sim$700 and $\sim$900 times less abundant than formamide in Sgr B2(N1S) and Sgr B2(N2), respectively." (5 Discussion)
