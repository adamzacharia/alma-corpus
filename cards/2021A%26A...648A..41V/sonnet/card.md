## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image 7m and 12m data, combine with TP**: ALMA standard calibration script in CASA; tclean (7m, 7m+12m concatenated); feather with TP images  
  > "The interferometric (7m and 12m) and total power images were combined using the CASA task `feather`." (3.5 Imaging and data combination)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Briggs robust=0.5 (continuum, 12CO), robust=2 (faint lines)  
  > "we set robust$=2$ – which ensures higher signal-to-noise ratio – for the C$^{18}$O(J=2-1), N$_2$D$^+$(3-2) and SiO(4-3) images" (3.5 Imaging and data combination)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum source parameters**: 2D Gaussian fits with imfit; inclination from axis ratio assuming circular disks  
  > "we fit 2D-Gaussians by using the CASA task `imfit`." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - assumption: Sources inherently circular, giving inclinations 50-70 deg  
  > "Assuming that the sources inherently have a circular shape, we estimate that the inclination angles of the disks are between 50$\ensuremath{^\circ}$ and 70$\ensuremath{^\circ}$" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian  
  > "we fit 2D-Gaussians by using the CASA task `imfit`." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - validation: Residual maps below 4 sigma  
  > "we found that the maximum values of the residual maps are below 4$\sigma$" (4.1 Continuum emission)
3. **Fit SED for dust temperature**: Simple blackbody fit to mm and FIR points; high-resolution ALMA fluxes excluded  
  > "The fit was optimized using the Python scipy (cite: 2020SciPy) task `curve_fit`, which uses a non-linear least squares algorithm to fit a function to data." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - software: scipy curve_fit
   - assumption: Simple blackbody rather than greybody due to lack of submm data  
  > "due to the lack of information in the submillimeter spectrum and the low resolution of some infrared data we opted to fit a simple black body model." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: non-linear least squares, errors from covariance matrix  
  > "which uses a non-linear least squares algorithm to fit a function to data. The errors come from the uncertainty of the black body fit, and are obtained from the covariance matrix provided by the task." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive dust masses**: Optically thin Md = S D^2/(B(Td) kappa); beta from power-law fit of flux vs frequency  
  > "Assuming isothermal dust emission, well-mixed gas and dust, and optically thin emission, the dust masses are given by," (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - parameter: kappa = 0.1(nu/1e12 Hz)^beta cm2/g; beta=1.8±0.2 default  
  > "we adopted a value of $\beta$ = 1.8$\pm$0.2 a typical opacity spectral index value for the ISM" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - assumption: Distance 150 pc; optically thin; no scattering; masses are lower limits  
  > "the derived masses should be considered as lower limits" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: errors propagated from fluxes, Td and beta; 3 rms upper limit for non-detections  
  > "The errors in the dust masses were calculated by propagating the errors in fluxes, dust temperatures and $\beta$." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - validation: 7m vs 12m masses compared  
  > "calculated from fluxes measured in the dedicated observations and in high resolution continuum archive images, showing similar results, which are within the errors." (4.1 Continuum emission)
5. **Extract TP spectra and fit lines**: Spectra from 2" box at continuum position in TP data; Gaussian fit  
  > "we extract the spectra within a 2" box at the position where the continuum sources are located." (4.2 Line emission)
   - software: scipy curve_fit
   - fitting: single Gaussian per line  
  > "The Gaussian fit performed to the lines is very simple, and although it is not perfect for lines with more complex structure (double peaked or asymmetric profiles)" (4.2 Line emission)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Measure outflow morphology, dynamical times, mass, momentum, energy**: Moment maps of 12CO(2-1) red/blue lobes (3 sigma); sizes deprojected by inclination; column density with Tex=25 and 100 K; mass from N/X_CO  
  > "we estimate the density for two different values of T$_{ex}$: 25 K and 100 K." (4.3.2 Mass, Momentum and Energy)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: opening angle at 1000 AU projected distance; cloud-velocity channels excluded  
  > "The outflow opening angles are derived from the width of the outflow at a projected distance of 1000 AU from the originating source." (4.3.1 First Glimpse)
   - assumption: mu=2.76, X_CO=1e-4, Tbg=2.7 K, outflow perpendicular to disk, optically thin  
  > "a CO abundance of X$_{CO}$ = 10$^{-4}$ (cite: Lacyetal1994)." (4.3.2 Mass, Momentum and Energy)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Bjerkeli+2016 and Dunham+2014 for IRAS 15398; Tex factor ~2.5 as uncertainty  
  > "this factor is a fair representation of the uncertainty in the mass measurements" (4.3.2 Mass, Momentum and Energy)
7. **Analyse outflow kinematics**: Moment 1/2 maps, PV diagrams, manually drawn ellipses on channel maps to identify ejections  
  > "Although these structures were identified manually, and the total number and position of the ellipses may not be exactly as presented here" (5.1.1 IRAS 15398-3359)
   - assumption: Wide-angle wind vs jet-driven models (Lee+2000) as interpretive frameworks  
  > "We adopt the wide-angle wind and the jet-driven wind models to discuss the nature of the outflows of our sample" (5.1 Individual outflows)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compute evolutionary indicators**: Tbol, Lbol, Lbol/Lsubmm (lambda>350 um) by integrating SEDs (Simpson and trapezoid), Mdisk/Menv from 12m and 7m masses; spectral indices; compare with literature  
  > "obtained integrating by two different methods (Simpson and trapezoidal rules), before further averaging (final estimate) and subtracting (uncertainty) them both." (5.2.1 SED evolution)
   - assumption: SEDs not extinction-corrected; Enoch+2009 Tbol class boundaries  
  > "We note at this point that these SEDs are not corrected by extinction" (5.2.1 SED evolution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-comparison between indicators  
  > "Overall, the most used evolutionary indicators ($T_{bol}$, and the ratios $L_{bol}$/$L_{submm}$ and $M_{disk}$/$M_{env}$) seem to agree in diagnosing which are generally more or less evolved sources, but they do not agree completely" (5.2.1 SED evolution)

## Qualitative findings

- Tbol, Lbol/Lsubmm and Mdisk/Menv identify broad classes but disagree on ordering within a class; spectral indices unreliable.  
  > "they are not as effective in arranging (by age) sources in similar evolutionary stages." (6 Summary)
- IRAS 15398 outflow interpreted as precessing episodic jet-driven; IRAS 16059 consistent with episodic wide-angle wind.  
  > "while data in IRAS 16059-3857 show similarities with a wide-angle wind model also showing signs of being episodic." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sources in a common environment with identical observing strategy allow consistent comparison across evolutionary stages.  
  > "Our selection of sources in a common environment, with identical observing strategy, allows for a consistent comparison across different evolutionary stages." (abstract)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Grey-body SED fit rejected due to lack of submm data; self-calibration of archival data not used.  
  > "Usually this emission is fitted by a grey-body model with a variable submillimeter slope, however due to the lack of information" (4.1 Continuum emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Outflow masses are lower limits (no correction for missing flux at cloud velocities or opacity); sample not statistically significant.  
  > "these values should be treated as lower limits, since we do not correct for the missing flux at cloud velocities, nor the opacity of the emission." (4.3.2 Mass, Momentum and Energy)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tiny sample (7) limits conclusions on tracer consistency
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Blackbody Td from poorly sampled SEDs, unreliable for Merin 28
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Manually drawn ellipses are subjective; no quantitative model fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low inclination estimates from Gaussian axis ratios feed deprojected ages
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow masses optically thin and with cloud channels excluded
