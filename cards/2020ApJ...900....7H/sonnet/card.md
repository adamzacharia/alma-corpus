## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit continuum sizes and fluxes**: imfit elliptical Gaussian; radius = deconvolved FWHM/2; inclination from axis ratio  
  > "We use the CASA task imfit to fit an elliptical Gaussian to the images and derive emitting region sizes (deconvolved from the beam) and dust continuum fluxes." (3.1 Continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Gaussian FWHM/2 as disk radius proxy  
  > "The disk sizes are thus estimated from the deconvolved Gaussian FWHM/2" (3.1 Continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CARMA flux and ALMA 0.12" data for UZ Tau E and Takami 2019 for V900 Mon  
  > "The disk radius we derive for V900 Mon (54 au) is consistent with the 45 au value inferred by (cite: takami2019)" (3.1 Continuum)
2. **Estimate dust and disk masses**: Optically thin M = F d^2/(kappa B(T))  
  > "We estimate the disk dust masses from the optically thin assumption that relates the observed flux, $F_\nu$, to the mass of solids" (4.1 Disk Masses)
   - parameter: T = 20 K and 60 K  
  > "We list calculations assuming two dust temperatures, 20 K and 60 K." (4.1 Disk Masses)
   - assumption: kappa_1.3mm ≈ 0.022 cm2/g (Beckwith 1990); gas-to-dust 100  
  > "$\kappa_{\rm 1.3 mm}\approx 0.022$ cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$" (4.1 Disk Masses); "assuming the standard gas-to-dust ratio of 100" (4.1 Disk Masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Long et al. 2018 dust mass for UZ Tau E  
  > "The dust mass estimate for UZ Tau E is consistent with the value derived by (cite: long2018)" (4.1 Disk Masses)
3. **Measure CO moment maps and integrated fluxes**: Moment maps and fluxes integrating channels above 3-sigma  
  > "computed integrating the channel with emission above 3-$\sigma$" (3.2 Spectral line data)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Gas mass from 13CO/C18O ratio**: Compare integrated line ratio to Williams & Best 2014 model grid  
  > "The comparison to the models yield a total gas mass of 3.2$\times$10$^{-3}$M$_{\odot}$." (4.1 Disk Masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Continuum radiative transfer model of UZ Tau E**: Passive disk, 5 free parameters, RADMC-3D, emcee  
  > "The disk model is described by 5 free parameters: the dust mass M$_{\rm disk}$, the slope of the surface density power law $\gamma$, the characteristic radius R$_{\rm c}$" (Appendix A.1)
   - software: RADMC-3D, emcee
   - parameter: 240 walkers, 1000 iterations  
  > "We run RT models with 240 walkers for 1000 iterations." (Appendix A.1)
   - assumption: Passive disk, kappa_abs=0.022 cm2/g, i and PA fixed to Long et al.  
  > "the dust absorption opacity at 1.3 mm is $\kappa_{\rm abs} =
0.022$ cm$^2\,$g$^{-1}$" (Appendix A.1)
   - fitting: emcee; best-fit = max likelihood  
  > "the best-fit (maximum likelihood set of parameters) model allows to obtain a crude estimate" (Appendix A.1)
   - validation: Posterior degeneracies noted  
  > "The parameters’ posterior distributions show that there could be some degeneracies in our modelling." (Appendix A.1)
6. **Keplerian gas model of 13CO and C18O**: pdspy models with RADMC-3D, GALARIO, emcee fit to visibilities; 13 parameters  
  > "We use the pdspy code from (cite: Sheehan2019) to generate synthetic molecular line emission channel maps from a model of a Keplerian-rotating, passively irradiated disk in hydrostatic equilibrium." (Appendix A.2)
   - software: pdspy, RADMC-3D, GALARIO, emcee
   - parameter: 100 walkers, ~2500 steps; distance 131.2±1.7 pc  
  > "Each run was started with 100 walkers and were run for $\sim$2500 steps." (Appendix A.2)
   - assumption: 12CO/H2 = 1e-4; canonical isotopologue ratios; vertically isothermal; q=0.5, gamma=1.0, i=56.16 deg fixed  
  > "The abundance of $^{12}$CO relative to H$_2$ was set to 10$^{-4}$ and assumed constant throughout the disk." (Appendix A.2)
   - fitting: Medians after burn-in; 68% ranges as uncertainties  
  > "the best fit parameters obtained computing the median value of the MCMC samples after removing the burn-in steps" (Appendix A.2)
   - validation: Stellar mass compared with Simon 2000 and Czekala 2019  
  > "comparable to the 1.3$\pm0.08$M$_{\odot}$ derived using $^{12}$CO from the IRAM Plateau de Bure interferometer" (Appendix A.2)
7. **Compare disk size-mass with other samples and measure outflow opening angles**: Compile disk radii and masses from literature; power-law fit for FUors; opening angles of 12CO cavities  
  > "The fit to the FUor sample indicates that disk radii increase with mass as R$\propto$ M$^{0.27 \pm 0.15}$ ." (4.2 Disk Sizes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- FUors are more massive than Class II sources of similar size; EXor disks resemble Class II  
  > "FUor disks are more massive than Class II sources of similar size. In contrast, the properties of the EXor disks are more consistent with those of Class II objects." (4.2 Disk Sizes)
- Binary FUor disks are compact  
  > "FUor disks around binaries are host to more compact disks than those in single-star systems, similar to non-eruptive young disks." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Deconvolved Gaussian FWHM/2 radius chosen as homogeneous size measure across datasets  
  > "We choose to use the deconvolved Gaussian FWHM/2 radius since it provides an homogeneous measure across the selected ALMA datasets." (4.2 Disk Sizes)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Optically thin and fixed-temperature assumptions make masses crude  
  > "A crude estimation of the disk dust masses can be obtained under the standard assumption that the continuum emission from disks is optically thin" (4.1 Disk Masses)
- Limitation (authors): V582 Aur unresolved so GI cannot be tested  
  > "it was unresolved by our observations and therefore we are unable to test the GI scenario" (4.4 Triggering mechanisms)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample; power-law fit for FUors poorly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Passive-disk models inappropriate for accreting systems
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gaussian size proxy mixed with other literature methods
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas-to-dust ratio estimates inconsistent (14 vs 2.8)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): V582 Aur CO contaminated by cloud
