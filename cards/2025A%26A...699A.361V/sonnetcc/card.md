## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce MIRI-MRS data to cubes**: All three JWST pipeline stages; dedicated detector-level background subtraction with astronomical emission masked; fringe flat and residual fringe correction; extra bad-pixel map; cubes per channel and sub-band with outlier rejection and master background off  
  > "The raw data were processed through all three steps of the JWST calibration pipeline." (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction); "This included the subtraction of the dedicated background on the detector level, where astronomical emission in the background was masked so that it was not subtracted in the science data." (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction); "Last, the final data cubes were constructed for each channel and sub-band separately with the outlier rejection and master background steps switched off." (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction)
   - software: JWST calibration pipeline, VIP
   - parameter: JWST calibration pipeline version 1.13.4; reference context jwst_1188.pmap  
  > "The MIRI-MRS data were reduced and calibrated using the JWST calibration pipeline version 1.13.4 (cite: Bushouse24) using reference context `jwst`$\_$`1188.pmap`" (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction)
   - parameter: Additional bad pixel map built with VIP 4.1 before cube building  
  > "Prior to building the cubes, an additional bad pixel map was created using the Vortex Imaging Package (VIP) version 4.1 (cite: Christiaens23)." (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra at chosen positions**: Manual extraction at selected positions with constant-diameter ('circle') or wavelength-growing PSF-following ('cone') apertures  
  > "Spectra were extracted manually from selected positions in the datacubes using either an aperture with a constant diameter as function of wavelength" (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction); "an aperture with its diameter increasing with wavelength (“cone”) following the size of the point spread function" (3.3 MIRI-MRS data reduction)
   - parameter: IRAS 4B knot spectrum: 1.4 arcsec aperture  
  > "extracted from the brightest knot position in a 1.4$''$ aperture" (6.3 NGC 1333 IRAS4B: Dense shock)
   - parameter: IRAS 18089 spectrum: 0.7 arcsec aperture at the mid-IR continuum peak  
  > "extracted from the peak mid-infrared continuum in a 0.7$''$ aperture" (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit H2 excitation and extinction**: H2 line fluxes in ~1 arcsec apertures at two knots placed in a rotation diagram; two-temperature fit with OPR and extinction  
  > "H$_2$ line fluxes have been extracted near two “knot” positions in a $\sim$1$''$ aperture and put in a rotational diagram." (5.1 H2 excitation and extinction determination)
   - parameter: KP5 extinction curve of Pontoppidan24ext  
  > "Here the KP5 extinction curve of (cite: Pontoppidan24ext) has been used" (5.1 H2 excitation and extinction determination)
   - assumption: At least two temperature components are needed for the H2 lines; S(3) deficit due to silicate extinction used to infer extinction  
  > "At least two temperature components are needed to fit the remainder of the H$_2$ lines" (5.1 H2 excitation and extinction determination); "the upper level column densities derived from the S(3) line clearly fall below the trend of the other lines due to additional extinction by the silicate feature in the envelope" (5.1 H2 excitation and extinction determination)
   - fitting: Simultaneous best fit of two temperatures, OPR and extinction (sampler/priors not stated)  
  > "Using a procedure to obtain a simultaneous best fit for the two temperatures plus ortho-to-para ratio (OPR) and extinction results in the values indicated in the panels" (5.1 H2 excitation and extinction determination)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive mass loss rates**: Mass loss from warm H2 column, emission area, tangential velocity and projected knot size; atomic-line rates from [Fe II] 26 micron in Appendix  
  > "The H$_2$ column densities found from the rotational diagrams can be used to calculate mass loss rates through" (5.1 Mass loss rates)
   - parameter: Blue knot width 625 au  
  > "The width of the blue-shifted knot $l_{\rm tg}$ is measured to be 625 au at the position of the extracted spectrum from the lowest $J$ lines" (5.1 Mass loss rates)
   - parameter: Line-of-sight velocity -18 km/s from H2 S(1)-S(4) shifts  
  > "the line-of-sight velocity is –18 km s$^{-1}$ based on the average of the velocity shifts from the H$_2$ S(1) to S(4) lines that best trace the warm component" (5.1 Mass loss rates)
   - parameter: Extinction-corrected warm H2 column 9.6e19 cm-2  
  > "taking the extinction corrected warm H$_2$ column of $9.6 \times 10^{19}$ cm$^{-2}$ derived from the rotation diagram" (5.1 Mass loss rates)
   - assumption: Inclination 50 deg from CO millimeter maps (Yildiz15)  
  > "Assuming an inclination of 50$^o$ estimated from CO millimeter maps (cite: Yildiz15)" (5.1 Mass loss rates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Atomic-line mass loss rates compared with H2 rate  
  > "These atomic diagnostics give values of $\dot{M}_{\rm jet}$ that are slightly lower than derived from H$_2$ by a factor of two to three when applied to one outflow lobe" (5.1 Mass loss rates)
5. **Estimate accretion rates from H I 7-6**: Rigliaco15 H I 7-6 (plus 11-8) luminosity relation, corrected for extinction and water contamination  
  > "Using the same relation of (cite: Rigliaco15) and correcting for extinction and water, a mass accretion rate of $6.6\times 10^{-7}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$ is found for TMC1A." (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion); "Any water contribution can be subtracted using a fit to the H$_2$O emission over a larger wavelength range" (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion)
   - assumption: Extinction from silicate optical depth, A_V = 18.5 x tau_9.7 (about 30 mag for TMC1A); 30-40 mag with McClure09 law for IRAS 23385  
  > "the extinction can be estimated, albeit with considerable uncertainty, to be $A_V\approx 30$ mag from the silicate optical depth using $A_V=18.5 \times \tau_{\rm 9.7}$" (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion); "Correcting this number for an estimated visual extinction of 30–40 mag based on the depth of the silicate feature, and using the (cite: McClure09) extinction law" (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with rates from bolometric luminosity  
  > "The value of $5\times 10^{-9}$ M$_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$ is an order of magnitude lower than that derived from the bolometric luminosity" (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion)
6. **Analyse gas-phase molecular lines and compare JWST with ALMA SO2**: Isothermal slab models fitted to continuum-subtracted spectra; non-LTE RADEX calculation to relate T_rot to T_kin; ALMA SO2 column used as consistency check  
  > "Mid-infrared emission lines are also usually interpreted with isothermal slab models fitted to continuum-subtracted spectra." (3.5 Analysis of gas-phase spectra); "A non-LTE excitation calculation using RADEX (cite: vanderTak07; vanderTak20) with collisional rate coefficients from (cite: Balanca16) is adopted to compute $T_{\rm rot}$" (6.1 SO2)
   - software: RADEX
   - assumption: Single excitation temperature for the slab; absorbers fill the continuum source  
  > "The gas is taken to be at a single excitation temperature $T_{\rm ex}$" (3.5 Analysis of gas-phase spectra); "It is usually assumed that the covering fraction of the absorbers against the continuum is unity and that the gas is at a single temperature" (3.5 Analysis of gas-phase spectra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: JWST and ALMA molecule numbers agree once infrared pumping is included  
  > "When the infrared pumping is included, the number of molecules derived from the JWST and ALMA data are consistent." (6.1 SO2)
7. **Compare column density ratios with hot core chemistry models**: Observed B1-c ratios relative to warm H2O compared with the Notsu21 gas-grain model at 150, 300 and 600 K with and without X-rays  
  > "the results of the gas-grain chemistry model of (cite: Notsu21) using the envelope structure of NGC 1333 IRAS2A derived by (cite: Kristensen12) at $r$=60 au" (6.2 Molecular abundances: Hot core chemistry)
   - parameter: Model position just inside the water snowline (~150 K), density 2e8 cm-3; L_X = 1e29 erg/s vs no X-rays  
  > "This position is just inside the water snowline ($\sim$150 K) where the hot core starts and the density is $2\times 10^8$ cm$^{-3}$." (6.2 Molecular abundances: Hot core chemistry); "Values without X-rays and for $L_X=10^{29}$ erg s$^{-1}$ are tabulated." (6.2 Molecular abundances: Hot core chemistry)
   - assumption: Warm H2O taken as the reference column  
  > "Hence, warm H$_2$O is taken as a reference." (6.2 Molecular abundances: Hot core chemistry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Models reproduce ratios within about an order of magnitude; C2H2 underproduced at 150 K  
  > "hot core models of ice sublimation and gas-phase chemistry with modest X-rays reproduce the observed abundance ratios reasonably well within the model uncertainties of up to an order of magnitude" (6.2 Molecular abundances: Hot core chemistry); "However, C$_2$H$_2$ is consistently underproduced by orders of magnitude at temperatures of only 150 K." (6.2 Molecular abundances: Hot core chemistry)
8. **Fit ice features (IRAS 18089 COMs and H2O)**: Remove global continuum, silicate feature and H2O libration band; choose local continuum (5th-order polynomial) over 6.9-9.0 micron; fit LIDA laboratory spectra with ENIIGMA (chi^2)  
  > "the spectrum is fitted using the infrared laboratory spectra of simple and complex molecules from the LIDA database, following (cite: Rocha24) using the ENIIGMA fitting tool (cite: Rocha21)" (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices); "obtained by fitting a fifth-order polynomial to guiding points to avoid unrealistic inflections" (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices)
   - software: ENIIGMA, LIDA database
   - fitting: ENIIGMA combines laboratory ice spectra in small groups, best solution by chi^2  
  > "This code combines a large number of laboratory ice spectra in small groups of components aiming to find the best solution given by the $\chi^2$." (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices)
   - validation: Identifications judged with Rocha24 statistical criteria in ENIIGMA; acetone tentative  
  > "Based on the statistical methods and criteria of (cite: Rocha24) and as embedded in the ENIIGMA code, most of these identifications are thought to be secure" (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices)
9. **Search for O2 ice in B1-c**: Local continuum subtraction at 6-6.6 micron (5th-order polynomial) and comparison with laboratory O2:H2O spectra to set a 3 sigma limit  
  > "A local continuum subtraction is performed between 6 and 6.6 $\mu$m, where any contribution of O$_2$ ice is expected" (7.3 Limits on O2 ice); "provides a $3\sigma$ limit of O$_2$" (7.3 Limits on O2 ice)
   - parameter: Band strength A = 3.9e-20 cm/molecule for 1:1 O2:H2O mixture (Muller18)  
  > "using a band strength $A=3.9\times 10^{-20}$ cm molecule$^{-1}$ appropriate for 1:1 O$_2$ ice mixtures with H$_2$O (cite: Muller18)" (7.3 Limits on O2 ice)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
10. **Measure HDO/H2O ice ratio in B1-c**: NIRSpec G395M spectrum; remove wing of CO2 ice feature with a local continuum; fit HDO + CH3OH feature; divide by H2O column from the libration mode  
  > "This wing has been removed using a local continuum in a similar manner as for L1527." (7.4 HDO/H2O in protostellar ices); "Using the same fitting procedure as in (cite: Slavicinska24HDO; Slavicinska25L1527), an HDO column of $1.2\times 10^{17}$ cm$^{-2}$ is derived." (7.4 HDO/H2O in protostellar ices)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with L1527 value  
  > "Together this gives HDO/H$_2$O=$(4.8 \pm 3.0)\times 10^{-3}$, consistent with that found toward L1527." (7.4 HDO/H2O in protostellar ices)

## Qualitative findings

- Atomic line maps differ among refractory, semi-refractory and volatile elements, linked to depletion and local shock conditions  
  > "The atomic line maps differ among refractory (e.g., Fe), semi-refractory (e.g., S), and volatile elements (e.g., Ne)" (abstract)
- Gas-phase molecular emission is cool (100-250 K), unlike in more evolved disks  
  > "but it is cool compared with what is found in more evolved disks, with excitation temperatures of only 100–250 K" (abstract)
- JWST and ALMA are complementary for molecules with a dipole moment  
  > "This study emphasizes the synergy between JWST and ALMA for molecules with a dipole moment." (6.1 SO2)
- Icy COM patterns are very similar between low- and high-mass protostars  
  > "Very similar patterns are seen: not only are the same molecules detected in all four sources" (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sources chosen to address the different science cases  
  > "They were chosen for their ability to address the different science cases outlined in Sect. 2 and 4–8." (4.1 Source sample)
- Why this method: MRS IFU maps the inner parts of jets and outflows at sub-arcsec scales in the mid-infrared  
  > "The Medium Resolution Spectrometer (MRS) enables mapping of the inner parts of jets and outflows through its Integral Field Unit (IFU)" (Introduction)
- Alternative: Not all bolometric luminosity need come from accretion; accretion from L_bol versus H I rates differ by large factors for SMM3  
  > "Alternatively, not all luminosity needs to come from accretion." (5.1 Mass loss rates)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): H I-based accretion rates are upper limits  
  > "Thus, the inferred accretion rates from H I should be considered as upper limits." (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion)
- Limitation (authors): Accretion rates depend strongly on the assumed extinction  
  > "We stress that all accretion rate determinations depend strongly on the assumed extinction toward the protostellar embryo" (4.2 H I recombination lines as a tracer of accretion)
- Limitation (authors): O2 ice limit uncertain by a factor of five owing to band strength dependence  
  > "Due to the strong dependence of the band strength on H$_2$O ice concentration, this value may be increased by a factor of five." (7.3 Limits on O2 ice)
- Limitation (authors): Icy COM detections sensitive to continuum placement  
  > "the detections and abundances of icy COMs remain sensitive to the details of the global and local continuum placement and subtraction" (7.1 Detection of complex molecules in ices)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA is only used as comparison data from cited papers; no ALMA reduction or imaging is described, so ALMA-based statements cannot be checked from this paper
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Many results are illustrative single-source examples (SMM3, B1-c, IRAS 18089) rather than sample statistics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Slab-model column densities depend on assumed pumping and line widths; fit uncertainties for most numbers are not given
