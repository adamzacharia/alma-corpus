## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure 3 mm fluxes and spectral indices**: Total flux from image plane; point-source fit check; alpha from ln flux ratio between 0.89 and 3 mm  
  > "We also use `uvmodelfit` in CASA to fit a point source, and find similar values as in the images for the total flux" (Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: uvmodelfit point-source fit compared with image fluxes  
  > "We also use `uvmodelfit` in CASA to fit a point source, and find similar values as in the images for the total flux" (Observations)
2. **Estimate dust masses**: Optically thin dust mass from 0.89 mm flux  
  > "we estimate the disk dust mass assuming optically thin emission (cite: hildebrand1983; andrews2013)" (2.1 Millimeter Spectral Index and Disk Dust Mass)
   - assumption: d = 140 pc; kappa=2.3 cm2/g (nu/230GHz)^0.4; T_dust≈25 (L/Lsun)^0.17 K  
  > "$T_{\rm{dust}}\approx25\times(L_\star/L_\odot)^{0.17}$ K obtained by (cite: vanderplas2016)" (2.1 Millimeter Spectral Index and Disk Dust Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Gap-opening criterion and hydro simulation**: Crida criterion; FARGO2D with Saturn-mass planet at 10 au, alpha=1e-3  
  > "For the hydrodynamical models, we used the legacy version of FARGO2D (cite: masset2000)." (Hydrodynamical simulation.)
   - software: FARGO2D
   - parameter: 512 radial x 950 azimuthal cells, 0.2 to 6 r_p, h0=0.14, flaring 0.25, disk mass 2 MJup  
  > "The disk aspect ratio at the planet position ($h_0$) is taken to be 0.14." (Hydrodynamical simulation.)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Dust evolution and model spectral indices**: Dust evolution on hydro gas profile, 180 sizes 1 um to 2 m, v_frag 30 m/s, Mie opacities, flux integrated, alpha between 1.3 and 3 mm  
  > "We follow the evolution of 180 grain sizes from 1 $\mu$m to 2 m." (Dust evolution and theoretical spectral indices.)
   - assumption: inclination 20 deg; distance 140 pc  
  > "which we assume to be $20^\circ$ (cite: scholz2006)" (Dust evolution and theoretical spectral indices.)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
