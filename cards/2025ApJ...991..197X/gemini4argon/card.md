## Science

- Fields: nearby_galaxies, star_formation
- Question: Whether the starburst SQ-A in the intragroup medium of Stephan's Quintet is triggered by a high-speed collision between the IGrM and the intruder galaxy NGC 7318b, and what physical mechanisms cause both star formation enhancement and suppression in colliding gas systems.

## Qualitative findings

- The 6900 and 6000 CO(2-1) kinematic components in SQ-A do not spatially overlap but are bridged by the intermediate-velocity 6600 component.  
  > "The 6900 and 6000 components do not overlap with each other but are bridged by the the 6600 component." (3. COLLISION OF SYSTEMS ASSOCIATED WITH IGRM AND INTRUDER)
- In the HI maps, shifting the 6600 component eastward by 12'' (~5 kpc) makes it fit into a gap in the extended 6000 component, showing complementary distributions characteristic of cloud-cloud collisions.  
  > "Fig. 4 demonstrates that, when the 6600 HI map is shifted relative to the 6000 HI map eastwardly by 12'' (corresponding to a linear scale of ~5 kpc for a distance of 94 Mpc), the maps of the two components become very "complementary"" (3. COLLISION OF SYSTEMS ASSOCIATED WITH IGRM AND INTRUDER)
- Clumps across the three kinematic components have similar dynamical properties (R_e, sigma_v, alpha_vir, P_e) and are mostly gravitationally unbound (alpha_vir > 2) and pressure-confined.  
  > "All but two of them have alpha_vir > 2, namely are unbound by gravity." (4.2. Dynamical Properties of Clumps); "There is a strong correlation between P_e/k_B and alpha_vir (the Spearman correlation coefficient r_s = 0.62 with a significance of p= 6 x 10^-5), indicating that the clumps are mainly confined by the external pressure instead of the self-gravity." (4.2. Dynamical Properties of Clumps)
- Star formation surface density Sigma_SFR in SQ-A clumps is stratified by kinematic component over ~2 orders of magnitude and shows no correlation with Sigma_mol or alpha_vir.  
  > "In contrast, their Sigma_SFR values are stratified over ~2 orders of magnitude range: 6600 clumps are on the top, 6000 clumps in the middle, and 6900 clumps at the bottom." (4.3. Star Formation Properties of Clumps); "In SQ-A clumps, Sigma_SFR does not depend on Sigma_mol at all: their Spearman rank correlation coefficient is r_s = -0.04 and the significance parameter (i.e. probability of the null hypothesis) p = 0.82." (4.3. Star Formation Properties of Clumps)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-resolution ALMA CO(2-1) data (down to 0.38'' x 0.23'', ~120 pc) are needed to resolve individual molecular clumps in SQ-A, while matched-resolution (~7''-8'') ACA CO(2-1) and VLA B+C+D HI cubes allow a robust comparison of atomic and molecular gas kinematics across the colliding systems; JWST 15 um provides an extinction-insensitive star formation tracer free from strong shock-excited H2 line contamination.  
  > "However both F770W and F1000W are significantly contaminated by shock-excited warm H_2 line emissions and therefore are disfavored: the F770W filter includes the H2S(4) line at 8.025 um and the F1000W filter the H2S(3) line at 9.665 um." (2.3. JWST MIRI images); "It is worth noting that the two cubes have very similar angular resolutions (ACA CO(2-1): 8.0'' x 7.0'', VLA B, C & D arrays HI: 7.9'' x 6.7'') and therefore the comparison between them is robust." (3. COLLISION OF SYSTEMS ASSOCIATED WITH IGRM AND INTRUDER); "In order to probe at the cloud level the physical conditions that lead to the conspicuous difference in SF activity in different kinematic components (Table 1), in this section we study "clumps" extracted from the high resolution CO(2-1) datacube in the Field 4" (4.1. Clumps Extracted from High Resolution CO(2-1) Datacube)
- Why this method: FellWalker was chosen for clump identification because it shows high completeness and accuracy compared to other algorithms; peak 15 um flux in a fixed r = 0.25'' aperture was used instead of integrated clump area because many clumps are smaller than the 15 um beam and blended in 2D.  
  > "According to C. Li et al. (2020), Fellwalker shows high completeness and accuracy compared to other clump identification algorithms." (4.1. Clumps Extracted from High Resolution CO(2-1) Datacube); "Two operational issues make the estimation of Sigma_SFR from integrated 15 um flux over the actual sky area of a clump unfavorable: (1) many clumps are smaller than the 15 um beam; (2) on the 2-D image of the 15 um emission many clumps are blinded with each other." (A. CLUMPS IN FIELD 4)
- Alternative: Tidally induced star formation with chance projection of multiple kinematic components (Mendes de Oliveira et al. 2001), rejected based on the CO velocity bridge and complementary HI distributions.  
  > "An alternative model to explain the starburst in SQ-A was proposed by C. Mendes de Oliveira et al. (2001) who listed the two kinematic systems in SQ-A as two separate tidal dwarf candidates, suggesting that the SF in SQ-A is tidally induced and the collocation of multiple kinematic components in the region is due to projection effect." (1. INTRODUCTION); "This new piece of supporting evidence enables us to conclude with high confidence that the multiple kinematic components in SQ-A are colliding with each other and their collocation in the region is not merely a projection effect." (3. COLLISION OF SYSTEMS ASSOCIATED WITH IGRM AND INTRUDER)
- Alternative: Using JWST MIRI F770W (7.7 um) and F1000W (10 um) bands as star formation indicators, rejected because they are contaminated by shock-excited H2 S(4) and H2 S(3) line emission.  
  > "However both F770W and F1000W are significantly contaminated by shock-excited warm H_2 line emissions and therefore are disfavored: the F770W filter includes the H2S(4) line at 8.025 um and the F1000W filter the H2S(3) line at 9.665 um." (2.3. JWST MIRI images)
- Alternative: Interpretation by Sulentic et al. (2001) that cold IGrM gas in the shock region was converted to hot X-ray and warm ionized gas after the collision, rejected because cooling times in cloud-cloud collisions are < 10^6 yr.  
  > "J. W. Sulentic et al. (2001) suggested that the reason for the lack of cold IGrM gas in the shock region is because it has been converted to hot X-ray gas and warm ionized gas after the collision. This interpretation is unlikely to be true because, as shown in simulations (e.g. R. Maeda et al. 2021), the cooling time in cloud-cloud collisions is so short that the post-shock gas becomes cold and neutral again soon after collision (on a timescale of < 10^6 yrs)." (5.2. Collision of Cloud with Diffuse Gas and Suppression of SFR)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The VLA HI observations only cover velocities up to 6885 km s^-1, missing the 6900 km s^-1 component.  
  > "The final HI data cube has a synthesized beam of 7.9'' x 6.7'' (FWHM) and a velocity coverage of 5550 - 6885 km s^-1." (2.2. VLA Observations of HI 21 cm Line); "Unfortunately the 6900 component is not covered by the VLA HI observations." (3. COLLISION OF SYSTEMS ASSOCIATED WITH IGRM AND INTRUDER)
- Limitation (authors): Two clumps (#35 and #36) lie outside the 22'' primary beam of the Field 4 observation, and the 6000 component in the north-western part of SQ-A falls outside the Field 4 primary beam where sensitivity is too low to detect clumps.  
  > "For the 6000 component in the north-western part no clumps are found because this region is outside the primary beam of the Field 4 (whose boundary is marked by the black dash-dot line) and the sensitivity becomes too low." (4.1. Clumps Extracted from High Resolution CO(2-1) Datacube); "Two clumps (#35 and #36) have r_SQA > 11'' and therefore are outside the primary beam. They should be treated with caution." (A. - Limitation (authors): With a spatial resolution of ~120 pc, small-scale turbulence within the clouds cannot be directly resolved or measured.  
  > "With a resolution of ~120 pc, we cannot resolve the small scale turbulence." (4.3. Star Formation Properties of Clumps); "While our result on the anti-correlation between t_dep and the C parameter seems to support this hypothesis, it is important to note that we do not have any direct measure of the turbulent motion." (4.3. Star Formation Properties of Clumps)
- Limitation (authors): The CO conversion factor alpha_CO10 and the CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) luminosity ratio r_21 are highly uncertain, leading to a factor of ~2 uncertainty in M_mol.

ALMA Observations of CO(2-1)); "f Logarithm of M_mol, with an error on the order of a factor of ~2 dominated by the uncertainty of the conversion factor alpha_CO10." (Table A1)
- Limitation (authors): Projection effects, beam smearing, clump evolutionary stage variations, and small-scale alpha_CO variations introduce substantial scatter into the clump parameters and correlations.  
  > "The projection effect and beam smearing effect can affect many parameters such as R_e, sigma_v, S parameter (projected specific angular momentum), C parameter (nonrotation-to-rotation energy ratio), and the velocity gradient (G_v parameter)." (4.3. Star Formation Properties of Clumps)
- Limitation (authors): The Sigma_SFR estimated from JWST 15 um flux has an uncertainty of about a factor of 2 due to potential [NIII] 15.56 um line contamination, small dust grain destruction, and clump-to-clump variations.  
  > "Given the multiple uncertainties listed above, the error of the Sigma_SFR so estimated is on the order of a factor of 2." (A. CLUMPS IN FIELD 4)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Many clumps have derived effective diameters (2 * R_e) smaller than the ~120 pc ALMA synthesized beam without beam deconvolution, meaning kinematic gradients (G_v) and rotational parameters (S, C) are heavily affected by beam smearing.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The C parameter is defined as the total-to-rotational kinetic energy ratio minus one, which conflates solenoidal turbulence, compressive turbulence, shear, and unresolved rotation rather than isolating compressive turbulence.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 5 of the 36 clumps belong to the 6000 km s^-1 component (and one of those is outside the primary beam at r_SQA = 13.64''), limiting statistical comparisons for that component.
