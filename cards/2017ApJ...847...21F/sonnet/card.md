## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit IR SEDs of analogs and local galaxies**: Casey (2012) graybody + mid-IR power-law fit to 24-500 um photometry; lambda0 fixed at 200 um  
  > "we fix the wavelength at which the optical depth is unity to $\lambda_{0}=200\,{\rm \mu m}$" (2.1.1)
   - parameter: beta_IR free; also fixed 1.6 and 2.0 as test  
  > "we also fit the photometry with a fixed $\beta_{\rm IR}$ of $1.6$ and $2.0$" (2.1.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: no significant differences with fixed beta_IR  
  > "we do not find any significant differences in the following results and conclusions" (2.1.1)
2. **Optical SED fitting of analogs**: BC03 templates with emission lines, Levenberg-Marquardt, Monte Carlo errors  
  > "The fit is performed using a Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm, as part of the `R/minpack.lm` package" (2.2.3)
   - software: R/minpack.lm
   - fitting: Levenberg-Marquardt  
  > "The fit is performed using a Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm, as part of the `R/minpack.lm` package" (Physical properties from optical SED fitting)
   - validation: photometric vs spectroscopic Halpha EW agree within 25%  
  > "we find an agreement on the order of $25\%$ or better between the photometrically and spectroscopically measured H$\alpha$ EW and line ratios" (2.2.3)
3. **Recompute L_FIR of z~5.5 galaxies with warm prior**: Casey SED normalized to 158 um continuum, T=60-90 K, marginalized over alpha and beta_IR, integrated 3-1100 um  
  > "The total FIR luminosity is derived by integration between $3\,{\rm \mu m}$ and $1100\,{\rm \mu m}$" (4.1)
   - parameter: 1.5<alpha<2.5, 1.0<beta_IR<2.0  
  > "The result is marginalized over a grid of mid-IR slopes and emissivities ($1.5 < \alpha < 2.5$, $1.0 < \beta_{\rm IR} < 2.0$)." (4.1)
   - assumption: temperature prior 60-90 K extrapolated from local trends and analogs  
  > "the applied warm IR SED prior is motivated by the extrapolation of trends found in local galaxy samples as well as three low-redshift analogs" (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings
