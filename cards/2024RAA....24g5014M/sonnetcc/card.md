## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image continuum and line cubes**: CASA pipeline calibration, mstransform to split the target, tclean continuum and spectral cubes, uvcontsub, primary beam correction  
  > "We separated the target data with all available rest frequencies using the CASA task MSTRANSFORM after gain calibration." (2 Observation and data reductions); "Next, we employ the UVCONTSUB task to remove the continuum emission from the UV plane of the calibrated data." (2 Observation and data reductions)
   - software: CASA 5.4.1, tclean, mstransform, uvcontsub, impbcor, setjy
   - parameter: Briggs weighting, robust = 0.5, specmode = cube  
  > "with the SPECMODE = CUBE parameter and Briggs weighting at a robust value of 0.5" (2 Observation and data reductions)
   - parameter: Perley-Butler 2017 flux model  
  > "We utilized the Perley-Butler 2017 flux calibrator model" (2 Observation and data reductions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra of MM1 and MM3**: Spectra extracted from a circular region over each hot core  
  > "The molecular spectra were extracted by drawing a 0.90$^{\prime\prime}$ diameter circular region over the MM1 and MM3" (3.1 Molecular lines in G358.93–0.03)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 0.90 arcsec diameter circular aperture, larger than the line emitting regions  
  > "which is greater than the line emitting regions of both hot cores" (3.1 Molecular lines in G358.93–0.03)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit LTE model to identify lines and derive N and Tex**: LTE spectral model fitted with an MCMC algorithm in CASSIS using CDMS and JPL line lists; 250 further transitions fitted to account for blending  
  > "algorithm in the CASSIS software package" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1); "we also fitted the rest of the 250 molecular transitions, which were taken from CDMS and JPL molecular databases" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1)
   - software: CASSIS, CDMS, JPL catalog
   - parameter: Detection criterion: non-blended lines at >=4 sigma (NH2CHO) and >=4.5 sigma (HNCO)  
  > "After spectral analysis, we noticed that all detected lines of NH$_{2}$CHO are non-blended" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1); "The non-blended emission lines of HNCO exhibited $\geq$4.5 $\sigma$ statistical significance." (3.3 Rotational emission lines of HNCO towards MM1)
   - assumption: LTE holds because the gas density of the inner MM1 region is high  
  > "indicating that the LTE assumptions are true for this source (cite: ste21)" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1)
   - fitting: MCMC in CASSIS; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "algorithm in the CASSIS software package" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1)
   - validation: Blending assessed by fitting 250 other CDMS/JPL transitions  
  > "we also fitted the rest of the 250 molecular transitions, which were taken from CDMS and JPL molecular databases" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1)
4. **Derive fractional abundances**: Best-fit column density divided by the H2 column density towards MM1 taken from the earlier paper  
  > "The abundance of NH$_{2}$CHO towards MM1 with respect to molecular H$_{2}$ is" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1)
   - parameter: N(H2) = (3.10±0.2)e24 cm-2 (MM1), (3.51±0.7)e23 cm-2 (MM3)  
  > "where the column density of molecular H$_{2}$ towards the MM3 is (3.51$\pm$0.7)$\times$10$^{23}$ cm$^{-2}$ (cite: man23b)" (3.4 Searching the emission lines of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Constrain non-detections (MM3 and other peptide-bond COMs)**: Upper-limit column densities from LTE-modelled spectra; the other peptide-bond molecules were all blended  
  > "However, we were unable to find the emission lines of those molecules towards MM3." (3.4 Searching the emission lines of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM3); "During the spectral analysis, we observed that all the above-mentioned molecules are blended with other molecular transitions." (3.6 Searching of other peptide bonds molecules towards MM1)
   - software: CASSIS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Map line emission and measure emitting-region size**: Integrated-intensity maps of non-blended lines from the cubes, overlaid on the 988 um continuum, with 2D Gaussian fits for sizes  
  > "by integrating the spectral data cubes using the channel ranges with the help of CASA task" (3.5 Spatial distribution of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1); "by fitting the 2D Gaussians over the emission maps of NH$_{2}$CHO and HNCO using the" (3.5 Spatial distribution of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
   - software: CASA immoments, CASA imfit
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit with imfit  
  > "by fitting the 2D Gaussians over the emission maps of NH$_{2}$CHO and HNCO using the" (3.5 Spatial distribution of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
   - validation: Emitting sizes compared with the beam sizes to judge whether sources are resolved  
  > "This suggests that the emission maps of NH$_{2}$CHO and HNCO are not spatially resolved towards MM1." (3.5 Spatial distribution of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
7. **Compare abundances with other sources and chemical model**: Compare MM1 abundances with other hot cores and a hot corino, with the Gorai et al. warm-up model, and with the López-Sepulcre et al. abundance correlation  
  > "we compare the fractional abundance of those molecules towards MM1 and other hot molecular cores such as G10.47+0.03, G31.41+0.31, Orion KL, Sgr B2" (4.1 Comparison of the abundances); "we compared our derived abundance of NH$_{2}$CHO and HNCO with the modelled abundance of (cite: gor20)" (4.2 Possible formation mechanism of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
   - assumption: MM1 gas density and temperature are comparable to the model conditions, so the model applies  
  > "This comparison is reasonable because the gas density and temperature of this source are" (4.2 Possible formation mechanism of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement of observed and modelled abundances taken as support  
  > "which are nearly similar to the modelled abundance results in (cite: gor20)" (4.2 Possible formation mechanism of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)

## Qualitative findings

- Only MM1 and MM3 show molecular line emission; no molecular lines in the other continuum sources  
  > "We did not find any molecular line emissions from other sources in G358.93–0.03." (3.1 Molecular lines in G358.93–0.03)
- NH2CHO and HNCO are not spatially resolved towards MM1, so their chemical morphologies cannot be determined  
  > "Therefore, we could not determine the chemical morphologies of NH$_{2}$CHO and HNCO from the integrated emission maps." (3.5 Spatial distribution of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
- MM1 abundances of NH2CHO and HNCO are lower than in G10.47+0.03 and G31.41+0.31, the most abundant sources

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Openly available ALMA Band 6 and 7 observations of the line-rich hot core MM1  
  > "The detection of the above-mentioned molecules indicates that the MM1 is an ideal candidate for studying the emission lines of different types of complex biomolecules" (Introduction)
- Why this method: LTE modelling justified by the high gas density of MM1  
  > "indicating that the LTE assumptions are true for this source (cite: ste21)" (3.2 Rotational emission lines NH2CHO towards MM1)
- How they decided it was enough: Lines counted as detected when non-blended and above the quoted sigma significance  
  > "The non-blended emission lines of HNCO exhibited $\geq$4.5 $\sigma$ statistical significance." (3.3 Rotational emission lines of HNCO towards MM1)
- Limitation (authors): Emission is not spatially resolved, so morphology of NH2CHO and HNCO cannot be determined  
  > "Higher spatial and angular resolution observations are needed to learn the spatial distribution of NH$_{2}$CHO and HNCO towards MM1." (3.5 Spatial distribution of NH2CHO and HNCO towards MM1)
- Limitation (authors): Seven other peptide-bond molecules are blended and could not be identified  
  > "So, there is a very low chance of identifying these molecules." (3.6 Searching of other peptide bonds molecules towards MM1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The frequency ranges are swapped in the text: Band 6 is quoted as 290.51–306.01 GHz and Band 7 as 225.44–242.06 GHz, while the SPW lists and species frequencies imply the reverse
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Formation-route conclusions rest on a single order-of-magnitude comparison with one published model; no model was run for MM1
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abundances depend on an H2 column density taken from another paper, with a different beam and assumptions, and on unresolved sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Upper limits on MM3 and other COMs give no assumed Tex, FWHM or source size
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Excitation temperatures of 165 and 170 K are nearly the same within errors yet used to argue a common region, while the emission is unresolved
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uncertainties for upper limits (written as value ± error) are not meaningful as stated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Statements about the NH2CHO conclusion count (four non-blended lines) and HNCO (six non-blended of seven) are consistent but the sigma limits differ (4 vs 4.5) without explanation
