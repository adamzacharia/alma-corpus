## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image**: CASA pipeline, CLEAN natural weighting without taper; continuum from line-free SPWs; uvcontsub for line cubes  
  > "we produced images and cubes with the natural weighting without taper to maximize point-source sensitivities" (3 ALMA Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure line fluxes and SNR**: moment 0 maps over 600 km/s; fluxes in 2 arcsec-radius apertures; SNR from 0.7 arcsec apertures randomly placed  
  > "total fluxes are measured on the $600\ \mathrm{km\ s^{-1}}$-integrated maps in $2\ensuremath{''}$-radius circular apertures" (4.1 Line Detections and Upper limits)
   - software: CASA immoments
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Peak position uncertainties from 1000 Monte Carlo noise realizations  
  > "We make a series of 1000 simulations for each dataset" (4.1 Line Detections and Upper limits)
3. **Fit line spectra for redshift and FWHM**: single Gaussian fit at rest frequency; systemic z as SNR-weighted mean of [OIII] and [CII]  
  > "We simply fit each spectrum with a single Gaussian profile" (4.1 Line Detections and Upper limits)
   - fitting: single Gaussian  
  > "We simply fit each spectrum with a single Gaussian profile" (4.1 Line Detections and Upper limits)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit dust SED for L_IR, T_dust, M_dust**: modified blackbody integrated 8-1000 um with CMB effects, T_dust above CMB temperature; dust mass from 160 um flux  
  > "We estimate IR luminosities, $L_\mathrm{IR}$, by integrating the modified black body radiation over $8-1000\ \mu$m." (5.2 L_IR and T_dust Estimates)
   - parameter: beta_d = 1.5; kappa0 = 10 cm2/g at 250 um  
  > "We fix the dust emissivity to $\beta_\mathrm{d}=1.5$" (5.2 L_IR and T_dust Estimates)
   - assumption: T_dust=50 K assumed for non-detection J0235-0532  
  > "we adopt $L_\mathrm{IR}<2.5\times10^{11}\ L_\odot$ assuming $T_\mathrm{dust}=50\ \mathrm{K}$ as the fiducial value" (5.2 L_IR and T_dust Estimates)
   - fitting: varied L_IR and T_dust; error contours (method not detailed)  
  > "We fit the observed fluxes with the modified black body by varying the IR luminosity and dust temperatures" (5.2 L_IR and T_dust Estimates)
   - validation: beta_d varied 1-3 changes L_IR <0.1 dex  
  > "if we change the dust emissivity in $1<\beta_\mathrm{d}<3$, the IR luminosity varies only $<0.1$ dex" (5.2 L_IR and T_dust Estimates)
5. **Velocity fields**: moment 1 and 2 maps of [CII], pixels >2 sigma; Delta v_obs/2 sigma_tot  
  > "We only use pixels with $>2\sigma$ detections." (4.4 Velocity Fields)
   - software: CASA immoments
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Cloudy modelling of line ratios**: Cloudy grid in log nH=0.5,2,3, Z=0.05,0.2,1 Zsun, log U -4 to -0.5; compare L/SFR; test C_PDR, C/O, CMB, halo, inclination  
  > "Here we examine gas clouds with $\mathrm{log}(n_\mathrm{H}/[\mathrm{cm^{-3}}])=0.5$, $2.0$, and $3.0$, and $Z=0.05$, $0.2$, and $1.0\ Z_\odot$" (6.1 Origin of High L_[OIII]/L_[CII] Ratios at z=6-9)
   - software: Cloudy
   - parameter: 1 Myr burst, BPASS v2.1 binary, A_V=100 mag stop, Orion graphite/silicate grains  
  > "Calculations are stopped at the depth of $A_\mathrm{V}=100\ \mathrm{mag}$" (6.1 Origin of High L_[OIII]/L_[CII] Ratios at z=6-9)
   - assumption: pressure-equilibrium plane-parallel cloud; stellar Z = gas Z; solar relative abundances  
  > "We assume a pressure-equilibrium gas cloud with a plain-parallel geometry" (6.1 Origin of High L_[OIII]/L_[CII] Ratios at z=6-9)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Correlation tests with Lya EW**: Kendall's tau test and power-law fit of L[CII]/SFR vs EW  
  > "we find that the anti-correlation holds with the $99.6\%$ significance level with the Kendall’s tau test" (6.3 Correlations with Ly$\alpha$ and Physical Interpretations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Median SFRs by EW bin to check selection effect; tests without upper limits, other SFRs, no IGM correction  
  > "we calculate median SFRs of galaxies in $EW^\mathrm{0,int}_\mathrm{Ly\alpha}$ bins" (6.3 Correlations with Ly$\alpha$ and Physical Interpretations)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Targets spectroscopically confirmed luminous LBGs with redshifts suitable for Bands 6,7,8  
  > "their redshifts, $6.0<z_\mathrm{Ly\alpha}<6.3$, are suitable for ALMA Band 6, 7, and 8 observations" (2 Target Selection)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Extended [CII] halo and inclination effect rejected as explanations  
  > "Thus the extended halo cannot explain the low $L_\mathrm{[CII]}/SFR$ ratios of the $z=6-9$ galaxies." (6.1 Origin of High L_[OIII]/L_[CII] Ratios at z=6-9)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Cool (~20 K) dust would be missed, causing large dust mass uncertainty  
  > "such dust would be missed in observations presented here, causing a large uncertainty in dust mass estimates" (5.4 Dust Mass)
- Limitation (authors): Low angular resolution limits kinematic interpretation  
  > "Given the low angular resolution of the observations, there are various interpretations of the velocity gradients." (4.4 Velocity Fields)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Degeneracy between U_ion and C_PDR cannot be broken with current data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous literature sample (SFR, apertures, IMFs) in correlations
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low-significance (~90%) correlation from small sample
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-zone plane-parallel Cloudy models with constant pressure
