## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate**: Standard EA-ARC calibration in CASA 4.2.1, then phase+amplitude self-cal on continuum  
  > "Self-calibration was then done by us in phase and amplitude using the bright and compact continuum source of Cen A, and only using line-free channels." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Calibrator fluxes compared with ALMA source catalogue  
  > "This is in very good agreement with fluxes in the ALMA source catalogue database at similar dates" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
2. **Image continuum and lines**: MFS continuum CLEAN; uvcontsub then line CLEAN, 10 km/s channels, Briggs 0.5  
  > "In the channel maps we binned the data to 10 km s$^{-1}$" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, MIRIAD
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "The weighting scheme for imaging of the lines we used was also Briggs and robust parameter 0.5." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked line-free channels for continuum subtraction artifacts  
  > "we paid special attention that continuum subtraction was done correctly by checking for possible artifacts in the line-free channels." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
3. **Moment maps and P-V diagrams**: IMMOMENTS for moment 0/1/2 (clipped at 5 sigma); P-V cuts along several PAs  
  > "The task `IMMOMENTS` in CASA was used to calculate the integrated intensity maps, intensity-weighted velocity field and velocity dispersion distributions" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Flux recovery and line ratios**: Compare ALMA fluxes to JCMT/APEX; CO(6-5)/CO(3-2) ratio maps at matched uv range and resolution; Gaussian fits to HCN/HCO+ spectra  
  > "We fitted single Gaussian profiles to the detected HCN(4–3) and HCO$^+$(4–3) lines" (3.5 HCO$^+$(4–3), HCN(4–3) Emission)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: Single Gaussians; HCN velocity and width fixed to HCO+  
  > "where the central velocity and width of the HCN(4–3) profile was fixed to that measured toward the brighter HCO$^+$(4–3)." (3.5 HCO$^+$(4–3), HCN(4–3) Emission)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Gas mass**: Mass from CO(3-2) flux with X_CO and CO(1-0)/(3-2)=0.1, corrected for ~50% flux loss  
  > "The gas mass obtained from the CO(3–2) map is $M_{\rm gas}$ $\simeq 4.8 \times 10^7$ M$_\odot$." (3.7 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - assumption: X = 4e20 cm^-2 (K km/s)^-1, factor of two uncertainty  
  > "with an uncertainty of a factor of two." (3.7 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Espada 2009 and Israel 2014 masses  
  > "This is consistent with the measurement $M_{\rm gas}$ $\simeq 8 \times 10^7$ M$_\odot$ inside a projected distance of $r$ $<$ 200 pc" (3.7 Molecular Gas Mass)
6. **Kinemetry of velocity field**: Kinemetry harmonic expansion on CO(3-2) velocity field in ellipse rings of 1.2'' width to get PA, inclination, k1, k5/k1  
  > "A ring width of 1.2$''$ was used and the following trends are present in the results." (4.1 A Warped Disk?)
   - software: Kinemetry
   - assumption: Circular orbits in warped disk, q = cos(i)  
  > "obtained from the axial ratio $q$ (i.e. flattening of the ellipse, where $q =$ cos$(i)$)" (4.1 A Warped Disk?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Other ring widths gave similar results  
  > "Note that other widths were also used showing similar results." (4.1 A Warped Disk?)
7. **Scale height and dynamical mass**: h = R q sigma / V under hydrostatic equilibrium; Mdyn = V^2 r / G  
  > "follow $h~=~R~q~\sigma~/~V$ (Eq. 3.1 of (cite: 1992ApJ...391..121Q))" (6 Comparison with Numerical Simulations)
   - assumption: q ~ 1, V ~ 200 km/s, sigma 10–40 km/s  
  > "$\sigma$ = 10 - 40 km s$^{-1}$" (6 Comparison with Numerical Simulations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Nuclear filaments at PA~120° with 180° rotational symmetry and steeper P-V, interpreted as shocks; CO filaments end at ~20 pc but continue as warm H2 spirals  
  > "which are interpreted as nuclear shocks due to non-circular motions." (abstract)
