## Analysis recipe

1. **Image cubes and continuum**: CASA calibration, uv-tables exported to GILDAS; UV-MAP imaging with robust 1 (MSR) and 0.1 (HSR); uv-plane continuum subtraction  
  > "We obtain two sets of angular resolutions by changing in the `GILDAS` task `UV-MAP` the robust parameter ($b$) from 1" (2.2 Band 7 data)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - parameter: 20 km/s channels  
  > "The line data cubes were binned to a common frequency resolution of 23.1 MHz (equivalent to $\sim$20 km s$^{-1}$ in Band 7)." (2.2 Band 7 data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared CND fluxes with GB14 CO(3-2) and Krips CO(2-1) maps to check missing flux  
  > "530 Jy km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Kri11) $\simeq$ 580 Jy km s$^{-1}$ (this work)" (2.3 Band 6 data)
2. **Continuum position and spectral index**: UV-FIT for AGN position; spectral index maps from 229.7, 344.5, 694 GHz at common 0.11'' aperture  
  > "derived for a common aperture size of $0.11\ensuremath{''}\times0.11\ensuremath{''}$ ($8$ pc$~\times$ 8 pc)" (3 Continuum maps)
   - software: GILDAS
   - assumption: Dust spectral index alpha ~3-4 in dust-dominated CND regions to estimate dust fraction at S1  
  > "We used the spectral index values measured in the CND regions dominated by dust emission ($\alpha\simeq$ 3–4) to derive the fraction of emission at 871 $\mu$m" (3 Continuum maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **CND and torus gas masses**: Integrated CO(2-1) flux converted to H2 mass (Bolatto+13 Eq. 3) incl. helium; column densities from line intensities  
  > "we estimated the molecular gas mass of the 2–1 torus to be $M^{\rm torus}_{\rm gas}\simeq2.9\times10^5~M_{\ensuremath{\odot}}$, including the mass of Helium." (5.2 Masses and column densities)
   - assumption: X_CO = 2e20 mol cm-2 (K km/s)-1; r21 = 2.2 (CND), 2.5 (torus); r31 = 2.9  
  > "a galactic CO–to–H$_2$ conversion factor ($X_{\rm CO}=2\times10^{20}$mol cm$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$)" (5.2 Masses and column densities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Torus size and orientation**: Elliptical Gaussian fits to MSR images in the sky plane, with deconvolution by beam  
  > "derived using elliptical Gaussians to fit the images in the plane of the sky" (5.1)
   - parameter: Full size defined at ~3 sigma level of the Gaussian  
  > "defined as the sizes of the disks measured at a $\simeq3\sigma$ intensity level of the Gaussian used in the fit" (5.1)
   - fitting: Elliptical Gaussian least-squares (details not stated)  
  > "Table [\[Tab2\]](#Tab2) lists the size, position and orientation of the molecular disks identified in Fig. [11](#torus-maps-lr), derived using elliptical Gaussians to fit the images in the plane of the sky" (5.1 Morphology, size and orientation)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **CND velocity field and coplanar outflow**: Fourier decomposition of CO(2-1) mean-velocity field with kinemetry at 20 radii (10-210 pc), fixed center, PA and i  
  > "re-derive the Fourier decomposition of Eq. [\[eq-1\]](#eq-1) for a set of 20 radii sampling the CND from $r=0.15\ensuremath{''}~(\simeq10$ pc) to $r=3\ensuremath{''}~(\simeq210$ pc)" (4.3.1)
   - software: kinemetry
   - parameter: PA=289±5, i=41±2; expansion to n=3  
  > "defined by $PA=289\pm5^\circ$ and $i=41\pm2^\circ$" (4.3.1)
   - assumption: coplanar orbits in a thin disk  
  > "This approach implicitly assumes that the molecular gas kinematics in the CND can be modeled by coplanar orbits in a thin disk" (4.3.1)
   - fitting: kinemetry harmonic fit  
  > "We can decompose $v_{\rm mean}(r, \psi)$ as a Fourier series with harmonic coefficients $c_j(r)$ and $s_j(r)$" (4.3.1 Modeling the mean-velocity field with kinemetry: a coplanar outflow?)
   - validation: Compared with GB14 outflow velocity  
  > "This result is compatible with the average value reported by (cite: GB14) for the CND" (4.3.1)
6. **Global 3D kinematic model**: 3DBarolo tilted-ring fits to CO(2-1) cube with v_rot, v_rad, sigma, PA, i, H free and v_vert explored at fixed values; two-step regularization; benchmarked against kinemetry  
  > "we allowed for a more generous exploration of the parameter space by only fixing the AGN position and $v_{\rm sys}$ and letting free $v_{\rm rot}$, $v_{\rm rad}$, $v_{\rm disp}$, $PA$, $i$, and $H$." (7.1)
   - software: 3DBarolo
   - parameter: 103 radii, 2-210 pc; v_vert tested at 0,25,50,100,150 km/s  
  > "we used a set of 103 radii sampling the CND from $r=0.03\ensuremath{''}~(\simeq2$ pc) to $r=3\ensuremath{''}~(\simeq210$ pc)" (7.1)
   - fitting: Two-step: all parameters free, then geometry fixed to Bezier interpolation  
  > "we fixed the geometrical parameters to a Bezier function that interpolates the values found in the previous iteration for $PA$ and $i$" (Appendix)
   - validation: p-v overlays along major/minor axes  
  > "We illustrate the goodness of the best-fit solution in Figure [23](#pv-models)" (7.1)
7. **Torus outflow mass fraction model**: Toroidal ringed disk model with rotation plus wind-bicone outflow; scan theta 0-90 deg and v_out 50-150 km/s; chi2 on major and minor axis p-v; compare OUT vs counter-rotation CR model  
  > "This angle is let to vary from $\theta=0^{\circ}$ (i.e., no outflow) to $\theta=90^{\circ}$" (7.2)
   - parameter: disk radius 4-5 pc, torus radius 5-6 pc, PA=113, i=-80, 5 pc FWHM beam  
  > "We adopt a $PA=113^{\circ}$ and an $i=-80^{\circ}$." (7.2)
   - assumption: axisymmetric, constant-density gas  
  > "which include the axisymmetry and the smoothness of the gas distribution in the torus" (7.2)
   - fitting: chi2 minimization over grid  
  > "we minimize the standard goodness-of-fit parameter" (7.2)
   - validation: OUT-model chi2 lower by factor 1.2-1.3 than CR-model  
  > "based not only on its factor $\simeq1.2-1.3$ lower $\chi^2$" (7.2)

## Qualitative findings

- CND is an asymmetric ring with a central ~130 pc gas deficit, inner edge matching NIR polarized arcs  
  > "The CND shows a marked deficit of molecular gas in its central $\simeq130$ pc-region." (abstract)
- Torus shows density radial stratification: denser tracer HCO+ is smaller  
  > "is a factor of 2–3 smaller than its CO(2-1) and CO(3-2) counterparts" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Multiple transitions span wide density range; CO(2-1) traces bulk H2 and connections  
  > "the choice of the CO(2–1) line, which is sensitive to densities $n$(H$_2$) $\simeq10^3$cm$^{-3}$, is motivated by the need to trace the bulk of the H$_2$ gas" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: 3DBarolo handles 3D cube and beam smearing better than kinemetry  
  > "provides a more complete exploration of the 3D geometrical and kinematic parameter space and also takes into account the effects of beam smearing" (7.1)
- Alternative: Counter-rotating torus (CR-model) disfavored: dynamically unstable and fits worse  
  > "would be nevertheless dynamically unstable: the dissipative nature of the gas would make the two disks collapse on the time scale of the orbital time." (5.4)
- Alternative: Inflow interpretation of streamers (Müller Sánchez 2009) rejected in favor of outflow  
  > "favors the outflow scenario, also invoked by (cite: Bar14) and (cite: May17), rather than the inflow scenario" (6.1.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No short-spacing correction; flux filtered beyond LAS  
  > "we expect that the flux will start to be filtered out on scales $\geq1.8\ensuremath{''}$ (130 pc)" (2.2 Band 7 data)
- Limitation (authors): Models are axisymmetric and chi2 values are large  
  > "the goodness-of-fit parameters are admittedly large in either case ($\geq$4)" (7.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Torus outflow model is simple and non-unique; mass fraction rests on assumed geometry
- Caveat (inferred, ours): X_CO and line ratios uncertain in AGN environment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): kinemetry fails inside 40 pc, relying on 3DBarolo
- Caveat (inferred, ours): v_vert not fitted, only scanned
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam sizes of tens of mas make the torus only marginally resolved
