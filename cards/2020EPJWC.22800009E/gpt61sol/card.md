## Analysis recipe

1. **Deproject lensing constraints in the CLUMP-3D framework**: Use X-ray and SZ shape and orientation information to deproject lensing mass and concentration.  
  > "In a nutshell, lensing constrains the 2D mass and concentration which are deprojected thanks to the information on shape and orientation from X-ray (surface brightness and temperature) and SZ." (Introduction)
   - assumption: No assumption of spherical symmetry or hydrostatic equilibrium  
  > "In our modelling, we do not rely on the assumptions of spherical symmetry or hydrostatic equilibrium, which could bias results." (Introduction)
   - fitting: Bayesian inference for mass, concentration, intrinsic shape and equilibrium status  
  > "The mass and concentration can be then determined together with the intrinsic shape and equilibrium status of the cluster as required by precision astronomy through a Bayesian inference method." (Introduction)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Constrain the temperature profile in the proposed joint analysis**: Combine SZ thermal pressure with X-ray gas density.  
  > "to constrain the temperature profile by combining the thermal pressure from SZ and the gas density from X-ray observations" (The case of MACS1206)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit gas and total-mass shapes in the proposed Heritage analysis**: Fit three-dimensional triaxial ellipses to combined X-ray, SZ and lensing data.  
  > "Multi-probes three-dimensional Bayesian analysis will be applied to combined X-ray, SZ and lensing data to fit three-dimensional triaxial ellipses to the gas and total mass distribution," (3D analysis in the context of the Heritage XMM-Newton Cluster Project)
   - assumption: Three-dimensional triaxial ellipses for gas and total mass  
  > "to fit three-dimensional triaxial ellipses to the gas and total mass distribution" (3D analysis in the context of the Heritage XMM-Newton Cluster Project)
   - fitting: Multi-probes three-dimensional Bayesian analysis  
  > "Multi-probes three-dimensional Bayesian analysis will be applied to combined X-ray, SZ and lensing data" (3D analysis in the context of the Heritage XMM-Newton Cluster Project)
   - validation: Proposed comparison with ΛCDM predictions after correcting geometrical biases  
  > "assessing their consistency with $\Lambda$CDM predictions and their equilibrium once geometrical biases (like projection) are corrected for." (3D analysis in the context of the Heritage XMM-Newton Cluster Project)

## Qualitative findings

- Earlier CLUMP-3D reconstruction places the main axis near the plane of the sky and permits thermal-pressure hydrostatic balance.  
  > "The CLUMP-3D analysis has allowed to reconstruct the triaxial shape of the cluster, showing that the main axis is near the plane of the sky, and that the thermal pressure can balance the cluster in hydrostatic equilibrium." (The case of MACS1206)
- Earlier cluster-shape results broadly agree with ΛCDM, with a slight preference for more extreme minor-to-major axial ratios than simulations.  
  > "In general, we obtained that the shapes are in good agreement with the predictions from the standard $\Lambda$CDM cosmological model." (3D analysis in the context of the Heritage XMM-Newton Cluster Project); "However, compared to simulations, the data show a slight preference for more extreme minor-to-major axial ratios." (3D analysis in the context of the Heritage XMM-Newton Cluster Project)
