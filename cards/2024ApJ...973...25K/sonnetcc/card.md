## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA Band 3 and 4 continuum and cubes**: Continuum multi-frequency synthesis images and spectral cubes in CASA with Briggs robust = 0.5 weighting  
  > "Continuum multi-frequency synthesis images and spectral cubes were created using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (casa) version 6.2.1 (cite: McMullin:2007aa), with `robust`$=$0.5 Briggs weighting (cite: Briggs:1995aa)." (ALMA Bands 3 and 4)
   - software: CASA 6.2.1
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; unweighted baselines 0.03 - 0.3 km (5th to 80th percentile)  
  > "The unweighted baselines ranged from $0.03 - 0.3$ km (5th to 80th percentile)." (ALMA Bands 3 and 4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: robust = -1 tested to improve resolution, but S/N was insufficient  
  > "Imaging with $\texttt{robust}=-1$ (i.e., closer to uniform weighting) was tested in an attempt to improve angular resolution, but S/N was insufficient to yield useful results." (Footnote 6)
2. **Measure mm and radio fluxes of the arcs**: Photometry of resolved arcs with the flood-filling algorithm blobcat on ALMA 2 mm, 3 mm and VLA 6 GHz images  
  > "As the lensed objects of interest are spatially resolved, photometry was performed using the flood-filling algorithm blobcat (cite: Hales:2012aa)." (ALMA Bands 3 and 4)
   - software: blobcat
   - assumption: Modified blackbody with dust emissivity index beta = 1.8 and S_1mm = 24 +/- 2.0 mJy as check on the 2 mm and 3 mm fluxes  
  > "a modified blackbody (dust emissivity index $\beta = 1.8$) with $S_{\rm 1mm} = 24 \pm 2.0$ mJy" (Table 1 notes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 2 mm and 3 mm fluxes compared with the LMT AzTEC 1 mm flux under a modified blackbody: relative consistency at 2 mm; 3 mm exceeds the prediction, possibly from synchrotron  
  > "we compare the measured 2 mm and 3 mm fluxes against the 1 mm flux from the Large Millimeter Telescope (LMT) AzTEC, reported by , under assumption of a modified blackbody SED, and find relative consistency." (ALMA Bands 3 and 4); "The observed flux at 3 mm (1.3 mJy) exceeds the modified blackbody prediction (0.5 mJy), possibly owing to contamination from synchrotron emission." (Table 1 notes)
3. **Build the parametric lens model and measure magnifications**: LENSTOOL parametric model with PIEMD halos and 165 cluster members; ALMA and VLA magnifications from the ratio of image-plane to source-plane area, with 300 resamplings of the MCMC posterior  
  > "we measured magnifications simply by taking the ratio of image-plane to source-plane area." (Lens modeling); "we resampled 300 iterations uniformly from the MCMC-derived posterior distribution, resulting in an approximately Gaussian distribution of magnifications." (Lens modeling)
   - software: LENSTOOL
   - parameter: Two cluster-scale PIEMD halos plus 165 cluster members; uniform priors with +/-5'' position range, ellipticity 0 to 0.85, maximum velocity dispersion 800 km/s, maximum core radius 20'' (~100 kpc); halo cut radii fixed to 1000 kpc  
  > "Uniform priors were used for these parameters, including a wide range of $\pm 5\ensuremath{''}$ in $x$ and $y$ position from the approximate center of both cluster cores," (Lens modeling); "As their cut/truncation radii are poorly constrained, these were fixed to values of 1000 kpc." (Lens modeling)
   - parameter: DSFG-3 redshift left free (prior z = 2.0 - 2.2), giving z_geom = 2.11 +/- 0.01  
  > "allowed the redshift to vary from $z=2.0 - 2.2$ in the final model." (Redshift of DSFG-3)
   - assumption: Cluster-member scaling relation normalized to L* galaxy (m_F200W = 17.0 AB) with sigma_0* = 120 km/s, r_core* = 0.15 kpc, r_cut* = 30 kpc  
  > "The power law was normalized such that a characteristic $L_\star$ galaxy at the redshift of the cluster ($m_{\rm F200W} = 17.0$ AB mag) has a velocity dispersion of $\sigma_0^\star = 120~{\rm km~s}^{-1}$," (Lens modeling)
   - fitting: MCMC (sampler not named); the median of each parameter's posterior is adopted; minimum-chi^2 solutions also provided  
  > "In this work, we adopt the median of each parameter’s posterior distribution" (Lens modeling); "We also provide the minimum-$\chi^2$ solutions, which are nearly always in close agreement with the median solution." (Lens modeling)
   - validation: Astrometric uncertainty from independent image-position markings by multiple team members; median offset 0.03''  
  > "multiple team members independently marked the positions for the known image family members, and found a median offset of $0.03\ensuremath{''}$" (Lens modeling)
4. **Reconstruct the source plane and measure the 2 mm dust size**: Pixel-by-pixel ray-tracing of the images with lenstool cleanlens, then 2-D Gaussian fits with CASA imfit to the 2 mm source-plane reconstructions  
  > "the observed images are ray-traced to the source plane pixel-by-pixel through the `cleanlens` function of lenstool, with the image-plane oversampled by a factor of 4 and the source-plane by a factor of 2." (Source-plane reconstruction); "we use the casa imfit task to fit the emission with 2-d Gaussian profiles (and adopt a minimum 10% fractional uncertainty)." (Source-plane reconstruction)
   - software: LENSTOOL, CASA imfit
   - parameter: Sizes measured at 2 mm because of higher S/N; minimum 10% fractional uncertainty  
  > "these dust sizes are measured for the 2 mm images given their higher S/N" (Source-plane reconstruction)
   - assumption: 3 mm sizes may be inflated by synchrotron contamination  
  > "the possible contamination from synchrotron at 3 mm is expected to result in slightly larger sizes than from thermal dust alone." (Source-plane reconstruction)
   - fitting: 2-D Gaussian fit with CASA imfit; no regularization or parameterization of the source in the reconstruction  
  > "The reconstruction of all images in this work in the source plane does not impose regularization or parameterization of the intrinsic structure of the source." (Source-plane reconstruction)
   - validation: Reconstructions from only the high-magnification arcs agree with the full reconstruction  
  > "adding confidence to this reconstruction." (Source-plane reconstruction)
5. **Derive IR-based SFR and L_IR for each DSFG**: Apportion the total apparent IR SFR between Arc 1abc and Arc 3abc by their mm flux ratio, then correct by the total 2 mm magnification of each arc system  
  > "The total apparent (i.e. lensing-uncorrected) IR-derived SFR of $12000\pm 2000 ~M_\odot {\rm yr}^{-1}$ from is divided between Arcs 1abc and 3abc according to their ratio of millimeter flux" (Table 2 notes); "Then, these apportioned SFRs are corrected for the total 2 mm magnifications of Arc 1abc and Arc 3abc, respectively." (Table 2 notes)
   - parameter: Flux ratio S_1abc / S_3abc = 3.1 +/- 0.3 (averaged for 2 mm and 3 mm)  
  > "according to their ratio of millimeter flux, $S_{\rm 1abc} / S_{\rm 3abc} = 3.1 \pm 0.3$ (averaged for 2 mm and 3 mm)" (Table 2 notes)
   - assumption: Both arc systems at similar redshifts; far-IR photometry does not resolve the systems; Kroupa IMF; flat LCDM with Omega_m = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7, H0 = 70  
  > "As the far-IR photometry largely does not resolve Arcs 1abc and 3abc, we make a preliminary estimate by apportioning the total ${\rm SFR}_{\rm IR}$ according to their share of flux at 2 mm and 3 mm" (Table 2 notes); "In this work, we adopt a $\Lambda$CDM cosmological model of $\Omega_m=0.3$, $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.7$, and $H_0 = 70~{\rm km}~{\rm s}^{-1}~{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$," (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Resolved pixel-by-pixel SED fitting of JWST/NIRCam photometry (ALMA not included)**: Bin pixels with piXedfit to S/N = 20 per filter, then fit each bin with bagpipes using an exponentially declining SFH  
  > "If a S/N threshold for each filter is met (a conservative S/N$=20$, in our case), the bin is established; otherwise, the radius of the bin is increased." (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes); "we fitted the SED of each bin with bagpipes (Bayesian Analysis of Galaxies for Physical Inference and Parameter EStimation; (cite: Carnall:2018aa))" (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes)
   - software: piXedfit, bagpipes, cloudy
   - parameter: Age 0.1 Gyr to age of Universe at that redshift; tau 0.3 - 10 Gyr; metallicity 0 - 3.5 Zsun; log(U) -4 to -2; A_V 0 - 3  
  > "The stellar population age for each bin could vary from 0.1 Gyr to the age of the Universe at the given redshift, and $\tau$ was allowed to vary from $0.3 - 10$ Gyr." (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes); "and metallicity could vary from 0 to 3.5 $Z_\odot$." (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes)
   - parameter: Bins larger than the F444W PSF FWHM (0.14''); all bands convolved to F444W resolution  
  > "each bin is required to be larger than the F444W PSF FWHM ($0.14\ensuremath{''}$)." (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes)
   - assumption: Calzetti et al. dust attenuation model; Kroupa IMF; nebular emission via cloudy models  
  > "A (cite: Calzetti:2000aa) dust attenuation model was imposed, with attenuation allowed to vary from $A_V = 0 - 3$" (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes)
   - fitting: bagpipes Bayesian SED fit with the priors listed; sampler and likelihood not stated  
  > "The total mass formed was allowed to vary widely, from ${\rm log}_{10}(M / M_\odot) = 1 - 15$, and metallicity could vary from 0 to 3.5 $Z_\odot$." (Pixel-by-pixel SED fitting with piXedfit and bagpipes)
   - validation: Integrated SED fits compared with summed bin-wise results and with Halpha-derived SFR and Balmer-decrement A_V from NIRSpec; close agreement reported  
  > "In all cases, we find quite close agreement with our independently-derived values." (Table 3 notes)
7. **Combined UV-through-FIR SED fit including ALMA 2 mm and 3 mm photometry**: bagpipes fit to the summed photometry of Arcs 1a, 1bc, 3ab and 3c, adding WISE, Herschel/SPIRE, LMT/AzTEC and ALMA 2 mm / 3 mm fluxes, with a dust emission model  
  > "we fold them into a combined SED fit for Arcs 1a, 1bc, 3ab, and 3c, so that we can take advantage of unresolved photometry" (Simultaneous UV-FIR fitting of the spatially-integrated SED)
   - software: bagpipes
   - parameter: q_PAH in [0.5, 4.0]; U_min in [1, 25]; gamma in [0.01, 0.99]; A_V < 8; redshift fixed at z = 2.236  
  > "These include the PAH mass fraction ($q_{\rm PAH} \in [0.5, 4.0]$), the lower cutoff of the incident starlight intensity on the dust ($U_{\rm min} \in [1, 25]$, effectively describing the dust temperature distribution)," (Simultaneous UV-FIR fitting of the spatially-integrated SED); "Redshift is held fixed at $z=2.236$." (Simultaneous UV-FIR fitting of the spatially-integrated SED)
   - assumption: Total flux-weighted magnification mu = 16 +/- 3 for both DSFGs combined; Planck photometry excluded because of confusion noise; radio SED not modeled  
  > "we use the total flux-weighted magnification for both DSFG-1 and DSFG-3 combined, $\mu = 16 \pm 3$, to derive intrinsic values." (Table 3 notes); "*Planck* photometry is excluded from the fit given its high confusion noise." (Simultaneous UV-FIR fitting of the spatially-integrated SED)
   - fitting: bagpipes with uniform priors on the dust parameters; sampler not stated  
  > "these priors are all uniformly distributed, and consistent with those used by (cite: Williams:2019ab)." (Simultaneous UV-FIR fitting of the spatially-integrated SED)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Dust sizes measured at 2 mm because of higher S/N than 3 mm; resolved ALMA+JWST SED fitting not attempted because of the coarse, elongated ALMA beam  
  > "these dust sizes are measured for the 2 mm images given their higher S/N" (Source-plane reconstruction); "it is therefore not worthwhile to attempt spatially-resolved SED fitting with both ALMA and JWST" (A comparison of integrated vs. resolved properties)
- Alternative: Briggs robust = -1 imaging was tested to improve angular resolution but rejected for insufficient S/N  
  > "Imaging with $\texttt{robust}=-1$ (i.e., closer to uniform weighting) was tested in an attempt to improve angular resolution, but S/N was insufficient to yield useful results." (Footnote 6)
- Alternative: Single burst and exponential+burst SFHs tested in the UV-FIR fit; no significant improvement over a single exponential  
  > "but find no significant improvement over the single exponential model." (Simultaneous UV-FIR fitting of the spatially-integrated SED)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Arcs 1a and 3c are only marginally resolved at 2 mm, so higher-resolution dust imaging is needed  
  > "as Arcs 1a and 3c are only marginally resolved at 2 mm." (Results)
- Limitation (authors): ALMA beam is highly elongated (high declination target) and aligned with the direction of greatest magnification  
  > "this elongation is essentially aligned with the orientation of greatest magnification, so lensing does not offer an easy remedy." (A comparison of integrated vs. resolved properties)
- Limitation (authors): 3 mm fluxes and sizes may be contaminated by synchrotron emission  
  > "the possible contamination from synchrotron at 3 mm is expected to result in slightly larger sizes than from thermal dust alone." (Source-plane reconstruction)
- Limitation (authors): DSFG-3 lacks a secure spectroscopic redshift; lens-model redshift uncertainty is likely underestimated  
  > "Since DSFG-3 lacks a secure spectroscopic redshift, we left this as a free parameter in our lens model" (Redshift of DSFG-3)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA is a minor ingredient; the IR SFR rests on an externally cited total SFR split by mm flux ratio, not an independent ALMA-based SED
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The Arc 1abc/3abc flux split assumes similar dust SEDs and redshifts for both systems
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source-plane sizes from 2 mm are marginally resolved with a large elongated beam, so R_e values depend on the lens model and the minimum 10% error floor
- Caveat (inferred, ours): DSFG-3 redshift remains uncertain, which affects magnification, SFR and the pair-interaction interpretation
