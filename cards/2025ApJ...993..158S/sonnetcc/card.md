## Analysis recipe

1. **Make continuum images and measure limits**: ~2 mm continuum images from the full bandwidth, excluding 500 km/s around CO(2-1); natural-weighted and UV-tapered  
  > "We created $\sim2$mm continuum images using the full bandwidth of our observations (excluding the 500 km s$^{-1}$ around the CO(2-1) line)." (ALMA observations)
   - parameter: 3 sigma limiting fluxes <~20 uJy; no continuum detected in any primary target  
  > "3$\sigma$ limiting fluxes $\lesssim20\mu$Jy" (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: nondetections consistent with low Halpha SFRs from Kriek et al. 2024  
  > "These continuum nondetections are consistent with the low ongoing SFRs inferred from" (ALMA observations)
2. **Extract CO(2-1) upper limits for the four undetected targets**: spectra extracted with CASA imfit, fixed centroid and size; upper limit from an 800 km/s channel  
  > "For the four undetected sources, we extract spectra using CASA" (ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA (imfit)
   - parameter: centroid fixed to the phase center, size fixed to 2 arcsec  
  > "fixing the centroid to the phase center and the size to 2"." (ALMA observations)
   - parameter: 3 sigma upper limits assuming an 800 km/s line width  
  > "Upper limits for the undetected sources are 3$\sigma$, and assume an 800km s$^{-1}$ line width." (Table 1)
   - assumption: 800 km/s line width, following Bezanson et al. 2022  
  > "Following (cite: bezanson22), we conservatively estimate the upper limit on the CO(2-1) line flux using a" (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure CO(2-1) line flux of the detected source**: fit position and size in a single ~1000 km/s channel, fix them in imfit, extract spectrum at ~200 km/s resolution, then fit a single gaussian  
  > "we first fit for the source position and size in a single $\sim$1000 km s$^{-1}$ channel, then fix the position and size in" (ALMA observations); "We fit a single gaussian to the spectrum to find the integrated CO(2-1) line flux and its uncertainty." (ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA (imfit)
   - parameter: only intensity free channel-to-channel; ~200 km/s resolution  
  > "allowing only the intensity to vary channel-to-channel" (ALMA observations)
   - assumption: source unresolved, no velocity gradients seen  
  > "we do not observe any evidence for velocity gradients or resolved structure at the depth and resolution of our observations" (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: single gaussian fit to the CO spectrum  
  > "We fit a single gaussian to the spectrum to find the integrated CO(2-1) line flux and its uncertainty." (ALMA observations)
   - validation: detected at ~5 sigma  
  > "We detect CO(2-1) emission at $\sim5\sigma$ in HM1-214340" (ALMA observations)
4. **Convert CO line flux to molecular gas mass**: CO(2-1) line flux converted to L'CO and M_H2 with thermalized emission and alpha_CO = 4.0  
  > "we convert (limits on) CO(2-1) line flux to molecular gas mass assuming thermalized emission" (ALMA observations)
   - assumption: thermalized CO emission (CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) ratio not given)  
  > "assuming thermalized emission (cite: combes07; dannerbauer09; young11)" (ALMA observations)
   - assumption: Milky Way-like alpha_CO = 4.0  
  > "and a Milky Way-like $\alpha_{CO}=4.0$ (see (cite: bolatto13) and references therein" (ALMA observations)
   - assumption: Omega_m = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7, h = 0.7  
  > "Throughout this Letter, we assume a cosmology of $\Omega_m = 0.3$, $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.7$, and $h = 0.7$" (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Apportion CO flux between the close pair**: single 2D gaussian to the moment-0 map, then two 2D gaussians fixed at the positions of UVISTA-214345 and HM1-214340  
  > "We then model the CO flux with two 2D gaussians, one fixed to the position of UVISTA-214345 and one fixed to the position of HM1-214340" (Results)
   - parameter: each gaussian fixed to the clean-beam size and position angle, only amplitudes free  
  > "fixing each gaussian to the size and position angle of the clean beam and leaving only the amplitude of each component free" (Results)
   - parameter: single-component fit with all parameters free including position  
  > "We first fit a single 2D gaussian to the moment-0 map of the CO flux, allowing all fit parameters including the position to be free." (Results)
   - assumption: CO emission is a point source at the data resolution  
  > "the CO emission is consistent with a point source at the resolution of our data" (Results)
   - fitting: least-squares style 2D gaussian fits to the moment-0 map (method not named)  
  > "We first fit a single 2D gaussian to the moment-0 map of the CO flux" (Results)
   - validation: sum of two-component fluxes agrees with single-component flux  
  > "We note that the sum of these two fluxes is in good agreement with the flux in the single-component fit." (Results)
6. **Derive stellar ages and t_quench with spectro-photometric SED fitting**: Prospector v2 joint fit of UltraVISTA photometry and Keck LRIS/MOSFIRE spectra  
  > "to simultaneously fit UltraVISTA photometry (cite: muzzin13) and Keck/LRIS-R, Keck/MOSFIRE-J, and Keck/MOSFIRE-H spectroscopy (cite: kriek24)" (Spectral modeling)
   - software: Prospector v2, FSPS, dynesty
   - parameter: each spectrum calibrated separately with a fifth-order polynomial  
  > "We calibrate each spectrum separately using a fifth-order polynomial." (Spectral modeling)
   - parameter: 5% error floor on photometry  
  > "We adopt a 5% error floor for all photometric data points." (Spectral modeling)
   - parameter: nebular emission with gas-phase metallicity tied to stellar metallicity, free ionization parameter  
  > "We include nebular emission, tying the gas-phase metallicity to the stellar metallicity and treating the ionization parameter as a free parameter." (Spectral modeling)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; Kriek & Conroy dust law with free index and normalization; young stars twice as attenuated as old  
  > "Our model assumes a (cite: chabrier03) IMF, the (cite: kriek13) dust attenuation law with free dust index and normalization" (Spectral modeling); "with young stars fixed to be twice as attenuated as old stars" (Spectral modeling)
   - assumption: FSPS stellar populations with MILES library and MIST isochrones  
  > "We generate stellar populations using FSPS (cite: conroy09; conroy10), adopting the MILES spectral library (cite: falcon11)" (Spectral modeling)
   - fitting: dynamic nested sampling (dynesty); uniform priors except stellar metallicity (mass-metallicity relation prior)  
  > "All parameters use uniform priors, except for stellar metallicity, which follows a prior approximating the stellar mass–metallicity relation of (cite: gallazzi05)." (Spectral modeling); "Posterior distributions are sampled using the dynamic nested sampling algorithm implemented in" (Spectral modeling)
   - validation: visual comparison of spectral features (Ca H&K asymmetry) supports the older HeavyMetal ages  
  > "A detailed comparison of the spectra suggests that this difference in age between the two samples is reasonable" (Discussion & Conclusions)
7. **Compare gas masses with literature and with time since quenching**: place the sample against z>0.5 quiescent-galaxy gas measurements and against SQuIGGLE in M_H2 vs t_quench  
  > "we place our sample in the context of existing observations of gas in quiescent galaxies at" (Results)
   - parameter: HM1-214340 placed at the 3 sigma upper limit from two-component modelling when CO is attributed to UVISTA-214345  
  > "and place HM1-214340 at the 3$\sigma$ upper limit implied by our two-component modeling" (Results)

   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Four of five young z~1.4 post-starburst galaxies are undetected in CO(2-1) and 2 mm continuum; one system (a close pair) is detected  
  > "While four galaxies are undetected in CO emission, we detect" (abstract)
- Two undetected primary targets have serendipitous CO-detected neighbours at similar redshift, suggesting close pairs or an overdense environment  
  > "we infer that they are likely part of close pairs" (Results)
- No correlation between gas mass and time since quenching in this sample; the detected system is an outlier at older t_quench  
  > "our detected system is an outlier" (Discussion & Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Observed the five youngest z~1.4 quiescent Heavy Metal galaxies, selected by UVJ colors, to probe gas near the peak quenching epoch  
  > "we observed the five youngest" (ALMA observations); "These galaxies were selected for ALMA observations by their rest-frame UVJ colors" (ALMA observations)
- Why this method: Prospector ages computed so that they are directly comparable to the SQuIGGLE ages of Bezanson et al. 2022  
  > "This test requires that we calculate galaxy ages that are directly comparable to those used by (cite: bezanson22)" (Spectral modeling)
- Alternative: CO emission could be partially associated with offset HST/F814W clumps; judged less likely than emission from UVISTA-214345  
  > "While it is also possible the CO emission is partially associated with one of these clumps" (Results)
- Alternative: UVJ color as age indicator vs Prospector t_quench: the two give different conclusions about sample ages  
  > "suggests that caution and careful methodology are required when computing and comparing the ages of quiescent galaxy samples" (Discussion & Conclusions)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No resolved structure or velocity gradient at the depth and resolution; coarse ~2.4 arcsec beam cannot separate the pair  
  > "we do not observe any evidence for velocity gradients or resolved structure at the depth and resolution of our observations" (ALMA observations)
- Limitation (authors): Merger-quenching link not established; cannot rule out unrelated quenching of the pair  
  > "However, we cannot rule out the possibility that the process(es) that quenched HM1-214340 and UVISTA-214345 are entirely unrelated to their close separation." (Discussion & Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): Sample cannot test whether an age trend persists, since youngest quenched galaxies were not probed  
  > "Observations at a wider age range, particularly probing the youngest quiescent galaxies, are required to determine whether a trend between age and molecular gas mass persists" (Discussion & Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only five targets, one detection: the merger interpretation rests on a single system
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO at 2.4 arcsec resolution cannot separate the two galaxies; the 80/25% split has large uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Upper limits assume an 800 km/s line width, thermalized CO and alpha_CO = 4.0; none is tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): t_quench comparison depends on Prospector SFH assumptions and on different spectral coverage between SQuIGGLE and HeavyMetal
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Nondetection attribution to non-merging status is not tested against the many neighbours seen
