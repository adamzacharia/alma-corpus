## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate**: Archive calibration scripts; self-calibration for CT Cha and DH Tau  
  > "We then applied phase and amplitude self-calibrations to the visibilities of the CT Cha and DH Tau" (2. Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: phase solint 400 s to 30 s; amplitude 30 s  
  > "We then carried out one round of amplitude calibration with a solution interval of 30 s." (2. Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Host star flux changed by <~5% after self-cal  
  > "while the flux densities of both stars only changed by $\lesssim$5%" (2. Observations and Data Reduction)
2. **Image continuum and measure fluxes/limits**: tclean natural-weighted MFS; aperture photometry of beam area for undetected sources; elliptical gaussian visibility fit for detected hosts  
  > "We use the software package `pdspy` (cite: Sheehan19) to fit an elliptical gaussian to the visibilities" (4.1. Continuum Emission: PMCs and Host Stars)
   - software: CASA, pdspy, uvmodelfit
   - assumption: companions are unresolved point sources  
  > "assuming they are point sources" (4.1. Continuum Emission: PMCs and Host Stars)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Similar values with uvmodelfit  
  > "Similar values were also obtained using the CASA task `uvmodelfit` when the primary beam response is accounted for." (4.1. Continuum Emission: PMCs and Host Stars)
3. **Fit disk flux vs host mass relation**: Bayesian linear regression with linmix including non-detections, fluxes scaled to 140 pc, extrapolated to planetary masses  
  > "we perform Bayesian linear regression to the stellar and brown dwarf disks (including non-detections) using the `linmix` package (cite: Kelly07)" (5.1. Disk Flux-Host Mass Relationship)
   - software: linmix
   - parameter: F scaled by (distance/140 pc)^2  
  > "$F_{\rm disk}=F_{\rm disk}\times(\mbox{distance}/140~\mbox{pc})^2$" (5.1. Disk Flux-Host Mass Relationship)
   - assumption: Fluxes not converted to dust masses because of optical depth uncertainty  
  > "We opt not to convert continuum emission to dust mass" (5.1. Disk Flux-Host Mass Relationship)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Regression repeated per region (Appendix)  
  > "In Figure [5](#fig:LmmMs_SFR) we show the individual relationships in Taurus, Chamaeleon I, and Lupus." (Appendix)
