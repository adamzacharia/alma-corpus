3. **Detect sources and estimate false detections**: Segment the uncorrected continuum image; compare cumulative positive and negative peaks  
  > "Source extraction is performed on the 1.17 mm continuum image before primary beam correction by using image segmentation routines in the `astropy` coordinated package `photutils` (cite: larry_bradley_2024_13989456)." (Source detection); "repeating source extraction on reversed data and comparing the cumulative number of positive and negative peaks above a given S/N" (Source detection)
   - software: astropy, photutils
   - parameter: Initial islands above 0.5×RMS and larger than one synthesized beam  
  > "We search for continuous pixel islands with amplitude above $0.5\times$RMS and total area larger than $1\times$synthesized beam as initial source candidates." (Source detection)
   - parameter: Final peak S/N>4.2; new sources with 4.2<S/N<5.0 marked tentative  
  > "we filter the initial source candidates by requiring peak S/N$>4.2$" (Source detection); "we mark the new sources with $4.2<{\rm S/N}<5.0$ as tentative detection." (Source detection)
   - parameter: Previous-catalogue matching radius: 0.425×beam FWHM, ≈0.7″  
  > "using a matching radius of $0.425\times$beam FWHM ($\approx0.7\ensuremath{''}$, equivalent to $1\sigma$ of Gaussian)." (Source detection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Reversed-image false-detection test; all 18 previous main-catalogue sources recovered  
  > "corresponding to a spurious rate of $<12\%$." (Source detection); "All 18 sources in the main catalogue of (cite: 2017ApJ...835...98U) are detected in the new deeper mosaic" (Source detection)
4. **Measure fluxes and correct selection biases**: Measure positions/fluxes with imfit; inject point sources to estimate completeness and flux boosting  
  > "Source position and flux are measured using the CASA `imfit` task." (Source detection); "The detection completeness is estimated by injecting point sources into random blank positions in the image before primary beam correction, and then running the same source detection script." (Source detection)
   - software: CASA, imfit
   - parameter: Recovery: peak S/N>4.2 within 0.7″ of injected position  
  > "The artificial source is considered recovered if it is detected at peak S/N$>4.2$ within $0.7\ensuremath{''}$ from the injected position." (Source detection)
   - parameter: For peak S/N<20, divide imfit flux by median measured-to-true flux ratio at that S/N  
  > "For sources with peak S/N$<20$, we divide their 1.17 mm fluxes from `imfit` task by the median measured-to-true flux ratio at the corresponding S/N to correct for flux boosting." (Source detection)
   - assumption: Artificial sources are point sources  
  > "injecting point sources into random blank positions" (Source detection)
   - fitting: CASA imfit; model and constraints not stated

## Qualitative findings

- Bright-source excess; faint-source counts consistent with the field  
  > "The resulting number count shows consistency with the field average for flux density $\lesssim1$ mJy but obvious excess at brighter fluxes." (1.17 mm number count)
- Removing known SSA22 members brings counts into rough agreement with random fields  
  > "When known SSA22 members are excluded, the number count becomes roughly consistent with random fields" (overabundant bright dusty star-forming galaxies in protocluster environment)
- Evidence for a declining, or at least flat, faint end of the protocluster FIR luminosity function  
  > "the luminosity function shows evidence of a declining (or at least flat) faint end, suggesting a lack of faint DSFGs in the SSA22 protocluster core region" (The rest-frame FIR luminosity function)
- Member SFRs overlap the SFMS; none exceed it by a factor of three  
  > "the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies have SFR overlapping with the SFMS" (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies); "none of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies have SFR $3\times$ above SFMS like the field DSFGs." (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies)
- Lower member SFRs and stellar masses than field DSFG samples can reflect the deeper flux limit  
  > "Overall, SSA22 protocluster galaxies have lower SFR and smaller $M_\star$" (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies); "The deeper flux limit of our survey can pick up lower SFR and less massive systems" (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies)
- Self-regulated star formation fueled by fresh gas is suggested  
  > "the above results suggest the current star formation in SSA22 protocluster member galaxies occurs in a self-regulated way powered by a large amount of fresh gas supply" (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: A deeper continuum mosaic probes the poorly explored faint DSFG population  
  > "the effect of protocluster environment on the abundance of fainter and more representative populations of dusty star-forming galaxies remains poorly explored." (Introduction); "to $>2\times$ fainter flux limit to study the faint dusty star-forming galaxy population in this protocluster region." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Point-scale continuum deconvolution because sources are expected to be at most marginally resolved  
  > "We choose the `mtmfs` deconvolver with scale=\[0\] because the continuum sources are expected to be at most marginally resolved." (ALMA band 6 continuum mosaic)
- Alternative: Mainly use spectroscopic membership because photometric membership can be unreliable  
  > "However, we note that membership based on photometric redshift can be unreliable" (1.17 mm number count); "As such, we mainly consider the results based on spectroscopic membership." (1.17 mm number count)
- Alternative: A scaled blank-field luminosity-function shape predicts too many faint sources  
  > "by scaling the $z\sim3$ field luminosity function to match the two brightest flux bins of SSA22, we will expect much more sources at S$_{\rm 1.17mm}<1$ mJy than what has been observed." (The rest-frame FIR luminosity function)
- Alternative: Different SED-fitting codes are considered unlikely to explain the field/member differences  
  > "it is unlikely that the differences are driven by the choice of SED fitting codes because their results usually agree within error (cite: 2023ApJ...944..141P)." (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies)
- Alternative: Deeper selection can explain lower member masses and SFRs relative to field DSFG samples  
  > "Instead, it can be driven by the fainter fluxes of our sample" (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies); "The deeper flux limit of our survey can pick up lower SFR and less massive systems" (Stellar mass and SFR of the SSA22 protocluster member galaxies)
- Alternative: Potential lensing or absent counterparts for A33, A58 and A64  
  > "Three sources (A33, A58 and A64) have a nearby NIR source located $>0.7\ensuremath{''}$ away from the ALMA position and no photometry." (Counterpart identification and source properties); "They are marked as potential lensed sources or no counterpart (L/N) in our catalogue." (Counterpart identification and source properties)
- How they decided it was enough: Adopt peak S/N>4.2 based on the reversed-image false-detection test  
  > "Based on these results, we filter the initial source candidates by requiring peak S/N$>4.2$" (Source detection); "corresponding to a spurious rate of $<12\%$." (Source detection)
- Limitation (authors): Low-S/N new detections are tentative  
  > "Considering the poor confirmation of (cite: 2017ApJ...835...98U) catalogue at S/N$<5$, we mark the new sources with $4.2<{\rm S/N}<5.0$ as tentative detection." (Source detection)
- Limitation (authors): Photometric-redshift membership can be unreliable  
  > "However, we note that membership based on photometric redshift can be unreliable" (1.17 mm number count)
- Limitation (authors): Small numbers of bright field sources can produce residual count deviations  
  > "the residual deviation at flux $\gtrsim1$ mJy can be attributed to the small number of bright field sources in the surveyed area." (overabundant bright dusty star-forming galaxies in protocluster environment)
- Limitation (authors): The footprint samples only the very center of the protocluster  
  > "our ALMA footprint covering only the very center of the $z = 3.09$ protocluster" (overabundant bright dusty star-forming galaxies in protocluster environment)
- Limitation (authors): Faint-end evidence permits a flat shape  
  > "the luminosity function shows evidence of a declining (or at least flat) faint end" (The rest-frame FIR luminosity function)
- Limitation (authors): MUSE coverage excludes the outer part of the ALMA map  
  > "The MUSE mosaic is almost co-spatial with the ALMA map, but the outer part of the ALMA map is not included." (MUSE spectroscopy)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spectroscopic incompleteness could affect the inferred faint-member deficit.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The faintest luminosity-function bin has large uncertainties; a declining slope is not uniquely established.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed dust temperature and emissivity introduce unquantified luminosity-conversion systematics.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Point-source injection may not capture completeness and flux biases for resolved sources.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The summary membership window Δz=0.1 differs from the Table 2 core interval z=3.09±0.01; their respective uses need clarification.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The luminosity-function discussion calls A42 a z=3.098 source, while the catalogue assigns that redshift to A41 and gives A42 a photometric redshift of 2.34.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SFMS consistency does not directly measure gas accretion or exclude merger contributions.
