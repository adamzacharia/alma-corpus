## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate visibility data and image 13CO(1-0) line cube and 104 GHz continuum**: Standard pipeline calibration followed by imaging with tclean using Briggs weighting  
  > "We first performed the standard pipeline calibration on the visibility data and then produced line cubes and continuum images using tclean." (Observations and Data Reduction); "We used Briggs weighting with the robust parameter set to 0.5 to improve the angular resolution of the final images without severely compromising the image sensitivity." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: pixel scale = 0.36 arcsec  
  > "We set the pixel scale for both the continuum and line images to 0.36 arcsec." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: channel width = 0.664 km s^-1 for 13CO image  
  > "The channel width for the $^{13}$CO image was set to 0.664 km s$^{-1}$." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "We used Briggs weighting with the robust parameter set to 0.5 to improve the angular resolution of the final images without severely compromising the image sensitivity." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Decompose 13CO(1-0) emission into hierarchical structures and identify molecular clouds**: Dendrogram decomposition using ASTRODENDRO to identify trunks, branches, and leaves  
  > "To identify structures within the $^{13}$CO(J=1-0) image cube, we used the ASTRODENDRO package, which decomposes emission into a hierarchy of nested structures (cite: Rosolowsky2008; Colombo2015)." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties); "We identified 20 structures in the entire dendrogram, consisting of 15 leaves and 4 branches, using the above parameters." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - software: ASTRODENDRO
   - parameter: local maxima threshold = 4 sigma_rms, delta above merge level = 3 sigma_rms  
  > "Parameters were chosen so that the algorithm could identify local maxima in the cube above the 4$\sigma_{rms}$ level that were also 3$\sigma_{rms}$ above the merge level with adjacent structures." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested a lower detection threshold of 3 sigma and compared with NMA detections from Miura2010  
  > "If we lower our detection threshold to 3$\sigma$, we can detect these sources, but we also detect additional spurious noise in the maps." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
3. **Extract basic cloud properties and derive physical quantities (size, linewidth, masses, surface density, virial parameter)**: Bijection approach in ASTRODENDRO for moments and fluxes; analytic equations for physical properties; bootstrap method with 50 iterations for uncertainties  
  > "The basic properties of the identified structures are also determined by ASTRODENDRO using the bijection approach (cite: Rosolowsky2008)." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties); "The uncertainties in the molecular clouds properties R, $\mathrm{\Delta v}$, $\mathrm{L_{^{13}CO}}$ and $\mathrm{M_{lum}}$ are computed using a bootstrap method with 50 iterations." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - software: ASTRODENDRO, ASTROPY
   - parameter: R = 1.91 sigma_r  
  > "The spherical radius $R$ is set to 1.91 $\sigma_r$ following (cite: Solomon1987) and (cite: Rosolowsky2006)." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - parameter: 50 bootstrap iterations  
  > "The uncertainties in the molecular clouds properties R, $\mathrm{\Delta v}$, $\mathrm{L_{^{13}CO}}$ and $\mathrm{M_{lum}}$ are computed using a bootstrap method with 50 iterations." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - assumption: Distance to M33 = 840 kpc  
  > "This corresponds to physical scales of 9 to 116 pc at the distance of $\mathrm{840~kpc}$ to M33." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - assumption: 13CO(1-0)-to-H2 conversion factor X_13CO = 1.0 x 10^21 cm^-2 / (K km s^-1) (X_2 = 5), including 1.36 helium factor  
  > "This calculation includes a factor of 1.36 to account for the mass of helium." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties); "We have adopted $X_2 = 5$ based on the average $\mathrm{^{13}CO(1-0)-to-H_2}$ conversion factor of $\mathrm{1.0\times 10^{21}cm^{-2}/(K~km~s^{-1})}$ for nearby disc spiral galaxies found by (cite: Cormier2018)." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - assumption: Truncated power-law density distribution rho propto R^-beta with beta = 1, and negligible magnetic fields and external pressure for virial mass  
  > "This formulation assumes a truncated power-law density distribution of $\mathrm{\rho \varpropto R^{-\beta}}$ with $\beta = 1$ and with the assumption that magnetic fields and external pressure are negligible (cite: Solomon1987)." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Bootstrap resampling with 50 iterations to estimate uncertainties  
  > "The uncertainties in the molecular clouds properties R, $\mathrm{\Delta v}$, $\mathrm{L_{^{13}CO}}$ and $\mathrm{M_{lum}}$ are computed using a bootstrap method with 50 iterations." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
4. **Analyze Larson's scaling relations (size-linewidth and virial-luminous mass)**: Compute Spearman rank correlation coefficients and compare against Milky Way, NGC 300, and previous NGC 604 relations  
  > "The Spearman correlation coefficient for these data has the value of $\mathrm{r_{s}=0.8}$, which indicates that there is a correlation between size and linewidths of GMCs in NGC 604." (Size - Line width Relation); "We show in Figure 6 that the clouds in NGC 604 are in near virial equilibrium and that the data are strongly correlated, with a Spearman coefficient of $r_{s}=0.98$." (Molecular Mass - Virial Mass Relations)

## Qualitative findings

- C18O(J=1-0) and CH3OH lines were not detected; only 13CO(J=1-0) and continuum emission were detected.  
  > "Only the $^{13}$CO (J=1-0) and continuum emission are detected in this data." (Observations and Data Reduction)
- 104 GHz continuum emission is detected near the centre of the GHR, and spatial offsets exist between the 13CO(1-0) peaks and 104 GHz continuum peaks.  
  > "We find spatial offsets between the $\mathrm{^{13}CO}$ and 104 GHz continuum emission and also detect continuum emission near the centre of the GHR." (Abstract); "The continuum peaks lie closer to the centre of the H region than the $^{13}$CO peaks." (NMA-8)
- NMA-8 is resolved into four individual molecular clouds (L3, L4, L5, L6), NMA-7 is resolved into three sources (L10, L11, L12), and NMA-9 is resolved into three sources (L7, L8, L9).  
  > "We have resolved NMA-8, the largest GMC in NGC 604 found by (cite: Miura2010), into four individual molecular clouds that we labelled L3, L4, L5 and L6." (NMA-8); "We have for the first time resolved NMA-7 into three sources (L10, L11, and L12) and NMA-9 into three sources (L7, L8, and L9)." (Other GMCs in NGC 604)
- GMCs in NGC 604 are at different evolutionary stages, with clouds near the centre associated with continuum and active star formation (L1/MMS4, L4/MMS2, L5/MMS1) and outer clouds only detected in 13CO line emission.  
  > "We have labelled the three continuum sources with the abbreviation MMS (millimetre source), with MMS1 corresponding to L5, MMS2 corresponding to L4, and MMS4 corresponding to L1 as seen in the bottom right panel of Figure 1." (Discussion); "We only detect 104 GHz continuum emission near the centre of GHR. Further out of the centre, only $^{13}$CO line emission is detected. This indicates that the GMCs in NGC 604 are in different evolutionary stages as previously suggested by (cite: Tosaki2007) and (cite: Miura2010)." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA Band 3 observations of NGC 604 have better resolution and sensitivity compared to prior observations, helping to resolve individual GMCs and overcome low-sensitivity biases in extragalactic GMC studies.  
  > "In this work, we present a dendrogram analysis of ALMA observations of $^{13}$CO(J=1-0) and 104 GHz continuum emission from NGC 604. These observations have better resolutions and sensitivities compared to prior observations, which helps to overcome many of the issues highlighted by (cite: Faesi2018)." (Introduction)
- Why this method: ASTRODENDRO decomposes emission into a hierarchy of nested structures, providing a precise representation of the topology of star-forming complexes; Briggs robust = 0.5 was chosen to improve angular resolution without severely compromising sensitivity.  
  > "We used Briggs weighting with the robust parameter set to 0.5 to improve the angular resolution of the final images without severely compromising the image sensitivity." (Observations and Data Reduction); "To identify structures within the $^{13}$CO(J=1-0) image cube, we used the ASTRODENDRO package, which decomposes emission into a hierarchy of nested structures (cite: Rosolowsky2008; Colombo2015). This dendrogram technique, provide a precise representation of the topology of star forming complexes." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
- Alternative: Lowering the ASTRODENDRO detection threshold from 4 sigma_rms to 3 sigma_rms to detect fainter NMA clouds (NMA 1, 3, 6, 11, 12) was rejected because it introduced spurious noise detections.  
  > "If we lower our detection threshold to 3$\sigma$, we can detect these sources, but we also detect additional spurious noise in the maps. Given this situation, we chose to use only sources detected at the higher threshold." (Structure Decomposition and Cloud Properties)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Scatter of 0.3 dex in the X_13CO conversion factor causes a systematic uncertainty of about a factor of 2 in the derived luminous masses.
