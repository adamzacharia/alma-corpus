## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image the JVLA data**: CASA calibration pipeline with manual flux calibration on J0416-1851, flagging, then tclean continuum (MFS) and cube imaging with natural weighting  
  > "using a standard high-frequency calibration pipeline, but with a manual flux calibration (i.e., no CASA fluxscale) due to the observation setup." (2 Data calibration and imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Cube channels 6 MHz, natural weighting, CASA tclean cube mode  
  > "imaged (CASA tclean in ‘cube’ mode, natural weighting, 6 MHz channels)" (2 Data calibration and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visibilities inspected, bad antennas flagged, one execution dropped  
  > "The resulting visibilites were inspected, and additional flagging of bad antennas and edge flagging was performed before the pipeline was re-run." (2 Data calibration and imaging)
2. **derive JVLA continuum upper limit**: 3-sigma limit from a large elliptical aperture on the non-detected continuum image  
  > "To place a conservative estimate on the continuum emission, we use a large elliptical aperture of double the deconvolved" (3.1 Continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Aperture 1.0'' x 0.6'' (double the [OIII] deconvolved FWHM)  
  > "resulting in $1.0''\times0.6''$" (3.1 Continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **derive ALMA Band 3 3.2 mm continuum upper limit**: ScriptForPI calibration on raw archival data, tclean MFS natural weighting with line-conservative frequency range  
  > "using CASA tclean with MFS mode, natural weighting, and a conservative frequency range to exclude possible line emission" (3.1 Continuum)
   - software: CASA, tclean, ScriptForPI.py
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **explore dust and non-thermal SED constraints**: Modified blackbody plus non-thermal model; non-thermal normalised to meet continuum limits (MaxR) or set by a lower SFR (MinR); dust temperature fixed at 40, 85 or 130 K and M_D and beta_IR solved for  
  > "we examine the FIR portion of the SED by assuming a dust temperature (40 K, 85 K, or 130 K)" (3.1 Continuum); "and normalise the non-thermal emission so that our continuum limits are met" (3.1 Continuum)
   - parameter: alpha_NT=0.8, f_th=0.1  
  > "we assume standard values for $\alpha_{NT}=0.8$ and $f_{th}=0.1$ (e.g., (cite: cond92; alge21))" (3.1 Continuum)
   - assumption: Dust emitted from about the same area as the [OIII] emission; kappa_0=0.04 m^2/kg at 250 GHz  
  > "adopt a dust absorption coefficient of $\kappa_o=0.04$ m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ at a critical density of 250 GHz ((cite: beel06))" (3.1 Continuum)
   - assumption: beta_IR above ~2.5 is rarely seen  
  > "the fact that $\beta_{IR}$ values greater than $\sim2.5$ are rarely seen" (3.1 Continuum)
   - fitting: No full fit: not all six parameters can be constrained, so parameter space is explored  
  > "we cannot place constraints on all six free parameters of this model simultaneously" (3.1 Continuum)
   - validation: Models are illustrative only (single detection)  
  > "Since this SED only contains a single point, these models are for illustration only." (3.1 Continuum)
5. **constrain CO(2-1) and M_H2 upper limit**: Extract spectrum, collapse cube over ~200 km/s, take 3-sigma limit on integrated flux, convert with the Solomon equation including CMB correction  
  > "we first collapse the data cube around the expected CO(2-1) frequency, assuming a width of $\sim200$ km s$^{-1}$" (4.2 Molecular gas); "Using the standard equation of (cite: solo92)" (4.2 Molecular gas)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 3-sigma S*dv < 72 mJy km/s from RMS 4.5 mJy/beam km/s  
  > "has an RMS noise level of 4.5 mJy beam$^{-1}$ km s$^{-1}$, corresponding to a $3\sigma$ upper limit on the integrated flux density of $S\Delta$v$_{\rm CO(2-1)}<72$ mJy km s$^{-1}$." (4.2 Molecular gas)
   - assumption: Starburst-like alpha_CO (0.8) and r_21; T_ex,CO = T_D ~ 90 K; mu=1.43  
  > "where we have assumed a starburst-like $\alpha_{CO}$ and $r_{21}$" (4.2 Molecular gas); "We assume that the dust and molecular gas are in thermal equilibrium ($\rm T_{ex,CO}=T_D\sim90$ K)." (4.2 Molecular gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared a 1e10 Msun Gaussian line profile against the spectrum: peaks at <1 sigma, so the sensitivity cannot confirm or rule out that gas mass  
  > "That is, we lack the sensitivity to confirm or rule out this amount of molecular gas." (4.2 Molecular gas)
6. **compare with [CII], dust and dynamical gas-mass estimates**: [CII] luminosity with alpha_[CII]; dust mass with metallicity-dependent dust-to-gas ratio; dynamical mass budget  
  > "Using $\alpha_{[CII]}\sim30$ M$_{\odot}$ L$_{\odot}$$^{-1}$ ((cite: zane18)) results in an estimated $M_{H_2}\sim4.2\times10^9$ M$_{\odot}$ for MACS0416_Y1." (4.2 Molecular gas); "by adopting the dust-to-gas ratio ($\delta_{DGR}$) prescription of (cite: li19)" (4.2 Molecular gas)
   - assumption: Z'~0.25 for the dust-to-gas ratio  
  > "If we assume $Z'\sim0.25$ ((cite: bakx20))" (4.2 Molecular gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Estimates tabulated and compared across tracers  
  > "the available data suggest a broad mass range of $M_{H_2}\sim10^{8.5-11.3}$ M$_{\odot}$" (4.2 Molecular gas)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: CO is the standard molecular gas tracer but few CO detections exist at z>8; JVLA observed CO(2-1) and rest-frame radio continuum  
  > "the standard approach of CO-based estimates has been limited to a small number of galaxies due to the intrinsic faintness of the line." (abstract); "Using the Jansky Very Large Array (JVLA), we observed this object in CO(2-1) emission and rest-frame radio continuum emission." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Large aperture chosen to give a conservative continuum limit  
  > "To place a conservative estimate on the continuum emission, we use a large elliptical aperture of double the deconvolved" (3.1 Continuum)
- Alternative: A model with beta_IR=1.5 and T_D=50 K (as in the prior UV-to-FIR analysis) could not be reproduced with the 850 um detection and 1.5 mm limit  
  > "We are unable to recreate a model that uses $\beta_{IR}=1.5$ and $\rm T_D=50$ K" (3.1 Continuum)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): CO sensitivity insufficient to confirm or rule out 1e10 Msun of gas  
  > "That is, we lack the sensitivity to confirm or rule out this amount of molecular gas." (4.2 Molecular gas)
- Limitation (authors): SED models are illustrative because the FIR SED has a single point  
  > "Since this SED only contains a single point, these models are for illustration only." (3.1 Continuum)
- Limitation (authors): CO excitation temperature cannot be derived without multiple CO detections  
  > "but a derivation of $T_{ex,CO}$ requires multiple CO detections and excitation modelling" (4.2 Molecular gas)
- Limitation (authors): [CII] dynamical mass is only approximate  
  > "this value may only be used as an approximate estimate." (4.2 Molecular gas)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA plays a supporting role: only one 3.2 mm upper limit, no ALMA reduction details
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux and bandpass calibration relied on a phase calibrator after the intended calibrator scans failed; flux scale uncertainty not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Starburst-like r_21 and alpha_CO are assumed without a stated r_21 number
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust SED conclusions rest on one detection and four upper limits with no formal fit or uncertainties
