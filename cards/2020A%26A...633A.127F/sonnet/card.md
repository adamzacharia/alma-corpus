## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and CO/CS cubes**: tclean with hogbom, briggs robust 2.0, auto-multithresh, uv continuum subtraction, primary beam correction  
  > "The image reconstruction was performed using the standard `hogbom` deconvolution algorithm with `briggs` weighting and a robustness value of $2.0$." (2.2 ALMA millimetre interferomery)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: 10 km/s channels, 0.02'' pixels  
  > "a channel with of $\sim 10\, \rm{\,\hbox{\hbox{km}\,\hbox{s}$^{-1}$}}$ and a pixel size of $0.02\ensuremath{''}$" (2.2 ALMA millimetre interferomery)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit disc rotation for kinematic axes and systemic velocity**: DISKFIT chi-square fit of circular rotation only, axis ratio fixed  
  > "This code applies a $\chi^2$ minimisation to find a global model that fits the circular speed of the disc measured at different radii." (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
   - software: DISKFIT
   - parameter: b/a fixed at 0.96  
  > "we fixed the axis ratio at $b/a = 0.96$" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
   - assumption: No radial flows or non-circular motions  
  > "thus radial flows or non-circular motions were not included in the fit" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
   - fitting: chi^2 minimisation  
  > "This code applies a $\chi^2$ minimisation" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract wind spectrum and derive mass**: 3D mask on cube to isolate outflow; integrate flux; convert to H2 mass with alpha_CO = 0.8  
  > "An optimal extraction of the outflow spectrum can be performed using a 3D mask in the ALMA datacube" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8 (alternative 4.3); optically thick  
  > "If instead a Milky Way value of $\alpha = 4.3\, \rm{M_\odot\,(K\,\,\hbox{\hbox{km}\,\hbox{s}$^{-1}$}\,pc^2)^{-1}}$ is assumed" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with opposite-side mask spectrum showing no counter-outflow  
  > "nor in the integrated spectrum of the opposite symmetric 3D mask (purple-dashed line)" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
4. **Compute outflow rate, kinetic luminosity and momentum rate**: Mdot = M V/d; Lkin = 1/2 M V^3/d  
  > "The wind kinetic luminosity ($L_{\rm kin}$) was computed from the product $\frac{1}{2} M V^{3} d^{-1}$" (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)
   - parameter: V = 160 km/s projected, d ~ 100 pc  
  > "then the average projected distance covered by the wind is $d \sim 100\, \rm{pc}$ ($0.4\ensuremath{''}$)" (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)
   - assumption: Wind starts at the jet-ISM impact point; values are lower limits due to projection  
  > "these values should be considered as lower limits due to the projection effect" (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate jet power from radio SED and compare with wind**: Fit radio-to-IR SED (modified blackbody plus broken power law with cutoff) by chi^2; use f_cav calibration for total mechanical power  
  > "Using a standard $\chi^2$ minimisation method, a minimum radio power of $10^{39}\, \rm{erg\,s^{-1}}$ was found." (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)
   - assumption: Synchrotron from nucleus dominates at low frequencies  
  > "Assuming that the synchrotron radiation from the nucleus dominates at low frequencies" (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)
   - fitting: chi^2 minimisation  
  > "Using a standard $\chi^2$ minimisation method" (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)
   - validation: Fit consistent with ALMA core fluxes not included in fit  
  > "which is in agreement with the ALMA fluxes for the radio core" (4.3 Energy and momentum balance)

## Qualitative findings

- Wind is redshifted, conical, starts 340 pc from the nucleus along the kinematic minor axis, with no counter-outflow  
  > "The wind extends from $\sim 1.5\ensuremath{''}$ to $2.5\ensuremath{''}$ away from the nucleus ($340\, \rm{pc}$ to $600\, \rm{pc}$)" (3.1 A molecular gas wind)
