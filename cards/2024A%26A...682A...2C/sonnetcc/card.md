## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image the ALMA continuum**: Calibrate in CASA, self-calibrate each band and configuration on line-free continuum, image with tclean (Briggs, robust 0.5)  
  > "The data were calibrated and imaged in CASA (cite: casa)." (2.3 Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array); "using Briggs weighting with the robust parameter set to 0.5." (2.3 Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: phase-only self-cal solution intervals 30 s and 6 s, then amplitude self-cal with solint = scan intervals  
  > "We first performed two phase-only self-calibration iterations with solution intervals of 30 and 6s, and then one iteration of amplitude self-calibration with the solution interval equal to the scan intervals." (2.3 Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array)
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "using Briggs weighting with the robust parameter set to 0.5." (2.3 Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify compact mm sources and measure positions and fluxes**: Visual identification of 12 compact Band 7 sources within 15'' of centre, plus three Band 6 southern cores; positions from 2D Gaussian fits, fluxes integrated within 1.5 x FWHM ellipse on primary beam corrected images  
  > "From the high-resolution Band 7 data, we identify 12 compact continuum sources within $15\ensuremath{''}$ from the center, which are readily apparent upon visual inspection" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180); "The coordinates of each source are obtained with 2D Gaussian fitting, and the fluxes are determined from primary beam corrected images by integrating within the fitted Gaussian ellipse at $1.5\times\mathrm{FWHM}$." (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: integration aperture 1.5 x FWHM of the fitted Gaussian  
  > "by integrating within the fitted Gaussian ellipse at $1.5\times\mathrm{FWHM}$." (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - assumption: fluxes and masses likely represent the disk or inner envelope rather than the full envelope  
  > "the fluxes and masses presented likely represent those of the disk or inner envelope of the sources, rather than the full envelope mass." (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fitting; software not stated  
  > "The coordinates of each source are obtained with 2D Gaussian fitting" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Convert mm fluxes to core masses**: Optically thin thermal dust emission, M = S d^2 / (kappa B(T_d))  
  > "The core masses are estimated from the fluxes following standard assuptions of optically thin thermal dust emission" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - parameter: 230 GHz (Band 6), 330 GHz (Band 7)  
  > "(230 GHz for Band 6; 330 GHz for Band 7)" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - assumption: distance 2 kpc  
  > "which we take to be 2 kpc (see" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - assumption: kappa = 0.899 (Band 6) and 1.77 (Band 7) cm2/g, Ossenkopf & Henning 1994  
  > "is the dust opacity per unit mass, which we take to be 0.899 $\mathrm{cm^{2}\:g^{-1}}$ for Band 6 and 1.77 $\mathrm{cm^{2}\:g^{-1}}$ for Band 7 (cite: ossenkopf94)" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - assumption: T_d = 50 K for S12-14, 42 K for the rest (Minier et al. 2005 SED fits)  
  > "which is taken to be 50 K for the southern complex (S12-14) and 42 K for the remaining sources based on Spectral Energy Distribution (SED) fitting results from (cite: minier05)" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - assumption: gas-to-dust ratio 100  
  > "A gas-to-dust ratio of 100 was assumed." (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Identify jet knots in NIR continuum-subtracted images and attribute them to ALMA cores**: Knots identified in continuum-subtracted H2 and [FeII] images with sigma thresholds; coordinates from 2D Gaussian fits; driving cores assigned by inspection of linear morphology  
  > "Associations between knots and driving sources were made by inspection of the jet morphology of the image to identify linear patterns that provide evidence of origin from a particular source/cluster." (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification); "Precise coordinates presented in Table [\[Knot_table\]](#Knot_table) were determined via 2D Gaussian fitting of each knot" (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification)
   - parameter: H2 knot peak threshold 3 sigma (seeing-limited and AO)  
  > "The minimum intensity peak for knot identification in the H$_2$ (seeing-limited and AO) images was set at $3\sigma$" (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification)
   - parameter: [FeII] knot peak threshold 5 sigma  
  > "the minimum intensity peak for knot identification in the \[FeII\] image was set at $5\sigma$" (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification)
   - assumption: knot 14 age from constant 100 km/s velocity and 2 kpc distance  
  > "Assuming a constant velocity of 100 $\mathrm{km\:s^{-1}}$ away from its driving core" (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fitting of knot positions  
  > "Precise coordinates presented in Table [\[Knot_table\]](#Knot_table) were determined via 2D Gaussian fitting of each knot" (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification)
   - validation: associations described as tentative; proper motions needed  
  > "These associations are, however, tentative" (3.3.2 Jet Feature Identification)
5. **Count YSOs and derive the radial surface number density profile**: DAOStarFinder on the K-band AO image, merged with ALMA-identified cores, counted in annuli of width 0.05 pc  
  > "implemented in the function *DAOStarFinder* in photutils (cite: photutils)" (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis); "The surface number density was derived by counting the number of YSO candidates within a given annulus." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - software: photutils, DAOFIND/DAOStarFinder
   - parameter: DAOStarFinder fwhm 3 pixels, threshold 5 sigma, others default  
  > "The input parameters are 3 pixels for the *fwhm* and $5\sigma$ for the *threshold*, and the remaining parameters are kept as default." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - parameter: annulus width 0.05 pc  
  > "evaluated in annuli of width 0.05 pc." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - assumption: A_V range 10-20 mag, fiducial 15 mag, distance 2 kpc, ~3 Myr isochrones, giving mass limit ~0.1 Msun  
  > "Thus we have considered a range of extinction values from $A_V\simeq 10$ to 20 mag." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis); "we obtain a mass limit of $\sim0.1\,M_\odot$." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - assumption: foreground field star contamination small compared to outer N_*  
  > "Our analysis has also assumed that the level of foreground field star contamination in the region is small compared to the outer value of $N_*\sim 4\times 10^2\:{\rm pc}^{-2}$." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: visual inspection to remove spurious detections; Poisson uncertainties noted  
  > "perform a visual inspection to make sure there are no spurious detections." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
6. **Compare the observed N_* profile with STARFORGE and Turbulent Clump models**: Measure N_*(r) of sink particles or stars >= 0.1 Msun around the most massive star in the simulations and compare by eye to the observed profile  
  > "Given our NIR sensitivity to YSOs, we only consider sink particles with masses $\geq0.1\:M_\odot$." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis); "reasonable matches to the observed profile are obtained at $t=0.5-2\:$Myr." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - parameter: STARFORGE snapshots with most massive sink 8-16 Msun, age 3.398-3.642 Myr  
  > "with an age range of $3.398-3.642\,$Myr and a mass range of $8-16\,M_\odot$ for the most massive sink particle" (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - parameter: Farias & Tan models: epsilon_ff = 0.03, M_cl = 300 Msun, Sigma_cloud = 0.1 g/cm2, most massive star 10-20 Msun  
  > "We only considered models with fiducial star formation efficiencies per free-fall time of $\epsilon_{\rm ff}=0.03$." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - fitting: no formal fit; best-matching models chosen by comparison  
  > "We found that the models that best fit the observed data are the those with initial clump masses of $M_{\rm cl}=300\,M_\odot$" (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
   - validation: STARFORGE profile checked against broader parameter study with scatter of about 0.3 dex  
  > "although there is some scatter of about 0.3 dex (cite: guszejnov22)" (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)

## Qualitative findings

- ALMA sources fall in two complexes, AFGL 5180 M and a cluster ~15'' (0.15 pc) south, AFGL 5180 S  
  > "Sub-millimeter sources detected by ALMA are shown to be grouped in two main complexes, AFGL 5180 M and a small cluster" (abstract)
- S4 is the likely driver of the main east-west outflow and the only core in the complex with a methanol maser  
  > "S4 appears to be the source primarily responsible for powering the main east-west outflow" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
- The east-west bipolar outflow is a previously uncatalogued Extended Green Object, named EGO G188.95+0.92  
  > "we name the EGO associated with AFGL 5180 EGO G188.95+0.92." (4.3 Placing AFGL 5180 into Context: Triggered Star Formation?)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Earlier ALMA analysis missed many compact Band 7 sources and treated diffuse emission as cores, so the data were re-reduced and re-analysed  
  > "inspection of the Band 7 data reveals many additional compact continuum sources which are unidentified in (cite: mutie21)" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180); "we present a re-analysis of the ALMA Band 6 and 7 data, for which we have re-reduced the data" (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
- Why this method: K-band AO data chosen for the YSO census for its sensitivity and resolution  
  > "we make use of the NIR K-band LUCI-1 SOUL AO data, chosen because of its high sensitivity and angular resolution." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
- Alternative: Explosive outflow scenario considered and rejected in favour of multiple outflows from multiple sources  
  > "we conclude that a scenario of multiple outflows driven by multiple sources is more plausible." (4.1 Implications for Outflow Launching)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): It is unclear how many compact sources (YSOs) the southern cores harbour because they lack high-resolution observations  
  > "it is unclear how many compact sources (YSOs) are really harbored in these cores due to the lack of high-resolution observations in these regions." (3.3.1 The Sub-mm Population in AFGL 5180)
- Limitation (authors): Poisson uncertainties in N_* are significant and completeness estimates are simple  
  > "We have made quite simple estimates of the level of completeness of the observed YSOs." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis); "there are significant Poisson uncertainties in the derived $N_*$ measurements given the number of sources present." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Nature of the ALMA-identified sources within 0.05 pc is uncertain  
  > "However, the nature of these sources is somewhat uncertain." (3.4 YSO Surface Number Density Analysis)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No error bars on core masses; flux uncertainties, flux scale and dust opacity/temperature uncertainties are not propagated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single dust temperature per group and no check on optical thickness for compact Band 7 sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model-data comparison for N_* is by eye with no quantitative fit or uncertainty on the profile
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Merged NIR plus ALMA census mixes tracers of different completeness and extinction
