## Science

- Question: Does the dense gas fraction (I_HCN/I_CO) drive the star formation rate, and does dynamical equilibrium pressure set the dense gas fraction and the star formation efficiency of dense gas?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and combine ALMA data with IRAM 30-m**: uvcontsub, natural-weighted imaging with taper, 10 km/s channels, smoothing to 8", feather with IRAM 30-m  
  > "Then we combined the two data sets using the CASA task `feather`." (2.1)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: taper 2-3"; 8" final beam; 10 km/s channels  
  > "applying a small $u-v$ taper ($2{-}3\ensuremath{''}$ depending on the line and target)" (2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Feathered cube spectral profile compared to IRAM 30-m at 30" resolution; flux recovery table  
  > "we verified that the feathered cube indeed matched the spectral profile of the IRAM 30-m cube when both were convolved to a common $30\ensuremath{''}$ resolution." (2.1)
2. **Build integrated-intensity maps**: CO-derived PPV mask, sum cubes, convert to T_B via Rayleigh-Jeans  
  > "We sum the masked line data cubes along each line of sight to produce maps of integrated intensity, in K km s$^{-1}$." (2.4)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: verified by eye no dense gas emission outside mask  
  > "We verify by eye that no clear dense gas tracer emission extends beyond this mask." (2.4)
3. **Derive SFR, stellar, gas surface densities**: Sigma_SFR = 634 I_Halpha + 0.0025 I_24um; stellar mass from 3.6um with M/L; HI from 21 cm; all convolved to 8"  
  > "\Sigma_{SFR} = 634~I_{H\alpha} + 0.0025~I_{24\mu m}~" (2.5.1)
   - parameter: M/L = 352 Msun/pc2 per MJy/sr at 3.6um  
  > "assuming a mass to light ratio of $352$ M$_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (MJy sr$^{-1}$) ((cite: MEIDT14))" (2.6)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.3, alpha_HCN = 10 Msun pc-2 (K km/s)-1, include helium  
  > "assuming a CO (1-0) to molecular gas mass conversion factor of $\alpha_{CO} \approx 4.3~\mbox{M$_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$}$." (2.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative TIR-based SFR tracers compared in appendix  
  > "The numerical results from these two main approaches to $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}$ differ at the $\sim 30\%$ level." (2.5)
4. **Compute dynamical equilibrium pressure**: P_DE = pi G Sigma_gas^2/2 + Sigma_gas (2 G rho_star)^0.5 sigma_gas,z with rho_star = Sigma_star/(2 h_star)  
  > "\rho_\star \approx \frac{\Sigma_\star}{2~h_\star}" (2.8)
   - parameter: sigma_z,gas = 15 km/s; l*/h* = 7.3  
  > "We adopt $\sigma_{z,gas} \approx 15$ km s$^{-1}$." (2.8)
   - assumption: Dark matter contribution to midplane density neglected  
  > "We expect the density of dark matter within the gas layer to be small compared to that of the stars, and so neglect that contribution to $\rho_\star$." (2.8)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Radial profiles and correlations/fits**: Tilted-ring-style radial bins of half-beam width (4"), mean in CO mask and ring; rank correlations and OLS bisector power-law fits  
  > "We set the width of each radial bin to be half the FWHM of the working beam ($15\ensuremath{''}$ for NGC 5194 and $4\ensuremath{''}$ for all other galaxies)." (2.10)
   - fitting: Ordinary least squares bisector fit, weighting all significant detections equally  
  > "The red dashed line in Figure [7](#fig:dgf_sfe) shows an ordinary least squares bisector fit to our data." (3.2.2)
   - validation: Repeat with different SFR tracers; check HCO+ and 13CO ratios  
  > "no matter the choice of tracer, the rank correlation coefficient relating $\Sigma_{SFR}/\Sigma_{mol}$ to $\Sigma_{dense}/\Sigma_{mol}$ is low when we consider all galaxies together." (Appendix)

## Qualitative findings

- Strongly barred galaxies show central enhancement of dense gas but low SFR per unit dense gas, resembling the Milky Way CMZ  
  > "We interpret our results as strong evidence that the behavior seen in the Milky Way is a general feature of strongly barred galaxies" (4)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Four galaxies chosen to span a wide range of CO-to-24um ratio  
  > "we chose these four because together they spanned a large range of CO-to-$24\mu$m ratios over their inner few kpc" (2)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: TIR-based SFR tracers tested instead of Halpha+24um; conclusions unchanged  
  > "the choice of SFR tracer does not change our qualitative results." (Appendix)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Sample not representative; simple alpha_CO and alpha_HCN; limited to inner few kpc  
  > "First, our survey does not yet offer a representative sample." (4.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_HCN and alpha_CO fixed, though known to vary with environment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Resolution of 8" (~500 pc) blends cloud structures
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Radial profiles with correlated rings and small number of independent points inflate significance
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous SFR calibrations when combining with literature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Poor flux recovery for NGC 4254 and CS lacks short spacings
