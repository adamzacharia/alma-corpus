1. **Image continuum and cubes**: CASA CLEAN; continuum from line-free channels of all SPWs, cubes from SPW1  
  > "while spectral cubes were generated from the SPW1 dataset." (Sample selection and observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect [CII] and derive line properties and offsets**: Spectra and moment maps (widths 100-440 km/s); S/N, FWHM, flux; offsets from near-IR centroids; astrometry checked with serendipitous continuum sources  
  > "we looked at bright serendipitous sources detected in the continuum band in our fields." (Offsets between [CII] and rest-frame UV position)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Independent Cycle-2 and Cycle-3 detections of NTTDF6345 and spatial coincidence with near-IR counterparts  
  > "the spatial coincidence (or very close spatial association) between the [CII] and the near-IR counterparts" ([CII] line detections)
3. **Dynamical masses**: M_dyn = 1.16e5 V_cir^2 D, V_cir = 1.763 sigma/sin(i), D upper limit from 0.9" resolution  
  > "$M_{dyn} = 1.16 \times 10^5 V_{cir}^2 D$" (Dynamical masses)
   - assumption: Ordered motions; D set by resolution upper limit; inclination unknown  
  > "Our sources are only marginally resolved so we assume an upper limit on D from the resolution of 0.9$''$." (Dynamical masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SED-fit stellar masses  
  > "The stellar masses are reported in Table 1 and are very similar to the dynamical masses" (Dynamical masses)
4. **Compare with SFR-L[CII] relations**: Place sources on SFR-L[CII] plane with UV-based SFR (no dust correction) versus local and z~5.5 samples and Vallini models  
  > "We remark that the SFR for our sources as well as previous $z\sim 7$ ones are UV-based, with no correction for dust extinction." (SFR-L[CII] relation)

## Qualitative findings

- Targets fall a factor 2-3 below z~5.5 galaxies of similar SFR in L[CII] and below local relations; earlier z~7 non-detections plausibly reflect selection of strong Lya emitters (low metallicity)  
  > "Our galaxies are a factor of 2-3 less luminous in L[CII] than $z \sim$5.5 galaxies with similar SFRs." (SFR-L[CII] relation)
