## Qualitative findings

- The mass of ionized gas is a factor of ~500 smaller than the molecular gas mass, indicating that ionized gas is negligible in the ATOMS star-forming clumps.  
  > "The mass of ionized gas is significantly smaller than molecular gas mass, indicating that ionized gas is negligible in the star forming clumps of the ATOMS sample." (abstract); "The median mass of ionized gas is a factor of ⇠500 smaller than the median molecular mass, indicating that ionized gas is negligible for the ATOMS sources." (4.2 The differences between sources with and without H40alpha emission)
- SFR estimated from H40alpha agrees well with SFR from Lbol when SFR >= 5 Msun Myr^-1, indicating mm-RRLs sample the upper IMF well and are good SFR tracers.  
  > "The star formation rate (SFR H 40 U ) estimated with RRL H 40 U agrees well with that (SFR L bol ) calculated with the total bolometric luminosity (L bol ) when SFR - 5 M ⊙ My A −1 , suggesting that millimeter RRLs could well sample the upper part of the initial mass function (IMF) and thus be good tracers for SFR." (abstract)
- The Lbol-L'mol correlations for both CS J=2-1 and HC3N J=11-10 are approximately linear and trace dense gas effectively.  
  > "(ii) The correlations between L bol , tracing the SFR, and molecular line luminosities L 0 mol of the CS J=2-1 and HC 3 N J=11-10 appear linear. Both of these two lines can be used to trace dense gas." (5 CONCLUSION)
- Lbol-to-L'mol ratios do not vary with Galactocentric distance RGC, regardless of whether UC HII regions have formed.  
  > "Again, there is no clear trend for L bol -to-L 0 mol ratios against R GC whether or not UC Hii regions have formed." (3.4 The Lbol-to-L'mol ratios)
- Clumps with H40alpha emission show higher Lbol-to-L'mol ratios (by ~0.5 dex) and shorter gas depletion times (by ~0.25 dex) than clumps without H40alpha emission, which is explained as an evolutionary effect.  
  > "Thus depletion times are shorter by about 0.25 dex for sources with Hii regions." (4.2 The differences between sources with and without H40alpha emission); "Thus depletion times are shorter by about 0.25 dex for sources with Hii regions." (4.2 The differences between sources with and without H40alpha emission); "(vii) A plausible explanation for the higher SFEs (or shorter g 34? ) for clumps associated with Hii regions is that the SFE is higher simply because the region is more evolved." (5 CONCLUSION)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ACA 7-m data provide a larger FOV than the 12-m array to match clump-scale bolometric luminosity information and better trace the overall spatial distribution of gas; millimeter RRLs (H40alpha) do not suffer significantly from dust extinction or strong maser amplification, and CS J=2-1 and HC3N J=11-10 have higher effective excitation densities than HCN/HCO+ J=1-0 to trace concentrated dense gas.  
  > "The millimeter recombination lines originating from low energy levels (n=20–50) that would not be much affected by potential maser amplification or dust extinction, may be reliable and quantitative probes of the EUV continuum at 13.6 eV to above 54.6 eV (Scoville & Murchikova 2013)." (1 INTRODUCTION); "CS J=2-1 and HC 3 N J=11-10 have higher effective excitation densities ( = eff ) than HCN J=1-0 and HCO + J=1-0 (Shirley 2015), and similar to those of the rare isotopologues, H 13 CN and H 13 CO + ." (1 INTRODUCTION); "We use only ACA data to obtain a larger FOV than we would have with the 12-m array data and the ACA data can match clump-scale luminosity information and better trace the overall spatial distribution of gas within these star forming regions." (2.2 ALMA ACA observations)

  > "Application of these models to individual regions of star formation in the Milky Way is not rigorously justified, but agreement between tracers with different dependence on the duration and IMF sampling provide empirical support for their use (e.g., Vutisalchavakul et al. 2016)."
