## Analysis recipe

1. **Image line cubes and continuum**: uvcontsub, Briggs-weighted cleaning to 15 and 2 km/s cubes; tclean MFS continuum; imfit of the nuclear source  
  > "The remaining line data were imaged into an RA-Dec-velocity cube with a channel width of $15\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$." (3.2 Line data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust -1 (line), -2 (continuum), 0.1" pixels  
  > "Baselines were weighted by the Briggs scheme with a robust parameter $-1$" (3.2 Line data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared integrated spectrum to IRAM 30-m and argued ACA recovers extended flux  
  > "we conclude that it is likely that almost all flux has been recovered." (3.2 Line data)
2. **Build stellar mass model**: MGE from Cappellari+2006, deprojected at disc inclination, multiplied by constant M/L; central unresolved component excluded; circular velocity from Cappellari 2002 Eq. 12  
  > "From this 3D light distribution, a mass distribution is obtained by multiplying by a spatially-constant mass-to-light ratio ($M/L$), another parameter included in our MCMC fit." (4.2 Stellar mass)
   - assumption: Constant M/L across disc; central MGE component is AGN; negligible dark matter  
  > "we can assume a negligible dark matter contribution over so small a fraction of the galaxy’s volume." (4.7.3 Mass model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested extinction-corrected MGE, inclusion of central component, ISM mass  
  > "We find that the SMBH mass is unchanged within our uncertainties, and therefore dust extinction is not a significant effect." (4.7.3 Mass model)
3. **Fit gas velocity dispersion**: Initial fit on 2 km/s cube; dispersion then fixed for 15 km/s cube fits  
  > "We thus opt to perform an initial fit using our cube with $2\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ channels, finding the best-fitting velocity dispersion, and then fixing this value" (4.4 Gas velocity dispersion)
   - software: KinMS, KinMS_MCMC
   - assumption: Spatially invariant dispersion, small perturbation  
  > "We assume that this dispersion is spatially invariant, and only a small linear perturbation on the circular velocity field." (4.4 Gas velocity dispersion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Forward-model cube with MCMC to get SMBH mass**: KinMS forward model of the central 6.4"x6.4" with Gibbs-sampling MCMC, chi-squared with covariance matrix; truncated exponential gas disc; fit SMBH mass, M/L, scale length, truncation radius, flux, PA, inclination, offsets  
  > "We use the Gibbs sampling MCMC code `KinMS_MCMC`[^4] with adaptive stepping to explore the parameter space." (4.3 Bayesian parameter estimation)
   - software: KinMS, KinMS_MCMC
   - parameter: chain length 3e5 steps, first 10% burn-in  
  > "The final chain length is $3\times10^5$ steps, with the first $10\%$ discarded as a burn-in phase." (4.3 Bayesian parameter estimation)
   - parameter: fit region 6.4"x6.4" (720 pc)  
  > "We therefore only fit the central $6.4\ensuremath{''}\times6.4\ensuremath{''}$ ($720\,\mathrm{pc}\times720\,\mathrm{pc}$) region of our cube." (4.3.1 Pixel–to–pixel correlations)
   - assumption: Thin axisymmetric disc, circular motions, distance 23.3 Mpc, inclination fixed to 20 deg  
  > "we re-run the fit with a fixed inclination of $20\degree$, adopted from the above arguments." (4.5 SMBH mass)
   - fitting: Likelihood lnP = -chi2/2 with chi2 divided by sqrt(2N); uniform priors (log-uniform for SMBH mass) within physical limits; circularised MGE used to free inclination  
  > "Samples are then drawn from the posterior distribution described by the the log-likelihood function $\ln P=-\sfrac{\chi^2}{2}$." (4.3 Bayesian parameter estimation)
   - validation: Bootstrapping 16 half-cube subsets with MPFIT gave consistent values and uncertainties  
  > "The best-fitting values are consistent with those estimated by the MCMC procedure, and the uncertainty estimates are reassuringly very similar." (4.6 Fit uncertainties)
5. **Add inclination uncertainty**: For each MCMC model draw inclination from Gaussian (3 sigma width 5 deg) and rescale mass and M/L via M proportional to (v/sin i)^2; take median and 99.7% interval  
  > "For each model in the final MCMC chain, we draw an inclination from a Gaussian distribution with a $3\sigma$ width of $5\degree$" (4.8 Discussion)
   - assumption: Inclination 20±5 deg from dust disc and tilted-ring fit  
  > "we adopt as the inclination uncertainty at $20\degree$ the representative value of $\pm5\degree$ quoted by (cite: Cappellari+2006MNRAS366.1126)." (4.7.2 Inclination)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Non-parametric gas distribution with SkySampler**: Sample summed CLEAN components (zeroth moment) as particles passed to KinMS via inClouds, deprojected within MCMC; rerun fixed-inclination fit  
  > "We thus supply these particles to `KinMS` using the `inClouds` variable" (5 SkySampler)
   - software: SkySampler, KinMS
   - assumption: Non-axisymmetric gas not significant to the potential  
  > "As long as this (non-axisymmetric) gas distribution is not a significant contributor to the total mass distribution, the assumption of axisymmetry for the potential will remain valid." (5 SkySampler)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with parametric fit (chi2_red 1.82 vs 1.84)  
  > "Our best-fitting model has $\chi^2_\mathrm{red}=1.82$." (5.1 Application to NGC 524)
7. **Test non-circular motions**: Residual velocity field, ridge-finding of residual spiral, MCMC with radial velocity term, kinemetry harmonic and tilted-ring fits, MCMC with PA fixed to kinemetry value  
  > "we fit higher order harmonics to ellipses at the fixed inclination and position angle determined by our MCMC model." (6.2.2 Higher-order terms)
   - software: Kinemetry, KinMS_MCMC
   - parameter: radial velocity prior ±100 km/s  
  > "that we constrain within priors of $\pm100\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$." (6.2.1 First-order term)
   - assumption: CO in thin disc, q0=0  
  > "the intrinsic axial ratio is $q_0=0$" (6.2.2 Higher-order terms)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SMBH mass unchanged with radial term or warp  
  > "There is no significant change of the best-fitting SMBH mass, nor of its uncertainties." (6.2.2 Higher-order terms)

## Software

- **CASA** : Calibration combination, uvcontsub, tclean, imfit  
  > "We fit this source with a Gaussian using the `CASA` task `imfit`" (3.3 Continuum data)
- **KinMS** : Forward-modelling simulated cubes  
  > "we forward-model the observed data cube using the `Kinematic Molecular Simulation (KinMS)` tool[^3]" (4 Model fitting)
- **KinMS_MCMC** : Gibbs-sampling MCMC fits  
  > "We use the Gibbs sampling MCMC code `KinMS_MCMC`[^4] with adaptive stepping" (4.3 Bayesian parameter estimation)
- **MPFIT** : Chi-squared minimisation for bootstrap subsamples  
  > "we minimise the $\chi^2$ defined in Equation [\[eq_chisq\]](#eq_chisq) using `MPFIT`" (4.6 Fit uncertainties)
- **LMFIT** : Chi-squared minimisation with extinction-corrected MGE  
  > "we only perform a chi-squared minimisation using `LMFIT`[^5]" (4.7.3 Mass model)
- **SkySampler** : Sampling CLEAN-component gas distribution into particles  
  > "Our cube sampling code is implemented in Python in the software package `SkySampler`[^6]." (5 SkySampler)
- **Kinemetry** : Harmonic expansion of velocity field  
  > "Using the `Kinemetry` package[^8] of (cite: Krajnovic+2006MNRAS366.787)" (6.2.2 Higher-order terms)
- **Ridge-finding algorithm (Steger 1998)** : Trace peaks of residual velocity spiral  
  > "using the ridge-finding algorithm[^7] of (cite: Steger1998IEEE20.113), to trace the peaks of the residual image." (6.1 Observed velocity field)

## Qualitative findings

- CO disc is dynamically relaxed, rotating around a compact 1.3 mm continuum source identified as the AGN, with a central hole slightly larger than the SMBH sphere of influence  
  > "There is a hole at the centre of the disc slightly larger than the SMBH sphere of influence." (abstract)
- Velocity-field distortion is explained by either a PA warp or radial flow; neither changes the SMBH mass  
  > "An azimuthal distortion of the observed velocity field is found to be due to either a position angle warp or radial gas flow over the inner $2.5\ensuremath{''}$." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Directly sampling the observed gas distribution avoids ever more complex parametric models for gas with holes and rings  
  > "either select arbitrarily complex models, adding free parameters until the gas distributions are well-described, or use the observed gas distributions as inputs to our models" (5 SkySampler)
- Alternative: Archimedean or logarithmic spiral parametrisation of residuals rejected as not matching data  
  > "We initially attempted to parameterise the spiral pattern and fit it with an additional velocity term with a phase described by an Archimedean or logarithmic spiral, but such fits do not match the data." (6.1 Observed velocity field)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Central hole means no Keplerian rise observed; mass relies on stellar mass model  
  > "Since the molecular gas disc has a central hole, we do not capture the Keplerian increase of the rotation velocities where the SMBH dominates the mass distribution." (4.7.3 Mass model)
- Limitation (authors): Model chi2 exceeds expectation, indicating unexplained features  
  > "We in fact find our best fitting model has a larger $\chi^2$, indicating there are features in the data unexplained by our relatively simple model." (4.8 Discussion)
- Limitation (authors): Distance uncertainty (~10%) not included in the systematic error  
  > "but since this is a simple normalisation, we follow standard practice and do not include it in our systematic uncertainty." (4.7.1 Distance)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inclination is essentially unconstrained by CO; fixed to dust value drives result
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rescaling chi2 by sqrt(2N) is ad hoc
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fit restricted to 6.4" region, with no use of larger-scale data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Constant M/L and neglect of dark matter assumed
