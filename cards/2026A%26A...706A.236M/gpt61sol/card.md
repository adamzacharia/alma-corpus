## Qualitative findings

- CO detected in five of nine MUSE group members, plus a previously unknown member without optical or NIR counterpart.  
  > "We report CO (2-1) detections toward 5 out of the 9 members of the galaxy group at $z_{\rm spec}=1.284$, and further identified a previously unknown member of the same group which shows no optical or NIR counterpart" (Conclusions)
- Detected galaxies show diverse sizes and dynamical states.  
  > "the gas detections display a wide range of diversity with no two sources alike in terms of their size and dynamical states." (Conclusions)
- Molecular gas and HST-traced star-formation regions are spatially offset in B15-27 and B15-35.  
  > "One can clearly see the spatial offset between star-formation regions traced by HST and the molecular gas emission in B15-27 and B15-35." (Comparison with rest-frame UV-optical emission)
- No line emission detected in the other groups or elsewhere in the field.  
  > "Finally, we have not detected any line emission towards the other groups listed in Table [2](#tab:grpcov) or within the whole field." (Line emitters)
- Group and field molecular gas mass functions appear similar within current uncertainties; the low-mass function has not turned down at the survey depth.  
  > "We find evidence that group environments have MFs undistinguishable from those of field galaxies (within the observed uncertainties)" (Conclusions); "the MFs show no hint of a decrease in number density with decreasing molecular gas" (Conclusions)
- Recovered group-environment molecular gas densities are comparable to blind CO survey estimates.  
  > "the molecular gas mass densities recovered by this survey[^6] are comparable to those of blind CO surveys in the literature." (Cosmic Molecular gas mass density in group environments)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Target MUSE-identified galaxy groups with a single tuning covering CO(2-1) at z=1.284 and CO(5-4) at z=4.699.  
  > "The tuning was chosen such that CO transitions were optimally covered towards two galaxy groups identified by MUSE." (Choice of Spatial and Spectral coverage); "Specifically, the groups identified by (cite: Bacon15) at $z=1.284$ (9 members) and 4.699 (6 members) have their CO (2-1) and (5-4) transitions, respectively, redshifted into the highest-frequency SPW in the data-set" (Choice of Spatial and Spectral coverage)
- Why this method: Natural weighting improves sensitivity and detection by angular scale range; initial ±600 km/s windows accommodate broad lines and MUSE redshift offsets.  
  > "Natural weighting is preferred since apart from giving higher sensitivity, it is also more desirable for detection by angular scale range." (Angular scales); "This value was done to accommodate potentially broad line profiles as well as significant offsets with respect to the redshift reported by MUSE." (Line emitters)
- Alternative: Blind SoFiA-2 search recovered only four reported detections; positional and redshift information was more effective here.  
  > "only four of the detections reported before have been automatically considered significant by SoFiA-2, showing that in this case, it is better to know where the sources are located with an initial guess of the systemic redshift." (Line emitters)
- Alternative: Extended or fainter continuum-source searches were outside scope.  
  > "It is not within the scope of this paper to push the continuum detections beyond simple point or compact sources towards extended structures or into the noise limit." (Continuum map)
- Alternative: More detailed high-redshift detection analyses, including stacking, were not pursued.  
  > "It is beyond the scope of this manuscript to pursue more detailed detection analysis (e.g., stacking) especially towards the high-redshift group for which the systemic redshifts are uncertain." (Line emitters)
- Alternative: No correction for intrinsic group evolution.  
  > "We do not attempt to correct for intrinsic evolution of this group" (Cosmic Molecular gas mass density in group environments)
- Alternative: Both raw and four-channel-smoothed spectra were fitted.  
  > "The best-fit is shown by a thick line for the raw spectral-resolution ( 24 km/s) cube and a dashed line for the 4-channel smoothed cube." (Line emitters)
- How they decided it was enough: Continuum threshold set by the lowest negative peak and <1 expected false detection; line significance >5 mad.  
  > "the lowest negative peak in the map is -10.5 $\mu$Jy/beam." (Continuum map); "with peak fluxes above 10.5 $\mu$Jy/beam (i.e., $>4.4~\sigma$; resulting in an expected number of false detections in our map of less than one)" (Continuum map); "If a significant detection in the moment-0 map was obtained (i.e., above 5 times the median absolute deviation, mad)" (Line emitters)
- Limitation (authors): Small projected survey area permits large uncertainty in population-level conclusions.  
  > "Given the small projected survey size we acknowledge that there is room for a large uncertainty in the result" (Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): The group is assumed representative; intrinsic group evolution is not corrected.  
  > "We use this \[OII\]$_{3727,3729}$-identified group at $z_{\rm spec}=1.284$ as reference, hence assumed to be representative." (Cosmic Molecular gas mass density in group environments); "We do not attempt to correct for intrinsic evolution of this group" (Cosmic Molecular gas mass density in group environments)
- Limitation (authors): No direct metallicity estimates for group members; αCO applicability checked indirectly.  
  > "Although there is no metallicity estimates for the members in the targeted group" (Line emitters)
- Limitation (authors): Lyman-α does not guarantee systemic redshifts, limiting high-redshift non-detection conclusions.  
  > "More reliable conclusions on source properties for this group would require the knowledge of the systemic redshift, which Lyman-$\alpha$ line emission does not guarantee." (Line emitters)
- Limitation (authors): Long baselines may resolve out emission from the z=0.172 group; velocity uncertainty and spread impair coverage of the z=3.013 group.  
  > "the long-baseline configurations may be resolving out extended emission from the members of the $z_{\rm spec}=0.172$ group" (Choice of Spatial and Spectral coverage); "the systemic velocity uncertainty of the members within the $z_{\rm spec}=3.013$ group combined with the velocity spread of the group makes the CO transitions fall outside our spectral coverage." (Choice of Spatial and Spectral coverage)
- Limitation (authors): More complete dynamical analysis deferred.  
  > "Given the multi-wavelength richness towards the detected sources, we differ a more complete dynamical analysis to a future work." (Line emitters)
