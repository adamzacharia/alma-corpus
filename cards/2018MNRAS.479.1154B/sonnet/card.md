## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA/JVLA continuum and line cubes**: CASA pipeline calibration, clean imaging, uvcontsub  
  > "Dust continuum images and spectral line cubes were produced from the calibrated visibilities using the CASA task *clean*." (2.3)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: natural weighting; robust=0.5 for J2315 continuum  
  > "a Briggs weighting with *robust*=0.5 was found to produce a better compromise between sensitivity and resolution" (2.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum and line fluxes**: 2D elliptical Gaussian fits to continuum; Gaussian fits to 1D spectra (single for CO(1-0), double for CO(7-6)+CI(2-1)); imfit for two-component J2315  
  > "continuum flux densities are estimated by fitting a 2-dimensional elliptical Gaussian to the continuum source in the image plane" (2.5)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: CO(1-0) spectra rebinned by 4, 7, 7, 4  
  > "re-binned the spectral axis by factors of 4, 7, 7 and 4 for ULASJ0123, ULASJ1234, G1234N and ULASJ2315 respectively" (3.2)
   - assumption: For J2315 resolved CO(7-6)/CI emission assumed unresolved and coincident with CO(3-2) components  
  > "assuming that the CO(7-6) and CI(2-1) emission is unresolved in each component of the merger in the current Band 6 data" (3.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked that u-v plane fitting gives the same results  
  > "we have checked that our results do not change if the fitting is done in the u-v plane instead" (2.5)
3. **Fit radio-to-FIR SED for SFR, non-thermal fraction, dust mass**: Synchrotron + free-free + greybody composite fit with MultiNest  
  > "Models are fit to the data using the Bayesian inference code MultiNest ((cite: Feroz:08)) in combination with the Python wrapper PyMultinest" (4.1)
   - software: MultiNest, PyMultiNest
   - parameter: log-uniform SFR 10-9000; uniform log f_nth 0.5-1.5; alpha 0.1-1.6  
  > "We assume a log-uniform prior for SFR (10-9000 M$_{\odot}$yr$^{-1}$) and uniform priors for log$_{10}f_{\rm nth}$ (0.5-1.5) and $\alpha$ (0.1-1.6)" (4.1)
   - assumption: T_d=47 K, beta=1.6, alpha_ff=-0.1, optically thin Rayleigh-Jeans dust, kappa0=0.045 m^2/kg at 250 GHz  
  > "$\kappa_0$ is assumed to be 0.045 m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ at $\nu_0$=250GHz" (4.1)
   - fitting: Ellipsoidal nested sampling (PyMultiNest)  
  > "PyMultiNest explores the parameter space using ellipsoidal nested sampling" (4.1)
   - validation: Tested fixed alpha=0.75 and 0.1 and beta 1.6-2.4; Herschel flux split alternatives  
  > "We have checked that dividing the *Herschel* flux according to the ratio of ALMA fluxes in the three galaxies does not affect the results significantly." (4.1)
4. **Constrain gas density and radiation field via PDR models**: Compare observed CO/CI line ratios with 3D-PDR grid  
  > "We compare the observed line ratios to outputs from the 3D-PDR code" (4.2.3)
   - software: 3D-PDR
   - parameter: zeta=1e-15 s^-1 fiducial, A_V=7 mag  
  > "We assume a cosmic ray (CR) ionization rate of 100$\times$ the Milky Way value - i.e. $\zeta$=10$^{-15}$s$^{-1}$" (4.2.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Varied zeta and A_V (2, 3 mag); checked including CO(1-0)  
  > "Qualitatively, the results are very similar for depths of A$_{\rm{V}}$=2 and 3 mag." (4.2.3)
5. **Derive atomic carbon mass and gas masses from CO, dust, CI**: CO luminosity with alpha_CO=0.8, dust masses with gas-to-dust ratio 42-130, CI with X_CI=3e-5 and Q10=0.6  
  > "We assume a value of X$_{\rm{CI}}$=3$\times10^{-5}$, based on a template of M82" (4.3.3)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=0.8 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1; r31=1.0 where undetermined  
  > "Assuming r$_{31}$=1.0 when L$^{\prime}_{\rm{CO(3-2)}}>$L$^{\prime}_{\rm{CO(1-0)}}$ or there is no constraint on L$^{\prime}_{\rm{CO(1-0)}}$" (Table 6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- ISM of the quasars is high-density (~1e5-1e6 cm^-3) gas  
  > "In general we find high gas densities of $\sim$10$^5$-10$^6$ cm$^{-3}$ in both sources." (4.2.3)
- ULASJ2315 is a close-separation major merger with different line properties in QSO and GAL  
  > "One of our quasar hosts is shown to be a close-separation ($<$2$\ensuremath{''}$) major merger" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: CI has lower critical density and was argued to trace total gas better than CO  
  > "CI has been argued to be a better tracer of the total molecular gas mass compared to CO" (Introduction)
- Alternative: Fixed alpha=0.75 or 0.1 for synchrotron slope; J2315 steep alpha=0.75 inconsistent with FIRST limit  
  > "the steep $\alpha$=0.75 SED is highly inconsistent with the FIRST upper limit" (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): J2315 SED and line ratios use blended flux from unresolved merger components  
  > "we again caution against over-interpretation of these numbers given the lack of constraints at the peak of the dust SED" (4.1)
- Limitation (authors): 3D-PDR lacks X-ray and mechanical heating  
  > "a caveat of our current modelling is that the 3D-PDR code does not include X-ray and mechanical heating" (4.2.3)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed dust temperature and beta drive SFR and dust masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Herschel flux split equally among three blended galaxies
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Modest S/N of CO(1-0) and low resolution make r31>1 uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Assumed X_CI, Q10 and alpha_CO drive gas mass discrepancies
