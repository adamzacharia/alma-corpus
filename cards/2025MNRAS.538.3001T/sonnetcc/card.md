## Analysis recipe

1. **Split line and continuum visibilities**: Split calibrated MS into emission-line and continuum windows; estimate line FWHM from a spectral profile; stack remaining spectrum to measure continuum  
  > "our initial step involves splitting the visibility into emission line and continuum windows through the `split` task." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA split, casaviewer
   - parameter: FWHM estimated with spectral profile tool in casaviewer  
  > "we extract the source spectrum and estimate the FWHM of the line using the spectral profile tool within `casaviewer`." (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: Continuum from spectra excluding +/-5xFWHM of the line, found negligible  
  > "Under our observation setups, the continuum of all of our five sources turn out to be negligible." (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make integrated CO(2-1) (M0) maps**: Split a window of width 2xFWHM centred on the line and run tclean in mfs mode with Multiscale deconvolver and natural weighting; make two M0 versions (one centred on each source) in double detections  
  > "We further split the emission line visibility using a window centered on the line with a width of $2 \times \mathrm{FWHM}$." (Observations and data reduction); "For the cases where we have double detections, we always make two versions of the M0 map, with each centered on one source." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 2 (natural), chosen to maximize sensitivity  
  > "we set the robustness parameter (R) for Briggs weighting (cite: briggs1995high) to 2 (i.e., natural weighting) to maximize sensitivity" (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: Multiscale deconvolver  
  > "Within `tclean`, the `Multiscale` deconvolver (cite: cornwell2008multiscale) is used to capture potential extended structures within the data." (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: mfs for the line follows Akins et al. 2022; map in Jy/beam  
  > "We extend the usage of `mfs` mode for the emission line following the method in (cite: akins2022alma)." (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Peak fluxes with R=0.5 differ from R=2 by <~15%  
  > "The peak fluxes of the sources with R=0.5 weighting differ from R=2 by $\lesssim$ 15%." (Observations and data reduction)
3. **Measure line flux, position and size on the M0 map**: Pre-estimate center, size and peak with casaviewer 2D fit, then fit with imfit to get the integrated line flux in Jy; physical sizes from imfit; non-detections assigned 3 sigma_bkg upper limits in one beam, assumed unresolved  
  > "On the M0 map, we use the interactive 2D fitting tool within casaviewer to pre-estimate the spatial center, size, and peak flux of the sources." (Observations and data reduction); "The physical sizes of the identified sources are determined using `imfit` and reported in column (6)." (CO measurements)
   - software: CASA imfit, casaviewer
   - parameter: Upper limit = 3 sigma_bkg in one beam; sigma_bkg is the std of the residual map  
  > "For the two non-detections, we assume them to be unresolved, and assign upper limits of $S_{\text{CO(2-1)}}$ to be $3\sigma_{\text{bkg}}$ in one beam" (CO measurements)
   - assumption: Non-detections are unresolved  
  > "For the two non-detections, we assume them to be unresolved, and assign upper limits of $S_{\text{CO(2-1)}}$ to be $3\sigma_{\text{bkg}}$ in one beam" (CO measurements)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fits via imfit (double Gaussian for J2209S)  
  > "A 2D double Gaussian model fitting in `imfit` estimated" (SDSS J2209+0045)
   - validation: Residual maps inspected (Figure panels)  
  > "Residual map by subtracting the model from the data (panel f)." (Details of individual pairs)
4. **Make cubes, moment maps and 1D spectra; fit line profiles**: tclean cube with 10 km/s channels; M1/M2 via immoments over about 2xFWHM keeping pixels >~3 sigma; spectra from elliptical aperture, noise spectrum as median of four offset apertures; fit single or double-peaked Gaussian and keep the better; derive W50 and S/N  
  > "we generate the moment 1 (M1) and moment 2 (M2) maps through `immoments`." (Observations and data reduction); "The final noise spectrum is the median of the four." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA tclean, CASA immoments, lmfit
   - parameter: Channel width 10 km/s  
  > "In handling the cube data, we uniformly set the channel width to 10 km/s across all sources." (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: Noise apertures offset 40-50 pixels up, down, left, right of the source  
  > "In practice, we put the aperture 40-50 pixels offset to upper, lower, left, and right of the source to extract four noise spectra." (Observations and data reduction)
   - parameter: S/N = model peak over noise std within +/-2xW50  
  > "Then the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) is defined as the peak of the model divided by the standard deviation of the noise spectrum within $\pm2\times W_{50}$ of the emission line." (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: lmfit with Levenberg-Marquardt (leastsq); single Gaussian vs symmetric Gaussian double-peak (Tiley et al. 2016 form); fit in frequency then convert W50 to velocity  
  > "The fitting process utilized the Python package `lmfit`, employing the Levenberg-Marquardt (leastsq) algorithm for optimization." (Line width measurements); "We first perform the above fitting in frequency space to get the precise position of the CO line, then convert the width $W_{50}$ to velocity" (Line width measurements)
   - validation: Model chosen by AIC, BIC and reduced chi-square (lower on at least two of three)  
  > "The preferred model was determined based on having lower values in at least two of these three criteria." (Line width measurements)
5. **Derive CO luminosity and molecular gas mass**: L'CO(2-1) from integrated flux with the Solomon et al. formula using Delta v = 2xFWHM; convert to CO(1-0) with R21 and to H2 mass with alpha_CO  
  > "Utilizing this line flux, we then calculate the line luminosity according to (cite: solomon1992warm)" (Observations and data reduction); "Based on our chosen values of $R_{21}$ and $\alpha_{\mathrm{CO}}$, we calculated $M_{\text{molgas}}$ as listed in Table" (Gas masses and fractions)
   - parameter: R21 = 0.62, alpha_CO = 3.1 Msun/(K km/s pc^2)  
  > "based on assumptions of $R_{21}=0.62$ and $\alpha_{\rm CO}=3.1$" (Conclusions)
   - parameter: Delta v = 2 x FWHM of the emission line (the tclean mfs width)  
  > "$\Delta v$ is $2 \times \mathrm{FWHM}$ of the emission line" (Observations and data reduction)
   - assumption: Flat LCDM, Omega_Lambda=0.7, Omega_m=0.3, H0=70 km/s/Mpc  
  > "Throughout this work, we adopt a $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with parameters" (Introduction)
   - assumption: R21 range seen in PG quasars 0.49-0.90 (0.99 from Carilli & Walter)  
  > "the highest $R_{21}$ value is 0.90, and the lowest is 0.49" (Gas masses and fractions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: FWHM from casa vs W50 differ by <10% for all sources  
  > "The differences between the two values are less than 10% for all of our samples." (Details of individual pairs)
6. **Derive stellar masses from host photometry**: GALIGHT 2D decomposition of HSC images gives host magnitudes (point sources removed) in five bands; CIGALE SED fit gives M*; merged hosts (J0847, J1416) get one M* for both  
  > "The stellar mass ($M_*$) of our dual quasars is measured from SED fitting based on the host magnitudes after removing the point sources from the images" (Gas masses and fractions); "Therefore, we only have one measurement of $M_*$ for both." (Gas masses and fractions)
   - software: GALIGHT, CIGALE
   - parameter: Delayed SFH, stellar population, dust attenuation, dust re-emission and nebular emission modules  
  > "The model encompasses a delayed star formation history (SFH) and stellar population (cite: bruzual2003stellar; maraston2005evolutionary), dust attenuation (cite: calzetti2000dust; charlot2000simple), re-emission (cite: dale2014two), and nebular emission (cite: inoue2011rest)" (Optical measurements)
   - fitting: CIGALE SED fitting (priors and grids not stated)  
  > "we conduct spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting using the Code Investigating GALaxy Emission (cite: boquien2019cigale) tool." (Optical measurements)
   - validation: Relative residual panel shown for example SED  
  > "The lower sub-panel displays the relative residual, defined as $\text{(data-model)}/\text{data}$." (Optical measurements)
7. **Compare gas fractions with the star-forming main sequence and compute SB/SFMS/QS probabilities**: Sum M_molgas and M* per pair; evaluate Tacconi et al. 2020 scaling relation at each redshift (deltaMS=0, +/-1, logM*=10.7); combine measurement and systematic uncertainties using split normal PDFs; integrate probabilities in three regions  
  > "For each dual quasar, we calculate the total $M_{\text{molgas}}$ and $M_*$ by summing the values of the two companions." (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?); "we estimate the probabilities of $\mu_{\text{molgas}}^{\text{dual}}$ falling into these three regions." (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
   - parameter: A=0.06, B=-3.33, F=0.65, C=0.51, D=-0.41  
  > "We apply their best-fit results $A=0.06$, $B=-3.33$, $F=0.65$, $C=0.51$, $D=-0.41$ to the function." (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
   - parameter: Systematics R21 = 0.62 +0.28/-0.13 and alpha_CO = 3.1 +1.2/-2.3 (converted to 1 sigma by halving the 95% bounds)  
  > "We assume the systematic uncertainties to be $R_{21} = 0.62^{+0.28}_{-0.13}$ and $\alpha_{\text{CO}} = 3.1^{+1.2}_{-2.3}$" (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
   - assumption: PDF follows a split normal distribution  
  > "Furthermore, we assume the probability density function (PDF) follows a split normal distribution." (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare CO luminosities of dual and single quasars**: Logrank (Mantel-Cox) test with upper limits against the Molina et al. 2023 PG sample  
  > "Accounting for the upper limits as well, the test yielded a p-value of 0.50" (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
   - software: lifelines
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **Test a rotating-disk model for J1214+0102N**: 3DBarolo ring-model fit to the cube with J1214S masked out; then dynamical mass from VROT and imfit major axis, and residual gas mass from Mdyn - M*  
  > "we perform a kinematic analysis of this feature using the tool named 3D-Based Analysis of Rotating Objects via Line Observations (cite: teodoro20153d)." (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)
   - software: 3DBarolo, DUCHAMP
   - parameter: SNRCUT=7, GROWTHCUT=4, MINCHANNELS=4  
  > "We raise the `SNRCUT` of detection to 7, `GROWTHCUT` to 4, and `MINCHANNELS` to four" (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)

## Software

- **CASA** 6.4.1: Data reduction, imaging and analysis with delivered MS files  
  > "Our data reduction process uses CASA version 6.4.1 (cite: bean2022casa) with the delivered Measurement Set (MS) files." (Observations and data reduction)
- **split (CASA)** : Separating line and continuum windows  
  > "our initial step involves splitting the visibility into emission line and continuum windows through the `split` task." (Observations and data reduction)
- **casaviewer** : Spectral profile (FWHM estimate) and interactive 2D pre-fit  
  > "we extract the source spectrum and estimate the FWHM of the line using the spectral profile tool within `casaviewer`." (Observations and data reduction)
- **tclean** : Line M0 map (mfs) and cubes  
  > "We then use the `tclean` task to generate a dedicated emission-line image." (Observations and data reduction)
- **imfit** : Integrated line flux and source sizes  
  > "these pre-estimated values serve as initial parameters for the subsequent `imfit` task, facilitating the estimation of integrated line flux measured in Jy." (Observations and data reduction)
- **immoments** : Moment 1 and moment 2 maps  
  > "we generate the moment 1 (M1) and moment 2 (M2) maps through `immoments`." (Observations and data reduction)
- **lmfit** : Single and double-peaked Gaussian fits to the CO spectra  
  > "with the python package `lmfit` and accept the better one" (Observations and data reduction)
- **lifelines** : Logrank test of L'CO between dual and single quasars  
  > "using the `lifelines` Python package" (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
- **GALIGHT** : 2D image modelling for candidate selection and host photometry  
  > "Using the 2D image modeling tool GALIGHT (cite: ding2022galight), we selected quasars accompanied by another nearby point source" (Sample selection)
- **CIGALE** : SED fitting for stellar masses  
  > "we conduct spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting using the Code Investigating GALaxy Emission (cite: boquien2019cigale) tool." (Optical measurements)
- **PyQSOFit** : Optical spectral fits for BH masses, redshifts and L_bol  
  > "BH properties are estimated using PyQSOFit (cite: guo2018pyqsofit)." (Optical measurements)
- **3DBarolo** : Rotating-disk kinematic model of J1214+0102N  
  > "we perform a kinematic analysis of this feature using the tool named 3D-Based Analysis of Rotating Objects via Line Observations (cite: teodoro20153d)." (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)
- **DUCHAMP** : Source mask in 3DBarolo SEARCH task  
  > "is based on DUCHAMP (cite: whiting2012duchamp), which produces a 3D source mask." (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)

## Qualitative findings

- Dual quasars show molecular gas fractions similar to or slightly above main-sequence star-forming galaxies; no significant gas depletion for the combined sample  
  > "Thus, at this merger phase, our dual quasars do not present significant evidence of cold molecular gas depletion when viewed as a combined sample." (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
- CO detections are biased to one companion in J1416, J2209 and J2337, with no preference for brighter, more massive or more actively accreting SMBHs  
  > "In SDSS J1416+0033, J2209+0045, and J2337+0056, the CO detections are strongly biased towards one of the companions." (Insights on galaxy evolution)
- J1214+0102N is the only source with a distinct velocity gradient, consistent with a merger or a rotating disk  
  > "Overall, both the merger and the rotation disk scenarios are consistent with our current data." (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)
- J0847S CO gas blob is offset 8.2 kpc from the optical center with no optical counterpart  
  > "There is no optical counterpart in the HSC image to this offset gas blob" (SDSS J0847-0013)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: No adequate prior molecular gas observations of dual quasars in this redshift range  
  > "In the literature, there are no adequate observations of molecular gas in samples of dual quasars at this redshift range." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting chosen because the aim is total molecular gas  
  > "(i.e., natural weighting) to maximize sensitivity, because the main aim of this work is to estimate the total amount of molecular gas." (Observations and data reduction)
- Alternative: R=0.5 weighting tested; peak fluxes differ from natural weighting by <~15%  
  > "The peak fluxes of the sources with R=0.5 weighting differ from R=2 by $\lesssim$ 15%." (Observations and data reduction)
- Alternative: J1214N: AGN-driven outflow disfavored because outflows give asymmetric line profiles while the two CO peaks are nearly identical  
  > "However, such outflows typically result in asymmetrical line profiles due to the likely asymmetric geometry of the outflow bicone (cite: nevin2018origin)." (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)
- Alternative: J0847S offset gas: ram-pressure stripping/infall versus ejection by multi-body interaction or AGN feedback  
  > "We consider two scenarios for this extended and offset emission: (1) stripped by ram pressure (or in-falling) and (2) an ejected gas blob by multi-body interaction or AGN feedback." (Insights on galaxy evolution)
- How they decided it was enough: Single vs double-peaked Gaussian chosen by lower AIC, BIC and reduced chi-square on at least two of three  
  > "The preferred model was determined based on having lower values in at least two of these three criteria." (Line width measurements)
- Limitation (authors): Limited sample statistics  
  > "Nevertheless, the samples in this work has limited statistics, and more follow-up observations on dual quasars with various separations will be favored to test the depletion scenario." (Do dual quasars show signs of gas depletion?)
- Limitation (authors): Detection limit for M_molgas is somewhat above 1e9 Msun  
  > "Nonetheless, it is crucial to reiterate that our detection limit for $M_{\text{molgas}}$ is somewhat above $10^9~\mathrm{M_{\odot}}$" (Insights on galaxy evolution)
- Limitation (authors): Size measurements of merging galaxies may be affected by tidal structures  
  > "Additionally, we should note that size measurements of merging galaxies could be affected by their tidal structures." (Insights on galaxy evolution)
- Limitation (authors): 3DBarolo needs careful tuning for low S/N, low-resolution data  
  > "However, careful tuning is required to produce a successful fit, especially for low S/N and low-resolution data." (J1214+0102N: Merger or disk?)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas masses rely on a fixed R21=0.62 and alpha_CO=3.1 taken from low-z PG quasars; AGN/merger systems may differ, and the systematic range spans a factor of several
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Most sources are only marginally resolved, so sizes, M1/M2 maps and the disk-vs-merger test for J1214N are weakly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar masses from five-band SED fitting with AGN point-source subtraction carry large systematics that feed directly into mu_molgas
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison samples use different instruments, alpha_CO and R21 assumptions and some assumed total masses (1e11 Msun); logrank test mixes upper limits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Table W50 for J1416W (273) disagrees with Section 4 text (237); text upper limit M<10^9.4 vs table 9.43; conclusions quote <5% vs table <3.1%
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The tclean mfs window (2xFWHM from casaviewer) is set by hand per source, so fluxes depend on manual choices
