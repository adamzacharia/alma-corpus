## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, combine and image data**: Calibrate and concatenate in CASA; image, clean and continuum-subtract in GILDAS  
  > "The data from Cycle 3 and 4 were calibrated and concatenated with the CASA software" (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - parameter: Hogbom CLEAN, natural weighting, 10.16 km/s channels, TM1 excluded for cubes  
  > "The data cubes were produced with a resolution of 10.16 km/s (11 MHz)." (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Estimate missing flux and H2 mass**: Compare ALMA flux with SEST-derived expected CO(3-2) flux; compute L'CO and M(H2)  
  > "we should expect some missing flux by a factor up to $\sim$40-50%" (3.3 CO luminosity and H2 mass)
   - parameter: r32=0.66, r21=1.12, r13=1 or 1.35  
  > "(r$_{\rm 13}$=1). If instead we use r$_{\rm 13}$=1.35" (3.3 CO luminosity and H2 mass)
   - assumption: Milky Way alpha_CO=4.36  
  > "$\alpha_{\rm CO}$=4.36 M$_{\rm \odot}(\rm K\,km\,s^{-1}\,pc^2)^{-1}$ (cite: bol13)" (3.3 CO luminosity and H2 mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared to single-dish masses  
  > "Our rather smaller value derived with the ALMA high resolution observations is due to lack of single-dish observations" (3.3 CO luminosity and H2 mass)
3. **Fit rotation and non-circular motions**: 2D tilted-ring fit of velocity field with Bertola rotation curve; 3DBarolo 3D tilted ring with radial velocities  
  > "We used the tilted-ring model (cite: rog74), which consists of dividing the velocity field in concentric rings" (3.4 CO(3-2) kinematics)
   - software: 3DBarolo
   - parameter: delta r=0.3''; PA=314 deg and i=57 deg fixed in final 3DBarolo run; center on nuclear spiral  
  > "we fixed the PA=314$\rm ^\circ$ and i=57$\rm^\circ$ to the mean values between them" (3.4 CO(3-2) kinematics)
   - assumption: circular orbits in a plane, 1<=p<=3/2  
  > "which considers that the gas is on circular orbits in a plane" (3.4 CO(3-2) kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement between 2D and 3D PA and inclination; residuals ±30 km/s  
  > "with small amplitude values in the residuals ($\pm$30 km/s)" (3.4 CO(3-2) kinematics)
4. **Dense-gas line ratios**: Measure HCN/HCO+ and HCN/CS intensity ratios in nucleus and SF clump (3 and 5 sigma per channel) and place on submm-HCN diagram  
  > "We measured the line intensity ratios R$_{\rm HCN/HCO^{\rm +}}$ and R$_{\rm HCN/CS}$ in the two regions" (3.5 Dense gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with Izumi et al. 2016 sample  
  > "The ratios in the nucleus of NGC 1808 are compatible with those found for AGN" (3.5 Dense gas)
5. **Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation**: Compute SFR from Pa-alpha and molecular gas surface density from CO(3-2) in apertures  
  > "we compute the surface densities of star formation rate (SFR) and molecular gas to build a resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt (KS) diagram" (4.2 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation)
   - parameter: aperture radius 0.75'' = 34 pc  
  > "where the radius of 0.75$''$ = 34 pc" (4.2 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=4.36, r31=0.27, 45% missing flux correction, inclination 57 deg, Calzetti extinction  
  > "The molecular masses were corrected from the 45% missing flux, as discussed in Section [3.3](#miss)" (4.2 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with Bigiel2008 and Casasola2015  
  > "All our points lie in the KS relation" (4.2 Resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation)
6. **Rotation curve mass model and resonances**: Fit mass components (Plummer bulge/dark matter, Toomre disk, BH) to HI and Halpha rotation curve to derive Omega, kappa and Lindblad resonances  
  > "We use Plummer spheres for spherical components, bulge and dark matter, and Toomre disks for the 2D ones." (4.4 Lindblad resonances)
   - parameter: corotation at ~1.2 bar radius  
  > "We have placed the corotation at $\sim$1.2 bar radius" (4.4 Lindblad resonances)
   - assumption: parameters not unique  
  > "the parameters are not unique and just a realistic representation of the potential" (4.4 Lindblad resonances)
   - fitting: Dark matter as free parameter to fit V(R)  
  > "The dark matter component is the free parameter to better fit the rotation curve V(R)" (4.4 Lindblad resonances)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Gravity torques and inflow timescale**: De-project NIR image, FFT potential, compute torques weighted by CO gas density, azimuthally average  
  > "The gravitational potential is derived using a fast Fourier transform of the stellar mass distribution from the de-projected NIR image." (4.5 Torque computation)
   - parameter: h_z/h_r=1/12; M/L=0.95; pixel 0.085'' = 3.8 pc  
  > "The final calibration was obtain using a constant mass-to-light ratio of M/L=0.95" (4.5 Torque computation)
   - assumption: Plummer bulge of 1'' subtracted, dark matter neglected within 1 kpc  
  > "We neglected the contribution of dark matter within a 1 kpc radius" (4.5 Torque computation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: M/L chosen to retrieve observed CO rotation curve  
  > "which is necessary to retrieve the observed CO rotation curve" (4.5 Torque computation)

## Qualitative findings

- CO(3-2) and dense gas tracers show a two-arm trailing nuclear spiral in the central ~50 pc inside the ILR.  
  > "This nuclear spiral is trailing, which is the configuration already encountered in two similar barred Seyferts, NGC 1566 and NGC 613" (Conclusion)
- A circumnuclear disk of radius ~6 pc is kinematically decoupled and shows AGN-like HCN/HCO+ ratios.  
  > "This molecular disk or torus has HCN and HCO+ line ratios compatible with an excitation from the AGN." (Conclusion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High resolution needed at ~10 pc to disentangle AGN feeding and feedback  
  > "Thus, to disentangle the AGN origin of the feedback, a high spatial resolution is also required" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: A decoupled secondary nuclear bar was rejected in favour of a nuclear bar continuing the primary bar.  
  > "We favor the first possibility, since the orbital precessing rate $\Omega-\kappa/2$ shown in Fig. [14](#Omegabar) is rather flat in the central kpc." (4.4 Lindblad resonances)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Missing flux ~40-50% due to lack of single-dish data.  
  > "Our rather smaller value derived with the ALMA high resolution observations is due to lack of single-dish observations to detect weak extended emission" (3.3 CO luminosity and H2 mass)
- Limitation (authors): Mass model parameters are degenerate.  
  > "Given the freedom and the degeneracy to distribute all mass components" (4.4 Lindblad resonances)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Missing-flux correction of 45% applied to KS masses is uncertain and uniform
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Torque results depend on M/L and thin disk assumptions, and on NIR image stitched from two sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inflow timescale rests on a non-unique potential and quasi-circular orbit assumption
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Kinematic fits lack stated uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dense-gas ratios based on a few regions with no stated ratio-to-AGN threshold
