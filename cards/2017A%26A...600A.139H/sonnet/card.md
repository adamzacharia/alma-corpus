## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA CO and continuum data**: CLEAN in CASA with boxes, smooth to 10 km/s (Band 7), primary beam correction  
  > "We image the data using the CLEAN algorithm in CASA, by defining cleaning boxes enclosing the gas emission at each channel." (Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure cluster and GMC sizes**: 2-D Gaussian fits to Br gamma, K-band and 345 GHz continuum after subtracting a scaled Gaussian for the water maser (factor 0.5)  
  > "This multiplicative factor of 0.5 is found to minimise the dispersion of the residuals after subtracting the water maser contribution" (4.2 Spatial extent)
   - fitting: 2-D Gaussian  
  > "The size of the molecular cloud is measured by fitting a 2-D Gaussian function to the continuum emission." (Mass of the GMC / Spatial extent of the ionized and molecular gas associated with SSC B1)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure CO line fluxes of low and high velocity components**: Aperture spectra (2.9'', 4.2'', 4.7'') fitted with 2 Gaussians  
  > "Line parameters were estimated by fitting 2 Gaussian curves, but only those of the low velocity component are listed" (4.3 Intensities)
   - fitting: 2 Gaussian  
  > "Line parameters were estimated by fitting 2 Gaussian curves, but only those of the low velocity component are listed" (Intensities of the ionized and molecular gas associated with SSC B1)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive GMC and molecular gas masses**: Grey-body dust continuum mass; CO-based mass with starburst X_CO; virial mass  
  > "We assume a typical dust temperature of 25 K and the emissivity per hydrogen measured in the Taurus molecular cloud" (4.4 Mass of the GMC)
   - assumption: Td = 25 K; Galactic dust properties; X_CO = 0.6e20; CO(3-2)/CO(1-0) ~0.6  
  > "We assume that the dust properties in the Antennae overlap region are the same as those in the Galaxy." (4.4 Mass of the GMC)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement between CO, continuum and virial masses  
  > "The estimated $M_{\rm mol}$ value agrees with the value estimated from the continuum emission and the virial theorem" (4.4 Mass of the GMC)
5. **Compare radiation, hot-gas and gravitational pressures**: Analytical P_rad, P_hot from X-ray, P_grav; PDR Meudon models of H2 2-1/1-0 S(1) ratio  
  > "compare our grid of $P_{\rm rad}$ and radiation field values with the outputs of the PDR Meudon code" (5.1 Gas pressure in molecular gas)
   - software: PDR Meudon code, Starburst99
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Test momentum-driven outflow and SFE**: Momentum balance L/c(1+tau) t = M_high v_exp; outflow rate; SFE within 100 pc  
  > "To test the hypothesis that this gas was accelerated by radiation pressure over a time scale of $t_{\rm feedback}$, we write," (6.2 Outflowing gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CO has a low velocity component at the cluster and a high velocity bubble-like component around it  
  > "The cluster has two CO velocity components, a low velocity one spatially associated with the cluster and a high velocity one distributed in a bubble-like shape around the cluster." (abstract)
