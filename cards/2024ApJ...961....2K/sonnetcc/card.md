## Analysis recipe

1. **Prepare 1 mm / 6 GHz continuum images and radio photometry**: JVLA data calibrated with the VLA pipeline, imaged in CASA with natural weighting and CLEAN to 2 sigma; 6 GHz photometry with BLOBCAT; ALMA 1.1 mm continuum images from the Cycle 5 program used for lens constraints  
  > "Each object was first imaged using natural weighting (maximal sensitivity at the expense of slightly degraded resolution)." ## Qualitative findings

- Magnifications are modest (more than half have mu < 10) and consistent with Herschel/SPT lensed DSFGs  
  > "with more than half at lower magnification, $\mu < 10$." (Conclusions); "The distribution of magnifications for (sub-)mm continuum in this work is largely consistent with those from similar lensed surveys defined from *Herschel* and SPT" (PASSAGES sources have modest magnifications)
- No clear redshift evolution of size over z = 1-3.5  
  > "do not observe a clear indication of redshift evolution for the PASSAGES sample alone, covering only $z=1-3.5$" (Redshift evolution of galaxy size)
- No sign of significant AGN contribution from the radio-FIR correlation (none below q_FIR 1.8)  
  > "Unsurprisingly, none of the star-formation-dominated PASSAGES galaxies fall below the $q_{\rm FIR} < 1.8$ threshold for AGN-powered radio sources proposed by (cite: Condon:2002aa)." (Radio-FIR correlation and intrinsic radio source size)
- Evidence for anti-correlation between magnification and effective radius at R_e above about 0.2 arcsec  
  > "Objects with sizes larger than $0.3^{\prime\prime}$ all have lower magnifications, $\mu \leq 10$, whereas smaller objects exhibit a large range of magnifications." (Possible source size bias?)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Subset of 15 chosen for simpler galaxy- or group-scale lensing and sufficient multi-wavelength data; high-resolution ALMA/JVLA/HST combined to constrain models  
  > "we select a subset of 15 members of the larger current sample, restricting our focus to primarily those objects with both a simpler lensing morphology" (Sample selection)
- Why this method: Image-plane parametric modelling suits merging optical and radio interferometric data and is computationally cheaper than uv-plane modelling  
  > "This has the additional benefit of improved computational efficiency." (Visual identification of multiple image families)
- Alternative: uv-plane (visibility) lens modelling and regularised semi-linear inversion not used; left for future work  
  > "Future work to apply a visibility-based modeling approach to these objects will allow for a more direct comparison." (Comparison with existing lens models)
- Alternative: For PJ105353 the 0.07 arcsec Band 6 image was not used for magnification; the Band 7 image was adopted for similar resolution to other targets  
  > "we opt to use the Band 7 image for our analysis in this work" (ALMA 1.1 mm continuum)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Statistical magnification uncertainties may understate larger systematic uncertainties (mis-identified images, mass parameterisation)  
  > "we caution that these statistical values can underestimate larger systematic" (Total magnification factors)
- Limitation (authors): Size measurement does not deconvolve the distorted source-plane beam and is best done in the visibility plane  
  > "As a caveat, this measurement is perhaps best made in the visibility-plane in order to deconvolve the effect of the synthesized beam." (Source-plane size)
- Limitation (authors): No stellar masses, so main-sequence position cannot be established  
  > "the dearth of stellar mass estimates makes it impossible to reliably place these other properties in context." (Intrinsic IR luminosities of highly-magnified DSFGs and their relation to the main sequence)
- Limitation (authors): Current ALMA resolution only marginally resolves the Sigma_SFR distribution, so peak values are close to averages  
  > "the current ALMA data are only marginally able to resolve this distribution." (Eddington-limited star-formation surface densities?)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Magnification and size depend on a model with few constraints per galaxy-scale lens; several fits have e near 0.65-0.77 and chi-square above 3 (PJ144653, PJ231356, PJ022633)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No lens-model priors, MCMC settings or run files released; ALMA calibration and imaging are only referenced to Berman et al. 2022
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single 1 mm magnification is used as a proxy for total L_IR magnification, ignoring differential magnification across the SED
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sizes from unconvolved source-plane Gaussians at roughly beam-sized scales; Eddington conclusion depends on this and on single-temperature SFRs
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Delta MS conclusions rest on assumed fiducial stellar masses
