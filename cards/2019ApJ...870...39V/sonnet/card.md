## Analysis recipe

1. **Recalibrate and combine two configurations, image**: Recalibrate in CASA 4.5.1, uvcontsub on lines before concat, clean with Briggs weighting; cubes at 5,10,20,40 km/s  
  > "data cubes were generated with multiple final velocity resolutions (i.e., 5, 10, 20 and 40 km s$^{-1}$, respectively)" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "Briggs weighting using a robust parameter of 0.5" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Continuum fluxes and spectral index**: Compare 3 mm with 1 mm archival continuum on images smoothed to CO(1-0) resolution; same regions  
  > "The 1mm data of the Prandoni et al. project in this Table were measured on smoothed images that matched the angular resolution of our CO(1-0) data." (Table 2)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Molecular gas mass**: Integrated CO(1-0) flux converted with standard X_CO; times 1.36 for helium  
  > "The total integrated CO(1-0) flux is 4.5$\pm$0.9 Jy km s$^{-1}$" (6 Molecular Gas Properties)
   - assumption: X_CO≈2.8e20 cm^-2 (K km/s)^-1; distance 40 Mpc; helium factor 1.36  
  > "i.e., X$_{CO}\approx$2.8$\times$10$^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$" (Footnote 5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked against APEX archive data and ALMA CO(2-1) flux  
  > "the ALMA CO(2-1) data gives an integrated flux of 7.5$\pm$0.3 Jy km s$^{-1}$" (6 Molecular Gas Properties)
4. **Rotation curve fit and non-circular motions**: 3DBAROLO tilted-ring fits to CO(1-0) and CO(2-1); model first-moment subtracted from observed in CASA  
  > "We have analyzed the kinematics of our CO(1-0) data and the publicly available CO(2-1) data using the $^{3D}$BAROLO code (cite: teo15)." (7 Molecular Gas Kinematics)
   - software: 3DBarolo, CASA
   - parameter: Data binned to 40 km/s; initial dispersion tested 10-40 km/s  
  > "The data used for the fits was binned to 40 km s$^{-1}$" (Footnote 8)
   - fitting: Tilted-ring, two-stage; code gives no error estimates  
  > "the code does not provide error estimates" (7 Molecular Gas Kinematics)
   - validation: Consistency between CO(1-0) and CO(2-1) fits within 10%  
  > "These values are all within 10$\%$ of the values derived for the CO(2-1) data" (7 Molecular Gas Kinematics)
5. **Disk stability and SFE**: Ring-averaged MOM0 converted to surface density, compared to critical density from kappa and sigma  
  > "the surface density stays clearly *below* the critical density for all radial distances" (8 Disk Stability)
   - assumption: Gas in an inclined disk with inclination from 3DBAROLO  
  > "Assuming that the molecular gas in NGC 3557 is mostly distributed in an inclined disk" (8 Disk Stability)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) ratio map and excitation modeling**: Beam-matched moment-0 ratio with immath divided by 4; compared to LVG and PDR models  
  > "The CASA task *immath* was then used to create the ratio[^11] CO(2-1)/CO(1-0)/4" (9 Molecular Gas Excitation)
   - software: CASA, LVG code (own)
   - assumption: Galactic CO abundances  
  > "Assuming Galactic CO molecule abundances[^13]" (9 Molecular Gas Excitation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Error propagation with image rms and 5% flux calibration error  
  > "The errorbars were computed using noise RMS measurements in emission-free areas of the moment 0 images" (Footnote 12)

## Qualitative findings

- CO gas concentrated within ~250 pc, rotating along same axis as stars and dust; radio jet not perpendicular to dust ring  
  > "The radio jet does not appear to be oriented perpendicularly to the nuclear dust ring nor along the stellar rotation axis." (Conclusions)
