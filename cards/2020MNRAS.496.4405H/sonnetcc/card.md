## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure ALMA Band 7 continuum flux and position of each host**: Fit the continuum images with CASA imfit to get flux, central position and errors  
  > "We detected the rest-frame FIR continuum fluxes of six host galaxies using $\it imfit$ task of CASA version 5.4.0, which allowed us to estimate fluxes, central positions, and their errors." (3 Data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Input is the ASC-reduced data; fit region and component model not stated  
  > "In this work, we use the reduced data provided by the ALMA Science Center." (3 Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Detections have S/N = 11, ~16, ~5, ~12, ~4, ~3.5; three hosts are only marginal  
  > "However, the host galaxies of GRB070306, GRB071021 and GRB050915A are marginally detected with S/Ns of $\sim5,\sim4$ and $\sim3.5$, respectively." (3 Data)
2. **Assemble multi-wavelength SED photometry**: Combine literature UV-to-radio photometry with the new Band 7 point; 3 sigma upper limits are not used in the fit; radio points are dropped in the no-radio configuration  
  > "We collected UV-to-radio photometric data of six GRB host galaxies reported in previous studies." (3 Data); "upper limit, which is not taken into account in the SED fitting analysis." (Table 2); "NOT used in the model without radio emission in SED fitting analysis to avoid the possible contaminated flux from the long-lived afterglow." (Table 2)
   - parameter: Filter effective wavelengths from the SVO Filter Profile Service  
  > "Effective wavelength retrieved from" (Table 2)
   - assumption: Radio emission may be contaminated by a long-lived radio afterglow  
  > "The options to include and exclude radio data are due to a possible contamination from a long-lived radio afterglow in the radio flux of the host galaxy (cite: Perley2013b)." (6 Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit the SEDs with CIGALE under several configurations**: CIGALE fits with sfh2exp or sfhdelayed SFH, bc03 SSP, nebular, power-law dust attenuation, Dale2014 dust+AGN (Draine2014 + Fritz2006 for GRB050915A) and redshifting modules; two IMFs (Salpeter, Chabrier); Group A fitted with/without AGN and with/without radio (4 configs), Group B with/without AGN (2 configs)  
  > "we chose a single exponential star formation history (sfh2exp hereafter) and delayed SFH with optional exponential burst (sfhdelayed hereafter) to simulate star-formation history (SFH) model" (4 SED Modelling and results); "We used the module from (cite: Dale2014) for AGN and dust emission models except for GRB050915A because the reduced $\chi^2$ was too large for this host when this model was applied." (4 SED Modelling and results); "We classified our GRB host galaxies into two groups: Group A with radio detection (GRB080207, GRB060814 and GRB070306) and Group B without radio detection (GRB081221, GRB071021 and GRB050915A)." (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Module choices and parameter grids listed in Tables 8 and 9  
  > "The module we used and the parameters setting are shown in Tables" (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - assumption: Single IMF per fit, Salpeter or Chabrier, chosen by reduced chi2  
  > "We simply decide the better fit by comparing the reduced $\chi^2$ between these two different IMFs." (6 Discussion)
   - fitting: CIGALE model-grid comparison to observed photometry; sampler, priors beyond the parameter grids and likelihood details not stated  
  > "investigate the physical properties such as SFR, stellar mass, IR luminosity, and dust mass of galaxies by comparing the SEDs of modelled galaxies to observed galaxies." (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Select best template and IMF per host**: Best-fit template chosen by reduced chi2 (closest to 1); fits with reduced chi2 well below 1 treated as over-fits and their AGN component rejected  
  > "Based on the reduced $\chi^2$, we selected the best-fit galaxy template from the conditions mentioned in the previous paragraph." (4 SED Modelling and results); "Our results show that, if we consider the AGN component in our fitting process, the reduced $\chi^2$ becomes smaller, suggesting over-fitting." (6 Discussion)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Reduced chi2 of the adopted fits: 2.32, 1.00, 2.34, 0.94, 0.99, 0.97 (GRB080207, 060814, 070306, 081221, 071021, 050915A)  
  > "reduced $\chi^2$ of 2.32, 1.00, 2.34, 0.94, 0.99 and 0.97 for GRB080207, GRB060814, GRB070306, GRB081221, GRB071021 and GRB050915A host galaxies, respectively" (4 SED Modelling and results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: The authors caution that other templates can have similar reduced chi2 and that the IMF test is simple  
  > "in some cases there exist other templates with similar reduced $\chi^2$ values." (4 SED Modelling and results); "We caution readers that our testing scheme is very simple, and therefore, the IMF differences could be masked by the simplifications of the model." (6 Discussion)
5. **Cross-check SED SFRs with the Kennicutt IR-luminosity relation**: Convert the CIGALE dust luminosity to SFR with the Kennicutt (1998) relation and compare with SED SFRs  
  > "1.722\times10^{-10}L_{\rm IR}(L_{\odot})" (6 Discussion); "We defined the IR luminosity of our results as the luminosity emitted by dust, which is almost similar to $L_{IR}$ in most of the star-forming galaxies." (6 Discussion)
   - parameter: L_IR integrated 8 to 1000 um in the Kennicutt definition  
  > "The IR luminosity in (cite: Kennicutt1998b) is defined as the luminosity integrated from 8 to 1000 $\mu$m." (6 Discussion)
   - assumption: Hosts follow the Kennicutt law  
  > "Our new SFR measurement is more reasonable than that in previous studies if GRB hosts follows the Kennicutt’s law." (6 Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SED SFRs compared with Kennicutt SFRs for each host  
  > "is closer to the estimation using Kennicutt’s law (SFR$_{\rm Kennicutt}=69 M_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$) compared to previous studies." (6 Discussion)
6. **Compare with earlier SED fits after IMF conversion**: Convert earlier results to Chabrier IMF (Salpeter divided by 1.8 or 1.6 in the literature) and convert this work's Salpeter results with Madau & Dickinson factors, then tabulate differences  
  > "we converted the derived physical properties of those with the same IMF in previous studies by the factor from (cite: Madau2014)" (6 Discussion); "Divided by a factor of 1.8 from (cite: Salpeter1955) to (cite: Chabrier2003)" (Table 12)
   - parameter: 0.61 for stellar mass and 0.63 for SFR  
  > "Multiplied by a factor in (cite: Madau2014) (0.61 for stellar mass and 0.63 for SFR) from (cite: Salpeter1955) to (cite: Chabrier2003)." (Table 12)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- GRB080207 host is the only ULIRG in the sample; the other five hosts have IR luminosity between 1e11 and 1e12 Lsun and may be LIRGs  
  > "We found one ULIRG within our sample: GRB080207 host galaxy" (Conclusion); "suggesting that they may be luminous infra-red galaxies (LIRGs)." (Conclusion)
- Best IMF is Salpeter for GRB080207 and GRB081221 hosts and Chabrier for GRB060814, GRB070306, GRB071021 and GRB050915A hosts  
  > "The best IMFs for our GRB host galaxies are (cite: Salpeter1955) for GRB080207 and GRB081221 host galaxies, whereas (cite: Chabrier2003) for GRB060814, GRB070306, GRB071021 and GRB050915A host galaxies." (Conclusion)
- Four hosts are detected in the rest-frame FIR for the first time  
  > "Among them, four host galaxies are detected for the first time in the rest-frame FIR." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Rest-frame FIR is free of dust extinction and sensitive to cold-dust emission, so ALMA gives better SFRs than UV-to-optical or radio  
  > "Rest-frame FIR observations are also free from dust extinction." (1 Introduction); "which allows us to estimate the SFR more accurately than simply relying on UV to optical or radio data." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: SED fitting separates AGN and star-formation contributions  
  > "Therefore, it is important to separate the contributions from AGN and SF through the SED fitting." (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: AGN on/off and radio on/off configurations (Group A) and AGN on/off (Group B); AGN models rejected as over-fits when reduced chi2 fell well below 1 (e.g. GRB081221, GRB071021, GRB050915A)  
  > "We also used the four configurations of the model setting, with and without AGN component, and then with and without the radio data and radio emission for Group A." (4 SED Modelling and results); "Based on the reduced $\chi^2$, if we add the AGN component to this modelling, the resulting fit is also an over-fit." (6 Discussion)
- Alternative: Dale2014 dust+AGN module dropped for GRB050915A in favour of Draine2014 plus Fritz2006 because reduced chi2 was too large  
  > "We used the module from (cite: Dale2014) for AGN and dust emission models except for GRB050915A because the reduced $\chi^2$ was too large for this host when this model was applied." (4 SED Modelling and results)
- How they decided it was enough: Best template picked by reduced chi2 closest to 1; chi2 well below 1 treated as over-fitting  
  > "Based on the reduced $\chi^2$, we selected the best-fit galaxy template from the conditions mentioned in the previous paragraph." (4 SED Modelling and results)
- Limitation (authors): Best templates are not unique; other configurations have similar reduced chi2  
  > "in some cases there exist other templates with similar reduced $\chi^2$ values." (4 SED Modelling and results)
- Limitation (authors): IMF test is simple and may mask IMF differences  
  > "We caution readers that our testing scheme is very simple, and therefore, the IMF differences could be masked by the simplifications of the model." (6 Discussion)
- Limitation (authors): Three hosts are only marginally detected (S/N ~5, ~4, ~3.5)  
  > "However, the host galaxies of GRB070306, GRB071021 and GRB050915A are marginally detected with S/Ns of $\sim5,\sim4$ and $\sim3.5$, respectively." (3 Data)
- Limitation (authors): Reduced chi2 of 1 is not expected to be reached because errors are not perfectly estimated  
  > "However this is probably not the case in our analysis." (6 Discussion)
- Limitation (authors): GRB050915A has several host candidates; the closest one to the host position was chosen  
  > "we found several candidates for the host galaxy" (6 Discussion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Three hosts have S/N 3.5-5, so a single Band 7 point drives their dust SED; deriving SFR of 38-90 Msun/yr from one marginal sub-mm point is fragile
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only one ALMA band (plus Herschel for some) constrains the dust peak; dust temperature is not constrained and CIGALE dust grids are coarse
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Reduced chi2 selection is crude: fits with chi2 of 2.3 are adopted while chi2 below 1 are rejected as over-fits, and the number of free parameters vs data points is not given
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Reported sensitivity of 0.04/0.02 uJy is implausible (probably mJy) and image rms is never given
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar mass and SFR tables contain inconsistent numbers (e.g. GRB050915A SFR 77 vs 76; GRB081221 SFR 56 in Table 11 vs 35 in Table 13 after IMF conversion), complicating answer keys
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison with literature SFRs mixes different SED codes, IMF conversions and SFR averaging timescales
