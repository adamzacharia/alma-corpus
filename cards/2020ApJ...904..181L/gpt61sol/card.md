## Qualitative findings

- Lower-K CH3CN transitions are optically thick.  
  > "We find that the CH$_3$CN emission is optically thick in the lower K transitions." (Summary)
- The gas spins up toward the center and is best described as a flattened rotating infalling envelope.  
  > "the increasing velocity gradient signifies a spin-up rotation toward the center." (CH$_3$CN: kinematic signatures); "the CH$_3$CN emission is best described by a flattened rotating envelope with infalling motion" (Summary)
- There is no direct evidence of an inner rotationally supported disc.  
  > "there appears no direct evidence for the presence inside this "envelope" of a rotationally supported disc" (An observed non-Keplerian velocity profile)
