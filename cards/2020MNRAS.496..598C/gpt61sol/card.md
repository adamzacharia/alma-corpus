  > "The images of continuum emission in both bands have been obtained running the CASA task *tclean* in the multi-frequency synthesis (mfs) mode on the visually selected line-free channels of the observed spectral ranges." (ALMA Observations and Data Reduction); "The HST and the ALMA band 4 continuum images were aligned relative to the isolated image D." (Analysis of the mm Continuum Emission of HS 0810+2554.); "To estimate the flux density of the continuum we used the CASA tool 2D Fit with the fitting region set to a circle of radius of 2$^{\prime\prime}\!\!.$5 centered on the source." (Analysis of the mm Continuum Emission of HS 0810+2554.)
   - software: CASA, tclean, 2D Fit
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 2  
  > "To obtain the maximum sensitivity we used the Briggs weighting scheme with the robust parameter set to 2 (natural weighting)." (ALMA Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Continuum mean frequencies 143 GHz and 97 GHz  
  > "We find the flux densities of the continuum at mean frequencies of 143 GHz and 97 GHz" (Analysis of the mm Continuum Emission of HS 0810+2554.)
   - fitting: CASA 2D Fit within a source-centered 2.5″ radius circle  
  > "To estimate the flux density of the continuum we used the CASA tool 2D Fit with the fitting region set to a circle of radius of 2$^{\prime\prime}\!\!.$5 centered on the source." (Analysis of the mm Continuum Emission of HS 0810+2554.)
   - validation: Compare with CARMA upper limit  
  > "the observed ALMA flux densities are found to be just below the 3$\sigma$ upper limit of $\sim$1 mJy inferred from the non-detection of HS 0810$+$2554 by CARMA (cite: 2011ApJ...730..108R)." (Analysis of the mm Continuum Emission of HS 0810+2554.)
2. **Measure integrated CO spectra**: Subtract a visibility-plane linear continuum, form cubes and moment 0 images, extract circular-aperture spectra, and fit three Gaussians.  
  > "A linear fit of the continuum in the visually selected line-free channels of the spectral windows has been calculated in the visibility plane and subtracted from the full spectral range."    - software: CASA, uvcontsub, immoments, uvmodelfit
   - parameter: Extended CO(3–2): 137.714–137.980 GHz; extraction radius 0.7″  
  > "The image of the CO(J=3$\rightarrow$2) line emission was produced from the continuum subtracted data, in the frequency range 137.714 GHz to 137.980 GHz."    - parameter: Compact CO(3–2): 137.7–138.0 GHz; CO(2–1): 91.8–92.0 GHz; extraction radii 3″  
  > "in the frequency range 137.7 GHz to 138.0 GHz and CO(J=2$\rightarrow$1) line in the frequency range 91.8 GHz to 92.0 GHz." ## Qualitative findings

- CO(3–2) emission forms a partial Einstein ring  
  > "the line emission forms a partial Einstein ring in addition to emission centered around the 4 images A, B, C, and D." (The Einstein Ring of the CO(J=3→2) Emission is resolved with the ALMA extended configuration.)
- Source-plane molecular gas rotates, north approaching and south receding  
  > "A clear rotation is seen in the moment 1 map, with the north(south) portions of the CO(J=3$\rightarrow$2) emission moving towards(away from) us." (Lensing Analysis)
- Inverted mm continuum spectral index does not support pure synchrotron origin  
  > "the inverted spectral index of the mm-continuum emission does not support pure synchrotron emission as the origin of the elongated mm-continuum emission" (Lensing Analysis)
- Molecular momentum boost lies slightly below the energy-conserving prediction  
  > "The momentum boost of the molecular wind of HS 0810$+$2554 is slightly below the value predicted for an energy-conserving outflow given the momentum flux observed in the ultrafast outflow." (Discussion and Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Resolve host continuum and molecular gas and search for an outflow in a quasar with known ultrafast winds  
  > "We expect that the small scale ultrafast outflows in HS 0810$+$2554 may be driving a larger scale outflow of molecular gas in the host galaxy." (Introduction); "detect the molecular gas in CO(J=3$\rightarrow$2) and CO(J=2$\rightarrow$1), measure the total molecular CO mass and its extent, and search for a possible outflow." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting maximizes sensitivity; channel-by-channel lens inversion accounts for frequency-dependent CO morphology  
  > "To obtain the maximum sensitivity we used the Briggs weighting scheme with the robust parameter set to 2 (natural weighting)." (ALMA Observations and Data Reduction); "To account for the frequency dependence of the morphology of the CO(J=3$\rightarrow$2) emission and to illustrate the rotation of the CO(J=3$\rightarrow$2) region in the source plane we perform a lens inversion to each frequency channel" (Lensing Analysis)
- Alternative: Single Gaussian spectral models rejected in favor of three Gaussians  
  > "This fit is not acceptable in a statistical sense with ${\chi^2}/\nu = 189/16$" (The Einstein Ring of the CO(J=3→2) Emission is resolved with the ALMA extended configuration.); "This fit is not acceptable in a statistical sense with ${\chi^2}/\nu = 49/23$" (Analysis of the CO(J=3→2) and CO(J=2→1) emission lines obtained with the ALMA compact configuration.); "This fit is not acceptable in a statistical sense with ${\chi^2}/\nu = 39.5/15$" (Analysis of the CO(J=3→2) and CO(J=2→1) emission lines obtained with the ALMA compact configuration.)
- Alternative: Point-source mm continuum model failed to converge  
  > "The fit with a point source model did not converge indicating that the observed $\sim$ 2 mm continuum emission likely does not originate from a point-like source." (Lensing Analysis)
- Alternative: Jet contribution considered; pure synchrotron origin disfavored  
  > "A second possibility is that the mm-continuum emission contains a jet component leading to the highly elongated morphology" (Lensing Analysis); "the inverted spectral index of the mm-continuum emission does not support pure synchrotron emission as the origin of the elongated mm-continuum emission" (Lensing Analysis)
- Alternative: Infall interpretation for highly redshifted emission considered unlikely  
  > "it is unlikely that the redshifted emission is produced by the doppler shift of infalling gas from the near side of the CO gas." (Possible detection of highly blueshifted and redshifted clumps of CO(J=3→2) emission)
- Alternative: Obscuration proposed as a possible explanation of mm–UV source offset  
  > "One possible explanation for this offset is that a portion of the extended mm emission is obscured along our line of sight." (Lensing Analysis)
- How they decided it was enough: Three-Gaussian fits considered statistically acceptable; outflow detections and continuum morphology need higher-S/N data  
  > "The fit with three Gaussians results in an acceptable fit with ${\chi^2}/\nu$ = 11.4/10."
