## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce MeerKAT data to H I cubes and continuum**: CARACal: per-track cross-calibration, flagging, self-calibration, continuum subtraction; then joint deconvolution of both tracks and cube imaging at three resolutions  
  > "Here, we briefly summarise the three main stages of the process: cross-calibration, self-calibration and spectral line imaging." (2.1 MeerKAT L-Band observations)
   - software: CARACal, AOflagger, wsclean, cubical, crystalball
   - parameter: 5.5 km/s channels; robust 1.0 / 1.5 / 0.0 and uv-taper on the 96 arcsec cube  
  > "The final stage involved combining the continuum-subtracted measurement set to generate the data cubes, with a 5.5 $\,$km$\,$s$^{-1}$ channel width and at multiple resolutions." (2.1 MeerKAT L-Band observations); "Robust weighting | 1.0 | 1.5 | 0.0" (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Find H I sources in the field**: Manual search of the three cubes, then SoFiA-2 smooth-and-clip detection with SNR threshold 4  
  > "We manually searched the three data cubes for H i sources and identified seven H i detections within the 1.5 deg$^2$ field of view surrounding NGC 5643." (2.2 Source Finding); "We set the source detection threshold to signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) of four." (2.2 Source Finding)
   - software: SoFiA-2, SoFiA Image Pipeline
   - parameter: Reliability 80, 75 and 70 per cent for the 96, 30 and 8 arcsec cubes  
  > "which was set to 80 per cent, 75 per cent and 70 per cent for the 96$''$, 30$''$ and 8$''$ cube, respectively" (2.2 Source Finding)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Automated SoFiA-2 run used to verify visually identified sources; reliability lowered to include visually identified sources with optical counterparts  
  > "The reliability threshold was lowered to include a few visually identified sources that were not automatically detected but have an optical counterpart" (2.2 Source Finding)
3. **Map H I emission and kinematics of NGC 5643**: Moment maps and position-velocity diagrams from the 30 arcsec cube along the major axis (PA 315) and the tail (PA 348)  
  > "To further investigate, we create position velocity diagrams from the 30$''$ data cube across different position angles (PA)" (3.2 H i emission in NGC 5643); "The cuts are made along the major rotation axis, PA=315$^\circ$, and along the tail feature, PA=348$^\circ$." (3.2 H i emission in NGC 5643)
   - software: CARTA
   - parameter: Contours at 2^n sigma with sigma = 1.4e19 cm^-2  
  > "with $\sigma\,=\,1.4\times10^{19}\text{cm}^{-2}$, the average column density at 3 SNR in the 30$''$ cube over four channels of 5.5 $\,$km$\,$s$^{-1}$" (3.2 H i emission in NGC 5643)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure the nuclear H I absorption**: Extract spectrum from the central beam of the 8 arcsec cube, compute optical depth and column density, Gaussian fit to the peak  
  > "was extracted from the 8$''$ data cube, with the central beam corresponding to a diameter of 664 pc" (3.3 H i Absorption); "The peak absorbed flux, determined using a Gaussian fit profile, is $S_{\text{abs}}\approx2\,\text{mJy/beam}$." (3.3 H i Absorption)
   - assumption: Spin temperature Ts = 100 K  
  > "Assuming a spin temperature of $T_s=100$ K, as commonly done for H i studies (cite: morganti2018interstellar)." (3.3 H i Absorption)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to absorption profile  
  > "The peak absorbed flux, determined using a Gaussian fit profile, is $S_{\text{abs}}\approx2\,\text{mJy/beam}$." (3.3 H i Absorption)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Model the H I disk with a 3D tilted-ring fit**: Tilted ring modelling of the 30 arcsec cube; model repeated for each cube; final model of seven rings  
  > "we analyse the H i kinematics in NGC 5643 using tilted ring modelling (cite: rogstad1974aperture)" (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics); "The final model consists of seven rings" (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
   - software: BBarolo, SoFiA-2
   - parameter: Initial PA = 308 deg and vrot = 120 km/s from SoFiA-2; inclination 35 deg free; vsys fixed at 1192 km/s; PA later fixed to 315 deg  
  > "The systemic velocity was fixed at $v_{\rm sys}=1192$ $\,$km$\,$s$^{-1}$" (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics); "Some parameters converged to a single value, while others were adjusted from free to fixed, specifically PA = $315^{\circ}$." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
   - fitting: Iterated fits per cube; vrot errors = 5 per cent of value plus 5.5 km/s channel width; priors/likelihood not stated  
  > "This process was repeated multiple times for each cube to optimise the model." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics); "The errors in $\text{V}_{\text{rot}}$ were calculated by taking 5 per cent of the value and adding the channel resolution" (Ring properties of NGC 5643)
   - validation: Models from different cubes consistent; compared with data in channel maps and PV diagrams  
  > "The models derived from different cubes were consistent with each other." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
6. **Measure the mass of the northern H I tail**: Subtract the model from the data cube to make a residual cube, apply a 3 sigma clip and isolate the tail with a box  
  > "we subtracted the model from the data cube, producing a residual cube" (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics); "We then applied a $3\sigma$ clip and selected a box region to isolate the northern tail." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
   - software: BBarolo
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tail kinematics compared with ram-pressure stripping expectations and tidal-origin scenarios  
  > "In contrast, the tail in NGC 5643 exhibits counter-rotation, inconsistent with this mechanism." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
7. **Test whether NGC 5643 is H I deficient**: H I deficiency from Chung et al. 2009 equations using mean HI surface density within the stellar disk; class compared with Yoon et al. 2017  
  > "Using the equations in (cite: Chung2009) and the mean H i surface density within the stellar disk, we calculate an H i deficiency of" (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics); "NGC 5643 falls into class 0, which includes galaxies that show no clear signs of gas stripping by the ICM." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare H2 (ALMA CO 2-1) and H I**: R_mol = Sigma_H2/Sigma_HI map from 8 arcsec HI smoothed to 11 arcsec ALMA resolution; CO PV slice at PA 315 overlaid on HI PV diagrams  
  > "by smoothing the 8$''$ MeerKAT intensity map to match the 11$''$ resolution of the ALMA map" (4.2 H2 and H i comparison); "The molecular gas position velocity diagram (green) is overlaid on the 8$''$ position velocity diagram (greyscale) at the same slice" (4.2 H2 and H i comparison)
   - parameter: PV slice across the centre at PA = 315 deg intersecting the left-most bright spot  
  > "The slice is taken across the centre of NGC 5643 at PA = 315$^\circ$, intersecting the left-most bright spot." (4.2 H2 and H i comparison)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.35 Msun pc^-2 (K km/s)^-1 and R21 = 0.65 (from PHANGS)  
  > "assumes a standard Milky Way conversion factor of $\alpha_{\text{CO}}\,=\,4.35\,$M$_\odot$ $\text{pc}^{-2}$(K $\,$km$\,$s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$" (4.2 H2 and H i comparison)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the 23-galaxy result of Leroy et al. 2008 for nuclear R_mol  
  > "Our results with NGC 5643 are consistent with their findings, as expected." (4.2 H2 and H i comparison)
9. **Derive gas depletion times**: HI and total gas depletion times from the MeerKAT HI mass and PHANGS H2 mass using equations of Jaskot et al. 2015  
  > "we calculate the H i depletion time to be $\text{t}_{\text{dep}}$(H i)$\,\approx\,1.79$ Gyrs" (4.2 H2 and H i comparison)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- The H I disk is large (30 kpc diameter), regularly rotating, with H I extending beyond the stellar disk; a low column density northern tail counter-rotates relative to the disk  
  > "North of the disk is an extended 30 kpc tail with counter-rotating velocities." (abstract)
- Extraplanar H I (beards) in the spiral arms trace galactic fountains with a molecular component and higher H2/H I  
  > "Within the spiral arms of the disk, we identify extraplanar gas components, tracing galactic fountains driven by star formation regions." (abstract)
- Optical counterparts found for ID4, ID5 and ID6 among the surrounding H I sources  
  > "When compared to the VST image ID4, ID5 and ID6 have identifiable optical counterparts." (3.1 Full field of view)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sensitive high-resolution H I observations were the missing component for NGC 5643, which is also covered by PHANGS-ALMA CO(2-1)  
  > "NGC 5643 has been studied at multiple wavelengths, however, a key missing component is sensitive, high-resolution neutral hydrogen (H i) observations." (abstract)
- Why this method: Three cubes at different resolutions; the 30 arcsec cube balances diffuse gas and resolution and is used for most analysis  
  > "The 30$''$ cube offers a good balance between capturing diffuse gas and resolving high-resolution features and is therefore used for most of the analysis in this paper." (2.1 MeerKAT L-Band observations)
- Alternative: Ram pressure stripping rejected as the origin of the tail because it counter-rotates and the H I deficiency is low  
  > "In contrast, the tail in NGC 5643 exhibits counter-rotation, inconsistent with this mechanism." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
- Alternative: Other tail origins (recent interaction, stripped dwarf, halo accretion) considered; origin not definitively determined  
  > "There are several possible explanations for the origin of this tail feature, including ram pressure stripping, recent interactions, or accretion from the environment." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single MeerKAT pointing  
  > "While these MeerKAT observations were limited to a single pointing" (5 Summary and Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): No metallicity measurement for the tail gas, so pristine halo gas vs stripped dwarf cannot be distinguished  
  > "Currently, we lack metallicity measurements for the gas in the tail." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
- Limitation (authors): High stellar density near the Galactic plane limits the VST depth  
  > "Due to NGC 5643 being close to the Galactic plane, the star density in the field of view is high, limiting the depth of our observations." (4.1 3D modelling of the H i disk kinematics)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tail HI mass is quoted inconsistently (4, 4.6 and 5.4 x 10^6 Msun) with no uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA CO(2-1) is only used through PHANGS products; its processing, mask and the R_mol uncertainty are not described
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Absorption analysis assumes Ts = 100 K and a single central beam; the negative column density sign convention and covering factor are not discussed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tilted-ring fit has no stated uncertainties beyond a 5 per cent + channel-width heuristic
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tail origin (halo vs stripped dwarf) remains unconstrained
