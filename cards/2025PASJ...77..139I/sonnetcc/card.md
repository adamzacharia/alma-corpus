## Analysis recipe

1. **Image the Band 9 dust continuum and measure its flux**: tclean continuum image (SPW1,3,4 plus line-free channels), imfit 2D Gaussian for flux density, position and size  
  > "Continuum maps were created using all channels except for SPW2 that included" (ALMA Band 9 Data); "With the CASA task `imfit`, we obtain the flux density of $S_{\rm \nu, 63 \mu m} = 9.6 \pm 1.0$ mJy" (63-um continuum emission)
   - software: CASA tclean, CASA imfit
   - parameter: natural weighting  
  > "with a natural weighting to optimize the point-source sensitivity." (ALMA Band 9 Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared size and flux with earlier continuum measurements and with Tripodi et al. 2024 using the same data  
  > "Our results are consistent with their results in the size and flux density measurements." (63-um continuum emission)
2. **Make continuum-subtracted [O I] cube**: imcontsub on SPW2 then tclean cube, 50 km/s channels, no uv-taper, natural weighting  
  > "To create line cubes in SPW2, the CASA task `imcontsub`" (ALMA Band 9 Data); "We then created cubes with a velocity width of $50$ km s$^{-1}$ without *uv*-tapering." (ALMA Band 9 Data)
   - software: CASA imcontsub, CASA tclean
   - parameter: velocity bin 50 km/s  
  > "We then created cubes with a velocity width of $50$ km s$^{-1}$ without *uv*-tapering." (ALMA Band 9 Data)
   - assumption: Phase-tracking center slightly offset from the quasar, so image-plane continuum subtraction is better than uvcontsub  
  > "In this case, `imcontsub` optimally subtracts the continuum compared with the CASA task `uvcontsub`" (Footnote 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Make moment-0 map and extract and fit the spectrum**: Integrate 673.96-674.44 GHz (250 km/s), extract the continuum-subtracted spectrum from the 2-sigma aperture, fit a Gaussian for FWHM and z  
  > "integrated over the frequency range of $673.96 - 674.44$ GHz, corresponding to a velocity width of $250$ km s$^{-1}$" ([O I] 63 um); "extracted from an aperture that encloses the $2\sigma$-region in the integrated intensity map" ([O I] 63 um)
   - software: scipy curve_fit
   - parameter: Spectrum noise measured in 671.3-673.5 GHz and 674.6-676.8 GHz  
  > "the noise level is measured from the frequency ranges in" ([O I] 63 um)
   - parameter: SPWs 1, 2, 3 used in the fit  
  > "where we use the SPWs of 1, 2, and 3." ([O I] 63 um)
   - assumption: Gaussian line profile  
  > "We apply a Gaussian profile to the spectrum with the *scipy* `curve_fit` function" ([O I] 63 um)
   - fitting: Gaussian via scipy curve_fit; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "We apply a Gaussian profile to the spectrum with the *scipy* `curve_fit` function" ([O I] 63 um)
   - validation: Detected at expected position and frequency; compared position with other lines  
  > "at the expected position and frequency" (Summary)
4. **Measure [O I] line flux and luminosity**: imfit 2D Gaussian on the 500 km/s integrated map, then L_line = 1.04e-3 S dv D_L^2 nu_obs  
  > "We measure the line flux with the CASA task `imfit` assuming a 2D Gaussian profile for the intensity map integrated over a velocity width of 500 km s$^{-1}$" ([O I] 63 um); "L_{\rm line} = 1.04 \times 10^{-3} \left(\frac{S_{\rm line}\Delta v}{\rm Jy\, km\, s^{-1}}\right)" ([O I] 63 um)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: integration width 500 km/s (about 2.6 x FWHM)  
  > "integrated over a velocity width of 500 km s$^{-1}$" ([O I] 63 um)
   - assumption: Line region unresolved; size upper limit taken as the continuum size  
  > "emitting region is not spatially resolved." ([O I] 63 um)
   - assumption: Standard cosmology (H0=70.4)  
  > "we assume a $\Lambda$ cold dark matter cosmology" (Introduction)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian via imfit; no priors  
  > "assuming a 2D Gaussian profile for the intensity map" ([O I] 63 um)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compute luminosity ratios and compare with literature samples**: Form L_[OI]/L_FIR and L_[OI]/L_[CII] using literature [C II] and CIGALE-based L_FIR, plot against local, z=1-3 and z>4 samples  
  > "We also list $L_{\rm FIR}$ obtained in (cite: salak2024) who have performed multi-wavelength spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting for J2054-0005 using `CIGALE`" (Luminosity Ratios); "The median and standard deviation of" (L_FIR vs. L_[OI]63/L_[CII]158)
   - parameter: Lensing magnification corrected for literature objects  
  > "In this plot, we have corrected for the lensing magnification factors, if any." (L_FIR vs. L_[OI]63/L_FIR)
   - assumption: SFR converted to L_FIR assuming Salpeter IMF for the local relation  
  > "where we convert SFR into" (L_FIR vs. L_[OI]63/L_FIR)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with local relation and individual z=1-3 detections  
  > "are well within the range of the local relation in (cite: delooze2014)" (L_FIR vs. L_[OI]63/L_FIR)
6. **Assess AGN/XDR contribution to the lines**: Compare observed [O I]/[C II] to AGN thresholds in the literature and use the [C II]/[C I](2-1) argument of Decarli et al. 2022  
  > "In conclusion, we do not find evidence in J2054-0005 that the emission lines are strongly powered due to the AGN activity." (AGN contribution to [O I] 63 um in J2054-0005)
   - assumption: Lines assumed to arise from PDRs, not XDRs, for the subsequent modeling  
  > "Under the assumption that the" (PDR Modeling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Literature criteria only; the ratio is close to the AGN criterion within uncertainties  
  > "which does not allow us to strongly conclude whether the AGN activity affects the" (AGN contribution to [O I] 63 um in J2054-0005)
7. **Constrain gas density and FUV field with PDR models**: Compare the [O I]/[C II] and ([C II]+[O I])/L_FIR ratios with PDR Toolbox wk2020 model grid (Z=1.0) after three luminosity corrections  
  > "The darker red and blue shaded regions indicate the parameter space allowed by" (Results of PDR Modeling); "We employ PDR Toolbox (PDRT; (cite: pound_wolfire2008; pound_wolfire2023))." (Model Descriptions)
   - software: PDR Toolbox (PDRT)
   - parameter: L_FIR(PDR) = 0.22 L_FIR; L_[CII](PDR) = 0.83 L_[CII]; L_[OI] multiplied by 2  
  > "We thus adopt the far-infrared luminosity from star formation activity, $L_{\rm FIR}$(PDR) $= 0.22~L_{\rm FIR}" (Corrections to Input Luminosities); "we multiply the observed luminosity of" (Corrections to Input Luminosities)
   - assumption: Plane-parallel PDR geometry; Z/Zsun = 1.0; [C II], [O I] and dust co-spatial; [C II] purely from PDR after removing 17% H II fraction  
  > "Briefly, the model assumes a plane-parallel geometry with UV radiation field, cosmic rays, and soft X-rays incident on one side." (Model Descriptions); "and dust continuum emission are co-spatial, but discuss this limitation in" (Model Descriptions); "We assume that $17$% of" (Corrections to Input Luminosities)
   - fitting: Overlap of allowed regions of two ratio constraints on the (n_H, G/G0) grid; no sampler or priors stated  
  > "one can constrain $n_{\rm H}$ and the FUV radiation field based on comparisons of the models with observed line intensities." (Model Descriptions)
   - validation: Repeated without L_FIR correction, with extra factor 2 for self-absorption, and with [C II] divided by 2; compared with literature PDR results  
  > "we also perform PDR modeling with the same models without applying the correction to" (Results of PDR Modeling); "In figure [5](#fig5), we compare the results of J2054-0005 with the literature values." (Comparisons of Our PDR Modeling to the Literatures)
8. **Test for [O I] self-absorption with a two-component Gaussian**: Fit two Gaussians to the [O I] spectrum, one forced to [C II] center and width with free amplitude, the second unconstrained, compare with [C II]  
  > "For the fit we assume that the" (Results of PDR Modeling); "The second component is left completely unconstrained." (Results of PDR Modeling)
   - software: scipy ODR
   - parameter: Spectrum at 30 km/s resolution  
  > "In this figure, we show the spectrum at a finer resolution of 30 km s$^{-1}$." (Results of PDR Modeling)
   - assumption: [O I] emission arises in the same regions as [C II]  
  > "we force one component to the same line-center and line-width as the" (Results of PDR Modeling)
   - fitting: Non-linear least squares via Scipy ODR; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "The result of the non-linear least-squares fit via *Scipy*’s `ODR` module is shown in figure [4](#fig4)." (Results of PDR Modeling)
   - validation: Authors state the result should be treated cautiously given low S/N  
  > "Given the low signal-to-noise ratio of the spectrum, this exact value should be treated cautiously" (Results of PDR Modeling)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 9 archival data cover [O I] 63 um at z~6  
  > "At $z \sim 4-7$, the" (Introduction); "based on ALMA Band 9 archival data" (Introduction)
- Why this method: [O I], [C II] and dust continuum together constrain gas density and temperature in PDRs; imcontsub used because of the phase center offset  
  > "allow us to estimate the gas density and temperature in photo-dissociated regions (PDRs)." (Introduction); "In this case, `imcontsub` optimally subtracts the continuum compared with the CASA task `uvcontsub`" (Footnote 2)
- Alternative: PDR modeling without the AGN correction to L_FIR (gives log G/G0 ~3.7, treated as upper limit)  
  > "we also perform PDR modeling with the same models without applying the correction to" (Results of PDR Modeling)
- Alternative: XDR-dominated emission disfavored by [C II]/[C I](2-1) > 30  
  > "indicates that the XDR does not strongly affect the emission lines in J2054-0005" (AGN contribution to [O I] 63 um in J2054-0005)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Data quality too limited to conclusively determine self-absorption  
  > "Given the limited quality of our data, it is difficult to determine conclusively if the line in J2054-0005 is indeed self-absorbed." (Results of PDR Modeling)
- Limitation (authors): [O I] emission is unresolved and its spatial extent unknown; co-spatiality of [C II], [O I] and dust is assumed  
  > "A detailed discussion is hampered by the fact that the spatial extent of" (Results of PDR Modeling); "and dust continuum emission are co-spatial, but discuss this limitation in" (Model Descriptions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detection is only 5 sigma peak in one 39-minute execution; flux depends on aperture and the 500 km/s integration choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Continuum subtraction with imcontsub on a single SPW that also contains the line may bias the line baseline; line-free channels not specified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): PDR solutions rely on stacked ad hoc corrections (78% AGN, 17% H II, factor 2 for optical depth) with no propagated uncertainty and no sampler
- Caveat (inferred, ours): [C II] and L_FIR are from other works with different apertures and beams, so ratios may not be matched in aperture
