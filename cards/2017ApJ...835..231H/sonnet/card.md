## Analysis recipe

1. **Recalibrate and self-calibrate visibilities**: Re-run ALMA scripts with corrected flux models/weights in CASA, phase self-cal on continuum, apply to line SPWs  
  > "the calibration scripts were modified for the 1.1 and 1.4 mm settings of AS 209, 1.4 mm setting of V4046 Sgr, 1.1 mm setting of IM Lup, and 1.1 and 1.4 mm settings of HD 163296 to scale visibility weights properly" (3.2 Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, analysisUtils
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared flux models with the ALMA calibrator catalogue  
  > "We verified that all other calibration scripts provided by ALMA (and available in the Archive) specify quasar flux models consistent with the ALMA calibrator catalogue." (Table 11)
2. **Image continuum and measure dust disk radius and flux**: CLEAN continuum (robust 0.5); fit ellipses to 3 sigma contours; integrate flux inside contour; uncertainty from 500 random positions  
  > "fit ellipses to the 3$\sigma$ contours of the 258 GHz continuum images" (3.2 Data Reduction); "measure flux densities at 500 random signal-free positions in a $25''\times25''$ continuum image, then taking the standard deviation of these measurements." (3.2 Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, scikit-image
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Image line cubes, moment maps and radial profiles**: Continuum-subtract, CLEAN at 0.5 km/s with masks; moment zero maps clipped at 1 sigma; deprojected azimuthally averaged radial profiles using literature PA and inclination  
  > "Figure [2](#fig:radialprofile) shows deprojected and azimuthally averaged radial profiles, which were produced from unclipped versions of the integrated intensity maps" (3.2 Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Integration range from DCO+ emission above 3 sigma  
  > "the maps were produced by summing over channels corresponding to where DCO$^+$ emission was detected above the $3\sigma$ level" (3.2 Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Channel maps inspected for emission outside DCO+ channels  
  > "The channel maps for the other lines were inspected to check that no emission above the 3$\sigma$ level appeared in the known disk region" (3.2 Data Reduction)
4. **Extract integrated line fluxes and uncertainties**: Elliptical aperture on cubes; 500 simulated integrated fluxes from random signal-free channels; std dev = uncertainty; detection if >3 sigma in at least 3 channels  
  > "For each image cube, 500 simulated integrated flux measurements were obtained, and their standard deviation was taken to be the uncertainty in the integrated line flux." (3.2 Data Reduction); "We classify a line as detected if emission exceeding the $3\sigma$ level is observed in at least three channels" (4.1 Line Detections)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SMA fluxes  
  > "the fluxes measured with ALMA agree with Submillimeter Array observations of HCN $J=3-2$, DCO$^+$ $J = 3-2$, and DCN $J = 3-2$" (4.1 Line Detections)
5. **Compute flux ratios with systematic uncertainty**: 100,000 Gaussian draws per line, each scaled by a factor ~N(1,0.15); 68% interval of ratio  
  > "we drew 100,000 simulated flux measurements for each line from a Gaussian distribution" (4.2.1 Line Flux Ratios)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo  
  > "we drew 100,000 simulated flux measurements for each line from a Gaussian distribution with mean and standard deviation given by the flux measurement and uncertainty provided in Table" (Line Flux Ratios)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Derive disk-averaged D/H abundance ratios**: Optically thin LTE column density ratio of DCX/H13CX at Tex 15 and 75 K, converted with 12C/13C=69; IM Lup DCN/HCN taken directly from flux ratio  
  > "Assumed excitation temperatures ranged from 15 to 75 degrees" (4.2.2 Disk-averaged Abundance Ratios); "we instead computed the DCN/HCN column density ratios directly from the DCN/HCN flux density ratio" (4.2.2 Disk-averaged Abundance Ratios)
   - parameter: Tex = 15-75 K  
  > "Assumed excitation temperatures ranged from 15 to 75 degrees" (4.2.2 Disk-averaged Abundance Ratios)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin lines, co-spatial isotopologues, negligible dust opacity, 12C/13C = 69  
  > "We also neglect dust opacity when estimating the D/H ratios." (4.2.2 Disk-averaged Abundance Ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Ratio changes only ~25% over the Tex range  
  > "changing only by $\sim25\%$ in the temperature range evaluated" (4.2.2 Disk-averaged Abundance Ratios)

## Qualitative findings

- DCN, DCO+, H13CO+ detected in all six disks; H13CN in all but IM Lup  
  > "DCN, DCO$^+$, and H$^{13}$CO$^+$ are detected in all disks, and H$^{13}$CN in all but the IM Lup disk." (abstract)
- DCN is spatially compact relative to DCO+ in V4046 Sgr and HD 163296 but similar extent in AS 209 and LkCa 15  
  > "Similar radial extents for DCN and DCO$^+$ emission in the AS 209 and LkCa 15 disks." (5.2.1 Formation of DCN)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Targets mostly chosen from disks with previous DCO+ detections in DISCS and spanning spectral types and disk morphologies  
  > "the targets were mainly chosen from disks in which DCO$^+$ had previously been observed in DISCS" (2 Sample Selection and Characteristics)
- Why this method: H13CN and H13CO+ targeted because main isotopologues expected to be optically thick  
  > "H$^{13}$CN and H$^{13}$CO$^+$ were targeted rather than the main isotopologues because the corresponding transitions of the latter are expected to be optically thick in disks" (3.1 Observational Setup)
- Alternative: CH2D+ pathway for DCO+ (Favre et al. 2015) not supported by observed central gaps  
  > "the DCO$^+$ emission features prominent central gaps where DCN is brightest" (5.2.2 Formation of DCO+)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Assumes co-spatial isotopologues, LTE, ISM 12C/13C; selection bias toward DCO+-bright massive disks; resolution limits morphology classification  
  > "inferences about deuterium fractionation based on optically thin isotopologues require the assumption that the $^{12}$C and $^{13}$C isotopologues are co-spatial." (5.1.3 Considerations on 12C/13C Fractionation); "The classification of emission morphologies is dependent on the spatial resolution" (4.3 Emission Morphologies)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Disk-averaged D/H mixes regions with different local ratios
- Caveat (inferred, ours): HCN optical depth for IM Lup assumed thin
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample with selection bias
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single excitation temperature for the whole disk
