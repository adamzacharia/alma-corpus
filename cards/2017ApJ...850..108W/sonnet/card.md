## Analysis recipe

1. **Image cubes and continuum**: CASA imaging with natural weighting, no channel binning, uv continuum subtraction; continuum from three line-free basebands  
  > "The three bands without the line were used to form a continuum image." (Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra, moment maps and fluxes**: Moment-zero from channels with >~2 sigma emission (central 20 channels for non-detections); elliptical aperture 1.5x source FWHM (1.5x beam for non-detections); errors from random apertures  
  > "Uncertainties in each line channel and in the continuum were determined by placing random apertures of equivalent size within regions of the maps free of genuine emission" (Observations)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits for sizes and line FWHM; three fixed-velocity Gaussians for PSO J167-13  
  > "only the amplitude and width of the Gaussians allowed to vary as free parameters." (PSO J167-13)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive LFIR and SFR**: LFIR (42.5-122.5 um) from 1.2 mm continuum with greybody; SFR=1.5e-10 LFIR; SFR=1e-7 L[CII]  
  > "${\rm SFR}\, (M_\odot\,{\rm yr}^{-1})=1.5\times10^{-10}L_{\rm FIR}\, (L_\odot)$" (4 [CII] and FIR Luminosities)
   - assumption: Td=47 K, beta=1.6, Chabrier IMF, all dust heated by star formation  
  > "Note this assumes that all the dust is heated by star formation and not by the quasar." (4 [CII] and FIR Luminosities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Temperatures 40 K and 60 K scale LFIR by 0.7 and 1.7  
  > "For $T_{\rm d} =40$ K and $T_{\rm d} =60$ K the scaling factors to apply to $L_{\rm FIR}$ are 0.7 and 1.7 respectively." (4 [CII] and FIR Luminosities)
4. **Fit L[CII]-LFIR relation**: Orthogonal distance regression on 22 z>5.7 quasars  
  > "using orthogonal distance regression that accounts for errors in both axes" (4 [CII] and FIR Luminosities)
   - fitting: ODR in log space  
  > "We fit the relationship between $L_{\rm [CII]}$ and $L_{\rm FIR}$ for the high-$z$ quasar sample using orthogonal distance regression that accounts for errors in both axes." ([CII] and FIR Luminosities)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Dynamical masses and comparison with BH mass and simulations**: Thin disk: i=arccos(amin/amaj), vcir=0.75 FWHM/sin i, Mdyn=1.16e5 vcir^2 D; D=1.5x deconvolved FWHM; compare with MassiveBlack-II  
  > "The circular velocity is $v_{\rm cir}=0.75 \,$FWHM$_{\rm [CII]} / \sin i$." (5 Dynamical Mass and Black hole mass)
   - assumption: D=4.5 kpc for unresolved; inclination 55 deg when minor axis unavailable  
  > "so for these we assume the random orientation angle of 55$^{\circ}$." (5 Dynamical Mass and Black hole mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
