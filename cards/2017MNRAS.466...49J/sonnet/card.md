## Analysis recipe

1. **Stack spectra**: Shift each spectrum to zero velocity using 13CO mean velocity, then co-add within central aperture  
  > "we first re-grid velocity axis of each spectrum in order to set the local mean velocity of the bulk molecular medium to zero km/s." (3 Methodology)
   - software: GILDAS/CLASS
   - parameter: 10" radius aperture for ALMA galaxies; 50" for M51  
  > "for the ALMA galaxies we stack spectra inside a central 10" radius aperture. For M51 we picked a larger aperture of 50" in radius" (3 Methodology)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit lines and get intensities and limits**: Single Gaussian fit; upper limit from Gaussian with 12C FWHM and 3x noise peak if below 3 sigma  
  > "we fit the stacked spectrum of each line with a single Gaussian." (3 Methodology)
   - software: MPFIT (IDL)
   - assumption: 12C and 13C lines well mixed  
  > "This assumes that the $^{12}$C and $^{13}$C lines are well mixed" (3 Methodology)
   - fitting: Free: centre velocity, peak, dispersion  
  > "the line centre velocity, the peak intensity, and the velocity dispersion." (3 Methodology)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Estimate optical depths (LTE)**: tau13 = -ln(1 - T13/T12); tau12 = tau13 x [12C]/[13C]  
  > "\tau^{13C}  = -\ln\left(1-\frac{T_{\rm obs}^{13C}}{T_{\rm obs}^{12C}}\right)" (5.2.1)
   - parameter: 12C/13C = 50  
  > "adopting a Carbon isotope ratio of $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C$=50$" (5.2.2)
   - assumption: LTE, equal Tex, optically thick 12C, thin 13C, equal beam filling  
  > "assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and in particular $T_{\textnormal{ex}}$(HCN)$=T_{\textnormal{ex}}$(H$^{13}$CN)" (5.2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Non-LTE correction via filling factors and effective critical density**: Adopt eta12/eta13 ~2; critical density reduced by tau  
  > "we revisit our LTE calculations but now assume that $\eta_{\rm bf}^{12C} / \eta_{\rm bf}^{13C} \approx 2$." (5.3)
   - assumption: Critical densities at Tex=20 K, optically thin  
  > "The critical densities were computed assuming optically thin transition lines for an excitation temperature of $20$K." (2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature ratios  
  > "Broadly, these agree with our measurements, with ratios often $\gtrsim 10$ and sometimes $\gtrsim 20$." (4.1)

## Qualitative findings

- 13C isotopologues are optically thin; HCN and HCO+ moderately thick (tau ~2-11), lowering critical densities by 2-6.  
  > "the critical densities of the HCN, HCO$^+$ and HNC are reduced by a factor of 2-6" (6)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: 13C lines too faint per sightline, so stacking used  
  > "The emission from these molecules is faint for individual lines of sight. To overcome this, we use spectral stacking." (3 Methodology)
- Alternative: Stacking over discs gave only weak upper limits  
  > "Stacking over the extended discs of galaxies typically yielded upper limits that barely constrained the $^{13}$C lines" (4 Results)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): 12C/13C ratio uncertain by factor ~2  
  > "we note that this is likely uncertain by a factor of $\sim 2$." (5.1)

- Caveat (inferred, ours): Non-LTE correction factor 2 is adopted, not derived
