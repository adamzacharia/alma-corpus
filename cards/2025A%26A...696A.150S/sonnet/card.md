## Processing

### Calibration

- pipeline or manual: Standard ALMA pipeline calibration (QA2 products), then ALMAGAL pipeline  
  > "the standard calibration products generated by the ALMA pipeline (cite: Hunter2023) were taken as a starting point for the ALMAGAL pipeline" (2.2 ALMA observations)
- casa version: CASA 5.6.1 for calibration and most steps; 6.2.0 for final joint-deconvolution imaging  
  > "version 5.6.1 for calibration and most of the steps within the ALMAGAL processing pipeline" (2.2 ALMA observations)
- pipeline version: _not stated_
- archive products used as is: Calibrated data from ARC Europe, same as scriptForPI.py output  
  > "correspond to the same output that any user can generate when running the `scriptForPI.py` file" (4.1 Calibration and data staging)
- flux calibrator: _not stated_
- flux scale uncertainty: _not stated_
- self calibration: Automatic phase-only self-cal, solint='int', applied if S/Nselfcal>3; kept if rms improves >7.5% and beam grows <15%  
  > "using a solution interval equal to the integration time $t_\mathrm{int}$ (i.e., `solint=’int’`)" (4.3 Self-calibration)

### Imaging

- task: tclean (CASA 5.6.1 first imaging; 6.2.0 final)  
  > "we jointly image the different array configurations using the `tclean` task in `CASA`" (4.4 Final joint-deconvolution imaging)
- deconvolver: multiscale  
  > "All images were produced using the *multiscale* deconvolver and the multithresh automasking routines" (4.4 Final joint-deconvolution imaging)
- weighting robust: Briggs robust = +0.5  
  > "with a weighting robust Briggs parameter of $+0.5$" (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
- uv taper: _not stated_
- cell size: _not stated_
- image size: _not stated_
- masking: auto-multithresh with per-array parameters (Table), merged TM2 and 7M masks, final PB mask  
  > "the pipeline uses a mask that is equal to the whole primary beam (PB)" (4.4.1 Continuum images)
- multiscale scales: 7M [0,30,60]; TM2/TM1 [0,6,18,30]; 7MTM2 and 7MTM2TM1 [0,6,18,30,60]  
  > "— scales | \[0, 30, 60\] | \[0, 6, 18, 30\] | \[0, 6, 18, 30\] | \[0, 6, 18, 30, 60\] | \[0, 6, 18, 30, 60\]" (Table tclean parameters)
- continuum subtraction: findcont line-free ranges, refined with REFIND.py (up to 6 iterations)  
  > "the ALMAGAL pipeline includes the `REFIND.py` script, which runs a new instance of `findcont` on the CLEANed cube image." (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
- line free channels: _not stated_
- primary beam correction: images not PB corrected; pb files supplied; FoV to 30% response  
  > "The `image` files are not primary-beam corrected, which results in a homogeneous noise level across the whole image" (4.5 Data products)
- array combination: _not stated_
- smoothing: cubes smoothed to the largest beam; beams not circularized  
  > "The final stitched cube is smoothed to a common beam, corresponding to the largest beam in the originally stitched cube." (4.4.2 Line-only cube images)
- final beam: median 0.47"x0.38" (Near), 0.28"x0.19" (Far) for 7M+TM2+TM1  
  > "The combined 7M+TM2+TM1 images have slightly larger beam sizes with a median of $0.28\ensuremath{''}\times0.19\ensuremath{''}$ and $0.47\ensuremath{''}\times0.38\ensuremath{''}$" (4.5.2 Angular resolution in final products)
- final rms: 0.04–0.1 mJy/beam continuum (MAD); 2.1–2.4 (broad) and 4.3–5.1 (narrow) mJy/beam cubes  
  > "The final MAD noise level in the fully combined (7M+TM2+TM1) images ranges from 0.04 to 0.1 mJy beam$^{-1}$" (4.5.3 Intensity and noise levels in final products)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Stage calibrated data**: Obtain calibrated MSs from ARC and split per source and EB with scriptToSplitSources.py  
  > "The ALMAGAL script[^4] `scriptToSplitSources.py` splits the data contained in the calibrated measurement sets of each EB into files containing only data for each individual scientific target" (4.1 Calibration and data staging)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: QA2 weblogs inspected for major problems  
  > "The weblogs produced by the ALMA observatory during the observation and the QA2 (quality assessment) phase were inspected to search for major calibration problems." (2.2 ALMA observations)
2. **Determine continuum ranges and first images**: findcont then REFIND.py iterative refinement using mom8_fc residuals; tclean first images per array  
  > "This proceeds iteratively until the criterion is met (i.e., no residual emission is identified in the `mom8_fc` images), or until 5 iterations have been completed" (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
   - software: CASA, findcont
   - parameter: robust +0.5; 6th iteration removes remaining emission channels  
  > "for the 6$^\mathrm{th}$ iteration, we simply remove any remaining channels that have emission above the given threshold" (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with STATCONT and LumberJack, within 5%  
  > "We obtained consistent results on the continuum emission level, within 5% differences, when using the three different software packages" (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
3. **Automatic self-calibration**: Iterative phase-only self-cal on continuum, cleaning to 4x, 3x, then 2x rms; solutions applied to all spws  
  > "From the fourth iteration on, the process is repeated, CLEANing down to a threshold of $2\times$ rms." (4.3 Self-calibration)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: S/N_selfcal>3; minsnr=2.5; accept if rms improves >7.5% and beam <15% larger  
  > "This can happen because they do not meet the S/N criteria (`minsnr=2.5`) when determining new calibration solutions." (Footnote 8)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare rms and beam before/after each iteration  
  > "Both images, before and after self-calibration, are compared" (4.3 Self-calibration)
4. **Joint deconvolution imaging of continuum and cubes**: Four-step iterative tclean in 500-iteration rounds, merging TM2 and 7M masks then PB mask; cubes in ~100-channel chunks then stitched and smoothed  
  > "additional runs of `tclean` are executed iteratively, in rounds of 500 iterations, until one of these two conditions are met" (4.4.1 Continuum images)
   - software: CASA 6.2.0
   - parameter: stop when model flux increases <1% or residual <2.5x theoretical noise  
  > "(1) the flux in the model (or CLEAN components) does not increase by more than 1%, or (2) the emission in the residual is less than $2.5\times$ the expected theoretical noise" (4.4.1 Continuum images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Convolved combined images compared to lower-resolution arrays, intensities and profiles consistent  
  > "The convolved images confirm that we recover the same structure and intensities." (4.5.1 Quality assessment of data combination)
5. **Noise and mass sensitivity**: MAD of residual image as rms; convert to mass sensitivity  
  > "The rms noise level shown in Fig. [23](#fig:continuum-properties) and throughout the paper correspond to the `AGMADRES` values." (4.5.3 Intensity and noise levels in final products)
   - assumption: T=20 K, kappa=0.899 cm2/g, gas-to-dust 100  
  > "assuming a conservative temperature of 20 K, a dust opacity of 0.899 cm$^{2}$ g$^{-1}$ (cite: OssenkopfHenning1994), and a gas-to-dust ratio of 100" (3.5 ALMAGAL science products)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Line richness of regions of interest**: Define RoIs at 8 sigma continuum contours; extract spectra; measure line bandwidth fraction (5 sigma), line luminosity and line/total luminosity fraction  
  > "We characterize the line richness by defining three complementary parameters: the fraction of bandwidth with line emission, the luminosity contained in the lines, and the fraction of the line luminosity to the total luminosity" (5.2 Statistical characterization of the chemical richness)
   - parameter: line threshold 5 sigma; assumed line width ~5 km/s  
  > "Assuming that each spectral line feature has a typical width of $\approx5$ km s$^{-1}$" (5.2 Statistical characterization of the chemical richness)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Sgr B2 hot cores  
  > "These line densities are comparable to those determined in the Sgr B2 region" (5.2 Statistical characterization of the chemical richness)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: findcont chosen for scripting structure compatibility  
  > "The decision to use `findcont` in the ALMAGAL pipeline was essentially related to the scripting structure and functionality" (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
- Alternative: JvM correction tested but not applied in first release  
  > "Based on this, we discarded the application of such a correction factor to the first ALMAGAL data release." (4.4.1 Continuum images)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): findcont conservative in line-rich sources; no manual continuum ranges  
  > "we found that `findcont` is conservative when defining the frequency ranges in line-rich sources" (4.2 Continuum determination and first imaging)
- Limitation (authors): TM1-only line cubes not produced in first release  
  > "the line cubes for the TM1-only data using the new strategy are not generated in this first data release" (4.5 Data products)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux scale accuracy not discussed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Image fidelity of non-JvM-corrected images unquantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cube noise values vary with beam smoothing
