## Analysis recipe

1. **Prepare the dense-gas cube and check recovered flux**: Reuse the final 12m+7m+Total Power cube, compare aperture spectra with IRAM-30m, and smooth spectrally  
  > "Images from the three configurations were combined using the task feather in casa." (ALMA data); "From the final combined data-cube, we extracted spectra toward the regions labelled as Clump, Ridge and Shock" (ALMA data); "In order to increase the signal-to-noise ratio of the map, we have smoothed the cube to have velocity resolution 0.2 km s$^{-1}$." (ALMA data)
   - software: casa, feather
   - parameter: Circular aperture ~30 arcsec  
  > "using a circular aperture comparable with the IRAM-30m beam size at the N$_2$H$^+$(1-0) frequency ($\sim$30$^{\prime\prime}$)." (ALMA data)
   - parameter: Smoothed velocity resolution 0.2 km s^-1  
  > "we have smoothed the cube to have velocity resolution 0.2 km s$^{-1}$." (ALMA data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ALMA and IRAM-30m spectra comparable within rms; all flux recovered within uncertainty  
  > "The three set of spectra are comparable within the rms, indicating that all flux is recovered in the ALMA images within the uncertainty." (ALMA data)
2. **Fit molecular columns and excitation temperatures**: Fit LTE models simultaneously to J=1-0 and J=3-2 spectra for each species  
  > "we consider the species excitation temperatures as free parameters and use madcuba to obtain the best LTE models that simultaneously reproduce the J=1-0 and J=3-2 transitions." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
   - software: madcuba, SLIM, AUTOFIT
   - parameter: APEX nFLASH240; tuning frequencies 231.320 and 279.512 GHz; frequency resolution 488 kHz  
  > "The nFLASH240 receiver was used with tuning frequencies 231.320 and 279.512 GHz and frequency resolution 488 kHz." (APEX and IRAM-30m observations)
   - parameter: N2D+(3-2): 0.7 km s^-1, 27 arcsec; N2H+(3-2): 0.5 km s^-1, 23 arcsec  
  > "the velocity resolutions and beam sizes are 0.7 km s$^{-1}$ and 27$^{\prime\prime}$ for N$_2$D$^+$(3-2), 0.5 km s$^{-1}$ and 23$^{\prime\prime}$ for N$_2$H$^+$(3-2)." (APEX and IRAM-30m observations)
   - parameter: Convert T*_A to T_mb with beam efficiency 0.77 and forward efficiency 0.95  
  > "Observations were obtained in unit of antenna temperature, T$^*_A$, and converted to main beam temperature T$_{\rm{mb}}$ using beam and forward efficiency of 0.77 and 0.95, respectively." (APEX and IRAM-30m observations)
   - parameter: APEX rms: 3 mK for N2D+(3-2), 7 mK for N2H+(3-2)  
  > "The achieved rms are 3 mK for N$_2$D$^+$(3-2) and 7 mK for N$_2$H$^+$(3-2)" (APEX and IRAM-30m observations)
   - assumption: LTE; beam filling factor 1 for all transitions  
  > "We set the angular size of the emission, $\theta_S$, to reflect the assumption that the emission fills the beam i.e., filling factor of 1." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach); "We thus maintain the assumption of emission filling the beam for all transitions." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
   - assumption: Shared velocity, FWHM, angular size and excitation temperature across transitions fitted simultaneously  
  > "AUTOFIT assumes that $v$, FWHM, $\theta_S$, and T$_{\rm{ex}}$ are the same for all transitions that are fitted simultaneously." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
   - fitting: SLIM AUTOFIT; parameters N_tot, radial systemic velocity, FWHM, T_ex and angular size; T_ex free  
  > "Hence, we used the built-in AUTOFIT function of SLIM to obtain the LTE models that best reproduce all transitions." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach); "assuming as input parameters the total molecular column density (N$_{\rm{tot}}$), the radial systemic velocity of the source ($v$), the line full width at half maximum (FWHM), the excitation temperature (T$_{\rm{ex}}$), and the angular size of the emission ($\theta_S$)." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
   - validation: Models compared with spectra; T_ex errors <10%; velocity and linewidth errors combined with channel width; fitted optical depths <<1  
  > "The uncertainties given by madcuba are $<$10% for T$_{\rm{ex}}$ and $\sim$2% for $v_{\rm{0}}$ and $\Delta v$." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach); "The final uncertainty on the last two quantities is obtained by summing in quadrature the madcuba error with the spectra channel width (0.6-0.8 km s$^{-1}$ or $\sim$10%)." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach); "For all species and toward all positions the optical depth, $\tau$ is $<<1$." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
3. **Derive deuterium fractionation**: D_frac(N2H+)=N(N2D+)/N(N2H+)  
  > "D_{\rm frac}^{\rm N_2H^+} = \frac{N({\rm N_2D^+})}{N({\rm N_2H^+})}," (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
   - software: madcuba
   - fitting: Column densities from the multi-transition LTE fits  
  > "we consider the species excitation temperatures as free parameters and use madcuba to obtain the best LTE models that simultaneously reproduce the J=1-0 and J=3-2 transitions." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)
   - validation: Comparison with previous D/H analysis; Ambient upper limit insensitive to changing T_ex to 4.5 K  
  > "The 3$\sigma$ D/H upper limit previously estimated for the Ambient region does not change significantly when T$_{\rm{ex}}$=4.5 K is assumed." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach); "The multi-transition approach here adopted confirms the results reported in (cite: cosentino2023) and its implications." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach)

   - parameter: Initial moment-map interval 39-45.5 km s^-1; mask below 3×A_rms  
  > "The N$_2$H$^+$ integrated intensity map (39-45.5 km s$^{-1}$; top panel)" (N$_2$H$^+$ kinematics and morphology: fragmented vs continuous material.); "the pixels below 3$\times$A$_{rms}$" (N$_2$H$^+$ kinematics and morphology: fragmented vs continuous material.)
   - assumption: Isolated N2H+ hyperfine component is optically thin  
  > "the isolated component of the N$_2$H$^+$ hyper-fine structure, which is optically thin." (N$_2$H$^+$ kinematics and morphology: fragmented vs continuous material.)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: N2H+ traces dense, cold material with high star-formation potential; high-resolution images resolve its morphology  
  > "N$_2$H$^+$ is a well known tracer of dense and cold material, optimal to identify gas with the highest potential to harbour star formation." (abstract); "In (cite: cosentino2023), the single pointing observations did not allow us to investigate the spatial morphology of this compressed material." (Discussion and conclusions)
- Why this method: Multiple transitions free the excitation temperature; dendrograms consistently identify hierarchical core-like structures  
  > "we consider the species excitation temperatures as free parameters and use madcuba to obtain the best LTE models that simultaneously reproduce the J=1-0 and J=3-2 transitions." (D/H at the W44-G034.77 shock interface: a multi-line approach); "Dendograms provide a robust and consistent method for the identification of hierarchical structures within maps and cubes." (Core identification.)
- Alternative: Continuous compressed slab versus fragmentation into cores  
  > "Hence, we were unable to rule out whether this post-shocked gas is organised as a continuous slab of compressed material or if it has fragmented into cores." (Discussion and conclusions); "In the high-resolution ALMA images here analysed, the dense gas appear as highly fragmented, especially toward the ridge, with several core-like structures." (Discussion and conclusions)
- Alternative: Primary-beam-corrected dendrogram analysis gives no significant difference  
  > "However, since most of the region of interest is located toward the centre of the map, the analysis performed on the primary beam corrected image is not significantly different." (Core identification.)
- Alternative: Magnetic support may prevent collapse despite thermal and turbulent instability  
  > "These values are sufficient to support the cores against collapse." (Core stability against thermal, turbulent and magnetic support.)
- Alternative: HII-region or pre-SNR wind compression considered unlikely for these cores  
  > "Hence, it is unlikely that the cores here identified have been produced by the HII region and/or pre-SNR stellar wind compression." (Discussion and conclusions)
- How they decided it was enough: Flux recovery checked against IRAM-30m and insignificant leaves rejected; evidence remains insufficient to confirm shock-triggered star formation
