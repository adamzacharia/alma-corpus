## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce band 4 scan data and search for lines**: Standard ALMA pipeline in CASA, conversion to uvfits, analysis with GILDAS following Jin et al. 2019  
  > "Then we converted the data into *uvfits* format and performed analysis with the IRAM GILDAS tool (cite: Gildas) using the same method as in (cite: Jin2019)." (2.1.2)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Determine spectroscopic redshifts of AGS4 and AGS17**: Identify the single line as CO(6-5) using SED/PDF of photometric redshift, far-IR SED dust temperature, L_IR-L_CO relation, [CI] non-detection, and redshift-peak probability  
  > "we decided to favor the CO(6-5) transition from a purely probabilistic point of view." (3.2 AGS17)
   - assumption: Typical dust temperature ~38-40 K at these redshifts (Schreiber18)  
  > "close to the typical dust temperature $T_{\rm dust}$ $\sim$ 40 K at $z$ = 3.5 (cite: Schreiber18)" (3.2 AGS17)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: consistency with photometric redshift PDF and 4000 A break  
  > "gives a $z_{spec}^{AGS4}$ = 3.566, which agrees with the 4000 Å discontinuity in the optical SED." (3.1 AGS4)
3. **Deblend optical/NIR photometry and derive photometric redshifts and stellar masses**: De-blend neighbors, fit SEDs with EAzY (redshift) and FAST++ (mass; delayed exponentially declining SFH, Calzetti attenuation)  
  > "derived from the observed optical to mid-infrared SED fitted with `FAST++` assuming a delayed exponentially declining SFH and a (cite: Calzetti00) attenuation law" (2.4.2)
   - software: EAzY, FAST++, qdeblend
   - assumption: Salpeter IMF; H0=70, Om=0.3, OL=0.7  
  > "We use cosmological parameters of H$_{0}$ = 70 km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_{M}$ = 0.3, and $\Omega_{\Lambda}$ = 0.7." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: AGS24 mass tested with varied SFH and attenuation laws (FAST++, CIGALE); stays within 10^11.31-10^11.52  
  > "the stellar mass of AGS24 remains within 10$^{11.31}$ and 10$^{11.52}$ M$_{\odot}$" (5.1)
4. **Derive infrared luminosities and SFRs, and test for radio AGN**: CIGALE fit to Herschel+ALMA for AGS4/17; Schreiber18 templates to 1.1 mm for others; SFR_IR from Kennicutt98; q_TIR and L1.4GHz/SFR vs radio-excess threshold  
  > "Infrared luminosities are measured by the SED fitting of the *Herschel* and ALMA flux densities for AGS4 and AGS17 using `CIGALE`" (2.4.3)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: radio spectral index alpha = -0.8  
  > "assuming a radio spectral index of $\alpha$ = $\alpha^{\rm 3GHz}_{\rm 1.4GHz}$ = $-0.8$" (2.4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: AGS24 L_IR checked with Draine 2007 SED within 10%  
  > "which gives the same result within 10%" (5.1)
5. **Measure galaxy overdensity and density map**: Projected local density within 2 cMpc for 364 galaxies at 3.42<=z<=3.57; 0.9" grid density map  
  > "comparing the projected local galaxy density within 2 cMpc ($\rho$) of a galaxy, to the average number density over the GOODS-ALMA field" (5.2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 4 of 6 in 5 arcmin2 has 0.4% chance from four million Monte Carlo realizations  
  > "is only 0.4% from four million Monte Carlo mock realizations" (5.1)
6. **Two-point correlation and halo mass**: Landy-Szalay auto/cross-correlation with bootstrap errors; halo mass limits from Behroozi13 relation  
  > "A (cite: Landy1993) estimator is adopted. Error bars are estimated from 1000 bootstrap samples." (5.2.3)
   - assumption: upper limit assumes structure virialized  
  > "assuming that this structure is already virialized" (5.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Spectroscopic scan to give broad redshift coverage with at least one CO line  
  > "that ensures a broad redshift coverage from $z$ = 2 to $z$ = 5" (2.1.2)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: CO(7-6) and H2O rejected for AGS4; CO(5-4) disfavoured for AGS17  
  > "We exclude H$_2$O(2$_{11}$-2$_{02}$)." (3.1 AGS4)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single lines cannot give definitive redshifts  
  > "One ALMA spectral line alone is not sufficient to obtain a definitive spectroscopic redshift for AGS4." (3.1 AGS4)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Redshifts of AGS11, 15, 24 are assumed from the peak, so cluster membership is partly circular
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small-number statistics and 3.5 sigma redshift peak
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Shallow density profile does not match NFW
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-line redshifts
