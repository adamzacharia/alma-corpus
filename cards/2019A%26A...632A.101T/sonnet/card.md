## Analysis recipe

1. **Image lines and continuum**: Self-calibrate on continuum, apply to lines, continuum subtract, tclean multiscale with hand masks, Briggs 0.5, 0.5 km/s channels  
  > "The line data were then continuum subtracted." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Briggs 0.5; 0.5 km/s channels  
  > "Data were imaged with Briggs weighting = 0.5 and re-binned to 0.5 km s$^{-1}$." (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Define velocity regimes**: Examine spectra of several molecules by eye; C18O off-outflow spectra set inner slow-wing limit; fast wing from H2CO decrease and SiO/HCN enhancement; EHV from rise of CO and SiO at high velocity  
  > "Spectra of C$^{18}$O of regions outside the outflow positions were used to assess by eye the velocity at which C$^{18}$O is still significant." (3.2 Velocity regimes)
   - parameter: systemic velocity 8.5 km/s  
  > "with respect to the systemic velocity of 8.5 km s$^{-1}$" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Changing border by 5 km/s between fast wing and EHV changes outflow properties by ~2-10%  
  > "Changing the velocity border by 5 km s$^{-1}$ between the fast wing and the EHV jet typically results in a change of $\sim$ 2–10% in the outflow properties." (3.4 Outflow force)
3. **Column densities and abundances relative to CO**: Optically thin LTE column density per pixel integrated over each velocity regime; average over pixels >3 sigma in hand-defined regions; abundance = N(X)/N(CO)  
  > "the average of the column density within the pre-defined region is calculated from only those pixels with signal above 3 $\sigma$." (3.3.2 Column densities and abundances)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: CO Tex = 75 K; SiO 9-47 K, H2CO 8-46 K, HCN 12-41 K from RADEX  
  > "| SiO | 5-4 | 217.104 | 1.7 x $10^{6}$ | 31.3 | 9 – 47 |" (Table 4)
   - parameter: RADEX grid: nH2 = 1e4-1e6 cm-3; Tkin = 75-700 K; dv = 10 km/s  
  > "n$_{\rm H2}$ = $10^4$ – $10^6$ cm$^{-3}$; T$_{kin}$ = 75 – 700 K; $\Delta \varv = 10$ km s$^{-1}$" (3.3.1 Analysis method)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission; HCN abundances lower limits  
  > "Optically thin emission is assumed for all the molecules." (3.3.1 Analysis method)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: RADEX opacity checks; 20% uncertainty assumed  
  > "We assume that values have 20 % uncertainty arising from calibration, arbitrary defining velocity regimes and other factors." (Tables appendix)
4. **Mass, momentum and outflow force**: Mass from N_tot with H2/CO, mu=2.8; velocity-weighted method M7 for mass loss rate; F_out = Mdot/R_lobe * vmax; fractions per velocity regime  
  > "$$\begin{equation} F_{\rm out} = \frac{\dot{M}}{R_{\rm lobe}}\varv_{\rm max}  \,\,. \end{equation}$$" (3.4 Outflow force)
   - parameter: H2/CO = 1.2e4; mu = 2.8  
  > "with a molecular weight $\mu = 2.8$ that takes helium into account (cite: Kauffmann2008)" (3.4 Outflow force)
   - assumption: No inclination correction applied (factors 1.2-7.1 would change absolute but not relative values)  
  > "This correction largely affects the absolute values of the outflow forces; however, the relative ratios between the velocity components should not be affected" (3.4 Outflow force)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared SMM1 slow+fast wing CO outflow force to single-dish Yildiz2015; consistent within factor of few; temperature test 250/300/700 K  
  > "Our results are thus consistent with single-dish data to within the typical uncertainties of a factor of few" (3.4 Outflow force)

## Qualitative findings

- SiO abundance generally enhanced with velocity; HCN in slow and fast wings but absent in EHV; H2CO mostly low velocity but in one Ser-emb 8 (N) EHV bullet  
  > "The SiO abundance is generally enhanced with velocity, while HCN is present in the slow and the fast wing, but disappears in the EHV jet." (abstract)
- No complex organic molecules associated with outflows  
  > "No complex organic molecules are found to be associated with the outflows." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Single transition per molecule so Tex assumed (CO 75 K; others via RADEX range)  
  > "Since only a single transition of each molecule was observed, it is not possible to derive an excitation temperature from these data." (3.3.1 Analysis method)
- Alternative: Higher excitation temperatures in fast wing and EHV (250/300/700 K) tested; does not change SMM1-a picture but changes SMM1-b and Ser-emb 8 (N)  
  > "For the case of SMM1-a, it does not change the general picture of the EHV jet contributing only a small fraction of the outflow force." (4.1 Jet and wind kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Outflow parameters are local/lower limits since ALMA field does not cover full outflows; inclination uncorrected; absolute forces uncertain by factors of a few  
  > "Their absolute values are therefore uncertain by factors of a few." (Tables appendix)
- Limitation (authors): HCN optically thick so abundances lower limits; H2CO may be underestimated  
  > "thus abundances of this molecule should be treated as lower limits." (3.3.1 Analysis method)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Velocity borders chosen by eye
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tex for SiO/H2CO/HCN unconstrained (factor ~4-5 range)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux resolved out at low velocities and with different resolutions for SiO/H2CO high velocities
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO optically thin assumption and H2/CO factor
