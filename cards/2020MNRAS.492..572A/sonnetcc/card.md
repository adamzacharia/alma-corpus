## Science

- Fields: protoplanetary_disks, stellar_physics
- Question: Are the outer-disc inclinations of dipper stars (resolved by ALMA) concentrated near edge-on, as the accretion-warp picture predicts, or isotropically distributed, implying inner-disc misalignments?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Select dipper sample**: Combine three literature dipper samples, require at least three dips and R_dip > 5.0, then keep those with ALMA-resolved discs  
  > "Combining these samples results in 122 unique dippers." (2.1 Sample); "This cut reduces the sample to 79 dippers." (2.1 Sample)
   - parameter: R_dip > 5.0; high-pass filter cut-on frequency of 1 day^-1; at least three dips  
  > "then require at least three dips to be present in the *K2*/C2 light curves and apply the $R_{\rm dip} > 5.0$ selection criteria" (2.1 Sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Re-extract K2 light curves**: Modified Pixel Level Decorrelation with hand-selected apertures and simultaneous Gaussian Process fit  
  > "we use a modified Pixel Level Decorrelation (PLD) method to remove the roll motion noise while preserving intrinsic, astrophysical variability." (2.2 K2 Light Curves); "Along with the weights, we also simultaneously fit a Gaussian Process to the light curve in order to capture the astrophysical variability of the dippers" (2.2 K2 Light Curves)
   - software: lightkurve
   - parameter: Each light curve split at cadence number 97682 with weights fitted separately to each half; fourth-order polynomials in time and in centroids  
  > "We split each light curve in half (at cadence number 97682) and fit the weights separately to each half" (2.2 K2 Light Curves)
   - fitting: Weights derived with linear algebra, plus Gaussian Process; kernel not stated  
  > "where optimum weights are derived for each component of the matrix using linear algebra" (2.2 K2 Light Curves)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit disc inclinations to ALMA visibilities**: Fit 2D Gaussian disc models directly to visibilities with GPU GALARIO inside emcee MCMC  
  > "which allows us to quickly fit 2D disc models to the ALMA visibilities by enabling the rapid exploration of parameter space" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations); "Because of its modular structure, we can use" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
   - software: GALARIO, emcee, CASA
   - parameter: 120 walkers and 5000 steps; accepted fraction 0.2-0.5  
  > "The fits typically use 120 walkers and 5000 steps for the MCMC" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations); "the mean accepted fraction of steps are all between 0.2 and 0.5, implying that the chains are converging." (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
   - parameter: Six free parameters: flux normalisation, FWHM along semi-major axis, inclination, position angle, RA and Dec offsets  
  > "we most often fit the visibilities using a simple 2D Gaussian disc model with six free parameters" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
   - parameter: Two Gaussians fit simultaneously for resolved binary discs; extra inner cutoff radius for four cavity discs  
  > "we simultaneously fit two 2D Gaussians to the data." (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations); "but with one more free parameter, an inner cutoff radius" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
   - assumption: Simple 2D Gaussian disc morphology; model-dependent uncertainty estimated at about 1 degree or less based on DSHARP comparison  
  > "This suggests that any effects from fitting the simple 2D Gaussian models in this work" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
   - fitting: emcee ensemble MCMC with GALARIO likelihood; priors not stated (inclination bounded to 0-90 degrees)  
  > "even if the priors are constrained to one period" (Rejected Dippers with Inclinations Reported in Literature)
   - validation: Inclinations match DSHARP-derived values of Huang et al. within about 1 degree for four overlapping dippers  
  > "This instills general confidence in the precision of our inclinations, which are derived from simple models of more moderate resolution data." (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
4. **Check stellar multiplicity**: Use AO imaging, Gaia DR2 parallax/proper motion and RUWE, and ALMA disc morphology to flag blended or binary systems  
  > "We flag these systems in the remaining analysis throughout this paper." (3.3 Stellar Multiplicity); "Large RUWE values ($>$1.4) are indicative of an unresolved companion" (3.3 Stellar Multiplicity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Test isotropy of the inclination distribution**: Draw inclinations from per-source posteriors, convert to cos(i), build ECDF 1000 times and compare with a uniform cos(i) sample using two-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests  
  > "We then repeat this 1000 times and take the mean and standard deviation as the final values and associated uncertainties of the ECDF." (4.1 Outer Disc Inclination Distribution); "p-values of two-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests" (4.1 Outer Disc Inclination Distribution)
   - software: statsmodels (ECDF)
   - parameter: Isotropic comparison: uniform in cos(i) between 0.26 and 1.0 (0-75 deg) with same sample size; KS only for cos(i) > 0.26  
  > "randomly sampling from a uniform distribution between 0.26 and 1.0" (4.1 Outer Disc Inclination Distribution)
   - assumption: Deficit above 75 degrees attributed to observational selection against edge-on discs  
  > "The deficit of highly inclined systems is an expected observational bias" (4.1 Outer Disc Inclination Distribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Result holds when the three binary discs are removed  
  > "this result holds even when removing the three binary discs" (4.1 Outer Disc Inclination Distribution)
6. **Quantify selection bias against edge-on discs**: MCFOST SED grid over six disc parameters at 15 inclinations; fraction of models with total flux at least 40 times weaker than the star taken as undetected by K2  
  > "then compute the fraction of models at a given inclination for which the flux from the system is at least 40 times weaker than that of the host star" (MCFOST Model Grid)
   - software: MCFOST
   - parameter: Mdust 1e-6 to 1e-3 Msun; Rc = 10, 30, 100 au; flaring 1.0-1.30; H0 = 5-20 au at 100 au; gamma 0-1.5; amax 10 to 10000 um  
  > "We also fix the stellar effective temperature" (MCFOST Model Grid)
   - assumption: Mie scattering, astronomical silicates, Teff = 4000 K, Rin = 0.1 au, amin = 0.01 um; factor 40 from median Kp ~ 13 mag and Kepler faint limit Kp ~ 17 mag  
  > "while assuming Mie scattering and astronomical silicates (cite: DL1984) for the dust grain properties" (MCFOST Model Grid)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with the observed deficit of high inclinations  
  > "consistent with Figure [3](#fig:inc_dist)" (4.1 Outer Disc Inclination Distribution)
7. **Test correlations with light curve and WISE excess**: Compare inclination with Q and M light-curve statistics and with W2, W3, W4 excess using Spearman rank tests  
  > "Figure [5](#fig:qm) illustrates that there is no perceptible correlation between disc inclination and light curve morphology" (4.2 Correlations with Light Curve and Disc Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Dipper outer-disc inclinations are consistent with isotropic over 0-75 degrees, with a deficit above that attributed to selection  
  > "We find that dipper disc inclinations are consistent with an isotropic distribution" (abstract)
- Dipper discs show diverse morphologies: compact, extended, cavities and azimuthal asymmetries  
  > "dipper discs exhibit a wide range of morphologies" (3.2 Disc Morphologies)
- No perceptible correlation between disc inclination and light-curve morphology (Q, M)  
  > "there is no perceptible correlation between disc inclination and light curve morphology" (4.2 Correlations with Light Curve and Disc Properties)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Targeted high-resolution programme plus archival surveys; archival data with higher resolution used when available  
  > "We also use data from selected archival ALMA programmes when they offered higher spatial resolution." (2.3 Sub-mm/mm ALMA Data)
- Why this method: GALARIO+emcee gives uniform treatment, handles cavity discs and provides posteriors useful for the population analysis  
  > "also does not provide posterior distributions that are useful for our analysis of the disc inclination distribution" (3.1 Outer Disc Inclinations)
- Alternative: Marginally resolved discs excluded because emcee struggles with multi-modal inclination posteriors  
  > "We do not include these marginally resolved sources in our sample because the key algorithm implemented in" (Rejected Dippers with Inclinations Reported in Literature)
- Alternative: Dippers with poorly resolved discs from Barenfeld et al. (large literature uncertainties) excluded; including them would not change results  
  > "would not change our overall results" (Rejected Dippers with Inclinations Reported in Literature)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Spectral types from inhomogeneous literature sources; WISE analysis should be repeated with homogeneous types  
  > "this analysis should be repeated once homogenous spectral types are derived for our sample" (4.2 Correlations with Light Curve and Disc Properties)
- Limitation (authors): Spatial resolution, even DSHARP, is insufficient to resolve small inner cavities  
  > "are insufficient to resolve such small inner cavities" (4.3 Expectations from Theory)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gaussian-disc inclinations of marginally resolved sources may be biased high or poorly constrained; only four DSHARP sources were used to validate
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Posterior sampling for the isotropy test does not propagate the multi-modal inclination posteriors the authors admit emcee handles poorly
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Selection of 'resolved' discs is itself a function of inclination and size, which could bias the distribution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 0.26 cutoff in cos(i) is chosen from the observed deficit rather than independently motivated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample (24, three flagged binaries) limits statistical power of the KS test; a non-rejection does not prove isotropy
