## Analysis recipe

1. **Process HST imaging and align**: DrizzlePac TweakReg/AstroDrizzle aligned to COSMOS F814W frame, TweakBack, resampled with SWarp to 0.06''/pix  
  > "The final drizzled images in the three bands were resampled to a common grid and a pixel scale of 0.06$''$ pix$^{-1}$ using SWarp" (2.2 HST Data)
   - software: DrizzlePac, SExtractor, SWarp
   - parameter: final_scale = 0.06, final_pixfrac = 0.8  
  > "$\rm{\texttt{final\_scale}} = 0.06$ and $\rm{\texttt{final\_pixfrac}} = 0.8$" (2.2 HST Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: absolute astrometric accuracy 0.05-0.1'' via COSMOS WCS  
  > "ensuring an absolute astrometric accuracy of 0.05$''$–0.1$''$ or better" (2.2 HST Data)
2. **Image ALMA continuum and measure fluxes**: CLEAN MFS imaging; 2D Gaussian fits with PyBDSF  
  > "Their fluxes were measured via 2D Gaussian fits using the python package `PyBDSF`" (2.3 ALMA Data)
   - software: CASA, PyBDSF
   - parameter: Briggs robust = -1.0  
  > "Briggs weighting scheme with a robust parameter of -1.0" (2.3 ALMA Data)
   - assumption: Rayleigh-Jeans slope 3.5 for conversion to 850 um  
  > "assuming a standard Rayleigh-Jeans slope of 3.5" (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with S2COSMOS SCUBA2 850 um fluxes  
  > "These fluxes are consistent with those measured 850 $\mu$m fluxes from the S2COSMOS/SCUBA2 survey" (2.3 ALMA Data)
3. **PSF matching and adaptive smoothing of HST**: IRAF PSFMATCH kernels to F160W (0.18'' FWHM) and to ALMA beam; ADAPTSMOOTH with min S/N=5, max smoothing 2 pixels  
  > "We required a minimum $\rm{S/N} = 5$ and a maximum smoothing length of two neighboring pixels in the code." (2.5 Adaptative Smoothing)
   - software: IRAF PSFMATCH, ADAPTSMOOTH
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Matched PSF FWHMs deviate by <2%  
  > "The matched PSFs FWHM in the different bands deviate by less than 2%." (2.4 PSF Matching)
4. **UV slope maps and SFR_UV**: Linear fit of slope in pixels with S/N>2 in at least two smoothed images; ~10000 Monte Carlo realizations for uncertainties; SFR_UV via Salim et al. 2007  
  > "constructed by fitting a linear slope to pixels which have $\rm{S/N} > 2$ detections in at least two smoothed images" (4.1 Spatially Resolved UV Slopes)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; observed UV not extinction corrected  
  > "$SFR_{\rm{UV}}$ derived this way corresponds to the observed value, i.e., not corrected from extinction." (4.1 Spatially Resolved UV Slopes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Deblended photometry and SED fitting for stellar masses**: Aperture photometry g-Ks; GALFIT PSF deblending of IRAC; LePHARE fit of 13-band SEDs  
  > "We fitted the resulting 13-band SEDs ($g$ to 4.5 $\mu$m, including the three *HST* bands) using LePHARE" (3.2 SED Fitting)
   - software: GALFIT, LePHARE
   - parameter: e-folding 0.1-30 Gyr; 0<A_V<5; age 1 Myr to age of Universe; Z=0.004, 0.008, 0.02  
  > "SFH e-folding times (0.1 Gyr–-30 Gyr), extinction ($0 < A_{V} < 5$), stellar age (1 Myr–age of the Universe at the source redshift)" (3.2 SED Fitting)
   - assumption: BC03 models with emission lines, Chabrier IMF, exponentially declining SFH, Calzetti dust law, redshift fixed  
  > "A (cite: 2003PASP..115..763C) IMF, exponentially declining star formation histories (SFHs) and a (cite: 2000ApJ...533..682C) dust law were assumed." (3.2 SED Fitting)
   - fitting: LePHARE grid; IRAC uncertainties from realizations varying centroids within 1 pix  
  > "performing a number of realizations varying the centroid coordinates randomly within 1 pix of the best fit centroid" (3.2 SED Fitting)
   - validation: Compared to MAGPHYS, 3D-HST and COSMOS2015 masses  
  > "This is $\sim 0.4$ dex higher than our derived median value for the most massive component." (5.2 Comparison to Previous Stellar Mass Estimates)
6. **Measure FIR sizes**: GALFIT Sérsic fits to ALMA continuum, n=1 and b/a=1 fixed; cross-check with UVMULTIFIT in uv-plane  
  > "we fixed $n = 1$ to report the rest-frame FIR sizes for our sample in Table [4]" (4.2 FIR Sizes)
   - software: GALFIT, UVMULTIFIT
   - parameter: n = 0.5, 1, 4 and free tested; errors add systematics from n and b/a in quadrature  
  > "computed by adding in quadrature the statistical uncertainty from `GALFIT` for the circular disk model and the difference between this model and the full range of models" (4.2 FIR Sizes)
   - assumption: Exponential disk motivated by Hodge et al. 2016  
  > "Motivated by this, we fixed $n = 1$" (4.2 FIR Sizes)
   - fitting: GALFIT least-squares; sampler/priors not stated  
  > "We measured the sizes of the rest-frame FIR dust continuum emission by modeling the ALMA continuum images using `GALFIT`. Sérsic and PSF profiles were fitted to compare both resolved and unresolved modeling of the objects." (4.2 FIR Sizes)
   - validation: UVMULTIFIT sizes in agreement with GALFIT  
  > "We find that these estimates are in agreement with the results derived in the reconstructed images using `GALFIT`" (4.2 FIR Sizes)
7. **Derive surface densities, extinction and IRX-beta**: Sigma_SFR, Sigma_dust from half of SFR/dust mass within r_e; mean A_V from sheet model; IRX in large vs ALMA apertures  
  > "We calculated the expected extinction assuming that the dust is distributed in a sheet with uniform density." (4.3 UV/FIR Spatial Disconnection)
   - assumption: GDR = 90; N_H/A_V = 2.2e21 cm^-2 mag^-1; DL07 optically thin dust  
  > "GDR) appropriate for SMGs of $\rm{GDR} = 90$ (cite: 2014MNRAS.438.1267S), and the gas surface number density-to-extinction ratio $N_{\rm{H}}/A_{V} = 2.2 \times 10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$ mag$^{-1}$" (4.3 UV/FIR Spatial Disconnection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Mass-size evolution to cQGs**: Compare SMG stellar mass and FIR r_e to cQGs; add expected growth from starburst (tau_gas 42 Myr) and minor mergers  
  > "The combined average stellar mass and size growth anticipated from completion of the starburst and the minor mergers is shown as the bottom-right solid arrow" (6)
   - parameter: minor merger timescale 0.49±0.24 Gyr; mean 1.2 mergers of ratio 6.5  
  > "The median stellar mass ratio of the ongoing minor mergers is 6.5 and the average number of them is 1.2." (6)
   - assumption: FIR size traces final stellar mass location; depletion time from Toft et al. 2014  
  > "(cite: 2014ApJ...782...68T) derived a depletion time-scale of $\tau_{\rm{gas}} = 42_{-0.29}^{+0.40}$ Myr" (6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistency within uncertainties with cQG mass and size  
  > "bringing the two populations into agreement within the uncertainties" (6)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: FIR size traces the starburst region and is the best proxy for the location of the final stellar mass  
  > "The FIR size traces the region where the starburst is taking place" (6)
- Alternative: cSFGs as alternative or intermediate progenitors of cQGs  
  > "Two different progenitor populations are not necessarily mutually exclusive." (7 Discussion)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Small samples; selection biases; stellar masses uncertain due to dust  
  > "These conclusions are based on small samples for both the SMGs and cQGs, possibly subject to selection biases" (7 Discussion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample of only six galaxies
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar masses depend on SED assumptions and possible hidden mass
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Merger status inferred without kinematics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sersic n and axis ratio unconstrained at resolution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Growth factors depend on assumed gas depletion time from earlier work
