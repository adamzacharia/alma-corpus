## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate the 12 m and ACA data**: Modified ALMA pipeline plus in-house Tsys/normalization routine; self-calibration on line-free continuum per configuration; solutions applied to all channels; cross-configuration alignment of amplitude and phase  
  > "using a modified version of the ALMA calibration pipeline and an additional in-house calibration routine (Mollenbrock et al. in prep)" (Observations); "The complex gain corrections derived from the self calibration were then applied to all channels in the data" (Observations)
   - software: CASA 5.4.1
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image continuum-subtracted line cubes**: tclean with Briggs weighting, primary beam correction, combining 12 m and 7 m data  
  > "Images were prepared by using the *tclean* task in CASA, where Briggs weighting with a robustness parameter of 0.5 was employed." (Observations)
   - software: CASA tclean
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "where Briggs weighting with a robustness parameter of 0.5 was employed." (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Arc-like structure checked to lie within the ACA field of view although near the edge of the 12 m field  
  > "Although the arc-like structure is near the edge of the field of view for the ALMA 12 m data, it lies within the field of view of ACA data." (3.2 Arc-Like Structure toward Southeast Direction)
3. **Map morphology with moment 0, channel maps and spectra at positions A-D**: Moment 0 maps and velocity channel maps of H2CO, SO, SiO, CH3OH, C18O; spectra at four positions in the arc and at the protostar  
  > "show the moment 0 maps of the H$_{2}$CO and SO line emission, respectively." (3.1 Primary Outflow along the Northeast to Southwest Direction); "These features are also apparent in the molecular line profiles (Figure [3](#spectral)) toward four positions within the arc-like structure and at the protostellar position" (3.2 Arc-Like Structure toward Southeast Direction)
   - assumption: Systemic velocity 5.2 km/s  
  > "where the systemic velocity of the protostellar core is 5.2 km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Yen et al.(2017))" (3.2 Arc-Like Structure toward Southeast Direction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Lack of C18O emission and continuum at the arc location used to argue it is not a dense prestellar core  
  > "sub-mm continuum emission is not detected at this location in our data sets" (3.3 Shocks in the Arc-Like Structure)
4. **Measure the velocity gradient along the linear structure**: Position-velocity diagrams of H2CO and C18O along P.A. 140 deg centred on the protostar  
  > "presents the position-velocity (PV) diagram for the H$_{2}$CO emission along the northwest to southeast axis (P.A. 140$^\circ$), centered at the protostellar position." (3.4 Linear Structure along the Northwest to Southeast Direction)
   - parameter: Cut P.A. 140 deg  
  > "along the northwest to southeast axis (P.A. 140$^\circ$), centered at the protostellar position." (3.4 Linear Structure along the Northwest to Southeast Direction)
   - assumption: Systemic velocity 5.2 km/s  
  > "assuming that the systemic velocity is 5.2 km s$^{-1}$" (3.4 Linear Structure along the Northwest to Southeast Direction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Similar velocity structure seen in the C18O PV diagram  
  > "A similar velocity structure can be seen in the PV diagram for the C$^{18}$O emission" (3.4 Linear Structure along the Northwest to Southeast Direction)
5. **Estimate dynamical timescale of the secondary outflow**: Distance over velocity: 1200 au and ~1.2 km/s give ~5000 cot(i) yr  
  > "The plane of the sky distance and the line-of-sight velocity shift from the systemic velocity are 1200 au and $\sim$1.2 km s$^{-1}$, respectively." (4.1 Dynamical Timescale of the Outflow and Outflow Mass)
   - assumption: Inclination i unknown (0 deg pole-on); axis may be near the plane of the sky so the timescale is an upper limit  
  > "the axis may be close to the plane of the sky ($i=$90$^\circ$) and the dynamical timescale is regarded as an upper limit" (4.1 Dynamical Timescale of the Outflow and Outflow Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SiO depletion time 1e3-1e4 yr and primary outflow timescale 1e2-1e3 yr  
  > "Hence, the secondary outflow would likely have formed before the primary outflow." (4.1 Dynamical Timescale of the Outflow and Outflow Mass)
6. **Estimate gas mass of secondary and primary outflows**: Mass from apparent size and an assumed H2 density  
  > "where we use the apparent size and an assumed H$_2$ density of 10$^{5}-10^{6}$ cm$^{-3}$ based on the critical densities of the observed lines" (4.1 Dynamical Timescale of the Outflow and Outflow Mass)
   - assumption: H2 density 1e5-1e6 cm^-3 from critical densities  
  > "an assumed H$_2$ density of 10$^{5}-10^{6}$ cm$^{-3}$ based on the critical densities of the observed lines" (4.1 Dynamical Timescale of the Outflow and Outflow Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Derive column densities and abundance ratios relative to C18O**: Non-LTE RADEX column densities at positions A-D and the protostar; abundance ratios relative to C18O at the same temperature; C18O upper limits from other lines' intensity and mean line width  
  > "by using the non-LTE radiative transfer code, RADEX" (4.2 Molecular Abundances around the Arc-like Structure); "Then, the abundance ratios relative to C$^{18}$O are evaluated from the column densities, where the same temperature is assumed for the molecular species and C$^{18}$O." (4.2 Molecular Abundances around the Arc-like Structure)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: Tgas 20-80 K, n(H2) 1e5-1e6 cm^-3 in the arc; 54 K and 1e5-1e7 cm^-3 at the protostar  
  > "assuming that the gas temperature is 20$-$80 K and the H$_2$ density is 10$^5-10^6$ cm$^{-3}$" (4.2 Molecular Abundances around the Arc-like Structure)
   - assumption: C18O emission at the systemic velocity may be resolved out, but the arc (blue-shifted 0.5 km/s) should not suffer; nominal C18O abundance 1.7e-7 for X(SiO)  
  > "the resolved-out effect should not affect the molecular abundances significantly" (4.2 Molecular Abundances around the Arc-like Structure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Ratios compared with L1157-B1 and HH7-11 (SVS13-A)  
  > "Moreover, the abundance ratios are as high as those reported for L1157-B1" (4.2 Molecular Abundances around the Arc-like Structure)
8. **Test the reorientation scenario with a shock dissipation timescale**: t_dis = (8/3)(rho/rho1)(r/v) with v = 10 km/s, r = 300 au, density ratio 10, giving ~4000 yr  
  > "In the case of IRAS 15398$-$3359, we assume the shock velocity to be 10 km s$^{-1}$ and employ the size of the shocked region of 300 au" (5.2 Scenario 2: Outflow Outburst and Reorientation)
   - assumption: Density ratio rho/rho1 = 10  
  > "In addition, the $\rho/\rho{_1}$ ratio is assumed to be 10." (5.2 Scenario 2: Outflow Outburst and Reorientation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparable to the secondary outflow dynamical timescale  
  > "This timescale is comparable to the dynamical timescale of the secondary outflow" (5.2 Scenario 2: Outflow Outburst and Reorientation)

## Qualitative findings

- SO, SiO and CH3OH trace an arc-like shocked structure SE of the protostar, blue-shifted from the systemic velocity  
  > "The arc-like structure is blue-shifted with respect to the systemic velocity." (abstract)
- The arc-like structure is not seen in C18O and has no continuum counterpart, so it is not a dense prestellar core  
  > "sub-mm continuum emission is not detected at this location in our data sets" (3.3 Shocks in the Arc-Like Structure)

- Why this data: Source selected for the FAUST Large Program because of its unique physical and chemical characteristics; shock tracers included to probe outflow-ambient gas interaction  
  > "Due to these unique physical and chemical characteristics, IRAS 15398$-$3359 was selected as a target of the ALMA Large Program FAUST" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Arc as part of an adjacent prestellar core rejected from low C18O column density  
  > "the low C$^{18}$O column density (calculated in Section 4.2) ensures that the arc-like structure is not part of an adjacent prestellar core." (3.2 Arc-Like Structure toward Southeast Direction)
- Alternative: Linear structure as gas accreting toward the protostar rejected in favor of outflow  
  > "strongly suggest that these features are part of an outflow structure, rather than a structure formed from gas accreting toward the protostar" (3.4 Linear Structure along the Northwest to Southeast Direction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only lower limits on abundance ratios at the four arc positions  
  > "Only lower limits are obtained for the abundance ratios at all the four positions." (4.2 Molecular Abundances around the Arc-like Structure)

  > "Since the maximum recoverable scales are 12.8$''$, any structures extended more than that size could be resolved-out." (Observations)
- Limitation (authors): Absence of a red-shifted component to the northwest is unexplained  
  > "A lack of a red-shifted component in the northwestern part is puzzling" (3.4 Linear Structure along the Northwest to Southeast Direction)
