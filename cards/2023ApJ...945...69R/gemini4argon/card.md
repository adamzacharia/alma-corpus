## Analysis recipe

1. **Split target fields, standardize visibility weights, and combine multi-cycle MSs**: Extract target galaxy fields with split, adjust visibility weights of Cycle 1 [C II] data using statwt (excluding serendipitous CO(5-4) channels), and combine MSs across cycles using concat.  
  > "We used the split task to pick out the fields containing the target galaxy and the concat task to combine different MSs." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging); "thus, we used the statwt task to adjust the visibility weights of the Cycle 1 [C II ] data set before combining it with data from later cycles." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: fitspw = all spws excluding serendipitous CO (J=5-4) channels from SSTSL2 J021856.14-051951.4  
  > "We input all the spws in the fitspw parameter while excluding channels containing the serendipitous CO (J = 5–4)" (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Create spectral line cubes and dust continuum images**: Image line cubes and continuum with tclean using natural weighting. Apply uv-tapering from 0.1" to 0.5" (step 0.1") on combined Cycles 2+3 [O III] data to maximize S/N. Exclude line frequency ranges when imaging Band 8 and Band 6 continuum.  
  > "Then, we used the tclean task to create cubes and continuum images." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging); "We applied a uv-taper using taper FWHMs from 0 1 to 0 5 with a step of 0 1." (3.1.1. Line Detection and Total Flux); "To make continuum images, we excluded channels covering the integral frequency ranges of the Cycles 2 + 3 [O III ] and Cycles 1 + 3 + 7 [C II ] data to avoid influence from any line flux." (2.4. Continuum Images)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: [O III] bin width = 20 km s^-1; [C II] bin width = 25 km s^-1  
  > "The bin width of the [O III ] 88 μm emission is 20 km s −1, which is the same as used by Inoue et al. (2016), and that of the [C II ] 158 μm emission is 25 km s −1 ." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging)
   - parameter: weighting = natural, outframe = BARY, veltype = optical  
  > "We used natural weighting for both cube and continuum images." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging); "we defined the velocities in the barycentric frame by setting the outframe parameter to be “BARY,” and the velocity type to be the optical velocity by setting the veltype parameter to be “optical.”" (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging)
   - parameter: Optimal [O III] Cycles 2+3 uv-taper = 0.3"  
  > "We found the highest S/N of 5.9σ with a 0 3 uv-taper (panel (d) of Figure 1)." (3.1.1. Line Detection and Total Flux)
   - assumption: z = 7.212; rest-frame frequencies 3393.006 GHz ([O III] 88 μm) and 1900.54 GHz ([C II] 158 μm)  
  > "by considering a redshift of 7.212, which is determined by the [O III ] 88 μm emission reported by Inoue et al. (2016), and their rest-frame frequencies, 3393.006 GHz and 1900.54 GHz, respectively." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked PSFs of Cycles 2+3 [O III] and Cycles 1+3+7 [C II] cubes, confirming well-distributed 2D Gaussians matching the synthesized beams.  
  > "We also checked the point-spread functions (PSFs) of the Cycles 2 + 3 [O III ] and Cycles 1 + 3 + 7 [C II ] data cubes. Both of them are well-distributed 2D Gaussians with the same shape as the beams." (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging)
3. **Generate integrated intensity (moment-0) maps for emission lines and sub-clumps**: Integrate data cubes over velocity using immoments for full lines and individual velocity-optimized clumps, masking atmospheric ozone channels at ~231.28 GHz and testing removal of a suspicious edge velocity bin in [C II].  
  > "From the cube images, we made integrated intensity (moment-0) maps by using the immoments task." (2.3. Emission Line Images); "When an atmospheric ozone line at ∼231.28 GHz was included in the integrated velocity range, we masked the corresponding channels." (2.3. Emission Line Images); "Therefore, we performed detailed integrals over velocity to get a better detection for each clump (see the detailed spectra in Figure 2)." (3.1.3. Detailed Integration of the Cycle 3 Data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: [O III] velocity integration ranges: -310 to +250 km s^-1 (Cycle 3), -280 to +260 km s^-1 (Cycles 2+3); detailed clump ranges in Table 4  
  > "we set the integrated velocity range for the Cycle 3 [O III ] data to be −310 to +250 km s −1 . The integrated velocity range for the combined [O III ] data of Cycle 2 and Cycle 3 is −280 to +260 km s −1" (2.3. Emission Line Images)
   - parameter: [C II] velocity integration ranges: -80 to +145 km s^-1 (Cycle 1, Cycles 1+3, Cycle 7, Cycles 1+3+7), -70 to +155 km s^-1 (Cycle 3), -80 to +120 km s^-1 (Cycles 1+3+7 without edge bin), +70 to +170 km s^-1 (SE), -80 to +170 km s^-1 (NW)  
  > "we integrated from −80 to +145 km s −1 for the Cycle 1, Cycles 1+3, Cycle 7, and Cycles 1 + 3 + 7 [C II ] data sets, and from −70 to +155 km s −1 for the Cycle 3 [C II ] data set." (2.3. Emission Line Images); "In this case, we also tried an integral of −80 to +120 km s −1 for the Cycles 1 + 3 + 7 [C II ] data without this bin." (2.3. Emission Line Images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested robustness of 4.5σ [C II] detection by excluding the +120 to +145 km s^-1 spike channel, resulting in a 3.6σ detection.  
  > "(i.e., avoiding the exceptionally bright channel). The resulting S/N decreases to 3.6σ and the integrated flux decreases to 0.051 ± 0.027 Jy km s" (3.2.2. Possible Fluctuation of the [C II] Detection Signal)
4. **Measure integrated fluxes, deconvolved source sizes, line profiles, and background rms**: Fit 2D Gaussians to sources in moment-0 maps using imfit (or manual FWHM contour deconvolution for 3.6σ [C II]), fit 1D Gaussians to peak-pixel spectra using specfit, and calculate background rms via 10 iterations of 3σ clipping around the median.  
  > "We used the imfit task to measure the integrated fluxes and beam deconvolved source sizes (FWHM) by fitting 2D Gaussian functions to sources in the images." (2.5. Flux Measurements); "We also used the specfit task to measure the FWHM velocities of the emission lines by fitting one or more 1D Gaussian functions to the spectrum extracted from a single pixel that has the largest brightness" (2.5. Flux Measurements); "To be specific, we measure the median and standard deviation of the image, then remove outliers out of the range of (median ±3σ). After that, we let this procedure iterate ten times" (2.5. Flux Measurements)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: specfit ngauss = 2 (clump A), ngauss = 3 (clumps C' and C'')  
  > "When we performed Gaussian fitting on signal A using the specfit task, we set the parameter ngauss to equal to 2" (3.1.2. High-resolution [O III ] 88 μm Image from the Cycle 3 Data); "We set ngauss equal to 3 when performing the Gaussian fitting on signals C å and C åå" (3.1.3. Detailed Integration of the Cycle 3 Data)
   - parameter: 3σ clipping iterated 10 times for background rms  
  > "To be specific, we measure the median and standard deviation of the image, then remove outliers out of the range of (median ±3σ). After that, we let this procedure iterate ten times to get a more accurate background noise level." (2.5. Flux Measurements)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fitting with imfit and 1D multi-Gaussian fitting with specfit  
  > "We used the imfit task to measure the integrated fluxes and beam deconvolved source sizes (FWHM) by fitting 2D Gaussian functions to sources in the images." (2.5. Flux Measurements); "We also used the specfit task to measure the FWHM velocities of the emission lines by fitting one or more 1D Gaussian functions to the spectrum" (2.5. Flux Measurements)
   - validation: Compared sum of Cycle 3 clump fluxes with tapered Cycles 2+3 total flux and Cycle 2 flux from Inoue et al. (2016), confirming consistency within 1-2σ.  
  > "From this table, the sum of the integrated fluxes of A and B is 0.403 ± 0.138 Jy km s −1, and the sum of A, B, and C is 0.637 ± 0.168 Jy km s −1 . Both of them are consistent with the total integrated flux of the 5.9σ detection (Section 3.1.1) within the 1σ confidence level." (3.1.2. High-resolution [O III ] 88 μm Image from the Cycle 3 Data)
5. **Derive upper limits on total IR luminosity and dust mass**: Integrate a modified blackbody function over 8-1000 μm for L_TIR and apply Equation (22) of Inoue et al. (2020) to the Band 6 3σ flux density upper limit for dust mass.  
  > "To calculate the total IR luminosity, we assumed the same dust temperature T dust (40 K) and emissivity index β (1.5) as Inoue et al. (2016), as well as using a modified blackbody function integrated over the 8–1000 μm wavelength range" (3.3. Dust Continuum Emission); "To calculate the dust mass, we used Equation (22) of Inoue et al. (2020) and the flux density in Band 6 with the same dust temperature and emissivity index as above." (3.3. Dust Continuum Emission)
   - parameter: Integration wavelength range = 8-1000 μm  
  > "as well as using a modified blackbody function integrated over the 8–1000 μm wavelength range" (3.3. Dust Continuum Emission)
   - assumption: T_dust = 40 K, β = 1.5, κ_ν = 30 cm^2 g^-1 x (100 μm / λ)^β, omitting CMB temperature effect  
  > "To calculate the total IR luminosity, we assumed the same dust temperature T dust (40 K) and emissivity index β (1.5) as Inoue et al. (2016)" (3.3. Dust Continuum Emission); "We also omitted the effect of the CMB temperature and assumed the mass absorption coefficient to be κ ν = 30 cm 2 g −1 × (100 μm/λ) β (Inoue et al. 2020)." (3.3. Dust Continuum Emission)
   - assumption: H_0 = 70 km s^-1 Mpc^-1, Ω_M = 0.3, Ω_Λ = 0.7  
  > "In this paper, we assume H 0 = 70 km s −1 Mpc −1, Ω M = 0.3, Ω Λ = 0.7, M e = 2.0 × 10 30 kg, and L e = 3.83 × 10 26 W, where appliable." (1. Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- High-resolution Cycle 3 ALMA observations reveal a clumpy structure in [O III] 88 μm emission with two or three marginal (>3σ) clumps on scales of 0.32-0.85 kpc.  
  > "The follow-up ALMA observations with higher angular resolution and sensitivity reveal a clumpy structure of the [O III ] emission on a scale of 0.32–0.85 kpc." (abstract); "With the high angular resolution and sensitivity observations, the [O III ] 88 μm emission shows a clumpy structure, having two or three components with marginal >3σ detections." (5. Conclusion)
- [C II] 158 μm emission is unstable across individual ALMA cycles (undetected in Cycle 7 REBELS data despite similar sensitivity to Cycle 3) and yields a 4.5σ (or 3.6σ without an edge spike channel) detection when combined.  
  > "Although the integrated frequency range is almost the same for each cycle data, the [C II ] emission signal is very unstable as seen in Figure 5." (3.2.1. Line Detection); "On the other hand, we cannot find any signal in the Cycle 7 data, although the angular resolution and sensitivity are very similar to those of Cycle 3." (3.2.1. Line Detection)
- The non-detection of [C II] in the REBELS large program data implies incompleteness in spectral-scan surveys using [C II] to detect high-z galaxies with high SFRs but marginal [C II] emission.  
  > "The non-detection of [C II ] with data from the REBELS large program implies the incompleteness of spectral-scan surveys using [C II ] to detect galaxies with high star formation rates (SFRs) but marginal [C II ] emission at high-z." (abstract)
- The [O III], [C II], and Lyα emissions spatially overlap, with [C II] extending up to ~10 kpc and enclosing the [O III] star-forming clumps.  
  > "From this figure, we can see that the [O III ], [C II ] and Lyα emission overlap with each other." (4.4. The Nature of SXDF-NB1006-2); "The [C II ] emission, which predominantly arises from H I gas, is further extended on a scale of ∼10 kpc." (4.4. The Nature of SXDF-NB1006-2)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Previously unanalysed high-resolution Cycle 3 [O III] data and Cycle 7 REBELS [C II] data were combined with previously studied Cycle 2 [O III] and Cycles 1 & 3 [C II] data to resolve controversy over [C II] detection, SBD effects, and the L_[C II]-SFR relation in SXDF-NB1006-2.  
  > "In this work, we reanalysed the ALMA archival data used in previous works, and we also combined it with additional ALMA archival data that has never been analysed before to give updated measurements" (1. Introduction)
- Why this method: Used statwt to standardize Cycle 1 visibility weights with later CASA versions before concatenation; applied a 0.3" uv-taper on Cycles 2+3 [O III] data to recover total extended emission with maximum S/N; used 3σ clipping to measure background noise accurately.  
  > "To combine the image data from different cycles accurately, one needs to ensure that the visibility weights of all data are proportional to 1 s ij 2 ; 16 thus, we used the statwt task to adjust the visibility weights of the Cycle 1 [C II ] data set" (2.2. Data Processing and Imaging); "To measure the total flux of the [O III ] 88 μm emission line integrated over the whole galaxy, we created lower angular resolution images of the Cycles 2 + 3 [O III ] data by applying a uv-taper." (3.1.1. Line Detection and Total Flux)
- Alternative: Evaluated whether clump C (outside the Cycle 2 2σ contour) belongs to SXDF-NB1006-2 or not, and evaluated both 4.5σ (with edge channel) and 3.6σ (without edge channel) [C II] integrations.  
  > "Therefore, from the observed fluxes, we cannot conclude if signal C belongs to the total [O III ] 88 μm emission." (3.1.2. High-resolution [O III ] 88 μm Image from the Cycle 3 Data); "Therefore, we conclude that the robustness of our 4.5σ [C II ] detection is not strong, and we will use both the 4.5σ and 3.6σ detections to discuss the [O III ]/[C II ] luminosity ratio" (3.2.2. Possible Fluctuation of the [C II ] Detection Signal)
- Alternative: Considered Lyα-based SFR corrected for escape fractions (~50-100 M_sun yr^-1) as an alternative to the SED-derived SFR.  
  > "We also plotted our result with the Lyα-based SFR corrected for the Lyα escape fraction, which shows a weaker [C II ] deficit than what we found when using the SED-derived SFR." (4.3. L [C II ] −SFR relation)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Cannot determine whether [O III] clump C belongs to the galaxy, nor rule out that high-resolution interferometric observations make a smooth disk appear clumpy.  
  > "Therefore, from the observed fluxes, we cannot conclude if signal C belongs to the total [O III ] 88 μm emission." (3.1.2. High-resolution [O III ] 88 μm Image from the Cycle 3 Data); "However, it is possible that a smooth disk can look clumpy in images obtained from high angular resolution interferometric observations (Gullberg et al. 2018), and we cannot rule out this possibility for our clumpy [O III ] detection." (5. Conclusion)
- Limitation (authors): The 4.5σ [C II] detection has low robustness because removing a single bright channel at the integration edge reduces S/N to 3.6σ and halves the flux, and the minor axis size is unconstrained.  
  > "The minor axis of the [C II ] emission is unconstrained because the estimated minor axis is too small and its error is too large (Table 5)." (3.2.1. Line Detection); "However, the robustness of this detection is low, because after we integrated without one velocity bin at the integral edge, the S/N decreases to 3.6σ, and the luminosity decreases by a factor of ∼2." (5. Conclusion)
- Limitation (authors): Cannot determine why [C II] was not detected in the Cycle 7 observation despite having similar sensitivity and resolution to Cycle 3.  
  > "In conclusion, we cannot find out the reason why the [C II ] emission was not detected with the Cycle 7 observation." (4.1. Non-detection of the Cycle 7 [C II ] Observation)
- Limitation (authors): Large uncertainties exist in both the SED-derived SFR and the Lyα escape fraction correction.  
  > "The other possible reason is the large uncertainty of measurements on the SFR (see also Section 4.4)." (4.3. L [C II ] −SFR relation); "Nevertheless, the uncertainty of the Lyα escape fraction is also large (Sobral & Matthee 2019)." (4.3. L [C II ] −SFR relation)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Velocity integration ranges for sub-clumps (A1, A2, B', C', C'', SE, NW) were tuned post-hoc to maximize S/N, which can artificially inflate detection significances of noise peaks.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Deconvolved source sizes measured from 3.6-4.5σ images have ~50% uncertainties (or are unconstrained along the minor axis), propagating large systematic uncertainties into the surface brightness dimming (SBD) correction factors.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust mass and total IR luminosity upper limits rely on a single assumed dust temperature (T_dust = 40 K) and emissivity index (β = 1.5), and explicitly omit CMB heating and contrast effects at z = 7.212.
