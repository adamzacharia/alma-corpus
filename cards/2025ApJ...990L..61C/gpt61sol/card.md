## Reasoning

- Why this data: Select bright, red MIRI-observed LRDs: [F444W]<27; [F770W]<26.5 with >5σ detection; [F277W]-[F444W]>1.7.  
  > "\[F444W\]$<$27," (Sample Selection & Observations); "\[F770W\]$<$26.5 with a detection $>$5$\sigma$, and" (Sample Selection & Observations); "\[F277W\] - \[F444W\]$>$1.7." (Sample Selection & Observations)
- Why this method: Stack non-detections to constrain the population; single-band mass and luminosity constraints are largely insensitive to redshift because of the negative K-correction.  
  > "Beyond the non-detection of individual sources, we also stack our data in the image plane." (Derived Limits on Dust Mass); "Thanks to the negative K-correction, both dust mass and IR luminosity constraints derived from a single flux density measurement are largely insensitive to redshift." (Derived Limits on Dust Mass)
- Alternative: Unweighted stacking gives a result not appreciably different from inverse-variance weighting.  
  > "We note this is not appreciably different from a direct, unweighted stack" (Derived Limits on Dust Mass); "because the RMS varies by less than 10% across the full sample." (Derived Limits on Dust Mass)
- Alternative: Cold dust ≲50 K is disfavored by the compact-morphology argument inherited from (cite: casey24b).  
  > "(cite: casey24b) present a discussion of why cold dust temperatures ($\lesssim$50 K) are not expected in LRDs given their compact morphologies." (Derived Limits on Dust Mass)
- Alternative: The selected sources lack mid-IR power-law SEDs and are inconsistent with hot dust tori.  
  > "providing direct evidence that the selected LRDs lack dominant mid-IR powerlaws (and are thus inconsistent with hot dust tori)." (Sample Selection & Observations)
- Alternative: Dense-gas reddening could require even less dust than the compact-dust prediction.  
  > "then the ’reddening’ would instead be caused by at atmosphere of dense gas relatively devoid of dust." (Summary); "If that hypothesis holds, it logically follows that their submillimeter emission would be even less significant than the already meager predictions." (Summary)
- How they decided it was enough: Choose 60 sources to achieve stacked flux-density uncertainty <5 μJy, corresponding to dust-mass uncertainty 2×10^6 M⊙ at ~100 K.  
  > "This sample size was motivated by the firm constraints it could set on the results, with a stacked uncertainty on the resulting flux density $<$5 $\mu$Jy," (Sample Selection & Observations); "translating to a dust mass uncertainty of 2$\times$10$^{6}$  M$_\odot$ for assumed dust temperature $\sim$100 K."
