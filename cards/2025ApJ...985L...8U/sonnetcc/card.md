## Analysis recipe

1. **Image the Band-3 data into a CO(3-2) cube**: CLEAN with tclean, natural weighting, auto-masking down to 2 sigma  
  > "Imaging was carried out using the CLEAN algorithm via the" (Observation and Reduction); "task with natural weighting, down to" (Observation and Reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting; masking down to 2 sigma  
  > "down to $2\sigma$ with auto-masking" (Observation and Reduction)
   - parameter: beam 3.00" x 2.46", PA 87 deg; rms 33 uJy/beam per 100 km/s channel at ~85 GHz  
  > "The resulting synthesized beam size was $3.00^{\prime\prime} \times 2.46^{\prime\prime}$ (PA = 87 deg)." (Observation and Reduction); "The typical rms noise level at the phase center was 33 $\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$ per 100 km s$^{-1}$ channel at around 85 GHz." (Observation and Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect and characterise CO(3-2) in ADF22-QG1**: Moment-0 map integrated over 600 km/s; single Gaussian fit to the extracted spectrum for redshift and velocity dispersion; line intensity taken as peak flux density in the moment-0 map  
  > "the flux was integrated across $\Delta v=600$ km s$^{-1}$ to fully cover the emission in ADF22-QG1" (Results); "The total line intensity of ADF22-QG1 was measured as peak flux density in the moment-0 map" (Results)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: moment-0 velocity width 600 km/s  
  > "the flux was integrated across $\Delta v=600$ km s$^{-1}$ to fully cover the emission in ADF22-QG1" (Results)
   - fitting: single Gaussian fit to the spectrum (sampler, priors, likelihood not stated)  
  > "A single Gaussian fit to the spectrum yields $z_{\rm CO}=3.0889\pm0.0007$" (Results)
   - validation: CO redshift compared with the Balmer absorption redshift; CO emission overlaps the stellar emission  
  > "which is broadly consistent with the previously reported redshift based on Balmer absorption lines" (Results); "spatially overlapping with the stellar emission" (Results)
3. **Upper limits for ADF22-QG2 and QG3**: Pseudo narrow-band image per galaxy (500 km/s) with 3 sigma upper limits  
  > "we created a pseudo narrow-band image for each with $\Delta v=500$ km s$^{-1}$" (Results); "measured $3\sigma$ upper limits" (Results)
   - parameter: velocity width 500 km/s; limits at 3 sigma; adopted z_[OIII]=3.0851 for QG2 (most dominant peak)  
  > "we created a pseudo narrow-band image for each with $\Delta v=500$ km s$^{-1}$" (Results); "we adopt $z_{\rm [OIII]}=3.0851$, which is the most dominant peak" (Footnote 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Contamination from DSFG emission judged negligible because of a velocity offset (Fig. 2)  
  > "Bright emission associated with DSFGs does not significantly contaminate the upper limit due to a velocity offset" (Results)
4. **Derive molecular gas masses and gas fractions**: Convert CO(3-2) intensity to ground-state CO luminosity with r_31, then to M_H2 with alpha_CO; f_gas = M_H2/M_*  
  > "In the calculation we assumed the brightness temperature ratio $r_{31}=0.5$" (Results); "the gas mass fraction ($f_{\rm gas}=M_{\rm H_2}/M_*$)" (Discussion)
   - parameter: r_31 = 0.5  
  > "In the calculation we assumed the brightness temperature ratio $r_{31}=0.5$" (Results)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.4 (Milky-Way-like) and alpha_CO = 0.8 (ULIRG-like)  
  > "$\alpha_{\rm CO}=4.4$ (for a Milkey-Way like galaxies) and $\alpha_{\rm CO}=0.8$ (for a ULIRG-like galaxies)" (Results)
   - assumption: Cosmology H0=70, Omega_m=0.30, Omega_Lambda=0.70  
  > "We adopt a standard concordance cosmology with $H_0=70$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_{\rm m}=0.30$, and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.70$." (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gas mass checked against dynamical mass (see step 6)  
  > "does not exceed $M_{\rm dyn}$ within the uncertainties for either case with $\alpha_{\rm CO}=4.4$ or 0.8" (Results)
5. **Dust mass limits and gas-to-dust ratio**: Dust mass upper limit from the 1.1 mm 3 sigma limit assuming kappa_850, beta and T_d; gas-to-dust ratio = M_H2/M_d  
  > "We also estimate upper limits on the dust mass $M_{\rm d}$" (Results); "the gas-to-dust ratio, $\delta_{\rm gdr}=M_{\rm H_2}/M_{\rm d}$" (Discussion)
   - parameter: S_1.1mm < 75 uJy (3 sigma point source)  
  > "The non detections place a $3\sigma$ point-source limit of $S_{\rm 1.1mm} <75$ $\mu$Jy" (Results)
   - assumption: kappa_850 = 0.05 m^2/kg, beta = 2.0, T_d = 20 K  
  > "assuming the dust mass absorption coefficient $\kappa_{850}=0.05$ m$^{2}$ kg$^{-1}$" (Results); "$\beta=2.0$ , and dust temperature of $T_{\rm d}=20$ K" (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with model predictions of delta_gdr >~ 500-1000 for high-z QGs  
  > "Models predict that QGs at high redshift may have significantly higher values" (Discussion)
6. **Stellar masses (SED fitting) and dynamical mass**: FAST++ SED fits with parametric SFHs including new JWST photometry; M_dyn = K_n K_q sigma_*^2 r_e / G  
  > "We updated the fits of ADF22 QGs with the SED models with parametric SFHs by using FAST$++$" (Results); "The dynamical mass of ADF22-QG1 is estimated as $M_{\rm dyn}=K_n K_q \sigma_*^2 r_e / G$" (Results)
   - software: FAST++
   - assumption: Dark matter mass 10-20% of M_dyn  
  > "the dark matter mass $M_{\rm DM}$ (assumed to be 10–20% of $M_{\rm dyn}$" (Results)
   - fitting: SED fitting with FAST++ (priors and grid not stated)  
  > "We updated the fits of ADF22 QGs with the SED models with parametric SFHs by using FAST$++$" (Results)
   - validation: M_* + M_H2 + M_DM does not exceed M_dyn within uncertainties for either alpha_CO  
  > "does not exceed $M_{\rm dyn}$ within the uncertainties for either case with $\alpha_{\rm CO}=4.4$ or 0.8" (Results)
7. **Gas-consumption history toy models and progenitor comparison**: Two closed-box toy models of f_gas evolution (constant t_dep; SFH-based conversion of gas to stars) compared with DSFGs at z~4-6 and scaling relations  
  > "we present two toy models of the evolution of $f_{\rm gas}$ in a closed box system" (Discussion); "simply assuming that molecular gas mass was converted to stellar mass at a rate identical with star-formation rate" (Discussion)
   - parameter: constant t_dep = 0.5 and 0.25 Gyr (first model)  
  > "The first model assumes a constant depletion timescale" (Discussion)
   - assumption: Closed box, no net inflow or outflow, no external gas supply exceeding outflow  
  > "assuming no additional external gas supply exceeding the amount of outflow" (Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Model tracks compared with observed DSFG f_gas and extrapolated scaling relations  
  > "both types of model tracks predict progenitors with $f_{\rm gas}$ broadly consistent with the observed DSFGs" (Discussion)

## Qualitative findings

- First direct detection of molecular gas in a quiescent galaxy from the early universe (ADF22-QG1)  
  > "marking the first direct detection of molecular gas in a quiescent galaxy from the early universe" (abstract)
- CO(3-2) not significantly detected in ADF22-QG2 and QG3; only marginal ~2 sigma signals  
  > "the CO(3–2) emission was not significantly detected, although very marginal signals ($\sim2\sigma$) are identified" (Results)
- None of the three QGs detected in the 1.1 mm dust continuum  
  > "None of the three ADF22-QGs were significantly detected in the 1.1 mm dust continuum" (Results)
- Gas velocity dispersion smaller than stellar one, suggesting gas and stellar bulge kinematics differ  
  > "this may suggest that the molecular gas distribution and kinematics are not identical as those of the stellar bulge components" (Results)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Molecular gas content of high-z QGs is a missing piece; detections beyond z~1 are elusive  
  > "One critical missing piece of this puzzle is the molecular gas reservoir in QGs." (Introduction); "reliable detections of molecular gas tracers at higher redshifts remain elusive" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Two CO-to-H2 conversion factors reported side by side (alpha_CO = 4.4 and 0.8); conclusions said not sensitive to the choice
