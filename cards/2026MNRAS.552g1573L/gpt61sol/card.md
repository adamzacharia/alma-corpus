## Analysis recipe

1. **Construct continuum images**: Select line-free channels, phase self-calibrate, and make 12 m-only and >40 kλ images with interactive tclean  
  > "a pseudo-continuum dataset was constructed from line-free channels identified using custom runs of the Python function `findContinuum` (cite: Hunter2023b)" (Observations); "iterative phase-only self-calibration was then performed for each continuum dataset." (Observations); "Both image sets were made via interactive clean using CASA’s `tclean` command" (Observations)
   - software: CASA, findContinuum, tclean
   - parameter: mtmfs; Brigg’s robust parameter 0  
  > "using multi-term multi frequency synthesis (`mtmfs`) and a Brigg’s robust parameter of 0." (Observations)
   - parameter: 40 kλ images: uvrange >40 kλ  
  > "The 40 k$\lambda$ images were made by considering only baselines $>$40 k$\lambda$, implemented via `tclean`’s `uvrange` parameter." (Observations)
   - parameter: 27-pointing mosaics; ~95 × 95 arcsec within 10% response  
  > "Each field was observed using a mosaic of 27 pointings of the 12 m array" (Observations); "The dimensions of the resulting mosaics are $\sim$ 95$''$$\times$ 95$''$ $\approx$ 1.6$'$ $\times$ 1.6$'$, within the 10% response level." (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Measure rms in the same emission-free region for both image sets  
  > "The rms noise of each image was measured in an emission-free region, with the same region used for all images of a given field." (Observations)
2. **Identify significant emission leaves**: Run Astrodendro on non-primary beam-corrected 40 kλ images and reject low-S/N leaves  
  > "Run *Astrodendro* on the non-primary beam-corrected 40 k$\lambda$ image." (Core Catalogue); "Select leaves with a peak intensity $>$ 5 $\sigma_{\mathrm{rms, pb}}$ in the primary beam-corrected 40 k$\lambda$ image." (Core Catalogue)
   - software: Astrodendro
   - parameter: min_flux=4 σ_rms; min_delta=σ_rms; min_npix=N_beam/3  
  > "we adopted `min`$\_$`flux` $=$ 4 $\sigma_{\text{rms}}$, `min`$\_$`delta` $=\sigma_{\text{rms}}$ and `min`$\_$`npix `$=N_{\text{beam}}$/3" (Astrodendro)
   - assumption: 40 kλ filtering removes extended filaments while retaining core scales  
  > "Extended filamentary emission is thus filtered out in the 40 k$\lambda$ images, which remain sensitive to emission on core scales." (Astrodendro)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Explore parameter combinations and visually check correspondence with obvious emission features  
  > "After exploring a range of *Astrodendro* input parameter combinations" (Astrodendro); "gave leaves which reliably corresponded to obvious 1.3 mm emission features." (Astrodendro)
3. **Locate blended compact sources**: Run Hyper high-pass detection on non-primary beam-corrected 12 m-only images  
  > "Since the high-pass filtering in *Hyper* already suppresses extended emission, we ran the algorithm on the non-primary beam-corrected 12 m-only images." (Hyper)
   - software: Hyper
   - parameter: σ_t=5 σ_rms in six fields  
  > "We used a threshold value $\sigma_t=$ 5 $\sigma_{\mathrm{rms}}$ in six of the ten regions." (Hyper)
   - parameter: σ_t=7 σ_rms in G10.29-0.13, G12.91-0.03, G18.89-0.47 and G28.83-0.25  
  > "In the remaining four (G10.29$-$0.13, G12.91$-$0.03, G18.89$-$0.47 and G28.83$-$0.25)" (Hyper); "We therefore used $\sigma_t=$ 7 $\sigma_{\mathrm{rms}}$ for these fields" (Hyper)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fits; simultaneous multi-Gaussian fits for companions within 2 × beam FWHM  
  > "A 2D Gaussian is then fit at each centroid in the input image" (Hyper); "If a given source has ‘companions’ (other source centroids within 2 $\times$ the beam FWHM), the source and its companions are fit via multi-Gaussian fitting" (Hyper)
   - validation: Higher thresholds prevent identification of residual filament variations  
  > "which we found prevented the identification of such features." (Hyper)
4. **Build the core catalogue and measure fluxes**: Retain Hyper centroids within accepted Astrodendro leaves; rerun Hyper photometry on primary beam-corrected 12 m-only images; manually add G22.04+0.22 MM3  
  > "Select *Hyper* sources with centroids lying within the *Astrodendro* leaves selected in step 2." (Core Catalogue); "Rerun *Hyper*, providing the source centroids from step 4 for (multi-)Gaussian fitting and aperture photometry, in the primary beam-corrected 12 m-only image." (Core Catalogue); "We therefore manually added the northern source to our core catalogue, despite it having no associated *Astrodendro* leaf." (Manual Addition of a Source in G22.04+0.22)
   - software: Hyper, Astrodendro
   - parameter: Photometric ellipse axes 2 × Gaussian FWHM; subtract companion models  
  > "defined as an ellipse with axes 2 $\times$ the FWHM of the Gaussian fit." (Hyper); "the companion model fits are subtracted prior to flux integration." (Hyper)
   - parameter: No Hyper background subtraction  
  > "We did not use *Hyper*’s background subtraction capability" (Hyper)
   - parameter: Integrated-flux uncertainty σ_rms,pb sqrt(N_b); no absolute flux-calibration uncertainty  
  > "Uncertainty of the integrated flux density, estimated as $\sigma_{S_\text{1.3mm}}=\sigma_\text{rms, pb}\;\sqrt{N_b}$" (Table 1); "Note that these uncertainties do not include the absolute flux calibration uncertainty" (Table 1)
   - assumption: Catalogue sources represent dense cores  
  > "This technique yields a total of 570 sources across the EGO-10 sample (an average of 57 per field), which we take to be the dense cores in these regions." (Core Catalogue)
   - fitting: Positive amplitudes initialized at centroid intensity; FWHM bounds 1–1.5 × image-beam FWHM using aper_inf and aper_sup  
  > "we limited the fit amplitudes to be greater than zero, with an initial estimate equal to the image intensity at the source centroid." (Core Catalogue); "We also limited the minimum and maximum allowed size of source FWHMs to within 1 and 1.5 times the FWHM of the image beam" (Core Catalogue); "with *Hyper*’s `aper_inf` and `aper_sup` parameters, respectively." (Core Catalogue)
   - validation: Visual inspection; all Hyper fits converged with STATUS=0  
  > "based on careful visual inspection of the 1.3 mm continuum images." (1.3 mm Continuum: Source Identification and Core Catalogue); "The (multi-)Gaussian fits to all our sources converged, that is, the *Hyper* `STATUS` output is 0 in all cases" (Core Catalogue)
5. **Measure core-separation structure**: Histogram unique core-pair projected separations; calculate cluster radius and fraction η above 0.5 R_clus  
  > "distribution of projected physical separation between unique core-pairs" (Distributions of Core Separations); "calculated as the distance from the mean position of all catalogued cores, which defines the cluster centre, to the catalogued core furthest from this position" (Distributions of Core Separations); "reports the fraction of the distribution with separations $>$0.5 $R_\text{clus}$ in each field" (Distributions of Core Separations)
   - assumption: Cluster centre is the mean position of all catalogued cores  
  > "the mean position of all catalogued cores, which defines the cluster centre" (Distributions of Core Separations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Separation peaks exceed the physical beam scale  
  > "All primary and secondary peaks occur at values larger than the physical scale of the beam" (Distributions of Core Separations)
6. **Quantify structure with Q and estimate uncertainty**: Compute core MSTs with MiSTree and Q=normalized mean MST edge length / normalized correlation length  
  > "we compute the $\mathcal{Q}$-parameter (cite: Cartwright2004), derived from the MST of the core spatial distribution." (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter); "computed with the publicly available *MiSTree* Python package" (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter); "where $\bar{m}$ is the mean edge length of the MST ($m$), normalised to account for the number of points in the distribution, and $\bar{s}$ is the normalised correlation length" (Introduction)
   - software: MiSTree
   - parameter: Q<0.8 subclustered; Q>0.8 centrally condensed  
  > "$\mathcal{Q}<$ 0.8 is taken to indicate a subclustered distribution" (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter); "$\mathcal{Q}>$ 0.8 is taken to indicate a centrally-condensed distribution" (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter)
   - parameter: Sampling test: randomly remove sqrt(N_cores) cores, 100 realizations  
  > "we assumed Poisson statistics and randomly removed $\sqrt{N_\text{cores}}$ of the cores catalogued in each field 100 times, recalculating $\mathcal{Q}$ for each realisation." (Estimating the Uncertainty on the Q-parameter)
   - parameter: Mass test: remove estimated core masses below 0.26 M_sun  
  > "We then remove cores in each field with masses less than the largest 5$\sigma_\text{rms, pb}$ point-mass sensitivity in the sample - 0.26 $M_\odot$" (Estimating the Uncertainty on the Q-parameter)
   - assumption: Mass sensitivity: gas-to-dust ratio R=100; dust temperature=T_NH3; κν=1 cm² g^-1  
  > "an assumed gas-to-dust mass ratio of $R=$ 100" (Observations); "dust temperature equal to the $T_{\text{NH}_3}$ of the clump" (Observations); "an assumed opacity of $\kappa_\nu=$ 1 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$." (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Recompute Q for mass-limited, resampled and Astrodendro-only catalogues; uncertainty is standard deviation across 4–5 estimates  
  > "testing the effect of mass sensitivity, sampling uncertainty, and an alternative source extraction procedure." (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter); "we recalculated $\mathcal{Q}$ using the $>$5 $\sigma_\text{rms, pb}$ *Astrodendro* leaves identified in our initial runs in the 12 m-only images, and again separately using those identified in the 40 k$\lambda$ images" (Estimating the Uncertainty on the Q-parameter); "the standard deviation of the $\mathcal{Q}$ values obtained in each field (i.e. the 4–5 values" (Estimating the Uncertainty on the Q-parameter)
7. **Identify protostellar core associations**: Cross-match published masers and cm continuum sources to Hyper core centroids; associate only within the core FWHM ellipse  
  > "we compute the projected separation to the *Hyper* centroid of the nearest 1.3 mm core" (Association of 1.3 mm Cores with Centimetre-Wavelength Tracers of Active Star Formation); "if the star formation tracer lies within the core’s *Hyper* FWHM ellipse" (Association of 1.3 mm Cores with Centimetre-Wavelength Tracers of Active Star Formation); "We classify these cores as protostellar" (Association of 1.3 mm Cores with Centimetre-Wavelength Tracers of Active Star Formation)
   - software: Hyper
   - parameter: Only tracers within the ALMA mosaic 10% response level  
  > "we consider all star formation tracers within the 10% response level of the ALMA mosaic of each field" (Association of 1.3 mm Cores with Centimetre-Wavelength Tracers of Active Star Formation)
   - parameter: H2O detections from both epochs; use 2018.1 position when detected twice  
  > "We considered all H$_2$O maser detections across both epochs for these fields" (22 GHz H2O Masers); "Where a given maser is detected in both (cite: Towner2021) epochs, we defined its position as that reported in the first (2018.1)." (22 GHz H2O Masers)
   - parameter: Rosero positions: mean 20.9/25.5 GHz positions; single detection if only one; mean 4.9/7.4 GHz positions if no 1.3 cm detection  
  > "The position of a given (cite: Rosero2016) source was therefore taken as the mean of its reported 20.9 GHz and 25.5 GHz positions." (Centimetre Continuum Sources); "If a source was detected in only one 1.3 cm baseband, we adopt that position" (Centimetre Continuum Sources); "for sources detected in neither 1.3 cm baseband, the position was taken as the mean of the reported 4.9 GHz and 7.4 GHz positions." (Centimetre Continuum Sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Test 2 × FWHM apertures, Astrodendro boundaries and larger Hyper fit sizes; tracer ranking remains unchanged  
  > "We tested whether rerunning *Hyper* with a maximum source size of 2$\times$beam$_{\rm FWHM}$" (Non-Associations); "the association statistics were unchanged." (Non-Associations); "For both of these cases, the tracer-core association rates increase" (Tests of Alternative Association Criteria)
8. **Measure MYSO centrality and nearby clustering**: Use 6.7 GHz maser positions for MYSOs; measure centre offsets, nearest-neighbour separations and nearby core counts  
  > "Taking the 6.7 GHz CH$_3$OH maser positions to mark the locations of MYSOs" (Spatial Distribution); "We compute the projected physical separation between each MYSO and its respective protocluster centre" (Spatial Distribution); "$\Delta_\text{nn}$ is the projected physical separation between a 6.7 GHz CH$_3$OH maser and its nearest-neighbouring core." (Clustered Massive Star Formation)
   - software: scipy.stats, mannwhitneyu
   - parameter: N_env counts cores within projected 10,000 AU; exclude the maser host-core  
  > "$N_\text{env}$ is the number of cores with projected physical separation $<$10,000 AU from the maser position" (Clustered Massive Star Formation); "In computing these parameters, the maser’s host-core was not considered" (Clustered Massive Star Formation)
   - assumption: Maser host-core contains the same MYSO driving the maser  
  > "we assume this core hosts the same MYSO driving the maser emission." (Clustered Massive Star Formation)
   - assumption: Core-number-density estimate assumes a spherical volume  
  > "assuming all sources lie within a spherical volume" (Clustered Massive Star Formation)
   - fitting: Directional Mann-Whitney U-test comparing MYSO-host and other core centre offsets  
  > "a directional Mann-Whitney U-test" (Spatial Distribution); "With `scipy.stats`’ `mannwhitneyu`." (Spatial Distribution); "the MYSO host-cores, as a population, lie closer to cluster centres than the population of cores which do not host MYSOs" (Spatial Distribution)
   - validation: Examine the d<3 kpc subset with better spatial resolution  
  > "for all five MYSOs at $d<$ 3 kpc" (Clustered Massive Star Formation); "$\Delta_\text{nn}\lesssim$ 4000 AU and $N_\text{env}>$ 10" (Clustered Massive Star Formation)
9. **Check non-dust continuum contributions**: Extrapolate published cm spectral indices to 1.3 mm and compare with Hyper integrated fluxes; calculate 1.3 cm–1.3 mm indices  
  > "We use $\alpha$ to extrapolate the contribution of these cm-$\lambda$ continuum sources to the 1.3 mm integrated flux density of their host-cores, as measured by *Hyper*" (Spectral Index Analysis); "From our observations we calculate $\alpha_{\rm 1.3cm\_1.3mm}=$ 1.91" (Spectral Index Analysis)
   - software: Hyper
   - assumption: Use the reported 1.3–5 cm spectral index for extrapolation to 1.3 mm  
  > "(cite: Towner2021) and (cite: Rosero2016) report the spectral indices ($\alpha$) of their cm-$\lambda$ continuum sources between 1.3 and 5 cm." (Spectral Index Analysis); "We use $\alpha$ to extrapolate the contribution" (Spectral Index Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare anomalous G19.36-0.03 CM1 with published infrared properties  
  > "Taken together, its observational characteristics suggest this source may not be a young object associated with the 1.3 mm protocluster." (Spectral Index Analysis)
10. **Construct evolutionary indicators and subsamples**: Sum S_cm d² within each mosaic at 1.3 and 5 cm; divide by ATLASGAL clump mass  
  > "L_\text{cm} = \sum{S_\text{cm}\,d^2}" (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection); "$L_\text{cm}/M_\text{AGAL}$ was then evaluated at each cm wavelength using the (cite: Urquhart2018) $M_\text{AGAL}$ values" (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection)
   - parameter: Exclude G16.59-0.05 and G22.04+0.22 from correlation analyses because of low core counts  
  > "excluding G16.59$-$0.05 and G22.04$+$0.22, for which we consider our measured $\mathcal{Q}$-parameters less reliable due to small $N_\text{cores}$" (Protocluster Structure and Evolutionary State); "We exclude these regions from our analyses in the following sections" (Protocluster Structure and Evolutionary State)
   - parameter: EGO-10′ excludes G19.36-0.03 CM1 and MM10; recomputed Q=0.74  
  > "we exclude the cm-$\lambda$ continuum source G19.36$-$0.03 CM1 from the calculation of $L_\text{cm}$" (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection); "we recompute the MST and $\mathcal{Q}$-parameter of G19.36$-$0.03 without G19.36$-$0.03 MM10" (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection); "from 0.73 to 0.74." (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection)
   - parameter: MYSO uses only MYSO-associated cm sources; CLUST uses other cm sources; seven regions each  
  > "MYSO: Only the integrated flux density of the cm-$\lambda$ source(s) associated with the MYSO host-core(s) contributes to $L_\text{cm}$ in a given field." (MYSO and Cluster-Scale Relationships); "CLUST: Only the integrated flux densities of cm-$\lambda$ source(s) not associated with the MYSO host-core(s) contribute to $L_\text{cm}$ in a given field." (MYSO and Cluster-Scale Relationships); "The MYSO and CLUST subsamples are therefore each comprised of seven regions" (MYSO and Cluster-Scale Relationships)
   - parameter: Sum both ATLASGAL clumps for G10.29-0.13 and G14.63-0.58  
  > "For G10.29$-$0.13 and G14.63$-$0.58 this is the sum of the masses of both ATLASGAL clumps with peak positions within the ALMA mosaic." (Protocluster Structure and Evolutionary State)
   - assumption: L_cm/M_AGAL traces evolutionary state  
  > "as an indicator of protocluster evolutionary state, and investigate trends with $\mathcal{Q}$." (L_cm/M_AGAL - An Alternative Evolutionary Indicator)
   - fitting: Propagate flux-density, distance and clump-mass uncertainties  
  > "propagating the integrated flux density uncertainties reported by (cite: Towner2021), the protocluster distance uncertainties" (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection); "the (cite: Urquhart2018) estimated $M_\text{AGAL}$ uncertainty." (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection)
   - validation: Compare full, evolved-source-excluded, MYSO and CLUST subsamples  
  > "could therefore significantly influence correlations and fitting results, motivating the construction of EGO-10$^\prime$." (Evaluating L_cm/M_AGAL and Subsample Selection); "we can investigate to what degree the correlations identified in Section [6.1.2](#sec:QvLM) are driven solely by the cm-$\lambda$ continuum source(s) associated with the EGO-10 MYSOs." (MYSO and Cluster-Scale Relationships)
11. **Test and fit structure–evolution relationships**: Spearman and Kendall correlations, empirical Monte Carlo permutation p-values, uncertainty realizations and Bayesian log-log linear regression  
  > "with the Spearman ($\rho$) and Kendall ($\tau$) correlation coefficients - computed with `scipy.stats`’ `spearmanr` and `kendalltau`, respectively" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL); "We compute the $p$-values empirically, from $n=$ 10,000 Monte Carlo realisations of the null distribution" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL); "LinMix uses a Bayesian technique to perform the linear regression and produce a distribution of fit parameters." (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL)
   - software: scipy.stats, spearmanr, kendalltau, LinMix
   - parameter: 10,000 Gaussian uncertainty realizations, followed by random permutation for null tests  
  > "made by sampling each ($\mathcal{Q}$, $L_\text{cm}/M_\text{AGAL}$) measurement from Gaussian distributions with standard deviations equal to their associated uncertainties and then randomly permuting the resulting pairs." (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL)
   - parameter: p=(b+1)/(n+1); significance p<0.05  
  > "$p=(b+1)/(n+1)$" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL); "the percentage of Monte Carlo realisations in which the correlation is statistically significant, with $p<$ 0.05" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL)
   - assumption: Gaussian measurement-uncertainty distributions  
  > "Gaussian distributions with standard deviations equal to their associated uncertainties" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL)
   - fitting: LinMix: log10(Q)=α+β log10(L_cm/M_AGAL); means and standard deviations of parameter distributions  
  > "$\text{log}_{10}(\mathcal{Q}) = \alpha + \beta\;\text{log}_{10}(L_\text{cm}/M_\text{AGAL})$" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL); "The ($\alpha$, $\beta$) values and their associated uncertainties are estimated as the mean and standard deviation of the resulting distributions, respectively." (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL)
   - validation: Uncertainty-resampled correlation distributions; distance and core-count tests; near/far subsamples  
  > "To assess the robustness of the $\mathcal{Q}$–$L_\text{cm}/M_\text{AGAL}$ relationship to uncertainty" (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL); "We find no statistically-significant correlations." (Effect of Distance on Protocluster Structure and Relationships); "we examined the relationship separately within these distance-limited subsamples" (Effect of Distance on Protocluster Structure and Relationships)

## Qualitative findings

- All targets contain morphologically diverse massive protoclusters  
  > "The EGO-10 fields are revealed as massive protoclusters, hosting morphologically diverse 1.3 mm continuum emission" (Conclusions)
- MYSOs preferentially lie near protocluster centres  
  > "the MYSO host-cores, as a population, lie closer to cluster centres than the population of cores which do not host MYSOs" (Spatial Distribution)
- No statistically significant Q–L_AGAL/M_AGAL relationship within EGO-10  
  > "We do not find any statistically significant relationship between $\mathcal{Q}$ and $L_\text{AGAL}/M_\text{AGAL}$ within the EGO-10 sample." (Protocluster Structure and Evolutionary State)
- The Q–L_cm/M_AGAL relationship is driven primarily by MYSO-associated emission  
  > "suggests the $\mathcal{Q}$–$L_\text{cm}/M_\text{AGAL}$ relationship identified in Section [6.1.2](#sec:QvLM) is driven by the MYSOs in these protoclusters." (MYSO and Cluster-Scale Relationships)
- Results suggest evolution from subclustered to centrally condensed structure  
  > "These results suggest dynamic massive protocluster structure, evolving from subclustered to centrally condensed in the early stage(s) of massive star formation signposted by EGOs" (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Wide, sensitive ALMA mosaics resolve core populations across clumps; complementary high-resolution data identify star-formation tracers  
  > "well-suited to resolving the dense core population across the entirety of the target clumps." (Introduction); "A notable strength of the EGO-10 sample is therefore the wealth of complementary sensitive, high-resolution, multiwavelength data available." (The EGO-10 Sample)
- Why this method: Combine Astrodendro significance selection with Hyper deblending; filter extended emission with >40 kλ images  
  > "In our mosaic images, *Astrodendro* reliably identifies significant emission features, while *Hyper* separates blended sources within these structures." (Core Catalogue); "To minimise the identification of filament fragments as leaves, we instead ran *Astrodendro* on our 40 k$\lambda$ images" (Astrodendro)
- Alternative: Astrodendro on 12 m-only images rejected because leaves traced filament fragments  
  > "This attempt, however, gave numerous irregular leaves that appeared to trace fragments of filamentary structure rather than cores." (Astrodendro)
- Alternative: Astrodendro alone inadequately separated blended central sources  
  > "A persistent issue, however, was the poor separation of blended sources in the crowded central regions of some fields" (Astrodendro)
- Alternative: Hyper background subtraction disabled after reducing bright-source fluxes  
  > "In initial tests, this procedure significantly reduced the measured integrated flux density of bright sources in crowded regions compared to the value in the original image, so we chose to disable it." (Hyper)
- Alternative: Global min_npix=N_beam/4 rejected because it produced weak spurious sources  
  > "this source is only recovered with `min_npix` $=N_\text{beam}$/4." (Manual Addition of a Source in G22.04+0.22); "implementation in other fields predictably gives many weak, spurious sources, precluding application across the sample." (Manual Addition of a Source in G22.04+0.22)
- Alternative: Less restrictive association criteria rejected because of ambiguous or false associations  
  > "The less-restrictive association criteria tested above increase tracer-core association rates, but also yield ambiguous associations in some cases." (Tests of Alternative Association Criteria); "Misleading or false associations are also more likely with less-restrictive association criteria." (Tests of Alternative Association Criteria)
- Alternative: Monolithic-collapse interpretation inconsistent with observed nearby core multiplicity  
  > "The presence of multiple 1.3 mm cores within 10,000 AU of the MYSOs in our sample is inconsistent with massive star formation by monolithic collapse" (Clustered Massive Star Formation)
- How they decided it was enough: Visual correspondence with core-scale emission and converged Gaussian fits  
  > "We found this approach gave sources which corresponded well to the core-scale emission in the 1.3 mm continuum images" (Core Catalogue); "The (multi-)Gaussian fits to all our sources converged" (Core Catalogue)
- Limitation (authors): Q less reliable for G16.59-0.05 and G22.04+0.22 because of small core counts  
  > "We therefore consider the $\mathcal{Q}$ values in these regions less reliable than those in the rest of the sample, and exclude them from our statistical analyses" (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter)
- Limitation (authors): Two-dimensional projection prevents assessment of true three-dimensional structure  
  > "Whilst we cannot assess the 3D structure of these protoclusters" (Minimum Spanning Trees and the Q-Parameter)
- Limitation (authors): Water-maser association rate likely a lower limit because of variability  
  > "given the highly variable and even transient nature of 22 GHz H$_2$O masers in star-forming regions" (22 GHz H2O Masers); "this association rate is likely a lower limit." (22 GHz H2O Masers)
- Limitation (authors): Sensitivity, unresolved blending and catalogue thresholds can explain missing tracer associations  
  > "Such isolated tracers could also be associated with weak cores below our sensitivity limit." (Non-Associations); "perhaps associated with as yet unresolved sources or with cores which do not meet the flux or size thresholds required for our source identification." (Non-Associations)
- Limitation (authors): No full core-mass analysis or quantitative mass-segregation measurement  
  > "we do not, in this work, calculate core masses and thus cannot further quantify any mass segregation that may be present" (Spatial Distribution)
- Limitation (authors): Narrow evolutionary range and large clump L/M uncertainties limit correlations  
  > "The $L_\text{AGAL}/M_\text{AGAL}$ range of the EGO-10 sample is relatively narrow" (Protocluster Structure and Evolutionary State); "which may contribute scatter to the relation." (Protocluster Structure and Evolutionary State)
- Limitation (authors): Warm dust may contribute to 1.3 cm continuum  
  > "which may reflect potential contributions to the observed 1.3 cm flux densities from warm dust emission (cite: Towner2021)." (Q and L_cm/M_AGAL)
- Limitation (authors): Core photometric uncertainties exclude absolute flux-calibration uncertainty  
  > "Note that these uncertainties do not include the absolute flux calibration uncertainty" (Table 1)
- Limitation (authors): Global collapse requires clump-scale kinematic evidence  
  > "Observational evidence of global collapse in a given protocluster requires the detection of clump-scale infalling velocity flows." (Global Collapse in Massive Protoclusters)
- Limitation (authors): Extended resolved-out cm sources excluded from analysis  
  > "These extended sources are resolved-out in their observations, such that they are only able to report approximate sizes, flux measurements and positions." (Centimetre Continuum Sources); "We therefore do not consider these sources in our analysis." (Centimetre Continuum Sources)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Seven- and eight-region correlations are sensitive to sample selection and individual sources.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The evolutionary indicator is proposed and tested within the same small sample; its monotonicity is not independently calibrated here.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Q and the new indicator reuse core associations and literature measurements, so correlated uncertainties deserve explicit treatment.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distance tests with very small subsamples have limited power to exclude resolution or completeness effects.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The standard deviation across a few catalogue variants is not a full probabilistic uncertainty model for Q.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A correlation across different protoclusters does not directly measure temporal evolution or establish global collapse.
