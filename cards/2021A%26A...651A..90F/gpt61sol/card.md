## Qualitative findings

- outer ring is azimuthally inhomogeneous, with northwestern S1 and southeastern S2 arm-like structures  
  > "the continuum emission is stronger in two arm-like structures in the northwestern (substructure “S1”) and southeastern (“S2”) direction, respectively." (Image inspection)
- inner ring and HST-traced outer disk have the same inclination and position angle  
  > "This implies that the inner ring and the outer disk (traced by the HST images) have the same inclination and position angle." (Geometrical properties)
- near-infrared disk is radially more extended; inner dark lane coincides with ALMA inner ring  
  > "At near-infrared wavelengths, the disk appears radially more extended than at millimeter wavelengths." (Comparison to near-infrared observations); "The inner dark lane is spatially coincident with the ALMA inner ring." (Comparison to near-infrared observations)
- two giant protoplanets are suggested by observations and simulations  
  > "The observational findings and the comparison with hydrodynamical simulations hint at the presence of two giant protoplanets at nearly 15 au and 110 au, with masses of $\sim 3.1\,M_{\rm J}$ and 8.5$\,M_{\rm J}$, respectively." (Conclusions)
- outer planet induces spiral patterns inward and outward of its orbit  
  > "The interaction between the outer planet and the disk gives rise to a spiral pattern inward and outward of the planetary orbit." (SPH results: Surface density evolution)
- wide-orbit massive planet challenges core-accretion formation  
  > "The massive outer planet challenges current formation models via core accretion." (Conclusions)
- dust gap may permit UV photo-desorption of water and must retain small grains  
  > "Because of the lower dust extinction in the dust gap, the stellar UV photons can reach the colder layers and evaporate water molecules via UV photo-desorption." (Dust gap and water emission); "This implies that the dust gap is not fully void of dust, that there must be a residual of small dust grains." (Dust gap and water emission)
