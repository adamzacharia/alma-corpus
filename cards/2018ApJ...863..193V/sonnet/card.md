## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO cubes and continuum**: CASA pipeline calibration, uvcontsub, clean with Briggs weighting, uv taper for CO(3-2), phase self-calibration of continuum  
  > "Continuum-subtracted data cubes were created using uvcontsub and clean." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; velocity bins 20 and 10 km/s  
  > "Images of the line emission were reconstructed using Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit AGN continuum SED**: Power law S ∝ nu^-alpha to ALMA plus literature radio points  
  > "has been fit with a power law of the form $S\propto \nu^{-\alpha}$." (3.1 AGN Continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract and fit integrated and regional spectra**: One or two Gaussian components fitted with LMFIT; Monte Carlo test with at least 1000 iterations and 3 sigma detection; numerical integration also done  
  > "Each spectral component was tested using a Monte Carlo analysis with at least 1000 iterations, with a detection requiring a $3\sigma$ significance (cite: Protassov02)." (3.2 Integrated Spectra)
   - software: LMFIT
   - parameter: 2500 MC iterations for per-pixel maps  
  > "2500 iterations were used for the Monte Carlo analysis, with a detection requiring $3\sigma$ significance." (3.4 Molecular Gas Distribution and Kinematics)
   - fitting: Gaussian least squares with instrumental broadening incorporated; components added only if previous detected  
  > "The presence of one component was required before attempting to fit a second. Instrumental broadening has been incorporated into the model." (3.2 Integrated Spectra)
   - validation: Compared ALMA CO(1-0) flux to IRAM-30m (45% recovered)  
  > "The integrated flux measured by ALMA is $45\pm8\%$ of this single dish measurement." (3.2 Integrated Spectra)
4. **Derive molecular gas masses**: M_mol = 1.05e4 (X_CO/X_CO,gal) S Dv D_L^2/(1+z); CO(3-2) converted with ratio 9  
  > "In order to compute molecular gas mass from a CO(3-2) flux, we first convert it to a CO(1-0) flux by adopting the CO (3-2)/(1-0) line ratio of 9 measured from the spatially-integrated spectra." (3.3 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - assumption: Galactic X_CO = 2e20 cm^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "adopt the Galactic value of $X_{\rm CO, gal} = 2\ensuremath{\times 10^{20}}\hbox{$\rm\; cm^{-2}\,$}(\rm\; K\hbox{$\rm\; km\rm\; s^{-1}\,$})^{-1}$ (cite: Bolatto13)." (3.3 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Map flux, velocity, FWHM and extract position-velocity curve**: Per-pixel Gaussian fitting; PV from CO(3-2) along filament path in 0.6 arcsec intervals with 0.75 arcsec width  
  > "Spectra were extracted in $0.6''$ ($2.1\rm\; kpc$) intervals along this path, each with a width of $0.75''$ ($2.6\rm\; kpc$)." (4.2 Motion Along the Filament)
   - software: PVExtractor, LMFIT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Test free-fall of the filament**: Fit PV curve with Hernquist-potential free-fall model v_obs = v sin i + v_ICM; v_ICM and i free, r0 fixed at 17.5 kpc  
  > "$v_{\rm ICM}$ and $i$ are allowed to vary, while $r_0$ is fixed to the maximum radial extent of the filament ($17.5\rm\; kpc$)." (4.2 Motion Along the Filament)
   - parameter: Hernquist a = 5.2 kpc; stellar mass 5e11 Msun  
  > "$a=5.2\rm\; kpc$ is a scale factor determined from the galaxy’s half-light radius" (4.2 Motion Along the Filament)
   - assumption: Cloud starts at rest with respect to ICM; Hernquist potential  
  > "For a hydrostatic atmosphere a condensing cloud should initially be at rest with respect to the ICM, so we take $v(r_0)=0$." (4.2 Motion Along the Filament)
   - fitting: Fit of velocity profile; sampler/priors not stated  
  > "The velocity profile of the filament is fitted with this expression. $v_{\rm ICM}$ and $i$ are allowed to vary, while $r_0$ is fixed to the maximum radial extent of the filament ($17.5\rm\; kpc$)." (4.2 Motion Along the Filament)
   - validation: Singular isothermal sphere gives indistinguishable fit  
  > "The best-fitting velocity profile for this potential is indistinguishable from the Hernquist profile" (4.2 Motion Along the Filament)
7. **Compute local SFRs from HST FUV and fit star formation law**: FUV count rates in regions scaled to global SFR; fit Sigma_SFR = A (Sigma_H2/100)^N in log space  
  > "Thus the SFR within each region is the ratio of its UV count rate to the total UV count rate multiplied by the global SFR." (4.3 Star Formation Along the Filament)
   - software: linmix
   - assumption: SFR uncertainty factor of two; Salim07 UV calibration (Chabrier IMF)  
  > "Throughout this work we take the SFR uncertainty to be a factor of two" (4.3 Star Formation Along the Filament)
   - fitting: Bayesian linear regression (Kelly 2007) via linmix  
  > "The fits were conducted in log space using the linmix package in Python, which employs the Bayesian approach to linear regression described in (cite: Kelly07)." (4.3 Star Formation Along the Filament)
   - validation: Compared with KS and Bigiel08 relations  
  > "The linear scaling measured between $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}$ and $\Sigma_{{\rm H}_2}$ in spiral galaxies (cite: Bigiel08) has also been plotted in Fig." (4.3 Star Formation Along the Filament)

## Qualitative findings

- Molecular gas is disturbed with no rotationally supported structure; 80% within central 5 kpc, remainder in a 20 kpc filament  
  > "The molecular gas morphology is complex and disturbed, showing no evidence for a rotationally-supported structure in equilibrium." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Galactic X_CO adopted as standard practice until direct calibrations in BCGs exist  
  > "We continue to follow the standard practice of adopting the Galactic $X_{\rm CO}$ until more direct calibrations in BCGs are available." (3.3 Molecular Gas Mass)
- Alternative: Mergers, tidal stripping and ram pressure stripping rejected as gas sources  
  > "minor mergers, tidal stripping, and ram pressure stripping are all unlikely to be viable mechanisms for supplying the rich reservoir of cold gas in BCGs." (4.1 Gas Origin)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The cavity is poorly defined and may not be real  
  > "The feature identified as a potential cavity is poorly defined, so it is not clear if it is a real cavity." (4.1 Gas Origin)
- Limitation (authors): HST images manually aligned, so small-scale offsets between gas and UV may be missed  
  > "Spatial offsets between the molecular gas and young stars could be present, but would be missed due to this manual alignment." (3.5 Spatial Correlation with UV Emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): X_CO uncertainty (factor of a few) propagates to masses and KS conclusions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Local SFRs rely on scaling a single global SFR by UV counts with no local extinction correction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Free-fall model is simplistic; fit uncertainties on inclination not given
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA recovers only 45% of single-dish flux, so extended gas is missed
