## Analysis recipe

1. **Define apertures and build the Halpha line map**: Elliptical apertures for E+W, E and W; Halpha line map from integrating the line around its peak  
  > "The H$\alpha$ line map is generated by integrating the H$\alpha$ line emission, centered at the H$\alpha$ peak of galaxies E+W (5.3510 $\mu$m)" (3.1 MIRI and NIRCam images and Halpha line map)
   - parameter: Galaxy E ellipse: semi-major 0.211 arcsec, semi-minor 0.201 arcsec, PA 112.83 deg  
  > "semi-major axis of 0.211 " and semi-minor axis of 0.201 ", position angle (PA) of 112.83º" (3.1 MIRI and NIRCam images and Halpha line map)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Apertures checked against the main emission in the MIRIM F560W image and the extended Halpha in the MRS map  
  > "The E and W elliptical apertures are compatible with the main emission of these galaxy components in our MIRIM F560W image." (3.1 MIRI and NIRCam images and Halpha line map)
2. **Extract Halpha spectra and fit the line**: Aperture spectra corrected for aperture losses and cross-contamination, background-subtracted with a median of empty-sky spectra, fitted with a Gaussian plus second-order polynomial  
  > "We extracted twelve 1D spectra using the same apertures as the E+W, E, and W galaxies, respectively, at random positions of the MRS FoV clean of emission." (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes); "The aperture correction is 1/0.57 for the galaxies E and W." (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes)
   - parameter: Single Gaussian plus second-order polynomial  
  > "The H$\alpha$ spectrum is modeled by a single Gaussian function, to fit the emission line, and a second-order polynomial, to fit any residual background gradient." (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes)
   - parameter: FWHM deconvolved with the MRS line spread function  
  > "after deconvolving with the line spread function of the MRS" (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes)
   - assumption: Galaxies E and W treated as unresolved sources for the MRS aperture correction  
  > "assuming that the two galaxies are unresolved sources for the MRS angular resolution" (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo: 1000 synthetic spectra with Gaussian noise from the continuum rms, refit; uncertainty = standard deviation  
  > "we generated 1000 synthetic spectra by adding random Gaussian noise to the original spectrum" (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes)
   - validation: Sum of E and W fluxes agrees with E+W flux; velocity offset compared with ALMA lines  
  > "The H$\alpha$ flux for galaxies E+W is in agreement within the uncertainties of the sum of the individual galaxy fluxes." (3.2 Halpha spectra and line fluxes)
3. **Measure MIRIM and PSF-homogenized NIRCam photometry**: Aperture photometry with the same apertures, local background in an elliptical annulus, uncertainties from 300 blank apertures  
  > "Additionally, we used 300 apertures on blank regions of the MIRI image with the same size as the galaxy apertures to estimate the flux uncertainties." (3.3 MIRIM and NIRCam photometry)
   - software: photutils
   - parameter: Aperture correction 1/0.67 for galaxy E (MIRIM F560W PSF); none for W  
  > "This aperture correction has a value of 1/0.67." (3.3 MIRIM and NIRCam photometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure stellar structure and effective radii**: GALFIT modelling of the F150W (and F115W) native-resolution image; statmorph morphology  
  > "we modeled the F150W data in GALFIT with a Sérsic profile (with n=0.5, which gives a Gaussian profile) to fit the diffuse emission" (3.4 Stellar structure and effective radius)
   - software: GALFIT, statmorph
   - parameter: Galaxy W modelled with four clumps; E = point source plus Sersic n = 0.5  
  > "It is well modeled by four different clumps." (3.4 Stellar structure and effective radius)
   - assumption: Circularized profile for W when computing its effective radius  
  > "When considering galaxy W as a single entity and circularizing the profile, the intrinsic effective radius is 348 pc" (3.4 Stellar structure and effective radius)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Extended analysis to F115W when the core remained unresolved in F150W  
  > "The core of galaxy E also remains unresolved in the F115W image" (3.4 Stellar structure and effective radius)
5. **SED fitting for stellar mass, ages, metallicity, SFR**: CIGALE fit of NIRCam+MIRIM photometry, ALMA continuum, Halpha and [O III] 88 um fluxes, with two SFH components  
  > "We performed an SED fitting using" (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history); "an instantaneous burst with ages ranging from 30 to 500 Myr to model the mature stellar population; and a constant burst with ages lower than 10 Myr to model the young stellar population" (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Metallicity free, 0.03 to 0.7 Zsun  
  > "The metallicity was treated as a free parameter, ranging from 0.03 Z$_{\odot}$ to 0.7 Z$_{\odot}$" (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history)
   - parameter: Electron density 100-1000 cm^-3; logU from -3.5 to -1.5  
  > "The nebular continuum and emission lines were modeled using electron densities from 100 to 1000 cm$^{-3}$ and the ionization parameter (logU) ranging from -3.5 to -1.5." (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history)
   - assumption: Bruzual and Charlot 2003 models, Chabrier IMF, Calzetti attenuation law, Draine 2014 dust emission  
  > "We used the (cite: BruzualandCharlot+03) stellar population models and the (cite: Chabrier+03) initial mass function (IMF)." (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history); "We used the dust attenuation law from (cite: Calzetti+00), and the dust emission was modeled using the (cite: Draine2014) models." (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history)
   - fitting: Best fit by chi-squared minimization  
  > "and ensuring the best agreement with observed photometry and line fluxes" (4.1 Spectral energy distribution and star formation history)
   - validation: A_FUV from SED compared with the IRX-based value  
  > "We obtain a value of $A_{FUV}$ = 1.50, which is compatible with that obtained with our SED fitting" (4.2 Nebular and stellar attenuation)
6. **Derive nebular attenuation from the Balmer decrement**: Halpha/Hbeta ratio against the case B value with the Cardelli law  
  > "Using (cite: Cardelli+89), we obtain that the nebular visual extinction" (4.2 Nebular and stellar attenuation)
   - parameter: Theoretical ratio 2.8 (case B, Te about 1.5e4 K, ne = 1e3 cm^-3)  
  > "Assuming a case B recombination, T$_{e}$ $\approx$ 1.5 $\times$ $10^{4}$ K and n$_{e}$ = $10^{3}$ cm$^{-3}$, the theoretical ratio is 2.8." (4.2 Nebular and stellar attenuation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Sugahara et al. 2024 attenuation values  
  > "in agreement, within the uncertainties, with the values of" (4.2 Nebular and stellar attenuation)
7. **Derive SFRs and burstiness**: Halpha SFR with metallicity-dependent conversion, corrected with the Balmer decrement; also [C II], UV, H-alpha+TIR and UV+TIR tracers; burstiness = SFR(Halpha)/SFR(UV)  
  > "We applied the corresponding SFR-to-H$\alpha$ conversion factor for each metallicity, which is approximately 1.7 times lower in the subsolar case ((cite: Reddy+18))." (4.3 Star formation rate and burstiness); "The final SFR values are corrected for attenuation using the H$\alpha$/H$\beta$ ratios" (4.3 Star formation rate and burstiness)
   - parameter: SFR(UV) for solar metallicity and constant SFH over 100 Myr  
  > "we used the SFR(UV) for solar metallicities and considering a constant SFH over 100 Myr" (4.3 Star formation rate and burstiness)
   - assumption: Solar and 0.29 Zsun metallicity cases  
  > "assuming solar (to facilitate comparison with other star formation tracers at other wavelengths) and subsolar metallicity" (4.3 Star formation rate and burstiness)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-checked against [C II], H-alpha+TIR and UV+TIR SFRs  
  > "The SFR derived from the H$\alpha$ and TIR luminosities are also in good agreement with those obtained from UV+TIR" (4.3 Star formation rate and burstiness)
8. **Derive Halpha equivalent width and ionizing photon production efficiency**: EW0 = Halpha flux over continuum under the line (F560W minus Halpha); zeta_ion from extinction-corrected Halpha ionizing photons over the 1500 A luminosity  
  > "The EW$_0$(H$\alpha$) is then derived as the ratio of the H$\alpha$ to the continuum flux under the H$\alpha$ line." (4.4 Equivalent width of Halpha and ionizing photon-production efficiency)
   - parameter: Zero escape fraction, T = 1.5e4 K  
  > "Assuming zero escape fraction" (4.4 Equivalent width of Halpha and ionizing photon-production efficiency)
   - assumption: Recombination; dust attenuation from Sect. 4.2  
  > "Assuming recombination, $\log(\zeta_\mathrm{ion}$) is empirically obtained as the ratio of the number of ionizing photons ($N_{LyC}$) derived from the H$\alpha$ line (after extinction correction)" (4.4 Equivalent width of Halpha and ionizing photon-production efficiency)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature samples and BPASS tracks in the zeta_ion-EW plane  
  > "We present the previous results in the $\log(\zeta_\mathrm{ion})$ – EW$_0$(H$\alpha$) plane" (4.4 Equivalent width of Halpha and ionizing photon-production efficiency)
9. **Compare gas kinematics and estimate dynamical masses**: Halpha FWHM and velocity offset compared with ALMA [C II] and [O III] widths; dynamical mass from velocity dispersion and NIRCam effective radius (Eq. 1 of Bellocchi et al. 2013)  
  > "assuming the system is close to relaxation and characterized by the random motions traced by the ionized gas velocity dispersion" (4.6 Stellar and dynamical mass)
   - parameter: Velocity dispersions 103 +/- 17 and 132 +/- 19 km/s for E and W  
  > "assuming velocity dispersions of 103 $\pm$ 17 km s$^{-1}$ and 132 $\pm$ 19 km s$^{-1}$ (obtained with the FWHM from our H$\alpha$ line)" (4.6 Stellar and dynamical mass)
   - assumption: System near relaxation, not rotation-dominated; treated as an upper limit  
  > "here we obtain an upper limit of the dynamical mass of each of the galaxies" (4.6 Stellar and dynamical mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SED stellar masses; narrow+broad NIRSpec line decomposition tested against the Halpha profile  
  > "We tested whether the observed H$\alpha$ line profile could be compatible with the combination of the narrow and broad" (4.5 Kinematics of the ionized ISM)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The merging system allows study of an early major merger, its stellar structure and ISM phases, and comparison with simulations  
  > "This system allows the investigation of the physical properties of an early major merger, including its stellar structure, as well as the different phases of the interstellar medium (ISM)." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: F150W was chosen for structural fitting for its S/N and resolution  
  > "we utilized the F150W image, as it offers the best combination of signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) and spatial resolution" (3.4 Stellar structure and effective radius)
- Alternative: Narrow+broad NIRSpec component model for the Halpha profile was tested and found only slightly compatible, with flux excess for galaxy E  
  > "Our tests (see Fig. [7](#fig:hbeta)) indicate that the narrow+broad (N+B) component could be slightly compatible with our observed H$\alpha$ line profiles." (4.5 Kinematics of the ionized ISM)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Dynamical mass of galaxy W is highly uncertain because the galaxy is far from relaxed  
  > "This suggests that this galaxy is far from relaxed, and therefore the estimated dynamical mass is highly uncertain." (4.6 Stellar and dynamical mass)
- Limitation (authors): Higher S/N MRS and higher-resolution NIRSpec spectra are needed to confirm any broad line emission  
  > "Further higher spectral resolution data with NIRSpec, and higher S/N MRS spectra, would be required to confirm the presence of such a broad line emission in the two galaxies." (4.5 Kinematics of the ionized ISM)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA enters only as ancillary published fluxes, so any ALMA-based quantity inherits unstated processing from earlier papers
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Large SED-fit uncertainties (about 50 percent on SFR and stellar mass) and limited MRS S/N (Halpha FWHM errors 15-20 percent) weaken the E versus W kinematic contrast
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical masses assume dispersion-supported, relaxed gas, which the authors themselves doubt
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The zeta_ion difference depends on UV and Halpha dust corrections and on the nebular continuum, which the paper notes are uncertain
