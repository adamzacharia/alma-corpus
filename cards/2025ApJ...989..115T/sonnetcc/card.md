## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the Band 3/4 ALMA data**: Standard CASA calibration and flagging; per-band continuum images from combined tunings (line channels excluded); 40 MHz cubes after uvcontsub, cleaned to 2 sigma, primary-beam corrected  
  > "The data were calibrated and flagged in the standard manner using the Common Astronomy Software Applications package" (2.4 ALMA follow-up line scan observations); "The emission is cleaned down to $2\sigma$ level." (2.4 ALMA follow-up line scan observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: natural weighting; channel width 40 MHz (~100 km/s)  
  > "using `CASA/tclean` task with the natural weighting." (2.4 ALMA follow-up line scan observations); "to produce a cube with a channel width of 40 MHz" (2.4 ALMA follow-up line scan observations)
   - parameter: pixel size 1 arcsec (M0417) and 0.2 arcsec (R0032)  
  > "We create images and cubes with a pixel size of 1$''$ for both band3 and 4 in the M0417. In the R0032 region, a pixel size of 0.2$''$ is used for both Band 3 and 4." (2.4 ALMA follow-up line scan observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra and measure line fluxes**: Integrated-intensity maps around 99.1 and 148.6 GHz locate peak pixels (M0417); circular 4 arcsec apertures (R0032); single Gaussian fit per line, total flux over 1.5 x FWHM with FWHM fixed to the CO(4-3) line  
  > "We first created integrated-intensity maps around 99.1 GHz for Band 3 and around 148.6 GHz for Band 4, where bright emission lines were detected." (3.1.1 M0417-z365); "We fitted the emission line with a single Gaussian and calculated the total flux as the integral over a frequency range of $1.5\times\text{FWHM}$." (3.1.1 M0417-z365); "We used a circular aperture with a diameter of $D=4\ensuremath{''}$ centered on the ALCS band 6 continuum positions to extract spectra and continuum fluxes." (3.1.2 R0032-z239)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: FWHM of all lines fixed to the highest-SNR CO(4-3) line  
  > "For a more robust measurement, the FWHM of all lines is fixed to that of the CO(4-3) line with the highest SNR." (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
   - parameter: noise = standard deviation of line-free channels within 50 channels of the line  
  > "the noise level is calculated as the standard deviation of the line-free channel fluxes at the same pixels, specifically those within 50 channels of the emission line" (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
   - assumption: M0417 detections treated as point sources because of the large beam  
  > "Due to the sufficiently large beam size, the detection can be considered as a point source." (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian line fit; error model not otherwise specified  
  > "We fitted the emission line with a single Gaussian" (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
   - validation: Growth curve confirms the 4 arcsec aperture includes the source flux (R0032)  
  > "By drawing a growth curve, we confirmed that this aperture is sufficient to include source fluxes." (3.1.2 R0032-z239)
3. **Identify spectroscopic redshifts from detected lines**: Search redshift solutions matching the detected line frequency combinations with the public redshift-search-graphs code  
  > "We search the redshift solution that matches the three detected line combinations using a publicly-available code[^1] (cite: Bakx2022)." (3.1.1 M0417-z365); "This code visually identifies the correct redshift solution by considering the observed frequencies, as well as the presence of detected lines and the absence of others." (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
   - software: redshift-search-graphs (Bakx & Dannerbauer 2022)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Redshifts are consistent with the lens-model predictions, and all three images of each system show the lines  
  > "This result is consistent with the initial prediction from our lens model, $z=3.0\pm0.5$." (3.1.3 R0032-z299); "This line detection confirms the initial prediction from our lens model that these three sources are triply lensed images." (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
4. **Build lens models and derive magnifications**: glafic parametric lens models for M0417 and R0032 constrained by literature multiple images plus the three new triply lensed systems; MCMC magnification uncertainty plus systematic error  
  > "We utilize the publicly available `glafic` code (cite: Oguri2010; Oguri2021) to construct our fiducial lens models, following (cite: Kawamata2016) and (cite: Okabe2020)." (3.2 Mass model); "Additionally, the three triply lensed systems identified in this study are included to provide further constraints." (3.2 Mass model)
   - software: glafic
   - parameter: image position uncertainty 0.4 arcsec and photometric-redshift uncertainty 0.6  
  > "we employ an MCMC method, taking into account uncertainties in the positions of the multiple images ($\Delta x= \Delta y=0.4\ensuremath{''}$) and photometric redshifts ($\Delta z= 0.6$)" (3.2 Mass model)
   - parameter: added systematic error 40% for mu<5 and 50% for mu>5  
  > "Specifically, we incorporate an error of 40% for magnifications below 5 and 50% for those above 5." (3.2 Mass model)
   - fitting: MCMC sampling of the lens parameters; median and 16th/84th percentiles reported  
  > "The median value is chosen as the representative value, with the 16th and 84th percentiles serving as the uncertainty range." (3.2 Mass model)
   - validation: Lens models predicted the multiple images later confirmed by spectroscopy; compared with other lens models for systematics  
  > "Thus, these three sources are triply lensed images as predicted from our lens model." (3.1.2 R0032-z239)
5. **Measure multi-band photometry for SED fitting**: Ks aperture photometry (1.5 arcsec, M0417 FourStar); SCUBA-2 peak values at ALMA Band 6 positions; ALMA Band 3/4 peak values near sources; HST/IRAC and Herschel from catalogs  
  > "We extracted photometries using a $D=1.5\ensuremath{''}$ aperture centered on the ALMA band 6 continuum positions for our target sources." (2.6 K_S-band); "Therefore, no deblending was required, and the peak values at the ALMA Band 6 continuum positions were extracted as the source photometries." (2.5 JCMT SCUBA-2 450 & 850 um); "We similarly measured the band 3 and 4 continuum fluxes by extracting peak values near the source positions." (3.1.1 M0417-z365)
   - software: PSFEx, STARLINK SMURF/DIMM/PICARD, CASA
   - parameter: Ks noise from 1000 random apertures; aperture correction ~1.4  
  > "The noise level is obtained by 1000 random apertures on the sigma-clipped map." (2.6 K_S-band); "The aperture correction factor, calculated with the obtained PSF, is $\sim1.4$." (2.6 K_S-band)
   - parameter: SCUBA-2 maps made with DIMM within SMURF, combined with PICARD  
  > "We reduced the data using the Dynamic Iterative Map Maker (DIMM) within the STARLINK Sub-Millimetre User Reduction Facility (SMURF) software package (cite: Chapin2013)." (2.5 JCMT SCUBA-2 450 & 850 um)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Ks aperture chosen by growth curve to maximise SNR  
  > "We confirmed that this aperture maximizes the SNR of the enclosed flux density by drawing a growth curve." (2.6 K_S-band)
6. **SED fitting for stellar mass, SFR, L_IR, A_V and dust mass**: CIGALE with a dynesty Bayesian search and flat priors, per lensed image; apparent quantities divided by magnification and combined by inverse-variance weighting  
  > "We use the `CIGALE` code (cite: Boquien2019) to perform SED fitting and estimate key physical parameters." (3.3 SED fitting); "After correcting each value for magnification, we took the intrinsic physical quantity as the average weighted by the inverse square of the uncertainties." (3.3 SED fitting)
   - software: CIGALE, dynesty
   - parameter: nonparametric SFH with 6 bins and continuity prior; SFR averaged over 100 Myr

## Qualitative findings

- The three NIR-dark galaxies lie on the star-formation main sequence at intrinsic stellar masses about an order of magnitude below typical SMGs  
  > "we find that the NIR-dark galaxies are located on the star formation main sequence in the intrinsic stellar mass range of log ($M_*$/$M_\odot$) = 9.8 – 10.4" (abstract)
- The three sources show a variety in gas depletion time and spatial extent of dust emission  
  > "These NIR-dark galaxies show a variety in gas depletion times and spatial extent of dust emission." (abstract)
- M0417-z365 and R0032-z239 have compact dust regions, as compact as NIR-dark SMGs, while R0032-z299 is more extended with much lower IR surface brightness  
  > "While the infrared luminosity of M0417-z365 and R0032-z239 is much lower than that of SMGs, their dust-emitting regions are as compact as those of NIR-dark SMGs." (3.5 Morphology of star-forming region); "On the other hand, R0032-z299, the faintest one in our sample, has a more extended dust continuum" (3.5 Morphology of star-forming region)
- M0417-z365 has lower [C I]/CO and [C I]/FIR luminosity ratios than R0032-z239, interpreted as higher gas density and stronger radiation field  
  > "indicating a higher gas density and stronger radiation field." (3.6 PDR properties)
- R0032-z299 is argued to be a normal star-forming galaxy seen edge-on, so such galaxies may be missed by deep WFC3/F160W surveys  
  > "we argue that normal star-forming galaxies in an edge-on configuration can be heavily dust-obscured." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Lensing makes intrinsically faint dust-obscured galaxies as bright as classical SMGs, and lens models gave redshift estimates for line-scan setups  
  > "Thanks to lens magnification, these galaxies appear as bright as classic SMGs (detectable with typical single-dish telescopes), making them suitable for follow-up observations." (1 Introduction); "We selected them since we are able to estimate their redshifts by the lens model, which allowed us to carry out line scan observations even though the photometric redshifts are unreliable." (2.3 Target selection)
- Why this method: The three lensed images are fitted individually because small-scale cluster substructure is not captured by the parametric mass model  
  > "However, galaxy cluster lenses have complex gravitational potential such as small-scale substructures that are not accounted for in the parametric mass model." (3.5 Morphology of star-forming region)
- Alternative: Both alpha_CO = 3.6 and alpha_CO = 1.0 are shown since the correct value is uncertain  
  > "In the following, we will show both results obtained with the assumption of" (3.4 Molecular gas properties)
- Alternative: Simultaneous fitting of the three lensed images with a single source model was preferable in principle but not done  
  > "Ideally, it would be preferable to simultaneously fit the triply lensed images using a single source model." (3.5 Morphology of star-forming region)
- Alternative: A face-on bar-like structure could also look elongated, alternative to an edge-on disk  
  > "although there is another case that even a face-on galaxy can appear elongated if we are viewing a bar-like structure" (4.2 R0032-z299: a normal DSFG with edge-on disk)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Line detections for R0032-z299 are marginal  
  > "even though the line detections are marginal" (3.1.3 R0032-z299)
- Limitation (authors): SED fitting codes differ systematically, especially in attenuation models, complicating comparison to literature  
  > "Different SED fitting codes often produce systematic differences in results due to various assumptions they employ, especially the dust attenuation models." (3.3 SED fitting)
- Limitation (authors): PDR modelling is whole-galaxy integrated and plane-parallel, so qualitative only  
  > "Since our measurements are integrated over the whole galaxy, the obtained results should be treated qualitatively." (3.6 PDR properties)
- Limitation (authors): Compactness as the cause of attenuation is not established; edge-on orientation is not the only factor  
  > "However, based only on our current results, it is unclear whether this compactness is the actual cause of the strong dust attenuation observed in these galaxies." (4.1 M0417-z365 and R0032-z239: compact dust-obscured galaxies)
- Limitation (authors): Degeneracy between dust mass and dust temperature remains with limited continuum bands  
  > "It would also be important to break the degeneracy between dust mass and dust temperature by observing the dust continuum with multiple bands." (4.2 R0032-z299: a normal DSFG with edge-on disk)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas masses depend strongly on alpha_CO and on assumed CO excitation ratios drawn from a different galaxy population; conclusions on depletion time flip between alpha_CO of 1.0 and 3.6
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The dust-to-gas ratio is defined as M_dust/M_gas yet quoted values of 23-31 only make sense as gas-to-dust ratio (Table 4 masses give M_dust/M_gas near 0.04)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Table 2 has an unlabelled row and the text does not say whether magnification-corrected fluxes were used in the gas masses
