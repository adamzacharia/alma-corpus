## Analysis recipe

1. **Continuum estimation and subtraction**: estimate continuum from line-free channels, subtract with uvcontsub, clean continuum and line cubes  
  > "We estimated the continuum flux level using channels that were unaffected by strong emission lines. The estimated continuum levels were subtracted using the task “uvcontsub”; the task “clean” was then applied" (Observations and Data Analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Superantennae continuum redetermined using only edge data points to avoid over-subtraction  
  > "We thus re-determined the continuum levels in these spectral windows using only a small number of data points at the edge of each spectral window" (3 Observations and Data Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: checked that outside-line signals in continuum-subtracted spectra became almost zero  
  > "signals outside the emission lines in the continuum-subtracted spectra became almost zero" (3 Observations and Data Analysis)
2. **Moment maps and line fluxes**: moment 0 by summing channels with discernible signal; fluxes from Gaussian fits to spectra within beam (adopted)  
  > "We will adopt the fluxes estimated from Gaussian fits for these detected emission lines." (4 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: peak-pixel and Gaussian fluxes agree within ~20%  
  > "Both estimates generally agree within $\sim$20%." (4 Results)
   - fitting: Gaussian line profile fits; single Gaussian after masking central dips for self-absorbed sources  
  > "Single Gaussian fit after removing data points affected by the central dips, based on the assumption of self-absorption." (Table 4)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Search for vibrationally excited lines**: moment 0 over ten channels at expected frequencies; 3-sigma upper limits; for LIRGs integrate range minimizing HCO+ contamination  
  > "We created their moment 0 maps by summing ten velocity channels at the expected frequencies of the HCN and HCO$^{+}$ v$_{2}$=1f J=3–2 lines" (4 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Source sizes**: deconvolved sizes with imfit  
  > "The deconvolved, intrinsic emission sizes, estimated using the CASA task “imfit”" (4 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **HCN/HCO+ flux ratio comparison and line opacity estimate**: compare HCN-to-HCO+ ratios by class (AGN vs starburst); estimate HCN attenuation via HCN/H13CN ratio  
  > "Assuming that the intrinsic $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C abundance ratios in these ULIRGs are 50–100" (5.2.3)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 50-100; H13CN optically thin; cosmology H0=71, OmegaM=0.27, OmegaL=0.73  
  > "we adopt H$_{0}$ $=$ 71 km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_{\rm M}$ = 0.27, and $\Omega_{\rm \Lambda}$ = 0.73" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: two methods (Gaussian fit vs isotopologue) compared  
  > "the flux attenuation estimated from the comparison of Gaussian fittings is smaller than that derived from the HCN-to-H$^{13}$CN J=3–2 flux comparison by a factor of 1–2" (5.2.3)

## Qualitative findings

- AGN-dominated nuclei tend to show elevated HCN-to-HCO+ J=3-2 flux ratios compared with starbursts, though some AGNs show non-high observed ratios  
  > "we see a clear trend for AGNs, including infrared-elusive buried AGN candidates, to show elevated HCN-to-HCO$^{+}$ J=3–2 flux ratios, compared with starburst regions." (5.2.1)
- IRAS 15250+3609 shows a P Cygni-like HCO+ profile interpreted as outflow  
  > "These features are interpreted to be due to the P Cygni profile by outflow activity of HCO$^{+}$." (Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 6 allows simultaneous coverage of HCN/HCO+ J=3-2 and v2=1f lines at z up to ~0.3 with better atmospheric conditions than band 7  
  > "HCN/HCO$^{+}$ J=3–2 observations in band 6 are less affected by the Earth’s atmospheric background noise" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: IC 4329 A non-detection: synchrotron-dominated L-band, extended IR, sub-thermal excitation and broad lines considered unlikely  
  > "Thus, this possibility also seems unlikely." (5.3)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Superantennae continuum determination ambiguity larger; HCN v2=1f detection may be contaminated by HCO+ v=0  
  > "although a possible contribution from the bright HCO$^{+}$ v=0 J=3–2 emission line cannot completely be ruled out" (4 Results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detections of HCN v2=1f are only ~4 sigma and blended with HCO+
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample is small and heterogeneously classified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line opacity not corrected for most sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ratio comparison uses non-matched beams/physical scales
