## Analysis recipe

1. **Image line cubes and continuum**: uvcontsub continuum subtraction, tclean imaging (Briggs 0.5 for [CII]; natural for CO(6-5)), plus uvtaper=1.5'' versions  
  > "We then image the line data cube using the *tclean* task with briggs weighting and a robustness parameter of 0.5" (2.1 [C ii] Fine Structure Line Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: moment-0 over -300 to 300 km/s  
  > "We use the data cube of \[C ii\] over the velocity range from –300 km s$^{-1}$ to 300 km s$^{-1}$ to derive the intensity map" (2.1 [C ii] Fine Structure Line Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure sizes and fluxes**: imfit 2D elliptical Gaussians on image plane, uvmodelfit on visibilities, Gaussian fits to extracted spectra, aperture growth curve  
  > "We fit the *uv*-data with the *uvmodelfit* routine in CASA by assuming an elliptical Gaussian profile." (2.1 [C ii] Fine Structure Line Observations)
   - software: CASA imfit, CASA uvmodelfit
   - fitting: elliptical Gaussians; errors per Condon 1997  
  > "the errors are estimated from the *imfit* fitting of elliptical Gaussians based on the work of (cite: Condon97)" (2.1 [C ii] Fine Structure Line Observations)
   - validation: image-plane size compared with uv-plane fit; tapered flux compared with previous PdBI  
  > "suggesting that the sizes measured in the image plane are robust" (2.1 [C ii] Fine Structure Line Observations)
3. **Fit dust SED**: graybody fits with free or fixed beta and T_dust, after CMB correction and radio subtraction  
  > "First, we set both dust emissivity index $\beta$ and dust temperature $\rm T_{dust}$ as free parameters" (3.1 Dust Temperature and Emissivity Index)
   - assumption: kappa_nu = 0.77 (nu/352 GHz)^beta cm2/g  
  > "assuming the dust opacity coefficient $\kappa_{\nu}(\beta)=0.77~(\nu/352~{\rm GHz})^\beta$ cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$" (3.2 FIR Luminosity and Dust Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: model-free comparison of continuum ratio with other z>6 quasars  
  > "we compare the observed continuum flux ratios of J0100+2802 with other luminous $z\gtrsim6$ quasars directly" (3.1 Dust Temperature and Emissivity Index)
4. **Model CO SLED**: RADEX single and two-component LVG; chi2 for single, MCMC for two components  
  > "we performed the Markov chain Monte Carlo (cite: mcmc) calculations to fit our observed fluxes with the fluxes generated from RADEX models" (4.1 CO Spectral Line Energy Distribution)
   - software: RADEX, emcee
   - parameter: n(H2)=10^2-10^7 cm-3, Tkin=Tcmb-1000 K, N_CO/dv=10^15.5-10^19.5  
  > "$n_{\rm (H_2)}$=10$^2$–10$^7$ cm$^{-3}$, T$_{\rm kin}$=T$_{\rm CMB}$-10$^3$ K" (4.1 CO Spectral Line Energy Distribution)
   - assumption: background temperature = CMB at z=6.327  
  > "We set the background temperature to be the CMB temperature at $z=6.327$." (4.1 CO Spectral Line Energy Distribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: single-component reduced chi2=2 does not reproduce SLED shape  
  > "the best-fit single LVG model does not reproduce the shape of CO SLED well" (4.1 CO Spectral Line Energy Distribution)
5. **Molecular gas mass**: CO(1-0) from model times alpha_CO per component  
  > "by assuming $\alpha_{\rm CO}$=0.8 $M_\odot$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$ for the cool gas and $\alpha_{\rm CO}$=0.16" (4.2 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=0.8 cool, 0.16 warm; gas-to-dust ratio 80  
  > "Assuming a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 80 (cite: Riechers13)" (4.2 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: compared with gas mass from dust  
  > "This gas mass is similar to the one we estimated from the LVG model." (4.2 Molecular Gas Mass)
6. **Kinematics and dynamical mass**: Blue/red channel maps; M_dyn = 3R sigma^2/2G virial, and rotating-disk M_dyn = 1.16e5 v_cir^2 D  
  > "The dynamical mass is measured to be $M_{\rm dyn}=3R\sigma^2/2G$=(3.25$\pm$0.46)$\times$10$^{10}$ M$_\odot$" (5.2 Dynamical Mass)
   - assumption: v_cir=0.75 FWHM/sin i; inclination from axis ratio  
  > "$v_{\rm cir}=0.75$ $\times$FWHM$_{\rm [CII]}$/sin($i$)" (5.2 Dynamical Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

- Limitation (authors): Continuum points lie on the Rayleigh-Jeans tail, so T_dust and beta are degenerate; shorter-wavelength data needed.  
  > "most of the continuum points are on the Rayleigh–Jeans tail of the dust emission and we cannot tightly constrain the temperature and emissivity at the same time" (3.1 Dust Temperature and Emissivity Index)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Faint, marginal detections (CO(7-6), CO(10-9)) enter SLED fit using peak-pixel fluxes
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(2-1) from lower-resolution VLA mixes beam sizes in the SLED
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical mass depends strongly on unconstrained inclination
