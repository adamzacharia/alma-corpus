## Qualitative findings

- The scenario of cold molecular gas accumulation (10^4 M_sun) next to the M31 nucleus from mass loss of the old stellar population is excluded at a level of 150sigma.  
  > "The scenario of cold gas accumulation next to the nucleus of M31 that originates from mass loss of the old stellar population is not verified and excluded at a level of 150σ." (abstract)
- The nucleus of M31 has less ionised gas in proportion than Sgr A*, with the H30alpha 3sigma upper limit being twice lower than expected from simple scaling.  
  > "We searched for an ionised gas disc around the nucleus of M31 with NOEMA, and we place a 3σ upper limit on the H30α recombination line at a level twice lower than expected with a simple scaling of the SgrA*." (abstract); "Therefore, our upper limit is twice below what was expected. This means that the nucleus of M31 has less ionised gas than SgrA* in proportion." (3.2. Hydrogen recombination line)
- The continuum upper limits are compatible with the synchrotron power-law emission derived from lower-frequency measurements, with no dust emission expected.  
  > "Our upper limits are compatible with the synchrotron power-law emission that was derived from lower-frequency measurements (Crane et al. 1992, 1993; Yang et al. 2017). Given the absence of gas in the nucleus, no dust emission is expected." (3.3. Continuum flux)
- Previous episodic AGN activity (rather than planetary nebulae or A0 stars in P3) likely explains the excitation of the inner optical Halpha disc and may have destroyed CO or quenched the central region.  
  > "The previous relic activity of the central engine might well explain the excitation of this inner Hα disc." (4. Discussion and conclusions); "Our upper limits on the molecular _H_ 2 mass (195 _M_ ⊙ (3σ) based on ALMA _CO_ (3 − 2) observations) and the detection of a weak Hα disc by Menezes et al. (2013) support the evidence of previous activity of the AGN (e.g. kinetic jet feedback)," (4. Discussion and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Previous NOEMA observations had a beam of 3.37'' x 2.44'' (13 pc x 9 pc) and may have missed gas due to signal dilution; new NOEMA and ALMA observations gained an order of magnitude in sensitivity and resolution, and CO(3–2) also constrains warm gas that escapes CO(1–0).  
  > "The CO(3–2) upper limit also constrains warm gas, which escapes detection in CO(1–0)." (abstract); "It is therefore possible that previous NOEMA observations with a beam of 3.37 [′′] × 2.44 [′′] (i.e. about 13 pc × 9 pc) did not succeed in detecting this gas because they lacked the sensitivity due to the dilution of the signal." (1. Introduction); "In this paper, we present new NOEMA and Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) observations of the M31 centre that gained an order of magnitude in sensitivity and resolution." (1. Introduction)

- Alternative: Stellar objects (planetary nebulae or the A0-star cluster in P3) as the ionisation source of the central Halpha disc are excluded because they produce far fewer ionising photons than the 10^48 photons/s required.
