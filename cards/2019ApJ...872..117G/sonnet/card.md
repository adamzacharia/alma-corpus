## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA/VLA cubes and continuum**: CASA clean with natural weighting (ALMA); Briggs robust 0.5 or natural (VLA)  
  > "HELAISS02 and HXMM20 data were mapped using the `CLEAN` algorithm with natural weighting to get the best point source sensitivity." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Blind CO(1-0) line search in HCOSMOS02**: MF3D matched filtering; S/N>5.8 threshold from purity analysis  
  > "The purities analysis revealed that $\rm{S/N} > 5.8$ is the threshold above which the ratio of spurious negative detections over positive detections is 0." (3.3 HCOSMOS02)
   - software: MF3D
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Candidates at 5.0<S/N<5.8 checked for symmetric negative peaks and counterparts, then discarded  
  > "Therefore, we ended up discarding the sources in the range $5.0 < \rm{S/N} < 5.8$ since they were not reliable." (3.3 HCOSMOS02)
3. **Measure line centroids, widths and fluxes**: specfit single Gaussian on spectra at 870 um peak; imfit 2D point-source fits per channel with fixed beam shape and position; uncertainties from moment-0 noise  
  > "we fixed the Gaussian width and position angle to those of the clean beam and the position to the 870 $\mu$m peak." (3.1 HELAISS02)
   - software: CASA specfit, CASA imfit
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Deblended fluxes compared with moment-0 over aperture enclosing blended sources  
  > "we checked that the recovered fluxes in these blended sources are consistent with that measured in a moment-0 map created by collapsing over the line channels" (3.2 HXMM20)
4. **CO-based molecular gas masses**: M_H2 = alpha_CO L'CO(1-0); CO(3-2) converted with r31  
  > "we adopted $\alpha_{\rm{CO}} = 3.5$ as reported in (cite: 2017A&A...603A..93M) for normal SFGs at solar metallicity" (4.1 CO-based Estimates)
   - parameter: r31 = 0.69 ± 0.09 from HXMM20 applied to HELAISS02  
  > "we used the line ratio $r_{\rm{31}} = 0.69 \pm 0.09$ derived for HXMM20 from our data" (4.1 CO-based Estimates)
   - assumption: Solar metallicity; alpha_CO = 3.5  
  > "we assumed a solar metallicity for all sources." (4.1 CO-based Estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **FIR SED fit for Mdust, LIR, SFR**: DL07 models chi2 minimization; MIPS 24um fluxes via GALFIT; SPIRE scaled by 870um flux ratios; 1000 perturbed realizations for errors  
  > "To estimate the uncertainties of the parameters we created 1000 realizations of the observed SEDs by perturbing the photometry within the errors and repeating the fit." (4.2 FIR Properties)
   - software: GALFIT
   - parameter: q_PAH 0.4–4.6%, Umin 0.7–25, gamma 0.0–0.8; Umax=1e6, alpha=2  
  > "we fixed $U_{\rm{max}} = 10^{6}$, and $\alpha = 2$" (4.2 FIR Properties)
   - assumption: SFR_IR from LIR via Kennicutt98 for Chabrier IMF; LIR integrated 8–1000 um  
  > "$L_{\rm{IR}}$ was calculated by integrating the best fit to the SED in the range 8–1000 $\mu$m." (4.2 FIR Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Dust-based gas masses and alpha_CO**: Gas-to-dust ratio (Magdis+12 relation) and RJ single-band (Scoville+14 eq. 12); alpha_CO = M_H2^GD / L'CO  
  > "We assumed a solar metallicity for all sources that corresponds to a $M_{\rm{gas}}/M_{\rm{dust}} \sim 90$." (4.3 Dust-based Estimates)
   - assumption: _not stated_
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: GD and RJ estimates agree, average ratio 1.24 ± 0.23  
  > "with an average ratio of 1.24 $\pm$ 0.23" (4.3 Dust-based Estimates)
7. **Stellar masses**: Deblended aperture/GALFIT photometry; LePHARE SED fit with BC03, Chabrier IMF, exponential SFH, Calzetti law, redshift fixed to CO  
  > "We assumed a (cite: 2003PASP..115..763C) IMF, exponentially declining star formation histories (SFHs) and a (cite: 2000ApJ...533..682C) dust law." (4.4 Stellar Masses)
   - software: LePHARE, GALFIT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare to MS and field scaling relations**: f_H2, SFE and DMS vs Scoville+17 field relations  
  > "we compare $f_{\rm{H_2}}$ and $\tau_{\rm{H_2}}$ with the gas scaling relations for field galaxies in (cite: 2017ApJ...837..150S)" (5.2 Gas Fractions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- All ALMA 870 um sources in all three cores lie at the same redshift  
  > "turned out to be located at the same redshift as confirmed by the CO observations presented in our work." (5.1 Blends of DSFGs)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Briggs robust 0.5 for HXMM20 as best compromise between resolution to deblend and sensitivity  
  > "as it gave the best compromise between the spatial resolution required to deblend the different ALMA counterparts and the sensitivity to detect them." (2.3 VLA Observations)
- Alternative: Pallas rejected as HXMM20 flux calibrator due to 30% discrepancy  
  > "therefore, it was rejected as flux calibrator." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): SFE trends are artificially created by scaling assumptions for LIR; no constraint on SED peak  
  > "our current data requires the use of assumptions that are artificially creating any trend in SFE ($\tau_{\rm{H_2}}$)." (5.2 Gas Fractions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO and r31 assumptions drive gas masses and SFE conclusions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small samples of three systems; protocluster nature requires wider-field confirmation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam mismatch between CARMA/NOEMA and VLA affects line ratios
