## Analysis recipe

1. **Continuum image and upper limit**: Average line-free LSB and USB windows into a 259.01 GHz continuum image; inspect for detection; 3σ upper limit  
  > "We conclude therefore that Himiko remains undetected in the 1.2mm continuum with a $3\sigma$ upper limit is $<52.1\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$." (ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: σ_cont = 17.4 μJy/beam  
  > "with a $1\sigma$ sensitivity of $17.4\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$." (ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ~2σ peak judged unreliable given nearby 2-3σ negative pixels  
  > "However, there are a series of negative pixels nearby that correspond to the $2-3 \sigma$ level per beam." (ALMA)
2. **Search for [CII] line**: Velocity channel maps at 200 km/s over ±600 km/s around z_Lyα; 3σ upper limit and luminosity  
  > "We searched for a signal of over 600 km $^{-1}$ around the frequency corresponding to $z_{\rm Ly\alpha}=6.595$." (ALMA); "The corresponding $3\sigma$ upper limit for is $250.0\mu$Jy in a channel width of 200 km s$^{-1}$." (ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: channel width 200 km/s  
  > "The line sensitivity is defined with a channel width of $200$ km$^{-1}$." (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 3σ noise peaks offset from optical centre rejected as counterparts  
  > "However, neither of these sources is a reliable counterpart of Himiko." (ALMA)
3. **FIR luminosity limit**: Optically thin graybody scaled to 1.2 mm limit; integrated over 8-1000 μm; Td and β varied  
  > "Assuming an optically thin graybody of modified blackbody radiation with a dust emissivity power-law spectral index of $\beta_{\rm d}=1.5$ and a dust temperature of $T_d=40$K" (Far Infrared SED)
   - parameter: adopted Td=40 K, βd=1.5 as conservative  
  > "Because the larger assumed dust temperature $T_d=40$ K with $\beta_{\rm d}=1.5$ provides a weaker upper limit, we adopt a conservative $3\sigma$ upper limit" (Far Infrared SED)
   - assumption: CMB sets lower limit Td=21 K; range Td≈20-40 K  
  > "this yields a lower limit of $T_d=21$ K. Thus, it is appropriate to consider a range of $T_d\simeq 20-40$ K with $\beta_{\rm d}\simeq 1.5$." (Far Infrared SED)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Sensitivity tested for Td=25,60 K and βd=0,2  
  > "For $T_d=25$K and $T_d=60$K, the $3\sigma$ upper limit luminosities in $8-1000\mu$m are $<2.7\times 10^{10}$ and $<3.0\times 10^{11}L_\odot$, respectively." (Far Infrared SED)
4. **HST and Spitzer photometry**: MultiDrizzle reduction, PSF-matched to H160 (0''.19), 0''.4 apertures for clumps and 2'' total; IRAC 3'' aperture after realignment  
  > "We perform $0''.4$-diameter aperture photometry for the clumps A-C and location D as well as $2''$-diameter aperture photometry which we adopt as the total magnitude of the system." (HST)
   - software: PyRAF, MULTIDRIZZLE, WFC3
   - parameter: final_pixfrac=0.5, pixel scale 0''.05132  
  > "in the multidrizzle processing we chose a final$\_$pixfrac$=0.5$ and pixel scale of $0''.05132$." (HST)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: IRAC–HST relative astrometry ≃0''.1 rms  
  > "The relative astrometric errors are estimated to be $\simeq 0''.1$ rms." (Spitzer)
5. **SED fitting for stellar population**: χ2 fitting of BC03 models with Calzetti attenuation, with and without nebular emission  
  > "we search for the best-fit model in a parameter space of $E(B-V)=0-1$ and age$=1-810$ Myr" (Improved Physical Properties from the Near-Infrared SED)
   - parameter: Salpeter IMF 0.1-100 Msun; Z=0.02-1.0 Zsun; constant and exponentially decaying SFH  
  > "We adopt Salpeter initial mass function (IMF; (cite: salpeter1955)) with lower and upper mass cutoffs of 0.1 and 100 $M_{\odot}$, respectively." (Improved Physical Properties from the Near-Infrared SED)
   - assumption: f_esc=0 for nebular models  
  > "we assume that all ionizing photons lead to nebular emission lines corresponding to an escape fraction $f_{\rm esc}$=0." (Improved Physical Properties from the Near-Infrared SED)
   - fitting: χ2 grid, ΔE(B-V)=0.01; dof=6  
  > "The degree of freedom (dof) is six." (Table 4)
   - validation: Reduced χ2 compared: 3.1 (pure stellar) vs 1.6 (stellar+nebular)  
  > "the best fit has a more satisfactory reduced $\chi^2$, $1.6$" (Improved Physical Properties from the Near-Infrared SED)
6. **Compare [CII] and FIR limits with scaling relations**: Place L[CII] limit vs SED SFR on de Looze relation; convert L_FIR to SFR(FIR) with Kennicutt 1998 and Buat & Xu 1996  
  > "Himiko clearly departs significantly from the scaling relation; the deficit amounts to a factor $\simeq\times$30." (ISM metallicity from Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **UV slopes, IRX-β and β-EW0 diagnostics**: β from J125-H160 colour; compared with Meurer IRX-β and Raiter et al. 2010 models  
  > "We estimated the UV slope, $\beta$, from the $J_{125}$ and $H_{160}$ photometry" (UV Spectral Slopes on the Spatially Resolved Images)
   - assumption: k_1600=10 Calzetti; A1600=4.43+1.99β  
  > "Calzetti extinction, $A_{1600}=k_{1600} E(B-V)_s$, where $k_{1600}$ is $10$ (cite: ouchi2004a)." (UV Spectral Slopes on the Spatially Resolved Images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Considered Δβ≃+0.1 photometric bias  
  > "Even including such a possible bias, Clump A remains significantly bluer than the average $\simeq L^*$ LBGs at the $\simeq 2\sigma$ level." (UV Spectral Slopes on the Spatially Resolved Images)

## Qualitative findings

- Himiko's mm emission is comparable with or weaker than local dwarf irregulars, implying low dust content  
  > "it is more comparable to those of dwarf galaxies of much lower mass." (Far Infrared SED)
- Himiko is a triple L* clump system (one LAE, two LBGs) in a 17 kpc Lyα halo; not lensed  
  > "Himiko is a triple $L^*$ galaxy system comprising one LAE and two LBGs surrounded by an extensive 17 kpc diffuse Ly$\alpha$ halo." (HST)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 6 targets redshifted [CII] and dust; USB placed high to maximise dust continuum  
  > "Because a brighter dust continuum is expected at a higher frequency in the 1.2mm regime, we extended our upper sideband (USB) to the high-frequency side." (ALMA)
- Why this method: Nebular emission added because pure stellar fit poor at 3.6 μm  
  > "this encourages us to include nebular emission in our fitting procedure." (Improved Physical Properties from the Near-Infrared SED)
- Alternative: Alternative Td (25, 60 K) and βd (0, 2) for L_FIR  
  > "Similarly, for $\beta_{\rm d}=0$ and $\beta_{\rm d}=2$, the $3\sigma$ upper limit luminosities in $8-1000\mu$m are $<3.5\times 10^{10}$ and $<1.2\times 10^{11}L_\odot$, respectively." (Far Infrared SED)
- Alternative: Four explanations for [CII] deficit: AGN, dense PDRs, low metallicity, heavy dust; AGN and heavy dust rejected  
  > "Himiko has a) a hard ionizing spectrum from an AGN, b) a very high density of photo-dissociation regions (PDRs), c) a low metallicity, and d) a large column density of dust." (The Low Dust and Metal Content of Himiko)
- Alternative: Single virialized galaxy and cold accretion scenarios considered  
  > "we consider that Himiko is unlikely to be a single virialized system" (Nature of Himiko)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): FIR limits depend on assumed Td and β  
  > "Note that these upper limits depend upon the assumed dust temperature and $\beta_{\rm d}$." (Far Infrared SED)
- Limitation (authors): Pure stellar SED fit unsatisfactory  
  > "On the other hand, the fit itself is not very satisfactory." (Improved Physical Properties from the Near-Infrared SED)
- Limitation (authors): F098M only marginally detects extended Lyα  
  > "The $F098M$ image in Figure [4](#fig:HimikoHstSpitzerSubaru_images) detects only marginal extended Ly$\alpha$ emission" (HST)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): [CII] limit assumes a single 200 km/s channel and point-source beam; a broader or spatially extended line would raise the limit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison relies on SED-derived SFR including extinction correction, while UV-only SFR is ~30 Msun/yr, reducing the deficit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No imaging parameters given, so the limits cannot be checked independently
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Lyα redshift used to center the search; Lyα can be offset from systemic
