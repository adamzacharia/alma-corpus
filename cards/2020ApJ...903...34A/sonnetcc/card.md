## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit GNIRS spectrum and derive black hole mass, Eddington ratio and line properties**: Multi-component fit (power law + UV Fe II continuum, single Gaussian broad lines) with a modified PyQSOFit; Mg II virial mass from the single-epoch scaling relation; L_bol = 5.15 x lambda L_3000  
  > "The global continuum was modelled using combined power-law and UV Fe II templates" (Modeling the emission lines and underlying continuum); "After subtracting the best-fit continuum and scaled iron templates, each broad emission line was modeled with single Gaussian functions." (Modeling the emission lines and underlying continuum); "we use the scaling relation for the Mg II line from (cite: 2009ApJ...699..800V):" (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio)
   - software: PyQSOFit (modified), PypeIt, Molecfit
   - parameter: Rest-frame continuum windows: 1285-1290, 1315-1325, 1350-1370, 1445-1465, 1580-1650, 2140-2300, 2340-2400, 2420-2480, 2630-2710, 2745-2765, 2850-3000 A  
  > "The following wavelength ranges were selected as continuum windows: $\lambda_\mathrm{rest}=$ 1285–1290 Å, 1315–1325 Å, 1350–1370 Å, 1445–1465 Å, 1580–1650 Å, 2140–2300 Å, 2340–2400 Å, 2420–2480 Å, 2630–2710 Å, 2745–2765 Å, and 2850–3000 Å." (Modeling the emission lines and underlying continuum)
   - parameter: Bolometric correction L_bol = 5.15 x lambda L_3000 (Richards et al. 2006)  
  > "Then, the bolometric luminosity $L_{\rm bol}$ was derived using the empirical correction from (cite: 2006ApJS..166..470R):" (Modeling the emission lines and underlying continuum)
   - assumption: Virial theorem valid for the BLR dynamics; systematic mass errors ~0.5 dex not included  
  > "With the assumption that the virial theorem is valid for the BLR dynamics" (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio); "we did not explicitly add the systematic errors which tend to be larger ($\approx 0.5$ dex) compared to random measurement errors" (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo: 1000 mock spectra with noise drawn from the noise spectrum; 16th/84th percentiles as limits  
  > "Random flux density errors are drawn assuming a normal distribution using the noise spectrum, then applied to the raw spectrum with 1000 iterations." (Modeling the emission lines and underlying continuum)
   - validation: Compared with values from the combined FIRE spectra  
  > "As a comparison, we derived $\log (M_{\rm BH}/M_\odot) = 9.06^{+0.24}_{-0.16}$ and $L_{\rm bol}/L_{\rm Edd} = 0.77_{-0.33}^{+0.33}$ from the combined FIRE spectra." (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio)
2. **Measure weak-line equivalent widths, proximity zone size and quasar lifetime**: Integrate flux above power-law continuum in 1160-1290 A (Ly-alpha+N V) and 1500-1600 A (C IV upper limit); proximity zone from continuum-normalized FIRE spectrum; lifetime from radiative transfer simulations  
  > "where the fluxes above the power-law continuum in the range of 1160–1290 Å were integrated" (Weak emission lines and young quasar accretion lifetime); "we applied the same procedure for the wavelength range of 1500–1600 Å to estimate the 3-sigma upper limit of the C IV $\lambda 1549$ equivalent width" (Weak emission lines and young quasar accretion lifetime); "Conversion of proximity zone size to quasar lifetime can be inferred from radiative transfer simulations (cite: 2016MNRAS.457.3006D)" (Weak emission lines and young quasar accretion lifetime)
   - parameter: FIRE spectrum continuum-normalized and convolved with a 20 A boxcar kernel; proximity zone edge where transmitted flux first drops below 10%  
  > "we continuum-normalized the quasar Magellan/FIRE spectrum and applied a convolution with a 20 Å resolution boxcar kernel." (Weak emission lines and young quasar accretion lifetime)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Absorber alternative for the small proximity zone discussed (hypothetical absorption system at z=6.295 judged unlikely to be significant)  
  > "the proximity zone might be influenced by such a hypothetical absorber, but would unlikely be significant." (A small caveat regarding the small proximity zone size)
3. **Test gravitational lensing with HST imaging**: PSF subtraction using eight field stars (Photutils); aperture photometry of foreground emission; BAGPIPES SED model of the foreground galaxy to get stellar mass; point-mass lens to bound Einstein angle and magnification  
  > "We chose eight stars in the field to construct a point-spread function (PSF) using median-averaging." (Modeling a possible gravitational lensing effect)
   - software: Photutils, BAGPIPES
   - parameter: Uniform priors on M_formed = 10^(1-15) Msun and T_0 = 0.5-0.8 Gyr  
  > "The model will fit the total formed stellar mass and time since star formation began with uniform priors in the value ranges of $M_{\rm formed} = 10^{1-15} M_\odot$ and $T_0 = 0.5-0.8$ Gyr, respectively." (Modeling a possible gravitational lensing effect)
   - parameter: Aperture photometry with 4-pixel (~0.5'') radius  
  > "Its AB-magnitude was estimated using aperture photometry, with an aperture of 4-pixel ($\sim 0.5\ensuremath{''}$) radius" (Modeling a possible gravitational lensing effect)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA Band 6 chosen to resolve [C II], get an accurate redshift and study the host galaxy  
  > "The ALMA band-6 observation were performed to spatially resolve the \[C II\] 158$\,\mu$m line, determine an accurate redshift, and investigate the host galaxy" (Sub-millimeter observation with ALMA)
- Why this method: Fixed dust temperature and emissivity used because only two continuum points on the Rayleigh-Jeans tail cannot constrain SED shape  
  > "This prohibits us to constrain the full SED shape and dust temperature due to degenerated fitting parameters." (Star formation rate)
- Alternative: Soft ionizing continuum models (super-Eddington accretion, cold disk, slim-disk shielding) judged inadequate, though shielding gas not excluded  
  > "Hence, overall soft continuum models seem inadequate to explain the weak-line nature of PSO J083+11." (Soft continuum due to super-Eddington accretion?)
- Alternative: Absorber truncating the proximity zone (DLA/LLS) considered as an alternative to short lifetime  
  > "There is a hypothetical alternative, the truncation of PSO J083+11’s proximity zone due to the presence of an absorption systems" (A small caveat regarding the small proximity zone size)
- How they decided it was enough: Aperture radius of 1.5'' set where no more [C II] flux is recovered  
  > "We chose this value, because there is no further \[C II\] flux recovered outside this radius." ([C II] spectral profile)
- Limitation (authors): Only two continuum points on the Rayleigh-Jeans tail, so dust temperature and SED shape unconstrained; dust mass has at least a factor of two systematic uncertainty  
  > "Note that we only have 2 data points of continuum measurements – at 244 and 258 GHz – both of them located on the Rayleigh–Jeans tail and not reaching the dust SED peak." (Star formation rate); "Note that the estimated dust mass will have at least a factor of two for the systematic uncertainty due to the adopted opacity coefficient scaling relation." (Star formation rate)
- Limitation (authors): Black hole mass systematic errors (~0.5 dex) not included; virial calibration may be inadequate for WLQs  
  > "However, please note that we did not explicitly add the systematic errors which tend to be larger ($\approx 0.5$ dex) compared to random measurement errors" (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio)
- Limitation (authors): Shielding gas between disk and BLR cannot be excluded without X-ray spectroscopy  
  > "However, we cannot completely rule out the possibility of the presence of shielding gas between accretion disk and BLR due to the lack of X-ray spectroscopy." (Summary and Conclusion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SFR and dust mass rest on fixed T_dust=47 K, beta=1.6 and a single opacity law with no free SED parameters, so L_FIR, L_TIR and M_dust are model-dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Proximity-zone lifetime depends on the FIRE spectrum (low S/N, R~500-6000) and on a simulation grid; it is sensitive to possible obscured earlier accretion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux correction epsilon ~ 0.6 for the [C II] flux is described only briefly and could change the flux, L[CII] and SFR
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mg II velocity offset (237 +/- 150 km/s) shifts the black hole redshift relative to the [C II] systemic redshift, which may affect proximity zone sizing
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA part is a single execution at ~0.4'' resolution, so kinematic interpretation (mild gradient, merger hint) is weakly constrained
