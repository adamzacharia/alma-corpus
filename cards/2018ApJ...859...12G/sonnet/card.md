## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum at multiple resolutions**: CLEAN with natural weighting and outer uv-tapers (3500 and 350 klambda)  
  > "We created maps at intermediate resolution by applying an outer $uv$-taper of 3500 k$\lambda$ to the data when imaging." (4 Observations and reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Recovered flux compared to Cycle 0/1 flux  
  > "which recover 80 to 100% of the continuum flux detected in the ALMA Cycle 0/1 observations" (5 Analysis)
2. **Measure continuum flux recovery**: Curve-of-growth optimized apertures, compared with Cycle 0/1 fluxes  
  > "with an optimized aperture size determined by using a curve of growth we recover between $44\pm3$ and $81\pm12$%" (5 Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract [CII] spectra**: Spectra from apertures chosen to maximise S/N of the line at 0.03'' resolution  
  > "select extraction apertures to maximize the recovered signal-to-noise of the line emission" (5.1 [CII] emission lines)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with lower-resolution Cycle 0/1 spectra  
  > "compares the recovered [CII] emission with the spectra from the lower resolution observations from ALMA Cycle 0 and 1" (5.1 [CII] emission lines)
4. **Measure sizes from uv-amplitude profiles**: Radially averaged visibility amplitudes vs uv-distance fitted with half-Gaussian plus constant (point source)  
  > "We fit a half-Gaussian profile plus a constant (representing a point source) to the amplitudes in order to measure the physical size of the emission" (5.2.1 Continuum)
   - parameter: bins 75 klambda for continuum; 300 klambda ([CII] ALESS 61.1, UDS 47.0) and 75 klambda (ALESS 65.1, 73.1)  
  > "we therefore have to apply a larger binning of 300 k$\lambda$ for ALESS 61.1 and UDS 47.0 and 75 k$\lambda$ for ALESS 65.1 and ALESS 73.1" (5.2.2 [CII] emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with De Breuck+14 sizes for ALESS 73.1  
  > "we derive a size from a single Gaussian fit without a point source of $0.38\ensuremath{''}\pm0.05\ensuremath{''}$ which is consistent" (5.2.1 Continuum)
5. **Test clumpiness against simulated smooth disks**: Simulate 50 n=1 exponential disks, fit Sersic profile, subtract, compare residual pixel histograms with KS test  
  > "A Kolmogorov-Smirnov test comparing the average of the residual pixel distribution from the simulated smooth disks and that of the observations" (6.3 Morphologies)
   - software: CASA simobserve, CASA simanalyze
   - assumption: Input flux 8.7 mJy and size 0.28'' as for UDS 47.0  
  > "flux densities and sizes of $8.7\pm0.6$ mJy and $0.28\ensuremath{''}\pm0.03\ensuremath{''}$" (6.3 Morphologies)
   - fitting: Sersic fits with free index; Gaussian fit to residual histogram  
  > "We do this by fitting single smooth profiles (with the Sérsic index as a free parameter, to the simulated maps), and subtracting the best fit model." (6.3 Morphologies)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Derive dust, gas and dynamical masses**: Dust mass from 345 GHz flux with kappa; gas via GDR=90; dynamical mass R v^2/G  
  > "We calculate the dust masses using the measured continuum flux from ALMA listed in [\[table:cont_cy0\]](#table:cont_cy0) and $M_{\text{d}}=S_{\nu}D_{\text{L}}^2/(\kappa B_{\nu}(T_{\text{d}})(1+z))$" (2.1 Physical properties)
   - assumption: Td=50 K, beta=1.5, kappa 0.076 m2/kg, GDR 90±25  
  > "We adopt an average dust temperature of $T_{\text{d}}=50\pm4$ K, a dust emissivity index of $\beta=1.5$" (2.1 Physical properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Dust-based and [CII]-based gas masses agree within uncertainties  
  > "The two independent methods of estimating the gas masses result in masses agreeing within the uncertainties." (2.1 Physical properties)
7. **Core vs annulus L[CII]/LFIR vs SigmaSFR**: Extract [CII] spectra in continuum aperture and [CII] aperture; annulus by difference; scale LFIR by recovered fraction  
  > "The [CII] luminosities are calculated by extracting two spectra, one within the continuum aperture and one within the [CII] aperture" (6.5 The [CII] deficit)
   - assumption: remaining FIR fraction originates from the annulus  
  > "and assume that the remaining fraction originates from the annulus" (6.5 The [CII] deficit)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Smith+17, Munoz & Oh 2016 and Diaz-Santos+17 relations  
  > "Both the power law from (cite: diaz-santos17) and the model of (cite: munoz16) are in agreement with our observations." (6.5 The [CII] deficit)

## Qualitative findings

- Three SMGs smooth at 0.03''; UDS 47.0 structure statistically consistent with smooth disk.  
  > "We conclude that it is therefore not possible to rule out the hypothesis that all four SMGs in our sample are smooth exponential disks." (6.3 Morphologies)
- Core has lower L[CII]/LFIR and higher SigmaSFR than annulus in all four SMGs, indicating a local origin of the [CII] deficit.  
  > "the core has lower $L_{\text{[CII]}}$/$L_{\text{FIR}}$ and higher star-formation rate surface density than the surrounding annulus" (6.5 The [CII] deficit)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Natural weighting used to recover as much extended emission as possible  
  > "natural weighting (ROBUST $=2$) in order to recover as much of the extended emission as possible" (4 Observations and reduction)
- Alternative: Clumps at 200 pc unlikely to be self-gravitating given typical SMG velocity dispersions  
  > "This suggests that the clumps in UDS 47.0, if real, are unlikely to represent self-gravitating physical structures." (6.4 Expected size of clumps)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Sparse short-baseline coverage (LAS ~0.42'') resolves out [CII] and extended dust; [CII] detections only 2.7–4.7 sigma.  
  > "such high-resolution data therefore have to be interpreted with care" (4 Observations and reduction)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature of 50 K assumed from a stack; FIR luminosity and SigmaSFR depend on it
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low-significance [CII] spectra make sizes and core/annulus ratios uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tension between the 2.1 and 1.6 size-ratio values in abstract vs. text
- Caveat (inferred, ours): uv size fits depend on binning and sparse short-baseline sampling
