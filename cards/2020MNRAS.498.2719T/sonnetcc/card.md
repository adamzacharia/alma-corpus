2. **measure the 600 GHz rest-frame continuum flux of b1 and a limit for a2**: Source b1 detected at 5 sigma in the 230 GHz continuum map; 3 sigma upper limit for a2; no line emission found in the SINFONI FOV  
  > "at 5$\sigma$ significance with a rest-frame 600 GHz flux of $0.80 \pm 0.15$ mJy." (4.2 Star formation activity); "a 3$\sigma$ upper limit on the flux has been computed to be \<0.59 mJy." (4.2 Star formation activity)

3. **convert the ALMA flux to a star formation rate**: Normalise a Mrk 231 QSO SED template (or a blackbody) to the ALMA flux and integrate 8-1000 um to get the IR luminosity-based SFR

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA resolution is high enough to separate single sources, unlike Herschel  
  > "ALMA observations provide sufficient angular resolution" (4.2 Star formation activity)
- Why this method: A typical QSO SED template was assumed to turn the single ALMA flux into an SFR  
  > "we derived the SFR by assuming a typical QSO SED, i.e. Mrk 231 template" (4.2 Star formation activity)
- Alternative: A simple 40 K blackbody template instead of Mrk 231 gives a lower SFR (~150 Msun/yr) for b1  
  > "a simple blackbody template normalized to the ALMA flux with dust temperature of 40 K" (4.2 Star formation activity)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The Herschel SFR blends all core sources plus a bright object to the north  
  > "this value includes all sources studied here plus an additional very bright object to the north of the studied region." (4.2 Star formation activity)
- Limitation (authors): BH masses may be underestimated because of high N_H extinction  
  > "We caution the reader that the values obtained for the BH masses can be underestimated due to the high" (4.1 Bolometric luminosity, BH masses and Eddington ratios)
- Limitation (authors): Final BCG stellar and BH masses and merger timescales are lower limits  
  > "Stellar and BH masses of the final BCG as well as merging timescales have to be considered as lower limits." (5.2 Mass assembly and time scales for the BCG formation)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SFR of b1 depends strongly on the assumed SED (490 vs ~150 Msun/yr) and rests on a single 5 sigma ALMA flux point
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA imaging settings are minimal and the stated spectral resolution (40 vs 10 km/s) and continuum frequency (230 vs 288 GHz) are inconsistent in the text
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The CO limit assumes a 500 km/s line width and an unspecified CO(5-4)/CO(1-0) ratio, so M(CO) is model dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Most conclusions rest on non-ALMA data (SINFONI, KMOS, HST, Chandra); ALMA contributes one continuum detection and limits
