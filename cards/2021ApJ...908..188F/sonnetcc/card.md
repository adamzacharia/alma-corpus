- Question: What are the HI column density, metallicity and kinematics of the circumgalactic medium of a massive starburst submillimeter galaxy at z = 2.674 (probed by two background QSOs), and do they match cold-mode gas streams? ## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and flag the Band-3 visibilities**: ALMA pipeline calibration, three sessions combined into one measurement set, extra flagging with flagcmd  
  > "The raw visibility data were flagged and calibrated by the ALMA pipeline (Pipeline ver. 42030M, CASA ver. 5.4.0-68)." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing); "The calibrated visibilities of the three observing sessions were then combined to form the final calibrated measurement set (MS)." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
   - software: ALMA pipeline, CASA
   - parameter: flagcmd flag of antenna pair DA62-DA65 in the 94.0 GHz window, channels 188-191  
  > "We used the CASA task `flagcmd` to flag the cross-correlation data of the antenna pair DA62 and DA65 in the 94.0 GHz spectral window between channels 188 and 191." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: visual inspection of calibrated visibility amplitudes  
  > "After inspecting the amplitudes of the calibrated visibilities, we found that additional flagging was only necessary for a tiny fraction of data." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
2. **Image the Band-3 spectral cubes and the continuum**: tclean per spectral window with natural weighting, Clark clean, 2 arcsec circular mask, BARY frame; two cubes per window; mtmfs continuum from BB1+BB2  
  > "We use the CASA task `tclean` to image the calibrated visibilities of each spectral window into spectral data cubes." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing); "We image the data to the solar System Barycenter (BARY) reference frame to be consistent with the velocities measured in the heliocentric-corrected optical and near-IR spectra." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: pixel 0.2 arcsec; channel spacing 7.8125 MHz retained  
  > "In the spectral dimension, we retain the original channel spacing of 7.8125 MHz." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
   - parameter: continuum: mtmfs, nterms = 2, natural weighting, 0.2 arcsec pixels  
  > "We used the Multi-term Multi-Frequency Synthesis (`mtmfs`) clean algorithm with a linear spectral model (i.e., `nterms` = 2)." (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: image rms compared with visibility noise measured with visstat  
  > "The rms values are consistent with the visibility noise that we measured with `visstat`" (3.3 ALMA Data Processing)
3. **Blind search for CO line emitters and fidelity estimate**: home-made IDL matched-filter search with top-hat kernels (half-width n = 1-9 channels) on the non-interpolated cubes; fidelity from the CDF of detections in noise-only simulated cubes  
  > "We wrote an IDL program to implement this simple algorithm." (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data); "The convolution kernel we chose to filter the data in the spectral dimension is a top-hat function with a variable half-width between $n = 1$ and $n = 9$." (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data)
   - software: IDL (custom program), tclean, visstat, numpy
   - parameter: pixel S/N threshold 4.5; duplicates within 2 arcsec and 0.2 GHz removed  
  > "The iterative process continues until the image contains no pixels above the S/N threshold of ${\rm S/N}_{\rm pix,th} = 4.5$." (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data); "discard all detections within 2$''$ and 0.2 GHz from the highest S/N source remaining in the list" (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data)
   - parameter: 10 noise-only simulated cubes per spectral window, niter = 0; sources kept at fidelity > 0.9  
  > "For each spectral window, we generate a set of 10 simulated data cubes to provide enough source counts for the source fidelity calculation." (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data); "We identified a total of six emission-line sources with ${\rm fidelity} > 0.9$ within the primary beam" (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data)
   - assumption: sources are unresolved point sources  
  > "Because our search is restricted to point sources" (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data)
   - fitting: single-Gaussian model per detected line for frequency, FWHM and flux  
  > "and line flux with a single-Gaussian model" (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data)
   - validation: search run on noise-only simulated visibilities imaged with the same tclean settings  
  > "To estimate the fidelity of the detected sources, we search for sources in simulated noise-only interferometer data" (Appendix: Blind Line Detection in the ALMA Band-3 Data)
4. **Fit the SMG CO(3-2) spectrum with one and two Gaussians**: spectrum extracted with an elliptical aperture matching the beam-convolved source size; double-Gaussian vs single-Gaussian fit using the amoeba + mcmc method; F-test comparison  
  > "The spectrum is extracted with an elliptical aperture matching the beam-convolved source size." (3.4 SMG Properties); "We thus model the CO spectrum with two Gaussians and compare its result with a single-Gaussian model." (3.4 SMG Properties)
   - software: amoeba, amoeba_sa, exofast_demc
   - fitting: amoeba with simulated annealing for the minimum chi2, then Differential Evolution MCMC (exofast_demc); medians and 15.8/84.1 percentiles adopted; priors not stated  
  > "we use the Differential Evolution MCMC algorithm (cite: Ter-Braak06) implemented in exofast_demc (cite: Eastman13; Eastman19)" (1.4 Organization); "Throughout this paper, we adopt a model optimization method that combines a heuristic $\chi^2$ minimization algorithm with a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm" (1.4 Organization)
   - validation: chi2 = 220.2 (DOF 209) for two Gaussians vs 251.8 (DOF 212) for one; F-test 4.6 sigma  
  > "The formal double-Gaussian solution achieves a $\chi^2 = 220.2$ for a degree-of-freedom (DOF) of 209." (3.4 SMG Properties); "For comparison, the formal single-Gaussian solution achieves a $\chi^2 = 251.8$ for DOF = 212." (3.4 SMG Properties)

## Qualitative findings

- The absorbers show kinematic and metallicity coherence across ~86 kpc between the two sightlines  
  > "The absorbers show remarkable kinematic and metallicity coherence across a separation of $\sim$86 kpc." (abstract)

  > "The CGM of the SMG forms its own category" (6 Summary & Discussion)
- Comp b's CGM resembles that of normal star-forming galaxies at z = 2-3  
  > "the CGM of the much less luminous Comp b is more consistent with that of normal star-forming galaxies at $z = 2-3$" (6 Summary & Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 7 gave sub-arcsec position for near-IR slit spectroscopy; Band 3 CO(3-2) tracks the molecular gas reservoir and gives the redshift  
  > "Near-IR slit spectroscopy requires sub-arcsec positions" (3.1 ALMA Position and Near-IR Spectroscopy); "To detect the molecular gas reservoir that fuels the intense star formation in the SMG" (3.2 ALMA CO (3-2) Spectral Line Imaging)
- Why this method: amoeba_sa avoids local minima in many-parameter fits; AODM is more direct and conservative than vpfit Voigt fitting for metals  
  > "amoeba_sa has the advantage of avoiding being trapped in local minima in a multidimensional parameter space" (1.4 Organization); "This is a more direct and conservative technique compared with Voigt profile fitting using `vpfit`" (4 Absorption-line Systems)
- Alternative: Single-Gaussian CO model rejected in favour of a two-Gaussian model  
  > "We thus model the CO spectrum with two Gaussians and compare its result with a single-Gaussian model." (3.4 SMG Properties)
- Alternative: Subsystem C assigned to the companion Comp b rather than to the SMG halo  
  > "Therefore, we consider subsystem A part of the CGM of the SMG at $z = 2.674$" (5 Emission-Absorption Connection)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ALMA resolution cannot separate disk from outflow origin for the broad CO component  
  > "Unfortunately, the spatial resolution of the ALMA data is inadequate to distinguish between the two scenarios." (3.4 SMG Properties)
- Limitation (authors): Voigt HI parameters carry significant systematic uncertainty from the empirical continuum placement  
  > "the Voigt parameters suffer from significant systematic uncertainties" (4.2 Voigt Profile Fitting of Neutral Hydrogen)
- Limitation (authors): Single system; larger samples needed for general CGM conclusions  
  > "larger samples are clearly desired to draw conclusions on the general properties of the CGM" (6 Summary & Discussion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Molecular gas mass rests on fixed r31 = 0.52 and alpha_CO = 1.0 with no uncertainty propagated for those choices.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA clean niter, line-free channels and continuum subtraction are not given for Band 3.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Accretion rate is a crude one-filament geometry scaled by an assumed three filaments and an assumed 10^13 Msun NFW halo.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Overdensity claim rests on ~1 companion versus <0.12 expected, small-number statistics, and ASPECS counts at different depth.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA flux calibration uncertainty is not stated or propagated into the SED fit.
