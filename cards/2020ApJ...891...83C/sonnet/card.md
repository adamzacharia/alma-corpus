## Analysis recipe

1. **SED fitting for M*, SFR, LFIR, Tdust**: MAGPHYS fit to UV-FIR photometry with ALMA band 4 and 6 continuum, redshift fixed to spectroscopic  
  > "We fitted the multi-band photometry with *MAGPHYS* (da Cunha, Charlot  &  Elbaz 2008), with the redshift fixed to the spectroscopic one" (2.1 Ancillary data and SED fitting)
   - software: MAGPHYS
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF  
  > "We use stellar masses and SFRs based on a Chabrier IMF" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Kennicutt (1998) FIR SFR (factor 1.5-3.5 higher)  
  > "in all cases larger, by a factor of 1.5-3.5, than the ones obtained from *MAGPHYS*" (2.1 Ancillary data and SED fitting)
2. **Image cubes and continuum**: CASA cleaning with natural weighting, 3 rms threshold, 40 km/s channels; continuum from 3 sidebands without CO  
  > "The cubes were cleaned and imaged adopting a natural weighting scheme" (2.2 ALMA data)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure line and continuum fluxes**: moment 0 maps (immoments) from channels above 1 sigma; segmentation map of pixels above 2 sigma contiguous to center; peak flux if unresolved  
  > "we first built a segmentation map, in which we keep all the pixels contiguous to the center in which the measured flux is above 2$\sigma$" (2.2 ALMA data)
   - software: CASA immoments
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Fluxes within 10% of GAUSSFIT or 2DFIT in CASA  
  > "We checked that these values are within $\pm10$% from the values obtained by using the GAUSSFIT or 2DFIT tool within CASA." (3 CO vs dust continuum vs UV)
4. **CO luminosity and SLED slope**: L'CO from Solomon et al. equation; SL5/4=S5-4/S4-3; Spearman test; CO(3-2) and CO(5-4) linearly interpolated for Gal3  
  > "for that object we linearly interpolate the CO(5-4) and CO(3-2) fluxes to obtain $S_{CO(4-3)}$" (4 CO luminosity and SLED slope)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Gas masses from CO and band 4 continuum**: extrapolate to CO(1-0) with L'5-4/L'1-0 of 25, 18, 8.4 per SLED slope; alpha_CO=3.8; dust gas mass from Groves et al. 500 micron calibration after RJ correction  
  > "we correct the observed fluxes by extrapolating along the $F\propto \lambda^{-4}$ Rayleigh-Jeans law, from the observed wavelength to exactly 500 $\mu$m" (5 Gas masses)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=3.8 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1 for all galaxies  
  > "we instead decided to use a single value for all galaxies" (5 Gas masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared CO vs continuum gas masses and with dynamical masses  
  > "only for two out of five objects in the sample the two estimates agree, within the errors" (5 Gas masses)
6. **Dynamical masses and alpha_CO constraint**: Mdyn=1.16e5 Vcir^2 D, Vcir=1.763 sigma/sin(i), i=arccos(b/a) from deconvolved sizes; i=57 deg for Gal2  
  > "$M_{dyn} = 1.16\times10^5 V_{cir}^2 D$" (6 Dynamical masses)
   - assumption: No dark matter (20% tested)  
  > "We did not include any dark matter (DM) in these calculations" (6 Dynamical masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared Mdyn with M*+Mgas  
  > "we compare $M_{dyn}$ with the sum of stellar mass $M_*$ and gas mass $M_{gas}$" (6 Dynamical masses)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Select brightest expected CO(5-4) targets with spectroscopic redshifts  
  > "by choosing the five sources for which we estimated the brightest CO(5-4) flux" (2 Sample and data)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: FIR-based Kennicutt SFR considered but MAGPHYS SFR preferred  
  > "we consider the SFRs from MAGPHYS more robust, and we prefer them in the remainder of the paper" (2.1 Ancillary data and SED fitting)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Sizes and axial ratios poorly constrained due to limited resolution and beam elongation  
  > "it suffers from the quite large uncertainties on the size and axial ratio" (6 Dynamical masses)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only five galaxies; no correlation significance beyond Spearman r
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(1-0) extrapolation ratios are chosen ad hoc
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical mass from unresolved data with inclination from elongated beams
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO constraint degenerate with dark matter and inclination
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text inconsistencies (Gal3/Gal4 intermediate slopes, SFR values)
