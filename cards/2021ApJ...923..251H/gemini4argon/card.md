## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract 321 GHz H2O maser spectra and measure continuum and line flux properties**: Integrate over the full line-free frequency range to produce 321 GHz continuum maps, extract maser spectra toward the galaxy centers, and measure peak and integrated fluxes and positions  
  > "The total integrated intensity estimated from the spectra of the 321 GHz H 2 O features detected at SNR >5 between V LSR = 200 and 800 km s [−][1] is 11.1±1.1Jy km s [−][1] ." (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS); "Sub-millimeter 321 GHz (Band 7) continuum images of NGC 4945 and the Circinus galaxy, obtained by integrating over the full frequency range (after the removal of the maser line emission), are shown in Figure 5." (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
   - software: CASA, AIPS
   - parameter: Velocity search range V_LSR = -319.5 to 1436.5 km s^-1 (NGC 4945) and -439.9 to 1314.1 km s^-1 (Circinus)  
  > "in the velocity range of V LSR =−319.5 – 1436.5 km s [−][1] for NGC 4945 and V LSR =−439.9 – 1314.1 km s [−][1] for the Circinus galaxy" (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
   - parameter: SNR > 5 between V_LSR = 200 and 800 km s^-1 for integrated intensity in Circinus  
  > "The total integrated intensity estimated from the spectra of the 321 GHz H 2 O features detected at SNR >5 between V LSR = 200 and 800 km s [−][1] is 11.1±1.1Jy km s [−][1] ." (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
   - assumption: Optical LSR velocity definition  
  > "The optical velocity definition is adopted throughout, and the velocities are calculated with respect to the Local Standard of Rest (LSR)." (1. INTRODUCTION)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Positions of continuum peaks compared with first-epoch 0.54 arcsec resolution maps and found consistent within ~0.01 arcsec; feature positions checked against ALMA registration accuracy ~theta/20 (~0.0165 arcsec)  
  > "which is also consistent with that measured at lower angular resolution (0.54 [′′] ) within uncertainties of ∼0.01 [′′] (Hagiwara et al. 2013)." (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS); "From the ALMA Cycle 4 Capabilities, the image registration accuracy is ∼θ/20.0, where θ is the angular resolution. Consequently, the registration accuracy is ∼0.33 [′′] /20 ∼ 0.0165 [′′] in our observations" (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
2. **Reduce Tidbinbilla 22 GHz H2O maser monitoring spectra and compare with ALMA 321 GHz spectra**: Perform baseline calibration, polarization averaging, and scan flagging on Tidbinbilla 70-m data to produce multi-epoch 22 GHz spectra for variability and velocity comparison with 321 GHz spectra  
  > "Data reduction, including baseline calibration, averaging the two circular polarizations, and flagging bad scans when required were carried out using GBTIDL [4]" (2.2. Tidbinbilla Observations); "It should be noted that the second epoch spectrum at 321 GHz and the last epoch spectrum at 22 GHz were obtained on the same date, making it possible to precisely compare velocities" (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
   - software: GBTIDL, ASAP
   - parameter: Spectral resolution 31.25 kHz or 0.42 km s^-1  
  > "at a spectral resolution of 31.25 kHz or 0.42 km s [−][1] ." (2.2. Tidbinbilla Observations)
   - assumption: Telescope sensitivity of 2.6 Jy K^-1 with 50% flux density uncertainty  
  > "We adopted a telescope sensitivity of 2.6 Jy K [−][1] to convert antenna temperature to flux density with a conservatively estimated uncertainty in the flux density of 50%" (2.2. Tidbinbilla Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simultaneous observation on 2017 May 6 used for direct velocity comparison of flaring features  
  > "It should be noted that the second epoch spectrum at 321 GHz and the last epoch spectrum at 22 GHz were obtained on the same date, making it possible to precisely compare velocities of the maser features between the 22 GHz and 321 GHz maser spectra." (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
3. **Compute moment maps and deconvolved source size of 321 GHz maser emission in Circinus**: Generate 0th, 1st, and 2nd moment maps from the spectral-line cube of the Circinus galaxy and deconvolve the 0th moment emission by the synthesized beam  
  > "Figure 6 displays the velocity-integrated line intensity (0th moment), overlaid on the 321 GHz continuum (contour map), mean velocity (first moment), and velocity dispersion (second moment) maps for the 321 GHz H 2 O maser in the Circinus galaxy." OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS); "From the 0th moment map, the source size of the maser emission deconvolved by the synthesized beam is calculated to be 266±7 mas × 158±2mas, corresponding to 5.6 pc × 3.3 pc, at a position angle (PA) of 35 [◦] ± 1 [◦] ." (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
   - software: CASA, AIPS
   - parameter: Velocity range V_LSR = 228-685 km s^-1 (or 256-670 km s^-1 for 1st/2nd moment maps)  
  > "The total velocity coverage of the figures is V LSR = 228−685 km s [−][1] ." (Figure 6 caption); "Figure 6(c) shows the second moment map which, as for Figure 6(b), is produced from the spectral-line cube containing maser features lying at V LSR = 256–670km s [−][1] ." (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)
   - assumption: Distance to Circinus D = 4.2 Mpc  
  > "Circinus 14 [h] 13 [m] 09 [s] .906 −65 [◦] 20 [′] 20 [′′] .46 4.2 2017-05-06 47 0.330×0.206, 31.0 28 0.6 13.9" (Table 1)
   - fitting: 2D beam deconvolution on 0th moment map  
  > "From the 0th moment map, the source size of the maser emission deconvolved by the synthesized beam is calculated to be 266±7 mas × 158±2mas" (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Calculate isotropic luminosity, disk radius, and dynamical mass for Circinus galaxy**: Compute isotropic luminosity from integrated intensity, and derive rotating disk radius and enclosed dynamical mass from the measured velocity gradient and maximum rotation velocity  
  > "Considering a simple circular motion around the nucleus, we estimate the dynamical mass (M dyn ) confined within the central 28 pc to be ∼4.3×10 [8] M ⊙," (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient); "the observed velocity gradient (dV /dl) along PA = 35 [◦] can be expressed as dV /dl ≃d(V rot sinθ sini)/d(rθ) ≃ V rot sini/r" (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient); "Considering a simple circular motion around the nucleus, we estimate the dynamical mass (M dyn ) confined within the central 28 pc to be ∼4.3×10 [8] M ⊙" (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)
   - parameter: dV/dl = 8.8 km s^-1 pc^-1 along PA = 35 deg, V_rot = 682 - 433 ~ 250 km s^-1  
  > "together with the observed parameters of dV/dl = 8.8 km s [−][1] pc [−][1] and V red − V sys = 682 km s [−][1] – 433 km s [−][1] = V rot ≃ 250 km s [−][1], r is estimated to be ∼28 pc." (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)
   - assumption: Isotropic radiation of the maser and distance D = 4.2 Mpc  
  > "where ν is the rest frequency (321.226 GHz), D is the distance to the Circinus galaxy of 4.2 Mpc (Freeman et al. 1977), and" (4.2.1. Origin of the maser and physical conditions giving rise to the maser)
   - assumption: Simple circular rotation disk with inclination i > 75 deg (sin i ~ 1) and sin(theta) ~ theta  
  > "Assuming that the observed masers are on a simple circular disk with a rotational velocity of V rot at a radius r from the nucleus" (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient); "Adopting the disk inclination of >75 [◦] (sini ≃1) estimated from the near-infrared emission (Tristram et al. 2014) or the CO disk with r = 100–600pc (Curran et al. 2008)" (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared derived dynamical mass and gas mass fraction M_H2/M_dyn with larger-scale CO dynamical mass and VLBI enclosed mass  
  > "We note that the derived value of the dynamical mass (r ≲ 28 pc) is comparable with that of the larger scale dynamical mass of ∼3.2×10 [8] M ⊙ within the central 140 pc (Curran et al. 1998)" (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient); "This gives a gas mass fraction of only M H 2 /M dyn ≈ 0.6% within the central 28 pc." (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)

## Qualitative findings

- The 321 GHz H2O emission in both NGC 4945 and the Circinus galaxy exhibits significant flux density variability over 4-5 years, confirming its nuclear maser origin.  
  > "The two epoch ALMA observations show that the strengths of the 321 GHz emission are variable by a factor of at least a few, confirming a maser origin." (abstract); "The significant variability in the flux densities has confirmed that the observed H 2 O emission arises from nuclear masers." (5. CONCLUSIONS)
- In NGC 4945, the 321 GHz maser dropped below the 38 mJy detection threshold in 2016 in apparent correlation with a decline in both the 22 GHz maser intensity and the 321 GHz nuclear dust continuum.  
  > "From the two-epoch observations at 321 GHz in 2012 and 2016 and five-epoch single-dish observations at 22 GHz from 2014–2016, we find that strengths of the 321 GHz and 22 GHz H 2 O masers vary in an apparently correlated fashion (Figure 1)." (4.1. No Detection and Intensity Variability of 321 GHz H 2 O Maser in NGC 4945); "Although we have only two epochs of data, there is an apparent correlation between the strength of the masers and the 321 GHz radio continuum emission from the obscured nucleus (Table 3)." (4.1. No Detection and Intensity Variability of 321 GHz H 2 O Maser in NGC 4945)
- In the Circinus galaxy, simultaneous 321 GHz and 22 GHz spectra on 2017 May 6 show flaring blueshifted features in a similar velocity range, likely tracing nuclear molecular outflows.  
  > "We interpret this to mean that these blueshifted features, that appeared at both 321 GHz and 22 GHz, are outflow masers that flared at the same period" (4.2.3. 22 GHz Maser and 321 GHz Maser Comparison); "This revealed in the Circinus galaxy the flaring features of the 321 GHz and 22 GHz maser emission were found largely in the same blueshifted velocity range on the same day." (5. CONCLUSIONS)
- The 321 GHz velocity gradient in Circinus yields an unphysically large dynamical mass (4.3x10^8 M_sun within 28 pc) if interpreted as circular rotation at r ~ 28 pc, implying the maser instead resides in the inner sub-parsec disk (r = 0.1-0.4 pc) or traces non-circular/outflow motions.  
  > "This discrepancy is probably caused by our assumption that the 321 GHz maser traces the outer part of the central disk within the central ∼28 pc, which would result in an overestimate of the dynamical mass on a scale of 10 pc." (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient); "We thus postulate that the 321 GHz maser traces the inner part of the masering disk with r = 0.1–0.4pc, as in the circumnuclear disk illuminated by X-rays the temperature increases closer to the central engine (e.g., Neufeld & Maloney 1995). Alternatively, the observed H 2 O velocity gradient could trace non-circular motions" (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Higher angular resolution (~0.3 arcsec) second-epoch ALMA Band 7 observations with 45-47 antennas and improved (u,v) coverage, paired with Tidbinbilla 22 GHz monitoring, to test variability and resolve the nuclear velocity field.  
  > "In this paper, we present new results from second epoch observations of the 321 GHz H 2 O masers in NGC 4945 and the Circinus galaxy, conducted with ALMA at higher angular resolution." (1. INTRODUCTION); "This is likely to have arisen from the improvement in (u, v) coverage due to the significantly larger number of baselines, as a result of the number of antennas increasing from 18 to 47, and the longer integration time of 28 minutes, compared to 18 minutes in the first epoch." (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Star-forming origin or thermal molecular gas emission for the 321 GHz H2O line is rejected in favor of an AGN-powered nuclear megamaser due to high isotropic luminosity (~65 L_sun), nuclear coincidence, and strong flux variability.  
  > "This strongly supports the belief that the maser in the galaxy is a nuclear maser arising from AGN-activity and not from star-forming activity in the galaxy." (4.2.1. Origin of the maser and physical conditions giving rise to the maser)
- Alternative: Tentative high-velocity features at V_LSR = 1069 and 1138.6 km s^-1 suggested in Epoch 1 for Circinus are rejected as likely artifacts of higher atmospheric absorption at that part of the band.  
  > "Signs of marginal features were suggested at V LSR = 1069 km s [−][1] and V LSR = 1138.6km s [−][1] in Hagiwara et al. (2013), however these features were not identified in the second epoch spectrum. As argued by Pesce et al. (2016), it is most likely that this portion of the band is impacted by higher atmospheric absorption" (3. OBSERVATIONAL RESULTS)
- Alternative: Outer disk circular rotation at r ~ 28 pc for the 321 GHz maser in Circinus is rejected because it overestimates the dynamical mass compared to the 140 pc CO dynamical mass and yields an unrealistically low gas mass fraction (~0.6%).  
  > "This discrepancy is probably caused by our assumption that the 321 GHz maser traces the outer part of the central disk within the central ∼28 pc, which would result in an overestimate of the dynamical mass on a scale of 10 pc." (4.2.2. Velocity Gradient)
