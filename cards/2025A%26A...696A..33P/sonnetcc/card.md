## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the ALMA data**: Run the ALMA pipeline scriptForPI in CASA; image band 6 with tclean (dirty natural-weighted mfs continuum, 25 km/s cubes, 90 klambda tapered continuum, cleaned images); image band 3 as dirty 100 km/s cubes  
  > "using the CASA pipeline versions 6.2.1 and 6.5.4 for band 6 and 3 data, respectively" (Data calibration and imaging); "adopting a natural weighting scheme to maximize the sensitivity per beam" (Data calibration and imaging); "of the LSB and USB with $25\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ channel width" (Data calibration and imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Pixel scale 0.08" (band 6), 0.4" (tapered), 0.3" (band 3); taper FWHM 90 klambda; band 3 channel width 100 km/s  
  > "We used a pixel scale of $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}08$ to achieve the Nyquist sampling of the longest baselines." (Data calibration and imaging); "applying a Gaussian taper in the $uv$ plane with ${\rm FWHM}_{uv}=90\,{\rm k}\lambda$ and setting the pixel size to $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}4$" (Data calibration and imaging); "We set the pixel scale at $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}3$ and the channel width at $100\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ at the reference frequency of $101\,{\rm GHz}$" (Data calibration and imaging)
   - parameter: Cleaning down to 2 sigma with 2.0" circular masks around S/N>5 sources  
  > "by applying the cleaning algorithm down to $2\sigma$, placing circular masks of $2\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}0$ diameter around sources extracted with $S/N>5$" (Data calibration and imaging)
   - assumption: No self-calibration performed  
  > "No self-calibration was performed on the datasets." (Data calibration and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Blind continuum source search**: Run LineSeeker on the untapered dirty 1.2-mm continuum image (PB response >= 50%, no PB correction), extract S/N>3 detections, keep fidelity F >= 90% (S/N >= 5.0); fidelity from negative-peak statistics  
  > "With LineSeeker we extracted all the detections above $S/N>3$ and selected the source candidates with $F\ge 90\%$, corresponding to $S/N\ge 5.0$." (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image); "the ratio between positive and negative detection distributions is used to estimate the fidelity (or reliability) of source candidates" (Extraction and characterization of ALMA sources)
   - software: LineSeeker
   - parameter: Exclude region below 50% of maximum PB response; dirty image used without PB correction  
  > "excluding the region below $50\%$ of the maximum PB response" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image); "since the former preserves the intrinsic properties of the noise" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
   - parameter: Low-fidelity (20% <= F < 90%) candidates cross-matched to HST F140W catalog within 0.5"; no associations found  
  > "we search HST counterparts around the sky position of ALMA continuum sources within a separation limit of $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}5$" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
   - assumption: Negative peaks are pure noise; cumulative negative-detection distribution modeled as Gaussian noise  
  > "The cumulative distribution of negative detections is then modeled assuming Gaussian-distributed noise by using a function of the form" (Extraction and characterization of ALMA sources)
   - fitting: Model N[1-erf(S/N/sqrt(2) sigma)] with N and sigma free, fitted to cumulative negative detections to get F(S/N)  
  > "where erf is the error function, and $N$, $\sigma$ are free parameters" (Extraction and characterization of ALMA sources)
   - validation: Completeness by injecting artificial point sources (20 at a time, 2-10 x RMS, 30 iterations): ~90% complete at S/N~7.5; flux boosting negligible at S/N>~5  
  > "we find that our source search is complete to $\approx90\%$ at $S/N\approx7.5$" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image); "we determined that it is negligible at $S/N \gtrsim 5$" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
3. **Blind line search in band 6 cubes and band 3 scans**: Run LineSeeker on LSB and USB cubes with a matched-filter Gaussian kernel (1 to 18 channels), S/N >= 3 then F >= 90%; band 3: line search on dirty cubes plus inspection of spectra at the 1.2-mm source positions  
  > "using a Gaussian kernel with width ranging from 1 to 18 channels" (Line emitters in band 6 data cubes); "We ran LineSeeker on band 3" (Source detection in band 3 spectral scans)
   - software: LineSeeker, scikit-learn
   - parameter: Kernel width corresponds to typical line FWHM ~50-1000 km/s; source extraction restricted to PB response >= 50%  
  > "adequate to match the typical linewidths of ${\rm FWHM}\sim 50-1000\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ observed in high-redshift galaxies" (Line emitters in band 6 data cubes)
   - parameter: Source extraction by DBSCAN clustering of connected voxels  
  > "The sources are extracted by grouping connected voxels using the Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Application with Noise algorithm" (Extraction and characterization of ALMA sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-match with HST catalog found no additional line emitters  
  > "This procedure did not reveal additional line emitters." (Line emitters in band 6 data cubes)
4. **Identify optical/NIR counterparts and redshifts**: Cross-match ALMA continuum sources to the HST F140W catalog within 0.5"; take redshifts from MUSE where high-quality, otherwise from ALMA band 3 CO(3-2)  
  > "we found an unambiguous counterpart for all ALMA detections, except for C5" (HST counterparts of ALMA continuum sources); "The redshifts of the latter, are accurately measured from the CO(3–2) lines detected in the band 3 spectral scans" (HST counterparts of ALMA continuum sources)
   - parameter: Match radius 0.5" (comparable to the untapered beam)  
  > "within a separation limit of $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}5$, comparable to the beam size of the 1.2-mm continuum untapered image" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: [CI] 609 um redshift in C6 consistent with MUSE redshift  
  > "The source redshift we estimated with this line is consistent with the one measured from the MUSE spectrum." (Line emitters in band 6 data cubes)
5. **Measure continuum fluxes and CO/[CI] line fluxes and luminosities**: 1.2 mm: single-pixel peak in the ~2" tapered image, PB-rescaled, with RMS rescaled for uncertainty; CO(3-2): fit peak-pixel spectra with a Gaussian plus constant; L_CO and L'_CO from Solomon et al. 1997 relations  
  > "through a single-pixel measurement of the continuum source flux peak that we then rescaled for the PB response at the corresponding location" (Source fluxes and luminosities); "by fitting the spectra extracted from the source peak employing a single Gaussian plus a constant to model the line and the continuum" (Source fluxes and luminosities); "We finally derived the line luminosities as (cite: Solomon+1997):" (Source fluxes and luminosities)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: Luminosity relation L_CO = 1.04e-3 S dv nu_obs D_L^2; L'_CO = 3.25e7 S dv D_L^2 / ((1+z)^3 nu_obs^2)

  > "we adopt a CO(3–2)-to-CO(1–0) conversion factor of $r_{31}=0.80\pm0.14$ from (cite: Boogaard+2020) and a range of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$" (Properties of the ALMA galaxies around absorbers); "At the upper end, we adopt $4.3\,M_{\astrosun}/({\rm K\,km\,s^{-1}\,pc^2)}$ as measured in giant molecular clouds in the Milky Way" (Properties of the ALMA galaxies around absorbers)

  > "count the number of HST-selected sources within a velocity window of $\left | \Delta\varv \right |\le 250\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$" (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas); "We then divide the number of galaxies by the projected area corrected for the coverage of the MUSE FoV." (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas)

   - parameter: Mg II used for C1, C6, C7; Al II for C3

  > "we fit the data with a linear model by employing the Python MCMC ensemble sampler" (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas); "adopting a Poisson maximum likelihood estimator and flat priors on the free parameters" (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas)

## Qualitative findings

- All seven continuum-selected sources have an HST counterpart (C5 ambiguous), and three (C1, C3, C4) lack MUSE redshifts and lie in the MUSE redshift desert  
  > "For all of these sources, we found associations in the HST catalog as described in Sect" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image); "and lie in the MUSE redshift desert" (Gas-galaxy association rate)
- ALMA-selected galaxies lie at the high-mass end of the stellar mass function and are near the main sequence but close to the starburst region  
  > "The ALMA sources lie at the high-mass end of the stellar mass function at those redshifts" (Properties of the ALMA galaxies around absorbers); "although close to the starburst region" (Properties of the ALMA galaxies around absorbers)
- Completeness-corrected 1.2-mm number counts agree with the ASPECS blank field  
  > "found that these are consistent with that derived in blank field from ASPECS" (Gas-galaxy association rate)
- Massive dusty galaxies appear to trace large-scale structure rather than host the absorbers  
  > "massive dusty galaxies appear to be more tracers of the large scale structure than the actual hosts of the absorbers" (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 6 mosaic covers the central MUDF where MUSE is deepest; band 3 scans were designed to measure redshifts of sources lacking robust MUSE redshifts  
  > "The ALMA band 6 mosaic has been designed to cover the central part of the MUDF, where the MUSE observations achieve the maximum depth" (Description of observations); "The latter observations were designed to accurately measure the redshift of sources lacking robust spectroscopic constraints from MUSE" (Description of observations)
- Why this method: Dirty images used for source search because they preserve the noise properties, enabling reliable fidelity estimates  
  > "since the former preserves the intrinsic properties of the noise" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
- Alternative: C5 continuum may arise from an interacting system or from gravitational lensing; not resolved  
  > "Alternatively, gravitational lensing may represent a viable explanation to the observed emission." (HST counterparts of ALMA continuum sources)
- Alternative: No flux-boosting correction applied because it is negligible at S/N >~ 5  
  > "Thus, we do not correct the measured fluxes of our source sample for this effect." (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
- Alternative: Cleaning of band 3 cubes not performed (faint, unresolved sources)  
  > "For these reasons, the cleaning of cubes is not required." (Data calibration and imaging)
- How they decided it was enough: Fiducial sample limited to high-fidelity (F >= 90%) candidates, since lower-fidelity candidates had no HST counterparts  
  > "we therefore include in our fiducial sample of ALMA continuum sources only the high-fidelity candidates selected as previously described" (Continuum-selected sources in the 1.2-mm image)
- Limitation (authors): Nature of C5 cannot be confidently assessed given limited S/N  
  > "given the limited $S/N$ of the ALMA data, we cannot confidently assess the nature of this emission" (HST counterparts of ALMA continuum sources)
- Limitation (authors): Dust masses are rough estimates from a single band and a single assumed temperature  
  > "we provide rough estimates of the dust masses" (Properties of the ALMA galaxies around absorbers)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample size limits statistics of the environment analysis  
  > "our analysis relies on a handful of sources and therefore is limited by the low statistics" (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas); "More SMG-QSO pairs at small separations are needed to statistically assess our conclusions." (The link between the large-scale galaxy environment and the absorbing gas)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only seven continuum sources and six robust associations, so the 100% association rate has a large binomial uncertainty that is not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A +-500 km/s window is wide relative to absorber velocity spreads and could give chance associations given the density of metal absorbers; no random-redshift control is described
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust masses use a single fixed T_dust = 32 K and beta = 1.8 from SMG samples; no uncertainty on M_dust is given
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Peak-pixel fluxes are used for sources that are called marginally resolved, so total fluxes may be underestimated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The surface-density versus EW correlation rests on four points (rho = 0.8, p = 0.1) and mixes Mg II and Al II
