## Analysis recipe

1. **identify filaments in H13CO+ and 1.3 mm continuum**: FilFinder run on H13CO+ moment-0 map (reproducing the earlier ATOMS identification) and on the 1.3 mm QUARKS continuum map; four filaments F1-F4 identified in the continuum  
  > "We also employed the `FilFinder` algorithm to the 1.3 mm continuum map obtained in the QUARKS survey" (Identification of filaments)
   - software: FilFinder
   - parameter: _not stated_
   - assumption: _not stated_
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Previously identified H13CO+ filaments were reproduced with the same algorithm; longest filaments from both tracers coincide  
  > "the previously identified filamentary structures were well-reproduced with the same `FilFinder` algorithm" (Identification of filaments)
2. **derive H2 column density map**: LTE, optically thin H13CO+ (1-0) column density from integrated intensity with Tex set equal to dust temperature; N(H2) from fixed abundance 1e-11  
  > "is equal to the dust temperature of the clump" (Column density map)
   - parameter: J=0, mu=3.89 D, B=43.377302 GHz, T_bg=2.73 K, Tex = Tdust  
  > "we used $J$ = 0, $\mu$ = 3.89 D, and $B$ = 43.377302 GHz" (Column density map)
   - parameter: H13CO+/H2 abundance 1e-11; pixels above 5 sigma  
  > "using the mean of the reported abundance ratio of" (Column density map)
   - assumption: LTE with Tex equal to dust temperature (uniform), optically thin emission (lower limit to N)  
  > "The derived $N$(H$_2$) value represents a lower limit to the true column density" (Column density map)
   - assumption: Uniform dust temperature across the region; uncertainty ~33-36% for +/-10 K  
  > "introduces an uncertainty of approximately 33% to 36%" (Column density map)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Sensitivity estimates for optical depth, temperature and abundance only  
  > "Adopting these extreme values would decrease or increase the inferred column density by an order of magnitude" (Column density map)
3. **measure filament velocity gradients from PV diagrams**: PV diagrams along centroid velocity of filament skeletons (F1&F2 and F3&F4 groups); least-squares slopes of parts of PV diagrams  
  > "The PV diagrams of the filaments were constructed by determining the centroid velocity along each of these filament skeletons" (Dynamical properties of filaments)
   - parameter: Starting point at eastern end of F1 and F3  
  > "The starting point for both the PV diagrams were defined at the eastern end of filaments F1 and F3" (Dynamical properties of filaments)
   - assumption: Velocity gradient along filament represents inflow  
  > "suggesting for an inflow of gas along the filaments toward the hub" (Dynamical properties of filaments)
   - fitting: Least-squares fit to PV segments; uncertainty from fit error  
  > "we performed least-square fits to different parts of the PV diagrams" (Dynamical properties of filaments)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **compute mass inflow rate along filaments**: Filament mass from N(H2) with distance uncertainty; Mdot = gradient x M_fil / tan(alpha) with tan(alpha)=1  
  > "We thus assume $\tan$ $\alpha$ = 1 in our calculation" (Mass inflow rate along the filaments)
   - parameter: tan(alpha) = 1 (inclination 45 degrees stated)  
  > "assuming the inclination angle ($\alpha$) of 45$^{\circ}$" (Mass inflow rate along the filaments)
   - assumption: Simple cylindrical model with axial flow  
  > "Assuming a simple cylindrical model" (Mass inflow rate along the filaments)
   - assumption: Filament distance uncertainty 0.5 kpc included in Mfil uncertainty  
  > "the uncertainty in distance to the protocluster (0.5 kpc)" (Mass inflow rate along the filaments)
   - fitting: Error from least-squares fit; not fully specified  
  > "The uncertainties for these gradients are obtained from the error associated with the least-squares fit" (Dynamical properties of filaments)
   - validation: Inclination sensitivity: factor 2 uncertainty for 30-60 degrees  
  > "are expected to be uncertain by a factor of 2 when the" (Mass inflow rate along the filaments)
5. **fit infall profiles of cores**: Average spectra of H13CO+ and CCH fitted with Hill5 two-layer infall model; S/N >~15; fit with 5 free parameters  
  > "we applied a signal-to-noise (S/N) threshold" (Gas infall rates in cores)
   - software: Hill5 infall model, emcee, FUNStools
   - parameter: Free parameters: Tpeak, tau, Vinfall, Vlsr, sigma; T0=0 at edges  
  > "These five parameters were set as free with initial guesses and bounds" (Hill5 Model Fit to the Infall Profiles)
   - parameter: Vlsr from Gaussian fits to optically thin CH3OH and H13CN (4-3) spectra  
  > "We fit Gaussians to the average spectra of these optically thin tracers to determine the V$_{\rm lsr}$ of each core" (Gas infall rates in cores)
   - assumption: Blue profile = infall (positive Vin); red = expansion  
  > "By convention, positive V$_{\rm in}$ correspond to a blue profile" (Gas infall rates in cores)
   - fitting: MCMC sampling with emcee; two trials, broad then narrowed bounds; corner plots checked  
  > "the fitting was performed by Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling using the" (Hill5 Model Fit to the Infall Profiles)
   - validation: Unreliable fits excluded (cores 7, 10, 14 for H13CO+; 5, 8, 9, 13, 14 for CCH); consistency checks for cores 10-12 between tracers  
  > "Fit to a few spectra (cores 7, 10, and 14 for H$^{13}$CO$^{+}$" (Gas infall rates in cores)
6. **derive core mass infall rates and compare with filament inflow**: Mdot_in = 3 M_in V_in / R_in using core mass and radius from the prior ALMA core catalogue  
  > "where $M_{\rm in}$ is the core mass, and $R_{\rm in}$ is the core radius" (Gas infall rates in cores)
   - parameter: Core masses and radii from 2024ApJS..270....9X  
  > "adopted from (cite: 2024ApJS..270....9X)" (Gas infall rates in cores)
   - assumption: Infall rate scaled from core mass and infall velocity  
  > "The estimated infall velocities were further used to derive the mass infall rate" (Gas infall rates in cores)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **mass-radius analysis of cores and link to massive star regime**: Compare cores to m(r) threshold and surface density thresholds; PV dip analysis for cores 10 and 11  
  > "The shaded region represents the area of low-mass star-forming cores" (M-R relationship of the cores)
   - parameter: m(r) > 870 Msun (r/pc)^1.33 criterion; Sigma 0.05 g cm^-2 lower bound; Sigma 1.0 g cm^-2 critical  
  > "Most of the cores lie below the critical surface density of" (M-R relationship of the cores)
   - assumption: Surface density and mass thresholds from literature  
  > "bounded below by the gravitational binding limit" (M-R relationship of the cores)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Majority of cores show blue asymmetric profiles (infall); cores 10, 11, 13 show red profiles (expansion); protostellar cores in hub show higher infall  
  > "Investigation of H$^{13}$CO$^{+}$ and CCH spectral profiles revealed the majority of the cores having the characteristic blue asymmetric line profiles" (abstract)
- Cores with red profiles reside in the massive star formation regime, but filaments suggest they lose material to the hub  
  > "The mass-radius relationship of the cores revealed that the cores with red profiles reside in the massive star formation regime" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Survey data combining 3 mm and 1.3 mm continuum and multiple lines were used to link large-scale inflow and small-scale infall  
  > "These systems offer a unique opportunity to study large-scale inflows and small-scale infall processes simultaneously" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Optically thin tracers used to check infall profiles of optically thick H13CO+ and CCH  
  > "To further confirm this, we use optically thin tracers" (Gas infall rates in cores)
- Alternative: Multi-component cloud line-of-sight structure rejected as cause of double-peaked profiles  
  > "These characteristics collectively suggest that the observed line asymmetries are primarily caused by infall motions rather than the presence of multiple velocity components along the line-of-sight" (Spectral profiles of core 5 and core 7)
- Alternative: Extreme inclination angles 0 and 90 degrees excluded  
  > "Two extreme cases for $\alpha$, i.e., 0$^{\circ}$ and 90$^{\circ}$" (Mass inflow rate along the filaments)
- How they decided it was enough: S/N threshold of about 15 applied to spectra before Hill5 fitting  
  > "we applied a signal-to-noise (S/N) threshold" (Gas infall rates in cores)
- Limitation (authors): Column densities are lower limits because optically thin assumption may fail in dense regions  
  > "The derived $N$(H$_2$) value represents a lower limit to the true column density" (Column density map)
- Limitation (authors): Abundance ratio uncertainty could change total mass estimates by up to a factor of 10  
  > "implying that the total mass estimates could vary by up to a factor of 10" (Column density map)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): F1 velocity gradient is 22.5 km/s/pc in the text but 17.8 km/s/pc in Table and Summary; F3 inflow is 5017 in Table but 5135 in Discussion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Summary units for F4 inflow rate given as Msun/yr instead of Msun/Myr
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inclination given as 45 degrees and tan(alpha)=1 but also described as a factor 2 uncertainty range; the alpha dependence is not propagated into reported uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abundance ratio of 1e-11 adopted from a range spanning 1e-10 to 1e-12, so masses are uncertain by up to a factor of 10
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Hill5 fit bounds, priors, and MCMC settings are not fully specified, and several cores were excluded from fits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Core-scale infall rates use a 3 M/R scaling with core masses and radii from an earlier paper, not re-derived
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Accretion timescales (2e4 yr) and SFE (20-30%) are assumed values rather than measured quantities
