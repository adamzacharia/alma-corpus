## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate NOEMA data and prepare uv-tables**: NOEMA pipeline in GILDAS, each SPW written to a uv-table; continuum from line-flagged wide SPWs; phase self-calibration applied to high-resolution SPWs  
  > "Continuum $uv-$tables were produced by flagging strong line emission in the wide-bandwidth SPWs and averaging the unflagged channels." (2 Observations and Data Reduction); "The resulting self-calibration tables were then applied to the high-resolution SPWs that fell within the same basebands." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: GILDAS
   - parameter: Self-calibration: three phase rounds, solution intervals 180, 90, 45 s  
  > "Three rounds of phase self-calibration were applied to each of the continuum SPWs using solution intervals of 180, 90, and 45 seconds, respectively." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Self-calibration improved continuum S/N by a factor of 7  
  > "Self-calibration improved the continuum S/N by a factor of 7." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
2. **Image lines, make moment maps and measure line fluxes**: CASA multi-scale CLEAN cubes, moment 0 maps, spectra from circular apertures, fluxes by integrating over the moment-map velocity range  
  > "Line images were produced with channel spacings of 0.2 km s$^{-1}$ using multi-scale CLEAN (cite: 2008ISTSP...2..793C) with scales of \[$0''$, $0.25\ensuremath{''}$, $0.5\ensuremath{''}$\] and circular masks with a radius of $2''$." (2 Observations and Data Reduction); "Line fluxes were measured from the spectra by integrating through the same velocity range used to make the moment maps." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Moment 0 velocity range: 6.6-12.4 km/s (HCO+); 9.2-10.6 km/s (other lines, set by CS)  
  > "Moment 0 (integrated intensity) maps were created for HCO$^+$ by integrating between LSRK velocities of 6.6 and 12.4 km s$^{-1}$ and for the other lines by integrating between LSRK velocities of 9.2 and 10.6 km s$^{-1}$." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Spectra extracted in circular apertures of 1.5'' radius  
  > "Spectra were extracted from the image cubes using circular apertures with radii of $1.5''$, chosen to cover the extent of emission detected above $3\sigma$." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Flux uncertainty sqrt(N) * dv * sigma_spec  
  > "The flux uncertainties were estimated as $\sqrt{N}\Delta v\sigma_\text{spec}$, where $N$ is the number of channels included in the integrated intensity map" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Stack C2H hyperfine components and validate the detection**: Stacked visibilities of the four C2H components with equal weights, imaged with a 0.3'' uv taper; matched filter validation  
  > "we stacked the visibilities (with each component equally weighted) and imaged them with a $uv$ taper of $0.3\ensuremath{''}$" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, VISIBLE
   - parameter: Matched filter template: CLEAN model of CS  
  > "The CLEAN model for CS was selected as the matched filter template" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - assumption: CS is a suitable template because it is detected at high significance and lacks obvious non-disk emission  
  > "because this line is detected at high significance and does not have obvious emission originating from non-disk components" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Matched filter confirms the four C2H hyperfine components above 5 sigma  
  > "confirming that the four targeted C$_2$H hyperfine components are detected above $5\sigma$" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
4. **Test SO clump kinematics against a Keplerian model**: Keplerian fit to the SO 7_6-6_5 velocity map was attempted but failed; a model Keplerian map with literature parameters was subtracted from the observed map  
  > "but the data quality was not sufficient to obtain a reliable fit" (Emission Morphology); "we generated a model Keplerian velocity map with `eddy` using parameters measured from previous observations of DR Tau" (Emission Morphology)
   - software: eddy
   - parameter: i = 5.4 deg, PA = 3.4 deg (Long 2019); v_sys = 9.9 km/s, M_star = 1.18 Msun (Braun 2021)  
  > "$i=5.4^\circ$, and P.A. $=3.4^\circ$ (cite: 2019ApJ...882...49L)" (Emission Morphology)
   - assumption: Literature M_star, i and PA are uncertain because of low inclination and compact disk  
  > "The literature values of $M_\star$, $i$, and P.A. have large uncertainties due to the low inclination and compactness of the disk." (Emission Morphology)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Clump deviates from Keplerian model by ~50 m/s, below the spectral resolution  
  > "The line-of-sight velocity clump differs from the Keplerian model by $\sim$ 50 m s$^{-1}$, which is smaller than the spectral resolution." (Emission Morphology)
5. **Derive disk-averaged column densities and CS/SO**: Optically thin LTE column density from CS 5-4 and SO 7_6-6_5 fluxes at assumed temperatures  
  > "Under the assumption that the molecular emission is in LTE and optically thin, the column density $N$ can be estimated by" (The CS/SO ratio); "We first estimated the source-averaged CS/SO ratio using the CS $5-4$ and SO $7_6-6_5$ flux measurements in Table" (The CS/SO ratio)
   - parameter: Assumed temperatures 30 K and 80 K  
  > "We therefore calculated column densities for assumed temperatures of 30 K and 80 K" (The CS/SO ratio)
   - parameter: Partition function Q(T) interpolated from CDMS  
  > "$Q(T)$ is computed by interpolating the values from the database" (The CS/SO ratio)
   - assumption: LTE and optically thin emission  
  > "Under the assumption that the molecular emission is in LTE and optically thin" (The CS/SO ratio)
   - assumption: Temperatures from CO brightness temperatures in other T Tauri disks  
  > "chosen based on gas temperatures estimated from CO brightness temperature measurements in other disks hosted by T Tauri stars" (The CS/SO ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Measure radial CS/SO profile and CS/SO at the SO clump**: Re-imaged CS (robust 0), smoothed CS and SO to a common beam, made azimuthally averaged radial profiles excluding the NE clump for SO; upper limit on CS/SO at the clump peak  
  > "we re-imaged CS with a robust value of 0 so that its beam size more closely matched that of SO $7_6-6_5$." (The CS/SO ratio); "The SO radial intensity profile was computed only with pixels located at position angles between 90 and 360$^\circ$ in order to exclude the northeast clump." (The CS/SO ratio)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Common beam 0.59'' x 0.29'' (12 deg)  
  > "to a common beam size of $0.59''\times0.29''$ ($12^\circ$)" (The CS/SO ratio)
   - parameter: Clump CS/SO upper limits use 3 sigma limits on N_CS  
  > "At $T=30$ K, $N_\text{SO}=3.5\pm0.7 \times10^{13}$, the $3\sigma$ upper limit for $N_\text{CS}$ is $5\times10^{12}$ cm$^{-2}$, and the $3\sigma$ upper limit for CS/SO is $<0.15$." (The CS/SO ratio)
   - assumption: Same optically thin LTE assumption at T = 30 K and 80 K  
  > "At $T=30$ K, the CS/SO values range from a minimum of $0.3$ at the disk center to a maximum of $0.6$ at a radius of $90$ au." (The CS/SO ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Re-reduce archival ALMA 1.3 mm continuum**: Re-calibrate with the ALMA pipeline in CASA, flag line channels, average, three rounds of phase self-cal, multiscale CLEAN with robust 0.5, aperture flux  
  > "Channels with line emission were flagged, and the remaining channels were spectrally averaged to produce a set of continuum-only visibilities." (DR Tau ALMA continuum data reduction); "The final continuum image was produced with multiscale `CLEAN ` (cite: 2008ISTSP...2..793C) and a robust value of 0.5." (DR Tau ALMA continuum data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Phase self-cal intervals 60, 30, 15 s; S/N improved by factor of 2  
  > "using solution intervals of 60, 30, and 15 seconds, respectively, which improved the continuum S/N by a factor of 2" (DR Tau ALMA continuum data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux consistent with Long et al. 2019; uncertainty from 500 random apertures  
  > "which is consistent with (cite: 2019ApJ...882...49L)" (DR Tau ALMA continuum data reduction); "taking the standard deviation of 500 measurements" (DR Tau ALMA continuum data reduction)
8. **Model disk physical structure (SED, 1.3 mm continuum, C18O) with DALI**: DALI models with two dust populations and a generalized surface density profile; two physical models A and B, parameters varied by hand to match SED, continuum profile and C18O radial profile; synthetic visibilities CLEANed for comparison  
  > "we used the DALI thermochemical code (cite: 2012AA...541A..91B; 2013AA...559A..46B) to model DR Tau’s SED, spatially resolved millimeter continuum, and C$^{18}$O $J=2-1$ images in order to constrain the disk density and temperature." (Modeling DR Tau's disk physical structure); "Synthetic visibilities sampled at the same $uv$ points as the 1.3 mm continuum observations were generated from the model images using the `vis_sample` package" (Dust modeling)
   - software: DALI, optool, vis_sample, CASA
   - parameter: f_lg = 0.99 (model A) and 0.90 (model B)  
  > "We fixed $f_{lg}$ to 0.99 for Physical Model A and to 0.90 for Physical Model B" (Dust modeling)
   - parameter: Grain size distribution slope p = 3.5 (MRN)  
  > "The value of $p$ is fixed to 3.5, corresponding to the MRN distribution from (cite: 1977ApJ...217..425M)." (Disk structure parametrization)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust ratio fixed to 100  
  > "and $M_\text{gas}/M_\text{dust}$ was fixed to the ISM value of 100" (Disk structure parametrization)
   - assumption: PAH abundance 0.1 x ISM; cosmic ray ionization rate 1e-18 s^-1  
  > "The PAH abundance was fixed to $0.1\times$ the ISM value" (Disk structure parametrization); "the cosmic ray ionization rate was set to a relatively low value of 10$^{-18}$ s$^{-1}$" (C18O modeling)
   - assumption: Axisymmetric disk; spiral structure not modelled  
  > "we employ an axisymmetric disk parametrization" (Disk structure parametrization)
   - fitting: Manual iteration of parameters to improve visual match; no sampler or priors  
  > "We then varied these values to improve the visual match between the dust models and the observations." (Dust modeling)
   - validation: C18O model radial profile matched to within ~10% interior to 200 au  
  > "This procedure was repeated until the model matched the observed C$^{18}$O radial intensity profile to within $\sim10\%$ interior to $r=200$ au." (C18O modeling)
9. **Model CS, SO and C2H chemistry and constrain C/O**: Chemistry-only DALI runs for 1 Myr on physical structures A and B for input C/O of 0.47, 0.7, 0.9, 1.0; sulfur abundance profile iteratively adjusted to match the observed CS radial profile; SO and C2H profiles then compared with observations  
  > "we ran chemistry-only models for 1 Myr each using input elemental C/O ratios of 0.47, 0.7, 0.9, and 1.0." (Modeling Procedure); "We first ran a model with a guess for the sulfur abundance, raytraced the CS image cube, subtracted the continuum, generated and CLEANed synthetic visibilities, and then extracted the radial intensity profile." (Modeling Procedure)
   - software: DALI, vis_sample, CASA
   - parameter: Network of 131 species and 1721 reactions  
  > "Overall, the network consists of 131 species and 1721 reactions." (Chemical Network)
   - parameter: Radial intensity profiles binned at 10 au  
  > "which were binned at intervals of 10 au" (Modeling Procedure)
   - assumption: All C and O initially in gas-phase CO and H2O ice  
  > "We assumed that all of the C and O are initially in gas-phase CO and H$_2$O ice." (Modeling Procedure)
   - assumption: Volatile sulfur begins in atomic form  
  > "For simplicity, volatile sulfur in our models begins in atomic form." (Chemical Network)
   - assumption: SO radial profiles exclude PA 0-90 deg because models lack the NE clump  
  > "azimuthal angles at P.A.s between 0 and $90^\circ$ were excluded, since the models did not include the northeast clump." (Chemical modeling results)
   - fitting: Iterative scaling of the input S abundance by the ratio of observed to model CS radial profiles, linearly interpolated onto model cells; no sampler or priors  
  > "We then performed new model runs after adjusting the input sulfur abundance profile based on the ratio of the observed and model CS radial intensity profiles" (Modeling Procedure)
   - validation: Iteration stopped when CS profiles matched within 10% inside 200 au; test with all sulfur initially in SO and test with input C/O = 0.2  
  > "We stopped this procedure after the observed and model CS radial intensity profiles matched within 10% at radii within 200 au." ## Qualitative findings

- Models indicate a gas-phase C/O < 1 in the disk regions traced by NOEMA  
  > "indicate that the gas-phase C/O ratio of DR Tau is $<1$, in contrast with most disks with reported C/O measurements." (Summary)
- The SO clump has no counterpart in other lines, suggesting a localized chemical change rather than a gas overdensity  
  > "The clump does not have a clear counterpart in any other line observed toward DR Tau, suggesting that it traces a localized chemical change rather than a gas overdensity." (Emission Morphology)
- HCN/C18O and C2H/C18O flux ratios are markedly lower than in MAPS disks  
  > "the HCN/C$^{18}$O and C$_2$H/C$^{18}$O ratios are markedly lower than the MAPS disks" (Comparison with other systems)
- DCO+ is compact and centrally peaked while H13CO+ is extended and ring-like, opposite to other disks  
  > "The DCO$^+$ emission is comparatively compact and centrally peaked, while the H$^{13}$CO$^+$ emission is more radially extended and ring-like." (Comparison with other systems)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: SO alone does not constrain C/O because it also depends on total sulfur, so resolved CS, SO and C2H images were obtained  
  > "SO emission by itself does not constrain the C/O ratio, since it is also dependent upon the total sulfur abundance." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: CS was used as the reference for the sulfur abundance matching because of its higher SNR  
  > "we selected CS as the common reference because of its higher SNR" (Modeling Procedure)
- Alternative: 12CO and 13CO not used to constrain models because disk emission is hard to separate from large-scale emission  
  > "we elected not to use them to constrain the models because it is not straightforward to distinguish the disk emission from the complex large-scale emission." (Observational constraints)
- Alternative: Higher-resolution ALMA CO isotopologue data of Sturm et al. not used because of spatial filtering  
  > "Given that their observations appear to be affected by spatial filtering, we did not include them in our analysis either." (Observational constraints)
- Alternative: Lower input C/O of 0.2 tested; no change in CS and SO beyond 100 au  
  > "We ran tests with an input C/O value of 0.2 and found that the CS and SO column densities barely changed beyond 100 au." (Chemical modeling results)
- Alternative: Initial reservoir of SO tested; does not enhance outer-disk SO  
  > "an initial reservoir of SO does not enhance the final abundance of SO in the outer disk because SO is efficiently converted to CS." (Chemical modeling results)
- Alternative: Protoplanet explanation for the SO clump considered less likely  
  > "so we consider the protoplanet explanation less likely for this system" (Origins of DR Tau’s SO clump)
- How they decided it was enough: Iteration stopped when model and observed CS profiles agreed within 10% inside 200 au  
  > "We stopped this procedure after the observed and model CS radial intensity profiles matched within 10% at radii within 200 au." (Modeling Procedure)
- Limitation (authors): Vertical temperature and density structure uncertain (face-on disk, one CO isotopologue line)  
  > "the vertical temperature and density structures are uncertain because the disk is nearly face-on and only one CO isotopologue line is used to constrain the models." (Model limitations)
- Limitation (authors): Chemical network lacks grain-surface SO pathways  
  > "The limited chemical network used in this study does not include grain surface pathways to produce SO" (Model limitations)
- Limitation (authors): Shock heating not included in the models  
  > "Our models do not include the effect of shock heating, which can enhance SO production" (Model limitations)
- Limitation (authors): Disk models have unexplored degeneracies  
  > "Disk modeling suffers from substantial degeneracies that are computationally expensive to explore" (Dust modeling)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): C/O < 1 conclusion rests on two hand-tuned physical models without a formal fit or uncertainty on C/O
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Disk-averaged CS/SO assumes optically thin LTE at two temperatures and CS is likely optically thick inside ~50 au
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The SO clump significance is only ~5-6 sigma and the infall link is qualitative
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Models underpredict SO in the inner 50 au and beyond 100 au and misfit C2H, so the C/O inference is partly model dependent
