## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO cubes and continuum**: CASA calibration scripts, UVCONTSUB, CLEAN with natural weighting (Hogbom), 30 km/s channels  
  > "The calibrated continuum-subtracted visibilities were then imaged and deconvolved with the CLEAN algorithm." (4.1 ALMA data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux calibrator continuum compared with SMA calibrator catalogue  
  > "we compared the flux measurements with the flux computed at the same frequency in a similar date of the observations using the SMA calibrator catalogue." (4.1 ALMA data reduction)
2. **Continuum flux and spectral index**: Continuum from line-free channels; power-law fit to radio SED with VLA/ATCA plus ALMA  
  > "We fitted a power-law to the radio spectral energy distribution (SED) using measurements from VLA and ATCA (cite: hogan15) plus our ALMA observations." (5.1)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit CO spectra and derive molecular gas masses**: One or two Gaussians from rectangular aperture of >=3 sigma emission; mass from CO(1-0) integrated flux with Galactic XCO  
  > "We fitted each CO line with one or two Gaussian profile extracted from a rectangular aperture encompassing all emission with a significance $\geq$3$\sigma$ from the primary beam corrected datacube." (4.3)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: XCO = 2e20 cm-2 (K km/s)-1; CO(2-1)/CO(1-0)=3.2 and CO(3-2)/CO(1-0)~0.8 in flux density  
  > "we assumed a flux density line ratio CO(2-1)/CO(1-0)$=$3.2 (cite: braine92) and CO(3-2)/CO(1-0) $\sim$ 0.8 (cite: edge01; russell16)" (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SPECFLUX, similar within 10–30%  
  > "Those values were compared with the SPECFLUX task in CASA, recovering similar values within about 10–30%." (4.3)
4. **Moment maps and filament lengths**: Masked moment maps; PV diagrams along filaments  
  > "A position-velocity diagram was extracted from each data-cube along coherent physical structures to measure the filament length using the Python package PV EXTRACTOR." (4.4)
   - software: PV EXTRACTOR, makeplots masked moment
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **MUSE Halpha maps and comparison with CO**: PLATEFIT emission-line fits; smooth MUSE to ALMA resolution; reproject ALMA onto MUSE grid; flux ratio, velocity difference, dispersion ratio  
  > "The MUSE maps were smoothed to match the resolution of the ALMA data for each object and, using the REPROJECT task from the ASTROPY package, we resampled the ALMA data onto the MUSE data pixel grids." (5.2)
   - software: MUSE pipeline, ZAP, PLATEFIT, AUTOZ, VDISPFIT, astropy reproject
   - parameter: S/N>7 for emission-line fluxes; WCS shifts below ~1 arcsec  
  > "we make a requirement that all our emission-line flux measurements have signal-to-noise ratio, S/N$>$7." (4.2 MUSE)
   - assumption: CO and Halpha aligned morphologically  
  > "assuming that the CO and H$\alpha$ are aligned morphologically" (5.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **ICM timescales vs filament extent**: Entropy, tcool, tff (NFW + isothermal), teddy from ACCEPT profiles; compare with Halpha filament radial extent  
  > "The mass profiles contain two components: a NFW (Navarro-Frenk-White) component" (5.3)
   - assumption: Hydrostatic equilibrium for NFW fit; central bin excluded  
  > "by assuming hydrostatic equilibrium" (5.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- 13/15 BCGs show extended filaments; 2 (Abell 262, Hydra-A) show compact rotating disks  
  > "most of the BCG have unrelaxed filamentary structure with complex kinematics (13 sources out of 15), while only 2 objects have morphologies and kinematics consistent with a relaxed" (Summary and Conclusions)
