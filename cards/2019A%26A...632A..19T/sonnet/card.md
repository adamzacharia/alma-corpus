## Analysis recipe

1. **image line cubes and integrated maps**: PdBI: continuum subtraction and natural-weighted cleaning in GILDAS; ALMA: imaging with CASA; maps integrated over Vsys±ΔV  
  > "we integrate the line emission over three channels given the low spectral resolution of the WIDEX data." (Integrated maps)
   - software: GILDAS, CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: maps compared with previous interferometric observations  
  > "These maps are in good agreement with previous interferometric observations of these sources" (Integrated maps)
2. **measure line fluxes**: flux integrated over elliptical mask with beam-like size and PA centered on emission peak  
  > "integrated over an elliptical mask with similar size and position angle to the synthesized beam and with a center at the molecular emission peak" (Integrated maps)
   - software: GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **derive column densities and rotational temperatures**: population diagram with reduced chi-square grid over Trot 50-350 K and Ntot 1e15-1e20 cm-2, LTE with opacity correction  
  > "total column density in the source $N_{\textrm{tot}}$ between $10^{15}$ and $10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$." (Population diagrams)
   - parameter: source sizes 0.36'' (IRAS2A), 0.20'' (IRAS4A), 0.19'' (HH212)  
  > "A source size of 0$\ensuremath{''}$36 and 0$\ensuremath{''}$20 in IRAS2A and IRAS4A, respectively" (Population diagrams)
   - assumption: CH3OD Trot fixed to that of CH2DOH; two CH3OD partition functions; 12C/13C = 70  
  > "For CH$_3$OD, we assume the same rotational temperature than CH$_2$DOH" (Population diagrams)
   - fitting: reduced chi-square minimisation on a grid  
  > "We perform a reduced chi-square ($\chi_{\textrm{red}}^2$) minimization by running a grid of models" (Population diagrams)
   - validation: HH212 result compared with Bianchi et al. 2017 (2.4 ± 0.4%)  
  > "in good agreement with the value of $2.4 \pm 0.4$ % found by (cite: Bianchi2017)" (Comparison with previous observations)
4. **compute deuteration ratios and compare with other sources**: ratios of derived column densities vs CH3OH from Taquet2015 or 13CH3OH; compared with literature hot cores and dust temperatures from Herschel/Planck  
  > "we only choose observations whose deuteration values were derived from $^{13}$CH$_3$OH or CH$_3^{18}$OH column densities" (Deuteration from low- to high-mass protostars)
   - assumption: gaussian fit of temperature histogram around main peak, pixels with AV>3 mag  
  > "fitting the temperature histogram arround the main peak with a gaussian function" (Deuteration from low- to high-mass protostars)
   - fitting: gaussian fit of dust temperature histogram  
  > "we filter out the "hot" regions locally heated by stars or "cold" regions of dense cores by fitting the temperature histogram arround the main peak with a gaussian function." (Discussion, 4.2 Deuteration from low- to high-mass protostars)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **compare with GRAINOBLE model**: pseudo-time-dependent ice chemistry runs vs temperature, density, time  
  > "We compare the observed methanol deuterations with the predictions of the GRAINOBLE astrochemical code" (Comparison with theoretical predictions)
   - software: GRAINOBLE
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Methanol deuteration in low-mass hot corinos is about ten times lower than earlier single-dish values but 1-2 orders of magnitude above massive hot cores.  
  > "These values are typically ten times lower than those derived with previous single-dish observations towards these sources" (abstract)

  > "Our model only focusing on cold surface chemistry is not able to explain \[CH$_2$DOH\]/\[CH$_3$OD\] ratios lower than 1 observed in massive hot cores." (Comparison with theoretical predictions)
