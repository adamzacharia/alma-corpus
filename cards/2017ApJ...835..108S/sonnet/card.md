## Analysis recipe

1. **Mass from 12CO(2-1)**: Convert 12CO(2-1) intensity to 1-0 with ratio 0.85, apply X(CO)  
  > "we assumed the typical $^{12}$CO(2-1)/$^{12}$CO(1-0) ratio toward HII regions of 0.85" (3.1)
   - assumption: X(CO) = (7±2)e20 cm-2  
  > "X(CO) = $(7 \pm 2) \times 10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ (cite: Fukui2008)" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with 13CO mass  
  > "It is consistent with the mass derived from $^{12}$CO(2-1)." (3.1)
2. **Mass from 13CO(2-1) in LTE**: LTE with Tex from optically thick 12CO peak temperature, floor 20 K  
  > "were assumed to be 20 K where the calculated excitation temperatures were below 20 K" (3.1)
   - assumption: 13CO/H2 = 3.2e-7  
  > "$^{13}$CO/H$_{2}$ abundance ratio of $3.2 \times 10^{-7}$" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **YSO SED fitting**: Robitaille 2007 model grid fit to 2MASS, Spitzer, Herschel photometry  
  > "Mass, Luminosity and age are the best fit results of SED fitting." (3.2 YSO characteristics)
   - software: Robitaille SED fitter
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Chen 2010 and Meynadier 2004  
  > "Our fittings result in higher mass and luminosity due to the addition of Herschel PACS and SPIRE photometry" (3.2 YSO characteristics)
4. **Ionized gas properties**: Te from H30alpha, then Mezger & Henderson equations with 98 GHz continuum  
  > "We determine the electron temperature of the gas using the H30$\alpha$ emission" (3.2 YSO characteristics)
   - assumption: no ionizing photons escape  
  > "therefore we are assuming that none of ionizing photons are escaping" (3.2 YSO characteristics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Dust mass in Papillon region**: 231 GHz continuum flux within 1 pc converted to mass  
  > "The total flux of 231GHz continuum emission around Papillon Nebula within the radius of 1pc is $\sim$ 90 mJy." (3.3)
   - assumption: Tdust 80 K scaling; kappa 0.77 cm2/g; dust-to-gas 3.5e-3  
  > "is 0.77 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$ and $3.5 \times10^{-3}$, respectively" (3.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Filament kinematics**: Velocity-component maps and PV diagrams along filaments; FWHM line widths  
  > "Figure [7](#N159E_Papillon_PV) shows the PV diagrams along each filament." (3.4)
   - parameter: blue -8.0 to -1.6, green -1.4 to +1.0, red +1.2 to +7.8 km/s  
  > "blue (-8.0 km s$^{-1}$ – -1.6 km s$^{-1}$), green (-1.4 km s$^{-1}$ – +1.0 km s$^{-1}$), and red (+1.2 km s$^{-1}$ – +7.8 km s$^{-1}$)" (3.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
