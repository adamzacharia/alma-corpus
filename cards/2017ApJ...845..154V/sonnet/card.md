## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract line spectra and fit fluxes**: Gaussian fit to spectra at [C II] position with redshift and width fixed to [C II] values  
  > "We fitted a Gaussian function to the lines, fixing the redshift and the width of the lines to those from the significantly higher signal-to-noise (S/N) \[C II\] line (cite: ven16)." (3)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Also let width and center vary; results similar  
  > "Allowing the width and center of the Gaussian to vary gives very similar parameters" (3.1)
2. **Measure 3 mm continuum**: Average line-free channels; measure flux density  
  > "Averaging the frequency channels not covered by emission lines resulted in a significant, 8$\sigma$ detection of the continuum" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Constrain dust temperature**: Modified blackbody fit to 1 mm and 3 mm points including CMB effects  
  > "We fitted a modified black body to the data points, taking into account the effects of the CMB described above" (4.1)
   - assumption: beta = 1.6 or 1.95; fixed redshift; 10% flux calibration error  
  > "we will assume two different values of $\beta$ from the literature: $\beta=1.6$ (cite: bee06) and $\beta=1.95$ (cite: pri01)." (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive CO(1-0) luminosity and H2 mass**: Convert fluxes to L' and scale to CO(1-0) via J1148+5251 LVG SLED; M_gas = alpha L'  
  > "we can obtain $L^\prime_\mathrm{CO(1-0)}$ luminosities by applying the $L^\prime_{\mathrm{CO(6-5)}}$/$L^\prime_\mathrm{CO(1-0)}$ and $L^\prime_{\mathrm{CO(7-6)}}$/$L^\prime_\mathrm{CO(1-0)}$ luminosity ratios given by the LVG model." (4.2)
   - assumption: alpha = 0.8 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1; H0=70, OmegaM=0.3  
  > "we further assume a value of $\alpha=0.8$ $M_\ensuremath{\odot}$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$, derived for local ultra-luminous infrared galaxies (cite: dow98)" (4.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with dust-derived gas masses (gas-to-dust 70–100) and with CO SLED of Carilli & Walter 2013  
  > "which we can use to derive gas masses by assuming the local gas-to-dust mass ratio of 70–100 (cite: dra07; san13)" (4.2)
5. **Atomic carbon mass**: Optically thin [C I] mass relation  
  > "If we assume that the \[C I\] emission is optically thin, then we can use the relation between \[C I\] brightness and the mass in neutral carbon" (4.3)
   - assumption: Tex = 30 K  
  > "we set the excitation temperature to $T_\mathrm{ex}=30$ K (cite: wal11)" (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **ISM diagnostics**: Compare line ratios with PDR/XDR models  
  > "can be confirmed, and ISM properties can be derived by applying the PDR and XDR models from (cite: mei05) and (cite: mei07)" (4.4)
   - assumption: Majority of [C II] from same region as CO and [C I]  
  > "we will assume that the majority of the \[C II\] emission comes from the same region as the CO and \[C I\] emission" (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: CO SLED from J1148+5251 used since only high-J CO measured  
  > "Since we only measured the CO(6–5) and/or CO(7–6) line flux in our sources, we have to assume a CO spectral line energy distribution (CO SLED) to estimate the CO(1–0) line strength." (4.2)
- Alternative: Carilli & Walter 2013 quasar CO SLED would give 57–95% higher CO(1-0) luminosities  
  > "then the estimated CO(1–0) luminosities (and molecular gas masses) are 57–95% higher compared to the luminosities derived using the J1148+5251 CO SLED" (4.2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Molecular gas uncertainties exclude CO SLED shape uncertainty; low-J CO needed  
  > "the uncertainties quoted in this section only include the uncertainties in the measured high-$J$ CO line fluxes and not the uncertainty in the shape of the CO excitation ladder" (4.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(1-0) extrapolation depends on a single assumed SLED
- Caveat (inferred, ours): [C I] detection only ~3 sigma
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spatially unresolved data limit ISM modeling
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical mass inconsistent for J0305
