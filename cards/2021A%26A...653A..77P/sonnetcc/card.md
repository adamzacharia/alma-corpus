## Analysis recipe

1. **Obtain calibrated data and subtract continuum**: Use archive QA2 calibrated data in CASA; subtract continuum from the line data with imcontsub  
  > "The Common Astronomy Software Applications (CASA) was used to handle and analyse the data." (Data and source selection); "was used to subtract the continuum from the spectral lines and a first order polynomial was used." (Data and source selection)
   - software: CASA, imcontsub
   - parameter: first order polynomial continuum fit  
  > "was used to subtract the continuum from the spectral lines and a first order polynomial was used." (Data and source selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Check imaging artefacts in spectra**: Reprocess raw data with clean for sources with spectral dips near the CN main component and compare with archive images  
  > "we reprocessed the raw data set of sources whose spectra exhibit conspicuous dips in, or close to, the main component of the CN emission" (Data and source selection)
   - software: CASA, clean
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Reprocessed images very similar to archival ones, so dips not attributed to clean  
  > "The images obtained from our data reprocessing are very similar to those obtained from the archival" (Data and source selection)
3. **Extract CN spectra per source**: Spectra of averaged emission from regions about 5 arcsec in radius centred on each source, converted to rest frequency using Table 1 velocities  
  > "we extracted spectra of the averaged emission obtained from regions of about 5$^{\prime\prime}$ in radius centered in each source." (Results); "The frequency was converted to rest frequency using the velocities listed in Table" (Results)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: displayed range 226860-226900 MHz  
  > "it is displayed the frequency range 226860–226900 MHz" (Results)
   - assumption: Source v_LSR from literature (Table 1)  
  > "The frequency was converted to rest frequency using the velocities listed in Table" (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Map CN emission and overlay continuum**: CN maps averaged over the frequency interval of the main component, with 1.3 mm continuum as contours  
  > "maps of the CN emission averaged along the frequency interval of the main component." (Results); "Additionally, the continuum emission at 1.3 mm is displayed in contours." (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Fit 1.3 mm continuum cores**: Two-dimensional Gaussian fits give peak intensity, integrated flux density (primary beam corrected), deconvolved size, and core size in au from mean semi-axis and distance  
  > "presents the parameters of the cores observed at 1.3 mm obtained from a two dimensional Gaussian fitting."    - assumption: Distances from literature; size error about 20% mainly from distance uncertainty  
  > "The source of the errors in the size is mainly the uncertainties in the distances, and it can be about 20$\%$." (Results)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian; fitting software, priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "obtained from a two dimensional Gaussian fitting." (Results)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compare CN and continuum with near-IR and Spitzer**: Compare Ks-band emission and IRAC 3.6, 4.5, 8 micron images with CN maps and 1.3 mm continuum cores to infer evolutionary stage  
  > "emission in comparison with the ALMA data with the aim of finding any connection relating the extended CN and near-IR emissions, and near-IR point-like sources with the 1.3 mm continuum cores." (Results)
   - assumption: Lack of near-IR emission or point source in a core can indicate an earlier (starless) stage  
  > "the lack of near-IR emission or a point-like source at this wavelength in a molecular core can indicate that it is an starless core" (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Analyse spectra at continuum core positions for absorption**: Extract spectra from circles of 1-1.5 arcsec radius at the continuum cores of G13.656, G20.747, G24.464 and G33.133, and judge absorption as optical depth, infall/expansion (P-Cygni) or missing flux  
  > "Thus, for each of the mentioned sources, we analyze the spectra at the positions of the continuum cores." (Analysis of sources with spectral absorption features); "shows the spectrum obtained from a circle of 1.5$''$ in radius at the position of G13.656-mm1." (At core G13.656-mm1)

  > "displays the spectra obtained towards cores G20.747-mm1 and -mm2 from a circle of 1.2$''$ in radius." (At cores G20.747-mm1/2)
   - parameter: G24.464-mm1 circle radius 1.3 arcsec  
  > "The spectrum obtained from a circle of 1.3$''$ in radius at the position of core G24.464-mm1" (At core G24.464-mm1)
   - parameter: G33.133-mm1/2/3 circle radius 1 arcsec  
  > "Spectra obtained from a circle of 1$^{\prime\prime}$ in radius towards cores G33.133-mm1, -mm2, and -mm3" (At cores G33.133-mm1/2/3)

## Qualitative findings

- CN traces both molecular condensations and diffuse, extended gas around them  
  > "we can conclude that the CN traces both molecular condensations and diffuse and extended gas surrounding them." (Discussion)
- CN emission maxima generally do not coincide with 1.3 mm continuum peaks

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archive Band 6 data of high-mass star-forming regions covering the CN N=2-1 line; sources chosen with CN line in the spectral window and clear lines for identification

  > "The images obtained from our data reprocessing are very similar to those obtained from the archival" (Data and source selection)
