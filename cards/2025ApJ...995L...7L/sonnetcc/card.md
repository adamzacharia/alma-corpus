1. **Image and combine ALMA 12m, 7m and TP data into C18O cubes**: Combine 12m and 7m with tclean at robust 0.5 and -2, then feather in total power data  
  > "the 12m and 7m data were combined using the tclean task in the Common Astronomy Software Applications (CASA) package" (2 Observations); "To recover the missing flux, the feather task (cite: Plunkett_2023PASP..135c4501P) was used to incorporate total power measurements into the final map." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean, feather
   - parameter: robust = 0.5 and -2  
  > "Robust weighting parameters of 0.5 and -2 for the visibilities yielded synthesized beam sizes of" (2 Observations)
   - parameter: velocity resolution 0.15 km/s  
  > "with a velocity resolution of 0.15 km s" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make moment maps and PV diagrams of C18O**: Moment 0/1 maps integrated over 7-12 km/s; PV cut along the disk major axis extending ~15 arcsec (~6000 au) north and south  
  > "emission integrated from 7 to 12 km s" (3.1 A Flattened Structure of the Envelope); "The cut extends 15$^{\prime\prime}$ ($\sim$6000 au) from the protostar to the north and south into the envelope" (3.2 Rotation and Infall Motion)
   - software: CARTA
   - parameter: PV cut at robust 0.5 and -2 for low and high resolution  
  > "Two PV diagrams are shown using two different robust weightings of visibilities" (3.2 Rotation and Infall Motion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Build flattened infalling-rotating envelope model and radiative transfer**: Parametric flattened envelope with power-law density and temperature, ballistic envelope kinematics, power-law angular momentum in transition region, Keplerian disk inside 160 au; LTE radiative transfer with RADMC-3D to make model channel maps and PV diagram  
  > "we constructed a flattened, infalling, and rotating envelope model based on (cite: Lee_2010ApJ) and extended it to include the transition region and Keplerian disk." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model); "Radiative transfer was performed using RADMC-3D (cite: Dullemond_2012ascl.soft02015D) under LTE to produce model channel maps and then the PV diagram of" (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: C18O abundance fixed at 1.7e-7 relative to H2  
  > "The C$^{18}$O abundance relative to molecular hydrogen is fixed at 1.7 $\times$ 10$^{-7}$." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - parameter: inclination ~18 deg to the plane of sky; model spans 10 au to 8000 au  
  > "The inclination angle of the flattened envelope to the plane of sky is $\sim$18$^\circ$, a mean value derived from the disk (cite: Lee_2020NatAs...4..142L) and jet (cite: Reipurth_1992ApJ)." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model); "The model spans from 10 au" (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - parameter: best-fit structure: H0 ~0.4 arcsec (160 au), theta0 ~18 deg, n0 ~1.3e6 cm^-3, p ~ -0.13+/-0.01, T0 ~20+/-1 K, q ~ -0.5+/-0.1  
  > "The best-fit physical parameters are: $H_0 \sim$ 0$\overset{\prime\prime}{.}$4 (160 au), $\theta_0 \sim 18^\circ$, $n_0 \sim 1.3 \times 10^6$ cm$^{-3}$, $p \sim -0.13\pm0.01$, $T_0 \sim 20\pm1$ K and $q \sim -0.5\pm0.1$." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - assumption: single power-law density and temperature across envelope, transition region and disk  
  > "We adopt single power-law forms across the envelope, transition region, and disk." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - assumption: total mass of protostar plus disk 1.8 Msun; envelope mass neglected in infall velocity  
  > "The mass of the envelope is $\sim$0.6$M_\odot$, which contributes less than 15% to the infall velocity and is therefore neglected." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - assumption: envelope ballistic with conserved angular momentum and energy; rotation and radial velocities same as mid-plane values  
  > "In the envelope, we assume a ballistic motion under the conservation of angular momentum and energy (cite: Sakai_2014Natur.507...78S)." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Model PV diagram overlaid on the observed PV diagram and residual map made by subtracting the model from the observed PV diagram  
  > "The residual map in Figure [3](#fig:pv_obs_model)(c), obtained by subtracting the model from the observed PV diagram" (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
4. **Baseline transition-region model with power-law angular momentum**: Power-law angular momentum l(R) = l0 (R/Re)^b between envelope and disk with b set from l0 and the Keplerian value at Rd; infall velocity from energy conservation  
  > "Since no analytic solution exists for the transition region, we adopted a power-law form as the angular momentum distribution for the first try" (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model); "These values yield *b* $\sim$ 0.38." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: b ~0.38 (l0 ~1333 au km/s, ~530 au km/s at Rd ~160 au)  
  > "The power-law index *b* is determined using the envelope angular momentum $\ell_0 \sim 1333$ au km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Lee_2010ApJ)" (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals show the model does not reproduce the sharp velocity drop in the intermediate region, motivating a rotation dip  
  > "This discrepancy motivates the additional modeling of a rotation velocity dip, as discussed in the next subsection." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
5. **Add log-Gaussian rotation dip and declining infall velocity**: Multiply the angular-momentum-conserving rotation profile by 1 - A exp(-(ln(R/Rdip))^2/(2 sigma^2)) with different widths inside and outside the dip; infall velocity given a log-Gaussian decline from Rc  
  > "we adopted the angular-momentum–conserving profile as the baseline and introduced a log-Gaussian term to model the dip" (3.4 A Dip in Rotation Velocity)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: first attempt: A_phi ~0.8, R_phi,dip ~1.5 arcsec (600 au), sigma_in ~1.6, sigma_out ~0.4; Rc ~5 arcsec, sigma_c ~1  
  > "The best-fit parameters are: $A_\phi$ $\sim$ 0.8, $R_{\phi,dip}$ $\sim$ 1$\overset{\prime\prime}{.}$5 (600 au), $\sigma_{\rm in}$ $\sim$ 1.6, and $\sigma_{\rm out}\sim$0.4." (3.4 A Dip in Rotation Velocity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Improved agreement between model and observed velocity profiles, especially the dip between ~4 and 1 arcsec; ~30% drop in the outer region (13 to 5 arcsec) still not captured  
  > "This refinement significantly improved the agreement between the model and the observed velocity profiles" (3.4 A Dip in Rotation Velocity)
6. **Add log-Gaussian dip in infall velocity and refine the best-fit model**: Log-Gaussian dip in infall velocity applied at radii >3 arcsec, peak infall shifted to 3.5 arcsec, and rotation profile slightly refined  
  > "we introduced a similar log-Gaussian dip in the infall profile, applied only at radii greater than 3" (3.5 A Dip in Infall Velocity); "we shift the position of the peak infall velocity from" (3.5 A Dip in Infall Velocity)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: infall: A_r ~0.35, R_r,dip ~7.5 arcsec (3000 au), sigma_r ~0.3, R_c ~3.5 arcsec (1400 au), sigma_c ~0.85  
  > "The best-fit parameters for infall velocity are: $A_r \sim$ 0.35, $R_{r,dip}\sim7\overset{\prime\prime}{.}$5 (3000 au), $\sigma_r\sim0.3$, $R_c\sim3\overset{\prime\prime}{.}$5 (1400 au), and $\sigma_c\sim0.85$." (3.5 A Dip in Infall Velocity)
   - parameter: rotation (refined): R_phi,dip ~1.5 arcsec (600 au), A_phi ~0.9, sigma_in ~1.3, sigma_out ~0.5  
  > "To further improve the fit, we also slightly refined the rotation velocity profile with $R_{\phi,dip}$ $\sim$ 1$\overset{\prime\prime}{.}$5 (600 au), $A_\phi$ $\sim$ 0.9, $\sigma_{\rm in}$ $\sim$ 1.3, and $\sigma_{\rm out}\sim$ 0.5." (3.5 A Dip in Infall Velocity)
   - assumption: density and temperature power-law simplifications do not affect the inferred velocity structure  
  > "However, the fitting results indicate that these assumptions do not affect the inferred velocity structure." (3.6 The Best-fit Model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals below ~10% of original values in most regions; up to 50% within 1 arcsec; velocity uncertainties estimated to be less than 20%  
  > "the residuals are reduced to below approximately 10% of the original values in most regions." (3.6 The Best-fit Model); "matches observation well, with velocity uncertainties estimated to be less than 20%." (3.6 The Best-fit Model)
7. **Compare best-fit profiles with ballistic model and MHD simulations**: Compare rotation and infall profiles of the best-fit model with free-fall, Keplerian, sound speed and ballistic profiles; split transition region into three zones and interpret them with non-ideal MHD simulation results  
  > "compares the rotation and infall velocity profiles of the best-fit model with free-fall, Keplerian rotation, sound speed" (4.1 Comparison to theoretical Model); "Based on these profiles, we identify three distinct kinematic zones in the transition region:" (4.1 Comparison to theoretical Model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Qualitative comparison with Li et al. 2011 simulations (infall reduction ~40% here vs at least 10% there)  
  > "In our case, the reduction is about" (4.1.1 Region I: Magnetic Cushion)

## Qualitative findings

- The transition region splits into three zones: outer with reduced infall velocity, middle with a sharp angular momentum and rotation velocity drop, and inner with rotation increasing inward to join the Keplerian disk and infall dropping to zero  
  > "The region can be further divided into three zones:" (abstract)
- Negative flux (absorption) features in the PV diagram are consistent with infalling gas in Region I  
  > "The observed absorption features in the southern and northern regions are consistent with infalling gas" (5 Conclusion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: C18O (2-1) from Band 6 used to trace envelope and disk structures  
  > "line from the 230 GHz receiver to trace the envelope and disk structures (see Table 1 for details)." (2 Observations)
- Why this method: 12m and 7m combined to present extended and compact structures; log-Gaussian chosen as a simple effective match among forms tested  
  > "To simultaneously present both extended and compact structures, the 12m and 7m data were combined using the tclean task" (2 Observations); "Among various forms tested, a log-Gaussian again provided a simple yet effective match" (3.5 A Dip in Infall Velocity)
- Alternative: Power-law angular momentum in the transition region tried first; failed to reproduce the sharp velocity drop, motivating the dip model  
  > "Since no analytic solution exists for the transition region, we adopted a power-law form as the angular momentum distribution for the first try" (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model); "This discrepancy motivates the additional modeling of a rotation velocity dip, as discussed in the next subsection." (3.3 A Flattened Infalling Rotating Envelope Model)
- How they decided it was enough: Adopted the model once residuals were small and the density/temperature simplifications did not affect the velocity structure  
  > "However, the fitting results indicate that these assumptions do not affect the inferred velocity structure. We therefore adopt this as our best-fit model." (3.6 The Best-fit Model)
- Limitation (authors): Residuals within 1 arcsec remain large, up to 50%, attributed to innermost-envelope asymmetry and the linear PV structures  
  > "they remain relatively large, with some areas retaining up to 50%, likely due to the reported asymmetry in the innermost envelope" (3.6 The Best-fit Model)
- Limitation (authors): Simplified single power-law density and temperature contribute to residuals  
  > "Notice that some residuals should also result from the simplified power-law assumptions for density and temperature." (3.6 The Best-fit Model)
- Limitation (authors): Efficiency of magnetic braking depends on uncertain physical parameters  
  > "the efficiency of angular momentum removal via magnetic braking depends on uncertain physical parameters" (4.1.2 Region II: Magnetic Braking)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Best-fit procedure (manual tuning vs optimization), parameter uncertainties and goodness-of-fit are not given for the dip parameters, so the 'significance' of the dip is not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Magnetic braking is inferred by comparison with simulations; the model is degenerate with other mechanisms (e.g. non-ballistic gas pressure effects, asymmetric inner envelope, outflow/ absorption contamination)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single power-law density and temperature and fixed C18O abundance may bias the derived velocity profile, and the model PV is compared at one resolution (robust 0.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interpretation relies on a 1D mid-plane velocity prescription for an inclination of ~18 deg, where projection effects are large
