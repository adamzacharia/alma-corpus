## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract uv-plane spectra and continuum**: Calibrate with CASA script, convert to uvfits, fit point or Gaussian source models in uv-space using GILDAS at centroid positions  
  > "used these positions for extracting spectra by fitting source models in the uv-space" (2 Sample selection, ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - parameter: 16 MHz per channel  
  > "The original spectra with noise are extracted with a resolution of 16MHz per channel." (2 Sample selection, ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Noise per channel checked with global chi^2  
  > "We verified that the noise estimates per channel are reliable from global $\chi^2$ statistics." (2 Sample selection, ALMA observations and data reduction)
2. **Blind line search and redshift determination**: Search for highest-significance line over channel ranges, then matching second lines assuming CO; chance probabilities from simulations  
  > "This algorithm returns both the SNR and the optimum velocity range of the line" (3 Redshift confirmation)
   - parameter: Acceptable linewidths 100 to 1000 km/s  
  > "the range of acceptable linewidths (from 100 to 1000 km s$^{-1}$ in this case)" (3.1 Chance probability of emission lines)
   - assumption: Gaussian noise  
  > "We assume well-behaved Gaussian noise in the data, as verified to hold for our ALMA spectra." (3.1 Chance probability of emission lines)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Two-line chance probabilities ~1e-4 or lower for three galaxies  
  > "The combined probability for the two-line match is in all cases $\sim10^{-4}$ or lower" (3.2 Measuring redshifts for our sample)
3. **Measure sizes**: Fit models in uv space to collapsed 3mm and 1/0.8 mm data  
  > "We measured their sizes by fitting models in uv space in the ALMA 1/0.8 mm and 3 mm collapsed datasets" (4.1 Multi-wavelength imaging and morphology)
   - software: GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Size of ID85001674 consistent between 1mm/0.8mm alone and combined  
  > "The sizes measured from 1mm/0.8mm data alone are $0''.32\pm0''.07$ and $0''.38\pm0''.04$" (4.4 CMB impacts observables)
4. **Fit dust SEDs with and without CMB**: Modified black-body fits (optically thin and thick) to super-deblended photometry plus ALMA continuum, with da Cunha CMB prescription; L_IR=1.35 L_MBB  
  > "We convert the luminosity of MBB models $L_{\rm MBB}$ to $L_{\rm IR,8-1000\mu m}$ multiplying by a constant of 1.35" (4.2 Cold dust against the CMB)
   - assumption: Free T_dust and beta; beta fixed to 2.3 for ID85000922 with CMB  
  > "When including the CMB we still fit its SED with a fixed $\beta=2.3$" (Footnote 4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Casey2012 MBB fits; T_dust consistent  
  > "which are also consistent with those derived by fitting (cite: Casey2012) models" (4.2 Cold dust against the CMB)
5. **Derive physical properties (SFR density, masses, gas masses)**: SFR from L_IR (Kennicutt98), Sigma_SFR=SFR/(2 pi Re^2); dust mass from DL07; stellar mass from SED fits with 200 Myr template; gas mass from dynamics, dust with FMR G/D, and [CI]  
  > "We fit stellar SEDs using a 200 Myr template allowing for dust attenuation, following (cite: Liu_DZ2017)." (5.1 Physical properties)
   - assumption: 20% dark matter in dynamical gas mass; T_ex=30 K for [CI]  
  > "we estimated its gas mass using the relation in (cite: Valentino2018CI) assuming an excitation temperature of $T_{ex}=$30 K" (5.1 Physical properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gas mass methods compared for ID85001674  
  > "These two latter estimates agree well between them but are lower than the estimate based on $M_{\rm dust}$ by factors of 4–5" (5.1 Physical properties)

## Qualitative findings

- Samples are cold, with apparently steep RJ slopes that return to beta~2–2.2 when the CMB is included  
  > "the SEDs corrected for CMB effect yield $\beta\sim$2-2.2" (4.2 Cold dust against the CMB)
