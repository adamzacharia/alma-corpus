2. **Subtract continuum and find line-free channels**: STATCONT determines the continuum level pixel by pixel in the image domain  
  > "STATCONT performs continuum subtraction in image domain pixel by pixel" (STATCONT for continuum subtraction)

  > "We used the default sigma-clipping algorithm, which removes the line-emitting channels by iteratively measuring the median and standard deviation" (STATCONT for continuum subtraction)

4. **Pixel-by-pixel one-layer LTE fit with dust absorption**: LTE fit to the K-ladder lines for each pixel using one-layer model with dust absorption term exp(-tau_dust); one-layer model for pixels without clear double peak  
  > "We conduct LTE models throughout the emitting regions pixel by pixel, adopting a one- or two-layer model." (Radiative transfer model)

   - parameter: Mask: S/N>12 from the K=3 integrated intensity map  
  > "A spatial mask is taken using a threshold of" (LTE model setup)

  > "so the boundary of the fitting is set to 800 K" (One-layer model)
   - parameter: Six free parameters: Tex, N_tot, tau_dust, V_LSR, Delta V, isotopologue ratio  
  > "six free parameters are included" (Radiative transfer model with dust optical depth)
   - assumption: Dust and gas co-located and at the same temperature Tex  
  > "This approach assumes that the dust and gas are co-located instead of assuming the dust as a background to the gas" (One-layer model)
   - assumption: Dust-absorption term exp(-tau_dust) scales the line emission  
  > "As a result, we have a scale factor of" (One-layer model)
   - fitting: Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling on 41 grid positions; boundaries tuned; results interpolated to initial-guess map  
  > "We first perform Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling of a grid of positions in the emitting regions" (Two-layer model)
   - validation: Fit results should not show dramatic differences between neighbouring positions  
  > "the fitting results should not show dramatic difference in between two neighboring positions" (Two-layer model)
5. **Two-layer model and one-/two-layer selection**: Two-layer model with separate Tex, tau, tau_dust, N, V_LSR, linewidth and isotopologue ratio per layer, selected against one-layer by AIC and Bayesian evidence  
  > "the two-layer model better reproduces the spectra profile with lower reduced" (Clustering into kinematic components)

   - parameter: Two-layer model has 12 free parameters  
  > "the two layer model has in total" (Two layer LTE radiative transfer model)
   - parameter: Two-layer chosen only if |V_LSR1 - V_LSR2| larger than both velocity dispersions  
  > "is larger than the velocity dispersions of both layers" (Clustering into kinematic components)
   - fitting: Bayesian evidence via nested sampling (Skilling) using Dynesty on 41 grid positions; AIC computed for each pixel  
  > "we have also calculated Bayesian evidence with nested sampling algorithm (cite: sk04) using Dynesty (cite: sp20; ko22)" (Degeneracy and model selection)

  > "The result is roughly consistent with AIC method favor two-layer model" (Degeneracy and model selection)
6. **Cluster fitted components into kinematic components**: DBSCAN clustering in position-position-velocity space into six groups; discarded points revert to one-layer model (component I)  
  > "We use the clustering algorithm DBSCAN (cite: es96; va23; gi24) with `eps=0.3` and `min_samples=10`, clustering these points into six groups." (Clustering into kinematic components)
   - software: DBSCAN
   - parameter: eps = 0.3; min_samples = 10  
  > "with `eps=0.3` and `min_samples=10`, clustering these points into six groups" (Clustering into kinematic components)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High angular and spectral resolution resolves the line emission and traces disks or rotating envelopes around the protobinary  
  > "The high angular- and spectral-resolution data resolved the line emission" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Dust absorption must be included because the classical two-layer model assumes negligible dust absorption  
  > "The original two-layer model is applied to infall motion at molecular core where the dust absorption might be negligible." (One-layer model)
- Alternative: LTE model without dust absorption failed to reproduce the line spectra and gave inconsistent line ratios  
  > "We note that an LTE model without considering dust absorption was also tried but failed to interpret the line spectrum" (One-layer model)
- Alternative: Gas-dust decoupling model (optically thick dust rear layer) noted as a limiting case, not adopted  
  > "It is noteworthy that this 2-layer model will also converge to the gas-dust decoupling model" (Two-layer model)
- How they decided it was enough: Masks and model choice are set by S/N and a velocity criterion; adequacy judged by smooth neighbouring solutions  
  > "We chose a high S/N= 12 as a mask in the integrated intensity map" (One-layer model)
- Limitation (authors): Infall versus expansion cannot be determined uniquely because of model degeneracy  
  > "However, there is a caveat to determine this given the degeneracy of the model" (Clustering into kinematic components)
- Limitation (authors): The two layers are frequently hard to disentangle  
  > "these two layers can frequently be hard to disentangle" (Clustering into kinematic components)
- Limitation (authors): Tau_dust of the front and rear layers is degenerate, requiring multi-wavelength data  
  > "To overcome this problem requires observations at longer wavelengths" (Degeneracy and model selection)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Summary quotes a spatial resolution of ~23x32 au, inconsistent with the 0.11x0.08 arcsec (~30 au) beam given in Observations
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Two-layer fit has 12 free parameters with hand-tuned MCMC priors and interpolated initial guesses; sensitivity to priors not shown
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Infall/outflow sign of component II is set by the initial guess convention, not uniquely constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust opacity and gas-to-dust ratio of 100 carry uncertainty of a few, which propagates into Q and the ratio maps
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Toomre Q uses one-layer Tex and tau maps together with literature stellar masses and inclination
- Caveat (inferred, ours): DBSCAN eps and min_samples values are given without justification or sensitivity test
