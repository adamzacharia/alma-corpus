  > "We used the default sigma-clipping algorithm" (STATCONT for continuum subtraction)

2. **integrated intensity maps of selected K transitions**: Integrated intensity maps of three CH3CN and three CH3^13CN transitions covering different energy levels and less contaminated  
  > "we show the integrated intensity maps from three transitions of and that cover different energy levels and suffer less from contamination." (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **define fitting mask**: Spatial mask S/N>12 on K=3 integrated intensity map; manual velocity masks against contamination  
  > "A spatial mask is taken using a threshold of S/N>12 from the K=3 integrated intensity map" (LTE model setup); "To avoid the contamination from other lines, a selected mask at specific velocities is provided manually" (LTE model setup)
   - parameter: S/N > 12 on K=3 moment 0

   - validation: _not stated_
4. **one-layer LTE fit with dust absorption**: Pixel-by-pixel single-component LTE model with dust absorption factor exp(-tau_dust); six free parameters  
  > "We first conduct a one velocity component LTE modeling which takes into account dust absorption." (One-layer model); "six free parameters are included: excitation temperature" (Radiative transfer model with dust optical depth)
   - parameter: T_bg = 2.7 K  
  > "where $T_{m bg}=2.7$ K" (One-layer model)
   - parameter: T_ex fit boundary 800 K (partition function from CDMS up to 900 K)  
  > "so the boundary of the fitting is set to 800 K." (One-layer model)
   - assumption: LTE; gas and dust co-located and at same temperature (T_ex = T_dust)  
  > "and that the dust and gas temperature are the same" (One-layer model)
   - assumption: Gaussian line optical depth profiles; 12C/13C ratio = N(CH3CN)/N(CH3^13CN)  
  > "The isotopologue ratio / is taken as" (One-layer model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Derived tau_dust compared with continuum emission  
  > "The derived $	au_{m dust}$ from the line fit is quite similar to the continuum emission, supporting that the dust optical depths are reasonably estimated." (One-layer model)
5. **two-layer LTE fit for double-peaked spectra**: Two-layer (front/rear) LTE model with dust absorption, 12 free parameters; MCMC at 41 grid positions to set initial guesses interpolated into a map, then fit throughout emitting region  
  > "We first perform Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling of a grid of positions in the emitting regions (in total 41 positions with gaps around emission of roughly beam size)." (Two-layer model); "These solutions at each points in the grid are then interpolated to a map of initial guesses for the fitting." (Two-layer model)
   - parameter: 12C/13C prior upper boundary 80  
  > "for which the sample reaches its upper boundary of 80 in the prior." (Degeneracy and model selection)
   - parameter: Initial guess: blue excess fit as infall, red excess as outward motion  
  > "In this work, we set the initial guess to fit the spectra with blue excess using infall and with red excess using outward motion as the classical picture." (Degeneracy and model selection)
   - assumption: Two layers along line of sight, each with own T_ex, tau_dust, N_tot, V_LSR, linewidth, isotopologue ratio  
  > "Each layer has its own excitation temperature" (Two-layer model)
   - fitting: MCMC with tuned prior ranges; smoothness between neighbouring positions  
  > "We tune the sampling boundary (prior range) of the MCMC fitting and find the most reasonable solution." (Two-layer model)
   - validation: Corner plots inspected for degeneracies  
  > "A correlation between the $	au_{m dust, 1}$ and $	au_{m dust, 2}$ from the two layers is shown." (Degeneracy and model selection)
6. **select one- vs two-layer model per pixel**: Two-layer used only if centroid velocity difference exceeds both layers' dispersions; AIC map and dynesty Bayesian evidence at 41 positions as cross-check  
  > "We use a criterion for the two layer-model: the difference of the centroid velocities" (Clustering into kinematic components); "we have also calculated Bayesian evidence with nested sampling algorithm (cite: sk04) using Dynesty (cite: sp20; ko22) toward the 41 grid positions" (Degeneracy and model selection)

   - parameter: |δV_relative| > σ of both layers  
  > "As a result, we use the criteria that $|\delta V_{m relative}|$ is larger than the velocity dispersion from both layers." (Degeneracy and model selection)
   - fitting: AIC = 2k + χ2 + C; nested sampling Bayesian evidence  
  > "The low $\Delta AIC$ would be in favor for two-layer model." (Degeneracy and model selection)
   - validation: Bayesian evidence roughly consistent with AIC  
  > "The result is roughly consistent with AIC method favor two-layer model especially for the component II region." (Degeneracy and model selection)
7. **cluster into kinematic components**: DBSCAN on position-position-velocity points; discarded points revert to one-layer component I

## Qualitative findings

- CH3CN peaks toward VLA4A while CH3^13CN peaks between the protostars; ring-like emission around VLA4B interpreted as dust absorption  
  > "However, the is the brightest towards the secondary protostar VLA4A while its isotopologue is the brightest in between the two protostars." (Summary); "For SVS13A VLA4B we favor the high dust optical depth scenario" (Results)
- Inhomogeneous temperature suggests shock heating from infalling streamer in addition to irradiation  
  > "The inhomogenous distribution of the temperature suggests that in addition to irradiation from the outbursting protostar VLA4A, there is also heating from shocks possibly induced by an infalling streamer." (Summary)
- Component II not directly associated with the dusty spiral given its ~9 km/s velocity  
  > "This implies that the component II from the emission is not directly associated with the dusty spiral." (Kinematic structures)
- No gas disk detected around VLA4B; thin-disk model broadly describes VLA4A disk  
  > "the model can broadly describe the VLA4A disk while in VLA4B there is no detection of a gas disk" (Kinematic structures)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Previous CH3CN emission unresolved; NOEMA COM data suggested multiple structures at tens of au  
  > "However, the emission had not been spatially resolved." (Introduction); "indicated that multiple structures should exist at tens of au scales based on multiple velocity components." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Double-peak profiles persist at resolved scales, indicating two components along the line of sight  
  > "the double peak spectral profiles are still present in particular regions, indicating two components along the line of sight. Thus, we conduct the two-layer LTE model to interpret these spectra." (Two-layer model)
- Alternative: LTE model without dust absorption rejected  
  > "We note that an LTE model without considering dust absorption was also tried but failed to interpret the line spectrum" (One-layer model)
- Alternative: Symmetric two-layer model (same σ and N_tot) insufficient  
  > "We find that a symmetric model, i.e., the same velocity dispersion $\sigma$ and $N_{m tot}$ for the two layers, is insufficient for some regions" (Two-layer model)
- Alternative: COM destruction by UV/X-rays considered for VLA4B ring, high dust optical depth favored  
  > "This can be interpreted either as due to the high optical depths of the dust or due to destruction of COMs in the inner disk via strong UV or X-ray radiation" (Results)
- Alternative: Two-layer model favoured by AIC more widely, but not adopted where no clear double peak  
  > "the one-layer model is chosen in case where no clear double peak is presented." (Clustering into kinematic components)
- How they decided it was enough: Conservative: one-layer used unless double peak clearly seen, given only eight energy levels  
  > "we decide to be more conservative, using one-layer model for positions without a clear double peak." (Clustering into kinematic components)
- Limitation (authors): Infall vs expansion degenerate in two-layer fits  
  > "As a result, the relative velocity can be obtained but it is difficult to determine an infalling or outward motion." (Degeneracy and model selection)
- Limitation (authors): Degeneracy between tau_dust,1 and tau_dust,2  
  > "Likely, the model shows degeneracy between $	au_{m dust, 1}$ and $	au_{m dust, 2}$ while the summation of these two values might be reasonable." (Degeneracy and model selection)
- Limitation (authors): Isotopologue ratio unconstrained at low CH3^13CN S/N  
  > "This is also expected toward the region with low S/N in" (Degeneracy and model selection)
