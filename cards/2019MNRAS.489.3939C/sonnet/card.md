## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image continuum and cubes**: CASA calibration; tclean natural weighting; uvcontsub continuum subtraction  
  > "The datacubes have been generated from the continuum-subtracted uv-datasets." (3 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit continuum size in uv-plane**: Sersic profile (n=1) fit to visibilities with GALARIO and emcee  
  > "We adopt a Sérsic radial profile (with fixed index $n=1$) as a model for the continuum brightness and we assume axisymmetry to produce a 2D model image." (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - software: GALARIO, emcee, uvplot
   - parameter: 40 walkers; priors p(log Ie)=U(-5,1), p(log Re)=U(-2,0), p(i)=U(0,90) deg, p(PA)=U(180,360) deg  
  > "we define the parameter ranges explored by the 40 walkers in the MCMC ensemble sampler." (8 Visibility analysis)
   - fitting: Gaussian likelihood exp(-chi2/2), uniform priors, MCMC with emcee  
  > "We assume uniform priors on the free parameters and a a Gaussian likelihood $\mathcal L \propto \exp(-\chi^2/2)$" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - validation: uv-plots, residual visibility CLEAN images, consistency with imfit and uvmodelfit  
  > "consistent with the flux densities and de-convolved size estimate measured both in the image plane by using the CASA task `imfit` and in the uv-plane by using the CASA task `uvmodelfit`." (4.1 Continuum emission)
3. **Fit dust SED for Mdust, Tdust, beta, LFIR**: CMB-corrected modified blackbody with finite optical depth, MCMC over Mdust, Tdust, beta  
  > "explore the three free parameters, $M_{\rm dust}$, $T_{\rm dust}$, and $\beta$, by using a MCMC algorithm" (4.2 FIR luminosities)
   - parameter: uniform priors, 30 K<Tdust<90 K, 1.0<beta<2.0; 50 chains, 3000 trials  
  > "we force $30~{\rm K}<T_{\rm dust}<90~{\rm K}$ and $1.0<\beta<2.0$" (4.2 FIR luminosities)
   - parameter: LFIR integrated 8-1000 um rest-frame  
  > "integrating the best-fit models from 8 $\mu$m to 1000 $\mu$m rest-frame" (4.2 FIR luminosities)
   - assumption: k_nu = 0.45 (nu/250 GHz)^beta cm2/g; T0=2.73 K; solid angle from fitted continuum size  
  > "We estimate the solid angle subtended by each galaxy from the continuum emission size inferred in Section" (4.2 FIR luminosities)
   - fitting: MCMC with uniform priors  
  > "We employ uniform distributions for the priors" (4.2 FIR luminosities)
   - validation: Compared with optically thin fit in Appendix  
  > "in Appendix [9](#app:thin) we report the best-fitting results in the optical thin assumptions" (4.2 FIR luminosities)
4. **Measure CO line fluxes and upper limits**: Integrate cubes; 3 sigma upper limits in 1.4''x1.4'' aperture with width 400 (J2310) or 500 (J1319) km/s  
  > "Upper limits are estimated in a aperture of $1.4\ensuremath{''}\times1.4\ensuremath{''}$, and spectral width of $\Delta v = 400$ ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$ for J2310" (Table 1)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Blind line search for OH+/CO emission, no >2 sigma emission  
  > "we do not find any emission with an intensity $>2\sigma$" (5.1)
5. **Build and compare CO SLEDs and interpret with PDR/XDR models**: CO(6-5)- and LFIR-normalised SLEDs compared with local AGN/starbursts and z~6 quasars; PDR/XDR models for J2310  
  > "We found that a PDR model alone can fairly explain the intensity of the CO lines observed in J2310." (5.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Only CO(8-7) and CO(9-8) detected in J2310; other lines upper limits, consistent with local AGN/starbursts and other z~6 quasars except J1148's CO(17-16)  
  > "Only CO(8-7) and CO(9-8) in J2310 are detected, while $3\sigma$ upper limits on luminosities are reported for the other lines of both quasars." (abstract)
