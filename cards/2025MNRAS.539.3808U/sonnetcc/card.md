## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image continuum and line cubes**: Standard CASA calibration; uvcontsub to split line-free continuum and line-only data; tclean with CLEAN, Briggs robust 0.5, primary-beam correction  
  > "The data calibration and imaging were performed using the standard procedures in" (2.2 ALMA archival data); "The line-free continuum and line-only data were separately imaged using" (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - software: CASA 6.2.1-7, uvcontsub, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "with a Briggs robust weight of 0.5 (cite: 1995AAS...18711202B), favouring sensitivity" (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **measure dust continuum core properties**: 2D Gaussian fit in the image plane with CASA gaussfit to get position, peak intensity, integrated flux and size of each core  
  > "The parameters of the bulk emission of the detected dust continuum objects were extracted using 2-dimensional Gaussian fit in the image plane." (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - software: CASA gaussfit
   - fitting: 2-dimensional Gaussian; priors/likelihood not stated  
  > "The parameters of the bulk emission of the detected dust continuum objects were extracted using 2-dimensional Gaussian fit in the image plane." (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **identify molecular lines and measure line properties**: Spectra extracted within a 0.5 arcsec diameter around each continuum peak; lines identified with MADCUBA SLIM autofit using CDMS/JPL; 2D Gaussian profile fits of lines give intensity and linewidth  
  > "that were extracted within a diameter of 0.5" (3.2 molecular line emission); "The molecular lines were identified using Spectral Line Identification and Modelling" (2.2 ALMA archival data); "Two-dimensional Gaussian profile fitting of the individual lines also provided some spectral line characteristics, like the intensity and linewidth." (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - software: MADCUBA (SLIM), CDMS, JPL catalog, CASA gaussfit
   - parameter: Detection threshold >=3 sigma with rms 4 mJy/beam  
  > "the threshold for line detection is $\geq$ 3$\sigma$ with an rms noise level of 4 mJy beam" (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - parameter: Spectral extraction diameter 0.5 arcsec (0.7 arcsec for CO in MM1a)  
  > "that were extracted within a diameter of 0.5" (3.2 molecular line emission); "The CO (J = 2 $-$ 1) spectrum extracted within a diameter of 0.7" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - assumption: Sources are isolated within the extraction diameter, comparable to the synthesized beam  
  > "the detected sources are well separated from each other and can be considered isolated within the diameter" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits; MADCUBA autofit details not stated  
  > "Two-dimensional Gaussian profile fitting of the individual lines" (2.2 ALMA archival data)
   - validation: Sensitivity ruled out as the cause of spectral diversity because weak lines were detected at all positions  
  > "Since the observations were very sensitive in detecting all the lines (including the weak lines) at all the locations, sensitivity problems could be ruled out as a potential possibility." (4.1 Chemical evolution of cores)
4. **derive CH3OH excitation temperatures**: Rotational diagram of CH3OH lines (v_t = 0-2) assuming LTE for MM1a, MM1b, MM1c  
  > "under the assumption of local thermodynamic equilibrium (cite: 1999ApJ...517..209G)" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - parameter: E_up range 46-508 K (MM1a, MM1b) and 46-190 K (MM1c)  
  > "upper energies ranging from 46 to 508 K (for MM1a and MM1b) and 46 to 190 K (for MM1c)" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - assumption: LTE  
  > "under the assumption of local thermodynamic equilibrium (cite: 1999ApJ...517..209G)" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **estimate source distance**: Near kinematic distance from V_LSR of the unblended CH3OH 10_{2,8}-9_{3,7} line, using the revised kinematic distance estimation  
  > "Based on the revised kinematic distance estimation of (cite: 2014ApJ...783..130R), we calculated a near kinematic distance of 2.8 kpc to the source, using" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - parameter: V_LSR = -42.6 km/s from Gaussian fit  
  > "systemic velocity) of $-$42.6 kms$^{-1}$ obtained from Gaussian fit to the CH" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - assumption: Near kinematic distance solution  
  > "we calculated a near kinematic distance of 2.8 kpc to the source" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to the line for V_LSR  
  > "obtained from Gaussian fit to the CH" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **derive core masses and beam-averaged H2 column densities**: Optically thin dust mass M_d = S_nu D^2 / (kappa_nu B_nu(T_d)) at ~230 GHz; column density from beam-averaged flux with gas-to-dust 100; T_d = CH3OH T_ex for MM1a-c and 20 K for MM2, MM3  
  > "taking into account optically thin dust emission (cite: 1983QJRAS..24..267H)" (3.1 continuum emission); "Using a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100, the mass of the various cores" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - parameter: kappa = 0.19 cm^2/g at 230 GHz  
  > "at 230 GHz is assumed to be = 0.19 cm" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - parameter: Distance 2.8 kpc  
  > "We adopted this recent near kinematic distance of 2.8 kpc to the source in this work." (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust ratio 100  
  > "is the ratio of gas-to-dust (100)" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - assumption: Mean molecular weight per H2 = 2.8  
  > "is the mean molecular weight per hydrogen molecule and is equal to 2.8" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - assumption: T_d for MM2 and MM3 = 20 K; T_ex used as T_d for MM1a-c  
  > "we assumed a dust temperature of 20 K (cite: 2006A&A...450..607W; 2014ApJ...790...84L) for both cores" (3.2 molecular line emission); "used as the dust temperature" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Column densities compared with a survey of HMSFRs and judged comparable  
  > "the obtained values are comparable to the values reported by (cite: 2021A&A...648A..66G) for large survey of HMSFRs" (4.1 Chemical evolution of cores)
7. **derive ionizing photon rate from MeerKAT free-free flux**: 2D Gaussian fit of the MeerKAT continuum (CASA gaussfit) for flux; ionizing photon rate formula with T_e = 6343 K; spectral type inferred  
  > "were obtained from 2-dimensional Gaussian fit of the radio continuum using" (2.1 MeerKAT archival data); "Using a radio integrated continuum flux of 8.61 mJy, derived from 2-dimensional Gaussian fit of the radio continuum and adopted electron temperature of 6343 K (cite: 2022A&A...664A.140K)" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - software: CASA gaussfit
   - parameter: T_e = 6343 K; D = 2.8 kpc; S_nu = 8.61 mJy  
  > "adopted electron temperature of 6343 K (cite: 2022A&A...664A.140K)" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - assumption: Free-free emission from an HII region ionized by a single star  
  > "This is an indication that the free-free emission is likely ionized by a spectral type B1 (III) star" (3.1 continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **test for a rotating structure with CH3OH moment maps**: Moment 0, 1 and 2 maps of CH3OH 10_{2,8}-9_{3,7}; 2D Gaussian size of the moment-0 emission; spectrum within 0.52 arcsec diameter  
  > "shows a distinct velocity gradient, with blue- and red-shifted emission, respectively, distributed east and west of MM1a continuum peak position" (4.2 Presence of rotating structure); "the extent of the emission obtained from 2-dimentional Gaussian fit with" (4.2 Presence of rotating structure)
   - software: CASA gaussfit
   - parameter: Spectrum extracted within a 0.52 arcsec diameter  
  > "The CH$_{3}$OH spectrum extracted within a diameter of 0.52" (4.2 Presence of rotating structure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Interpretation compared with envelope/disk velocity-gradient diagnostics from earlier works (qualitative)  
  > "spatially more extended molecular lines with a less steep velocity gradient trace the envelope" (4.2 Presence of rotating structure)
9. **characterize CO outflow and its dynamical timescale**: CO 2-1 emission integrated over blue- and red-shifted velocity ranges and overlaid on the continuum; t_d = R_max / Delta v_max, averaged over lobes  
  > "The CO emission within the velocity ranges of the blue- and red-shifted peaks were extracted and imaged as blue- and red-shifted emission." (4.3 Outflow); "is the maximum size of the outflow measured in the integrated map" (4.3 Outflow)
   - parameter: Blue lobe -51.8 to -46.4 km/s; red lobe -36.2 to -27.3 km/s  
  > "The velocity range of the blue-shifted peak is from $-$51.8 to $-$46.4 kms" (3.2 molecular line emission); "that of the red-shifted peak is from $-$36.2 to $-$27.3 kms" (3.2 molecular line emission)
   - parameter: Outflow inclination ~46 deg to the plane of the sky  
  > "The inclination of the outflow is $\sim 46^\circ$ with respect to the plane of the sky." (4.3 Outflow)
   - assumption: MM1a is the driving source of the outflow  
  > "we suggest that the possible driving source of the outflow is likely MM1a" (4.3 Outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Size and timescale compared with outflow surveys and simulations  
  > "The size and dynamical timescale limit of the outflow are consistent with large-scale surveys" (4.3 Outflow)

## Qualitative findings

- MM1a, MM1b and MM1c show line forests of complex oxygen-rich organics and are in the hot molecular core phase; MM2 and MM3 are in the HMPO phase  
  > "Based on (cite: 2014A&A...563A..97G) classification scheme, MM1a, MM1b and MM1c are in their HMCs phase." (4.1 Chemical evolution of cores)
- Dust continuum peaks coincide with the weaker MeerKAT peak, not the strongest  
  > "The dust continuum peaks do not coincide with the strongest radio continuum peak previously detected in the region in a MeerKAT observation" (abstract)
- CO profile at MM1a shows double peaks and absorption; bipolar outflow with red lobe NE and blue lobe SW  
  > "The bipolar outflow is distributed northeast (red-shifted lobe) and southwest (blue-shifted lobe) of MM1a." (4.3 Outflow)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High resolution and sensitivity ALMA continuum and line data are needed to resolve the cores and their kinematics; earlier work used low-resolution survey data  
  > "High resolution and sensitivity ALMA continuum and spectral lines observations provide a great opportunity to uncover the fine structure of G328.24" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: CH3OH used for the rotational diagram because it has equal numbers of transitions in MM1a and MM1b and many in MM1c  
  > "The choice of using CH$_3$OH line for the rotational diagram analysis is due to the fact that equal number of CH$_3$OH transitions were observed in MM1a" (3.2 molecular line emission)
- Alternative: Spectral diversity could also arise from observational biases, grain-mantle composition differences or excitation; sensitivity bias was ruled out and excitation favoured  
  > "The diversity in millimeter spectra from the several cores could also be the result of other factors, including observational biases" (4.1 Chemical evolution of cores)
- Alternative: Positional offsets between ATCA, Spitzer and ALMA peaks attributed to absolute astrometric accuracy rather than physical offsets  
  > "appears to be an instrumental effect, likely due to differences in the absolute coordinate accuracy of these observations" (4.1 Chemical evolution of cores)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No excitation temperature for MM2 and MM3 (too few CH3OH lines), so their masses and column densities depend on an assumed 20 K  
  > "determining the specific temperatures of MM2 and MM3 will be of great importance in estimating their precise masses and column densities" (3.1 continuum emission)
- Limitation (authors): MM1b may host an undetected disk; its evolutionary status is uncertain  
  > "it may still host a disk that remains undetected due to weak emission or an unfavorable orientation" (4.3 Outflow)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CH3OH T_ex (gas, hot-core, LTE) is used as dust temperature for mass; no uncertainty propagation into masses and T_d error treatment is inconsistent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A single optically thin dust opacity of 0.19 cm^2/g and gas-to-dust 100 with no discussion of free-free or line contamination in the 1.3 mm continuum or of optical depth in the dense cores
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotation claim rests on one moment-1 map of a line with FWHM ~6 km/s at about beam-size scales; no PV diagram, Keplerian fit or comparison with outflow contamination
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line-ID table lists typos (HNCO at 19.737 GHz) and identical linewidths repeated across many lines (7.42 km/s), suggesting fit limits rather than measurements
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Statement that MM1a is the outflow driver is inferred, not established; MM1b/c as drivers not tested
