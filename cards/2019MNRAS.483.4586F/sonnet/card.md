## Analysis recipe

1. **Detect outflow signature in integrated CO spectrum**: Fit single vs double Gaussian; two Gaussians preferred if reduced chi2 drops by >=10 per cent, plus visual check of narrow/broad components  
  > "to a decrease in χ red [2] [of] [10] [per] [cent] [or] [more,] [then] [we] [con-]" (2.2 Identification of Outflows)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: sigma_narrow <~100 km/s; sigma_broad ~100 to several 100 km/s  
  > "a narrow (σ narrow ≲ 100 km s [−][1] ) and a broad component (σ broad ranging from ∼100 km s [−][1] to several 100 km s [−][1]" (2.2 Identification of Outflows)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Verify with pv diagrams and wing maps; wings >5 sigma, velocities >2x narrow width, not along rotation  
  > "The line wings are identified as due to outflows if they have velocities in excess of two times the width of the narrow component and are not located in the direction of rotation." (2.2 Identification of Outflows)
2. **Derive outflow mass, velocity, radius, rate**: Broad-component flux to L'CO then M = alpha_CO L'CO; v_outf = FWHM_broad/2 + |v_broad - v_narrow|; radius = deconvolved major-axis FWHM/2 of 2D Gaussian fit to wing map; Mdot = v M / r  
  > "a 2D-Gaussian profile to the wing map and use the beamdeconvolved major axis (FWHM) divided by two as the radius of the outflow." (2.3 Outflow Properties)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8 Msun/(K km/s pc^2); CO thermalised and optically thick so L'CO(3-2)=L'CO(2-1)=L'CO(1-0)  
  > "we assume that the CO emission is thermalised and optically thick, hence _L_ CO(3 [′] −2) [=] _[L]_ CO(2 [′] −1) [=] _[L]_ CO(1 [′] −0)" (2.3 Outflow Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Average uncertainty on Mdot about 0.3 dex; kinetic power and momentum rate up to 0.5 dex  
  > "we infer that the average uncertainty on the mass outflow rate is about 0.3 dex." (2.3 Outflow Properties)
3. **Homogenise host galaxy properties**: AGN luminosity from X-ray via Marconi+04 bolometric correction, or [OIII], or mid-IR alpha_bol; SFR from LIR (Kennicutt & Evans 2012, AGN-corrected); stellar masses from K/J/H-band with colour M/L (Bell+03); gas masses from CO(1-0) with alpha_CO 0.8/1.2/4.4  
  > "we use the _L_ IR -SFR relation given in Kennicutt & Evans (2012), which assumes a Chabrier IMF" (2.4.3 Star Formation Rate)
   - assumption: alpha_CO: 0.8 ULIRGs, 1.2 LIRGs, 4.4 others  
  > "For ULIRGs, we adopt α CO = 0.8 M ⊙ /(K km s [−][1] pc [2] ), for LIRGs we use α CO = 1.2" (2.4.4 Gas content)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit scaling relations**: Linear regression with linmix including x/y errors and upper limits; multi-parameter fit of Mdot vs SFR, L_AGN, M* minimising dispersion  
  > "fits are performed by using linmix (Kelly 2007), considering the error bars both in x and y and including upper limits." (3.2.1 Dependence on SFR and L_AGN)
   - software: linmix
   - assumption: Candidate fossil outflows excluded from the multi-parameter fit  
  > "We have excluded our candidate fossil outflows as they are expected not to follow a relation with AGN or SFR" (3.2.3)
   - fitting: log Mdot = x log(alpha SFR + beta L_AGN/1e43) + y log(M*/1e11)  
  > "log( _M_ [�] outf ) = x log(αSFR+β _L_ AGN ) + y log _M_ ⋆, (4)" (3.2.3)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Escape fraction from galaxy and halo**: McIntosh+05 size-mass relation, Hernquist profile escape velocity; NFW halo with Moster+13 and Duffy+08; fraction of broad wing above v_esc  
  > "The escape fraction is then defined as the fraction of the outflow that has a velocity higher than the escape velocity." (4.4)
   - assumption: Ballistic motions  
  > "the equations here only hold if we consider ballistic motions." (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Monte Carlo gives error as large as 50 per cent  
  > "The error in the escape fraction is as large as 50 per cent as inferred by running a Monte Carlo simulation" (4.4)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA data include programmes not aimed at extreme AGN/starbursts, giving a less biased sample  
  > "we have included targets from the ALMA archive culled from observing programmes that were not aimed at extreme classes of galaxies" (2.1 Sample Selection)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Momentum-driven scenario probably excluded since outflows are on kpc scales  
  > "the momentum-driven scenario can probably be excluded as the observed outflows are mostly on kpc-scales" (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Sample still biased; not a census; most archival data too shallow to detect wings  
  > "our sample is still heavily biased" (2.1 Sample Selection)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow detection relies on double Gaussian fits to integrated spectra; non-uniform sensitivity biases detections
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO=0.8 and thin-shell geometry are uncertain by factors of a few
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Literature and archival outflows may not be homogeneous in detection thresholds
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fossil outflow interpretation not directly tested; alternatives such as SF-driven or jets not excluded quantitatively
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 7 ALMA detections drive the new data; fits dominated by literature points
