   - parameter: Exclude channels within 3× [OIII] FWHM around line center.  
  > "removing all channels within $3\times$ the [OIII] FWHM around the line center." (ALMA Band 8 Observations)
   - parameter: Taper REBELS-25 to circular 1.1'' and REBELS-38 to circular 1.2''.  
  > "we therefore taper the beam to a circular Gaussian of $1.1\ensuremath{''}$." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission); "For REBELS-38, we apply tapering to obtain a circular beam of $1.2\ensuremath{''}$ in order to better match the Band 6 resolution." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission)

  > "The quoted upper limit is $3\sigma$." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission); "the primary beam sensitivity of the Band 8 map at the location of REBELS-12 is $\approx0.70$." (ALMA Band 8 Observations)
   - fitting: Two-dimensional Gaussian source fits  
  > "finds islands of contiguous emission and fits these with two-dimensional Gaussians to extract their flux densities." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission)
   - validation: REBELS-38 tapered flux agrees with the sum of the two native-resolution components.  
  > "The flux in the tapered map is consistent with the sum of the two individual components as measured in the higher resolution image." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission)
2. **Measure [OIII] and line ratios**: Subtract continuum, iterate line center/FWHM, collapse across FWHM, extract spectra, integrate Gaussian fits and compare with inherited [CII] luminosities.  
  > "We fit the line center and FWHM iteratively until the fit converges to a stable solution, and then create a moment-0 map by collapsing channels across the FWHM to maximize its S/N." (ALMA Band 8 Observations); "We fit the spectra with a Gaussian profile, and determine the total line flux by integrating the area under the Gaussian curve." ([OIII] Emission); "In this work, we adopt the total flux density in the [CII] line as given by Schouws et al. (in prep), integrated over the full linewidth." ([OIII] Emission)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Zeroth-order continuum polynomial; naturally weighted cubes.  
  > "For this, we first run CASA task to subtract any continuum emission, assuming a zeroth-order polynomial." (ALMA Band 8 Observations); "We use natural weighting to produce the cubes" (ALMA Band 8 Observations)
   - parameter: Extract pixels with moment-0 significance ≥2σ within 2.0'' of the peak.  
  > "To extract the 1D spectra, we sum all pixels in the moment-0 map with significance $\geq2\sigma$ within a radius of $2.0\ensuremath{''}$ around the peak pixel." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission)
   - parameter: Set v=0 km s^-1 at the higher-S/N [CII] line center.  
  > "adopting $v=0\,\mathrm{km}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ as the center of the [CII] line, given its higher S/N compared to [OIII]." ([OIII] Emission)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile; integrate area for total flux.  
  > "We fit the spectra with a Gaussian profile, and determine the total line flux by integrating the area under the Gaussian curve." ([OIII] Emission)
   - validation: Results unchanged when [CII] fluxes are re-extracted with the [OIII] procedure.  
  > "our results remain unchanged if we re-extract the [CII] fluxes following the procedure adopted for the [OIII] line described in Section" ([OIII] Emission)
3. **Fit dust temperatures and masses**: Fit CMB-affected modified blackbodies to Band 6 and Band 8 fluxes or upper limits; compare optically thin and thick models.  
  > "we may write the general functional form of a modified blackbody affected by the CMB as" (Modified Blackbody Formalism); "We fit to the measured flux densities where available, and implement upper limits following the procedure described in (cite: sawicki2012) (see also (cite: bakx2020; witstok2022))." (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "We first adopt an optically thin MBB, before considering optically thick dust." (Dust Emission at $z\sim7$)
   - software: Python
   - parameter: 32 walkers; 10^5 steps each; discard first 10^4 steps.  
  > "We execute our MCMC routine with 32 walkers for a total of $10^5\,$steps each, thereby conservatively discarding the first $10^4$ steps as the burn-in phase." (Modified Blackbody Fitting)
   - parameter: Posterior medians; errors are 16th and 84th percentiles.  
  > "we therefore adopt the median of the posterior distributions as the fitted dust temperature, mass and emissivity, while the quoted errors represent the 16th and 84th percentiles of the corresponding posterior." (Modified Blackbody Fitting)
   - assumption: Milky Way opacity κν=κ0(ν/ν0)^β; κ0=10.41 cm² g^-1 at 1900 GHz.  
  > "$\kappa_\nu = \kappa_0 (\nu / \nu_0)^\beta$" (Modified Blackbody Formalism); "We adopt Milky Way dust with $\kappa_0 = \kappa(\nu_0=1900\,\mathrm{GHz}) = 10.41\,\mathrm{cm}^2\mathrm{g}^{-1}$" (Modified Blackbody Formalism)
   - assumption: Fiducial optically thin β=2.0; also fixed β=1.5 and Gaussian β prior.  
  > "we adopt $\beta=2.0$ as our fiducial model" (Dust SED: Optically Thin Scenario); "However, we also use MBB fitting with a fixed value of $\beta = (1.5, 2.0)$." (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "For $\beta$ we adopt a Gaussian prior centered around a mean of $\langle\beta\rangle = 1.8$ with a standard deviation of $\sigma_\beta = 0.5$" (Modified Blackbody Fitting)
   - assumption: Optically thick comparison fixes λ_thick=100 μm.  
  > "Nevertheless, we investigate the effect of allowing for optically thick dust by adopting a fixed $\lambda_\mathrm{thick} = 100\,\mu$m (following e.g., (cite: faisst2020; dacunha2021))." (Dust SED: Optically Thick Scenario)
   - assumption: H0=70 km s^-1 Mpc^-1; Ωm=0.30; ΩΛ=0.70.  
  > "Throughout this work, we assume a standard $\Lambda$CDM cosmology, with $H_0=70\,\text{km\,s}^{-1}\text{\,Mpc}^{-1}$, $\Omega_m=0.30$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.70$." (Introduction)
   - fitting: MCMC; uniform log10(Mdust/M_sun)∈[4,10]; temperature uniform from T_CMB,z to 150 K with σ=30 K Gaussian decrease above; Gaussian β prior 1.8±0.5 when free; Sawicki upper-limit likelihood.  
  > "we introduce a simple uniform prior of $\log_{10}\left(M_\mathrm{dust}/M_\odot\right) \in [4, 10]$." (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "we adopt a uniform prior between $T_{\mathrm{CMB},z}$ and $T_\mathrm{dust,max}=150\,$K, while beyond $T_\mathrm{dust,max}$ we smoothly decrease the prior probability by a Gaussian with a width of $\sigma=30\,$K." (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "For $\beta$ we adopt a Gaussian prior centered around a mean of $\langle\beta\rangle = 1.8$ with a standard deviation of $\sigma_\beta = 0.5$" (Modified Blackbody Fitting)
   - validation: Samples exceed 50× autocorrelation time; mean acceptance fractions 0.33 fixed β and 0.22 free β; inspect trace plots.  
  > "We confirm that the number of samples utilized in the MCMC fitting is greater than $50\times$ the autocorrelation time to ensure sufficient sampling of the parameter space" (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "averaging $0.33$ across the three REBELS sources when $\beta$ is fixed ($1.5, 2.0$), and $0.22$ when beta is included in the fitting" (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "through the visual inspection of trace plots for $M_\mathrm{dust}, T_\mathrm{dust}$ and $\beta$." (Modified Blackbody Fitting)
4. **Derive luminosities and dust enrichment**: Integrate posterior MBBs over 8–1000 μm; convert L_IR to obscured SFR; derive obscured fractions and dust-to-stellar mass ratios; compare dust-production models.  
  > "By propagating the full posterior distributions of the various MBB parameters determined through MCMC, we compute the infrared luminosities of our targets by integrating the sampled MBBs between rest-frame $8-1000\,\mu$m." (Dust SED: Optically Thin Scenario); "We convert the measured IR luminosities to dust-obscured star formation rates via $\mathrm{SFR}_\mathrm{IR}/(M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}) = 1.2\times10^{-10} \times (L_\mathrm{IR}/L_\odot)$ (cite: bouwens2022)." ## Qualitative findings

- REBELS-25 and REBELS-38 have cold dust under the fiducial optically thin β=2.0 model.  
  > "Through modified blackbody fitting we determine cold dust temperatures ($T_\mathrm{dust} \approx 30 - 35\,$K) in both of the dual-band detected targets, given a fiducial model of optically thin emission with $\beta = 2.0$." (Abstract)
- The large dust masses remain consistent with early-Universe dust-production models.  
  > "Nevertheless, these large dust masses are still consistent with predictions from models of dust production in the early Universe." (Abstract)
- Low [OIII]/[CII] ratios suggest weaker ionizing fields and less bursty star formation.  
  > "We argue the lower line ratios are due to a comparatively weaker ionizing radiation field resulting from the less starbursty nature of our targets." (Abstract)
- REBELS-38 contains two dusty clumps; a late-stage merger remains possible.  
  > "it is likely that the two dusty clumps are individual components of a singular galaxy, although we note the possibility that REBELS-38 could be a late-stage merger." (The Dust Morphology of REBELS-38)
- REBELS-12 may be a merger; its high optical-line EW conflicts with the total-system low line ratio.  
  > "the EW of REBELS-12 implies significant bursty star formation, in apparent contrast with its observed [OIII]/[CII] line ratio." (An Evolved Star-forming Population at Redshift 7); "The broad [CII] profile may indicate that REBELS-12 is an ongoing merger" (An Evolved Star-forming Population at Redshift 7)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Multi-band continuum constrains dust temperatures; the sample includes the two brightest Band 6 sources.  
  > "Accurately measuring dust properties observationally, however, requires multi-band continuum photometry." (Introduction); "This sample includes the two brightest Band 6 continuum sources, REBELS-25" (ALMA Band 8 Observations)
- Why this method: Natural weighting optimizes sensitivity; tapering matches Band 6 resolution; fiducial β=2.0 maintains REBELS consistency.  
  > "Continuum imaging was performed using `TCLEAN` with natural weighting to optimize sensitivity" (ALMA Band 8 Observations); "We additionally create tapered maps of REBELS-25 and REBELS-38 in order to match the Band 6 resolution and more fairly compare the continuum flux densities across wavelength." (Identification of Continuum and [OIII] Emission); "In what follows, we adopt $\beta=2.0$ as our fiducial model, to ensure consistency with the value assumed by (cite: sommovigo2022), and the series of REBELS papers in general." (Dust SED: Optically Thin Scenario)
- Alternative: Test fixed β=1.5 and free β; emissivity remains unconstrained.  
  > "However, we also use MBB fitting with a fixed value of $\beta = (1.5, 2.0)$." (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "The value of $\beta$ itself is not constrained" (Dust SED: Optically Thin Scenario)
- Alternative: Test optically thick dust at λ_thick=100 μm; expected λ_thick≲40 μm favors the thin interpretation at observed rest wavelengths.  
  > "With these estimates, we expect $\lambda_\mathrm{thick} \lesssim 40\,\mu$m to be a sensible upper limit on the wavelength where the dust transitions to optically thick." (Dust SED: Optically Thick Scenario); "Nevertheless, we investigate the effect of allowing for optically thick dust by adopting a fixed $\lambda_\mathrm{thick} = 100\,\mu$m (following e.g., (cite: faisst2020; dacunha2021))." (Dust SED: Optically Thick Scenario)
- Alternative: Ionization parameter and PDR covering fraction are degenerate; analysis focuses on the former.  
  > "While in practice there is a degeneracy between a lower/higher ionization parameter and higher/lower PDR covering fraction, we here focus on the former scenario." ([OIII]/[CII] as a proxy for Burstiness)
- Alternative: REBELS-12 broad [CII] could indicate a merger or disk rotation.  
  > "indicating that REBELS-12 may be a merger, although it could also be a signature of disk rotation (e.g., (cite: kohandel2019))." ([OIII] Emission)
- Alternative: Spatial segregation of dust and star formation could also lower dust temperatures.  
  > "A low dust temperature, on the other hand, could therefore also be the result of spatial segregation between the sites of star formation and the dust." (An Evolved Star-forming Population at Redshift 7)
- How they decided it was enough: Iterative line fits reach a stable solution; MCMC sampling exceeds 50× autocorrelation time and is checked with acceptance fractions and trace plots.  
  > "We fit the line center and FWHM iteratively until the fit converges to a stable solution" (ALMA Band 8 Observations); "We confirm that the number of samples utilized in the MCMC fitting is greater than $50\times$ the autocorrelation time to ensure sufficient sampling of the parameter space" (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "through the visual inspection of trace plots for $M_\mathrm{dust}, T_\mathrm{dust}$ and $\beta$." (Modified Blackbody Fitting)
- Limitation (authors): Only two continuum wavelengths; β and λ_thick cannot be directly constrained.  
  > "we cannot robustly constrain $\beta$ due to a lack of sampling of the dust SED" (Modified Blackbody Fitting); "we cannot fit $\lambda_\mathrm{thick}$ directly given that the dust SED is sampled at only two distinct wavelengths." (Dust SED: Optically Thick Scenario)
- Limitation (authors): REBELS-12 temperature is prior-sensitive because of its low-S/N Band 6 detection and Band 8 upper limit.  
  > "While the Band 8 upper limit is relatively constraining, the main uncertainty in our analysis is at present the S/N of the Band 6 data." (Dust SED: Optically Thin Scenario); "fitted dust temperatures will be quite sensitive to the adopted prior on the dust temperature." (Dust Temperatures at High Redshift)
- Limitation (authors): REBELS-12 dust association and kinematics remain ambiguous.  
  > "it remains unclear if both dust components are physically associated to REBELS-12, or whether the brighter one corresponds to a foreground object." ([OIII]/[CII] as a proxy for Burstiness); "Clearly, deeper and higher resolution ALMA observations of REBELS-12 are required to better understand the nature of its [CII] and dust emission." ([OIII] Emission)
- Limitation (authors): Size measurements have moderate resolution; several sizes used by GLAM are assumed.  
  > "Given the moderate resolution of our data, we further assume a fiducial $30\%$ uncertainty on all of the size measurements" ([OIII]/[CII] as a proxy for Burstiness); "we adopt an identical size for the unresolved [OIII] emission." ([OIII]/[CII] as a proxy for Burstiness)
- Limitation (authors): Dust geometry can affect temperature and radiative-equilibrium modelling.  
  > "the spatial distribution of dust within the galaxy is expected to additionally affect the measured dust temperatures." (An Evolved Star-forming Population at Redshift 7); "show evidence for spatial offsets between their dust and UV emission (cite: inami2022), which introduces additional uncertainty in the applicability of dust temperature models based upon radiative equilibrium" (Comparison to (cite: fudamoto2022b))
- Limitation (authors): Three selected galaxies cannot establish how common the inferred evolved population is.  
  > "Further dual/multi-band ALMA observations targeting a larger number of $z\sim7$ galaxies are required to elucidate whether this relatively evolved evolutionary state may indeed be common in high-redshift sources" (Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cold temperatures and large dust masses depend on the fixed opacity normalization, emissivity and optical-depth model.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The selected bright-continuum sample is too small to infer population prevalence.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): GLAM burstiness depends on assumed unresolved sizes and the interpretation of total-system line emission.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No [OIII] measurement exists for REBELS-38; its burstiness is assumed in the single-band re-analysis.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The REBELS-12 red-component ratio has inconsistent lower uncertainties: -1.1 in the discussion and -1.0 in the appendix.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Two-band posterior medians are reproducible more directly than the broader evolutionary interpretation.
