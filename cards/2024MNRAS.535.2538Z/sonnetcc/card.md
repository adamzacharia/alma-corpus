## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit stellar kinematics in MUSE cubes**: Penalized pixel fitting with pPXF on Voronoi-binned spectra  
  > "we use the Penalized PiXel-Fitting method" (3.1 Stellar kinematics)
   - software: pPXF
   - parameter: MILES stellar library templates  
  > "The full MILES stellar library was used to provide templates" (3.1 Stellar kinematics)
   - parameter: Fit wavelength range 4750 - 5850 A  
  > "The pPXF fit was limited to a wavelength range of $4750 - 5850$ Å" (3.1 Stellar kinematics)
   - parameter: Voronoi binning to fixed S/N of 50  
  > "the spaxels were Voronoi-binned (cite: Cappellari2003) to a fixed $S/N$ of 50" (3.1 Stellar kinematics)
   - parameter: Additive polynomial correction of order 10  
  > "an additional additive polynomial correction of order 10 was used" (3.1 Stellar kinematics)
   - fitting: Maximum penalised likelihood (pPXF)  
  > "which uses a maximum penalised likelihood approach" (3.1 Stellar kinematics)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit emission lines and set detection threshold**: Spaxel-by-spaxel emission-line fitting with GANDALF; stellar velocity of the Voronoi bin adopted; reddening from Balmer decrement  
  > "were fitted on a spaxel-by-spaxel basis using Gas AND Absorption Line Fitting" (3.3 Ionised gas measurements); "For each spaxel the stellar velocity of the corresponding Voronoi bin was adopted." (3.3 Ionised gas measurements)
   - software: GANDALF
   - parameter: Maps restricted to regions where A/N exceeds the 99% false-positive threshold for each line  
  > "We then restricted our maps to regions where for each line the A/N values exceeded their corresponding 99% false-positive threshold." (3.3 Ionised gas measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Check ionisation source with BPT and WHaD diagrams**: BPT diagnostic per pixel with Kauffmann2003, Kewley2001 and Schawinski2007 boundaries; WHaD diagram from Sanchez2024 as additional test  
  > "We use the boundary line from (cite: Kauffmann2003) as a limit for a pixel to be considered star-forming" (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams); "we use the recently developed method by (cite: Sanchez2024) as an additional test to ensure the emission is indeed star forming." (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams)
   - parameter: WHaD AGN-like boundary at Halpha velocity dispersion ~55 km/s (from Sanchez2024)  
  > "The boundary line for AGN-like emission occurs at a velocity dispersion of $\sigma_{\text{H}\alpha}\sim 55\ \text{km s}^{-1}$." (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: In FCC332 and FCC335 non-SF ionisation is significant, so SFR is treated as an upper limit  
  > "sources other than star formation are responsible for ionising a significant amount of the ionised gas." (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams)
4. **Derive gas-phase metallicities**: Dopita2016 calibration from [NII]/[SII] and [NII]/Halpha; global value is Halpha-flux-weighted average of the map; FCC335 uses the mass-metallicity relation instead  
  > "We derive this abundance using the calibration from (cite: Dopita2016):" (3.5 Gas-phase metallicities); "we rely on the mass-metallicity relation from (cite: Sanchez2017), using the calibration from (cite: Pettini2004), to estimate its global metallicity" (3.5 Gas-phase metallicities)
   - parameter: Spaxels weighted by de-reddened Halpha flux and averaged for global value  
  > "the spaxels were weighted by the de-reddened H$\alpha$ flux and averaged" (3.5 Gas-phase metallicities)
   - assumption: For FCC332 only spaxels with detected star formation emission are used  
  > "for FCC332 we have estimated the oxygen abundance based on spaxels with detected star formation emission only" (3.5 Gas-phase metallicities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive HI and H2 masses and deficiencies**: HI masses adopted from the MFS (41 arcsec cubes); H2 masses from integrated CO flux with metallicity-dependent XCO (Accurso2017); deficiencies relative to xGASS (HI) and xCOLD GASS (H2) control-sample medians  
  > "We adopt HI masses from the MFS, which were derived using the standard equation:" (3.6.1 HI masses); "Following past work, we use the metallicity-dependent conversion factor from (cite: Accurso2017)" (3.6.2 H2 masses); "Here we use xGASS as a reference sample for the expected HI mass." (3.6.3 HI deficiency)
   - parameter: Distance to the Fornax cluster 19.95 Mpc for all galaxies  
  > "Throughout the paper we adopt the distance to the Fornax cluster (19.95 Mpc, (cite: Tonry2001)) as a common distance to all galaxies in the sample." (Introduction)
   - parameter: Expected mass from 10 stellar-mass bins with a shift of half a bin  
  > "We divide the sample into 10 stellar mass bins and use a shift of half a bin." (3.6.3 HI deficiency)
   - parameter: SFMS reference from Saintonge2017 used for Delta(MS) in the XCO prescription  
  > "for which we adopt the main sequence from (cite: Saintonge2017) as a reference." (3.6.2 H2 masses)
   - assumption: Galaxies at the cluster distance (about 20 Mpc) for HI masses  
  > "here assumed to be equal to the the distance to the Fornax cluster" (3.6.1 HI masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Build resolved molecular KS relation**: Convert CO intensity maps to Sigma_H2 and Halpha maps (Calzetti2012 SFR calibration, dereddened) to Sigma_SFR; convolve and regrid Halpha to the CO beam; each Sigma_H2 pixel paired with the median Sigma_SFR in its beam-size neighbourhood  
  > "In order to calculate the rKS relation, we convert the CO intensity maps to" (3.7.1 Molecular gas surface density); "In order to convert our dereddened H$\alpha$ fluxes to SFRs, we use the following equation from (cite: Calzetti2012)" (3.7.2 Star formation rate surface density); "we convolve and regrid the dereddened H$\alpha$ maps to match the resolution of the CO maps" (3.7.2 Star formation rate surface density)
   - parameter: 0.5 arcsec pixels smaller than the ALMA beam; pixels not independent, handled via Zabel2020 approach  
  > "we adopt the approach of (cite: Zabel2020)" (3.7.2 Star formation rate surface density)
   - assumption: Same XCO and constants as for global H2 masses; distance to Fornax (20 Mpc) for Halpha luminosity  
  > "The remaining parameters are identical to those described in" (3.7.1 Molecular gas surface density)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with literature relations (Kennicutt1998, Bigiel2008, Donaire2023) and Fornax comparison galaxies  
  > "the rKS relation from (cite: Bigiel2008) as a blue line, and three different rKS relations for the Virgo cluster from (cite: Donaire2023)" (5.4 The resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation)
7. **Make molecular depletion time maps**: Divide Sigma_H2 map by Sigma_SFR map; fill undetected CO regions with the lowest detected Sigma_H2 and undetected Halpha regions with the lowest Sigma_SFR to give limits  
  > "Here we have done so by setting the molecular gas surface density in these regions equal to the lowest detected value in the galaxy." (3.8 Depletion time maps); "We set the SFR surface density in these areas equal to the lowest value measured." (3.8 Depletion time maps)
   - assumption: Halpha and CO are assumed co-spatial everywhere, CO undetected because column density is below detection limit  
  > "If we assume that H$\alpha$ and CO are co-spatial everywhere in the galaxies" (3.8 Depletion time maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare HI and CO kinematics with position-velocity diagrams**: Position-velocity diagrams of HI and H2 along the major axis of the HI emission  
  > "we create position-velocity diagrams (PVDs) of the HI and H$_2$ in FCC090 and the other objects in the sample, along the major axis of the HI emission" (5.2.1 FCC090)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- FCC207 has a relatively relaxed but truncated ISM and is the only sample galaxy close to the cluster centre in projection and phase space  
  > "The exception is FCC207, whose ISM is more settled and more truncated than in the other objects." (Summary)
- Ionisation in FCC332 and FCC335 is dominated by sources other than star formation, so their SFR and Sigma_SFR are upper limits  
  > "sources other than star formation are responsible for ionising a significant amount of the ionised gas." (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams)
- Dwarfs with HI features are generally offset from galaxy centre and the three gas phases are spatially and kinematically decoupled  
  > "they tend to be decoupled both spatially and in velocity" (Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Multi-wavelength follow-up (MUSE, MeerKAT) to add morphology and kinematics of atomic and ionised gas and the stellar component to the CO data; highest-resolution HI cubes chosen to match MUSE and ALMA  
  > "These additional datasets provide information on the morphology and kinematics of the atomic and ionised gas, as well as the stellar distribution and kinematics." (Introduction); "in order to match the MUSE and ALMA data as closely as possible, we use the highest-resolution version of the data cubes available" (2.2 HI data from MeerKAT)
- Why this method: The resolved molecular KS relation is chosen because the KS relation depends much more strongly on H2 than HI  
  > "Therefore, here we will derive the resolved, molecular KS relation (hereafter rKS)" (3.7 The resolved Kennicutt-Schmidt relation)
- Alternative: Ram pressure stripping rejected as sole explanation for the HI velocity offset in FCC090 (offset opposite to the expected RPS direction); recent interaction favoured  
  > "Therefore, the most likely explanation is that this was caused by a recent interaction with another galaxy." (5.2.1 FCC090)
- Alternative: BPT-only classification of ionisation questioned; WHaD diagram used as an additional test  
  > "However, the use of BPT diagrams only to determine the source of ionisation has been questioned by many studies" (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Non-SF ionisation in FCC332 and FCC335 means SFR, Sigma_SFR and depletion times are only limits  
  > "sources other than star formation are responsible for ionising a significant amount of the ionised gas." (3.4 Baldwin, Phillips & Terlevich diagrams)
- Limitation (authors): Stellar masses of comparison samples with different IMFs are not corrected, assumed negligible  
  > "in practise the difference between Kroupa/Chabrier is small compared to the uncertanties from other sources, making such corrections negligible." (4.2 Stellar masses & star formation rates)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Common distance of 19.95 Mpc is assumed for all galaxies, so no individual distance uncertainty enters masses or surface densities
- Caveat (inferred, ours): XCO depends on global Halpha-weighted metallicity and Delta(MS), with FCC335 metallicity from a scaling relation rather than a direct measurement
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Halpha-based SFRs in FCC332 and FCC335 are upper limits so rKS positions for them are biased high
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample of six hand-picked galaxies with the most disturbed CO, no control for selection effects, and first-infall claim is inferred, not tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA data processing is not described, so CO flux and moment maps depend on an earlier paper
