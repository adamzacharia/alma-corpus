- Question: What is the spatially resolved relationship between the dust-to-gas mass ratio (DGR) and gas-phase metallicity (Z_gas) across a broad metallicity range in nearby galaxies at (sub-)kpc scales?  
  > "We present the spatially resolved relationship between the dust-to-gas mass ratio (DGR) and gas-phase metallicity ( 𝑍 gas or 12+log(O/H)) (i.e., DGR– 𝑍 gas relation) of 11 nearby galaxies with a large metallicity range (1.5 dex of 12+log(O/H)) at (sub-)kpc scales." ## Qualitative findings

- Global DGR–Z_gas relation of the 11 TYPHOON galaxies agrees well with literature trends within 1 sigma, except for NGC 1512 and Sextans A.  
  > "The global DGR– 𝑍 gas relationship for TYPHOON galaxies aligns well with the literature trend, falling within 1 𝜎 (see circles for a fair comparison in terms of the same calibrations used)." (4.1 The global DGR–𝑍gas relation)
- Intermediate metallicity regions (7.5 < 12+log(O/H) < 8.2) exhibit a large scatter in DGR, consistent with dust evolution models where ISM grain growth begins to dominate.  
  > "A large scatter of DGR in the intermediate metallicity galaxies (8.0 < 12+log(O/H) < 8.3) is found, which is in line with dust evolution models, where grain growth begins to dominate the mechanism of dust mass accumulation." (abstract); "A large scatter stands out at the intermediate or critical metallicity regime (0.05–0.3 𝑍 / 𝑍 ⊙ ; 7.5 < 12+log(O/H) (Scal) < 8.2), agreeing with the chemical evolution models (e.g., Asano et al. 2013 and De Vis et al. 2017)." (4.3 The resolved DGR–𝑍gas relation)
- Star-forming regions in Sextans A have DGR values 0.5–2 dex higher than global emission-line literature estimates at the same metallicity, aligning with DLA metal depletion measurements and high column density sightlines.  
  > "In the lowest metallicity galaxy of our sample, Sextans A (12+log(O/H) < 7.6), the star-forming regions have significantly higher DGR values (by 0.5–2 dex) than the global estimates from literature at the same metallicity" (abstract); "One of the most notable features from our measurements is that the several star-forming regions in Sextans A have DGR values of 0.5-2 dex higher than the literature values at fixed metallicity." (4.3 The resolved DGR–𝑍gas relation)
- The resolved DGR–Z_gas relation shows smaller residuals against a broken power law than a single power law.  
  > "The broken power law proposed by Rémy-Ruyer et al. (2014) has a smaller residual and standard deviation than the single power law by De Vis et al. (2019)." (5.1.3 Broken vs. single power law)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: TYPHOON provides a large FoV (~18' x (1.65'' x N)) covering the entire optical disk of nearby galaxies, avoiding fixed metallicity gradient assumptions; sample of 11 galaxies chosen based on availability of Herschel far-IR, CO, and HI 21 cm data.  
  > "However, assuming a metallicity gradient of galaxies can overlook the locally varying metal abundances in galaxies, which can be related to the age of the stellar cluster, molecular gas surface density, dust column density, and electron density" (1 INTRODUCTION); "For this study, we select our sample galaxies based on the availability of ancillary data ( Herschel far-IR, CO (1-0) or CO (2-1), and Hi 21 cm)." (2.1 TYPHOON Survey)
- Why this method: Excluded SPIRE 500 um to retain finer spatial resolution (25'' vs 37.5'') while keeping M_dust error < 30%; chose Scal metallicity diagnostic because it avoids noisy blue [O II] 3726,3729 lines, agrees with T_e within ~0.1 dex, and is reliable at low metallicity.  
  > "Additionally, it is important to note that the SPIRE 500 𝜇 m image has a considerably coarse resolution of 37.5 ′′, thus excluding it allows us to obtain more pixels with reliable 𝑀 dust at the same time." (2.3 Image processing); "Among the many popular metallicity tracers, we exclude diagnostics that rely on [O II] 𝜆 3726, 𝜆 3729 emission lines. These lines are located at the blue wavelength end of TYPHOON spectra, where the sensitivity is notably lower relative to the red end." (3.2 Gas-phase metallicity: Scal)
- Alternative: Including Herschel SPIRE 500 um band in SED modelling (rejected due to coarse 37.5'' resolution)  
  > "We note that the SPIRE 500 𝜇 m images were not considered in this study." (2.3 Image processing)
- Alternative: Metallicity-dependent CO-to-H2 conversion factor alpha_CO,Z (computed for comparison in global relation, but main resolved analysis uses constant alpha_CO,MW to reduce uncertainty)  
  > "to reduce any uncertainty that could be raised by employing" (4 Results (ISM scaling relations))
- Alternative: Alternative metallicity diagnostics N2S2Halpha, O3N2, and R23 (evaluated in Appendix A and Section 5.1.3)  
  > "We show the resolved DGR– 𝑍 gas relationship using different gas-phase metallicity diagnostics, N2S2H 𝛼, O3N2, and R 23 (Figure A1)." (APPENDIX A: THE DGR–𝑍gas RELATIONSHIP WITH DIFFERENT METALLICITY CALIBRATIONS)
- Alternative: Inferred molecular gas mass for Sextans A from M_atom/M_* scaling relation or [C II] 158 um M_mol/M_atom = 4  
  > "More recently, Cigan et al. (2021) has inferred that the 𝑀 mol / 𝑀 atom for Sextans A is ∼ 4, from the [C II] line detected in this galaxy. We consider this molecular gas fraction and estimate the ‘conservative’ gas masses and DGRs" (5.1.2 Low metallicity regime - Sextans A)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only one galaxy (Sextans A) is probed in the low-metallicity regime, and CO is not detected in it.  
  > "Nonetheless, the fact that molecular gas has not been factored into the total 𝑀 gas for these regions and that only one galaxy has been considered can be problematic when concluding." (5.1.1 Emission vs. absorption line methods); "A major limitation of this study is that only one very low metallicity galaxy is available with the required data." (6 SUMMARY)
- Limitation (authors): MAGPHYS assumes a fixed dust emissivity index, which can introduce systematic biases in M_dust without longer-wavelength data.  
  > "However, MAGPHYS (and most SED modelling codes) assume a fixed dust emissivity index (i.e., the slope of the RJ tail), and this can introduce systematic biases on the resulting 𝑀 dust values in the absence of longer wavelength data (Galliano et al. 2018)." (5.1.1 Emission vs. absorption line methods)
- Limitation (authors): BEDE is a one-zone chemical and dust evolution model, which may not capture sub-galactic environmental variations (bars, spiral arms, local inflows/outflows) and suffers from parameter degeneracies (IMF, SN yield tables, k_frag vs SN_red).  
  > "This model comparison has caveats such as the dust evolution model we utilized assumes one-zone chemical and dust evolution. Thus, it may not be suitable for spatially resolved studies" (5.2.3 Caveats on our dust evolution modelling); "Also, it is worthwhile to note that the DGR values in the low-metallicity systems are regularised by 𝑘 frag and 𝑆𝑁 red at the same time, which can lead to another degeneracy." (5.2.3 Caveats on our dust evolution modelling)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Physical resolution varies by more than a factor of 10 across the sample (from 0.200 kpc in NGC 6822 and Sextans A to 2.279 kpc in NGC 1512), mixing sub-kpc and multi-kpc scales in the combined resolved relations.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA CO observations for NGC 6822 (7m only) and NGC 7793 (12m only) lack Total Power data, potentially filtering out extended diffuse molecular gas when convolved to 25''–82.5''.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fitting a one-zone galaxy-evolution grid (8 < log M_tot/M_sun < 10) simultaneously to 346 individual kpc/sub-kpc pixels treats correlated regions within the same galaxy as independent evolutionary systems.
