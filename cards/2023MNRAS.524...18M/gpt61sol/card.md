## Qualitative findings

- ALMA did not establish disc rotation for the seven candidates; all were excluded from further analysis.  
  > "Unfortunately, we likewise could not find evidence of disc rotation, due to either a synthesised beam larger than the angular size of the objects, or to lack of bright emission lines." (Galaxy sample); "We exclude these galaxies from further analysis." (Galaxy sample)
- Most objects show smaller absolute pitch angles at shorter wavelengths, consistent with normal colour jumps.  
  > "For most of the objects we find the expected colour jump trend, i.e., the absolute value of the pitch angle $\vert P \vert$ decreases for shorter wavelengths." (Results and discussion)
- Reverse trends occur in UDF 4929, UDF 8585 and UDF 9253; the effect is important only for UDF 9253.

(Results and discussion); "In the cases of UDF 4929 and UDF 8585, the pitch angles are almost constant with wavelength." (Results and discussion)

  > "Also, the behaviour of the pitch angle as a function of rest wavelength is not linear, but resembles a wavy pattern."
