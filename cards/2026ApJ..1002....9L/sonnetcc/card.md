## Analysis recipe

1. **select X-ray AGNs with JWST grism and MIRI coverage**: Cross-match CCL X-ray AGNs with C3D footprints and visually inspect F444W grism spectra for He I absorption  
  > "We visually inspected the F444W grism spectra of these 106 AGNs" (Target selection)
   - parameter: 342 AGNs in NIRCam footprint, 106 with MIRI coverage  
  > "342 fall within the C3D NIRCam imaging footprint, and 106 of these 342 are covered by C3D MIRI observations" (Target selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **fit He I 10830 and Pa gamma emission and absorption lines**: Gaussian profiles for emission and absorption with a linear continuum; broad-only, narrow-only and broad+narrow models compared  
  > "line structure using Gaussian profiles for both the emission and absorption components" (Emission and absorption line measurements); "using three models: broad only, narrow only, and broad+narrow components" (Emission and absorption line measurements)
   - parameter: Linear continuum fitted in rest-frame 1.02–1.05 um and 1.11–1.14 um  
  > "A linear continuum is fitted around the emission lines in the rest-frame wavelength ranges 1.02–1.05 $\mu$m and 1.11–1.14 $\mu$m." (Emission and absorption line measurements)
   - parameter: Instrumental broadening corrected with the NIRCam grism line-spread function  
  > "We correct the line instrumental broadening based on the NIRCam grism line-spread function (cite: greene17)." (cid_414)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: chi-square values of the three emission models are similar  
  > "we find that the resulting $\chi^{2}$ values are similar" (Emission and absorption line measurements)
3. **derive He I 10830 absorption column density**: Apparent optical depth (AOD) integration over the absorption velocity range  
  > "We use the apparent optical depth (AOD) method to estimate the column density of the He i$\lambda$10830 absorption line." (cid_414)
   - parameter: cid_947: three absorption components included; fourth (~ -12000 km/s) excluded as not detected above 3 sigma in 2D spectra  
  > "since it is not detected above $3\sigma$ in the 2D spectra, we do not include it when calculating the He i$\lambda$10830 absorption-line column density" (cid_947)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **measure JWST photometry and decompose AGN and host**: SEP detection and SE++ photometry on chi-square detection images; PSF built from stars; multi-band PSF + Sersic decomposition with GALFITS  
  > "we performed photometric measurements using SEP for source detection and SE++ for photometry (cite: bertin20)" (JWST photometry); "simultaneously fits images taken in multiple bands and incorporates physically motivated SED models into the fitting process" (Point-Spread Function Construction and Image Decomposition)
   - software: SEP, SE++, SExtractor, GALFITS
   - parameter: PSF from stars within 2 arcsec, oversampled by 3, recentered and median stacked  
  > "The stellar cutouts are oversampled by a factor of three, recentered, and median stacked to generate the final PSF." (Point-Spread Function Construction and Image Decomposition)
   - assumption: two Sersic components adopted for cid_414 host, second component excluded from host mass and SED fitting  
  > "We exclude the flux from the second Sérsic component when estimating the host galaxy mass and in the subsequent SED fitting" (Point-Spread Function Construction and Image Decomposition)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **fit the UV-to-radio SED for stellar mass and SFR**: Two independent SED codes, CIGALE (energy balance) and AGNfitter, with AGN and stellar components  
  > "we perform multiwavelength spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting using UV–to–radio photometry" (SED fitting and host galaxy properties); "To mitigate systematics arising from the assumptions built into any single SED fitting tool (choices of AGN and stellar components, dust treatment, and star formation history etc)" (SED fitting and host galaxy properties)
   - software: CIGALE, AGNfitter
   - parameter: CIGALE: BC03, Chabrier IMF, delayed-exponential SFH, Calzetti attenuation, Draine14 dust emission, SKIRTOR AGN  
  > "adopting BC03 stellar models (cite: bc03), a Chabrier IMF (cite: chabrier), a delayed-exponential star formation history (SFH)" (SED fitting and host galaxy properties)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; flat LCDM with H0 = 70, Omega_M = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7  
  > "we adopt a standard $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $H_0 = 70\ {\rm km}\ {\rm s}^{-1}\ {\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, $\Omega_{M}$ = 0.3 and $\Omega_{\lambda}$ = 0.7" (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: cid_414 stellar mass cross-checked with F200W Sersic 1 flux and M/L from 2 to 7.6  
  > "varying the $M/L$ from 2 to 7.6 as a consistency check" (SED fitting and host galaxy properties)
6. **estimate black hole masses and Eddington ratios**: cid_414: empirical He I / Hbeta scaling from the local BASS sample using He I FWHM and X-ray luminosity; cid_947: broad Hbeta mass adopted; L_bol from 2-10 keV luminosity  
  > "we instead adopt an indirect empirical scaling relation based on the He i and H$\beta$ lines, as calibrated from the local BASS sample (cite: ricci17)" (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio measurements); "The bolometric luminosities of the two targets are converted from the 2 to 10 keV luminosity using the bolometric correction from (cite: duras20)." (Black hole mass and Eddington ratio measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **model the photoionization state of the absorber**: CLOUDY C25.00 grid with AGN continuum plus UV background, matching observed He I 10830 column densities  
  > "adopting an AGN continuum plus the UV background (cite: mf87)" (Dense Gas in Different SMBH Growth Phase)
   - software: CLOUDY
   - parameter: log U from -4 to 2, log n_H from 2 to 10, metallicity varied, N_H fixed to observed value  
  > "We vary $\log U$ ($-4$ to $2$), $\log n_{\rm H}$ ($2$ to $10$), and metallicity, fixing $N_{\rm H}$ to the observed value." (Dense Gas in Different SMBH Growth Phase)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: results insensitive to metallicity between 0.1 and 1 Zsun  
  > "The results are insensitive to metallicity between 0.1 and 1 $Z_\odot$." (Dense Gas in Different SMBH Growth Phase)
8. **estimate absorber size, distance and dust torus radius**: Single-slab thickness l = N_H/n_H, distance from the ionization parameter, torus inner radius from thermal equilibrium  
  > "Under a single-slab assumption, the absorber thickness is $l = N_{\rm H}/n_{\rm H}$" (Dust torus and dense absorbing gas geometry)
   - parameter: dust sublimation temperature T = 1500 K  
  > "we adopt a dust sublimation temperature of $T = 1500$ K (cite: zhang15)" (Dust torus and dense absorbing gas geometry)
   - assumption: geometry is simplified; single slab  
  > "The geometry of the dense absorbing gas is likely complex, and our discussion below provides only a simplified estimate." (Dust torus and dense absorbing gas geometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **estimate a lower-limit luminosity function**: Phi = N_src/(Vmax dlogL) over z=2.7-3.6 and 0.14 deg2 of C3D MIRI coverage, no completeness correction  
  > "we do not apply any completeness correction. Instead, we treat the resulting LF as a lower limit and plot it in Figure" (Luminosity function and comparison with other AGN populations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Focus on the roles of absorbing gas and hot dust at different stages of black hole growth  
  > "In this work, we focus on investigating the roles of absorbing gas and hot dust at different stages of black hole growth." (Target selection)
- Why this method: Two independent SED codes used to mitigate systematics from any single tool  
  > "To mitigate systematics arising from the assumptions built into any single SED fitting tool (choices of AGN and stellar components, dust treatment, and star formation history etc)" (SED fitting and host galaxy properties)
- Alternative: A single Sersic fit including the extended region of cid_414 gives an implausibly massive host, so two Sersic components were adopted  
  > "We therefore adopt two Sérsic components to model the host galaxy emission of" (Point-Spread Function Construction and Image Decomposition)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Luminosity function based on only two objects; larger sample needed
