## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and lines**: CASA CLEAN with natural weighting; uv-plane continuum subtraction; primary beam correction  
  > "All images of the line and continuum emissions were reconstructed with the CASA task `CLEAN` (gain = 0.1, threshold = 1.0 mJy, weighting = natural)." (2.1)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: gain 0.1, threshold 1.0 mJy  
  > "gain = 0.1, threshold = 1.0 mJy, weighting = natural" (2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Continuum peak coincides with VLA 8.4 GHz peak  
  > "This precise match supports the accuracy of the astrometry of ALMA." (3)
2. **Continuum SED and thermal/nonthermal origin**: Fit synchrotron power law to cm data, extrapolate to 860 um; compare LSB/USB fluxes  
  > "the SED indicates that the centimeter continuum emission can be well reproduced as synchrotron ($\alpha$ = -0.62$\pm$0.02; flux $\propto$ $\nu^\alpha$)" (3)
   - fitting: power-law fit to centimeter fluxes  
  > "the SED indicates that the centimeter continuum emission can be well reproduced as synchrotron ($\alpha$ = -0.62$\pm$0.02; flux $\propto$ $\nu^\alpha$)" (3. 860 μm continuum emission)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract spectra and line ratios**: Spectra from single beam at positions A-D; Gaussian fits to get integrated intensities and ratios  
  > "They are calculated by the Gaussian fit to the data." (4.1)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - fitting: Gaussian line profiles; CO(3-2) fit with HCN centroid and FWHM  
  > "we used the velocity centroid and the FWHM of HCN(4-3) emission line to fit the Gaussian profile to CO(3-2) line" (4.2)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Moment maps and CND size**: MIRIAD MOMENT zeroth moment without clipping; 2D Gaussian fit to HCN map with Monte Carlo errors (10^3 realizations)  
  > "We used the MIRIAD task `MOMENT` to make the integrated intensity maps presented in Section [5](#sec5), by computing the zeroth moment of each data cube without any clipping." (2.1)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian; Monte Carlo noise realizations  
  > "We derived these uncertainties using Monte Carlo techniques by creating 10$^3$ realizations of the image" (5)
   - validation: Compared with APEX HCO+ flux (54±16% recovered)  
  > "we recovered only 54$\pm$16 % of the HCO$^+$(4-3) flux measured by APEX" (5)
5. **Dense gas mass and dynamical mass**: Convert L'HCN(4-3) to HCN(1-0), apply conversion factor 10; Keplerian thin-disk M_dyn  
  > "$M_{\rm dyn}$ = 230 ($r$/pc) \[$v(r)^2$/(km s$^{-1}$)\] (sin $i$)$^{-2}$ $M_\odot$" (4.3)
   - assumption: HCN(4-3)/HCN(1-0)=0.6±0.2; conversion factor 10; i=45 deg; FWHM~200 km/s  
  > "putting the FWHM $\sim$ 200 km s$^{-1}$ at the CND (1$''$ = 330 pc diameter) and $i$ = 45$^\circ$" (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: M_dyn consistent with Fathi et al. 2015  
  > "This is almost identical to the value derived in (cite: 2015ApJ...806L..34F)" (4.3)
6. **XDR size toy model**: 1D slab X-ray attenuation with photoelectric cross sections; compare X-ray and UV heating rates  
  > "Subsequent radiative transfer is calculated by adopting a 1D slab geometry with a uniform gas density of $n_{\rm H}$ = 10$^5$ cm$^{-3}$." (A. The size of an XDR)
   - assumption: alpha=1.0, Ec=100 keV, 30% X-ray heating efficiency, G0=10^2.5  
  > "We set $\alpha$ = 1.0" (A. The size of an XDR)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- HCN, HCO+ and CS are concentrated in the central ~1'' CND; CO(3-2) spreads over CND and ring; gas tracers anti-correlated with optical peaks  
  > "these dense gas tracers are significantly concentrated towards the central $\sim$ 1$''$" (abstract)
