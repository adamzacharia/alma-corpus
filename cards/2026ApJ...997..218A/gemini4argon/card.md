## Qualitative findings

- Neither CO(7-6) nor dust continuum emission is detected in any of the three targeted LRDs.  
  > "No lines are detected at the expected wavelength of CO($7$–$6$) in the three targets." (CO(7–6) and [CI](2–1) line spectra); "We do not detect any target in the continuum observations, consistent with the overall LRD population, which are undetected at far-infrared wavelengths (cite: labbeUNCOVER2023; akinsCOSMOSWeb2024)." (Dust continuum)
- A tentative (4.9sigma) detection of narrow [CI](2-1) emission (FWHM ~ 80 km/s) is found in A2744-45924.  
  > "We report the tentative ($4.9\sigma$) detection of the \[C I\]($2$–$1$) line in A2744-45924 ($z=4.46$), one of the brightest known LRDs." (Abstract); "The \[C I\] line is narrow (${\rm FWHM}\sim 80$ km s$^{-1}$), implying a dynamical mass $\lesssim 10^{10}\,M_\odot$, adopting conservative limits for the galaxy size." (Abstract)
- The low dynamical mass of A2744-45924 indicates its black hole is overmassive relative to the local M_BH-M_dyn relation and is incompatible with a stellar origin for the Balmer break.  
  > "The dynamical mass limit is significantly lower than expected from the local $M_{\rm BH}$–$M_{\rm dyn}$ relation, and is an order of magnitude below the stellar mass derived from SED fitting, potentially supporting a non-stellar origin of the Balmer break." (Abstract)
- LRDs reside in gas-poor and dust-poor host galaxies compared to typical high-z quasars and luminous obscured AGN.  
  > "Our observations of \[C I\] and CO suggest that the LRDs do not live in particularly gas-rich host galaxies." (Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: CO(7-6) and [CI](2-1) have very similar rest frequencies (806.65 and 809.34 GHz), allowing simultaneous observation of molecular and atomic gas phases in a single tuning; targets were chosen for relative brightness, lensing magnification, and accurate medium/high-res JWST spectroscopic redshifts.  
  > "While CO and \[C I\] are typically fainter than \[C II\] 158 $\mu$m, their similar frequencies make it possible to simultaneously trace multiple gas phases to better characterize the host galaxies of LRDs." (Introduction); "These objects were selected for their relative brightness and accessibility to ALMA." (ALMA observations and data reduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting maximizes point-source sensitivity; [CI](2-1) provides an independent molecular gas mass estimate that avoids the highly uncertain high-J CO excitation ladder (SLED).  
  > "All images are made using natural weighting to maximize sensitivity, and datacubes are produced at the native channel width of each observation." (ALMA observations and data reduction); "These limits are similar to the conservative limits from CO($7$–$6$) but depend only on the empirically-derived $\alpha_{[{\rm C}\,\textsc{i}](2-1)}$ conversion factor, and not on the (highly uncertain) CO line SLED." (Molecular gas mass)
- Alternative: Manual circular aperture masking around target positions during tclean was tested as an alternative to auto-multithresh and gave consistent results.  
  > "We also explored manually masking in a circular aperture around the known positions of each target, and find consistent results using this method." (ALMA observations and data reduction)
- Alternative: Considered whether the 0.4'' spatial offset of the [CI](2-1) peak in A2744-45924 indicates a spurious noise peak, but rejected this because random noise offsets >0.4'' occur ~30% of the time at this SNR and beam size.  
  > "Given the slight positional offset, we consider the possibility that the detection is spurious." (Detection of [CI](2–1) in A2744-45924); "We therefore consider the positional offset to be negligible, statistically consistent with emission from phase center." (Detection of [CI](2–1) in A2744-45924)
- How they decided it was enough: Integration times were calculated by scaling measured black hole masses to host bulge masses using local relations, converting to CO luminosities, and scaling down by a conservative factor.  
  > "Integration times were determined by scaling the measured black hole masses to host galaxy bulge masses assuming local relations (cite: kormendyCoevolution2013), converting to CO luminosities, and scaling down by a conservative factor." (ALMA observations and data reduction)
- Limitation (authors): The [CI](2-1) detection in A2744-45924 has a modest SNR (4.9sigma integrated, 3.9sigma peak) and could still be spurious.  
  > "However, given the relatively low SNR ($4.9\sigma$), it remains possible that the \[C I\] detection in A2744-45924 is spurious, or that it traces only a part of the overall host galaxy ISM, impacting the interpretation of the dynamical mass measurement." (Summary)
- Limitation (authors): The [CI](2-1) emission is both spatially and spectrally unresolved, leaving the true emitting gas size and inclination unknown.  
  > "The line width is remarkably narrow, and in fact is unresolved; the velocity resolution for the ALMA observations is $\sim 70$ km s$^{-1}$." (Detection of [CI](2–1) in A2744-45924); "However, the relevant radius is not obvious, as the \[C I\] line is spatially unresolved." (Dynamical mass)
- Limitation (authors): Using high-J CO(7-6) to constrain molecular gas mass introduces large uncertainties from the unknown CO excitation ladder (SLED) and alpha_CO.  
  > "Molecular gas masses are commonly derived from CO observations, though the use of high-$J$ CO lines imparts additional uncertainty from the CO excitation ladder." (Molecular gas mass)
- Limitation (authors): The [CI] emission may trace only part of the host galaxy ISM or may be excited by non-standard mechanisms (e.g., XDRs, shocks, or dense atomic gas), which would affect both H2 mass and dynamical mass interpretations.  
  > "Moreover, the molecular gas mass as estimated from the \[C I\] line is more than an order of magnitude larger than the dynamical mass measured from the same line, perhaps suggesting that the mechanism exciting \[C I\] in A2744-45924 may be different than in the local galaxies used to calibrate the \[C I\]-to-H$_2$ relation (cite: papadopoulosCI2004)." (Origin of the [CI] emission); "However, given the relatively low SNR ($4.9\sigma$), it remains possible that the \[C I\] detection in A2744-45924 is spurious, or that it traces only a part of the overall host galaxy ISM, impacting the interpretation of the dynamical mass measurement." (Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Extracting the 1D spectrum at the spatial peak of a low-SNR (~3.9sigma peak, 0.4'' offset) feature can introduce upward flux boosting and artificial narrowing due to noise selection bias.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The [CI](2-1)-derived molecular gas mass (2.0e10 M_sun) exceeds the [CI](2-1)-derived fiducial dynamical mass (1.4e9 M_sun) by over an order of magnitude, indicating internal tension in applying standard galaxy scaling relations to this line.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Because the line FWHM (~80 km/s) is comparable to the native channel width (~70 km/s), instrumental spectral response broadening is significant and is not explicitly deconvolved in the reported velocity dispersion.
