## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image line cubes and continuum maps**: QA2 pipeline calibration with CASA 6.2.1; 25 km/s channel cubes from SPWs containing each line; MFS continuum from continuum SPWs; clean to 3x dirty-image RMS, max 500 iterations  
  > "To create data cubes using the task of CASA, we applied a fixed velocity channel binning of $25\,{\rm km/s}$ for all data cubes." (2.3 ALMA data reduction); "Dust continuum maps of all observations were made using the continuum SPWs and using the task of CASA and using the multi-frequency synthesis () imaging mode." (2.3 ALMA data reduction)
   - software: CASA 6.2.1
   - parameter: Velocity channel width 25 km/s; clean threshold 3x pixel-by-pixel dirty RMS; 500 iterations max  
  > "we performed synthesized beam deconvolutions using the by setting stopping thresholds to be $3\times$ the pixel-by-pixel root-mean-square (RMS) of the dirty images and by setting the maximum number of iterations to 500 times." (2.3 ALMA data reduction)
   - parameter: Rest-frequency of each cube set from the [C II] redshift  
  > "Rest-frame frequencies of each data cube were set to the sky frequency of each emission line observation based on the \[C ii\] emission line detections" (2.3 ALMA data reduction)
   - assumption: Threshold and iteration limits do not affect data quality because no bright sources are in the fields  
  > "The threshold and parameters did not affect final data quality as there were no particularly bright sources in the fields of views." (2.3 ALMA data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Subtract continuum and measure dust continuum fluxes**: Flat continuum fit in line-free SPWs subtracted from cubes; continuum fluxes from 2-D Gaussian fits to the continuum maps  
  > "Underlying dust continua of the target galaxies were subtracted using CASA task by fitting flat continua for the SPWs that do not include emission lines." (2.3 ALMA data reduction); "We measured continuum fluxes by 2-D Gaussian fittings using the CASA task ." (3.2 Dust Continuum Detection)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: 2-D Gaussian fit (task name not printed)  
  > "We measured continuum fluxes by 2-D Gaussian fittings using the CASA task ." (3.2 Dust Continuum Detection)
   - validation: Continua detected at >4.5 sigma at the source positions (>8 sigma in the Appendix)  
  > "Dust continua were all clearly detected from all targets with $>4.5\,\sigma$ significance at the source positions." (3.2 Dust Continuum Detection)
3. **Build [O I] moment-0 maps and measure [O I] fluxes**: Iterative 1D Gaussian fit to the extracted spectrum, re-integration over 2-sigma line widths, re-extraction from pixels above 2 sigma, repeated until the channel selection converged; flux from 2-D Gaussian fit to the moment-0 map  
  > "First, we performed 1D Gaussian fits on the extracted spectra and re-integrated the data cube using frequency ranges that encompass the $2\,\sigma$ line widths." (3.1 Emission Line Detections); "This process was repeated until the selected channels for moment-0 image creation converged." (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Moment-0 width: 2-sigma line width from 1D Gaussian fits; spectrum re-extracted from pixels above 2 sigma  
  > "Next, we re-extracted spectra from regions in the moment-0 images where pixel values exceeded $2\,\sigma$, further refining the emission line spectra." (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
   - fitting: 1D Gaussian line fits; 2-D Gaussian fits for fluxes  
  > "For the detected \[O i\] $145\,{\rm \mu m}$ emission lines, we performed 2-D Gaussian fittings using CASA task ." (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
   - validation: [O I] detected from all targets at >4 sigma  
  > "we successfully detect \[O i\] $145\,{\rm \mu m}$ emission from all targets at $>4\,\sigma$ significance" (abstract)
4. **Measure [N II] upper limits**: Moment-0 maps integrated over 1x and 2x the [C II] FWHM; 3-sigma upper limits from 3x the pixel-by-pixel RMS of moment-0 maps integrated over the [C II] FWHM; A1689-zD1 tentative signal treated as an upper limit  
  > "For non-detected \[N ii\] $205\,{\rm \mu m}$ emission lines, we measured $3\,\sigma$ upper limit fluxes by calculating the $3\times$ pixel-by-pixel RMSs of moment-0 maps integrated over the FWHMs of \[C ii\] emission lines." (3.1 Emission Line Detections); "we treated the \[N ii\] $205\,{\rm \mu m}$ of A1689-zD1 as an upper limit" (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: [N II] line width equal to the [C II] FWHM  
  > "we created moment-0 images by using velocity widths fixed to those of \[C ii\] $158\,{\rm \mu m}$ emission lines ($1\times$ FWHMs and $2\times$ FWHMs)." (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Only <~3 sigma signals at the expected positions; treating the A1689-zD1 signal as a detection would not change the results  
  > "However, even if we treat the \[N ii\] $205\,{\rm \mu m}$ as detection, our results do not change." (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
5. **Adopt literature [C II], [O III], dust SED and lens magnification**: Take [C II]/[O III] line data, T_d, beta_d and L_IR (8-1000 um) from earlier work; redo the Abell 1689 lens model to get a new magnification for A1689-zD1  
  > "We adopt dust temperatures ($T_{\rm d}$), emissivity indexes ($\beta_{\rm d}$), and IR luminosities ($L_{\rm IR}$) measured in these previous studies." (4.1 IR Luminosity and FIR SED); "we re-analyze the mass model of Abell 1689 using the updated multiple image data and using an updated lens modeling code." (Appendix A)
   - software: lens modeling code (name not printed; cite Oguri2010, Oguri2021)
   - parameter: 130 multiple images of 40 source galaxies (14 with spectroscopic redshifts); five NFW components, cluster galaxies, external shear, m=3 and 4 multipoles  
  > "We used 130 multiple images of 40 source galaxies, 14 of which have spectroscopic redshifts." (Appendix A)
   - parameter: Image-position error 0.8 arcsec; best-fit RMS offset 0.64 arcsec  
  > "The root-mean-square of differences between observed and model-predicted multiple image positions is $0.64\ensuremath{''}$ for the best-fitting model." (Appendix A)
   - assumption: L_IR integrated over rest-frame 8-1000 um  
  > "Throughout this work, $L_{\rm IR}$ are calculated using the rest-frame wavelength range of $8-1000\,{\rm \mu m}$." (4.1 IR Luminosity and FIR SED)
   - fitting: Minimum chi^2 fit to image positions; magnification error from MCMC around the best fit  
  > "The error is calculated using MCMC calculation around the best-fit solutions." (Appendix A)
   - validation: Magnification only affects absolute quantities; the main focus is line ratios  
  > "The exact value of the magnification factor does not strongly affect our conclusion, as the main focus of this study is line ratios." (Appendix A)
6. **Derive the PDR fraction of [C II] from [C II]/[N II]**: f_PDR = 1 - R_ion x (L_[NII]205 / L_[CII]158) with fiducial R_ion = 3.6, also tested with R_ion = 5.7; 3-sigma lower limits  
  > "In this study, we use $R_{\rm ion}=3.6$ as our fiducial value by taking an average of $R_{\rm ion}$ found in the analysis of (cite: Witstok2022)." (5.1 Origins of [C II] 158 um Emission Lines); "Later, we will discuss how the results change by using a higher value of $R_{\rm ion}=5.7$." (5.1 Origins of [C II] 158 um Emission Lines)
   - parameter: R_ion = 3.6 (fiducial); 5.7 (high alternative)

(5.1 Origins of [C II] 158 um Emission Lines)
   - software: Cloudy
   - parameter: Blackbody T = 50000 K; cosmic-ray background; plane-parallel, constant density; ISM abundances with PAH grains; stop at log N_H = 23; iterate to converge  
  > "We used the plane-parallel geometry with a constant hydrogen density by setting `constant density`." (5.2.1 CLOUDY settings and grids); "Finally, we set a stopping criteria of reaching a gas column density of $\rm{log(}N_{\rm H}/{\rm cm^{-2}})=23$." (5.2.1 CLOUDY settings and grids)

## Qualitative findings

- [C II] emission of these z~7 galaxies is dominated by neutral gas (PDR), and [C II] is an excellent tracer of neutral ISM  
  > "These results indicate that \[C ii\] $158\,{\rm \mu m}$ emission is an excellent tracer of neutral ISM." (5.1 Origins of [C II] 158 um Emission Lines)
- Neutral gas densities are similar to high-z starbursts while FUV strengths are lower than local and high-z starbursts  
  > "While the neutral gas densities are similar to those of high-redshift starburst galaxies, the FUV strengths are lower compared to both local and high-redshift starbursts." (abstract)
- The [O I] line of REBELS-38 is significantly narrower than its [C II] line, suggesting different ISM components  
  > "The difference of the emission line profile suggests that the \[O i\] and \[C ii\] of REBELS-38 are originated from different ISM components." (4.4 Origin of [O I] and [C II] emission lines of REBELS-38)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Flux-limited selection of [C II]-luminous z>6.5 main-sequence-like star-forming galaxies so [O I] and [N II] would be detectable  
  > "we performed flux limited selection of galaxies with their apparent \[C ii\] luminosity of $L_{\rm [CII]}>1.1\times10^{9}\,{\rm L_{\odot}}$." (2.1 Target Galaxies)
- Why this method: N+ exists only in ionized gas while C+ exists in both, and O0 is an ideal probe of neutral gas  
  > "neutral oxygen, O$^0$, is an ideal probe for the neutral star-forming gas." (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: A1689-zD1 [N II] weak signal treated as an upper limit, not a detection, because of two off-center peaks and a non-Gaussian shape  
  > "we treated the \[N ii\] $205\,{\rm \mu m}$ of A1689-zD1 as an upper limit based on the following reasons." (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
- Alternative: Higher R_ion = 5.7 tested; f_PDR lower limits stay high (>0.74 to >0.93)  
  > "Even using the large value of $R_{\rm ion}=5.7$" (5.1 Origins of [C II] 158 um Emission Lines)
- Alternative: Lower metallicity 12+log(O/H)=8.05 for REBELS-25 gives a clear Cloudy solution, unlike 8.63  
  > "as we do find that clear solution for $G_0$ and $n_{\rm H}$ if we assume lower metallicity (e.g., $12+{\rm log(O/H)}=8.05$) rather than higher metallicity of $12+{\rm log(O/H)}=8.63$." (5.2.2 Gas densities and FUV field strengths)
- How they decided it was enough: Clean threshold and iteration limit judged not to matter because no bright sources are in the fields  
  > "The threshold and parameters did not affect final data quality as there were no particularly bright sources in the fields of views." (2.3 ALMA data reduction)
- Limitation (authors): Large systematic uncertainty in oxygen masses from the assumed excitation temperature  
  > "Thus, there would be a large systemic uncertainty to the estimated atomic oxygen masses from the assumed excitation temperature." (5.3.1 Oxygen mass)
- Limitation (authors): [O I]/[C II] analysis may break down for REBELS-38 because the lines have different profiles  
  > "In such case, analysis and discussions using \[O i\]/\[C ii\] of REBELS-38 may breaks down." (4.4 Origin of [O I] and [C II] emission lines of REBELS-38)
- Limitation (authors): Spatial resolution too low to resolve clumps in A1689-zD1  
  > "As our current observations of \[N ii\] $205\,{\rm \mu m}$ and \[O i\] $145\,{\rm \mu m}$ does not have enough spatial resolution to resolve the clumps" (3.1 Emission Line Detections)
- Limitation (authors): Lens magnification of A1689-zD1 is uncertain because of the complex cluster  
  > "Although the magnification factor is relatively uncertain due to the complex structure of the Abell 1689 cluster" (Appendix A)
- Limitation (authors): REBELS-25 gas conditions are uncertain because the neutral-gas metallicity is uncertain  
  > "The difficulty of explaining REBELS-25’s emission line ratios is likely due to the uncertainty of the gas-phase metallicity of the neutral gas" (5.2.2 Gas densities and FUV field strengths)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only four targets, selected as [C II]-bright, so conclusions are not representative of the general EoR population
- Caveat (inferred, ours): R_ion fiducial value 3.6 is the average of a model range 1.5-5.7 and drives the f_PDR limits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A1689-zD1 values are not corrected for lensing in tables while some masses carry a 4.14/mu factor
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas fraction range differs between the abstract (0.3-0.8) and Section 5.3.2 (0.3-0.9); REBELS-18 abundance differs between text (8.50) and footnote 7 (8.53)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): T_ex = 50 K is assumed for all targets, and hydrogen masses rely on ionized-gas JWST abundances applied to neutral gas
- Caveat (inferred, ours): REBELS-25 G0 and n_H are adopted by judgement and the Table 4 n_H (~10^6.5) differs from the 10^6 quoted in the Section 5.2.2 text and conclusion
