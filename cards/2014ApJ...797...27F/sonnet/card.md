## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum**: MFS imaging of all four spws; uniform weighting for SM1 after self-cal, natural multiscale for SM1N  
  > "To image the SM1 condensation, we performed a multi-frequency synthesis deconvolution over all spectral windows and self-calibrated on the initial source model." (2.1 ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit Gaussian models to visibilities**: Single 2D Gaussian fit with uvmodelfit to 359 GHz windows  
  > "we fit both the SM1 and SM1N condensations with single 2D Gaussians in the $uv$-plane using the CASA task *uvmodelfit*." (3.1)
   - software: CASA uvmodelfit, Miriad uvamp
   - fitting: least-squares minimization in amplitude and phase  
  > "This task performs a least-squares minimization of a model to the observed visibilities in amplitude and phase." (3.1)
   - validation: Residual images after subtracting best-fit model  
  > "We then subtracted the best-fit model visibilities from the observed visibilities, and imaged the residuals to determine the quality of the fit." (3.1)
3. **Derive condensation masses**: Optically thin dust mass M = d^2 S/(kappa B(T_d))  
  > "Assuming the dust emission is optically thin, we directly calculate the mass from $S_\nu$ for each source" (3.2)
   - parameter: kappa_0 = 0.1 cm2/g at 1000 GHz; beta 1.8 (SM1N), 0.4 (SM1) from SMA/ALMA  
  > "Assuming the emission is in the Rayleigh-Jeans regime at both 227 GHz and 359 GHz (cite: friesen05), and $\sim 10$ % flux calibration uncertainty, we find $\beta = 0.4 \pm 0.3$." (3.2)
   - assumption: T_d = gas temperature; SM1N 16 K from NH3; SM1 >=30 K; d = 120 pc  
  > "We constrain $T_d$ by assuming it is similar to the gas temperature" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Herschel 70 um non-detection rules out uniform T >~50 K  
  > "At a uniform temperature $T \gtrsim 50$ K, SM1 would be sufficiently bright at 70 $\mu$m  to be detected in the archival Herschel data" (3.2)
4. **Fit H2D+ line and derive column density and abundance**: 1D Gaussian per pixel above 3 sigma; opacity and column density with Tex = 12 K; N(H2) from tapered continuum at Td = 17 K  
  > "We fit the H$_2$D$^+$  emission profile detected by ALMA with a single 1D Gaussian at pixels where the peak line emission is greater than three times the rms noise." (3.3)
   - parameter: T_ex = 12 K, mu = 2.8  
  > "For SM1N, we thus set $T_{ex} = 12$ K, less than the gas temperature $T_K = 16$ K." (3.3)
   - fitting: single Gaussian  
  > "we fit both the SM1 and SM1N condensations with single 2D Gaussians in the $uv$-plane using the CASA task *uvmodelfit*." (Continuum $uv$-modeling)
   - validation: compared to JCMT H2D+ spectra and 15 arcsec abundance  
  > "The maximum ortho-H$_2$D$^+$  abundance observed by ALMA is thus similar to that observed at larger scales" (3.3)
5. **Limit internal luminosity**: Herschel 70 um 3-sigma limits converted via Dunham et al. 2008 relation  
  > "We use this relation to place limits on any embedded sources within SM1 and SM1N" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- H2D+ is detected only toward SM1N and is offset ~1.5 arcsec from the continuum peak  
  > "shows that the emission peaks are offset by $\sim 1.5$$''$  ($\sim 150-200$ AU at 120 pc)" (3)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Fit visibilities directly to avoid deconvolution  
  > "we circumvent the deconvolution step and analyze the data in the domain where the measurements are made" (3.1)
- Alternative: Grain size distribution and ionization-rate changes rejected as causes of the H2D+ offset  
  > "we do not expect the grain size distribution to play a role in the observed H$_2$D$^+$  distribution toward SM1N" (4.4)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No short spacings or total power; large-scale emission unconstrained  
  > "Further observations with additional short-spacing information are needed to discern the source of the larger-scale emission." (3.1)
- Limitation (authors): Masses uncertain to factors of a few  
  > "While the uncertainty in the derived masses is thus uncertain to factors of a few" (3.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Temperature of SM1 only a lower limit, so masses are weakly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Disk interpretation of SM1 not kinematically tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beta from SMA depends on unpublished data and resolved-out flux
- Caveat (inferred, ours): H2D+ detection is low SNR; the offset is ~1 beam
