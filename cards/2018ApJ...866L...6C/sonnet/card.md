## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum**: multi-scale CLEAN of calibrated visibilities  
  > "we imaged the calibrated visibilities using the multi-scale CLEAN algorithm" (Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5  
  > "Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5" (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit axisymmetric visibility model**: envelope plus three logistic gaps, fitted to visibilities with GALARIO and emcee  
  > "fitting the continuum visibilities with an axisymmetric parametric model consisting of an envelope and three gaps" (3 Visibility modelling)
   - software: GALARIO, emcee
   - parameter: 120 walkers, 50e3 steps after 30e3 burn-in  
  > "We run the MCMC sampler with 120 walkers for $50\times 10^3$ steps after a burn in phase of $30\times 10^3$ steps." (3 Visibility modelling)
   - fitting: Gaussian likelihood; uniform priors except p(i)=sin(i)  
  > "We choose uniform priors on all parameters, except for inclination for which $p(i)=\sin(i)$ for $0\leq{}i\leq\pi/2$." (3 Visibility modelling)
   - validation: Chain traces, autocorrelation time ~150 steps; residual image  
  > "resulting in $\sim$150 steps on average for all parameters" (3 Visibility modelling)
3. **Hydrodynamic modelling of gaps with planets**: 2D FARGO3D with dust, synthetic emissivity compared to fitted profile, then synthetic image via CASA ft and CLEAN  
  > "using the 2D version of the FARGO3D code (cite: fargo3d) with our implementation of drag coupled dust (cite: Rosotti2016)" (4 Modeling)
   - software: FARGO3D, CASA
   - parameter: alpha=0.014, disc mass 20 MJ within 200 au, 350x512 cells, 5.6-378 au  
  > "parameters $\alpha=0.014$ and total disc mass within 200 au of $20 \,M_\mathrm{Jupiter}$" (4 Modeling)
   - assumption: fragmentation velocity 10 m/s; dust-to-gas 0.01; a^-3 size distribution of compact silicate grains  
  > "We assume an initial dust to gas ratio of 0.01." (4 Modeling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare with accretion rate and spectral index  
  > "yield a profile of $a_{max}$ that is compatible with measurements of the disc-averaged spectral index in CI Tau" (4 Modeling)
