## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate ALMA data**: ALMA pipeline in CASA to calibrated uv data  
  > "Data reduction was carried out using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (casa) and using the ALMA pipeline up to the production of calibrated $uv$ data products." (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA, ALMA pipeline
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **image continuum and detect sources**: tclean with Briggs (robust=0.5), natural weighting and uv-tapered (~1'') imaging; interactive cleaning; 5 sigma detection criterion in at least one imaging  
  > "Three imaging approaches were performed using the task tclean, exploring a Briggs weighting (robust=0.5), natural weighting, and $uv$ tapering such that we created a synthesized beam of $\sim 1''$" (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: clean down to 2-3 sigma, rms ~25 uJy/beam  
  > "the signal is interactively cleaned down to 2–3 $\sigma$ (r.m.s. $\sim$ 25$\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$)" (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - parameter: detection threshold >5 sigma in at least one of the three imagings  
  > "were detected by ALMA at a significance higher than 5$\sigma$ in at least one of the three imaging approaches" (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: astrometric accuracy of the Band-7 image around 1.5 mas  
  > "The astrometric accuracy of the ALMA Band-7 image is around 1.5 mas." (2.1 ALMA observations)
3. **align ancillary HST and IFU astrometry to common frame**: align HiZELS narrow-band images to Gaia DR2, correct SINFONI H-alpha moment-0 via Gaussian peak fit at 0.7'' resolution, align HST to HSC DR2  
  > "we align the astrometry of the HiZELS narrow-band images by the public *Gaia* DR2 catalogue" (2.2 Global astrometry); "Then we fit a Gaussian profile to this low-resolution image in order to obtain the position of the peak" (2.2 Global astrometry)
   - software: SExtractor
   - parameter: H-alpha moment-0 degraded to 0.7'' with a Gaussian kernel  
  > "We degrade the resolution of the H$\alpha$ moment-0 SINFONI image down to $0.7\ensuremath{''}$ using a Gaussian kernel" (2.2 Global astrometry)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile fit to low-resolution H-alpha image  
  > "Then we fit a Gaussian profile to this low-resolution image" (2.2 Global astrometry)
   - validation: HST-HSC match consistent with zero offset, systematic ~0.04''  
  > "After our astrometric correction, the catalogue match between *HST* and HSC catalogues is consistent with an offset of zero, with systematic errors of $\sim 0.04\ensuremath{''}$." (2.2 Global astrometry)
4. **derive FIR luminosity, dust temperature, dust mass and obscured SFR**: normalise template FIR SED (Ibar2013 stacks at z~1.47, Thomson2017 at z~2.23) to the ALMA flux density; modified blackbody; SFR_FIR from Kennicutt & Evans relation  
  > "To estimate FIR luminosities, we normalise the assumed SED to the observed ALMA flux densities." (2.5 Global SFRs); "\log {\rm SFR}_{\rm FIR}({M_{\odot}\,\rm yr^{-1}}) = \log L_{\rm FIR (8-1000\mu m)} ({\rm erg\,s^{-1}}) -  43.47" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - software: Monte-Carlo sampling (custom)
   - parameter: dust emissivity index beta = 1.8 fixed  
  > "assuming a fixed power-law index for the dust emissivity, $\beta = 1.8$" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - parameter: upper limits for non-detections: 5 x r.m.s. of the tapered image  
  > "we use global upper limits using $5\times$ the r.m.s. of the tapered image" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF  
  > "assuming a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: Chabrier2003)." (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - assumption: template-based dust temperature uncertainty of about 5 K  
  > "a scatter $\sigma_{T_{\rm dust}} = 5$ K" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - fitting: modified-blackbody fit to templates; Monte-Carlo sampling of T_dust (Gaussian, sigma 5 K) and ALMA flux density  
  > "We run a Monte-Carlo simulation to sample the dust temperature, assuming a Gaussian distribution centred at $T_{\rm dust}$ from the FIR SED templates" (2.5 Global SFRs); "We fit each FIR template SED following the modified-blackbody fitting method described by (cite: Beelen2006)" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - validation: dust masses from SED and MAGPHYS found consistent  
  > "the dust masses derived from SED fitting and the MAGPHYS are consistent" (2.5 Global SFRs)
5. **refit SEDs with ALMA flux (MAGPHYS)**: re-fit SEDs of the four detected galaxies including the new ALMA FIR flux; for non-detections use MAGPHYS dust mass from optical-to-NIR SED  
  > "we re-fit the SEDs of the four ALMA detected galaxies including the FIR flux we obtained in this work" (2.4 MAGPHYS fitting with ALMA flux); "For the ALMA non-detected galaxies, we list the dust mass given by MAGPHYS from fitting the optical-to-NIR SEDs." (2.4 MAGPHYS fitting with ALMA flux)
   - software: MAGPHYS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: stellar and dust masses consistent between approaches  
  > "The stellar mass and dust mass show reasonable consistencies from the different approach." (2.4 MAGPHYS fitting with ALMA flux)
6. **extinction-corrected H-alpha SFR**: correct narrow-band H-alpha luminosity with A_Halpha(M*) polynomial and convert to SFR  
  > "we derive the extinction-corrected H$\alpha$ luminosity ($L^{\rm corr}_{\rm H\alpha}$) to obtain the SFR" (2.5 Global SFRs); "\log {\rm SFR}_{\rm H\alpha}({{\rm M}_{\odot}\,\rm yr^{-1}}) = \log L^{\rm corr}_{\rm H\alpha} ({\rm erg\,s^{-1}}) -  41.27" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - assumption: same Chabrier IMF  
  > "assuming the same Chabrier IMF." (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - assumption: extinction depends on stellar mass following Garn & Best 2010  
  > "we assume a parametrisation of the H$\alpha$ extinction as a function of stellar mass following (cite: Garn2010)" (2.5 Global SFRs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SFR_FIR and SFR_Halpha compared (Fig. 3)  
  > "A comparison between these two SFR estimates is shown in Fig." (2.5 Global SFRs)
7. **derive metallicity, gas-to-dust ratio and ISM mass**: 12+log(O/H) from [N II]/H-alpha; delta_GDR from metallicity relation; ISM mass from 355 um flux  
  > "estimate it from the \[N ii\]/H$\alpha$ emission line ratio (cite: Pettini2004; Curti2017)" (3.1 Stellar mass and metallicity); "We estimate ISM mass using the ALMA-derived 355 $\mu$m flux, following (cite: Scoville2016) (Eq 16)." (3.1 Stellar mass and metallicity)
   - assumption: gas-phase oxygen abundance as proxy for metallicity  
  > "We consider the gas-phase oxygen abundance as a proxy for metallicity" (3.1 Stellar mass and metallicity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: GDR-based dust masses in good agreement with SED-fitting values  
  > "these estimates are in good agreement with the values derived from SED fitting" (3.1 Stellar mass and metallicity)
8. **measure dust sizes and radial profiles**: uvmodelfit disc model to the uv visibilities; image-plane half-light radius from flux growth curve; Sersic fit to ring-aperture surface brightness profile in natural-weighted images  
  > "we fit the $uv$-real visibility of the ALMA detected targets by casa task uvmodelfit" (2.6 Dust radius); "we also measure the half-light radius ($R^{\rm half-light}_{\rm dust}$) of the ALMA images from the surface brightness distribution in the image plane" (2.6 Dust radius); "We fit the ALMA surface brightness profile with a Sersic function" (Appendix: Dust surface brightness profiles)
   - software: CASA, uvmodelfit
   - parameter: disc model in uvmodelfit; circularised radius sqrt(R_maj R_min)  
  > "We chose the $disc$ model in uvmodelfit to fit the $uv$ profile." (2.6 Dust radius); "The circularised radius $R^{\rm uvfit}_{\rm dust} = \sqrt{R_{\rm maj}\, R_{\rm min}}$" (2.6 Dust radius)
   - parameter: annuli in steps of 0.25'' centred on peak pixel of tapered images, measured in natural-weighted images  
  > "we perform aperture photometry by using a series of ring apertures in steps of $0.25\ensuremath{''}$ (similar to the synthesised beam) in the natural-weighted ALMA images" (Appendix: Dust surface brightness profiles)
   - fitting: uvmodelfit disc model; Sersic function to surface brightness profile  
  > "We chose the $disc$ model in uvmodelfit to fit the $uv$ profile." (2.6 Dust radius)
   - validation: different radius approaches agree at 4-5 +/- 0.5 kpc; half-light radius used because FIR morphology is irregular  
  > "The different approaches adopted for the measurements of the dust radii all show that the radii are 4-5 $\pm$ 0.5 kpc" (2.6 Dust radius)
9. **stack non-detections**: stack 1.0'' tapered images (8''x8'' stamps centred on optical positions), mean and median; peak to noise from 2-D Gaussian with fixed centre and FWHM 1''  
  > "we stack the ALMA tapered image which are generated at $1.0\ensuremath{''}$ resolution" (Appendix: Stacking analyses); "We generate postage stamps (of $8''\times8''$) for each ALMA continuum image, centred at the optical RA and Dec., to stack them based on median and average statistics" (Appendix: Stacking analyses)
   - assumption: point-like detections, Rayleigh-Jeans tail for ISM mass limits  
  > "The peak values are obtained from a 2-D Gaussian profile using a fixed centre and fixed width (FWHM) at 1$''$ (assuming point like detections)." (Appendix: Stacking analyses)
   - fitting: 2-D Gaussian peak with fixed centre and width  
  > "The peak values are obtained from a 2-D Gaussian profile using a fixed centre and fixed width (FWHM) at 1$''$" (Appendix: Stacking analyses)
   - validation: stacks only ~3 sigma and treated as non-detections  
  > "these peaks are only at $\sim$3$\sigma$ significance. We consider these stacks as non-detections." (Appendix: Stacking analyses)

## Software

- **CASA** : data reduction, tclean imaging, uvmodelfit  
  > "Data reduction was carried out using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (casa) and using the ALMA pipeline" (2.1 ALMA observations)
- **tclean** : continuum imaging with Briggs, natural and tapered weighting  
  > "Three imaging approaches were performed using the task tclean" (2.1 ALMA observations)
- **uvmodelfit** : disc model fit to uv visibilities for dust radius  
  > "we fit the $uv$-real visibility of the ALMA detected targets by casa task uvmodelfit" (2.6 Dust radius)
- **ALMA pipeline** : calibration up to calibrated uv data products  
  > "using the ALMA pipeline up to the production of calibrated $uv$ data products" (2.1 ALMA observations)
- **MAGPHYS** : SED fitting for stellar mass, SFR, dust mass, including ALMA flux  
  > "we re-fit the SEDs of the four ALMA detected galaxies including the FIR flux we obtained in this work" (2.4 MAGPHYS fitting with ALMA flux)
- **SExtractor** : generated the HiZELS catalogue used for astrometric alignment  
  > "The offset between the HiZELS catalogue generated by SExtractor and the *Gaia* catalogue" (2.2 Global astrometry)

## Qualitative findings

- ALMA continuum shows no clear counterparts to the H-alpha clumps at kpc scales  
  > "However, our ALMA continuum images do not show clear counterparts of the H$\alpha$ clumps, at least at $\sim$ kpc scales" (3.2.2 Spatial correlation between dust and ionised gas)
- Dust morphology is disk-like/smooth; Sersic indices below 2  
  > "Sersic indices n of the ALMA images are lower than $n=2$, implying a disk-like morphology of the dust emission." (Appendix: Dust surface brightness profiles)
- ALMA-detected galaxies are biased to massive, high-metallicity systems  
  > "thus our ALMA-detected sample is indeed biased towards massive and high-metallicity galaxies" (3.1 Stellar mass and metallicity)
- Dust radii and IR luminosities resemble local KINGFISH star-forming galaxies rather than SMGs  
  > "Our results show that the SHiZELS galaxies have very similar dust and H$\alpha$ radii as the star-forming galaxies from KINGFISH at low redshift" (3.2.2 Spatial correlation between dust and ionised gas)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Compare H-alpha/UV and dust emission at similar kpc-scale resolution for normal z~1.5 and 2.2 galaxies  
  > "to characterise the spatial correlation between the the H$\alpha$/UV and dust emission of high-redshift $z\simeq$ 1.47 and 2.23 galaxies" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Half-light radius also measured because FIR morphology is irregular and disk fits may not represent flux distribution; simple template SED used because only one FIR band  
  > "Since the FIR morphology is not regular, and thus the disk fitting results may not represent the flux distribution, we also measure the half-light radius" (2.6 Dust radius); "Since we only have one detected FIR band for most sources, we cannot sensibly adopt a more complex model to understand the FIR properties." (2.5 Global SFRs)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only one FIR band for most sources, so no more complex FIR model can be used  
  > "Since we only have one detected FIR band for most sources, we cannot sensibly adopt a more complex model to understand the FIR properties." (2.5 Global SFRs)
- Limitation (authors): Rest-frame UV/optical morphology can be misleading in dusty, star-forming sources  
  > "We caution that morphological information derived from the rest-frame UV/optical can be potentially very misleading in sources with substantial dust extinction and on-going star formation" (3.1 Individual galaxy properties)
- Limitation (authors): Stacks of non-detections reach only ~3 sigma and are treated as non-detections  
  > "these peaks are only at $\sim$3$\sigma$ significance. We consider these stacks as non-detections." (Appendix: Stacking analyses)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux densities and radii depend on which of the three imaging approaches was used; the choice is not stated per source
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sizes of ~0.25'' resolution data with 5-sigma detections on only 4 of 9 sources give a small, mass-biased sample
- Caveat (inferred, ours): FIR luminosity relies on a single ALMA band scaled to literature stack templates, so L_FIR and SFR_FIR are template-dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No uncertainty on uvmodelfit disc radii is described, and no simulated tests of recovery of sizes are given
