## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure the light distribution**: Fit the F444W image with single Sérsic, two Sérsic, and Sérsic plus point-source models; convolve intrinsic profiles with the empirical PSF.  
  > "we use `pysersic` (cite: Pasha2023) to perform Sérsic  fits to the 2D image of the galaxy." (Light-Weighted Structure); "we also perform two component modeling analysis, one with two Sérsic  profiles and another with a Sérsic  and point source component." (Light-Weighted Structure); "In our fitting, we adopt the empirical PSF measured in (cite: Weaver2024) and smooth all intrinsic profiles to the resolution of the F444W image before fitting." (Light-Weighted Structure)
   - software: pysersic
   - parameter: Component centers vary independently  
  > "For these, we allow the central locations of the components to vary independently." (Light-Weighted Structure)
   - parameter: PSF HWHM = 0.0725 inches of arc; use the compact component for annular geometry  
  > "following (cite: Suess2019a) and using the most compact effective radius as our benchmark $r_e$, and $r_\mathrm{PSF \ HWHM} = 0.0725$"." (Light-Weighted Structure); "we carry forward with this profile as our benchmark for annular extraction" (Light-Weighted Structure)
   - assumption: Physical effective radii corrected by dividing by √μ  
  > "The physical effective radii presented are corrected for the effect of gravitational lensing by dividing by $\sqrt{\mu}$." (Table 1)
   - fitting: Block Neural Auto regressive flow reparameterization; No U-turn sampler; two chains, 1000 warm-up and 1000 sampling steps  
  > "for each model we re-parameterize the posterior following the procedure in  (cite: Hoffman2019) using a Block Neural Auto regressive flow. (cite: DeCao2020)" (Light-Weighted Structure); "We then sample the posterior using a No U-turn sampler with two chains 1000 warm-up and 1000 sampling steps (cite: Hoffman2014; Phan2019)." (Light-Weighted Structure)
   - validation: r-hat < 1.01 and effective sample size > 300; compare χ² and residuals; conclusions insensitive to elliptical versus circular annuli  
  > "computing the $\hat{r}$ and effective sample size metrics which are $<1.01$ and $>300$ for all variables in all of the models" (Light-Weighted Structure); "Using $\chi^2$ as a goodness-of-fit metric, it is clear that the single Sérsic  model is disfavored relative to the two component models." (Light-Weighted Structure); "none of our conclusions are strongly impacted by the choice to extract photometry with elliptical versus circular annuli." (Light-Weighted Structure)
2. **Constrain star formation from Hα**: Flux-calibrate MSA3 and MSA4 to photometry and coadd; subtract a fitted stellar continuum; jointly fit Hα, [NII], and [SII] with Gaussians.  
  > "We then take the inverse variance weighted average of the MSA3 and MSA4 spectra to produce the flux calibrated data product that we will utilize for this analysis." (Spectroscopic Reduction); "We subtract the best fitting continuum model from our spectrum, and we then utilize `emcee` (cite: Foreman-Mackey2013) to fit the covariant posteriors of the line strengths." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "modeling $H\alpha$ along with the \[NII\] and \[SII\] doublets as Gaussians with tied $\sigma$" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: Second-order polynomial spectral calibration against 0.48-inch diameter aperture photometry  
  > "we fit a 2nd order polynomial to synthetic photometry measured from the MSA3 and MSA4 spectra" (Spectroscopic Reduction); "UNCOVER DR2 0.48" diameter aperture photometry (cite: Weaver2024)" (Spectroscopic Reduction); "we multiply the observed spectra and error vectors by the ratio of these polynomials." (Spectroscopic Reduction)
   - parameter: 0.63 μm < λ_rest < 0.68 μm; tied σ and one free redshift; [NII] ratio 1:3; [SII] ratio 0.4375–1.4484  
  > "we jointly fit the spectrum at $0.63 \ \mu m <\lambda_\mathrm{rest}< 0.68 \ \mu m$" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "and a single free redshift." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "We fix the flux ratio of the \[NII\] doublets at 1:3, and allow the ratio of \[SII\]$\lambda6716/\lambda6731$ to vary from 0.4375 to 1.4484 following (cite: Sanders2016)." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - parameter: Continuum SSP with free dust law, convolved to PRISM resolution; fit redward of 2 μm, mask Hα, double errors  
  > "we fit a simple stellar population with a free dust law, convolved to the wavelength dependent PRISM resolution, to the flux-calibrated spectrum redward of 2 $\mu$m." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "We mask $H\alpha$ in this fit but otherwise leave the spectrum free, and we double the error vector to account for uncertainties in the small scale flux calibration." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - assumption: Magnification correction; Hα-to-SFR coefficient 5.5 × 10^-42 (M_⊙ yr^-1)/(erg/s)  
  > "with the assumption of $5.5\times10^{-42} \ \frac{M_\odot \ \mathrm{yr^{-1}}}{\mathrm{erg/s}}$ (cite: Leitherer1999) after correcting for magnification" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - assumption: For obscured limits, A_v ∼ 1–3 magnitudes and the cited Kriek2016 dust law with dust index 0  
  > "Under the assumption of $A_v\sim1-3$ magnitudes and a (cite: Kriek2016) dust law with a dust index of 0" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - fitting: emcee sampling of covariant line-strength posteriors  
  > "we then utilize `emcee` (cite: Foreman-Mackey2013) to fit the covariant posteriors of the line strengths." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - validation: Independent corroboration from the DUALZ ALMA non-detection; treat Hα SFR as an upper limit because AGN ionization cannot be ruled out  
  > "also corroborated by the ALMA non-detection of the galaxy in the DUALZ survey (cite: Fujimoto2023dualZ)." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "This further motivates our choice to treat the $H\alpha$ star formation rate as an upper limit rather than a firm measurement." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
3. **Constrain obscured star formation**: Convert the DUALZ 1.2-mm non-detection to a lensing-corrected SFR upper limit with a modified black body FIR SED  
  > "Assuming a FIR SED with a modified black body ($T_\mathrm{dust}$ = 35K and spectral index = 1.8) and accounting for the magnification due to lensing" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "the ALMA non-detection places a 3$\sigma$ upper limit of $8.5 \ M_\odot \ \mathrm{yr^{-1}}$ on the galaxy star formation rate." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - parameter: Observed 3σ 1.2-mm flux upper limit: 113 μJy  
  > "The 3$\sigma$ upper limit at 1.2-mm flux observed is 113 $\mu$Jy." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - assumption: T_dust = 35K; spectral index = 1.8; lensing magnification accounted for  
  > "Assuming a FIR SED with a modified black body ($T_\mathrm{dust}$ = 35K and spectral index = 1.8) and accounting for the magnification due to lensing" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare with weak Hα; joint constraint SFR should not exceed ∼10 M_⊙ yr^-1  
  > "in conjunction with the weak observed $H\alpha$, it provides a strong constraint that the total star formation rate for UNCOVER 18407 should not exceed $\sim10$ $M_\odot$yr$^{-1}$." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
4. **Infer radial stellar populations**: Extract five elliptical annuli using the compact Sérsic geometry and fit their SEDs with non-parametric Prospector SFHs  
  > "Using the best-fitting structural parameters from the compact component of the two-component Sérsic  fit and the corrected axis ratio as in Section [3.1](#subsec:structure), we extract annular photometry in annular bins" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We extract annuli in five bins, out to $\sim3.6$ kpc" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We utilize the non-parametric star formation histories in `Prospector` (cite: Johnson2017; Leja2017; Johnson2021) to fit the annular photometry in nine fixed bins of constant star formation." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
   - software: Prospector, Flexible Stellar Population Synthesis, dynesty
   - parameter: Minimum annulus size 0.14 inches of arc (∼0.7 kpc); S/N threshold 20; use 19/20 bands, omitting F140M  
  > "with a minimum annulus size of 0.14" ($\sim$0.7 kpc), the PSF FWHM of F444W PSF-matched images (cite: Weaver2024)." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We enforce a signal-to-noise threshold of 20 on our photometry to account for systematic uncertainties in the models and the flux calibration (cite: WangB2024_uncoverpops)." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We perform our SED fits using 19/20 available bands, omitting F140M" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
   - parameter: SFH bin widths: 5, 25, 70, 100, 100, 150 Myr; final three bins logarithmically fill remaining cosmic time  
  > "5 Myr, 25 Myr, 70 Myr, 100 Myr, 100 Myr, 150 Myr, and the final three bins are set to logarithmically fill the remaining time since the Big Bang" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
   - parameter: A_v in [0,2.5], dust index in [-1,0.4]; stellar metallicity sampled linearly from 0.01Z_⊙ to 2Z_⊙; log(U) in [-4,-1]  
  > "We assume the (cite: Kriek2013) dust law with a free $A_v$ and dust index (spanning \[0,2.5\] and \[-1,0.4\] respectively)." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We leave stellar metallicity as a free parameter, and sample linearly in the range $0.01Z_\odot<Z<2Z_\odot$." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We include nebular emission in our fits, with the ionization parameter log(U) varying in the range \[-4,-1\] and the gas phase metallicity fixed to the stellar metallicity." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; spectroscopic redshift fixed; FSPS, MILES library, MIST isochrones  
  > "We assume a (cite: Chabrier2003) Initial Mass Function and fix the model redshift to the spectroscopic redshift." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We utilize the Flexible Stellar Population Synthesis (FSPS) stellar population synthesis models (cite: Conroy2009; Conroy2010)" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "the MILES spectral library (cite: Sanchez-Blazquez2006a) and MIST isochrones (cite: Choi2016; Dotter2016)." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
   - assumption: Young-star attenuation twice that of other stars; fixed Draine IR SED; no fitted IR AGN contribution  
  > "following (cite: Wild2020), we fix the optical depth of attenuation around young ($<10^7$ yr) stars to be double the optical depth for the rest of the stars." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "we fix the shape of the IR SED following the (cite: Draine2007) dust emission templates, with $U_\mathrm{min}$ = 1.0, $\gamma_e$ = 0.01, and $q_\mathrm{PAH}$ = 2.0." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "For similar reasons, we do not fit for any IR AGN contribution." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)

  > "following the continuity prior in (cite: Leja2019), the logarithmic difference between the SFR in neighboring bins is sampled over with a Student-T distribution prior centered at 0 with a width of 0.3 and $\nu=2$." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting); "We sample our posteriors using the `dynesty` nested sampling package (cite: Speagle2020)." (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Test a dust-free old core**: Compare central, integrated, and outer rest-frame UVJ colors with solar-metallicity SSP tracks

## Qualitative findings

- The single Sérsic model is disfavored; the two-Sérsic model is only marginally preferred over Sérsic plus point source.  
  > "Using $\chi^2$ as a goodness-of-fit metric, it is clear that the single Sérsic  model is disfavored relative to the two component models." (Light-Weighted Structure); "the two component Sérsic  model is only marginally preferred over the Sérsic  +point source model." (Light-Weighted Structure)
- The central colors require significant extinction; integrated colors can admit a dust-free old population.  
  > "the dust free curve does not reach high enough values in $U-V$ or $V-J$ to produce the observed central colors." (Can the core be maximally old and dust free?); "the integrated galaxy $UVJ$ colors are still within the realm of possibility for a dust free SSP" (Can the core be maximally old and dust free?)
- Flat age gradients remain allowed; strong positive age gradients are ruled out.  
  > "we are unable to rule out flat gradients due to the uncertainty in the mass weighted age measurements." (Radial dust and age gradients); "We can, however, conclusively rule out any strong positive age gradients." (Radial dust and age gradients)
- The stellar mass distribution is comparably dense to z ∼ 2 quiescent systems and more compact than typical star-forming galaxies at similar mass.  
  > "the structure of UNCOVER 18407 is comparably dense to $z\sim2$ quiescent systems, and has a mass distribution that is more compact than typical star forming galaxies at similar mass." (Implications for dense core formation in the first Gyr of cosmic time)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA non-detection independently constrains dust-obscured star formation and dust mass.  
  > "also corroborated by the ALMA non-detection of the galaxy in the DUALZ survey (cite: Fujimoto2023dualZ)." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "we also use the ALMA Band 6 non-detection (cite: Fujimoto2023dualZ) to constrain the dust mass under the assumption of cold dust" (Constraints on the Dust and Gas Mass)
- Why this method: Model annuli because strong dust gradients are smoothed over by global photometry.  
  > "This method of determining the total star formation history is preferred over a global SED fit due to the strong dust gradients, which are smoothed over when all photometry is summed." (Global galaxy properties)
- Alternative: Single Sérsic, two-Sérsic, and Sérsic plus point-source structural models compared.  
  > "in addition to a traditional single component Sérsic  fit, we also perform two component modeling analysis, one with two Sérsic  profiles and another with a Sérsic  and point source component." (Light-Weighted Structure)
- Alternative: Exclude MSA2 from the coadd because it misses the galaxy core.  
  > "the MSA2 slit is missing the core of the galaxy, as evidenced by the significantly lower flux in the spectrum. As such, we elect to combine only MSA3 and MSA4 in our coadd." (Spectroscopic Reduction)
- Alternative: Use empty slits rather than vertically shifted spectra for background subtraction to avoid self-subtraction.  
  > "Because the source is extended, we do not elect to background subtract using vertically shifted spectra, but instead utilize empty slits to avoid self-subtraction." (Spectroscopic Reduction)
- Alternative: Reject joint spectrophotometric fitting because color gradients and differential slit losses prevent robust spectral-shape recovery.  
  > "the strong gradients measured in this section preclude the possibility of performing a combined spectrophotometric fit for this galaxy." (Global galaxy properties); "we are not confident we can robustly recover the shape of the spectrum and the corresponding photometric aperture after correcting for slit losses." (Global galaxy properties)
- Alternative: Omit F140M because its colors disagree with F150W without a corresponding strong spectral feature.  
  > "omitting F140M as it exhibits discrepant colors with F150W despite the lack of any strong features in that part of the spectrum blueward of the break" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
- How they decided it was enough: Structural sampling checked with r-hat < 1.01 and effective sample size > 300 for all variables.  
  > "computing the $\hat{r}$ and effective sample size metrics which are $<1.01$ and $>300$ for all variables in all of the models" (Light-Weighted Structure)
- Limitation (authors): ALMA-derived SFR and mass limits depend sensitively on assumed dust temperature.  
  > "This limit is quite sensitive to the assumed dust temperature" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "However, we note that this upper limit is quite sensitive to the assumed dust temperature (cite: Cochrane2022)." (Constraints on the Dust and Gas Mass)
- Limitation (authors): PRISM cannot effectively deblend Hα and [NII]; a dust-obscured AGN cannot be excluded.  
  > "At the NIRSpec/PRISM resolution, we are not able to deblend $H\alpha$ and \[NII\] effectively" (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints); "we cannot conclusively rule out a dust-obscured AGN, and therefore cannot conclusively ascribe all $H\alpha$ flux to star formation." (Hα and FIR Star Formation Rate Constraints)
- Limitation (authors): Age gradients are uncertain and age-dust degeneracy is not fully disentangled.  
  > "there are also hints of a negative age gradient, and we cannot entirely disentangle these trends due to age-dust degeneracy." (Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): No observed wavelengths beyond approximately rest-frame 1 μm constrain the IR SED shape.  
  > "Because at $z_{spec}=3.97$ even the reddest NIRCam/NIRSpec does not constrain wavelengths $\gtrsim1 \ \mu$m" (Annular Stellar Population Fitting)
- Limitation (authors): A single galaxy cannot establish how common dusty quiescence is.  
  > "With a sample of one galaxy, we cannot yet quantify how common this dusty phase of quiescence is." (Constraints on the Dust and Gas Mass)
- Limitation (authors): Deeper sub-mm observations are needed to quantify the remaining ISM reservoirs.  
  > "significantly deeper sub-mm observations would be needed to quantify the remaining ISM reservoirs" (A old, centrally dusty, quiescent galaxy at z_spec = 3.97)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The Conclusions call the FIR result a detection, whereas the analysis repeatedly identifies an ALMA non-detection.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 35K SFR limit and 20K dust-mass limit use different temperature assumptions and should be reproduced separately.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): An attenuation gradient does not directly measure a dust-mass distribution.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The marginal preference over Sérsic plus point source limits confidence in the resolved central component.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The treatment of covariance between annular posteriors is unspecified.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The descendant interpretation is indirect and based on one galaxy.
