## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and combine measurement sets**: CASA 5.1.1 standard pipeline calibration, visual inspection, concat per target; archival data re-calibrated the same way  
  > "We visually inspected the pipeline calibrated data and used the casa / concat task to combine the observations of each target into a single measurement set for imaging." (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: phase calibrator J 0948+0022 on a 7 min cycle; flux and bandpass on J 1058+0133  
  > "which was observed periodically on a 7 min cycle" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction); "while the absolute flux scale and bandpass were set on J 1058+0133" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: visual inspection of the pipeline-calibrated data; minor issues (e.g. additional channel flagging) corrected for archival data  
  > "Each of the calibrated data sets was visually inspected and any minor issues were corrected (e.g. additional channel flagging)." (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
2. **image the continuum maps**: tclean with Briggs weighting, Gaussian uv taper to common ~0.8 arcsec beam, two-step auto-masked clean  
  > "we use the tclean task within casa and a two-step procedure" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "adopting Briggs weighting (robust parameter = 0.5)" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
   - parameter: second clean: sources >= 4.25 sigma cleaned to 1 sigma with auto-masking  
  > "any sources detected at $\ge$ 4.25 $\sigma$ and clean these to 1" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
   - parameter: map sensitivity estimated by iterative sigma-clipping  
  > "was estimated using an iterative, sigma-clipping technique" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: beam homogeneity checked: variation < 0.02 arcsec across 181 of 182 maps  
  > "with a variation of $<$ 0.02$''$ across 181 out of 182 maps" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
3. **extract the source catalogue**: SExtractor >2 sigma peaks in non-primary-beam-corrected maps, peak and aperture flux; significance cut set where inverted-map false detections reach zero  
  > "we first use sextractor (cite: Bertin96) to identify any" (2.3 Source Extraction); "we invert the ALMA maps and repeat our source extraction procedure" (2.3 Source Extraction)
   - software: SExtractor
   - parameter: aperture diameter 1.5 x synthesized beam major-axis FWHM  
  > "using a aperture with a diameter 1.5 $\times$ the major axis (FWHM) of the synthesized beam" (2.3 Source Extraction)
   - parameter: within primary beam: peak > 4.8 sigma or aperture > 4.9 sigma  
  > "falls to zero at a peak or aperture significance of $>$ 4.8 $\sigma$ and $>$ 4.9 $\sigma$, respectively" (2.3 Source Extraction)
   - parameter: aperture-flux uncertainty from 100 random apertures on source-subtracted maps  
  > "The associated uncertainty on the integrated fluxes is calculated by placing 100 apertures at random on the source-subtracted ALMA maps and taking the standard deviation of the resulting aperture flux densities." (2.3 Source Extraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: false detections estimated from inverted maps  
  > "we invert the ALMA maps and repeat our source extraction procedure" (2.3 Source Extraction)
4. **measure total flux densities and correct flux boosting**: uv-plane fit of Sersic profiles with proFit + galario + lmfit; correction from simulated injections  
  > "Calibrated visibilities for each ALMA target were extracted using casa and modelled using a custom written code utilising three publically-available packages." (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - software: CASA, proFit, galario, lmfit
   - parameter: five free parameters: R.A., Dec., flux density, half-light radius, axial ratio  
  > "we leave five parameters of our model free to vary (R.A., Dec., flux density, half-light radius, and axial ratio)" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - parameter: optimisation repeated ten times from random starting values; lowest chi-square kept  
  > "repeating the parameter optimisation ten times using randomly-selected starting parameter values" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - parameter: boosting correction from running median of recovered/input flux vs detection significance  
  > "We use the running median to correct the flux densities of the sources in our AS2COSMOS catalogue, based on their detection significance" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - assumption: Sersic index fixed at n = 1  
  > "we fix the Sersic index at $n$ = 1" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - fitting: chi-square minimisation of observed vs model visibilities with lmfit  
  > "and the lmfit non-linear optimisation suite" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - validation: 10^6 injected-source simulations; Sersic fluxes agree with aperture fluxes (after aperture correction) at the +/-1.5 per cent level  
  > "agreement with the results of our Sersic profile fitting at the $\pm$ 1.5 per cent level, on average" (2.3 Flux Estimation); "Each simulated data set is constructed by injecting a single model source into the residual data for a randomly selected AS2COSMOS target." (2.3 Flux Estimation)
5. **estimate completeness of the ALMA survey**: Monte Carlo injection of model sources with random sizes and fluxes into residual maps, then rerun detection and modelling  
  > "We run our source-detection and visibility-modelling procedures on the simulated data" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - parameter: axial ratio uniform 0-1; half-light radius uniform 0.05-0.30 arcsec  
  > "drawn at random from a uniform distribution between zero and one, and 0.05$''$ and 0.30$''$, respectively" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - assumption: input fluxes sampled from the Hatsukade et al. counts with low flux cut-off 0.05 mJy  
  > "is randomly sampled from the parameterised estimate of the sub- / millimetre counts presented by (cite: Hatsukade18)" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: completeness > 99.9 per cent for sources with half-light radii < 1 arcsec  
  > "we estimate that our survey is $>$ 99.9 per cent complete for sources with half-light radii $<$ 1$''$" (2.4 Completeness and Flux Recovery)
6. **compare ALMA and SCUBA-2 flux scales with end-to-end simulations**: convolve ALMA model images with the SCUBA-2 beam; compare to observed and deboosted SCUBA-2 fluxes; 100 end-to-end S2COSMOS simulations to separate Eddington bias from line-of-sight multiplicity  
  > "We create 100 simulated maps of the S2COSMOS survey following the prescription in (cite: Simpson19)" (2.4 Completeness and Flux Recovery)
   - parameter: emitters >= 0.05 mJy injected within 8.7 arcsec of recovered SCUBA-2 positions  
  > "that were injected within 8.7$''$ of each of the recovered SCUBA-2 positions" (2.4 Completeness and Flux Recovery)
   - assumption: input counts model is the best-fit Schechter parameterisation (no clustering)  
  > "with the best-fit parameterisation for the sub-millimetre number counts as the input model" (2.4 Completeness and Flux Recovery)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: median convolved ALMA/deboosted SCUBA-2 flux ratio 0.99 +/- 0.01  
  > "we find a median ratio of convolved ALMA to deboosted SCUBA-2 flux density of 0.99 $\pm$ 0.01" (2.4 Completeness and Flux Recovery)
7. **assemble multiwavelength photometry**: cross-match ALMA positions with COSMOS2015, HSC, IRAC (iraclean deblending), MIPS/PACS (super-deblended), SPIRE (prior-based deblending), VLA 3 GHz and Chandra catalogues  
  > "To estimate deblended IRAC photometry for the AS2COSMOS SMGs we again use iraclean but update the prior" (Mid-infrared imaging)
   - software: iraclean, casa / imfit
   - parameter: optical/near-IR matching radius 0.85 arcsec (false-match ~6.6 per cent)  
  > "The probability of a false-match is estimated at $\sim$ 6.6 per cent at a separation of 0.85$''$ and we adopt this as our matching radius." (Optical-to-Near-infrared imaging)
   - parameter: 3 GHz matching radius 1 arcsec  
  > "identify 191 counterparts to the AS2COSMOS SMGs within a matching radius of 1$''$" (Radio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: false-match probabilities estimated from random positions  
  > "We construct a catalogue of 10$^{4}$ random positions across the field and cross match these with the COSMOS2015 source positions." (Optical-to-Near-infrared imaging)
8. **SED fitting and photometric redshifts**: magphys energy-balance SED fitting to the multiwavelength photometry, median of redshift PDF  
  > "we now use the magphys spectral energy distribution (SED) modelling programme (cite: daCunha08; daCunha15; Battisti19) to fit the multiwavelength SEDs of these sources." (2.6 Panchromatic SED fitting)
   - software: magphys
   - parameter: latest magphys version (Battisti et al.), optimised for high-redshift star-forming galaxies, fits redshift  
  > "We used the latest version of magphys (cite: Battisti19)" (2.6 Panchromatic SED fitting)
   - fitting: chi-square matching of model SED library to photometry; median redshift taken from the PDF  
  > "The code selects models that best-fit the multi-wavelength photometry by matching the model SEDs to the data using a $\chi^2$ test" (2.6 Panchromatic SED fitting)
   - validation: photometric-redshift precision tested in the cited AS2UDS work against ~7,000 spectroscopic galaxies  
  > "These include testing of the precision of the derived photometric redshifts using a sample of $\sim$ 7,000 spectroscopically-identified galaxies" (2.6 Panchromatic SED fitting)
9. **derive 870-um number counts and fit a Schechter function**: completeness-corrected cumulative and differential counts over 1.6 deg^2; Schechter fit jointly to AS2COSMOS and literature ALMA counts  
  > "The counts are constructed to a lower flux limit of 6.2 mJy, corrected for sample incompleteness using the completeness curve" (3.1 Number counts); "We assume that the sub-millimetre counts follow a simple Schechter function" (3.1 Number counts)
   - parameter: 10^4 catalogue realisations with fluxes perturbed by their uncertainties, combined with Poisson errors  
  > "constructing 10$^{4}$ realisations of the AS2COSMOS source catalogue" (3.1 Number counts)
   - assumption: 1.2-mm ASAGAO counts converted to 870 um with a modified blackbody, beta = 1.8, T = 32 K, z = 2.6  
  > "assuming a modified blackbody with $\beta=$ 1.8 and dust temperature of 32 K, at a redshift of $z=$ 2.6" (3.1 Number counts)
   - fitting: Schechter function fit to combined counts; algorithm and likelihood not stated  
  > "We assume that the sub-millimetre counts follow a simple Schechter function" (3.1 Number counts)
   - validation: reduced chi-square = 1.5 at 0.4-20 mJy  
  > "provides a reasonable description of the observed counts (reduced $\chi^{2}=$ 1.5)" (3.1 Number counts)
10. **estimate cosmic variance in the counts**: split AS2COSMOS + AS2UDS into 26 sub-regions of ~0.1 deg^2, combine into 0.1-0.8 deg^2 surveys, subtract Poisson and statistical variance, compare with galform  
  > "we first sub-divide the AS2COSMOS and AS2UDS surveys into 26 regions each with an area of $\sim$ 0.1 deg$^2$" (3.2 Cosmic Variance)
   - software: galform
   - parameter: 10^3 Monte Carlo realisations per sub-field for the statistical uncertainty; Gehrels Poisson errors  
  > "comprising 10$^3$ realisations of the integrated counts for each sub-field" (3.2 Cosmic Variance)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: compared with variance in 20 deg^2 of galform light-cones  
  > "We construct a 20 deg$^2$ of model sub-millimetre sky using galform, in five distinct light-cones" (3.2 Cosmic Variance)
11. **measure multiplicity and physical association of SMG pairs**: count SCUBA-2 sources with two or more SMGs (S870 >= 1 mJy) in the ALMA primary beam; compare with the unclustered end-to-end simulation to infer the physically associated fraction  
  > "two-or-more SMGs with flux densities $S_{870\mu \rm m}\ge$ 1 mJy are identified within the primary beam of the corresponding ALMA map" (3.3 Multiplicity)
   - parameter: primary beam radius 8.7 arcsec  
  > "(i.e. within 8.7$''$ of the SCUBA-2 position)" (3.3 Multiplicity)
   - assumption: simulation input has no clustering, so all simulated multiples are line-of-sight chance associations  
  > "The input model for the simulation does not include clustering" (3.3 Multiplicity)
   - fitting: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: retained targets with prior interferometric data to keep the survey homogeneous at 870 um  
  > "To ensure that AS2COSMOS represents a homogenous study into the 850 $\mu$m-luminous population we retained these 45 targets in our ALMA Cycle-4 programme" (2.1 Sample Selection)
- Why this method: Sersic profile adopted because comparable high-resolution SMG data are well described by it  
  > "High-resolution ALMA imaging of comparable samples of single-dish-identified SMGs indicate that the observed 870 $\mu$m emission can be well-described by a Sersic profile" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
- Alternative: Super-GOODS sample not used for the multiplicity comparison at matched flux limit  
  > "We do not consider the Super-GOODS sample presented by (cite: Cowie18) as it contains only four SCUBA-2 sources with flux densities above the S2COSMOS selection limit." (3.3 Multiplicity)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): multiplicity is a lower limit because the sample is very incomplete near 1 mJy  
  > "our estimate of the multiplicity of SCUBA-2 sources is undoubtably a lower limit" (3.3 Multiplicity)
- Limitation (authors): observational constraints on projected versus associated companions are weak  
  > "current observational constraints on the relative mix of projected and associated companions in blended SMG maps are weak" (3.3 Multiplicity)
- Limitation (authors): simulation-based line-of-sight correction assumes no clustering  
  > "the statistical correction for line-of-sight multiplicity assumes no clustering" (2.4 Completeness and Flux Recovery)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The fraction of physically associated pairs rests on an unclustered simulation whose input counts model is itself fitted from these data, so the inference is partly circular.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample selection from a preliminary S2COSMOS catalogue plus archival fields of mixed depth makes the completeness correction (22 per cent at 6.2 mJy) significant for the counts normalisation.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Photometric redshifts and the flux-redshift trend depend on magphys settings that are only described in companion papers.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 1.4-2.0x higher counts than AS2UDS are attributed to cosmic variance at only ~2.3 sigma.

## Reproducibility

- Data public (paper): seven archival ALMA projects described as publicly available; Cycle-4 project ID given  
  > "which were observed in seven publicly-available, archival ALMA projects" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction)
- Products in archive: measurement sets retrieved from the ALMA archive and re-calibrated; reduced maps and the full catalogue are not stated as released beyond the online journal  
  > "we retrieved the relevant measurement sets from the ALMA archive" (2.2 ALMA Data Reduction); "The full catalogue is available in the online journal." (Table 1)
- Code: custom visibility-fitting code built on public packages; no release stated  
  > "modelled using a custom written code utilising three publically-available packages" (2.3 Flux Estimation)
- Missing: tclean parameters beyond robust, thresholds and pixel scale; per-map uv-taper values
- Missing: S2COSMOS SCUBA-2 catalogue/deboosting factors and the end-to-end simulation code from Simpson et al. 2019
- Missing: magphys configuration and multiwavelength photometry tables (in Ikarashi et al. 2020)
- Missing: mapping between the seven archival projects and the 24 sources, and their pipeline versions
- Data volume (inferred): Roughly 100-500 GB of raw measurement sets for the four Cycle-4 MSs plus seven archival projects; images are small (25.6 x 25.6 arcsec each, 0.1 arcsec pixels).
- Score (inferred): 3/5, ALMA continuum steps (pipeline, tclean robust 0.5, taper to ~0.8 arcsec, SExtractor thresholds, Sersic uv-fit) and counts/multiplicity numbers are given with numeric results, but the parent catalogue, tclean details, SED-fit settings and several simulation details are deferred to other papers.
- Rediscovery candidate: yes, Archival projects are stated to be public and the continuum imaging, source extraction, flux fitting, counts and multiplicity recipe is largely complete with numeric answers; the SED/photo-z part is not self-contained.
