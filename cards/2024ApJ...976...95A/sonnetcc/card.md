## Analysis recipe

1. **fit laboratory rotational constants**: Combined fit of microwave and millimeter-wave transitions with weights using Watson's A-reduced Hamiltonian, I^r representation  
  > "The final fit, combining the transitions from both microwave and millimeter-wave experiments with their corresponding weights, was obtained using Watson’s A-reduced Hamiltonian in the I$^r$-representation" (Analysis of the spectra)
   - software: SPFIT/SPCAT
   - parameter: 13 microwave transitions (43 hyperfine components) at assumed accuracy 10 kHz; 271 additional millimeter-wave transitions at assumed accuracy 30 kHz  
  > "A total of 13 transitions with 43 hyperfine components were measured with an assumed frequency accuracy of 10 kHz." (Analysis of the spectra); "to measure a total of 271 additional transitions of the molecule with an assumed frequency accuracy of 30 kHz" (Analysis of the spectra)
   - fitting: Watson A-reduced semi-rigid rotor Hamiltonian plus nuclear quadrupole coupling term, fitted with SPFIT  
  > "using a Watson’s A-reduced semi-rigid rotor analysis in the I$^{r}$-representation, adding a term to account for the nuclear quadrupole coupling contribution" (Analysis of the spectra)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **compute rotational partition function**: Boltzmann-factor summation over ground-vibrational-state levels with SPCAT using the fitted constants, up to J=250 and Ka=150  
  > "We used the SPCAT program (cite: pickett1998) to undertake this summation numerically employing the spectroscopic constants from Table [\[constants\]](#constants) and all rotational states up to J= 250 and $K_{a}$= 150." (Sgr B2N)
   - software: SPCAT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **ALMA upper limit on HCCSCN in NGC 6334I**: Search near 280.8 GHz at the extraction position with fixed Tex, linewidth and Vlsr from the earlier methoxymethanol work, source assumed to fill the beam; derive N_T upper limit, then fractional abundance using N(H2)  
  > "We use the same excitation temperature ($T_{ex} = 200$ K), linewidth ($\Delta V = 2.4$ km s$^{-1}$), and $V_{lsr}$ = -7 km s$^{-1}$ as in that work, and assume the source fills the beam." (NGC 6334I); "We derive an upper limit of $N_T \leq 5\times10^{15}$ cm$^{-2}$." (NGC 6334I)
   - parameter: Tex = 200 K; linewidth 2.4 km/s; Vlsr = -7 km/s; hyperfine splitting unresolved  
  > "As with Sgr B2(N), hyperfine splitting is unresolved." (NGC 6334I); "We use the same excitation temperature ($T_{ex} = 200$ K), linewidth ($\Delta V = 2.4$ km s$^{-1}$)" (NGC 6334I)
   - assumption: Source fills the beam  
  > "and assume the source fills the beam" (NGC 6334I)
   - assumption: N(H2) = 3e24 cm^-2 from Herschel over a homogeneous 10 arcsec hot-core region; true gas distribution is heterogeneous  
  > "assume a homogenous gas distribution over a 10$^{\prime\prime}$ emitting region toward the hot core" (NGC 6334I); "but that the underlying gas distribution is heterogenous" (NGC 6334I)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **upper limits in other sources with single-dish data**: Synthetic LTE spectra (single-excitation model for Sgr B2(N); MADCUBA SLIM for G+0.693; MADEX for TMC-1) compared with survey data; 3 sigma upper limits on column density  
  > "We therefore searched for HCCSCN under two sets of conditions using a single-excitation model" (Sgr B2N); "Using the SLIM (Spectral Line Identification and Modeling) tool of MADCUBA, we generated a synthetic spectra under the assumption of Local Thermodynamic Equilibrium (LTE)" (G+0.693-0.027); "The spectroscopic information of HCCSCN derived in this work has been implemented in the MADEX" (TMC-1)
   - software: MADCUBA, MADEX
   - parameter: Sgr B2(N): cold Tex 5.8 K with 20 arcsec source, warm Tex 150 K with 5 arcsec source; linewidth 8 km/s; Vlsr +64 km/s  
  > "a cold model with $T_{ex} = 5.8$ K with a 20$^{\prime\prime}$ source size and a warm model with $T_{ex} = 150$ K with a 5$^{\prime\prime}$ source size" (Sgr B2N); "The linewidth was 8 km s$^{-1}$ and the $V_{LSR}$ was +64 km s$^{-1}$ in both cases." (Sgr B2N)
   - parameter: G+0.693: linewidth 20 km/s, Tex 8 K, velocity 68 km/s  
  > "line width of 20 km s$^{-1}$, excitation temperature of 8 K, and a velocity of 68 km s$^{-1}$" (G+0.693-0.027)
   - parameter: TMC-1: linewidth 0.6 km/s, rotational temperature 5 K, source diameter 80 arcsec, upper-level energy limited to 14 K  
  > "The adopted linewidth is 0.6 km s$^{-1}$" (TMC-1); "We also adopted a rotational temperature of 5 K similar to that derived for HCSCN." (TMC-1); "we limited the energy of the upper levels of the transitions to 14 K" (TMC-1)
   - assumption: LTE for G+0.693 modelling  
  > "under the assumption of Local Thermodynamic Equilibrium (LTE)" (G+0.693-0.027)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Software

- **SPFIT/SPCAT** : Fit of the laboratory transitions and prediction  
  > "SPFIT/SPCAT program suite" (Analysis of the spectra)
- **SPCAT** : Rotational partition function by numerical summation  
  > "We used the SPCAT program" (Sgr B2N)
- **Gaussian 16** : B3LYP-D3(BJ) and MP2 geometry optimization and vibrational calculations of HCCSCN  
  > "methods implemented in Gaussian 16" (Quantum-chemical calculations)
- **MADCUBA (SLIM)** 04/07/2023: LTE synthetic spectra and 3 sigma upper limit for G+0.693  
  > "version 04/07/2023" (G+0.693-0.027); "We have thus derived with SLIM a 3$\sigma$ upper limit (in integrated intensity)" (G+0.693-0.027)
- **MADEX** : Modelling expected line intensities for the TMC-1 search  
  > "In order to model the expected intensity we have assumed a source of uniform brightness temperature with a diameter of 80$''$" (TMC-1)
- **Molpro** 2020.2: CCSD(T)/cc-pVTZ potential energy surface exploration of formation reactions  
  > "The exploration of the potential energy surface for the different reactions was done using the Molpro 2020.2" (Astrochemical implications)

## Qualitative findings

- HCCSCN is not detected toward any of the four sources; precise laboratory constants now allow future searches  
  > "This new laboratory data allowed us to search for it towards several interstellar sources without success." (Conclusions)
