## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA continuum and cubes**: CLEAN with natural and robust 0.5 weighting in CASA  
  > "Data were mapped using the CLEAN algorithm with “natural” and robust 0.5 weighting" (2.3 ALMA 870 um)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum flux densities and sizes**: Two-dimensional Gaussian fitting of the two components  
  > "Two-dimensional Gaussian fitting yields deconvolved sizes of (0.303$\pm$0.030)$\times$(0.213$\pm$0.027)" (Continuum Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Find lines and measure redshift and line properties**: Search 3 mm sweep; Gaussian fits to line profiles for flux, FWHM; unique redshift solution  
  > "From Gaussian fitting to the line profiles, we obtain peak flux densities of" (Line Emission)
   - fitting: Gaussian line profile fits  
  > "From Gaussian fitting to the line profiles, we obtain peak flux densities of" (Line Emission)
   - validation: Redshift of three features consistent; H2O tentative  
  > "The H$_2$O(2$_{11}$$\to$2$_{02}$) line recovery is marginal at best and near the edge of the spectral range." (Line Emission)
4. **Fit SED for dust temperature, L_IR, dust mass, SFR**: Modified blackbody plus blue power law, optically thin and general (optical depth) models, affine-invariant MCMC, 500 um flux as normalization  
  > "We fit optically-thin models, with the power-law slope $\alpha$, the dust temperature $T_{\rm dust}$, and the spectral slope of the dust emissivity $\beta_{\rm IR}$ as fitting parameters" (3.1 Spectral Energy Distribution Properties)
   - assumption: kappa=2.64 m2/kg at 125 um; Chabrier IMF  
  > "Assuming a (cite: chabrier03) stellar initial mass function, these parameters suggest a total star formation rate (SFR) of $\sim$2400 $M_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$." (3.1 Spectral Energy Distribution Properties)
   - fitting: affine-invariant MCMC; priors not stated  
  > "using an affine-invariant Markov Chain Monte-Carlo (MCMC) approach, and joining the MBB to a $\nu^\alpha$ power law on the blue side of the SED peak" (Spectral Energy Distribution Properties)
   - validation: L_IR agrees within ~2% with HFLS3 template integration  
  > "The measured $L_{\rm IR}$ agrees to within $\sim$2% with independent estimates" (Footnote 8)
5. **Derive molecular gas mass, gas-to-dust, depletion time**: L'CO(1-0) from CO(5-4) with r51=0.39, times alpha_CO  
  > "The $L'_{\rm CO(1-0)}$ value of ADFS-27 (based on the adopted $r_{51}$=0.39) implies a total molecular gas mass" (3.2 Molecular Gas Mass)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8 Msun (K km/s pc2)^-1; r51=0.39  
  > "We here adopt a conversion factor of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$$=$0.8 $M_{\odot}$ (K kms$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$" (Footnote 10)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Derive surface densities, dynamical masses, alpha_CO limit**: Sizes and flux ratio from 870 um; isotropic virial estimator with radii x1.5; common linewidth  
  > "Assuming a common CO linewidth and using the sizes and flux ratio measured in the 870 $\mu$m continuum emission" (3.3 Star Formation Rate and Gas Surface Densities)
   - assumption: 100% of dynamical mass is molecular gas for alpha_CO upper limit  
  > "conservatively assuming that 100% of the dynamical mass is due to molecular gas" (3.3 Star Formation Rate and Gas Surface Densities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- ADFS-27 is a binary HyLIRG, likely a major merger at z~6, with maximum-starburst SFR surface densities  
  > "We have identified a massive, dust-obscured binary HyLIRG at a redshift of $z$=5.655, using ALMA." (Conclusions)
