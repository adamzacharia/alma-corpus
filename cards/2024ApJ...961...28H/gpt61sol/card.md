  > "For this paper, we have fixed the location of the arcs at a single $R_{\rm arc}$. In total, we have 18 free parameters." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
   - parameter: Fit intensity, radial and angular locations and widths, PA, inclination, and phase-center offsets  
  > "The free parameters are intensity $A$, radial position of the arc $R_{\rm arc}$, the width in radial direction $\sigma_r$" ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "the width in the position angle directions $\sigma_{\theta_1}$ and $\sigma_{\theta_2}$, position angle $PA$, inclination $i$, and the offset from the phase center ($\Delta \alpha$, $\Delta \delta$)." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "the angular position $\theta_1$ or $\theta_2$" ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
   - assumption: Arc model based on the vortex description of (cite: lyra13), also (cite: cazzoletti18)  
  > "The arc model is based on a description of a vortex in a protoplanetary disk by (cite: lyra13) (see also (cite: cazzoletti18))." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)

  > "The best-fit parameters are determined using *Galario* and the *emcee* packages (cite: emcee) to explore the parameter space efficiently." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "For each model, Gaussian and GR2ARC, we performed 10000 steps with a burn-in that is determined through the autocorrelation function ($\sim 1500-2000$ steps)." 4. **Measure continuum profiles and spectral indices**: Deproject with fitted inclination and PA; calculate α = log(I_B3/I_B6)/log(ν_B3/ν_B6) from matched images  
  > "using the inclination and position angles found in Sect." (Dust continuum emission around the primary); "We directly calculate the dust spectral index $\alpha_{\rm B6 - B3}$ in the image plane by evaluating $\log \left ( I_{B3} / I_{B6} \right )/\log \left ( \nu_{\rm B3}/\nu_{\rm B6} \right )$." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - software: CASA, FIXPLANETS, TCLEAN
   - parameter: Pixels with intensities >5σ in both bands  
  > "The value $\alpha_{\rm B6-B3}$ is evaluated using pixels with intensities $>5 \sigma$ in both Bands 3 and 6 images." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Align with FIXPLANETS and check concatenated-data MTMFS; three and four Taylor coefficients agree better with image-plane indices than two  
  > "To construct the spectral index, we first align Bands 3 and 6 data using FIXPLANETS." (Dust continuum emission around the primary); "The concatenated visibilities are deconvolved using TCLEAN with the Multi-term Multi-Frequency Synthesis (MTMFS) with three Taylor coefficients (cite: tsukagoshi22)." (Dust continuum emission around the primary); "We find that the indices that are obtained using three and four Taylor coefficients are more consistent with those values obtained in the image plane." (Continuum images)
5. **Estimate continuum optical depths**: Compare observed brightness temperature with the predicted dust temperature using T_brightness = T_dust(1 − exp(−τ_v))  
  > "we compare the observed intensity with the expected dust emission such that $T_{\rm brightness} = T_{\rm dust} \left ( 1 - e^{-\tau_{\rm v}} \right )$" (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - parameter: Dust temperature at the ring ≈25 K  
  > "At the location of the ring, the dust temperature is $\approx 25$ K" (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - assumption: Irradiated flared-disk power-law temperature profile; flaring angle 0.05  
  > "The dust temperature profile is approximated as a power-law following the irradiated flared disk model of (cite: kenyon87)" (Dust continuum emission around the primary); "the flaring angle of 0.05 (cite: dullemond04)" (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - assumption: T_star = 3592 K; R_star ≈1.2 R_sun; distance 175 pc  
  > "a stellar temperature $T_{\star}$ of 3592 K, and a stellar radius $R_{\star}$ of $\sim 1.2$ $R_{\odot}$ (values are obtained from (cite: herczeg14) adjusted to a distance of 175 pc)." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - assumption: Absorption-dominated emission evenly distributed within the beam  
  > "For simplicity, we consider dust emission from the dust absorption-dominated region." (Dust continuum emission around the primary); "while considering that the dust emission is evenly distributed within the beam." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Assess temperature range 15–25 K and 10% brightness-temperature error; compare native resolution, where optical depth doubles  
  > "The typical error in $\tau$ is $<0.1$, depending on the adopted temperature (15 – 25 K) and 10% error on the brightness temperature." (Dust continuum emission around the primary); "At the native resolution, the optical depth increases by a factor of two, indicating that the dust ring is still unresolved." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)

  > "Using these models, we constructed the model images within *CASA* to determine the spectral index." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "Using the models, we can further estimate the dust spectral index of the dust ring itself without deconvolution." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Best-fit Gaussian and arc intensity models  
  > "Using these models, we constructed the model images within *CASA* to determine the spectral index." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Dual-band observations test the origin of the cavity and asymmetric dust disk through wavelength-dependent structure  
  > "We obtained ALMA dual-band observations of the disk around CIDA 9A to better understand the origin of its large millimeter cavity and asymmetric dust disk." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Visibility fitting determines dust-peak positions for measuring radial and azimuthal shifts; matched-beam images permit comparison  
  > "By fitting the $(u,v)$ data, we can accurately determine the location of the observed dust peaks in Bands 3 and 6" ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "In order to compare the Band 3 and Band 6 images, the images are deconvolved including a $(u,v)$ tapering at 0$''$09 and a common 0$''$135 beam" (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
- Alternative: Axisymmetric Gaussian ring versus GR2ARC: lower residuals do not establish GR2ARC as the better model  
  > "Even though the GR2ARC model returns a lower residual map in Band 6" ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "its BIC value is much greater than the Gaussian ring model by a factor of 3." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "This implies that we cannot conclusively determine that GR2ARC is the better model for the observations presented here." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
- Alternative: Unrestricted versus common-radius arcs: unrestricted model gives an angular difference ~80°  
  > "The second set of runs restricts the arcs, $I_{1}$ and $I_{2}$, to be at the same radius $R_{\rm arc}$." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "without restricting the radial locations of the ring and the two arcs (the first set of runs), the angular difference is $\sim 80\ensuremath{^\circ}$." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
- Alternative: Gas inclination 56° versus adopted 47°: different baseline datasets prefer different solutions  
  > "If we adopted an inclination of $56^{\circ}$ instead (cite: rota22), emission models would provide a better fit to the high spatial resolution CO lines" (Molecular line emission); "but a worse fit with larger residuals in the short baseline data presented here." (Molecular line emission)
- Alternative: MTMFS with two Taylor terms gives α ~2; three or four coefficients agree better with image-plane α  
  > "The images that are produced with *mtmfs* using two Taylor terms tend to produce $\alpha \sim 2$ which is indicative of optically thick emission." (Continuum images); "We find that the indices that are obtained using three and four Taylor coefficients are more consistent with those values obtained in the image plane." (Continuum images)
- Alternative: Planet, photoevaporation, and dead-zone vortex origins remain indistinguishable  
  > "such as planets, photoevaporation, and dead zones." (Dead zone.); "The current data does not seem to favor any of these origins." (Dead zone.)
- How they decided it was enough: Autocorrelation-based burn-in and 1–3σ model residuals; BIC does not conclusively favor GR2ARC  
  > "For each model, Gaussian and GR2ARC, we performed 10000 steps with a burn-in that is determined through the autocorrelation function ($\sim 1500-2000$ steps)." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "The models capture most of the observed features with 1–3 $\sigma$ residuals" ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "This implies that we cannot conclusively determine that GR2ARC is the better model for the observations presented here." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
- Limitation (authors): Band 3 flux calibration is uncertain following Return to Operations observations and substantial flagging  
  > "Due to the flux uncertainties of the phase calibrator and the high fraction of flagging, we adopt a 20% flux uncertainty in Band 3 observations throughout this paper." (ALMA observations)
- Limitation (authors): Dust ring remains unresolved; inferred optical depths depend on resolution and beam filling  
  > "At the native resolution, the optical depth increases by a factor of two, indicating that the dust ring is still unresolved." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
- Limitation (authors): Spectral index can vary by 0.5 with deconvolution parameters  
  > "Depending on the parameters used during the deconvolution, the spectral index can vary by 0.5." (Dust continuum emission around the primary)
- Limitation (authors): Azimuthal shift is tentative and GR2ARC is not conclusively preferred  
  > "we find a tentative azimuthal shift in the main dust peak between Bands 3 and 6" ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A); "This implies that we cannot conclusively determine that GR2ARC is the better model for the observations presented here." ((u,v) modelling of the dust morphology around CIDA 9A)
- Limitation (authors): Gas observations lack sensitivity for cavity kinematic disturbances and lose line emission at 3–5 km s⁻¹ on long baselines  
  > "Unfortunately, the data is not deep enough to search for any kinematical disturbances within the millimeter cavity." (Molecular line emission); "With the longer baseline configuration, a significant part of the line emission at 3–5 km s$^{-1}$ is not recovered." (Molecular line emission)
- Limitation (authors): Low-J lines suffer foreground absorption  
  > "The low-$J$ lines suffer from foreground absorption." (Molecular line emission)
- Limitation (authors): CO data do not constrain the underlying gas distribution for comparison with dust  
  > "our high-spatial CO data observations do not have the spatial resolution and sensitivity to infer the underlying gas distribution for comparison with the dust distribution." (Giant protoplanet:)
- Limitation (authors): Stellar variability and spots make individual ages and radii uncertain  
  > "the radius may be underestimated if the star is heavily spotted" (CIDA 9A stellar properties: mass and age); "which also introduces significant uncertainties in any radius estimate." (CIDA 9A stellar properties: mass and age); "age estimates are uncertain on any individual star (cite: soderblom14), especially one with such high amplitude variability." (CIDA 9A stellar properties: mass and age)
