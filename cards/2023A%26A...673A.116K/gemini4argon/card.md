- Question: Characterize the physical properties, environment, outflow signatures, and linear polarization (magnetic field limits) of the submillimeter galaxy AzTEC-3 at z = 5.3 and the lensed quasar BRI 0952-0115 at z = 4.4 using ALMA Band 7 [C II] and continuum observations.

## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract linear polarization spectra and derive 3-sigma upper limits**: Extract Stokes Q and U spectra and continuum in a single beam towards the peak of the [C II] lines and continuum; compute linear polarization spectrum I_p = sqrt(Q^2 + U^2 - sigma_p^2) where sigma_p = sqrt(sigma_Q^2 + sigma_U^2), and derive 3-sigma upper limits on linear polarization fraction  
  > "The linear polarization spectra and continuum values were extracted in a single beam (see Section 3.1 for the size) towards the peak of the lines and continuum, respectively." (Polarization); "The linear polarization spectrum was produced from the Q and U spectra using $I_p=\sqrt{Q^2+U^2-\sigma_p^2}$, where $\sigma_p=\sqrt{\sigma_Q^2+\sigma_U^2}$ corresponds to the rms error on the linear polarization and $\sigma_{Q,U}$ are the rms errors in Q and U, respectively." (Polarization)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Single beam extraction; 3-sigma upper limits including 0.1% ALMA calibration uncertainty  
  > "Since we detect no polarization, the derived limits include any potential remaining calibration uncertainty. According to ALMA specifications, this $3\sigma$ limit corresponds to $0.1\%$." (Polarization)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Model gravitational lensing for BRI0952 in the uv-plane**: Fit a single isothermal ellipsoid lens and a Sersic source directly to the Band 7 continuum uv-data using Visilens, then fix the lens parameters and fit the source profile on the continuum and [C II] line uv-data (including across five frequency bins)  
  > "We determined the lensing parameters of both the source and the lens by utilizing Visilens (cite: Spilker16)." (3.2.1 Lensing magnification); "Initially, we ran Visilens on band-7 continuum data in order to determine the best-fit parameters for the lens." (3.2.1 Lensing magnification); "Once the lens was optimized, we reran the continuum fit with fixed lens parameters but still allowed the Sérsic source profile to vary. Similarly, for the band-7 [C ii] emission data, we ran Visilens with the previously optimized lens parameters on the uv line emission data while allowing the source to vary in an attempt to increase the quality of our lensing model." (3.2.1 Lensing magnification)

## Qualitative findings

- No linear polarization is detected in [C II] or dust continuum for either BRI0952 or AzTEC-3, indicating that strong ordered magnetic fields are unlikely to exist at kiloparsec scales unless ground state alignment is less effective than expected.  
  > "No polarization was detected, and upper limits are provided. The results suggest that strong ordered magnetic fields are unlikely to exists at the kiloparsec scale in the two studied sources, unless ground state alignment is a less effective mechanism than expected." (Conclusions)
- Two companion sources (Comp-N and Comp-SW) are detected in [C II] around BRI0952 and three companion sources (LBG-3, Gal-S, Gal-SW) are detected in [C II] around AzTEC-3 within 3'' (18 kpc).  
  > "We report serendipitous detections of [C ii] emission from two previously unreported companion sources around BRI0952 and new detections of [C ii] emission from three companions surrounding AzTEC-3. These companions are each located within 3$\,''$ (18 kpc) of the central source." (Conclusions)
- A gas bridge (~12 kpc) links AzTEC-3 and companion Gal-S, and a tentative velocity gradient exists between AzTEC-3 and LBG-3, indicating ongoing tidal interaction or gas exchange.  
  > "We detect a bridge-like structure between the companion galaxy Gal-S and AzTEC-3, suggesting the possible occurrence of a gas-exchange between the two galaxies extending over $\sim 12$ kpc." ## Reasoning

- Why this data: Selected based on previously known extremely bright [C II] emission and observed in full polarization mode at high sensitivity and sub-arcsecond resolution to search for magnetic fields and faint companions.  
  > "In order to better constrain massive galaxy evolution at high redshift, high-sensitivity data is required to find faint emission from companion sources and to resolve the detailed physics ongoing within these systems." (Introduction); "These data were obtained through a proposal designed to look for magnetic fields in the early Universe and were selected based on their previously known extremely bright [C ii] emission." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Used robust = 0.0 for [C II] cubes to increase angular resolution for companion analysis; used Visilens directly on uv-data to avoid CLEAN imaging bias; used a modified blackbody for companions due to poorly sampled FIR SEDs and blending.  
  > "However, for further analysis of the companion sources surrounding both primary targets, a robust factor of 0.0 was utilized to increase the angular resolution of the [C ii] emission cubes; these images were subsequently used for the analyses described in this paper." (Observations and analysis); "Visilens calculates the magnification factor by directly modeling the uv data rather than introducing bias by using images produced from algorithms such as CASA's CLEAN." (3.2.1 Lensing magnification); "We utilized this approach rather than an SED fit as the SED of the companions is poorly sampled, especially in the far-IR, and the accuracy of sub-mm photometry is affected by the blending of the continuum emission from the central SMG." (Infrared luminosity)
- Alternative: Alternative continuum subtraction tools (imcontsub, statcont) were tested and yielded unchanged results.  
  > "We note that the results of this paper do not change when using other tools such as imcontsub or statcont (cite: statcont18)." (Footnote 3)
- Alternative: Alternative spectral extraction methods (including uv-plane fitting) were considered; regional image-plane extraction was kept because outcomes are consistent and uv-plane fitting also introduces model biases.  
  > "This extraction could be done through a number of different methods; however, we find that the outcomes of these methods remain consistent with the method employed in this paper. We also note that methods such as uv-plane fitting introduce bias in a similar way to image-plane fitting through base-assumptions of, e.g., a model to describe the source, boundary conditions, and so on." (Footnote 4)
- Alternative: Including additional faint sources for Comp-N and Comp-SW in Visilens was attempted, but did not enable a good fit.  
  > "We note that including additional faint sources in the lensing analysis with Visilens did not enable a good fit for the two companion sources; however, given their location, they are likely to be magnified by a factor close to $\mu \sim 1$." (3.2.2 [C ii] emission)
- Alternative: Alternative outflow mass calculation using only the residual flux above a single Gaussian fit was performed, yielding lower limits of 28 +/- 8 M_odot/yr (AzTEC-3) and 12 +/- 4 M_odot/yr (BRI0952).  
  > "As an alternative, we determined the strength of the outflow by utilizing only the flux not accounted for in a single Gaussian fit to the [C ii] emission; effectively subtracting the single component from the broad one. This yields a significantly lower mass outflow rate, which we treated as a lower limit for BRI0952 and AzTEC-3." (Outflows and turbulence)

- Limitation (authors): Differential lensing occurs across BRI0952 and may affect correlations between emission strength and spatial location as well as the broad [C II] wings.  
  > "We determine that it is likely that some amount of differential lensing across the surface of BRI0952 is occurring, and thus we caution that conclusions drawn that correlate strength of emission with location will be affected by this." (3.2.1 Lensing magnification)
- Limitation (authors): Higher resolution data and a source-plane reconstruction are required to reach a robust conclusion on the nature of the BRI0952 companions and the spatial offset in LBG-3.  
  > "Higher resolution data and a source plane reconstruction of this system would be required to come to a robust conclusion about the nature of this emission." (3.2.2 [C ii] emission); "Without additional higher resolution data, a robust conclusion about the nature of this offset is not possible." (3.3.2 Continuum)
- Limitation (authors): Lack of far-IR coverage in the SED fitting of AzTEC-3 and its companions limits the accuracy of the derived L_IR values.  
  > "(cite: Riechers14) used a higher number of data points than in our SED fitting; however, both analyses lack IR coverage, making it difficult to obtain high levels of accuracy, and thus $L_{\rm FIR}$ or $L_{\rm IR}$, from the SED fit." (Infrared luminosity)
- Limitation (authors): Broad [C II] wings cannot be uniquely confirmed as outflows without additional outflow tracers because companion blending or tidal interactions may produce the broad component.  
  > "therefore, we caution that the true nature of the broad component cannot be confirmed as an outflow in BRI0952 and AzTEC-3 without additional outflow tracers detected in these galaxies." (Outflows and turbulence)
