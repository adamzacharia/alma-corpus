## Analysis recipe

(2017), selecting antennas with better T_sys (PM03 for Band 3, PM04 for Band 6, PM02 for Band 7) and applying CASA sdgaincal before mapping to suppress arc-like brightness modulations in Band 7.  
  > "In Bands 3 and 6, we only used the images that were provided by antennas PM03 and PM04, respectively, because of their better system temperature, $T_{sys}$." (Observations and data reduction); "The TP data were acquired and processed using the methodology described by (cite: 2017SoPh..292...88W)." (Observations and data reduction); "We were able to largely suppress them by invoking CASA’s task sdgaincal before mapping." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, sdgaincal
   - parameter: Band 7 beam FWHM = 21 arcsec; scan duration = 3 min  
  > "The full width at half maximum (FWHM) of the Band 7 single dish beam was 21$''$ and each TP antenna scanned the full solar disk in 3 min." (Observations and data reduction)
   - assumption: Arc-like modulations are associated with opacity variations and can be corrected by adjusting gains relative to the central field position in double-circle scans  
  > "It is possible that these modulations are associated with opacity variations during observations." (Observations and data reduction); "This task takes advantage of the fact that the double-circle mode observes the same position in the center of the field of view and adjusts the gains throughout the whole dataset relative to the measured intensity at the center." (Observations and data reduction)

  > "Considering that Band 3 is more reliable (cite: 2017SoPh..292...88W), we set the normalization factor of the commissioning data for that at unity, leaving the factors for the other data sets to be determined by the fit." (Center-to-limb variation and modeling)

## Qualitative findings

- Quiet Sun chromospheric network structures in Band 7 (0.86 mm) show practically a one-to-one correspondence with AIA 1600/1700 Å and 304 Å images and negative H-alpha images smoothed to the ALMA resolution.  
  > "Quiet Sun structures on the disk are similar to those in Atmospheric Imaging Assembly (AIA) images at 1600 Å and 304 Å, after the latter are smoothed to the ALMA resolution" (abstract); "We note that there is practically a one-to-one correspondence between the ALMA and the AIA network structures, as previously reported for Bands 6 and 3 by (cite: 2017A&A...605A..78A)." (Structures on the solar disk)
- Polar coronal holes clearly seen at 304 Å and small H-alpha filaments are not detectable in Band 7 at 0.86 mm.  
  > "Polar coronal holes, which are clearly seen in the 304 Å band and small H$\alpha$ filaments, are not detectable at 0.86 mm." (abstract)
- The combined T_b(log mu_100) curve and the inverted T_e(log tau_100) profile flatten at high frequencies / large optical depths, indicating that Band 7 observations approach the solar temperature minimum.  
  > "More importantly, the $T_b(\log\mu_{100})$ curve flattens at large values of $\mu_{100}$, and so does the corresponding $T_e(\log	au_{100})$ at large $	au_{100}$. This is probably an indication that we are approaching the temperature minimum." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: First full-disk solar observations in ALMA Band 7 (347 GHz) obtained in the same campaign as Bands 3 and 6 provide a homogeneous multi-frequency dataset without needing older 350 GHz literature data.  
  > "With the present Band 7 observations, we have a homogeneous set of data at our disposal, which we subsequently exploit in this section in order to obtain more reliable results." (Center-to-limb variation and modeling); "The present result is more reliable since we have a homogeneous data set and thus there was no need to resort to the old measurements of (cite: 1993ApJ...415..364B) at 350 GHz, as we had done before." (Summary and conclusions)
- Why this method: Combining multi-frequency CLV data via f^-2 opacity scaling and inverting T_b(mu) treats frequencies simultaneously, avoids primary beam effects near the limb, and yields T_e(tau) directly; CASA sdgaincal suppresses double-circle scan modulations.  
  > "We were able to largely suppress them by invoking CASA’s task sdgaincal before mapping." (Observations and data reduction); "(a) data at different frequencies are treated simultaneously; (b) there is no need to go close to the limb, thus avoiding primary beam effects; and (c) it provides the physically meaningful quantity $T_e(	au)$ directly." (Center-to-limb variation and modeling)
- Alternative: Comparing observed CLV curves at individual frequencies to curves computed from models or determining limb brightening coefficients  
  > "We note that this approach has a number of advantages over the comparison of the CLV observed at various frequencies to curves obtained from models (cite: 2019ApJ...871...45S), or the determination of limb brightening coefficients (cite: 2019SoPh..294..163S)" (Center-to-limb variation and modeling)
- Alternative: Linear and quadratic fits to T_b(ln mu), rejected in favor of a third-degree polynomial fit due to higher rms deviations (26.6 K and 16.0 K vs 13 K) and the flattening in Band 7  
  > "In order to accommodate for the flattening of the curve in Band 7, we used the following third degree fit: $$egin{equation} T_b(\mu)=A_0+A_1\ln\mu+A_2\ln^2\mu+A_3\ln^3\mu \label{logmu0}, \end{equation}$$ rather than the linear fit used in previous works." (Center-to-limb variation and modeling); "The root mean square (rms) deviation was about 13 K, whereas linear and quadratic fits gave rms deviations of 26.6 K and 16.0 K, respectively." (Center-to-limb variation and modeling)

- Limitation (authors): Low spatial resolution (21 arcsec) of the single-dish Band 7 images prevents a detailed study of the association between ALMA and H-alpha features.  
  > "In spite of the low spatial resolution (21$''$), several interesting results were obtained." - Limitation (authors): No simultaneous full-disk AIA or HMI data were available at the time of the ALMA observations, and the 1600 Å AIA image suffered from flat-field and diffuse light problems.  
  > "We note that no full disk AIA images and no HMI data were available at the time of our ALMA observations, and thus the nearest ones to the ALMA image are given in the figure" (Structures on the solar disk); "moreover, the 1600 Å AIA image was affected by flat-field and diffuse light problems, hence the less affected 1700 Å image is shown instead." (Structures on the solar disk)
- Limitation (authors): Absolute calibration jumps exist between datasets, and cross-normalization cannot replace true absolute calibration (e.g., using the Moon).
