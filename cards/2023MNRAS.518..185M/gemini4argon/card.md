- Fields: astrochemistry, star_formation
- Question: Improve the laboratory rotational spectroscopic parameters of mono-deuterated oxirane ($c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO) and use the updated rest frequencies to detect it and determine its abundance and deuteration ratio towards IRAS 16293$-$2422 B.

## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit laboratory rotational transitions and calculate rest frequencies and spectroscopic parameters of c-C2H3DO**: Global least-squares fitting of new submillimetre laboratory transitions (490-750 GHz and 810-1060 GHz) combined with previous MW, mmW, and recalibrated FIR data using Watson's S reduction (III^l representation) and A reduction (I^r representation).  
  > "Fitting and calculation of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO transition frequencies was performed with Pickett’s SPFIT and SPCAT programmes (cite: spfit_1991)." (Treatment of previous data); "The parameter set in the III$^l$ representation employing Watson’s S reduction should be favoured over the set in the I$^r$ representation employing the A reduction because two fewer parameters are needed for a satisfactory fit." (Discussion of the laboratory spectroscopic results)
   - software: SPFIT, SPCAT
   - parameter: 1504 new sub-mmW transitions (1091 frequencies) in 490-750 GHz and 810-1060 GHz; global fit of 2416 transitions (1613 frequencies) with J_max = 58, K_a,max = 43, K_c,max = 56  
  > "| sub-mmW, present | 1504 | 1091 | 58 | 43 | 55 | 9 | .13 | 0.884 |" (Table 1); "| global | 2416 | 1613 | 58 | 43 | 56 | | $c$ | 0.892 |" (Table 1)
   - parameter: FIR recalibration factor of 1.0000037  
  > "Trial fits with the aim to minimize the average shift yielded a recalibration factor of 1.0000037." (Treatment of previous data)
   - assumption: Dipole moment components derived from structure: mu_a = 0.36 D, mu_b = 1.86 D, mu_c = 0.08 D  
  > "We derived from the structural parameters of the molecule that the rotation of the principal inertial axis system of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO with respect to that of $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O leads to a small $\mu _a$ dipole moment component of 0.36 D, a very small $\mu _c = 0.08$ D and an almost unchanged $\mu _b = 1.86$ D." (Spectroscopy of oxirane)
   - assumption: Assigned uncertainties: 50 kHz for MW data, 5 kHz for mmW data, 0.00008 cm^-1 for FIR data, and 5 to 50 kHz for new sub-mmW lines  
  > "We assumed 50 kHz for the data from (cite: c-C2H4O_div-isos_rot_1974)" (Treatment of previous data); "We used 10 kHz as uncertainties initially and 5 kHz later." (Treatment of previous data); "we employed therefore 0.00008 cm$^{-1}$ as uncertainties of the FIR data." (Treatment of previous data)
   - fitting: Weighted least-squares fit in SPFIT minimizing rms error, reaching global rms error of 0.892 (S reduction, III^l) and 0.901 (A reduction, I^r)  
  > "| global | 2416 | 1613 | 58 | 43 | 56 | | $c$ | 0.892 |" (Table 1); "The quality of the A reduction fit in the I$^r$ representation is very similar; the rms error of the overall fit is 0.901." (Observed spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
   - validation: Tested omission of previous data, compared S reduction (III^l) vs A reduction (I^r), and checked relative line intensities of a-, b-, and c-type transitions against calculated dipole moment components  
  > "Omission of all previously reported transition frequencies changed the parameter values within the uncertainties and increased the parameter uncertainies only slightly" (Observed spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters); "Fig. [3](#fig:dipole_etc) displays a $c$-type transition of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO accompanied by an $a$-type transition and two oblate paired $b$-type transitions supporting the magnitudes of the dipole moment components calculated from the structure." (Observed spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
2. **Fit LTE synthetic spectra to ALMA PILS Band 7 and Band 6 data to identify c-C2H3DO and derive column density and D/H ratio**: Calculate LTE synthetic spectra using updated c-C2H3DO rest frequencies (from S reduction, III^l representation) and fit to ALMA PILS Band 7 and Band 6 spectra at the offset position of IRAS 16293-2422 B.  
  > "The search for $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO towards IRAS 16293$-$2422 B was carried out by fitting synthetic spectra to the PILS data similar to other searches for complex molecules in this source." (Astronomical results); "$^a$ Derived from our parameter set emplyoing the S reduction in the III$^l$ representation." (Table 3)
   - parameter: T_rot = 125 K, FWHM linewidth = 1 km s^-1, LSR velocity offset = 2.6 km s^-1  
  > "We assumed $T_{\rm rot} = 125$ K in our initial calculations as was determined for $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O (cite: PILS_COMs_2017). We employed a linewidth (FWHM) of 1 km s$^{-1}$ and a velocity offset relative to the local standard of rest of 2.6 km s$^{-1}$ for the same reasons." (Astronomical results)
   - assumption: Local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) and 4 equivalent H atoms in c-C2H4O for D-to-H conversion  
  > "The synthetic spectra were calculated assuming the molecules are in local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) which is sensible given the high densities in the molecular cloud surrounding the protostar that is probed by our ALMA observations (cite: PILS_overview_2016)." (Astronomical results); "This corresponds to a D-to-H ratio of $\sim$0.036 per H atom because all four H atoms in $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O are equivalent with respect to substitution by one D." (Astronomical results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Applied the Band 7 LTE model to Band 6 observations and verified match to an unblended transition at 235180.99 MHz; cross-checked with rotation diagram analysis  
  > "We also applied our model based on the Band 7 data to the observations in Band 6: a small number of lines were detected in the more limited Band 6 data and only one appeared to be unblended (Fig. [5](#band6_figure)). Still, the good match to the single transition is an additional verification of the results." (Astronomical results)
3. **Construct rotation diagram to independently derive rotational temperature and column density**: Fit a rotation diagram to the unblended or slightly blended c-C2H3DO transitions spanning a range of upper state energies (33 to 122 K).  
  > "We constructed a rotation diagram from the unblended or only slightly blended transitions because these were numerous enough and span a range of upper state energies as can be seen in Table [1](#tab_obs-lines)." (Astronomical results); "Fits of the detected lines using the rotation diagrams yield a temperature of $T_{\rm rot} = 103 \pm 19$ K, which in turn agrees well with 125 K derived for the $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O main isotopologue previously." (Abstract)
   - assumption: LTE optically thin lines (standard rotation diagram assumption)  
  > "Such a rotation diagram may serve as a check of the modelling and it also provides a measure of the statistical uncertainties originating from the observational data." (Astronomical results)
   - fitting: Linear fit to rotation diagram yielding T_rot and column density with statistical uncertainties  
  > "The temperature of $103 \pm 19$ K determined from the rotation diagram $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO agrees well with the 125 K obtained for $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O (cite: PILS_COMs_2017). The column density from the rotation diagram of $(7.8 \pm 1.5) \times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ is slightly lower than $8.9 \times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ derived from the modelling applying 125 K" (Discussion of astronomical results)
   - validation: Compared T_rot (103 +/- 19 K) and N ((7.8 +/- 1.5) x 10^14 cm^-2) with the 125 K LTE synthetic spectrum model results  
  > "The temperature of $103 \pm 19$ K determined from the rotation diagram $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO agrees well with the 125 K obtained for $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O (cite: PILS_COMs_2017). The column density from the rotation diagram of $(7.8 \pm 1.5) \times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ is slightly lower than $8.9 \times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$ derived from the modelling applying 125 K" (Discussion of astronomical results)

## Qualitative findings

- Mono-deuterated oxirane (c-C2H3DO) is detected for the first time in the interstellar medium towards IRAS 16293-2422 B.  
  > "The updated rest frequencies were employed to detect $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO for the first time in the interstellar medium in the Atacama Large Millimetre/submillimetre Array (ALMA) Protostellar Interferometric Line Survey (PILS) of the Class 0 protostellar system IRAS 16293$-$242." (Abstract)
- The D-to-H ratio per H atom of oxirane (~0.036) is slightly higher than species such as methanol, formaldehyde, and ketene (0.02-0.03), but lower than larger complex organics such as ethanol, methylformate, and glycolaldehyde (0.05-0.06), suggesting oxirane forms fairly early in prestellar core evolution.  
  > "The degree of deuteration of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO referenced to one H atom of $\sim$0.036 is slightly larger than species such as formaldehyde, methanol, ketene, HNCO, formamide and cyanamide with ratios of 0.02–0.03 but lower than some of the larger complex species such as ethanol, methylformate, glycolaldehyde and acetaldehyde with ratios of 0.05–0.06 (cite: PILS_div-isos_2018; PILS_formamide_2016; PILS_cyanamide_2018)." (Discussion of astronomical results); "This difference may reflect differences in the formation time with the species with the lower ratios forming earlier in the evolution of the prestellar cores." (Discussion of astronomical results)
- Doubly-deuterated oxirane isotopomers may be detectable in the PILS data if their deuteration degree is enhanced similarly to other multiply deuterated molecules (yielding ratios of 0.01 to 0.025 relative to the main species) and if lines are not severely blended.  
  > "If the deuteration of the three dideutero-oxirane isotopomers is enhanced in a similar way compared with that of monodeutero-oxirane we may observe column density ratios of 0.01 to 0.025 for each of the three dideutero-oxirane isotopomers compared to the main species, which may be enough for identifications provided not all of the stronger lines of these three species are blended with stronger lines of other species." (Discussion of astronomical results)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The PILS ALMA survey of IRAS 16293-2422 B provides high sensitivity and narrow lines (~1 km s^-1) at an offset position where continuum opacity is lower and absorption lines are weaker, minimizing line confusion for detecting rare deuterated isotopologues.  
  > "Through the high sensitivity of PILS and narrow lines towards one component of IRAS 16293-2422 (limiting line confusion) a number of species have been detected for the first time" (Introduction); "We focus on Source B in the present work because its narrow lines ($\sim$1 km s$^{-1}$) make it an ideal target for searches for rare species." (Overview of the observations)
- Why this method: LTE synthetic spectrum fitting is appropriate because of the high gas densities in the protostellar envelope probed by ALMA, while a rotation diagram of unblended lines provides an independent check on T_rot and statistical uncertainties.  
  > "The synthetic spectra were calculated assuming the molecules are in local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) which is sensible given the high densities in the molecular cloud surrounding the protostar that is probed by our ALMA observations (cite: PILS_overview_2016)." (Astronomical results); "Such a rotation diagram may serve as a check of the modelling and it also provides a measure of the statistical uncertainties originating from the observational data." (Astronomical results)
