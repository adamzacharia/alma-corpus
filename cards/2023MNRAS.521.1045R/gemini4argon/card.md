## Qualitative findings

- Four out of five z ~ 4.5 galaxies (BRI1335-0417, J081740, SGP38326-1, SGP38326-2) are rotationally supported discs with flat rotation curves and declining velocity dispersion profiles, whereas AzTEC1 is likely an unresolved merger  
  > "The five galaxies show velocity gradients, but four were found to be rotating discs while the remaining, AzTEC1, is likely a merger." (Abstract); "We see that the galaxies have typically flat rotation curves and declining velocity dispersion profiles." (Rotation velocities and velocity dispersions)
- Non-circular motions (possible inflows, outflows, bars, warps, or tidal interactions) are ubiquitous across all five galaxies despite regular disc rotation  
  > "In spite of the presence of regularly rotating discs, we found that non-circular motions are ubiquitous among the galaxies in our sample." (Conclusions)
- Discovery of [CII] emission in two companion galaxies (SGP38326-3 and SGP38326-4) at ~14 kpc projected distance from SGP38326-1, indicating a rich group or early proto-cluster  
  > "However, we also report the discovery of [CII] emission in two other companions in the system: SGP38326-3 and SGP38326-4, they are shown in Figure 3." (Presentation of the data); "indicates that this might be a rich group, possibly the early stages of a proto-cluster." (Gas kinematics at z ~ 4.5)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Selected all public non-lensed, non-protocluster ALMA [CII] 158 um datasets at z = 4-5 with angular resolution < 0.3" and spectral resolution < 30 km s^-1 to ensure at least a few independent resolution elements across the disc major axis and high brightness without CMB attenuation.  
  > "Thus, even for the most extended galaxies an angular resolution of at least 0.3" is necessary." (Introduction); "Our selection criteria were the following: observations with angular resolution $<0.3$"; spectral resolution $<30$ km s$^{-1}$; redshift of the galaxy in the range of *z* $= 4-5$." (Data)
- Why this method: Used 3DBAROLO non-parametric 3D tilted-ring modelling to account for beam smearing in low-resolution data, CANNUBI to pre-determine disc geometry and reduce free parameters, and PVsplit in 3D p-v space to distinguish rotating discs from mergers.  
  > "Therefore, it accounts for the effect of beam smearing (cite: bosma78; begeman87), which is very important for retrieving robust measurements of velocity dispersions and rotation velocities at the relatively low angular resolution of the present data." (Modelling the disc kinematics with 3DBAROLO); "To reduce the number of free parameters in the kinematic fitting we derive the geometric parameters beforehand using `CANNUBI`, a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) python routine for estimating the disc geometry of galaxies." (Estimating the disc geometry); "For this, we use the PVsplit diagram, which is a new 3D kinematic classification method to identify the dynamical state of galaxies with the typical resolutions of high-*z* emission-line observations (cite: rizzo22)." (Mergers or rotating discs?)
- Alternative: Using uvcontsub for SGP38326 was rejected in favour of image-plane continuum subtraction with imcontsub because SGP38326 is a complex system with many continuum sources.  
  > "For SGP38326, we followed a slightly different procedure: since it is a complex system with many continuum sources, we avoided using `uvcontsub` to subtract the continuum." (Data Reduction and Imaging); "To extract the final line-emission cube and continuum map we used the task `imcontsub` that removes the continuum from the cube in the image space rather than in the uv space." (Data Reduction and Imaging)
- Alternative: Using morphological PA instead of kinematic PA for 3DBAROLO fitting was rejected because morphological PA can miss the region of highest line-of-sight velocities.  
  > "The disc PA is fixed to the kinematic PA to ensure that we cross the region of the highest line-of-sight velocities in the disc, see Table 3." (Input parameters); "Therefore, the morphological PA would not be appropriate to derive the kinematics of the disc as the region with highest line-of-sight velocities would be missed." (AzTEC1)
- Alternative: Fitting both sides of the rotation curve for BRI1335-0417 was rejected in favour of fitting only the approaching side because of an asymmetric high-velocity central feature (possible outflow) on the receding side.  
  > "Therefore, for our fiducial model we prefer to fit the data on the approaching side only to ensure that we are accurately probing the rotation curve." (BRI1335-0417)
- Alternative: Fitting a free PA for BRI1335-0417 was tested and rejected because it did not significantly improve the kinematic model.  
  > "We tried to fit the kinematics with a free PA, but we found that it does not improve the model significantly." (BRI1335-0417)
- Alternative: Keeping PA fixed for SGP38326-1 was replaced by a free (warped) PA model because the free PA model yielded lower residuals.  
  > "Such warped disc has a very similar kinematics to the model with constant PA, but given the lower residuals, we consider it as our fiducial model." (SGP38326-1)
- How they decided it was enough: Chose imaging weights and channel widths to reach an average SNR >= 4 per channel across at least 10 channels, set ring spacing close to the beam size to avoid oversampling, and ran 20 MCMC walkers in CANNUBI until autocorrelation-time convergence.  
  > "Our choices for the weighting schemes and channel widths were motivated to obtain an average SNR of at least 4 per velocity channel in at least 10 channels of the data cubes." (Data Reduction and Imaging); "Moreover, we strive to not oversample the rotation curves thus we keep the number of modelled tilted rings as close as possible to the real number of resolution elements in the data with a separation close to the angular resolution." (Modelling the disc kinematics with 3DBAROLO); "With `CANNUBI` we run an MCMC exploration on the total flux map of the galactic centre, radial extension, inclination and thickness with 20 walkers until convergence on all mock galaxies, which is enough to probe the parameter space of these mock discs." (Creating a sample of mock galaxies)
- Limitation (authors): Non-circular motions or emission in forbidden p-v regions (e.g. in AzTEC1, BRI1335-0417, SGP38326-1) can be absorbed by 3DBAROLO as increased velocity dispersion, potentially overestimating sigma at some radii.  
  > "Similarly to AzTEC1, the broadness of the emission and the emission in the "forbidden" regions can only be reproduced in our best-fit rotating disc model by increasing the velocity dispersion." (BRI1335-0417); "This may drive an overestimation of the velocity dispersion at some radii." (BRI1335-0417)
- Limitation (authors): Direct disc thickness determinations with CANNUBI on present data have limited reliability and need validation against mock data from hydrodynamical simulations; 3DBAROLO ring-by-ring fitting is also not ideal when disc thickness exceeds spatial resolution.  
  > "Given the quality of the present data we are not fully confident that we can determine the disc thickness in our galaxies reliably." (Estimating the disc geometry); "However, the reliability of these results needs to be checked in particular using mock data derived from simulated galaxies, an investigation that we leave to future works." (Disc thickness); "An important caveat here is that the ring by ring approach used in $^{\text{3D}}$`BAROLO` is not ideal to perform fits where the thickness of the discs is larger than the spatial resolution of the observations, as it happens in particular in the case of BRI1335-0417." (Disc thickness)
- Limitation (authors): Rotation velocities are not corrected for asymmetric drift / pressure support in this paper, so they represent rotation velocities rather than circular velocities and should not be used directly for dynamical mass modelling without correction.  
  > "We note that here we are reporting the rotation velocities, not the circular velocities, thus, these rotation curves should not be used to retrieve dynamical models." (Rotation velocities and velocity dispersions); "These rotation velocities will be corrected for pressure support (cite: iorio17) in the following paper where we will analyse the dynamics in detail." (Rotation velocities and velocity dispersions)
- Limitation (authors): Cannot distinguish between a warped disc and radial motions in SGP38326-1.  
  > "We note that such distortions could also be due to the presence of radial motions and distinguishing between these two scenarios is difficult (cite: diteodoro21)." (SGP38326-1)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample size (five galaxies) precludes a statistical picture of high-z galaxy kinematics.  
  > "Although it is hard to create a statistical picture of the kinematics at high-*z* due to the lack of highly resolved observations of large samples of galaxies, this study shows that rotating discs can be common occurrences at *z* $\sim$ 4.5 in spite of the existence of several complex features in their kinematics." (Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Manual adjustment of the CANNUBI-derived kinematic centre by up to 2 pixels and manual tuning of RADSEP based on visual inspection of p-v diagrams introduce subjective choices into the kinematic fits.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fitting only the approaching side of BRI1335-0417 while fitting both sides for the other galaxies means the kinematic metrics are not derived identically across the sample.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SGP38326-2 is only resolved across 3 rings and is undergoing a probable tidal interaction with SGP38326-1, making its equilibrium disc assumption and high V/sigma uncertain.
