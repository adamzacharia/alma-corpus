## Analysis recipe

1. **Continuum self-calibration and subtraction**: Self-calibrate channel-averaged line-free continuum in 1-2 rounds, apply to native visibilities, subtract continuum in uv plane  
  > "The continuum was substracted from the self-calibrated visibilities in the $uv$ plane using line-free channels." (2.1 Calibration)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image cubes and extract source spectrum**: tclean with Briggs robust 0.5 and auto-mask; spectrum extracted at the continuum peak pixel  
  > "The spectrum of the source at the central pixel, which corresponds to the position of the continuum peak, was then extracted" (2.2 Generation of the source spectrum)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "The robustness parameter was set to 0.5 for the Briggs weighting of the baselines during this first round of imaging." (2.2 Generation of the source spectrum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: C18O 2-1 re-imaged with manual masks gave same result  
  > "For confirmation purposes, we imaged the C$^{18}$O 2 $-$ 1 line manually masking every channel, and obtained the same result." (2.2 Generation of the source spectrum)
3. **Identify molecular lines**: Assign lines above 4 sigma using JPL and CDMS catalogs; detection criteria based on number of lines/isotopologs or >10 sigma  
  > "We attempted to assign every observed line with an intensity above 4$\sigma$" (3.1 Molecular detections)
   - software: MADCUBAIJ
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked competing identifications and missing lines for each species  
  > "Then, for every putative assignment, we checked for competing identifications" (3.1 Molecular detections)
4. **CH3OH rotational diagram and column densities**: Gaussian fits to each CH3OH line (fixed VLSR 8.64, FWHM 3.33 km/s), rotational diagram linear fit, beam-dilution correction with filling factor ~7.5; Trot applied to CH2DOH, CH3OCH3, CH3OCHO  
  > "using the Python function *curve_fit* with a fixed $V_{LSR}$ = 8.64 km s$^{-1}$, and FWHM = 3.33 km s$^{-1}$" (3.2.1 Rotational temperature and column densities)
   - software: Python curve_fit
   - parameter: Source size <= (0.29±0.15)"x(0.12±0.10)" from 2D Gaussian fit of 10(-3)-11(-2) moment 0 map  
  > "The source size was estimated with a 2D Gaussian fit of the 10$_{-3}$ $-$ 11$_{-2}$[^5] moment 0 map after deconvolving the beam size." (3.2.1 Rotational temperature and column densities)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin lines; column densities are lower limits; CH3OCHO corrected by factor 2.1 for torsional states  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) and optically thin lines" (7 Rotational diagram)
   - fitting: Linear least squares fit with curve_fit; priors/likelihood not stated  
  > "The rotational diagram fit was performed with the function *curve_fit* in Python." (3.2.1 Rotational temperature and column densities)
   - validation: Refit excluding brightest line gives same column density; optical depth tau=0.1  
  > "The optical thickness of the brightest detected line is $\tau$ = 0.1." (3.2.1 Rotational temperature and column densities)
5. **Map spatial distributions**: Re-image identified lines at robust 0.0 and make moment 0 maps; 2D Gaussian fit to size of methanol emission  
  > "the individual data cubes cleaned with a robustness parameter of 0.0 for a better angular resolution (Sect. [2.3](#obs_im)) were integrated over the full line width" (3.3 Spatial distributions)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian after beam deconvolution  
  > "obtained with a 2D Gaussian fit after deconvolving the beam size, is (0.26 $\pm$ 0.08)$\ensuremath{''}$ x (0.12 $\pm$ 0.11)$\ensuremath{''}$" (3.3 Spatial distributions)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Search for rotation signature**: High-velocity red/blue moment maps and PV diagrams perpendicular and parallel to the outflow, compared with Keplerian (v∝R^-0.5) and infalling-rotating (v∝R^-1) curves  
  > "a Keplerian rotation curve ($v \propto R^{-0.5}$, shown in red in Fig. [8](#pv)) is able to reproduce the observed spin-up feature" (3.4 Rotation signature)
   - assumption: Outflow direction is rotation axis of potential disk  
  > "the velocity gradient is orthogonal to the observed outflow, which is assumed to be the axis of rotation of a potential protostellar disk" (3.4 Rotation signature)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Infalling-rotating envelope cannot be ruled out  
  > "but the model considering an infalling-rotating envelope ($v \propto R^{-1}$, shown in orange in Fig. [8](#pv)) cannot be ruled out." (3.4 Rotation signature)

## Qualitative findings

- CH3OH, CH3OCH3 and CH3OCHO emission is compact/unresolved around the protostar, whereas small molecules extend over the slow outflow.  
  > "By contrast, the emission of all organic molecules with 5 or more atoms is unresolved in a compact region around the protostar." (3.3 Spatial distributions)
- Hydrocarbon-poor chemistry (C2H not detected at the continuum peak) similar to other hot corinos.  
  > "implies a hydrocarbon-poor, organic chemistry similar to other detected hot corinos." (Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Spatially and spectrally resolved line observations needed to confirm a rotationally supported disk  
  > "However, spatially and spectrally resolved line observations are needed to confirm the presence of a rotationally supported disk." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Infalling-rotating envelope (v∝R^-1) instead of Keplerian disk cannot be ruled out  
  > "but the model considering an infalling-rotating envelope ($v \propto R^{-1}$, shown in orange in Fig. [8](#pv)) cannot be ruled out." (3.4 Rotation signature)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Column densities are lower limits because of maximum source size; extended methanol may be filtered out by lack of short baselines; possible optical thickness  
  > "Therefore, the calculated column density should be considered a lower limit." (3.2.1 Rotational temperature and column densities); "that could be filtered out in these kind of observations that lack short baselines" (3.2.1 Rotational temperature and column densities)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotational temperature from only four CH3OH lines, with the same Trot assumed for other molecules
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Resolution (~100 au) too coarse to distinguish Keplerian disk from infalling-rotating envelope; no quantitative kinematic fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inconsistent Trot quoted in discussion (249 ± 42 K) versus 261 ± 46 K
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source size upper limit makes filling factor and column densities highly uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No short-spacing data, so extended emission filtered
