## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image 12 m + 7 m + total power CO(2-1) cube**: Standard calibration, concatenation of 12 m and 7 m visibilities, tclean imaging with the total power cube as model  
  > "After standard calibration, we concatenated the visibilities corresponding to the 7 m and 12 m interferometric data and imaged them together." (2.1 ALMA data: molecular gas emission); "We used the total power observations as model within `tclean` in CASA, so that the final cube recovers all the flux." (2.1 ALMA data: molecular gas emission)
   - software: CASA 5.4.1, tclean
   - parameter: Hogbom deconvolution, natural weighting, 2 mJy/beam threshold, 2.5 km/s channels  
  > "we cleaned using the Hogbom algorithm with natural weighting, down to a threshold of 2 mJy per beam" (2.1 ALMA data: molecular gas emission)
   - parameter: Total power baseline: order-1 polynomial in line-free windows 755-855 and 1500-1600 km/s  
  > "we fitted a polynomial of order 1 in a fixed velocity window (755 to 855 km s$^{-1}$ and 1500 to 1600 km s$^{-1}$)" (2.1 ALMA data: molecular gas emission)
   - parameter: Pixel 0.082 arcsec, image 1344 x 1440 pixels  
  > "We chose a pixel size of $0.082''$ and an image size of $1344 \times 1440$ pixels" (2.1 ALMA data: molecular gas emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Mask NGC 3227 and build moment maps of the TDG**: Mask built from smoothed, clipped HI cube; dilated-mask moment maps (4 sigma seed, 1.5 sigma extension) integrated over 1060-1360 km/s  
  > "Firstly, we smoothed the H I cube to 30$''$ resolution in space and 50 km s$^{-1}$ in velocity, boosting the signal-to-noise ratio." (2.3 Moment maps of the TDG emission); "we started from a threshold of $4\sigma$ based on the rms map, and then dilated the mask to include any adjacent voxels above $1.5\sigma$" (2.3 Moment maps of the TDG emission)
   - software: CASA 5.4.1
   - parameter: HI clipped at 3 sigma_sm with sigma_sm = 1 mJy/beam  
  > "Then, we clipped pixels below 3$\sigma_{\rm sm}$, where $\sigma_{\rm sm} = 1$ mJy/beam is the rms noise per channel in the smoothed H I cube." (2.3 Moment maps of the TDG emission)
   - parameter: Line-free range 1360-1450 km/s used for pixel-by-pixel rms  
  > "We consider the velocity range \[1360, 1450\] km s$^{-1}$ as line-free and use those channels to estimate the rms noise on a pixel-by-pixel basis." (2.3 Moment maps of the TDG emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Convert CO and HI intensities to gas surface densities**: Constant alpha_CO^2-1 for CO(2-1); optically thin HI formula  
  > "which is the average value measured by (cite: 2013ApJ...777....5S) directly on the CO(2-1) line for a set of resolved nearby galaxy discs" (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities); "this is equivalent to multiplying by 0.0145 to transform from K km s$^{-1}$" (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities)
   - parameter: alpha_CO^2-1 = 4.4 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "we apply a constant factor of $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}^{2-1} = 4.4\,M_\odot$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2)^{-1}$" (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities)
   - assumption: Near-solar metallicity of the TDG makes a constant alpha_CO reasonable; includes helium factor 1.36  
  > "The near-solar metallicity of the TDG suggests that this is probably a reasonable assumption." (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities); "including a factor of 1.36 to correct for the presence of helium" (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure H2/HI ratio, diffuse CO fraction and CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) ratio**: Measurements at matched 6.3 arcsec using 4 sigma dilated masks; diffuse component = (12m+7m+TP) minus 12m+7m channel by channel; R21 from TP CO(2-1) over IRAM CO(1-0) convolved to 28 arcsec  
  > "Measurements at matched spatial resolution ($6.3''$) applying a mask of significance to both cubes ($4\sigma$ dilated mask)" (Table 1 notes); "the result of subtracting the intensity from ALMA 12 m$+$7 m$+$TP minus the intensity from 12 m$+$7 m only, channel by channel" (4.1.2 Diffuse molecular gas); "We first match resolutions by convolving the IRAM 30 m cube to $28''$ resolution." (4.1.3 CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) ratio)
   - parameter: North/south split at Dec = 19:51:50  
  > "separated by a declination cut at Dec${=}19^\circ 51' 50''$" (Our Target: The Tidal Dwarf Galaxy J1023+1952)
   - assumption: Extended emission is interpreted as diffuse molecular gas  
  > "The extended emission is likely associated with diffuse molecular gas" (4.1.2 Diffuse molecular gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Identify GMCs with CPROPS (and SCIMES as cross-check)**: CPROPS segmentation from a 4 sigma mask expanded to 1.5 sigma, local maxima criteria; SCIMES uses the same mask of significance  
  > "we start from a mask of significance defined as those voxels with signal above $4\sigma$ expanded to any adjacent voxels with emission above $1.5\sigma$" (3.1 GMC segmentation with CPROPS); "The same mask of significance is used for SCIMES (cite: 2015MNRAS.454.2067C)" (3.1 GMC segmentation with CPROPS)
   - software: CPROPS, SCIMES
   - parameter: Local maxima must be 2 sigma_rms above the merge level  
  > "Additionally, the local maxima must be 2$\sigma_{\rm rms}$ above the merge level with other clouds." (3.1 GMC segmentation with CPROPS)
   - parameter: Maxima kept only if associated area exceeds one synthesised beam; islands of 1-2 beams not decomposed  
  > "local maxima are subsequently identified and kept only if the voxels that are closer to a given maximum than to any other maxima define an area larger than the synthesised beam" (3.1 GMC segmentation with CPROPS)
   - parameter: Emission considered where primary beam response >= 20% (~80 x 70 arcsec)  
  > "We consider emission as long as the primary beam response from ALMA is at least 20% of the maximum" (3.1 GMC segmentation with CPROPS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Results compared against SCIMES segmentation  
  > "The results for the cloud ensemble derived using CPROPS and SCIMES are very similar" (4.3 GMC properties and scaling relations)
6. **Derive GMC physical properties (L_CO, R, sigma_v, M_lum, M_vir)**: Intensity-weighted moments from the segmentation; moment extrapolation to 0 K and beam deconvolution; bootstrap for uncertainties; unresolved clouds excluded  
  > "we used the extrapolation of the moments to the 0 K contour implemented in CPROPS and SCIMES" (3.2 Deriving GMC properties); "Additionally, the synthesised beam was deconvolved from the extrapolated spatial moments." (3.2 Deriving GMC properties); "When the cloud size provided by the segmentation codes is smaller than the synthesized beam, the GMC is considered unresolved and is excluded from the analysis." (3.2 Deriving GMC properties)
   - software: CPROPS, SCIMES
   - parameter: M_lum = alpha_CO L_CO; M_vir = 1040 sigma_v^2 R (rho proportional to R^-1)  
  > "while the virial mass is estimated as $M_{\rm vir} = 1040 \sigma_v^2 R$ ($M_{\rm vir}$ in M$_\odot$ if $\sigma_v$ is in km s$^{-1}$ and $R$ in pc)" (3.2 Deriving GMC properties)
   - parameter: R = eta sigma_r with eta = 1.91  
  > "We adopt $\eta = 1.91$ (cite: 1987ApJ...319..730S), which is the most standard assumption in Galactic and extragalactic studies." (3.2 Deriving GMC properties)
   - parameter: CPROPS bootstrap with 1000 iterations  
  > "we ran CPROPS with the bootstrap option, performing 1000 iterations." (3.2 Deriving GMC properties)
   - assumption: alpha_CO^2-1 = 4.4 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1; distance 14.5 Mpc; density profile rho proportional to R^-1 for virial mass  
  > "which assumes clouds with density profile $\rho \propto R^{-1}$" (3.2 Deriving GMC properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SCIMES used as an alternative segmentation; no correlation between GMC orientation and beam PA checked  
  > "reassuringly we find no correlation between the orientation of the GMCs and the PA of the ALMA synthesised beam" (4.3.1 GMC properties)
7. **Fit the GMC mass spectrum**: Fit cumulative mass distribution with truncated and non-truncated power laws; truncated version preferred  
  > "Rather than a binned histogram (differential form), we fit a cumulative mass distribution, which has been argued to be more robust in the case of small samples" (4.3.2 GMC mass spectra); "We fitted both a truncated and non-truncated power law and found that the truncated version applies better to our data." (4.3.2 GMC mass spectra)
   - parameter: M_GMC = alpha_CO^2-1 L_CO using all CPROPS clouds (extrapolated luminosities); 5 sigma completeness limit 5e4 Msun  
  > "We estimate our $5\sigma$ completeness limit as $5 \times 10^4$ M$_\odot$" (4.3 GMC properties and scaling relations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with slopes from Milky Way and other galaxies; repeated with SCIMES  
  > "the slope of the global truncated power-law fit is slightly higher with SCIMES, $\gamma = -1.84 \pm 0.15$" (7 Results with SCIMES)
8. **Fit GMC scaling relations (Larson, Heyer plot)**: Linear regression in log-log with ODR and with the Bayesian BayesLineFit, on extrapolated and deconvolved properties; Spearman rank correlation  
  > "The linear regressions are obtained with two different methods." (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations); "In both cases, these fits are based on the extrapolated and deconvolved GMC properties" (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations)
   - software: scipy.odr, BayesLineFit
   - fitting: ODR (scipy.odr, uncertainties in both variables) and BayesLineFit (Bayesian, includes intrinsic scatter, full posterior); priors and sampler settings not stated  
  > "As a complementary approach, we also fitted the observations using the publicly available Bayesian fitting code" (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations)
   - validation: Two fitting methods compared; CPROPS compared with SCIMES  
  > "`BayesLineFit` provides similar results as the ODR method but larger uncertainties" (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations)
9. **Compare GMC positions with young star-forming knots and clusters**: Overlay GMCs on astrometry-corrected HST F547M and H-alpha images and measure projected offsets from the knots of Lisenfeld et al. 2008

  > "the data are fitted in the log-log plane using the Python implementation" (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations)
- **BayesLineFit** : Bayesian line fitting with intrinsic scatter for scaling relations  
  > "As a complementary approach, we also fitted the observations using the publicly available Bayesian fitting code" (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations)

## Qualitative findings

- GMC masses, sizes and linewidths in the TDG resemble those of GMCs in the Milky Way and nearby galaxies  
  > "They show similar sizes, masses, and linewidths as GMCs in the Milky Way and in other nearby galaxies" (Summary and conclusions)
- GMCs are spread throughout the TDG, not concentrated in the star-forming south  
  > "These GMCs are spread throughout the entire TDG, and not preferentially concentrated in the star-forming part." (Summary and conclusions)
- Molecular-to-atomic ratio is higher in the quiescent north than in the star-forming south  
  > "the molecular-to-atomic gas ratio is surprisingly higher in the quiescent north, whereas star formation is enhanced in the more atomic gas-dominated south" (4.1.1 Molecular-to-atomic gas ratio)
- Diffuse CO and HI show a north-south velocity gradient and are well coupled; GMCs in the NE ridge are offset to lower velocities, suggesting a gaseous bridge toward NGC 3227  
  > "This may indicate that a gaseous bridge connects the TDG with the background spiral galaxy." (4.2 Kinematics)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Previous molecular gas studies of TDGs lacked the resolution to resolve individual GMCs  
  > "these studies target spatial resolutions that are too coarse to resolve individual giant molecular clouds" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Constant alpha_CO keeps surface density maps proportional to measured CO(2-1) intensity; CPROPS used to ease comparison with literature  
  > "has the advantage that our surface density maps remain proportional to the directly measured CO(2-1) intensity" (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities); "We focus on CPROPS to minimise discrepancies with studies from the literature due to different segmentation codes." (4.3.3 GMC scaling relations)
- Alternative: Spatially varying alpha_CO modulated by R21 rejected because of the coarse resolution of the R21 map  
  > "We prefer to avoid this modulation of $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}$ by $R_{21}$ because of the coarse resolution ($\sim$28$''$) of the available $R_{21}$ map" (2.4 Conversion to gas surface densities)
- Alternative: SCIMES segmentation tried as alternative to CPROPS; results similar  
  > "The results from this section confirm that GMC properties do not look very different when identified with CPROPS or SCIMES." (7 Results with SCIMES)
- Alternative: Non-truncated power law fitted but truncated version preferred  
  > "We fitted both a truncated and non-truncated power law and found that the truncated version applies better to our data." (4.3.2 GMC mass spectra)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Observed projected surface densities may not track volumetric densities  
  > "the largest limitation is that we are dealing with projected surface densities, and the observed surface densities might not necessarily track the volumetric gas densities" (5.5 What drives star formation in TDGs?)
- Limitation (authors): alpha_CO uncharacterised in TDGs; factor ~10 variation cannot be ruled out  
  > "technically, we cannot rule out a variation of a factor ${\sim}10$ in $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}$" (4.3.3 Virial relation)
- Limitation (authors): Smallest clouds may be unresolved and blending is likely for some  
  > "This means that the smallest clouds may be unresolved and blending is likely for some." (4.3.2 GMC mass spectra)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): All GMC masses depend on one assumed alpha_CO and one distance; the 1 dex virial/luminous offset is degenerate with both
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cloud sizes are comparable to the 45 pc beam; extrapolation and deconvolution raise radius errors to ~55%, and size-linewidth slope depends on this
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ODR and Bayesian slopes differ substantially for the mass-size relation (2.08 vs 3.25)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Diffuse fraction depends on total power calibration and flux scaling, with no error budget given
- Caveat (inferred, ours): North/south GMC counts are inconsistent between sections (62/19 vs 82/29 with 111 total)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mask of NGC 3227 emission relies on HI-defined regions; contamination from the background galaxy is not quantified
