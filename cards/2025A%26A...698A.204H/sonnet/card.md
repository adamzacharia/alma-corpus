# An ALMA spectroscopic survey of the Planck high-redshift object PLCK G073.4−57.5 confirms two protoclusters

`2025A&A...698A.204H` | Astronomy and Astrophysics 2025 | first author: Hill, Ryley | arXiv: 2412.00294 | DOI: 10.1051/0004-6361/202453238

## Science

- Fields: high_z_galaxies, star_formation
- Question: What is the redshift distribution of the galaxies in the Planck-selected field G073, are the two suspected structures (z~1.5 and z~2) real protoclusters, and what are their gas and star-formation properties?  
  > "Characterizing the full redshift distribution of the galaxies within G073 is needed in order to better understand this representative example of Planck-selected objects, and connect them to the emergence of galaxy clusters." (abstract)
- Target **PLCK G073.4-57.5 (G073)**: coordinates _not stated_; z/distance structures at <z> = 1.53 and <z> = 2.31; SCUBA-2 galaxies at z = 2.61  
  > "Eleven of these galaxies are SPIRE-selected and lie in two structures at $\langle z \rangle\,{=}\,1.53$ and $\langle z \rangle\,{=}\,2.31$" (abstract); distance from spectroscopic redshifts from ALMA CO/Ci lines (this work)  
  > "We find 15 emission lines in 13 galaxies, and using existing photometry information, we determined the spectroscopic redshift of all 13 galaxies." (abstract)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Search cubes for emission lines**: LineSeeker on primary-beam-uncorrected LSB and USB cubes with Gaussian spectral convolution; cutoff set from most significant negative peak (S/N 6.1)  
  > "we found that the most significant negative peak across all of the data cubes had an S/N of 6.1; thus, we used this as our cutoff for identifying real positive peaks." (3.1 CO and Ci line search for protocluster galaxies)
   - software: LineSeeker
   - parameter: maximum kernel width 1000 km/s  
  > "We set the maximum width of the spectral convolution kernel to be 1000 km s$^{-1}$" (3.1 CO and Ci line search for protocluster galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Negative-peak search to set threshold; 5 sigma search at known positions found no extra lines  
  > "we systematically searched their spectra for fainter peaks down to 5$\sigma$ (without any Gaussian convolution), but this did not return any additional lines." (3.1 CO and Ci line search for protocluster galaxies)
2. **Search for Band 4 continuum sources**: Noise-weighted mean continuum maps per pointing; threshold equal to the largest negative peak (S/N 5.1)  
  > "finding that the most significant negative peak across all 12 maps was 5.1." (3.2 ALMA Band 4 continuum search)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract spectra and measure lines and continuum**: Elliptical apertures from the 2 sigma contours; continuum subtraction with imcontsub; single Gaussian fits; line flux summed over ±3 sigma; L' per Solomon+97; continuum from line-free channels with S/N>2  
  > "we fit single Gaussian functions to the observed lines in order to estimate various line properties. From the fit, line strengths are estimated by summing all channels within ${\pm}3\sigma$ of the mean of the fit" (3.3 Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: average aperture semi-major axis 1.6''  
  > "the average aperture semi-major axis size was found to be 1.6$''$" (3.3 Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
   - fitting: single Gaussian per line  
  > "For the 13 galaxies with line emission, we fit single Gaussian functions to the observed lines in order to estimate various line properties." (Data analysis, Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Assign line identification and redshift**: Likelihood ratio test for CO(3-2)+CI vs single line, threshold 0.05; chi2 SED fit for ID 24; ID 4 assumed at ID 3 redshift  
  > "we applied a threshold of 0.05 below which we rejected the null hypothesis." (3.3 Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
   - assumption: only two redshift possibilities (z~1.5 and z~2.3) for non-SCUBA-2 lines  
  > "we assumed that there are only two redshift possibilities for all of the lines we detected." (2.2 Cycle 6 ALMA observations)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits with L = exp(-chi2/2)  
  > "We first fit a single Gaussian with three free parameters (amplitude, mean, and standard deviation) to the LSB line and calculate the likelihood function (here given by $\mathcal{L}\,{=}\,\exp(-\chi^2/2)$)." (Data analysis, Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
   - validation: Compared with Kneissl+19 photometric redshifts  
  > "the spectroscopic redshift of ID 1 is 2.306, compared to the photometric redshift of 2.42$\pm$0.15" (3.3 Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
5. **Derive stellar mass and SFR**: CIGALE SED fitting (delayed SFH with burst, BC03, Draine14 dust, Calzetti attenuation, solar metallicity); SFR_IR from cmcirsed modified blackbody with Kennicutt&Evans relation  
  > "we assumed a delayed star-formation history with an optional burst, a (cite: chabrier03) IMF, the stellar population models of (cite: bruzual03), the dust models from (cite: draine14), the (cite: calzetti00) attenuation law, and solar metallicity" (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
   - software: CIGALE, cmcirsed
   - parameter: beta = 1.8, dust temperature free  
  > "assuming the CO-derived redshift and a dust emissivity-index $\beta$ equal to 1.8 (cite: cortese14; pokhrel16), leaving the dust temperatures as free parameters." (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; SFR_IR = 1.40e-10 L_IR  
  > "${\rm SFR_{\rm IR}}\,=\,1.40\times 10^{-10}\times L_{\rm IR}$" (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
   - fitting: chi2 best fit; parameters are likelihood-weighted means and standard deviations  
  > "The best-fit is assessed through $\chi^2$, and the best-fit parameters and associated uncertainties are estimated as the likelihood-weighted means and standard deviations." (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
   - validation: Compared with Kneissl+19 SFRs and CIGALE with cmcirsed dust model  
  > "However, the differences were within the uncertainties." (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
6. **Derive molecular gas masses**: M_gas = alpha_CO L'CO(1-0) with CO(3-2) and CO(4-3) converted via r31=0.60 and r41=0.32; CI mass using alpha_CI = 5.2 alpha_CO  
  > "using the  and  to  ratios $r_{3,1}\,{=}\,0.60\,{\pm}\,0.11$ and $r_{4,1}\,{=}\,0.32\,{\pm}\,0.05$ (cite: birkin2021)." (4.2 Molecular gas masses)
   - parameter: alpha_CO = 4.36  
  > "we adopted a conservative $\alpha_\mathrm{CO}$ = 4.36 M$_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$" (4.2 Molecular gas masses)
   - assumption: same alpha_CO for all sources regardless of MS classification  
  > "for all sources, independent of their MS-based classification." (4.2 Molecular gas masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-check with dust continuum gas masses (Scoville+16, Liu+19), mean ratio 1.04  
  > "with a mean ratio of the dust continuum gas mass over the CO/Ci gas mass of 1.04." (4.2 Molecular gas masses)
7. **Compare gas properties to field and cluster samples and to TNG300**: Compute depletion time and gas-to-stellar ratio, MS offsets (Popesso+23), scaling relations of Liu+19 and Sargent+14, update Gouin+22 simulation comparison with spectroscopic redshifts  
  > "We corrected all the gas mass estimates from this literature cluster sample to have the same $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ conversion factor of 4.36 (when possible)" (5.2 Depletion timescales)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

  > "we first used the publicly available tool `LineSeeker`" (3.1 CO and Ci line search for protocluster galaxies)
- **CIGALE** : SED fitting for stellar mass and SFR  
  > "by modelling their SEDs with the Code Investigating GAlaxy Emission" (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
- **cmcirsed** : modified blackbody fit for L_IR  
  > "using the `cmcirsed` package (cite: casey12c)" (4.1 Stellar masses and star-formation rates)
- **EAZY** : photometric redshift probability densities (from earlier work) summed for Fig. 4  
  > "the combined probability density was calculated by summing the probability densities estimated by `EAZY` over the whole sample." (5.1 Redshift distribution)

## Qualitative findings

- No SCUBA-2-selected sources lie in the z~1.5 or z~2.3 structures; they may trace a third, more distant structure  
  > "This suggests a possibility that there are in fact three (or more) protoclusters along the line of sight, although this remains to be confirmed." (6.3 The nature of 850 um-bright sources in PHz fields)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 4 chosen to cover CO(3-2) at z=1.5 and CI(1-0) at z~2 and to enlarge the primary beam to cover all 18 Band 6 galaxies  
  > "Since the ALMA primary beam is larger at 2 mm than at 1 mm, our observations covered all 18 galaxies detected by (cite: kneissl19)." (2.1 Target selection)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Tentative CO(5-4) in ID 8 at z=1.545 from Kneissl+19 rejected as noise excursion  
  > "we conclude that the possible  line was in fact a noise excursion." (3.3 Redshifts, line measurements and continuum measurements)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Gas mass has large systematic uncertainties from alpha_CO, which are ignored; CI excitation not constrained  
  > "there are large systematic uncertainties involved in estimating the gas mass, which we ignore here for simplicity." (4.2 Molecular gas masses)
- Limitation (authors): Simulation comparison is only qualitative  
  > "this comparison only qualitatively illustrates broad agreement." (6.1 The large-scale structure probed by G073)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line identifications for single-line sources rely on a two-redshift prior and low-S/N tests
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas fractions biased by line-luminosity selection
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Selection of the comparison literature sample is heterogeneous
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed alpha_CO for starbursts may overestimate masses
