## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine ALMA 7m with IRAM 30m and make moment maps**: Merge 30m pseudo-visibilities (0-7 m) with 7m data, CLEAN, primary beam correct, convert to Tmb; zeroth moment maps  
  > "The resulting data cube was then scaled from Jy beam$^{-1}$ to $T_{\mathrm{mb}}$ temperature scale using the synthesized beam size." (2.3)
   - software: GILDAS/MAPPING, CASA immoments
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract horizontal intensity profiles**: Rotate maps 14 deg; average over 10 arcsec in dy at PDR (dy~1.2) and dense core (dy~16.5)  
  > "we averaged the integrated line intensity over $10^{\prime\prime}$ in the $\delta y$ direction" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Calibrate C17O-to-H2 factor and derive nH2 profiles**: Fit C17O hyperfine lines in LTE with Tex=15 K at PDR and core; X = N(H2)/N(C17O); nH2 = X N(C17O)/l_depth with interpolated X  
  > "Assuming a typical cloud depth along the line of sight of $\ell_{\mathrm{depth}} \simeq 0.1$ pc" (3.2.2)
   - software: SpectralRadex
   - parameter: Tex(C17O)=15 K; l_depth=0.1 pc  
  > "with the excitation temperature fixed at $T_{\mathrm{ex}}=15$ K, we repeated the fitting procedure but now for the ALMA 7m+30m data" (3.2.1)
   - assumption: LTE for C17O 1-0; N(13CO)/N(C18O)~8 for consistency check  
  > "Assuming $N(^{13}\mathrm{CO})/N(\mathrm{C^{18}O}) \sim 8$" (3.2.1)
   - fitting: Gaussian hyperfine components fit with MCMC  
  > "Each hyperfine component was modeled as an independent Gaussian with fixed velocity separations" (3.2.1)
   - validation: Compared with Habart2005 density model and 13CO conversion factors  
  > "along with the profile modeled by (cite: Habart2005) convolved with a $15^{\prime\prime}$ full width at half maximum (FWHM) Gaussian" (3.2.2)
4. **Derive Tkin from CH3CN K-ladder**: Non-LTE RADEX fit of CH3CN J=6-5 K=0,1,2 with nH2 fixed from C17O; free Tkin and N  
  > "the modeled CH$_3$CN intensities only depend on $T_{\mathrm{kin}}$ and $N(\mathrm{CH_3CN})$ as free parameters" (3.2.3)
   - software: SpectralRadex, RADEX, emcee
   - parameter: E/A = 1; Tbg=2.73 K; dv=0.6 km/s; K=2 used as upper limit for dx>=30 arcsec  
  > "we assumed a ratio of $\mathrm{E/A} = 1$ between the two noninteracting torsional substates" (3.2.3)
   - fitting: emcee affine-invariant MCMC, walkers = 4x free parameters, 2000 burn-in + 3000 steps, 16/50/84 percentiles, chi2 likelihood, points every ~7.5 arcsec  
  > "The number of walkers was set to four times the number of free model parameters, with 2000 burn-in steps and an additional 3000 steps used to sample the posterior distributions." (3.2)
   - validation: Compared with earlier CH3CN temperatures  
  > "which is in good agreement with previous estimates using CH$_3$CN (cite: Gratier2013)" (3.2.3)
5. **Thermal pressure**: P_th = nH Tkin with nH ~ 2 nH2  
  > "$P_{\mathrm{th}} = n_{\mathrm{H}} T_{\mathrm{kin}}$, where $n_{\mathrm{H}}=n(\mathrm{H})+2n(\mathrm{H_2})\sim 2n_{\mathrm{H_2}}$" (3.2.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with range from Hernandez-Vera2023  
  > "together with the range of values determined from high angular resolution observations by (cite: Hernandez-Vera2023)" (3.2.4)
6. **Column density profiles of other organics**: Non-LTE RADEX for H2CO, CH3OH, HNCO, HC3N; rotational-diagram (optically thin, fixed Tex) for CH2CO and CH3CHO; only N free  
  > "the only free parameter to fit is $N$" (3.2.5)
   - software: SpectralRadex, emcee
   - parameter: E/A=1 for CH3OH, CH3CHO; OPR=3 for H2CO, CH2CO  
  > "we assumed $\mathrm{E/A} = 1$ for CH$_3$OH and CH$_3$CHO and an ortho-to-para ratio of $\mathrm{OPR} = 3$ for H$_2$CO and CH$_2$CO" (3.2.5)
   - fitting: Same MCMC strategy as CH3CN  
  > "The fitting strategy was essentially the same as for CH$_3$CN" (3.2.5)
   - validation: Compared with WHISPER-based column densities at PDR and core  
  > "We checked the consistency of our results by comparing them with previous efforts." (3.2.5)

## Qualitative findings

- O-bearing and N-bearing species have different spatial distributions; HC3N is clumpy near the dense core while CH3CN forms a thin PDR filament  
  > "O-bearing and N-bearing molecules apparently have different spatial distributions, which probably implies disparities in their chemistry" (3.1)
- Thermal pressure is high and roughly isobaric across the PDR  
  > "to first order, the Horsehead PDR appears to maintain an isobaric structure" (3.2.4)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Non-LTE approach used because complex molecules are subthermally excited  
  > "previous studies have shown that complex molecules in the Horsehead edge are, in general, subthermally excited" (3.2)
- Alternative: Older CH3CN collisional rates (Green1986) reproduce earlier column densities but are considered underestimated at low T  
  > "When we repeated our analysis using the older rates from (cite: Green1986), our derived $N$ values matched those reported by (cite: Gratier2013)." (3.2.5)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Did not recover high temperatures (>100 K) at the cloud edge; limited angular resolution and sensitivity  
  > "This discrepancy likely stems from the limited angular resolution and sensitivity of the observations" (3.2.3)
- Limitation (authors): Uncertainties exclude systematics from fixed parameters  
  > "without including systematic uncertainties on the fixed parameters" (3.2.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Many parameters fixed (Tex, linewidth, E/A, OPR, depth 0.1 pc) with no systematic error budget
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam pickup from colder gas biases C17O density and temperature at the edge
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Atomic C interpretation is qualitative; no chemical modelling
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tkin from only two N(H2) calibrations extrapolated across profile
