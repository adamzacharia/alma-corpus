## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce ALMA-SV data and image cubes**: Public calibrated data; CASA continuum subtraction (zeroth-order fit) and Clark cleaning with Briggs robust 0  
  > "Data reduction and continuum subtraction were performed using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (CASA) software" (2.1. ALMA Science Verification observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: pixel 0.4", robust 0.0  
  > "a Briggs weighting with a robustness parameter of 0.0 was applied" (2.1. ALMA Science Verification observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Compute 13C-MF partition functions**: Rotational (direct sum, RAM/BELGI energies) x torsional x vibrational partition function  
  > "the partition function was approximated as the product of the rotational (Q rot ), torsional (Q tor ) and vibrational (without the torsional contribution, Q vib ) contributions" (Appendix A)
   - software: BELGI
   - parameter: rotational states up to J = 79; torsional up to vt=6  
  > "the torsional-rotational states (v t = 0 and A-symmetry) up to J = 79 were included in Eq. (A2)" (A.1. Rotational partition function)
   - assumption: 13C-MF vibrational fundamentals equal to the main isotopologue; same dipole moments as 12C-MF  
  > "we assumed that the vibrational fundamental frequencies of [13] C 1 –MF and [13] C 2 – MF are approximately the same as for the main isotopologue" (A.3. Vibrational partition function)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Convergence study; comparison to asymmetric-top approximation  
  > "a convergence study for the partition functions of [13] Cisotopologues, which ensures that high enough energy levels have been included for a particular temperature, has been carried out in this work." (3.1. Partition functions)
3. **LTE modelling of MF and 13C-MF per ALMA spw**: XCLASS LTE models, only column density free; 13C-MF same size, Trot, velocity and linewidth as 12C-MF; each spw fit individually  
  > "we assumed that the 13 C 1 –MF and 13 C 2 –MF species emits within the same source size, at the same rotational temperature and velocity, and with the same line–width as the methyl formate molecule." (4.2. XCLASS modeling and Herschel/HIFI spectral fit)
   - software: XCLASS
   - parameter: Source size 3"; Trot 80 K (Compact Ridge), 128 K (Hot Core-SW); vLSR 7.3 km/s; linewidth 1.2 and 2.4 km/s  
  > "a rotation temperature of 80 K toward the Compact Ridge and of 128 K toward the HotCore–SW" (5.1.2. Spectra)
   - assumption: LTE; optically thick lines excluded  
  > "The optically thick lines, although shown in appendix, are excluded from our model due to optical depth problem in the model." (5.1.2. Spectra)
   - fitting: Best-fit by reduced chi2 (0.3-2.4); no sampler stated  
  > "The quality of our models is based on the reduced χ [2], which lies in the range 0.3–2.4" (5.1.2. Spectra)
   - validation: Single parameter set fit to all data gives similar ratio; outlier test  
  > "a single set of parameters (not shown here) also fit all the data and give rise, within the uncertainties, to a similar isotopic abundance ratio." (6.1.3. Scattering on the isotopic ratio)
4. **Derive 12C/13C ratio and uncertainty**: Ratio of column densities per spw, averaged; relative errors from spw scatter set the absolute uncertainty  
  > "we have chosen to fit the [12] C/ [13] C ratio in each sub-band individually and to use the relative errors in the fit from those bands to set the absolute uncertainty to our measurement." (6.1.3. Scattering on the isotopic ratio)
   - assumption: Both 13C-MF isotopologues have similar abundances when combined  
  > "If we assume the ratio to be the same for both isotopologues, meaning there is no significant difference" (5.1.3. Isotopic 12C/13C ratio)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CO ratios from Milam et al. gradient  
  > "The same conclusion applies for the isotopic ratios derived towards the Orion–KL hot core–SW and compact ridge positions (ALMA-SV data)." (6.4. The [13] C budget in the galaxy)

## Qualitative findings

- 13C-MF isotopologues are imaged for the first time, concentrated at the Compact Ridge and Hot Core-SW (5 sigma)  
  > "the [13] C–MF isotopologues are mainly detected toward the Compact Ridge and the Hot Core-SW (5σ detection level, see Fig 1)." (5.1.1. Emission maps)
- Partition function including all torsional-vibrational states is larger than the JPL value, raising derived MF abundances  
  > "The partition function used here is higher than that in the JPL catalog by a factor of 1.2 at a temperature of 150 K, and by a factor of 2.5 at 300 K." (4.1. Database and MF, [13] C–MF frequencies)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Homogeneous data and accurate partition functions are needed for a precise isotope ratio  
  > "in order to derive a [12] C/ [13] C ratio with accuracy and to significantly reduce uncertainties, homogeneous data are a necessity." (1. Introduction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): APEX data lack sensitivity for definitive 13C-MF detection; ~10% sub-band calibration differences  
  > "we cannot claim a definitive detection of this molecule in the APEX observations." (5.2.3. HCOO13CH3)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LTE with shared source size/Trot for all isotopologues and optically thick lines excluded
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ratio uncertainty derived from spw scatter, not a formal fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Image-sideband contamination in APEX data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Different spectral resolution/beam for ALMA vs APEX
