## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and combine visibilities**: Reduced in CASA; phase-shifted April 2014 BDF3299 data to the October 2013 phase center; manually rescaled relative weights to match measured cube noise variance  
  > "we manually re-scaled their relative weights so that they matched the noise variance measured in the two cubes." (2 Sample selection, observations and data analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make cubes and continuum maps**: Natural-weighted uncleaned cubes; continuum from collapsing four SPWs with proper weighting  
  > "The continuum maps were obtained by collapsing the four spectral windows" (2 Sample selection, observations and data analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **[CII] upper limits at optical positions**: Extract spectrum on central beam, rms in 100 km/s bins; 3 sigma limits assuming 100 km/s line width  
  > "To estimate the upper limit on the line flux we assume a line width of $\rm 100~km/s$" (3 [CII] emission at the location of the optical sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure offset [CII] clump in BDF3299**: Fit 2D Gaussian, extract spectrum from half-maximum ellipse aperture, apply aperture correction 2.5, continuum subtraction, integrate -200<V<0 km/s  
  > "we have applied a flux aperture correction (inferred from the flux extracted with the same aperture from the continuum serendipitae), which results to be a factor of 2.5." (4.1 Line detection)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian; line properties averaged from Gaussian fit and moment analysis  
  > "are calculated both through the moment analysis and through a Gaussian fit; the values reported in the table are average values." (Table 3)
   - validation: Split data in three subsets and compared line flux; compared positive vs negative detection counts in the cube  
  > "We have separated the visibilities obtained for BDF3299 in three data sets" (4.2 Reliability of the detection)
5. **Exclude low-z CO identification**: Compare with CO source counts from da Cunha et al. (M82 and MW SLEDs) and Schmidt-Kennicutt expectation for counterparts in Y-band and continuum  
  > "We have first conservatively adopted the CO SLED of the M82 center" (4.3 Line identification)
   - assumption: Local S-K relation; solar metallicity foreground  
  > "we have conservatively assumed the putative foreground galaxies to have solar metallicity" (4.3 Line identification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compare with galaxy-formation model**: Smooth Vallini+2013 simulation to optical seeing and ALMA beam, compare clump offsets  
  > "we have smoothed the simulation to the angular resolution of our optical observations (0.5$''$ seeing) and to the ALMA beam of our observations." (4.4 Interpretation of the [CII] emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Dust continuum limits: FIR/UV and dust mass**: 3 sigma upper limits at 1.2 mm corrected for CMB; dust masses for assumed T of 27.6 K and 45 K  
  > "We follow a similar approach as these papers to infer an upper limit on the dust mass from our 3$\sigma$ upper limits at $\rm \lambda _{obs}=1.2~mm$, corrected for the effects of CMB." (5 Continuum dust emission)
   - assumption: Dust temperature 27.6 K (beta=1.5) or 45 K; all sources at z=7 for CMB correction  
  > "for the sake of simplicity we have adopted the same correction assuming all three sources at z=7" (5 Continuum dust emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- No [CII] at the optical positions; [CII] emitted by an offset clump 0.7 arcsec (4 kpc) away, consistent with feedback-disrupted molecular clouds  
  > "These results suggest that molecular clouds in the central parts of primordial galaxies are rapidly disrupted by stellar feedback." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Target typical z~7 galaxies with SFR 5-15 Msun/yr rather than extreme systems, with spectroscopic redshifts needed for ALMA tuning  
  > "we recall that, even with the broad ALMA band, a spectroscopic redshift is needed to observe far-IR transitions redshifted into the millimeter band." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Line being low-z CO from a foreground galaxy: rejected on probability and counterpart brightness  
  > "the identification of the detected line with another transition associated with a foreground galaxy is extremely remote" (4.3 Line identification)
- Alternative: Dust-obscured star formation in the clump: not fully excluded  
  > "the current data are not deep enough to completely exclude the scenario of dust obscuration." (4.4 Interpretation of the [CII] emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Dust temperature unconstrained from single upper limit; dynamical mass extremely uncertain; B1/B2 marginal; lensing speculative  
  > "Constraints on dust masses are difficult to infer, because only one single photometric upper limit per source is available for the IR SED" (Conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Lya redshift may be offset from systemic, so [CII] limits depend on the searched velocity range
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Aperture correction factor 2.5 strongly affects flux and significance
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Positive/negative detection statistics from a small, non-independent search
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Manual weight rescaling not documented, could affect noise
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detection near optical position at 4.5 sigma peak; significance rests on aperture spectrum
