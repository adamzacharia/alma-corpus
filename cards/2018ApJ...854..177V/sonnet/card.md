## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit dust continuum and SED with DALI**: Manual stepwise fitting of Sigma_c, r_cav, h_c, psi, delta_dust to continuum visibility curve and SED  
  > "Find first estimates of $\Sigma_c$ and $r_{\rm cav}$ roughly to the continuum visibility curve, in particular the total flux and the location of the null." (3.2 Modeling approach)
   - software: DALI
   - parameter: gas-to-dust ratio 100 in outer disk; f_ls=0.85, chi=0.2 fixed; outer grid radius 400 AU  
  > "In the outer disk, the gas-to-dust ratio is set to GDR=100." (3.1 Physical model)
   - assumption: Surface density power law gamma=1 with exponential cut-off; gas and dust share profile  
  > "The gas and dust follow the same density profile, but the gas-to-dust ratio is varied throughout the disk" (3.1 Physical model)
   - fitting: Manual fitting; MCMC or chi2 not practical  
  > "As physical-chemical modeling is computationally expensive, MCMC or $\chi^2$ fitting is not practical." (3 Modeling)
   - validation: Compare model with visibility amplitudes, null location and total flux  
  > "The final models generally agree well with the data visibility curves." (4.1 Final models)
2. **Fit 13CO gas cavity and depletion**: Set delta_gas=1, compute 13CO, adjust r_c, h_c, psi, then vary delta_gas and r_cavgas to fit zero-moment map and visibilities  
  > "Vary $\delta_{\rm gas}$ and $r_{\rm cavgas}$ (for the disks where the $^{13}$CO emission is spatially resolved) to fit the inner part of the $^{13}$CO zero-moment map and visibility curve." (3.2 Modeling approach)
   - software: DALI
   - assumption: Isotope-selective photodissociation not included  
  > "The DALI module for isotope-selective photodissociation (cite: Miotello2014) is not included to limit the model computational times." (3.1 Physical model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Model C18O fluxes compared with observed integrated fluxes  
  > "The C$^{18}$O data have too low S/N to constrain the structure, but the integrated fluxes are compared with the model output." (3.2 Modeling approach)
3. **Quantify maximum gas and dust inside cavity**: Vary delta_dustcav and delta_gas for each model to derive upper limits  
  > "for each model we vary both $\delta_{\rm dustcav}$ and $\delta_{\rm gas}$." (4.2 Quantification of gas and dust inside the cavity)
   - software: DALI
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive transition disk fraction and compare disk populations**: Fraction of large-cavity disks among the 96 Lupus disks; outer radius via curve of growth at 90% flux; compare with exoplanet statistics  
  > "The latter is measured using a curve of growth method on the images, taking the outer radius at 90% of the flux." (5.4 Comparison with exoplanet statistics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- In three disks (Sz 91, J16083070, Sz 111) the gas cavity is smaller than the dust cavity.  
  > "the gas cavity radius is smaller than the dust cavity radius and the gas gaps are deep, with $\delta_{\rm gas}\leq10^{-2}$" (4.2 Quantification of gas and dust inside the cavity)
- RY Lup shows no gas gap.  
  > "RY Lup, although it has a large dust cavity, does not show any evidence of a gas gap in the $^{13}$CO" (4.2 Quantification of gas and dust inside the cavity)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Physical-chemical code needed because CO abundances and temperatures vary through the disk  
  > "Such a code is necessary to interpret the CO emission, as CO abundances and gas temperatures highly vary throughout the disk" (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Photoevaporation considered unlikely given accretion rates and large cavities.  
  > "which makes, in combination with the large cavity radius the photoevaporation mechanism an unlikely explanation" (5.1 Clearing mechanism)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Models are not necessarily unique; gas surface density uncertain by an order of magnitude.  
  > "we stress that the derived model parameters are not necessarily unique." (3 Modeling)
- Limitation (authors): Low S/N from 1-2 minute observations.  
  > "As the observations were only 1-2 minutes per sources, the S/N is lower" (2.1 Data reduction)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Manual fitting with no formal uncertainties; results depend on the fitter
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed GDR=100 conflicts with C18O fluxes suggesting lower
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Resolution cannot distinguish gap from cavity
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Transition fraction depends on the 20 AU threshold and sample of 96
