## Analysis recipe

1. **Use pipeline-calibrated ALMA CO cubes and search for detections**: Visual inspection of pipeline cubes and SoFiA-2 smooth+clip source finding with reliability module  
  > "we used the well-tested "smooth+clip" (S+C) algorithm, and the reliability module to reject false positives." (2.1)
   - software: SoFiA-2, iDaVIE
   - parameter: pixel threshold 3.8 sigma; kernelsXY = 0,3,6,9,12; kernelsZ = 0,3,7,15; linking lengths 5 spatial/3 spectral; min size 5 pixels; reliability 0.75  
  > "The linker module was run with spatial and spectral linking lengths of 5 and 3 pixels, respectively, and minimum sizes in both directions of 5 pixels." (2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with ALMA pipeline auto-masking; lowering thresholds (3.3, 0.5) gave no more detections  
  > "we also experimented with lowering the S+C noise threshold to 3.3, and the probability threshold in the reliability module down to 0.5" (2.1)
2. **Process H i VLA cubes and find/CLEAN H i sources**: Cubelets extracted per galaxy, SoFiA-2 masks, image-plane Hogbom CLEAN within masks  
  > "Using the source detection masks output by SoFiA-2, we performed an image-plane Högbom CLEAN (cite: hogbom74) in the cubelets centered on each detection." (2.2)
   - software: SoFiA-2, CASA
   - parameter: kernels 0,3,6 and 0,3,5; linking length 2; reliability off; CLEAN gain 10%, threshold 1x local rms  
  > "This process is done for all pixels in the mask above one times the local rms noise" (2.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Epoch 1 results  
  > "In Figure [8](#fig:epoch1), we show the comparison between Epoch 1 H i masses and H i masses from the full survey." (5.2)
3. **Derive gas masses**: M_HI = 49.7 S_HI D_L^2; M_H2 = alpha_CO x 6.36e-3 S_CO D_L^2 with fluxes in Jy Hz  
  > "M_{H_2} = \alpha_{CO} \times 6.36\times10^{-3}\, S_{CO}\, D_L^2" (4.1)
   - software: CASA, SoFiA-2
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.35 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "We assume $\alpha_{CO}=4.35$ M$_{\odot}$ (K  km s$^{-1}$pc$^{-2}$)$^{-1}$" (4.1)
   - assumption: LCDM Om=0.27, OL=0.73, H0=70  
  > "we assume a $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $\Omega_m = 0.27$, $\Omega_{\Lambda} = 0.73$, and $H_0 = 70$  km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Stack CO non-detections**: Cubelets centred on optical position and redshift, co-added with equal weights, binned by 20 channels; spectra extracted in 2, 4, 6 arcsec apertures  
  > "We extracted cubelets centered on the optical position and optical spectroscopic redshift of each galaxy, spanning 36$''$ $\times$36$''$ and $\sim$1000  km s$^{-1}$" (3)
   - parameter: 4 arcsec aperture chosen as best SNR  
  > "we find an optimal circular aperture of 4 arcsecs for both the CO detections and non-detections." (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Stacked the 5 detections separately, and stacked HST and H i images to test centring  
  > "we also stacked the HST images, and CHILES H i total intensity maps" (3)
5. **Compare with star formation and stellar mass surface density**: CO contours overlaid on CCP 1.4 GHz images and Spitzer 3.6 um-derived stellar mass surface density; 1.4 GHz SFR vs UV+IR SFR  
  > "we plot CO contours on binned maps of stellar mass surface density derived from Spitzer 3.6 $\mu$m images" (4.3)
   - assumption: Mass-to-light ratio 0.47 at 3.6 um  
  > "assuming a mass-to-light ratio of 0.47 (cite: McGaugh14)" (2.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compare gas fractions with literature samples and evolution models**: Mass/colour-matched comparison with xCOLD GASS, COOL BUDHIES, HIGHz; overlay of Walter20/Peroux20 fits and empirical models  
  > "we attempt to homogenize the samples with our subset of CHILES galaxies by applying mass- and color-selections" (2.5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- H i is always more extended than CO; CO shows rotating disk kinematics consistent with H i  
  > "The 14 H i detections show a range of morphologies, but the H i reservoir is always more extended than the CO." (abstract)
- CO traces dusty obscured star formation, coincident with 1.4 GHz continuum  
  > "We interpret the redder colors as the molecular gas being coincident with dusty regions of obscured star formation." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Resolved CO+H i beyond the local universe lacking at lower stellar masses  
  > "We are missing both the resolved details, and the majority of the galaxy population which is at lower stellar mass" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Two CHILES imaging pipelines (Dodson22 vs Luber25b) compared; Dodson22 chosen for 15.9% better rms  
  > "we found (cite: Dodson22) cubes have channels with, on average, 15.9% better rms values" (2.2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Fixed alpha_CO makes H2 limits uncertain; CO-to-H2 uncertainty may dominate  
  > "They do not take into account the uncertainty in the CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor which may be an even larger contributor to the uncertainty" (5.1)
- Limitation (authors): Comparison samples heterogeneous with different selection effects  
  > "derived from heterogeneous samples which may have little to no overlap, and each of which suffer from their own selection effects" (5.1.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stacking uses optical redshifts and equal weights; significance of 4.7 sigma is modest
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single alpha_CO for all galaxies despite metallicity range
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison samples only approximately matched in selection and environment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Upper limits depend on assumed 300 km/s width
