## Analysis recipe

1. **Self-calibrate and image line cubes**: Phase self-cal on continuum, continuum subtraction, CLEAN imaging with Briggs weighting  
  > "Following continuum subtraction, the data cubes were imaged in CASA using the CLEAN task with a 3$\sigma$ noise threshold." (2.2 Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 2.0 (1.0 for V4046 Sgr); 0.5 and 1.1 km/s channels  
  > "a value of 1.0 was adopted to improve the angular resolution" (2.2 Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract moment-zero maps, spectra and fluxes with Keplerian masks**: Keplerian mask per channel from literature disk/stellar parameters; noise by bootstrapping 1000 off-source masks  
  > "the same Keplerian mask used to obtain the moment zero map is applied to 1000 off-source positions" (2.2 Data reduction)
   - parameter: blended lines: lower-energy flux = 2x low-velocity horn; extra 30% error added in quadrature  
  > "an additional error of 30% the integrated flux was added in quadrature with the bootstrapped integrated flux uncertainty for blended lines" (2.2 Data reduction)
   - assumption: Blended line emission symmetric around source rest velocity  
  > "we assume that emission is symmetric around the rest velocity of the source" (2.2 Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Masks verified with H13CN emission  
  > "The masks were verified to fit the actual disk profile using H$^{13}$CN emission" (2.2 Data reduction)
3. **Rotational diagram for Trot and column density**: Convert disk-integrated fluxes to upper-level populations and fit Boltzmann distribution  
  > "Assuming LTE and optically thin emission, the disk-integrated flux density $S_\nu \Delta V$ can be converted to an upper level population $N_u$" (3.2 Population diagrams)
   - parameter: Omega from circle with radius of maximal emission extent in brightest line radial profile  
  > "$\Omega$ is taken to be the solid angle subtended by a circle with this radius." (3.2 Population diagrams)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin  
  > "Assuming LTE and optically thin emission" (3.2 Population diagrams)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Disk-averaged abundance ratios relative to HCN**: Use fluxes at assumed Trot of 30, 50, 70 K; H13CN to HCN with 12C/13C = 70  
  > "HC$_3$N/HCN and CH$_3$CN/HCN abundance ratios are calculated for rotational temperatures of 30, 50, and 70K" (3.3 Disk-averaged abundance ratios)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 70 (15% uncertainty); HC3N and CH3CN co-spatial with HCN  
  > "we assume a standard $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C ratio of 70 (with an uncertainty of 15%)" (3.3 Disk-averaged abundance ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Parametric radial abundance modeling of V4046 Sgr and MWC 480**: Power-law abundance X(r) in fixed physical disk model; RADMC-3D LTE images sampled in uv plane; emcee MCMC fit of X100 and alpha  
  > "The affine-invariant MCMC package emcee (cite: Foreman-Mackey2013) was used to sample posterior distributions of both parameters $X_{100}$ and $\alpha$." (4.1 V4046 Sgr model)
   - software: RADMC-3D, vis_sample, emcee
   - parameter: R100 = 100 AU; Rout 100 AU (V4046 Sgr), 180 AU (MWC 480); abundance reduced 1e8 above z/r=0.5; reduced 1e3 below 25 K (V4046 Sgr) or z/r<0.05 (MWC 480)  
  > "molecule abundances are attenuated by a factor of 10$^{3}$ at temperatures below 25K" (4.1 V4046 Sgr model)
   - assumption: LTE level populations; gas density and temperature held fixed  
  > "holding the gas density and temperature profiles constant" (4.1 V4046 Sgr model)
   - fitting: Likelihood from weighted visibility residuals; flat priors 1e-20<X100<1e-8, -3<alpha<2  
  > "A flat prior was used for each parameter when generating new samples: $10^{-20}  < X_{100} <$ 10$^{-8}$ and -3 $< \alpha <$ 2." (4.1 V4046 Sgr model)
   - validation: Tested depletion boundaries 19 and 30 K; predicted CH3CN 14_2-13_2 from best fit; compared model rotational temperatures; reduced chi2  
  > "The higher-frequency transition is well reproduced by the lower-frequency best-fit values" (4.1 V4046 Sgr model)
6. **Emitting-gas temperature distribution and comparison with other environments**: Fraction of upper-state molecules in temperature bins; compare ratios to comets and protostellar envelopes  
  > "to determine the fraction of emitting molecules (i.e., molecules in the upper energy state of the observed transition) in temperature bins from 10 to 200K" (4.3 CH3CN and HC3N column densities and abundances)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- HC3N detected in all disks except IM Lup; CH3CN detected in V4046 Sgr, MWC 480, HD 163296  
  > "HC$_3$N is detected towards all disks except IM Lup, and CH$_3$CN is detected towards V4046 Sgr, MWC 480, and HD 163296." (abstract)
- No CH3OH detected despite strong nitrile emission, suggesting N-rich, O-poor chemistry  
  > "our observations covered a number of CH$_3$OH transitions in the 5–4 ladder, with no CH$_3$OH detections despite the strong nitrile emission" (5.3 Nitriles in different circumstellar environments)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Keplerian masking used as established for spectral extraction  
  > "The use of Keplerian masks as CLEAN templates and for spectral extraction is well established" (2.2 Data reduction)
- Alternative: 29 AU cavity (mm dust hole) gives worse fit than 3 AU for V4046 Sgr  
  > "An adopted 29 AU radius, corresponding to the mm dust radius hole, produces a worse fit to the data for both molecules." (4.1 V4046 Sgr model)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): High upper-state energy lines are insensitive to cool gas; CH3CN Trot poorly constrained due to blending; sub-thermal excitation possible  
  > "given the line blending of CH$_3$CN (see Section [2.2](#sec_datareduce)) the rotational temperature is rather poorly constrained" (3.2 Population diagrams)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotational temperatures from 2-3 blended lines with small energy lever arm
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LTE assumed though lines may be sub-thermal
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abundance results depend on assumed physical model and ad hoc depletion boundary
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only six sources, so correlation claims are tentative
