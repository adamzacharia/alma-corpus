## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image continuum and cubes**: ALMA pipeline, combine with compact data, flag, self-cal PJ231-20, tclean natural weighting, uvcontsub  
  > "All this was done using standard routines available in CASA." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: 16-channel averaging (~35 km/s)  
  > "spectrally averaged over 16 channels ($\approx$31 MHz or $\approx$35 km s$^{-1}$)" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Robust 0.5 imaging compared; choice does not affect analysis  
  > "This choice does not affect the analysis presented in this paper." (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
2. **Measure positions, sizes, fluxes, redshifts and FWHM**: 2D Gaussian imfit; fluxes in 1.5'' diameter region; Gaussian fit to spectrum  
  > "The continuum and [C II] flux densities were estimated from a $1.5\ensuremath{''}$ diameter region centered on the emission." (3 Notes on Individual Sources)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: Gaussian fits to image and spectrum  
  > "derived from a Gaussian fit to the spectrum derived from this region." (3 Notes on Individual Sources)
   - validation: Compared Gaussian vs observed area over flux cuts; sizes may be off by factor 2  
  > "the size/area estimates from the Gaussian routine could be off by as much as a factor of two." (4.2 Size Estimates and Extent of Emission)
3. **Derive luminosities, TIR and SFRs**: L_CII from standard equations; L_TIR from modified blackbody; SFR from De Looze and Kennicutt relations  
  > "We vary the dust temperature ($T_d = 30 - 50$ K) and spectral index ($\beta = 1.2 - 2.0$)" (3 Notes on Individual Sources)
   - assumption: Td = 30-50 K, beta = 1.2-2.0; 0.5 dex scatter on SFR_CII  
  > "where the quoted range in SFR includes a 0.5 dex uncertainty arising from the scatter around this relationship." (3 Notes on Individual Sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Map the [CII] deficit**: Spatially resolved L_CII/L_TIR vs Sigma_TIR compared with local galaxies  
  > "Figure [5] shows the spatial extent of the [C II] deficit for all sources." (4.3 [CII] Deficit)
   - assumption: Gas physical conditions constant across the galaxy at fiducial values  
  > "we assume that the physical conditions of the gas do not vary across the galaxy and are equal to the fiducial values" (4.3 [CII] Deficit)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Kinematic modelling and dynamical masses**: Custom python code fitting 3D cube with thin-disk and dispersion-dominated exponential models, convolved with beam, chi2 with bootstrap  
  > "we use a custom, python-based code which fits the three-dimensional data cube to a model data cube generated from a user-defined model." (4.4 Constraints on Dynamical Mass)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: v_circ = 0.75 FWHM/sin i with i=55 deg for line-width estimate; M_dyn = 1.16e5 v_circ^2 D  
  > "$M_{\rm dyn} = 1.16 \times 10^5~v_{\rm circ}^2~D$" (4.4 Constraints on Dynamical Mass)
   - assumption: Thin disk with constant v_circ and sigma; exponential intensity  
  > "gas in a thin disk with constant circular velocity and constant velocity dispersion" (4.4 Constraints on Dynamical Mass)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC, chi2 likelihood with bootstrap for pixel correlation; priors not stated  
  > "the full parameter space is sampled through a Markov Chain Monte Carlo method by employing the `emcee` package" (4.4 Constraints on Dynamical Mass)
   - validation: Linearly rising velocity profile gave similar v_circ  
  > "the resulting circular velocity estimates at the maximal extent of the emission are similar to the previous estimates." (4.4 Constraints on Dynamical Mass)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Pairs selected by [CII] strength to be observable in reasonable time  
  > "selected these five quasar-companion galaxy pairs from the larger quasar sample solely by the [C II] emission strength" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting chosen for higher S/N  
  > "we opted for the increased signal-to-noise of the natural weighting scheme" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
- Alternative: Robust 0.5 weighting considered, rejected  
  > "we opted for the increased signal-to-noise of the natural weighting scheme" (2 Observations and Data Reduction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Marginal resolution cannot rule out merging clumps; size estimates uncertain by factor 2  
  > "the resolution is insufficient to rule out other possible scenarios, such as merging clumps." (4.4 Constraints on Dynamical Mass)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample of five fields selected by [CII] brightness is biased
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Marginally resolved data limit kinematic interpretation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-band continuum forces assumed Td and beta
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gaussian size estimates are uncertain
