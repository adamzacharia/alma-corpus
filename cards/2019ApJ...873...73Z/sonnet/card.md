## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image**: Pipeline calibration, self-calibration on continuum spw, combine two configurations, clean with robust 0.5  
  > "After pipeline calibration, we performed self-calibration using the continuum data obtained from a 2 GHz-wide spectral window with line-free bandwidth of about 1.6 GHz." (2.2 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "using robust weighting with the robust parameter of 0.5" (2.2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Continuum flux and gas mass**: Sum flux above 3 sigma within 6" and 0.5"; convert to mass with dust opacity, gas-to-dust ratio and assumed temperature  
  > "The total flux of the continuum emission above $3\sigma$ within $6\ensuremath{''}$ from the central source is 0.71 Jy." (3.1 1.3 mm Continuum Emission)
   - assumption: kappa_1.3mm=0.899 cm2/g, gas-to-dust 141, T=30/70/200 K  
  > "and a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 141 ((cite: Draine11))" (3.1 1.3 mm Continuum Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Moment maps, PV diagrams, spectra to identify rotation and centrifugal barrier**: PV diagrams along cut at P.A.=20 deg perpendicular to SiO jet; centrifugal barrier radius and velocity read from most blue/red-shifted offset emission; mass from r v^2/(2G)  
  > "in Figure [6](#fig:pvdiagram) we show the position-velocity (PV) diagrams of these molecular line emissions, along a cut passing through the continuum peak and perpendicular to the SiO jet (i.e., $\mathrm{P.A.}=20^\circ$)." (4.1 Velocity Structure)
   - assumption: Disk mass neglected for Keplerian curve; inclination i defined edge-on = 0  
  > "assuming a negligible disk mass" (4.1 Velocity Structure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with SED-fit mass 12-16 Msun  
  > "The estimated dynamical mass is roughly consistent with the mass estimate of $12-16\:M_\odot$ based on SED fitting ((cite: Liu19))." (4.1 Velocity Structure)
4. **Kinematic model of infalling-rotating envelope plus Keplerian disk**: Grid over i (0-40 deg, step 10), r_CB/d (0.1-0.4", step 0.05"), v_CB cos i (3-8 km/s, step 1); best fit by eye against PV diagrams  
  > "and approximately determine the best-fit model by eye (given the approximate nature of the modeling)." (4.2 Kinematic Model of the Rotating Envelope-Disk)
   - parameter: h(r)=0.2r; envelope rho~r^-1.5; disk rho~r^-2.5; r_out/d=0.8"  
  > "We assume the envelope has a height of $h(r)=0.2r$ on each side of the mid-plane, and the density distribution follows $\rho(r)\propto
r^{-1.5}$." (4.2 Kinematic Model of the Rotating Envelope-Disk)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission, universal excitation  
  > "For simplicity, we assume the emissions are optically thin and the excitation conditions are universal across the region." (4.2 Kinematic Model of the Rotating Envelope-Disk)
   - fitting: Manual grid comparison by eye, no sampler  
  > "approximately determine the best-fit model by eye" (4.2 Kinematic Model of the Rotating Envelope-Disk)
   - validation: Alternative pure Keplerian disk model fit (m*=23 Msun) rejected  
  > "The best-fit model shown in Appendix Figure [21](#fig:model_disk) (solid contours) has a protostellar mass of $m_*=23~M_\odot$." (10 Appendix)
5. **Outflow mass, momentum, rates**: 12CO(2-1) channel emission >3 sigma, optically thin, primary beam response >0.2, excluding |V-Vsys|<5 km/s; Tex=17.5 K  
  > "In each velocity channel, we only include the primary beam corrected emissions above $3\sigma$ within the region with the primary beam response $>0.2$." (5.2 Properties of the Large-scale CO Outflow)
   - parameter: 12CO/H2 = 1e-4, 2.34e-24 g per H2, outflow length 15"  
  > "adopt an abundance of $^{12}$CO of $10^{-4}$ relative to H$_2$ and a gas mass of $2.34\times10^{-24}$ g per H$_2$ molecule" (5.2 Properties of the Large-scale CO Outflow)
   - assumption: Inclination correction with i=20 deg; correction factors 10 (mass), 8 (momentum) from low-mass outflow  
  > "Assuming an inclination of $i=20^\circ$, which is likely to be an upper limit" (5.2 Properties of the Large-scale CO Outflow)
   - fitting: Two power laws fit to mass spectra  
  > "The mass spectra can be characterized by two power laws" (5.2 Properties of the Large-scale CO Outflow)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Highly collimated red-shifted SiO jet (P.A. ~110 deg) connects to CO outflow (P.A. ~120 deg, half opening angle ~20 deg)  
  > "The position angle of the SiO jet is about $110^\circ$, slightly different from the large-scale $^{12}$CO outflow with a position angle of $120^\circ$." (3.2 Outflow Tracers)
- Different molecules trace different parts: SiO disk+inner envelope; CH3OH and H2CO envelope; SO2 and H2S outer disk and centrifugal barrier  
  > "The SiO emission traces the disk and the inner envelope, the CH$_3$OH and H$_2$CO emissions trace the envelope, and the SO$_2$ and H$_2$S emissions trace the outer part of the disk, as well as the inner envelope." (4.1 Velocity Structure)
