## Analysis recipe

1. **Build red-source JWST catalogs**: Original catalog from F277W+F444W extraction with aperture-to-total flux correction; threshold catalog from sources above 10 sigma in the F444W-F150W difference map with isophotal fluxes in the same segmentation  
  > "The F444W-F150W difference map was first generated by subtracting the F150W flux from the F444W flux." (2.1 Infrared Data); "Sources with a detection above 10$\sigma$ in this difference map were extracted to form the threshold catalog." (2.1 Infrared Data)
   - software: JWST pipeline, snowblind
   - parameter: 10 sigma threshold in the F444W-F150W difference map  
  > "Sources with a detection above 10$\sigma$ in this difference map were extracted to form the threshold catalog." (2.1 Infrared Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cut corresponds to F444W/F150W of about 1, so no red sources meeting the color criteria are lost  
  > "The cut of 10 $\sigma$ in the threshold catalog corresponds to the F444W-to-F150W flux ratio of about 1" (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
2. **Select strong-lens candidates**: Visual inspection of JWST F444W-F277W-F150W false-color cutouts, applied to STUDIES 450 um sources and to color/flux-selected red sources  
  > "We first select strongly lensed candidates by visually inspecting the JWST r-g-b (F444W-F277W-F150W) false-color cutout images." (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
   - parameter: F444W flux density above 1 uJy and F444W/F150W flux ratio above 3.5 for the color-selected set  
  > "the F444W flux density is required to exceed 1 $\mu$Jy and a flux ratio between F444W and F150W is greater than 3.5" (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
   - assumption: Conservative approach that selects all sources that could potentially be lensed  
  > "At this stage, we take a conservative approach that aims to select all sources that could potentially be lensed." (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Stacked submillimeter flux of the red sources confirms their dusty nature  
  > "To confirm the dusty nature of these red sources, we performed a stacking analysis of submillimeter data." (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
3. **Check individual submillimeter detections of the candidates**: Inspect SCUBA-2 450/850 um and archival ALMA data for the 18 candidates (3 sigma detection threshold)  
  > "we checked their individual submillimeter detections using SCUBA-2 and ALMA data." (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs); "Looking at ALMA archival data, we found that 15 sources had been observed and three of them exhibit detections above 3$\sigma$." (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Estimate photometric redshifts of sources and lenses**: EAZY SED fitting; sources with dusty-galaxy template set and four JWST bands; lenses with 36 to 38 bands including COSMOS2020 photometry  
  > "with photometry from the four JWST bands (F115W, F150W, F277W, and F444W)" (3.2 Photometric redshift); "SED fitting was performed with also EAZY template in 36 to 38 bands" (3.2 Photometric redshift)
   - software: EAZY
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Photo-z agree with lens spec-z for most lenses and with the spectroscopic redshift of source 019  
  > "Our photo-$z$ results are consistent with spectroscopic redshifts for most lenses." (3.2 Photometric redshift); "again validating our redshift analyses" (3.2 Photometric redshift)
5. **Model the foreground lenses and derive magnifications**: Lenstronomy lens modeling of masked 6 arcsec cutouts, simultaneous fit to F444W and F150W; SIE or EPL mass with or without shear, Sersic light profiles; best model chosen per system  
  > "we performed detailed foreground lens modeling using Lenstronomy" (3.3 Lens modeling); "we tried Singular Isothermal Ellipse (SIE) and Elliptical Power Law (EPL), with and without cosmic shear, and selected the best fit model for each system" (3.3 Lens modeling)
   - software: Lenstronomy
   - parameter: Sersic ellipse light profiles for lens and source; 2 Sersic ellipses for COSMOS-005  
  > "Sérsic ellipse is considered as the light profile for both lens and source." (3.3 Lens modeling)
   - parameter: Strong lensing defined as magnification greater than 2  
  > "which is typically defined as having a lensing magnification greater than 2" (3.3 Lens modeling)
   - assumption: Flat LCDM, H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc, OmegaM = 0.3, OmegaLambda = 0.7  
  > "We assume flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $H_0=70$ km s$^{-1}$Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_M=0.3$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.7$ throughout this paper." (Introduction)
   - fitting: Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC); priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "The fitting was performed simultaneously on images from two bands, F444W and F150W, using Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods." (3.3 Lens modeling)
   - validation: Magnifications compared with Nightingale et al. for nine matched sources, median ratio 1.2 +/- 0.3  
  > "we find a good agreement in the reported lensing magnification, with a median ratio of 1.2$\pm$0.3" (3.3 Lens modeling)
6. **Construct number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs**: Cumulative 450 um counts of STUDIES lensed sources following the STUDIES counts method with corrections for spurious fraction, flux boosting and completeness; color-selected sample plotted at weighted stacked flux and treated as a lower limit  
  > "where corrections for spurious fraction, flux boosting, and completeness are properly taken into account via extensive simulations" (3.4 Number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs); "We therefore treat the color-base measurement as a lower limit, implying that our results are consistent with the model." (3.4 Number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs)
   - parameter: Area is the combined STUDIES footprint in COSMOS and UDS  
  > "We note that the total area considered is the combined area of STUDIES footprint in both COSMOS and UDS." (3.4 Number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature counts, an earlier pilot search and two model predictions  
  > "Our counts are slightly higher than the pilot search within the STUDIES footprint (cite: 2024MNRAS.52712044P) due to more sources discovered." (3.4 Number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: STUDIES is deep enough to detect faint DSFGs, making it ideal for a faint lensed DSFG search  
  > "making STUDIES an ideal venue to search for strongly lensed faint DSFGs" (2.2 Submillimeter Data)
- Why this method: A conservative visual selection is followed by redshift analysis and lens modeling to confirm lensing  
  > "These candidates are then passed to the second stage where we perform redshift analyses and lens modeling in order to confirm their strongly lensed nature." (3.1 Identification of strongly lensed DSFGs)
- Alternative: SIE and EPL mass models, each with and without shear, tried; best fit kept per system  
  > "we tried Singular Isothermal Ellipse (SIE) and Elliptical Power Law (EPL), with and without cosmic shear, and selected the best fit model for each system" (3.3 Lens modeling)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Red color selection is likely incomplete  
  > "However, the red color selection method, while proven efficient, is likely incomplete." (3.4 Number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs)
- Limitation (authors): Visual selection may cause incompleteness relative to other catalogs  
  > "it is possible that the mismatch is caused by some level of incompleteness" (3.3 Lens modeling)
- Limitation (authors): Background-source photo-z have large uncertainties  
  > "Although the photometric redshifts of the background sources have large uncertainties" (3.2 Photometric redshift)
- Limitation (authors): Follow-up needed to rule out 450 um emission from the foreground lenses  
  > "follow-up observations are needed" (3.4 Number counts of strongly lensed faint DSFGs)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA plays only a supporting role (detection checks for 15 candidates); no ALMA flux measurements or imaging are reported
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample (13 systems) and no stated Poisson treatment in the text for the counts
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Visual lens selection is subjective and its completeness is not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Lens-model priors and convergence diagnostics are not reported
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Counts depend on unpublished (in preparation) catalog and counterpart procedures
