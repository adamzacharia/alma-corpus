## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce JWST NIRSpec IFS data and extract optical emission line fluxes**: Process raw G395H/F290LP data with JWST pipeline, apply sigma clipping and median background subtraction, drizzle to 0.05'' pixels, match PSF to Halpha (0.21'' FWHM), extract spectra from >= 2sigma region in [O III]5008 map, fit Gaussians to line profiles, and apply 10% aperture correction.  
  > "The final data cube was combined using the “drizzle” method, sampled with a pixel size of 0 _[′′]_ _._ 05." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data); "The fluxes were obtained by applying Gaussian fitting to each line profile, where the spectra were extracted from regions with ≳ 2 _σ_ significance in the [O iii]5008 intensity map (Figure 1)." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data); "Therefore, we applied a 10% aperture correction to all line flux values, because we use the PSF-matched data." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data)
   - software: JWST Calibration Pipeline
   - parameter: pixel size = 0.05'', PSF FWHM = 0.21'' at 5.125 um, extraction threshold >= 2 sigma in [O III]5008 map, aperture correction = 10%  
  > "The final data cube was combined using the “drizzle” method, sampled with a pixel size of 0 _[′′]_ _._ 05." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data); "The orange circle at the bottom left indicates the FWHM of 0 _[′′]_ _._ 21 at the observed wavelength of H _α_ (5 _._ 125 _µ_ m)." (Figure 1 caption); "Therefore, we applied a 10% aperture correction to all line flux values, because we use the PSF-matched data." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data)
   - assumption: Adopted aperture misses ~10% of total flux based on curve-of-growth analysis  
  > "Based on a curve-of-growth analysis, we estimate that the adopted aperture misses _≈_ 10% of the total flux." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data)
   - fitting: Gaussian fitting to each line profile  
  > "The fluxes were obtained by applying Gaussian fitting to each line profile, where the spectra were extracted from regions with ≳ 2 _σ_ significance in the [O iii]5008 intensity map (Figure 1)." (2.1. JWST NIRSpec IFS Data)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image ALMA Band 8 data and measure [O III] 88 um line flux and profile**: Image Band 8 data with CASA tclean using natural weighting, integrate cube over 434.2-434.9 GHz (480 km s^-1) to make moment-0 map, measure integrated line flux, and fit 1D velocity profile with a single Gaussian corrected for instrumental broadening.  
  > "Imaging was performed with the CASA `tclean` task, adopting natural weighting to optimize point-source sensitivity." (2.2. ALMA [O iii] 88 µm data); "we integrated the emission over the frequency range of 434.2–434.9 GHz, corresponding to a velocity width of 480 km s _[−]_ [1] (i.e., _≈_ 3 times the FWHM of the optical lines), to generate the integrated intensity (moment-0) map of the [O iii]88 emission." (2.2. ALMA [O iii] 88 µm data); "Fitting each line with a single Gaussian profile and correcting for instrumental broadening, we obtain FWHM 5008 = 146 _±_ 2 km s _[−]_ [1] and FWHM 88 = 239 _±_ 45 km s _[−]_ [1] ." (C. LINE PROFILES OF [O iii]5008 AND [O iii]88)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting, 30 km s^-1 channel width, integration range 434.2-434.9 GHz (480 km s^-1)  
  > "The typical rms noise of the cube is 0 _._ 576 mJy beam _[−]_ [1] per 30 km s _[−]_ [1] channel." (2.2. ALMA [O iii] 88 µm data); "we integrated the emission over the frequency range of 434.2–434.9 GHz, corresponding to a velocity width of 480 km s _[−]_ [1]" (2.2. ALMA [O iii] 88 µm data)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian profile fit to 1D velocity spectra  
  > "Fitting each line with a single Gaussian profile and correcting for instrumental broadening, we obtain FWHM 5008 = 146 _±_ 2 km s _[−]_ [1] and FWHM 88 = 239 _±_ 45 km s _[−]_ [1] ." (C. LINE PROFILES OF [O iii]5008 AND [O iii]88)
   - validation: Synthesized beam size and integrated line flux are consistent with Witstok et al. (2022) within uncertainties.  
  > "We measure an integrated line flux of 1 _._ 06 _±_ 0 _._ 23 Jy km s _[−]_ [1], corresponding to a flux of (15 _._ 3 _±_ 3 _._ 3) _×_ 10 _[−]_ [18] erg s _[−]_ [1] cm _[−]_ [2], consistent with the values reported in Witstok et al. (2022) within uncertainties." (2.2. ALMA [O iii] 88 µm data)
3. **Derive integrated dust extinction, electron temperature, electron density, and gas-phase metallicity from optical lines**: Iteratively compute dust extinction A_V from Halpha/Hbeta Balmer decrement, electron temperature Te([O III]) from [O III]4364/5008, Te([O II]) from scaling relation, electron density ne([O II]) from [O II]3730/3727, and oxygen abundance 12+log(O/H) via direct-Te method using PyNeb.  
  > "We then derive _A_ _V_ from eq. (1), correct the observed emission line fluxes for dust attenuation, and remeasure the electron density and temperature from the [O ii]3730/3727 and [O iii]5008/4364 line ratios, respectively. This process is repeated until the change in temperature between iterations becomes less than 10 K." (3.1. Integrated Internal Dust Extinction); "We estimate the oxygen abundance, 12 + log(O _/_ H), as a proxy for gas-phase metallicity using the direct_T_ e method with the `PyNeb` `getIonAbundance` function (Luridiana et al. 2015)." (3.4. Gas-Phase Metallicity based on the direct-Te method)
   - software: PyNeb
   - parameter: Convergence threshold delta_Te < 10 K; 10,000 Monte Carlo realizations  
  > "This process is repeated until the change in temperature between iterations becomes less than 10 K." (3.1. Integrated Internal Dust Extinction); "To estimate the uncertainty in _A_ _V_, we perform a Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 realizations, randomly perturbing the observed H _α_ and H _β_ fluxes within their uncertainties." (3.1. Integrated Internal Dust Extinction)
   - assumption: SMC extinction curve with R_V = 2.74; Case B recombination with initial ne = 100 cm^-3 and Te = 10^4 K; Galactic extinction negligible; Te([O II]) = 0.7 * Te([O III]) + 3000 K; O/H = O++/H+ + O+/H+  
  > "We adopt the SMC extinction curve with _R_ _V_ = 2 _._ 74 (Gordon et al. 2003)." (3.1. Integrated Internal Dust Extinction); "_T_ e ([O ii]) = 0 _._ 7 _× T_ e ([O iii]) + 3000 K _._ (2)" (3.2. Electron Temperature); "We assume that the total oxygen abundance O/H is the sum of O [++] _/_ H [+] and O [+] _/_ H [+] ." (3.4. Gas-Phase Metallicity based on the direct-Te method)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 realizations for uncertainty estimation  
  > "To estimate the uncertainty in _A_ _V_, we perform a Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 realizations, randomly perturbing the observed H _α_ and H _β_ fluxes within their uncertainties." (3.1. Integrated Internal Dust Extinction)
   - validation: Derived low A_V is consistent with ALMA dust continuum non-detection; Te and ne are consistent with z ~ 7 galaxies in the literature.  
  > "This result indicates that COS-2987 is dust-poor, consistent with the nondetection of dust continuum emission by ALMA (Smit et al. 2018; Witstok et al. 2022)." (3.1. Integrated Internal Dust Extinction)
4. **Test inhomogeneous dust distribution via spatially resolved pixel-by-pixel dust correction**: Apply Voronoi pixel binning to Hbeta and Halpha intensity maps within 3sigma regions, generate an A_V map, correct [O III]4364 and [O III]5008 maps pixel-by-pixel, and measure total fluxes using 2sigma isophotal aperture of [O III]5008 with 10% aperture correction.  
  > "First, we generate an _A_ _V_ map from the PSF-matched H _α_ /H _β_ ratio map within the 3 _σ_ regions of both lineintensity maps (right panel of Figure 3). We apply Voronoi pixel binning (Cappellari & Copin 2003; Cappellari 2009) to the integrated intensity maps of H _β_ and H _α_ in order to optimize the signal-to-noise ratio in each pixel." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust); "We perform isophotal photometry on both dust-corrected line intensity maps, where we use the 2 _σ_ isophotal aperture defined in the [O iii]5008 map (the outermost gray contour in the right panel of Figure 3). Then, we applied a 10% aperture correction (see § 2.1) to both line flux values." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust)
   - parameter: 3sigma threshold for Halpha and Hbeta maps; 2sigma [O III]5008 isophotal aperture; 10% aperture correction  
  > "First, we generate an _A_ _V_ map from the PSF-matched H _α_ /H _β_ ratio map within the 3 _σ_ regions of both lineintensity maps (right panel of Figure 3)." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust); "We perform isophotal photometry on both dust-corrected line intensity maps, where we use the 2 _σ_ isophotal aperture defined in the [O iii]5008 map (the outermost gray contour in the right panel of Figure 3)." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust)
   - assumption: A_V = 0 outside the 3sigma detection region of Halpha and Hbeta  
  > "Outside the region where the _A_ V map is not reliably defined (i.e., where either H _α_ or H _β_ is not detected above 3 _σ_ ), we assume _A_ V = 0 for simplicity." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare pixel-by-pixel dust-corrected line ratios to homogeneous PyNeb model grids.  
  > "However, even after this correction, the integrated [O iii] ratios of COS-2987 still cannot be explained by a homogeneous ionized gas with densities comparable to those inferred from the [O ii] lines." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust)
5. **Model [O III] 4364, 5008, and 88 um line ratios with a two-component density-stratified ionized gas toy model**: Calculate cooling rates Lambda_line for two ionized gas components using PyNeb and combine their line luminosities linearly as a function of volume ratio V1/V2 to reproduce observed [O III]88/5008 and [O III]4364/5008 ratios.  
  > "We calculate Λ line _,i_ for each line using `PyNeb` (Luridiana et al. 2015) by assuming specific values of _n_ e _,i_ and _T_ e _,i_ ." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM); "By comparing the model predictions with the observed values of [O iii]88/5008 and [O iii]4364/5008, we derive a volume ratio of _V_ 1 _/V_ 2 _∼_ 1 _/_ 300." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
   - software: PyNeb
   - parameter: Component 1: (Te,1, ne,1) = (26,000 K, 500 cm^-3); Component 2: (Te,2, ne,2) = (8,000 K, 50 cm^-3)  
  > "We find that the observed [O iii] line ratios are well reproduced by a model consisting of component 1 with ( _T_ e _,_ 1 _, n_ e _,_ 1 ) = (26 _,_ 000 K _,_ 500 cm _[−]_ [3] ) and component 2 with ( _T_ e _,_ 2 _, n_ e _,_ 2 ) = (8 _,_ 000 K _,_ 50 cm _[−]_ [3] )." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
   - assumption: n_O++,i = n_e,i * 10^-4 (12 + log(O++/H+) = 8); 10% absolute flux calibration uncertainty on both [O III]5008 and [O III]88  
  > "We assume _n_ O ++ _,i_ = _n_ e _,i_ _×_ 10 _[−]_ [4], corresponding to O [++] _/_ H [+] = 10 _[−]_ [4] (i.e., 12 + log(O [++] _/_ H [+] ) = 8)." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM); "where the [O iii]88/5008 ratio incorporates a 10% absolute flux calibration uncertainty for both [O iii]5008 (B¨oker et al. 2023 [7] ) and [O iii]88 (ALMA Cycle 6 Proposer’s Guide [8] )." (4. [O iii] LINE RATIO DIAGNOSTICS COMBINING JWST AND ALMA)
   - fitting: Manual forward modeling with fixed (Te, ne) for each component and varying volume ratio V1/V2  
  > "The aim of this analysis is not to determine best-fit parameters, but rather to assess whether the observed [O iii] luminosity ratios can be reproduced within the framework of a density-stratified ionized ISM." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM); "The top right panel of Figure 4 shows the predicted line ratios of the two-component model as a function of the volume ratio _V_ 1 _/V_ 2, with _T_ e _,_ 1, _n_ e _,_ 1, _T_ e _,_ 2, and _n_ e _,_ 2 held fixed." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
   - validation: Compare spatial radial profiles (after convolving [O III]5008 to ALMA beam) and 1D velocity profiles of [O III]5008 and [O III]88.  
  > "We tested this possibility by examining the spatial and kinematic distributions of the [O iii]5008 and [O iii]88 lines. As shown in Figure B.2 (Appendix B), the radial profiles of the two lines are consistent within uncertainties, indicating no significant spatial segregation at the current ALMA resolution." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)

## Qualitative findings

- The observed [O III] 4364/5008 and [O III] 88/5008 line ratios of COS-2987 lie outside the PyNeb model grids for a homogeneous ionized gas with a single electron temperature and density.  
  > "The results indicate that the [O iii] line ratios of COS2987 cannot be reproduced by a simple model of homogeneous ionized gas with uniform temperature and density." (4. [O iii] LINE RATIO DIAGNOSTICS COMBINING JWST AND ALMA)
- Spatially resolved pixel-by-pixel dust extinction correction changes [O III]4364/5008 by ~1.07 and [O III]88/5008 by ~0.82, which is insufficient to bring the ratios into agreement with a single-component ISM.  
  > "As a result, we find that the [O iii]4364/5008 and [O iii]88/5008 ratios change by factors of _∼_ 1 _._ 07 and _∼_ 0 _._ 82, respectively." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust); "However, even after this correction, the integrated [O iii] ratios of COS-2987 still cannot be explained by a homogeneous ionized gas with densities comparable to those inferred from the [O ii] lines." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust)
- No significant spatial or kinematic segregation is detected between [O III] 5008 and [O III] 88 um at the current ALMA resolution.  
  > "As shown in Figure B.2 (Appendix B), the radial profiles of the two lines are consistent within uncertainties, indicating no significant spatial segregation at the current ALMA resolution." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM); "Similarly, Figure C.1 in Appendix C shows that the spectral profiles of the two lines are broadly consistent, although the [O iii]88 line appears marginally broader." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Combining JWST NIRSpec IFS and ALMA avoids the slit-loss and position-angle uncertainties of NIRSpec MOS and provides four [O III] transitions from the same ion with vastly different critical densities, enabling nebular diagnostics independent of abundance ratio assumptions.  
  > "these studies utilized the NIRSpec multi-object spectroscopy (MOS) mode to observe optical [O iii] emission lines, which are subject to uncertainties arising from slit-loss corrections and from effects related to the position angle" (1. INTRODUCTION); "Since these are emitted from the same ion of oxygen, we can derive the physical properties of nebulae without assumptions of abundance ratios." (4. [O iii] LINE RATIO DIAGNOSTICS COMBINING JWST AND ALMA)
- Why this method: A simplified two-component toy model focusing on three [O III] lines limits the number of free parameters compared to comprehensive models like MULTIGRIS, allowing identification of the key physical conditions.  
  > "While comprehensive models for ionized gas with multiple physical conditions, such as `MULTIGRIS` (Lebouteiller & Ramambason 2022; Ramambason et al. 2022a), are available, we adopt a simplified two-component toy model that focuses on three emission lines from doubly ionized oxygen: [O iii]4364, [O iii]5008, and [O iii]88." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM); "By limiting the number of free parameters, our aim is to identify the key physical conditions responsible for the observed line ratios." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
- Alternative: Single-component homogeneous ionized ISM model (rejected because observed [O III] ratios fall outside the model grid)  
  > "The results indicate that the [O iii] line ratios of COS2987 cannot be reproduced by a simple model of homogeneous ionized gas with uniform temperature and density." (4. [O iii] LINE RATIO DIAGNOSTICS COMBINING JWST AND ALMA)
- Alternative: Inhomogeneously distributed dust attenuating optical lines (rejected after pixel-by-pixel dust extinction correction and velocity profile comparison)  
  > "This supports the view that inhomogeneously distributed dust is not the primary cause of the observed discrepancy in the [O iii] line ratios of COS-2987." (5.1. Inhomogeneously Distributed Dust); "Moreover, we do not find any velocity components that are detected in the [O iii]88 line but absent in the [O iii]5008 line, which would otherwise be expected if dust attenuation played a major role." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
- Alternative: Comprehensive multi-component photoionization modeling with MULTIGRIS (not used in favor of a simplified two-component toy model to limit free parameters)  
  > "While comprehensive models for ionized gas with multiple physical conditions, such as `MULTIGRIS` (Lebouteiller & Ramambason 2022; Ramambason et al. 2022a), are available, we adopt a simplified two-component toy model that focuses on three emission lines from doubly ionized oxygen: [O iii]4364, [O iii]5008, and [O iii]88." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The parameters (Te, ne, V1/V2) of the two-component ionized gas model are not a unique solution.  
  > "We stress again that the specific combination of _T_ e, _n_ e, and _V_ 1 _/V_ 2 parameters presented above is not a unique solution." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
- Limitation (authors): The [O II] 3727,3730 emission likely originates from a different, third ionization phase not included in the two-component [O III] model.  
  > "We also note that the observed [O ii]3727,3730 emission likely originates from a different ionization phase not included in our current model." (5.2. Evidence for a density-stratified ionized ISM)
- Limitation (authors): Current ALMA spatial resolution (0.80'' x 0.61'') cannot spatially resolve the two ionized gas components or subtle morphological differences between [O III]5008 and [O III]88.  
  > "The two emission lines exhibit consistent radial distributions within the uncertainties, indicating that there is no significant evidence of spatial separation between the two lines at the current ALMA resolution." (B. RADIAL PROFILES OF [O iii]5008 AND [O iii]88)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The two-component toy model has more free parameters than observed line ratios (5 parameters: Te1, ne1, Te2, ne2, V1/V2 for 2 ratios), making the parameter values illustrative rather than uniquely constrained.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Pixel-by-pixel dust correction still averages over the ~0.21'' JWST PSF (~1.1 kpc at z=6.81), which cannot rule out sub-resolution clumpy dust mixed with individual star-forming cores.
