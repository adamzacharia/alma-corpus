## Analysis recipe

1. **adopt continuum core catalog**: Use the 16 compact 1.3 mm cores previously extracted with getsf in QUARKS-III and plot them on the continuum map  
  > "A total of 16 compact cores were identified in the region." (1.3 mm Continuum Emission)
   - software: getsf
   - parameter: 10 hot molecular cores and 6 UCHII regions  
  > "Among them, 10 were classified as hot molecular cores (HMCs) and the remaining 6 were classified as UCHii regions." (1.3 mm Continuum Emission)
   - assumption: Dust temperature 100 K for evolved cores; free-free emission may bias masses high  
  > "The authors estimated the masses of the cores assuming a dust temperature of 100 K for the evolved cores (cite: yang2025)." (1.3 mm Continuum Emission); "this could lead to the overestimation of the masses of the continuum cores" (1.3 mm Continuum Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **make CO moment maps and choose wing velocity ranges**: Moment-0 and moment-1 maps of 12CO 2-1; blue and red wing maps integrated outside the self-absorbed cloud velocities  
  > "We generate the integrated intensity (i.e., moment-0), and the intensity-weighted velocity (i.e., moment-1) maps using the" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics); "We generate the blue- and red-shifted outflow wing emission maps by integrating emission in the velocity ranges $[-76.0, -50.0]$ km s$^{-1}$ and $[-36.0, -12.0]$ km s$^{-1}$, respectively." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - software: spectral-cube, astropy
   - parameter: excluded velocity range -50.0 to -36.0 km/s; wings [-76,-50] and [-36,-12] km/s  
  > "we exclude the velocity range $-50.0$ km s$^{-1}$ to $-36.0$ km s$^{-1}$ for the estimation of outflow parameters and kinematics." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - assumption: Central velocities are contaminated by cloud emission and CO self-absorption  
  > "The CO emissions show self-absorption toward the central velocity of the cloud." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **identify outflow fingers and their common center**: 2 km/s channel maps; 5 sigma contours per channel with spines traced along emission peaks from the outermost channels inward; spines aggregated into finger trajectories; common center defined where backward-extended fingers converge  
  > "Outflow fingers are identified by investigating consecutive velocity channels, starting from the outermost velocity channels in the blue- and red-shifted directions with respect to the V$_{\text{lsr}}$." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics); "We generate contour at emissions $\geq5\sigma$ (where $\sigma$ represents the rms of the background noise) in each velocity channel" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - parameter: channel width 2 km/s; 5 sigma threshold; common-center box 4.5'' x 4.5''  
  > "with each channel representing integrated intensity within 2 km s$^{-1}$ velocity width" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - assumption: Fingers originate from a common center  
  > "Although the outflow fingers are believed to originate from a common center, pinpointing the exact location is challenging" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Searched opposite-velocity channels for bipolar counterparts and found none  
  > "and do not find any bipolar components associated with the outflow fingers." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
4. **measure radial velocity versus projected distance**: Radial velocity of each finger taken from the farthest channel with >5 sigma emission; plotted against sky-projected distance from the common center to test a Hubble-Lemaitre law  
  > "We estimate the radial velocity of the outflow fingers from the farthest velocity channel having emissions $>5\sigma$ within the outflow fingers" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics); "We find a linearly increasing trend of the radial velocity of the outflowing gas with distance (Hubble-Lemaître velocity law)." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - parameter: sigma from emission-free regions  
  > "where $\sigma$ is the rms of the background noise, estimated from emission-free regions" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - assumption: Different inclinations give different slopes for individual fingers  
  > "the PV diagrams of the outflow fingers will appear to have different slopes as seen in Figure" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with kinematics reported for earlier explosive outflows  
  > "are one of the most defining characteristics of this category of outflows." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
5. **derive CO column density with optical depth correction**: LTE optically thin 12CO 2-1 column density, then corrected by tau/(1-exp(-tau)) with tau from the 12CO/13CO intensity ratio  
  > "With the assumptions for the CO emission to be optically thin and also in local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE), we derive the CO column density following the equation" (3.3 Outflow Energetics); "Assuming the two isotopologues $^{12}$CO $(2-1)$ and $^{13}$CO $(2-1)$ have same excitation temperature, we estimate $\tau$ (cite: Qiu2009) from," (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - software: XCLASS database, CDMS
   - parameter: Tex = 50 K; Tbg = 2.7 K; Qrot = 18.45; g_u = 5; A_ul = 6.911e-7 s^-1; E_l = 5.53 K  
  > "we use a $T_{\text{ex}}$ of 50 K for the outflow materials" (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - parameter: 12CO/13CO abundance ratio chi = (7.5+/-1.9) D_GC + (7.6+/-12.9), D_GC = 6.2 kpc  
  > "where $\chi$ is the abundance ratio of $^{12}$CO $(2-1)$ and $^{13}$CO $(2-1)$ derived using the relation, $\chi= (7.5\pm1.9) \text{D}_{\text{GC}}+(7.6\pm12.9)$ (cite: Wilson1994)." (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - assumption: Same Tex for 12CO and 13CO  
  > "have same excitation temperature" (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - assumption: Tex 50 K chosen from outflow temperatures of ~20-100 K in the literature  
  > "In general, the temperature of the outflowing material lies within $\sim20-100$ K (cite: hatchell1999; Shimajiri2009)." (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **compute outflow mass, momentum, energy and dynamical age**: Sum per-channel masses over emission >=5 sigma; momentum and energy from M v and 1/2 M v^2 with inclination corrections 1/cos(theta) and 1/cos^2(theta); dynamical age from longest projected finger and fastest finger  
  > "We included emissions $\geq5\sigma$ in each channel for the estimation of outflow parameters, where $\sigma$ is calculated from emission-free channels." (3.3 Outflow Energetics); "Therefore, the most likely estimate of the true extent of the explosive outflow would come from the longest projected outflow finger." (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - parameter: mean molecular weight 2.8; [CO/H2] = 1e-4; inclination 57.3 deg  
  > "is the mean molecular weight (adopted as 2.8)" (3.3 Outflow Energetics); "we assumed a mean inclination angle, $\theta$ of 57.3$^{\circ}$ (cite: dunham2014)" (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - assumption: Fingers isotropic with the same 3D velocity; differences in projected extent and radial velocity arise from inclination  
  > "by assuming that outflow fingers are isotropic and have the same v$_{3D}$" (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - assumption: Biases from missing flux, distance, constant temperature and constant H2/CO ratio  
  > "Overall, these factors may result in the underestimation or overestimation of the outflow parameters by a factor of two to three." (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared the energy, mass and momentum with other explosive outflows and with bipolar outflows  
  > "Finally, the mass and momentum of the outflow in I15520 are also similar to those found in other explosive outflows." (4.1 Evidence of an Explosive event)
7. **estimate Galactic rate of explosive events**: Re-estimate the rate with the eight known explosive outflows, assuming events evenly distributed in time and space, within a thin Galactic disk  
  > "We also assume that the Galaxy is a thin disk with a radius of 15 kpc and estimate the rate of explosive events, which is one event in every 83 years." (Rate of Explosive Events in the Milky Way)
   - parameter: time span 19000 yr; circle diameter 5.6 kpc; Galactic radius 15 kpc  
  > "We assume that the explosive outflows are evenly distributed in time over a time span of 19000 years covering the time period of all eight explosive outflows" (4.3 Rate of Explosive Events in the Milky Way); "and are distributed within a circle of diameter 5.6 kpc" (4.3 Rate of Explosive Events in the Milky Way)
   - assumption: Simplified: size of the Galaxy, constant star formation rate with galactocentric distance, rough dynamical timescale  
  > "Note that the method employed here is simplified and relies on several assumptions" (4.3 Rate of Explosive Events in the Milky Way)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with earlier rate estimates and with massive star formation and supernova rates  
  > "The estimated rate of explosive outflows is very similar to the massive star formation rate" (4.3 Rate of Explosive Events in the Milky Way)
8. **compare with shocked gas, radio and methanol shell**: Spitzer IRAC Ch2/Ch1 ratio map, CORNISH-South 5.5 GHz contours, H30alpha moment map and CH3OH moment/PV maps compared with the outflow fingers and common center  
  > "We use the *Spitzer*-IRAC ratio map Ch2(4.5$\mu$m)/Ch1(3.6$\mu$m) to identify the large-scale shock extent of the explosive source." (3.4 Radio Emission and NIR Shocked Gas); "We further investigate the expanding CH$_3$OH shell by generating a PV diagram along a rectangular strip across the shell." (4.2 Expansion of CH3OH Shell)
   - parameter: Ch2/Ch1 >= 1.5 indicates shocked emission (criterion from takami2010)  
  > "a flux ratio (Ch2/Ch1) $\geq1.5$ is dominated by shocked emissions" (Radio Emission and NIR Shocked Gas Associated with the Explosive Outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Fingers show no preferred orientation, have no bipolar counterparts, and point back to a common center near the protocluster center  
  > "The outflow fingers point back to a common center, which approximately coincides with the center of the protocluster, where most of the massive cores are located." (4.1 Evidence of an Explosive event)
- 5.5 GHz radio emission shows a shell-like HII region at the center and extended jet-like structures outside; jet nature unconfirmed  
  > "Although the morphology of these extended emission suggests the possibility of candidate ionized jets, further high-resolution and high-sensitivity radio observations are required to confirm the nature of these structures." (3.4 Radio Emission and NIR Shocked Gas)
- Massive cores are mass segregated toward the protocluster center  
  > "we find that the continuum cores located within the protocluster are mass segregated" (4.4 Possible Driving Mechanism)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-resolution QUARKS band 6 data used to detect and characterize the outflow fingers  
  > "In this study, we utilized high-resolution ALMA band 6 data from the QUARKS survey and detected 28 outflow fingers associated with I15520." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Velocity gap near cloud velocity excluded because of self-absorption and cloud contamination; Tex of 50 K chosen from literature range of outflow temperatures  
  > "Therefore, to exclude the contamination of the central cloud and mitigate the effect of self-absorption, we exclude the velocity range" (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics); "In general, the temperature of the outflowing material lies within $\sim20-100$ K (cite: hatchell1999; Shimajiri2009)." (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
- Alternative: Bipolar outflows searched for in velocity-opposite channels and not found associated with the fingers  
  > "and do not find any bipolar components associated with the outflow fingers." (3.2 Identification of Outflows and their Kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Outflow parameters affected by missing flux, distance uncertainty, constant temperature and constant H2/CO; factor of two to three over- or underestimate  
  > "Overall, these factors may result in the underestimation or overestimation of the outflow parameters by a factor of two to three." (3.3 Outflow Energetics)
- Limitation (authors): Resolution and sensitivity cannot rule out a few individual bipolar outflows  
  > "Furthermore, due to the limitations in resolution and sensitivity, we cannot rule out the presence of a few individual bipolar outflows in the region" (4.1 Evidence of an Explosive event)
- Limitation (authors): 1.3 mm continuum may be affected by free-free emission, overestimating core masses  
  > "this could lead to the overestimation of the masses of the continuum cores" (1.3 mm Continuum Emission)

- Caveat (inferred, ours): Central -50 to -36 km/s excluded, so mass, momentum and energy are lower limits of unknown size
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Galactic rate depends on a small sample of eight events and a simplified geometric model
