## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and cubes**: CASA calibration, self-calibration, continuum and 50 km/s cubes  
  > "These self-calibrated data sets were subsequently used to construct data cubes centred around the redshifted CH$^+$ frequency" (Methods 1 - ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5  
  > "a Briggs parameter of 0.5" (Methods 1 - ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit emission and absorption line profiles**: simultaneous fit of emission and absorption Gaussians; tau_0 and N(CH+) derived  
  > "Fits have been performed simultaneously on the emission and absorption lines" (Methods 3 - Exploitation of the CH+ lineprofiles)
   - parameter: N(CH+)=3e12 cm-2 tau_0 dv_abs  
  > "$$N({\rm CH^+})=3\times10^{12}{\,{\rm cm}^{-2}}\ \tau_0 \,(\Delta v_{\rm abs}/1\hbox{\,km\,s$^{-1}$})$$" (Methods 3 - Exploitation of the CH+ lineprofiles)
   - assumption: absorbing screen uniformly covers background source of radius r_SMG  
  > "of the background source assumed to be uniformly covered by the absorbing screen" (Methods 3 - Exploitation of the CH+ lineprofiles)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits; bootstrap uncertainties  
  > "have been evaluated with the bootstrap method to take into account the correlation between these parameters" (Methods 3 - Exploitation of the CH+ lineprofiles)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive turbulent reservoir radius, mass and kinetic luminosity**: turbulent energy flux balance sustaining CH+; r_TR = v_abs t_TR with t_TR~50 Myr; mass for n_H ∝ r^-2  
  > "so that the average density of the reservoir decreases with radius as $\overline{n}_{\rm H} \propto r^{-2}$." (Methods 5)
   - parameter: t_TR=50 Myr; v_abs=0.7 dv_abs  
  > "computed for $t_{\rm TR} = 50$ Myr" (Table 2)
   - assumption: f_H2=0.4, n_H 30-50 cm-3, mu_p=1.27  
  > "A mean mass per particle $\mu_{\rm p}=1.27$ is adopted." (Methods 5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Eyelash and SDP17b dynamical times consistent with escape velocity  
  > "the mean turbulent velocities are equal to the escape velocities" (abstract)
4. **Model CH+ production in irradiated shocks**: Paris-Durham shock code grid; LVG for line brightness  
  > "The production of CH$^+$ in low-velocity interstellar shocks has been studied using the Paris-Durham shock code" (Methods 6 - Models of irradiated shocks)
   - software: Paris-Durham shock code, RADEX/LVG
   - assumption: B=2[nH]^1/2 microG, Av=1 mag  
  > "assuming a magnetic field of 2$[n_{\rm H}/1{\rm \,cm^{-3}}]^{1/2}\mu$G, and a visible extinction" (Methods 6 - Models of irradiated shocks)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CH+ is detected in absorption in five of six targets and in emission in all, with broad emission over 1000 km/s.  
  > "We detect absorption in five targets, out of the six observed." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: CH+ forms only with supra-thermal energy input and its J=1-0 line is emitted in dense gas yet absorbed in dilute gas  
  > "This line has such a high critical density for excitation that it is emitted only in very dense ($>10^5$  cm$^{-3}$) gas, and is absorbed in low-density gas." (abstract)
- Alternative: J-type shocks rejected as far less efficient than C-type for CH+ production  
  > "We conclude that $J$-type shocks are far less efficient than $C$-type shocks to produce CH$^+$." (Methods 6 - Models of irradiated shocks)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Coarse velocity resolution; N(CH+) may be lower limits due to possible saturation  
  > "The estimated values of $N({\rm CH^+})$ could therefore be lower limits" (Methods 3 - Exploitation of the CH+ lineprofiles)
- Limitation (authors): Sample size is small  
  > "Although the sample size is still small" (abstract)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Reservoir age t_TR~50 Myr is assumed from two sources and drives radii and masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Differential lensing not modelled
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uniform covering absorbing screen assumed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dependence on free parameters alpha, f_H2, n_50 is degenerate
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fits for NAv1.56 and NAv1.144 unreliable
