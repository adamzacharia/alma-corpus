## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit torsion-rotation Hamiltonian to laboratory spectra of CD3OH**: Rho-axis-method (RAM) least-squares fitting of 7272 microwave and 5619 FIR line frequencies using a two-step diagonalization procedure  
  > "We employed the RAM36 code (cite: Ilyushin:2010; Ilyushin:2013)" (Spectroscopic properties of CD$_3$OH and our theoretical approach); "In the current study, we keep 31 torsional basis functions at the first diagonalization step and 11 torsional basis functions at the second diagonalization step." (Spectroscopic properties of CD$_3$OH and our theoretical approach); "The final dataset in this work involves 7272 microwave and 5619 FIR line frequencies, which, as a result of blending, correspond to 15798 transitions with $J_{\rm max}$ = 55." (Spectroscopic results)
   - software: RAM36
   - parameter: 31 torsional basis functions at first step, 11 at second step; 117 molecular parameters (1 fixed: V9); J_max = 55  
  > "In the current study, we keep 31 torsional basis functions at the first diagonalization step and 11 torsional basis functions at the second diagonalization step." (Spectroscopic properties of CD$_3$OH and our theoretical approach); "A fit with a weighted root mean square (rms) deviation of 0.81 with 117 parameters included in the model (one fixed parameter) was chosen as our “best fit” for this paper." (Spectroscopic results)
   - assumption: Only the lowest three K series for A (K = -3, 0, 3) and E (K = -4, -1, 2) species in v_t = 2 are retained to avoid intervibrational perturbations  
  > "Only the lowest three $K$ series for the A and E species in $\varv_{\rm t}=2$ were retained in order to constrain the torsional parameters in the Hamiltonian model better." (Spectroscopic results); "This corresponds to $K = -4, -1, 2$ for the E species in $\varv_{\rm t}=2$ and to $K = -3, 0, 3$ for the A species." (Spectroscopic results)
   - fitting: Weighted least-squares fit yielding overall weighted rms deviation of 0.81  
  > "A fit with a weighted root mean square (rms) deviation of 0.81 with 117 parameters included in the model (one fixed parameter) was chosen as our “best fit” for this paper." (Spectroscopic results)
   - validation: All data groups fit within experimental uncertainties; duplicate measurements from Kharkiv and Cologne agree within uncertainties  
  > "We emphasize that a rather good agreement within the experimental uncertainties was observed for this limited set of duplicate measurements." (Spectroscopic results); "The overall weighted rms deviation of 0.81 and the additional fact that all data groups are fit within experimental uncertainties seems satisfactory to us" (Spectroscopic results)
2. **Calculate CD3OH transition line list and partition function for astronomical searches**: Compute transition frequencies, uncertainties, lower state energies, transition strengths, and direct-summation torsion-rotation partition function Q_rt(T) from the final fit parameters  
  > "We calculated a list of CD$_3$OH transitions in the ground and first excited torsional states for astronomical observations using the parameters of our final fit." (Spectroscopic results); "In addition we provide the torsion-rotation part of the partition function $Q_{rt}$(T) of CD$_3$OH calculated from first principles, that is, via direct summation over the torsion-rotational states." (Spectroscopic results)
   - software: RAM36
   - parameter: v_t <= 1, J <= 60, |K_a| <= 24, frequency <= 1.3 THz, calculated uncertainty < 0.1 MHz; partition function summed up to J = 100 and n_vt = 11  
  > "we chose a torsional state limit of $\varv_{\rm t} \leq 1$ and rotational limits of $J \leq 60$ and $|K_{a}| \leq 24$." (Spectroscopic results); "The calculations were truncated above 1.3 THz. Additionally, we limited our calculations to transitions for which the uncertainties are less than 0.1 MHz." (Spectroscopic results); "The maximum $J$ value is 100 and $n_{\varv_{\rm t}} = 11$ torsional states were taken into account." (Spectroscopic results)
   - assumption: Dipole moment function of Mekhtiev et al. (1999) with permanent dipole moment components replaced by 13CD3OH values mu_a = 0.9080(9) D and mu_b = 1.4378(10) D  
  > "We employed the dipole moment function of (cite: MEKHTIEV1999171) in our calculations, where the values for the permanent dipole moment components were replaced by $\mu_a = 0.9080(9)$ D and $\mu_b = 1.4378(10)$ D." (Spectroscopic results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison of simulated and observed intensity patterns of the laboratory CD3OH spectrum  
  > "As seen from Fig. 4, this choice of dipole moment components provides an opportunity to reproduce the observed intensity pattern of the CD$_3$OH spectrum rather well." (Spectroscopic results)
3. **Search for CD3OH and fit synthetic spectra to ALMA PILS observations toward IRAS 16293B**: Fit LTE synthetic spectra to optically thin lines in the ALMA PILS spectrum at an offset position from IRAS 16293B  
  > "The search was conducted in a similar manner as for other complex organics in the PILS survey by fitting synthetic spectra to the PILS data." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH); "We focus in the analysis on lines that are optically thin (fitted values of $\tau < 0.2$ km s$^{-1}$), leaving the kinetic temperature and column density of the species as the two free parameters." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
   - parameter: v_LSR = 2.6 km s^-1, line width = 1 km s^-1 (FWHM), optical depth tau < 0.2  
  > "Furthermore, a common velocity offset relative to the local standard of rest is adopted (2.6 km s$^{-1}$) and the lines are fit with a similar width (1 km s$^{-1}$; FWHM). We focus in the analysis on lines that are optically thin (fitted values of $\tau < 0.2$ km s$^{-1}$)" (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
   - assumption: Local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE)  
  > "Such synthetic spectra are calculated assuming that the excitation of the molecule is characterized by local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE), which is reasonable at the densities on the scales probed by PILS (cite: PILS_2016)." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
   - fitting: Fitting kinetic temperature and column density as the two free parameters  
  > "We focus in the analysis on lines that are optically thin (fitted values of $\tau < 0.2$ km s$^{-1}$), leaving the kinetic temperature and column density of the species as the two free parameters." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
   - validation: Varied excitation temperature from 200 to 250 K, and tested excluding lowest excitation lines (E_up < 100 K) up to 300 K  
  > "Varying the excitation temperature from 200 to 250 K does not change the quality of the fits significantly and results in column densities of $2.8 \times 10^{16}$ to $3.2 \times 10^{16}$ cm$^{-2}$." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH); "If those lines are excluded, a reasonable fit can be obtained for temperatures up to 300 K with a column density of $3.7 \times 10^{16}$ cm$^{-2}$." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
4. **Derive CD3OH/CH3OH abundance ratio and statistical D/H ratio**: Compare derived CD3OH column density with CH3OH column density from CH3^18OH, and infer D/H assuming equal probability of deuterium substitution reactions  
  > "We obtained a CD$_3$OH/CH$_3$OH ratio of $\sim$0.33% from this column density and that of CH$_3$OH, which was estimated from optically thin lines of CH$_3^{18}$OH (cite: PILS_2016; deuteration_16293_2018)." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH); "If all substitution reactions of deuterium in CH$_3$OH were equally probable (cite: det_CD3OH_2004; D2-MeFo_2019), this CD$_3$OH/CH$_3$OH ratio would imply a D/H ratio of 15%." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
   - assumption: All substitution reactions of deuterium in CH3OH are equally probable  
  > "If all substitution reactions of deuterium in CH$_3$OH were equally probable (cite: det_CD3OH_2004; D2-MeFo_2019), this CD$_3$OH/CH$_3$OH ratio would imply a D/H ratio of 15%." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)

## Qualitative findings

- CD3OH is securely detected in both v_t = 0 and v_t = 1 torsional states toward IRAS 16293B in the ALMA PILS data.  
  > "We also detected several unblended or only slightly blended transitions pertaining to the $\varv_{\rm t} = 1$ torsional state." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH); "CD$_3$OH is securely detected in the data at a high level of confidence with a number of clearly separated and well-reproduced lines." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
- The v_t = 2 torsional state of CD3OH exhibits significant perturbations likely due to intervibrational interactions with low-lying small-amplitude vibrations.  
  > "After revealing perturbations in the second excited torsional state of CD$_3$OH, presumably caused by the intervibrational interactions arising from low-lying small-amplitude vibrations in this molecule, we concentrated our efforts on refining the theoretical model for the ground and first excited torsional states only." (Conclusion)
- The D/H ratio inferred from CD3OH/CH3OH is about a factor of 7 higher than that inferred from singly deuterated methanol isotopologs, supporting formation in outer ice mantles late in protostellar evolution, though the CD3OH/CH3OH ratio still exceeds predictions from ice deuteration models.  
  > "This ratio is about a factor of 7 higher than the D/H ratios inferred from measuring the singly deuterated (CH$_2$DOH and CH$_3$OD) isotopologs relative to the main isotopolog" (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH); "However, it should be noted that the measured abundance ratios of CD$_3$OH relative to CH$_3$OH are still significantly above those predicted in the models by (cite: deuteration_in_ice_2014) of $\sim$0.01%" (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The ALMA PILS survey is an unbiased, high-sensitivity spectral survey with narrow lines (~1 km s^-1) toward the offset position of IRAS 16293B, enabling identification of ~10,000 lines of complex organic molecules and their isotopologs.  
  > "Toward one specific region that is slightly offset from the “B” component of the system (IRAS16293B in the following), the lines are narrow, $\sim$1 km s$^{-1}$, and with the sensitivity of the ALMA data $\sim$10,000 individual lines can be identified" (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
- Why this method: The rho-axis-method (RAM) is the most effective approach for treating torsional large amplitude motions in methanol-like molecules with high coupling between internal and overall rotations; LTE is reasonable at the high densities probed by PILS.  
  > "The approach in the present study is the so-called rho-axis-method (RAM), which has proven to be the most effective approach so far in treating torsional large amplitude motions in methanol-like molecules." (Spectroscopic properties of CD$_3$OH and our theoretical approach); "Such synthetic spectra are calculated assuming that the excitation of the molecule is characterized by local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE), which is reasonable at the densities on the scales probed by PILS (cite: PILS_2016)." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
- Alternative: Combined fit including the full v_t = 2 torsional state up to J = 54 was rejected because intervibrational perturbations resulted in a large weighted rms deviation of 4.4.  
  > "the best weighted root mean square deviation we could obtain for a combined fit of the $\varv_{\rm t}=0$ to 2 CD$_3$OH states with $J$ up to 54 was as large as 4.4." (Spectroscopic results); "Taking into account the astrophysical significance of methanol and its isotopologs, we decided subsequently to limit our fitting attempts mainly to the ground and first excited torsional states." (Spectroscopic results)
- Alternative: Varying V9 or omitting V9 from the Hamiltonian model were rejected due to slow convergence/parameter correlations when varied and a significant increase in weighted rms deviation when omitted.  
  > "We decided to fix the $V_9$ parameter in the final fit to improve the convergence of the fit." (Spectroscopic results); "Omitting $V_9$ from our model was not an option because this would lead to a significant increase in the weighted rms deviation of the fit." (Spectroscopic results)
- How they decided it was enough: The spectroscopic fit achieved an overall weighted rms deviation of 0.81 with all data groups fit within experimental uncertainties; calculation extrapolations were truncated at v_t <= 1, J <= 60, |K_a| <= 24, and < 1.3 THz where uncertainties are < 0.1 MHz.  
  > "The overall weighted rms deviation of 0.81 and the additional fact that all data groups are fit within experimental uncertainties seems satisfactory to us" (Spectroscopic results); "Since extrapolation beyond the quantum number coverage of a given experimental dataset becomes rapidly unreliable, especially in the case of molecules with large amplitude motions, we chose a torsional state limit of $\varv_{\rm t} \leq 1$ and rotational limits of $J \leq 60$ and $|K_{a}| \leq 24$." (Spectroscopic results)
- Limitation (authors): The 5320 FIR transitions from Walsh et al. (1998) could not be recovered from the literature.  
  > "Unfortunately, we were able to recover only the microwave part of the dataset from this paper, which was presented in Tables 3a and 3b of (cite: CD3OH_fitting_1998)." (Spectroscopic results)
- Limitation (authors): Hamiltonian terms needed to account explicitly for intervibrational perturbations in v_t = 2 are not yet built into the present model, restricting the fit and predictions to v_t <= 1 (plus only 3 K-series in v_t = 2).  
  > "the Hamiltonian terms needed to account explicitly for the perturbations are not yet built in our present model" (Spectroscopic results)
- Limitation (authors): Extrapolation beyond the experimental quantum number coverage becomes rapidly unreliable.  
  > "Since extrapolation beyond the quantum number coverage of a given experimental dataset becomes rapidly unreliable, especially in the case of molecules with large amplitude motions, we chose a torsional state limit of $\varv_{\rm t} \leq 1$ and rotational limits of $J \leq 60$ and $|K_{a}| \leq 24$." (Spectroscopic results)
- Limitation (authors): Lowest excitation lines (E_up < 100 K) in the ALMA spectrum may trace a more extended, cold foreground layer.  
  > "The lowest excitation lines ($E_{\rm up} < 100$ K) may be tracing a potentially more extended, cold foreground layer (cite: deuteration_16293_2018)." (Astronomical search for CD$_3$OH)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Permanent dipole moment components used to compute transition strengths were measured for 13CD3OH rather than 12CD3OH, introducing a potential small systematic uncertainty in derived column densities.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-excitation-temperature LTE modeling of IRAS 16293B may not fully capture line-of-sight temperature gradients or dust continuum optical depth effects at Band 7 frequencies.
