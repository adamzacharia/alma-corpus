## Science

- Fields: agn_and_quasars, nearby_galaxies
- Question: Whether the two close-separation (~230 pc) near-IR nuclei in the late-stage major galaxy merger UGC 4211 are both powered by accreting supermassive black holes (a confirmed dual AGN).  
  > "Using Hubble Space Telescope/STIS, VLT/MUSE+AO, Keck/OSIRIS+AO spectroscopy, and ALMA observations, we show that the spatial distribution, optical and NIR emission lines, and millimeter continuum emission are all consistent with both nuclei being powered by accreting supermassive black holes (SMBHs)." (Abstract); "We focus in this Letter on the dual AGN nature of the two NIR nuclei." (Analysis and Results)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure ALMA 231 GHz nuclear continuum positions, flux densities, separation, and spectral index**: Coadd line-free channels in four spectral windows (221-240 GHz) to make high-resolution (~0''.07 and ~0''.04 with robust -0.5) continuum maps, and fit spatial 2D Gaussian models using CASA imfit.  
  > "The continuum map analyzed here was computed coadding emission in line-free regions in four ALMA spectral windows ranging from 221 to 240 GHz" (ALMA); "Using the CASA imfit task to fit model Gaussians spatially, we find two sources of continuum emission consistent with the northern and southern nuclei (both in separation and position angle)." (Imaging Analysis)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Frequency 221-240 GHz (231 GHz); resolution ~0''.07 and ~0''.04 (robust = -0.5)  
  > "The nuclear ALMA 231 GHz continuum emission (<200 pc), has been found to be a good proxy of the AGN luminosity" (Imaging Analysis); "Both sources are unresolved at this resolution, and remain unresolved on a higher resolution, \sim0''04 (28 pc), map obtained using a Briggs robust parameter of -0.5." (Imaging Analysis)
   - assumption: Cosmology: Omega_m=0.3, Omega_Lambda=0.7, H_0=70 km s^-1 Mpc^-1, scale 0''.69 kpc^-1 at z=0.03474  
  > "Throughout this study, we adopt \Omega_m=0.3, \Omega_\Lambda=0.7, and H_0=70 km s^{-1} Mpc^{-1}, and a scale of 0''69 kpc^{-1} based on the redshift of the system." (Introduction)
   - fitting: 2D spatial Gaussian fitting via CASA imfit  
  > "Using the CASA imfit task to fit model Gaussians spatially, we find two sources of continuum emission consistent with the northern and southern nuclei (both in separation and position angle)." (Imaging Analysis)
   - validation: Comparison of ALMA positions, separation, and PA with NIR K' band nuclei positions  
  > "Therefore, the position, separation, and PA of the NIR nuclei closely match the 2 millimeter (mm) sources given the relative astrometric and centroiding errors (\sim0''1)." (Imaging Analysis)
2. **Extract and fit optical and NIR nuclear emission line spectra and BPT diagnostics**: Extract 1D spectra from HST/STIS (0''.25 x 0''.2), Keck/OSIRIS (0''.3 diameter apertures), and VLT/MUSE (three 0''.15 diameter apertures N, S, M and Voronoi-binned spaxels), fit continuum and Gaussian emission lines, and place line ratios on BPT diagrams.  
  > "We extracted a 5 pixel (0''25) spectrum along the N–S direction across the nuclei peak \[O iii\] emission in HST/STIS which is at similar separation to the two NIR nuclei (0''3)." (Nuclear Emission Line Analysis); "With the excellent pixel sampling and PSF of VLT/MUSE, we extract three smaller spectral apertures (0''15 diameter) with two centered on the NIR nuclei and an additional region midway between them" (Nuclear Emission Line Analysis); "We use PySpecKit to fit the optical emission lines from HST/STIS, the MUSE \[O iii\] \lambda 5007 map, and the NIR emission lines from Keck/OSIRIS, which uses a Levenberg–Marquardt algorithm for spectral fitting (cite: Ginsburg:2011:1109.001) with Gaussians following the approach of (cite: Koss:2017:74)." (Emission and absorption line fitting)
   - software: stistools, Reflex, QFitsView, MPDAF, xtellcor, PySpecKit, vorbin
   - parameter: STIS aperture 5 pixels (0''.25); OSIRIS aperture 0''.3 diameter; MUSE apertures 0''.15 diameter; Voronoi binning S/N=10 for [O III]; 3 pixel median filter on MUSE cube  
  > "With Keck/OSIRIS ([2](#fig:stis)), we extract a spectrum with a 0''3 diameter aperture around each nuclei" (Nuclear Emission Line Analysis); "We use the MUSE python data analysis framework (cite: 2016ascl.soft11003B) to extract cubes and perform a 3 pixel median filter due to improve S/N." (Optical IFS Spectroscopy); "For measurements of the \[O iii\] \lambda 5007 emission line velocity, we also use voronoi binning before fitting, requiring a S/N of 10." (Emission and absorption line fitting)
   - assumption: Kewley et al. (2006) strong-line AGN diagnostic classifications  
  > "Relying on commonly used diagnostics, specifically those revised by (cite: Kewley:2006:961), we find that all three regions are classified as AGN-(or “Seyfert"-)powered, rather than H ii, or composites." (Nuclear Emission Line Analysis)
   - fitting: Levenberg-Marquardt Gaussian fitting in PySpecKit; first-order polynomial continuum fit on spaxel-by-spaxel basis; galaxy template plus emission line fitting for MUSE N, S, M apertures  
  > "To measure the equivalent width, line emission, and BPT ratio, we performed a first-order polynomial fit to nearby line-free regions to measure and subtract the continuum on a spaxel-by-spaxel basis." (Optical IFS Spectroscopy); "We use PySpecKit to fit the optical emission lines from HST/STIS, the MUSE \[O iii\] \lambda 5007 map, and the NIR emission lines from Keck/OSIRIS, which uses a Levenberg–Marquardt algorithm for spectral fitting (cite: Ginsburg:2011:1109.001) with Gaussians following the approach of (cite: Koss:2017:74)." (Emission and absorption line fitting)
   - validation: Consistency of line brightnesses and velocity offsets between HST/STIS and VLT/MUSE spectra  
  > "The extracted MUSE spectra are consistent with the HST/STIS results, with relatively brighter \[O iii\] emission from the northern NIR nucleus and \[N II\] emission that is brighter in the southern nucleus." (Nuclear Emission Line Analysis)
3. **Measure nuclear stellar kinematics, [O III] kinematics, and ALMA CO(2-1) gas velocity distribution**: Fit MUSE Ca II triplet absorption (8350-8900 Å) using pPXF on Voronoi-binned data (S/N=35) and N, S, M apertures; fit [O III] emission velocities; generate ALMA 12CO(2-1) velocity map from 15 km s^-1 channel cube.  
  > "The emission line and stellar absorption velocities of the northern and southern nuclei are derived from MUSE using the \[O iii\] \lambda 5007 emission and Ca II \,\lambda 8498, 8542, 8662 triplet absorption region (CaT, 8350–8900 Å)." (Redshift and Kinematics of the Nuclei); "For velocity dispersion measurements, we follow (cite: Koss:2017:74; Koss:2022:6), using the penalized PiXel Fitting (pPXF) software (cite: Cappellari:2017:798) to measure stellar kinematics and the central stellar velocity dispersion (\sigma_{*})." (Emission and absorption line fitting); "while a cube covering the ^{12}CO(2-1) emission line was generated with a spectral width of 15 km s^{-1}, and a Briggs robust parameter of 0.5, resulting in a beam size of 0''061\times0''073." (ALMA)
   - software: pPXF, vorbin, PySpecKit, CASA
   - parameter: CaT region 8350–8900 Å; Voronoi S/N = 35 for stellar kinematics, S/N = 10 for [O III]; ALMA CO(2-1) cube channel width 15 km s^-1, robust = 0.5  
  > "Unbiased measurements of stellar kinematics require a minimum S/N, so for an adaptive spatial-binning scheme we use the Voronoi algorithm as implemented in vorbin (cite: 2003MNRAS.342..345C), requiring a S/N of 35 for each bin." (Emission and absorption line fitting); "while a cube covering the ^{12}CO(2-1) emission line was generated with a spectral width of 15 km s^{-1}, and a Briggs robust parameter of 0.5, resulting in a beam size of 0''061\times0''073." (ALMA)
   - assumption: X-Shooter Spectral Library DR2 stellar templates  
  > "We used the X-Shooter Spectral Library (XSL, specifically DR2; (cite: Gonneau:2020:A133))." (Emission and absorption line fitting)
   - fitting: Penalized PiXel Fitting (pPXF) for stellar absorption lines  
  > "For velocity dispersion measurements, we follow (cite: Koss:2017:74; Koss:2022:6), using the penalized PiXel Fitting (pPXF) software (cite: Cappellari:2017:798) to measure stellar kinematics and the central stellar velocity dispersion (\sigma_{*})." (Emission and absorption line fitting)
   - validation: Agreement within error of [O III], CaT stellar absorption, and CO(2-1) velocity offsets across the apertures  
  > "The \[O iii\] and absorption line velocities match within error for each of the three apertures and also show a similar offset between the two nuclei" (Redshift and Kinematics of the Nuclei)
4. **Derive AGN bolometric luminosities, black hole masses, sphere of influence, and Eddington ratios**: Convert ALMA nuclear mm luminosity to intrinsic 2-10 keV luminosity using Kawamuro et al. (2022) relation and apply bolometric correction of 20; compute M_BH from broad Pa beta (south) and stellar velocity dispersion sigma_* (north and south); compute Eddington ratios.

## Qualitative findings

- Both the northern and southern nuclei of UGC 4211 are unresolved (<28 pc) in ALMA 231 GHz continuum, spatially coincident with the two NIR stellar nuclei separated by ~230 pc.  
  > "Both sources are unresolved at this resolution, and remain unresolved on a higher resolution, \sim0''04 (28 pc), map obtained using a Briggs robust parameter of -0.5." (Imaging Analysis); "Therefore, the position, separation, and PA of the NIR nuclei closely match the 2 millimeter (mm) sources given the relative astrometric and centroiding errors (\sim0''1)." (Imaging Analysis)
- The southern nucleus exhibits a hidden broad-line region in NIR hydrogen recombination lines (Pa beta, Br gamma) and a flat mm spectral index (alpha = 0.02 +/- 0.26), while the northern nucleus shows peak [O III] emission and harder Seyfert BPT ionization ratios.  
  > "A hidden broad-line region (BLR) component (>1000 km s^{-1}) is also found in the NIR hydrogen recombination lines (e.g. Pa\beta, FWHM=1996\pm180 km s^{-1}; Br\gamma, FWHM=2530\pm160 km s^{-1}) of the southern nucleus, but no BLR is found in the northern nucleus." (Nuclear Emission Line Analysis); "The high-resolution MUSE AO (and HST/STIS) observations, show two separate emission sources of \[O iii\] \lambda 5007, each of them unresolved at \sim0''1 resolution. When placed on the BPT diagram, both nuclei are clearly in the Seyfert locus with the northern AGN further in the ionized AGN region." (Conclusions)
- ALMA CO(2-1) and MUSE CaT stellar absorption velocity maps show matching velocity gradients and a ~150 km s^-1 velocity offset between the two nuclei, proving they are kinematically independent nuclei rather than jet knots.  
  > "Using the CO(2-1) emission line as a tracer of the molecular gas, the velocity map shows a clear velocity gradient centered on the positions of the northern and southern continuum emitters. A similar gradient is also seen in the Ca II \,\lambda 8498, 8542, 8662 stellar absorption lines. This demonstrates that both sources are kinematically independent nuclei, while rules out other possibilities, such as a jet knots." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Subkpc dual AGN in late-stage mergers are heavily obscured and separated by ~0''.3, requiring high-spatial resolution (~0''.06-0''.1) multiwavelength data (NIR AO, optical AO IFS, and ALMA mm continuum/CO) to resolve both nuclei and avoid false positives from single diagnostics.  
  > "Ultimately, it is critical to identify subkpc dual AGN using a multiwavelength analysis to confirm the nature of their nuclei." (Introduction); "The nuclear ALMA 231 GHz continuum emission (<200 pc), has been found to be a good proxy of the AGN luminosity, with a tight correlation with the 14–150 keV (cite: 2022ApJ...938...87K)." (Imaging Analysis)
- Why this method: Extracting 0''.15 MUSE apertures and applying Voronoi spatial binning (S/N=35 for stellar kinematics, S/N=10 for [O III]) ensures sufficient S/N for weak Hbeta fitting and unbiased velocity dispersion measurements.  
  > "This is done because the individual spaxels are too low S/N to perform emission line fitting of the weak H\beta emission."
