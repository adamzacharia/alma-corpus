## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra and continuum**: Spectra and continuum flux taken from a 0.5" diameter circular region at the 840 um continuum peak after uvcontsub  
  > "The spectra as well as continuum flux are extracted from the circular region with a diameter of 0.5$\ensuremath{''}$ centered at RA = 05$^\mathrm{h}$26$^\mathrm{m}$46$s$60" (2.3 Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure line parameters**: Single Gaussian fit per line (double Gaussian for blended NO); CO by visual estimate and direct integration 230–285 km/s  
  > "The line parameters are measured by fitting a single Gaussian profile to the observed lines." (4.1 Spectral fitting)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile fitting  
  > "The line parameters are measured by fitting a single Gaussian profile to the observed lines." (4.1 Spectral fitting)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Rotation diagram for SO2 isotopologues**: Optically thin LTE rotation diagram, straight-line fit of ln(Nu/gu) vs Eu/k  
  > "we perform the rotation diagram analysis assuming an optically thin condition and an local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)" (4.2 Rotation diagram)
   - software: CDMS, JPL catalog
   - assumption: Optically thin, LTE  
  > "assuming an optically thin condition and an local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)" (4.2 Rotation diagram)
   - fitting: straight-line fit; 2 sigma uncertainties  
  > "Uncertainties in the table are of 2 $\sigma$ level" (4.2 Rotation diagram)
   - validation: Same analysis on Orion data with similar lines for fair comparison  
  > "we also perform the rotation diagram analysis for the Orion hot core using the lines with the similar spectroscopic properties" (4.2 Rotation diagram)
4. **Column densities of other molecules**: LTE optically thin with Trot fixed; upper limits with FWHM 6 km/s  
  > "$T_{rot}$ is assumed to be 100 K, which is roughly the average rotational temperature of SO$_2$, $^{34}$SO$_2$ and $^{33}$SO$_2$." (4.3 Column densities of other molecules)
   - assumption: Same region as SO2; Trot = 100 K  
  > "We here assume that the observed molecular species are located in the same region as SO$_2$ and its isotopologues" (4.3 Column densities of other molecules)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: NO column density scatter among lines <20%  
  > "The scatter of column densities estimated from different lines is less than 20 $\%$." (4.3 Column densities of other molecules)
5. **H2 column density and gas mass from dust continuum**: Optically thin dust emission, N_H2 = (F/Omega)/(2 kappa B(Td) Z mu mH)  
  > "the column density of molecular hydrogen is derived by the following equation" (4.4 Column density of H2)
   - parameter: F/Omega = 0.34 Jy/beam in 0.5" aperture  
  > "With the aperture size of 0.5$\ensuremath{''}$ in diameter, the $F_{\nu}/\Omega$ is measured to be 0.34 Jy/beam." (4.4 Column density of H2)
   - assumption: Td = 40 K, Z = 0.0027, mu = 1.41, Ossenkopf & Henning ice-coated opacity  
  > "we assume $T_{d}$ = 40 K, which is a typical dust temperature of high-mass YSOs in the LMC" (4.4 Column density of H2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Av compared with SED fit (factor of two difference)  
  > "which differs by a factor of two from the value estimated based on the dust continuum data" (4.5 Luminosity and stellar mass)
6. **SED luminosity and stellar mass**: Integrate interpolated SED 1–1000 um; Robitaille Online SED Fitter on 2–840 um data excluding SPIRE 350/500  
  > "The stellar mass of ST11 is estimated using the Online SED Fitter (cite: Rob07)." (4.5 Luminosity and stellar mass)
   - software: Online SED Fitter
   - assumption: Distance to ST11 equals LMC distance  
  > "The distance to ST11 is assumed to be the same as the distance to the LMC." (4.5 Luminosity and stellar mass)
   - fitting: best-fit model from SED fitter  
  > "The estimated stellar mass of ST11 in the best-fit model is 50 M$_{\ensuremath{\odot}}$." (4.5 Luminosity and stellar mass)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Fractional abundances vs Galactic hot cores**: N(X)/N(H2) with LMC isotope ratios; Galactic sources re-estimated with local ISM ratios  
  > "Their abundances are re-estimated using the following isotope ratios of the local ISM and the Sun" (5.2 Molecular abundances)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with beam-matched Galactic data  
  > "we here compare the abundances which are derived from the data with similar beam sizes" (5.2 Molecular abundances)
8. **Outflow parameters from CO(3-2) wings**: Blue wing 230.5–240.5 km/s, red wing 265.5–285.5 km/s; mass from CO intensity conversion factor; timescale from 0.24 pc / 20 km/s  
  > "We here define the velocity range of the blue-shifted wing to be 230.5 km/s to 240.5 km/s and the red-shifted wing to be 265.5 km/s to 285.5 km/s." (5.3 Molecular outflows)
   - parameter: Conversion factor 8.8 Msun (K km/s)^-1 pc^-2; S/N > 6  
  > "we use a conversion factor of 8.8 M$_{\ensuremath{\odot}}$ (K km/s)$^{-1}$ pc$^{-2}$, which is derived from the present observations toward the ST11 center" (5.3 Molecular outflows)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Galactic high-mass YSOs of similar luminosity  
  > "roughly consistent with those observed in Galactic high-mass YSOs that have similar luminosities with ST11" (5.3 Molecular outflows)

## Qualitative findings

- CH3OH, H2CO and HNCO are strongly under-abundant relative to Galactic hot cores, attributed to suppressed CO hydrogenation on warm grains  
  > "We suggest that these abundances are characterized by the deficiency of molecules whose formation requires the hydrogenation of CO on grain surfaces." (abstract)
