## Analysis recipe

1. **Construct continuum images**: Flag line channels, concatenate configurations and image line-free visibilities with tclean.  
  > "Channels with spectral line emission in the visibility data were visually identified with the `plotms` task in CASA, and were flagged to keep only line-free channels." (ALMA 3 mm); "The calibrated and flagged visibility data of the C43-4 and C43-7 configurations were then merged using the `concat` task." (ALMA 3 mm); "Continuum images were reconstructed from the line-free channels, using the multi-term, multifrequency synthesis method (`mtmfs`) with nterm=2 in the `tclean` task in CASA 6.5.2-26." (ALMA 3 mm)
   - software: CASA, plotms, concat, tclean, mtmfs
   - parameter: 3 mm: Briggs robust 0.5; multiscale [0, 5, 15, 50, 150]; pixel 0.04 arcsec.  
  > "Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5 was used." (ALMA 3 mm); "The multi-scale deconvolution algorithm was adopted with multi-scale parameters of \[0, 5, 15, 50, 150\] for a pixel size of 0$''$04." (ALMA 3 mm)
   - parameter: Continuum reference frequencies: 93.6 and 226.0 GHz.  
  > "The reference frequency of the continuum map produced by `mtmfs` is 93.6 GHz." (ALMA 3 mm); "The continuum reference frequency is 226.0 GHz." (ALMA 1.3 mm)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Measure uncorrected-image rms by iterative ±3σ clipping to convergence.  
  > "was measured on the image without primary beam corrections with uniform thermal noise by iteratively clipping until convergence at $\pm3\sigma$ around its median (cite: Stetson1987; SExtractor)." (ALMA 3 mm)
2. **Extract mono-band and dual-band catalogs**: getsf: separate flattened components; detect and measure individual bands; restart detection and measurement using both bands; retain overlapping fields for dbcat.  
  > "The main getsf procedure was divided into three rounds: preparing flattened components across the two bands; performing single-band extractions for monochromatic catalogs; and rerunning a dual-band detection to obtain a combined catalog." (Source extraction strategy); "We removed such sources to obtain the cleanest source sample within the overlapping FoVs." (Dual-band source catalogs)
   - software: getsf
   - parameter: Original-resolution images without primary-beam correction; pb ≥0.3.  
  > "The inputs are the 1.3 mm and 3 mm continuum images at the original resolutions to avoid potential loss of detection due to the smoothing process." (Source extraction strategy); "The images are without primary beam corrections for a more uniform noise distribution." (Source extraction strategy); "The extraction mask includes regions where the primary beam response is greater than 0.3" (Source extraction strategy)
   - parameter: Minimum size: original beam; maximum size: five times the 1.3 mm beam, 1.2 arcsec (~0.05 pc or 10,000 au).  
  > "The minimum source size is set as the original resolution" (Source extraction strategy); "the maximum source size was set as five times the 1.3 mm beam size, 1.2$\ensuremath{''}$ (approximately 0.05 pc or 10,000 au at the CMZ distance)." (Source extraction strategy)

## Qualitative findings

- Mono-band catalogs prioritize completeness; dual-band catalogs improve robustness and recover faint or blended counterparts.  
  > "`mbcat`s are optimized for monochromatic completeness, while `dbcat` has improved robustness by dual-band cross check." (Completeness and robustness of the catalogs); "However, all eight sources were successfully separated and identified in the `dbcat`." (Dual-band source catalogs)
- Sources are marginally resolved with finite spatial extent.  
  > "almost all the sources have total fluxes higher than peak intensities, suggesting that they have finite sizes rather than being true point sources." (Conclusions); "In this context, we refer to them as marginally resolved sources." (Conclusions)
- Low spectral indices and low brightness temperatures are alternative descriptions of the MBB discrepancy.  
  > "we find that $>70$% of the `dbcat` sources deviate from MBB, being characterized either as the “low spectral indices” problem or as the “low brightness temperature” problem." (Conclusions)
- The three explanations may operate together.  
  > "We note that the three hypotheses are not mutually exclusive." (Possible explanations)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Cloud-wide dual-band mosaics address optical depth, mixed emission and limited single-pointing coverage.  
  > "dust emission is not always optically thin" (DUET: dual-band mosaic of three CMZ clouds); "these sources may consist of a mix of thermal and non-thermal emissions" (DUET: dual-band mosaic of three CMZ clouds); "previous observations are mostly single pointings, which could miss the population outside fields." (DUET: dual-band mosaic of three CMZ clouds)
- Why this method: getsf handles multiscale and multiband data, complex interferometric backgrounds and crowded blends.  
  > "as it can identify sources from multi-scale and also from multiband data." (Source extraction strategy); "it effectively handles various backgrounds from filamentary structures to negative bowls originated from imperfect uv-sampling and can separate blended sources in crowded fields like protoclusters" (Source extraction strategy)
- Alternative: Compact optically thick class 0/I YSOs, large grains and free-free contamination remain possible, non-exclusive explanations.  
  > "We note that the three hypotheses are not mutually exclusive." (Possible explanations); "Future work aimed at quantifying the SFR in the CMZ based on the complete protostellar mass spectrum will need to resolve the ambiguity between these possible explanations." (Conclusions)
- Alternative: Micron-sized grains alone do not explain many α <3 detections.  
  > "with $a_{\rm max}\sim0.01$ mm, a large amount of sources with low spectral indices $\alpha < 3$ cannot be explained." (Large dust grains)
- Alternative: Further intensity-profile filtering of mono-band catalogs was omitted to preserve completeness.  
  > "We did not perform any further filtering criteria based on their intensity profiles, to maintain `mbcat`’s completeness for future studies at individual bands." (Mono-band source catalogs)
- Alternative: Original-beam catalogs retained for publication because common-beam extraction loses blended detections.  
  > "the source extractions are still from the images with original beams, because common-beam `dbcat` will miss detections of blended sources due to its coarser resolution." (Low spectral indices versus low brightnesses)
- How they decided it was enough: Maximum measured source size lies below the extraction cutoff; image-noise clipping continued to convergence.  
  > "the maximum source size is $\sim$0$.\!\ensuremath{''}$8, which is much smaller than the adopted maximum size." (Source extraction procedure and catalogs); "by iteratively clipping until convergence at $\pm3\sigma$ around its median" (ALMA 3 mm)
- Limitation (authors): Interferometric missing flux and negative bowls affect extended-source and flux measurements.  
  > "Due to the missing flux effect, prestellar cores may appear even fainter than expected." (Mono-band source catalogs); "this happens when there is a noisy background or the source is near an interferometric negative bowl" (Flux measurement)
- Limitation (authors): Common-beam spectral-index catalogs lose resolution and blended sources.  
  > "The caveat of common-beam `dbcat` lies in losing the advantage of higher resolution at 1.3 mm, so some blended sources are not resolved." (Common $uv$-range at dual bands)
- Limitation (authors): Fully unresolved sources have undetermined intrinsic sizes.  
  > "In such a case, the source size is undetermined." (Source deconvolution from beam)
- Limitation (authors): Current centimeter sensitivity cannot exclude faint free-free contamination.  
  > "This is well below the sensitivity of the current JVLA data." (Free-free emission); "To firmly exclude the possibility, the sensitivity needs to be at least (sub-)$\mu$Jy." (Free-free emission)
- Limitation (authors): Radio resolution prevents unique counterpart assignment and some contamination corrections.  
  > "But due to the limited resolution, we cannot tell which one of the DUET sources is associated with H1."   > "The origin of mm/cm-sized grains is uncertain." (Large dust grains)
