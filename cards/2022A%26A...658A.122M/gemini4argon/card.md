## Qualitative findings

- No circumbinary dust continuum emission is detected by ALMA in Band 7 around BD-11 162 or BD-7 5977 above the noise level.  
  > "For both systems no signal of CB matter is detected in the observations." (Alma observations of BD$-$11$^{\rm{o}}$162 and BD$-$7$^{\rm{o}}$5977)
- Feige 80 belongs to the main group in the period-eccentricity diagram and has a canonical subdwarf mass consistent with the standard post-EHB formation channel, whereas BD-11 162 belongs to the minority group II and has a lower sdO mass.  
  > "In the bimodal period-eccentricity diagram, the orbital parameters indicate that Feige 80 matches the same correlation as the majority of the systems. The analysis suggests that Feige 80 has a canonical subdwarf mass and followed a standard formation channel." (abstract); "However, BD$-$11$^{\rm{o}}$162 is an exceptional system with a lower mass." (abstract)
- Both MS companions show a depletion of yttrium and the BD-11 162 MS companion shows a strong enrichment of carbon compared to Galactic chemical evolution trends.  
  > "They match general trends expected from Galactic chemical evolution but show a depletion of yttrium in both systems and an enrichment of carbon in the BD$-$11$^{\rm{o}}$162 MS." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA Band 7 (950 μm) was chosen for the circumbinary disk search as the best compromise between weather dependence and highest expected dust continuum flux.  
  > "ALMA observations were taken in Band 7 (950 $\mu$m) as it offered the best compromise between weather dependence and highest flux expectations." (Alma observations of BD$-$11$^{\rm{o}}$162 and BD$-$7$^{\rm{o}}$5977)
- Why this method: Macro-turbulent and micro-turbulent velocities were fixed using empirical calibration relations to avoid degeneracy with rotational velocity at the available spectral resolution and S/N.  
  > "In order to avoid this degeneracy and to establish a standard procedure for studying spectra of lower resolution and S/N, the $v_{\rm mic}$ and $v_{\rm mac}$ are set using the (cite: Bruntt2010) and (cite: Doyle2014) relations." (Atmospheric parameters)
- Alternative: Post-AGB formation channel for both hot subdwarfs was rejected because barium (a prototype second-peak s-process element) shows near scaled-solar abundances.  
  > "The abundances of Ba as an s-process prototype element are close to the scaled solar values. This supports the exclusion of the post-AGB formation channel for both hot subdwarfs." (Discussion and conclusions)
- Alternative: Using the surface gravity log g of the MS companions to constrain the MIST evolutionary mass fit was rejected because its uncertainty is too large.  
  > "It would be possible to include the surface gravity, but the error on that parameter is too large to add constrain to the fit." (Mass determination)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only two systems were chemically analysed, which is too small a sample to draw definitive conclusions about the Y depletion or C enhancement.  
  > "Nevertheless, a chemical analysis of a larger sample is necessary to draw strong conclusions." (abstract)
- Limitation (authors): The O I triplet at 7771-7775 Å is strongly affected by non-LTE effects, and poor constraints on surface gravity introduce high uncertainty into the interpolated 3D non-LTE corrections.  
  > "The abundances obtained from the O I triplet at 7771.94, 7774.15, and 7775.39 Å must be taken with precaution as these lines are known to be affected by strong non-LTE effects (cite: Amarsi2015; Amarsi2016)" (CNO abundances); "Nevertheless, the poor constraint of the surface gravity introduces high uncertainty into the interpolations on the grid and the obtained non-LTE correction." (CNO abundances)
- Limitation (authors): Nitrogen abundances could only be constrained as upper limits due to the low S/N and weakness of the N I 8683.40 Å line.  
  > "However, nitrogen analysis of BD$-$11$^{\rm{o}}$162 and Feige 80 is limited by the low S/N and quality of the N I 8683 Å line. As a consequence, only upper limits of nitrogen abundances could be derived." (CNO abundances)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA Band 7 observations are only briefly summarized in Appendix B without reporting the achieved RMS noise, synthesized beam size, or calibration/imaging details.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The interpretation of Y depletion as evidence of a past circumbinary disk relies on a single element in two stars, while other refractory elements do not show a clear depletion pattern.
