## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit continuum and line emission sizes**: 2-D Gaussian fits to continuum and integrated CO maps  
  > "We fit the continuum maps with a 2-dimensional Gaussian function using the CASA task imfit." (3.1)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract intensity profiles**: Cuts perpendicular to outflow axis, width 4"  
  > "The cuts cross the source centers and have a width of 4$''$, about $1-3$ times the beam size" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive CO and N2H+ column densities**: Optically thin LTE, Tex=10 K; N2H+ from isolated 101-012 component  
  > "under the assumptions of optically thin emission and LTE with an excitation temperature of 10 K" (4.2.2)
   - software: RADEX
   - assumption: 13CO/C18O~7.3, C18O/C17O~3.2 isotopic ratios  
  > "Taking the isotopic ratio, $^{13}$CO/C$^{18}$O $\sim7.3$, in the local ISM" (4.2.1)
   - fitting: Hyperfine fits for optical depth check  
  > "To derive the optical depth, we fit the hyperfine structure for each pixel (0.4$''$) in the N$_2$H$^+$ maps." (Hyperfine fitting appendix)
   - validation: Optically thin underestimates column by ~1.9-2.4, smaller than depletion  
  > "the optically thin assumption will result in an underestimation of the column density by a factor of $\sim 1.9-2.4$" (Hyperfine fitting appendix)
4. **Abundance profiles and N2H+ peak radius**: Divide column densities by H2 column from a spherical broken power-law core; average two sides  
  > "We assume three core masses of 0.5, 1.0, and 3.0 $M_\odot$ within a radius of 10,000 au" (4.3)
   - parameter: Transition radius 3000 au  
  > "The transition radius from static to free-fall is adopted to be 3000 au" (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with pure power laws p=-1.5, -2.0; range adopted as uncertainty  
  > "This difference is taken as the uncertainty in this measurement" (4.3)
5. **Model temperature profiles and CO snow line**: DUSTY radiative transfer, CO sublimation at 20 K  
  > "we use the one-dimensional radiative transfer code DUSTY (cite: iv99) to model the temperature profiles of our VeLLOs" (4.4)
   - software: DUSTY
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust 100; dust opacity 1063.8 cm2/g at 8 um (Ossenkopf & Henning)  
  > "Assuming a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100 and a dust opacity of 1063.8 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$ at 8 $\mu$m (cite: os94)" (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Fit burst luminosity with time-dependent chemical model**: Adjust DUSTY luminosity so N2H+ peak matches observed radius; quiescent-burst-post-burst chemistry with deuterated network  
  > "We adjust the luminosity used as an input for the DUSTY simulation so that the temperature profile leads to a N$_2$H$^+$ peak located at a radius $R_{\rm N_2H^+,peak}$" (4.5)
   - software: DUSTY
   - parameter: X_CO=4e-4, X_N2=1e-4; CO binding energy 1150 K, N2 955 K; burst 100 and 200 yr; post-burst up to 1e5 yr  
  > "fixed at a constant value of 4 $\times$ 10$^{-4}$ and 1 $\times$ 10$^{-4}$, respectively." (4.5)
   - fitting: Manual adjustment of luminosity  
  > "We adjust the luminosity used as an input for the DUSTY simulation so that the temperature profile leads to a N$_2$H$^+$ peak located at a radius $R_{\rm N_2H^+,peak}$ as determined from the observations" (Chemical modeling)
   - validation: Modeled profiles convolved with beam and compared with observed abundance profiles  
  > "We convolve the profiles with a Gaussian of FWHM equal to the FWHM of the beam" (4.5)
7. **Mass accretion rate during burst**: Lacc = G M Mdot / R with Lacc = Lburst  
  > "Assuming $L_{\rm acc}=L_{\rm burst}$, we estimate the mass accretion rate" (5.2)
   - assumption: R=3 Rsun; M=40 MJup  
  > "assumed to be 3 $R_\odot$; (cite: du10a)) and $M_{\rm star}$ is the mass of the central source (assumed to be 40 $M_{\rm Jupiter}$" (5.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- N2H+ and CO isotopologues are anti-correlated in five sources, tracing the CO snow line  
  > "In five of the sources the spatial distribution of emission from N$_2$H$^+$ and CO isotopologues shows a clear anti-correlation." (abstract)
- DCE161 likely traces a transition disk  
  > "The N$_2$H$^+$ and CO isotopologue emission from DCE161, one of the other three sources, is most likely tracing a transition disk at a later evolutionary stage." (abstract)
