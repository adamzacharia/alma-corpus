## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract continuum sources and measure their properties**: astrodendro dendrogram on the self-calibrated 1.05 mm continuum image  
  > "As detailed in Paper , sources were extracted using the astrodendro algorithm" (3.1 ALMA 1.05 mm continuum emission)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: minimum isocontour value 5 sigma_rms (sigma_rms = 0.25 mJy/beam)  
  > "with a minimum isocontour value ($I_{\mathrm{min}}$) of $5\sigma_{\mathrm{rms}}$" (3.1 ALMA 1.05 mm continuum emission)
   - parameter: minimum isocontour spacing 1 sigma_rms  
  > "minimum isocontour spacing ($\Delta I_{\mathrm{min}}$) of $1\sigma_{\mathrm{rms}}$" (3.1 ALMA 1.05 mm continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Two further >5 sigma peaks only extracted with relaxed dendrogram parameters (0.9 sigma, 15 pixels) are not considered firm detections  
  > "We therefore do not consider these firm detections." (3.1 ALMA 1.05 mm continuum emission)
2. **Identify molecular lines in the wide spws**: Match emission peaks to JPL/CDMS rest frequencies at the systemic velocity and confirm with LTE synthetic spectra  
  > "a molecular transition was positively identified when all lines for that species predicted in the synthetic spectrum were present in the observed spectrum" (3.2.1 COMs towards MM1 with ALMA)
   - software: CLASS/WEEDS, Splatalogue
   - parameter: typical hot core model parameters, T > 100 K, used for the identification spectra  
  > "for typical model parameters expected of a hot core (e.g. $T>100$ K)" (3.2.1 COMs towards MM1 with ALMA)
   - assumption: Lines at the source systemic velocity of MM1  
  > "at the source systemic velocity (cite: cyganowski11a)" (3.2.1 COMs towards MM1 with ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Criteria of Herbst & van Dishoeck 2009 for line identification  
  > "We follow the criteria presented by (cite: herbst09) in identifying molecular lines" (3.2.1 COMs towards MM1 with ALMA)
3. **Rotation diagram of CH3OH at the MM1 continuum peak**: LTE rotation diagram, with and without opacity correction, of eleven CH3OH transitions  
  > "We produce a rotation diagram towards the ALMA 1.05 mm continuum peak of MM1" (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH)
   - software: CASA, Python
   - parameter: eleven CH3OH transitions (blended 15(1,0)-14(2,0) and 6(1,2)-5(1,2) excluded)  
  > "using eleven CH$_{3}$OH transitions" (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH)
   - parameter: A and E transitions fit together  
  > "We include both A and E transitions in this analysis since we do not detect enough transitions to to fit them separately." (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH)
   - assumption: LTE and optically thin emission, single temperature  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium and optically thin emission, a single temperature may describe the emission of all observed lines." (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH)
   - fitting: Weighted least-squares with Gaussian-fit errors propagated; opacity-corrected version iteratively solves tau_i and T_rot by minimising chi2  
  > "Errors on the Gaussian fits are propagated through to the rotation diagram, and the best fit is found following a weighted least-squares fit." (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH); "by iteratively solving for the $\tau_{i}$ and $T_{\mathrm{rot}}$ values that produce the best fit, minimising the $\chi^{2}$ value" (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH)
   - validation: Two outflow-tracing lines deviate from the trend and may be optically thick, motivating the opacity correction  
  > "deviate from the general trend of the other nine lines" (4.1.1 Rotation diagram analysis of CH3OH)
4. **Bayesian LTE fit of CH3OH at the continuum peak and pixel by pixel across MM1**: emcee MCMC over WEEDS/CLASS LTE synthetic spectra of six CH3OH lines in the ~278 GHz band, repeated per pixel within the MM1 continuum source size  
  > "we conduct our MCMC analysis on the six remaining CH$_{3}$OH lines in the" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra); "we find the highest likelihood model on a pixel-by-pixel basis across MM1" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - software: emcee, CLASS/WEEDS, WeedsPy_MCMC, toddTools, analysisUtils
   - parameter: 60 walkers, n_burn = 200, n_prod = 500  
  > "We initialise 60 walkers, and allow them to run for $n_{\mathrm{burn}}=200$ and $n_{\mathrm{prod}}=500$ iterations" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - parameter: Uniform priors: N 0.05-3.0e18 cm-2, T 80-300 K, v_cen 56.0-64.0 km/s, dV 2.0-8.0 km/s  
  > "are set to be $(0.05-3.0)\times10^{18}$ cm$^{-2}$ for the column density, $80-300$ K for the temperature, $56.0 - 64.0$ km s$^{-1}$ for the centroid velocity, and $2.0 - 8.0$ km s$^{-1}$ for the velocity width" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - parameter: source size fixed to the geometric mean of the ~278 GHz synthesised beam (~0.5''); ALMA projected diameter 558 m  
  > "we fix the source size to the geometric mean of the" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra); "The projected diameter of the ALMA array was calculated to be 558 metres" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - assumption: LTE with background continuum included; all modelled transitions arise in the same emitting region (outflow-tracing 9(-1,9)-8(0,8) and 6(1,5)-5(1,4), blended lines and the vt=1 line excluded)  
  > "WEEDS solves the radiative transfer equation assuming a state of local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE), and takes into account the background continuum emission." (4.1.2 Synthetic CH3OH spectra); "since an assumption made here is that the emitting region is the same for all modelled transitions" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - assumption: Background continuum from the ALMA 1.05 mm continuum converted to brightness temperature  
  > "with the ALMA 1.05 mm continuum converted to brightness temperature" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - fitting: emcee affine-invariant sampler; Gaussian log-likelihood with observed spectrum rms; uninformative uniform priors; uncertainties from 0.16 and 0.84 quantiles of the posterior  
  > "using the Python package EMCEE (cite: emcee), an affine-invariant Monte Carlo Markov Chain (MCMC) sampler" (4.1.2 Synthetic CH3OH spectra); "The statistical uncertainty on each model parameter is evaluated from the 0.16 and 0.84 quantiles of the posterior" (4.1.2 Synthetic CH3OH spectra); "We assume uniform, uninformative priors" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
   - validation: Results unchanged with a different random seed and n_prod > 3000; consistent with the opacity-corrected rotation diagram; model reproduces the observed spectrum  
  > "These remain unchanged (within the errors) when walkers are initiated with a different random number seed" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra); "These column density and temperature values are remarkably consistent with those derived from the opacity-corrected rotation diagram" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
5. **Column densities and fractional abundances of other species**: Order-of-magnitude LTE fits with only column density free, per species, then ratios to CH3OH and H2 column densities  
  > "by keeping only the column density as a free parameter (cite: csengeri19) in the emcee sampler with weedspy_mcmc" (4.3.1 Estimation of likely column densities and abundances); "Fractional abundances of each species with respect to CH$_{3}$OH and H$_{2}$ are evaluated from the ratio of their respective column densities." (4.3.1 Estimation of likely column densities and abundances)
   - software: emcee, WeedsPy_MCMC, CLASS/WEEDS
   - parameter: T = 162.7 K, v = 59.7 km/s, dV = 6.4 km/s fixed from the CH3OH fit; source size ~0.5''  
  > "The temperature, centroid velocity and velocity width are set to the highest likelihood values evaluated at the ALMA 1.05 mm continuum peak from the CH$_{3}$OH spectra" (4.3.1 Estimation of likely column densities and abundances)
   - assumption: Line emission of MM1 species has the same extent as the continuum  
  > "the extent of the line emission is generally consistent across species and with the extent of the 1.05 mm continuum emission" (4.3.1 Estimation of likely column densities and abundances)

## Qualitative findings

- MM2...MM5 lack maser and outflow emission, so are candidate prestellar cores  
  > "all lacking maser or outflow emission, indicating they may be prestellar cores" (abstract)
- ALMA CH3OH and H2CO trace outflow-cloud interaction regions or cavity walls at lower velocity than 12CO  
  > "tracing lower velocity, outflow–cloud interaction regions or outflow cavity walls" (3.2.2 Extended emission with ALMA)
- NH3(3,3) masers sit in the outer outflow lobes, while 44 GHz CH3OH masers also lie closer to the driving source  
  > "are located in the outer portions of the bipolar outflow lobes" (3.2.3 Ammonia emission with the VLA)
- MM3 and MM5 show no molecular lines in the wide bands; MM2 and MM4 lines are attributed to the MM1 outflow  
  > "MM3 and MM5 appear devoid of molecular line emission in these bands" (3.2.5 MM2...MM5 with ALMA and the VLA)
- Low- and high-mass sources show similar CH3OH column density, temperature and COM abundances in ALMA observations  
  > "the chemistry of low- and high-mass sources appearing similar in this set of interferometric observations" (4.3.2 Comparison to other YSOs observed at high resolution)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Cycle 2 observations designed to search for low-mass cores in the clump gas reservoir  
  > "Our ALMA Cycle 2 observations were designed to search for low-mass cores within the clump-scale gas reservoir" (2.1 Observations (ALMA))
- Why this method: CH3OH used for the temperature and column density because it has the most identified transitions  
  > "as it is the species with the highest number of identified transitions in our tuning" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
- Alternative: MM2...MM5 may be starless condensations, prestellar cores, or mm knots in the MM1 outflow lobes  
  > "these sources may be starless condensations, prestellar cores, millimetre knots in" (3.2.5 MM2...MM5 with ALMA and the VLA)
- Alternative: Unresolved 0.2'' source size tested for the 12C/13C ratio, found to be a poorer fit to the 12CH3OH emission  
  > "To test for dependence on the assumed source size, we reran our WEEDSPY_MCMC analysis at the 1.05 mm continuum peak for the case of unresolved emission" (4.3.3 12C/13C isotope ratio)
- How they decided it was enough: MCMC convergence checked by a different random seed and a longer production run  
  > "These remain unchanged (within the errors) when walkers are initiated with a different random number seed" (4.1.3 Pixel-by-pixel analysis of CH3OH synthetic spectra)
- Limitation (authors): 25 GHz CH3OH maser/thermal result is tentative because of low S/N  
  > "we emphasise that this is a tentative result due to the low S/N of the 25 GHz CH$_3$OH emission towards MM1" (3.2.4 25 GHz methanol emission with the VLA)
- Limitation (authors): Abundances of species other than CH3OH are only order-of-magnitude estimates  
  > "We instead evaluate order of magnitude estimates for the column densities of these species" (4.3.1 Estimation of likely column densities and abundances)
- Limitation (authors): Disc mass may be underestimated because temperature, opacity and optical depth variations are ignored  
  > "This approach does however risk underestimating the disc mass" (4.2.1 Temperature, mass and stability of MM1)
- Limitation (authors): Optical depth effects cannot be excluded as a contributor to the low 12C/13C ratio  
  > "we cannot rule out optical depth effects contributing to this result" (4.3.3 12C/13C isotope ratio)
- Limitation (authors): Column density comparisons across the literature depend on beam size, distance and beam dilution  
  > "comparisons of column densities are subject to a number of varying effects including beam sizes, source distance and beam dilution" (4.3.2 Comparison to other YSOs observed at high resolution)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Quoted MCMC uncertainties (e.g. 0.01e18 cm-2 on N, +0.3/-0.5 K on T) are statistical only and ignore flux calibration, the fixed 0.5'' source size and LTE model systematics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source size is fixed rather than fit, which drives the column density and opacity; the rotation diagram and MCMC taus differ by a factor ~3
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperatures for MM2...MM5 are assumed from clump-scale data, so masses and the prestellar interpretation depend strongly on that choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Virial analysis uses one tracer (DCN) and one source (MM4) with an assumed flat density profile
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abundances rely on a single H2 column from the dust continuum with assumed gas-to-dust ratio and opacity, and text and table disagree slightly (6.6 vs 6.7e23 cm-2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The low 12C/13C ratio rests on a single CH3OH line pair at modest opacity
