## Analysis recipe

1. **Prepare HST stellar image and subtract lens**: Coadd WFC3 F160W frames with MultiDrizzle, astrometry tied to SDSS DR12, galfit two-Sérsic lens subtraction  
  > "then coadded them by ourselves using the iraf MultiDrizzle package. The final pixel scale was set to 0.064$''$" (2.2 HST Data); "We used 46 SDSS sources located in the image to derive the astrometric fit using iraf." (2.2 HST Data)
   - software: iraf, MultiDrizzle, galfit
   - parameter: pixel scale 0.064''  
  > "The final pixel scale was set to 0.064$''$ to improve the resolution and quality of the image." (2.2 HST Data)
   - fitting: two Sérsic profiles fitted simultaneously with galfit  
  > "According to (cite: negr14), two Sérsic profiles were simultaneously fitted to the foreground lens galaxy to cleanly subtract it." (2.2 HST Data)
   - validation: SDSS lens position matches ALMA continuum within 0.015''; profiles consistent with Negrello et al.  
  > "The profiles are consistent with those obtained by (cite: negr14)." (2.2 HST Data)
2. **Identify molecular clumps**: Peaks ≥3.5σ in CO(5–4) moment-0 map (pixels >1.5σ in cube) plus clumpfind on cube; keep clumps found by both  
  > "We first searched for the peak positions with $\ge$3.5$\sigma$ in the velocity-integrated intensity map created by including pixels above 1.5$\sigma$ in the line cube." (3.1 Clump Identification); "We identified 14 molecular clumps along the arcs detected by both methods" (3.1 Clump Identification)
   - software: clumpfind
   - parameter: spectral resolution 21 km/s  
  > "line cube (spectral resolution of 21 km s$^{-1}$)" (3.1 Clump Identification)
   - parameter: exclude 4 clumps with magnification >100 (ID 4,5,7,8)  
  > "we exclude these clumps in the discussion of star formation properties." (3.1 Clump Identification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Clump photometry of CO and dust**: Photometry in regions above 2σ in CO(5–4) integrated map; same regions on 1.0 mm continuum  
  > "We used regions above 2$\sigma$ in the CO(5–4) velocity-integrated map for conducting photometry and estimating the physical quantities of each clump." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: filling factor of unity; differential magnification negligible  
  > "we assume a filling factor of unity in this study." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive molecular gas mass**: L'CO from Solomon & Vanden Bout formula, CO(5–4)/CO(1–0)=0.3, M_H2 = alpha_CO L'CO(1-0)  
  > "The molecular gas mass is derived from $M_{\rm H_2} = \alpha_{\rm CO} L'_{\rm CO(1-0)}$" (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "We adopted a conversion factor of $\alpha_{\rm CO} = 0.8$ $M_{\odot}$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$, the standard value for ULIRGs (cite: down98)." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: CO line ratio 0.3  
  > "We used a CO line ratio of CO(5–4)/CO(1–0) $= 0.3$ (cite: alma15b)." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive dust mass, FIR luminosity and SFR**: Modified blackbody from 1.0 mm flux; SFR = 1.72e-10 L_FIR  
  > "The SFR was derived from SFR $= 1.72 \times 10^{-10} L_{\rm FIR}$ (cite: kenn98a)." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: kappa_d(125 um)=2.64 m^2/kg, beta = 1.5  
  > "adopt $\kappa_d(125\ \mu m) = 2.64 \pm 0.29$ m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ (cite: dunn03) and $\beta = 1.5$." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: T_dust = 39.3 K for every clump  
  > "We adopted the dust temperature for each clump." (3.2 Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compare clumps in Sigma_H2–Sigma_SFR plane**: Plot surface densities (no magnification correction needed) against local/high-z relations  
  > "the magnification correction is not necessary when discussing the surface density of molecular gas and SFR, because the gravitational lensing conserves the surface brightness for the identified clumps." (4.1 Star-formation Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested Galactic alpha_CO = 4.3 alternative  
  > "If we adopt the Galactic conversion factor ($\alpha_{\rm CO} = 4.3 M_{\odot}$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$; (cite: bola13)), these clumps are located on the sequence of normal star-forming galaxies." (4.1 Star-formation Properties)
7. **Source-plane positions and kinematics**: Map clumps and HST stellar points to source plane with glafic and the tamu15 lens model; inspect mean velocities  
  > "By using our lens model (cite: tamu15) and the glafic software, we determined the positions of molecular clumps on the source plane" (4.2 The Nature of SDP.81)
   - software: glafic
   - assumption: isothermal ellipsoid with 400-pc core  
  > "The lensed images are explained by the best-fit mass model with a mass profile of an isothermal ellipsoid with a 400-pc core." (Lens Model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Clump surface densities exceed local spirals by >3 orders of magnitude and lie on the burst sequence  
  > "The surface density of molecular gas ($\Sigma_{\rm H_2}$) and star-formation rate ($\Sigma_{\rm SFR}$) of the clumps are more than three orders of magnitude higher than those found in local spiral galaxies." (abstract)
- Sigma_SFR/Sigma_H2 ratios consistent with Milky Way clumps  
  > "the derived $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}/\Sigma_{\rm H_2}$ ratios are consistent with the values found in the Milky Way clumps" (4.1 Star-formation Properties)
- Some clumps appear unresolved  
  > "We found that some of the clumps do not appear to be extended in the transverse direction, and it is likely that they are unresolved." (3.1 Clump Identification)
- Gas surface density appears higher in centre of disk  
  > "The molecular gas surface density appears to be higher in the central part of the molecular/dust" (4.2 The Nature of SDP.81)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Lensing plus long-baseline ALMA gives ~200 pc resolution  
  > "offering an excellent opportunity to study the spatially-resolved properties of an SMG." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Galactic alpha_CO = 4.3 moves clumps to normal sequence  
  > "these clumps are located on the sequence of normal star-forming galaxies." (4.1 Star-formation Properties)
- Alternative: Merger scenario vs rotating disk  
  > "Alternatively, SDP.81 may be a merging system, where dusty starbursts occur in the interface between the two colliding galaxies while the tidal features are widely extended." (4.2 The Nature of SDP.81)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Multiple images hard to identify due to low S/N  
  > "because of the low SN, it may be difficult to identify all of the multiples in the CO(5–4) image." (3.1 Clump Identification)
- Limitation (authors): Filling factor may be below unity  
  > "Note that if the filling factor is less than unity, the data points in Figure [1](#fig:sk) will shift to the upper-right direction." (4.1 Star-formation Properties)
- Limitation (authors): CO(5–4) may not trace whole disk  
  > "The CO(5–4) line we used in this study has a relatively high critical density ($10^{5}$ cm$^{-3}$) and it may not trace the gas of the entire disk." (4.2 The Nature of SDP.81)
- Limitation (authors): Spatial extent of stellar components not considered  
  > "the spatial extent of each component is not taken into account." (4.2 The Nature of SDP.81)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single integrated T_dust applied to all clumps ignores temperature gradients
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed CO(5–4)/CO(1–0)=0.3 and alpha_CO dominate surface density systematics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Clump detection at low S/N with low thresholds may include spurious clumps
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Velocity gradient based on few clumps without kinematic modelling
