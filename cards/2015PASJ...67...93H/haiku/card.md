## Analysis recipe

1. **identify CO(5–4) molecular clumps**: Search for peaks at ≥3.5σ in the velocity-integrated map made from pixels above 1.5σ in the line cube, then run clumpfind on the line cube; 14 clumps retained along the arcs  
  > "We first searched for the peak positions with $\ge$3.5$\sigma$ in the velocity-integrated intensity map created by including pixels above 1.5$\sigma$ in the line cube." (Molecular Clumps, Clump Identification)
   - software: clumpfind
   - parameter: Line cube spectral resolution 21 km/s  
  > "We identified molecular clumps by using the line cube (spectral resolution of 21 km s$^{-1}$)" (Molecular Clumps, Clump Identification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Multiple images suggested by position, source-plane position and mean velocity; clumps 1 and 13 and clumps 4 and 5 flagged as possible counter images  
  > "we suggest that clumps 1 and 13 and clumps 4 and 5 are possibly counter images of the same source." (Molecular Clumps, Clump Identification)
2. **derive source-plane positions of clumps and stellar components**: Map image-plane positions to the source plane with the lens model of tamu15 computed by glafic  
  > "This lens model is used to compute the positions on the source plane" (Lens Model)
   - software: glafic
   - parameter: Isothermal ellipsoid mass profile with a 400-pc core  
  > "The lensed images are explained by the best-fit mass model with a mass profile of an isothermal ellipsoid with a 400-pc core." (Lens Model)
   - assumption: Lens model includes the stellar contribution from the HST image, so stellar extent is treated as real  
  > "Since the lensing model of (cite: tamu15) includes the contribution from the stellar emission detected in the *HST* image, we argue that the extension of the stellar emission is real" (Discussion, The Nature of SDP.81)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Core radius agrees with stellar light in HST image; mass centroid consistent with ALMA continuum emission  
  > "the position of the predicted mass centroid is consistent with that of the emission detected in the ALMA continuum images." (Lens Model)
3. **measure molecular gas and SFR surface densities and compare with the star-formation law**: Photometry in regions above 2σ of the CO(5–4) velocity-integrated map; L'CO converted to H2 mass with alpha_CO; L_FIR from dust modelling of the 1.0 mm image; SFR from L_FIR; surface densities per clump; magnification correction not applied to surface densities  
  > "We used regions above 2$\sigma$ in the CO(5–4) velocity-integrated map for conducting photometry and estimating the physical quantities of each clump." (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - parameter: alpha_CO = 0.8 M_sun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "We adopted a conversion factor of $\alpha_{\rm CO} = 0.8$ $M_{\odot}$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$" (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - parameter: CO(5–4)/CO(1–0) = 0.3  
  > "We used a CO line ratio of CO(5–4)/CO(1–0) $= 0.3$ (cite: alma15b)." (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - parameter: kappa_d(125 um) = 2.64 ± 0.29 m^2 kg^-1, beta = 1.5  
  > "adopt $\kappa_d(125\ \mu m) = 2.64 \pm 0.29$ m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ (cite: dunn03) and $\beta = 1.5$" (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: Filling factor of unity for molecular clouds and star-forming regions smaller than the beam  
  > "we assume a filling factor of unity in this study" (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: No significant difference in CO and dust distribution within each clump, so differential magnification is negligible  
  > "We assume that there is no significant difference in the distribution of CO and dust emission within the measured region for each clump" (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - assumption: Dust temperature adopted per clump; integrated T_dust = 39.3 K from negr14  
  > "We adopted the dust temperature for each clump." (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - fitting: Dust SED modelled with a modified blackbody, T_dust and beta as above; no fitting procedure stated  
  > "We assume that the absorption coefficient varies as $\kappa_d \propto \nu^{\beta}$" (Molecular Clumps, Surface densities of molecular gas and SFR)
   - validation: Derived Sigma_SFR/Sigma_H2 ratios compared with Milky Way clumps  
  > "the derived $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}/\Sigma_{\rm H_2}$ ratios are consistent with the values found in the Milky Way clumps" (Discussion, Star-formation Properties)
4. **compare clump kinematics and gas distribution with source-plane geometry**: Color-code image-plane mean velocities and surface densities on the source plane; compare clump, dust and stellar extents and offsets  
  > "The mean velocity of each molecular clump measured in the image plane is shown in color in Figure [2](#fig:source-plane) (top)." (Discussion, The Nature of SDP.81)
   - assumption: Spatial extent of each stellar component not taken into account; only marked points were mapped  
  > "the spatial extent of each component is not taken into account." (Discussion, The Nature of SDP.81)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gas surface density uncertainty acknowledged as large  
  > "although the uncertainty is large." (Discussion, The Nature of SDP.81)

## Qualitative findings

- Molecular clumps follow the burst sequence and the star-formation law of local starburst galaxies  
  > "suggesting that $z \sim 3$ molecular clumps follow the star-formation law derived for local starburst galaxies." (abstract)
- Molecular and dust clumps are confined within ~2 kpc while stellar emission extends ~6 kpc and is offset toward the west  
  > "The molecular and dust clumps are confined within a $\sim$2 kpc region, while the spatial extent of the stellar components is as large as $\sim$6 kpc and offset toward the west." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Publicly available ALMA Science Verification calibrated data with high angular resolution enable spatially resolved study of an SMG  
  > "offering an excellent opportunity to study the spatially-resolved properties of an SMG." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Magnification does not affect surface densities because lensing conserves surface brightness, so surface densities need no magnification correction  
  > "We reiterate that the magnification correction is not necessary when discussing the surface density of molecular gas and SFR, because the gravitational lensing conserves the surface brightness for the identified clumps." (Discussion, Star-formation Properties)
- Alternative: Merging system scenario as an alternative to a rotating disk  
  > "Alternatively, SDP.81 can be explained by a merging system, where dusty starbursts occur in the region where the two galaxies collide" (abstract)
- Alternative: Galactic alpha_CO = 4.3 would place clumps on the normal star-forming sequence; not adopted for the main analysis  
  > "If we adopt the Galactic conversion factor ($\alpha_{\rm CO} = 4.3 M_{\odot}$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$; (cite: bola13)), these clumps are located on the sequence of normal star-forming galaxies." (Discussion, Star-formation Properties)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Low S/N may make it difficult to identify all multiple images in the CO(5–4) image  
  > "However, because of the low SN, it may be difficult to identify all of the multiples in the CO(5–4) image." (Molecular Clumps, Clump Identification)
- Limitation (authors): CO(5–4) with high critical density may not trace the entire disk gas  
  > "The CO(5–4) line we used in this study has a relatively high critical density ($10^{5}$ cm$^{-3}$) and it may not trace the gas of the entire disk." (Discussion, The Nature of SDP.81)
- Limitation (authors): Many clumps are unresolved, so sizes cannot be measured  
  > "We found that some of the clumps do not appear to be extended in the transverse direction, and it is likely that they are unresolved." (Molecular Clumps, Clump Identification)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Surface densities depend on the assumed filling factor of unity; the authors note the values would shift if it is below unity.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Clump-property fits rest on a single adopted alpha_CO=0.8 and dust parameters, with no stated uncertainty propagation to the surface densities.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Clumpfind settings and imaging parameters are not reported, so the clump catalogue is hard to reproduce exactly.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Lens-model dependence: source-plane positions rely entirely on the external tamu15 model.
