## Analysis recipe

1. **Derive CO column densities and abundance ratio at clump scales**: Extract JCMT 12CO, 13CO, and C18O J=3-2 spectra towards G33.133-mm3 and apply LTE formulae to calculate excitation temperature, optical depths, column densities, and the 13CO/C18O abundance ratio  
  > "By assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE), and following the typical formulae (see for instance (cite: areal18)) we calculate the $^{13}$CO and C$^{18}$O column densities (N$^{13}$ and N$^{18}$) at the position of G33.133-mm3 to obtain the $^{13}$CO/C$^{18}$O abundance ratio" (The $^{13}$CO/C$^{18}$O abundance ratio)
   - parameter: spatial scale of about 0.3 pc

## Qualitative findings

- Methanol (CH3OH), methyl formate (CH3OCHO), acetonitrile (CH3CN), propyne (CH3CCH), and the cyanide radical (CN.) are detected in G33.133-mm3.  
  > "From the ALMA data set, molecules such as methanol (CH$_{3}$OH), methyl formate (CH$_{3}$OCHO), and acetonitrile (CH$_{3}$CN) were detected." (Complex molecules in G33.133-mm3); "Additionally, in the spectrum of CH$_{3}$CN, emission of propyne (CH$_{3}$CCH) is also observed, while in the CH$_{3}$OCHO spectrum, it is also observed emission from the nitrile or cyanide radical (CN$^{.}$)." (Complex molecules in G33.133-mm3)
- The 13CO/C18O ratio of 3.8 could be mapping the external gaseous layers of the dense clump where UV radiation is impinging.  
  > "This ratio could be mapping the external gaseous layers of the dense clump in which G33.133-mm3 and others cores are embedded, where the ultraviolet radiation, responsible of the PDRs, is impinging." (The $^{13}$CO/C$^{18}$O abundance ratio)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The location of the HMCs in an extended PDR makes it interesting to study the 13CO/C18O abundance ratio to probe the relation between UV radiation and molecular gas at clump scales, complemented by ALMA at core scales.  
  > "Given to the location of the HMCs, it is interesting to study the $^{13}$CO/C$^{18}$O abundance ratio, as done for instance by (cite: areal18) in many sources, because it gives us information about the relation between the UV radiation and the molecular gas at clump spatial scales." (Presentation of the studied HMC)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No uncertainties are reported for any of the derived LTE parameters in Table 1.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line identifications from the ALMA data are based purely on visual frequency matching with the NIST database without synthetic spectrum modeling to check for line blending in a line-rich HMC.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Exact target coordinates and spectral extraction apertures are omitted, relying entirely on companion paper Paron et al. (2021).
