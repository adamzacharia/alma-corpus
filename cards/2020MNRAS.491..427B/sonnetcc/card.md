## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image continuum and cube**: Followed Kong et al. 2017 calibration, then CASA clean (Hogbom, Briggs) and self-calibration applied to continuum and cube  
  > "After following the same calibration procedure applied by (cite: kon17), we used the standard Hogboom algorithm available in CASA to clean the image with Briggs weighting." (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 256x256 pixels, 0.05 arcsec cell  
  > "256$\times$256 pixels map with $0.05\ensuremath{''}$ cell sizes" (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - parameter: 1920 channels of 980 kHz (1.26 km/s)  
  > "The ALMA data cube is formed by 1920 channels, with a frequency bandwidth of $980$ KHz per channel" (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Self-calibration applied to continuum and cube  
  > "We obtained our final continuum band image after performing self-calibration, which was also applied to the ALMA data cube." (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract core and tail spectra and identify lines**: Spectra integrated over the spatial extent of core and tail; line identification with spectroscopic databases; line areas/FWHM/velocity tabulated  
  > "we show the spectra of the core and of the tail, integrated over the spatial distribution of each component." (3.1 Continuum and line emission); "The spectral line analyses was carried out with the CLASS/GILDAS" (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - software: CLASS/GILDAS, CASSIS, Splatalogue, CDMS, JPL
   - parameter: Spectrum rms 44 mK (core), 36 mK (tail)  
  > "The rms of the spectrum in the core and in the tail are 44 and 36 mK, respectively." (3.1 Continuum and line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Unidentified line at 232.41721 GHz assigned to CH3CH2CN because other lines of that molecule were detected; contamination not excluded  
  > "we attributed it to CH$_{3}$CH$_{2}$CN, because we have detected other lines of this molecule." (3.1 Continuum and line emission)
3. **LTE rotation diagram for CH3CHO**: Rotation diagram from five CH3CHO lines under LTE  
  > "By means of the rotational diagram analysis (e.g. (cite: gol1999)), we estimate the CH$_3$CHO column density ($N$) and excitation temperature ($T_{exc}$)" (3.2.2 CH3CHO)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: Beam dilution correction for a 2 arcsec source size  
  > "obtained after applying a beam dilution correction for a source size of" (3.2.2 CH3CHO)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin emission, source fills the beam  
  > "we carried out radiative analysis under LTE, assuming that the emission is optically thin and considering that the source fills the beam." (3.2.2 CH3CHO)
   - fitting: Linear least-squares regression of ln(Nu/gu) vs Eu  
  > "From the linear least square regression" (3.2.2 CH3CHO)
   - validation: Synthetic spectra of the CH3CHO lines compared with observations  
  > "The synthetic spectra of the observed CH$_3$CHO lines are shown in Fig." (3.2.2 CH3CHO)
4. **Non-LTE RADEX/MCMC for single-line molecules (OCS, 13CS, methanol)**: RADEX non-LTE with MCMC minimising chi-square for column density and kinetic temperature  
  > "we ran the RADEX non-LTE code using the Markov Chain Monte Carlo algorithm to estimate the physical conditions of the region." (3.2.1 OCS and 13CS)
   - software: RADEX, emcee
   - parameter: n(H2)=4.6e6 cm-3 adopted from IRDC-C9A  
  > "we use the value of $n$(H$_2$)=4.6 $\times$ 10$^6$ cm$^{-3}$ obtained by (cite: kon17) for IRDC-C9A" (2.2 Modelling Methods)
   - parameter: Source size 2 arcsec (convolved) for the core OCS fit  
  > "for a convoluted source size of 2 $arcsec$" (3.2.1 OCS and 13CS)
   - assumption: Free parameters can include column density, kinetic temperature, FWHM, line position, source angular size and n(H2)  
  > "different free parameters, such as the column density, kinetic temperature, full width at half maximum (FWHM), line position, source angular size and volume density $n$(H$_2$)" (2.2 Modelling Methods)
   - fitting: MCMC chains searching for minimum chi-square  
  > "The best solution is calculated and achieved through stochastic processes to find the minimum value of the $\chi^2$ function." (2.2 Modelling Methods)
   - validation: Reduced chi-square values and corner diagrams, synthetic vs observed lines  
  > "In order to show the convergence of the free parameters column density and kinetic temperature, considering the applied RADEX/MCMC sampling" (3.2.1 OCS and 13CS)
5. **LTE rotation diagram and LTE/MCMC for 13CH3CN k-ladder**: Population/rotation diagram and an LTE/MCMC model because no collisional coefficients exist  
  > "Due to the absence of collisional coefficients for $^{13}$CH$_3$CN, we used the population diagram method" (3.2.3 The nitriles)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: LTE/MCMC computed for temperatures up to 500 K  
  > "The LTE/MCMC computations of $^{13}$CH$_3$CN were carried out for temperatures up to 500 K." (3.2.3 The nitriles)
   - fitting: Straight-line rotation-diagram fit, plus LTE/MCMC with Nup and Texc as free parameters  
  > "we also computed a LTE model using the MCMC algorithm with the column density and excitation temperature as free parameters" (3.2.3 The nitriles)
   - validation: LTE spectral model overplotted on the k-ladder; MCMC agrees with rotation diagram  
  > "In agreement with the rotational diagram, we obtained a similar solution with" (The nitriles CH3CN and CH3CH2CN)
6. **Derive H2 column density from continuum and molecular abundances**: N(H2) from continuum flux density via Motte et al. equation, then abundance = N/N(H2)  
  > "we obtain their abundances estimating the column density of H$_{2}$ from the continuum flux density." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - parameter: Beam size 1.5 arcsec  
  > "taking into account the beam size of 1.5 arcsec" (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - assumption: Dust opacity 0.01 cm2/g and dust temperature 15 K  
  > "using the dust opacity of 0.01 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$, typical for pre-stellar cores, and a dust temperature of 15 K" (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Abundances compared with L134 and TMC-1  
  > "shows compatible values for CH$_3$CHO and $^{13}$CS, considering C/$^{13}$C = 45" (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
7. **Estimate core and tail masses from continuum**: Grey-body mass estimate from the 231 GHz continuum  
  > "We can estimate the mass of core and tail using the grey body equation" (Core and tail characteristics)
   - assumption: Absorption coefficient 0.01 cm2/g at 230 GHz, T = 15 K, distance 5 kpc  
  > "Again, we use an absorption coefficient at 230 GHz of $0.01$cm$^2 g^{-1}$ (cite: pre93) and a temperature of 15 K." (Core and tail characteristics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Hot-gas (100 K) alternative gives only 3 Msun, taken as evidence that dust is cold  
  > "If we suppose the continuum is a emitted by the hot gas, at 100 K, the mass should be only 3M~$\odot$~." (Core and tail characteristics)
8. **Nautilus chemical modelling for chemical age**: Zero-dimensional three-phase gas-grain Nautilus model compared to measured abundances of CS, OCS, CH3CHO, CH3OCHO  
  > "The simulations are zero-dimensional, i.e., total density, gas temperature, and other physical condition are uniform within the considered cloud and throughout the simulation time." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - software: Nautilus, KIDA
   - parameter: n = 4.6e4 cm-3, T = 15 K gas and grain  
  > "We have assumed density of 4.6 $\times$10$^4$ cm$^{-3}$ and temperature of 15K for gas and grain." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - parameter: Cosmic ionization rate 1.3e-17 s-1; Av = 10 mag  
  > "We assumed a standard cosmic ionization rate of 1.3 $\times$ 10$^{-17}$ s$^{-1}$." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances); "We used a typical value for visual extinction of 10 mag." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - assumption: 13C isotopologues not in the code; derived using C/13C ~ 45  
  > "The code does not consider the isotopotologues $^{13}$CS and $^{13}$CH$_{3}$CN in the simulations." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Distance-of-disagreement merit function comparing predicted and measured abundances over time  
  > "We have used the distance of disagreement method (cite: wak06), a merit function that check for each time the predict abundances relative to the measured abundances." (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)

## Qualitative findings

- Hot gas in the core suggests a protostar, absent in the tail  
  > "The existence of hot gas in the core of IRDC-C9 Main suggests the presence of a protostar, which is not present in the tail." (abstract)
- Core and tail lines differ by about 1 km/s in velocity

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA observations of IRDCs offer high sensitivity and resolution to detect weak lines in cold sources  
  > "In the present work, we use the ALMA observations of a sample of infrared dark clouds obtained by (cite: kon17) to search for new molecular line emission and study the chemistry in starless cold cores." (Introduction); "The high sensitivity of ALMA make this instrument ideal to detect such weak cold sources at high resolution." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Rotation diagram for molecules with at least three lines; RADEX non-LTE MCMC otherwise; population diagram for 13CH3CN because no collisional coefficients exist  
  > "For the molecules that have at least three detected lines, we use the rotation diagram as usual (e.g. (cite: gol1999))." (2.2 Modelling Methods); "Due to the absence of collisional coefficients for $^{13}$CH$_3$CN, we used the population diagram method" (3.2.3 The nitriles)
- Alternative: The fifth bright line near 232.4 GHz could be dimethyl ether, another propionitrile line or 33SO2; attributed to CH3CH2CN  
  > "it could be CH$_{3}$OCH$_{3}$ (dimethyl ether), other propionitrile line or even $^{33}$SO$_{2}$." (3.1 Continuum and line emission)
- Alternative: Tail-only line at 230.97 GHz could be SO2 or ethanol; ethanol judged more reasonable by analogy with Sgr B2  
  > "Based on the spectrum of Sgr B2 (cite: num98), it is more reasonable to attribute this line to the molecule C$_{2}$H$_{5}$OH, but this remains as an open question." (Core and tail characteristics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): 13CH3CN temperature is only an estimate because few lines are available  
  > "we can only estimate the $^{13}$CH$_3$CN temperature" (3.2.3 The nitriles)
- Limitation (authors): Single-line OCS and 13CS in the tail give only rough column density and temperature  
  > "they can only provide a rough estimation of column density and temperature, because we only count with a single line for each of them." (3.3 Molecules in the tail)
- Limitation (authors): Possible line contamination in the core spectrum  
  > "In this way, we can not rule out the possibility of contamination." (3.1 Continuum and line emission)
- Limitation (authors): H2 column density derived for the cold region only, so 13CH3CN abundance not estimated  
  > "We have not estimated the abundance of $^{13}$CH$_3$CN" (Discussion: Molecular Abundances)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Nautilus density (4.6e4 cm-3) differs from the RADEX density (4.6e6 cm-3) with no justification of the mismatch.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A single-temperature (15 K) dust assumption and a fixed kappa give the mass and N(H2); no uncertainties are given for masses or abundances.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Several column densities rest on a single line with an assumed 2 arcsec source size; RADEX results are strongly degenerate.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text and abstract quote slightly different temperatures (25 K vs 27 +/- 5 K for CH3CHO; 450 vs 460 K for 13CH3CN) and velocity values in the text differ from the line table.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 450 K 13CH3CN result rests on marginal k-ladder detections (many lines at or below 0.15 K with ~5 sigma) and the Eu of 896.78 K for one CH3CH2CN line looks like a typo.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Hot (450 K) gas interpreted as protostar without continuum or line evidence of a protostar beyond temperature; no outflow tracer analysed in this paper.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 'tail' being a separate source or part of the core is not resolved.
