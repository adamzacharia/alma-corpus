## Analysis recipe

1. **Nobeyama line parameters and deuterium fractions**: Hyperfine fitting of N2D+ and N2H+; LTE column densities; ratios N(N2D+)/N(N2H+), N(DNC)/N(HN13C)  
  > "For N$_2$D$^+$ and N$_2$H$^+$, we show the results of hyperfine fitting." (3.1 Results from Nobeyama observations)
   - software: NewStar, NoStar
   - parameter: Tex = 8 K for other molecules; 12C/13C = 60  
  > "that for the other molecules was assumed to be 8 K, which is half the typical dust temperature for PGCCs (16 K)" (3.1 Results from Nobeyama observations)
   - assumption: Tkin equal to dust temperature 16 K; Tex/Tkin = 50%  
  > "The ratio of the excitation temperature to the gas kinetic temperature (assumed to be equal to the dust temperature) for the $J$ = 1$-$0 molecular lines was assumed to be 50%" (3.1 Results from Nobeyama observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **ACA continuum flux and dust mass**: 2D Gaussian fit with imfit on primary-beam-corrected continuum; compare with SCUBA-2 850 um to get recovered fraction  
  > "measured by fitting a two-dimensional Gaussian to the map using the task “imfit” in CASA" (3.2 Results from the ACA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: beta = 1.8 for flux scaling  
  > "If we assume a spectral index of $\beta$ = 1.8 (cite: 2014A&A...566A..45L), 41% of the dust emission is recovered by the ACA observations." (3.2 Results from the ACA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **ACA line column densities and abundances**: LTE optically thin column densities with Tex from RADEX; HCO+ excluded due to self-absorption  
  > "we obtain $T_{ex}$ = 15 K (almost fully thermalized) and assume this value for all molecular lines observed with the ACA." (4.2 G211)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: Tkin = 16 K, Tex = 15 K  
  > "We assume the gas kinetic temperature to be 16 K from the dust temperature of the associated Planck cold clump." (4.2 G211)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: RADEX test models of DCO+  
  > "To obtain estimates of the excitation temperature, we ran the RADEX software" (4.2 G211)
4. **Virial analysis**: Virial mass from deconvolved HWHM radius and DCO+ FWHM linewidth; alpha_vir = Mvir/Mdust  
  > "The virial parameter $\alpha_{vir}$ is defined as $\alpha_{vir} = M_{vir}/M_{dust}$." (4.3 Fragmentation, Virial Mass, and Motion)
   - assumption: Constant density sphere  
  > "assuming the constant density sphere (cite: 1988ApJ...333..821M)" (4.3 Fragmentation, Virial Mass, and Motion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Kinematics: moment maps, PV diagram, infall signature, outflow**: Moment-1 maps, PV along P.A. 51°, Gaussian fit of DCO+ absorption in G211D, high-velocity CO lobe maps  
  > "Figure 10 shows the position-velocity diagram along the strip line passing through the two N$_2$D$^+$ peaks with a P.A. angle of 51$\degree$." (4.1 G210)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: Gaussian fitting of absorption and core velocity  
  > "The absorption feature ($v_{LSR}$ = 3.695$\pm$0.035 km s$^{-1}$ through Gaussian fitting)" (4.3 Fragmentation, Virial Mass, and Motion)
   - validation: Compared with HCO+, N2D+, DNC dip velocities and Nobeyama CO(1-0) to check missing flux  
  > "It is possible that the inverse P Cygni profile cannot be explained in terms of missing flux, but represents the infall motion." (4.3 Fragmentation, Virial Mass, and Motion)

## Qualitative findings

- G210 shows two symmetric N2D+ peaks about a single dust continuum peak; G211 is clumpy with five continuum peaks and chemical differences among sub-cores.  
  > "G210 exhibits only a single peak while G211 exhibits five emission peaks in the fields of view." (3.2 Results from the ACA observations)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Selected two cores bright in N2D+ in Orion, a nearby GMC, with minimal distance uncertainty  
  > "To minimize the distance uncertainty, we selected the Orion star-forming region for this study." (2.1 Source Selection)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ACA missing flux; limited resolution cannot resolve protoplanetary disk; velocity difference near spectral resolution  
  > "With our spatial resolution, however, it is impossible to see the protoplanetary disk ($\sim$ 100 AU)." (4.1 G210); "Occasionally, we see absorption due to missing flux for extended emission." (4.3 Fragmentation, Virial Mass, and Motion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-dish N2D+ cores selected for brightness; only two targets, no statistics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Infall evidence rests on a single absorption dip with missing-flux confusion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust mass assumptions undocumented
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Short-spacing ACA-only data with no total power
