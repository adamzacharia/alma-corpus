## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and CO, derive geometry**: Clean images; stellar position, inclination, PA and v_src from channel and first-moment maps with Keplerian overlay  
  > "We derive the stellar position, inclination, position angle and source velocity from the channel maps and first moment map" (2.2 Continuum and line maps)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Stellar masses from literature  
  > "For the stellar mass, we use values from the literature" (2.2 Continuum and line maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent within 5 deg with literature  
  > "The derived parameters are consistent within 5$^{\circ}$ with values from the literature" (2.2 Continuum and line maps)
2. **Fit dust structure to SED and continuum visibilities**: DALI model manually tuned to SED, visibility amplitude and image cuts; vary delta_dustcav 1e-2 to 1e-6  
  > "the $\delta_{\rm dustcav}$ value (drop of dust density inside the cavity) is varied with values between 10$^{-2}$ and 10$^{-6}$" (Results)
   - software: DALI
   - parameter: gamma = 1, GDR = 100, turbulent width 0.2 km/s, PAH 0.1%  
  > "The turbulent width is set to 0.2 km s$^{-1}$." (Physical model)
   - assumption: Axisymmetric model; asymmetries ignored  
  > "thus any azimuthal asymmetries are ignored" (Modeling)
   - fitting: Manual fitting by eye, no chi2 or MCMC  
  > "We do not use a $\chi^2$ or Markov-Chain-Monte-Carlo (MCMC) method, as the computational time of the models is too long" (Model fitting approach)
   - validation: Comparison with SED, visibility null, image cuts  
  > "Reasonable fits are found for the continuum visibilities and SED within the constraints of our physical model for all targets." (Results)
3. **Constrain gas density drop with 12CO**: Vary delta_gas from 1e0 to 1e-4 in steps of 10 holding other parameters fixed; compare spectra, intensity cuts, visibilities  
  > "we present the same physical model with $\delta_{\rm gas}$ varying from 10$^{0}$ to 10$^{-4}$ in steps of 10" (Results)
   - software: DALI
   - assumption: GDR = 100 in outer disk  
  > "There was no need to change the gas to dust ratio of 100 in any of the cases in the outer disk." (Results)
   - fitting: Manual  
  > "In the fitting procedure, we use an approach of manual fitting by eye, varying the parameters in a logical order, converging to a model that fits the data" (Method, Model fitting approach)
   - validation: Tested alternative 30 AU gas cavity for HD135344B  
  > "It turns out that there is no significant difference in the outcome for 30 AU or 40 AU gas cavity radius." (Results of individual targets)

## Qualitative findings

- All disks show gas inside the dust cavity; gas drops by 1-2 orders of magnitude while dust drops by at least 3  
  > "All disks show clear evidence for gas inside the dust cavity." (Conclusions)
