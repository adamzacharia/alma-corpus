## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine ALMA Cycle 1+2 and measure 1100 μm flux**: Combined visibilities, imaged; total flux densities from images  
  > "ASXDF1100.053.1 and 231.1 were detected with $S_{\rm peak}/N=$ 27 and 29, respectively" (3.1)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent within 1σ with AzTEC/ASTE flux  
  > "their ALMA 1100-$\mu$m flux densities are consistent (within 1$\sigma$) with those measured by AzTEC/ASTE" (3.1)
2. **Radio/submm photometric redshifts**: Fit SED templates (random SEDs weighted by dust temperature distribution, bootstrapped) to ALMA 1100, SCUBA-2 850, VLA 6/1.4 GHz fluxes, with SPIRE 3σ upper limits as survival functions  
  > "We estimate the radio/submm photometric redshifts of ASXDF1100.053.1 and ASXDF1100.231.1 by fitting dust SED templates to ALMA 1100-$\mu$m, SCUBA-2 850-$\mu$m and (J)VLA 6- or 1.4-GHz flux densities." (4.1 Method)
   - parameter: z_max=10; 68% confidence intervals; off-diagonal covariance zero  
  > "The asymmetric error bars ($z_{-}$,$z_{+}$) correspond to 68% confidence levels such that $\int^{z_{+}}_{z_{-}} P(z)$ d$z=0.68$" (4.1 Method)
   - assumption: Gaussian flux errors; SED temperatures 19–52 K  
  > "We assume that the flux density errors follow Gaussian distributions." (4.1 Method)
   - fitting: Multivariate Gaussian likelihood times survival functions, summed over SEDs  
  > "The final redshift probability distribution, $P(z)$, of any galaxy is the sum of the individual probabilities from the SEDs" (4.1 Method)
   - validation: Benchmarks with 7 spectroscopic SMGs and 46 ALESS SMGs; median Δz=-0.01, dispersion 0.27; average-SED cross-check; no-850 μm test  
  > "Its median and 1$\sigma$ dispersion are $-$0.01 and 0.27, respectively." (4.3)
3. **Assess CMB effects on photo-z**: Toy models of CMB effects on submm dust emission and radio synchrotron suppression with B=100 and 300 μG  
  > "we investigate how the effect of the CMB on radio emission contributes to radio/mm photometric redshift estimates where $B=100$ and 300 $\mu$G" (4.5)
   - assumption: B=300 μG for SMGs, 100 μG weak case  
  > "300 $\mu$G is taken as the value for SMGs, and 100 $\mu$G is used to examine what happens if the magnetic field is weaker" (4.5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure mm-wave sizes from uv-amp plots**: Gaussian fit to uv-amplitude plots, binned, corrected by Monte Carlo  
  > "Correcting these ‘raw’ mm-wave sizes for systematic effects using Monte Carlo simulations" (5.1)
   - parameter: bins 100 kλ to 500 kλ, 500 kλ from 500–1500 kλ  
  > "Bin sizes adopted in $uv$-distance are 100 k$\lambda$ out to 500 k$\lambda$ and 500 k$\lambda$ between 500–1500 k$\lambda$." (5.1)
   - assumption: symmetric Gaussian profile  
  > "We assume symmetric Gaussian profiles, as is usually done in the literature." (5.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with previous measurements  
  > "consistent with our previous measurements" (5.1)
5. **Measure sizes from clean component enclosed flux**: Enclosed flux vs radius in clean component maps; errors from 100 Monte Carlo simulations with Gaussian models of sizes 0.025–0.800 arcsec  
  > "We repeated this process with source sizes between 0$''$.025 and 0$''$.800 in steps of 0$''$.025 (FWHM)" (5.2)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simulations show method useful down to Rc,e=0.025 arcsec; missing flux ≲2%  
  > "this method of measuring source sizes is useful down to $R_{\rm c,e}=0''$.025" (5.2)
6. **Optical/near-IR SED fitting for stellar mass**: Le Phare fits at fixed z with Chabrier IMF, BC03, Calzetti extinction, with/without emission lines  
  > "We adopted a Chabrier IMF (cite: chab03) and stellar population synthesis models by (cite: bru03)." (6.1)
   - software: Le Phare
   - parameter: z=5.5,6.5,7.5 (053.1) and 3.5,4.5,5.5 (231.1); free: SFH, age, extinction  
  > "Free parameters were star-formation history, age and dust extinction." (6.1)
   - assumption: Z=0.02  
  > "We adopted metallicity, $Z_{\rm \odot}=0.02$." (6.1)
   - fitting: chi-square minimisation  
  > "Here we assume that any non-diagonal elements in the covariance matrix are zero, therefore $(\bm{c}_i-\bm{c}_0)'{\rm {\boldsymbol A}}^{-1}(\bm{c}_i-\bm{c}_0)$ can be substituted by standard $\chi^2$." (Radio/mm-wave photometric redshifts, Method)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Derive LIR, SFR and surface densities**: Scale average SMG SED to ALMA 1100 μm flux; SFR = 1.0e-10 LIR; Sigma from LIR and Rc,e  
  > "SFR ({\rm M_{\odot}\,yr^{-1}}) = 1.0\times10^{-10} L_{\rm IR} ({\rm L_{\odot}})" (6.2)
   - assumption: Average ALESS SMG SED from Swinbank+14; Chabrier IMF; Kennicutt 1998  
  > "having adopted the average SED of ALMA-identified SMGs from (cite: swi14)" (6.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with empirical LIR–Sigma relation of Lutz+16  
  > "consistent with an empirical $L_{\rm IR}-\Sigma_{L_{\rm IR}}$ relation" (6.2)

## Qualitative findings

- ASXDF1100.231.1 shows IRAC 3.6 μm excess consistent with Hα at z≈4–5  
  > "ASXDF1100.231.1 has near-/mid-IR colors consistent with the existence of a redshifted H$\alpha$ line at $z=4$–5 in the IRAC 3.6-$\mu$m band." (7 Summary)
- CMB effects push photometric redshifts to higher values but do not significantly overestimate  
  > "We see that taking the effects of the CMB into account pushes the photometric redshifts to higher values." (4.5)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Selected for Herschel non-detection and radio faintness to probe high-z tail  
  > "aiming to reveal the tail of the SMG redshift distribution" (2)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Dust SED from average template only vs. multi-SED library compared  
  > "The respective photometric redshifts based on the average SED are $6.7^{+1.0}_{-1.0}$ and $4.0^{+0.5}_{-0.4}$." (4.4)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No spectroscopic redshifts, so CMB effect strength undetermined  
  > "as we do not have spectroscopic redshifts for the two SMGs, we cannot determine how strong this effect really is." (4.5)
- Limitation (authors): 231.1 optical/mid-IR SED poorly fit  
  > "The observed optical/mid-IR SED of ASXDF1100.231.1 is not well fit by the model SEDs." (6.1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Photometric redshifts rely on assumed dust SED library and degeneracy with temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Very short Cycle 2 integrations limit size constraints
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Size of 231.1 is unresolved or near limit, so Sigma is a lower limit
