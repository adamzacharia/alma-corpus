## Qualitative findings

- C34S, SiO and CO show a rotating disk-like structure perpendicular to the jet, with sub-Keplerian rotation  
  > "In the molecular lines a sub-Keplerian disk around NIRS3 about 400 au in diameter is observed." (abstract)
- Absorption against the central source, deepest at red-shifted velocity, may indicate infall  
  > "The absorption features in the molecular lines towards the central bright source may indicate an infall." (abstract)
- Molecular line emission is very inhomogeneous at small scales, which may indicate clumpiness in the disk  
  > "The molecular line emission appears very inhomogeneous at small scales, which may indicate a small-scale clumpiness in the disk." (abstract)
- CH3CN near the NE2 and SW2 knots is shifted relative to C34S, suggesting C34S traces ambient gas and CH3CN also jet-entrained gas  
  > "These features hint that the C$^{34}$S(7–6) emission arises mainly in the ambient gas" (4.2.2 Knots in the jet)
