## Analysis recipe

1. **Identify cores and fit sizes and fluxes in continuum**: Two-component 2D Gaussian fitting with Dendrogram and CASA imfit, following an earlier procedure  
  > "We performed two-component two-dimensional Gaussian fitting toward E1 and E2 following the same procedure as in (cite: 2021MNRAS.505.2801L), which is a combination of *Dendrogram* (cite: 2008ApJ...679.1338R) and *CASA-imfit* for consistency." (Results 3.1)
   - software: Astrodendro, CASA
   - parameter: MM1-E core split into E1 and E2 using higher-resolution 1.3 mm emission  
  > "Reexamination of the Band 3 continuum emission revealed that the MM1-E core identified in (cite: 2022MNRAS.510.5009L) is in fact two separate cores (namely E1, E2)" (Results 3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive core gas mass and density from 3 mm continuum**: Optically thin LTE dust emission, M_gas = R_gd S_nu D^2 / (kappa_nu B_nu(T_dust)), using Band 3 continuum; spherical geometry for density  
  > "we use the 3 mm continuum emission to calculate the total gas masses of dense cores MM1-C and MM1-E1." (Results 3.1); "by assuming a spherical geometry, the number density can be estimated as" (Results 3.1)
   - parameter: Distance D = 3.0 kpc  
  > "$D$ is the distance to the region (3.0 kpc)" (Results 3.1)
   - parameter: mu_H2 = 2.8; R_core = (FWHM_major x FWHM_minor)^(1/2)/2 x D  
  > "the molecular weight per hydrogen molecule $\mu_{\rm H_2}$ is 2.8" (Results 3.1)
   - assumption: Optically thin 3 mm emission in LTE  
  > "Assuming that the 3 mm continuum emission is optically thin under local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) conditions" (Results 3.1)
   - assumption: T_dust = 14.2 K (MM1-C) and 22.6 K (MM1-E1) from NH3 gas kinetic temperature  
  > "The dust temperature $T_{\rm dust}=$14.2 K and 22.6 K for MM1-C and E1 respectively" (Results 3.1)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust ratio 100  
  > "We assume a typical gas-dust mass ratio $R_{\rm gd}$ of 100." (Results 3.1)
   - assumption: kappa_nu = 0.18 cm2 g-1 (OH5 model)  
  > "A value for $\kappa_\nu$ of 0.18 $\rm cm^{2}~g^{-1}$ is adopted from the ‘OH5’ model" (Results 3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Combined mass uncertainty of ~50% from 25% on R_dg and kappa, 5% flux, distance error  
  > "The combined uncertainty of mass determination is $\sim$ 50%." (Results 3.1)
3. **Resolve Band 6 substructures C1 and C2 and derive their masses and densities**: Dendrogram identification at Band 6, simultaneous 2D Gaussian fitting of the two condensations, mass from the same dust formula with Band 6 kappa  
  > "Due to the proximity, we performed a simultaneous 2D Gaussian fitting of the cores." (Results 3.2); "As shown by the black contour in Panel (c) in Fig. [1](#fig:continuum), two condensations were identified by *Dendrogram*" (Results 3.2)
   - software: Astrodendro, CASA
   - parameter: Dendrogram: threshold 2.5 sigma_b6, step 1 sigma_b6, minimum pixels 44 (pixels in a restoring beam)  
  > "An intensity threshold of 2.5$\sigma_{\rm b6}$, step of 1$\sigma_{\rm b6}$, and a minimum pixel number contained in a restoring beam, 44 in this case, were fed into the *Dendrogram* algorithm." (Footnote 6)
   - parameter: kappa_nu = 0.9 cm2 g-1 at Band 6  
  > "For $\kappa_\nu$ at Band 6, we adopt a value of 0.9 $\rm cm^2\,g^{-1}$ (cite: 1994A&A...291..943O)." (Results 3.2)
   - parameter: Mass also computed for T = 10 K and 6.5 K  
  > "We further calculate their masses assuming lower temperatures of 10 K and 6.5 K." (Results 3.2)
   - assumption: C1 and C2 have the same temperature as the natal core (14.2 K) in the fiducial case  
  > "Assuming they have the same temperature as the natal core, the masses of C1 and C2 are $\sim$2.1 $M_{\odot}$ and $\sim$7.3 $M_{\odot}$, respectively." (Results 3.2)
   - fitting: Simultaneous 2D Gaussian fit of C1 and C2  
  > "Due to the proximity, we performed a simultaneous 2D Gaussian fitting of the cores." (Results 3.2)
   - validation: Fragments detected at ~10 sigma, separated by 2400 au (about 3 beams) and reliably identified by Dendrogram, argued not to be interferometric artifacts  
  > "In particular, the fragments are well separated by 2400 au (about 3 beam sizes, or 0.8$''$ at 3 kpc), and can be reliably identified by the *Dendrogram* algorithm." (Results 3.2)
4. **Test for star-formation activity (starless classification)**: Inspect core-averaged Band 6 and Band 3 spectra, CH3OH moment-0 maps, 12CO and SiO high-velocity emission, outflow lobes and GLOSTAR radio jets  
  > "Taking into account both the paucity of spectral lines and the absence of outflows, we postulate that there is no ongoing star-formation activity within cores MM1-C and E1." (Results 3.1); "no clear emission or absorption features of CH$_3$OH with signal-to-noise ratios (SNRs) larger than 3$\sigma$ were detected toward both MM1-C and E1." (Results 3.1)
   - software: CARTA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Absorption features attributed to missing flux; beam-averaged spectrum at MM1-C1 peak shows no H2CO or CH3OH absorption  
  > "the beam-averaged spectra at band 6 toward the continuum emission peak of MM1-C1 show no absorption features in either H$_2$CO or CH$_3$OH lines" (Results 3.1)
5. **Assess dynamical state with virial and Jeans analysis**: Velocity dispersions from N2D+ (Band 6) and H13CO+ (Band 3); virial parameter alpha_vir = 5 sigma_3D^2 R_eff / (G M_gas); Jeans length and mass at T = 14.2, 10 and 6.5 K  
  > "The virial parameter $\alpha_{\rm vir}=M_{\rm vir}/M_{\rm gas}=5\sigma_{\rm 3D}^2 R_{\rm eff}/G M_{\rm gas}$, where the 3D velocity dispersion is $\sigma_{\rm 3D}=\sqrt{3}c_{\rm s}^{\rm eff}$." (Discussion 4.1); "The Jeans length is given as (cite: 2014MNRAS.439.3275W)" (Discussion 4.1)
   - parameter: Total velocity dispersion sigma_v^2 = sigma_th^2 + sigma_nt^2, with sigma_th = sqrt(k_B T / mu m_H)  
  > "The total velocity dispersion is $\sigma_v^2=\sigma_{\rm th}^2+\sigma_{\rm nt}^2$, where $\sigma_{\rm th}=\sqrt{k_B T/\mu m_H}$." (Discussion 4.1)
   - assumption: Core-scale sigma_v applied to both condensations C1 and C2  
  > "We use the same $\sigma_v$ for the two condensations in virial analysis." (Discussion 4.1)
   - assumption: Gas temperature 14.2 K fiducial (also 10 K and 6.5 K)  
  > "We also derive virial parameters assuming a gas temperature of 10$\,$K or 6.5 K." (Discussion 4.1)
   - assumption: Magnetic field support ignored  
  > "which suggests that the whole core is likely collapsing if additional support from the magnetic field is ignored" (Discussion 4.1)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to N2D+ line (hyperfine structure not modelled; value is an upper limit)  
  > "the velocity dispersion measured from gaussian fit of $\rm N_2D^+$ should be treated as an upper limit because of its hyperfine structure" (Discussion 4.1)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Measure velocity gradients of cold N2D+ filaments**: Position-velocity diagram extracted along the filaments and fitted for velocity gradients, using N2D+ data smoothed to 1''  
  > "We extracted a position-velocity (PV) diagram along the filaments. By fitting the PV diagram, we determined the velocity gradients of $\sim$ 15 and 10 $\rm km~s^{-1}~pc^{-1}$ for the southern and northern filaments, respectively." (Discussion 4.2); "To increase the signal-to-noise ratio, the $\rm N_2D^+$ data was smoothed to 1$''$ resolution." (Discussion 4.2)
   - software: CARTA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Gradients compared with other observations and simulations of small-scale filaments  
  > "The velocity gradients are quite comparable with the velocity gradients along small-scale filaments in other observations of massive star forming regions" (Discussion 4.2)

## Qualitative findings

- MM1-C and E1 show very few spectral lines in contrast to the line forest of the hot core MM1-A  
  > "It can be easily noticed that very few lines are detected toward MM1-C and E1, which is in strong contrast with the ‘line forest’ of MM1-A." (Results 3.1)
- No outflow lobes or radio jets are associated with MM1-C and E1  
  > "Both MM1-C and E1 are far away from CO outflow lobes." (Results 3.1); "no radio jets are detected by the GLOSTAR survey (cite: 2023A&A...670A...9D) associated with these two cores" (Results 3.1)
- MM1-E1 stays a single condensation at 0.3'' resolution while MM1-C splits into C1 and C2  
  > "MM1-E1 shows no evidence of fragmentation, remaining a single condensation at this resolution" (Results 3.2)
- Internal fragmentation and a connected cold filament challenge the monolithic Turbulent Core picture and favour clump-fed accretion, but clump-fed models do not favour such a massive prestellar core  
  > "the presence of internal fragmentation and the identification of a cold gas filament connecting to the core (MM1-C) challenges the model’s expectation" (Discussion 4.3)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: QUARKS Band 6 gives much higher resolution than ATOMS Band 3 to verify massive starless cores in G34-MM1  
  > "providing the possibility of verifying the existence of massive starless cores in G34-MM1 in a spatial resolution of $\sim 1000$ au" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Use Band 3 continuum for masses because its MRS is larger and the 3 mm emission is more optically thin  
  > "Since the MRS of the Band 3 data is two times larger than that of the Band 6 data and the 3 mm continuum emission is relatively optically thin than the 1.3 mm continuum emission," (Results 3.1)
- Alternative: Fragmentation as interferometric artifacts (rejected: ~10 sigma detection vs 3 sigma in synthetic observations)  
  > "The fragmentation could be interferometric artifacts, as discussed in (cite: 2019ApJ...874...89C)." (Results 3.2)
- Alternative: Absorption features in H2CO, 13CO, C18O and CH3OH spectra attributed to missing flux rather than real absorption by a background source  
  > "likely caused by the missing flux issue as a result of interferometer observations due to sidelobe or a lack of shorter baselines and total power (TP) data" (Results 3.1)
- How they decided it was enough: Fragmentation judged reliable because detection is ~10 sigma and fragments are well separated  
  > "the detection of the fragments is more reliable, at $\sim10\sigma$ level" (Results 3.2)
- Limitation (authors): Core-average temperature may be an upper limit so masses are lower limits  
  > "The core-average temperature is possibly an upper limit, and therefore the estimated masses are lower limits." (Results 3.2)
- Limitation (authors): N2D+ velocity dispersion is an upper limit because hyperfine structure is not modelled  
  > "the velocity dispersion measured from gaussian fit of $\rm N_2D^+$ should be treated as an upper limit because of its hyperfine structure" (Discussion 4.1)
- Limitation (authors): Cannot fully rule out that MM1-C forms only a cluster of low-mass stars  
  > "However, we cannot fully rule out the possibility that the massive prestellar core MM1-C will just form a cluster of low-mass stars if it undergoes further fragmentation." (abstract)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Starless classification rests on line-poor spectra and absence of outflows at 0.3'' resolution; no kinematic or chemical evidence of infall is shown
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Missing flux and lack of total-power data affect line absorption and extended continuum interpretation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature of 14.2 K is an NH3 gas temperature at ~3'' scale applied to 700-1400 au condensations, so C1/C2 masses and densities have large systematic uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Internal inconsistencies: C2 radius 1381 au in text vs 1387 au in table; N2D+ sigma_v 0.73 km/s in text vs 0.74 km/s in table header; C1/C2 mass ranges in Conclusions (1.9-7.0, 6.5-24.3) do not match the 6.5 K table values (8.2, 28.4)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dendrogram plus Gaussian fitting of fragments separated by ~3 beams is sensitive to deconvolution choices; no robustness test across imaging weights is shown
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Evidence for the further fragmentation of C2 is below the resolution limit
