## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine and image data**: Concatenate 12 m and 7 m, clean with Briggs robust 0.5, subtract continuum in uv for lines, feather Total Power  
  > "The visibilities on the baselines in common for both the 7 m array and the 12 m array interferometric data sets were in good agreement, therefore they were concatenated into a single measurement set." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "cleaning with Briggs weighting using a robustness parameter of 0.5" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Common baselines in agreement between 7 m and 12 m  
  > "The visibilities on the baselines in common for both the 7 m array and the 12 m array interferometric data sets were in good agreement" (2 Observations)
2. **Continuum fit and dust mass**: Two elliptical 2D Gaussians fitted; optically thin dust mass m=f d^2/(kappa B(T)); aperture flux for extended part  
  > "we fitted the brightness distribution by two elliptical 2D Gaussians." (3.1 Continuum image)
   - parameter: aperture radius 9 arcsec (6300 au) for extended emission  
  > "We found that an aperture radius of 9$''$ (6300 au) includes all these features." (3.1 Continuum image)
   - assumption: d=700 pc, kappa850=0.17 m2/kg, T=50 K, beta=1 (central); 20 K, beta=2 (extended)  
  > "assuming 20 K for the temperature and $\beta$=2 for the dust emissivity (cite: sw2001)" (3.1 Continuum image)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit  
  > "In an attempt to separate the central peak from this extension, we fitted the brightness distribution by two elliptical 2D Gaussians." (Results and Analysis, Continuum image)
   - validation: Tau map shows peak optical depth ~0.01 (<0.03 at 20 K), supporting optically thin assumption  
  > "The resulting peak $\tau$ was around 0.01, and even with $T_{\rm dust}$ = 20 K, the $\tau$ stays below 0.03." (3.1 Continuum image)
3. **CO spectra and velocity ranges**: Extract spectra over 20 arcsec region and central beam; define LV/MV/HV ranges around systemic velocity -3.55 km/s; integrate moment maps; exclude OFF-position artifacts  
  > "we defined three velocity ranges symmetrically around the systemic velocity: low velocity (LV) within 1.5 km s$^{-1}$, medium velocity (MV) between 1.5...3.5 km s$^{-1}$, and high velocity (HV) between 3.5...8.5 km s$^{-1}$." (3.2 CO spectra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Artifacts absent in interferometric-only data  
  > "they are not present in the interferometric-only data" (3.2 CO spectra)
4. **Spectro-astrometry and kinematics**: Centroid of C18O emission per channel, PV diagram along major axis, compare r^-0.5 Keplerian and r^-1 pseudo-disk curves  
  > "We performed spectro-astrometry in our C$^{18}$O data cube by calculating the centroid of the emission for each velocity channel." (3.4 Spectro-astrometry)
   - parameter: major axis PA 129±3 deg  
  > "The centroid positions lined up very precisely along a position angle of 129$\pm$3$^{\circ}$ east of north" (3.4 Spectro-astrometry)
   - assumption: d = 700±100 pc; mass independent of inclination along major axis  
  > "This value is independent of the inclination, because it was measured along the major axis." (3.4 Spectro-astrometry)
   - fitting: Best-fitting Keplerian curve (method not detailed)  
  > "The stellar mass of the best-fitting Keplerian curve is 0.1 M$_{\odot}$, corresponding to a rather low mass central star." (Results and Analysis, Spectro-astrometry)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Gas mass of the disk from 13CO**: Integrate 13CO flux in 0.5 and 1.0 arcsec apertures, optically thin  
  > "We estimated the mass of the disk by integrating the $^{13}$CO line flux in a circular aperture within 0$\ensuremath{''}$5 and within 1$\ensuremath{''}$0" (3.4 Spectro-astrometry)
   - assumption: optically thin, 50 K gas, 12CO/13CO=69, CO/H2=1e-4  
  > "We assumed that the emission is optically thin and assuming 50 K average gas temperature" (3.4 Spectro-astrometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: C18O gives consistent results  
  > "Mass estimates from C$^{18}$O give consistent results" (3.4 Spectro-astrometry)
6. **Envelope infall rate and accretion comparison**: Momose et al. 1998 formula; accretion rates from L_bol R*/(G M*) and Br gamma luminosity  
  > "we estimated the infall rate from the envelope onto the disk in V346 Nor using the equation above, obtaining 6$\times$10$^{-6}\,M_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$." (5.3 Infall/accretion mismatch)
   - parameter: M*=0.1 Msun, R*=3 Rsun, L_bol=160 Lsun  
  > "using $M_*=0.1\,M_{\odot}$ and $R_* = 3\,R_{\odot}$" (5.3 Infall/accretion mismatch)
   - assumption: V346 Nor velocity field similar to L1551 IRS 5  
  > "Using the similarity of the velocity field observed in V346 Nor to that of L1551 IRS 5" (5.3 Infall/accretion mismatch)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Infall rate uncertain within a factor of 3  
  > "we estimate that this number is correct within a factor of 3" (5.3 Infall/accretion mismatch)
