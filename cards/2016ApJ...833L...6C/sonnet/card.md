## Analysis recipe

1. **Make continuum and CO line maps**: Pipeline calibration in CASA, uv-plane continuum subtraction, convolution of all maps to common beam, integrated intensity maps above sigma cutoffs  
  > "Data calibration using CASA followed the pipeline. Continuum emission was subtracted in the (u,v) plane before making line maps." (Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 1.5 sigma cutoff CO(1-0); 2.5 sigma cutoff CO(3-2)  
  > "in CO(1–0) with a 1.5$\sigma$ cutoff, and 14 mJy beam$^{-1}$ km s$^{-1}$ for CO(3–2) with a 2.5$\sigma$ cutoff" (Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with single-dish fluxes from literature  
  > "We detect most, perhaps all of the CO(3–2) single dish flux, based on comparison with single dish upper limits." (Observations)
2. **Derive Lyman continuum rate from 3 mm free-free**: Convert 3mm flux density to N_Lyc with Draine expression, then correct for dust absorption of UV photons (72% ionize H)  
  > "we obtain a Lyman continuum rate of $N_{Lyc}=8.3\pm0.2\times 10^{52}\,\rm s^{-1}$" (3)
   - parameter: Te = 13000 K; D = 10 Mpc  
  > "for T$_{e}$= 13000 K" (3)
   - assumption: Synchrotron and dust negligible at 3mm  
  > "synchrotron and dust contributions are negligible" (3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Separate dust from free-free at 870um**: Extrapolate 3mm free-free to 870um with spectral index -0.118 and subtract to get dust-only map  
  > "we extrapolate the 3mm free-free emission to 870$\mu$m using the spectral index -0.118" (4.1); "Subtracting the scaled 3mm free-free continuum from the 870$\mu$m gives a map of the dust emission alone" (4.1)
   - assumption: Dust contribution at 3mm negligible for beta=1.7  
  > "for dust emissivity $\beta = 1.7$, is $S_{3mm}^{dust}<0.1$ mJy" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Dust flux agrees with Kepley et al. 2016  
  > "This value agrees with that of (cite: 2016arXiv160701779K)." (4.1)
4. **Derive dust masses, gas masses and GTD for clouds**: Dust mass from 870um dust flux with kappa and temperature; gas mass from CO(1-0) with X_CO (and CO(3-2) via R31=7.4); GTD = gas/dust in three 0.6" regions and central 5"  
  > "We calculate GTD by determining dust and gas masses separately." (4.3 Gas-to-Dust)
   - parameter: kappa_870=0.9 cm2/g, beta=1.7; Tdust 33 K (Clouds C, W), 20 K (Cloud E)  
  > "For Clouds C and W, we use the 33K IRAS 60/100 color temperature (cite: 2008ApJ...678..804E) and for the isolated Cloud E, we use T$_{Dust}$=20K." (4.3 Gas-to-Dust)
   - assumption: LMC X_CO = 4.7e20 cm-2 (K km/s)-1; R31=7.4  
  > "using $R_{31}$=7.4 to obtain the equivalent CO(1-0) flux" (4.3 Gas-to-Dust)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with GTD predicted from metallicity (~400-600)  
  > "so the predicted GTD is $\sim$400-600." (4.3 Gas-to-Dust)

## Qualitative findings

- Poor correspondence between gas, dust and free-free emission; dust is enhanced toward the star-forming region relative to CO.  
  > "Dust continuum is enhanced toward the star-forming region as compared to the CO emission." (abstract)

## Claim tuples

- H: Is the GTD in II Zw 40 consistent with that predicted from its metallicity? | E: Dust masses from dust-only 870um map and gas masses from CO(1-0) with LMC X_CO, in three clouds and the starburst | R: GTD ~60-170, versus predicted ~400-600; Cloud E >270 | F: GTD is unexpectedly low and spatially variable | **supported**

  > "it requires that $X_{CO}$ vary by a factors of 4-10 across a region of $\lesssim$200 pc extent." (5)

- Limitation (authors): Large structures (>~12") undersampled in Band 7; could matter for dust  
  > "However, it could be an issue for dust, which traces both atomic and molecular gas." (2 Observations)
- Limitation (authors): Dust mass may be underestimated since warm dust dominates SED peak; LMC X_CO may overestimate gas  
  > "This may underestimate the true dust mass, since the SED peak is dominated by the warmest dust, which will not dominate the mass." (4.3 Gas-to-Dust)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): GTD relies on LMC kappa and X_CO and assumed dust temperatures with no uncertainty propagation shown
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Missing short spacings for CO(1-0) and dust; weak CO(1-0) detection
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Free-free subtraction assumes optically thin extrapolation from 3mm
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uncertain Td (33 K from IRAS vs 20 K) strongly affects dust masses
