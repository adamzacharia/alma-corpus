## Analysis recipe

1. **Align optical and ALMA images**: Match ALMA calibrator and foreground sources to GAIA DR1; compare [CII] and rest-frame UV locations  
  > "The relative astrometry of ALMA and *HST* images have been calibrated by matching ALMA calibrator and foreground sources (if any) to the GAIA Data Release 1 catalogue (cite: Gaia-Collaboration:2016)." (Observations and data analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare spatial offsets with ALMA astrometric uncertainty  
  > "In this case the spatial offset is $3.5$ times larger than the $\sigma_p=0.2\ensuremath{''}$ and is unlikely to be related to the astrometric calibration of ALMA dataset." (BDF-3299)
2. **Find faint extended lines**: Image calibrated data with natural weighting and a taper grid; average frequency channels over trial widths, find peaks, remove duplicates and select the highest-confidence candidate  
  > "We have used a natural weighting that returns the best surface brightness sensitivity." (Observations and data analysis); "The code generates channel maps with different input line widths by averaging the ALMA datacube in frequency." (Blind-line search method); "Finally the code performs a cross-match between all the extracted candidates to identify duplicates with similar position and frequency, and keeps only the candidates with the highest SNR." (Blind-line search method)
   - software: CASA, tclean, customised line finder code, Python
   - parameter: Ten taper values: 0.2'' to 2'' in steps of 0.2''  
  > "from 0.2$''$ to 2$''$ in steps of 0.2$''$." (SXDF-NB1006-2, A2744-YD4, and MACSJJ149-JD1); "We have thus generated 10 different ALMA cubes for each target" (SXDF-NB1006-2, A2744-YD4, and MACSJJ149-JD1)
   - parameter: Search within 1000 km s−1 of [OIII] and within 5'' of the UV counterpart  
  > "For our study, we have searched candidate lines within 1000 ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$ from the \[O$\scriptstyle\rm III$\] and within 5$''$ from the UV counterpart." (Blind-line search method)
   - parameter: Trial widths 50–500 km s−1; peak SNR threshold 3.6  
  > "we have used a set of different line widths from 50 ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$ to 500 ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$." (Blind-line search method); "The SNR threshold of the peaks has been fixed to 3.6" (Blind-line search method)
   - parameter: Duplicate discrimination: one ALMA beam and |Δv=500| km s−1  
  > "Finally, we have set a distance of one ALMA beam and $|\Delta v = 500|$ ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$ to discriminate duplicates." (Blind-line search method)
   - assumption: Finder optimised for point-source emission; tapered cubes used to detect extended emission  
  > "We note that our line finder code is optimised to detect point-source emission." (Blind-line search method); "To detect extended emission, we have generated *uv*-tapered datacubes with different angular resolution for each target and run our customised code on them" (Blind-line search method)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Three recovered candidates have significance >3.8σ and redshifts matching either [OIII] or Lyα  
  > "In all three galaxies we have found a candidate line with a significance level $>3.8\sigma$ in the integrated *uv*-tapered map at the redshift of either \[O$\scriptstyle\rm III$\] or Ly$\alpha$ line." (SXDF-NB1006-2, A2744-YD4, and MACSJJ149-JD1)
3. **Measure line fluxes and luminosities**: Integrate line channels with immoments; measure flux inside 2σ contours or a beam-scaled circular aperture; calculate luminosities  
  > "We have constructed the \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] flux map with the `CASA` task `immoments` by integrating the channels of the line in the final datacube." (Observations and data analysis); "The integrated flux density has been estimated from the region that encompasses the 2$\sigma$ contours around the peak in the flux map." (Observations and data analysis); "We thus infer a \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] luminosity of $(6.7\pm0.9)\times10^7$ L${_\odot}$." (BDF-3299)
   - software: CASA, immoments
   - parameter: If the 2σ region is smaller than twice the beam area, use a circular aperture of diameter 1.5 times the beam major axis, centred on the flux-map peak  
  > "where the area of the 2$\sigma$-contour region is smaller than 2$\times$ ALMA-beam area" (Observations and data analysis); "a circular aperture with diameter as large as 1.5$\times$ the major-axis of the ALMA beam and centred at the emission peak of the flux map." ## Reasoning

- Why this data: Use galaxies observed in both [CII] and [OIII] to test non-detections and compare line extents  
  > "in order to verify the robustness of some \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] non-detection and compare the extent of the two FIR emission lines." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting maximises surface-brightness sensitivity; taper trials find the best sensitivity–resolution combination for extended emission  
  > "We have used a natural weighting that returns the best surface brightness sensitivity." (Observations and data analysis); "enables us to find the best sensitivity-angular resolution combination that optimises the detection of extended emission." (SXDF-NB1006-2, A2744-YD4, and MACSJJ149-JD1)
- Alternative: SED-fitting SFRs instead of UV+FIR SFRs produce a systematic offset below the local relation  
  > "if we adopt the SFR from SED fitting, high-$z$ galaxies appear systematically below the local relation." (\[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] as tracer of SFR in the EOR?)
- Alternative: BDF-3299 extended [CII] may trace a satellite or metal-enriched circumgalactic medium  
  > "We conjecture that the extended emission toward East represents an additional fainter and smaller satellite member of BDF-3299 system." (BDF-3299); "Another possibility is that the extended \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] emission is tracing metal-enriched circumgalactic medium (CGM) around BDF-3299." (BDF-3299)
- Alternative: Target-specific ALMA simulations are an alternative for complicated morphologies  
  > "In those cases, ALMA simulations of the target provide a valid alternative to predict the missing flux due to the surface brightness dimming effect." (Comparing mock data with observations)
- Alternative: Physical explanations also considered: low metallicity, starburst radiation fields, low PDR covering fraction and line-width effects  
  > "the \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] deficit may indicate that these galaxies are very metal-poor systems." (Introduction); "Upward deviations with respect to the Kennicutt-Schmidt relation due to a starburst phase, could strongly depress \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] emission." (Introduction); "the line width of the FIR line, and thus disc inclination, may be responsible of the non-detections." (Introduction)
- How they decided it was enough: Retain detections above 3.6σ and select the highest-confidence candidate; check size agreement and mock-data agreement with observations  
  > "and among this final sample we have extracted the line candidate with the highest confidence level." (SXDF-NB1006-2, A2744-YD4, and MACSJJ149-JD1); "The SNR threshold of the peaks has been fixed to 3.6" (Blind-line search method); "The simulations are fully consistent with the observations" (Comparing mock data with observations)
- Limitation (authors): Correction-factor uncertainty prevents total-flux accuracy better than ~20%-30%  
  > "the correction factors suffer from large uncertainty and the total flux cannot be estimated with an error lower than $\sim20\%-30\%$." (Comparing mock data with observations)
- Limitation (authors): Correction factors may be inappropriate for very complicated morphologies  
  > "Moreover, our correction factors may not be appropriate for very complicated morphologies." (Comparing mock data with observations)
- Limitation (authors): Low statistics above z=7 prevent a definitive evolution measurement  
  > "the low statistics at $z>7$ does not allow us to determine definitively whether the $L_{\rm [CII]}$-SFR relation evolves with redshift or not." (\[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] as tracer of SFR in the EOR?)
- Limitation (authors): Tapering sacrifices sensitivity and does not by itself recover total galaxy emission  
  > "The *uv*-tapering is a possible alternative to recover the "missing" extended emission to the detriment of sensitivity." (Summary and Conclusions); "However, this method is not sufficient to infer the total emission arising from the galaxy." (Summary and Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): MS0451-H line association is unconfirmed; magnification systematics and differential magnification complicate its luminosity  
  > "we cannot confirm if this potential emission is associate to the arc." (MS0451-H); "the error associated with such a large magnification factor might plausibly be higher than 50% when systematics are included (cite: Meneghetti:2017)." (MS0451-H); "a significant differential magnification can affect the two spatially offset regions." (MS0451-H)
- Limitation (authors): BDF-3299 satellite and CGM interpretations require more sensitive [CII] or JWST observations  
  > "distinguishing between satellites or CGM requires either more sensitive \[C$\scriptstyle\rm II$\] images or JWST observations" (BDF-3299)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Selecting the strongest candidate across multiple tapers and line widths needs an explicit false-positive assessment beyond a borrowed single SNR threshold.
