## Analysis recipe

1. **Model foreground lenses in HST**: GALFIT Sersic (plus exponential disk and point source) fits to F110W images, with ALMA contours used to check residuals  
  > "we perform profile fitting using GALFIT (cite: peng10), which can simultaneously fit profiles to multiple galaxies." (3.1 Modeling the lenses in HST)
   - software: GALFIT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SPT0538-50 used as a test case against Bothwell et al. 2013  
  > "SPT0538-50, which has been modeled in (cite: bothwell13b), serves as a test case for our analysis." (3.1 Modeling the lenses in HST)
2. **De-blend lens and source in IRAC**: Convolve HST models with the empirical IRAC PSF and fit blended IRAC images by chi2 minimisation; aperture photometry on residuals  
  > "Before fitting the IRAC images by performing a $\chi^2$ minimization, the HST models are convolved with the IRAC PSF, which is empirically extracted by stacking at least five bright and isolated stars in the field." (3.2 De-blending the source/lens in IRAC)
   - software: PyGFit
   - parameter: Only positions and fluxes free; Sersic parameters fixed; position shifts less than a pixel  
  > "During the $\chi^2$ minimization only the positions and fluxes of the objects are left as free parameters." (3.2 De-blending the source/lens in IRAC)
   - assumption: Morphological k-corrections negligible between WFC3 and IRAC  
  > "morphological *k*-corrections are negligible" (3.2 De-blending the source/lens in IRAC)
   - fitting: Delta chi2 = 2.3 for 1 sigma errors, varying maximum positional shift  
  > "We change the allowed maximum positional shift during fitting from 0 to 0.6$\ensuremath{''}$ (1 pixel) until $\chi^2$ increases by 2.3." (3.2.1 SPT0538-50)
   - validation: PSF tested on field stars; residual under 1%  
  > "The residual flux from these stars is less than 1% of the original flux in all cases." (3.2 De-blending the source/lens in IRAC)
3. **SED fitting for stellar mass, LIR and SFR**: CIGALE fit of HST to SPT photometry with single-component tau-model SFH, grid of parameters, magnification correction  
  > "we utilize the code CIGALE (cite: burgarella05; noll09)[^3] to fit our photometric data from near-IR to far-IR." (5 SED fitting)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Maraston05 SPS, Kroupa IMF, solar metallicity; grids in age, tau, A_V, delta, alpha  
  > "We adopt the (cite: maraston05) (2005; M05) stellar population synthesis model, a (cite: kroupa01) initial mass function (IMF), and a solar metallicity as our baseline." (5 SED fitting)
   - assumption: Flat LCDM H0=70, OmegaM=0.3; ALMA magnification applied to stellar emission  
  > "we use a standard cosmological model with $H_0=70$ ${\rm km}$ ${\rm s}^{-1}$${\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, $\Omega_M=0.3$, and $\Omega_{\Lambda}=0.7$." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: Expectation values and standard deviations from the PDF of parameter-bin best-fit models  
  > "Expectation values and standard deviations for the output galaxy properties are derived from the probability distribution function of the parameter-bin-specific best-fit models." (5.2 SED fitting results)
   - validation: Compared with SFRs from LIR conversions and with BC03 and two-component SFH  
  > "All of the SFRs derived by CIGALE SED fitting are consistent with those by the conversion recipes." (5.2 SED fitting results)

## Qualitative findings

- All six DSFGs lie above the star-forming main sequence, two about 10 times above.  
  > "the six SPT DSFGs all lie above the MS. Two of them are $\sim$ 10 times above the star-forming main-sequence." (Summary and Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Single-component SFH adopted because data cannot constrain more complex SFH  
  > "We adopt the simplest single-component SFH model hereafter since we do not have enough data to constrain a more complex SFH." (5.1.2 Two-component SFH model)
- Alternative: Two-component SFH rejected as poorly constrained  
  > "The derived instantaneous SFRs and stellar masses are poorly constrained." (5.1.2 Two-component SFH model)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Stellar mass depends strongly on inputs; uncertainties dominated by few optical/NIR points; differential lensing not measured  
  > "Our uncertainties are dominated by the limited number of photometric data points at optical/near-IR wavelengths." (5.2.1 Systematics)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar masses poorly constrained (upper limits for two) and model dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Magnification of dust applied to stellar light
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample of six selected as best cases
