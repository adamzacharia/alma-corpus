## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image e-MERLIN cubes**: eMCP pipeline for wide band; CASA narrow-band calibration, self-cal on bright channel, full-polarization cubes  
  > "We applied the solutions to all narrow channels and made spectral image cubes for each target (in full polarization, for ex-OH)." (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
   - software: CASA, eMCP
   - parameter: robust 0.5, restoring beam 65x45 mas PA -50  
  > "we used an intermediate weighting (robust 0.5) and a standard restoring beam of 65 mas$\times$45 mas at a PA of $-$50$^\circ$" (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
   - assumption: 3C84 unpolarized for leakage; 3C286 PA 33 deg  
  > "using 3C84, assumed to be unpolarized" (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **measure maser spots**: 2D Gaussian fits in Stokes I, then Q, U, LHCP, RHCP at same positions; keep spots >2 sigma in at least two consecutive channels  
  > "we took only those spots with a total intensity above 2$\sigma$ that appear in at least two consecutive channels" (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
   - software: AIPS JMFIT
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian components  
  > "We used the JMFIT procedure in AIPS (cite: greisen2003) to fit 2D Gaussian components" (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
   - validation: astrometric error budget delta_tot  
  > "The total astrometric accuracy is $\delta_{\mathrm{tot}}=(\delta_1^2+\delta_2^2+\delta_3^2+\delta_4^2)^{0.5}$." (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
3. **test coexistence of the two masers**: Lines coincide if spectra overlap in velocity and angular separation < 0.5(gamma_6.7+gamma_6.035)  
  > "The angular separation between methanol and ex-OH spots is less than $0.5(\gamma_{6.7}+\gamma_{6.035})$" (Observations, data reduction, and methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **derive magnetic field and polarization**: Gaussian fits to RHCP/LHCP spectra give Zeeman splitting, B_los from 0.056 km/s/mG; m_l, m_c, chi_l from Stokes  
  > "Using the formula $\Delta V_{\mathrm{Z}}/B=$0.056 from (cite: baudry1997), we calculated the magnetic field along the line of sight" (Results)
   - assumption: field perpendicular to linear polarization vector for sigma components  
  > "The orientation of the magnetic field, $\Phi_{\mathrm{B}}$, is perpendicular to the linear polarization vector for $\sigma$ components." (Results)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile fits  
  > "the parameters of the fit Gaussian profile to the spectrum; the peak amplitude ($S_{\mathrm {fit}}$), the full width at half maximum of the profile ($FWHM_{\mathrm {fit}}$), and the peak velocity ($V_{\mathrm {fit}}$)" (Results)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **compare maser structure with outflows and field via position angles**: least-squares PA of spot distributions; Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests against outflow PA and field PA  
  > "Based on the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test, we cannot find any correlation for the linear distributions of both masers with the outflows" (4.3.2 Sky-plane B orientation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **derive CH3CN rotational temperatures from ALMA archive**: eight Gaussians fit to K=0-7, common linewidth fixed from K=3; rotation diagram; optical depth from CH3 13CN  
  > "We used eight Gaussians to fit contemporaneously the $K=0$ to $K=7$ components" (Methyl cyanide)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 50; beam filling factor of unity; 13CN optically thin  
  > "the $\mathrm{^{12}C/^{13}C}$ line ratio of 50" (Methyl cyanide)
   - fitting: simultaneous Gaussians  
  > "We used eight Gaussians to fit contemporaneously the $K=0$ to $K=7$ components, constraining the line width of all lines to be the same as for the bright and unblended $K=3$ component" (Methyl cyanide)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Masers coexist in six sources and avoid each other in four; no link to evolutionary stage  
  > "we do not notice a relationship between the phase of the evolution of YSO and the presence of regions with coexistence or avoidance of methanol and ex-OH masers" (4.4 Coincidence and avoidance)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA lines used to trace outflows, disks and temperature around masers  
  > "To trace outflows, and in particular the outflow direction, which is interesting to compare to the magnetic field orientation, we used 22 GHz water masers and archival ALMA" (4.1 Counterparts)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Better angular and spectral resolution needed to confirm Zeeman triplets  
  > "However, better angular and spectral resolution is needed to confirm that." (4.3.3 Zeeman triplets)
- Limitation (authors): CH3CN optically thick, so temperatures overestimated  
  > "all of our estimated temperatures are overestimated, and our column densities are underestimated" (Methyl cyanide)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA analysis is described only qualitatively; outflow PAs not reproducible
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distance ambiguities propagate into au-scale separations
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample (10) for KS tests
