## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and Stokes Q/U and derive polarization quantities**: tclean imaging; debiased PI, P and position angle computed from Q,U,I  
  > "The debiased polarized intensity $PI$ and its corresponding uncertainty $\sigma_{PI}$ are calculated as:" (Observation)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "with a briggs weighting parameter of robust = 0.5." (2 Observation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify condensations in MM1 and derive masses and densities**: astrodendro on continuum; optically thin dust mass with kappa from beta=1.5, gas-to-dust 100  
  > "we applied the dendrogram (cite: 2008ApJ...679.1338R) technique on the dust continuum using the Python package *astrodendro*." (3.1 Dust continuum)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: min_value = 4 sigma, min_delta = 1 sigma, min_npix = half beam area  
  > "we adopt min_value = 4$\sigma$, min_delta = 1$\sigma$, and set min_npix to be the number of pixels in half beam area." (3.1 Dust continuum)
   - assumption: optically thin, isothermal, gas-to-dust 100:1, beta~1.5 (kappa=0.11 m2/kg), mu_H2=2.86, 50% mass uncertainty  
  > "With the assumptions of the optically thin dust emission, isothermal, and a **gas-to-dust ratio $\Lambda$ of 100:1**" (3.1 Dust continuum)
   - assumption: Condensation 1 temperature from power-law profile (T0=38 K, r0=100 AU, q=0.4, background 20 K); MM1-Core1 30 K; MM4-Core4 16 K  
  > "Adopting $T_0 = 38$ K, $r_0 =$ 100 AU, $L_0 =$ 1 $L_{\odot}$, $q=0.4$" (3.1 Dust continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **P-I relation**: chi2 minimization power-law fit P ∝ I^alpha where P/dP>3  
  > "Using $\chi^2$ minimization, we fit the $P$-$I$ anti-correlations in MM1 and MM4 where $P/\delta P >3$ with a simple power-law" (3.2 Polarized emission of dust)
   - fitting: chi2 minimization  
  > "Using $\chi^2$ minimization, we fit the $P$-$I$ anti-correlations in MM1 and MM4 where $P/\delta P >3$ with a simple power-law $P \propto I^{\alpha}$" (Polarized emission of dust)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Magnetic field strength via ADF**: Houde et al. 2016 ADF fit, chi2 minimization, segments with PI/sigma_PI>3, B0 from Houde 2009 relation with NH3 turbulent dispersion  
  > "The best fit is obtained via $\chi^2$ minimization. The $B_{\mathrm{pos}}$ for MM1-Core1 and MM4-Core4 are calculated to be 1.6 mG and 0.32 mG, respectively." (3.5)
   - parameter: fit over l<2.6 arcsec (MM1-Core1) and l<3.4 arcsec (MM4-Core4)  
  > "we fit the ADFs of MM1-Core1 and MM4-Core4 over $l < 2.6\ensuremath{''}$ and $l < 3.4\ensuremath{''}$, respectively" (3.5)
   - assumption: constant turbulent-to-ordered field ratio for partially covered MM4-Core4; B=4Bpos/pi  
  > "an assumption of constant turbulent-to-ordered magnetic field ratio in the core needs to be adopted." (3.5)
   - fitting: chi2 minimization  
  > "The best fit is obtained via $\chi^2$ minimization." (Magnetic field strength of MM1-Core1 and MM4-Core4)
   - validation: ADF accurate within factor ~3 per Houde et al. 2016  
  > "the $B_{\mathrm{pos}}$ estimated from the ADF method is accurate within a factor of $\sim$3." (3.5)
5. **Virial analysis**: kinetic and magnetic virial masses, total virial parameter alpha_k+B  
  > "The corresponding total virial parameter is" (4.2.1)
   - assumption: density profile rho ∝ r^-a  
  > "We adopted radial density profiles $\rho \propto r^{-a}$ (cite: 2009ApJ...696..268Z) for the two cores." (4.2.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: mass overestimated by factor 2 test  
  > "If the mass is overestimated by a factor of 2" (4.2.1)
6. **PIL analysis of B, intensity gradient, local gravity**: pixel masses from I>3sigma; local gravity summed; angles psi, delta, omega, Sigma_B  
  > "we derived the gas mass ($m_{i}$) for each pixel of the map with $I > 3\sigma_{I}$ for the three clumps" (3.6)
   - assumption: hydrostatic gas pressure neglected  
  > "If the hydrostatic gas pressure is neglectable" (3.6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Outflow vs field alignment and fragmentation**: B angle within one beam per condensation vs outflow PA; MST with MiSTree; Jeans length/mass comparison  
  > "we applied the minimum spanning tree (hereafter MST) technique on these condensations using the python package MiSTree" (4.4)
   - software: MiSTree
   - parameter: projection correction factor pi/2  
  > "we multiplied the observed separation by a factor of $\pi /2$ to correct for the statistical projection effect" (4.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- MM1 field is radial; depolarization with increasing intensity in MM1 and MM4  
  > "The magnetic field structure in MM1 is well resolved and shows a radial pattern, indicating that the field might be dragged towards the center by gravity." (3.2 Polarized emission of dust)
