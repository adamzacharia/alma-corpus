## Reasoning

- Why this data: Spatially resolved molecular gas traces local explosion environments; two SN types in the same host permit an internal comparison.  
  > "Spatially resolving observations of molecular gas provide the physical properties of the interstellar medium (ISM) in the local environment of stellar explosions, such as molecular gas content, star-formation efficiency, and velocity field" (Introduction); "This enables us to compare the environments between a SLSN-I and a Type II SN located in the same galaxy." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Adopt Galactic αCO for near-solar metallicity; test its consistency with metallicity-dependent estimates.
