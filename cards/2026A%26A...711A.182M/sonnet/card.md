## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and line cubes**: ALMA: pipeline restore then manual tclean per SPW, STATCONT continuum subtraction; NOEMA: GILDAS calibration, imaging  
  > "The image of each SPW (continuum plus line emission) was produced manually using the TCLEAN task" (2.2 ALMA archive data)
   - software: CASA, STATCONT, GILDAS
   - parameter: robust 0.5 (ALMA)  
  > "with the robust parameter set to 0.5" (2.2 ALMA archive data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Moment/velocity maps of lines**: Select intense lines representing kinematic structures and map V_LSR gradients  
  > "being sufficiently intense to map an extended velocity range;  2) being representative of well-defined kinematic structures" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive physical conditions**: Extract spectra over continuum contour and outflow squares; fit LTE synthetic spectra with MADCUBA AUTOFIT for Tex, N, v0, FWHM  
  > "we used the tool AUTOFIT of MADCUBA, which compares the observed spectra with the LTE synthetic spectra" (3.2)
   - software: MADCUBA
   - assumption: Single component per species, LTE, negligible density and temperature gradients  
  > "a single component was fit for each molecular species, under the assumption of negligible density and temperature gradients" (3.2)
   - fitting: Levenberg-Marquardt non-linear least squares; errors from covariance matrix  
  > "AUTOFIT provides the best nonlinear least-squares fit using the Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm" (3.2)
   - validation: Fit compared visually with spectra  
  > "The emission profile of each transition is reasonably well fit with MADCUBA" (3.2)
4. **Position-velocity analysis and Keplerian profile**: PV plots along disk midplane averaged across a beam perpendicular; compared with off-disk PV plots along jet axis  
  > "Before producing the PV plot, the emission was averaged across a beam in the direction perpendicular to the positional cut." (4 Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Off-disk PV plots consistent with midplane PV  
  > "These off-disk PV plots show velocity profiles consistent with that at the disk midplane" (4.1.1)
5. **Rule out envelope rotation**: Centrifugal equilibrium estimate of dVrot/dR along green strip gives implied mass  
  > "we derive a value for the YSO mass,  $M$ $\approx$ 44 $M_{\odot}$" (4.1.1)
   - assumption: Centrifugal equilibrium under YSO gravity; d=1.63 kpc  
  > "Assuming centrifugal equilibrium we can write" (4.1.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with 5.6±2 Msun  
  > "This value greatly exceeds the value of the YSO mass of 5.6$\pm$2 $M_{\odot}$ determined by studying the kinematics of the molecular lines tracing the disk rotation" (Discussion, IRAS 21078+5211)

## Qualitative findings

- In G035.02+0.35 only SO extends outside the disk; the other molecules are confined to the disk  
  > "SO is the only molecular species whose emission extends well outside the disk." (abstract)

## Claim tuples

- H: SO emission traces a rotating magneto-centrifugal wind in both YSOs | E: Maps and PV plots of SO along and off the disk midplane | R: Keplerian-like PV profiles, consistent at off-disk positions | F: SO traces a wind launched from the disk | **supported**
- H: The transversal V_LSR gradient in IRAS 21078+5211 could be envelope rotation | E: Centrifugal equilibrium estimate with observed gradient | R: Implied mass ≈44 Msun vs 5.6±2 | F: Gradient must be wind rotation | **supported**
- H: The wind is an extended MHD DW rather than an X-wind | E: Compare molecular distributions and excitation with shock models | R: Radial molecular stratification in IRAS 21078; lack of other molecules in G035.02 | F: Favors radially extended MHD DW over X-wind | **partially**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Two datasets overlap in frequency with comparable ≈250 au and ≈0.5 km/s resolutions, allowing a comparison  
  > "provide comparable linear ($\approx$250 au at the target distance) and velocity ($\approx$0.5 km s$^{-1}$) resolutions" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: X-wind with entrained ambient gas rejected because only SO traces the wind in G035.02+0.35  
  > "That already rules out the possibility that we are observing ambient gas entrained in a fast wide-angle X-wind" (4.2.2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Angular resolution insufficient to resolve disk vertical structure  
  > "That is certainly due to insufficient angular resolution to resolve the disk structure vertically" (4.1.2)
- Limitation (authors): SiO 5-4 resolved out at these scales  
  > "the SiO 5–4 emission, another potential outflow tracer, is resolved out at these small scales" (3.1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Keplerian claims are qualitative; no quantitative PV fits or launching radii
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-component LTE fits for blended, multi-component gas
- Caveat (inferred, ours): DW vs X-wind discrimination relies on qualitative comparison with shock models
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Different instruments/uv-coverage between the two sources
