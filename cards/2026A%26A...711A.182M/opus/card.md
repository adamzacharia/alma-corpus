## Analysis recipe

1. **image and continuum-subtract the ALMA SPWs**: TCLEAN Hogbom, Briggs robust 0.5 per SPW; continuum level per pixel with STATCONT and subtracted; continuum from average of four SPWs  
  > "The image of each SPW (continuum plus line emission) was produced manually using the TCLEAN task" (ALMA archive data); "For each SPW, to determine the continuum level of the spectra and subtract it from the line emission, we used STATCONT" (ALMA archive data)
   - software: CASA, tclean, STATCONT
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "with the robust parameter set to 0.5 as a compromise between resolution and sensitivity to extended emission" (ALMA archive data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **map structure and LSR velocity of selected lines**: Survey spectral images and select intense lines representative of kinematic structures; velocity maps of SO, CH3CN, CH3OH, HC3N, SO2 vs continuum, masers, radio jet  
  > "To study the kinematics, we surveyed the spectral images and selected the molecular lines that satisfy two conditions" (Structure and kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **derive gas physical conditions via LTE fitting**: Extract spectra inside lowest 1.3 mm continuum contour (and two 0.2'' outflow boxes in IRAS 21078); identify species with SLIM; LTE fit with AUTOFIT of MADCUBA, single component per species  
  > "we derived the spectral emission in the direction of the YSO by integrating the signal over the area inside the lowest 1.3 mm continuum contour" (Physical conditions); "we used the tool AUTOFIT of MADCUBA, which compares the observed spectra with the LTE synthetic spectra, taking into account all transitions and the line opacities" (Physical conditions)
   - software: MADCUBA (SLIM, AUTOFIT)
   - parameter: Free parameters: N_col, T_ex, v0, FWHM  
  > "Leaving four parameters free to vary, that is, the column density" (Physical conditions)
   - parameter: Species with optical depths below 0.25  
  > "that in both targets have optical depths of less (or much less) than 0.25" (Physical conditions)
   - assumption: LTE; single component, negligible density and temperature gradients  
  > "We warn that a single component was fit for each molecular species, under the assumption of negligible density and temperature gradients." (Physical conditions)
   - fitting: Levenberg-Marquardt nonlinear least squares; errors from covariance matrix diagonal at chi2 minimum  
  > "AUTOFIT provides the best nonlinear least-squares fit using the Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm" (Physical conditions); "Nominal errors of the fit parameters were derived from the diagonal elements of the covariance matrix calculated at the" (Physical conditions)
   - validation: Visual comparison of fits to unblended lines (Figs. 6–8)  
  > "The emission profile of each transition is reasonably well fit with MADCUBA" (Physical conditions)
4. **construct PV plots along disk midplane**: PV cuts along disk midplane, emission averaged across a beam perpendicular to the cut; off-disk PV plots of SO along rotation axis for comparison  
  > "Before producing the PV plot, the emission was averaged across a beam in the direction perpendicular to the positional cut." (Discussion); "These off-disk PV plots show velocity profiles consistent with that at the disk midplane, confirming that the SO emission traces mainly the wind kinematics." (IRAS 21078+5211)
   - parameter: averaging width ≈250 au (IRAS 21078)  
  > "were obtained by averaging the emission perpendicularly to the disk midplane across a distance of" (IRAS 21078+5211)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Off-disk PV plots consistent with midplane PV  
  > "are fully consistent with each other, which ensures that SO is tracing the rotation profile of the gas in the wind" (G035.02+0.35)
5. **rule out envelope rotation in IRAS 21078+5211**: Centrifugal equilibrium estimate of mass needed to produce transverse velocity gradient across green strip (ΔV≈6 km/s over ΔR≈0.2'' at |Z|≈0.3'')  
  > "Assuming centrifugal equilibrium we can write" (IRAS 21078+5211); "we derive a value for the YSO mass" (IRAS 21078+5211)
   - assumption: |Z|^2 >> |R|^2 along green strip; distance from Table 1

## Qualitative findings

- In G035.02+0.35 all lines except SO are flattened along the disk major axis and trace disk rotation with Keplerian PV profiles; SO extends well outside the disk  
  > "SO is the only molecular species whose emission extends well outside the disk." (abstract)
- In IRAS 21078+5211 molecular emission is elongated along the jet with a velocity gradient transverse to the jet axis; SO PV shows Keplerian rotation  
  > "Position-velocity (PV) plots of the SO lines show patterns consistent with Keplerian rotation." (abstract)
- SO2 is compact, closest to the rotation axis, with steepest velocity-position profile in both YSOs  
  > "emerges from gas seen in projection close to the disk rotation axis that yields the steepest profile of velocity with position" (G035.02+0.35)
- SiO 5–4 resolved out at these scales  
  > "another potential outflow tracer, is resolved out at these small scales" (Structure and kinematics)
- SO2 Tex systematically higher than SO at all positions in IRAS 21078+5211  
  > "is found to be systematically higher than that of SO at all the explored positions" (IRAS 21078+5211)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: NOEMA and ALMA datasets overlap in frequency with comparable ≈250 au and ≈0.5 km/s resolution, enabling same-tracer comparison  
  > "which is optimal for a comparison of the gas kinematics between the two sources" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Lines selected for intensity and well-defined kinematic structure; optically thin species with wide Eu range for Tex  
  > "cover a sufficiently wide range in excitation energy to allow for a reliable estimate of the excitation temperature" (Physical conditions)
- Alternative: Envelope rotation induced by YSO gravity rejected for IRAS 21078+5211  
  > "We can reasonably rule out that the observed" (IRAS 21078+5211)
- Alternative: X-wind entrainment rejected for G035.02+0.35  
  > "That already rules out the possibility that we are observing ambient gas entrained in a fast wide-angle X-wind" (G035.02+0.35)
- Alternative: Photoevaporative disk wind disfavored by high maser velocities  
  > "favor a MHD DW rather than a photoevaporated DW" (G035.02+0.35)
- Alternative: Emission reflecting primary wind properties instead of local shocks considered  
  > "alternatively, they could mainly reflect the properties of the original gas of the primary wind" (IRAS 21078+5211)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single-component LTE fits neglecting gradients  
  > "We warn that a single component was fit for each molecular species" (Physical conditions)
- Limitation (authors): Insufficient resolution to resolve G035 disk vertically  
  > "That is certainly due to insufficient angular resolution to resolve the disk structure vertically" (G035.02+0.35)
- Limitation (authors): SiO resolved out  
  > "is resolved out at these small scales" (Structure and kinematics)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Keplerian nature of PV plots asserted visually without fitted rotation curves or mass from the ALMA data itself
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison with shock models is qualitative; no chemical modelling of the observed abundances
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass for G035 taken from another paper (Olg26) rather than derived here
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SO optical depths reach τmax 5–16, so LTE SO results are less reliable
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sky-projected disk/outflow geometry and inclination effects not quantified
