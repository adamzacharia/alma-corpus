2. **extract and fit molecular spectra (T_ex, N_col, V_LSR, FWHM)**: Spectra integrated inside the lowest 1.3 mm continuum contour for the YSO; outflow positions extracted for IRAS 21078+5211; LTE AUTOFIT fits in MADCUBA  
  > "we derived the spectral emission in the direction of the YSO by integrating the signal over the area inside the lowest 1.3 mm continuum contour" (Physical conditions); "To derive the physical parameters of molecular gas, we used the tool AUTOFIT of MADCUBA" (Physical conditions)

   - parameter: Four free parameters: N_col, T_ex, V_LSR, FWHM; single component per species  
  > "Leaving four parameters free to vary, that is, the column density" (Physical conditions)
   - parameter: CH3CN, CH3OH, SO2 selected (plus SO and HC3N for IRAS 21078+5211)  
  > "We selected the CH$_3$CN, CH$_3$OH, and SO$_2$ molecules (see Table [3](#mol_fit))" (Physical conditions)

  > "We warn that a single component was fit for each molecular species, under the assumption of negligible density and temperature gradients." (Physical conditions)

## Qualitative findings

- In IRAS 21078+5211, molecular emissions are elongated along the jet and trace a V_LSR gradient transverse to the jet axis; SO and SO2 show Keplerian-like PV profiles  
  > "Position-velocity (PV) plots of the SO lines show patterns consistent with Keplerian rotation." (abstract)
- In G035.02+0.35, only SO extends well outside the disk; other molecules are confined to the disk  
  > "In G035.02$+$0.35, only SO traces the wind, while the other molecular emissions are confined inside the disk." (Conclusions)
- SO2 is spatially compact and yields the steepest velocity-position profile in both sources  
  > "while SO$_2$ is spatially compact and yields the steepest velocity-position profile." (Conclusions)
- The IRAS 21078+5211 transverse V_LSR gradient is attributed to wind rotation rather than envelope rotation  
  > "has to be linked instead with the rotation of the wind." (Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: To test the water-maser MHD disk-wind predictions with thermal tracers at 100-1000 au in two YSOs of different mass  
  > "The goal of this article is to further test the water maser MHD DW predictions focusing on the YSOs IRAS 21078$+$5211 and G035.02$+$0.35" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Same molecular tracers and comparable linear and velocity resolution in both sources  
  > "which allows us to investigate the two sources by employing the same molecular tracers." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Compact X-wind with entrained ambient gas, rejected for G035.02+0.35 because only SO traces the wind  
  > "That already rules out the possibility that we are observing ambient gas entrained in a fast wide-angle X-wind" (Discussion)
- Alternative: Photoevaporated disk wind, disfavoured by high maser velocities of 20 km/s or more  
  > "The high maser velocities, $\ge$20 km s$^{-1}$, favor a MHD DW rather than a photoevaporated DW" (Discussion)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single LTE component per species with no density or temperature gradients assumed  
  > "We warn that a single component was fit for each molecular species, under the assumption of negligible density and temperature gradients." (Physical conditions)
- Limitation (authors): Fit errors are nominal, from the covariance matrix  
  > "Nominal errors of the fit parameters were derived from the diagonal elements of the covariance matrix" (Physical conditions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fitted T_ex and N_col for CH3CN and CH3OH in the disk region may mix disk and wind gas, so the outflow-position comparison depends on positional choices
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 44 Msun mass estimate depends on inclination and the assumed geometry and is an order-of-magnitude argument
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The G035.02+0.35 ionization and UV-flux argument for missing molecules is qualitative and not tested with a model
- Caveat (inferred, ours): NOEMA and ALMA data differ in sensitivity and array, so the line comparison is not fully like-for-like
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison with shock models is qualitative; no model fits to the observed abundances are presented
