## Analysis recipe

1. **Image a line-search cube from all four tunings**: tclean cube with natural weighting, 30 km/s channels, 0.1 arcsec pixels, 30x30 arcsec image; deconvolution to 3x rms with niter 500  
  > "To search the \[C ii\]$\,158\,\rm{\mu m}$ emission line, we created a data cube combining all four tunings" (Imaging data cube); "we performed the synthesized beam deconvolution using a fixed stopping threshold of three times the pixel-by-pixel root mean square (RMS) for all channels and the maximum iteration of 500 times." (Imaging data cube)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting; 30 km/s binning; 0.1 arcsec pixel; 30x30 arcsec image  
  > "using `NATURAL` weighting scheme, a spectral binning of $30\,\rm{km/s}$, a pixel scale of $0.1^{\prime\prime}$, and an image size of $30^{\prime\prime}\times30^{\prime\prime}$" (Imaging data cube)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Conservative threshold does not affect products because no bright sources in this binning  
  > "The conservative deconvolution threshold does not affect the final data products" (Imaging data cube)
2. **Detect [C ii] and measure the spectroscopic redshift**: Search for the line using the rest-frame UV position as a spatial prior  
  > "Using the location of the rest-frame UV emission as a prior of the spatial position of \[C ii\]" (Imaging data cube)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Line frequency 263.6995 +/- 0.004 GHz  
  > "at a frequency of $263.6995\pm0.004\,{\rm GHz}$" (Imaging data cube)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with prior photometric redshift estimates  
  > "the result is consistent with previous photometric redshift estimates (cite: Salmon2020; Weaver2022)." (Conclusion)
3. **Image the dust continuum and derive an IR luminosity limit**: Multi-frequency-synthesis continuum image excluding the spw with [C ii]; 3 sigma upper limit from pixel rms; modified blackbody to L_IR; SFR_IR and obscured fraction  
  > "we estimated the $3\,\sigma$ upper limit of the rest-frame $158\,\rm{\mu m}$ continuum of MACS0308-zD1 as $<27\,\rm{\mu Jy}$, adopting $3\times$ the pixel-by-pixel RMS." (Dust Continuum); "using a stopping threshold of $27\,\rm{\mu Jy/beam}$ (i.e., $3\times$ the pixel-by-pixel RMS of the dirty image) and by setting the maximum number of iteration to 500 times." (Imaging continuum map)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Dust temperature 45 K (also 60 K and 80 K tested), beta = 1.8, mu = 20  
  > "Assuming a dust temperature of $\sim45\,\rm{K}$ and $\beta=1.8$ (cite: Bethermin2020; Sommovigo2022) for a modified black body emission" (Dust Continuum); "For a high dust temperature case of $60\,\rm{K}$, the de-magnified $3\,\sigma$ upper limit IR luminosity is $5.5\times10^{9}\,\rm{L_{\odot}}$" (Dust-obscured Star-Formation Activity)
   - assumption: Dust emission co-spatial with the galaxy; dust temperature is the major uncertainty  
  > "Assuming that the dust emission is co-spatial with MACS0308-zD1" (Dust-obscured Star-Formation Activity); "The major uncertainty in estimating the IR luminosity from a single ALMA observation is the unknown dust temperature" (Dust-obscured Star-Formation Activity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Only ~2 sigma offset signal, below the >3.5 sigma threshold, so treated as non-detection; dust temperature variants tested  
  > "there is only a $\sim2\,\sigma$ positive signal close to but offset from the rest-frame UV position of the MACS0308-zD1" (Dust Continuum)
4. **Extract [C ii] moment-0 map, spectrum and total flux**: Iterative moment-0 and masked spectrum extraction until the channel range converges; total flux from 2D Gaussian fit with imfit  
  > "we used pixels that had $>2\,\sigma$ signals in the moment-0 map, and extracted integrated spectra that were again used to create moment-0 map using channels having continuous positive signals." ([C ii] 158 um emission line); "using CASA task `imfit`; 2-dimensional Gaussian fitting." ([C ii] 158 um emission line)
   - software: CASA, imfit
   - parameter: Mask: pixels >2 sigma in the moment-0 map  
  > "we used pixels that had $>2\,\sigma$ signals in the moment-0 map" ([C ii] 158 um emission line)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit (imfit) to the integrated moment-0 map  
  > "using CASA task `imfit`; 2-dimensional Gaussian fitting." ([C ii] 158 um emission line)
   - validation: Iteration repeated until the channels used converged (a few iterations)  
  > "The procedures are repeated until the channels used to create moment-0 map converged." ([C ii] 158 um emission line)
5. **Decompose the [C ii] line into narrow and broad components**: Least-squares two-component Gaussian fit to the integrated 1-D spectrum; component moment-0 maps from separate frequency ranges  
  > "we performed a least square fitting using a two component Gaussian function to the integrated 1-D spectrum of the \[C ii\] emission." (Multi-Component Analysis of the [C ii] line); "by integrating frequency ranges of $263.62$ to $263.72\,\rm{GHz}$ and $263.73$ to $263.92\,\rm{GHz}$, respectively." (Multi-Component Analysis of the [C ii] line)
   - parameter: Narrow map 263.62-263.72 GHz; broad map 263.73-263.92 GHz  
  > "$263.62$ to $263.72\,\rm{GHz}$ and $263.73$ to $263.92\,\rm{GHz}$" (Multi-Component Analysis of the [C ii] line)
   - fitting: Least-squares two-Gaussian fit (narrow + broader); no sampler or priors stated  
  > "We found that the best fitting results arose from the combination of a narrow and a broader Gaussian" (Multi-Component Analysis of the [C ii] line)
   - validation: Residuals consistent with random noise; cross-contamination between component maps estimated at ~7% and ~8%  
  > "This resulted in a reasonable fit with only small residuals that were consistent with random noise." (Multi-Component Analysis of the [C ii] line); "we estimated that $\sim7\,\%$ of the narrow \[C ii\] component contaminates the broad image, while $\sim8\%$ of the broader \[C ii\] component contaminates the narrow \[C ii\] moment-0 maps." (Multi-Component Analysis of the [C ii] line)
6. **Derive L[CII], molecular gas mass and gas surface density**: L[CII] to M_H2 with the Zanella et al. method; Sigma_H2 assuming the clump radius r = 27 pc  
  > "Using the method presented in (cite: Zanella2018), we estimated separately the molecular gas masses" (Molecular Gas Mass); "assuming the size of the star-forming region is $r=27\,\rm{pc}$ (cite: Welch2023)" (Molecular Gas Mass)
   - parameter: Lensing magnification mu = 22 applied to the Table values (except flux densities)  
  > "The measured values (except for the \[C ii\] flux densities) are corrected for gravitational lensing magnification of $\mu=22$." (Table 1)
   - assumption: Narrow [C ii] associated with the UV-bright clump of radius 27 pc  
  > "The most extreme case is found by assuming that the narrow \[C ii\] is associated with the UV-bright component MACS0308-zD1.1" (Molecular Gas Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Compare with SFR-L[CII] relations and test virial and outflow interpretations**: Place zD1.1 on the SFR-L[CII] plane using SFR_UV = 3+/-1 and negligible SFR_IR; virial velocity dispersion sqrt(GM/3r) for the narrow component  
  > "Here, we assume that the $\rm{SFR_{IR}}$ is negligible, given the faint IR luminosity upper limits and low SFR$_{\rm IR}$s found in" (SFR-[C ii] correlation); "the line-of-sight velocity dispersion of a gas cloud will be $\sqrt{{\rm G} M/3r} = 167\,\rm{km/s}$, assuming virial equilibrium" ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)
   - parameter: M_H2 = 5.1e8 Msun, r = 27 pc  
  > "Assuming a gas mass of $M_{\rm H2}\,{\rm/M_{\odot}}=5.1\times10^{8}$ and a radius of $r=27\,{\rm pc}$" ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)
   - assumption: Virial equilibrium for the narrow component  
  > "assuming virial equilibrium" ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Predicted 167 km/s consistent with measured narrow line width  
  > "This value is consistent with the measured line width of the narrow component" ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)

## Qualitative findings

- The [C ii] emission appears to be a narrow component co-spatial with the UV-bright clump plus a broader, blueshifted and spatially offset component  
  > "a narrow FWHM $\sim110\,\rm{km/s}$ \[C ii\] that is spatially coincident with the UV-bright star-forming region" ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)
- No dust continuum detected, so star formation is almost dust-free  
  > "meaning that the star formation activity of MACS0308-zD1 is almost completely dust-free." (Dust-obscured Star-Formation Activity)
- zD1.1 is consistent with the non-evolving SFR-L[CII] relation  
  > "our observation supports a non-evolving picture for the SFR-$L_{\rm [CII]}$ correlation" (SFR-[C ii] correlation)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Strong lensing lets ALMA study faint low-mass high-z galaxies in feasible time  
  > "One method to push to fainter limits is to adopt strongly lensed galaxies to investigate the detailed properties of high-redshift, low-mass galaxies in a feasible observing time" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Four tunings were used to scan the full range allowed by the photometric redshift probability  
  > "To efficiently scan the frequency range, the observations used four separate spectral tunings (T1 to T4;" (ALMA observations)
- Alternative: Broad [C ii] could instead be fragmented cold-stream gas clumps (inflow) rather than an outflow  
  > "An alternative interpretation of the broad \[C ii\] component would be gas clumps generated as a result of the fragmentation of cold gas streams (i.e., inflow)." ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Both [C ii] components are unresolved or only marginally resolved at ~1 arcsec resolution  
  > "both components of the \[C ii\] emission are either unresolved or only marginally resolved" (Molecular Gas Mass)
- Limitation (authors): Dust temperature is the major uncertainty in the IR luminosity from one ALMA band  
  > "The major uncertainty in estimating the IR luminosity from a single ALMA observation is the unknown dust temperature" (Dust-obscured Star-Formation Activity)
- Limitation (authors): Virial velocity of the broad component is uncertain without a size measurement  
  > "Although the estimated virial velocity of the cloud is uncertain without a confident size measurement" ([C ii] outflow or another structure?)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Magnification is mu = 20 in the dust section and mu = 22 in the Table and Conclusion, with no source for 22
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 60 K SFR_IR limit (<0.3 Msun/yr) does not match 1.2e-10 x 5.5e9 Lsun (~0.7), so the conversion used is unclear
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Narrow and broad FWHM are quoted as 110 +/- 20 and 230 +/- 20 in the abstract/Table but 110 +/- 19 and 233 +/- 19 in the text
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Broad-component significance (~4.2 sigma) and the spatial offset rest on a ~1 arcsec beam; the outflow reading is not tested against inflow
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sigma_H2 of 5.34 dex uses the UV clump radius of 27 pc, far below the beam, so it is an extreme upper bound on density
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The single-clump SFR_UV and no-evolution conclusion rest on one data point
