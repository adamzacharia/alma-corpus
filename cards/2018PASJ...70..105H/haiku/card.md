## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and combine ALMA visibilities**: Averaged ASAGAO data and archival Cycle 1–2 and Cycle 3 visibilities to 32 channels and 10.08 s, relabelled archival coordinates to ICRS, and combined the data sets  
  > "we average the data in frequency and time directions with 32 channels" (Data Reduction); "we relabel the coordinates of Cycle 1 and 2 data from J2000.0 to the International Celestial Reference System (ICRS)" (Combined Map)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 32 channels (Δν = 0.5 GHz); 10.08 s averaging  
  > "we average the data in frequency and time directions with 32 channels" (Data Reduction)
   - assumption: Bandwidth smearing from channel averaging negligible (<1%)  
  > "less than 1% even at the edge of the primary beam" (Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Time averaging effect checked with bandpass calibrator imaging  
  > "We also confirm that the effect of the time averaging on the flux density is negligible based on the imaging of the bandpass calibrator." (Data Reduction)
2. **make the combined continuum map**: Imaging with CASA using the same parameters as the ASAGAO map, 250 kλ uv-taper, representative frequency 243.047 GHz  
  > "The representative frequency of the map is 243.047 GHz (1.23 mm)." (Combined Map)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: uv-taper 250 kλ; alternative untapered and 160 kλ maps  
  > "Maps without $uv$-taper" (Combined Map)
   - assumption: _not stated_
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Maps with different tapers used to check whether sources are spatially resolved  
  > "were also created to see whether detected sources are spatially resolved" (Combined Map)
3. **detect sources and measure flux densities**: Aegean source finding on the signal map (before primary beam correction) with BANE background/noise; 2D elliptical Gaussian fits; S_int = S_peak (ab)/(θmaj θmin), with S_peak adopted when S_int < S_peak  
  > "We adopt the source-finding algorithm called Aegean (cite: hanc12; hanc18)" (Source Detection); "We adopt $S_{\rm int}$ as the source flux density." (Source Detection)
   - software: Aegean, BANE
   - parameter: Peak SN thresholds 5σ and 4.5σ; 45 sources at 4.5σ used for number counts  
  > "We find 25 (45) sources with a peak SN of" (Source Detection)
   - assumption: When S_int < S_peak, S_peak adopted since source fitting may fail at low SN  
  > "When $S_{\rm int} < S_{\rm peak}$, we adopt $S_{\rm peak}$" (Source Detection)
   - fitting: 2D elliptical Gaussian fit per detected source  
  > "The detected sources are fitted with a 2D elliptical Gaussian to estimate the source size and integrated flux density." (Source Detection)
   - validation: Negative-peak counting as function of SN to estimate spurious detections; comparison with HUDF, GOODS-ALMA, ASPECS and AzTEC sources  
  > "we count the number of negative peaks as a function of SN threshold" (Source Detection)
4. **check astrometry and spurious-source contamination**: Cross-match 5σ sources with VLA 5-cm sources within 0.5'' radius; median positional offset compared with expected ~0.1'' uncertainty; negative peak statistics  
  > "The radio counterparts are found for 20 out of the 25 ASAGAO 5$\sigma$ sources within a 0.5$''$ search radius" (Astrometry)
   - parameter: Search radius 0.5''  
  > "within a 0.5$''$ search radius" (Astrometry)
   - assumption: Radio sources more suitable astrometric reference than optical sources  
  > "The radio sources are more suitable for evaluating the astrometry of the ALMA sources" (Astrometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Expected positional uncertainty ~0.1'' from ALMA and radio uncertainties  
  > "which is within the expected positional uncertainty between the ALMA and the radio sources" (Astrometry)
5. **completeness and flux boosting**: Inject 30000 artificial elliptical Gaussian sources at random positions with convolving sizes, recover with same extraction; completeness vs SN; ratio of input to output flux for boosting  
  > "We input 30000 artificial sources into the signal map one at a time" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - parameter: Convolving beam size uniformly distributed from 0.01'' to 0.5''  
  > "we take a convolving beam size to be uniformly distributed from" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - parameter: Input flux 0.05–2 mJy  
  > "each with an integrated flux density randomly selected from 0.05–2 mJy" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - assumption: Source sizes in the 0.08''–0.8'' range from SMG size measurements; input source sizes modelled by convolution  
  > "Recent ALMA measurements of source size of SMGs" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - fitting: Best-fit functions of completeness and boosting vs SN  
  > "the counts are divided by the completeness by using the best-fit function as a function of SN" (1.2mm Number Counts)
   - validation: Completeness consistent between central deep and outer regions; completeness 60% at SN 4.5, 100% at SN ≳ 7  
  > "The completeness is 60% at SN" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
6. **derive 1.2 mm differential and cumulative number counts**: dN/dS = (1/ΔS) Σ (1 − f_neg)/(A C) with Poisson 84.13% errors; Schechter fit to ASAGAO plus other ALMA counts and single-dish follow-up scaled to 1.2 mm  
  > "By using the 4.5$\sigma$ sources, we create differential and cumulative number counts." (1.2mm Number Counts)
   - parameter: Modified blackbody β = 1.5, dust temperature 35 K, z = 2 for flux scaling to 1.2 mm  
  > "Here we assume a modified black body with a dust emissivity index of $\beta = 1.5$, dust temperature of 35 K, and $z = 2$." (1.2mm Number Counts)
   - assumption: Negative fraction f_neg estimated from negative/positive peak ratio as function of SN  
  > "Figure [4](#fig:fdr_frac) shows the differential fraction of the number of negative peaks to positive peaks" (1.2mm Number Counts)
   - fitting: Schechter function fit to differential number counts  
  > "The differential number counts obtained in this study and previous studies are fitted to a Schechter function" (1.2mm Number Counts)
   - validation: Comparison with previous ALMA blank-field and serendipitous counts  
  > "We find that our number counts are consistent with those of the previous ALMA blank-field surveys." (Comparison with Previous ALMA Studies)
7. **resolved fraction of 1.2 mm EBL**: Integrate ASAGAO differential counts above 135 μJy and divide by Planck-based EBL at 1.2 mm  
  > "The integration of the ASAGAO differential number counts yields" (Contribution to Extragalactic Background Light)
   - parameter: Flux limit S_1.2mm > 135 μJy  
  > "($S_{\rm 1.2mm} > 135$ $\mu$Jy)" (Contribution to Extragalactic Background Light)
   - assumption: EBL from interpolation of Planck 217 and 353 GHz measurements  
  > "By interpolating the measurements at 217 and 353 GHz" (Contribution to Extragalactic Background Light)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Best-fit Schechter integration reaches 100% at ~20 μJy, noted as uncertain extrapolation  
  > "although we note that there is a large uncertainty to extend the function to the faint flux regime" (Contribution to Extragalactic Background Light)
8. **IR luminosity functions at z = 1–3**: Match 5σ sources to ZFOURGE K_S counterparts; SED fitting for IR luminosity and redshift; V_max method with ΔlogL = 0.6 and effective solid angle from local noise  
  > "The number of ASAGAO sources with ZFOURGE counterparts are 20 (80%) and 25 (56%) for 5$\sigma$ and 4.5$\sigma$ sources" (IR Luminosity Function of ASAGAO Sources)
   - software: magphys
   - parameter: Luminosity bin width Δlog(L) = 0.6  
  > "We adopt a luminosity bin width of" (IR Luminosity Function of ASAGAO Sources)
   - parameter: Redshift bins 1.0–2.0, 1.5–2.5, 2.0–3.0 with bin width 1.0  
  > "To increase the number of sources in each redshift bin, we adopt the bin width of 1.0" (IR Luminosity Function of ASAGAO Sources)
   - assumption: SED templates of Bruzual & Charlot and dust extinction of Charlot & Fall  
  > "The SED templates of (cite: bruz03) and the dust extinction model of (cite: char00) are adopted." (IR Luminosity Function of ASAGAO Sources)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; cosmology H0=70, Ωm=0.3, ΩΛ=0.7  
  > "Throughout the paper, we adopt a cosmology with $H_0=70$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$" (Introduction)
   - fitting: Schechter fit to 1.5<z<2.5 LF combined with Koprowski et al. data, alpha free; Poisson 84.13% errors  
  > "We fit the IR LFs at $1.5 < z < 2.5$ obtained from the ASAGAO sources and the results of (cite: kopr17)" (Comparison with Previous Studies)
   - validation: Consistency with Koprowski et al. LFs and Herschel LFs compared  
  > "We find that the ASAGAO LFs are consistent with those of (cite: kopr17) within the errors" (Comparison with Previous Studies)
9. **cosmic SFRD contribution at z~2**: Integrate best-fit LF down to lowest luminosity of sources; convert IR luminosity to SFR  
  > "The integration of the best-fitting luminosity function down to the lowest luminosity of the sources" (Contribution to the Cosmic SFR Density)
   - parameter: log(L_IR/Lsun) = 11.78 lower limit  
  > "($\log{(L_{\rm IR}/L_{\odot})} = 11.78$)" (Contribution to the Cosmic SFR Density)
   - assumption: SFR from IR via Kennicutt 1998 relation, Chabrier IMF  
  > "SFR is converted from IR luminosity by using the relation of (cite: kenn98) and corrected to a (cite: chab03) IMF." (Contribution to the Cosmic SFR Density)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with yama18 SFRD from individual sources  
  > "This is consistent with the results of (cite: yama18)" (Contribution to the Cosmic SFR Density)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Contiguous unbiased survey avoids biases of serendipitous detections around targets  
  > "It is necessary to conduct “unbiased” surveys in a contiguous field rather than collecting discrete fields" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Aegean chosen for reliability and completeness in radio maps  
  > "which achieves high reliability and completeness performance for radio maps" (Source Detection)
- Alternative: ASPECS archival data rejected because its synthesized beam differs strongly from the others  
  > "We do not use the data set of ASPECS, where the synthesized beam size" (ALMA Archival Data)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Large LF uncertainties from small sample and limited luminosity coverage; cannot constrain evolution well  
  > "the large uncertainties of our LFs due to the small sample size and the limited coverage of IR luminosity" (Comparison with Previous Studies)
- Limitation (authors): Schechter extrapolation to faint flux highly uncertain  
  > "there is a large uncertainty to extend the function to the faint flux regime" (Contribution to Extragalactic Background Light)
