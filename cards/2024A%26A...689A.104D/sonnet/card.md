## Analysis recipe

1. **Self-calibrate and image Band 8 continuum**: Flag lines, average, fix phase center, rescale fluxes, concatenate, self-calibrate, tclean  
  > "Finally, the three datasets were concatenated." (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5, cell 0.02", multiscale 0,0.06,0.16,0.24"  
  > "We cleaned using multi-scale, with components of 0”, 0.06”, 0.16” and 0.24”." (3.1 Continuum image)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: S/N improved ~36% after self-cal  
  > "The final dataset[^1] showed a S/N improvement of $\sim$ 36% compared to the initial dataset." (2 Observations and data reduction)
2. **Non-parametric visibility fit with frank**: FrankFitter Normal and logNormal, subtracting 20 mJy point source  
  > "We subtracted 20 mJy from the real part of our visibilities, assuming it to be the flux density of the point source." (3.2.1 Non-parametric modeling with frank)
   - software: Frankenstein
   - parameter: alpha={1.05,1.2,1.3}, wsmooth={1e-1,1e-4}, Rmax=5.0", p0=1e-35, 300 radial points  
  > "R_{max} = 5.0''$, $p_0 = 10^{-35}$ with 300 radial points" (3.2.1 Non-parametric modeling with frank)
   - assumption: azimuthally symmetric, geometrically thin, optically thick disk  
  > "it assumes that the emission originates from a geometrically thin and optically thick disk" (3.2.1 Non-parametric modeling with frank)
   - fitting: logNormal with alpha=1.3, wsmooth=1e-4 preferred  
  > "We find that the logNormal fit with $\alpha$ = 1.3 and w$_{smooth}$ = 10$^{-4}$ gives the most consistent results" (3.2.1 Non-parametric modeling with frank)
   - validation: Shoulder flux robust to alpha and wsmooth  
  > "is robust against changes in the $\alpha$ and $w_{smooth}$ values" (3.2.1 Non-parametric modeling with frank)
3. **Parametric visibility fit (3 Gaussians) with galario+emcee**: One central Gaussian plus two Gaussian rings, 10 free parameters, inclination and PA fixed  
  > "Thus, a total of 10 parameters were fit with the MCMC algorithm." (3.2.2 Parametric modeling)
   - software: galario, emcee
   - parameter: 80 walkers, 10,000 steps after 2000 burn-in  
  > "We run our MCMC sampler with 80 walkers, for a total of 10,000 steps after a burn-in of 2000 steps." (MCMC fitting and results)
   - assumption: axisymmetric; i=5.8 deg, PA=151.6 deg fixed  
  > "In our model, we assumed that the TW Hya disk is azimuthally symmetric." (3.2.2 Parametric modeling)
   - fitting: emcee, uniform priors (e.g. log I in [-7,2], R2 in [1.16",1.66"], offsets in [-2",2"]); best fit = max likelihood  
  > "We assume our parameters to have the following uniform priors" (MCMC fitting and results)
   - validation: Residual/data amplitude ratio ~0.0013; model recovers 99.88% of peak  
  > "The model describes 99.88% of the original peak intensity showing that our axisymmetric model is well suited to describe the 0.65 mm disk." (3.2.2 Parametric modeling)
4. **Azimuthal intensity profiles and disk sizes for Bands 3-7**: Deproject, azimuthally average, cumulative profiles for R68/R95/R99, substructure location/width/contrast  
  > "We also calculate an equivalent radius by averaging all the radial points within the bin." (Appendix C)
   - parameter: uncertainty u = rms/(2 sqrt(n))  
  > "u = \frac{rms}{2\sqrt{n}}." (Appendix C)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Spectral index of outer ring**: Fit F_nu ∝ nu^alpha to outer-ring flux across bands; ring flux from imstat masks (Bands 3-7) and Gaussian integral (Band 8); 0.87-1.3 mm radial spectral index profile  
  > "This is done with the `imstat` task in CASA." (3.4.1 Spectral index)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 10% flux calibration uncertainty  
  > "the 10% uncertainty in the total flux calibration considered in our analysis of the spectral index" (2 Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Dust masses**: Optically thin blackbody mass formula M = D^2 F/(kappa B(T))  
  > "The dust masses of the B86 ring at various frequencies were calculated under the assumption of optically thin emission" (3.4.2 Dust mass)
   - assumption: kappa = 2.3 cm2/g (nu/230 GHz)^0.4, T_dust = 20 K, D=59.5 pc  
  > "$T_{dust}$ = 20 K and D is the distance from the TW Hya disk in pc." (3.4.2 Dust mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Andrews 2016 (~80 Mearth) total dust mass  
  > "This is consistent with the dust mass ($\sim$ 80 $M_\oplus$) from (cite: andrews2016_twhya)" (3.4.2 Dust mass)

## Qualitative findings

- Outer disk emission at 60-110 au found at 0.65, 0.87 and 1.3 mm but not at 2.1 and 3.1 mm  
  > "However, we do not recover any outer-disk emission at 2.1 mm and 3.1 mm to the noise level." (3.3 Image-plane retrieval)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Azimuthally averaged profiles showed no substructure at Band 8, so visibility-plane fitting was used  
  > "On azimuthally averaging the intensity obtained from the continuum image, we were not able to detect any substructures." (3.2 Characterizing the continuum emission)
- Alternative: Normal frank method rejected in favor of logNormal for fewer noise artifacts  
  > "We direct our focus to the logNormal profiles since they contain fewer noise-induced artifacts." (3.2.1 Non-parametric modeling with frank)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Band 8 resolution insufficient to resolve the substructure; large location uncertainties  
  > "higher angular resolution ALMA Band 8 observations (at least $\sim$ 0.1”, 6 au), with high sensitivity and S/N, are required to resolve the separation and morphology of this substructure." (3.2.2 Parametric modeling)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outer ring at Band 8 is only ~1 sigma; Gaussian ring model choice drives detection
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Frank logNormal shows a shoulder rather than a ring, model dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Optically thin single-temperature (20 K) mass is uncertain; opacity law not tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spectral index mixes image-plane and model-derived fluxes at different resolutions
