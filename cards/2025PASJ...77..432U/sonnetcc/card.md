## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA and JVLA data and reduce JWST NIRCam**: ALMA data reduced in CASA (6.5.0 or 6.1.0) and imaged with tclean per project; JVLA calibrated with the CASA VLA pipeline and imaged with tclean; NIRCam reduced with the JWST pipeline 1.8.4 following the CEERS recipe  
  > "Data reduction was performed using version 6.5.0. of the Common Astronomy Software Applications (CASA) package." (ALMA observations); "Data reduction was performed using the JWST calibration pipeline (v.1.8.4), following the recipe provided by the CEERS team (cite: 2023ApJ...946L..12B)." (JWST NIRCam imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean, JWST calibration pipeline
   - parameter: [CII] high-res: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "After continuum subtraction the data were mapped with the tclean task, adopting Briggs weighting (robust = 0.5)." (ALMA observations)
   - parameter: [CII] low-res: natural weighting with uvtaper = 0.75 arcsec  
  > "Imaging used the the tclean task adopting natural weighting and uvtaper=0.75$^{\prime\prime}$, to trace extended emission" (ALMA observations)
   - parameter: NIRCam: snowball removal, wisp subtraction, 1/f noise subtraction; PSFs matched to F444W  
  > "We applied “snowball” removal, wisp subtraction and “1/f” noise subtraction in addition to the standard reduction." (JWST NIRCam imaging); "The point spread function (PSF) of each image was matched to that of the F444W image." (JWST NIRCam imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Align JWST and ALMA astrometry**: Align the four NIRCam bands to Gaia stars, then match NIRCam F444W to ALMA 870 um by profile fit, minimizing systematic offsets between peaks  
  > "We matched the ALMA and JWST images minimizing the systematic offsets between the peaks measured with the both images." (Astrometry)
   - parameter: Nominal ALMA Band-7 positional accuracy 11 mas  
  > "Then we adopt the nominal positional accuracy for a compact source of 11 mas, for the Band-7 data" (Astrometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Both images match within the F444W pixel scale (0.063 arcsec/pix)  
  > "Consequently both images match within a pixel scale of the JWST F444W image" (Astrometry)
3. **Measure integrated fluxes and fit the panchromatic SED**: Elliptical-aperture NIRCam fluxes and curve-of-growth 870 um flux added to the existing X-ray-to-IR SED, refit with lightning to get SFR, M*, M_BH, Eddington ratio  
  > "Total flux densities of ADF22.A1 in the NIRCam images were measured with an elliptical aperture" (Panchromatic SED Fit); "We improve the fit by including the NIRCam and ALMA measurements." (Panchromatic SED Fit)
   - software: lightning
   - fitting: lightning SED fit simultaneously constraining star-formation heated dust and AGN dust emission; priors and sampler not stated  
  > "This code allows us to simultaneously constrain emission from star-formation heated dust and AGN dust emission." (Panchromatic SED Fit)
   - validation: Estimates generally consistent with previous fits  
  > "The estimates are generally consistent with the previous ones." (Panchromatic SED Fit)
4. **Fit Sersic profiles to stellar, dust and [CII] emission**: galfit Sersic fit to F444W; Sersic fit to the 0.2 arcsec 870 um map (5 sigma peaks masked); Sersic fit to the [CII] moment-0 map extracted with 3DBarolo (no bright-emission mask)  
  > "We performed Sérsic profile fitting using the F444W image using galfit" (Profile Fit); "We fit a Sérsic profile without a bright emission mask and obtained $r_{\rm e, [CII]}=7.6\pm0.2$ kpc and Sérsic index $n=0.4\pm0.1$." (Profile Fit)
   - software: galfit, 3DBarolo
   - parameter: 3DBarolo search: SNRCUT=4.5, GROWTHCUT=4.5, MINCHANNELS=3 on a cube with 40 km/s bins  
  > "The genuine emission was identified with the search task with SNRCUT=4.5, GROWTHCUT=4.5, and MINCHANNELS=3." (Profile Fit)
   - parameter: 870 um fit masked 5 sigma peaks  
  > "We masked $5\sigma$ peaks as presented in (cite: 2019ApJ...876..130H)" (Profile Fit)
   - assumption: F444W (rest-frame ~1 um) taken as a tracer of stellar mass distribution  
  > "which is expected to be sensitive to stellar mass distribution." (Profile Fit)
   - fitting: Sersic profile fits (galfit); sampler and priors not stated  
  > "The best-fit model and residula images are shown in Fig." (Profile Fit)
   - validation: Residual images inspected; models leave residuals from core, clumps and spiral arms  
  > "We note that the models still leave residuals, which demonstrate the power of high-resolution images at the 0.2$^{\prime\prime}$ resolution." (Profile Fit)
5. **Model [CII] kinematics and derive rotation curve**: 3DBarolo rotating-disk fits to the 0.2 arcsec cube (40 km/s bins, Hanning smoothed) and to a 0.9 arcsec smoothed cube with parameters fixed to the high-res medians; spacepar used to constrain velocity dispersion; arctangent model fitted to the 0.9 arcsec rotation curve  
  > "The $^{\rm 3D}$barolo package was utilized to model the" (Kinematic modeling); "We fit the rotation curve based on the 0.9$^{\prime\prime}$ cube with an arctangent model:" (Kinematic modeling)
   - software: 3DBarolo
   - parameter: Free: kinematic centre, PA, inclination, Vsys, Vrot, sigma; scale height fixed to 300 pc  
  > "were free-parameters. The scale-height of the disc was fixed to be 300 pc" (Kinematic modeling)
   - parameter: Ring width 0.097 arcsec (0.2 arcsec cube) and 0.35 arcsec (0.9 arcsec cube)  
  > "We adopted a ring width of $0.097^{\prime\prime}$, which is half of the resolution element" (Kinematic modeling); "We adopt a ring width of 0.$^{\prime\prime}$35, which is 40% of the resolution element" (Kinematic modeling)
   - parameter: spacepar grid: Vrot 1-700 km/s, sigma 1-100 km/s, steps of 2 km/s  
  > "The explored parameter space is a range of $V_{\rm rot}$ from 1 to 700 km s$^{-1}$ and $\sigma$ from 1 to 100 km s$^{-1}$, respectively." (Kinematic modeling); "Steps are 2 km s$^{-1}$ for each." (Kinematic modeling)
   - assumption: Rotation velocity approximately equals circular velocity (rotation supported, Vrot/sigma=8.8)  
  > "Based on this result, we assume that the rotation velocity is approximated equal to the circular velocity." (Kinematic modeling)
   - assumption: Non-axisymmetric gas distribution handled by renormalising flux per spatial pixel to the observed intensity map  
  > "a disk model with non-axisymmetric gas distributions was studied by renormalizing the flux density of each spatial pixel to the observed intensity map." (Kinematic modeling)
   - fitting: 3DBarolo disk fit; arctangent rotation curve v(r) = (2/pi) v_inf arctan(r/r_t); optimiser and priors not stated  
  > "The best fit model is also shown in Fig." (Kinematic modeling)
   - validation: PV diagrams of model vs observed; velocity dispersion checked with a 20 km/s-bin cube giving a similar range  
  > "This test resulted in a similar $\sigma$ range, which support the validity of the measurement with the original cube." (Kinematic modeling); "In this case, the model generally reproduces the observed emission." (Kinematic modeling)
6. **Derive stellar mass profile, B/T and specific stellar angular momentum**: Pixel-by-pixel magphys SED fit to F115W, F200W, F356W, F444W plus 0.2 arcsec 870 um map (F444W as detection mask); elliptical aperture photometry of the stellar mass map; bulge (Sersic n=3) + exponential disk fit; j* from integral of Sigma(r) v(r) r^2 over Sigma(r) r with the arctangent rotation curve  
  > "Utilizing the F444W image as a detection mask, we use the magphys code ((cite: 2012IAUS..284..292D)) to fit the SED of each pixel." (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum); "For the bulge, we adopt the Sérsic profile with $n=3$, while we assume an exponential profile for the disk." (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum); "To suppress the influence of non-circular motion in the bar region, we adopt the arctangent model as the rotation velocity profile." (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum)
   - software: magphys
   - parameter: Stellar mass per pixel = median of the distribution; 16th and 84th percentiles as bounds  
  > "We adopt the median of the stellar mass distribution as a representative value for a pixel, while lower and upper values are determined as 16th and 84th percentiles." (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum)
   - assumption: Gas kinematics trace stellar kinematics

## Qualitative findings

- Spiral structure visible in NIRCam; [CII] traces the stellar light and 870 um dust is distributed across the disk, not only in the core  
  > "Our images uncover spiral structure in the galaxy" (Resolved views in stellar, gas, and dust components); "occurs not only at the galactic center but across the whole disk." (Resolved views in stellar, gas, and dust components)
- A pair of curved dust lanes (offset ridges) around a bright dusty core indicates a bar; low-velocity non-circular [CII] components along the ridges indicate gas inflow  
  > "Thus the resolved structure traced by dust continuum shows that ADF22.A1 has a bar." (a bar traced in dust continuum); "Hence as summarized in Fig." (a bar traced in gas kinematics)
- AGN outflow is not dominating the [CII] kinematics  
  > "Thus observational evidences obtained so far suggest that any outflow from the AGN is not dominating the" (Contribution from AGNs)
- ADF22.A1 lies in the 'cold in hot' regime so cold-mode accretion can still supply gas and angular momentum  
  > "This suggests that while ADF22.A1 resides in a very massive halo, cold mode accretion can still survive to provide fuel and angular momentum for the galaxy." (Origin of the rapidly rotating, giant disk)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: JWST and ALMA together allow the dust-obscured structure and kinematics to be resolved  
  > "However, with the advent of the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) and ALMA, we can now resolve its structure and kinematics" (Introduction)
- Why this method: A 0.9 arcsec smoothed cube was added to detect outer emission and suppress central non-circular motions  
  > "which improves the detectability of emission in the outskirts and suppresses the deviations produced by the non-circular motions at the center." (Kinematic modeling)
- Alternative: Approximate j* from effective radius and rotation velocity (j_tilde ~5100) rejected in favour of the resolved profile, as it is ~50% larger  
  > "this approximation gives $\tilde{j_*}\simeq5100$ kpc km s$^{-1}$ which is $\sim50$ % larger than the estimate based on the resolved profile and rotation curve." (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum)
- Alternative: AGN biconical outflow as the origin of non-circular motion judged unlikely  
  > "we note that a typical biconical outflow which is perpendicular to the disk plane is unlikely to produce the non-circular motion." (Contribution from AGNs)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Stellar mass profile outside R~8 kpc is extrapolated for lack of sensitivity  
  > "is not sufficient to uncover the stellar mass surface distribution in outer parts due to the lack of sensitivity" (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum)
- Limitation (authors): CO(1-0) is not well resolved by the JVLA, so the Toomre Q rests on assumed profile  
  > "The CO(1–0) emission is not well resolved with JVLA" (Molecular gas mass and Toomre-Q paramter)
- Limitation (authors): Limited profile sampling prevents fitting a third (bar) component in the stellar profile  
  > "but the limited sampling of the profile prevent us from adding the third component." (Resolved stellar profile and specific stellar angular momentum)
- Limitation (authors): Limited NIRCam spatial coverage and few Gaia stars make absolute astrometry hard  
  > "The limited spatial coverage of the NIRCam image means only a small number of Gaia stars are available." (Astrometry)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas mass depends directly on the adopted alpha_CO = 2.5 and on an unresolved CO(1-0) profile assumed equal to [CII]; Toomre Q and f_gas inherit this.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): j* relies on gas rotation curve applied to stars, an extrapolated outer stellar profile and an assumed n=3 bulge; uncertainty budget is not described.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Bulge mass (B/T~0.2) comes from an n=3 decomposition with a possible unmodelled bar, so the M_BH/M_bulge comparison is uncertain.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The spiral/bar interpretation of 0.2 arcsec [CII] data with blended components is qualitative; no independent kinematic model of the bar is fitted.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single object; claims about general proto-cluster behaviour are extrapolations.
