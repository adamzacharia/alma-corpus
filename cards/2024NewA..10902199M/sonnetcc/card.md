  > "We have used the LTE-RADEX module in CASSIS for the LTE modelling (cite: vas15)." (3.2 Identification)
   - validation: Non-blended lines checked to be above 5 sigma from the LTE model; LTE-fitted lines compared with the observed spectra  
  > "All the non-blended emission lines of are identified as higher than 5$\sigma$ (confirmed from LTE modelling)." (3.2 Identification)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Hot cores are chosen as targets, and MM1 is line-rich; ALMA band 7 data on G358.93–0.03 had not yet been searched for glycine precursors  
  > "We first attempted to search for possible glycine precursor molecules towards the G358.93–0.03 MM1 using ALMA band 7." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: LTE is justified by the high gas density of the inner hot core  
  > "The LTE assumptions are reasonable in the inner region of the hot molecular core candidate G358.93–0.03 MM1 because the gas density of the warm inner region of the hot core was 2$\times$10$^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ (cite: ste21)." (3.2 Identification)
- Alternative: Gas-phase formation routes proposed in the literature were not tested; further chemical modelling is needed  
  > "Additional chemical modelling was required to establish whether these gas-phase reactions can effectively reproduce the observed abundance of" (4.2 Possible formation mechanism)
- How they decided it was enough: Detection accepted when non-blended lines exceed 5 sigma and the LTE model matches the observed spectrum  
  > "All the non-blended emission lines of are identified as higher than 5$\sigma$ (confirmed from LTE modelling)." (3.2 Identification)
- Limitation (authors): Only five of sixteen methylamine lines are non-blended; the rest are blended  
  > "From Figure [2](#fig:ltespec), it is clear that all LTE-fitted lines of are consistent with the observed spectra, but some of them are contaminated by stronger lines from other species." (3.2 Identification)
- Limitation (authors): Hyperfine structure not considered because the spectral resolution is too coarse  
  > "current spectral resolution is not sufficient to resolve the hyperfine lines of" (3.2 Identification)
- Limitation (authors): Emission is unresolved; higher resolution needed  
  > "Higher spatial and angular resolution observations are required to understand the spatial distribution of towards the G358.93–0.03 MM1." (3.5 Spatial distribution)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detection rests on an LTE model with only five non-blended lines; blended lines and model degeneracy (N, Tex, source size) are not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fractional abundance and ratio depend on column densities from earlier papers and on the 0.45 arcsec beam treatment, which are not given in detail
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The claim of the most abundant source ever known compares abundances from different instruments, resolutions and methods
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The comparison with the Garrod (2013) models is by eye against three discrete values, with no uncertainty on the models
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source size of 0.5 arcsec in the LTE fit is close to the beam (0.42 arcsec) and the emitting region is barely resolved
