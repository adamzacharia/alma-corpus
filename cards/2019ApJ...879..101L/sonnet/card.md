## Analysis recipe

1. **Make Stokes I, Q, U continuum and C18O maps**: Calibrate in CASA, phase-only self-cal, image with robust 0.5  
  > "A visibility weighting with a robust factor of 0.5 was used to make the continuum maps (including Stokes *I*, *Q*, and *U* parameters)" (Band 6)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; C18O channels 0.2 km/s  
  > "The velocity resolution and noise level in the C$^{18}$O channel maps are 0.2 km s$^{-1}$ per channel" (Band 6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive polarization intensity, fraction and angle**: Bias-corrected P_i, detection if P_i >= 2.5 sigma_p; P_d=P_i/I; E vectors  
  > "Thus, we consider it a detection if $P_i \geq 2.5
\sigma_p$." (Polarization Measurements)
   - assumption: B-field perpendicular to polarization when envelope optically thin  
  > "Hence the inferred magnetic field orientations are perpendicular to the polarization orientations" (Magnetic Field Morphology)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Accuracy better than 0.3% from ALMA Cycle 4 Technical Handbook  
  > "we can achieve an accuracy better than 0.3% (3$\sigma$) in polarization detection within the inner 1/3 of the primary beam" (Polarization Measurements)
3. **Spectral index and dust opacity index**: alpha from intensity ratio of convolved 358 and 232 GHz maps; beta=alpha-2  
  > "Then we estimate the dust opacity index with $\beta=\alpha-2$, assuming Rayleigh-Jean limits for the two frequencies." (Spectral Index and Dust Opacity Index)
   - assumption: Rayleigh-Jeans limit  
  > "assuming Rayleigh-Jean limits for the two frequencies" (Spectral Index and Dust Opacity Index)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit envelope PV diagram kinematics**: Fit outer PV boundary with ballistic infalling-rotating model with M*=0.08 Msun fixed, free l; mainly northeastern envelope  
  > "With this mass, the outer boundaries of the PV structure can be fitted reasonably well with $l \sim 55\pm15$ au km s$^{-1}$" (Envelope Kinematics in C18O)
   - parameter: Vsys refined to 9.10 km/s LSR  
  > "It is refined slightly here to be $V_\textrm{\scriptsize sys}\sim 9.10$ km s$^{-1}$ LSR" (Envelope Kinematics in C18O)
   - assumption: Constant specific angular momentum and total energy; M* from disk Keplerian rotation  
  > "in which the material is assumed to spiral inwards with constant specific angular momentum and total energy" (Envelope Kinematics in C18O)
   - fitting: Reasonable fit by eye of outer boundaries (method not stated)  
  > "With this mass, the outer boundaries of the PV structure can be fitted reasonably well with $l \sim 55\pm15$ au km s$^{-1}$" (Results, Envelope Kinematics in C18O)
   - validation: Consistent with HCO+ angular momentum and disk radius  
  > "which is also well consistent with previous measurement of angular momentum in HCO$^+$ (cite: Lee2009HH211)" (Envelope Kinematics in C18O)
5. **Inside-out collapse comparison, accretion and infall rates, field strength**: Fit NH3 l_e(r) power law, run Shu1977 model at three ages, estimate infall rate from 232 GHz column density, and B=sqrt(4 pi rho) v_phi  
  > "by equating the magnetic energy to the rotation energy, we have the field strength to be $B = \sqrt{4\pi \rho} v_\phi$" (Magnetic Field Strength)
   - parameter: kappa=0.016 cm2/g, Texc~40 K, path length ~100 au, subtended angle ~10 deg  
  > "Assuming an excitation temperature of $\sim$ 40 K" (Inside-Out Collapse)
   - assumption: Isothermal model, no rotation or B in collapse model; a~0.23 km/s for 15 K  
  > "an isothermal sound speed $a \sim$ 0.23 km s$^{-1}$" (Inside-Out Collapse)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Infall-derived accretion rate cross-checked with inside-out prediction  
  > "the rough estimate here is consistent with the above" (Inside-Out Collapse)

## Qualitative findings

- Polarization in envelope is mainly from magnetically aligned grains, with pinched poloidal field and point-symmetric polarization implying a toroidal component  
  > "a point symmetric polarization distribution is also seen in the flattened envelope, implying that the pinched field lines also have a significant toroidal component generated by the rotation." (abstract)

## Claim tuples

- H: The flattened envelope is a pseudodisk with infall and rotation | E: C18O PV diagram fit with ballistic infall-rotation model | R: l~55±15 au km/s, centrifugal barrier ~21 au | F: Envelope spirals inward and becomes a disk at ~20 au | **supported**
- H: Constant specific angular momentum results from inside-out collapse of the NH3 envelope | E: Compare Shu model applied to NH3 l_e(r) with C18O l | R: t_c~35000 yr fits | F: Flattened envelope comes from thin shell of extended envelope | **supported**
- H: Field in the envelope is pinched with toroidal component | E: 232/358 GHz polarization maps compared to Hirano2019 and Kataoka2012 simulations | R: Pinched field lines and point-symmetric polarization | F: Pseudodisk threaded by toroidal and pinched poloidal field | **partially**
- H: Magnetic braking is efficient in the envelope | E: Angular momentum loss in ballistic model; field misalignment | R: Only ~30% loss before disk | F: No clear magnetic braking, likely due to misalignment | **supported**
