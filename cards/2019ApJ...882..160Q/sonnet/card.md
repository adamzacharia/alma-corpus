## Analysis recipe

1. **Continuum imaging and disk geometry**: Self-calibrate and image 284 GHz continuum; fit elliptical Gaussian with IMFIT for inclination and PA; 3 sigma radius  
  > "by fitting an elliptical Gaussian component on the high SNR continuum image using the CASA task IMFIT." (Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5  
  > "imaged by CLEANing with a robust parameter of 0.5" (Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: AS 209 and IM Lup consistent with Huang et al. 2018b  
  > "The values for AS 209 and IM Lup are consistent with those in (cite: Huang2018b)" (Data reduction)
2. **N2H+ moment maps, spectra and radial profiles**: Sum channels over detected velocity ranges; extract spectra in elliptical masks; deproject and azimuthally average  
  > "Finally, Figure. [2](#fig:profiles) shows the corresponding deprojected and azimuthally averaged radial profiles for each disk" (3.1)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **SED fitting with DIAD to get vertical temperature structure**: Tune 6–12 parameters (epsilon, alpha, wall T and height, a_max, optically thin dust) to fit SEDs  
  > "We tune 6-12 parameters for each object to fit its spectral energy distribution" (3.3)
   - software: DIAD
   - parameter: epsilon tested at 1.0, 0.5, 0.1, 0.01, 0.001; alpha 0.00001–0.02  
  > "we test dust settling coefficient values ($\epsilon$) of 1.0, 0.5, 0.1, .01, and 0.001 and vary accretion as parameterized by $\alpha$ between 0.00001 – .02" (3.3)
   - assumption: Draine astronomical silicates and graphite, dust-to-gas 0.0065, n(a) ∝ a^-3.5 from 0.005 μm, large grains 1 mm within 0.1H  
  > "At any given location in the disk, the grain size ($a$) distribution of these dust particles is assumed to be a power-law, $n(a) \propto a^{-3.5}$" (3.3)
   - fitting: Manual tuning of parameters to best fit SED  
  > "The best-fit values are listed in Table [5](#tab:seds)" (3.3)
   - validation: Visual agreement with SEDs  
  > "Figure. [4](#fig:seds) shows that the best-fit models yield excellent fits to the SEDs of the six disks." (3.3)
4. **Initial inner-edge estimate from highest velocity channel**: R = 100 M_star (2.98 sin i / δV)^2 from most blue-shifted >3σ channel  
  > "we derive the inner edge radii R (in unit of au) as $100 \times M_{star} (\frac{2.98 \sin{i}}{\delta V})^2$" (3.4)
   - assumption: Keplerian rotation  
  > "considering the disk in Keplerian rotation" (3.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with later chi2 results  
  > "The derived CO snowlines are consistent with the initial estimates." (3.4)
5. **Fit jump-and-drop N2H+ model to visibilities**: Parametric N2H+ column density (N_p, J1, D2, R1, R2, R_out) with RATRAN radiative transfer on DIAD structure; grid chi2 minimization on complex visibilities; stellar masses refit iteratively  
  > "The best-fit parameter estimates are obtained by minimizing $\chi^2$, the weighted residual of the complex visibilities" (3.4)
   - software: RATRAN, DIAD
   - parameter: R1 and R2 grid interval of 5 au; vertical boundaries Σ21 = 3.2 and 100; i and PA fixed from continuum  
  > "We fix the boundary values to be 3.2 and 100" (3.4)
   - assumption: Hyperfine levels in LTE; HCO+ collisional rates used for N2H+  
  > "relative populations between the hyper-fine levels are assumed to be in LTE" (3.4)
   - fitting: Grid chi2 minimization in uv plane; uncertainties from chi2 contours  
  > "derived from the $\chi^2$ contours" (3.4)
   - validation: Compare deprojected profiles with best-fit models; second temperature-boundary model grid  
  > "we therefore ran a second grid of parametric models characterized by the CO freeze-out temperature T$_{\rm{CO}}$" (3.4)
6. **Snowline temperatures**: Read midplane temperatures of DIAD models at R1 and R2  
  > "Based on comparison between R1 and R2, and the disk temperature structures we can derive snowline temperatures for CO and N$_2$" (3.4)
   - software: DIAD
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Complementary temperature-boundary model agrees  
  > "in perfect agreement with the results obtained from the jump-and-drop models" (3.4)

## Qualitative findings

- Two N2H+ morphologies: bright narrow ring with tenuous extended emission, or broad ring  
  > "N$_2$H$^+$ is either present in a bright, narrow ring surrounded by extended tenuous emission, or in a broad ring." (abstract)
- Second tenuous N2H+ ring near the dust continuum edge in LkCa 15, GM Aur, IM Lup  
  > "In all sources the second ring appears to be close to the edge of the mm dust continuum emission" (3.1)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sample of six disks with SMA N2H+ detections spanning a range of vertical thermal structure  
  > "These disks span a range of disk thermal vertical structure and therefore present a perfect test-bed" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Explanations for DM Tau low temperatures (mixing, photodesorption, warmer disk) considered but speculative  
  > "but all are highly speculative considering the large opacities and low turbulence levels expected in disk midplanes" (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Velocity-offset uncertainty hard to determine for low-inclination disks  
  > "the uncertainty in the velocity offset analysis described here is hard to determine" (3.4)
- Limitation (authors): No uncertainties on T_CO and T_N2 in the complementary model  
  > "There is no attempt to determine uncertainties on T$_{\rm{CO}}$ and T$_{\rm{N_2}}$ for the complementary model." (Footnote 4)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Snowline temperatures depend on DIAD models fit to SEDs, with no uncertainty propagation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Grid chi2 fits with a 5 au grid and no formal sampler
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only three disks yield snowline pairs; sample is small
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Settling and VIRaM thickness are tied to a single parameter epsilon
