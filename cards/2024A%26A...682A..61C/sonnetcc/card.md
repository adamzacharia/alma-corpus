## Analysis recipe

1. **Further calibration and self-calibration of the visibilities**: Flag lines in each execution block, channel-average spws, locate the emission peak with tclean + imfit, shift the phase centre with fixvis, check amplitude offsets between blocks, then three rounds of phase-only self-calibration with gaincal/applycal  
  > "Next, we used the *fixvis* function to shift the phase center to the position of the peak of M512 at the epoch of every observation." (2 Observations); "We computed the phase corrections on the continuum spectral windows of each execution block using *gaincal* and applied them with *applycal*." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA 6.2.1.7, tclean, imfit, fixvis, gaincal, applycal
   - parameter: Round 1: solint = inf, scans combined within each execution block, gaintype G  
  > "In the first round, we set the solution interval of *gaincal* to *inf*, combined the scans within each execution block and set gaintype $=$ "G"" (2 Observations)
   - parameter: Rounds 2 and 3: spws combined, solint 60 s and 10 s, gaintype T  
  > "In the second and third rounds, we combined the spectral windows of each block, shortened the solution intervals to 60 and 10 seconds, respectively, and set gaintype $=$ "T"" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No amplitude offsets above 3% between execution blocks; ~15% peak S/N improvement; no further gain from shorter solution intervals  
  > "We did not find any appreciable improvement in the noise and signal-to-noise properties for phase-only selfcalibration steps with even smaller time intervals." (2 Observations)
2. **Image B3 and B6 continuum at matched resolution**: tclean with Hogbom deconvolution, Briggs robust = 1, pbcor, manual masks, uvtaper to match beams, then smoothing to a common beam  
  > "Since the emission is extended, we ran the *tclean* routine setting *pbcor = True*, in order to correct for the primary beam attenuation." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA tclean
   - parameter: robust = 1, pixel 0.2 arcsec, 300 pixels, CLEAN to 1.5 x rms  
  > "We have finally carried out the analysis on images with robust $=$ 1" (2 Observations); "and an image size of 300 pixels" (2 Observations)
   - parameter: uvtaper to a common ~1.5 x 1.2 arcsec beam, then smoothing to 1.6 arcsec  
  > "Finally, we smoothed the images to the same resolution of 1.6$''$" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: A number of robust choices were tried to balance resolution and sensitivity  
  > "we tried a number of "robust" parameter choices to find the optimal balance between resolution and sensitivity" (2 Observations)
3. **Test infall kinematics of the arc with C18O**: Make a C18O moment-one map and PV diagram; fit analytical infall trajectories with TIPSY over a range of systemic velocities  
  > "We analysed the spectral cube with the *Trajectory of Infalling Particles in Streamers around Young stars* (TIPSY, Gupta et al., subm.)." (3 Streamer's kinematics); "obtained using pvextractor (cite: Ginsburg2016)" (3 Streamer's kinematics)
   - software: TIPSY, pvextractor, CASA tclean
   - parameter: Central mass 0.15 Msun; v_sys explored 2.9 to 3.9 km/s  
  > "we explored a range of systemic velocities consistent with the observed C$^{18}$O spectral line, between 2.9 and 3.9 km/s" (3 Streamer's kinematics); "For the former, we set to 0.15 M$_{\odot}$, as reported in Table [1](#tab:m512)." (3 Streamer's kinematics)
   - parameter: PV path width equal to the beam, 1.6 arcsec  
  > "The width of the selected path is equivalent to the beam, 1.6$''$." (3 Streamer's kinematics)
   - assumption: Parcel of gas feels only the gravity of the central source; inner envelope and streamer mass not included  
  > "TIPSY solves analytical kinematics equations for an infalling parcel of gas that feels the gravity of a central source from (cite: Mendoza2009)" (3 Streamer's kinematics)
   - fitting: Analytical streamline model overplotted on the moment-one map and PVD; fit method not stated  
  > "We overplot a TIPSY fit model to the moment-one map in Fig.[2](#fig:moment1) to show how the arc-like feature around M512 is consistent with the infall of material." (3 Streamer's kinematics)
   - validation: TIPSY velocities compared with observed PVD  
  > "The TIPSY model is consistent with the radial velocities of the observed emission." (3 Streamer's kinematics)
4. **Make spectral-index maps and remove the compact inner emission**: Compute alpha per pixel from the smoothed B3 and B6 maps, fit and subtract a 2D Gaussian to the inner region, recompute alpha for the streamer; cross-check in the uv plane with a point-source subtraction  
  > "Using the ALMA B3 and B6 maps, we calculate the spectral index $\alpha$ at each pixel in our smoothed maps." (4.1 Spectral index maps); "We fit a 2D Gaussian to the inner region in the image plane." (4.1 Spectral index maps)
   - software: CASA uvmodelfit
   - parameter: Inner Gaussian extends 1.4 arcsec (2 sigma, 574 au)  
  > "The compact emission extends for 1.4$''$ (2$\sigma$, 574 au)" (4.1 Spectral index maps)
   - parameter: uv-plane check: uvmodelfit point source, 16.1 mJy in B6 and 2.6 mJy in B3  
  > "a total integrated flux density of 16.1 mJy in B6 and 2.6 mJy in B3" (8 Compact emission modelling)
   - assumption: Optically thin dust and Rayleigh-Jeans: beta = alpha - 2  
  > "in the optically thin regime and if the Rayleigh-Jeans (RJ) approximation holds, $\beta = \alpha-2$" (4 Spectral index)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit in the image plane; point-source fit to visibilities with uvmodelfit  
  > "We have used the *uvmodelfit* CASA routine to fit a point source component to the visibilities" (8 Compact emission modelling)
   - validation: Image-plane and uv-plane analyses give consistent alpha_S and beta  
  > "both azimuthally averaged spectral indices are consistent with what found in the image plane" (8 Compact emission modelling)
5. **Derive dust opacity spectral index profile along the streamer**: Place circular apertures along a manually tuned logarithmic spiral, measure B3 and B6 flux per bin, correct for non-Rayleigh-Jeans Planck ratio with an assumed T(r), and compute beta and alpha with statistical errors  
  > "we use a logarithmic spiral with manually-tuned parameters to adapt it to the shape of the extended emission" (4.2 Dust opacity index profile); "each aperture contains roughly 160 pixels" (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
   - parameter: Representative frequencies 230 GHz (B6) and 100 GHz (B3)  
  > "where the ALMA B6 and B3 representative frequecies are $\nu_2 = 230$ GHz, $\nu_1 = 100$ GHz" (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
   - parameter: T(r) = 38 K (L/Lsun)^0.2 (r/100 au)^-0.4, giving 21 to 8 K over 600 to 4,000 au  
  > "This equation yields temperatures in a range of 21 to 8 K across the scales of the streamer, that is 600 to 4,000 au." (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
   - assumption: Dust heated mainly by irradiation from the central source  
  > "If we assume that the main heating mechanism for the dust is the irradiation from the central source" (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
   - assumption: Streamer lies in the plane of the sky for projected distances  
  > "We note that these distances from the central source assume the streamer lays in the plane of the sky." (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
   - assumption: Exact temperature assumed, so beta error equals alpha statistical error; systematic calibration 5% on top  
  > "We consider the same error for $\beta$ since we assume an exact value for the temperature in Eq." (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Fixed temperatures of 21 K or 8 K change beta by less than 10%  
  > "the $\beta$ measured with both fixed temperatures only changes by $<$10% with respect to the one obtained using the temperature profile" (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
6. **Estimate dust masses of the streamer and the inner region**: Optically thin Hildebrand mass M = d^2 F / (kappa B(T)) summed in bins of pixels above 3 sigma assigned to the nearest aperture on the spiral, with T from the temperature profile  
  > "For each selected aperture on the spiral, we assign each pixel above the 3$\sigma$ threshold to the closest aperture center, measure the total flux in the bin so defined" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - parameter: d = 420 pc  
  > "We consider a distance of 420 pc" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - parameter: Streamer: DSHARP kappa_1.3mm = 0.4 g/cm2; inner region: T = 20 K, kappa_1.3mm = 2.25 g/cm2  
  > "is $\kappa_{1.3mm} = 0.4$ g/cm$^{-2}$." (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate); "a temperature of 20 K and an absorption opacity $\kappa_{1.3mm} =$ 2.25 g/cm$^{-2}$" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - assumption: DSHARP opacity for ISM-like spectral index with n(a) proportional to a^-3.5  
  > "a typical power-law $n(a) \propto a^{-3.5}$" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - assumption: Quoted mass uncertainties reflect only a 10% flux calibration error; inner-region mass may be off by up to ~5 because it is optically thick  
  > "The reported uncertainties on the derived masses only reflect the dominant flux calibration error of 10%." (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate); "mass estimations for the inner region can be wrong by a factor up to $\sim$5" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative DIANA or solar-system opacities would lower the masses by up to a factor 3 to 10, but the streamer-to-disk mass ratio is unchanged  
  > "We point out that consistent choices of opacities imply the same ratio between the streamer and the disk's mass." (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
7. **Derive the mass infall rate**: Free-fall time from 4000 au onto the 0.15 Msun star (~50 kyr) divided into the gas mass from the dust mass and dust-to-gas ratio  
  > "a particle with null initial velocity would fall from 4000 au onto the 0.15 $M_{\odot}$ protostar in roughly 50 kyr" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - parameter: Dust-to-gas ratio 0.01; free-fall time ~50 kyr  
  > "Thus, assuming the whole streamer will infall onto the inner system, and a typical dust-to-gas ratio of 0.01, the total mass infall rate" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - assumption: Whole streamer falls onto the inner system; rate is an upper estimate  
  > "the infall rate may be lower if the streamer will not interact in its entirety with the inner protoplanetary disk" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Free-fall estimate consistent with TIPSY infall time  
  > "This estimate is consistent with the TIPSY outputs ran in Sect." (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)

## Qualitative findings

- C18O moment-one map shows a velocity progression from low to high velocity toward the protostar, consistent with infall  
  > "it is characterized by a progression from lower to higher velocities when approaching the protostar from afar" (3 Streamer's kinematics)
- The same arc is detected in DCO+ and N2D+, but these lines are not spectrally resolved so were not used for kinematics  
  > "These lines are not spectrally resolved and thus could not be used in our kinematic analysis" (2 Observations)
- Compact inner region is optically thick; streamer is optically thin  
  > "The streamer is optically thin with $\alpha_{S}\sim3.2$ along the whole structure." (6 Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: New B3 data were designed to get the 3 mm flux of the extended emission seen at 1.3 mm, at similar resolution  
  > "These were designed to retrieve the 3 mm continuum flux of the extended emission around M512, first reported by (cite: grant2021) at 1.3 mm, with a similar resolution." (2 Observations)
- Why this method: Temperature-corrected beta because the Rayleigh-Jeans approximation fails at low T  
  > "We thus introduce a correction to the simple $\alpha = \beta - 2$ case for the low temperatures which would otherwise artificially lower the derived $\beta$" (4.2 Dust opacity index profile)
- Alternative: Stellar flyby stripping (2MASS J05401156-0730409) judged hard to reconcile; not completely ruled out  
  > "we suggest that a flyby of M512 with 2MASS J05401156-0730409 appears harder to reconcile with the observed extended and massive arc than the streamer scenario, but cannot be completely ruled out at this stage" (9 Any flyby candidates?)
- Alternative: Outflow cavity wall emission tentatively discarded  
  > "We thus tentatively discard this scenario." (5.3 Origins and frequency)
- How they decided it was enough: Self-calibration stopped when shorter solution intervals gave no appreciable improvement  
  > "We did not find any appreciable improvement in the noise and signal-to-noise properties for phase-only selfcalibration steps with even smaller time intervals." (2 Observations)
- Limitation (authors): Streamer length is a lower limit because interferometric filtering may remove larger-scale emission  
  > "We note that that the length of the streamer, as observed in this study, is to be considered a lower limit since the interferometric observations might have filtered out larger-scale emission." (2 Observations)
- Limitation (authors): Inner-region mass may be wrong by up to a factor ~5 because the emission is optically thick  
  > "mass estimations for the inner region can be wrong by a factor up to $\sim$5" (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
- Limitation (authors): Inner disk geometry, mass and system velocity are uncertain, limiting the kinematic interpretation  
  > "both the mass and system velocity are uncertain" (3 Streamer's kinematics)
- Limitation (authors): Dust opacity choice could change masses by up to a factor ten  
  > "Thus the mass derived through them would be up to ten times lower both for the streamer and the inner disk." (5.2 Mass constraints and infall rate)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Kinematic support for infall rests on one unpublished code (TIPSY) with a loosely constrained v_sys and a point-mass assumption, so 'consistent with infall' does not exclude rotation or outflow.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass and beta depend on an assumed envelope temperature profile (Motte and Andre) and a single DSHARP opacity; dust-to-gas ratio of 0.01 and whole-streamer infall make the infall rate an upper estimate.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): B3 and B6 have different uv coverage and recoverable scales; the first uv bin shows beta ~2, so flux filtering may bias alpha in the outer arc.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass uncertainty (+/-25 Earth masses) includes only the 10% flux calibration error, not opacity, temperature or Gaussian-subtraction systematics.
