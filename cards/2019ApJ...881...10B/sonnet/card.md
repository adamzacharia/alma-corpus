## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure stellar luminosity profile and mass model**: Surface brightness of HST H-band and CGS V-band fit with 14-component MGE, deprojected assuming oblate shape; circular velocity v_c* computed with Upsilon_H=1 then scaled  
  > "The final MGE model of the galaxy consists of 14 concentric elliptical Gaussians, for which the best-fit parameters are provided in Table [1]" (2.3 Stellar Luminosity Profile)
   - software: MGE (Cappellari 2002), Tiny Tim, AstroDrizzle
   - parameter: flattening q' > 0.72; common center and PA 77 deg  
  > "constrained the observed flattening (the ratio between the projected major and minor axis of the 2D Gaussian, $q^\prime$) to be $>0.72$" (2.3 Stellar Luminosity Profile)
   - assumption: oblate axisymmetric galaxy inclined at i~48 deg, no dark matter halo  
  > "We do not include contributions from a dark matter halo, as these are negligible within the central few kpc of the galaxy (cite: bru04)." (2.3 Stellar Luminosity Profile)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: MGE compared to data along major axis (Figure 2)  
  > "This MGE is a good description of the $H$-band surface brightness measurements" (2.3 Stellar Luminosity Profile)
2. **Model dust extinction and create dust-corrected mass profiles**: Embedded thin-disk screen model predicts B-I and J-H color excess vs A_V; central H-band profile corrected by modifying Nuker gamma to 0.09, 0.17, 0.26 (A_H=0.31, 0.75, 1.50) with GALFIT, then re-fit with MGE  
  > "we adjusted $\gamma$ to 0.09, 0.17, and 0.26 to approximately correct for absorption of ¼, ½, and ¾ of the integrated stellar light originating *behind* the disk" (2.4 Disk Extinction Modeling)
   - software: GALFIT
   - parameter: R_V=3.1 extinction law, w->0 thin disk, i=48 deg  
  > "We used the same $R_V=3.1$ extinction law to characterize $A_\lambda$, and for simplicity assumed a very thin ($w\rightarrow0$) disk." (2.4 Disk Extinction Modeling)
   - assumption: no straightforward extinction correction; three fiducial A_H values bracket the likely range  
  > "adjusting the central $H$-band surface brightness profile to correct for three fiducial values of disk extinction that bracket the likely range" (2.4 Disk Extinction Modeling)
   - fitting: PSF-convolved 2D Nuker function fit to central R<~10''  
  > "we fit the central $R \lesssim 10\ensuremath{''}$ of the mosaic with a PSF-convolved 2D Nuker function (cite: pen02)" (2.4 Disk Extinction Modeling)
   - validation: model color excess maxima agree with observed B-I and J-H maps  
  > "this simple model predicts maximum color excess values that are in very close agreement with both the $B-I$ and $J-H$ color maps of NGC 3258" (2.4 Disk Extinction Modeling)
3. **Image ALMA CO cube and characterize emission**: tclean of Cycle 2+4 data into CO cube; Gauss-Hermite moment maps, PVD, and kinemetry of the velocity field  
  > "we decomposed the $v_{\rm LOS}$ map using kinemetry (cite: kra06) to measure the kinematic PA $\Gamma_k$ and axis ratio $q_k$" (3.2 Emission Line Properties)
   - software: CASA, kinemetry
   - parameter: Briggs r=0.5, 10.2 km/s channels, 0.015'' pixels  
  > "Briggs ($r=0.5$) weighting produced a line cube with $\theta_\mathrm{FWHM}=0.12\ensuremath{''}\times0.09\ensuremath{''}$" (3.1 Observations and data processing)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit flat-disk gas-dynamical models to the data cube**: Forward model of thin flat disk in BH + extended mass potential, Gaussian line profiles, beam convolution on oversampled grid (s=4), chi^2 fit to the cube over block-averaged 5x5 pixel cells within r_fit=1''  
  > "Models are optimized by $\chi^2$ minimization using a downhill simplex minimization method (cite: pre92) by fitting the calculated cubes directly to the ALMA data cube." (4.1.1 Method)
   - software: IRAF STSDAS LUCY, custom modeling code
   - parameter: CO surface brightness map from 10 iterations of Richardson-Lucy deconvolution  
  > "applied ten iterations of the IRAF STSDAS Richardson-Lucy deconvolution (cite: ric72; luc74) task `LUCY` using the elliptical Gaussian synthesized beam." (4.1.1 Method)
   - parameter: fit region 415 spatial pixels x 94 channels = 39010 data points; spectral range 2310-3273 km/s  
  > "The block-averaged fitting region used to compute $\chi^2$ contains 415 spatial pixels and 94 frequency channels, for a total of 39010 data points." (4.1.1 Method)
   - assumption: spatially uniform M/L; gas disk mass not included; thin, cold disk with no asymmetric drift  
  > "We assume a spatially uniform $M/L$ ratio in our model calculations." (4.1.1 Method)
   - fitting: chi^2 downhill simplex; at least nine free parameters (M_BH, Upsilon_H, x_c, y_c, i, Gamma, v_sys, sigma_turb, f_0); uniform or Gaussian radial sigma_turb  
  > "These basic models employ at least nine free parameters: the black hole mass $M_\mathrm{BH}$, the stellar $H$-band $M/L$ ratio $\Upsilon_H$" (4.1.1 Method)
   - validation: oversampling factor tests (stable for s>=4); comparison with Cycle 2 fits  
  > "we find that the best-fitting BH mass converges to stable values for $s\geq 4$" (4.1.2 Flat disk model results)
5. **Tilted-ring model with kinematic host mass profile (final model F1)**: Ten-ring tilted-ring disk (Gamma, q per ring, cubic spline) plus ten free v_ext values replacing the MGE mass model; 39 free parameters  
  > "The ten free $v_\mathrm{ext}$ parameters were optimized simultaneously with the tilted-ring angular parameters and the other disk parameters, for a total of 39 free parameters in the final model." (4.2.2 Host galaxy mass profile from CO kinematics)
   - software: custom modeling code
   - parameter: v_ext increasing with radius and v_ext=0 at r=0  
  > "The only constraints we applied to the circular velocity model is that $\ensuremath{v_\mathrm{ext}}(r)$ was required to be an increasing function of radius and that $\ensuremath{v_\mathrm{ext}}=0$ at $r=0$." (4.2.2 Host galaxy mass profile from CO kinematics)
   - fitting: chi^2 simplex minimization  
  > "Models are optimized by $\chi^2$ minimization using a downhill simplex minimization method (cite: pre92) by fitting the calculated cubes directly to the ALMA data cube." (4.1.1 Method)
   - validation: lowest chi^2_nu (1.179) of all models; residual maps  
  > "Model F1 attains the lowest $\chi^2_\nu$ value of any of our trial models, and we consider it to be our final preferred model for the NGC 3258 disk." (4.3 Detailed modeling results)
6. **Statistical uncertainty via Monte Carlo**: 100 Monte Carlo realizations of best-fit model cube with noise slices from line-free channels, refit each; standard deviation as 1 sigma  
  > "we carried out 100 Monte Carlo realizations of the best-fit model F1 cube." (4.4 Monte Carlo error analysis)
   - parameter: ~100 line-free channels with |v_LOS - v_sys|>500 km/s used as noise  
  > "We extracted nearly 100 line-free channels from the data cube where $|\ensuremath{v_\mathrm{LOS}}-\ensuremath{v_\mathrm{sys}}|>500$ km s$^{-1}$" (4.4 Monte Carlo error analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: compared with Delta chi^2 bounds  
  > "While somewhat broader than the $\chi^2$ bounds, the distribution of $M_\mathrm{BH}$ values remains very narrow." (4.4 Monte Carlo error analysis)
7. **Systematic error budget tests**: Vary oversampling, block averaging, number of rings, fitting region, central CO hole, radial flow, turbulent pressure, distance (+-1 sigma), peculiar velocity; combine positive and negative terms in quadrature  
  > "we separately combined in quadrature the positive and negative $\Delta \ensuremath{M_\mathrm{BH}}$ contributions listed above" (4.5 Additional tests and error budget)
   - parameter: distance D_L=35.8 and 28.0 Mpc for +-1 sigma  
  > "(i.e., $D_L=35.8$ and 28.0 Mpc, corresponding to angular scales of 170 and 133 pc arcsec$^{-1}$, respectively)" (4.5 Additional tests and error budget)
   - assumption: M_BH proportional to adopted angular size distance  
  > "the inferred BH mass should in principle be directly proportional to the assumed angular size distance" (4.5 Additional tests and error budget)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: each test refit with all free parameters  
  > "After optimizing over all free parameters, we obtain best-fit BH masses that are $\Delta \ensuremath{M_\mathrm{BH}}= \pm 2.7\times10^8$ $M_\odot$ (or about 12%)" (4.5 Additional tests and error budget)

## Qualitative findings

- CO emission shows quasi-Keplerian rotation to within ~20 pc of the nucleus ending in a central hole of radius ~0.13''  
  > "We identify a central hole in CO surface brightness with a radius of $\sim 0.13\ensuremath{''}$ that corresponds to the innermost emission detected in the PVD." (3.2 Emission Line Properties)
- The disk is mildly warped, modeled with a tilted-ring giving ~3% higher M_BH than the flat disk  
  > "The $\sim$3% difference between flat and warped disk model BH mass measurements is large relative to the other sub-percent level modeling systematics." (6 Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Cycle 2 data showed a rapid central rise in velocity, making NGC 3258 a promising target for high-resolution imaging  
  > "These attributes made NGC 3258 a promising target for high-resolution ALMA imaging in order to determine its BH mass to high precision." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Fit cube directly to use all information; v_ext method avoids dust-extinction uncertainty in stellar mass profile  
  > "This is the only method that can potentially reduce the systematic uncertainties to the percent level (or better) for such dusty disks" (4.2.2 Host galaxy mass profile from CO kinematics)
- Alternative: Delta chi^2 contours not used for error budget because correlated noise makes them underestimate; Monte Carlo used instead  
  > "Thus, we do not use the $\Delta \ensuremath{\chi^2}$ curves when determining the error budget on $M_\mathrm{BH}$" (4.1.2 Flat disk model results)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Model not a formally acceptable fit (chi^2_nu=1.179); correlated noise; local irregularities in velocity/dispersion fields  
  > "the final $\ensuremath{\chi^2_\nu}= 1.179$ for model F1 indicates that the model is not formally an acceptable fit to the data" (4.4 Monte Carlo error analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Distance disagreements among estimates imply M_BH may lie in (2.0-3.8)e9 Msun  
  > "the $M_\mathrm{BH}$ may plausibly lie in the range $(2.0-3.8)\times 10^9$ $M_\odot$ based on these other distance estimates" (4.5 Additional tests and error budget)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Thin, cold disk assumption with correction for turbulent pressure only estimated, not modeled
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fit code is custom and not released, so exact reproduction is hard
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Receding/approaching asymmetry (sub-Keplerian west side) not modeled
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Block averaging only approximately handles correlated noise; quoted precision may be optimistic
- Caveat (inferred, ours): v_ext freedom and 39 parameters could absorb unmodeled structure
