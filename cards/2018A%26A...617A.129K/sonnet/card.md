## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA/SMA data**: ALMA default CASA pipeline; SMA calibrated in MIR and imaged in CASA; Briggs R=0.5 (V4332 Sgr), natural (V1309 Band 6, V838), phase refinement on SiO peak  
  > "All data were calibrated in MIR[^10] and imaged in CASA." (5.1 SMA observations)
   - software: CASA, MIR
   - parameter: robust 0.5 for V4332 Sgr  
  > "with Briggs weighting and the robust parameter $R$=0.5" (3.1 ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Line fluxes on consecutive days consistent within 2.2%; flux drop of 18% over 11 months attributed to calibration  
  > "The line fluxes determined from data taken in the two consecutive days are consistent within 2.2%." (3.1 ALMA observations)
2. **Identify spectral lines**: Compare spectra with JPL catalog lines in CASSIS and with O-rich envelope surveys  
  > "The identification of spectral features was performed in CASSIS and was based on standard catalogs of mm/submm lines, mainly on the Jet Propulsion Laboratory catalog" (3.2 Identification of spectral features)
   - software: CASSIS
   - assumption: Oxygen-rich chemistry (C/O<1)  
  > "i.e. the molecular species expected in this source are characteristic of chemistry at a C-to-O ratio $<1.0$." (3.2 Identification of spectral features)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Velocity match and earlier optical identifications  
  > "Because of a very close match in velocity and previous identifications in visual spectra of V4332 Sgr" (3.2 Identification of spectral features)
3. **Measure positions, sizes and line profiles**: Fit Gaussians to integrated intensity maps for position and beam-deconvolved size; Gaussian/Voigt fits to line profiles for velocity and FWHM  
  > "We measured the beam-deconvolved sizes by finding a Gaussian profile best representing the emission region." (3.3 Location and size)
   - fitting: Elliptical Gaussian fits; uncertainties statistical only  
  > "The uncertainties quoted for position measurements in this paper express only the statistical error related to the fit of an elliptical Gaussian to the observed emission region." (footnote 4)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Model 3D structure of V4332 Sgr CO emission**: Shape model with homologous expansion and shapemol radiative transfer, optimized against total intensity, channel maps, PV diagrams and profiles  
  > "The model was optimized by referring to the total intensity and channel maps, PV diagrams, and spectral profiles of the CO 3–2 emission." (3.6 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
   - software: Shape, shapemol
   - parameter: opening angle ~60 deg, radius 0.28 arcsec, inclination 0<i<~30 deg, max velocity ~420 km/s  
  > "a wide opening angle of $\sim$60$^\circ$, a de-projected radius corresponding to 0.28$''$, and low inclination angle of 0$^\circ$ $< i \lesssim$30$^\circ$" (3.6 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
   - assumption: Homologous expansion velocity field  
  > "we adopted homologous expansion, where the velocity is proportional to the distance from the source" (3.6 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
   - fitting: Manual model optimization by visual comparison (no formal fitting stated)  
  > "The model was optimized by referring to the total intensity and channel maps, PV diagrams, and spectral profiles of the CO 3–2 emission." (3.5 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
   - validation: Degeneracy between velocity field and density noted  
  > "There is strong degeneracy between the velocity field and the density distribution which could not be broken with the current observations." (3.6 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
5. **LTE excitation analysis for column densities and masses**: CASSIS LTE chi2 minimization of Gaussian line profiles corrected for opacity; masses from CO column density  
  > "allow CASSIS to search for best set of physical parameters (line width and positions, an excitation temperature, and a column density) by performing a $\chi^2$ minimization procedure." (3.7 Column densities, excitation, and the mass)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: Source sizes fixed at values from Gaussian fits  
  > "Source sizes were fixed at values derived in Sect. [3.3](#sec-v4332-sizes)." (3.7 Column densities, excitation, and the mass)
   - assumption: CO/H2 = 3e-4, He at solar abundance; distances 5, 2.5, 6.1 kpc  
  > "we adopt a typical value of $f_{CO}$=3$\times$10$^{-4}$" (6.2 Masses dispersed in the merger events)
   - fitting: chi2 minimization, LTE; Tex for other species assumed equal to SO2 value  
  > "we assumed the same $T_{\rm ex}$ for the Al-bearing species as that of SO$_2$" (3.7 Column densities, excitation, and the mass)
   - validation: 34SO2 non-detection consistent with solar isotope ratio; Herschel CO column density compared for V838 Mon  
  > "It is consistent with the solar isotopic abundance ratio, $^{34}$S/$^{32}$S=1/22.35." (3.7 Column densities, excitation, and the mass)
6. **Compare masses, energies and abundances with models and other objects**: Compare ejecta masses with theoretical mass fraction formula, kinetic energies with post-AGB outflows, abundances with VY CMa and OH231.8  
  > "the ejected mass fraction (relative to the mass of the binary) is $\varphi$=0.3$q/(1+q)^2$" (6.2 Masses dispersed in the merger events)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Red-nova remnants have broad (~400 km/s full width) molecular emission lines and O-rich chemistry similar to VY CMa; AlO is not exceptionally abundant.  
  > "The abundance patterns observed in the red novae are particularly close to these of VY CMa" (6.3 Astro-chemistry of red novae)
- V4332 Sgr CO emission is consistent with a wide-angle bipolar outflow seen at low inclination.  
  > "The ALMA maps of V4332 Sgr indicate that the remnants, or at least their molecular component, have axial symmetry consistent with a wide-angle bipolar outflow." (6.4 The structure and kinematics of the ejecta)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: CO abundance assumed from literature range since no constraints exist for red novae  
  > "With no information on this ratio in the three red novae, we adopt a typical value of $f_{CO}$=3$\times$10$^{-4}$." (6.2 Masses dispersed in the merger events)
- Alternative: Hollow-shell bipolar models rejected for CO in V4332 Sgr  
  > "Models in which the bipolar structure is a hollow shell do not reproduce observations well" (3.6 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Masses are lower limits, accurate to about a factor of ~2 at best owing to the CO/H2 conversion; line saturation uncertain  
  > "The minimum masses we derive are accurate to within a factor of about $\sim$2, at best, owing to uncertain conversion factor" (6.2 Masses dispersed in the merger events)
- Limitation (authors): Velocity field and density degenerate in the Shape model  
  > "There is strong degeneracy between the velocity field and the density distribution which could not be broken with the current observations." (3.6 A 3-dimensional model of the CO emitter)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LTE single-component assumptions with saturated CO/SiO lines make column densities and masses highly uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Shape model is hand-tuned without formal fit statistics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distances are uncertain and propagate into masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): V838 Mon results come from SMA, not ALMA
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux calibration scale of 18% is an ad hoc assumption
