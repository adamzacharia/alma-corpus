## Analysis recipe

1. **Select archive maps and galaxies**: Retrieve PHANGS-ALMA v4.0; compare strict and broad mask products; select broad mask maps and 73 adequately resolved, fully covered galaxies; exclude NGC 6744.  
  > "We analyzed the molecular gas morphology according to the modified CAS system presented in Section 2.2 using both products, and we find that the results are overall similar." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data); "We selected 73 galaxies by visually inspecting the images and selecting galaxies with sufficient spatial resolution for depicting galaxy structures with no missing parts in the image." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data); "Therefore, NGC 6744 image was excluded from this analysis." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
   - parameter: PHANGS-ALMA archive data v4.0  
  > "We retrieved the PHANGS-ALMA archive data v4.0" (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
   - parameter: Broad mask products  
  > "Therefore, we have proceeded to use the broad mask products for the analysis in this study." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Strict and broad mask results overall similar; broad mask Moment 0 maps cover 98% of total flux.  
  > "we find that the results are overall similar." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data); "However, the Moment 0 maps of the broad mask products are more complete and cover larger fractions of the total flux (98%)." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
2. **Standardize resolution and intensity threshold**: Convolve all images to ∼180 pc and clip at 0.3 K km s−1.  
  > "We convolved all images to the physical resolution of $\sim 180$ pc to ensure a fair comparison across the sample." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data); "Finally, we clipped all data at $0.3\ \mathrm{K\ km\ s^{-1}}$" (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
   - parameter: Threshold is three times the least sensitive map RMS, σ = 0.1 K km s−1.  
  > "which is three times the RMS noise level ($\sigma=0.1\ \mathrm{K\ km\ s^{-1}}$) of the least sensitive data" (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
   - assumption: No inclination correction; sample inclinations all less than 75°.  
  > "no correlations were observed between the CAS morphological parameters and the inclination angle of the galaxies, and therefore, we did not correct the inclination angle throughout this paper." (Clumpiness ($S$)); "The inclination angle ($i$) of the sample are all less than $75^\circ$." (Clumpiness ($S$))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Check for correlations between CAS parameters and inclination; none observed.  
  > "no correlations were observed between the CAS morphological parameters and the inclination angle of the galaxies" (Clumpiness ($S$))
3. **Measure concentration**: C = 5 × log(r90%/r10%), using radii enclosing 90% and 10% of total flux.  
  > "We define the Concentration ($C$) as the logarithm of the ratio of the radius containing 90% of the total flux ($r_{90\%}$) to the radius containing 10% of the total flux ($r_{10\%}$)." (Concentration ($C$)); "C = 5 \times \log(r_{90\%}/r_{10\%})," (Concentration ($C$))
   - software: Custom Python script, NumPy, SciPy, Astropy, Matplotlib
   - parameter: No radius limit  
  > "However, we did not apply a radius limit in our measurements, as the bulk of molecular gas is concentrated in the galaxy’s inner regions." (Concentration ($C$))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Recalculate with Conselice2003 r80%/r20% definition; reported difference negligible.  
  > "we derived $C$ using the (cite: Conselice2003) definition" (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies); "As seen in these figures, the difference between the two definitions is negligible." (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies)
4. **Measure asymmetry**: Rotate by 180° about the adopted center; A = Σ|I−R|/(2ΣI).  
  > "The Asymmetry ($A$) is computed by rotating the galaxy image 180 degrees around its center and then subtracting it from the original image." (Asymmetry ($A$)); "A = \frac{\Sigma_{x,y=1,1}^{M,N}|I_{x,y}-R_{x,y}|}{2\ \Sigma_{x,y=1,1}^{M,N} I_{x,y}}," (Asymmetry ($A$))
   - software: Custom Python script, NumPy, SciPy, Astropy, Matplotlib, OpenCV
   - parameter: Centers from Leroy2021b table 3; NGC 1511 center replaced by molecular concentration peak.  
  > "In this study, we utilized table 3 of  (cite: Leroy2021b)." (Asymmetry ($A$)); "we have designated the peak of this molecular concentration as the center of NGC 1511" (Asymmetry ($A$))
   - parameter: Fiducial center uncertainty 1 arcsecond  
  > "a fiducial uncertainty of $1''$ was adopted." (Asymmetry ($A$))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Estimate A error from the range obtained by shifting center coordinates by the beam size.  
  > "the error associated with the $A$ was estimated by shifting the center coordinates by the beam size and quantifying the range of $A$ obtained through this process." (Asymmetry ($A$))
5. **Measure clumpiness**: S = 10 × Σ[(I−Iσ)/I], with Iσ the smoothed image and background zero.  
  > "S=10\times \Sigma_{x,y=1,1}^{M,N} \frac{I_{x,y}-I_{x,y}^\sigma}{I_{x,y}}," (Clumpiness ($S$)); "The definition of $S$ adopted in this study, where the background value is zero" (Clumpiness ($S$))
   - software: Custom Python script, NumPy, SciPy, Astropy, Matplotlib
   - parameter: σ = 500 pc  
  > "we used $\sigma = 500 \mathrm{pc}$ as in (cite: Davis2022)." (Clumpiness ($S$))
   - parameter: Original image physical resolution 180 pc  
  > "The spatial resolution of $I_{x,y}$ is 180 pc and enables us to identify giant molecular cloud associations (GMAs) larger than that scale." (Clumpiness ($S$))
   - assumption: S measures the relative distribution of GMAs against the overall molecular gas distribution.  
  > "The $S$ index measures the ratio of the distribution of GMAs to the overall molecular gas distribution in the galaxy." (Clumpiness ($S$))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual comparison of S images with Rosolowsky2021 figure 2.  
  > "and figure 2 of (cite: Rosolowsky2021) suggests that difference in resolution and adopted method are relatively insignificant." (Clumpiness ($S$))
6. **Test local-clump contributions to asymmetry**: Subtract each pixel of S from the corresponding original-image pixel, recalculate A′, and correlate A′ with S.  
  > "we subtracted the value of each pixel of $S$ from the value of the same pixel in the original image and re-calculated $A$ (called $A^{\prime}$)." (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare A′–S Spearman correlation with the original A–S correlation.  
  > "The Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient is 0.44 with an associated $p$-value of $1\times 10^{-4}$, which are similar to those derived for the correlation between $A$ and $S$." (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
7. **Derive molecular mass and star formation ratios**: Convert CO luminosity to MH2; calculate SFE = SFR/MH2 and sSFR = SFR/Mstar.  
  > "\mathrm{SFE(yr^{-1})} = \frac{\mathrm{SFR}}{M_{\mathrm{H_2}}}," (Relationship between CAS parameters and star formation efficiency); "M_{\mathrm{H_2}} = \alpha_{\mathrm{co}} L_{\mathrm{co}}," (Relationship between CAS parameters and star formation efficiency); "\mathrm{sSFR(yr^{-1})} = \frac{\mathrm{SFR}}{M_{\star}}," (Relationship between CAS parameters and star formation efficiency)
   - assumption: αco = 4.35 M⊙ (K km s−1 pc²)−1  
  > "$\alpha_{\mathrm{co}}=4.35\ \mathrm{M_{\odot}} (\mathrm{K\ km\ s^{-1}\ pc^2})^{-1}$ ((cite: Bolatto2013); (cite: Leroy2021b))" (Relationship between CAS parameters and star formation efficiency)
   - assumption: R21 = 0.65 to obtain CO J = 1–0 luminosity  
  > "Following the approach taken by  (cite: Leroy2021b), the CO (*J* = 2–1) to CO (*J* = 1–0) ratio of $R_{21}=0.65$ was used to calculate $L_{\mathrm{co}}$." (Relationship between CAS parameters and star formation efficiency)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare CAS statistics and environments**: Measure Spearman A–S correlation; compare cluster and non-cluster A and S distributions using medians and KS tests.  
  > "with a Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient of 0.52 and the associated $p$-value of $2\times 10^{-6}$." (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness); "We further ran the Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) test on $A$" (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness); "The KS test on $S$ returned with $p=0.90$ ($> 0.05$)." (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
   - parameter: Cluster population includes Virgo Cluster and poor clusters; comparison population is non-cluster galaxies.  
  > "the two populations, all cluster galaxies (Virgo Cluster and poor clusters) and non-cluster galaxies" (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: A′–S test addresses whether local clumps drive the A–S relation.  
  > "To test how the small-scale asymmetry due to locally condensed clumps affects the value of $A$" (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
9. **Test concentration against bars and stellar mass**: Compare C medians and KS distributions for SA versus SB + SAB; measure C–stellar-mass Spearman correlations for the full and morphology-selected samples.  
  > "Subsequently, we categorized galaxies into two groups: barred spiral galaxies (SB + SAB) and non-barred spiral galaxies (SA)." (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies); "We obtain the KS probability value of $8.6 \times 10^{-6}$" (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies); "Limiting the sample to barred spiral galaxies alone gives a Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient of 0.39, with a $p$-value of $8\times10^{-3}$" (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies)
   - parameter: SB + SAB combined as barred spirals; SA as non-barred spirals  
  > "barred spiral galaxies (SB + SAB) and non-barred spiral galaxies (SA)." (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeat concentration comparison using the Conselice2003 definition.  
  > "we derived $C$ using the (cite: Conselice2003) definition" (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies); "As seen in these figures, the difference between the two definitions is negligible." (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies)
10. **Correlate CAS with physical properties**: Measure Spearman correlations of CAS with log10 SFE, stellar mass, and molecular-to-stellar mass ratio; compare with Davis2022.  
  > "We analyzed the correlation between $\mathrm{log_{10}SFE}$ and the CAS parameters" (Relationship between CAS parameters and star formation efficiency); "Here we attempt to quantitatively compare the results of the correlation measurement of (cite: Davis2022) to this study." (Qualitative comparison with (cite: Davis2022) correlation measurements); "our measurement yielded a Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient of -0.42 with a $p$-value of $2\times 10^{-4}$" (Qualitative comparison with (cite: Davis2022) correlation measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare literature correlations while recognizing different ETG sample composition.  
  > "However, we note that the sample galaxies used in both analyses are different" (Qualitative comparison with (cite: Davis2022) correlation measurements)
11. **Test concentration against normalized bar length**: Calculate Rbar/R25 from Lang2020 table 1 and measure its Spearman correlation with C; inspect NGC 4424 and NGC 4731 as deviations.  
  > "$R_\mathrm{bar}/R_{25}$ is calculated from the data in table 1 of (cite: Lang2020)." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration); "The Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient computed for this analysis is 0.63, with a $p$-value of $3.8\times 10^{-5}$." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration); "Finally, we note two barred galaxies (NGC 4424 and NGC 4731) that do not follow the correlation" (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)
   - parameter: Bar length normalized by isophotal radius R25  
  > "the bar length ($R_\mathrm{bar}$) normalized by the isophotal radius $R_{25}$ ($R_\mathrm{bar}/R_{25}$)" (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Inspect Virgo versus non-Virgo behavior and interaction evidence for outliers.  
  > "While overall there is no significant difference in the Virgo cluster and non-Virgo population as a whole" (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration); "some Virgo Cluster galaxies, including NGC 4424 which is possibly experiencing ram pressure stripping (cite: Boselli2018), deviate downward from the main part of this correlation." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)
12. **Interpret morphology in CAS space**: Compare SA, SB + SAB, and S0 locations in C–A and C–S diagrams and propose molecular-gas morphological evolution.

## Qualitative findings

- A–S correlation likely reflects global molecular gas distribution rather than local-clump asymmetry.  
  > "This suggests that the correlation between $A$ and $S$ is likely reflecting the global distribution rather than the asymmetries caused by the distribution of the local clumps." (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
- Barred spirals generally have higher central molecular concentration.  
  > "These results further suggest a trend that barred spiral galaxies exhibit higher gas concentration overall." (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies)
- Longer bars are associated with higher concentration and may promote gas transport.  
  > "This correlation suggests that an extended bar structure is associated with higher $C$ values" (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration); "a longer bar structure can collect molecular gas from a relatively large area, resulting in more efficient angular momentum transfer." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)
- Cluster and non-cluster samples show no statistically significant A–S differences.  
  > "we find no statistically significant differences in the relationship between $A$ and $S$ when Virgo Cluster, poor clusters (Fornax Cluster, Eridanus Cluster and Dorado group) and non-cluster galaxies are compared" (Correlation between Asymmetry and Clumpiness)
- SA, SB + SAB, and S0 galaxies generally occupy distinct CAS regions.  
  > "non-barred spiral galaxies (SA), barred spiral galaxies (SB + SAB) and lenticular galaxies (S0) generally occupy distinct regions in the CAS space." (Gas Morphological Evolution in the CAS plane)
- NGC 4424 has nearly absent central CO J = 2–1 emission.  
  > "Indeed, CO (*J* = 2–1) is nearly absent in the center of NGC 4424" (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-resolution PHANGS-ALMA CO maps enable systematic molecular morphology analysis; CO J = 2–1 distribution resembles J = 1–0.  
  > "enabling a systematic study of the structure of molecular gas and investigate how it is related to large-scale properties such as bars or the global star-formation activity." (Introduction); "convincingly justifies the use of CO (*J* = 2–1) emission line rather than the commonly used CO (*J* = 1–0) emission line." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
- Why this method: Model-independent CAS; r90%/r10% exploits high dynamic range; common 180 pc resolution permits fair comparison.  
  > "We utilize the model-independent, non-parametric CAS analysis often used in characterizing optical galaxy morphology." (Conclusion); "We opt to use $r_{90\%}$ and $r_{10\%}$ as the dynamic range of PHANGS-ALMA data is sufficiently high and allows for a better parameterization of the gas concentration" (Concentration ($C$)); "We convolved all images to the physical resolution of $\sim 180$ pc to ensure a fair comparison across the sample." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
- Alternative: Strict mask products rejected in favor of more complete broad mask products.  
  > "The completeness of strict mask-to-broad mask is 65%, and the strict mask products have a loss of 35% of broad mask flux." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data); "Therefore, we have proceeded to use the broad mask products for the analysis in this study." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
- Alternative: NGC 6744 excluded because the middle strip was unobserved.  
  > "The middle strip has not been observed. Therefore, NGC 6744 image was excluded from this analysis." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
- Alternative: Original r80%/r20% concentration definition tested; modified r90%/r10% retained.  
  > "who uses the radius containing 80% of the total flux ($r_{80\%}$) to 20% of the total flux ($r_{20\%}$) instead of $r_{90\%}$ to $r_{10\%}$." (Concentration ($C$)); "As seen in these figures, the difference between the two definitions is negligible." (Central concentration of molecular gas in barred spiral galaxies)
- Alternative: No 1.5× Petrosian radius measurement limit.  
  > "were measured within the $1.5\times$ Petrosian radius." (Concentration ($C$)); "However, we did not apply a radius limit in our measurements, as the bulk of molecular gas is concentrated in the galaxy’s inner regions." (Concentration ($C$))
- Alternative: Fixed 500 pc S smoothing rather than 0.3× Petrosian radius.  
  > "Here, (cite: Conselice2003) used $\sigma = 0.3\times r$ (the Petrosian radius), but we used $\sigma = 500 \mathrm{pc}$ as in (cite: Davis2022)." (Clumpiness ($S$))
- Alternative: Gini coefficient considered unsuitable for central concentration because it loses positional information.  
  > "We argue that the use of the Gini coefficient must be treated with caution when used for quantifying the central concentration of gas." (Clumpiness ($S$)); "The Gini coefficient loses information on the position coordinates." (Clumpiness ($S$))
- Alternative: Interaction-driven destruction or expulsion versus delayed concentration after recent bar formation as explanations for bar-length outliers.  
  > "which may indicate the destruction or expulsion of molecular gas during the interaction." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration); "An alternate explanation for the observed deviation from the general trend is the delayed central concentration of gas." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration); "Such a delay may arise from the recent formation of the bar structure, as illustrated in a recent simulation by (cite: Iles2022)." (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)
- How they decided it was enough: Visual selection required sufficient spatial resolution and complete coverage; broad masks retained 98% of total flux.  
  > "We selected 73 galaxies by visually inspecting the images and selecting galaxies with sufficient spatial resolution for depicting galaxy structures with no missing parts in the image." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data); "However, the Moment 0 maps of the broad mask products are more complete and cover larger fractions of the total flux (98%)." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
- Limitation (authors): Only four S0 and three peculiar galaxies; their analysis has small-sample uncertainties.  
  > "The sample only contains four S0 (lenticular galaxies) and three peculiar galaxies, and therefore, the analysis of these galaxies contains uncertainties pertaining to the small sample size." (PHANGS-ALMA archival data)
- Limitation (authors): S can be sensitive to smoothing width.  
  > "The final value of $S$ can be sensitive to the adopted value of $\sigma$." (Clumpiness ($S$))
- Limitation (authors): A is sensitive to center determination and preferentially weights high-flux features.  
  > "The determination of the galaxy center is crucial for a reliable measurement of the $A$." (Asymmetry ($A$)); "By definition, $A$ is most sensitive to asymmetric features where the pixel flux is highest." (Asymmetry ($A$))
- Limitation (authors): Different ETG sample composition limits comparison with Davis2022.  
  > "However, we note that the sample galaxies used in both analyses are different" (Qualitative comparison with (cite: Davis2022) correlation measurements); "However, some differences are observed, and they may arise due to the morphological and physical characteristics of the WISDOM ETGs." (Qualitative comparison with (cite: Davis2022) correlation measurements)

  > "It is unclear why these galaxies display particularly low $C$ values" (Relationship between the galactic bar and gas concentration)
