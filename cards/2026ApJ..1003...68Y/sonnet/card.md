## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA+APEX CO cubes**: Combine 12 m and 7 m visibilities and joint deconvolution with APEX single-dish using sdintimaging  
  > "To enhance the flux recovery, we used the joint deconvolution scheme `sdintimaging`" (2.1 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA, sdintimaging
   - parameter: Hogbom, threshold 0.2 Jy/beam, auto-masking  
  > "performed the CLEAN iteration to a threshold depth of 0.2 Jy beam$^{-1}$ with the Hogbom deconvolver" (2.1 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Appendix A compares APEX 12CO(2-1) with Mopra 12CO(1-0) spatial and velocity distributions  
  > "To verify that there is no significant difference in their spatial or velocity distributions, we compared the APEX" (Comparison of the MOPRA data and APEX data)
2. **Moment maps and CO gas mass**: Moments 0,1,2 over -10 to 10 km/s in V_offset; mass from integrated intensity with X_CO  
  > "Each moment is calculated in a velocity range from $-10$ to 10 km s$^{-1}$ in the $V_\mathrm{offset}$ frame." (3.1 The CO cloud distribution)
   - assumption: X_CO = 6.5e20 cm-2 (K km/s)-1; 2-1/1-0 ratio 0.8; distance 50 kpc  
  > "we adopted an $X_{\rm CO}$ factor of 6.5 $\times$10$^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$ (cite: Naslim2018)" (3.1 The CO cloud distribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Identify 13CO clumps and test boundedness**: astrodendro on 13CO cube to extract leaves; LTE surface density; sigma_v^2/R versus surface density diagram  
  > "We used the `astrodendro` package (cite: Rosolowsky2008) to hierarchically segment and identify the cloud sub-structures as trunk, branch and, leaf." (3.2 Physical parameters of molecular clouds)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: min_value 2.5 K (5 sigma), min_pix 50, min_delta 2.5 K  
  > "We adopt `min_value` of 2.5 K, corresponding to 5 times the rms noise level." (3.2 Physical parameters of molecular clouds)
   - assumption: LTE; [H2]/[13CO] = 3.2e6  
  > "we assumed local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) to calculate the CO surface density" (3.2 Physical parameters of molecular clouds)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **H I column density and masses of L and D components**: Optically thin N(HI)=1.823e18 W(HI) per pixel; integrate over L and D velocity ranges  
  > "by assuming the optically thin condition, using the equation of $N$(H$\;$i) (cm$^{-2}$) = 1.823 $\times$ 10$^{18}$ $W$(H$\;$i) (K km s$^{-1}$) (cite: Dickey1990)" (3.3 H I clouds toward N113)
   - assumption: optically thin H I  
  > "by assuming the optically thin condition" (3.3 H I clouds toward N113)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Complementarity and displacement between L and D components**: Shift L-component map pixel by pixel and compute Pearson correlation with D-component at each offset; pick minima  
  > "we shifted the integrated intensity map of the L-component pixel-by-pixel and calculated the Pearson’s correlation coefficients between D- and L-components at each offset pixel." (4.1 Evidence of a CCC)
   - assumption: collision angle 45 deg to line of sight; velocity 40 km/s projected  
  > "for an assumed angle of 45 degrees between the line of sight and the collision path in 3D" (4.1 Evidence of a CCC)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Second minimum chosen over global minimum by visual match with GMC and 8 um features  
  > "the complementary matching between the L-component with the GMC and the 8 $\mu$m features at the tip of the L-component is more reasonable" (4.1 Evidence of a CCC)

## Qualitative findings

- The CO cloud forms a V-shaped pair of ~10 pc filaments; gravitationally bound clumps lie along the V and are associated with YSOs.  
  > "A notable feature is that gravitationally bound clumps are distributed along the V-shaped cloud, while clumps in other locations are less bound." (3.2 Physical parameters of molecular clouds)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Joint deconvolution with APEX used to enhance flux recovery  
  > "To enhance the flux recovery, we used the joint deconvolution scheme `sdintimaging`" (2.1 ALMA Observations)
- Alternative: The 122.6 pc displacement (global correlation minimum) was rejected in favor of 53.4 pc based on matching to GMC and 8 um features.  
  > "We, therefore, adopt the latter displacement in the following as a probable signature of a CCC." (4.1 Evidence of a CCC)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): H I column densities likely underestimated due to low resolution dilution  
  > "the H I column densities given above are likely lower than those at smaller scales due to dilution by the low resolution" (3.3 H I clouds toward N113)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Displacement choice is subjective; global correlation minimum was rejected
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Collision timescale depends on assumed 45 deg inclination and 40 km/s
- Caveat (inferred, ours): H I at 15 pc resolution cannot resolve cloud-scale collision; alternative origins (feedback, expanding shells) not excluded
- Caveat (inferred, ours): X_CO and abundance ratio assumptions unvalidated at 0.2 pc
