## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image data**: CASA 4.2 pipeline calibration and imaging; GILDAS analysis of line images  
  > "The data were calibrated and imaged using the CASA[^1] 4.2 version pipeline (cite: McMullin2007)." (2.3 Data reduction)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Check missing flux**: Reconstruct ALMA data with single-dish beams and compare integrated fluxes with published single-dish values  
  > "To evaluate the missing flux we reconstruct our ALMA data with beams of $\sim$22$''$ (IRAM 30-m; (cite: Henkel2014; Aladro2015))" (2.4 Spatial filtering)
   - parameter: Recovered 12CN/13CN: ~89%/86% NGC 253; ~30%/53% NGC 1068; ~63% 12CN NGC 4945  
  > "Only $\sim$30% and $\sim$53% of the $^{12}$CN and $^{13}$CN single-dish integrated flux of NGC 1068 is recovered, respectively." (2.4 Spatial filtering)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with single-dish  
  > "With similar missing flux levels related to our $^{12}$CN and $^{13}$CN data in NGC 253 and NGC 1068, which is based on a comparison of our interferometric measurements with previously published single-dish observations" (Spatial filtering)
3. **Make 12CN/13CN ratio maps**: Ratio of velocity-integrated intensities where 13CN detected at S/N >~ 5 sigma  
  > "The line ratios are calculated using velocity-integrated intensities where the $^{13}$CN lines are detected with signal-to-noise ratios of S/N $\gtrsim$ 5$\sigma$." (3.2 12CN/13CN line ratios)
   - software: GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive 12CN opacity**: From I(3/2-1/2)/I(1/2-1/2) = (1-e^-tau1)/(1-e^-tau2) with tau1 = 2 tau2  
  > "where $\tau_1$ and $\tau_2$ are the optical depths of $^{12}$CN $J$ = 3/2–1/2 and 1/2–1/2, respectively, and $\tau_1$ = 2$\tau_2$" (3.3 Opacities)
   - assumption: 13CN optically thin; LTE  
  > "In this work we assume that the $^{13}$CN $N$ = 1–0 line is optically thin in all of our three studied objects." (3.3 Opacities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 13CN hyperfine ratios consistent with LTE optically thin  
  > "the corresponding average line integrated intensity ratios are 1.04 $\pm$ 0.08, 1.03 $\pm$ 0.14, and 1.25 $\pm$ 0.01, which is consistent with LTE and optically thin emission" (Opacities of $^{12}$CN and $^{13}$CN)
5. **Derive 12C/13C**: Opacity-corrected 12CN intensities divided by 1.082 x I(13CN)  
  > "The factor of 1.082 is caused by the fact that a weak $^{13}$CN ($F_1$, $F_2$ = 1–0) hyperfine feature near 108.4 GHz is both below our detection threshold" (3.4 The 12C/13C isotopic ratios from CN)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin 13CN  
  > "Assuming the $^{13}$CN $N$ = 1–0 lines are optically thin and assuming that LTE holds in NGC 253, NGC 1068, and NGC 4945" (3.4 The 12C/13C isotopic ratios from CN)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with single-dish ratios  
  > "For NGC 253 and NGC 1068, our average measured $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C isotope ratios agree well with those obtained from the single-dish observations" (Comparison to previous $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C isotopic ratio measurements)

## Qualitative findings

- Highest ratios in outskirts; lowest at NE/SW molecular peaks in NGC 253, NE/SW edges in NGC 1068, and the center of NGC 4945  
  > "The highest $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C isotopic ratios are determined in some of the outskirts of the nuclear regions of the three starburst galaxies." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: CN has hyperfine structure for opacity check and is not affected by fractionation  
  > "Presently, there is only one molecule matching all these conditions, the cyanide radical (CN)." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Missing flux, particularly NGC 1068 (~30% 12CN recovered); possible CH3OH blending with 13CN; low 13CN S/N at edges  
  > "This could indicate that the $I$($^{12}$CN)/$I$($^{13}$CN) ratio may be underestimated from our ALMA data in NGC 1068." (2.4 Spatial filtering)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Missing flux in NGC 1068 differs between 12CN and 13CN (30% vs 53%) yet is assumed to affect ratios weakly
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Opacity assumes tau1=2 tau2 and LTE; excitation effects ignored
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interpretation of inflow/outflow is speculative given beam dilution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Average ratios from pixels with S/N cut may bias high or low
