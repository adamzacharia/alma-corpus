  > "We model the galaxy kinematics using GalPaK 3D 1 (Bouch´e et al. 2015), a 3D galaxy disk modeling code that fits directly a model on the datacubes using a Markov-Chain Monte Carlo method." (2.3.3. Kinematics); "To obtain beam-smearing corrected velocity dispersion profiles of each galaxy, we subtract in quadrature the unresolved velocity shear obtained from our models with GalPak 3D to the observed velocity dispersion map, already corrected for instrumental broadening (Epinat et al. 2012; Bassett et al. 2014; Green et al. 2014; Levy et al. 2018)." (2.3.3. Kinematics)
   - software: GalPaK3D
   - parameter: arctan velocity profile; exponential radial flux profile; constant velocity dispersion over disk; radial bins of 300 pc  
  > "We adopt an arctan function to model the velocity (Courteau 1997) and an exponential profile for the radial flux profile." (2.3.3.   > "We model the galaxy kinematics using GalPaK 3D 1 (Bouch´e et al. 2015), a 3D galaxy disk modeling code that fits directly a model on the datacubes using a Markov-Chain Monte Carlo method." ## Qualitative findings

- Molecular gas velocity dispersion is systematically lower than ionized gas velocity dispersion at all radii, indicating the co-existence of a thin molecular disk and a thick ionized gas disk.  
  > "This offset is constant within the galaxy disks and indicates the co-existence of a thin molecular and thick ionized gas disks." (abstract); "We find that at all radii, in all galaxies the molecular gas velocity dispersion is systematically lower than that of ionized gas, with σ 0 ,mol ∼ 8 − 27 km s − 1 and σ 0 ,ion ∼ 25 − 71 km s − 1 for the molecular and ionized gas, respectively." (3.1. Difference between σ 0 ,ion and σ 0 ,mol and correlation with gas fraction)
- Deep CO(3-2) stacked spectra do not reveal a broad secondary molecular gas velocity dispersion component in DYNAMO galaxies.  
  > "The deep CO stacked data does not show a broader component compared to the shallower stacked CO data, which is different than what is found in local spirals." (3.1. Difference between σ 0 ,ion and σ 0 ,mol and correlation with gas fraction)
- FIRE and EAGLE cosmological simulations overall overestimate the cold molecular gas velocity dispersion at high redshift, whereas TNG50 is closer to the observations.  
  > "We also obtain an increase with redshift and show that the EAGLE and FIRE simulations overall overestimate σ 0 ,mol at high redshift." (abstract); "The values from TNG50 are however lower than FIRE and EAGLE, and more similar to our observations." (3.2. Redshift evolution of σ 0 ,mol)
- Star formation models with variable star formation efficiency (ϵ_ff) and/or variable feedback efficiency (p_*/m_*) better match molecular gas kinematic observations than constant-efficiency models with or without gravitational transport.  
  > "When using the molecular gas as a tracer, our sample is not consistent with predictions from constant efficiency star formation models, even when including transport as a source of turbulence. Feedback models with variable star formation efficiency, ϵ ff, and/or feedback efficiency, p ∗ /m ∗, better predict our observations." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: DYNAMO galaxies at 0.075 < z < 0.2 have physical and kinematic properties matching z ~ 1-2 main-sequence galaxies, allowing resolved (~1.2 kpc) comparison of molecular and ionized gas kinematics with ALMA and GMOS/Gemini, plus deep ALMA CO(3-2) data to test for a broad molecular component.  
  > "In this work, we use DYNAMO (Green et al. 2014; Fisher et al. 2017a), a sample of rare nearby galaxies at 0 . 075 < z < 0 . 2 showing similar physical properties to z ∼ 1 − 2 main sequence galaxies, to probe the molecular and ionized gas kinematics." (1. INTRODUCTION); "We use deep ALMA CO(3-2) data of the galaxies G04-1, G08-5 and G14-1 obtained in 2019 (PI: R. Herrera-Camus) to compare with the shallower data." (3.1. Difference between σ 0 ,ion and σ 0 ,mol and correlation with gas fraction)
- Why this method: GalPaK3D fits a 3D model directly to datacubes convolved with the PSF and LSF so that beam-smearing is directly accounted for during fitting.  
  > "The model is convolved with the pointspread function (PSF) and line-spread function (LSF). In this way, the beam-smearing effect is directly taken into account during the fitting." (2.3.3. Kinematics)
- Alternative: Two-Gaussian component fit to stacked CO(3-2) spectra rejected in favor of a single Gaussian fit based on Bayesian Information Criterion (ΔBIC > 10).  
  > "Using the Bayesian information criterion (BIC), we also find that a single Gaussian fits is more adequate than a two Gaussian component for these spectra (difference of BIC higher than 10)." (3.1. Difference between σ 0 ,ion and σ 0 ,mol and correlation with gas fraction)
- Alternative: Alternative beam-smearing corrections noted to affect only the steep central velocity gradient and not the disk dispersion values.  
  > "We note that adopting a different beam-smearing correction could make the central part of the dispersion profile flatter or steeper, but this would not affect significantly the values in the disk." (2.3.3. Kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Spectral resolution of GMOS data is insufficient to determine from line ratios whether wide Hβ profiles in DYNAMO galaxies are due to shocks vs photoionization.  
  > "We cannot say from our data if the wider H β profiles have similar lines ratios in DYNAMO galaxies, though we note the dispersion is similar. More work at high spectral resolution is needed to determine the nature of the wide emission lines in DYNAMO galaxies." (3.1. Difference between σ 0 ,ion and σ 0 ,mol and correlation with gas fraction)
- Limitation (authors): High-redshift comparison galaxies lack individually resolved CO, SFR, and stellar half-light radii, requiring the assumption R_1/2,SFR ≈ R_1/2,CO ≈ R_1/2,*, which could shift PHIBSS pressures by 0.2-0.35 dex.  
  > "We note that assuming R 1 / 2 ,H α = 1 . 1 R 1 / 2 ,CO and R 1 / 2 ,CO = 0 . 8 R 1 / 2 ,⋆ , as found in this work for DYNAMO galaxies, could increase the pressure by a factor 1.52.0. (0.2-0.35 dex) for the PHIBSS galaxies." (4.2. Correlation of Σ SF R with pressure)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Molecular gas kinematics are traced with higher-J transitions (CO 3-2 and CO 4-3) rather than CO(1-0), which may preferentially trace denser/warmer gas near the midplane compared to the bulk molecular reservoir.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA beam sizes for the 2017.1.00239.S dataset (0.6"-0.9", ~1.3-2.0 kpc) are coarser than the GMOS seeing (~0.5", ~1.1 kpc) and approach the CO half-light radii (1.1-3.3 kpc), making the kinematic fits heavily reliant on GalPaK3D forward modeling of beam smearing.
