## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure dust disk size and flux**: Gaussian model fit to continuum visibilities; cross-check with 1D Gaussian cuts deconvolved from beam  
  > "Assuming a Gaussian model for the disk, we fitted for the disk size and its flux in the uv visibility plane." (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
   - assumption: geometrically thin disk for inclination lower limit  
  > "a lower limit on inclination angle from the line of sight of $i {\sim}30^{\circ}-35^{\circ}$ is found assuming a geometrically thin disk" (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 1D Gaussian fits consistent with uv fit  
  > "provides the disk FWHMs fully consistent with the measured disk size from the uv visibility fit" (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
2. **Estimate disk mass**: Optically thin 9 mm formula M=D^2F/(B(T)kappa), rescaled with T=100 K and Ossenkopf & Henning opacity  
  > "The higher dust disk temperature of 100 K revealed by our observations reduces the mass to 0.08 $M_\odot$." (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
   - assumption: dust T=100 K; aggregates without ice mantles, coagulation at >=1e8 cm-3 raises kappa by 6.5  
  > "Using the corresponding model in (cite: Ossenkopf1994) increases $\kappa_\nu$ by a factor 6.5, decreasing the disk mass further to 0.01$M_\odot$." (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit envelope infall/rotation to get stellar mass and angular momentum**: Ballistic rotating-infalling envelope model compared to H13CO+ PV diagram perpendicular to jet  
  > "well reproduced by a ballistic model of rotating and infalling flattened envelope" (3.2 Infall)
   - assumption: inclination 30-44 deg  
  > "considering inclination angles ${\sim}30^\circ-44^\circ$" (3.2 Infall)
   - fitting: by-eye comparison to PV  
  > "We find that the morphology of the brightest features in the observed P-V diagrams match well with the thin shell model." (Wind morphology and kinematics)
   - validation: compared with Gaudel et al. 2020 l_env  
  > "We checked that their measured $V(R)$ agree very well with the brightest "branches" of our PV cut in H$^{13}$CO$^+$." (3.2 Infall)
4. **Accretion rate from luminosity**: L_acc = G M Mdot / R_star with L=9 Lsun, R=3 Rsun  
  > "taking the appropriate stellar radius for an accreting protostar of this mass, $R_{\star}=3$ R$_{\odot}$ ((cite: Stahler1988))" (3.2 Infall)
   - assumption: bolometric luminosity dominated by accretion  
  > "assuming that the bolometric luminosity is dominated by the accretion luminosity" (3.2 Infall)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Retrieve outflow shape and kinematics from transverse PV**: Thin axisymmetric rotating shell; measure max projected radii and velocities at z_proj=30-120 au, derive r(z), v_phi, v_z; model PV overlay with conical shape, linear v_z, constant v_phi=3.5 km/s  
  > "Our model assumes a thin, axisymmetric rotating shell of gas to retrieve the flow shape $r(z)$, $v_{\phi}$ (rotational velocity), and $v_z$ (velocity along the axis)." (4.1 Wind morphology and kinematics)
   - assumption: poloidal velocity tangential to flow  
  > "we find $v_r$ by assuming that the poloidal velocity is tangential to the flow shape" (Appendix A)
   - fitting: parameters adjusted manually  
  > "we played with the various parameters to find the best-fit ring-like structure for the P-V diagrams" (Appendix A)
   - validation: black curves from the model compared to PV diagrams  
  > "We find that the morphology of the brightest features in the observed P-V diagrams match well with the thin shell model." (4.1 Wind morphology and kinematics)
6. **Wind launching radius and lever arm**: Anderson relation with measured r v_phi and v_tot for M*=0.3, 0.5 Msun; lambda_phi from r v_phi v_tot/GM = lambda sqrt(2 lambda-3)  
  > "(rv_{\phi} \times v_{\rm tot})/GM_\star = \lambda_\phi \sqrt{2\lambda_\phi -3}." (4.2 Wind launching point)
   - assumption: cold, steady, axisymmetric MHD wind, gravity neglected  
  > "valid at any point in a cold, steady, axisymmetric MHD disk wind where gravitational potential can be neglected" (4.2 Wind launching point)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: including gravity changes r0 by ~1-5 au  
  > "would only affect the launching radius by ${\sim}1-5$ au" (4.2 Wind launching point)
7. **Wind mass-loss rate and angular momentum budget**: H13CN column density from CASSIS in aperture at 60 au; Mdot=1.4 mp N dr v_z/X; compare with f_M from Tabone 2020  
  > "The column density of HCN, $N_{\rm HCN}$, was calculated from that of H$^{13}$CN using the CASSIS spectral analysis tool" (4.3 Wind mass-flux)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: aperture 2" x ~0.1" at 60 au SE; v_z=2.5 km/s; two Gaussian components  
  > "we fit two Gaussian profiles to find the column density using CASSIS" (4.3 Wind mass-flux)
   - assumption: Tex=100 K, 12C/13C=70, X_HCN=1e-7, optically thin  
  > "the optically thin assumption remains valid" (4.3 Wind mass-flux)
   - fitting: two-Gaussian fit in CASSIS  
  > "because the H$^{13}$CN line profile is asymmetric, we fit two Gaussian profiles to find the column density using CASSIS" (Wind mass-flux)
   - validation: optical depth check; f_M ~4 agrees with observed ratio  
  > "we find $f_{\rm M} {\sim} 4$, in agreement with the observationally derived mass-flux" (4.3 Wind mass-flux)

## Qualitative findings

- CH3OH and H13CN emission shows a central hole attributed to continuum over-subtraction of optically thick dust.  
  > "Therefore, continuum over-subtraction is likely causing this hole in methanol emission." (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
- H13CN PV cuts show nested onion-like velocity structure and ring at 120 au.  
  > "suggestive of an "onion-like" nested velocity structure inside the low-velocity rotating flow" (3.3 Rotating small-scale outflow)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Need resolution of tens of au and high sensitivity to resolve base of the outflow.  
  > "the new ALMA observations presented here are the first able to resolve the base of the outflow in this object" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: X-wind origin excluded by launching radius  
  > "It excludes an X-wind origin, with launching radius $\lesssim0.1$ au." (4.2 Wind launching point)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Evidence is suggestive; detailed model comparison and alternative entrainment models needed.  
  > "are now required to obtain more robust constraints and definitely test the MHD interpretation" (5 Concluding remarks)
- Limitation (authors): Disk mass quite uncertain.  
  > "The disk mass is quite uncertain, but is probably below 0.1 M$_\ensuremath{\odot}$" (3.1 Dust disk size and mass)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): PV measurements have large uncertainties (v_phi 4±2 km/s) and are read by eye
- Caveat (inferred, ours): X_HCN uncertain by an order of magnitude drives mass-loss rate
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Entrainment by jet not excluded
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inclination uncertain (30-44 deg)
