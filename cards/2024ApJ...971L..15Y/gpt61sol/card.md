  > "spectrum at the peak position" (Localized SO emission); "To measure the line width, we fit a Gaussian to the spectrum and obtained an FWHM of $0.28\ \rm km\ s^{-1}$" (Localized SO emission)

5. **Fit the outflow trajectory**: Ballistic knots under stellar gravity; modify vertical gravity by (1-epsilon); launch velocity v+v_p; project trajectory into RA-DEC-v_LOS and compare with five emission peaks  
  > "\frac{d^2{z}}{dt^2} = -\frac{GM_\star}{|\bm{r}|^3} z ( 1 - \epsilon )," (Outflow trajectory model); "the outflow has a velocity of ${\bm v} + {\bm v_p}$ with respect to the central star." (Outflow trajectory model); "We specified the knot position and line of sight velocity by taking the five emission peaks in the image cubes." (Outflow trajectory model)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: Free parameters: v_out, phi, psi, epsilon, theta_p, t_dyn  
  > "Then, we set $v_{\rm out} (\equiv |{\bm v}|)$, $\phi$, $\psi$, $\epsilon$, $\theta_p$, and the dynamical time scale of the outflow $t_{\rm dyn}$ as free parameters" (Outflow trajectory model)
   - parameter: Velocity uncertainty 0.2 km s^-1; RA/DEC errors from nominal ALMA astrometric accuracy  
  > "We estimated the errors in RA and DEC using the nominal astrometric accuracy of ALMA (ALMA Technical Handbook)." (Outflow trajectory model); "For the velocity, we conservatively assume an uncertainty of twice the velocity resolution, $0.2\ \rm km\ s^{-1}$." (Outflow trajectory model)
   - parameter: 24 walkers; 5000 steps; 2000 burn-in steps  
  > "We employed 24 walkers with 5000 steps (and 2000 burn-in steps) to sample the posterior distribution." (Outflow trajectory model)
   - assumption: Protoplanet at 42 au; its gravity negligible in the observed outflow  
  > "Here we only consider the gravity force from the central star since the protoplanet mass would be negligible in the observed outflow." (Outflow trajectory model); "The protoplanet is assumed to be located at the dust gap radius of 42 au from the central star" (Outflow trajectory model)
   - assumption: Clock-wise rotation; theta_p prior places the protoplanet near, but not beyond, the first knot  
  > "so that the protoplanet is located near the first ( rightmost) knot but does not exceed it assuming clock-wise rotation." (Outflow trajectory model)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC; log P=-sum_i sum_d D_i,d^2/(2 sigma_i,d^2), using orthogonal distances along RA, DEC and LOS velocity; mostly uniform priors; theta_p one-sided Gaussian with mean 185° and standard deviation 30°  
  > "\log P = - \sum_i \sum_d \frac{D_{i, d}^2}{2 \sigma_{i, d}^2}." (Outflow trajectory model); "$D_{i, d}$ is the orthogonal distance between the knot $i$ and the trajectory along the $d$ axis." (Outflow trajectory model); "While we applied uniform priors for most parameters, we adopted a one-sided Gaussian with a mean of $185^\circ$ and a standard deviation of $30^\circ$ for $\theta_p$" (Outflow trajectory model)
   - validation: Reported well-converged MCMC, corner plot and agreement of best-fit trajectory with observed morphology  
  > "The MCMC sampling was well-converged and the corner plot is shown in Appendix" (Outflow trajectory model); "The best-fit trajectory is well-fitted to the observed emission morphology" (Outflow trajectory model)
6. **Derive SO and total outflow masses**: Convert integrated SO J=8_7 - 7_6 flux to SO mass and averaged column density; convert SO mass to total gas mass using SO/H2  
  > "we estimated the total mass of SO molecules to be $(1-4) \times 10^{20}\ {\rm g}$ and the averaged SO column density of $(1-4) \times 10^{12}\ {\rm cm^{-2}}$" (Dynamical Parameters); "To convert the SO mass to the total gas mass of the outflow $M_{\rm out}$, it is necessary to assume the SO/H$_2$ ratio in the outflow." (Dynamical Parameters)
   - parameter: Integrated SO flux 13±3 mJy km s^-1  
  > "($13\pm3\ {\rm mJy\ \rm km\ s^{-1}}$)" (Dynamical Parameters)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission and local thermal equilibrium  
  > "Assuming that the emission is optically thin and local thermal equilibrium is valid" (Dynamical Parameters)
   - assumption: Temperature 30-300 K; SO/H2 =10^-7 - 10^-6  
  > "for a temperature range of $30-300$ K" (Dynamical Parameters); "Overall, it would be reasonable to assume the SO/H$_2$ ratio in the outflow lies in $10^{-7} - 10^{-6}$." (Dynamical Parameters)

## Qualitative findings

- SO emission is localized southeast of TW Hya, extends over several beams, and follows an arc from the dust gap outward  
  > "line emission is localized to the southeast part of the disk but has an extension over several beams." (Localized SO emission); "The emission has an arc-like morphology that starts from the dust gap and continues to the outer region." (Localized SO emission)
- No Keplerian SO component found  
  > "It is notable that we did not find any Keplerian component of SO." (Localized SO emission)
- SO J=8_7 - 7_6 traces hotter gas than SO J=5_6 - 4_5  
  > "while the upper state energy of the $\rm SO\ J=8_7 - 7_6\ $" (Localized SO emission); "line is $\sim81$ K, that of the $\rm SO\ J=5_6 - 4_5\ $" (Localized SO emission); "line is just $\sim35$ K." (Localized SO emission)
- A simple ballistic outflow model explains the observed morphology  
  > "In summary, the observed morphology of the SO emission can be well explained by the simple ballistic outflow model." (Outflow trajectory model)
- Possible mono-polar outflow: no signature of the predicted blue-shifted counterpart  
  > "We checked the image cube but did not see any signature. Therefore, the planet-driven outflow may be mono-polar." (Consistency with non-detections)
- Results imply a circumplanetary disk around an embedded super-Earth  
  > "Our results imply that a $4\ M_\oplus$ planet at 42 au can have a circumplanetary disk, constraining disk formation processes." (Outflow-driving source)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival SO lines test shock-traced planet-driven outflow; deep CO observations provide a counterpart search  
  > "During mass accretion, planet-driven outflows may be launched, which could be observable by shock tracers such as sulfur monoxide (SO)." (abstract); "We searched for a counterpart of the SO emission in the deep CO $J=3-2$ line observations" (Dynamical Parameters)
- Why this method: Natural weighting maximizes point-source sensitivity; ballistic modeling tests morphology changed by stellar gravity and orbital source motion  
  > "We adopted natural weighting to achieve the highest point source sensitivity." (Archival Observations); "the driving source (protoplanet) and its outflow feel gravity force from the central star, which may change the outflow morphology." (Outflow trajectory model); "To test this case, we constructed a simple ballistic trajectory model." (Outflow trajectory model)
- Alternative: Other molecular identifications searched for; no candidates found  
  > "We searched other molecular lines around this frequency but did not find any candidates." (Localized SO emission)

  > "It is notable that we did not find any Keplerian component of SO." (Localized SO emission)
