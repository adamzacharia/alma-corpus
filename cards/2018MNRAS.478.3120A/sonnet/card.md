## Analysis recipe

1. **CO(3-2) upper limit**: Use pipeline cube, no line detected; 5-sigma limit assuming 200 km/s width  
  > "Assuming a line width of 200 km/s, we derive a 5-sigma limit on the integrated flux density of \<140 mJy km/s." (New ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: RMS 0.139 mJy per 150 km/s channel; line width 200 km/s  
  > "The resulting RMS at the observed frequency is 0.139 mJy in 150 km/s channel width." (New ALMA Observations)
   - assumption: Milky-Way like J-transition ratio; alpha_CO = 4.6 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "we choose to use a galactic H2-CO conversion factor of 4.6 M$_{\odot}$ (K km/s pc$^2$)$^{-1}$" (New ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Molecular gas mass limit**: Convert CO(1-0) limit to luminosity and mass with alpha_CO  
  > "leading to a 5-sigma limit on the molecular mass of log($M_{\rm mol}$/M$_{\odot}$) \< 9.89." (New ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **HST photometry and SED fit for stellar mass**: Aperture photometry with GALFIT aperture correction; LE PHARE SED fit with single burst, BC03, Calzetti, Chabrier IMF  
  > "We used the code with a single burst of star formation, templates from (cite: bruzual03) stellar population models, a Calzetti extinction law and a (cite: chabrier03) initial mass function." (4.3 Spectral Energy Distribution Fit)
   - software: GALFIT, LE PHARE, calwf3
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature stellar masses (Christensen et al. 2014, Krogager et al. 2013)  
  > "For the DLA counterpart towards Q0302$-$223 we find log($M_*/\text{M}_{\odot}$) = 9.5 $\pm$ 0.2, which is consistent with (cite: christensen14) who found 9.65 $\pm$ 0.08." (4.3 Spectral Energy Distribution Fit)
