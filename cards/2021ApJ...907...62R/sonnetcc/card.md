## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce and image ATCA and ALMA data, measure continuum and line fluxes**: ATCA reduced in MIRIAD; ALMA calibrated with CASA calibration pipeline and imaged manually with tclean (natural and Briggs robust +0.5 / -0.5)  
  > "Data reduction was performed using the miriad package (Sault et al. (cite: sault95))." (Data / ATCA); "Data reduction was performed using version 5.4.0 or 5.6.1 of the casa package (McMullin et al. (cite: mcmullin07)), aided by the calibration pipeline included with each version." (Data / ALMA)
   - software: MIRIAD, CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Weighting natural, Briggs robust +0.5 and robust -0.5; image parameters in Table 1  
  > "Briggs robust +0.5, and Briggs robust –0.5 weighting" (Data / ALMA)
   - parameter: ALMA continuum maps over the entire line-free bandwidths  
  > "Continuum maps are created over the entire line-free bandwidths." (Data / ALMA)
   - parameter: ATCA data binned to 40-80 km/s  
  > "Data were binned to 40–80 kms$^{-1}$ resolution in the subsequent analysis." (Data / ATCA)
   - parameter: ATCA outrigger antenna flagged manually (first track only)  
  > "it was manually flagged due to the significantly poorer phase stability on the longest baselines" (Data / ATCA)
   - parameter: ALMA CO(10-9) compact-configuration upper sideband discarded  
  > "had to be discarded due to unreliable calibration given the modest weather conditions at the time of observing" (Data / ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ATCA absolute flux cross-checked against Uranus; relative accuracy between tracks 2%  
  > "which we cross-checked based on observations of Uranus" (Data / ATCA); "with a relative accuracy at the 2% level between the two tracks" (Data / ATCA)
2. **Reduce HST imaging and extract photometry**: Standard calibration and AstroDrizzle combination; SExtractor catalogs; astrometry matched to Gaia DR2 (mean shift); dual-mode SExtractor photometry with F160W as detection image  
  > "the combination of exposures with `AstroDrizzle`" (Data / Hubble Space Telescope); "Photometry catalogs were made using SExtractor in dual mode, with F160W as the detection image" (Data / Hubble Space Telescope)
   - software: AstroDrizzle, SExtractor
   - parameter: Gaia DR2 matching radius of one FWHM, typically 14 matches per WFC3 band, final shift the mean of matched-source shifts  
  > "with a matching radius of one full width at half maximum, which resulted in typically 14 matches per band in the WFC3 filters" (Data / Hubble Space Telescope); "The final shift applied was taken as the mean of the shifts for these matched sources." (Data / Hubble Space Telescope)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Search for V-band dropout Lyman-break candidates and estimate overdensity**: Color-cut selection using F606W, F098M and F160W, rejecting stars and edge sources; field density from COSMOS2015 photometric redshifts; overdensity delta_g = N_r/(Sigma_field pi r^2) - 1  
  > "As such, we used the F606W–F098M color as the main selection criterion to reject" (Results / HST); "Adopting a redshift slice of $\Delta{z}$=0.64 and using the photometric redshifts reported in the COSMOS2015 catalog" (Results / HST)
   - parameter: >5 sigma detection in all WFC3-IR bands  
  > "we required a detection at $>$5$\sigma$ in all WFC3-IR bands" (Results / HST)
   - parameter: F606W-F098M > 2.3 and F098M-F160W < 1.2 (AB), plus F606W-F098M > 2.3 + (F098M-F160W)  
  > "F606W – F098M $>$ 2.3" (Results / HST); "F098M – F160W $<$ 1.2" (Results / HST)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Candidates with F606W detections or point-source morphology discarded; Lyman-alpha imaging or spectroscopy needed to confirm  
  > "Given the custom selection criteria, narrow-band Lyman–$\alpha$ imaging and/or follow-up spectroscopy is required" (Results / HST)
4. **Fit line profiles: de-blend CO(10-9) and H2O, fit CO(9-8) with OH+**: Fix CO and H2O line centroids to the CO(9-8) centroid and assume a common line width to de-blend; simultaneous fit of CO(9-8) and OH+ emission  
  > "we fix the line centroids of both lines to the high signal-to-noise ratio CO($J$=9$\to$8) lines, and we assume a common width of the CO and H$_2$O lines" (Results / CO Emission); "We also simultaneously fit the CO($J$=9$\to$8) and OH$^+$ emission" (Results / CO Emission)
   - assumption: Common line width for CO(10-9) and H2O 3_12-2_21; centroids fixed to CO(9-8)  
  > "we assume a common width of the CO and H$_2$O lines" (Results / CO Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CO(9-8) and CO(10-9) profiles compared per component to test OH+ absorption and H2O contamination  
  > "we compare the profiles of the CO $J$=9$\to$8 and 10$\to$9 lines for each component individually" (Results / CO Emission)
5. **SED modelling of stellar and dust emission with CIGALE**: CIGALE on all photometry for the whole system and for N and S separately; two series of SFH limits (<=1.0 Gyr and <=0.2 Gyr)  
  > "We sampled standard ranges for all main parameters within CIGALE" (Analysis / CIGALE); "with star-formation histories (SFHs) either limited to approximately the age of the universe at $z$=5.655 (i.e., $\leq$1.0 Gyr), or to an age of $\leq$0.2 Gyr" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Bruzual & Charlot single stellar population, Chabrier IMF, delayed SFH  
  > "used a Bruzual & Charlot ((cite: bc03)) single stellar population and a Chabrier ((cite: chabrier03)) stellar initial mass function" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - parameter: Dust attenuation power-law slope fixed to -0.7 for ISM and birth clouds  
  > "We fixed the power-law slope of the dust attenuation law to –0.7 for both the interstellar medium and birth clouds" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - parameter: PAH mass fraction q_PAH = 3.9% (not constrained by the data)  
  > "assumed a polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon (PAH) mass fraction (which is not directly constrained by the data)" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - parameter: U_max = 10^6 fixed  
  > "is fixed to the value found for nearby star-forming galaxies" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - assumption: Simplified Draine & Li dust prescription used by CIGALE  
  > "CIGALE uses the simplified Draine & Li ((cite: dl07)) prescription" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - fitting: CIGALE fit with standard parameter ranges; fit statistic and priors not stated  
  > "We sampled standard ranges for all main parameters within CIGALE" (Analysis / CIGALE)
   - validation: Results from the two SFH series compared; differences 0.6%, 3% and -1% for the whole system  
  > "These values would differ by only 0.6%, 3%, and –1% when adopting the more restricted parameter study" (Analysis / CIGALE)
6. **Fit modified black-body dust SED and derive L_IR and SFR_IR**: MCMC modified black-body (mbb_emcee) joined to a nu^alpha power law on the Wien side; fit parameters T_dust, beta_IR, lambda_0, alpha; 500 um flux as normalization  
  > "We adopt the same Markov Chain Monte Carlo-based method (mbb_emcee) as described in our previous work" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits); "we join the MBB function to a $\nu^\alpha$ power law toward the far Wien side of the spectral energy distribution" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits)
   - software: mbb_emcee
   - parameter: Observed-frame 500 um flux density used as normalization parameter  
  > "We also use the observed-frame 500 $\mu$m flux density as a normalization parameter" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF for SFR_IR  
  > "when assuming a Chabrier ((cite: chabrier03)) stellar initial mass function" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits)
   - fitting: MCMC; prior of 1.8+/-0.6 on beta_IR, no prior on other parameters  
  > "We place a relatively broad prior of 1.8$\pm$0.6 on" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits); "and leave all other parameters without a prior" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits)
   - validation: Results agree with previous estimates; SFR_IR about 30% lower than CIGALE SFR, giving at least 30% systematic uncertainty  
  > "and they agree with our previous estimates within the uncertainties" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits); "we thus estimate that the systematic uncertainty on the SFR and quantities derived from it is at least 30%" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits)
7. **Measure source sizes and SFR surface densities**: Diameters of CO, OH+ and continuum emission (Table 15); final sizes are the median of all measurements; Sigma_SFR from SFR_IR and size  
  > "By taking the median of all measurements, we find final sizes of" (Analysis / Extent of the Gas and Dust Emission)
   - assumption: 0.87 mm flux ratio between N and S representative of the SED peak for splitting SFR  
  > "assuming that the flux ratio at 0.87 mm is representative of the peak of the SED" (Analysis / Extent of the Gas and Dust Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **CO line excitation with LVG models**: Grid of LVG models; three components needed to fit the CO ladder simultaneously  
  > "we investigated the CO line excitation by calculating a grid of large velocity gradient (LVG) models" (Analysis / CO Large Velocity Gradient Modeling); "three LVG components are required to fit all lines simultaneously" (Analysis / CO Large Velocity Gradient Modeling)
   - parameter: H2 ortho-to-para ratio 3:1; CMB temperature at z=5.655 of 18.135 K; CO/H2 abundance over velocity gradient 1e-5 pc (km/s)^-1  
  > "we kept the H$_2$ ortho-to-para ratio fixed to 3:1, the cosmic microwave background (CMB) temperature at $z$=5.655 set to 18.135 K" (Analysis / CO Large Velocity Gradient Modeling)
   - parameter: Flower (2001) CO collision rates  
  > "We also adopted the Flower ((cite: flower01)) CO collision rates" (Analysis / CO Large Velocity Gradient Modeling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CO excitation up to J=10-9 fully explained by collisional excitation with the three components  
  > "we are able to fully explain the CO excitation up to the $J$=10$\to$9 level with collisional excitation" (Analysis / CO Large Velocity Gradient Modeling)
9. **Dynamical masses, alpha_CO limits, gas fraction and gas-to-dust ratio**: Isotropic virial estimator with CO(9-8) line widths and sizes (30% larger for ADFS-27N); alpha_CO upper limits from M_dyn minus stellar and dust mass, with and without 25% dark matter  
  > "we adopt an isotropic virial estimator (e.g., Engel et al. (cite: engel10))" (Analysis / Dynamical Masses, Gas Mass Fraction, and Gas-to-Dust Ratio); "sizes that are 30% larger than the averages obtained from the moment-0 and continuum maps for ADFS-27N" (Analysis / Dynamical Masses, Gas Mass Fraction, and Gas-to-Dust Ratio)
   - parameter: Gas mass ratio taken as the CO(9-8) line flux ratio  
  > "When assuming the CO($J$=9$\to$8) line flux ratio as the gas mass ratio" (Analysis / Dynamical Masses, Gas Mass Fraction, and Gas-to-Dust Ratio)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 1.0 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1 adopted throughout  
  > "We thus adopt this value throughout this work." (Analysis / Dynamical Masses, Gas Mass Fraction, and Gas-to-Dust Ratio)
   - assumption: No dark matter in baseline; 25% dark matter contribution to M_dyn in alternative

## Qualitative findings

- OH+ P-Cygni absorption/emission and tentative CH+ absorption are detected only toward ADFS-27N, not ADFS-27S  
  > "The OH$^+$ features are only detected towards the northern component" (abstract)
- ADFS-27N is undetected in all WFC3-IR filters, so its stellar light is more dust-obscured than ADFS-27S  
  > "This suggests that the stellar light in ADFS-27N is significantly more heavily dust-obscured than in ADFS-27S" (Results / HST)
- H2O emission is stronger in ADFS-27S, consistent with its warmer dust SED  
  > "The comparatively brighter H$_2$O emission in ADFS-27S is consistent with its warmer dust spectral energy distribution shape" (Results / H2O Emission)
- No significant difference between the extent of the high-J CO and long-wavelength dust emission  
  > "There is no significant difference between the extent of the high-$J$ CO and long-wavelength dust continuum emission for either source component." (Analysis / Extent of the Gas and Dust Emission)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: New ATCA, ALMA and HST observations to better understand exceptionally luminous HyLIRGs  
  > "to obtain a more detailed understanding of the physical properties of such exceptionally luminous systems" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Kinematics focus on CO(9-8) because CO(10-9) is hard to de-blend from H2O  
  > "the CO($J$=10$\to$9) line is difficult to de-blend from the H$_2$O contribution to the velocity field, we focus on the CO($J$=9$\to$8) kinematic structure in the following" (Analysis / Dynamical Masses, Gas Mass Fraction, and Gas-to-Dust Ratio)
- Alternative: A low covering fraction of diffuse gas around ADFS-27S could explain the lack of OH+/CH+ absorption, but this interpretation is not preferred  
  > "However, this interpretation is not preferred when taking into account the finding that CH$^+$ absorption in particular appears to be ubiquitous towards" (Conclusions)
- Alternative: Two series of CIGALE fits with different SFH age limits; the less constrained series adopted for the whole system and ADFS-27S, the more constrained series for ADFS-27N  
  > "We thus adopt those from the series with less constraints on the SFH in the following" (Analysis / CIGALE); "the series with more stringent constraints on the SFH appears to provide more reasonable results" (Analysis / CIGALE)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Kinematic interpretation limited by the spatial resolution of the current data  
  > "but this interpretation remains limited by the spatial resolution of the current data" (abstract)
- Limitation (authors): Systematic uncertainty on SFR and derived quantities is at least 30%  
  > "we thus estimate that the systematic uncertainty on the SFR and quantities derived from it is at least 30%" (Analysis / Modified Black-Body Fits)
- Limitation (authors): ADFS-27N stellar mass uncertain by at least a factor of three  
  > "for ADFS-27N is uncertain by at least a factor of three in practice" (Analysis / CIGALE)
- Limitation (authors): CH+ detection is tentative and no profile fit is reported  
  > "Given the limited signal-to-noise ratio of the detection, we do not report parameters from a fit to the line profile." (Results / CH+ Absorption)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas mass depends on the LVG-extrapolated CO(1-0) flux and an assumed alpha_CO=1.0; no low-J CO(1-0) measurement exists
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source sizes and dynamical masses rest on unresolved or barely resolved CO and continuum data with an unstated size-fitting method
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CIGALE stellar mass and age for the HST-undetected ADFS-27N are poorly constrained by construction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow rate chain (5% halo fraction, 45 Myr timescale, OH+ to H column) multiplies several uncertain assumptions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(10-9) and H2O(3_12-2_21) de-blending relies on an assumed common line width
