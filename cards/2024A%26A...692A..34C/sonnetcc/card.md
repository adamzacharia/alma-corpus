## Science

- Fields: high_z_galaxies
- Question: Are kiloparsec-scale ordered magnetic fields already present in a strongly lensed dusty star-forming galaxy at z=5.6, as traced by polarised dust emission?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate the full-polarisation Band 7 data**: Standard pipeline calibration of XX and YY, split, then polarisation calibration with J0423-0120 as polarisation calibrator to correct leakage  
  > "Then, the calibrated data were split for polarisation calibration. We followed the official template to apply the polarisation calibration." (Observation and data reduction); "the blazar J0423$-$0120 was used as the polarisation calibrator to correct the instrumental polarisation leakage." (Observation and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Bandpass J0519-4546; phase J0253-5441; flux J0519-4546 (about 5 percent uncertainty)  
  > "J0519$-$4546 and J0253$-$5441 were used to calibrate the bandpass and phase variation, respectively." (Observation and data reduction); "with an uncertainty of about 5 percent." (Observation and data reduction)
   - parameter: Parallactic angle coverage wider than 120 degrees in both sessions  
  > "In both observations, the coverage of parallactic angles is wider than 120 degrees, which delivers independent full-polarisation calibration." (Observation and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Polarisation calibrator fraction 4.7 +/- 0.1 percent consistent with monitoring (3.2-5.1 percent); angle uncertainty 0.2 deg  
  > "The best-fit polarisation fraction of J0423$-$0120 is around 4.7$\pm$0.1 percent, consistent with the long-term monitoring of its polarisation status of 3.2-5.1 percent in the same period." (Observation and data reduction); "The measured uncertainty in polarisation angle is 0.2 deg." (Observation and data reduction)
2. **Image Stokes I cubes (line search) and full Stokes continuum images**: tclean: hogbom natural-weighted Stokes I cubes to search lines; clarkstokes natural-weighted full Stokes images, cleaned to 12 uJy/beam  
  > "we used the task TCLEAN within CASA to make the images and cubes." (Observation and data reduction); "The cubes were created with the ‘hogbom’ deconvolver and natural weighting." (Observation and data reduction); "we created the full Stokes images with the ‘clarkstokes’ deconvolver and natural weighting to maximise the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N)." (Observation and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Stop at 1 sigma RMS of 12 uJy/beam; beam 0.38 x 0.3 arcsec; polarised-intensity RMS 8.5 uJy/beam  
  > "All the clean processes stop when the residuals reach the $1\sigma$ root mean square (RMS) level of 12 $\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$" (Observation and data reduction); "The RMS achieved for the polarised intensity is 8.5 $\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$." (Observation and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No strong (>5 sigma) lines found in the spectral windows  
  > "We did not identify any strong lines ($>5\sigma$) within our spectral windows." (Observation and data reduction)
3. **Reduce and image the archival [C II] data**: scriptForPI reduction in CASA 4.2.2, uvcontsub (fitorder = 1), natural-weighted tclean in CASA 6.2.1, channels binned to 100 km/s  
  > "We reduced the data following the ‘scriptforPI.py’ with CASA (ver.4.2.2)." (Observation and data reduction); "the continuum was subtracted from the visibility using UVCONTSUB with the ‘fitorder’ = 1." (Observation and data reduction); "We also binned the channel width of the \[C II\] datacube to 100 km s$^{-1}$." (Observation and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: Natural weighting instead of the robust weighting of the earlier paper  
  > "we adopted a natural weighting scheme when cleaning the images with TCLEAN shipped with CASA (ver.6.2.1)." (Observation and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Natural weighting chosen for sensitivity and resolution match to the polarisation data  
  > "The natural weighting delivers better sensitivity and better matches the resolution of our full-polarisation dust observations." (Observation and data reduction)
4. **Derive polarisation fraction and magnetic-field position angle on a Nyquist grid**: Resample Stokes maps with apertures of half the clean beam; per-sample median I, Q, U; P = sqrt(Q^2+U^2)/I; mask P/sigma_P < 3; chi_B = 0.5 arctan(U/Q) + pi/2  
  > "Following the Nyquist theorem, we re-sampled the Stokes maps with an aperture that is half the size of the clean beam." (Results and analysis); "Within each sample, we calculated the median values of $Q$ and $U$." (Results and analysis); "we masked the regions with $P/\sigma_{P}<3$." (Results and analysis)
   - software: not named in the paper
   - parameter: Aperture = half the clean beam; P/sigma_P < 3 masked before position-angle calculation  
  > "we re-sampled the Stokes maps with an aperture that is half the size of the clean beam." (Results and analysis); "we masked the regions with $P/\sigma_{P}<3$." (Results and analysis)
   - assumption: Projected B-field is perpendicular to the dust polarisation angle (pi/2 offset)  
  > "the additional $\pi/2$ was added because the projected magnetic field orientation is perpendicular to the measured polarisation angle of dust emission." (Results and analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Using the mean instead of the median does not change the results  
  > "Changing the median to the mean values does not change our results." (Results and analysis)
5. **Reconstruct the source-plane dust and polarised emission with a pixellated lens model**: Pixellated lensing reconstruction (Warren & Dye) with publicly available software; central r < 0.05 arcsec and outer r > 0.18 arcsec masked  
  > "we used a pixellated lensing reconstruction technique developed by" (Results and analysis); "we masked the central area with $r$$<$ 0.05 arcsec and the outer area with $r$ $>$ 0.18 arcsec." (Results and analysis)
   - software: publicly available lens-reconstruction software (cite: Nightingale2015; Nightingale2021; Dye2018; Dye2022), name not given
   - parameter: Mask r < 0.05 arcsec and r > 0.18 arcsec; best-fit lens parameters in a table  
  > "we masked the central area with $r$$<$ 0.05 arcsec and the outer area with $r$ $>$ 0.18 arcsec." (Results and analysis)
   - assumption: No assumption on source structure  
  > "This method makes no assumption about the source structure, enabling complex source features to be recovered." (Results and analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Stokes I source structure consistent with existing lens models; polarised structure only marginally recovered  
  > "which is consistent with existing lens models" (Results and analysis); "we can only marginally recover its structure in the source plane by modelling the region with possible signals." (Results and analysis)
6. **Compare polarisation with [C II] kinematics**: Compare the spatial distribution of polarisation with [C II] kinematics and channel maps  
  > "we compare the spatial distribution of polarisation with the kinematics from \[C II\]." (Polarisation fraction and morphology of magnetic fields)
   - parameter: Median velocity dispersion of 140 km/s in the region with polarisation  
  > "We adopted the median velocity dispersion of 140 km s$^{-1}$ from the region with the lowest velocity gradient" (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Compare polarisation fraction with local starbursts and 9io9**: Compare measured fraction at 131 um (~700 pc) with SALSA HAWC+ 154 um results and 9io9  
  > "we compare the measured dust polarisation fraction in SPT0346-52 with the measurements from the SALSA Legacy Program." (Polarisation fraction and morphology of magnetic fields)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Test the alpha-Omega disk dynamo via the dynamo number**: D_d ~ 9 (h Omega/sigma)^2 ~ 9 (h/r_max)^2 (v_max/sigma)^2 compared with critical value ~7  
  > "Then, the $v_\text{max}/\sigma$ is about 2.5, which predicts $D_d\approx2.3$, well below the $D_{cr,d}\sim7$" (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
   - parameter: r_max about 1.5 kpc from the lens model; h = 300 pc; h/r_max ~0.2  
  > "The $r_\text{max}$ from our lens model is about 1.5 kpc" (Disk-driven versus merger-driven); "Assuming a typical disk scale height of 300 pc" (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
   - parameter: v_max about 350 km/s, sigma 140 km/s  
  > "the maximum rotational velocity will be $\varv_\text{max}\approx350\,$km s$^{-1}$." (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
   - assumption: Velocity gradient solely produced by a galactic disk (most extreme case)  
  > "assuming the velocity gradient is solely produced by a galactic disk" (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Authors note v_max/sigma is hard to estimate without biased assumptions  
  > "it is not straightforward to estimate the $v_\text{max}/\sigma$ without some biased assumptions." (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
9. **Estimate an equipartition upper limit on the turbulent magnetic field**: B_eq = sqrt(4 pi rho) sigma_v with rho from 50 cm^-3 and [C II] dispersion 140 km/s  
  > "where $\rho$ is the volume density of the ISM and $\sigma_\varv$ is the velocity dispersion of the gas." (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields); "We estimate the equipartition turbulent magnetic field strength to be $\leq450$ $\mu$G." (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields)
   - parameter: [C II] line dispersion 140 km/s at the polarised region; density 50 cm^-3  
  > "the observed \[C II\] line dispersion at the region with polarisation detection is about 140 km s$^{-1}$" (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields); "We assume a preferred gas density of 50 cm$^{-3}$ from photodissociation region modelling" (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields)
   - assumption: Turbulence dominates the line width; equipartition between turbulent kinetic and magnetic energy  
  > "with the assumption that the turbulence dominates the line width." (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Stated only as a theoretical upper limit  
  > "We emphasise that this is only a theoretical upper limit for the turbulent magnetic fields" (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields)

- Polarised dust emission is patchy, offset from the total dust emission, and mostly overlaps with [C II] at about -150 km/s  
  > "The polarised dust emission is patchy, offset from the total dust emission and mostly overlaps with the \[C II\] emission at a velocity of about $-$150 km s$^{-1}$." (abstract)

- Similar polarisation fraction to local starbursts despite two orders of magnitude higher SFR  
  > "The similar polarisation fraction between SPT0346$-$52 and the local starbursts is surprising" (Polarisation fraction and morphology of magnetic fields)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Lensing boosts faint polarised signal and resolution; the source is the brightest, most intensely star-forming SPT galaxy  
  > "The lens amplification can facilitate the detection of faint polarised dust emission and boost the spatial resolution." (Introduction); "which makes it the best target to detect and resolve the polarised dust emissions back to the Cosmic Dawn." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Median chosen over mean because of low S/N per Stokes parameter; natural weighting for sensitivity and resolution match  
  > "The median value is more robust than the mean value in SPT0346$-$52 due to the general low S/N of each Stokes parameter." (Results and analysis); "The natural weighting delivers better sensitivity and better matches the resolution of our full-polarisation dust observations." (Observation and data reduction)
- Alternative: Disk alpha-Omega dynamo disfavoured: dynamo number below critical and predicts axisymmetric fields inconsistent with observed structure  
  > "the $\alpha-\Omega$ dynamo predicts regular magnetic fields with an axisymmetric position angle in the disk, which is not fully consistent with the field structures from the lens reconstruction" (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
- Alternative: Outflow-driven scenario only partially explains the observations  
  > "However, the outflow can only partially explain the observations." (Outflow-driven scenario)
- Alternative: Mean instead of median gives the same result  
  > "Changing the median to the mean values does not change our results." (Results and analysis)
- How they decided it was enough: Clean stopped at residual RMS of 12 uJy/beam  
  > "All the clean processes stop when the residuals reach the $1\sigma$ root mean square (RMS) level of 12 $\mu$Jy beam$^{-1}$" (Observation and data reduction)
- Limitation (authors): Polarised structure only marginally recovered in the source plane because of faintness  
  > "due to the faintness of the polarised dust emission, we can only marginally recover its structure in the source plane" (Results and analysis)
- Limitation (authors): v_max/sigma estimate relies on biased assumptions given the complex velocity field  
  > "it is not straightforward to estimate the $v_\text{max}/\sigma$ without some biased assumptions." (Disk-driven versus merger-driven)
- Limitation (authors): Sample of two extreme starbursts biases results  
  > "which biases our results to the most extreme SFGs in the early Universe" (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields)
- Limitation (authors): Field strength cannot be reliably estimated because resolution is coarser than the turbulence scale  
  > "preventing us from estimating the strength of magnetic fields reliably." (Formation timescales of galactic magnetic fields)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Polarised fractions are medians of low-S/N Stokes values without a stated debiasing, so P may be biased high in low-S/N samples
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Half-beam Nyquist samples are oversampled relative to independent beams, so the P distribution and chi_B histogram are correlated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Bimodality of chi_B in only a few resolution elements could be affected by lensing-induced beam averaging
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inconsistent lens redshift (about 1.1 in text vs 0.9 in the lens table) and a typo (SPT0346-56) weaken traceability of the lens model
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Merger interpretation rests on indirect arguments and an unconstrained v_max/sigma
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 140 km/s [C II] dispersion in the equipartition estimate may include rotation or merger motion
