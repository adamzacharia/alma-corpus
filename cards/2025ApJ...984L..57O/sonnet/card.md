## Analysis recipe

1. **Set up fiducial disk**: Parameterized initial gas surface density, 1 MJup planet at 57 au injected fully grown  
  > "The initial gas surface density of the disk is parameterized as:" (2.1 The fiducial disk)
   - software: PLANETALP
   - parameter: Rc=120 au, gamma=0.8, Mdisk=0.1 Msun, alpha=1e-3, dust-to-gas 0.02, Mstar=0.8 Msun  
  > "Our fiducial disk has an $\alpha$ viscosity of $10^{-3}$, and an initial dust-to-gas ratio of 0.02" (2.1 The fiducial disk)
   - assumption: Dust-to-gas ratio of 0.02 chosen to match the 1.3 mm flux of Elias 2-24  
  > "this allows us to match the integrated flux of Elias 2-24 at 1.3 mm ($\sim$0.3 Jy)." (2.1 The fiducial disk)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Evolve gas and dust**: 1D viscous gas disk with Duffell gap; dust coagulation, drift and fragmentation solved by advection-diffusion  
  > "The time evolution of the dust surface density, $\Sigma_{\rm d}$ obeys an advection-diffusion equation" (7.1.2 Dust component)
   - software: PLANETALP
   - parameter: 2000 radial bins 0.1-1000 au, 200 size bins, fragmentation velocity 1 m/s inside and 10 m/s outside the water ice line  
  > "We adopt that inside the water-ice line, silicate particles have a fragmentation velocity of 1 m/s" (7.1.2 Dust component)
   - assumption: Planet on circular coplanar orbit, no planet growth or migration  
  > "corresponding to $\sim$230 orbits of the giant planet assumed to be in a circular and coplanar orbit." (2.2 Exploration of the Parameter Space)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Coarse one-at-a-time parameter variation (Mp, alpha, dust-to-gas, disk mass)  
  > "we vary one parameter at a time, over the ranges of values listed in Table [1](#Tab:ParamList)" (3.2 Exploration of the Parameter Space)
3. **Radiative transfer**: Rebin to 9 size bins, optool opacities, Monte Carlo thermal calculation and ray tracing  
  > "we perform both Monte Carlo thermal calculations and ray-tracing simulations. In our Monte Carlo runs, we utilize $10^8$ photon packages" (7.2 Radiative transfer)
   - software: RADMC-3D, optool
   - parameter: 99% astro silicates and 1% graphite; 10^8 photon packages  
  > "a dust grain composition consisting of 99% astro silicates and 1% graphite." (7.2 Radiative transfer)
   - assumption: Disk located at distance of Elias 2-24  
  > "The synthetic images are generated under the assumption that the disk is located at the distance of Elias 2-24" (7.2 Radiative transfer)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Simulate ALMA observations and compare**: ft on model images with Elias 2-24 u-v coverage, add noise, clean with tclean, compare with ODISEA/DSHARP images  
  > "These visibility sets have the same $u-v$ coverage as the real observations of Elias 2-24." (2.3 Radiative Transfer Modeling and Synthetic Images)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: briggs robust 0.5, 1000 iterations, multiscale  
  > "We used multi-scale `tclean`, with briggs weighting, a robust value of 0.5, and 1000 iterations," (2.3 Radiative Transfer Modeling and Synthetic Images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual comparison of model epochs with observed disks, no quantitative fit  
  > "We find that our simple models are able to reproduce the main features of each Stage:" (4.1 Towards an evolutionary sequence of substructures in massive disks)

## Software

- **PLANETALP** : 1D gas and dust evolution with planet-induced gap  
  > "We apply the global planet formation model PLANETALP to study the dust growth and evolution" (2.2 Dust growth and evolution under planet-disk interactions)
- **RADMC-3D** : radiative transfer and synthetic 1.3 mm images  
  > "We use this code to convert the surface density profiles generated by PlanetaLP into synthetic images at 1.3 mm" (2.3 Radiative Transfer Modeling and Synthetic Images)
- **CASA** : ft, setnoise and tclean for synthetic observations  
  > "Using the `setnoise` task of the `Simulator` tool within CASA, we corrupted the synthetic visibilities" (2.3 Radiative Transfer Modeling and Synthetic Images)
- **optool** : dust opacities per size bin  
  > "Dust opacities for each size bin are computed independenyly using the `optool` application" (7.2 Radiative transfer)

## Qualitative findings

- Larger planet mass gives deeper, wider gaps; higher alpha suppresses gap and ring; planet mass and viscosity are degenerate  
  > "the larger the planetary mass, the deeper and wider the gap." (3.2 Exploration of the Parameter Space)
- A Mars-mass planet gives no gap but an inflection point in the profile  
  > "We find that, as expected, no gap is formed." (3.1 Fiducial model)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: 1D models are computationally efficient for exploring dust evolution and large parameter spaces  
  > "One significant advantage of 1-D models of protoplanetary disk evolution like PLANETALP is computational efficiency." (4.3 Caveats and future prospects)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only one planet, coarse parameter exploration, no planet growth or migration, injected fully grown planet gives too fast gap formation  
  > "This very short timescale is most likely an artifact of injecting a fully grown giant planet." (4.3 Caveats and future prospects)
- Limitation (authors): Model lacks inner disk persistence seen in RXJ1633.9-2442 and differs from WSB 82 and SR 24S  
  > "Our fiducial model at Stage IV looks very similar to DoAr 44, but different from WSB 82 and SR 24S." (4.3 Caveats and future prospects)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison with observations is qualitative and by eye, with no goodness-of-fit metric
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Matching is partly tuned (dust-to-gas ratio doubled) to reproduce Elias 2-24 flux
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Degeneracy of planet mass, viscosity and disk mass limits uniqueness
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model sensitive to assumed Duffell gap prescription and single-planet geometry
