## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate, combine and image**: CASA calibration, phase-only self-cal per epoch, combine, tclean continuum (robust 0.5) and YCLEAN cubes  
  > "Phase only self-calibration was performed using the continuum data for short-baseline and long-baseline observations separately." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean, YCLEAN
   - parameter: robust 0.5; CH3CN cubes robust 2  
  > "A robust parameter of 0.5 is used to image the data cubes." (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify condensations and measure fluxes**: CASA imfit Gaussian fits, preselected from uv>1.3 km image, then fit on full combined image  
  > "we employed the CASA-`imfit` task to preselect condensations from the continuum image generated using a uv range of $>$1.3 km" (Condensation identification)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: 4 components for C1,C2,C4,C5; 1 for C3; 2 for C6,C7  
  > "We fitted four components to extract C1, C2, C4, and C5, one component was used for C3, and two components were used for C6 and C7." (Condensation identification)
   - fitting: Gaussian imfit  
  > "we employed the CASA-`imfit` task to preselect condensations from the continuum image generated using a uv range of $>$1.3 km" (Condensation identification)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive gas temperature maps**: XCLASS fitting of 13CH3CN J=13-12 K=0-6 ladders per pixel, with CH3COOH, CH3OCHO, CH3OCH3 included  
  > "using the `XCLASS` package (cite: Moller2017)" (Estimate of gas temperatures)
   - software: XCLASS
   - assumption: Gas temperature approximates dust temperature  
  > "Thus, we used the gas temperature derived from $^{13}$CH$_3$CN as an approximation of the dust temperature." (Estimating gas mass from dust continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive masses, densities, surface densities**: Optically thin dust emission formula with T from 13CH3CN  
  > "where $\eta$ = 100 is the gas-to-dust ratio" (Estimating gas mass from dust continuum emission)
   - parameter: kappa_1.3mm = 0.9 cm2/g; mu=2.8; D=1.3 kpc  
  > "we adopted a value of 0.9 cm$^{2}$ g$^{-1}$ for $\kappa_{\rm 1.3 mm}$" (Estimating gas mass from dust continuum emission)
   - assumption: gas-to-dust ratio 100  
  > "where $\eta$ = 100 is the gas-to-dust ratio" (Estimating gas mass from dust continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Error propagation gives 53% mass uncertainty  
  > "we estimate the uncertainties in the mass, volume density, and surface density are 53%, 50%, and 49%, respectively." (Estimating gas mass from dust continuum emission)
5. **Fit disk kinematics and enclosed mass**: 3DBarolo tilted-ring fit to low-resolution CH3CN 12_4-11_4 cube; power-law fit to rotation velocities; Keplerian enclosed mass  
  > "We employ the `3DBarolo` code to fit the disk kinematics by modeling the three-dimensional (3D) position-position-velocity image cube" (Dynamical mass of the embedded structures)
   - software: 3DBarolo
   - assumption: circular geometry, Keplerian rotation V∝r^-0.5  
  > "The enclosed mass of the disk can be estimated under the assumption of Keplerian rotation, $V_{\rm rot} \propto r^{-0.5}$." (Dynamical mass of the embedded structures)
   - fitting: tilted-ring 3D fit; priors not stated  
  > "which uses a series of concentric tilted rings to derive the rotation velocities, inclination angle, and position angle of the disk by assuming a circular geometry" (Dynamical mass of the embedded structures)
   - validation: Compared with high-res PV diagram and with ~10 Msun of Hunter2014  
  > "which is close to 10 $M_\odot$ estimated from the relatively lower resolution and lower sensitivity observations reported by ref. (cite: Hunter2014)." (Dynamical mass of the embedded structures)
6. **Toomre Q maps**: Q = c_s kappa/(pi G Sigma) with Keplerian kappa from M_cl=7.6 Msun, Sigma from disk mass 2.71 Msun, T maps  
  > "$\kappa$ is epicyclic frequency that is equivalent to the angular velocity $\Omega$ = $\sqrt{GM/r^3}$ in the case of a Keplerian disk." (Calculation of the Toomre $Q$ parameter)
   - assumption: Q<1-2 unstable  
  > "The disk becomes unstable against axisymmetric gravitational stability if $Q <$ 1–2" (Calculation of the Toomre $Q$ parameter)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Uncertainty 54% propagated  
  > "Then the uncertainty in the Toomre $Q$ parameter is estimated to be 54%." (Calculation of the Toomre $Q$ parameter)
7. **Test boundedness and virial state, Jeans lengths**: Virial parameter (Bertoldi & McKee), E_i/|W_i| from 13CH3CN centroid velocities, Jeans length comparison  
  > "A condensation with $E_{i}/|W_{\rm i}| \, <$ 1 is considered to be bound to the system." (Boundedness of the septuple system)
   - parameter: b=2 density profile; M_tot=10 M_gas; V_disk=-3 km/s  
  > "Therefore, we assume the total mass of the condensations is 10 times of their gas mass, i.e., $M_{\rm tot}$ = 10 $M_{\rm gas}$" (Virial parameter of condensations)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits to averaged spectra  
  > "we measure the centroid velocity of each condensation and parent disk by performing a Gaussian fit to the condensation-averaged and disk-averaged $^{13}$CH$_3$CN $13_{3}-12_{3}$ spectra, respectively" (Boundedness of the septuple system)
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare with disk fragmentation simulation**: Pluto radiation-hydro simulation post-processed with RADMC-3D to synthetic 1.3 mm continuum and CH3CN cubes  
  > "we use the open-source software RADMC-3D to generate dust continuum emission at 1.3 mm and molecular line emissions for the CH$_3$CN $12_{4} -11_{4}$ transition" (Simulations of disk fragmentation and multiplicity formation)
   - software: Pluto, RADMC-3D
   - assumption: T_dust=200 K for fragment masses  
  > "assuming a dust temperature of 200 K" (Virial parameter of condensations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Qualitative match in fragment configuration, masses, velocity gradients  
  > "The consistency between the simulations and observations in the continuum emission and kinematic features indicates that disk fragmentation is responsible for the formation of the septuple system in NGC 6334IN." (Introduction)

## Qualitative findings

- Seven bound condensations are embedded in a Keplerian-like disk; Q is low in the outer disk and at condensations; a simulation of disk fragmentation reproduces the configuration and kinematics.  
  > "The consistency between the simulations and observations in the continuum emission and kinematic features indicates that disk fragmentation is responsible for the formation of the septuple system in NGC 6334IN." (Simulations of disk fragmentation and multiplicity formation)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Low-resolution cube used for Keplerian fit because high-res CH3CN SNR insufficient  
  > "as the SNR of the high-resolution CH$_3$CN image is insufficient for robustly determining the disk parameters" (Dynamical mass of the embedded structures)
- Alternative: Core and filament fragmentation considered unlikely given separations and early stage  
  > "These results suggest that core and filament fragmentation are unlikely to be responsible for the formation of the observed septuple system" (Main text)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Masses are lower limits due to optical depth and missing flux  
  > "The derived masses are considered as lower limits due to the optical depth effects and missing flux in the interferometric data." (Main text)
- Limitation (authors): Secondary disks cannot be confirmed with current sensitivity and resolution  
  > "Higher resolution and sensitivity observations are needed to confirm the presence of individual secondary disks around the condensations." (Gas properties around the septuple system)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas temperature from 13CH3CN used as dust temperature may be biased in optically thick disk
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Q values depend on assumed disk mass and temperature with 54% uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Simulation comparison is qualitative and the disk is smaller
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Keplerian fit from 0.3 arcsec data only; total mass ×10 assumption for virial analysis is ad hoc
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Core fragmentation plus migration not excluded
