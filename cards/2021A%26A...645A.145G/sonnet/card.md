## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image**: Standard CASA calibration, phase self-calibration on continuum applied to line SPWs, interactive tclean with Briggs 0.0 and 0.2 km/s channels  
  > "The maps presented in this work are obtained using Briggs weighting of 0.0 and setting a channel width of 0.2 km s$^{-1}$." (3 Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: self-cal solint down to 10 s; Briggs 0.0; 0.2 km/s channels  
  > "solution intervals of the self-calibration could be decreased down to 10 sec without rejecting any solution." (3 Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual inspection of residuals  
  > "iterating until the visual inspection of the residuals revealed no significant source emission." (3 Observations and data reduction)
2. **Constrain disk geometry and emission height**: Gaussian fit to continuum for centroid, PA, i; z/r from shift of ring ellipse along minor axis, visually matched to synthetic ellipse  
  > "First, we constrained the disk centroid, position angle (PA), and inclination, $i,$ with a Gaussian fit to the continuum emission." (4.3.1 Vertical distribution)
   - parameter: z/r increased until visual fit of observed substructure  
  > "The offset of this ellipse along the minor axis increases as we increase the $z/r$ and this is done until we visually fit the observed substructure with the synthetic ellipse." (4.3.1 Vertical distribution)
   - assumption: Substructures azimuthally symmetric  
  > "Assuming that all apparent substructures are azimuthally symmetric, we applied this method" (4.3.1 Vertical distribution)
   - fitting: visual, no formal fit  
  > "The offset of this ellipse along the minor axis increases as we increase the $z/r$ and this is done until we visually fit the observed substructure with the synthetic ellipse." (4.3.1 Vertical distribution)
   - validation: Consistency with higher resolution images (HL Tau)  
  > "The results are consistent with those obtained from higher resolution images such as that of HL Tau (cite: ALMA2015)." (4.3.1 Vertical distribution)
3. **Radial profiles and disk sizes**: Azimuthal averaging over annuli with PA, i, z/r; outer radius defined as enclosing 90% of total flux  
  > "we defined the outer radius of a certain type of emission as the separation from the star enclosing 90% of the total flux in the image." (4.3.2 Radial distribution)
   - parameter: gas outer radius from H2CO  
  > "To constrain the gas outer radius, we used the H$_2$CO emission since this line is the least affected by the surrounding envelope" (4.3.2 Radial distribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Integrated flux and column density**: Moment 0 integrated over regions from Table 3; optically thin LTE column density for T = 20-100 K  
  > "Our poor knowledge of the gaseous temperature was addressed by adopting values between 20 K and 100 K and determining the range of column densities for this range of temperatures." (4.4 Line brightness and column density)
   - parameter: ortho-to-para H2CO 1.8-2.8; H2CS 3.0; CH3OH A/E = 1  
  > "the ratio between these types is one (cite: Carney2019)" (4.4 Line brightness and column density)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission and LTE  
  > "The line brightness can be converted into column density in the scenario of optically thin emission and local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)." (4.4 Line brightness and column density)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Critical densities < 2e6 cm-3 support LTE; optical thinness checked via brightness temperature and literature  
  > "Thus, in absence of better constraints, we assume that the H$_2$CO and CS lines are optically thin." (5.1 Line optical depth)
5. **Test origin of inner depression**: Compare minimum central CO moment 0 flux with A_V for ALMA-DOT and DSHARP sources normalised to 0.1'' beam and 140 pc  
  > "after normalizing each source to the same beam size (0.1$''$$\times$0.1$''$) and distance (140 pc)" (5.2 Origin of the inner depression)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Stack undetected lines**: Stack CH3OH and HDO maps centered on continuum, smooth by 1''  
  > "after centering each map on the center of the continuum emission and smoothing the resulting map by 1$''$." (5.5 Weak lines)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CS and H2CO trace the gaseous disk with little or no contamination, H2CO being the best; CN does not trace the disk and appears as a faint ring beyond the dust disk; 12CO is contaminated by outflow/envelope.  
  > "Both CS and H$_2$CO are good tracers of the gaseous disk having little (the CS) or no (the H$_2$CO) contamination from the surrounding medium." (6 Summary and conclusions)
- All lines show inner depression, partly explained by foreground absorption and continuum over-subtraction, but likely an actual column density decrease in the inner ~50 au.  
  > "The dimmed flux in the inner $\sim$50 au reflects a probable decrease in column density" (6 Summary and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Resolve disk from envelope and outflow at ~0.3'' angular resolution; Band 6 covers many lines  
  > "This goal requires us to resolve structures of a few hundred au, which translates, at the distance of Taurus, into an angular resolution on the order of 0.3$''$." (Survey goals and sample)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Moderate resolution and symmetry assumption limit z/r accuracy; optical thinness uncertain  
  > "We however caution that the moderate angular resolution, the small size of the rings (in particular in Haro 6-13), and the assumption of azimuthally symmetric structures (cite: Podio2019) limit the accuracy of our constraints." (4.3.1 Vertical distribution)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Column densities rely on optically thin LTE with a wide assumed temperature range, giving factor-of-several ranges
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Visual fitting of z/r has no formal uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous resolution and recoverable scale between Cycle 4 and Cycle 6 data complicate comparisons
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Continuum over-subtraction and absorption affect inner-disk results
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interactive cleaning limits exact reproducibility
