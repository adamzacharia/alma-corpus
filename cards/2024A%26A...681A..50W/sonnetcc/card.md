## Analysis recipe

1. **Make continuum-subtracted cubes**: Calibrate, self-calibrate on line-free continuum, subtract continuum and image cubes separately for each spatial resolution  
  > "Image cubes were made (after continuum subtraction) for each separate spatial resolution." (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: channel spacing 0.9765625 MHz  
  > "The channel spacing of 0.9765625 MHz gives a velocity resolution of" (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - assumption: flux scale uncertainty 15%  
  > "we adopt a conservative flux scale uncertainty of 15% (although in some cases it may be much better than this)" (2.1 ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra in circular apertures**: Circular apertures centered on the continuum peak at three apertures per resolution  
  > "Spectra were extracted in circular apertures, centered on the continuum peak, with diameters of" (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - parameter: Aperture diameters: high 0.04, 0.08, 0.12 arcsec; medium 0.4, 1.2, 3.6 arcsec; low 1.2, 3.6, 10.8 arcsec  
  > "At each resolution, the smallest aperture was chosen to be the average beam size rounded up to one significant figure, for consistency." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Identify and fit spectral lines**: Fit a soft parabola to each expected line (starting from the IK Tau/R Dor line list) with least squares; visual inspection to reject spurious fits and add or adjust weak and blended lines; detection threshold 2.5 sigma of the line-free rms  
  > "a soft parabola function (cite: de_beck_probing_2010) was fit with a least squares fitting algorithm." (2.2 Processing and analysis); "A visual inspection then ruled out spurious fits, added missing lines, and adjusted the fit of weak and blended lines." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - parameter: rms measured from spectrum parts more than 3 v_exp from any line; lines below 2.5 sigma treated as non-detections  
  > "was measured from the parts of the spectrum more than three times the wind expansion velocity" (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - fitting: Least squares fit of a soft parabola function  
  > "a soft parabola function (cite: de_beck_probing_2010) was fit with a least squares fitting algorithm." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - validation: Moment 0 maps inspected by eye for coherence across channels; brightness of detected lines checked against the brightest predicted lines at 300 K  
  > "Inspection of integrated intensity (moment 0) maps by eye further ensured any weak lines correspond to coherent emission across several channels rather than a noise fluctuation." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
4. **Measure line parameters**: Peak flux, integrated intensity and velocity width (full width at zero power) measured per spectrum; spectral extent between channels where each wing reaches 2.5 sigma, minimum four channels  
  > "peak flux, integrated intensity, velocity width (full width at zero power), and angular extent" (2.2 Processing and analysis); "with a minimum width of four channels" (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - parameter: Peak flux uncertainty = rms plus 15% absolute flux uncertainty; width uncertainty = channel width  
  > "The uncertainty on the peak flux was taken to be the rms plus the 15% absolute flux uncertainty" (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - assumption: Automatic extent measurement may underestimate widths and integrated intensities of broad weak lines  
  > "As this was done automatically, broad weak lines may have their velocity widths and hence integrated intensities underestimated." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Blended, absorption and double-peaked lines checked manually  
  > "Potentially blended lines and lines with absorption or double-peaked profiles were checked manually to ensure that the correct line region was chosen." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
5. **Measure angular extent of line emission**: Moment 0 maps from the measured line width; maximum radial extent of the 3 sigma contour enclosing the star, azimuthally smoothed  
  > "The measured line width was used to make integrated intensity (moment 0) maps by summing the intensities of all the spectral channels covered by a line." (2.2 Processing and analysis); "The contours were azimuthally smoothed (in bins with at least ten samples, corresponding to angular ranges of" (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - parameter: Upper limit = half the major axis of the restoring beam when no extent is measured  
  > "For transitions with no measured extent from this method an upper limit is given, equal to half the major axis of the restoring beam" (2.2 Processing and analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Paper notes the method may underestimate extent for clumpy emission and miss diffuse emission filtered by the MRS  
  > "Note that for transitions with clumpy emission, this method may underestimate the extent of the emission." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
6. **Rescale literature mass-loss rates to new distances**: M_new = M_old (D_new/D_old)^2  
  > "This has been done using a simple formula to account for the dilution factor stemming from the distance to the star:" (2.3 Mass-loss rate uncertainties)
   - assumption: Literature mass-loss rates assume constant, spherically symmetric mass loss; uncertainties up to an order of magnitude  
  > "Taken together, these factors result in typical uncertainties on the mass-loss rates of up to an order of magnitude." (2.3 Mass-loss rate uncertainties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Distance update changes mass-loss rates by at most a factor of 2.5  
  > "Updating the distances to these sources has changed the derived mass-loss rate by at most a factor of 2.5." (2.3 Mass-loss rate uncertainties)
7. **Correlate molecular content with stellar parameters**: Kendall tau_b rank correlations between stellar parameters and number of detected lines per molecule (including isotopologues); dendrogram by complete linkage using correlations of at least 1 sigma significance  
  > "the sources were ranked (by measured value for stellar parameters and by number of detected lines for molecules) for each variable, and the ranks between every pair of variables were compared (adjusting for ties)." (3.3 Correlations); "using the farthest point (a.k.a. complete linkage) algorithm for hierarchical clustering" (3.3 Correlations)
   - parameter: Variables: Teff, mass-loss rate, expansion velocity, pulsation period and line counts; significance via normal approximation with p<0.15865, 0.02275, 0.00135 for 1, 2, 3 sigma  
  > "The variables being correlated are the effective temperatures, mass-loss rates, terminal expansion velocities, and pulsation periods" (3.3 Correlations); "As we have more than ten sources to compare," (3.3 Correlations)
   - assumption: Number of detected lines is a rough proxy for relative abundance  
  > "This allows us to use the differing number of detected lines of a given molecule as a (very rough) proxy for its relative abundance in different sources." (3.3 Correlations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Classify SO and SO2 spatial distributions and compare with chemical model**: Classify each source as centralized or shell-like from integrated intensity and channel maps; compare measured maximum 3 sigma SO2 extent with modeled SO2 e-folding radius from the Van de Sande et al. 2018 chemical model  
  > "for the majority of detected lines, the peak of the molecular emission in centred on the continuum peak and decreases monotonically with distance from the star." (3.4 SO and SO2 spatial distributions); "As the temperature profile in the outflows is generally not known, a range of models with different temperature profiles," (3.4.1 AGB stars)
   - assumption: Chemical model assumes power-law temperature profile and a smooth, spherically symmetric outflow with constant expansion velocity and mass-loss rate  
  > "The chemical model assumes a power-law temperature profile with exponent" (3.4.1 AGB stars); "it assumes a smooth, spherically symmetric outflow, with a constant expansion velocity and mass-loss rate." (3.4.1 AGB stars)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked that the energy-distribution dichotomy is not caused by distance or by line energy differences by using only the 244.254 GHz line  
  > "since our two most distant sources" (3.4.1 AGB stars)
9. **Derive isotopic ratios from SiS isotopologues**: Ratio of integrated intensities of isotopologue transitions with a nu^-2 correction; integrated intensity taken in the spectrum where the sum of both transitions is maximized; weighted average when several line pairs are available  
  > "For each pair of transitions we measured the integrated intensity in the spectrum where the sum of the intensities of both transitions is maximized." (3.5 Isotopic ratios); "contains the weighted average of these ratios." (3.5 Isotopic ratios)
   - parameter: Only v=0 transition pairs with at least one minor isotope in both molecules; same angular resolution and aperture so beam filling factors match  
  > "to avoid the problem of potential missing flux or high optical depth in the main isotopologue lines." (3.5 Isotopic ratios); "By taking the integrated intensities from spectra extracted at the same angular resolution and aperture size, the beam filling factors should be the same for both transitions." (3.5 Isotopic ratios)
   - assumption: Lines optically thin with no missing flux and not masing  
  > "Hence we assume all the lines used to calculate isotopic ratios are optically thin and have no missing flux" (3.5 Isotopic ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Assumptions supported by narrow line shapes and limited angular extents; checked lines are not excessively bright or narrow  
  > "assumptions which are supported by their narrow line shapes and limited angular extents." (3.5 Isotopic ratios)

## Qualitative findings

- CO, HCN and SiO are detected in all 17 sources  
  > "CO, HCN, and SiO are ubiquitous, as expected from previous studies" (3.1 Overview of molecular inventory)
- U Del and KW Sgr are line-poor, lacking high-v SiO lines  
  > "Two sources are very line-poor: U Del and the RSG KW Sgr." (3.1 Overview of molecular inventory)
- SO2 distributions tend to be centralized in low mass-loss-rate AGB stars and shell-like in higher mass-loss-rate AGB stars; the RSGs do not follow the AGB trend  
  > "shows a general tendency for the low mass-loss-rate sources to have centralized distributions and higher mass-loss-rate sources to have shell-like distributions" (3.4.1 AGB stars)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: A homogeneous sample with identical observing setups is needed to compare chemical inventories across stars with different parameters  
  > "The next step to elucidate the circumstellar chemistry is to observe a sample of stars with a range of stellar parameters, with identical observing setups." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Kendall rank correlation is non-parametric and handles ordinal data such as line counts  
  > "that does not assume linear relationships between variables and is applicable to ordinal data." (3.3 Correlations)
- Alternative: Pearson correlations and principal component analysis not used because they assume linear relationships  
  > "assume linear relationships between variables and hence are less applicable to our data at this initial analysis stage." (3.3 Correlations)
- Alternative: Line stacking or different data reduction to find further lines is left to future work  
  > "We do not exclude the possibility that further lines may be detected with a different data reduction method or by stacking data; however, we leave this to future papers." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Missing flux for some molecules (notably CO) in some sources, not quantified for most sources  
  > "The full extent of missing flux has not been quantified as single-dish spectra of the same frequency ranges are not available for most of our sources." (2.1 ALMA observations)
- Limitation (authors): Mass-loss rates uncertain by up to an order of magnitude  
  > "Taken together, these factors result in typical uncertainties on the mass-loss rates of up to an order of magnitude." (2.3 Mass-loss rate uncertainties)
- Limitation (authors): Number of detected lines depends on excitation and molecular parameters as well as abundance; no radiative transfer modeling  
  > "The number of detected lines is a function of molecular parameters and excitation conditions as well as abundance, which we cannot properly take into account without radiative transfer modeling." (3.3 Correlations)
- Limitation (authors): High-resolution data sample only one stellar phase per source  
  > "and hence essentially samples, for each source, only one part of the stellar phase" (2.1 ALMA observations)
- Limitation (authors): Automatic line-width extraction may underestimate widths and integrated intensities of broad weak lines  
  > "As this was done automatically, broad weak lines may have their velocity widths and hence integrated intensities underestimated." (2.2 Processing and analysis)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Counting detected lines as an abundance proxy mixes excitation, sensitivity and abundance; sensitivity differs between sources (distance, rms) and no completeness correction is applied.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 17 sources, with strong collinearity among mass-loss rate, period and v_exp, so many correlations are driven by a handful of sources (the paper itself notes this).
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass-loss rates come from heterogeneous literature methods with large uncertainties, weakening the SO/SO2 distribution versus mass-loss-rate trend.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text and Table 4 disagree on which of IRC+10011 and IRC-10529 has 34S/32S of 0.04 versus 0.11; text also gives 287 lines and 29 unidentified against 291 transitions and 28 unidentified lines elsewhere.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Visual line identification and manual fit adjustment are not reproducible automatically.
