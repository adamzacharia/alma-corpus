1. **Identify and validate serendipitous SMG**: Visually identify bright point sources; retain SNR>5σ detections; check individual EBs, negative peaks and K-band counterparts  
  > "We did not run any source extraction algorithms, but merely noted when a bright point source was present in the imaging" (ALMA observations and data reduction); "Firstly, we noted the flux and SNR of each SMG in all of the different Execution Blocks (EB) in which they were observed."    - validation: Every source >3σ in every contributing EB; no negative peaks ≤−5σ; four K-band counterparts; expected 0.002 false detections at 5σ  
  > "they are positive peaks above 3$\sigma$ in each of the separate observations." (ALMA observations and data reduction); "and in no cases are any negative peaks at or below $-5\sigma$ found." (ALMA observations and data reduction)
2. **Measure SMG fluxes and sizes**: Image-plane Gaussian peak fluxes and constrained 2D Gaussian visibility fits; correct fluxes/errors for primary-beam attenuation

## Qualitative findings

- Significant SMG over-density persists across reference counts and completeness choices  
  > "So, even though our over-density estimates have large uncertainties, we are confident that there *is* a significant over-density in our fields." (Over-density calculation)
- Group-scale halo lensing is a plausible explanation, without direct halo-parameter constraints  
  > "Thus lensing is certainly a plausible explanation for the excess SMG detected around these sources." (Conclusions); "The data do not allow a direct constraint with the current statistics" (Lensing Magnification Bias)
- SMG contamination disproportionately boosts longer-wavelength SPIRE photometry  
  > "Flux boosting from the SMG will affect all SPIRE fluxes, but those at longer wavelengths disproportionately so for two reasons." (Boosting of the H-ATLAS fluxes by SMG in the beam.)
- Herschel-only fits can underestimate cold dust temperature and overestimate dust mass and extrapolated L850  
  > "This mimics the effect of colder dust in the SED and leads to an underestimate of the cold dust temperature in magphys or two-component MBB fitting." (Summary of flux boosting and its implications); "There are likely to be significant over-estimates of sub-mm fluxes if they are estimated by extrapolating SEDs fitted to *Herschel* fluxes which have been boosted in this way." (Summary of flux boosting and its implications); "For the dust masses the bias is greater with an average offset" (Impact of ALMA data on SED fits to Herschel photometry)
- ALMA 850 μm data strongly reduce SED sensitivity to SPIRE contamination  
  > "The effect of the flux boosting on the SED fits is much smaller *once ALMA data are present* because the ALMA 850$\mu$m data force such a tight constraint on the SED shape." (Impact of ALMA data on SED fits to Herschel photometry)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Complete blind 250 μm sample in a tuning-constrained redshift range; original aim was three-tracer molecular-gas calibration  
  > "to make a calibration of molecular gas mass based on three tracers." (Sample and Data); "We selected *all* the H-ATLAS SDP sources within this redshift range, making this sample of twelve representative of sources from a blind 250$\mu$m  selected sample at $z\sim 0.3-0.4$." (Sample and Data)
- Why this method: Natural weighting maximizes SNR; image/visibility fits check systematics; visibility fitting avoids deconvolution uncertainties and improves sub-beam size information  
  > "natural weighting was used in order to maximise signal-to-noise." (ALMA observations and data reduction); "We used both methods as a check for systematics, and also because very little size information can be derived in the image plane for sources which are smaller than the synthesised beam." (Flux and size measurements); "Fitting in the $(u,v)$ plane is more direct and avoids the uncertainties associated with the non-linear process of deconvolution in the imaging." (Flux and size measurements)
- Alternative: Unconstrained axial-ratio fitting rejected because near-zero ratios bias fluxes and sizes high  
  > "This tendency produces a bias in the value for $S_{\rm  fit}$ and $\theta_{m1}$, both of them being pushed towards the maximum allowable values." (Size fitting procedure); "To avoid the bias we chose to constrain the axial ratio to physically sensible values." (Size fitting procedure)
- Alternative: Peak versus integrated fluxes tested; conclusions unchanged  
  > "we checked that repeating the analysis with the peak fluxes instead of the integrated fluxes does not change the conclusions." (Flux and size measurements)
- Alternative: No completeness correction, O16 point-source correction and B20 size-dependent correction all evaluated  
  > "No completeness correction applied – gives a minimum estimate of the counts for our fields." (Over-density calculation); "Completeness correction following O16 simulations" (Over-density calculation); "Completeness correction following B20 size dependent simulations" (Over-density calculation)
- Alternative: Use B20 robust z<4 counts to exclude clustering at the high-redshift target redshifts  
  > "we compare to the robust $z<4$ counts from B20 which will have removed any contamination from clustered sources at the same redshift as the targets." (Over-density calculation)
- Alternative: PACS photometry remeasured in five discrepant cases  
  > "However, in 5 cases there was a significant difference between the two catalogues, so we returned to the original H-ATLAS PACS maps and remeasured our own photometry" (Sample and Data)
- Alternative: Extended magphys infrared libraries used because standard libraries were too cold for some targets  
  > "We use extended infrared libraries which have an ISM cold temperature range of 10–30 K, as several of the sources favoured warmer ISM temperatures than allowed in the standard magphys infrared libraries." (Impact of ALMA data on SED fits to Herschel photometry)
- How they decided it was enough: Robust detections in all EBs and negative-image checks; significant over-density under both background-count comparisons  
  > "This combined with the detection of all SMG in all the individual EB confirms that this is a very robust set of sources." (ALMA observations and data reduction); "So, even though our over-density estimates have large uncertainties, we are confident that there *is* a significant over-density in our fields." (Over-density calculation)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample; count uncertainties dominated by counting statistics  
  > "With such small numbers, we cannot hope to make an accurate measure of the counts since our uncertainties are always going to be dominated by counting statistics." (Over-density calculation)
- Limitation (authors): Halo mass–concentration degeneracy; larger sample and more radial bins needed  
  > "There is intrinsic degeneracy between mass and concentration in the fitting, and to provide direct constraints will require a larger sample which could be split over more radial bins." (Lensing Magnification Bias)
- Limitation (authors): SMG redshift–temperature degeneracy; contamination estimates do not constrain either parameter  
  > "The redshift and  $\rm{T_d}$ are roughly degenerate in this process but we are not attempting to constrain either" (Boosting of the H-ATLAS fluxes by SMG in the beam.)
- Limitation (authors): Broad range of contamination values fits maps and SEDs  
  > "There is clearly a lot of uncertainty in this rough estimate because a wide range of contaminating 250$\mu$m fluxes still produce acceptable fits to both the *Herschel* maps and the SED fits." (Boosting of the H-ATLAS fluxes by SMG in the beam.)
- Limitation (authors): Boosting-dependent sample selection not included in quantitative halo modelling  
  > "it would need to be accounted for in the modelling to derive more accurate parameters." (Boosting of the H-ATLAS fluxes by SMG in the beam.); "This is beyond the scope of this study" (Boosting of the H-ATLAS fluxes by SMG in the beam.)
- Limitation (authors): GAMA group catalogue likely incomplete for intermediate-mass groups at these redshifts  
  > "the group catalogue is likely to be incomplete for intermediate mass groups at the redshifts of this sample." (Lensing Magnification Bias)
- Limitation (authors): Larger samples over redshift and stellar mass needed to determine general boosting corrections  
  > "A larger sample of $0.2<z<0.7$ galaxies selected at 250$\mu$m and imaged with ALMA will be required to understand and address these issues, and to determine the statistics of boosting due to this effect in bins of redshift and stellar mass." (Impact of ALMA data on SED fits to Herschel photometry)

- Caveat (inferred, ours): The main text names uvmdodelfit with a MartiVidal2014 citation, while the appendix names CASA uvmodelfit; the actual fitting implementation is ambiguous.
