## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and CO**: CASA clean MFS, robust 0.5, multiscale, optional taper, self-cal on SM1 and GSS 30; CO continuum-subtracted in uv  
  > "Continuum images for each target were made with the *clean* task in CASA, using multifrequency synthesis to combine data from the three continuum spectral windows." (2.3 Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5; scales 0,5,10 beams  
  > "multiscale clean with scales of 0, 5, and 10 times the synthesized beam" (2.3 Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure compact source properties**: Fit 1-2 2D Gaussians with imfit; uvmodelfit disk vs Gaussian for GSS 30 IRS3 and SM1  
  > "we fit the continuum image with one or two 2D Gaussians using the CASA task `imfit`" (3.1.1 Compact sources)
   - software: CASA imfit, CASA uvmodelfit
   - fitting: Gaussian least squares; reduced chi2 compares disk and Gaussian uv models  
  > "found that both geometries fit the uv-data equally well as measured by the reduced $\chi^2$ between the data and model" (3.1.1 Compact sources)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive masses and densities**: Optically thin M = d^2 S / (kappa B(Td)); n = M/(mu mH V) with prolate spheroid volume  
  > "We assume the sources are prolate spheroids such that the volume $V = 4/3 \ \pi \sigma_\mathrm{min}^2\sigma_\mathrm{maj}$." (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - parameter: kappa0 = 0.1 cm2/g at 1000 GHz; beta=1.7 (and derived beta)  
  > "we set $\kappa_0 = 0.1$ cm$^{2}$ g$^{-1}$ at a frequency $\nu_0 = 1000$ GHz" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - assumption: Td = 15 K for extended emission, 30 K for compact sources; d = 137.3 pc  
  > "we use $T_d = 15$ K when calculating the mass of the extended emission seen by ALMA, and set $T_d = 30$ K for the compact sources" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Paper I mass of SM1N (~0.02 Msun)  
  > "These values are consistent within uncertainties with those found in Paper I at 359 GHz assuming similar dust parameters" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
4. **Dust spectral index**: beta from 1.1 mm and 3 mm total fluxes following Schnee et al. 2014  
  > "we follow (cite: schnee_2014) to determine $\beta$ from the total flux of all four detected compact sources at these two wavelengths, assuming $T_d = 30$ K" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - assumption: 10% calibration error at both frequencies  
  > "Here, we assume a 10% calibration error at both frequencies" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **SM1N radial profile and density profile**: Radially averaged intensity fit with beam-convolved Gaussian; shell densities from enclosed mass; power-law fit  
  > "The Figure also shows the density profile is well fit by a single power law with exponent -1.3 at radii $\lesssim 1.5$$''$" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - assumption: Spherical symmetry, isothermal Td = 15 K  
  > "calculated assuming spherical symmetry" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
   - fitting: Gaussian and power-law fits; method not detailed  
  > "We fit the observed intensity profile with a single Gaussian, convolved with the synthesized beam, shown as the blue curve in the Figure." (Results and Analysis, Extended structure)
   - validation: Compared with SIS profile  
  > "We have overlaid the density profile of a singular isothermal sphere (SIS)" (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
6. **CO outflow and infall analysis**: Blue/red-shifted CO maps; inverse P-Cygni fit for SM1 giving v_in and mass accretion rate Mdot = 4 pi r^2 rho v_in  
  > "we estimate a mass accretion rate, $\dot{M} = 4~\pi~r^2 \rho~v_{in} \sim 3 \times 10^{-5}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$" (3.3.2 Absorption)
   - assumption: Infall near the compact source; otherwise upper limit  
  > "If the infall motions toward SM1 instead originate in less dense, more extended gas, the derived mass accretion rate is an upper limit." (3.3.2 Absorption)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with B335, L1451-mm, IRAS16293, NGC1333 IRAS4  
  > "Compared with Class 0 protostars, the SM1 estimate agrees well with results for the Class 0 protostar IRAS16293 in Ophiuchus" (3.3.2 Absorption)

## Qualitative findings

- CO is heavily self-absorbed at cloud vLSR; GSS 30 IRS3 and SM1 show compact CO absorption against the continuum; SM1 shows an inverse P-Cygni profile.  
  > "Toward SM1, the CO spectrum exhibits a clear inverse P-Cygni profile suggestive of infall motions" (3.3.2 Absorption)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Td set from gas temperatures since gas and dust are coupled at high density; 30 K for compact sources for comparison with earlier disk studies  
  > "At the densities expected for the ALMA-detected sources, the gas and dust are well-coupled (cite: goldsmith_2001)." (3.2 Masses of continuum condensations)
- Alternative: SM1N could not be fit uniquely by a combination of compact and extended components or a single Gaussian  
  > "In addition, we are unable to find an unique solution for a combination of compact and extended emission." (3.1.2 Extended structure)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): CO suffers self-absorption and missing short spacings; temperature on small scales unknown  
  > "in most cases it suffers from severe self-absorption at the cloud $v_{\mathrm{LSR}}$, as well as from the lack of short-spacing data" (3.3.1 Outflows)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Masses depend on assumed Td and kappa; optically thick emission gives lower limits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): beta from 1 mm and 3 mm with differing resolutions and uncertain flux scales
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Evolutionary classification is qualitative
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO outflow detection limited by self-absorption and resolving out
