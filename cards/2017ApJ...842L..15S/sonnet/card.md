## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce band 3 scan and derive redshift**: 3 mm continuum image and natural-weighted cube; spectrum extracted at continuum centroid; noise-weighted line frequencies give z  
  > "their noise-weighted line frequencies yield a redshift of $z=6.900 \pm 0.002$" (2.1 Determining the redshift)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 19.5 MHz channels, natural weighting  
  > "using natural weighting with a channel width of 19.5 MHz" (2.1 Determining the redshift)
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits; CO(7-6) and [CI](2-1) line width fixed to mean of unblended lines  
  > "For the fit to the CO(7–6) and \[C\](2–1) lines we fix the line width to the mean value derived from the unblended lines." (2.1 Determining the redshift)
   - validation: Redshift confirmed by ATCA CO(3-2) and APEX [CI] detections  
  > "subsequently confirmed by detections of CO(3–2) with ATCA and \[C\] with APEX" (abstract)
2. **Assemble line and continuum photometry and FIR luminosity**: Combine SPT, LABOCA, SPIRE, ALMA fluxes; integrate SED 40-120 um rest for L_FIR  
  > "we derive an apparent far-infrared (FIR) luminosity (integrated between 40–120 $\mu$m rest) of L$_{{\rm FIR}}=4.1\pm\,0.7\,\times\,10^{13}$ $L_{\odot}$" (2.3 FIR dust continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Radiative transfer modelling of dust, CO SLED and [CI]**: Two-component model simultaneously fitting dust continuum, CO SLED and [CI](2-1) following Weiss et al. 2007; Tkin >= Tdust prior; CO SLED shape of Arp220 or HFLS3 as prior for high-J  
  > "The calculations treat the dust and the kinetic temperature as independent parameters, but with the prior that the kinetic gas temperature has to be equal to or higher than the dust temperature." (3.2 Radiative Transfer Models)
   - software: Monte-Carlo Bees algorithm
   - parameter: ~10^7 models sampled; CO/H2 fixed at 8e-5; [C] abundance and GDMR free  
  > "In total, we sample $\sim 10^7$ models." (3.2 Radiative Transfer Models)
   - assumption: kappa_d = 0.04 (nu/250 GHz)^beta m2/kg; mu = 1.9; eastern source negligible; same GDMR in both components; 36% He correction  
  > "we assume that the contribution from the eastern source is negligible and model the system as a single object" (3.1 Source properties from high resolution imaging)
   - fitting: Bees algorithm with chi2 evaluation; probability-weighted mean and standard deviation of solutions  
  > "Parameter values and uncertainties were calculated using the probability-weighted mean of all solutions and the standard deviations." (3.2 Radiative Transfer Models)
   - validation: Compared Arp220 and HFLS3 CO SLED priors; gas mass changes by ~30%  
  > "For the HFLS3 prior, the gas mass is $\sim30$% higher." (3.3 Model results)
4. **Derive gas mass, GDMR, alpha_CO, and [CII] ratios**: Gas mass from kinematic parameters, size and density; alpha_CO from model-predicted CO(1-0); L[CII]/LFIR ratio  
  > "we use the flux density from the radiative transfer model which predicts I$_{\rm CO(1-0)}$=0.10 $\pm$ 0.03 Jy km/s" (4.1 Gas mass conversion factor)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
