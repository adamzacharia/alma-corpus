## Analysis recipe

1. **Select resolved molecular central rings**: Visually select ring-like CO structures in ~1″ moment-0 maps; require resolved substructure, either a central hole or a distinct nuclear component; compare with the previous PHANGS classification.  
  > "We visually selected a sample of central rings from the original PHANGS-ALMA sample (cite: Leroy_2021) using the following criteria:" (Sample selection); "In the molecular gas distribution a central ring-like structure must be visible with a clearly identifiable shape and size." (Sample selection); "we must be able to resolve substructure within the ring-region, i.e. either a hole in the center" (Sample selection)
   - parameter: 81 galaxies with sufficiently resolved maps; 20 of 22 previously reported rings retained  
  > "which reduces our parent sample to $81$ PHANGS-ALMA galaxies." (Sample selection); "We compare their classification based on the two before-mentioned criteria and confirm $20$ out of $22$ central rings reported by (cite: stuber_gas_2023)." (Sample selection)
   - parameter: Include classical rings, tightly wound spiral pseudo-rings and other rings; no host-morphology cut  
  > "we consider the following geometries: (i) “classical” circum-nuclear ring within a bar, (ii) tightly wound spiral arms forming a pseudo-ring, and (iii) other types of rings." (Sample selection); "We also do not apply a morphological cut for the host galaxies and include central rings in both spiral and early type galaxies that are part of the PHANGS-ALMA sample." (Sample selection)
   - assumption: At the selected resolution, rings with r >100 pc can in principle be resolved  
  > "such that central rings with a radius of $r > 100\,$pc can in principle be resolved" (Sample selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Check the Stuber classifications against both criteria; reject Circinus and NGC 1068 because only 7 m and total-power data are available  
  > "We compare their classification based on the two before-mentioned criteria and confirm $20$ out of $22$ central rings reported by (cite: stuber_gas_2023)." (Sample selection); "these galaxies were observed with ALMA’s $7\,$m array and total power antennas only, and are therefore not considered in this work." (Sample selection)
2. **Define ring apertures and radii**: Visually define best elliptical annuli on projected CO maps; also define broad and strict uncertainty annuli; deproject the mean inner and outer best semi-major axes for the radius.  
  > "We used the SAOImageDS9[^4] tool (hereafter DS9), to visually determine elliptical annuli which best describe the shape of central rings in the molecular gas distribution as traced by CO(2-1)." (Determination of ring geometry); "The central ring radius $r_\text{ring}$ is the deprojected mean of the outer and inner “best” semi-major axes" (Determination of ring geometry)
   - software: SAOImageDS9
   - parameter: Seven annulus parameters: inner/outer major and minor axes, center coordinates and PA  
  > "An elliptical annulus can be described by seven parameters: major and minor axes of both the inner and outer ellipse ($h_\text{in/out}$ and $w_\text{in/out}$), position of the center $(x_\text{c},y_\text{c})$ of the ellipses, and their position angle PA." (Determination of ring geometry)
   - parameter: Inner and outer ellipses may have different ellipticities  
  > "We allowed the inner and outer ellipse of the ring region to have different ellipticities $\varepsilon = 1-w/h$" (Determination of ring geometry)
   - parameter: Default centers are host-galaxy centers; 12/20 shifted, mostly 0.5–1.5″  
  > "As default, we used the host galaxy centers as the center for the ellipses." (Determination of ring geometry); "For $12/20$ central rings, we needed to slightly shift (mostly $0.5{-}1.5\,\ensuremath{''}$) the center to correctly capture the molecular gas distribution." (Determination of ring geometry)
   - parameter: Uncertainty ellipses differ by at least 0.5 beam; retain the best-annulus PA  
  > "we define the uncertainty ellipses to differ from the nominal best ring at least by $0.5$ times the beam size." (Determination of ring geometry); "The PAs for the uncertainty annuli are the same as for the “best” ones" (Determination of ring geometry)
   - assumption: Determine aperture shapes in projected images to avoid deprojection uncertainties  
  > "We use the projected images of the galaxies to avoid introducing uncertainties by deprojection." (Determination of ring geometry)
   - fitting: Visual selection of heights, widths and PAs using different DS9 color stretches  
  > "Using different color stretches in DS9, we determined heights, widths, and PAs that best describe the central rings" (Determination of ring geometry)
   - validation: Broad ellipses include possible ring material; strict ellipses include only definite ring emission  
  > "an ellipse that contains all the molecular gas emission which might be still ring material (“broad” uncertainty ellipses), and a second one that only captures emission that is definitely in the ring (“strict” uncertainty ellipses)." (Determination of ring geometry)
3. **Measure ring CO luminosities and molecular masses**: Integrate ring CO luminosity and sum αCO(2–1) I_CO(2–1) times physical pixel area within each annulus to obtain molecular mass.  
  > "We integrate the ring CO luminosity and convert it into the ring molecular gas mass." (Summary and conclusion); "M_\text{mol}^\text{region} = \sum_\text{region (px)} \Sigma_\text{mol, px} A_\text{px} = \sum_\text{region (px)} \alpha_\text{CO}^{2-1} I_\text{CO, px}^{2-1} A_\text{px}" (PHANGS-ALMA data)
   - parameter: Broad-mask CO integrated-intensity maps  
  > "We use the “broad mask” versions of the integrated intensity maps" (PHANGS-ALMA data)
   - parameter: Uncertainty combines annulus variation, moment-0 noise and distance errors in quadrature  
  > "The error on the central ring molecular gas mass is dominated by the uncertainty from varying the ring shape, i.e. from integrating the “broad” and “strict” uncertainty annuli." (Central ring molecular gas mass); "The three uncertainty components are then added in quadrature yielding the total uncertainty $\Delta M_\text{mol}^\text{ring}$." (Central ring molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: Use Sun αCO(2–1) maps following the Schinnerer prescription  
  > "In this study we use maps of $\alpha_\text{CO}^{2-1}$ created by (cite: sun_2025_alphaCO) using the prescription by (cite: schinner_2024)." (PHANGS-ALMA data)
   - assumption: Exclude the ~0.2–0.3 dex αCO systematic uncertainty from reported mass-error calculations  
  > "The $\alpha_\text{CO}^{2-1}$ determination has an uncertainty of ${\sim}0.2{-}0.3\,$dex (cite: sun_2025_alphaCO) depending on the exact prescription used." (Central ring molecular gas mass); "Given that this is a systematic bias, we do not include it in our error calculations here" (Central ring molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Reintegrate the broad and strict annuli and use CO noise maps with standard Gaussian error propagation  
  > "from integrating the “broad” and “strict” uncertainty annuli." (Central ring molecular gas mass); "to obtain a second uncertainty component based on standard Gaussian error propagation:" (Central ring molecular gas mass)
4. **Measure central-ring star formation rates**: Apply the same CO-defined elliptical annuli to MUSE SFR surface-density maps and integrate over each ring.  
  > "We apply the same elliptical annular masks to the $\Sigma_\text{SFR}$ maps and integrate the SFR surface density over the area of the central rings." (Central ring SFRs)
   - parameter: 14 galaxies with SFR maps  
  > "For the $14$ galaxies of our ring sample with SFR maps" (Central ring SFRs)
   - parameter: SFR(M⊙ yr−1) = 5.5×10−42 L(Hα)corr(ergs s−1)  
  > "\text{SFR}(M_{\odot} \, \text{yr}^{-1}) = 5.5 \cdot 10 ^{-42}L(\text{H}\alpha)_\text{corr} (\text{ergs s}^{-1})." (PHANGS-MUSE – $\Sigma_\text{SFR}$ maps)
   - parameter: Input maps corrected for dust attenuation with the Balmer decrement  
  > "All maps are derived by correcting the observed H$\alpha$ emission for dust attenuation based on the Balmer decrement" (PHANGS-MUSE – $\Sigma_\text{SFR}$ maps)
   - assumption: Inherited Hα calibration: constant star formation history, age 100 Myr, solar metallicity and Kroupa IMF  
  > "This conversion factor was computed with Starburst99 (cite: leitherer_1999_starburst99), assuming a constant star formation history, an age of $100\,$Myr, solar metallicity, and a (cite: Kroupa_2001_IMF) initial mass function (IMF)." (PHANGS-MUSE – $\Sigma_\text{SFR}$ maps)

## Qualitative findings

- Molecular gas is an effective central-ring tracer, with ring incidence and radius distributions consistent with other tracers  
  > "Molecular gas is an excellent tracer for identifying central rings." (Summary and conclusion); "The ring size distribution (both absolute and relative to the disk radius) is similar to that reported by AINUR (cite: comeron_ainur_2010)." (Summary and conclusion)
- MW CMZ is smaller and less massive, but similar in relative gas content and star-formation properties  
  > "We find that when compared to central rings in PHANGS spiral galaxies, the MW CMZ is smaller and less massive, but apart from this it is not special:" (The MW CMZ is not a special central ring); "It exhibits similar star-formation properties (SFR fraction and $t_\text{dep}$) and hints toward a similar funneling dynamics by the bar (molecular gas fraction) than extragalactic central rings." (The MW CMZ is not a special central ring)
- Longer bars contain more massive rings; the relative bar-length correlation is tentative and conversion-factor dependent  
  > "we find that longer bars contain more massive rings" (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$); "There is only tentative evidence for a correlation between relative bar length and fraction of molecular gas within the central rings" (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$); "Therefore, the adopted conversion factor has a relevant effect on the measured correlations" (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$)
- No correlation of classical bar strengths with ring molecular-gas fraction  
  > "the typical bar strength parameters ($Q_\text{b}$, $\varepsilon_\text{bar}$ and $A_2^\text{max}$) show no correlation with the ring molecular gas fraction." (Summary and conclusion)
- Ring molecular mass increases with host stellar mass; the SFMS-offset trend is difficult to separate from ETG morphology  
  > "The central rings with more molecular gas are found in more massive galaxies." (Central ring properties along the SFMS); "it is difficult to determine if the trend is due to the different morphology or due to other galaxy properties." (Central rings on the SFMS - Stellar mass limit on ring formation)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: PHANGS provides resolved molecular-gas maps for a large sample, enabling detailed ring measurements and population comparisons  
  > "With the same data set, it is possible for the first time to study these molecular central rings in detail, yielding molecular gas masses and star formation rates based on the CO ring geometry." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Use integrated masses and SFRs because they are more robust than surface densities to uncertain ring geometry and low-density edges  
  > "they are more robust than surface densities of molecular gas and star formation against the exact determination of the ring shape and presence of regions of low surface density such as, e.g., the ring edges." (Central ring molecular gas mass); "In particular, the ring area in the galaxy plane is sensitive to the inclination and its uncertainty, which we wanted to avoid." (Central ring molecular gas mass)
- Alternative: Surface-density ring diagnostics rejected in favor of integrated quantities because ring area is inclination-sensitive  
  > "In particular, the ring area in the galaxy plane is sensitive to the inclination and its uncertainty, which we wanted to avoid." (Central ring molecular gas mass)
- Alternative: Determine aperture shapes on projected rather than deprojected images  
  > "We use the projected images of the galaxies to avoid introducing uncertainties by deprojection." (Determination of ring geometry)
- Alternative: Do not vary uncertainty-annulus PAs because width changes dominate the error  
  > "varying the PA introduces only a small error compared to the one introduced by varying the annulus width." (Determination of ring geometry)
- Alternative: Test Bolatto αCO instead of Schinnerer αCO; the inferred relative bar-length correlation changes  
  > "Using the (cite: bolatto_alpha_2013) $\alpha_\text{CO}$ prescription instead of the (cite: schinner_2024) one, changes the Pearson correlation coefficient to $\rho_\text{Pearson} = 0.74$" (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$)
- Alternative: Exclude ring-size versus ring-mass scaling as the cause of the relative bar-length relation  
  > "Thus, we exclude this theoretical scaling between ring size and ring gas mass to cause the $M_\text{mol}^\text{ring} / M_\text{mol}^\text{gal}$ - $L_\text{bar} / r_{25}$ correlation." (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$)
- Alternative: Suggest future automated radial-flux analysis and geometry identification with smoother JWST dust/PAH maps  
  > "Given the smoother appearance of central rings in such maps, the application of automated radial flux analysis and central ring geometry identification can allow for more consistent determination than with the visual detection adopted here." (Molecular gas as tracer for central rings)
- How they decided it was enough: Require resolved ring substructure and identifiable shape; bracket aperture uncertainty by at least half a beam  
  > "In the molecular gas distribution a central ring-like structure must be visible with a clearly identifiable shape and size." (Sample selection); "we define the uncertainty ellipses to differ from the nominal best ring at least by $0.5$ times the beam size." (Determination of ring geometry)
- Limitation (authors): Miss rings near or below ~1″ ≲100 pc and rings blended with nuclear emission  
  > "With our method we will miss central rings that have sizes close to or below our resolution limit of ${\sim}1\,\ensuremath{''} \lesssim 100\,$pc" (Are we missing central rings?); "as well as rings that cannot be well distinguished from potential nuclear CO emission and hence appear as central gas disks without a notable depression inside them." (Are we missing central rings?)
- Limitation (authors): Visual identification requires reasonably smooth CO geometry  
  > "Further our visual identifications requires rings having a reasonably smooth CO geometry." (Are we missing central rings?)
- Limitation (authors): Pseudo-rings, unbarred rings and ETG rings may not be directly comparable to classical barred spiral rings  
  > "These rings might not be directly comparable with the “classical” central ring." (Central ring sample); "their gas dynamics might differ from those in late-type spiral galaxies" (Central ring sample)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample, especially only 14 rings with SFR maps, limits correlations  
  > "Here we note that the small number of central rings in our study limits the conclusions that can be drawn." (Central ring properties along the SFMS); "Especially the limited sample of $14$ central rings with SFR maps caused further correlations we considered, e.g. between the ring’s molecular gas masses and their SFRs, not to be reliable." (Central ring properties along the SFMS)
- Limitation (authors): Conservative aperture uncertainties may overestimate a 1σ mass uncertainty  
  > "might have in particular overestimated the uncertainty (assumed to be a $1\sigma$ standard deviation) on $M_\text{mol}^\text{ring}$." (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$)
- Limitation (authors): αCO systematics are excluded from reported mass errors and affect correlations  
  > "Given that this is a systematic bias, we do not include it in our error calculations here" (Central ring molecular gas mass); "Therefore, the adopted conversion factor has a relevant effect on the measured correlations" (The role of bars and $\alpha_\text{CO}$)
- Limitation (authors): Snapshot data cannot resolve the dynamic fueling process  
  > "although we cannot resolve this highly dynamic process with the data at hand." (Summary and conclusion)
- Limitation (authors): SFMS-offset trends are entangled with ETG morphology  
  > "it is difficult to determine if the trend is due to the different morphology or due to other galaxy properties." (Central rings on the SFMS - Stellar mass limit on ring formation)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Exact aperture reproduction is blocked by missing centers even though inner/outer axes and PAs are tabulated.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ring and whole-galaxy masses use differently described αCO prescriptions; relative gas fractions require checking their consistency.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Shared distances and host totals can correlate errors in masses and ratios; the resampling description does not specify covariance handling.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Agreement of tracer-selected samples does not establish equal completeness, particularly near the CO resolution limit.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A non-detection of a bar-strength correlation does not establish that bar strength has no causal effect.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The NGC 4476 molecular fraction uncertainty is ±18% in the ring table but ±17% in the results prose.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The abstract's molecular-mass summary and the conclusion's quoted percentile range are not identical; reproduce each stated summary rather than treating them as interchangeable.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The inference that similar integrated properties imply similar gas-inflow and star-formation processes is suggestive rather than a direct dynamical measurement.
