## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra at three positions**: Average spectra over a beam-sized region at MM1, MM2, MM3 positions and compute rms in line-free channels  
  > "The extracted spectra are the average of a 1$\overset{\second}{.}$00$\times$0$\overset{\second}{.}$74 region, equivalent to the area of the synthesised beam." (2.2 Method)
   - parameter: rms ~0.9 K, ~0.6 K, ~0.04 K for MM1, MM2, MM3  
  > "These are $\sim$0.9 K (68 mJy beam$^{-1}$) for MM1, $\sim$0.6 K (45 mJy beam$^{-1}$) for MM2, and $\sim$0.04 K (3 mJy beam$^{-1}$) for MM3." (2.2 Method)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit LTE synthetic spectra grids for Tex and N**: Grids of LTE optically thin models varying Tex and N, minimum chi2 as best fit; vLSR and FWHM fixed  
  > "Excitation temperatures and column densities are determined for the detected species by creating grids of model spectra varying $T_{\textrm{ex}}$ and $N_{\textrm{s}}$ and identifying the model spectrum with the minimal $\chi^2$ as the best fit." (2.2 Method)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: Tex 10–500 K in 10 K steps; N sampled by 20 log steps; channels within ±2×FWHM  
  > "All grids have $T_{\textrm{ex}}$ spanning 10 – 500 K in steps of 10 K and $N_{\textrm{s}}$ sampled by 20 logarithmically spaced steps." (Table 1 notes)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin lines, source size equal to synthesised beam  
  > "Assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and optically thin lines, synthetic spectra are constructed for each species." (2.2 Method)
   - assumption: CH2NH and NH2CHO Tex fixed to CH3OH values  
  > "The reported CH$_2$NH and NH$_2$CHO column densities are therefore derived assuming $T_{\textrm{ex}}$ to be fixed at the value derived for CH$_3$OH" (2.2 Method)
   - fitting: chi2 with sigma = sqrt(rms^2 + (0.1 I_obs)^2); uncertainties from std of models within 1 sigma of best fit  
  > "The uncertainty on $N_{\textrm{s}}$ and $T_{\textrm{ex}}$ is listed as the standard deviation of model spectra with $\chi^2$ within 1$\sigma$ of the best-fit model." (2.2 Method)
   - validation: Fixed vLSR and widths checked against data in line plots  
  > "the fixed $v_{\textrm{LSR}}$ and line widths are consistent with the data for all species" (2.2 Method)
3. **Assess line blending**: Model all catalogued overlapping species at maximal column density, sum, and refit with and without them  
  > "Two fits are then preformed; the first fit takes only the studied species into account and is used to set an upper limit on the column density for each of these; the second fit includes the contributions from all potential blending species." (2.2 Method)
   - software: CASSIS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared N with and without blending species (e.g. <30% change for MM1 CH3NH2)  
  > "the variation between the column density of the fit which only takes into account CH$_3$NH$_2$ and the fit which includes all potential blending species is less then 30%" (3.1 Methylamine)
4. **Derive column density ratios and compare**: Ratios to CH3OH (13C and 18O isotopologue references), CH2NH, NH2CHO, CH3CN, compared with models and other sources  
  > "column density ratios for each of the studied species with respect to CH$_{3}$OH are derived" (4 Discussion)
   - parameter: Ratio uncertainty ~40% (sqrt2 x 30%)  
  > "the uncertainty on listed column density ratios is conservatively estimated to be $\sim$40% ($\sqrt{2}\times30\%$)" (2.2 Method)
   - assumption: 12C/13C=62, 16O/18O=450, 14N/15N=422 from dGC = 7.02 kpc  
  > "all derived assuming $d_{\textrm{GC}}$ = 7.02 kpc" (2.2 Method)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Garrod2013 hot core models, Sgr B2, Orion KL, IRAS 16293B  
  > "will be compared with the predictions of the chemical models of (cite: Garrod2013)" (4 Discussion)

## Qualitative findings

- CH3NH2 securely detected in MM1 and MM3, less clearly in MM2 (line ratio not reproduced by single-component LTE model)  
  > "CH$_3$NH$_2$ is securely detected towards both the MM1 and MM3 regions while the detection towards MM2 is slightly less clear." (3.1 Methylamine)
- CH3NH2 Tex higher than for other species  
  > "the CH$_3$NH$_2$ excitation temperatures are higher than for any of the other species." (4.1 Methylamine towards NGC 6334)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: CH3OH chosen as reference because abundant and well studied in NGC 6334  
  > "CH$_3$OH is chosen as a reference because it is one of the most abundant COMs in the ISM" (4 Discussion)
- Alternative: Two-component warm/cool model for MM2 did not improve the fit  
  > "introducing a two-component model to account for a warm and cool emission region respectively, does not improve the fit." (3.1 Methylamine)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single CH2NH line blended, giving only upper limits; Tex of CH2NH and NH2CHO unconstrained  
  > "the transition is blended with CH$_3$OCHO and the column density of CH$_2$NH is therefore reported as an upper limit" (3.2)
- Limitation (authors): Models not optimised to NGC 6334 conditions  
  > "the comparison between observed and modelled column density ratios should only be considered as indicative of trends." (4.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed vLSR/FWHM and beam-size source assumption affect N
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heavy line blending makes detection model-dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tex of CH2NH/NH2CHO borrowed from CH3OH
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison with single-dish and other sources suffers from beam dilution
