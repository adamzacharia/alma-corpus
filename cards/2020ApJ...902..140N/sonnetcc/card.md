## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image line cubes**: ALMA pipeline calibrated visibilities imaged with CASA tclean at 0.4 km/s channels, Briggs weighting  
  > "The ALMA pipeline calibrated visibilities were imaged using CASA task TCLEAN with a channel resolution of 0.4 km s$^{-1}$." (ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "We applied the Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5." (ALMA Observations)
   - parameter: Channels binned from 0.2 to 0.4 km/s  
  > "We binned the channels to a resolution of 0.4 km s$^{-1}$ for the analysis." (ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make velocity-integrated intensity maps**: Integrate HCO+ and HCN cubes along the velocity axis  
  > "These maps are obtained by integrating the emission line data cubes along the velocity axis." (HCO and HCN emission in N 55)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Identify clumps and measure size, linewidth and flux**: astrodendro dendrogram on the data cubes; leaves taken as clumps; parameters by bisection method  
  > "**Astrodendro** determines the basic parameters of the identified structures using the bisection method." (Clump identification and characterization); "These are all identified as leaves by **astrodendro**." (Clump identification and characterization)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: Local maxima with flux >3 sigma_rms  
  > "Local maxima are identified from the data cube each with flux $>$3$\sigma_{\rm rms}$ and the iso-surfaces around the maxima are classified as leaves, branches or trunks." (Clump identification and characterization)
   - assumption: Gaussian line profiles for FWHM from sigma_v  
  > "For Gaussian line profiles, the FWHM linewidth ($\Delta v$) is given by $\Delta v$ = $\sqrt{8ln(2)}$ $\sigma_{v}$." (Clump identification and characterization)
   - fitting: Bootstrapping for parameter uncertainties  
  > "We use bootstrapping technique to determine the uncertainties in the derived parameters." (Clump identification and characterization)
   - validation: Minimum spectral S/N of 4 for HCO+ emission peaks  
  > "We assert that all detections are strong with HCO$^{+}$(1$-$0) emission peaks detected with a minimum spectral signal to noise ratio of 4." (Clump identification and characterization)
4. **Derive HCO+ and H2 column density and optical depth**: LTE analysis with peak optical depth from peak brightness temperature, then N_H2 from assumed HCO+ abundance  
  > "We obtain the peak optical depth ($\tau_{\rm p}$) from the peak brightness temperature of each clump using the equation (cite: barnes2011)" (Clump column density)
   - parameter: Tex = 30 K  
  > "We assume $T_{\rm ex}=30$ K as the excitation temperature of the HCO$^{+}$(1$-$0) clumps in the N 55-S for further calculations." (Clump column density)
   - parameter: Background temperature 2.72 K  
  > "$T_{\rm bg}$ is the background temperature taken to be 2.72 K." (Clump column density)
   - parameter: Q_rot = 14.4  
  > "The partition function $Q_{rot}$ of HCO$^{+}$($1-0$) transition is 14.4 at $T_{\rm ex}=30$ K (cite: rohlfs2004; mangum2015)." (Clump column density)
   - assumption: HCO+/H2 abundance X = 1e-9  
  > "Assuming the relative abundance of HCO$^{+}$ to H$_{2}$ to be X=10$^{-9}$" (Clump column density)
   - assumption: Gaussian emission line profile  
  > "we assume the emission line profiles, $\phi(V)$, to be Gaussian" (Clump column density)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tex sensitivity: 40 K lowers N_p by ~28%, 20 K raises it by ~39%  
  > "For $T_{\rm ex}$ = 40 K, the $N_{\rm p}$ values of the clumps are $\sim$ 28% lower compared to the values derived for $T_{\rm ex}$ = 30 K and $\sim$ 39% higher for $T_{\rm ex}$ = 20 K." (Clump column density)
5. **Derive volume density, surface density, LTE and virial masses and virial parameter**: n_col, Sigma_p, M_LTE, M_vir equations; virial parameter alpha = M_vir/M_LTE  
  > "The $M_{\rm LTE}$ and $M_{\rm vir}$ masses of the clumps, as well as the virial parameter, $\alpha$ = $M_{\rm vir}/M_{\rm LTE}$ , are tabulated in Table" (Mass of molecular clumps)
   - parameter: mu_mol = 2.3; beta = 1  
  > "where $\mu_{\rm mol}$ is the mean molecular weight in the gas which is taken to be 2.3 (cite: barnes2011)." (Mass surface density); "we assume $\beta = 1$ (cite: vandertak2000)." (Mass of molecular clumps)
   - assumption: Spherical clumps with radial power-law density profile  
  > "This equation is based on the assumption that clumps are spherical in shape with a radial power-law density profile of index $\beta$." (Mass of molecular clumps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with mass at critical density gives beam filling factor f  
  > "Comparing the above derived mass with the cloud mass derived from LTE analysis yields $f=(0.001-0.01)$ for the clumps in our sample." (Low volume densities of dense gas clumps)
6. **Estimate dense gas fraction**: CO-traced H2 mass (L_CO, X_CO, 30% missing-flux correction) compared with HCO+ mass sum  
  > "Thus the dense gas fraction in the N 55-S region is 0.025$\pm$0.005." (Dense gas fraction)
   - parameter: X_CO = 2e20 cm-2 K-1 km-1 s  
  > "Hence we use $X_{\rm CO}$=2$\times$10$^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ K$^{-1}$ km$^{-1}$ s in our calculation." (Dense gas fraction)
   - assumption: Galactic X_CO is adequate for metallicities down to ~0.5 Zsun  
  > "However, a Galactic value can be approximated for environments with metallicities down to $\sim$0.5 $Z_{\odot}$" (Dense gas fraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative X_CO = 4e20 gives 0.013  
  > "A higher value of $X_{\rm CO}$ = 4$\times$10$^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ K$^{-1}$ km$^{-1}$ s (cite: hughes2010) will further decrease the dense gas fraction to 0.013." (Dense gas fraction)
7. **Fit size-linewidth relation and test virial state**: Power-law fit of linewidth versus radius for HCO+ clumps; virial parameter examined  
  > "The size and linewidth of HCO$^{+}$($1-0$) clumps follow a power-law, $\Delta v$ $\propto$ $R^{0.65\pm0.32}$" (size-linewidth relation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Index compared with CMZ, extragalactic and Milky Way values  
  > "Thus the size-linewidth power-law index of HCO$^{+}$($1-0$) clumps of the N 55-S is consistent with the CMZ, extragalactic, and the Milky Way clouds." (size-linewidth relation)
8. **Associate clumps with YSOs and compare with other LMC clouds**: Positional match of Spitzer YSOs to emission peaks; clumps with and without YSOs compared in mass, surface density and linewidth; comparison with Seale et al. 2012 and Anderson et al. 2014 clumps  
  > "The positional offset between the emission peak and YSO position is 1.16, 1.21, 0.43 and 1.69 arcsec for clump 2, 3, 7, and 8 respectively." (Clump association with YSOs)
   - assumption: 8 micron magnitude used as proxy for YSO mass  
  > "suggest that the 8$\mu$m magnitude of YSOs can be treated as a good proxy of the YSO mass" (Clump association with YSOs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Chance-coincidence probability 0.04% for a random YSO within 2 arcsec of a peak  
  > "If the YSOs are randomly distributed, the probability of a YSO locating within 2 arcsec of an emission peak is only 0.04%" (Clump association with YSOs)

## Qualitative findings

- All four identified YSOs lie near HCO+ and HCN emission peaks (clumps 2, 3, 7, 8)  
  > "All identified young stellar objects (YSOs) in the N 55-S are found to be near the HCO$^{+}$ and HCN emission peaks showing the association of these clumps with recent star formation activity." (abstract)
- N 55-S clumps have similar size but lower linewidth and larger HCN/HCO+ ratio than other LMC GMCs  
  > "find that N 55-S clumps possess similar size but relatively lower linewidth and larger HCN/HCO$^{+}$(1$-$0) flux ratio." (abstract)
- Clump volume densities lie well below the HCO+(1-0) critical density  
  > "The HCO$^{+}$($1-0$) line emission towards all the clumps in our sample is excited well below the critical density." (Clump volume density)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: First HCO+ and HCN observations of N 55, a relatively quiescent LMC region, to extend the sample of dense-gas studies  
  > "This is first time HCO$^{+}$ and HCN emission are observed toward N 55." (Introduction); "Our work extends this sample including N 55-S with a goal to explore how different star formation environments affect the properties of dense molecular clumps." (Introduction)
- Why this method: HCO+ used as the primary clump tracer for physical properties  
  > "We use HCO$^{+}$ as the primary clump tracer and further investigate various clump physical properties." (Clump identification and characterization)
- Alternative: Tex of 20 K and 40 K tested against the adopted 30 K; X_CO = 4e20 tested against the Galactic value  
  > "For $T_{\rm ex}$ = 40 K, the $N_{\rm p}$ values of the clumps are $\sim$ 28% lower compared to the values derived for $T_{\rm ex}$ = 30 K and $\sim$ 39% higher for $T_{\rm ex}$ = 20 K." (Clump column density); "A higher value of $X_{\rm CO}$ = 4$\times$10$^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ K$^{-1}$ km$^{-1}$ s (cite: hughes2010) will further decrease the dense gas fraction to 0.013." (Dense gas fraction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only one HCO+ transition, so Tex could not be derived from the data and was assumed  
  > "The excitation temperature ($T_{\rm ex}$) can be precisely determined if we have multiple transitions of HCO$^{+}$, while our observation is limited to single transition." (Clump column density)
- Limitation (authors): Spatial resolution limits the smallest clump sizes that can be resolved  
  > "Given the limit in our spatial resolution, the smallest detected molecular structures in the N 55-S seem compatible with the Milky Way clumps." (Clump identification and characterization)
- Limitation (authors): M_LTE may be underestimated, and optical depth may be underestimated because of a small beam filling factor  
  > "The relatively high value of virial parameter could be due to an underestimation of $M_{\rm LTE}$ in these region or due to dynamical impact of star formation." (M_vir versus M_LTE); "Another possibility is a severe underestimation of optical depth of the emission line due to small beam filling factor." (Low volume densities of dense gas clumps)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tex = 30 K is assumed for every clump and the Tex-driven uncertainty is not propagated into the tabulated errors
- Caveat (inferred, ours): HCO+ abundance of 1e-9 is a literature value with no check for this LMC environment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 4 YSOs, so the YSO-association trends rest on small numbers (4 YSO clumps against 6 without)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single short execution (796 s) with no stated missing-flux or short-spacing assessment for 4 arcsec clumps
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison clumps come from ATCA studies with different resolution and clump-finding methods
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Galactic X_CO applied to the LMC affects the dense gas fraction directly
