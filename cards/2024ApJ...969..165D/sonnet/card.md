## Analysis recipe

1. **Self-calibrate and image continuum**: Self-cal on pseudo-continuum, then tclean continuum at robust +0.5 with elliptical masks  
  > "The first step was to create pseudo-continuum visibilities by flagging all channels that contained line emission." (Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 7 phase-only + 1 amplitude iteration; robust +0.5  
  > "we ran a total of seven phase-only calibration iterations and one amplitude calibration iteration" (Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SNR improvement factors reported  
  > "improved the SNR of the continuum emission only by a factor of $\sim 1.2$ for 216–0939" (Data reduction)
2. **Image line cubes**: uvcontsub then tclean with robust +0.5, uvtaper 0.5 arcsec, Keplerian masks, 0.4 km/s channels  
  > "We also used the `uvtaper` parameter in `tclean` to reduce the weight of the longest baselines" (Data reduction)
   - software: CASA, keplerian_mask
   - parameter: 253-1536B mask: inclination 45 deg, PA -90 deg  
  > "we instead used a more conservative mask with an inclination of $45^{\circ}$ and PA of $-90^\circ$" (Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested uv cuts to minimize cloud contamination; no significant improvement  
  > "we did not find a significant improvement in the quality of our observations after removing the shortest baselines" (Data reduction)
3. **Measure continuum disk sizes and radial profiles**: Deprojected radial profiles; size = radius containing 95% of flux  
  > "defined as the radius containing $95\%$ of the total flux" (Dust continuum emission)
   - software: GoFish
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Detect lines**: >=3 sigma in >=3 channels within Keplerian mask; matched filter in visibilities using HCN and C18O as filters  
  > "A line was considered to be detected if emission within the Keplerian mask was $\ge 3\sigma$ (rms) in at least $3$ velocity channels." (Line detections)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Matched filter confirms H2CO and C2H detections  
  > "we confirm the detection of both H$_2$CO and C$_2$H $(3-2)$ lines in both disks" (Line detections)
5. **Disk-integrated fluxes**: Multiply cubes by Keplerian mask (immath) and sum; uncertainty from 1000 bootstrap samples of shifted centers plus 10% calibration in quadrature; 3 sigma upper limits  
  > "changing the disk center in $1000$ random samples in regions without emission outside of the original Keplerian mask via bootstrapping" (Disk integrated fluxes)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Number of samples large enough that uncertainty is stable  
  > "The number of samples is chosen to be sufficiently large so that the estimated uncertainty does not vary significantly." (Disk integrated fluxes)
6. **Column densities**: LTE, optically thin, Tex 20-50 K  
  > "We assume the optically thin approximation and a disk-averaged excitation temperature that ranges from $20-50$ K" (Column density retrieval)
   - assumption: LTE and optically thin; Tex 20-50 K  
  > "we consider that the gas obeys local thermal equilibrium (LTE)" (Column density retrieval)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **HCN excitation temperature**: Ratio of HCN 3-2 (smoothed to Band 7 beam) to HCN 4-3, optically thin  
  > "we smoothed the HCN $(3-2)$ emission to match the angular resolution of the HCN $(4-3)$ line" (HCN excitation temperature)
   - assumption: tau=0  
  > "assumed $\tau=0$ (the optically thin case)" (HCN excitation temperature)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare with isolated disks**: Distance-normalized fluxes and flux ratios vs stellar mass with Spearman correlations against literature sample  
  > "we find a positive correlation between the fluxes and stellar mass, for all the lines presented here" (Comparison between irradiated and isolated disks)
   - fitting: Spearman rank  
  > "The estimated Spearman correlation coefficient of the combined disk sample is shown in the bottom right corner of each panel" (Discussion: Comparison between irradiated and isolated disks)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- 216-0939 disk shows a central cavity and southern side 23% brighter than northern side  
  > "with the southern side being more elongated and $23\%$ brighter than the northern side" (Dust continuum emission)
- Line emission is more extended than dust continuum; 12CO heavily affected by cloud contamination  
  > "all molecular line emission is more extended than the dust continuum emission" (Spatial distribution)
