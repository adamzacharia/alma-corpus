## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra from uv data**: GILDAS uvfit with point-source model at prior positions from COSMOS2020 or ALMA continuum; ALMA spectral windows combined  
  > "we adopted a point source model for the sources, using prior positions from the COSMOS2020 catalogue or ALMA continuum images" (3.3 Spectrum extraction pipeline)
   - software: GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect lines and determine redshifts**: Line search of highest S/N lines; power-law continuum with slope 3.7; Gaussian fit with MPFIT; single-line redshifts resolved by comparing with COSMOS2020 photo-z PDF(z)  
  > "The continuum-subtracted spectra are then fitted with a Gaussian line-profile using `MPFIT`" (4.1 Line detection and redshift determination)
   - software: MPFIT, LePhare
   - parameter: P_chance<5% for masking lines; robust lines sigma>7, p_chance<0.001  
  > "We detect robust ($\sigma>7$,$p_{\rm chance}<0.001$) emission lines in seven out of the eight pointings" (5.1 Emission lines and redshifts)
   - assumption: Modified blackbody with beta~1.7  
  > "assuming modified blackbody emission with $\beta\sim1.7$ (cite: Magdis2012SED)" (4.1 Line detection and redshift determination)
   - fitting: Gaussian line fit with MPFIT  
  > "The continuum-subtracted spectra are then fitted with a Gaussian line-profile using `MPFIT`[^3], at the frequencies identified by the line searching algorithm." (4.1 Line detection and redshift determination)
   - validation: 2023.1.00652.S [CI] observation confirmed one source identified from PDF(z) comparison  
  > "which further validates our identification method" (4.1 Line detection and redshift determination)
3. **Select candidate members**: Photo-z selection |zphot - zspec,group|<0.1(1+zspec,group) within virial radius  
  > "we selected candidate group members with $|z_{\rm phot}-z_{\rm spec,group}|<0.1(1+z_{\rm spec,group})$ within the virial projected radius" (4.2 Candidate member selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Interloper fractions estimated from background counts and tested on CLJ1001  
  > "We find two clear interlopers and a total interloper fraction of $17-35\%$ in a $r=30''$ aperture" (6.4 Interlopers in candidate members)
4. **Integrated FIR photometry and SED fit**: Super-deblending with one SCUBA2 peak prior per group, PSF fitting of Herschel/SCUBA2/MeerKAT maps; STARDUST SED fit; radio extrapolated from IR-radio relation  
  > "we fit the integrated FIR spectral energy distribution (SED) using `STARDUST` (cite: Kokorev2021)" (4.3 Integrated FIR spectral energy distributions)
   - software: STARDUST
   - parameter: Exclude sources within 10'' of prior; >3 sigma detections, otherwise 3 sigma upper limits  
  > "Only photometric points with a significant detection ($>3\sigma$) were considered in the fitting" (4.3 Integrated FIR spectral energy distributions)
   - assumption: Dust templates of Magdis2012; optically thin dust mass  
  > "based on dust templates from (cite: Magdis2012SED)" (5.2 Integrated FIR properties)
   - fitting: STARDUST fit at group zspec  
  > "The fitting was performed at the spectroscopic redshift of each group" (4.3 Integrated FIR spectral energy distributions)
   - validation: Residual maps checked; fluxes calibrated by Monte Carlo simulations  
  > "the measured fluxes and their uncertainties are then calibrated by Monte Carlo simulations performed on the maps" (4.3 Integrated FIR spectral energy distributions)
5. **Estimate halo masses (six methods)**: SHMR scaling of BCG mass (1) and total stellar mass (2a, 2b); overdensity with Tinker bias (3); projected NFW fit to stellar mass surface density (4, 5); best Mh = average of 2a, 3, 4  
  > "We adopted an average of methods $M_{\rm h}(2a)$, $M_{\rm h}(3)$, and $M_{\rm h}(4)$ as the median of the best estimate" (5.3 Dark matter halos)
   - software: hmf, cluster_toolkit
   - parameter: Group velocity dispersion 400 km/s for depth correction; radial annuli 10 pkpc to ~2 pMpc; 10000 random annuli for background  
  > "The radial bins are annuli with increasing radius $R$ from $R\sim10$ pkpc out to $\sim2\,{\rm pMpc}$." (4.4 Estimate of dark matter halo mass)
   - assumption: DM-to-baryon ratio 5.4 (Planck); NFW profile; stellar mass traces DM; M_h(5) uses Ludlow2016 concentration  
  > "We adopted a dark matter to baryonic mass ratio of $\Omega_{m}/\Omega_{b}-1\approx5.4$ from PLANCK cosmology" (4.4 Estimate of dark matter halo mass)
   - fitting: Maximize combined Poisson probability of N_obs given N_model across annuli  
  > "We found the best model by maximizing the combined probability of finding $N_{\rm obs}$ when expecting $N_{\rm model}$" (4.4 Estimate of dark matter halo mass)
   - validation: Method 4 applied to CLJ1001 recovers (8±2)e13 Msun vs X-ray/velocity dispersion (5.0±2.3)e13; methods agree within 0.2-0.3 dex  
  > "The recovered halo mass is in excellent agreement with the X-ray and velocity dispersion halo masses of $M_{\rm h}=(5.0\pm2.3)\times10^{13}\,M_\odot$" (6.1 Dark matter halo mass estimates: Prospects and caveats)
6. **Baryonic accretion rate and SFR/BAR relation**: BAR from Eq. 5 of Goerdt2010; expected SFR from C_SFR and M_stream/M_h; fit quasi-linear SFR/BAR vs M_stream/M_h including literature groups, excluding the CEERS group  
  > "Following the procedure in (cite: Daddi2022Lya) and using Eq. (5) in (cite: Goerdt2010core), we estimated the total BAR" (5.4 Baryonic accretion rate)
   - assumption: M_stream from Eq. 2 of Daddi2022_mstream  
  > "adopted from Eq. (2) in (cite: Daddi2022_mstream)" (5.4 Baryonic accretion rate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative M_stream definition from Dekel2006 does not change fit significantly  
  > "Adopting instead the definition of $M_{\rm stream}$ from (cite: Dekel2006), does not significantly change the fitting results" (5.4 Baryonic accretion rate)
7. **Compare with cluster-formation simulations**: Compare halo masses with Chiang2013 and TNG300 progenitors; BCG stellar masses with TNG300  
  > "We find all groups to have halo masses consistent with progenitors of clusters with present day halo masses of at least $M_{\rm h}(z=0)>10^{14}\,{\rm M_\odot}$" (6.2 Nature and fate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Stellar mass density profiles of all eight groups are consistent with an NFW profile, suggesting collapsed structures in a single halo  
  > "We find that the radial stellar mass densities of the eight groups are consistent with a NFW profile" (abstract)
- Halo masses of all groups are consistent with progenitors of Mh(z=0)>1e14 Msun clusters  
  > "the halo masses of all structures are consistent with those of progenitors of $M_{\rm h}(z=0)>10^{14}\,{\rm M_{\odot}}$ galaxy clusters" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: COSMOS rich ancillary data gives best quality for initial statistical study of NICE  
  > "the eight high-$z$ group candidates are entitled to the best data quality in the NICE sample" (2 NOEMA formIng Cluster survEy (NICE))
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Mh(1) from BCG stellar mass rejected as unreliable for groups  
  > "we caution against the use of this method for massive galaxy groups, and massive individual galaxies" (6.1 Dark matter halo mass estimates: Prospects and caveats)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Halo mass estimates carry large uncertainties; group centre and concentration degeneracies; interloper contamination  
  > "Given that the dark matter halo estimates are prone to large uncertainties" (5.4 Baryonic accretion rate)
- Limitation (authors): Cold-stream regime scatter based on only six points  
  > "This increased observed scatter is only from six points and would require a larger sample to confirm" (6.3 Baryonic accretion: Flattening or not?)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Photo-z membership leaves substantial interloper contamination, particularly in outer annuli
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-line redshifts depend on photo-z PDF
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Halo mass uncertainty (0.3 dex) propagates strongly to BAR and the fitted SFR/BAR slope
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Concentrations poorly constrained with large errors
- Caveat (inferred, ours): NOEMA 4 arcsec beam blends HPC1001 core members
