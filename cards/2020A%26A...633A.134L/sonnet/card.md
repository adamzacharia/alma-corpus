## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO(1-0) cubes**: GILDAS (NOEMA) or CASA mapping of pipeline-calibrated data (ALMA); continuum subtraction; cubes at 20/60/200 km/s; ALMA IRAS 13120 combines 2012.1.00306.S and 2015.1.00287.S  
  > "For IRAS 13120-5453, we combined our 2012.1.00306.S CO(1-0) data with a similar dataset from 2015.1.00287.S (PI K. Sliwa), in order to improve signal-to-noise." (2.2 ALMA observations)
   - software: GILDAS, CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit continuum position and size**: UV-plane Gaussian fit to NOEMA continuum; Gaussian fit to ALMA continuum image  
  > "Results are from UV fitting a Gaussian model to the NOEMA continuum visibilities, and from fitting a Gaussian model to the ALMA continuum image." (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Decompose spectra and measure outflow v_out, flux and R_out**: Multi-Gaussian fit to nuclear/aperture spectra with visual choice of host components; v_out=|dv|+FW10%/2; S_CO(wings) integrates the broad component where it contributes >50%; R_out=|dR|+FWHM/2 from UV-plane Gaussian fit to outflow velocity range  
  > "fit multiple Gaussians to the spectrum which, is the sum of host emission and (if present) broad outflow. Visual inspection is used to choose the number of Gaussians needed to fit the host emission." (Outflow properties of the targets); "Visual inspection is used to choose the number of Gaussians needed to fit the host emission." (3)
   - parameter: upper limit S_CO,wings < 10 x RMS_200 x 200 for non-detections  
  > "For objects without outflow detection, we assign limits for the outflow CO flux: $S_{\rm CO,wings} < 10\times RMS_{200} \times 200$" (3)
   - assumption: No correction for opening angle or inclination  
  > "We do not attempt to correct the measured $R_{\rm out}$ or $v_{\rm out}$" (3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Inspection of channel maps, PV diagrams, positions in velocity channels  
  > "on the basis of total CO maps and selected channel maps, spectra, positions in different velocity channels, and position-velocity diagrams" (3)
4. **Remove NS contamination in NGC 4418**: Fit blue NS complex with fixed relative fluxes and velocities (LTE ratios at 150 K), subtract blue and red complexes  
  > "We fix relative fluxes of the NS line components and their velocities relative to CO, but vary the overall flux normalization and line width to fit the blue nitrogen sulfide complex" (3.1)
   - assumption: T=150 K LTE line ratios  
  > "The adopted intensities are for T=150 K" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive outflow masses and rates**: M_out from CO luminosity with alpha_CO=0.8; Mdot = C M_out v_out/R_out with C=1; brightness temperature ratios from other CO transitions for literature sources  
  > "We hence proceed computing outflow rates with C=1 in Eqn." (4.2)
   - assumption: alpha_CO(1-0)=0.8 Msun/(K km/s pc^2)  
  > "adopt $\alpha_{\rm CO(1-0)}$ = 0.8 M$_\odot$ / (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)" (4.1)
   - assumption: constant outflow rate since -R_out/v_out  
  > "Assuming that the outflow has started at a point in the past at $-t_{\rm flow} = -R_{\rm out}/v_{\rm out}$ and has continued with a constant mass outflow rate until now directly leads to C=1." (4.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CO line ratios R31/R21 for four sources; comparison with OH-based results  
  > "These results do not point toward the low conversion factor of the optically thin case." (4.1)
6. **Fit scaling relations with bolometric luminosity**: Linear fits in log space with linmix_err, errors 0.15 dex on L_bol and 0.3 dex on outflow quantities; mass fit includes upper limits  
  > "Here we have used linmix_err (cite: kelly07) and adopted errors of 0.15 dex for the bolometric luminosities and 0.3 dex for the outflow quantities." (5.2)
   - software: linmix_err
   - fitting: linmix_err (Kelly 2007)  
  > "Here we have used linmix_err (cite: kelly07)" (5.2)
   - validation: Tested adding fiducial R_out=0.7 kpc, v_out=450 km/s for limits: slope unchanged, normalization -0.1 dex  
  > "effectively unchanged, and reduces the normalization by $\sim$0.1 dex" (Relation of outflow properties to FIR surface brightness, AGN luminosity, and bolometric luminosity)

## Qualitative findings

- CO and OH outflow velocities agree broadly, with ~0.36 dex scatter around 1:1  
  > "CO- and OH-based velocities are in broad agreement, but there is considerable $\sim$0.36 dex scatter around the 1:1 line." (5.1)
- Significant outflows also occur in sources with low or no AGN luminosity, with some requiring momentum boosts >100 beyond current AGN  
  > "This boost reaches up to more than a factor 100" (5.2)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Compact FIR sources lacking deep CO data chosen as likely outflow laboratories  
  > "make it an obvious basis for a search for local outflow laboratories, well suited for detailed study" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Conservative wings-only flux and v_out definition to avoid confusion with host gas motions  
  > "we conservatively identify outflowing gas with line wings only" (1)
- Alternative: C=3 outflow-rate geometry rejected in favour of C=1, since resolved outflows lack brightening at large radius  
  > "lacking the brightening at large radius that is expected for the constant volume density or thin shell cases" (4.2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Selection effects in the literature-based combined sample; alpha_CO uncertainty and geometry uncertainty  
  > "we caution again about possible selection effects in the combined sample that is significantly literature based" (5.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow decomposition into Gaussians is subjective (visual choice of components)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flow-time argument assumes constant velocity from the nucleus
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO=0.8 applied uniformly despite optically thin case
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous literature sample with reconstructed parameters
