## Analysis recipe

1. **Image CO(2-1), CO(5-4) and Band 6 continuum from calibrated visibilities**: uv-plane continuum subtraction, tclean imaging with natural weighting in 75 km/s channels, moment-0 maps integrated over a selected velocity range  
  > "we perform uv-plane continuum subtraction (with `uvcontsub`), and image the CO(2–1) emission with natural weighting and channel width $\Delta v = 75$ km s$^{-1}$ (with `tclean`)." (2.1 ALMA)
   - software: CASA, uvcontsub, tclean, immoments
   - parameter: CO(2-1) moment-0: -575 to +475 km/s (channels 55 to 69)  
  > "by integrating between -575km/s to +475km/s (channels 55 to 69)" (2.1 ALMA)
   - parameter: CO(5-4) moment-0: -605 to +445 km/s (channels 38 to 52)  
  > "we integrate this emission from -605km/s to +445km/s (channels 38 to 52)" (2.1 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Velocity range chosen where the line clearly shows in a 2'' aperture spectrum of the whole system  
  > "We choose this range because this is where the line clearly shows in a spectrum extracted with the same" (2.1 ALMA)
2. **Identify the merger by comparing ALMA with HST, VISTA, Spitzer and VLA**: Compare the morphology and peaks of CO and dust continuum with ancillary images; North is the VLA and ALMA peak with no HST emission, South is the HST galaxy  
  > "The VLA/3GHz data, tracing high-energy sources such as AGN and dust-unbiased star formation, is completely offset from the HST data but fully consistent with the peak of the ALMA data" (3.1 Identification of the dusty galaxy merger)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Image the velocity offset between negative and positive velocity peaks**: Moment-0 maps integrated separately over negative and positive velocities for CO(2-1) and CO(5-4)  
  > "by integrating between -575km/s and -50km/s and the positive velocities peak" (3.2 Velocity and spatial offsets of the dusty galaxy merger); "by integrating between -50km/s and +475km/s" (3.2 Velocity and spatial offsets of the dusty galaxy merger)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Deblend and measure CO(2-1), CO(5-4) and continuum fluxes of North and South**: Flux of North measured within a single-beam element centred on it; South taken as 3 sigma upper limit from the remaining emission inside the CO(2-1) 3 sigma contours; same procedure for CO(5-4) and continuum  
  > "we associate this bright component to North and estimate the CO(2–1) flux by measuring the flux within the single-beam element centred on North." (3.3 The CO flux measurements); "We associate the remaining emission within the CO(2–1) 3 $\sigma$ contours to South." (3.3 The CO flux measurements)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Flux error is the standard deviation of the intensity map with the merger emission within 2'' masked  
  > "The corresponding flux error is the standard deviation of the CO(2–1) intensity map, masking the emission of the merger" (3.3 The CO flux measurements)
   - assumption: All gas not associated with North belongs to South  
  > "With this method, we make the assumption that all the gas that is not associated with North belongs to South." (3.3 The CO flux measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Convert CO(2-1) flux to line luminosity and molecular gas mass**: Flux to L'CO(2-1) with the standard equation, then to CO(1-0) with a ratio of 0.85, then to M_H2 with alpha_CO  
  > "assuming a ratio of 0.85 between the CO(2–1) and CO(1–0) luminosities ((cite: Carilli2013))" (3.4 The molecular gas masses from the CO(2-1) emission)
   - parameter: alpha_CO = 3.4 +/- 2  
  > "with $\alpha_{\text{CO}} = 3.4 \pm 2$" (3.4 The molecular gas masses from the CO(2-1) emission)
   - assumption: alpha_CO from z=1-3 DSFGs; r21 = 0.85; Lambda-CDM H0 = 70, Om = 0.3, OL = 0.7  
  > "the mean and standard deviation measured for a sample of $z=1-3$ DSFGs in (cite: Harrington2021)" (3.4 The molecular gas masses from the CO(2-1) emission); "$H_0 = 70 \text{km}\, \text{s}^{-1}\, \text{Mpc}^{-1}$, $\Omega_m = 0.3$ and $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.7$" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Caution given that alpha_CO ~ 0.9 for starbursts would give 5 times lower masses  
  > "would result in 5 times lower molecular gas masses" (3.4 The molecular gas masses from the CO(2-1) emission)
6. **Measure the CO(5-4)/CO(2-1) excitation ratio**: Ratio of the deblended CO(5-4) and CO(2-1) fluxes of North, compared with literature samples; South omitted because only upper limits  
  > "We measure a CO(5–4) to CO(2–1) line ratio of $F_{\mathrm{CO(5-4)}}/F_{\mathrm{CO(2-1)}} = 1.03 \pm 0.35$ for North." (3.5 The molecular ISM excitation conditions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Deblend and extract multi-band photometry for North and South**: PhoEBO deblending using radio and NIR high-resolution priors with PSF matching, followed by fixed-aperture photometry with Photutils; 0.15 mag added in quadrature to uncertainties  
  > "we perform a standard aperture photometry on the two galaxies with Photutils ((cite: Bradley2023))" (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting); "we add in quadrature a constant value of 0.15 mag to the uncertainties reported by PhoEBO before giving them in input to the SED-fitting code" (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting)
   - software: PhoEBO, Photutils
   - parameter: Aperture diameter 4'' fixed; background from a two arcsec-wide annulus  
  > "locally subtracting the background noise evaluated in a two arcsec-wide annulus around each source" (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **SED fitting for stellar mass, dust mass and SFR**: Separate MAGPHYS SED fits of North and South with the redshift fixed, including the ALMA Band 6 continuum; SFR cross-checked with VLA 3 GHz  
  > "we perform SED fitting with `MAGPHYS`, fixing the redshift to $z=1.17223$." (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting); "We include the ALMA band 6 dust-continuum data in the SED fits" (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting)
   - software: MAGPHYS
   - parameter: z fixed at 1.17223; Chabrier (2003) IMF  
  > "We use a (cite: Chabrier2003) stellar Initial Mass Function (IMF)." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: MAGPHYS best-fit chi2 of 0.71 (North) and 0.81 (South); priors not stated  
  > "best-fit models with $\chi^2 = 0.71$ and $\chi^2 = 0.81$ for North and South, respectively" (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting)
   - validation: SFR from VLA 3 GHz (Kennicutt & Evans 2012 eq. 12) consistent with SED SFR within errors  
  > "The results between the SFRs resulting from SED fitting and the ones based on the VLA data are thus consistent, within the errors." (3.6 The stellar mass, dust mass and star formation rate from SED fitting)
9. **Compare with main-sequence scaling relations (SFR, depletion time, gas fraction)**: Compare North and South SFR with the Speagle main sequence, and depletion time and gas fraction with the Tacconi 2018 relations at z=1.17  
  > "We also evaluate the depletion time, i.e., $t_{depl} = M_{H2}/$SFR, of the two galaxies according to (cite: Tacconi2018)" (4.2 The dusty galaxy merger in context of typical star-forming galaxies); "We determine the molecular gas fraction, i.e.," (4.2 The dusty galaxy merger in context of typical star-forming galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Only North is highly dust-obscured and bright in the VLA; South is the HST-visible galaxy  
  > "We find that only one of the two galaxies is highly dust-obscured" (abstract)
- North's CO(5-4)/CO(2-1) ratio is similar to main-sequence galaxies at similar redshift  
  > "Therefore, North exhibits ISM excitation conditions similar to MS galaxies at similar redshifts." (3.5 The molecular ISM excitation conditions)
- North forms stars about 3 times faster than main-sequence galaxies and is about 0.8 dex more efficient than South  
  > "Therefore, North is much more efficient at forming stars than South (difference of $\sim 0.8$ dex)" (4.2 The dusty galaxy merger in context of typical star-forming galaxies)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA data on a COSMOS target, where the merger was found serendipitously  
  > "we present the serendipitous detection of a system within the Cosmic Evolution Survey" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Simple one-beam deblending of CO used instead of PhoEBO because PhoEBO needs priors from stellar emission positions that may not align with gas and dust  
  > "because this code requires priors based on the position of the stellar emission, which we cannot assume to align with the molecular gas and dust." (3.3 The CO flux measurements)
- Alternative: PhoEBO not used for the CO deblending (needs stellar-position priors)  
  > "because this code requires priors based on the position of the stellar emission, which we cannot assume to align with the molecular gas and dust." (3.3 The CO flux measurements)
- Alternative: A starburst alpha_CO of about 0.9 would give 5 times lower gas masses (not adopted)  
  > "would result in 5 times lower molecular gas masses" (3.4 The molecular gas masses from the CO(2-1) emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Gas masses depend strongly on the adopted alpha_CO  
  > "We caution that the resulting molecular gas masses are highly dependent on the adopted $\alpha_{\text{CO}}$ value." (3.4 The molecular gas masses from the CO(2-1) emission)
- Limitation (authors): Deblending assumes all gas not on North belongs to South; gas between the galaxies may break this; limited resolution forces simplifying assumptions  
  > "There could be gas in between the two interacting galaxies making this assumption incorrect." (3.3 The CO flux measurements); "But given the limited resolution of the observations, we are forced to make such simplifying assumptions." (3.3 The CO flux measurements)
- Limitation (authors): South gas mass and related quantities are upper limits, so South could be forming stars even faster  
  > "therefore South could be forming stars even faster" (4.2 The dusty galaxy merger in context of typical star-forming galaxies)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Deblending of CO by a single-beam aperture on North with all remaining flux assigned to South is crude at 1.5 arcsec resolution; North flux may include South emission and vice versa
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(2-1) and CO(5-4) have different beams and the fluxes are not matched in resolution before taking the ratio
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Peak SNR of 6 for the CO lines makes the deblended fluxes and upper limits sensitive to the aperture choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO taken from a DSFG sample is applied to both components; gas masses and f_gas can shift by a factor of 5
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No dynamical evidence is quantified (velocity-peak offsets are qualitative), so the major-merger claim rests on position and mass ratios with large uncertainties (mu = 2 +3/-2)
