## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract dense structures across multi-scale continuum maps and measure their sizes and masses**: Run Astrodendro on Herschel 500 um, JCMT 850 um, ACA 1.3 mm, and combined ALMA 1.3 mm images to identify leaves; for elongated structures with axis ratio > 2 (ACA 1.3 mm main structure), fit three 2D Gaussians in CASA imfit using Spitzer 4.5 um as a reference; compute beam-deconvolved effective radii and optically thin dust masses.  
  > "Dense structures were extracted using the Astrodendro algorithm (Rosolowsky et al. 2008)." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS); "For elongated structures with a major-to-minor axis ratio > 2, we searched for sub-peaks within the structure’s area and then performed 2D Gaussian fitting using the CASA task imfit." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS); "Assuming the dust continuum emission is optically thin, the mass of each structure is estimated following (Hildebrand 1983):" (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS)
   - software: Astrodendro, CASA
   - parameter: min_value = 5 sigma, min_delta = 1 sigma, min_npix = 1 synthesized beam, major-to-minor axis ratio < 2  
  > "Three key parameters need to be set in Astrodendro: (1) min value, the minimum value included in the dendrogram, set to 5σ; (2) min delta, the threshold for differentiating leaves, set to 1σ; and (3) min npix, the minimum size considered as an independent leaf, set to one synthesized beam." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS); "To mitigate this issue, we required the dendro-identified structures to have a major-to-minor axis ratio < 2." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS)
   - assumption: Optically thin dust continuum emission; gas-to-dust ratio R_gd = 100; dust opacity kappa_nu = 0.9, 1.5, and 3.9 cm^2 g^-1 for 1.3 mm, 850 um, and 500 um; dust temperatures from Herschel (13-16 K); mean molecular weight mu = 2.37; distance D = 404 pc  
  > "The gas-to-dust mass ratio Rgd is assumed to be 100 in this work. κν is set to 0.9, 1.5, and 3.9 cm2g−1 for ALMA (ACA) 1.3 mm, JCMT 850 µm, and Herschel 500 µm, respectively" (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS); "Particle number density n and mass density ρ are related as ρ=µmHn, where µ=2.37." (Table A1)

  > "Only one elongated structure was extracted, corresponding to the main emission structure in the ACA 1.3 mm image. Using the Spitzer 4.5 µm emission as a reference, three 2D Gaussians were fitted to this elongated structure." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS)
   - validation: Varied min_value from 4 sigma to 6 sigma in Astrodendro and performed visual checks

## Qualitative findings

- The filament G205.46-14.56 undergoes hierarchical fragmentation governed primarily by thermal Jeans instability.  
  > "The filament exhibits a hierarchical fragmentation process primarily governed by thermal Jeans instability." (abstract)
- The central region of G205.46-14.56-N2 hosts a mirror-symmetric twin binary protostellar system (N2-M1, N2-M2, N2-W1, N2-W2) with aligned collimated outflows.  
  > "The central region of the dense core G205.46-14.56-N2 hosts a remarkable mirror-symmetric twin binary protostellar system. We detect well-collimated, aligned outflows from all four protostars." (abstract)
- The redshifted lobe of N2-M1 shows a prominent jet-like structure threaded by a linear chain of knots.  
  > "In contrast, the redshifted lobe of N2-M1 shows prominent jet-like structure threaded by a linear chain of knots (Fig. 3), a feature that was not identified in the earlier analysis of Reipurth et al. (2023)." (3.2. The central twin binary system in the G205.46-14.56-N2 core)

  > "The twin binary components are connected by a prominent bar-like or bridge-like elongated gaseous structure (hereafter referred to as “the bar”), clearly detected in both 1.3 mm continuum emission and molecular C18O and H2CO line emission (Fig. 4)." The central twin binary system in the G205.46-14.56-N2 core); "These streamer-like substructures likely trace gas accretion flows toward the disks, as also observed in numerical simulations (see Fig. 4b; Mignon-Risse et al. 2023)." ## Reasoning

- Why this data: G205.46-14.56-N2 hosts five embedded protostars with resolved disks and jets, and higher-resolution ALMA CO (2-1) data (twice the angular resolution of Reipurth et al. 2023) plus multi-scale continuum/line data allow a detailed investigation of its outflows and formation mechanism.  
  > "In contrast, all five embedded members of G205.46-14.56-N2 have already formed protostars (Reipurth et al. 2023), making this multiple system a uniquely valuable laboratory for investigating the formation and early dynamical evolution of compact protostellar clusters." (1. INTRODUCTION); "The ALMA CO (2-1) data used in this work have twice the angular resolution of those used in Reipurth et al. (2023), enabling a more detailed investigation of the molecular outflows." (C. MOLECULAR OUTFLOWS)
- Why this method: Used deconvolved effective radius instead of Astrodendro rms radius because rms radius is smaller than sub-condensation separations; used CASA imfit for elongated structures with axis ratio > 2 because Astrodendro can fail in crowded environments; stacked three H2CO J=3-2 transitions to enhance sensitivity.  
  > "Astrodendro may fail to identify leaves in crowded environments (Kong et al. 2021). To mitigate this issue, we required the dendro-identified structures to have a major-to-minor axis ratio < 2." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS); "After deconvolution, Rdendro is generally smaller than the separations between embedded smaller condensations in our cases; therefore, it is not suitable for fragmentation analysis." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS); "To enhance sensitivity, we stacked data from three H2CO J=3-2 line transitions." (D. DIFFERENTIAL ROTATION)
- Alternative: Turbulent fragmentation paradigm rejected as the primary mechanism for the central quadruple system because it predicts stochastic angular momentum distribution and fragmentation outcomes, contrary to the observed ordered mirror-symmetric twin binaries and aligned outflows.  
  > "Such ordered, symmetric fragmentation and their aligned collimated outflows are inconsistent with predictions from the turbulent fragmentation paradigm, in which angular momentum distribution and fragmentation outcomes are expected to be stochastic (Offner et al. 2010; Lee et al. 2017)." (3.2. The central twin binary system in the G205.46-14.56-N2 core)
- Alternative: Non-ideal radiation-MHD simulation outcomes rejected/disfavored because their morphology differs substantially from the observed quadruple system.  
  > "Notably, the observed central quadruple protostellar system (Fig. 4a) shows striking morphological similarity to the multiple systems formed in pure hydrodynamical simulations (Boss 1991; Mignon-Risse et al. 2023) but differs substantially from the outcomes of non-ideal radiation-MHD models (Mignon-Risse et al. 2023)." (4. DISCUSSIONS AND SUMMARY)
- Alternative: Astrodendro rms radius (R_dendro) rejected for fragmentation analysis because after deconvolution it is smaller than the separations between embedded smaller condensations.  
  > "After deconvolution, Rdendro is generally smaller than the separations between embedded smaller condensations in our cases; therefore, it is not suitable for fragmentation analysis." (A. FRAGMENTATION ANALYSIS)
