## Qualitative findings

- MQN01-QC is a rotationally supported cold disk with no strong evidence of non-circular motions.  
  > "the kinematics of the MQN01-QC is broadly reproduced by the rotating thin-disk model with no strong evidence of non-circular motions." (Kinematical parameter derivation)
- Quasar host velocity field is irregular; rotation cannot be established securely.  
  > "The velocity field of the quasar host appears irregular." (CO(4–3) line flux distributions and velocity fields); "with the current data it is difficult to determine with certainty if the observed gradient is a signature of rotation." (CO(4–3) line flux distributions and velocity fields)
- MQN01-QC lies above the star-forming main sequence, suggesting a dust-obscured starburst.  
  > "place the MQN01-QC above to the star-forming main sequence at $z\approx3$ (cite: Popesso+2023), thus suggesting that the galaxy is in a dust-obscured starburst phase" (Stellar mass estimation of MQN01-QC from JWST/NIRCam images)
- Quasar CO(9–8) lacks a blueshifted wing like that in CO(4–3), implying different physical conditions or excitation.  
  > "the CO(9–8) line of the quasar host lacks of similar blushifted wing as observed in the CO(4–3) line profile" (Flux and luminosity measurements from ALMA data); "the latter component is associated to molecular gas exposed to different physical conditions and excitation mechanisms" (Flux and luminosity measurements from ALMA data)
- The quasar may be a satellite in an early-stage merger; tidal stripping and an outflow remain alternative explanations of the broad component.  
  > "suggests that the quasar host may be a satellite galaxy in the early stages of a merger." (Summary and Conclusions); "we cannot rule out any of the two alternative scenarios (i.e., merger or outflow) with the available information." (Origin of the blueshifted emission of the quasar host galaxy)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Higher-resolution band 3 observations separate the previously blended quasar host and companion for spatially resolved analysis.  
  > "caused the quasar host and its companion (previously dubbed as Object B in Paper I) to appear blended, preventing spatially resolved analysis." (Introduction); "ALMA band 3 single-pointing observation targeting the CO(4–3) line at a much higher resolution" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting maximizes surface-brightness sensitivity; CANNUBI constrains geometry before kinematics because S/N and resolution limit simultaneous fitting; PSF-convolved models address beam smearing.  
  > "adopting a “natural” weighting scheme of the visibilities to maximize the surface brightness sensitivity per beam." (Reduction and imaging of ALMA data); "the limited S/N and angular resolution of the current data hamper a simultaneous determination of all the free parameters." (Rotating disk modeling of the MQN01-QC); "This accounts for the beam smearing effect" (Rotating disk modeling of the MQN01-QC)
- Alternative: Single-Gaussian CO(4–3) fits are inadequate; use double Gaussians.  
  > "The observed CO(4–3) line profiles of our sources cannot be adequately reproduced with a single Gaussian component." (Flux and luminosity measurements from ALMA data)
- Alternative: Companion's two-clump CO morphology may trace spiral structure, central gas depletion or optically thick central line emission.  
  > "such as, e.g., spiral arms." (CO(4–3) line flux distributions and velocity fields); "could result from significant molecular gas depletion or optically thick line emission in the galaxy central region compared to its outskirts." (CO(4–3) line flux distributions and velocity fields)
- Alternative: Fit dust SEDs with both 35±5 K and 45±5 K temperature priors.  
  > "two different Gaussian priors for the $T_{\rm dust}$" (Molecular gas mass and dust characteristics); "$35\,{\rm K}$" (Molecular gas mass and dust characteristics); "$45\,{\rm K}$" (Molecular gas mass and dust characteristics)
- Alternative: Quasar mass bracketed by dispersion-dominated and rotation-dominated cases.  
  > "under the assumption of dispersion-dominated system" (Dynamical and virial mass estimates of galaxies); "the bulk of the emitting gas is dominated by regular rotation" (Dynamical and virial mass estimates of galaxies)
- Alternative: Tidal stripping and molecular outflow remain viable; gas inflow from satellites, filaments or circumgalactic medium is also possible but difficult to test.  
  > "we cannot rule out any of the two alternative scenarios (i.e., merger or outflow) with the available information." (Origin of the blueshifted emission of the quasar host galaxy); "a molecular gas inflow from a low-mass satellite galaxy, or along one or more cosmic web filaments, or from the circumgalactic medium (cite: Emonts+2023), would potentially explain the observed blueshifted CO(4–3) emission." (Origin of the blueshifted emission of the quasar host galaxy)
- How they decided it was enough: Iterate spectral extraction until convergence; cleaning masks cover observable emission; three annuli cover the line-emitting region.  
  > "we iterated the procedure until convergence." (Flux and luminosity measurements from ALMA data); "These were sufficient to encompass the observable emission of both the sources." (Reduction and imaging of ALMA data); "These are sufficient to cover the entire emitting line region" (Geometrical parameter derivation)
- Limitation (authors): Gas-mass uncertainties omit conversion-factor systematics.  
  > "the reported uncertainties on molecular gas masses do not take into account systematic uncertainties on the assumed conversion factors." (Molecular gas mass and dust characteristics)
- Limitation (authors): No continuum measurements near the SED peak; temperature weakly constrained; AGN FIR contribution could overestimate SFR.  
  > "Due to the lack of continuum measurements near to the peak of the dust SED, we are able to put only shallow constraints on the dust temperature." (Molecular gas mass and dust characteristics); "any possible contribution from the central AGN to the measured FIR luminosity would result in an overestimation of the ${\rm SFR}_{\rm IR}$." (Molecular gas mass and dust characteristics)
- Limitation (authors): Stellar mass has quasar-subtraction and limited-filter systematics; quoted aperture-flux errors exclude potential systematic errors.  
  > "considering the photometric systematic uncertainties originating from the quasar light subtraction and the lack of flux measurements from other filters." (Stellar mass estimation of MQN01-QC from JWST/NIRCam images); "do not take into account any potential systematic errors." (Stellar mass estimation of MQN01-QC from JWST/NIRCam images)
- Limitation (authors): Ring parameters are correlated; kinematic uncertainties omit geometry uncertainty and are underestimated.  
  > "the parameters derived for each ring are not fully independent." (Table 2); "The reported errors on kinematical quantities do not take into account that on disk geometry and are therefore underestimated." (Table 2)
