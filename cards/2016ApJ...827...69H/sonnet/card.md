## Analysis recipe

1. **Search spectra for maser emission**: Inspect continuum-subtracted cubes within ~18 arcsec field for features above 5 sigma  
  > "We searched for maser emission in each AGN within a field of view of $\sim$ 18$''$, centered on the phase-tracking center of each source" (4 Results)
   - software: CASA, AIPS
   - parameter: detection threshold 5 sigma  
  > "We set the detection threshold at the 5$\sigma$ level" (3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure maser and continuum positions**: Gaussian fits of phase-referenced peak positions; compare maser with continuum peak with error beam/(2 SNR)  
  > "the Gaussian-fitted phase-referenced position of the V$_{\rm LSR}$= 718.0 km s$^{-1}\;$peak emission is" (4 Results)
   - fitting: Gaussian  
  > "Based on the ALMA data, the Gaussian-fitted phase-referenced position of the V$_{\rm LSR}$= 718.0 km s$^{-1}\;$peak emission is" (RESULTS)
   - validation: Compared with 22 GHz position  
  > "coincides with those of the 321 GHz maser within uncertainties." (4 Results)
3. **Estimate radius of high-velocity feature**: Keplerian v ∝ r^-0.5 scaling from 22 GHz disk model  
  > "based on the Keplerian ($v$ $\propto$ $r^{-0.5}$) rotating disk model in (cite: linc97)" (6.3 High-Velocity Dense Gas)
   - assumption: BH mass 1.4e6 Msun, edge-on disk  
  > "estimated the central binding mass in NGC 4945, by assuming the rotation of gases on a Keplerian disk of 150 km s$^{-1}\;$at 0.3 pc from the central engine and a disk inclination of 90$\ensuremath{^\circ}$" (DISCUSSION – High-Velocity Dense Gas in NGC 4945)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Only NGC 4945 (and Circinus earlier) showed >5 sigma maser emission; NGC 1068, NGC 1386, NGC 5793 not detected  
  > "The only emission exceeding a signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) of 5 is seen in NGC 4945" (4 Results)
