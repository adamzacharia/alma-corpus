## Analysis recipe

1. **Image [CII], CO(10-9) and continuum**: Calibrate in CASA, CLEAN with natural weighting, optional uv-taper; line cubes binned; continuum from line-free channels  
  > "were then combined, imaged, and deconvolved using the CLEAN algorithm with natural weighting" (2.4 ALMA [CII])
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: taper 500 klambda; bins 25, 150, 300 km/s  
  > "We also imaged the visibilities with $uv$-tapering applied at 500 k$\lambda$ (311.5 m)" (2.4 ALMA [CII])
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux scale checked against phase calibrator across tracks  
  > "The flux scale was also verified by comparing the calibrated amplitudes of the same phase calibrator across the two tracks." (2.4 ALMA [CII])
2. **Fit line profiles and sizes**: Single and double Gaussians to spectra; 2D Gaussians to moment-0 maps  
  > "We fit a 2D Gaussian to the tapered intensity map of X-Main" (3.2 [CII] Line Emission)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits  
  > "We fit single Gaussian profiles to the line spectra, as shown in Figure [1](#fig:spectra)." (Results: CO Line Emission and Redshift Identification)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **SED modelling for L_IR, T_d, dust mass, SFR, M***: MBB fits with mbb_emcee and full SED with magphys  
  > "using the code mbb_emcee (cite: Riechers13a; Dowell14a)" (4.1 Spectral Energy Distribution Modeling)
   - software: mbb_emcee, MAGPHYS
   - parameter: uniform priors T_d>1 K, beta in [0.1,20], lambda0 in [1,400] um, alpha in [0.1,20]; extra 15% flux uncertainty  
  > "We impose uniform priors such that $T_d$ $>$ 1 K, $\beta$ $\in$ \[0.1, 20.0\]" (4.1.1)
   - assumption: kappa = 2.64 m^2/kg at 125 um; Chabrier IMF  
  > "we assume an absorption mass coefficient of $\kappa$ = 2.64 m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ at $\lambda$ = 125.0 $\ensuremath{\mu}m$ (cite: Dunne03a)" (4.1.1)
   - fitting: MCMC posterior means and 68th percentiles  
  > "We adopt the statistical means and 68$^{\rm th}$ percentiles of the resulting posterior probability distributions as the “best-fit” parameters." (4.1.1)
   - validation: Refit excluding the 160 um outlier  
  > "we re-model the SED excluding this outlier" (4.1.2)
4. **Disk kinematics: harmonic decomposition and tilted-ring**: K5/B1 harmonic test; tilted rings with rotcur iteratively; arctangent fit to RC  
  > "we fit tilted-ring models (cite: Begeman89a) to the observed velocity field using the task rotcur provided in the gipsy software package" (4.2.1)
   - software: GIPSY rotcur, velfi
   - parameter: v_exp = 0; ring width = beam, then half beam; Rt limited to 0-25 kpc  
  > "We limit the turnover radius to 0$<$ $R_{t}$ $<$25 kpc" (4.2.1)
   - assumption: circular thin disk, zero expansion velocity  
  > "by setting the expansion velocity to 0 km s$^{-1}$" (4.2.1)
   - fitting: least squares; Levenberg-Marquardt for arctangent  
  > "We perform non-linear least-squares fitting using the Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm" (4.2.1)
   - validation: Residual velocity map after subtracting beam-convolved model  
  > "A residual image (Figure [11](#fig:gipsy)) is obtained by subtracting the model (after convolving with the beam) from the data." (4.2.1)
5. **Envelope-tracing rotation velocity and dynamical mass**: Gauss-Hermite h3 fits to PV spectra; terminal velocity at 8% flux; M_dyn = v^2 R/G  
  > "The rotation curve (traced by the “envelope”) is derived from the terminal velocity ($v_t^{\rm obs}$) at which 8% of the total flux under the fitted curve is outside $v_t^{\rm obs}$." (4.2.2)
   - parameter: sigma_v = 75 km/s subtracted; inclination 41.3 deg  
  > "Here, we adopt the observed velocity dispersion of $\sigma_v$ = 75 km s$^{-1}$ as the subtracted term." (4.2.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with arctangent result  
  > "which is consistent with the rotation velocity of $v_{\rm rot}$ = 537 $\pm$ 83 km s$^{-1}$ derived from the arctangent model within the uncertainties." (4.2.2)
6. **PDR modelling**: Chi-square against PDR Toolbox grids of line ratios  
  > "The best-fit model is determined based on the global minimum $\chi^2$, corresponding to $\log\,n$ = 4.5 cm$^{-3}$ and $\log\,G_0$ = 2.25." (4.3 PDR modeling)
   - software: PDR Toolbox
   - assumption: 30% of [CII] from non-PDR; CO intensities multiplied by 2  
  > "we adopt a canonical value of 30% to account for this non-PDR contribution" (4.3 PDR modeling)
   - fitting: chi^2 minimum  
  > "The best-fit model is determined based on the global minimum $\chi^2$, corresponding to $\log\,n$ = 4.5 cm$^{-3}$ and $\log\,G_0$ = 2.25." (PDR modeling)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Gas mass from CO(1-0)**: CO(1-0) luminosity times alpha_CO  
  > "assuming a CO luminosity-to-H$_2$ mass conversion factor of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ = 0.8 $M_{\odot}~($K  km  pc$^2)^{-1}$(cite: Downes98a)" (5.2.2)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8  
  > "assuming a CO luminosity-to-H$_2$ mass conversion factor of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ = 0.8 $M_{\odot}~($K  km  pc$^2)^{-1}$(cite: Downes98a)" (Gas Mass, Gas-to-Dust Ratio, and Metallicity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Resolved [CII]/FIR relations**: Pixel-level power-law fits on maps clipped at 3 sigma  
  > "we clipped both the \[CII\] and the 635 $\ensuremath{\mu}m$ continuum maps at 3$\sigma$" (5.9)
   - fitting: power-law fits  
  > "We fit power-laws of the forms $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}$ = A$\Sigma_{\rm [C{\scriptsize II}]}^N$ and $L_{\rm [C{\scriptsize II}]}/L_{\rm FIR}$ = A$\Sigma_{\rm FIR}^N$ to our data for HXMM05" (Spatially Resolved $L_{\rm [CII]}$/$L_{\rm FIR}$ Map and Star Formation)
   - validation: Test with 1 sigma clipping  
  > "We also test our results with less clipping (at 1$\sigma$)" (footnote 20)

## Qualitative findings

- [CII] shows a monotonic velocity gradient consistent with a rotating disk; K5/B1 is insignificant  
  > "We thus interpret HXMM05 to be a rotating disk for the remainder of this paper." (4.2.1)
- Two compact dust nuclei embedded in extended dust; gas and dust co-spatial  
  > "Two dust peaks, separated by 2.4 kpc, are detected at high significance." (3.4 Continuum)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Tilted-ring parameters fixed iteratively because v_rot and i are degenerate and data S/N is limited  
  > "We adopted this approach because each ring would have six free parameters otherwise" (4.2.1)
- Alternative: Low-G0 PDR solution rejected as it would need an unphysically large emitting region  
  > "We reject this low $G_0$ solution since it would require a physically enormous emitting region" (4.3 PDR modeling)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Stellar mass accurate only to ≲0.5 dex; possible AGN contamination  
  > "the $M_*$ estimate is accurate to only $\lesssim$0.5 dex" (footnote 13)
- Limitation (authors): Outer rotation curve points limited by S/N  
  > "this trend is likely an artifact due to the limited S/N at those PV-positions" (4.2.1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single alpha_CO=0.8 drives gas mass and fractions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Tilted-ring with fixed parameters and low resolution leaves model-dependent v_rot
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar mass uncertain and possibly overestimated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(5-4) PdBI data have strong sidelobes
