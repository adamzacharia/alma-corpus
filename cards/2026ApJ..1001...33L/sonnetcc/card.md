## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate archival 12m and 07m CO(2-1) data**: Run observatory pipeline scripts on archival MSs; check bandpass/gain amplitude and phase flatness and 12m vs 07m amplitude-uv consistency; fix flux scale for NGC 0628  
  > "To calibrate the archival measurement sets (MSs), we ran the distributed pipeline scripts from the observatory, either generated for the PI, or a generic pipeline script." (Calibration); "The consistency between the 12m and 07m calibrated data was checked by looking at the galaxy amplitude plotted against uv distance." (Calibration)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: CASA pipeline v.4.7.2, v.5.1.2 or v.5.4.1 depending on project  
  > "Different versions (v.4.7.2, v.5.1.2, and v.5.4.1) of the CASA pipeline were used across the galaxy sample." (Calibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Calibration judged successful if calibrator amplitudes were flat/constant vs channel and time and phases concentrated about 0 degrees  
  > "We considered the calibration successful if their amplitudes were flat and constant as functions of channel and time" (Calibration)
2. **Extract the CO(2-1) SPW and regrid to common velocity grid**: CASA split and mstransform to isolate the narrowband SPW and regrid to 5 km/s channels; convert to MIRIAD format  
  > "we used the CASA tasks *split* and *mstransform* to split the narrowband spectral window (SPW) containing the CO(2-1) emission out from each 12m and 07m MS" (Imaging); "These were converted into MIRIAD format." (Imaging)
   - software: CASA, MIRIAD
   - parameter: Channel width 5 km/s  
  > "regrid them to a common velocity grid with channel width 5 km/s" (Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Image and clean CO(2-1) 12m+07m cubes in MIRIAD**: invert with robust=+2, cell 0.25 arcsec; mossdi2 clean in two steps using CO(1-0)-based SN masks, then clean residual without mask; for CO(1-0) first image without a mask, then build mask from that cube  
  > "the dirty CO(2-1) cubes were cleaned in two steps: \[1\] within the mask and then \[2\] without a mask but cleaning the residual from \[1\]" (Imaging); "We went through the full imaging process without any mask and generated a 12m+07m+TP cube in CO(1-0)." (CO(1-0))
   - software: MIRIAD
   - parameter: robust = +2, cellsize = 0.25 arcsec, clean gain 0.05, options=positive, 10^7 components per channel  
  > "We used robust = +2 (approximately) corresponding to natural weighting)" (Imaging); "We set a gain parameter of 0.05, options=positive" (Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residual emission within mask after two-step cleaning about 0.1 sigma  
  > "The residual emission within the mask after the two-step cleaning were about $\sim$0.1$\sigma$." (Imaging)
4. **Combine with total-power data and measure rms**: Regrid TP cube to the 12m+07m grid on common velocity channels and combine with immerge; rms per 5 km/s channel measured in emission-free channels of the dirty 12m+07m cube combined with TP  
  > "The cleaned 12m+07m data cubes were combined with the TP data cubes using the immerge task in MIRIAD." (Imaging); "The RMS is measured in emission-free channels." (Imaging)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Smooth to common beam and build integrated intensity maps**: Smooth CO(1-0) and CO(2-1) cubes to 2.5 arcsec, regrid CO(2-1) onto CO(1-0), mask using CO(1-0) SN cube (>=80 pixels above 5 sigma, expanded spatially by 2.5 arcsec, not in velocity), integrate along velocity and make error maps  
  > "The mask was based on the (cleaned) CO(1-0) signal-to-noise (SN) cube starting from volumes that have at least 80 pixels with $>$ 5.0$\sigma$." (Maps); "The mask was not expanded in velocity." (Maps); "corresponding error maps were generated" (Maps)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - parameter: Common resolution 2.5 arcsec; mask expansion 2.5 arcsec (10 pixels)  
  > "This mask was then expanded spatially by 2.5$\ensuremath{''}$ (=10 pixels in length) to include potential diffuse extended emission (if it exists)." (Maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Compute R21 maps and classify gas**: R21 = I_CO(2-1)/I_CO(1-0) per pixel with SN>5 in both lines; classify LRG (<0.7), HRG (0.7-1.0), VHRG (>1.0); compute area-weighted and I_CO(1-0)-weighted means and fractions per galaxy and for central 1 and 2 kpc  
  > "We applied a signal-to-noise (SN) cut of $>$5 in both CO(1-0) and CO(2-1)." (Results); "Low-ratio gas (LRG) have $R_{21} < 0.7$ (blue), high-ratio gas (HRG) are in between $0.7 < R_{21} < 1.0$ (red), and very-high-ratio gas (VHRG) have $R_{21} > 1.0$." (R21 Maps and Distributions)
   - parameter: SN cut >5 in both lines; R21 thresholds 0.7 and 1.0  
  > "We applied a signal-to-noise (SN) cut of $>$5 in both CO(1-0) and CO(2-1)." (Results)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.35 for converting I_CO(1-0) to H2 surface density (Table 1, Table 2)  
  > "If we simply assume the Milky-Way CO-to-H$_{2}$ conversion factor of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ = 4.35" (Classification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Compare R21 across galactic structures**: Define masks for center (1 or 2 kpc), bar, bar ends (2 kpc circles at ellipse tips), spiral arms and interarm; compare adjacent structure pairs with two-sample KS tests on every fifth pixel, per galaxy and combined  
  > "The bar ends are 2 kpc circles centered on the “tips” of the ellipse." (R21 by Structure); "We applied this test including only every fifth pixel (Nyquist sampling)" (R21 by Structure)
   - software: scipy
   - parameter: Central region 1 kpc for unbarred and 2 kpc for barred galaxies except NGC 3351 and NGC 3627  
  > "We adopt 2 kpc for all barred galaxies except for NGC3351 and NGC3627." (R21 by Structure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Null hypothesis that two samples share a parent distribution tested; also checked with full-beam sampling  
  > "The null hypothesis is that the two samples originate from the same parent distribution." (R21 by Structure)

## Qualitative findings

- R21 is high in the center, low along the bar, higher at bar ends, and fluctuates in spiral arms for barred galaxies; unbarred galaxies resemble the outer parts of barred ones  
  > "The radial distributions of the unbarred galaxies fluctuate in $R_{21}$ and are similar to the outer parts (spiral arms) of the barred galaxies." (Summary)
- NGC 4826 is the only unbarred galaxy with a central gas condensation and high R21  
  > "NGC 4826 is the only unbarred spiral galaxy in our sample that shows the central condensation and high $R_{21}$." (Summary)
- Interarm emission is poorly detected, and when detected appears low-ratio  
  > "but when it is detected, it appears to have low-ratios." (Systematic Trends and Comparison to Literature)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: A survey of R21 at high resolution with well-calibrated CO(1-0) and CO(2-1) from the same telescope was needed  
  > "it is clear that a survey of $R_{21}$ in nearby galaxies at high-resolution and with well-calibrated data, is needed" (Introduction)
- Why this method: MIRIAD imaging chosen over CASA because it accounts for spatial variations in the PSF  
  > "because it takes into account spatial variations in the point spread function (PSF)" (Imaging)
- Alternative: Querejeta et al. bar-end definitions rejected because they are not identified for short bars; custom definitions adopted  
  > "This does not assure a consistent analysis across the galaxy sample, and thus, we adopted the above definitions." (R21 by Structure)
- How they decided it was enough: Cleaning accepted when residual within mask reached ~0.1 sigma  
  > "The residual emission within the mask after the two-step cleaning were about $\sim$0.1$\sigma$." (Imaging)
- Limitation (authors): Sensitivity limits detection of interarm regions  
  > "due to the sensitivity limitations, most interarm regions of the galaxies are not readily detected" (Structural Dependence of R21)
- Limitation (authors): alpha_CO may vary especially in galaxy centers  
  > "has the caveat that $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ may vary especially in the galaxy centers" (Classification)
- Limitation (authors): Bar-end explanation is a hypothesis awaiting verification  
  > "This is, of course, a hypothesis and needs to be verified by future observational and theoretical studies." (Bar Ends: High R21)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Equal flux SN>5 cut in both lines biases low-R21 regions against CO(2-1) detection.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Structure masks (bar, bar ends, arms) are rough and drawn from external catalogs, so KS results depend on definitions.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): KS tests on neighbouring structure pairs ignore correlated pixels beyond a every-fifth-pixel thinning.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO(1-0) data reduction deferred to an unpublished paper.
