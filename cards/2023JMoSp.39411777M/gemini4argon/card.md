## Analysis recipe

1. **Calculate initial scaled quantum-chemical spectroscopic parameters**: Perform B3LYP/aug-cc-pVTZ density functional calculations for c-CD2CH2O, c-C2H3DO, and c-C2H4O, and scale the c-CD2CH2O parameters using experimental-to-calculated ratios of the other two isotopologs.  
  > "We performed B3LYP hybrid density functional calculations (cite: Becke_1993; LYP_1988), utilizing the aug-cc-pVTZ (abbreviated aVTZ) basis set (cite: cc-pVXZ_1989) to evaluate ground state rotational and quartic and sextic equilibrium distortion parameters" (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters); "The results of the last two calculations and the corresponding experimental values of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO (cite: c-C2H3DO_rot_2023) and $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O (cite: c-C2H4O_rot_2022) were taken to estimate correction factors" (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
   - software: Gaussian 09
   - parameter: B3LYP/aug-cc-pVTZ basis set, S reduction, III^l representation  
  > "utilizing the aug-cc-pVTZ (abbreviated aVTZ) basis set (cite: cc-pVXZ_1989) to evaluate ground state rotational and quartic and sextic equilibrium distortion parameters of $c$-CD$_2$CH$_2$O, $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO, and $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O in the S reduction and in the III$^l$ representation." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared scaled and unscaled B3LYP parameters against final experimental fit parameters.  
  > "The rotational and quartic centrifugal distortion parameters from a quantum-chemical calculation agree quite well after scaling with the experimental ones, as can be seen in Table [\[tab_para_S-red\]](#tab_para_S-red)" (Discussion of the laboratory results)
2. **Fit laboratory rotational spectrum and predict rest frequencies**: Iteratively assign and fit 1289 laboratory transition frequencies (1734 transitions) across 120-181 GHz and 490-1094 GHz to Watson's S and A reduction Hamiltonians in III^l and I^r representations, then calculate catalog rest frequencies.  
  > "We used Pickett’s SPFIT and SPCAT programs (cite: spfit_1991) to fit and to calculate transition frequencies of oxirane-*2,2*-$d_2$." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters); "These data add up to 1289 different transition frequencies and 1734 transitions. The $J$ quantum numbers range from 2 to 58 with $K_a \le 42$ and $K_c \le 53$." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
   - software: SPFIT, SPCAT
   - parameter: Spectroscopic parameters up to eighth order; dipole moment components mu_a = 0.50 D, mu_b = 1.81 D  
  > "The molecular symmetry is $C_{\rm s}$, and it has a calculated $a$-dipole moment component of 0.50 D and a $b$-component of 1.81 D." (Spectroscopic properties of oxirane); "These were fit with a full set of distortion parameters up to eighth order with all of them significantly determined, albeit $L_J$ just barely." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
   - assumption: Line uncertainties from 2 kHz to 50 kHz based on line shape and S/N  
  > "We assigned 5 kHz to strong, isolated, and very symmetric lines up to 50 kHz for weak and somewhat asymmetric lines or lines fairly close to other lines." (Laboratory spectroscopic details)
   - fitting: Weighted least-squares fit in SPFIT yielding wrms = 0.794 (S reduction) and rms ~ 12 kHz.  
  > "We also mention that the rms values for the four fits are all very close to 12 kHz." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters); "| wrms | 0 | .794 | | | | | | 0 | .794 |" (Table 1)
   - validation: Compared fits across S and A reductions and III^l and I^r representations, and checked wrms of individual assignment rounds (0.64 to 0.97).  
  > "We also determined the weighted standard deviations of the lines from each assignment round. These values were between 0.64 and 0.97, mostly closer to the value of the entire fit." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
3. **Fit LTE synthetic spectra to ALMA PILS data and derive column density and deuteration ratios**: Calculate optically thin LTE synthetic spectra of c-CD2CH2O using CDMS catalog predictions and fit to the ALMA PILS spectrum at a position offset by one beam from IRAS 16293-2422 B to constrain column density and compare with c-C2H3DO and c-C2H4O.  
  > "We performed the search by fitting synthetic spectra for $c$-CD$_2$CH$_2$O to the ALMA data. The synthetic spectra are calculated under the assumption of the emission being optically thin and the excitation being in local thermodynamical equilibrium." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B); "Another test of the possible assignment can be taken from the derived column density from the tentative lines: the line strengths of those imply a column density of $3.3\times 10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
   - parameter: T_rot = 125 K, FWHM linewidth = 1 km s^-1, v_LSR = 2.6 km s^-1  
  > "We assumed $T_{\rm rot} = 125$ K in our initial calculations as was determined for $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O (cite: PILS_COMs_2017) and employed a linewidth (FWHM) of 1 km s$^{-1}$ and a velocity offset relative to the local standard of rest of 2.6 km s$^{-1}$" (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission and local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE)  
  > "The synthetic spectra are calculated under the assumption of the emission being optically thin and the excitation being in local thermodynamical equilibrium." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
   - assumption: Statistical factor of 2 ways to form c-CD2CH2O compared to 1 way for c-C2H4O  
  > "Considering that there are statistically two ways to form $c$-CD$_2$CH$_2$O, but only one for $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O, this gives a D/H ratio of $\sim$16% per H atom." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
   - fitting: Synthetic spectrum fitting constraining maximum column density from tentative emission lines  
  > "The transitions, their frequencies, energies of their upper levels above the ground state and predicted strengths assuming the maximum possible column density are summarized in Table [2](#table:obslines)." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
   - validation: Checked that no lines are predicted to be significant where no emission is observed in the ALMA spectrum, and compared D/H enhancement with other doubly deuterated COMs.  
  > "Importantly, if the column density is constrained based on these transitions, no lines are predicted to be significant where no observed emission is seen $-$ an important criterion for the assignment of species in astronomical spectra." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)

## Qualitative findings

- Tentative identification of a handful of emission lines of c-CD2CH2O toward IRAS 16293-2422 B in the ALMA PILS survey.  
  > "Using PILS data of IRAS 16293$-$2422, we tentatively identified a handful of lines of this isotopolog toward source B: with a column density derived on basis of these lines, no other non-detected features are predicted." (Conclusion)
- Watson's A reduction in the oblate representation III^l yields a fit of essentially identical quality to the S reduction for c-CD2CH2O, in contrast to the main isotopolog c-C2H4O.  
  > "It is noteworthy that the A reduction in the III$^l$ representation yields a fit of essentially identical quality with the same number of spectroscopic parameters." (Discussion of the laboratory results)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The degree of deuteration and line intensities of c-C2H3DO in PILS were high enough to make detecting doubly deuterated oxirane plausible; the one-beam offset position from Source B has narrow lines (~1 km s^-1) and limited continuum opacity.  
  > "The degree of deuteration in $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO and its line intensities were sufficiently high in the PILS data that it appeared plausible to detect doubly deuterated oxirane as well (cite: c-C2H3DO_rot_2023)." (Introduction); "This position is ideal because of the narrow lines seen there ($\sim$1 km s$^{-1}$), which allows for searches for rare species and limited continuum opacity causing absorption to be less severe." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
- Why this method: Optically thin LTE synthetic spectra are valid because of the high densities in the molecular cloud surrounding the protostar probed by the ALMA observations.  
  > "The synthetic spectra are calculated under the assumption of the emission being optically thin and the excitation being in local thermodynamical equilibrium. This is a valid assumption given the high densities in the molecular cloud surrounding the protostar" (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
- Alternative: Evaluated both Watson's S and A reductions in both III^l and I^r representations to compare Hamiltonian convergence.  
  > "After having determined the final line list and the final set of spectroscopic parameters in the S reduction and the III$^l$ representation, we also evaluated parameters applying the A reduction and the S and A reductions in the I$^r$ representation." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
- Alternative: Omitted weak a-type transitions of oblate paired transitions from the SPFIT line list because they would only inflate the list without affecting parameter values or uncertainties.  
  > "Adding the weaker $a$-type transitions to the list would only inflate the line list, but would have no effect on the parameter values or their uncertainties." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
- How they decided it was enough: In the laboratory spectrum, 1289 transition frequencies up to J=58 determined a full eighth-order parameter set accurate enough for all radio astronomical observations; in ALMA data, only a handful of lines matched, so the detection was deemed tentative.  
  > "The $c$-CD$_2$CH$_2$O parameter set in the S reduction and the III$^l$ representation is very similar in extent and absolute uncertainties to those of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO (cite: c-C2H3DO_rot_2023) and $c$-C$_2$H$_4$O (cite: c-C2H4O_rot_2022). Therefore, the data should be sufficiently accurate for all radio astronomical observations." (Discussion of the laboratory results); "Still, our astronomical results on $c$-CD$_2$CH$_2$O should be viewed with some caution as the number of identified lines is on the low side to claim a definitive detection." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
- Limitation (authors): The astronomical detection of c-CD2CH2O is only tentative because the number of identified lines is on the low side, and several predicted lines fall below the RMS noise or are blended.  
  > "For some of these predicted lines, no clear signal is seen down to the RMS noise level of the data (1$\sigma$ being 4$-$5 mJy beam$^{-1}$ km s$^{-1}$)" (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B); "Still, our astronomical results on $c$-CD$_2$CH$_2$O should be viewed with some caution as the number of identified lines is on the low side to claim a definitive detection." (Astronomical search toward IRAS 16293-2422 B)
- Limitation (authors): Very weak laboratory lines and some intended high-Kc transitions around 1 THz were affected by line blending with other isotopologs, excited vibrational states, or neighboring lines.  
  > "line blending with other isotopic species or with excited vibrational states appeared to be only slightly more important than in our investigation of $c$-C$_2$H$_3$DO (cite: c-C2H3DO_rot_2023) for the most part, except probably for the very weak lines" (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters); "Many of the intended high $K_c$ transitions were too weak, heavily blended, or too close to neighboring lines such that only few of them were added to the line list." (Analysis of the c-CD2CH2O rotational spectrum and determination of spectroscopic parameters)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The column density of c-CD2CH2O is derived with T_rot, FWHM, and v_LSR held fixed to values from c-C2H4O without a formal uncertainty estimate given for N(c-CD2CH2O).
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line confusion in the line-rich spectrum of IRAS 16293-2422 B makes tentative assignments based on a few weak features (~20-32 mJy km s^-1) susceptible to false positives from uncatalogued lines of other COMs.
