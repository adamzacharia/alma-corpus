## Science

- Fields: star_formation, milky_way_ism, astrochemistry
- Question: Do molecular line observations of the candidate interaction zones near GRS 1758-258 and 1E 1740.7-2942 show jet-blown cavities, and what do they imply for jet power and distance?  
  > "we aim to characterize the properties of the molecular gas at these sites, test whether these candidate zones are consistent with being powered by a BHXB jet, and estimate the energy input into the ISM" (1 Introduction)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and line cubes**: tclean on ACA and combined ACA+12m data with auto-multithresh; natural weighting  
  > "All line imaging was done with the `tclean` task in CASA, through combining the ACA and 12m data, using natural weighting" (2.1 ALMA 12m Array)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Map molecular emission and extract spectra and PV diagrams**: maximum intensity maps, spectra over lobe regions, PV slices along lobe edge/ring  
  > "we created position-velocity diagrams of the $^{13}$CO, HCN, and CS molecules through a slice along the eastern outer edge of the lobe" (3.3 Properties of the Molecular Gas)
   - software: spectral-cube, pvextractor
   - fitting: HCN hyperfine pattern fit with component ratios free  
  > "we determined the velocity by fitting the HCN hyperfine emission pattern to the line allowing the ratios between the components to vary as free parameters" (4.2 Evidence for jet-ISM interactions)
   - validation: compare with spectra away from interaction site and with single-dish IRAM/SEST detections  
  > "consistent, in terms of central velocities (70–80 ${\rm km\, s}^{-1}$) and peak intensities ($<1$ K), with past lower resolution IRAM/SEST molecular detections" (3.2 Molecular Emission)
3. **Constrain distance via size-line width relation**: dendrogram of HCO+ cube; compare sigma_v vs R to disc and CMZ relations at D=8 kpc  
  > "Assuming $D=8~\mathrm{kpc}$, the gas around our candidate jet-ISM regions clearly shows turbulent velocity scalings consistent with being found in the high-pressure CMZ." (4.1 Distance constraints)
   - software: astrodendro
   - assumption: fiducial distance 8 kpc; sizes scale linearly with distance  
  > "We measure a physical size for the regions by assuming a fiducial distance of $D=8$ kpc" (4.1 Distance constraints)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive gas mass, density and time-averaged jet power**: 13CO/HCO+ column density to mass; ellipsoid volume; Kaiser-Alexander jet power Q_jet  
  > "We then model the GRS 1758$-$258 molecular structure as an ellipsoid" (4.3 Deriving jet properties)
   - parameter: v=15 km/s, l=90''/25'', w=30''/8'', f=0.1, i=30 or 80 deg, D=7-9 kpc, Gamma=5/3  
  > "filling factor of $f=0.1$ (a reasonable assumption given that the jet is displacing molecular gas), $i=30$ deg or $i=80$ deg" (4.3 Deriving jet properties)
   - assumption: optically thin emission; 13CO/H2=6e-5, HCO+/H2=3e-9  
  > "assuming optically thin emission and fractional abundance ratios of $^{13}$CO/H$_2=6\times10^{-5}$, HCO$+$/H$_2=3\times10^{-9}$" (4.3 Deriving jet properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: column densities compared to X-ray N_H; densities compared to critical densities  
  > "To check that our H$_2$ column density measurements are reasonable, we compare the range of column densities that we calculated in each region to the column density obtained independently from X-ray spectral fitting" (4.3 Deriving jet properties)

## Qualitative findings

- Molecular structure on eastern edge of GRS 1758-258 southern lobe; ring of HCN/HCO+ around 1E 1740.7-2942 consistent with jet-blown cavities  
  > "This emission revealed new molecular structures in both regions that appear to trace jet-blown cavities in the surrounding gas." (Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ACA matches resolution of radio observations and covers lobe extent; 12m for higher-resolution follow-up  
  > "while the 12m array provided higher resolution followup observations of key molecular emission regions identified in the ACA data" (1.1 GRS 1758–258 & 1E 1740.7–2942)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Supernova remnant for the 1E 1740.7-2942 ring judged unlikely (slow shock velocity, no extended radio/X-ray emission)  
  > "we hypothesize that the molecular ring structure near 1E 1740.7$-$2942 is unlikely to represent a supernova remnant" (4.4 Other mechanisms)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No single-dish data, so extended emission may be filtered out  
  > "The absence of single dish data in our observations may indicate that a significant amount of the emission has been spatially filtered out by the interferometer." (4.1 Distance constraints)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Association of gas with jets is circumstantial; distance constraint assumes the interaction is real
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Jet-power depends on assumed f, i, v and abundances
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Chance alignment with CMZ clouds is possible
