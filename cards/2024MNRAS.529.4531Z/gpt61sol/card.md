## Analysis recipe

1. **Make continuum, line, and morphology maps**: Subtract a linear continuum in the uv-plane; image individual cycles and C46; form integrated maps and fit beam-deconvolved 2D Gaussian sizes.  
  > "We then estimated the continuum emission by a linear function to the line-free channels and subtracted it in the *uv*-plane by using the task `uvcontsub`." (ALMA Band 6); "the integrated intensity (mom0) map of CO(6-5) line emission integrated over $-500$ to $+250\ \mathrm{km\ s^{-1}}$" (Morphology); "$R$ is determined by fitting a 2D Gaussian component to the mom0 map of Cycle 6 observations and being deconvolved from the beam size." (Dynamical Mass)
   - software: CASA, uvcontsub
   - parameter: Velocity reference: 236.69 GHz, z=1.9214  
  > "the reference frequency was set as 236.69 GHz, corresponding to the line at $z=1.9214$." (ALMA Band 6)
   - assumption: 1″ corresponds to 8.62 kpc  
  > "$1\ensuremath{''}$ corresponds to a projected physical scale of 8.62 kpc." (Introduction)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian; beam deconvolution  
  > "$R$ is determined by fitting a 2D Gaussian component to the mom0 map of Cycle 6 observations and being deconvolved from the beam size." (Dynamical Mass)
   - validation: Compare NW integrated intensities across Cycles 2, 4, and 6  
  > "we compare the integrated intensity ${I}_\mathrm{CO(6-5)}$ calculated in three cycles" (Morphology)
2. **Extract spectra and separate velocity components**: Circular apertures enclosing 3σ contours; two Gaussian components for NW; three for SE; separate #Main and #Tidal spectra.  
  > "For both NW and SE galaxies, the line profiles are extracted using a circular aperture that encloses the spatial area defined by 3$\sigma$-level contours." (Line Profiles); "the line profile can be fitted by two Gaussian components with velocity center (${v}_\mathrm{cen}$), amplitude, and full width at half maximum (FWHM) as fitting parameters." (Line Profiles); "The SE galaxy has its line profile decomposable into three Gaussian components in Cycle 4" (Line Profiles)
   - parameter: Cycle 6 NW: Hanning smoothing; main-component center fixed at −20 km s−1  
  > "In Cycle 6, we first applied Hanning smooth to the spectrum" (Line Profiles); "fixing ${v}_\mathrm{cen}$ of the main component (NW - 2) to -20 km s$^{-1}$" (Line Profiles)
   - fitting: Gaussian velocity centers, amplitudes, and FWHMs; Cycle 6 NW main center fixed  
  > "velocity center (${v}_\mathrm{cen}$), amplitude, and full width at half maximum (FWHM) as fitting parameters." (Line Profiles); "fixing ${v}_\mathrm{cen}$ of the main component (NW - 2) to -20 km s$^{-1}$" (Line Profiles)
   - validation: Compare components against Cycle 2/4; reject unconstrained third NW component  
  > "found in both Cycle 2 and 4 observations within $1\sigma$ uncertainty." (Line Profiles); "Otherwise, given the low signal-to-noise ratio, the fitting algorithm returned a third component with uncertainties exceeding 100%." (Line Profiles)
3. **Convert CO fluxes to molecular gas masses**: Compute CO(6-5) brightness-temperature luminosity, convert to CO(1-0) using intensity ratios, and multiply by αCO.  
  > "We calculated the integrated source brightness temperature from CO(6-5) line intensity using the following equation (cite: 2013ARA&A..51..105C)" (Molecular gas mass); "$L_\text{CO(6-5)}^\prime$ has to be converted to ${L}_\mathrm{CO(1-0)}^\prime$ via the line intensity ratio" (Molecular gas mass); "M_\mathrm{H_2}=\alpha_\text{CO}\times L_\text{CO(1-0)}^\prime" (Molecular gas mass)
   - parameter: r65/10∼13 for bulk gas; 17–36 for blueshifted high-excitation components  
  > "the bulk molecular gas component has a line intensity ratio ${r}_\mathrm{65/10}\sim13$." (Molecular gas mass); "$17\leq{r}_\mathrm{65/10(blue)}\leq36$ for the blueshifted, high-excitation components." (Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: αCO∼0.8 M⊙ (K km s−1 pc²)−1  
  > "An ${\alpha}_\mathrm{CO}\sim0.8\ \mathrm{M_\odot}\ \mathrm{(K\ \mathrm{km\ s^{-1}}\ pc^2)}^{-1}$ (cite: 1998ApJ...507..615D)" (Molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Both galaxies are rotation-dominated, with NW kinematics disturbed by non-circular motions.  
  > "indicative of a rotation-dominated disc" (SE Galaxy); "suggesting that the NW galaxy is rotation-dominated as well." (NW Galaxy); "The non-circular motions make it more difficult to model the rotational velocity field." (NW Galaxy)
- The outflow is compact, perpendicular to the jet axis, and lopsided.  
  > "the high-velocity component is likely to extend along a direction perpendicular to the jet axis." (Outflow geometry); "This component also shows lopsidedness – only towards the southwest direction." (Outflow geometry)
- Recent starburst and radiatively efficient BH growth coexist with powerful jets.  
  > "Our study reveals a unique object where starburst activity, powerful jets, and rapid BH growth co-exist" (abstract)
- The observed molecular outflow alone does not imply short-term quenching.  
  > "this mass represents merely $\lesssim10$% of the total molecular gas in the NW galaxy." (Outflow energetics); "the AGN feedback is negative but not responsible for the quenching in the short-term." (Conclusion)
