## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and combine data**: tclean multiscale with auto-multithresh, separately for 12 m and 7 m, then feather; feather with IRAM 30 m 12CO  
  > "We combined the individually imaged 12 and 7 m array data sets with the `feathering` task." (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Briggs robust 0.5 (12 m), natural (7 m)  
  > "We applied the Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5 and the natural weighting to the 12 m and 7 m array data, respectively." (2 Observations)
   - assumption: 13CO, C18O and continuum have no significant missing flux  
  > "We assume that the $^{13}$CO, C$^{18}$O, and continuum observations have no significant missing flux" (2 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative visibility-domain combination gave similar results; combined image reproduces IRAM total flux  
  > "we confirmed that the combined image reproduces the total flux measured with the IRAM data alone" (2 Observations)
2. **Estimate gas masses of GMC and filaments**: 12CO mass from XCO; 13CO mass by LTE with Tex from 12CO  
  > "The mass of higher density regions traced by $^{13}$CO is estimated by the local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) calculation applying the excitation temperature derived from the $^{12}$CO data" (3.1)
   - assumption: XCO 2.0e20, CO(2-1)/(1-0)=0.7, [H2]/[13CO]=1.4e6  
  > "relative abundance of \[H$_{2}$\]/\[$^{13}$CO\] of 1.4 $\times$ 10$^{6}$" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 13CO/12CO mass fraction compared with Galactic plane  
  > "which is consistent with that in the Galactic plane (cite: Torii19)" (3.1)
3. **Kinematics of filaments and extended gas**: Channel maps (4 km/s bins) and position-velocity diagram along selected lines  
  > "We made channel maps with a velocity bin of 4 km s$^{-1}$ of the $^{12}$CO ($J$ = 2–1) data" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Mass and boundness of MMS clump**: Dust continuum mass above 3 sigma, virial mass from C18O linewidth and radius 1.6 pc  
  > "With a radius of 1.6 pc (=geometric mean of the major and minor axis) the resultant virial mass, $\sim$2 $\times$ 10$^{4}$ $M_{\odot}$, is consistent with the $M_{\rm total}^{MMS}$" (3.2)
   - assumption: kappa1.3mm = 1 cm2/g, dust-to-gas 3e-3, Td = 20 K  
  > "assuming the dust opacity of $\kappa_{\rm 1.3 mm}$ of 1 cm$^{2}$ g$^{-1}$ for protostellar envelopes (cite: Oss94), a dust-to-gas ratio of $\sim$3 $\times$ 10$^{-3}$" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Continuum mass and C18O virial mass consistent  
  > "because the gas mass estimated from the 1.3 mm continuum and that from the C$^{18}$O emission are similar to each other" (3.2)
5. **Identify outflow candidate and timescale**: Systemic velocity from Gaussian fit to 13CO; 12CO wings; 3x3 profile maps  
  > "we use it to estimate the systemic velocity of MMS using Gaussian fitting" (3.2)
   - assumption: Inclination 45-60 deg for dynamical time  
  > "depending on the assumption of the inclination angle (45$^\circ$–60$^\circ$)" (4.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No similar wings found in other profile-map positions  
  > "We could not find similar high-velocity wing emission except in the middle panel, i.e., at the location of MMS." (4.2)
6. **Compare CO with Halpha and spiral shock timescale**: Offset of 10-20 pc between filaments and HII regions converted to timescale using rotation speed minus pattern speed  
  > "is calculated to be $\sim$1 Myr (=20 pc/ (80–50) km s$^{-1}$)" (4.1)
   - assumption: GMFs and HII regions at the same height in the disk  
  > "assuming that the GMFs and the H$\;$ii regions are located at the same height in the galactic disk" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with HII region ages from sizes  
  > "This is consistent with the age of the H$\;$ii regions judged from their sizes based on the Galactic studies (cite: Tremblin14)" (4.1)

## Qualitative findings

- GMC-16 is composed of filaments of 50-70 pc length, comparable to Galactic GMFs, elongated roughly perpendicular to galactic rotation, with HII regions downstream  
  > "The elongations of the GMFs are roughly perpendicular to the direction of the galaxy’s rotation, and several H$\;$ii regions are located at the downstream side" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Need ~1 pc resolution to understand internal gas properties and kinematics, and M33's proximity and spiral arms allow it  
  > "we need at least $\sim$1 pc resolution to understand the internal gas properties of molecular clouds and their kinematics" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Ring-like structure of HII regions, and collect-and-collapse triggering, considered unlikely  
  > "it is unlikely that the GMFs are regarded as a part of the ring-like structure associated with the H$\;$ii regions" (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No spatial offset of red and blue lobes due to limited resolution; outflow nature uncertain  
  > "there is no significant spatial offset between the red and blue velocity components" (4.2)
- Limitation (authors): Galactic shock alone may not explain high-mass star formation or the hub filament  
  > "the formation of the small-scale hub filament may not be explained by the spiral shock alone" (4.3)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spiral shock origin rests on indirect evidence from a single cloud; nonsteady arm models also possible
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distance, XCO and abundance assumptions drive masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow identification lacks spatial separation of lobes
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-dish missing flux 40% in 12CO affects filament mass
