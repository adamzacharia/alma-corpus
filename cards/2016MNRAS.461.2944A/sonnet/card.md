## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA maps**: Iterative CASA clean with natural weighting and tight clean boxes  
  > "For each target we adopt an iterative approach to the clean procedure, following the method outlined in Hodge et al. (2013) and Simpson et al. (2015)." (2.2 ALMA Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: clean to 1.5 sigma within boxes around >5 sigma then >3.5 sigma sources; 0.1 arcsec pixels  
  > "repeat the clean procedure on all sources detected at $>$ 3.5 $\sigma$ in these maps" (2.2 ALMA Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect sources and set threshold**: Find >3.5 sigma peaks in non-PB-corrected maps, repeat on inverted maps; adopt >4.5 sigma threshold; point-source fit for extension  
  > "We find that the number of “negative” sources falls to zero at $>$ 4.5 $\sigma$" (2.3 ALMA Source Detection, Matching, and Properties)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 4.5 sigma threshold; upper limits = 4.5 x rms  
  > "by taking 4.5 times the rms, adjusting for any small primary beam corrections" (2.3 ALMA Source Detection, Matching, and Properties)
   - fitting: point source model at each source position  
  > "We measured the peak flux density and fitted a point source model at the position of each source" (2.3 ALMA Source Detection, Matching, and Properties)
   - validation: inverted-map false detection test; chance-coincidence expectation from Simpson et al. (2015) number counts (0.17 sources over all maps)  
  > "we predict 0.17 ALMA sources by chance within the primary beam across all of our eight ALMA maps" (2.3 ALMA Source Detection, Matching, and Properties)
3. **Match ALMA sources to X-ray AGNs**: Combine Chandra (1.5 x 80% confidence) and ALMA (0.4 arcsec) positional uncertainties  
  > "For the uncertainty in the X-ray source position we used 1.5 times the 80% confidence level of each X-ray source from Lehmer et al. (2009b)" (2.3 ALMA Source Detection, Matching, and Properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive L_IR and SFR**: 870 um flux to L_IR(8–1000 um) with Bethermin et al. (2015) average SEDs, then SFR = L_IR/3.778e43 erg/s  
  > "the infrared luminosities are calculated from the 870 $\mu$m flux densities over rest-frame 8–1000 $\mu$m using the source redshifts and the average SEDs of the star-forming galaxies in Bethermin et al. (2015)" (2.4 Measurement of star-formation rates)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; L_IR uncertainty ~0.3 dex; z=3.09  
  > "calculated assuming $z=3.09$, and has an uncertainty of $\approx$ 0.3 dex" (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: AGN contamination check using Spitzer 24 um and Mullaney et al. (2011) AGN template: ~9% for AGN 1, <~4% others  
  > "we therefore predict that the AGN contributes to $\approx$ 9% of the 870 $\mu$m flux density for AGN 1" (2.4 Measurement of star-formation rates)
5. **Compare protocluster and field AGN mean SFRs**: Mean SFR ranges bracketing upper limits as 0 and as the limit; field AGNs evolved to z=3.09 using Schreiber et al. (2015)  
  > "The lower limit on the mean SFR is calculated assuming that the SFRs of the sources with SFR upper limits have the extreme value of 0 $M_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$" (3.1)
   - assumption: Field AGN SFR evolution follows M* star-forming galaxies  
  > "we have assumed the measured SFR evolution for $M_{\rm *}$ star-forming galaxies from Schreiber et al. (2015)" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: stellar mass comparison of field vs protocluster AGNs  
  > "On the basis of this stellar mass comparison there are no significant differences between the field and protocluster AGNs" (3.1)
6. **Assess star formation as Lya power source in LABs**: Convert IR star-formation luminosity to 200–912 A luminosity with Geach et al. (2009) template; derive required escape fraction  
  > "we take our infrared-derived star-formation luminosities and convert them to 200–912 Å luminosities assuming the star-forming galaxy template adopted in Geach et al. (2009)" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: comparison to escape fractions for z~3 star-forming galaxies  
  > "our calculated escape fractions are broadly consistent with those estimated for star-forming galaxies at $z\approx$ 3" (3.2)

## Qualitative findings

- All four LABs host an ALMA source within the Lya extent; ALMA sources in the giant LABs are offset from the X-ray AGNs  
  > "All four of the protocluster LABs are associated with an ALMA source within the extent of their Ly $\alpha$ emission" (Conclusions)
