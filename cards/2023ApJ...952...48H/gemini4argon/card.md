## Science

- Fields: high_z_galaxies, star_formation
- Question: Investigate the nature and mass of the molecular gas in the bright Lyman-break galaxy B14-65666 at z = 7.1520 using ALMA Band 3 observations of CO(6-5), CO(7-6), and [CI](2-1) and multiwavelength constraints.

## Analysis recipe

1. **Create continuum and line cubes and measure upper limits on continuum and line fluxes**: Image continuum and 50 km/s spectral cubes with natural weighting and uv-tapering (0.40''-0.45''), integrate cubes over -200 to +200 km/s with CASA immoments to produce moment 0 maps, extract spectra in a 1.5'' diameter aperture, and measure 3-sigma upper limits.  
  > "In these maps, we integrate the velocity range from −200 to +200 km s−1 with the CASA task immoments, which is comparable to the FWHM of [Oiii] and [Cii] (Hashimoto et al. 2019)" (3. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION); "Figure 2 shows the spectra obtained in a 1′′.5-diameter aperture centered on the target" (3. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION); "From the integrated intensity maps, we obtain the 3σ upper limits on the velocity-integrated flux, Sline∆v, as 0.0581, 0.0546, and 0.0542 Jy km s−1 for CO(6-5), CO(7-6), and [Ci](2-1), respectively." (4.1. CO and [C i] line fluxes)
   - software: CASA, tclean, immoments
   - parameter: velocity range -200 to +200 km s^-1  
  > "In these maps, we integrate the velocity range from −200 to +200 km s−1 with the CASA task immoments" (3. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION)
   - parameter: velocity resolution 50 km s^-1  
  > "The cubes were rebinned to a velocity resolution of 50 km s−1." (3. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION)
   - assumption: CO emitting region size does not exceed that of [CII] emission (covered by the uv-tapered beam)  
  > "Here we assumed that the size of the CO emitting region should not exceed that of the [Cii] emission, which is a better tracer of more extended and multi-phase gas." (4.1. CO and [C i] line fluxes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Correct line fluxes for CMB and calculate line luminosity upper limits**: Apply CMB correction factor f_CMB using da Cunha et al. (2013) Eq. 32 and convert velocity-integrated flux limits to L_line and L'_line using Solomon et al. (1992) formulas.  
  > "Following Eq. (32) of da Cunha et al. (2013), the fraction of the intrinsic line flux observed against the CMB is written as" (4.1. CO and [C i] line fluxes); "We obtain the 3σ upper limits on two types of line luminosities (Solomon et al. 1992; Carilli & Walter 2013), which are summarized in Table 3." (4.2. Upper limits on CO and [C i] line luminosities)
   - parameter: f_CMB = 0.1 - 0.9 for T_exc = 23 - 80 K  
  > "In summary, fCMB is highly uncertain, ranging from ∼ 0.1 − 0.9 at Texc = 23 − 80 K." (4.1. CO and [C i] line fluxes)
   - assumption: LambdaCDM cosmology with Omega_m = 0.272, Omega_b = 0.045, Omega_Lambda = 0.728, H_0 = 70.4 km s^-1 Mpc^-1  
  > "we assume a ΛCDM cosmology with Ωm = 0.272, Ωb = 0.045, ΩΛ = 0.728, and H0 = 70.4 km s−1 Mpc−1 (Komatsu et al. 2011)." (1. INTRODUCTION)
   - assumption: T_CMB(z) = (1 + z) x 2.73 K  
  > "TCMB(z) = (1+z)×2.73 K is the CMB temperature at z, and Texc is the excitation temperature in units of K." (4.1. CO and [C i] line fluxes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Estimate molecular gas mass using five independent techniques**: Calculate M_mol from (1) mid-J CO upper limits with excitation and metallicity-dependent alpha_CO, (2) [CI](2-1) upper limit with partition function and carbon abundance, (3) [CII] 158 um luminosity with Madden et al. (2020) relation, (4) dust mass and metallicity-dependent DGR, and (5) dynamical mass minus stellar mass.  
  > "We estimate the molecular gas mass of B14-65666. In light of the rich dataset, we adopt five techniques as summarized in Table 4." (4.3. Molecular gas mass estimates); "To summarize, by combining the Mmol estimates from the [Cii] luminosity, dust mass, and dynamical mass, we obtain Mmol = (0.05 − 11) × 1010 M⊙" (4.3.5. Upper limit with dynamical mass)
   - parameter: r_61 = 0.28 +0.04/-0.02, r_71 = 0.17 +0.02/-0.02  
  > "_r_ 61 = 0 _._ 28 [+0] _−_ 0 _[.]_ _._ [04] 02 [and] _r_ 71 = 0 _._ 17 _−_ [+0] 0 _[.]_ _._ [02] 02 [.]" (4.3.2 Estimate with [CI](2-1))
   - parameter: alpha_CO = 25 M_sun (K km s^-1 pc^2)^-1 (range 5 - 25 for Z = 0.2 - 0.8 Z_sun)  
  > "With _α_ CO = 25 _M_ _⊙_ (K km s _[−]_ [1] pc [2] ) _[−]_ [1] and the 3 _σ_" (4.3.1 Estimates with CO(6-5) and CO(7-6))
   - parameter: DGR ≈ (1.8 - 53) x 10^-4  
  > "we obtain DGR ≈ (1.8 − 53) × 10−4 at the metallicity of the target." (4.3.4. Estimate with dust continuum)
   - assumption: X[CI]/X[H2] ~ 1.6 x 10^-5 (and up to 10x higher conversion factor at low Z)  
  > "Assuming the abundance ratio of X[Ci]/X[H2] ∼ 1.6 × 10−5 as obtained in z ∼ 1 main-sequence galaxies (Valentino et al. 2018)" (4.3.2. Estimate with [C i](2-1)); "If we assume that the mass conversion factor of [Ci](2-1) similarly changes with metallicity, our upper limits can be higher by a factor of 10" (4.3.2. Estimate with [C i](2-1))
   - assumption: HII region contribution to [CII] is ~30% and CMB attenuation factor for [CII] is f_CMB = 0.5 - 1.0  
  > "From the metallicity of the target and Fig. 9 of Cormier et al. (2019), we estimate the contribution from the Hii region to be ≈ 30%." (4.3.3. Estimate with [C ii] 158 µm); "Given the uncertainty, we assume fCMB = 0.5 − 1.0 in B14-65666." (4.3.3. Estimate with [C ii] 158 µm)
   - assumption: Gas is predominantly in the molecular phase for the dust-based estimate  
  > "If we assume that gas is predominantly in the molecular phase (Riechers et al. 2013), this can be regarded as the molecular gas mass." (4.3.4. Estimate with dust continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistency across the five independent techniques  
  > "we obtain Mmol = (0.05 − 11) × 1010 M⊙, which is consistent with the upper limits on Mmol inferred from the nondetections of mid-J CO and [Ci](2-1)." (4.3.5. Upper limit with dynamical mass)
4. **Constrain PDR gas density and FUV radiation field using PDR Toolbox**: Compare observed L_[CII]/L_FIR, L_[CII]/L_CO(6-5), L_[CII]/L_CO(7-6), and L_[CII]/L_[CI](2-1) ratios against PDR Toolbox (version wk2020) model grids to constrain hydrogen nuclei density n(H) and incident FUV field G.  
  > "We thus compare the line ratios of B14-65666 to the model predictions of PDR Toolbox (version wk2020) to place constraints on the physical properties of the PDRs." (5.2. PDR modelling)
   - software: PDR Toolbox
   - assumption: Divide optically thin emission luminosities by a factor of 2 for spherical cloud geometry; PDR fraction of [CII] is 70%  
  > "We therefore divide the luminosities of optically thin emission by a factor of two. We also assume that the [Cii] contribution from the PDR is 70% (§4.3.3)." (5.2. PDR modelling)
   - fitting: Parameter space overlap on PDR model grid  
  > "In Figure 5, the overlapped region of the four luminosity ratios is log(n(H)/cm−3) ∼ 1 − 5 with a moderate FUV radiation field ∼ 102 − 103 G0." (5.2. PDR modelling)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compute molecular gas fraction and gas depletion time**: Divide the derived M_mol range by SFR and stellar mass from SED fitting to obtain tau_dep and mu_gas, and compare with high-z literature samples and scaling relations.  
  > "With Mmol and SFR = 200+82−38 M⊙ yr−1 from SED fitting (Hashimoto et al. 2019), we obtain τdep ≈ 2.5 − 550 Myr." (5.3. Gas fractions and depletion timescales); "With Mmol = (0.05 − 11) × 1010 M⊙ and the stellar mass obtained from SED fitting (Hashimoto et al. 2019), M∗ = 7.7+1.0−0.8 × 108 M⊙, we obtain µmol ≈ 0.65 − 140." (5.3. Gas fractions and depletion timescales)
   - assumption: SFR = 200 +82/-38 M_sun yr^-1 and M_* = 7.7 +1.0/-0.8 x 10^8 M_sun

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Mid-J CO transitions peak around J_u = 6-7 in EoR galaxies and are less affected by the CMB and easier to observe with ALMA than low-J CO transitions, while [CI](2-1) is observed simultaneously and traces the bulk cold molecular gas.  
  > "Hence, to efficiently detect the molecular gas component, we target the brighter mid-J transitions, which can be observed far more efficiently with sub-/mm facilities." (1. INTRODUCTION); "Our observational setup efficiently covers CO(J = 6 → 5), CO(J = 7 → 6), and [Ci] 3P2 → 3P1. [Ci] is highly complementary to mid-J CO" (1. INTRODUCTION)
- Why this method: Applied a Gaussian uv-taper of 0.40''-0.45'' so the synthesized beam (~0.8'') fully covers the spatially extended (~0.8'' x 0.4'') dust and [CII] emitting region, and combined five gas mass estimation methods to overcome individual conversion uncertainties.  
  > "We adopted a taper value of 0′′.45 because the resultant beam size (0′′.82×0′′.72) fully covers the dust continuum emitting region." (3. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION); "We estimate the molecular gas mass of B14-65666. In light of the rich dataset, we adopt five techniques as summarized in Table 4." (4.3. Molecular gas mass estimates)
- Alternative: X-ray dominated region (XDR) heating of the emission lines was rejected because L_[CII]/L_[CI](2-1) >= 40 exceeds the XDR expectation of <= 6.  
  > "The high ratio, ≳ 40, excludes the possibility that the lines are heated by the X-ray dominated regions, where L[CII]/L[CI](2−1) ≲ 6 is expected." (5.2. PDR modelling)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Current Band 3 data are not deep enough to detect the continuum or mid-J CO/[CI] lines, or to constrain the dust emissivity index.  
  > "The current data is not deep enough to obtain a meaningful constraint on the dust emissivity index." (3. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION); "we note that the tightest constraint on Mmol comes from the previous observations of dust and [Cii] 158 µm, not from mid-J CO or [Ci](2-1), due to the insufficient sensitivity of the Band 3 observations." (4.3.5. Upper limit with dynamical mass)
- Limitation (authors): The CMB attenuation factor f_CMB is highly uncertain (0.1 - 0.9), complicating the interpretation of line luminosity ratios and PDR modeling.  
  > "In summary, fCMB is highly uncertain, ranging from ∼ 0.1 − 0.9 at Texc = 23 − 80 K." (4.1. CO and [C i] line fluxes); "the CMB effect prevents us from obtaining a conclusion." (5.2. PDR modelling)
- Limitation (authors): Large uncertainty in M_mol spans over two orders of magnitude ((0.05 - 11) x 10^10 M_sun) due to uncertainties in metallicity, DGR, and conversion factors.  
  > "Despite the large uncertauinty in Mmol, we estimate a molecular gas-to-stellar mass ratio (µgas) of 0.65 − 140 and a gas depletion time (τdep) of 2.5 − 550 Myr" (abstract)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): All newly reported ALMA Band 3 measurements are 3-sigma upper limits (non-detections); the bounded range on M_mol relies entirely on previously published [CII], dust continuum, and dynamical mass measurements.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The dust-to-gas ratio (DGR) and conversion factors (alpha_CO, alpha_[CII], alpha_[CI]) are calibrated at lower redshifts and have large systematic uncertainties at z = 7.15.
