- Caveat (inferred, ours): The J = 11(3,9)–10(3,8) V = 0 (220.126 GHz) and J = 11(3,8)–10(3,7) V = 0 (220.127 GHz) transitions are completely blended into a single spectral profile, yet the total integrated intensity of the blended feature (28.392 K km s^-1) appears to be attributed to the single 11(3,9)–10(3,8) transition in the rotational diagram without dividing by two or fitting a joint synthetic spectrum, which overestimates N_u/g_u at E_u = 193.72 K by roughly a factor of two.

- Caveat (inferred, ours): No beam dilution correction factor was applied in the rotational diagram analysis despite extracting the spectrum from a 0.781'' aperture while the deconvolved source size is ~0.50''.
