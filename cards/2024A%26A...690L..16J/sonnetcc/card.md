## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate ALMA data**: Reduce and calibrate raw data with the standard ALMA CASA pipeline; Band 6 DDT data were also self-calibrated  
  > "The raw data of the ALMA programs mentioned above were reduced and calibrated using the standard ALMA CASA pipeline (cite: McMullin2007CASA)." (ALMA); "The on-source integration is 15 mins, and self-calibration was performed." (ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **extract 1D spectrum in the uv plane**: Convert calibrated measurement sets to uvfits and fit a point-source model at the fixed ALMA continuum peak position at all frequencies with GILDAS uvfit  
  > "for further analysis in $uv$ space with the GILDAS software" (ALMA); "on the $uv$ tables at all frequencies, where we adopted a point-source model on the fixed position of the ALMA continuum peak" (ALMA)
   - software: GILDAS
   - parameter: point-source model at fixed position of the ALMA continuum peak  
  > "on the $uv$ tables at all frequencies, where we adopted a point-source model on the fixed position of the ALMA continuum peak" (ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **clean maps and measure continuum and line fluxes**: Clean continuum and line maps with GILDAS HOGBOM and measure continuum and integrated line fluxes in an aperture on the clean images  
  > "The continuum and line maps are cleaned using the GILDAS HOGBOM clean routine." (ALMA); "we measured the continuum and integrated line fluxes on the clean images using an aperture of" (ALMA)
   - software: GILDAS
   - parameter: aperture r~2.5'' chosen to maximize integrated S/N  
  > "using an aperture of $r\sim2.5''$, which maximizes the integrated S/Ns" (ALMA)
   - assumption: source is resolved in the ALMA data  
  > "Given that COSBO-7 is resolved in the ALMA data" (ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **identify lines and determine spectroscopic redshift**: Test the z=7.458 solution (CO(7-6) at 95.4 GHz, [CII] expected at 224.7 GHz); find two Band 6 lines matching CO(7-6) and [CI](2-1) at z=2.625 and the Band 3 line matching CO(3-2)  
  > "the DDT program did not detect any line at the expected frequency of 224.7 GHz of \[CII\] at $z=7.458$" (Redshift confirmation); "The two lines perfectly match the CO(7-6) and \[CI\](2-1) transitions at $z=2.625$." (Redshift confirmation)
   - parameter: lines at 222.52 GHz (S/N=11 or 23) and 223.27 GHz in Band 6; 95.4 GHz in Band 3  
  > "two lines are solidly detected at 222.52 GHz and 223.27 GHz with S/N$=11$ and 23, respectively" (Redshift confirmation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Three lines consistent with one redshift and line widths consistent (FWZI = 850 km/s)  
  > "also consistent with a full width at zero intensity FWZI$=$850 km s$^{-1}$" (Redshift confirmation); "Moreover, the 95.4 GHz line is also fitted at the exact frequency of $z=2.625$ CO(3-2)." (Redshift confirmation)
5. **compile multiwavelength photometry**: Adopt JWST photometry from Ling et al. 2024; re-run super-deblending on Herschel maps; measure ALMA Band 4 photometry; use A3COSMOS 870 micron  
  > "therefore we directly adopt the photometry from (cite: Ling2024cosbo7)" (JWST); "with improved priors on Herschel maps as done by (cite: Sillassen2024NICE), assuming that COSBO-7 is the only source contributing to the Herschel fluxes" (Ancillary data)
   - parameter: F090W limit: larger-aperture measurement from Ling et al., but 3 sigma limit used  
  > "but use the $3\sigma$ limit in this work for reasons discussed in Sect." (JWST)
   - assumption: COSBO-7 is the only source contributing to the Herschel fluxes  
  > "assuming that COSBO-7 is the only source contributing to the Herschel fluxes" (Ancillary data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ALMA maps show no other submm source in the Band 7 and Band 3 primary beams  
  > "indicating negligible blending and no contributions from neighboring sources" (Ancillary data)
6. **fit NIR-to-radio SED for physical parameters**: SED fitting from NIR to radio with Stardust at z_spec=2.625  
  > "the SED is well fitted from NIR to radio wavelengths using" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - software: Stardust
   - assumption: flat LCDM, H0=70, Omega_M=0.27, Chabrier IMF  
  > "We adopt a flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $H_0=70$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$ and $\Omega_M=0.27$, and a Chabrier initial mass function" (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 3 mm continuum not well fitted by Stardust, suggesting a steep beta slope or optically thick dust  
  > "which suggests a steep $\beta$ slope or optically thick dust in FIR (cite: Jin2022)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
7. **fit FIR SED with modified blackbody (thin and thick dust)**: Modified blackbody fits to the FIR SED for optically thin and thick dust; thick model preferred  
  > "We thus performed FIR SED fitting with modified blackbody models using the" (Physical properties and lensing model); "the optically thick model performs slightly better than the thin ones" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - software: Mercurius
   - assumption: both optically thin and optically thick dust in FIR considered  
  > "accounting for both cases of optically thin and thick dust in FIR (cite: Jin2022)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: T_dust consistent with the T_dust-z relation of main sequence galaxies  
  > "relation of main sequence galaxies by (cite: Schreiber2018Tdust)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
8. **model the lens to derive magnification**: Lens modeling of the F777W image following Vegetti & Koopmans 2009 and Rizzo et al. 2018  
  > "we performed lens modeling of the F777W image by adopting the methodology from (cite: Vegetti2009lensing) and (cite: Rizzo2018lensing)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Magnification consistent with Pearson et al. 2024 (F777W) and slightly higher than Ling et al. 2024 (F444W)  
  > "This magnification is consistent with the findings of (cite: Pearson2024cosbo7), which are based on F777W data" (Physical properties and lensing model)
9. **derive gas mass and depletion time**: Gas mass from [CI](2-1) via scaling relation; CO(3-2) converted to CO(1-0) luminosity and gas mass for two alpha_CO values as a check; adopt M_gas,CI for depletion time  
  > "we obtain a gas mass of $M_{\rm gas,CI}=(5.04\pm1.70)\times10^{11}" (Physical properties and lensing model); "we simply adopted $M_{\rm gas,CI}$ and derived a median gas depletion time of" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - assumption: R_[CI] = 0.3 +/- 0.1  
  > "=0.3\pm0.1$ (cite: Jiao2019CI)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - assumption: T_exc = T_dust,thick  
  > "assuming the excitation $T_{\rm exc}=T_{\rm dust, thick}$" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - assumption: r_31 = 0.84 +/- 0.26  
  > "=0.84\pm0.26$ from (cite: Riechers2020COLF)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 3.6 or 0.4  
  > "$M_{\rm gas,\alpha_{CO}=3.6}=(2.8\pm0.9)" (Physical properties and lensing model); "$M_{\rm gas,\alpha_{CO}=0.4}=(3.1\pm1.0)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CO-derived gas masses agree with M_gas,CI within the alpha_CO uncertainty  
  > "The CO-derived gas masses agree with $M_{\rm gas,CI}$ within the uncertainty of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$." (Physical properties and lensing model)
10. **test causes of the photo-z failure**: Refit NIR-to-MIR SED at z=2.625 and z=7.458, with EAZY at F090W 3 sigma limit, with Bagpipes without the F090W limit, and with MIPS 24 micron included  
  > "we tested fitting NIR-to-MIR SEDs at both $z=2.625$ and $z=7.458$" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure); "fitting without the F090W upper limit" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure); "Therefore, we tested fitting the SED by including the MIPS 24" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure)
   - software: EAZY, Bagpipes
   - parameter: MIPS 24 micron S = 188.4 +/- 45.9 uJy  
  > "=188.4\pm45.9" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure)
   - parameter: F090W 3 sigma upper limit  
  > "by adopting the F090W $3\sigma$ upper limit" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Including 24 micron gives z_phot = 2.3 with chi2 two times smaller than the fixed z=2.625 fit without 24 micron  
  > "and the $\chi^2$ is two times smaller than that of the fitting at fixed $z=2.625$ without 24$\mu$m" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure)

## Qualitative findings

- The photo-z redshift PDF of Ling et al. showed an insignificant peak at z~2.6 from EAZY and CIGALE  
  > "shows an insignificant peak at $z\sim2.6$ from" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure)
- NIR-to-MIR photometry is degenerate between z~2.6 and z~7.5  
  > "fully degenerated between $z\sim2.6$ and $z\sim7.5$" (Possible causes of the catastrophic photo-z failure)
- Low-z solutions that look statistically insignificant in the PDF(z) cannot be ruled out; photo-z plus a single mm line may be insufficient  
  > "cannot be ruled out" (Caution on photo-z); "a combination of photo-$z$ with a single line detection in the mm might not be sufficient for a robust determination of the redshift of DSFGs" (Caution on photo-z)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: A second line was needed to settle the redshift; Band 6 DDT time was requested to detect [CII]  
  > "However, without a robust detection of a second line, the redshift of the source remained ambiguous." (ALMA); "Consequently, we proposed 0.5 hr of ALMA Band 6 observations through DDT time" (ALMA)
- Why this method: Aperture photometry on clean images because the source is resolved; modified blackbody fit because Stardust did not fit the 3 mm continuum  
  > "Given that COSBO-7 is resolved in the ALMA data, we measured the continuum and integrated line fluxes on the clean images" (ALMA); "which suggests a steep $\beta$ slope or optically thick dust in FIR (cite: Jin2022)" (Physical properties and lensing model)
- Alternative: z=7.458 solution (CO(7-6) at 95.4 GHz) rejected: no [CII] at 224.7 GHz, and lines match z=2.625  
  > "the DDT program did not detect any line at the expected frequency of 224.7 GHz of \[CII\] at $z=7.458$" (Redshift confirmation)

  > "we simply adopted $M_{\rm gas,CI}$ and derived a median gas depletion time of" (Physical properties and lensing model)
- Alternative: Optically thin versus thick dust in FIR fits
