## Analysis recipe

1. **Merge MSs and resample channels**: Merge calibrated MSs in CASA; resample to ~0.02 GHz channels; first tclean with natural weighting to check rms  
  > "we re-sampled the native spectral resolution to obtain $\sim0.02$ GHz channels ($\sim$ 50 km/s at the reference frequency of 100 GHz)" (2.1 ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting  
  > "employing natural weighting to maximize the sensitivity" (2.1 ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Median rms 0.2 mJy/beam, slightly better than requested  
  > "we verified that the median rms across the stacked channels was 0.2 mJy/beam" (2.1 ALMA observations and data reduction)
2. **Measure continuum flux at 3 mm**: tclean mfs with natural weighting after masking lines; aperture photometry in CARTA on 2 sigma contour  
  > "we perform an aperture photometry with CARTA (cite: CARTA_21)" (3.1 Continuum images)
   - software: CASA, tclean, CARTA, imfit
   - parameter: aperture = 2 sigma contour of the continuum image  
  > "by employing an aperture corresponding to the $2\sigma$ contour of the continuum image" (3.1 Continuum images)
   - parameter: detection threshold S/N>3, 3 sigma upper limits otherwise  
  > "We consider a source as robustly detected with a S/N$>$3, therefore we report a $3\sigma$ upper limit for the undetected targets." (Table 1 caption)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CASA imfit 2D profile fit; compatible within uncertainties  
  > "We verify that this estimate is compatible – within the estimated uncertainties – with the flux estimated through a 2D profile-fitting performed with the CASA task" (3.1 Continuum images)
3. **Search for emission lines**: uvcontsub, 0th moment map, natural-weight cube, boxcar convolution, S/N cubes, S/N spectrum extraction with interferopy  
  > "The presence of emission lines inside our datacubes is unveiled through a line-finding algorithm analogous to those employed in several previous studies" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - software: CASA, uvcontsub, immoments, tclean, Interferopy
   - parameter: boxcar widths 1-13 channels (60-780 km/s at 100 GHz)  
  > "We convolve the cleaned datacube with a series of boxcar kernels with variable widths between 1 and 13 channels" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - parameter: rms from sigma clipping in the inner primary beam  
  > "This quantity is computed through a sigma clipping performed on the inner region of the primary beam" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - parameter: extraction region = 2 sigma contour of the 0th moment map  
  > "We employ as extracting region the 2$\sigma$ contour of the 0th moment map obtained in step number 2." (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 0th moment overlaps the 3 GHz radio emission in all targets  
  > "In all the analyzed targets, the 0th moment significantly overlaps with the radio emission at 3GHz visible in the maps by (cite: Smolcic_17)." (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
4. **Assess line reliability**: Spurious probability following Jin et al. 2019 (N_Eff formula) and FINDCLUMPS positive/negative peak ratio  
  > "in this study we follow two complementary approaches" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - software: Interferopy
   - parameter: all lines S/N>6 have spurious probability < 1e-6 (Jin) and 100% reliability (Walter)  
  > "we can estimate for all of them a spurious probability lower than $10^{-6}$ following (cite: Jin_19)" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - assumption: Noise at phase centre approximately Gaussian (Jin method)  
  > "it relies on the hypothesis that the noise at the phase center of our observations is approximately Gaussian" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Two complementary reliability estimates agree  
  > "since they have a S/N higher than every negative peak in the analysed datacubes, we can estimate for all of them a 100% reliability following (cite: Walter_16)" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
5. **Measure integrated line fluxes**: 0th moment maps of continuum-subtracted lines; CARTA aperture photometry on 2 sigma contour  
  > "by performing an aperture photometry with CARTA on the $2\sigma$ contour of these maps, we measure the integrated line fluxes reported in Table 2" (3.2 Line identification and reliability)
   - software: CASA, immoments, CARTA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Derive spectroscopic redshifts**: Model the highest-S/N line as each CO transition for 0<z<8, test for second line, pick highest joint reliability; for single-line sources fix z at allowed values and choose lowest chi2 in CIGALE SED fit including the 3 mm continuum point  
  > "we consider the line with the highest S/N in each cube (i.e. that with the highest reliability) and model it as each of the CO transitions that should be visible in the redshift range $0<z<8$" (3.3 Redshift estimation)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: N_Eff for second line = number of CO transitions possible for the first line  
  > "the $N_{\rm Eff}$ just corresponds to the number of possible CO transitions with which we can model the first line" (3.3 Redshift estimation)
   - parameter: joint spurious probability = product of first and second line probabilities  
  > "we can finally estimate a joint spurious probability given by the product between the spurious probability of the first and the second line" (3.3 Redshift estimation)
   - assumption: Lines are CO or [CI](1-0) transitions  
  > "at least one line among the CO and \[CI\] transitions should be detected for almost all the redshifts in the range $0<z<8$" (2.1 ALMA observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: Single-line sources: best chi2 among candidate redshifts in CIGALE fit  
  > "the redshift with the best agreement between the modeled SED and the photometry (i.e. that with the lowest value of the $\chi^2$)" (3.3 Redshift estimation)
   - validation: Single-line redshifts are more uncertain; RSN-361 second line only tentative  
  > "It is important to notice how these redshifts based on a single detected line are clearly more uncertain than those relying on a double detection." (3.3 Redshift estimation)
7. **Test line profiles for double-peaked structure**: Fit single vs double Gaussian to the highest-S/N line and compare with partial F-test  
  > "In our case, the null hypothesis consists in the modeling of the line with a single Gaussian, while the alternative hypothesis consists in the modeling with two Gaussians." (3.4 Initial insights on the ISM kinematics)
   - parameter: significance threshold 0.05; 4 vs 7 free parameters (with residual continuum)  
  > "we perform a partial F-test (cite: Bevington_03) employing a threshold of 0.05 for the level of significance to reject the null hypothesis" (3.4 Initial insights on the ISM kinematics); "with four and seven free parameters, respectively, since we allow a residual continuum component" (3.4 Initial insights on the ISM kinematics)
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits; sampler, priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "the null hypothesis consists in the modeling of the line with a single Gaussian, while the alternative hypothesis consists in the modeling with two Gaussians." (Initial insights on the ISM kinematics)
   - validation: Double component statistically significant for 5/9 targets; resolution too coarse to distinguish disk vs merger  
  > "we obtain that the presence of a double component is statistically significant for five out of nine targets ($\sim 55\%$)" (3.4 Initial insights on the ISM kinematics)
8. **SED fitting for physical properties**: CIGALE with BC03 stellar populations, delayed-style exponentially declining SFH plus bursts, Charlot and Fall attenuation, Draine 2014 dust, Fritz 2006 torus, with redshift fixed to spec-z; SFR from L_IR (Kennicutt 2012)  
  > "we include the stellar emission through the stellar population libraries by (cite: Bruzual_03), combined through an exponentially declining star formation history with random bursts of star formation." (3.5 SED-Fitting); "The stellar attenuation is modeled following (cite: Charlot_00), while the dust thermal emission is included in the templates through the (cite: Draine_14) models." (3.5 SED-Fitting)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: AGN torus component added (Fritz et al. 2006)  
  > "by adding a dusty torus component as modeled by (cite: Fritz_06)" (3.5 SED-Fitting)
   - parameter: median radio slope assumed for sources lacking a second radio detection (q_TIR)  
  > "we assume the median slope computed on the rest of the sample" (3.5 SED-Fitting)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; flat LCDM with Omega_m=0.3, Omega_Lambda=0.7, h=0.7  
  > "we assume a (cite: Chabrier_03) Initial Mass Function (IMF) and a flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $[\Omega_m,\Omega_\Lambda,h]=[0.3,0.7,0.7]$" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: CIGALE fitting with parameters of Donevski et al. 2020; priors not stated  
  > "For all the models, we employ the parameters used by (cite: Donevski_20) in the analysis of their sample of DSFGs with CIGALE." (3.5 SED-Fitting)
   - validation: f_AGN = 0 for all targets; q_TIR in 2.45-2.55 consistent with star-forming galaxies  
  > "CIGALE reports a $f_{\rm AGN}=0$ for all our targets" (3.5 SED-Fitting); "We obtain that all the $q_{\rm TIR}$ are in the range \[2.45,2.55\]" (3.5 SED-Fitting)
9. **Derive molecular gas masses**: [CI](1-0) gas mass with the Papadopoulos et al. 2004 relation where [CI] detected; otherwise CO-based mass with alpha_CO and CO SLED rescaling to CO(1-0)

## Qualitative findings

- All nine galaxies have at least one bright line (S/N>6) and five have two lines  
  > "we managed to identify at least one bright emission line in all the targets and two lines in five out of nine objects" (Summary)
- Targets have shorter depletion times than main-sequence galaxies, indicating starbursts  
  > "Our targets result to have a shorter depletion time than main sequence galaxies." (4.3 Gas mass and depletion time)
- Targets are more gas rich, with longer depletion time, than comparison SMGs  
  > "We obtain that our RS-NIRdark galaxies are – on average – more gas rich than the SMGs analyzed in those studies" (4.3 Gas mass and depletion time)
- No AGN torus component required by the SED fit  
  > "we can safely conclude that the photometry of our galaxies can be correctly reproduced without requiring a dusty torus component" (3.5 SED-Fitting)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Main goal was to obtain spectroscopic redshifts, requiring the full band 3 scan  
  > "The main scientific goal of the observing program was to assess the spectroscopic redshifts of the nine targets." (2.1 ALMA observations and data reduction)
- Why this method: [CI] gas masses preferred because they depend on fewer assumptions than CO-based masses  
  > "since the values estimated from the \[CI\](1-0) line rely on less assumption than those based on the CO lines" (4.3 Gas mass and depletion time)
- Alternative: Double-peaked profiles could signal either a rotating structure or a late-stage major merger; not distinguished  
  > "The presence of two components with different velocities in our galaxies can be explained with both the presence of a rotating structure or as the signature of the late stage of a major merger." (3.4 Initial insights on the ISM kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Single-line redshifts are less certain; RSN-361 second line is tentative  
  > "It is important to notice how these redshifts based on a single detected line are clearly more uncertain than those relying on a double detection." (Redshift estimation)
- Limitation (authors): Stellar mass and main-sequence position are uncertain because UV/optical is obscured  
  > "the location on the main sequence strongly relies on the estimated stellar mass, that is quite uncertain for our targets" (4.2 Analysis of the physical properties)
- Limitation (authors): Evolution model is simplistic and ignores quenching and accretion  
  > "Clearly, this model does not account for any quenching mechanism" (4.4 Possible evolutionary path)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample does not allow comparison of double-peak fraction with other selections  
  > "the limited size of our sample does not allow us to unambiguously establish if this difference is due to the different selections" (3.4 Initial insights on the ISM kinematics)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Pilot sample of nine sources chosen for FIR detections, so not representative of RS-NIRdark galaxies
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas masses depend on assumed alpha_CO, X_CI and CO SLED applied without testing alternatives
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Double-peak fraction from low-S/N lines at ~1.4 arcsec resolution; F-test on few channels is sensitive to noise
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Constant-SFR evolutionary model used to claim progenitor status without comparison to number densities in this paper
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar masses poorly constrained with only upper limits in NIR
