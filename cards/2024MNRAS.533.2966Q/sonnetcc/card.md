## Analysis recipe

1. **Select red-Herschel sources and cross-match with ALMA archive**: Select S250<S350<S500 in H-ATLAS catalogues, cross-match to ALMA archive within 5 arcsec using PyVO  
  > "We crossmatch our catalog with public data from the ALMA archive using Python Virtual Observatory (PyVo) v1.5.1 and adopting a 5 arcseconds search radius." (2.1 Red H-ATLAS galaxies observed with ALMA)
   - software: PyVO
   - parameter: 5 arcsec search radius; PyVO v1.5.1  
  > "using Python Virtual Observatory (PyVo) v1.5.1 and adopting a 5 arcseconds search radius." (2.1 Red H-ATLAS galaxies observed with ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image the calibrated visibilities**: Combine MS per field; make dirty maps, estimate global rms with sigma clipping, then clean with tclean to 3 sigma using natural weighting  
  > "First, we create the dirty maps in which we use `Sigma clip` from Astropy v5.3.1 to obtain a global value of the noise r.m.s in the maps." (2.2 ALMA data); "As a second step for the imaging, we use the `tclean` task to produce clean maps of each field with a clean threshold of 3$\sigma$" (2.2 ALMA data)
   - software: CASA, tclean, Astropy sigma clip
   - parameter: sigma clip at +-5 sigma  
  > "The sigma clipping method removes all the data that have values outside a given threshold, in our case $\pm 5\sigma$." (2.2 ALMA data)
   - parameter: natural weighting; 216 x 216 pixels; 0.14-0.25 arcsec pixels  
  > "All the maps have a size of $216 \times 216$ pixels, with pixel sizes of 0.14–0.25 arcseconds" (2.2 ALMA data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Detect sources and set threshold from false detection rate**: Iterative pixel search above threshold within 16.6 arcsec of map centre, masking 1.5 times the beam around each detection; FDR from detections in sign-flipped maps; adopt 5 sigma  
  > "The detection is performed by searching the maps for pixels that have values above a certain threshold within a radius of 16.6 arcseconds to avoid the noisiest regions at the edges of the map." (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate); "we mask a region equal to 1.5 times the beam-size." (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate)
   - parameter: FDR from D_F/D_P with maps multiplied by -1; minimum threshold 3 sigma in the test; adopted 5 sigma  
  > "Then, we multiply the maps by $-1$ and run again the detection algorithm" (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: FDR below about 1 per cent at >5 sigma; results not significantly changed at 4.5 sigma  
  > "As it can be seen, at $>5\sigma$, the FDR drops below $\sim1$ per cent." (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate); "We note that the main results do not change significantly if we adopt a different threshold" (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate)
4. **Measure 1.3 mm flux densities**: Peak pixel flux (PB corrected) and aperture flux from growth curve with apertures 1.5 to 3 beam sizes, divided by the synthesized beam; calibration error added in quadrature  
  > "For each detected source, we measure the flux density at the brightest pixel and the corresponding value within a given aperture." (2.4 Flux density measurements); "we coadd the flux density within different apertures (from 1.5 to 3 times the beam-size) and correct them dividing by the synthesized beam." (2.4 Flux density measurements)
   - parameter: adopt flux where aperture growth curve becomes constant; sigma_ca = 0.1  
  > "We adopt the flux density value where its aperture growth curve becomes constant." (2.4 Flux density measurements)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Remove AGN and low-redshift interlopers**: Discard ALMA detections within 2 arcsec of a MILLIQUAS or SDSS DR17 source  
  > "Any ALMA detection lying at $\leq 2\,$ arcsecond from a source in the MILLIQUAS and SDSS catalogs is considered a match and is therefore discarded from our analysis." (2.5 Sample cleaning)
   - parameter: 43 sources removed  
  > "We classified 43 ALMA sources as AGN or galaxies at $z<1$." (2.5 Sample cleaning)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Re-estimate deblended SPIRE flux densities**: Simultaneous 2D Gaussian fits in 120x120 arcsec postage stamps, with fixed FWHM from Neptune PSF and fixed positions from ALMA and 250 um sources; free amplitude and background  
  > "Third, we performed a simultaneous 2D Gaussian fit for each source detected in the postage stamp" (2.6 Flux density estimation in SPIRE maps); "we fix the FWHM of the Gaussian to the FWHM measured from the Neptune PSF maps and fix the position to that from the ALMA and the 250 $\mu$m maps." (2.6 Flux density estimation in SPIRE maps)
   - parameter: 250 um neighbours found at 4 sigma outside the 16.6 arcsec ALMA radius  
  > "we searched for 250 $\mu$m-detected sources (using a 4$\sigma$ threshold) outside of our ALMA detection radius (16.6 arcsecond)" (2.6 Flux density estimation in SPIRE maps)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian with free amplitude and background; errors combined with confusion noise and calibration  
  > "The Gaussian amplitude and the background level are set as free parameters." (2.6 Flux density estimation in SPIRE maps)
   - validation: Compared with H-ATLAS catalogue flux densities; very good agreement, 500 um catalogue values about 10 per cent higher  
  > "Overall, there is a very good agreement between the two sets of values, with a small dispersion." (2.6 Flux density estimation in SPIRE maps); "are $\sim10$ per cent higher than our deblended estimates." (2.6 Flux density estimation in SPIRE maps)
7. **Classify fields into S, PLUM, M and N**: By number and separation of 5 sigma detections within 16.6 arcsec; 3 arcsec separation separates PLUM from M; visual inspection for arcs  
  > "Fields that have only a single detection above 5$\sigma$ within the 16.6 arcseconds search radius are classified as S - Fields." (3.1.1 S - Fields); "Potential Lenses and Unidentified Mergers (PLUM - Fields) are fields with at least two detections separated by no more than 3 arcseconds, which may be lensed systems or closely interacting galaxies." (3.1.2 PLUM - Fields); "Multiple fields include those with two or more sources that are separated by more than 3 arcseconds." (3.1.3 M - Fields)
   - parameter: visual inspection of whole sample for arc-like or extended structures larger than the synthesized beam found 46 cases  
  > "An additional visual inspection of the whole sample was performed to identify arc-like or extended structures (larger than the synthesized beam)" (3.1.2 PLUM - Fields)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature-confirmed lenses; 12 PLUM-Fields are robust lenses or show strong arcs  
  > "we identified 12 fields that are robust lenses confirmed in the literature" (3.1.2 PLUM - Fields)
8. **Derive photometric redshift, L_IR and SFR from SPIRE+ALMA photometry**: Fit modified black bodies to deblended SPIRE and ALMA 1.3 mm photometry with MMPz, pick best set via lambda_peak-L_IR relation; SFR from L_IR via Kennicutt relation; for PLUM-Fields total ALMA flux is sum of components  
  > "This software fits modified black bodies to photometric data-points and obtains the most probable combinations of $z_{\rm phot}$, $L_{\mathrm{IR}}$ and $\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}}$." (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates); "For PLUM - Fields, the total ALMA 1.3 mm flux density is estimated by adding the flux density from individual components with their uncertainties added in quadrature." (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
   - software: Millimeter Photometric Redshift (MMPz)
   - parameter: lambda_0 = 102.8 um, L_t = 1e12 Lsun, eta = -0.068  
  > "where $\lambda_0=102.8\ \pm\ 0.4\ \mu$m, $L_t=10^{12}$ L$_\odot$ and $\eta=-0.068\ \pm\ 0.001$." (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
   - parameter: SFR = 1.48e-10 L_IR  
  > "$\mathrm{SFR \,[M_{\odot}\,yr^{-1}]}= 1.48\times 10^{-10} \ L_{\mathrm{IR} }\mathrm{\,[L_{\odot}]}$" (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
   - assumption: Kroupa IMF  
  > "which assumes a (cite: Kroupa_2001) IMF." (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
   - assumption: LambdaCDM with H0 = 70, Omega_M = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7  
  > "In this work we assume a $\Lambda$CDM cosmology using ${H}_{0}=70\,\mathrm{km}\,{{\rm{s}}}^{-1}\,{\mathrm{Mpc}}^{-1}$, ${{\rm{\Omega }}}_{{\rm{M}}}=0.3$ and ${{\rm{\Omega }}}_{{\rm{\Lambda }}}=0.7$." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: Modified black body fits; sampler, priors and likelihood not given  
  > "This software fits modified black bodies to photometric data-points" (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
   - validation: Photometric vs literature spectroscopic redshift difference about 8 per cent; SPIRE-only fit gave loose constraints and was rejected  
  > "is $\sim$8 per cent. Thus, our photometric redshifts are accurate enough for a statistical characterization" (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates); "This alternative, however, resulted in loose constraints on the SFR and, in some cases, overestimated redshifts" (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
9. **Derive gas and dust masses**: Gas mass from the Scoville et al. 2016 relation using 1.3 mm flux density; dust mass from optically thin Rayleigh-Jeans emission with k(nu)=k0 (nu/nu850)^beta  
  > "To infer the gas mass content of the galaxies in our sample, we use the relation given by" (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses); "Dust masses are estimated using:" (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses)
   - parameter: alpha_850 = (6.17 +- 1.7)e19 erg/s/Hz/Msun  
  > "where $\alpha_{850}=(6.17\pm1.7)\times 10^{19}$ ergs s$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$ M$_{\odot}^{-1}$" (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses)
   - parameter: k0 = 0.07 m^2 kg^-1  
  > "assuming $k_0=0.07\,\mathrm{m^2\,kg^{-1} }$" (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses)
   - assumption: T = 25 K mass-weighted cold gas temperature, beta = 1.8  
  > "We assume a temperature of 25 K, a common mass-weighted value for the cold molecular gas, and a dust emissivity index $\beta = 1.8$." (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses)
   - assumption: T_dust = 25 K for dust masses  
  > "for which we adopt a value of 25 K typical of high-$z$ SMG." (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Mean ratio about 0.8 between these gas masses and CO-based gas masses for 21 sources  
  > "we find a mean ratio of $\sim 0.8$ between the gas masses estimated in this work and those inferred from the CO line detections." (3.3 Gas and Dust Masses)
10. **Test 1.3 mm flux density as a lensing criterion and derive multiplicity and depletion times**: Compare S(1.3mm) with PLUM/S-Field class and archival higher-resolution data; count multiples; tau_dep = M_gas/SFR compared with main-sequence and starburst relations  
  > "almost all sources with $S_{\rm 1.3mm}  \geq 13.0$ mJy correspond to PLUM - Fields, with the exception of 4 S - Fields." (4.1 Flux densities and colours); "The depletion times ($\tau_{\mathrm{dep}} = M_{\mathrm{gas}}/\mathrm{SFR}$)" (4.6 Depletion times and Star formation efficiencies)
   - parameter: S(1.3mm) >= 13.0 mJy threshold  
  > "Hence we postulate that DSFG with $S_{\rm 1.3\,mm}\geq13.0$ mJy are most likely gravitationally amplified." (4.1 Flux densities and colours)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 1 alternative scaling for gas masses  
  > "adopting $\alpha_{\mathrm{CO}}=1 \mathrm{M_\odot(K \,km\ ,s^{-1}\,pc^{2})^{-1}}$" (4.5 Gas and Dust Mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Three of four exceptional S-Fields are clearly lensed in higher-resolution archival ALMA data; fourth has CO detections departing from L_CO-FWHM relation  
  > "are clearly gravitationally lensed at higher angular resolution ($<1$ arcsecond) ALMA archival data" (4.1 Flux densities and colours)

## Qualitative findings

- Red-Herschel galaxies are mostly single systems at 1 arcsec resolution  
  > "73 per cent of our detections are single systems, suggesting that most of these galaxies are isolated and/or post-merger galaxies." (abstract)
- Redshift distribution of single red-Herschel sources resembles that of classic SMGs selected at longer wavelengths  
  > "The general agreement between these studies and our estimations suggests that the selection of red-*Herschel* sources overlaps with that of the classic SMG population selected at longer wavelengths" (4.3 Redshift Distribution)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 6 chosen because it dominates the archive sample and has homogeneous depth and resolution  
  > "We thus focus our analysis on this Band 6 data-set, which benefits from an homogeneous depth and angular resolution." (2.1 Red H-ATLAS galaxies observed with ALMA)
- Why this method: 5 sigma threshold chosen for low false detection rate  
  > "Therefore, to be conservative, in this work we use a 5$\sigma$ detection threshold." (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate)
- Alternative: SED fit using only the three SPIRE points rejected for loose constraints and overestimated redshifts  
  > "This alternative, however, resulted in loose constraints on the SFR and, in some cases, overestimated redshifts" (3.2 Redshifts, Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
- Alternative: 4.5 sigma detection threshold gives similar main results  
  > "We note that the main results do not change significantly if we adopt a different threshold" (2.3 Source Detection and False Detection Rate)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Gas masses derive from the same ALMA photometry as the SED-based SFR, so tau_dep is not independent  
  > "These results, however, should be taken with caution since the $M_{\mathrm{gas}}$ are inferred from the ALMA 1.3 mm photometry and are not totally independent from the SED-based SFR determinations." (4.6 Depletion times and Star formation efficiencies)
- Limitation (authors): SFR upper limit is a loose constraint since some S-Fields may be lensed  
  > "However, this limit only represents a loose constraint (probably being overestimated) since some of the S - Fields might still be contaminated by gravitational lensing." (4.4 Infrared Luminosities and Star Formation Rates)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Parameters of the MMPz fit and its sensitivity to the 25 K / beta = 1.8 assumptions are not tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text gives 474 and 475 for the number of M-Fields in different places
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample is archival and heterogeneous; selection biases of the original ALMA proposals are not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Photometric redshift scatter of 8 per cent is from a small spectroscopic subset
