## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the mosaics**: Run the delivered pipeline scripts in CASA, then make natural-weighted mosaic cubes (25 and 40 km/s channels) and mfs continuum images with tclean  
  > "We calibrated the data of both band 3 and 6 observations by running the pipeline scripts" (Data reduction); "by adopting a natural weighting scheme, and by using “mosaic” as a gridding convolution function" (Data reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Pixel sizes 0.2 arcsec (Band 3) and 0.15 arcsec (Band 6)  
  > "we set pixel sizes of $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}2$ and $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}15$ for band 3 and 6 data, respectively" (Data reduction)
   - parameter: Cleaned cubes and images made with tight apertures on >=5 sigma sources, cleaning down to 2 sigma  
  > "we obtained “cleaned” data cubes and continuum images by setting tight circular apertures on $\ge 5\sigma$ sources in their continuum" (Data reduction); "down to $2\sigma$" (Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Blind search for 1.2-mm continuum, CO(4-3) and 3-mm continuum sources**: LineSeeker matched-filter search on dirty images and cubes (Gaussian spectral kernels), DBSCAN grouping, fidelity from negative detections; cuts at S/N>=3 then fidelity F>=90%  
  > "LineSeeker adopts a matched-filter approach." (Source search and characterization); "corresponding to unique sources by using the Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Application with Noise algorithm (cite: Ester+1996)" (Source search and characterization)
   - software: LineSeeker, scikit-learn
   - parameter: 1.2-mm continuum: S/N>=3 detections, F>=90% (S/N>=4.7), excluding PB response below 50%  
  > "We therefore extracted all ${\rm S/N}\ge 3$ detections, and we selected the source candidates with estimated fidelity of $F\ge 90\%$ corresponding to ${\rm S/N}\ge 4.7$." (1.2-mm continuum-selected candidates); "excluding the region with PB response $<50\%$ in which the low telescope sensitivity enhances the fraction of spurious candidates" (1.2-mm continuum-selected candidates)
   - parameter: CO(4-3): cube binned at 25 km/s, Gaussian kernels of 0 to 18 channels, S/N>=3, then F>=90%  
  > "using Gaussian kernels with widths ranging from 0 to 18 channels" (CO(4–3) line-emitting candidates); "We extracted all line-emitting candidates with estimated ${\rm S/N}\ge 3$." (CO(4–3) line-emitting candidates)
   - assumption: Noise Gaussian; fidelity F = 1 - N_neg/N_pos from the search on the negated cube  
  > "LineSeeker assumes that the noise in the data is Gaussian distributed" (Source search and characterization); "the code is run on the negative (i.e., multiplied by $-1$) cube or image" (Source search and characterization)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Dirty data preferred because they preserve the noise properties  
  > "The “dirty” data are preferred over the “cleaned” ones since in the former the intrinsic properties of the noise are preserved." (1.2-mm continuum-selected candidates)
3. **Add low-fidelity sources by cross-matching with MUSE**: Cross-match LineSeeker candidates with F>20% against the MUSE z>2.5 catalog within 0.6 arcsec  
  > "we cross-matched the on-sky spatial position of the MUSE and ALMA continuum sources within a separation limit of $0\rlap{.}{\ensuremath{''}}6$" (1.2-mm continuum-selected candidates); "with the low-fidelity ($F> 20\%$) sources selected by LineSeeker" (1.2-mm continuum-selected candidates)
   - software: LineSeeker
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure completeness and flux boosting by source injection**: Inject artificial point-like continuum sources and Gaussian-profile line sources into the dirty data, rescaled by PB response, re-run LineSeeker; recovered if within 1 arcsec with F>=90%  
  > "We injected artificial sources in the real data and we then performed the source search by using LINESEEKER." (Completeness and Flux Boosting); "We considered a source recovered if it is extracted within $1\ensuremath{''}$ from its input location with a fidelity $\ge 90\%$." (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - software: LineSeeker
   - parameter: Continuum: 20 sources per image, 100 iterations per flux value, 0.02-0.46 mJy in steps of 0.02 mJy (46000 sources)  
  > "We repeated this procedure by injecting $20$ sources simultaneously in the image and iterating for $100$ times for each 1.2-mm flux density value within the range $0.02-0.46\,{\rm mJy}$ in steps of $0.02\,{\rm mJy}$." (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - parameter: Lines: FWHM 100-1000 km/s and flux 0.02-0.5 Jy km/s, 50 sources per cube, 12500 sources total  
  > "we injected simultaneously 50 sources in the cube for a total of 12500 sources injected in the whole simulation" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - assumption: Injected line is a Gaussian with the input FWHM  
  > "Here we assumed that the recovered line is a Gaussian with the input FWHM." (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Result: continuum completeness 50-100% in the flux range of detected sources; line completeness for fluxes >~0.4 Jy km/s; flux boosting negligible  
  > "As a result, the completeness in the flux range of the detected sources is in the range $50-100\%$" (Completeness and Flux Boosting); "As a result the selected sample is complete for line fluxes $\gtrsim 0.4\,{\rm Jy\,km\,s^{-1}}$" (Completeness and Flux Boosting)
5. **Identify low-z interlopers**: Use MUSE spectra, FORS2 UBR colours and inspection of band 3 and 6 cubes for other lines; compare with expected counts from the blank-field CO LF  
  > "In order to unambiguously identify the emission lines observed in our survey, we made use of our multiwavelength datasets of the MQN01 field." (Identification of interlopers across the redshift range); "By using the available best-fit model to the CO LFs from (cite: Boogaard+2023), we computed the number of expected galaxies at various redshifts" (Identification of interlopers across the redshift range)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Five interlopers found, consistent with predictions from blank-field CO LFs  
  > "we unambiguously identified five interlopers located to different redshifts with respect to the QSO" (Identification of interlopers across the redshift range); "these findings are consistent to our predictions based on the CO LFs of blank fields" (Identification of interlopers across the redshift range)
6. **Measure continuum fluxes, CO line fluxes and luminosities**: Continuum: 2-sigma-clipped photometry on cleaned images; CO: iterative Gaussian plus constant fit of spectra summed over S/N>=2 pixels of the moment-0 map, then a final MCMC fit; luminosities from Solomon et al. formulae  
  > "We then scaled the source spectrum by the PB response, and we performed a fit using a Gaussian profile for the line and a constant for the underlying 3-mm dust continuum" (Source fluxes and luminosities); "We repeated the aforementioned steps a few times until convergence." (Source fluxes and luminosities)
   - software: CASA, SciPy, emcee
   - parameter: Spectra extracted from the cleaned Band 3 cube binned at 40 km/s  
  > "we extracted the spectrum at the peak position of the source from the “cleaned” ALMA band 3 datacube binned at $40\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$" (Source fluxes and luminosities)
   - parameter: QSO and Object B fluxes measured on the ~0.25 arcsec band 3 data where they are resolved  
  > "To measure their fluxes in what follows, we employed the ALMA band 3 high-resolution" (Source fluxes and luminosities)
   - assumption: No CMB correction applied to continuum or line fluxes  
  > "in this work we opted to not apply the CMB-related corrections (cite: Decarli+2020; Boogaard+2023)" (Source fluxes and luminosities)
   - fitting: emcee ensemble sampler, flat priors based on the last iteration best-fit, Gaussian likelihood  
  > "through the Python Monte Carlo Markow Chain (MCMC) ensemble sampler" (Source fluxes and luminosities); "We employed flat priors on the basis of the best-fit parameters derived from the last iteration and we assume Gaussian uncertainties in the definition of the likelihood." (Source fluxes and luminosities)
   - validation: Spectra stable after fewer than 10 iterations  
  > "All the extracted source spectra remain stable after a few ($<10$) iterations." (Source fluxes and luminosities)
7. **Derive the CO(4-3) luminosity function, cumulative counts and overdensity**: Monte Carlo of 1000 LF realizations varying luminosity, FWHM and fidelity; Poisson errors in 0.5 dex bins, divided by completeness, volume and bin width; overdensity as ratio of cumulative counts to blank-field counts  
  > "To obtain the CO LF in the MQN01 field, we performed a Monte Carlo simulation of 1000 independent realizations of the LF." (CO Luminosity Function Analysis); "by computing the bin-to-bin ratios between the cumulative number counts in MQNQ01 and those in blank fields" (CO Luminosity Function Analysis)
   - parameter: Volumes 2395 cMpc^3 (within 4000 km/s) and 599 cMpc^3 (within 1000 km/s)  
  > "The corresponding cosmological volumes are respectively $\Delta V_{4000}=2395\,{\rm cMpc^{3}}$, and $\Delta V_{1000}=599\,{\rm cMpc^{3}}$." (CO Luminosity Function Analysis)
   - parameter: Bins of 0.5 dex, repeated five times with 0.1 dex shifts  
  > "We repeated the entire simulation five times by shifting the luminosity bins of $0.1\,{\rm dex}$" (CO Luminosity Function Analysis)
   - assumption: Secure sources have fidelity 1; other sources' fidelity is treated as an upper limit; no completeness correction in the 1000 km/s volume  
  > "For secure sources, we fixed the fidelity value to $F=1$, for all the other sources we treated the fidelity as upper limit." (CO Luminosity Function Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with blank-field Schechter fit and SIDES, recomputed with common luminosity bins  
  > "we recomputed LFs consistently as described above adopting common luminosity bins" (CO Luminosity Function Analysis)
8. **Compute 1.2-mm source number counts with k-correction**: Differential and cumulative counts of continuum sources with spectroscopic redshift within 1000 km/s of the QSO, compared with ASPECS and SSA22 after k-correcting fluxes to 1.2 mm at z0=3.25  
  > "This selection yielded to a sample of six sources (including the QSO and the Object B" (1.2-mm continuum source number counts); "Under such assumptions and by using a reference redshift of $z_{0}=3.25$" (1.2-mm continuum source number counts)
   - parameter: Common bins of 0.4 dex, repeated twice with 0.1 dex shifts  
  > "We used common bins for all the different fields corresponding to $\Delta\,{\rm log}\,S=0.4$, and we repeated the simulation two times by shifting the flux bins of $0.1\,{\rm dex}$." (1.2-mm continuum source number counts)
   - parameter: All fidelities set to 1; completeness corrections ignored  
  > "Since all the sources involved in this computation are detected via multiple tracers, we set the fidelity of all sources to $1$." (1.2-mm continuum source number counts); "In addition, we opted to ignore the completeness corrections which we expect to be not relevant due to the large uncertainties involved in such analysis." (1.2-mm continuum source number counts)
   - assumption: Modified black body with T_dust = 20-45 K and beta = 1.5-2.0, CMB contrast included  
  > "Here we estimated $k_{\rm \lambda obs}$ by assuming a modified black body emission with typical dust temperature ranging in $T_{\rm dust}=20-45\,{\rm K}$, and spectral index $\beta=1.5-2.0$." (1.2-mm continuum source number counts)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Overdensity consistent with the CO-based one and with SSA22, but analysis stated to be affected by large uncertainties  
  > "We stress that such analysis is affected by large uncertainties due to low statistics of the selected samples." (Discussion)
9. **Estimate the molecular gas density and core gas mass**: Sum of H2 masses weighted by F/C over the volume, via Monte Carlo varying measurements, r41 and alpha_CO  
  > "we computed $\rho({\rm H_{2}})$ by performing a Monte Carlo Simulation by varying all the measurements within their uncertainties" (Molecular gas density in MQN01 field)
   - parameter: r41 = 0.61 +/- 0.13; alpha_CO drawn uniformly in 0.8-4.3  
  > "We adopted $r_{41}=0.61\pm0.13$ obtained via modeling of the CO ladders of sources at $z=2.0-2.7$ in the ASPECS field (cite: Boogaard+2020)." (Molecular gas density in MQN01 field); "we varied $\alpha_{\rm CO}/(M_{\astrosun}\,({\rm K\,km\,s^{-1}\,pc^{2}})^{-1})$ uniformly in the range $0.8-4.3$" (Molecular gas density in MQN01 field)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8 for the core (quasar hosts and SMGs)  
  > "assuming $\alpha_{\rm CO} = 0.8\,M_{\astrosun}\,({\rm K\,km\,s^{-1}\,pc^{2}})^{-1}$ typical of quasar hosts and SMGs (cite: Downes+1998)" (Halo mass estimate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with blank-field molecular gas density across cosmic time and with the Spiderweb protocluster  
  > "In Fig. [10](#fig:rho_h2), we compare our results with the molecular gas density across cosmic time in blank fields as reported by several works in the literature." (Molecular gas density in MQN01 field)
10. **Analyse the velocity distribution and estimate the halo mass**: Lilliefors test on core galaxies within 1000 km/s; M200 from the Evrard et al. sigma-M relation, iterating on members within R_vir until convergence, with Monte Carlo errors  
  > "we employed the scaling relation found by (cite: Evrard+2008) which is based on a suite of $N$-body simulations with various cosmologies" (Halo mass estimate); "We then iterated the procedure by selecting all galaxies within $R_{\rm vir}$ until convergence." (Halo mass estimate)
   - software: Statsmodel
   - parameter: Core galaxies: |dv_QSO| < 1000 km/s  
  > "We took all galaxies within $\left | \Delta\varv_{\rm QSO} \right |< 1000\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}}$" (Halo mass estimate)
   - parameter: Uncertainties by Monte Carlo sampling, 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles  
  > "where the nominal values and uncertainties are computed with a Monte Carlo sampling and by taking the 5th, 50th, and 95th percentile of the distribution" (Halo mass estimate)
   - assumption: Core galaxies virialized in one spherical halo; line-of-sight velocities purely peculiar  
  > "Under the assumption that the observed galaxies in the “core” of MQN01 field are virialized within a large dark matter (DM) halo" (Halo mass estimate); "we have assumed that the halo is spherical and that thus the velocity dispersion can be simply derived from the line-of-sight velocity" (Halo mass estimate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Lilliefors p-value 0.94, cannot reject Gaussianity but small numbers prevent strong conclusions  
  > "and we obtained a $p$-value of $0.94$" (Galaxy velocity distribution); "but the small number statistics prevents here any strong conclusion" (Galaxy velocity distribution)
11. **Measure the QSO-CO emitter cross-correlation length**: Cylindrical-shell pair counts around the QSO vs expected blank-field pairs from the Boogaard et al. LF; fit r0 with fixed gamma = 1.8 using emcee and a Poisson likelihood; repeated 900 times over LF-parameter realizations  
  > "adopting a Poisson maximum likelihood estimator (cite: Hennawi+2015; Garcia-Vergara+2017) and a fixed slope of $\gamma=1.8$" (Clustering analysis); "we repeated the fit 900 times by using the different realizations of $\left\langle {\rm QR}(R)\right\rangle$ computed as described above" (Clustering analysis)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: Constant 5 sigma limiting luminosity L'lim = 2.8e9 K km/s pc^2 for FWHM = 331 km/s  
  > "constant limiting luminosity at $5\sigma$ of $L'_{\rm lim} =2.8\times10^{9}\,{\rm K\,km\,s^{-1}\,pc^{2}}$" (Clustering analysis)
   - assumption: No completeness correction applied; Poisson noise dominates each bin  
  > "we then opted to do not apply the completeness correction" (Clustering analysis)
   - fitting: emcee with Poisson maximum-likelihood estimator, fixed slope gamma = 1.8; final r0 from 50th, 16th and 84th percentiles of the summed posteriors  
  > "We finally computed the best-fit $r_{0}$ value and its $1\sigma$ uncertainties by taking the 50th, 16th, and 84th percentile, respectively" (Clustering analysis)
   - validation: Tested a radially declining sensitivity limit, which raises r0 to 23 cMpc  
  > "such that the fit of our data yields a larger best-fit correlation length of $r_{0, \rm CO}=23^{+22}_{-10}\,{\rm cMpc}$" (Clustering analysis)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Mosaic ALMA observations to cover the MUSE field and obtain a full picture of the field in CO(4-3) and 1.2-mm and 3-mm continuum  
  > "To obtain a full picture of this exceptional field we used ALMA to perform mosaic observations targeting the mm dust continuum and the CO(4–3) rotational transition in galaxies embedded in MQN01." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Source search done on dirty data because they preserve the noise properties  
  > "The “dirty” data are preferred over the “cleaned” ones since in the former the intrinsic properties of the noise are preserved." (1.2-mm continuum-selected candidates)
- Alternative: CMB-related corrections were considered but not applied because the excitation temperature and LTE are unverified  
  > "in this work we opted to not apply the CMB-related corrections (cite: Decarli+2020; Boogaard+2023)" (Source fluxes and luminosities)
- Alternative: Completeness correction not applied to the 1.2-mm counts because of large uncertainties  
  > "In addition, we opted to ignore the completeness corrections which we expect to be not relevant due to the large uncertainties involved in such analysis." (1.2-mm continuum source number counts)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Last Band 6 execution block had increased noise, reducing sensitivity in the northern part of the field by about 30%  
  > "The last EB of band 6 observations has been affected by increased noise during the last two scans" (Survey design)
- Limitation (authors): Line-only sources L05-L08 could be false positives or interlopers  
  > "Therefore, we cannot rule out that the latters sources actually represent either false-positive detections or interlopers located at different redshift." (Identification of interlopers across the redshift range)
- Limitation (authors): Halo mass estimate rests on strong assumptions and low number statistics  
  > "we note that such estimates involve a set of assumptions and therefore has to be taken with caution" (Halo mass estimate)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Very small samples (11 CO emitters, 6 continuum sources in the 1000 km/s volume) make the LF shape and bright-end flattening weakly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Blank-field comparison depends on rescaled literature LFs and different selections (ASPECS and SSA22 at other redshifts and wavelengths), so overdensity values carry systematics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fidelity treatment for non-secure sources as upper limits is ad hoc
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Table 1 and the text differ slightly on observing dates and Band 3 on-source time (16.3 vs 16.4 hours; January 6 vs January 16)
