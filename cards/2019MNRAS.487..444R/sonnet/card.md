## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image CO(2-1) cubes**: CASA calibration, uv continuum subtraction, self-cal applied, natural-weighted imaging at 2.6 km/s  
  > "Data were calibrated and imaged using CASA 4.7.0 (cite: McMullin2007)." (3 Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make moment maps M0, M1, M2**: Moments computed with 4 sigma threshold using all channels with CO emission  
  > "we generated moment maps assuming a threshold of $4\sigma$ with the noise level shown in Table [\[obsPar\]](#obsPar)." (3.1 Moment maps)
   - parameter: channels with CO emission, typically 250 to 600  
  > "All channels with visible CO emission (typically 250 to 600 channels) were used in the calculation of the moment maps." (3.1 Moment maps)
   - assumption: M2 treated as upper limit on intrinsic dispersion  
  > "even though they show the upper limit of the intrinsic velocity dispersion especially in the nuclear regions" (3.1 Moment maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit Bertola rotation model to velocity maps**: Fit eq. of Bertola 1991 to M1 map, five parameters free, p in 1.0-1.5, also fit centre; minimise rms of residuals  
  > "allowing five of the six parameters of the model to vary freely, with the sixth ($p$) allowed to vary in the range $1.0 - 1.5$." (4.1 Bertola model)
   - software: MADS (NOMAD)
   - assumption: circular orbits in a spherical potential  
  > "we assume circular orbits in a spherical potential as given in (cite: Bertola1991)." (4.1 Bertola model)
   - fitting: MADS direct search, minimising rms of residuals  
  > "The best-fitting parameters were obtained by minimising a root-mean-square value of the residuals" (4.1 Bertola model)
   - validation: 1000 simulated velocity maps recovered within 1%  
  > "In all our simulations, the optimisation routine was able to recover the parameters to within an error of 1%." (4.3.1 Optimisation algorithm)
4. **Run modified Kinemetry (modKin) on M1 maps**: Fourier decomposition in elliptical rings; 4 terms for PA, flattening and centre, then rerun at fixed values with ten Fourier terms; velocity map weighted 1/M1err^2  
  > "Upon obtaining these values, we ran the *modKin* code again at these fixed values for ten Fourier terms." (4.2.1 Modifications to Kinemetry)
   - software: modKin (MATLAB), MADS (NOMAD)
   - parameter: COVER = 0.50; 100 ellipses initially; ring spacing set by beam size  
  > "we provided the *COVER* parameter of 0.50 as input along with the boundary values for the centre coordinates." (4.2.1 Modifications to Kinemetry)
   - fitting: MADS replaces grid plus Levenberg-Marquardt; bounds Gamma in [-90,90], q in [0,1]  
  > "for the position angle ($\Gamma \in [-90, 90]$) and the flattening ($q \in [0, 1]$)" (4.2.1 Modifications to Kinemetry)
   - validation: 1000 simulated maps recovered within 1%  
  > "We tested the adopted optimisation algorithm with the Bertola model and *modKin* code on 1000 different simulated velocity maps with known sets of parameters." (4.3.1 Optimisation algorithm)
5. **Determine kinematic centres**: Search best-fit centre within 5 arcsec of continuum peak; adopt modKin; new estimate only if offset from 2MASS exceeds beam major axis  
  > "we consider the centre coordinates as a new estimate only if the difference in value is larger than the synthesized beam major axis." (4.1 Kinematic centres)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Bertola and modKin agree within a few pixels  
  > "The result obtained from both the models seem to agree within a few ($\pm5$) pixels." (4.1 Kinematic centres)
6. **Classify kinematics and compute photometric-kinematic misalignment**: Classification by SAURON/ATLAS3D scheme; misalignment via Franx 1991 sinPsi relation, misaligned if Psi>10 deg  
  > "We classify all galaxies with $\Psi > 10^{\circ}$ as being misaligned." (5.3 Misalignment)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- All seven galaxies show deviations from pure circular rotation in the inner kpc: twists, decoupled and counter-rotating cores  
  > "reveals various deviations from pure circular rotation in the inner kiloparsec of all seven galaxies, including kinematic twists, decoupled and counter-rotating cores." (abstract)
- CO and stellar PA and Vsys agree, inclination differs more  
  > "show overall agreement in the position angle of the major axis and the systemic velocity, but larger discrepancies in the disc inclination." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: 2D modelling used because 3D packages lack features to constrain non-axisymmetric structure  
  > "we only consider the 2D modelling approach owing to the lack of features in the mentioned 3D packages to constrain or demarcate the non-axisymmetric features effectively." (4.4 3D-modelling algorithms)
- Alternative: 3D codes GalPaK, 3DBarolo, TiRiFiC not used  
  > "Some of the well-known packages are GalPaK (cite: Bouche2015), $^{3{D}}$BAROLO (cite: DiTeodoro2015) and TiRiFiC (cite: Jozsa2007)." (4.4 3D-modelling algorithms)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Results rely on intensity-weighted mean velocity maps; non-axisymmetric features not discussed  
  > "the results reported in this work are primarily based on the interpretation of the intensity weighted mean velocity maps." (5.3 Global results)
- Limitation (authors): NGC 7213 sparse data prevented modKin fits  
  > "NGC 7213 (was impossible to obtain meaningful fits from owing to the sparse information)" (5.2 Local kinematic results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Moment-1 maps with 4 sigma clipping can bias velocities near the nucleus due to beam smearing
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar comparison values come from heterogeneous literature sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Uncertainties quoted only as 'within 5%' without a rigorous error analysis
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interpretations (mergers, external gas origin) are speculative
