## Qualitative findings

- First robust interstellar MC detection toward G358.93 MM1  
  > "Using ALMA 1 mm observations, we present the first robust interstellar detection of methyl carbamate ($\rm CH_{3}OC(O)NH_{2}$, MC) toward the hot molecular core G358.93 MM1." (Conclusions)
- MC emission nearly co-spatial with the 1 mm continuum peak  
  > "As shown in Figure [4](#fig:4), the MC emission is nearly co-spatial with the 1 mm continuum peak of G358.93 MM1." (Spectrum Extraction Position And Spatial distribution of MC)
- MC is the most abundant C2H5O2N isomer detected in this source to date  
  > "establishing MC as the most abundant $\rm C_{2}H_{5}O_{2}N$ isomer detected in this source to date." (Conclusions)
- Observed isomer abundances favor pathway-specific kinetics over equilibrium chemistry  
  > "Taken together, the systematic deviation from thermodynamic trends across two chemically distinct environments shows that the $\rm C_{2}H_{5}O_{2}N$ family is not governed by equilibrium chemistry." (Detectability of several key isomers in the $\rm C_{2}H_{5}O_{2}N$ family)
- MC-to-precursor ratios broadly consistent across environments within current uncertainties  
  > "these ratios are broadly consistent across sources within current uncertainties." (Formation of methyl carbamate in space)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: G358.93 MM1 already has securely detected glycolamide; models predict comparable MC abundances in HMCs.  
  > "In particular, (cite: Gar22) showed that MC can reach abundances in HMCs comparable to that of GA." (Introduction); "The HMC G358.93 MM1, where glycolamide has already been securely detected (cite: Duan26), thus provides a well-motivated target in which to search for methyl carbamate." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Choose an offset spectrum for clean MC lines; use a full source model to assess blends and 13CH3OH to avoid optically thick methanol lines.  
  > "This position was selected not to maximize the absolute MC intensity, but to maximize the number of clean, unblended MC transitions available for robust identification and fitting." (Results); "we constructed a full source model including the molecules reported in previous studies and the species identified in this work, in order to assess potential blends and overlaps in the candidate MC lines." (Results); "we derived the methanol column density from the optically thin $^{13}$CH$_{3}$OH emission and then scaled by the $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C isotopic ratio." (Identification of CH$_{3}$OH and $^{13}$CH$_{3}$OH)
- Alternative: Continuum-peak extraction rejected because absorption, line crowding and blending prevent reliable fits.  
  > "To mitigate absorption and severe line confusion at the continuum peak, we extracted spectra at a position offset from the 1 mm continuum maximum." (Spectrum Extraction Position And Spatial distribution of MC)
- Alternative: Main-isotopologue methanol avoided for column-density derivation because its lines are optically thick.  
  > "Because the main-isotopologue CH$_{3}$OH lines are optically thick in this dataset, we derived the methanol column density from the optically thin $^{13}$CH$_{3}$OH emission" (Identification of CH$_{3}$OH and $^{13}$CH$_{3}$OH)
- Alternative: Spectroscopic favorability alone cannot explain the observed isomer detectability pattern.  
  > "spectroscopic favorability alone cannot account for the observed pattern, pointing instead to chemical abundance differences as the dominant factor." (Detectability of several key isomers in the $\rm C_{2}H_{5}O_{2}N$ family)
- Alternative: High-temperature terrestrial synthetic pathways considered unlikely under interstellar conditions.  
  > "High-temperature synthetic pathways known from terrestrial chemistry (cite: sun2004) are unlikely to operate under typical interstellar conditions, pointing instead to grain-surface chemistry during the warm-up phase." (Formation of methyl carbamate in space)
- Alternative: NH2 addition to methoxycarbonyl considered, but CH3OCO formation and network role remain uncertain.  
  > "Alternative formation routes have been proposed, such as $\rm NH_{2}$ addition to methoxycarbonyl (cite: 2009ApJ...697..428L), but the formation of $\rm CH_{3}OCO$ and its role in astrochemical networks remain uncertain." (Formation of methyl carbamate in space)
- How they decided it was enough: Ten clean transitions, seven above 3σ, and nearby-position checks support the detection and abundance interpretation.  
  > "We identified ten unblended MC transitions toward G358.93 MM1, spanning upper-level energies $E_{\rm u}$ = 103-333 K." (Results); "representative LTE fits, including those closer to the continuum peak, yield MC column densities within one order of magnitude of the adopted value." (Spectrum Extraction Position And Spatial distribution of MC)

  > "the uncertainties are dominated not only by the fitting procedure, but also by line blending and the simplifying assumptions of the LTE model" (Results)

  > "supporting—but not uniquely confirming—a linked grain-surface formation scenario." (Formation of methyl carbamate in space)
