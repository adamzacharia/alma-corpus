## Analysis recipe

1. **extract hot-core spectra at the methanol peak**: extract each source's spectrum from the peak pixel of the integrated-intensity map of the CH3OH 8(0,8)-7(1,6) line (E_up = 97 K), using sources where this line is above 3 sigma  
  > "the spectra were extracted from the peak pixel for this line in the integrated intensity maps" (2.1 The observations); "above the $3\sigma$ level" (2.1 The observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: exception: G023.3891p00.1851 spectrum taken at the 17(1,16)e0-17(0,17)e0 (E_up = 336 K) peak  
  > "peak is chosen to extract the spectrum since it is a more reliable tracer of the hot core" (2.1 The observations)
   - assumption: methanol peak maximizes S/N of the SO2 and OCS isotopologue emission from the hot core  
  > "The choice of extracting the spectra from the methanol peak intends to maximize the signal-to-noise ratio of both and originating from the hot core region." (2.1 The observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **LTE grid fit of 34SO2 and O13CS column density and FWHM**: CASSIS LTE model grid; best fit = lowest chi2 over a grid of N and FWHM; Vlsr fixed to the by-eye value  
  > "assuming that the excitation is under local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)" (2.2 Spectral analysis); "a grid of column densities and FWHMs is tested and the best fit model is assigned to the combination with the lowest $\chi ^2$" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - software: CASSIS, CDMS
   - parameter: Tex fixed to 150 K  
  > "the excitation temperature is fixed to $T_{\text{ex}}=150$ K in the spectral fittings" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: N grid 1e13 to 1e17 cm-2, step 0.1 in log10  
  > "In this work $N$ was varied from $1\times10^{13}$ cm$^{−2}$ to $1\times10^{17}$ cm$^{−2}$ with a step of 0.1 on a logarithmic scale" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: FWHM grid 3 to 11 km/s, spacing 0.1 km/s linear  
  > "the FWHM was varied from 3 km s$^{−1}$ to 11 km s$^{−1}$ with a spacing of 0.1 km s$^{−1}$ on a linear scale" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: Vlsr derived by eye in 0.1 km/s increments, then fixed  
  > "The radial velocities ($V_{\text{lsr}}$) are derived by eye using increments of 0.1 km s$^{-1}$" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: 2-sigma errors from reduced chi2 of each grid point; minimum 20% uncertainty adopted  
  > "The $2\sigma$ errors are derived from the reduced $\chi ^2$ calculated from the comparison between the resulting model of each grid point and the observed spectrum." (2.2 Spectral analysis); "we assume a 20% uncertainty as a conservative estimate to account for systematic sources of errors" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: two-component sources (G025.6498p01.0491 for one species; 126348 and 707948 for the other): fit one Gaussian with Vlsr fixed to the mean of the by-eye component values  
  > "we perform the grid fits assuming one Gaussian and fixing the $V_{\text{lsr}}$ to the mean between the values derived by eye for each component" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: 3-sigma upper limits when the line does not exceed the detection threshold (manual fitting)  
  > "$3\sigma$ upper limits are provided when line intensities do not surpass this detection threshold." (3.2 Fitting results)
   - assumption: LTE, single Tex = 150 K (only one line per species per source)  
  > "Since we only detect one line per species per source, the excitation temperature is fixed to" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: beam dilution factor of unity (source fills the beam)  
  > "we assume that the source fully fills the beam (i.e., a beam dilution factor of unity)" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: chi2 grid search (no sampler or priors); N and FWHM free, Vlsr fixed  
  > "a grid of column densities and FWHMs is tested and the best fit model is assigned to the combination with the lowest $\chi ^2$" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - validation: Tex sensitivity: fixing Tex to 60-250 K changes column densities by up to a factor ~3, both molecules in the same direction; ratios therefore robust; two-component fits vs one Gaussian agree within 20%  
  > "only changes the derived column densities by up to a factor of $\sim$ 3, and both molecules in the same direction" (2.2 Spectral analysis); "This procedure yields column densities within 20$\%$ of the ones obtained by manually fitting each component separately." (2.2 Spectral analysis)
3. **fit the blended 33SO2 lines by eye**: by-eye fit on top of the best 34SO2... models from Nazari et al. 2022 (as stated); Vlsr fixed to those of the other isotopologue; N and FWHM varied; upper limits when little emission remains  
  > "The fitting was thus performed by eye on top of the best models for derived by (cite: Nazari2022)." (2.2 Spectral analysis); "In such cases we assign as upper limits." (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: column densities varied in steps of 0.1 in log space, FWHMs in steps of 0.1 in linear space  
  > "while its column densities and FWHMs were varied in steps of 0.1 in log space and 0.1 in linear space, respectively" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: approximate column densities derived for four sources (615590, 644284A, 693050, G343.1261-00.0623) where the model underfit emission by up to ~50%  
  > "for four sources (615590, 644284A, 693050, G343.1261-00.0623) the model underfitted the emission significantly" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: by-eye fit, no formal sampler  
  > "The fitting was thus performed by eye on top of the best models for derived by (cite: Nazari2022)." (2.2 Spectral analysis)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **convert isotopologue columns to SO2 and OCS using D_GC-dependent isotope ratios**: multiply minor-isotopologue columns by 32S/34S, 32S/33S, 12C/13C ratios from the Yan et al. 2023 D_GC relations, with D_GC computed from source distance and coordinates; errors propagated  
  > "are obtained by propagating the errors in the derived column densities of the minor isotopologues together with the uncertainties in Equations" (2.3 Isotope ratio callibration)
   - parameter: 32S/34S = (0.73 +/- 0.36) D_GC + (16.50 +/- 2.07)  
  > "(^{32}\text{S}/^{34}\text{S}) = (0.73 \pm 0.36)D_\text{GC} + (16.50 \pm 2.07)" (2.3 Isotope ratio callibration)
   - parameter: 32S/33S = (2.64 +/- 0.77) D_GC + (70.80 +/- 5.57)  
  > "(^{32}\text{S}/^{33}\text{S}) = (2.64 \pm 0.77)D_\text{GC} + (70.80 \pm 5.57)" (2.3 Isotope ratio callibration)
   - parameter: 12C/13C = (4.77 +/- 0.81) D_GC + (20.76 +/- 4.61)  
  > "(^{12}\text{C}/^{13}\text{C}) = (4.77 \pm 0.81)D_\text{GC} + (20.76 \pm 4.61)" (2.3 Isotope ratio callibration)
   - assumption: Sun at D_GC = 8.05 kpc; typical D_GC error ~0.5 kpc  
  > "as 8.05 kpc (cite: Honma2015)" (Appendix 7 table, footnote d); "the typical error of $\sim$0.5 kpc in D$_\text{GC}$ (see (cite: Nazari2022))" (2.3 Isotope ratio callibration)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **test whether 34SO2 emission is optically thin**: derive N(SO2) separately from N(34SO2) and N(33SO2) in the four sources where 33SO2 is detected and compare; ratio near unity means optically thin  
  > "The resulting values are all remarkably close to unity, confirming that both the and lines are indeed optically thin." (3.3 Is optically thin?)
   - assumption: isotope ratios of step 4 applied to both isotopologues  
  > "using their isotope ratios as described in Section" (3.3 Is optically thin?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: small discrepancies attributed to heavy blending of the 33SO2 lines  
  > "this strongly suggests that the small discrepancies in column densities calculated from $N$() and $N$() are mostly due to being heavily blended" (3.3 Is optically thin?)
6. **form ratios w.r.t. CH3OH, medians, spread factors, correlations and compare with literature**: take N(X)/N(CH3OH) using van Gelder et al. 2022a CH3OH columns; compute weighted medians, spread factor f (10^weighted 1-sigma in log10) and Pearson/Spearman correlations vs luminosity; compare histograms with ice, gas and comet literature ratios  
  > "A spread factor $f$, defined by 10 to the power of the weighted 1$\sigma$ standard deviation measured in log10 space" (4.1 Discussion); "Spearman correlation tests are also performed and yield similar results to" (4.1 Discussion)
   - parameter: ice lower limits excluded from medians (change median by <5%)  
  > "Ice lower limits affect the median value by $<5\%$ and are thus not included in the analysis." (4.1 Discussion)
   - parameter: single-dish literature points shown but excluded from the analysis  
  > "they are not included in the analysis" (4.1 Discussion)
   - assumption: CH3OH of ice origin so ratios w.r.t. CH3OH trace chemical history  
  > "Given the ice origin of methanol, it is possible to deduct information on the chemical history of and from their relative abundances" (4 Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: line widths and Vlsr of the isotopologues compared with CH3OH to confirm a common emitting gas  
  > "serves as validation for a comparative analysis of their column density ratios" (3.2 Fitting results)

## Qualitative findings

- rare-isotopologue emission is compact and mostly unresolved, tracing the hot core  
  > "The emission areas of both species are compact" (3.1 Morphology)
- the 693050 emission peaks are offset ~4000 au from the continuum peak, possibly by optically thick dust  
  > "This could be the result of optically thick dust at these wavelengths" (3.1 Morphology)
- N(SO2)/N(CH3OH) in dark-cloud ices is about one order of magnitude lower than in the protostellar phase  
  > "are generally lower than in the protostellar phase (both for ice and gas observations) by about one order of magnitude" (4.2 N(SO2)/N(CH3OH))

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMAGAL gives a large sample of line-rich massive protostars; first interferometric study with a statistically significant sample of detections of these two molecules  
  > "representing the first interferometric study on a statistically significant sample of detections for these two molecules in the gas" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: rare isotopologues avoid extended emission, outflow contamination and optical depth effects  
  > "The molecular column densities are derived from the rare isotopologues and to avoid contamination from extended emission, outflows, and to limit line optical depth effects." (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Tex varied 60-250 K as a test; fixed 150 K retained since excitation temperatures for >~80% of sources fall in 120-170 K (factor up to 1.6 in column density)  
  > "corresponding to only up to a factor of 1.6 difference in column densities" (2.2 Spectral analysis)
- Alternative: main isotopologue line of SO2 (one covered line) not used because of likely outflow contamination and optical thickness  
  > "it is not included in this work due to its likely contamination from outflow emission and because it is probably optically thick" (2.1 The observations)
- Alternative: a source with divergent line profiles across species was excluded  
  > "we exclude sources for which the line profiles of the targeted molecules differed significantly across species" (2.1 The observations)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): sample biased toward line-rich sources  
  > "the dataset studied here is biased in favor of line rich sources and therefore our results might nor represent all massive protostars" (5 Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): absolute column densities depend on the assumed emitting area; only ratios are reliable  
  > "Absolute column densities are nevertheless subject to biases such as the assumed emitting area and thus are not ideal to be directly compared." (3.2 Fitting results)
- Limitation (authors): only two dark-cloud ice data points available  
  > "only two data points are available for ices in prestellar cores so far" (4.1 N(OCS)/N(CH3OH))
- Limitation (authors): low-mass gas ratios mostly from the main isotopologue, possibly outflow-contaminated or optically thick  
  > "which could contain appreciable contamination from outflow emission and could be optically thick" (4.2 N(SO2)/N(CH3OH))
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single transition per species forces fixed Tex = 150 K; the factor ~3 uncertainty and its effect on ratios across sources with different true temperatures is only argued, not propagated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ratios rely on CH3OH columns from a different paper and different spectral fits, so systematic offsets between SO2/OCS and CH3OH analyses are not tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mixed beams (0.5-1.5 arcsec) and a unity beam-filling assumption could bias ratios differently for species with different emitting regions
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Vlsr and 33SO2 fits are done by eye, which is hard to reproduce, and the 33SO2 test uses only 4 sources
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Correlation/median statistics mix upper limits, low-mass gas and ice data of different provenance; p = 0.05 for the SO2-OCS correlation is marginal
