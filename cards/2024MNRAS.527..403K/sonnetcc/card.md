## Analysis recipe

1. **simulate model galaxy colours**: CIGALE model SEDs with BC03, Chabrier IMF, solar metallicity, nebular emission and Calzetti-type attenuation over a grid of SFH parameters  
  > "In CIGALE, galaxy models are generated by adopting (cite: 2003MNRAS.344.1000B) (BC03) stellar population synthesis modeling code" (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: tau = 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10 Gyr  
  > "0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, & 10" (Table 1)
   - parameter: age t = 0.1 to 3.0 Gyr in 0.1 steps, 5, 10 Gyr  
  > "0.1 to 3.0 with 0.1 steps, 5, 10" (Table 1)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF and solar metallicity  
  > "(cite: 2003PASP..115..763C) Initial Mass Function (IMF), and solar metallicity." (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - assumption: continuum to line E(B-V) ratio fixed at 0.44  
  > "with a constant continuum to line $E(B-V)$ ratio, $E(B-V)_{\rm continuum}/E(B-V)_{\rm line} = 0.44$." (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **define colour criteria**: set gri, i[3.6][4.5] and iJH colour cuts from model tracks and observed z~2 galaxies  
  > "The color criteria are set also considering the colors of the observed galaxies at photometric and spectroscopic redshifts" (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - parameter: gri criterion: g-r > 1.3, -1 < r-i < 1 (plus g-r > 1.5(r-i)+0.8)  
  > "g-r > 1.3,\ -1<r-i<1," (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - parameter: i-[3.6] > 3.5, [3.6]-[4.5] > 0 (plus i-[3.6] > 8.0([3.6]-[4.5])+2.5)  
  > "i-\rm[3.6] >3.5,\ [3.6]-[4.5]>0," (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - parameter: iJH criterion: i-J > 1.5, 0.3 < J-H < 1.4  
  > "i-\rm J >1.5,\ 0.3<J-H<1.4," (New technique to select fast-quenching galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **apply criteria to COSMOS2020**: apply gri plus i[3.6][4.5] or iJH cuts to the COSMOS2020 CLASSIC catalog and compare with photo-z, sSFR and rest-frame UVJ  
  > "We tested the above color criteria using the COSMOS2020 catalog (cite: 2022ApJS..258...11W)." (Result)
   - software: Python 3
   - parameter: 3 arcsec aperture photometry for HSC g,r,i and UltraVISTA J,H; PSF-fitted photometry for IRAC; tested for i<26 and [3.6]<22.5 or i<26 and H<23.4  
  > "The color criteria were tested for galaxies with $i<26$ and \[3.6\] $<22.5$ mag or $i<26$ and $H<23.4$ mag." (Result)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: compared with spectroscopically confirmed quiescent galaxies and the UVJ diagram  
  > "Most of the galaxies selected by the" (i[3.6][4.5] color criterion)
4. **limit obscured SFR with 24 micron stacking**: median stacking of 24 micron image after rejecting individually detected and nearby bright sources  
  > "a median stacking analysis of the sample was performed. The flux value and error are the median and standard deviation of 1000 times boot-strap resampling." (i[3.6][4.5] color criterion)
   - parameter: 1000 bootstrap resamplings  
  > "the median and standard deviation of 1000 times boot-strap resampling." (i[3.6][4.5] color criterion)
   - assumption: median empirical SED model used to convert flux to SFR  
  > "Adopting the median of the empirical SED models in (cite: 2017ApJ...840...78D)" (i[3.6][4.5] color criterion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **ALMA SFR limits for spectroscopic galaxies**: check two spectroscopically confirmed objects in archival ALMA data for continuum detection and convert 2 sigma flux limits to SFR using the median SED model  
  > "Two are in the existing ALMA data (PID #2015.1.00379.S & #2013.1.01292.S) and not detected with" (Spectroscopically confirmed galaxies)
   - parameter: F_1.25mm < 0.11 mJy and F_0.87mm < 0.36 mJy (2 sigma)

   - assumption: median SED model of (cite: 2017ApJ...840...78D)  
  > "similarly assuming the median SED model of (cite: 2017ApJ...840...78D)" (Spectroscopically confirmed galaxies)

## Qualitative findings

- The colour criteria are sensitive only to massive quiescent galaxies with tau <= 0.1 Gyr and about 1 Gyr after quenching at z~2, so the selection is incomplete.  
  > "These selection methods are only sensitive to massive quiescent galaxies quenched with $\tau\leq0.1$ Gyr and $\sim1$ Gyr after quenching at $z\sim2$." (Completeness and purity of the selection method)
- The three spectroscopic galaxies have compact elliptical-like morphologies, which suggests a link between quenching timescale and morphological transformation.  
  > "show elliptical-like compact morphologies." (Morphologies of fast quenching galaxies)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Rest-frame UVJ needs spectroscopic or reliable photometric redshifts, so a colour-only selection is proposed  
  > "But to construct the rest-frame $UVJ$ color diagram, spectroscopic redshifts or reliable photometric redshifts based on multi-wavelength photometry are necessary." (Introduction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The true dusty-SFG contamination rate cannot be determined with existing data  
  > "The true contamination rate of dusty SFGs is not available with the existing data" (Completeness and purity of the selection method)
- Limitation (authors): The method cannot select the whole quiescent population at z~2  
  > "Thus, it cannot completely select the whole quiescent galaxy population at" (Completeness and purity of the selection method)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA enters only as two flux limits with no reduction details, so the SFR limits cannot be checked
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only three spectroscopic galaxies test the selection, and only one comes from earlier quiescent samples
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Over half of the colour-selected galaxies have SED-fit sSFR >1e-10, so purity is weakly constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Number-density progenitor argument rests on a rough conservative estimate
