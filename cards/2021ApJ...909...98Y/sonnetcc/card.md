## Analysis recipe

1. **Image line cubes**: Pipeline-calibrated data; uv-domain continuum subtraction; imaging at various channel widths; cleaning to about 1 sigma; primary beam correction  
  > "We used the standard pipeline-calibrated raw data and carried out continuum subtraction, imaging, and cleaning ourselves." (3 Observations); "Line emission in the data cubes was cleaned down to about the 1$\sigma$ level and primary beam corrections were applied." (3 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Continuum subtraction with zero-order or first-order fits to line-free channels in the visibility domain  
  > "For continuum subtraction we used zero-order or first-order fits to the line-free channels in the visibility domain" (3 Observations)
   - parameter: Fiducial cubes use natural weighting with channel widths listed in Table 1  
  > "Beam sizes and rms noise levels refer to images made with “natural" uv-weighting and the listed channel widths." (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Phase self-calibration tested and found not to improve images  
  > "An additional round of phase self calibration was not found to offer any improvement in the images because of the relatively modest signal-to-noise ratios." (3 Observations)
2. **Image continuum and measure spectral index**: Continuum imaged from line-free frequencies; two-point spectral index between 86.15 and 107.9 GHz and multi-term multi-frequency synthesis  
  > "the data are suitable for imaging the continuum intensity and estimating the spectral index of any emission." (Radio continuum and ionized gas); "using all of the available line-free frequencies in CASA’s multi-term, multi-frequency synthesis deconvolver (cite: rau_mfs)" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Flux densities measured at 86.15 and 107.9 GHz  
  > "its flux densities measured at 86.15 and 107.9 GHz are 742 $\pm$ 12 $\mu$Jy and 616 $\pm$ 18 $\mu$Jy, respectively." (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
   - assumption: Power-law flux density S_nu proportional to nu^alpha  
  > "Parametrizing the flux density as $S_\nu \propto \nu^\alpha$ therefore gives $\alpha = -0.83 \pm 0.13.$" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
   - fitting: Two-point spectral index; MTMFS fit with CASA  
  > "using all of the available line-free frequencies in CASA’s multi-term, multi-frequency synthesis deconvolver (cite: rau_mfs), we find $\alpha = -0.59 \pm 0.12.$" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
   - validation: The two spectral-index estimates are stated to be consistent with each other  
  > "These estimates are consistent with each other and both clearly indicate synchrotron emission in the nucleus of the galaxy" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
3. **Estimate ionized-gas metallicity from literature line fluxes**: Emission-line ratios from a 50x60 arcsec region converted to oxygen abundance with two calibrations  
  > "We use line fluxes integrated over the large 50$^{\prime\prime}$ $\times$ 60$^{\prime\prime}$ region; following the prescriptions outlined in (cite: moustakas10)" (Radio continuum and ionized gas); "We find 12 + log(O/H) = 8.38 $\pm$ 0.06 using the calibration of Pilyugin & Thuan (2005)" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Each result compared with the median of SINGS galaxies for the same calibration  
  > "each of these is consistent with its respective median of the SINGS galaxies (mostly spirals) in (cite: moustakas10)" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
4. **Make integrated intensity maps and kinematic comparison**: 12CO masks from smoothed cubes applied to all other lines; moment maps and position-velocity slices compared with stellar and ionized-gas kinematics  
  > "a velocity-dependent mask tracing the $^{12}$CO emission is used for all of the other spectral lines" (Other molecular species in the inner kpc); "created by modestly smoothing the data cube in spatial and velocity dimensions and clipping on the smoothed cubes to create a velocity-dependent mask defining the volume with emission." (3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Same 12CO mask used for all lines to avoid biases from different signal-to-noise  
  > "To avoid biases related to the different signal-to-noise ratios, a velocity-dependent mask tracing the $^{12}$CO emission is used for all of the other spectral lines." (Other molecular species in the inner kpc)
5. **Measure region line fluxes and line ratios**: Sum cubes within spatial regions to get spectra, then sum over velocity ranges defined by 12CO; ratios in K km/s units; shift-and-stack in annuli as an alternative  
  > "summing the cubes within the spatial regions to produce integrated spectra" (Line strengths: CO isotopologues); "and then summing the spectra over velocity ranges defined by the $^{12}$CO emission." (Line strengths: CO isotopologues); "All line ratios reported in this paper are computed using integrated fluxes expressed in temperature units (K km s$^{-1}$)." (Footnote 1)
   - parameter: Upper limits are 3 times the statistical uncertainty of the sum over the adopted velocity range  
  > "the upper limits are quoted as 3 times the statistical uncertainty of the sum over the adopted velocity range." (Table 2)
   - parameter: Shift-and-stack aligns spectra in each annulus using the 12CO velocity field  
  > "all of the spectra within a given annulus (about the nuclear 3mm continuum peak) can be aligned in velocity, using the $^{12}$CO velocity field at each position to determine the velocity shift." (Line strengths: CO isotopologues)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Same data volume used for both lines of a ratio; flux errors are thermal only  
  > "This procedure ensures that the same data volume is used for both lines of a ratio, even if their signal-to-noise ratios are very different." (Line strengths: CO isotopologues)
6. **Derive LTE column densities for the nucleus**: Optically thin LTE column densities for a 2 arcsec nuclear region at two temperatures  
  > "Column densities are calculated for two representative temperatures in the optically thin LTE assumption." (Table 2)
   - parameter: Temperatures of 20 K and 90 K  
  > "$\log N$ (20 K)" (Table 2); "$\log N$ (90 K)" (Table 2)
   - parameter: Region is a circle 2 arcsec (280 pc) in diameter centered on the nuclear 3mm continuum source  
  > "Line fluxes are measured in a circular region 2$^{\prime\prime}$ (280 pc) in diameter, centered on the nuclear 3mm continuum source" (Table 2)
   - assumption: Optically thin, LTE  
  > "Column densities are calculated for two representative temperatures in the optically thin LTE assumption." (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Authors note 12CO column densities are underestimates, probably less than a factor of 2 too low  
  > "they are probably less than a factor of 2 too low" (Discussion: constraints on abundances and galaxy evolution)
7. **Constrain abundances, density and temperature with RADEX**: RADEX large velocity gradient models of HCN/HCO+ and 12CO/13CO ratios  
  > "we make calculations using the RADEX large velocity gradient code (cite: radex) to account for optical depth and excitation effects on the HCN/HCO$^+$ line ratio" (Physical properties of the dense molecular gas); "calculations using the RADEX code (cite: radex) suggest that increases in the temperature of a factor of 3 (e.g. from 30 K to 90 K) could account for the larger $^{12}$CO/$^{13}$CO 1-0 ratio" (Discussion: constraints on abundances and galaxy evolution)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: Assumed [HCN/HCO+] = 3 gives n_H2 <= 10^4.3 cm^-3  
  > "For an assumed \[HCN/HCO$^+$\] = 3, relatively low densities $n_{\rm H_2} \leq 10^{4.3}$ cm$^{-3}$ are required to reproduce the observed line ratios." (Physical properties of the dense molecular gas)
   - assumption: Typical 12CO/13CO abundance ratio of 40 to 60 in spiral disks  
  > "if the \[$^{12}$CO/$^{13}$CO\] abundance ratio in the center of NGC 7465 is in the typical range of 40–60, then the $^{12}$CO emission is unusually optically thin" (Discussion: constraints on abundances and galaxy evolution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cannot distinguish temperature from linewidth effects with present data  
  > "With the present data it is not possible to distinguish whether the $^{12}$CO optical depths in NGC 7465 are more significantly affected by temperature changes or increases of the line widths." (Discussion: constraints on abundances and galaxy evolution)
8. **Characterize the unidentified background line source**: Test candidate CO identifications; compare dynamical and molecular mass; derive luminosity and mass from line flux  
  > "Assuming the object is in dynamical equilibrium, so that we can compare its inferred dynamical and molecular masses" (A distant, unidentified source near NGC 7465); "The linear diameter is estimated from the emission centroids in the outermost channels." (Table 3)
   - parameter: Flat cosmology with H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc, Omega_m = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7  
  > "Calculations are made assuming a flat universe with H$_0$ = 70 km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_m = 0.3$, and $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.7$." (Table 3)
   - assumption: Dynamical equilibrium; CO-to-H2 alpha = 3.6; assumed excitation for higher-J lines  
  > "using a conversion factor $\alpha = 3.6$ M$_\odot$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$ (cite: boogaard2019)" (Table 3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with ALMA blind CO surveys of the Hubble Ultra-Deep Field  
  > "But overall, this source is compatible with being a similar object to those." (A distant, unidentified source near NGC 7465)

## Qualitative findings

- Two 3mm continuum point sources: a nuclear synchrotron (falling spectrum) source and a fainter star-forming source 5 arcsec south  
  > "NGC 7465 has two continuum point sources, one in the nucleus and one 5$^{\prime\prime}$ south of the nucleus" (Radio continuum and ionized gas)
- 13CO and C18O peak off the nucleus while 12CO, HCN, HCO+, CN and C2H peak on it  
  > "whereas $^{13}$CO and C$^{18}$O show peaks a couple of arcseconds ($\approx$ 1 beam) north and east of the nucleus rather than on the nucleus itself." (Other molecular species in the inner kpc)
- Shock tracers SiO, CH3OH and HNCO are not detected  
  > "Finally, HNCO, CH$_3$OH and SiO emission are not detected in these data." (Other molecular species in the inner kpc)
- CN emission is optically thin  
  > "Thus, the CN emission in NGC 7465 is optically thin." (C$_2$H and CN: photodissociation regions)
- C2H is less centrally concentrated than HCN and HCO+, opposite to NGC 253  
  > "In NGC 7465 it is the opposite – C$_2$H is less centrally concentrated than HCN and HCO$^+$." (C$_2$H and CN: photodissociation regions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: NGC 7465 is an ATLAS3D galaxy that is CO-bright and known to have recently accreted its gas  
  > "NGC 7465 was originally selected because it is a member of the ATLAS$^{\rm 3D}$ sample of early-type galaxies (cite: cappellari_a3d1)" (About NGC 7465)
- Why this method: Same 12CO mask for all lines and the same data volume for both lines of a ratio to avoid signal-to-noise biases  
  > "To avoid biases related to the different signal-to-noise ratios, a velocity-dependent mask tracing the $^{12}$CO emission is used for all of the other spectral lines." (Other molecular species in the inner kpc)
- Alternative: Four explanations for the high 12CO/13CO ratio considered: starburst isotopic abundances, selective photodissociation, gas temperature/density, and lower opacity from broad linewidths  
  > "We thus consider several possible explanations for the unusually high $^{12}$CO/$^{13}$CO line ratio in the center of NGC 7465 and its strong radial gradient." (Discussion: constraints on abundances and galaxy evolution)
- Alternative: Additional phase self-calibration was tried and rejected  
  > "An additional round of phase self calibration was not found to offer any improvement in the images because of the relatively modest signal-to-noise ratios." (3 Observations)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Line-flux errors are thermal only and omit absolute flux-scale uncertainty, which matters for ratios between different tunings such as 12CO/13CO  
  > "they do not include uncertainties in the absolute flux calibration scale, such as those due to secular variability in the calibrator sources." (3 Observations); "have an additional uncertainty due to this absolute flux calibration" (3 Observations)
- Limitation (authors): Cannot separate temperature from linewidth effects on 12CO optical depth  
  > "With the present data it is not possible to distinguish whether the $^{12}$CO optical depths in NGC 7465 are more significantly affected by temperature changes or increases of the line widths." (Discussion: constraints on abundances and galaxy evolution)
- Limitation (authors): Significant molecular gas may lie beyond the imaged region (primary beam)  
  > "Some emission is also detected beyond the half-power point of the ALMA primary beam" (Large-scale disk)
- Limitation (authors): Only one line detected for the background source, so its redshift is not identified  
  > "as we only have one detected spectral line in the frequencies covered by these data, it is difficult to identify the line" (A distant, unidentified source near NGC 7465)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ratios between different tunings (12CO/13CO) rely on unquantified flux-scale agreement across projects observed in 2016 and 2018.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LTE column densities and the RADEX exploration have no stated uncertainties or parameter grids, so the density and abundance limits are hard to verify.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Temperature versus linewidth explanations of the high 12CO/13CO ratio are not distinguished; both are speculative without higher-J data.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 13CO/C18O rests on a tentative C18O detection with about 30% errors, so the spiral-origin argument is weakly constrained.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison samples use single-dish or lower-resolution data, so resolution-dependent biases in ratios are possible.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam sizes differ between lines (0.8 to 2.2 arcsec); the matching procedure for ratio maps is not specified.
