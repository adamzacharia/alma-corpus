## Qualitative findings

- All five XRBs show unresolved continuum emission with flat or inverted spectral index (except Cir X-1), consistent with a jet origin  
  > "the spectral index between 84 and 99 GHz is flat or inverted for all the sources except for Cir X" (Results)
- No line emission at the position of any XRB, only upper limits  
  > "our exploratory search for line emission only resulted in upper limits at the position of all sources" (Summary and conclusions)
- Three line emission features in the GX 13+1 field are probably unrelated to GX 13+1  
  > "An association of these features with GX 13+1 remains speculative at this stage." (Summary and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sample of persistently accreting NS and BH sources with LMXB and HMXB companions, all known jet emitters  
  > "For this exploratory study we chose a sample of persistently accreting sources that includes both NS and BH systems and both low- and high-mass stellar companions." (Introduction)
- Why this method: 12 m array only, for sensitivity and angular resolution suited to weak small line regions  
  > "For these exploratory observations we only used the 12 m array due to its superior sensitivity and angular resolution" (Observations and data analysis)
- Alternative: H(40)alpha and H2O identifications of the 99.29 and 96.73 GHz lines rejected in favour of SO and CH3OH  
  > "The spatial coincidence of two lines at the SE position indicates that a similar redshift of 18–20 km s$^{-1}$ for the lines, and thus an identification with SO and CH$_3$OH, is more plausible." (Line emission in the field of view of GX 13+1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Line identification and maser nature need higher resolution follow-up and other SiO transitions  
  > "Further observations of the detected line and of other SiO transitions at a higher angular resolution are necessary to confirm the maser nature of the emission and the line identification." (Line emission in the field of view of GX 13+1)
- Limitation (authors): Many jet-ISM molecular tracers (CS, HNCO, HCO+) were not covered  
  > "many of the molecular tracers of jet-ISM interactions, such as the shock tracer CS or the high-density tracers HNCO or HCO+, remain unexplored for the sources of this study" (Discussion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text says six spectral windows but lists seven central frequencies and the table has seven spws
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line search methods (channel width, velocity window for upper limits, K conversion) are unspecified, so limits cannot be reproduced exactly
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Line identifications in the GX 13+1 field rest on one or two lines each and assumed redshifts
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Non-detections near Cir X-1 and Cyg X-1 are limited by the ~15 arcsec maximum recoverable scale
