## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and continuum-subtract**: CASA clean per spectral window; STATCONT continuum estimation with sigma clipping, subtracted from cubes  
  > "The continuum image obtained in this way is then subtracted from the corresponding data cube." (3.1 Continuum image and continuum subtraction)
   - software: CASA, STATCONT
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; clipping at µ ± 2σ  
  > "all channels whose flux lies beyond the interval µ ± 2 σ are not used in the next step" (3.1 Continuum image and continuum subtraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested with simulations in Sánchez-Monge et al. (2017)  
  > "The validity of our procedure is described in detail and tested with numerical simulations in Sánchez-Monge et al. (2017)." (3.1 Continuum image and continuum subtraction)
2. **Characterise continuum fragmentation and recovered flux**: Compare integrated ALMA flux to flux in 33'' beam from BOLOCAM GPS (or Faúndez 1.2 mm)  
  > "we have divided the integrated flux density over the whole ALMA map by the flux density measured inside a 33 [′′] beam" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - assumption: Gaussian source for rescaling Faúndez fluxes to HPBW 33''  
  > "the fluxes were rescaled to a HPBW of 33 [′′] by assuming a Gaussian source" (4.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Quantify line richness and map kinematics**: Compute f (fraction of channels with line emission) and I (mean intensity scaled by d^2); moment 0/1/2 maps of CH3CN and 13CH3CN K=2; SiO blue/red integrated maps  
  > "we define two parameters: _f_, the fraction of spectral channels where line emission is detected, and _I_" (4.2 Line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Search for Keplerian-like rotation with PV plots**: PV plots along cuts through cores compared with butterfly pattern for Keplerian rotation; compare ground-state 13CH3CN with vibrationally excited CH3CN lines by ratio of PV plots  
  > "dividing the PV plot in the bottom panel by that in the top panel, we find that in these objects the highest ratio between the vibrationally excited line and the ground state line is found close to the continuum peak" (6. Are there discs around O-type stars?)
   - assumption: Keplerian pattern edge-on, zero line width, infinite resolution, not a fit  
  > "the former assumes and edge-on geometry, zero line width, and infinite angular resolution." (5.3 G29.96)
   - fitting: No fit; patterns for 10-20 Msun overlaid for comparison  
  > "We note that this is shown only for the sake of comparison and is not a fit to the data." (5.3 G29.96)
   - validation: Degraded resolution by factor 3.5 to test detectability at 7 kpc  
  > "the angular resolution of the original data has been degraded by a factor 3.5" (6. Are there discs around O-type stars?)
5. **Estimate core gas mass**: Mass from continuum with fiducial T and kappa  
  > "we assume a fiducial value of 200 K, a dust absorption coefficient κ dust = 1 cm [2] g [−][1] at 1.4 mm (Ossenkopf & Henning 1994), and a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100." (6. Are there discs around O-type stars?)
   - assumption: Tdust = 200 K, kappa = 1 cm2/g at 1.4 mm, gas-to-dust 100  
  > "we assume a fiducial value of 200 K, a dust absorption coefficient κ dust = 1 cm [2] g [−][1] at 1.4 mm" (6. Are there discs around O-type stars?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- SiO shows clear but poorly collimated bipolar outflows in only two objects (three detected bipolar by text: G29.96, G31.41; G345.50 complex)  
  > "The SiO data reveal clear but poorly collimated bipolar outflow signatures towards two objects only." (abstract)
