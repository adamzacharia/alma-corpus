## Qualitative findings

- Central regions of AGN and regions along the projected direction of AGN outflows show a larger fraction of neutral PAH molecules (elevated 11.3/6.2 and 11.3/7.7 µm PAH ratios) compared to star-forming regions, up to kpc scales.  
  > "Our findings align with previous JWST studies, showing that the central regions of AGN show a larger fraction of neutral PAH molecules (i.e. elevated 11.3/6.2 and 11.3/7.7 µm PAH ratios) compared to starforming galaxies." (abstract); "We find that the AGN might affect not only the PAH population in the innermost region but also in the extended regions up to ∼ kpc scales." (abstract)
- Ionized PAH molecules (carriers of 6.2 and 7.7 µm bands) are less resilient to AGN radiation fields and shocks than neutral PAH molecules (11.3 µm band), while PAH molecular sizes remain similar.  
  > "However, PAH molecular sizes are rather similar. The carriers of the ionized PAH bands (6.2 and 7.7 µm) are less resilience than those of neutral PAH bands (11.3 µm), which might be particularly important for strongly AGN-host coupled systems." (abstract)
- Cold molecular gas traced by ALMA CO(3–2) and warm molecular gas traced by JWST H2 0-0 S(1) correlate strongly across all three galaxies, whereas PAH 11.3 µm correlates poorly with CO(3–2) and H2 S(1) over the full FoV in the strongly coupled system NGC 5728.  
  > "Similarly, the H 2 0-0 S(1) warm molecular gas and the 11.3µm PAH emission show a poor correlation in NGC 5728, while these are tight for NGC 5506 and NGC 7172 (see Table 2)." (4.1. Distribution of the circumnuclear molecular and ionized gas); "The cold molecular gas from ALMA and the warm phase traced by the H 2 0-0 S(1) show an excellent correlation in the three galaxies studied here (see Table 2;" (4.1. Distribution of the circumnuclear molecular and ionized gas)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sub-arcsecond mid-IR JWST/MIRI-MRS spectroscopy (4.9–28.1 µm) probes multiple ionized and neutral PAH bands at ~75 pc to kpc scales in obscured regions, while ALMA CO(3–2) traces the cold molecular gas distribution, and the three targets span weak to strong outflow-host galaxy coupling at similar AGN luminosities and distances.  
  > "Mid-IR subarcsecond angular observations (<0.5 ′′ ) of local AGN (at distances of ∼tens of Mpc) enable us to probe their nuclear/circumnuclear regions (inner ∼100 pc scales)." (1. Introduction); "The targets have been selected to have different degrees of outflowhost galaxy coupling from very strong to weak (see e.g. Fig. 4 in Ramos Almeida et al. 2022)." (2. Targets and observations)
- Why this method: Extracting spectra in larger targeted apertures increases signal-to-noise for faint PAH bands, and a differential extinction model is required for nuclear regions where complex dust continuum and high extinction cause standard PAHFIT to fail.  
  > "Thus, extractions in a larger aperture to increase the signal-to-noise ratio in the faint emission are needed to confirm the larger fraction of neutral PAH molecules in these regions (see Section 5.1)." (5. PAH bands as a tool for tracing AGN feedback); "However, PAHFIT does not produce successful fits for the nuclear regions of the sources studied here due to the complexity of the dust continuum and the high levels of extinction present." (Appendix A: Mid-IR modelling and aperture selection)
