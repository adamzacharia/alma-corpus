## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the archival CO and continuum data**: Calibrate with CASA and the ALMA Pipeline, image with tclean to 3 sigma using Briggs robust 2, subtract continuum with uvcontsub  
  > "they were calibrated using the appropriate version of Common Astronomy Software Application (CASA) software (cite: 2007ASPC..376..127M) and ALMA Pipeline used for the quality assurance." (Data Reduction); "The line and underlying continuum emissions were reconstructed using the CASA `tclean` task down to $3\:\sigma$ level." (Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: Briggs weighting, robust = 2 (natural weighting)  
  > "we adopted Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 2 (natural weighting)" (Data Reduction)
   - parameter: uvcontsub fitorder=1 using line-free channels  
  > "we subtracted the continuum using line-free channels with the CASA task `uvcontsub` (fitorder=1)" (Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Make moment maps of the CO emission**: CASA immoments for integrated intensity, velocity centre and velocity dispersion maps  
  > "Using the CASA task `immoments`, images of the integrated intensity" (Results)
   - software: CASA, immoments
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract central 500 pc spectra and fit Gaussian components**: Extract spectrum from the central 500 pc region centred on the AGN and fit one or two Gaussian components; ignore features with |v| >~ 500 km/s; for Hydra A exclude the innermost 160 pc and treat east and west separately  
  > "We extracted spectra from the central 500 pc region centered on the AGNs." (Results); "Each spectrum was fitted with a model consisting of one or two Gaussian components" (Results); "Thus, we excluded the innermost 160 pc, and we extracted and examined the spectra in the eastern and western regions of the AGN separately" (Results)
   - parameter: Line components kept only if significance > 5 sigma and |v| below about 500 km/s relative to galaxy rest frame  
  > "We ignored line-like features if the line center velocity is $|v|\gtrsim 500\rm\: km\: s^{-1}$ because the corresponding gases are unlikely to be bound to the nuclei" (Results)
   - fitting: One or two Gaussian components per spectrum; sampler, priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "Each spectrum was fitted with a model consisting of one or two Gaussian components" (Results)
   - validation: Checked that maximum recoverable scales far exceed 500 pc so missed flux is negligible  
  > "We confirmed that the maximum recoverable scales are much larger than 500 pc for all the targets and the missed flux from the 500 pc region should be ignorable." (Results)
4. **Derive molecular gas mass within 500 pc**: Convert CO integrated intensity to M_mol with the Bolatto et al. relation, correcting higher-J lines to CO(1-0) with F_ul; sum two components when present  
  > "using the following relation from (cite: 2013ARA&A..51..207B)" (Molecular gas mass); "When there are two gaussian components in the spectrum, the mass is their summation." (Molecular gas mass)
   - parameter: F21 = 3.2 (CO(2-1)/CO(1-0)), F32 = 7.2 (CO(3-2)/CO(1-0)) in flux density scale  
  > "gives a CO(J=2-1)/CO(J=1-0) line ratio in flux density scale, 3.2" (Molecular gas mass); "is a CO(J=3-2)/CO(J=1-0) ratio of 7.2 (cite: 2019MNRAS.485..229R; 2019MNRAS.490.3025R)" (Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: X_CO = 2e20 cm^-2 (K km/s)^-1 (Milky Way value); H0=70, Omega_m=0.3, Omega_Lambda=0.7  
  > "we adopt a standard value of $X_{\rm CO}=2\times 10^{20}\rm\: cm^{-2}(K\: km\: s^{-1})^{-1}$ measured in the Milky Way" (Molecular gas mass); "We assume $H_0=70\rm\: km\: s^{-1}\: Mpc^{-1}$, $\Omega_m=0.3$, and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.7$ in this study." (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate gas accretion rate from turbulent diffusion**: Accretion time t_acc ~ r_g^2/nu with diffusion coefficient nu ~ l_t v_t, and accretion rate Mdot ~ M_mol/t_acc, with r_g = 500 pc  
  > "the accretion process can be regarded as a diffusion process of gas clumps, and the diffusion coefficient is $\nu \sim \ell_{\rm t} v_{\rm t}$" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power); "and the accretion rate is $\dot{M}\sim M_{\rm mol}/t_{\rm acc}$" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
   - software: 3D-Barolo
   - parameter: v_t = FWHM/2.35, with flux-averaged FWHM when two Gaussian components are present  
  > "For the FWHM, we use the flux averaged values when there are two gaussian components" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power); "$\approx {\rm FWHM}/2.35$ and $\ell_{\rm t}=\alpha r_{\rm g}$ (cite: 1983bhwd.book.....S)" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
   - parameter: r_g = 500 pc, alpha = 0.15  
  > "where $r_{\rm g}=500$ pc and $\alpha = 0.15$" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
   - assumption: Turbulent angular momentum transport drives accretion regardless of disk-like morphology; derived v_t is an upper limit because rotation may contaminate it  
  > "we adopt the relation of $\dot{M}= M_{\rm mol}/t_{\rm acc}$ regardless of whether molecular gas has a disk-like structure or not" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power); "and it should be regarded as the upper limit" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 3D-Barolo cube model for Abell 262 gives v_t ~ 100 km/s, close to FWHM/2.35 ~ 90 km/s  
  > "using a cube model code 3D-Barolo" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power); "The model fit shows that $v_t\sim 100\rm\: km\: s^{-1}$" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
6. **Correlate M_mol and Mdot with jet power**: Pearson correlation on logarithmic scales and BCES (Y|X) power-law fits of log P_cav against log M_mol and log Mdot, with and without Abell 262  
  > "Pearson correlation coefficient on the logarithmic scales is 0.754" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power); "Using the bivariate correlated errors and intrinsic scatter BCES" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
   - software: BCES
   - fitting: BCES (Y|X) estimator, accounting for errors in both axes and possible intrinsic scatter  
  > "which accounts for errors in both axes and the presence of possible intrinsic scatter" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
   - validation: Abell 262 flagged as outlier outside the 3 sigma confidence range; fits and correlation also given without it  
  > "Abell 262 appears to be an outlier of this correlation because it is outside the" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
7. **Derive continuum luminosities and compare with M_mol**: Measure continuum flux at line-free channels, scale to rest-frame 115.3 GHz with a power law, compute L_con and L_1.4 from NVSS, and test correlation with M_mol  
  > "We derive the continuum flux at line-free channels." (Circumnuclear gas and continuum emission); "compares the radio luminosity estimated from 1.4 GHz observations" (Circumnuclear gas and continuum emission)
   - parameter: Spectral index gamma = -0.75; eta = 1, ~2, ~3 for CO(1-0), (2-1), (3-2)  
  > "We adopt a typical spectral index of $\gamma=-0.75$." (Circumnuclear gas and continuum emission); "$\eta=1$ for CO(J=1-0), $\eta\approx 2$ for CO(J=2-1)[^2], and $\eta\approx 3$ for CO(J=3-2)" (Circumnuclear gas and continuum emission)
   - assumption: Continuum is a power law S_nu ~ nu^gamma  
  > "assuming that the continuum emission is represented by a power-law" (Circumnuclear gas and continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Constrain the origin of the mm/submm continuum**: Estimate spectral index from upper and lower sideband fluxes, compare L_con with L_1.4 by BCES, and build a power-law plus modified blackbody SED  
  > "by comparing the fluxes between the upper and lower sidebands of the CO lines" (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission); "We assume that the spectrum is simply represented by a combination of power-law (synchrotron) and modified blackbody (dust) components" (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission)
   - software: BCES
   - parameter: gamma = -0.75, nu_0 = 1.5 THz, b = 1.5  
  > "where $\gamma=-0.75$, $\nu_0=1.5$ THz, $b=1.5$, and" (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission)
   - assumption: Dust temperature T = 10 K  
  > "The dust temperature is assumed to be $T=10$ K" (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission)
   - fitting: Normalisations N_AGN and N_BB adjusted to reproduce NGC 5044  
  > "The constants $N_{\rm AGN}$ and $N_{\rm BB}$ are adjusted to reproduce the observations of NGC 5044." (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission)
   - validation: Checked that F_con and F_1.4 are uncorrelated, so the A3 != 1 slope is not a redshift selection effect  
  > "We have confirmed that there is no correlation between fluxes $F_{\rm con}$ and $F_{\rm 1.4}$" (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission)

## Qualitative findings

- Abell 262 is an outlier in the M_mol-P_cav relation, with a large gas mass but low jet power  
  > "Abell 262 appears to be an outlier of this correlation because it is outside the" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
- Circumnuclear gas appears to fuel AGN activity persistently on a long timescale, while continuum luminosities vary on short timescales  
  > "suggest that the circumnuclear gas is rather persistently fueling the AGN activity on a longer time scale" (Circumnuclear gas and AGN activity)
- Spectral indices are negative for all but NGC 5044, so mm continuum is mostly synchrotron  
  > "except for NGC 5044, which indicates that the emission is likely to be dominated by synchrotron radiation" (The origin of the mm/submm continuum emission)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Use ALMA archival CO data where the inner 500 pc is resolved and X-ray cavity jet powers exist; fixed physical radius avoids distance-driven beam bias  
  > "Moreover, we chose those for which the inner 500 pc (1 kpc in diameter) is resolved with ALMA." (Data Reduction); "To lessen such biases, we focus on the mass within a fixed physical radius." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Turbulent diffusion is adopted to estimate accretion rate regardless of gas morphology  
  > "The accretion is likely to be induced by the angular momentum transfer by turbulence (cite: 2010MNRAS.408..961P; 2017MNRAS.466..677G)." (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
- Alternative: M87 excluded because its molecular gas is in the outer galaxy and unconnected to the AGN  
  > "the molecular gas detected in M87 is located in the outer region of the galaxy and has no connection to the AGN" (Data Reduction)
- Alternative: RXJ 0821+0752 excluded because of poor calibration from a water vapor radiometer issue  
  > "the data of RXJ 0821+0752 are poorly calibrated because of an issue of water vapor radiometer" (Data Reduction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): X_CO is not well understood for elliptical galaxies  
  > "is not well-understood for elliptical galaxies" (Molecular gas mass)
- Limitation (authors): Derived turbulent velocity may include unresolved rotation and is an upper limit  
  > "and it should be regarded as the upper limit" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
- Limitation (authors): Not all inflowing gas reaches the black hole because some forms stars  
  > "is eventually swallowed by the black hole because some of it is consumed in star formation" (Circumnuclear gas and jet power)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample of 9 objects limits conclusions  
  > "once a larger sample becomes available" (Circumnuclear gas and AGN activity)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 9 objects, and Abell 262 strongly affects most correlations (e.g. L_con vs M_mol goes from p = 0.30 to 0.036 when excluded)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed Milky Way X_CO and fixed CO line ratios may introduce systematic, possibly galaxy-dependent, errors in M_mol
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Heterogeneous archival data (different projects, bands, resolutions) with P_cav from literature at different cavity ages
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust SED model uses fixed T = 10 K and normalisation tuned to NGC 5044 alone
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Accretion rate depends on assumed alpha = 0.15 and turbulence-diffusion picture; Mdot adds little independent information beyond M_mol
