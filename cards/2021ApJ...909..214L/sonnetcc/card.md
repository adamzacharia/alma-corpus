## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the line cubes**: Calibration by ALMA/NAASC staff, then imaging of data cubes with tclean using Briggs robust 0.5  
  > "Initial data calibration was performed by ALMA / NAASC staff." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging); "The data cubes were imaged using the `tclean` task" (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; 0.018 arcsec pixels; cubes trimmed to inner 9 x 7 arcsec  
  > "with a Briggs robust weighting parameter of 0.5 to achieve a compromise between resolution and sensitivity to extended emission." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging); "The resulting images have a square pixel size of $0.018^{\prime \prime}$" (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Subtract continuum in the image plane**: Per-pixel continuum level from the distribution of channel intensities, following Jorgensen et al. 2016  
  > "we were unable to subtract the continuum emission in the Fourier domain and instead performed the continuum subtraction in the image plane." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging); "We followed the procedures of (cite: Jorgensen16) to define the continuum in a homogeneous and automated way based on the density distribution of channel intensities in each pixel." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
   - parameter: Symmetric Gaussian fit to each pixel's flux density distribution, then a skewed Gaussian over a range defined by that centroid; the new centroid is the continuum level  
  > "In brief, the flux density distribution of each pixel was first fit with a symmetric Gaussian." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging); "Then, the centroid of this fit was used to define a fitting range for a skewed Gaussian fit and the new centroid value (no longer necessarily symmetric) was recorded as the continuum level for that particular pixel." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Accuracy confirmed by visual inspection; reported method accuracy 2 sigma, negligible for lines above 20 sigma  
  > "The accuracy of our continuum subtraction was confirmed via visual inspection" (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
3. **Measure continuum rms per spectral window**: Median over pixels without significant continuum emission, excluding the central 0.06 pc region and the western UC HII region  
  > "We derived the continuum rms for each spectral window as the median value of all pixels without significant continuum emission." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
   - parameter: typical continuum rms ~0.60-0.65 mJy/beam (~0.79-0.87 K)  
  > "Overall, we found a typical continuum rms of ${\sim}0.60$–$0.65$ mJy beam$^{-1}$ (${\sim}0.79$–$0.87$ K)" (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit line profiles pixel by pixel**: Single Gaussian fits to all unblended transitions of each species, with a template line per species setting limits for the other lines  
  > "we identified and fit all unblended transitions with single Gaussian profiles." (Fitting Process); "the FWHM was restricted to 0.25–1.25$\times$ the FWHM of the template line" (Line Fitting)
   - parameter: Pixel mask at 5 sigma peak intensity within a 10 km/s window around each transition  
  > "a mask corresponding to a 5$\sigma$ level in peak intensity in a 10 km s$^{-1}$ window centered on the transition frequency was used to define a pixel-by-pixel mask." (Line Fitting)
   - parameter: Initial template fits: FWHM allowed 1.3 to 15 km/s  
  > "the FWHM was allowed to vary between 1.3 km s$^{-1}$ (i.e., the velocity resolution) and 15 km s$^{-1}$" (Line Fitting)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian per line; unresolved multiplets combined as one line  
  > "Integrated intensities for each observed line were determined by fitting a single Gaussian profile to each feature." (Line Fitting)
   - validation: Blended lines removed by visual inspection; random fits inspected; LTE model spectra from MADCUBA SLIM compared with data to assess blending  
  > "Blended lines were removed via visual inspection" (Fitting Process); "We used the SLIM (Spectral Line Identification and LTE Modelling) tool within `MADCUBA`" (Line Blending and Example LTE Spectra)
5. **Derive rotational temperature and column density maps**: Pixel-by-pixel rotational diagram with optical depth correction, posterior sampled with emcee  
  > "We used the Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) code `emcee` (cite: Foreman13) to generate posterior probability distributions of column densities and rotational temperatures consistent with the observed data." (Fitting Process)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: Initial parameter space 1e14-1e20 cm^-2 and 10-800 K, then a second iteration with narrower species-specific grids  
  > "In general, we initially explored a parameter space from $10^{14}$ cm$^{-2} \leq$ N$_{\rm{col}} \leq 10^{20}$ cm$^{-2}$ and 10 K $\leq$ T$_{\rm{rot}} \leq 800$ K" (Fitting Process)
   - parameter: Partition functions linearly interpolated from catalog values (JPL, CDMS)  
  > "Partition functions were linearly interpolated from catalog values for all molecules" (Rotational Temperature and Column Density Derivation)
   - parameter: Reported values are the 50th, 16th and 84th percentiles of the marginalized posteriors  
  > "The derived parameters and uncertainties are listed as the 50th, 16th, and 84th percentiles from the marginalized posterior distributions, respectively." (Fitting Process)
   - assumption: LTE level populations; optical depth correction factor applied; solid angle set by the 0.018 arcsec pixel  
  > "we use the pixel size ($0.018^{\prime \prime}$) to estimate the solid angle $\Omega$." (Rotational Temperature and Column Density Derivation)
   - assumption: Optically thick lines excluded  
  > "Optically-thick lines were excluded from the rotational diagram analysis." (Fitting Process)
   - fitting: affine invariant MCMC (emcee) on a chi-square likelihood including the optical depth correction; priors are the stated N and T ranges  
  > "we then used the affine invariant MCMC code `emcee` (cite: Foreman13) to fit the data and generate posterior probability distributions for N$_{\rm{T}}$ and T$_{\rm{rot}}$" (Rotational Temperature and Column Density Derivation); "to construct a likelihood function $L (\rm{data}, N_{\rm{T}}, T_{\rm{rot}})$ to be used for $\chi^2$ minimization (cite: Loomis18)" (Rotational Temperature and Column Density Derivation)
   - validation: Quality checks that fits were not peaked at prior edges; pixels with uncertainties above 200% and outliers excluded  
  > "we also excluded pixels with particularly high uncertainties (${>}200\%$) and those with highly discontinuous and outlier values." (Fitting Process)
6. **Treat special cases (CH3OCHO, CH3CH2CN, CH3CN)**: CH3OCHO uses CH3OH temperature; CH3CH2CN fit with two temperature components where warranted; optically thick CH3CN replaced by CH3 13CN scaled by a 12C/13C ratio from CH3OH/13CH3OH  
  > "instead adopted those of the chemically-similar molecule CH$_3$OH (cite: Garrod06; Garrod08) to derive column density." (Fitting Process); "we restricted this two-component analysis to instances where the secondary component had a rotational temperature that was $1.5\times$ in excess of that of the primary component." (Two CH3CH2CN Temperature Components); "we determined the $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C ratio from the column density ratios of CH$_3$OH/$^{13}$CH$_3$OH." (Optically Thick CH3CN and Isotopologues)
   - parameter: Median CH3OH/13CH3OH column density ratio of 22.8 adopted as 12C/13C  
  > "Overall, we find a median column density ratio of 22.8, which is the value we adopt." (Optically Thick CH3CN and Isotopologues)
   - assumption: 13CH3OH uses the CH3OH rotational temperature; HC1 excluded when deriving 13CH3OH column densities  
  > "Thus, we purposely excluded HC1 when deriving $^{13}$CH$_3$OH column densities." (Deriving the 12C/13C Ratio in G10.6)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CH3CN column densities in high density regions considered accurate only within a factor of 2  
  > "the reported CH$_3$CN column densities in high density regions should only be considered accurate within a factor of 2." (Optically Thick CH3CN and Isotopologues)
7. **Quantify spatial correlations between species**: Cross-correlation coefficient between column density maps sampled on a common quarter-beam grid, with and without masking HC1; scatter plots of N vs N and N vs T  
  > "we first performed 1/4-beam sampling, i.e., 4 data points extracted for each beam area" (Spatial Column Density Correlations); "we masked the pixels in a 0.16$^{\prime \prime}$ region centered on HC1." (Spatial Column Density Correlations)
   - parameter: CH2CHCN excluded from the cross-correlation for lack of independent points  
  > "excluding CH$_2$CHCN, which lacked a sufficient number of independent data points for meaningful comparison." (Spatial Column Density Correlations)
   - assumption: No fractional abundances relative to H2; column densities compared directly  
  > "We did not attempt to derive fractional abundances with respect to hydrogen due to a lack of H$_2$ gas tracers on these scales" (Spatial Column Density Correlations)
   - fitting: Power-law fit to NH2CHO vs HNCO column density; other fitting details not stated  
  > "It is well-characterized by a single power law given by \[N$_{\rm{NH}{_2}\rm{CHO}}] = 0.01 \times [\rm{N}_{\rm{HNCO}}]^{1.09}$." (HNCO and NH2CHO)
   - validation: Percentage pixel overlap reported per pair to flag trends valid only over small shared regions  
  > "We labeled the percentage overlap between the two molecules being compared in the upper left corner of each panel" (Spatial Column Density Correlations)
8. **Compare hot cores with literature sources**: Compare HC1 and HC2 fractional abundances and column density ratios with literature MYSOs, Galactic center clouds, comets and low-mass YSOs  
  > "shows the fractional abundances with respect to CH$_3$OH of G10.6 and the literature sample." (Massive Star-forming Regions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- COM emission is highly structured across the central 20,000 au, including two hot cores and several shells or filaments  
  > "We find highly-structured emission from complex organic molecules (COMs) throughout the central 20,000 au, including two hot molecular cores and several shells or filaments." (abstract)
- No single rotational temperature characterizes all COMs at a location  
  > "The rotational temperature maps reveal that there is no single temperature that characterizes all COMs at a specific location." (Rotational Temperatures and Column Densities)
- CH3OCH3 is distributed differently from CH3OCHO despite proposed direct chemical links  
  > "a spatial distribution of CH$_3$OCH$_3$ that is strikingly dissimilar to that of CH$_3$OCHO" (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The molecular line data were an unplanned by-product of a dataset designed for H30alpha kinematics  
  > "The auxiliary molecular line data were obtained gratuitously, and for this reason, the dataset was not necessarily optimized for maximal coverage of COM lines." (Observational Details)
- Why this method: Optically thick CH3CN regions required the optically thin isotopologue CH3 13CN; CH3CH2CN needed two temperature components  
  > "A two component rotational diagram was required to fit the majority of CH$_3$CH$_2$CN emission and regions of optically thick CH$_3$CN emission required the use of optically thin isotope CH$_3^{13}$CN." (Fitting Process)
- Alternative: Fourier-domain continuum subtraction rejected because no line-free channels exist across the map  
  > "we were unable to subtract the continuum emission in the Fourier domain and instead performed the continuum subtraction in the image plane." (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
- How they decided it was enough: Continuum subtraction judged adequate by visual inspection, so no more sophisticated method was used  
  > "we did not find it necessary to implement more sophisticated continuum subtraction methods" (Data Reduction, Continuum Subtraction, and Imaging)
- Limitation (authors): Dataset not optimized for COM line coverage  
  > "the dataset was not necessarily optimized for maximal coverage of COM lines." (Observational Details)
- Limitation (authors): CH3CN column densities in dense regions only good to a factor of 2 because the 12C/13C ratio is uncertain  
  > "the reported CH$_3$CN column densities in high density regions should only be considered accurate within a factor of 2." (Optically Thick CH3CN and Isotopologues)
- Limitation (authors): No fractional abundances relative to H2  
  > "We did not attempt to derive fractional abundances with respect to hydrogen due to a lack of H$_2$ gas tracers on these scales" (Spatial Column Density Correlations)
- Limitation (authors): Few independent beams for NH2CHO correlations  
  > "only ${\sim}$10 independent beams are available for correlations involving NH$_2$CHO" (Spatial Column Density Correlations)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LTE and a single temperature per pixel are assumed over line-of-sight columns that the authors say probe multiple environments
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Source size set by the 0.018 arcsec pixel, so beam filling and an unresolved sub-beam structure are not treated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 12C/13C of 22.8 is derived from CH3OH/13CH3OH with possible opacity effects and is low versus the expected 43
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Image-plane continuum subtraction in a line-rich, high continuum region may bias weak lines
