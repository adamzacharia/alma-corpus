## Science

- Fields: protoplanetary_disks, exoplanets
- Question: Does the apparently structureless disk of MP Mus hide substructures at 3 mm, and can a companion in the inner disk explain both the Gaia proper motion anomaly and the 3 mm cavity?  
  > "To better understand MP Mus we obtained new ALMA observations at 3 mm and with a spatial resolution (4 au) similar to that of the 1.3 mm data presented by (cite: Ribas_2023)" (Hidden substructures at 3 mm); "To explore if a protoplanet within the mass range and orbital separations suggested by the *Gaia* PMa could explain the inner cavity seen at 3 mm, we ran hydrodynamical simulations" (Confronting observations with simulations)
- Target **MP Mus**: coordinates _not stated_; z/distance 97.9 +/- 0.1 pc  
  > "This K1V star is located 97.9$\pm$0.1 pc away from Earth (cite: Gaia_2021b) and has an estimated age of 7-10 Myr (cite: Ribas_2023)." (Main); distance from Gaia (cite: Gaia_2021b)  
  > "This K1V star is located 97.9$\pm$0.1 pc away from Earth (cite: Gaia_2021b) and has an estimated age of 7-10 Myr (cite: Ribas_2023)." (Main)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate ALMA Band 3 and Band 6 data**: Pipeline calibration, then phase-only self-calibration per configuration, re-centring, flux rescaling between executions and a final combined self-cal round  
  > "In particular, we first performed phase-only self-calibration on the extended and compact configurations separately." (ALMA data processing and imaging); "We then ran one final round of phase-only self-calibration in which both the compact and extended configurations were combined." (ALMA data processing and imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: CASA 6.4.1.12 pipeline then 6.5.6.22 (Band 3); CASA 5.1.1-5 pipeline then 6.5.6.22 (Band 6)  
  > "All the observations were first calibrated using the pipeline script and CASA V.6.4.1.12, and further processing was performed with CASA V.6.5.6.22." (ALMA data processing and imaging); "The observations were calibrated with the pipeline and CASA V.5.1.1-5" (ALMA data processing and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image the continuum at 1.3 and 3 mm**: tclean with mtmfs, nterms=1, multiscale 0,1,3,5 beams, robust = 0 for both bands  
  > "we used the `mtmfs` option with `nterms=1` and scales of 0, 1, 3, and 5 beams" (ALMA data processing and imaging); "We adopted a `robust` parameter of 0 for both wavelengths" (ALMA data processing and imaging)
   - software: CASA tclean
   - parameter: rms 37 uJy/beam (1.3 mm) and 7.7 uJy/beam (3 mm); peak S/N 105 and 90  
  > "The peak S/N of these images is 105 at 1.3 mm and 90 at 3 mm." (ALMA data processing and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive visibility-space radial intensity profiles**: FRANK fit of the axisymmetric profile from visibilities (Hankel transform), geometry fitted non-parametrically first  
  > "Under the assumption that the the disk is axisymmetric, this software calculates the radial profile of the disk directly from the observed visibilities using a Hankel transform." (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - software: FRANK
   - parameter: 1.3 mm: alpha = 1.2, w_smooth = 5e-3  
  > "we adopted $\alpha$=1.2 and $w_{\rm smooth}=5\times10^{-3}$" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - parameter: 3 mm: alpha = 1.3, w_smooth = 1e-2 (lower values gave artificial ripples)  
  > "we found that going below $\alpha$=1.3 or $w_{\rm smooth}=10^{-2}$ produced clearly artificial ripples in the profile, and we thus used these values" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - assumption: Disk is axisymmetric  
  > "Under the assumption that the the disk is axisymmetric" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - fitting: FRANK non-parametric geometry fit with inclination and PA initialised from Ribas et al. 2023; hyper-parameters alpha and w_smooth explored  
  > "we first fit the geometry of the system with the non-parametric option and using the measured disk inclination and position angles as initial guesses (cite: Ribas_2023)" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - validation: Residual visibilities imaged: below 3 rms at 3 mm; two blobs near 5 rms at 1.3 mm. Fitted i and PA compatible with 1 deg uncertainty  
  > "the residuals are below three times the image noise (rms) in the case of the 3 mm observations, and the 1.3 mm show only two localised blobs around the 5 rms level" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
4. **Check substructures are not FRANK artefacts**: Radial profiles from robust=-0.5 tclean images with GOFISH, and a GPUVMEM image with no entropy term (positivity only)  
  > "we also calculated the radial profiles directly from the synthesised images using the GOFISH code (cite: GoFish)" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks); "The self-calibrated continuum data were super-resolved using GPUVMEM with no entropy term in the objective function (cite: Carcamo_2018); image positivity alone provided sufficient regularisation (cite: Perez_2020)." (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - software: GOFISH, GPUVMEM, CASA tclean
   - parameter: robust = -0.5 for the 3 mm image  
  > "we produced images with `robust=-0.5` to improve the angular resolution" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - fitting: GPUVMEM maximum-entropy-family image reconstruction with positivity only  
  > "The self-calibrated continuum data were super-resolved using GPUVMEM with no entropy term in the objective function" (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
   - validation: Ring and two gaps visible in the image profile; cavity only tentative in GOFISH profile but visible in GPUVMEM image  
  > "The ring and two gaps are also visible in the image profile. The cavity is only tentatively detected in the profile as a flattening of the intensity in the inner regions." (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks); "The GPUVMEM image reproduces the same substructures identified in the `tclean` image while also revealing a small central cavity." (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
5. **Measure 3 mm flux, dust mass and disk radii**: Flux from robust=0.5 image; dust mass from optically thin emission with bootstrap over flux, temperature and beta; radii from the cumulative intensity profile of robust=0 images  
  > "To determine the disk mass and its uncertainty we bootstrapped 1000 estimates by varying the 3 mm flux within its uncertainty" (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm); "based on the cumulative curve of the intensity profiles" (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Dust temperature 20 K (Normal, sigma 2.5 K in bootstrap); beta uniform 0 to 0.5; 1000 bootstrap draws; median and 16/84 percentiles reported  
  > "as well as the temperature value following a Normal distribution with a standard deviation of 2.5 K, and $\beta$ with a uniform distribution between 0 and 0.5" (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm); "The reported dust mass and uncertainties correspond to the median and the 16% and 84% values." (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm)
   - parameter: kappa_230GHz = 2.3 cm2/g  
  > "where $\kappa_\text{230 GHz}$=2.3 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$ and $\beta$ values between 0 and 0.5" (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission, average dust temperature 20 K, power-law dust opacity; gas-to-dust ratio 100 used later for disk mass in simulations  
  > "optically thin emission, an average dust temperature of 20 K, and a power-law dependence of the dust opacity with frequency" (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm)
   - fitting: Bootstrap of 1000 estimates  
  > "we bootstrapped 1000 estimates" (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm)
   - validation: Dust mass compatible with that from the 1.3 mm flux in Ribas et al. 2023  
  > "This mass is compatible with the dust mass derived from the 1.3 mm flux in (cite: Ribas_2023)." (Properties and additional analysis of the ALMA data at 3 mm)
6. **Constrain companion mass and separation from the Gaia proper motion anomaly**: Compare Gaia DR2 and DR3 proper motions, then simulate Gaia observations of a two-body system with 500 000 random draws and keep those matching the observed anomaly and RUWE  
  > "We then used these epochs and angles, together with realistic *Gaia* uncertainties, to simulate the *Gaia* DR2 and DR3 observations of MP Mus" (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus); "We then selected only those simulations which resulted in astrometric properties compatible with what is observed in MP Mus" (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus)
   - software: ASTROMET
   - parameter: Mass ratio 0.0001 to 1 and period 0.016 to 1585 yr, log-uniform; selection UWE_DR3 < 1.1; stellar mass 1.30 +/- 0.08 Msun, inclination 32 +/- 1 deg, PA 10 +/- 1 deg  
  > "simulations randomly sampling the log-uniform space of mass ratios (from 0.0001 to 1) and periods (from 0.016 to 1585 yr)" (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus); "We hence imposed a conservative threshold to the simulations of $\rm{UWE_{DR3}}<1.1$ (cite: Penoyre_2022a)." (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus)
   - assumption: No eccentricity; light ratio l = q^3.5; PMa dominated by a single companion  
  > "We assumed no eccentricity and a relation between the mass" (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus); "In this work we assume that, although there may be other planets in MP Mus, its PMa is dominated by a single companion." (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo rejection sampling (no fit)  
  > "The mass of the primary source, the viewing angles, and the orbital phase were sampled at random every time" (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Run SPH hydrodynamic models of a star plus planet in the disk**: Grid of 25 3D PHANTOM simulations, one-fluid dust with five grain sizes, evolved for 1000 planet orbits  
  > "We ran 3D global hydrodynamical simulations using the SPH code PHANTOM (cite: Price_2018)" (PHANTOM simulations); "We let the simulations evolve for $1000$ planet orbits" (PHANTOM simulations)
   - software: PHANTOM
   - parameter: N = 1e6 particles; R_out = 50 au; R_in = 1.5 a_p; alpha_AV = 0.2, beta_AV = 2 (alpha_ss = 5e-3); disk mass 0.01 Msun; star 1.3 Msun; H/R = 0.1 at 100 au; grains 1 um to 0.5 cm, n(s) proportional to s^-3.5, 3 g/cm3  
  > "The resolution is set by the number of particles ($N=10^6$)." (PHANTOM simulations); "We simulated five dust grains sizes ranging from $1~\mu$m to 0.5 cm and with intrinsic density 3 g cm$^{-3}$." (PHANTOM simulations); "The outer radius is initialised to 50 au as traced by the 1.3 mm continuum observations" (PHANTOM simulations)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust ratio 100, locally isothermal disk with T proportional to R^-0.5 and surface density proportional to R^-1  
  > "The dust component is set by adopting an initial gas-to-dust ratio of 100 uniformly across the entire disk." (PHANTOM simulations); "the initial surface density profile follows $\Sigma \propto R^{-1}$" (PHANTOM simulations)
   - fitting: No fit; grid over a_p and M_p compared with observed cavity  
  > "we ran a grid of 25 simulations varying the semi-major axis and the mass of the companion within ranges compatible with the *Gaia* PMa" (PHANTOM simulations)
   - validation: Cavity radius is 3-4 times the primary sink radius, so it is tidal and not a numerical artefact  
  > "the radius of cavity we observe is always 3-4 times bigger, meaning that it is carved by the tidal interaction rather than being a numerical artefact" (PHANTOM simulations)
8. **Radiative transfer of the SPH models and comparison with ALMA profiles**: MCFOST Monte Carlo radiative transfer on the SPH Voronoi mesh, 1.3 and 3 mm images convolved with ALMA beams, radial profiles with GOFISH  
  > "We then performed radiative transfer with the MCFOST code (cite: MCFOST; MCFOST2) to produce continuum images at 1.3 and 3 mm and convolved the results with the corresponding ALMA beams." (Confronting observations with simulations)
   - software: MCFOST, GOFISH
   - parameter: Star: 5000 K black body, R = 1.25 Rsun, M = 1.3 Msun; N_gamma = 1e8 photons for temperature and for ray-tracing images  
  > "modelled as a black body with temperature of 5000 K" (PHANTOM simulations); "Images are then computed via ray-tracing again with $N_\gamma = 10^8$ photons." (PHANTOM simulations)
   - assumption: DIANA dust: 60% silicates, 15% amorphous carbon, 25% porosity; local thermal equilibrium; passive stellar heating  
  > "i.e. 60% silicates, 15% amorphous carbon (cite: Dorschner_1995; Zubko_1996) and 25% porosity" (PHANTOM simulations); "in order to compute the temperature structure of the disk assuming local thermal equilibrium" (PHANTOM simulations)
   - fitting: No fit; qualitative comparison of cavity size and depth at 1.3 vs 3 mm  
  > "The mass of the companion does not have a strong effect on the cavity size, but more massive objects result in deeper cavities." (Confronting observations with simulations)
   - validation: Models at 1-2 au reproduce the cavity size; some also show the cavity at 3 mm but not at 1.3 mm  
  > "the simulations show that the observed cavity can be successfully explained by a gas giant orbiting at 1-2 au" (Confronting observations with simulations)

## Qualitative findings

- The 3 mm image shows a ring and two gaps and a barely resolved inner cavity that are invisible at 1.3 mm at the same resolution  
  > "the new 3 mm data reveal a ring at 10.5 au and two gaps at 7.5 and 15 au that are undetected at shorter wavelengths" (Hidden substructures at 3 mm)
- SPH models with a gas giant at 1-2 au reproduce the cavity size; planets at 0.5 au give no resolvable cavity and at 2 au or more give cavities that are too large  
  > "none of the models with an orbital separation of 0.5 au carve an inner cavity large enough to be discernible with the current spatial resolution, regardless of their mass" (Confronting observations with simulations); "planets at $\geq$2 au produce cavities that are too large to be compatible with the observations" (Confronting observations with simulations)
- Census of disk substructures is potentially very incomplete and long-wavelength high-resolution surveys are needed  
  > "indicate that our current census of disk substructures (cite: Bae_2023) is potentially very incomplete" (Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Longer wavelengths have lower dust optical depth and trace larger, more strongly trapped grains, so substructures may show higher contrast  
  > "Longer wavelengths offer two main advantages to search for substructures in disks." (Hidden substructures at 3 mm)
- Why this method: FRANK works in visibility space and reaches higher angular resolution than synthesised images  
  > "We do this in visibility space with the code FRANK (cite: frank), which achieves higher angular resolution and recovers smaller structures than working with synthesised images." (Hidden substructures at 3 mm)
- Alternative: Each of the cavity and two gaps carved by a different planet, or a single planet carving multiple gaps, or non-planet mechanisms, are acknowledged but not tested  
  > "we caution that mechanisms other than planets can open cavities and gaps" (Hidden substructures at 3 mm)
- Alternative: Companion in either gap (7.5 or 15 au) rejected as the PMa source because it would need >10 MJup  
  > "even if planets may be carving those gaps, they are unlikely to be the cause of the PMa" (Hidden substructures at 3 mm)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only a two-body interaction is modelled in the Gaia analysis; multi-body interpretation is more complex  
  > "We note that we only model a two-body interaction, and the interpretation of the PMa becomes more complex for multi-body systems." (The Gaia proper motion anomaly of MP Mus)
- Limitation (authors): Simulation outcome is highly sensitive to disk and dust properties that were not explored in detail  
  > "this is highly sensitive to the disk and dust properties which we have not explored in detail" (Full set of PHANTOM simulations)
- Limitation (authors): Cavity only tentatively detected in image-plane profile  
  > "The cavity is only tentatively detected in the profile as a flattening of the intensity in the inner regions." (Radial profiles and additional sanity checks)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The planet case rests on a single Gaia PMa at 4.5 sigma; no independent detection of the companion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The inner cavity is near the beam size (~4 au beam vs ~3 au radius) and depends on FRANK hyper-parameters
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SPH models used a fixed disk and dust setup without exploring it, so the 3 mm vs 1.3 mm cavity contrast is not robust
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust mass assumes optically thin 3 mm emission and 20 K, while inner disk may be optically thick
