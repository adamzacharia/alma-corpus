## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and combine ALMA data**: Standard pipeline calibration in CASA; executions from different cycles combined before imaging  
  > "The data have been reduced and calibrated using a standard pipeline running on Common Astronomy Software Application (CASA) version 5.1.1-5 for the band 6 data taken in cycle 5" (3 ALMA observations and imaging); "Data from different execution blocks and the different cycles was combined before imaging." (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Flux calibration uncertainty estimated from historical calibrator monitoring: ~30% band 8, ~10% band 6  
  > "we extracted historical measurements of the flux calibrators from the ALMA source catalogue and used the maximum and minimum spectral index within a month around each observing date" (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **image cubes and continuum, with tapering**: CASA clean with natural weighting; dirty image rms measured first, then cleaning threshold set to 2x rms; several spectral resolutions and tapers  
  > "Imaging was done using the CASA task `clean`." (3 ALMA observations and imaging); "For the clean image, we set the cleaning threshold to 2 times the rms measured in the dirty image." (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
   - software: CASA clean
   - parameter: natural weighting; tapers up to 0.35'' and 0.5'' ([C II]); spectral resolution from native to ~200 km/s  
  > "We create image cubes and continuum images using a natural weighting." (3 ALMA observations and imaging); "We have also created \[C ii\] image cubes with a tapering parameter of $0.5\ensuremath{''}$" (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
   - assumption: [C II] emitting region may be 2-3 times larger than the UV component, motivating the 0.5'' taper  
  > "since recent results indicate that the \[C ii\]-emitting region may be 2 – 3 times larger than the UV component" (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Measured rms in dirty images checked against theoretical noise  
  > "We verify that the measured rms in these images is consistent with the theoretically expected noise level." (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
3. **search for line emission near the target**: Visual inspection of cubes in velocity slices, then systematic search for pixels above 3x rms within 1'' of the target; moment-0 maps and spectra; imfit integrated SNR for brightest features  
  > "In the search for potential signal, we select all pixels above 3 times the image rms within $1\ensuremath{''}$ of the target position." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes); "we calculate an integrated SNR by fitting a 2D-Gaussian using the CASA task `imfit`" (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: Search range -2000 to 700 km/s ([O III]) and -2900 to 1300 km/s ([C II]) relative to expected line position  
  > "For the \[O iii\] line, we search for line emission between $\approx -2000 \text{ to } 700 \ \mathrm{km \ s^{-1}}$ relative to the expected position of the line" (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
   - parameter: 5 sigma desired for a firm detection; marginal features also examined  
  > "Ideally, in order to claim a firm detection, we would want a signal at the $5\sigma$ level." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit with imfit for brightest candidate features  
  > "we calculate an integrated SNR by fitting a 2D-Gaussian using the CASA task `imfit`" (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
   - validation: Features rejected for large spatial/velocity offsets and because negative pixels of equal significance exist; pixel noise Gaussianity checked  
  > "In most of our collapsed maps, we are also able to find collections of negative pixels at the same significance as the positive ones" (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
4. **derive 3 sigma upper limits on [O III] and [C II] line luminosities**: Integrated rms measured in images collapsed over 100 and 400 km/s around expected line positions; 3 sigma used as upper limit assuming unresolved source  
  > "we thus calculate the integrated rms in a collapse of $\Delta v = 100$ and $400 \ \mathrm{km \ s^{-1}}$ around the expected positions of the lines given the source redshift" (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes); "We adopted a 3$\sigma$ threshold as an upper limit for a non-detection in our analysis." (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Fiducial line FWHM 100 km/s, also 400 km/s  
  > "Thus, as our fiducial line width for both the \[C ii\] and \[O iii\] line width, we use $100 \ \mathrm{km \ s^{-1}}$." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
   - assumption: Target unresolved with native beams; no continuum subtraction  
  > "Assuming that the target is unresolved with the native beam sizes, these fluxes correspond to $3\sigma$ upper limits on the line luminosities" (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Variation of integrated rms with assumed line centre is about +/-2% (band 8) and +2%/-4% (band 6) between -500 and 250 km/s  
  > "Thus, different assumptions on the line centers should not alter our results significantly." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
5. **dust continuum upper limits and total IR luminosity**: Continuum rms in full-band images after masking foreground source; modified black-body integrated over 8-1000 um with CMB correction; T_d 35 and 55 K as variations  
  > "by integrating a modified black-body (cite: DeBreuck_emission_2003) curve over 8 – 1000 $\mu \mathrm{m}$" (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum); "In this procedure, we correct for dust heating and the background effect of the CMB following (cite: Ota_ALMA_2014)." (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum)
   - parameter: beta_d = 1.5, T_d = 45 K (also 35 and 55 K)  
  > "while assuming an emissivity index of $\beta_d=1.5$ and a dust temperature of $T_d = 45 \ \mathrm{K}$" (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum)
   - assumption: Dust temperature of 45 K and beta_d = 1.5 chosen to match other studies  
  > "These values are largely consistent with assumptions made in other studies" (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Sensitivity to T_d checked with 35 K and 55 K  
  > "For comparison, if we instead assume a dust temperature of 35 K or 55 K, we find:" (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum)
6. **compare [C II] luminosity and SFR with local and high-z relations**: UV and IR SFRs from Kennicutt & Evans relations; UV+IR SFR with IR 1 sigma limit as upper error bar; compare with Matthee et al. compilation rescaled to 100 km/s and De Looze local relations  
  > "Using the UV magnitude and the relationship between UV luminosity and SFR by (cite: kennicutt_star_2012)" (4.3 The [C II]-SFR relation); "we have gone back to the individual publications and re-scaled all the upper limits to the same line-width as z7_GSD_3811" (4.3 The [C II]-SFR relation)
   - parameter: T_d = 45 K for SFR_IR; velocity-integrated rms scales as sqrt(line width)  
  > "by assuming that the velocity-integrated rms scales as the square root of the line width" (4.3 The [C II]-SFR relation)
   - assumption: UV magnitude error bars assumed symmetric and equal to the larger error  
  > "assume that the error bars are symmetric and equal to the larger error presented there" (4.3 The [C II]-SFR relation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison repeated for 4x wider line and with 6 sigma upper limits  
  > "Even if we assume a \[C ii\] line which is 4 times wider, our upper limit still places z7_GSD_3811 below the local relations" (4.3 The [C II]-SFR relation)
7. **place the target in the IRX-beta plane**: UV slope from CANDELS F125W and F160W; IRX from 3 sigma band 6 L_IR upper limit; compared to Calzetti, SMC and ALPINE relations  
  > "Following (cite: ono_stellar_2010), we find a UV slope for z7_GSD_3811 using the F125W and F160W fluxes" (4.4 IRX-beta); "In Fig. [4](#fig:IRX_Beta), we show the IRX-$\beta$ relations of the Calzetti and SMC dust laws calculated following (cite: bouwens_ALMA_2016) assuming an intrinsic UV slope $\beta_0=-2.23$." (4.4 IRX-beta)
   - parameter: T_d = 45 K, beta_d = 1.5 for L_IR; L_UV = nu L_nu at 1500 A rest-frame  
  > "UV luminosities are calculated as $L_{\mathrm{UV}}=\nu L_{\nu}$ at $1500 \ \mathrm{\AA}$ rest-frame" (4.4 IRX-beta)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative bluer UV slope from Song et al. (beta ~ -2.4) considered  
  > "If we would use the values presented there ($25.8$, $25.9$, for the two filters, respectively) we would obtain a significantly bluer UV slope $\beta \approx -2.4$." (4.4 IRX-beta)
8. **compare L[O III]/L_UV with high-z detections and local dwarfs**: Compute L_UV for literature [O III] detections (with lensing magnification) and compare the L[O III]/L_UV upper limit with high-z objects and local dwarf galaxies versus oxygen abundance  
  > "While we consider effects of lensing magnification, the lensing errors are not included." (4.5 [O III] and UV luminosity); "Our $3 \sigma$ upper limit places z7_GSD_3811 at similar $L_{O iii} / L_{\mathrm{UV}}$ ratio as nearby dwarf galaxies that have an oxygen abundance of $\approx 4\%$ to $30\%$ of the solar abundance." (4.5 [O III] and UV luminosity)
   - parameter: Solar oxygen abundance 12+log(O/H) = 8.69  
  > "where we use $12 \ + \ \mathrm{log_{10}}(n_O/n_H)_{\odot} \ = \ 8.69$ (cite: asplund_chemical_2009)" (4.5 [O III] and UV luminosity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Result repeated for a 4x wider line  
  > "This value does not change significantly in the case that we assume a four times wider line." (4.5 [O III] and UV luminosity)
9. **SED fitting with ALMA upper limits**: PANHIT SED fit to HST/HAWK-I photometry plus ALMA [O III] and band 6/8 continuum upper limits, constant SFR, Chabrier IMF, GALAXEV stellar models, Calzetti attenuation  
  > "we fit the SED using the PANHIT code" (5 SED fitting); "We assume a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: chabrier_galactic_2003) and a single stellar component." (5 SED fitting)
   - software: PANHIT
   - parameter: Redshift fixed to 7.664 and escape fraction zero; Z = 0.0001-0.02; A_V = 0-0.5 in steps of 0.05  
  > "In the SED fitting procedure, we fix the redshift to $z=7.664$ and the escape fraction of ionizing photons to zero." (5 SED fitting); "The metallicity range used for the fitting is $Z = 0.0001$ – $0.02$" (5 SED fitting)
   - parameter: 7 data points, 4 fitted parameters, 3 degrees of freedom; age restricted to 300 Myr for the alternative solutions  
  > "This leaves us with 7 observed data-points and 4 parameters to be fitted, leading to 3 degrees of freedom." (5 SED fitting); "If we restrict the age of our constant SFR models to 300 Myr" (5 SED fitting)
   - assumption: Constant SFR; [C II] upper limit not included  
  > "we assume the simplified case of a constant SFR" (5 SED fitting); "We do not include the upper limit on the \[C ii\] line" (5 SED fitting)
   - fitting: Chi-square minimisation with PANHIT; priors not stated  
  > "the best-fitting solution ($\chi_{\nu}^2 \  = \ 3.40$) obtained from PANHIT" (5 SED fitting)
   - validation: Exponentially increasing SFRs and SMC extinction law tested; fits not essentially different  
  > "Note that we have also performed tests with exponentially increasing SFRs, which produce fits that are not essentially different than the ones obtained from a constant SFR model." (5 SED fitting)

## Qualitative findings

- No [O III], [C II] or dust continuum emission is detected at the target position; candidate features are offset and cannot be distinguished from noise  
  > "After all these checks, we are unable to find any potential \[C ii\] and \[O iii\] emission that can be reliably distinguished from noise and which can be associated with our target." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
- A foreground continuum source is detected in both bands, away from the target  
  > "a foreground continuum source is detected in both the band 6 and band 8 dust continuum images" (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum)
- The SED fits do not uniquely favour a low metallicity solution  
  > "Hence, the SED fitting does not uniquely favour a low metallicity solution." (5 SED fitting)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Target at z=7.664 places [O III] 88 um and [C II] 158 um in bands 8 and 6, and similar UV-luminosity objects were detected in these lines, so it is a suitable target  
  > "Thus, z7_GSD_3811 should be a suitable target for expanding the currently limited number of \[O iii\]-detected objects" (2 Target)
- Why this method: Fiducial 100 km/s line width chosen from similar-UV-luminosity objects; tapering motivated by source size and possibly extended [C II]  
  > "Thus, as our fiducial line width for both the \[C ii\] and \[O iii\] line width, we use $100 \ \mathrm{km \ s^{-1}}$." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes); "Given the apparent size of the source in the UV images (with an estimated diameter of $\sim 0.3\ensuremath{''}$) and the synthesized beam in band 8" (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
- Alternative: Detected line could be [O II] 3727 at z~1.8 rather than Lyman-alpha (not ruled out by Song et al.)  
  > "There is, however, a possibility that this line belongs to the \[O ii\] $\lambda 3727$ doublet at $z\approx1.8$." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
- Alternative: Dust temperatures of 35 K and 55 K tested alongside 45 K  
  > "For comparison, if we instead assume a dust temperature of 35 K or 55 K, we find:" (4.2 Upper limits for the 87 um and 152 um dust continuum)
- Alternative: Candidate [O III] and [C II] features rejected because of large spatial and velocity offsets and narrow widths  
  > "and thus are unlikely to belong to the \[O iii\] line." (4.1 Upper limits for the [O III] and [C II] line fluxes)
- How they decided it was enough: Non-detection declared after visual and systematic searches found nothing above 3 sigma at the target; 3 sigma adopted for upper limits  
  > "We adopted a 3$\sigma$ threshold as an upper limit for a non-detection in our analysis." (3 ALMA observations and imaging)
- Limitation (authors): SFH and age poorly constrained with limited rest-frame optical/UV data  
  > "With the limited number of observations in the rest-frame optical and UV, it is difficult to accurately constrain the SFH and age (and therefore also the SFR) of the object." (6 Discussion & Summary)
- Limitation (authors): Upper limit does not exclude higher metallicity  
  > "Thus, we emphasize that our upper limit does not exclude the possibility that the object has a higher metallicity" (6 Discussion & Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Upper limits depend strongly on the assumed 100 km/s line width and the unresolved-source assumption
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The T_d = 45 K, beta_d = 1.5 assumption drives the IR luminosity and SFR_IR limits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 7 data points constrain a 4-parameter SED fit, with chi2_nu 3.4-4.0; solutions are degenerate
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA flux scale uncertainty up to ~30% in band 8 may shift the [O III] comparison
