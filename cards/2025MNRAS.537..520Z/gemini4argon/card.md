## Qualitative findings

- The nuclear 1.3 mm continuum source is spatially unresolved down to 0.080 arcsec × 0.051 arcsec (and at robust = 0.5) near the galaxy's kinematic centre.  
  > "The deconvolved size of the source, derived by fitting a two-dimensional (2D) Gaussian with the CASA task `imfit`, is consistent with it being spatially unresolved (i.e. a point source)." (2.3 Continuum emission)
- There is a physical decrease (dip) in the CO(2-1) surface brightness within a radius of ~0.1 arcsec, rather than a masking artefact, though sufficient emission remains to trace the kinematics near the SMBH.  
  > "The slight central dip in the zeroth-moment map is likely a physical decrease of the CO surface brightness within a radius of ≈ 0 [′′] _._ 1, rather than an artefact of the masking procedure as suggested by North et al. (2019), as the dip remains as the clipping threshold of the mask is decreased." (2.4.2 Moment maps)
- The high-resolution velocity map and PVDs reveal a slight twist of the isovelocity contours within the central ~0.3 arcsec, forbidden-quadrant emission, an offset between the kinematic and morphological centres, and an asymmetry between the innermost blueshifted (~635 km s^-1) and redshifted (~535 km s^-1) velocity peaks.  
  > "We detect a clear Keplerian increase (from the outside in) of the line-of-sight rotation velocities, a slight offset between the gas disc kinematic (i.e. the position of the SMBH) and morphological (i.e. the centre of the molecular gas emission) centres, an asymmetry of the innermost rotation velocity peaks and evidence for a mild position angle warp and/or non-circular motions within the central ≈ 0 [′′] _._ 3." (abstract)
- The asymmetry between the blueshifted and redshifted central velocity peaks is reproduced by the regular circular-motion disc model using the deconvolved CLEAN components, indicating it is caused by a deficiency of CO(2-1) emission on the redshifted side rather than non-circular motions.  
  > "Despite the evidence of non-circular motions, the asymmetry between the redshifted and blueshifted central velocity peaks of NGC 383 is unlikely to be caused by non-circular motions, as this feature is well reproduced by our best-fitting regular disc model assuming pure circular motions (see Figure 6)." (4.1.3 Implications for the SMBH mass)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Ultra-high angular resolution C43-10 ALMA observations (~0.034 arcsec) were obtained to resolve the SMBH sphere of influence of NGC 383 by a factor of ~24 and probe down to ~41,300 Schwarzschild radii, and were combined with two intermediate-resolution tracks (C36-4 and C40-7) because the C43-10 MRS (0.5 arcsec) is smaller than R_SoI (~1.0 arcsec).  
  > "With ≈ 4 times better angular resolution, this new study aims to achieve the highest spatial resolution measurement (in the unit of _𝑅_ Sch ) of all SMBH mass measurements to date, spatially resolving the central molecular gas disc with unprecedented detail to derive a much more precise SMBH mass." (1 INTRODUCTION); "Because the MRS is smaller than the expected _𝑅_ SoI, we combine those data with two prior intermediate-resolution observing tracks to improve the _𝑢𝑣_ -plane coverage and fully recover the SoI." (2.2 Observations and data reduction)
- Why this method: SkySampler was used to sample deconvolved CLEAN components because mild spiral structure makes a simple axisymmetric parametric gas profile inappropriate; informative priors from the intermediate-resolution cube were placed on inclination and M/L because the lower S/N of the high-resolution cube otherwise degrades the inclination constraint; natural weighting (robust = 2.0) was used to model the central velocity twist to maximise S/N.  
  > "The mild spiral structure in the molecular gas disc of NGC 383 makes it inappropriate to assume a smooth axisymmetric gas distribution described by a simple parametric function (e.g. an exponential disc)." (3.2 Gas distribution); "Hence, we place informative priors on the inclination and the _𝑀_ / _𝐿_ using constraints derived from fitting the previous intermediate-resolution data cube." (3.4 Prior distributions); "Instead, we re-image the data with Briggs weighting and a robust parameter of 2 _._ 0 (equivalent to natural weighting), to maximize the _𝑆_ / _𝑁_ ." (4.1 Warps and/or non-circular motions)
- Alternative: Smooth axisymmetric parametric gas distribution (rejected due to mild spiral structure in the molecular gas disc; used SkySampler on CLEAN components instead)  
  > "The mild spiral structure in the molecular gas disc of NGC 383 makes it inappropriate to assume a smooth axisymmetric gas distribution described by a simple parametric function (e.g. an exponential disc)." (3.2 Gas distribution)
