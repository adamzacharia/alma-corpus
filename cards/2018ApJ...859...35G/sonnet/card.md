## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO(1-0)**: ALMA pipeline calibration, uvcontsub, statwt, natural-weighted clean with ~100 km/s channels  
  > "Prior to using uvmodelfit, the task statwt was used to calculate the weights for the visibilities based on line-free channels only." (2.1.1)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: cell 0.3", 71-channel binning  
  > "a pixel size of $0''.3 \times 0''.3$, after binning over 71 spectral channels" (2.1.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Define outflow wings iteratively and measure wing fluxes/positions**: Fit 3 Gaussians (core, red, blue) to spectrum from 5x5" region, set velocity limits, rebin, re-extract and repeat until convergence; uvmodelfit 2-D Gaussian to binned visibilities  
  > "we simultaneously fit 3 Gaussian profiles to the spectrum, one each for the red wing, the blue wing and the line core" (3.1.2)
   - software: CASA uvmodelfit
   - parameter: 5"x5" extraction region at peak pixel  
  > "The initial spectrum is extracted from a $5'' \times 5''$ region centered on the peak pixel" (3.1.2)
   - fitting: 3-Gaussian spectral fit; 2-D Gaussian uv model  
  > "assuming a 2-D Gaussian source to obtain the FWHM spatial extent and the flux" (3.1.2)
   - validation: Results consistent with those from cleaned image  
  > "The resultant properties are consistent with those calculated from the cleaned image." (3.1.2)
3. **Derive gas masses and outflow rates**: M_OF from L'_CO with alpha_CO; Mdot = M_OF v_OF/R_OF; R_OF half the red-blue peak separation; momentum and energy fluxes  
  > "We assume the radius of the outflow ($R_{\rm OF}$) to be half the separation of the peaks of the red and blue emission peak" (4.1)
   - parameter: mass-weighted mean outflow velocity of red and blue wings; no inclination correction  
  > "We use the mass-weighted outflow velocity, averaged between the red and blue outflow wings, as the representative outflow velocity $v_{\rm OF}$." (4.1)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 0.8 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1, 100% molecular; tidal components 3.6  
  > "assuming $\alpha_{\rm CO}$$\sim 0.8$ $M_{\odot}$(K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^{-2}$)$^{-1}$$$ to calculate the outflow mass" (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: alpha_CO systematic uncertainty discussed (factor 2.4 lower, 5.5 higher)  
  > "The value of $\alpha_{\rm CO}$$$ can be lower by a factor of $\sim 2.4$ or higher by a factor of $\sim 5.5$" (4.1)
4. **SED fitting for SFR, dust mass, AGN fraction**: CIGALE with double-exponential SFH, Calzetti attenuation, Draine & Li dust emission, Fritz torus AGN module  
  > "we assume the star formation history to be a double exponentially decreasing function of time, the dust attenuation as in (cite: calzetti2000), and the dust emission models from (cite: dl2014)" (4.2.1)
   - software: CIGALE
   - fitting: CIGALE probability distribution function analysis, likelihood-weighted mean  
  > "CIGALE performs a probability distribution function analysis for our specified module parameters and obtains the likelihood-weighted mean value for each." (4.2.1)
   - validation: Fit A with and without SPIRE photometry for IRAS 03158+4227: no significant differences  
  > "We therefore perform the SED fitting for A with and without the SPIRE photometry, to test for contamination from component B, and find no significant differences in our results." (4.2.1)
5. **Mid-IR spectral decomposition for f_AGN and L_AGN, applied to comparison sample**: Fit starburst template (average of five SB ULIRGs) plus power-law AGN with extinction, water ice and HAC features  
  > "We create the starburst template by averaging the mid-IR spectra from five starburst-dominated ULIRGs" (4.2.2)
   - parameter: AGN power law f_nu ∝ lambda^gamma, gamma ~0.7-1.5; tau ∝ lambda^-1.75; L_AGN ~ f_AGN L_IR  
  > "use a power-law template for the AGN contribution ($f_{\nu} \propto \lambda^{\gamma}$, with $\gamma \sim 0.7 - 1.5$)" (4.2.2)
   - fitting: free: ice features, alpha_6, optical depth at 6 um, normalization  
  > "We leave the parameters for the ice features, $\alpha_{6}$, the optical depth at 6 $\mu$m and the overall normalization as free parameters in the fitting." (4.2.2)
   - validation: Compared with SED fits, X-ray and literature f_AGN (within 0.1 dex except IRAS 10565+2448)  
  > "These are consistent within the uncertainties with $f_{\rm AGN}$ found using SED fitting" (4.2.2)
6. **Compare outflow momentum and energy with AGN and starburst predictions; test correlations**: Compare Pdot_OF and Edot_OF with L_AGN/c, L*/c, predicted sums; Spearman correlations  
  > "We detect no significant correlation between the outflow momentum flux and $L_{*}/c$ and $L_{\rm IR}/c$" (5.1.1)
   - assumption: Pdot_sum ~ 20 L_AGN/c + 3.5 L*/c; Edot_sum = 0.05 L_AGN + 0.02 L*  
  > "$\dot E_{\rm sum} = 0.05 L_{\rm AGN} + 0.02 L_{*}$" (5.1.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Spearman and Pearson both show no significant correlation  
  > "We did not recover significant correlations using either diagnostic." (Footnote 7)

## Qualitative findings

- Outflow properties correlate no better with L_AGN than with L* or L_IR; f_AGN ~0.2-0.9 in the strongest outflows  
  > "We also find that the strongest outflows span a large range in $f_{\rm AGN} = 0.2 - 0.9$" (5.2)

## Claim tuples

- H: Molecular outflows deplete gas faster than star formation | E: CO(1-0) wing mass outflow rates vs gas mass and SFR | R: tau_dep,OF ~11 and ~16 Myr vs ~33 and ~46 Myr | F: Outflows can starve the galaxies of gas quickly | **supported**
- H: The most powerful outflows are driven by AGN alone | E: Compare Pdot and Edot with L_AGN, L*, L_IR across a ULIRG sample | R: No significant correlation with any luminosity | F: AGN luminosity alone is a poor tracer; AGN plus starburst may drive them | **partially**
- H: OH megamaser traces molecular outflows | E: VLA OH 1.667 GHz spectra and comparison to CO wings | R: OHM wing emission matches CO velocity extent | F: OHM is a possible cheaper outflow tracer | **partially**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Targets selected for deepest far-IR OH absorption and highest outflow velocities in HERUS  
  > "the two selected sources display the deepest absorption profiles in far-IR OH lines and/or the highest maximum outflow velocities" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Thin-shell outflow geometry rejected for lack of resolution; steady-state used  
  > "In order to distinguish between these two cases observations with a higher spatial resolution (at least $\sim 100$ pc) are required." (4.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Systematic uncertainty in outflow mass from alpha_CO  
  > "we emphasize that there is a systematic uncertainty in the outflow mass, momentum flux, and energy flux due to $\alpha_{\rm CO}$" (4.1)
- Limitation (authors): Extreme obscuration may hide AGN; mid-IR decomposition may be biased  
  > "we cannot rule out more luminous and deeply buried AGN with their dust torus completely obscured by cooler molecular gas" (4.2.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample is small (9 sources) and correlation tests low in power
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow radius from marginally resolved beam, large uncertainty for IRAS 03158
- Caveat (inferred, ours): f_AGN for IRAS 20100 consistent with zero
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inclination not corrected
