## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA CO(2-1) and continuum**: Reprocess with ALMA pipeline script, tclean with Briggs weighting, bin channels  
  > "For the imaging, we used the tclean CASA’s task with the Briggs weighting scheme (cite: briggs95)" (4.1 ALMA)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: robust 0 / 2 / 0.5; uv-taper 0.5'' for Mrk 915  
  > "To improve the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) of the Mrk 915, we used a “robustness” value of 2" (4.1 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Build HST [O III] images and align**: Subtract K_c-scaled F547M from FR533N, subtract sky plane, shift HST astrometry to ALMA continuum peak  
  > "\[O iii\] = (FR533N) $-$ $K_c\,\cdot$( F547M)" (A [O iii] continuum subtraction); "we modified their CRPIX/CRVAL header key values, such that the *HST* continuum peak matched the ALMA millimeter continuum peak" (4.2 Archival data)
   - parameter: K_c = 1.14 for NGC 6860; central negative pixels interpolated  
  > "the optical continuum was better subtracted using $K_c$ = 1.14" (A [O iii] continuum subtraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with original published contours and astrometry check against DECam stars (~0.1'' max error)  
  > "The resulting \[O iii\] contours are similar to the original data published in (cite: schmitt+03)." (A [O iii] continuum subtraction)
3. **Structure maps of host galaxies**: S=[I/(I⊗P)]⊗P^t on DECam i-band, PSF from Moffat fits to field stars  
  > "To obtain the PSF, we modeled the flux distributions of field stars with Moffat profiles (cite: trujillo+01)." (5.1 Structure maps)
   - software: astropy
   - fitting: Moffat profiles  
  > "To obtain the PSF, we modeled the flux distributions of field stars with Moffat profiles" (Analysis, Structure maps)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive CO flux, luminosity and molecular mass**: Per-spaxel Gaussian fits (max two components), integrate flux, L' via Solomon+97, r21=0.8-1.2, alpha_CO=0.8-4.3  
  > "The CO flux – in each spaxel – was derived from the Gaussian profiles fitted to the CO profile, with a maximum of two components" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: r21 = 0.8–1.2; alpha_CO = 0.8–4.3 Msun/(K km/s pc^2)  
  > "we assumed a large range of values: $\alpha_{\rm{CO}}$ = $M_{\rm{H_2}}/L'_{\rm{CO(1-0)}}$ = 0.8 – 4.3" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits; uncertainties following Lenz & Ayres 1992  
  > "The corresponding uncertainties are obtained following (cite: lenzs_ayres92)" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - validation: Compared with SEST and APEX single-dish fluxes (missing flux)  
  > "To illustrate this, we can compare our total flux measurements with values obtained using single-dish telescopes." (Analysis, Molecular gas mass)
5. **Compare CO and [O III] distributions**: Visual overlay of CO and [O III] contours (plus radio) and inspection of literature for the other 10 objects  
  > "For the remaining 4 objects, we performed a visual inspection using CO and ionized gas images from different studies." (6 Discussions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Argued gaps are on scales below the maximum recoverable scale  
  > "However, this is unlikely to be the reason behind the observed CO gaps, since they are identified on smaller scales ($\lesssim$ 1 kpc, see Fig. [4](#fig:oiii_co_grid))." (Discussions)

## Qualitative findings

- CO emission has gaps co-spatial with [O III] within the inner kpc in all three galaxies  
  > "Within the inner kiloparsec of the three galaxies, we observe gaps in the CO emission in regions co-spatial with the \[O iii\] flux distribution" (abstract)
