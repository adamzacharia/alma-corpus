## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure and fit the laboratory rotational spectrum**: Synthesized 3-hydroxypropanal and measured absorption spectra 130-485 GHz with FLASH and Prague spectrometers; fitted ground state and the v27=1 and v26=1 states (the latter two in a coupled two-state fit)  
  > "The rotational spectrum of 3-hydroxypropanal was measured in the frequency region 130–485 GHz using two spectrometers." (3.2 Spectroscopic Experiments); "Therefore, these states were fitted with a combined fit using SPFIT code (cite: spcat)." (4.2 Vibrationally Excited States)
   - software: SPCAT, SPFIT, ASFIT, PIFORM, Gaussian 16, CFOUR, MacroModel
   - parameter: Ground-state fit: 1194 distinct transition frequencies  
  > "Overall, the fit contains 1194 distinct transition frequencies." (4.1 Ground State)
   - parameter: Excited-state fit: 2172 transitions, 800 distinct lines  
  > "Ultimately, a total of 2172 transitions were included in the excited-state fit, corresponding to 800 distinct lines." (4.2 Vibrationally Excited States)
   - assumption: Conformer 1 is the only conformer with notable population  
  > "Therefore, it is the only conformer that is expected to have a notable population in our spectrum." (2 Quantum Chemical Calculations)
   - fitting: A reduction, Ir representation using ASFIT; excited states with Coriolis-coupled two-state fit, G_a fixed, G_b, Delta E, G_b^J, G_b^K varied; perturbed-line uncertainty extended to 100 kHz  
  > "the final fit was performed by fixing $G_a$ to its computed value while allowing $G_b$, $\Delta E$, $G_b^{J}$, and $G_b^{K}$ to vary." (4.2 Vibrationally Excited States)
   - validation: Calculated rotational constants agree with experiment within 3%  
  > "The calculated rotational constants of Conformer 1 agree with the experimentally determined values within 3% for each of the functional/basis set combinations." (4.1 Ground State)
2. **Build spectroscopic catalog and partition function**: SPCAT catalog from fitted parameters; Qrot by SPCAT summed to J=200; Qvib from approximation formalism with CCSD/aug-cc-pVDZ anharmonic frequencies; Qtot = Qvib x Qrot  
  > "The rotational partition function was calculated with SPCAT by simulating the spectrum up to $J$ = 200." (4.1 Ground State); "The total partition function was calculated as $Q_{\mbox{vib}} (T) \times Q_{\mbox{rot}} (T)$ at each temperature." (4.1 Ground State)
   - software: SPCAT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Search for and fit 3-hydroxypropanal in G+0.693 (Yebes/IRAM)**: Generated LTE synthetic spectra in MADCUBA SLIM and ran AUTOFIT-SLIM nonlinear least-squares LTE fit with Tex, vLSR and FWHM fixed from s-propanal, column density free  
  > "Using the Spectral Line Identification and Modelling (SLIM) tool of the MADCUBA package (cite: Martin2019), we generated the expected local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) synthetic spectra" (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud); "we employed the AUTOFIT-SLIM tool (cite: Martin2019) and conducted a nonlinear least-squares LTE fit of the 3-hydroxypropanal emission to the observed spectra." (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
   - software: MADCUBA, SLIM, AUTOFIT-SLIM
   - parameter: Tex = 12 K, vLSR = 69 km/s, FWHM = 21 km/s; column density the only free parameter  
  > "$T_{\rm ex}$ = 12 K, $v_{\rm LSR}$ = 69 km s$^{-1}$ and FWHM = 21 km s$^{-1}$, leaving the column density as the only free parameter in the fit." (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
   - assumption: Vibrational contribution to the partition function not included  
  > "we did not include the vibrational contribution to the partition function ($Q_{\mbox{vib}}$) in this case." (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
   - assumption: N(H2) = 1.35e23 cm-2 with 15% uncertainty for the abundance  
  > "adopting a $N_{\rm H_2}$ = 1.35$\times$10$^{23}$ cm$^{-2}$ (cite: martin2008tracing) and assuming an uncertainty of 15% of its value." (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
   - fitting: Nonlinear least-squares LTE fit; priors not applicable/not stated  
  > "conducted a nonlinear least-squares LTE fit of the 3-hydroxypropanal emission to the observed spectra." (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
   - validation: Model including 3-hydroxypropanal compared to observed spectrum; most lines blended  
  > "Overall, the inclusion of 3-hydroxypropanal greatly enhances the agreement between the model and the observed spectra" (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
4. **Upper limit toward Sgr B2(N1S) in ALMA ReMoCA**: Compared ReMoCA spectra to LTE synthetic spectra computed with Weeds, assuming the acetaldehyde LTE parameters and fitting only the column density  
  > "The survey was analyzed by comparing the observed spectra to synthetic spectra computed assuming LTE with the software Weeds (cite: Maret11)." (5.2 Sgr B2(N) Star-Forming Region); "keeping only the column density as a free parameter." (5.2 Sgr B2(N) Star-Forming Region)
   - software: Weeds
   - parameter: Tex = 250 K, delta V = 5.0 km/s; source size 2.0'' (table)  
  > "we assumed the same LTE parameters as acetaldehyde ($T_{ex}$ = 250 K, $\Delta V$ = 5.0 km s$^{-1}$" (5.2 Sgr B2(N) Star-Forming Region)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Upper limit toward IRAS 16293B in ALMA PILS**: Synthetic spectra at Tex = 125 K compared with PILS spectra at a position one beam offset from IRAS 16293B; 3 sigma upper limit from the fits  
  > "Adopting an excitation temperature of 125 K and calculating synthetic spectra for 3-hydroxypropanal, we do not see any indications of lines that can be attributed to the species" (5.3 IRAS 16293-2422B Star-Forming Region)
   - assumption: Vibrational partition function at 125 K included  
  > "The vibrational partition function at 125K was considered when determining this upper limit." (5.3 IRAS 16293-2422B Star-Forming Region)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Upper limit toward NGC 6334I MM1 in ALMA Bands 4, 7, 9, 10**: Smoothed all bands to 0.26'' with CASA imsmooth, simulated emission with molsim, used the unblended line with the highest S/N and found the column density giving a 3 sigma peak  
  > "To establish the upper limit, we identified the 3-hydroxypropanal line in the observations with the highest signal-to-noise ratio under the given simulation parameters that was not blended with the emission of other molecular species." (5.4 NGC 6334I Star-Forming Region); "We then calculated the column density necessary for this peak to reach an intensity at least three times the RMS noise level." (5.4 NGC 6334I Star-Forming Region)
   - software: CASA imsmooth, molsim
   - parameter: Tex = 225 K, vlsr = -5.2 km/s, linewidth = 3.0 km/s  
  > "were an excitation temperature of 225 K, a $v_{lsr}$ of -5.2 km s$^{-1}$, and a linewidth of 3.0 km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Ligterink2020)" (5.4 NGC 6334I Star-Forming Region)
   - assumption: Single excitation temperature; vibrational partition function and conformational correction at 225 K applied  
  > "The vibrational partition function and conformational correction at 225 K was taken into account when determining the upper limit column density." (5.4 NGC 6334I Star-Forming Region)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Upper limit toward TMC-1 in GBT GOTHAM DR5**: Same upper-limit method as NGC 6334I applied to GOTHAM spectra with molsim  
  > "The upper limit was determined using the same method as Section [5.4](#subsec:ngc)." (5.5 TMC-1 Molecular Cloud)
   - software: molsim
   - parameter: Tex = 7 K, vlsr = 5.8 km/s, linewidth = 0.3 km/s  
  > "assuming an excitation temperature of 7 K, a $v_{lsr}$ of 5.8 km s$^{-1}$, and a linewidth of 0.3 km s$^{-1}$" (5.5 TMC-1 Molecular Cloud)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

  > "resulted in some observable Coriolis coupling between the two states that impacted a fairly small number of transitions." (4.2 Vibrationally Excited States)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: G+0.693 is chemically rich and propanal and propenal are abundant there  
  > "which makes G+0.693 a good target for the interstellar search of 3-hydroxypropanal." (5.1 G+0.693-0.027 Molecular Cloud)
- Why this method: Upper limit set where the strongest unblended line reaches 3 times the RMS noise  
  > "We then calculated the column density necessary for this peak to reach an intensity at least three times the RMS noise level." (5.4 NGC 6334I Star-Forming Region)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Most G+0.693 lines are heavily blended with other species
