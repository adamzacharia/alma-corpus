## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and lines**: Joint 12m+ACA imaging in CASA; mfs continuum robust=0; lines robust=2 with hanning smoothing  
  > "The continuum image was constructed by jointly imaging all spectral line-free channels using the multi-frequency imaging (`mfs`) algorithm." (2.1 ALMA 12m+ACA Array 1.3 mm)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SMA 1.3 mm, SHARC2 0.35 mm and VLA NH3; morphology not significantly biased by missing flux on scales <~30 arcsec  
  > "We confirmed that the resolved morphology by ALMA is not significantly biased by the missing flux on the $\lesssim$30$''$ scale." (2.1 ALMA 12m+ACA Array 1.3 mm)
2. **Subtract free-free from 1.3 mm continuum**: Estimate optically thin free-free from H30alpha peak and width with Te~8000 K; subtract from smoothed continuum  
  > "This free-free emission model is then subtracted from the 1.3 mm continuum image to yield the 1.3 mm dust continuum emission image." (3.1 Continuum emission)
   - parameter: Te ~ 8000 K  
  > "We adopt the nominal value of $T_{e}$$\sim$8000 K for the electron temperature" (3.1 Continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive gas masses and column densities**: Optically thin dust emission formula (Hildebrand 1983)  
  > "The gas mass $M_{\mbox{\scriptsize{H$_{2}$}}}$ as well as column density in the extended structures can be approximated by the optically thin dust emission formula" (4.1 Matter Distribution)
   - assumption: R=100, a=0.1 um, rho=3 g/cm3, Td=20 K large scale, ~30 K within inner 0.3 pc, beta=1 for cores  
  > "we adopt a constant $T_{d}$=20 K on the large scale, and $T_{d}$$\sim$30 K in the inner 0.3 pc ($\sim$9$''$) radius of G33.92+0.11 A." (4.1 Matter Distribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Test core virial stability**: Fit 2D Gaussians to cores for mass and size; fit 1D Gaussian to DCN spectrum for sigma_v; compare with v_vir  
  > "we fit 2-dimensional Gaussians to the 1.3 mm dust continuum images (Figure [2](#fig_continuum2)) using the CASA task `imfit`" (4.2 Gravitational stability of the satellite cores)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - assumption: Td~30 K, beta=1, alpha between 1 and 5/3  
  > "We assume the dust temperature $T_{d}$$\sim$30 K, and adopt $\beta$=1." (4.2 Gravitational stability of the satellite cores)
   - fitting: 2D and 1D Gaussian fits  
  > "we fit 2-dimensional Gaussians to the 1.3 mm dust continuum images (Figure [2](#fig_continuum2)) using the CASA task `imfit`" (4.2 Gravitational stability of the satellite cores)
   - validation: Fits made to both 12m+ACA and 12m-only continuum to account for missing flux and ambient gas  
  > "we perform Gaussian fittings to both the ALMA 12m+ACA Array 1.3 mm dust continuum image, and the dust continuum image generated from the 12m Array data alone." (4.2 Gravitational stability of the satellite cores)
5. **Interpret kinematics**: Moment maps, channel maps, PV diagrams; Gaussian line fits for core velocities; Keplerian estimate near A1  
  > "The position-velocity (PV) diagrams of these two lines, generated from the selected slices (see Figure [7](#fig_moment2)), are shown in Figure [10](#fig_pvlarge)." (3.2 Spectral lines)
   - assumption: circular orbit with 100-300 Msun in A1 at 0.0068 pc  
  > "the rotating speed $v_{R}(\mbox{0.0068 pc})$ can be estimated by" (4.3 Velocity Fields)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Spiral arm-like molecular arms and mini-arms connect to central massive cores; DCN traces dust structures; 13CS enhanced in mini-arms  
  > "We found that the DCN 3-2 line emission correlates well with the 1.3 mm dust continuum emission" (5 Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Nearly face-on massive clump minimizes line-of-sight confusion; ALMA gives higher spectral resolution and sensitivity  
  > "Therefore we expect minimized confusion among structures in the line-of-sight." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Rotation vs free-fall for the parsec-scale gradient; cannot separate with current data  
  > "In addition, our observations do not allow us to separate the rotational motion from infall." (4.3 Velocity Fields)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No zero-spacing data and significant missing flux (>=24% vs SMA)  
  > "There is no zero-spacing information available for our 1.3 mm continuum map." (2.1 ALMA 12m+ACA Array 1.3 mm)
- Limitation (authors): Kinematic analysis preliminary since arm inclinations unknown  
  > "We note that this analysis is preliminary because we do not know exactly how the inclination of the individual molecular arms vary with position." (4.3 Velocity Fields)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature and beta assumed, so masses and virial results are uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interpretation of velocity gradients is qualitative with no kinematic model fitting
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distance uncertainty of ~17% propagates into masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Free-free subtraction assumes Te and optically thin emission
