## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra from cubes**: 8" diameter circular aperture on continuum-subtracted tapered CLEAN cubes  
  > "using a circular aperture with an 8$\ensuremath{''}$ diameter centered on the source" (3.1 Line extraction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Stack lines**: Stack integrated intensity maps (v=2.4–9.2 km/s) in image plane; uv stacking with cvel; band 7 regridded to 0.303 km/s  
  > "Stacking in the $uv$ plane is done using the CASA `cvel` function" (3.1.1 Line stacking)
   - software: CASA cvel
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Keplerian-masked upper limits**: 3 sigma, sigma = dv sqrt(N) rms, N = mask pixels / pixels per beam  
  > "The upper limit is set at 3$\sigma$ where $\sigma = \delta v \sqrt{N} \sigma_{\mathrm{rms}}$" (3.1.2)
   - software: Python
   - assumption: Keplerian rotation, M=2.3 Msun, inclination 44 deg, PA 133 deg  
  > "The disk has an inclination of 44$^{\circ}$, a position angle of 133$^\circ$" (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Matched filter in uv plane**: Sample H2CO 3_03-2_02 template (v=1.6–10 km/s) in uv space and cross-correlate with CH3OH visibilities  
  > "Channels with H$_2$CO emission ($v = 1.6-10$ km s$^{-1}$) are sampled in $uv$ space using the PYTHON `vis_sample` routine." (3.1.3)
   - software: vis_sample, Python
   - assumption: CH3OH and H2CO co-spatial  
  > "under the assumption that CH$_3$OH and H$_2$CO reside in similar regions" (3.1.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Confirms non-detection from image plane  
  > "The matched filter analyses confirm the non-detection of CH$_3$OH found during analysis in the image plane." (3.1.3)
5. **Column density and abundance upper limits**: Optically thin LTE formula with Tex=25 K; total emitting area 7"x7"; N(H2)=Mdisk/mH2  
  > "The CH$_3$OH emitting area is set to $a = b = 7\ensuremath{''}$ based on the H$_2$CO emission diameter in the HD 163296 disk" (3.2)
   - software: CDMS
   - parameter: Tex = 25, 50, 75 K  
  > "The excitation temperature $T_{\rm ex}$ is set to 25, 50 and 75 K" (Appendix)
   - assumption: Optically thin, LTE, disk mass ~0.09 Msun all H2, d=122 pc  
  > "Assuming the total disk mass is primarily molecular hydrogen" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Abundances vary at most factor 2–3 with Tex  
  > "The abundances do not vary with $T_{\rm ex}$ by more than a factor of 2–3 in the most extreme cases." (3.2)
6. **CH3OH/H2CO ratio vs TW Hya**: Same formula applied to literature H2CO 3_12-2_11 fluxes; TW Hya area 3" and stacked CH3OH assumed from strongest line  
  > "derived in the same manner as described in Section [3.2](#sec:res_abun_uplims) to ensure consistency" (3.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Forward model with LIME**: LTE LIME models with CH3OH scaled to H2CO, vis_sample, CLEAN, compare with noise  
  > "The Line Modeling Engine (cite: Brinch2010) 3D radiative transfer code is run in LTE with 10000 grid points" (3.4)
   - software: LIME, vis_sample, CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with ratio limit ~0.2  
  > "This result is consistent with the upper limit on this ratio derived from the integrated intensity of the Keplerian mask cube" (3.4)

## Qualitative findings

- No CH3OH line detected via aperture, Keplerian masking, matched filter, or stacking  
  > "No methanol lines listed in Table [\[tab:obs_par\]](#tab:obs_par) are detected in the disk around HD 163296 neither individually nor after line stacking." (3 Results)
