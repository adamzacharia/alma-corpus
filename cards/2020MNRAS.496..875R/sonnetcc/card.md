## Analysis recipe

1. **Image the cube and identify the serendipitous source**: Calibrate with standard ALMA pipeline in CASA, image with tclean, inspect the cube and find a more significant emission offset ~6 arcsec from the ALPINE target  
  > "The data are analysed using standard pipelines for ALMA reduction included in the software CASA" (2.1 ALMA data); "we identify a more significant emission with a spatial offset of" (2.2 Identification of the serendipitous source)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: threshold 3 sigma_rms from the dirty image; natural weighting  
  > "task on the visibilities, setting a threshold of 3$\mathrm{\sigma_{rms}}$ on the noise level when cleaning the data" (2.1 ALMA data); "with a natural weighting scheme to increase the sensitivity" (2.1 ALMA data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract the spectrum and fit the line profile**: Spectrum from a circular region 2 arcsec wide including the 2 sigma moment-0 contours, Gaussian fit with the CASA viewer spectral profile tool  
  > "within the CASA viewer, we fit the line profile with a Gaussian function" (2.2 Identification of the serendipitous source)
   - software: CASA viewer
   - parameter: circular region 2" wide, including the 2 sigma contours of the moment-0 map  
  > "it is extracted from a circular region 2” wide, including the 2$\sigma$ contours from the moment-0 map of the source" (2.2 Identification of the serendipitous source)
   - fitting: single Gaussian; priors/likelihood not stated  
  > "within the CASA viewer, we fit the line profile with a Gaussian function" (2.2 Identification of the serendipitous source)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Separate continuum and line and measure fluxes**: imcontsub with line-free channels to get continuum map and continuum-subtracted cube; moment-0 from consecutive channels above 1 sigma_spec; imfit on a region keeping pixels above 2 sigma  
  > "We then select all the consecutive channels having emission above 1$\mathrm{\sigma_{spec}}$" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source); "We define a region surrounding the emissions and then select only the pixels with a flux density larger than 2$\mathrm{\sigma}$" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - software: CASA imcontsub, CASA immoments, CASA imfit
   - parameter: moment-0 channels: consecutive channels above 1 sigma_spec (rms of the line spectrum)  
  > "We then select all the consecutive channels having emission above 1$\mathrm{\sigma_{spec}}$" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - assumption: Source unresolved, peak flux taken as total flux  
  > "we assume that the source is unresolved and we take the peak flux as the total flux" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive L_FIR, SFR and line luminosities for the three line identifications**: L_FIR(8-1000 um) from an assumed SED shape normalised to the continuum flux; SFR from Kennicutt relation scaled to Chabrier; line luminosity from the Solomon relation  
  > "assuming a shape of its SED from (cite: Magdis12), and normalizing its flux to" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source); "Finally, we estimate the line luminosities as in (cite: Solomon92) using the following relation" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - parameter: L_FIR integrated between 8 and 1000 um; continuum observed at ~845-880 um  
  > "We derive the total infrared (between 8 and 1000 $\mu$m) luminosity of the source" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - assumption: LambdaCDM H0 = 70, Om = 0.3, OL = 0.7; Chabrier IMF  
  > "we assume a $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $\mathrm{H_0}$ = 70 km/s/Mpc, $\mathrm{\Omega_m}$ = 0.3, and $\mathrm{\Omega_\Lambda}$ = 0.7 (cite: Planck18)" (1 Introduction)
   - assumption: 0.5 dex systematic error from FIR SED template choice added in quadrature  
  > "and a systematic error of 0.5 dex which takes into account possible variations in the luminosity caused by different FIR SED templates" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - assumption: SFR scaled from Salpeter to Chabrier by dividing by 1.7  
  > "We scale the SFR from Salpeter to Chabrier IMF by dividing by 1.7" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Measure multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A**: Fixed 1.4 arcsec aperture photometry in each band; Ks flux via SExtractor deblending; IRAC deblending with GALFIT treating Gal-B and Gal-C as point sources, then aperture photometry on residuals  
  > "For each band, we centre a fixed aperture of 1.4” of diameter on Gal-A (enclosing the 3$\sigma$ contours of the emission line detected by ALMA) and estimate its flux." (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A); "we attempt a deblending procedure using the 2D GALFIT fitting algorithm (cite: Peng02)" (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
   - software: SExtractor, GALFIT
   - parameter: IRAC aperture corrections: divide by 0.61, 0.59, 0.49, 0.45 (3.6 to 8.0 um)  
  > "we divide the flux measured in these bands by 0.61, 0.59, 0.49 and 0.45, going from 3.6 to 8.0 $\mu$m" (footnote 2)
   - assumption: IRAC SED fit uses fluxes between deblended (lower) and blended (higher) values  
  > "we decide to consider IRAC fluxes ranging between the deblended (lower) and blended (higher) values" (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
   - fitting: GALFIT point-source models for Gal-B and Gal-C; three-component fit with Gal-A free failed  
  > "However, probably due to the small distance between the galaxies, the code is not able to perform the fit." (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
   - validation: Conclusions checked to be similar when using deblended fluxes in the SED fitting  
  > "We find, however, that our conclusions do not depend on this assumption" (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
6. **Fit the SED for stellar mass, SFR, age and attenuation**: LePHARE with BC03 templates, Calzetti attenuation, constant/exponentially declining/delayed SFHs, two metallicities, at z=4.6 and z=2.2; 1000 Monte Carlo perturbed SEDs  
  > "We thus run a Montecarlo simulation, building 1000 perturbed SEDs that we then refit" (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
   - software: LePHARE, MAGPHYS
   - parameter: SFHs: constant, exponentially declining (tau = 0.1,0.3,1,3 Gyr), delayed (tau = 0.1,0.5,1,3 Gyr)  
  > "We explore constant, exponentially declining (with $\tau=0.1,0.3,1,3$ Gyrs) and delayed (with $\tau=0.1,0.5,1,3$ Gyrs) SFHs." (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
   - parameter: Metallicity solar and 0.2 solar; Calzetti law with E_s(B-V) 0 to 0.7 in steps of 0.05  
  > "We then account for dust attenuation using the (cite: Calzetti00) attenuation law with a stellar $E_s(B-V)$ ranging from 0 to 0.7 in steps of 0.05." (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
   - assumption: Perturbed fluxes drawn from a Gaussian centred on the measured flux with sigma equal to the measured rms  
  > "we extract the perturbed flux in each band from a gaussian distribution centred on the measured flux and with standard deviation equal to the measured rms" (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
   - fitting: Best-fit chi2 template fitting in flux density space; priors not stated  
  > "Finally, following (cite: Faisst19), we perform the fit in flux density space and add systematic errors (depending on the filter)" (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
   - validation: Compared with MAGPHYS including the ALMA Band 7 continuum; results very similar within uncertainties  
  > "This reassures us about the robustness of our estimates." (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
7. **Compare with empirical relations to constrain the line identification and position on the main sequence**: Place Gal-A on the L_CO-L_FIR relations of Liu et al. (rescaling L_FIR to 40-400 um), on the L_[CII]-L_FIR relation, and on the Speagle et al. main sequence at z=2.2 and 4.6  
  > "to the same integration interval as in (cite: Liu15), for consistency" (3.1 On the nature of the serendipitous source); "we show the MS relations, assuming a Chabrier IMF" (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
   - parameter: L_FIR(8-1000)/L_FIR(40-400) ~ 1.4 on average  
  > "$\mathrm{L_{FIR}^{8-1000}/L_{FIR}^{40-400} \sim 1.4}$, on average" (3.1 On the nature of the serendipitous source)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Estimate the dynamical mass**: Rotating disk formula from the line FWHM and size from the 2D Gaussian major axis; cross-check with 3D-BAROLO on the continuum-subtracted cube  
  > "Following (cite: Wang13), we assume a rotating disk geometry for the gas as a first approximation" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass); "we also run the 3D-BAROLO algorithm" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)
   - software: 3D-BAROLO
   - parameter: sin(i) = 0.45 and 1; size = 1.06 +/- 0.04 arcsec (7.09 +/- 0.27 kpc at z~4.6)  
  > "Following (cite: Capak15), we assume the two values for the inclination angle" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass); "($1.06 \pm 0.04$ arcsec, which corresponds to $7.09 \pm 0.27$ kpc at $z \sim 4.6$" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)
   - assumption: Source unresolved; size taken as FWHM of the major axis of the fitted 2D Gaussian  
  > "Since Gal-A is not resolved, we take the FWHM of the major axis of the 2D Gaussian fitted to the emission line, as the size of our galaxy" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)
   - fitting: 3D-BAROLO forward-modelling of the cube; details not stated  
  > "finding the kinematical and geometrical parameters which best describe the data" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)
   - validation: 3D-BAROLO result compared with the disk formula; agreement within large errors  
  > "These results are quite in agreement with the former, given the large error on $\mathrm{M_{dyn}}$." (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)

## Software

- **CASA** 5.4.0: Standard pipeline calibration and all ALMA imaging/analysis tasks  
  > "The data are analysed using standard pipelines for ALMA reduction included in the software CASA" (2.1 ALMA data)
- **tclean** : Imaging of the visibilities  
  > "task on the visibilities, setting a threshold of 3$\mathrm{\sigma_{rms}}$ on the noise level when cleaning the data" (2.1 ALMA data)
- **imcontsub** : Continuum map and continuum-subtracted cube  
  > "this task creates a continuum map of the source and a continuum-subtracted cube" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
- **immoments** : Moment-0 map  
  > "in order to compute the moment-0 map with the CASA" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
- **imfit** : Line and continuum fluxes  
  > "The line and continuum fluxes are computed using the CASA" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source)
- **CASA viewer (spectral profile tool)** : Gaussian fit of the line profile  
  > "within the CASA viewer, we fit the line profile with a Gaussian function" (2.2 Identification of the serendipitous source)
- **SExtractor** : Deblending Gal-A in the Ks band for its magnitude  
  > "(cite: Bertin96), we manage to deblend the analysed galaxy from the other two nearby sources" (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
- **GALFIT** : IRAC deblending of Gal-A from Gal-B and Gal-C  
  > "we attempt a deblending procedure using the 2D GALFIT fitting algorithm (cite: Peng02)" (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
- **LePHARE** : SED fitting for stellar mass, SFR, age  
  > "We derive the stellar mass of Gal-A through SED-fitting using LePHARE" (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
- **MAGPHYS** : Cross-check SED fit including the ALMA continuum  
  > "We further compare the results obtained with LePHARE with the MAGPHYS code (cite: daCunha08)" (3.2 Estimate of the stellar mass)
- **3D-BAROLO** : Dynamical mass from the continuum-subtracted cube  
  > "we also run the 3D-BAROLO algorithm" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Source found serendipitously in the ALPINE pointing; brightest line emitter among ALPINE serendipitous sources without optical counterpart, so used as a benchmark  
  > "this work can be exploited as a benchmark for future analysis on these types of sources" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Only CO J=9,10 considered because z<2 galaxies would be expected in optical bands  
  > "we would expect it to be visible in the optical bands" (2.4 Analysis of the serendipitous source); "It is particularly useful to retrieve information on low-resolution data where the kinematics is biased by the size of the beam" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)
- Alternative: Emission at the position of Gal-A associated with DC_665626 rejected (offset >~6 arcsec exceeds modelled 1-2 arcsec)  
  > "Therefore, we exclude that the observed ALMA emission at the position of Gal-A is directly linked to" (2.2 Identification of the serendipitous source)
- Alternative: Three-component GALFIT with Gal-A flux free failed  
  > "However, probably due to the small distance between the galaxies, the code is not able to perform the fit." (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Source unresolved and kinematic mass poorly constrained with current data  
  > "which cannot be well constrained with the current data and our poor resolution" (3.3 Estimate of the dynamical mass)
- Limitation (authors): IRAC fluxes may be underestimated by the deblending  
  > "We are aware that with this procedure we may underestimate the flux of Gal-A in the IRAC channels" (2.3 Multi-wavelength photometry of Gal-A)
- Limitation (authors): Line identification unconfirmed; more data needed  
  > "However, to validate this hypothesis, more data are needed." (3.1 On the nature of the serendipitous source)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No spectroscopic redshift: the [CII] vs CO identification rests on indirect diagnostics with 0.5 dex L_FIR uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): L_FIR depends on one assumed SED template and a single continuum point at ~850 um
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Unresolved source with ~1 dex dynamical mass uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Table 2 SED SFR (log ~2.1 at z=4.6) differs from the ALMA-derived log SFR ~1.4; the paper calls them in agreement
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SED fit is dominated by upper limits and a 2.3 sigma Ks detection
