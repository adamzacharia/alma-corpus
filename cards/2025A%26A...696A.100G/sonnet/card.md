## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine ALMA 12m, 7m and TP data into cubes**: Pseudo-visibilities from TP and 7m merged with interferometer data in GILDAS/MAPPING, Högbom CLEAN, converted to Tmb  
  > "These visibilities were then merged with the ALMA-12m interferometric observations and the resulting dataset was deconvolved as in step 1." (2.2 ALMA imaging)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS/MAPPING
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract crosscuts and compare stratification of tracers**: Normalized intensity cuts perpendicular to the Bar through ALMA and JWST/Keck images; images rotated 37.5 deg  
  > "We chose two crosscuts passing through $\delta y$ = $+$2$''$ (cut A) and $\delta y$ = $-$20$''$ (cut B), roughly parallel to the incoming FUV radiation." (3.1 Crosscuts A and B)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive H2 rotational temperature and warm H2 column**: T64 from S(4) and S(2) MIRI-MRS lines, Boltzmann populations, extinction correction, N_H = N(H)+2N(H2) with N(H)=N(H2)  
  > "Assuming a Boltzmann population of the $N_4$ and $N_6$ levels at $T_{\rm 64}$, we determine $N_{\rm warm}$($p$-H$_2$)$_{\rm LTE}$" (4.1)
   - assumption: optically thin H2 lines; ortho-to-para ratio 3; A_lambda/A_V = 0.045  
  > "The H$_2$ OTP ratio across the Bar is fairly constant and equal to three (cite: Putte24)." (4.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Beam-dilution correct single-dish C2H lines and build population diagram**: Beam coupling factors from smoothed IRAM 30m and ALMA C2H 4-3 maps; rotational diagram fit with two temperatures  
  > "The final beam coupling correction factor is $f_{\rm b}$ = $f_{\rm b}$(single-dish) $\cdot$$f_{\rm b}$(ALMA)." (Beam dilution appendix)
   - assumption: all C2H rotational lines share the same spatial distribution; optically thin  
  > "we assume that the emission from all C$_2$H rotational lines have the same spatial distribution." (Beam dilution appendix)
   - fitting: two-component rotational diagram  
  > "The resulting diagram (Fig. [21](#fig:DRs_corr_c2h)) can be fitted with two rotational temperature components, 22 $\pm$ 2 K and 59 $\pm$ 7 K" (C$_2$H rotational temperatures and $N$(C$_2$H) toward the SDLS position)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive C2H and C column densities and abundances**: Nonlocal non-LTE Monte Carlo radiative transfer grid in n_H and T_k; f(H2)=2/3; dust continuum excitation; new C-H2 rates to 3000 K  
  > "we updated our nonlocal and non-LTE radiative transfer Monte Carlo model (cite: Goico22) to treat the rotational excitation of C$_2$H" (4.4)
   - parameter: T_k = 600 K upper limit; sigma_turb = 1 km/s  
  > "Taking $T_{\rm k}$ = 600 K as an upper limit" (4.4)
   - assumption: dust modified blackbody, T=50 K, opacity 0.03(160/lambda)^1.6  
  > "with a color temperature of 50 K and a wavelength-dependent (in $\upmu$m) dust continuum opacity that varies as 0.03(160/$\lambda$)$^{1.6}$" (Nonlocal and non-LTE appendix)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: T_rot(C2H 4-3)=20-24 K agrees with population diagram  
  > "in agreement with the rotational temperature inferred from a population diagram" (Nonlocal and non-LTE appendix)
6. **Model chemistry with Meudon PDR code and constrain pressure**: Isobaric Meudon models, updated network with H2* reactions; compare normalized C2H N-N'/4-3 ratios to find P_th  
  > "The best agreement is for models with $P_{\rm th}$/$k_{\rm B}$ $\simeq$ 10$^8$ K cm$^{-3}$." (P_th determination appendix)
   - software: Meudon PDR code
   - parameter: G0 = 2e4 Habing, A_V = 10 mag, zeta_CR = 1e-16 s^-1, C/H = 1.4e-4, O/H = 2.6e-4  
  > "| Abundance C / H | 1.4$\times$10$^{-4}$ | Orion$^b$ |" (Table 1)
   - assumption: tilt angle ~5 deg  
  > "corresponding to a tilt angle of $\alpha$ $\approx$ 5$^o$, as commonly found for the Bar" (Footnote 9)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Predicted [C2H] and N(C)/N(C2H) match observed values  
  > "The predicted[^9] $N$(C)/$N$(C$_2$H) column density ratio across the PDR, $\sim$10$^3$, also matches the observed value" (6.1)
7. **Correlate C2H with H2, AIB, [CI] intensities**: Smooth images to 0.8 arcsec, clip at 3 sigma, weighted least-squares log-log fits, Pearson/Spearman coefficients  
  > "after clipping intensities below a 3$\sigma$ detection threshold" (Line intensity correlations)
   - fitting: weighted least squares log10 y = a log10 x + b  
  > "Regressions fitted to the molecular PDR data (bluish areas in Fig. [18](#fig:PDR_correlations)) in a weighted least-square fit, log$_{10}$ $y$ = $a$ log$_{10}$ $x$ + $b$." (Table 3 notes)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- C2H emission traces hydrocarbon radical peaks in the DFs and follows FUV-pumped H2 emission rather than gas temperature.  
  > "The C$_2$H emission profile more closely follows the IR H$_2$ emission than the gas temperature profile" (Summary and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Non-LTE modeling needed because C2H is subthermally excited; high-T C-H2 rates did not exist  
  > "Since high-temperature, $>$100 K, C ($^3$P)–$o$/$p$-H$_2$ inelastic collisional rates did not exist in the literature, we extended the scattering calculations" (4.5)
- Alternative: Shock or terraced-field geometry for DF1/DF2 considered; hydrodynamical simulations needed  
  > "Hydrodynamical simulations will be needed to confirm these two scenarios" (4.2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Exact chemical pathways and relative contributions of bottom-up and top-down processes remain unquantified; models omit PAH chemistry  
  > "the exact chemical pathways and their relative contributions remain to be quantified" (abstract); "Our model does not include PAH chemistry." (5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Spatial coincidence of C2H and 3.4/3.3 ratio is correlational
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam-dilution correction assumes identical distribution of all C2H lines
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-pressure model and adopted G0 and tilt angle are simplified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): C2H-H2 rates above 500 K are extrapolated
