## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and measure fluxes**: Natural-weighted tclean continuum; peak flux within 1 arcsec of HST centroid; Gaussian fit for total flux for detection and stacks; 2sigma upper limits as peak+2xRMS  
  > "In the case of a non-detection at the $3\sigma$ level for the individual LBGs, we conservatively provide $2\sigma$ upper limits calculated as $f_{\rm peak} + 2\,\times\,{\rm RMS}$." (2 Data & Sample)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tapered image comparison  
  > "We found a slight ($10$%) increase in signal-to-noise for ID65666 when using the $1$ arcsec tapered image" (3 Results)
2. **Stack ALMA images**: Stack at UltraVISTA Ks centroids, all six and excluding ID65666  
  > "We produced two stacks, one with all six galaxies included, and one where our detected galaxy ID65666 was excluded." (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Two stacking methods for IRX consistent to 0.01 dex  
  > "The results of these two methods were consistent to within $\Delta {\rm log}({\rm IRX}) = 0.01$" (4)
3. **Derive L_FIR**: Modified blackbody with 1.3 mm flux, CMB correction, integrated 8-1000 um  
  > "The total luminosity was calculated by integrating under the FIR SED from $8\,\mu$m to $1000\,\mu$m." (3 Results)
   - parameter: beta = 1.6, T = 40 K fiducial (also 50 K)  
  > "A dust temperature of $T = 40\,$K was taken as our fiducial value." (3 Results)
   - assumption: optically thin; CMB contrast correction following DaCunha2013  
  > "We account for the reduced contrast due to the higher temperature of the Cosmic Microwave Background" (3 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with alternative SED templates (Arp220, M82, SMG templates, Capak2015 parameterisation)  
  > "Using the range of empirical galaxy templates results in an increase of $\simeq 1$ for Apr220, $\simeq 2$ for M82" (3 Results)
4. **SED fitting for photo-z and stellar mass**: LePhare with BC03 models, exponentially declining SFH, Chabrier IMF, 1/5 Zsun, Calzetti dust, 16 bands  
  > "We fitted (cite: Bruzual2003) models assuming an exponentially declining star-formation history (SFH), a (cite: Chabrier2003) initial-mass function, and a metallicity of $1/5\,{\rm Z}_{\odot}$." (2 Data & Sample)
   - software: LePhare
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No acceptable fit at z<6.5 with low-z contaminant templates  
  > "none of the galaxies show an acceptable fit at $z < 6.5$, demonstrating the robustness of the high-redshift solution" (SED fitting results)
5. **Measure UV slope beta and IRX**: Power-law fit to photometry at 1270-2580 A; Y band excluded for three highest-z galaxies; IRX = L_FIR/L_UV  
  > "The rest-frame UV slope was measured by fitting a power law to the photometry between $\lambda_{\rm rest} = 1270$–$2580$Å (cite: Calzetti1994)." (4)
   - software: SExtractor
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Injection-recovery simulations show no significant bias in beta  
  > "We find no significant bias in the recovery of the rest-frame UV slope from the UltraVISTA data" (Appendix B)
6. **Assess UV-FIR offset and astrometry**: Compare ALMA and HST centroids for ID65666; blind source extraction of serendipitous sources and cross-match to HST to test astrometry; positional accuracy 0.6xFWHM/SNR  
  > "The expected positional accuracy in the ALMA data is given as $0.6 \times {\rm FWHM}/{\rm SNR}$ (cite: Ivison2007)" (5.1)
   - software: SExtractor
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Serendipitous sources C1, C2 show no systematic offset  
  > "We therefore find no evidence for any significant systematic astrometric offset between the ALMA data and near-infrared data" (3.2)
7. **Derive SFRs**: FIR SFR from Kennicutt2012 and UV SFR from Madau1998, both with Chabrier IMF  
  > "We calculated the FIR star-formation rate using the calibration from (cite: Kennicutt2012), correcting to a (cite: Chabrier2003) initial-mass function (IMF)." (5.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- No extremely low IRX at z>5 in this bright sample; results consistent with a Calzetti-like attenuation law.  
  > "Instead, our results are consistent with the predictions of the Calzetti-like dust attenuation law found for star-forming galaxies at lower redshifts." (6 Conclusions)

## Claim tuples

- H: Bright z~7 LBGs show very low IRX as claimed by earlier z>5 studies | E: ALMA Band 6 imaging and stacking of six LBGs compared with IRX-beta | R: Stack log IRX = -0.08, higher than previous z>5 values | F: Results consistent with Calzetti-like attenuation, not extremely low IRX | **supported**
- H: Dust is significant in bright z~7 LBGs | E: Stacked FIR luminosity converted to SFR and A_UV | R: SFR_FIR ~30 Msun/yr comparable to UV SFR; A_UV ~0.5-1 mag | F: Dust obscuration is important in shaping the bright end of the UV LF | **supported**
- H: The dust and UV emission in ID65666 are physically offset | E: Compare ALMA and HST centroids and test astrometry with serendipitous sources | R: 0.6 arcsec offset (3 kpc), ~4 sigma | F: Inhomogeneous dust distribution with a UV-dark obscured component | **supported**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Brightest LBGs predicted to be dustiest and can be studied with modest telescope time  
  > "1) predicted to be the dustiest galaxies from the colour-magnitude relation and 2) can be studied in detail with modest telescope time." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Astrometric systematics as the cause of the offset were rejected using serendipitous sources  
  > "The physical nature of this offset is strengthened by the presence of a bright ALMA source in the same primary beam as ID65666" (5.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): L_FIR depends on assumed dust temperature and SED from a single 1.3 mm point; beta errors are large  
  > "requires the assumption of a spectral-energy distribution in the FIR" (3 Results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-band continuum so L_FIR is dominated by assumed T and beta
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only one detection with 5.2 sigma; stacks are marginal (2.9-4 sigma)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beta measured from ground-based photometry may be biased by clumpy morphology
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample of six galaxies
