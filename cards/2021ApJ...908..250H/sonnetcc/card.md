## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate, self-calibrate and image the Band 6 continuum and 12CO**: Calibrate with ALMA scripts in CASA, one round of phase self-cal, correct phase centers with fixvis using Gaia proper motions, CLEAN with multi-frequency deconvolution and uv-taper; subtract continuum with uvcontsub and image 12CO cube at 1.0 km/s  
  > "The phase centers were corrected using" (2 Observations); "using a multi-frequency deconvolution algorithm" (2 Observations); "were extracted by subtracting the continuum in the" (2 Observations)
   - software: CASA, CLEAN, fixvis, uvcontsub
   - parameter: robust = -2.0 for continuum, 2.0 for 12CO  
  > "Bobust clean parameter" (Table 1); "$-2.0$" (Table 1)
   - parameter: uv-taper 50.0 x 0.6 Mlambda at 183.0 deg  
  > "50.0 $\times$ 0.6 M$\lambda$ with 183.0$\ensuremath{^\circ}$" (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit the Band 6 (and Band 3, 7) visibilities with a parametric brightness profile**: Power-law radial profile with two cavity radii and a depth delta; model image converted to visibilities with vis_sample, deprojected, azimuthally averaged in 20 klambda bins (real part) and fit with emcee  
  > "We describe the surface brightness distributions of the disk in our model with a simple power-law radial profile" (4.1 Visibility fitting); "The modeled disk image was converted into complex visibilities with the public Python code" (4.1 Visibility fitting); "were calculated as azimuthal mean values ($V_{\rm mean}$) and standard deviations ($\sigma$) within 20 k$\lambda$ bins in the real part." (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - software: vis_sample, emcee
   - parameter: 8 free parameters (F_total, r_cav1, r_cav2, delta, gamma1, gamma2, i, PA); phase center fixed  
  > "In total, there are 8 free parameters in our model" (4.1 Visibility fitting); "The phase center was fixed." (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - parameter: 2000 burn-in steps plus 2000 steps, 4000 total, 200 walkers  
  > "The burn-in phase (from initial conditions to reasonable sampling) employed 2000 steps, and we ran another 2000 steps for convergence, for a total of 4000 steps with 200 walkers." (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - parameter: Outer radius of model 300 au; delta left free after delta=0 failed  
  > "However, we immediately found that the model with $\delta=$0 produces visibilities with an excessively deep gap" (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - assumption: No emission inside r_cav1 because the SED shows negligible NIR excess  
  > "Because the SED of Sz 84 shows negligible NIR excess, we set no emissions at" (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC, flat priors on ranges in Table 2, log-likelihood = -0.5 sum of squared residuals of binned real visibilities over sigma squared; errors from 16th and 84th percentiles  
  > "Our calculations used flat priors with the parameter ranges summarized in" (4.1 Visibility fitting); "The fitting results with errors computed from the 16th and 84th percentiles" (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - validation: Reduced chi-square 1.24; band 3 and 7 fits gave multiple peaks or flat posteriors so only the band 3 flux was used  
  > "the best-fit visibilities with a reduced-$\chi^{2}$ of 1.24" (4.1 Visibility fitting); "Therefore, we decided to use only the flux density from band 3 data (i.e., band 7 data were not used)." (4.1 Visibility fitting)
3. **Derive the mm spectral index**: Spectral index from total flux densities at bands 3, 6 (this work, visibility fits) and 7 (literature)  
  > "We derived the spectral index $\alpha$ based on band 3, 6, and 7 data." (3 Results)
   - parameter: Band 3 1.70 +/- 0.06 mJy, Band 6 13.43 +/- 0.13 mJy, Band 7 33 +/- 0.4 mJy  
  > "are 1.70 $\pm$ 0.06 and 13.43 $\pm$ 0.13 mJy, respectively, and that at band 7 taken from" (3 Results); "is 33 $\pm$ 0.4 mJy." (3 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with alpha ~2.7 of other transitional disks  
  > "which is lower than the value of $\alpha \sim$2.7 found in other transitional disk systems" (3 Results)
4. **Measure 12CO moment maps and dynamical mass**: Moment 0 and moment 1 maps, PV diagram along PA 165.71 deg with i = 75.13 deg, overplotting Keplerian peak-emission loci for stellar masses 0.2 to 0.6 Msun  
  > "We plotted the loci of the peak emission of a Keplerian disk around the central star by changing its mass from 0.2 to 0.6 $M_{\odot}$ in the PV diagram" (3 Results)
   - parameter: PA 165.71 deg and i 75.13 deg taken from the visibility fit  
  > "a PA of 165.71$\ensuremath{^\circ}$ and a disk inclination of 75.13$\ensuremath{^\circ}$ taken from" (3 Results)
   - assumption: Foreground cloud absorption negligible in the central CO region because of high inclination  
  > "Note that we assumed that the flux attenuation in the central region of the CO gas disk caused by the foreground cloud absorption is negligible" (4.1 Visibility fitting)
   - fitting: Visual comparison of Keplerian loci, no formal fit  
  > "We plotted the loci of the peak emission of a Keplerian disk around the central star" (3 Results)
   - validation: Compared with literature dynamical and spectroscopic masses  
  > "This value is consistent with the dynamical mass estimated from the" (3 Results)
5. **Constrain small- and large-grain cavity sizes with MCRT SED modeling**: HOCHUNK3D Monte Carlo radiative transfer with a two-layer disk; four-step manual fitting: initial h_small and M_disk at r_cav1=10 au, r_cav2_small=55 au; iterate q1, q2 to match band 6 surface brightness; vary r_cav2_small 40-80 au; vary r_cav1_large 1, 8, 26 au  
  > "by running radiative transfer modeling using a Monte Carlo radiative transfer (MCRT) code" (4.2 SED fitting); "Our fitting procedure included the following four steps." (4.2 SED fitting)
   - software: HOCHUNK3D
   - parameter: Free parameters r_cav1_large, r_cav2_small, h_small(100 au), M_disk  
  > "$r^{\rm large}_{\rm rcav1}$ and $r^{\rm small}_{\rm rcav2}$ were free parameters." (4.2 SED fitting)
   - parameter: r_cav2_large fixed to 26 au from the visibility fit  
  > "The value of $r^{\rm large}_{\rm rcav2} =$26 au was taken from the result in" (4.2 SED fitting)
   - parameter: q1 = -1.0, q2 = 4.5 (fiducial)  
  > "found that the values of $q_{1}=-$1.0 and $q_{2}=$4.5 (fiducial model) well reproduce the surface brightness" (4.2 SED fitting)
   - parameter: Scale height of large grains fixed at 1 au at 100 au; small grains varied to match 60-70 um flux  
  > "We fixed the scale heights of 1 au at $r=$ 100 au for the disk of large dust grains" (4.2 SED fitting); "were varied to reproduce the flux at $\lambda \sim$60–70 $\mu$m." (4.2 SED fitting)
   - parameter: Large-grain mass fraction 0.9; accretion rate 1.3e-9 Msun/yr; inclination 75 deg  
  > "The mass fraction ($f$) of large dust grains in the total dust mass was set to 0.9." (4.2 SED fitting); "We set a mass accretion rate of $\dot{M} =$ 1.3 $\times$10$^{-9}$ $M_{\odot}$/yr" (4.2 SED fitting)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust mass ratio 100  
  > "assuming a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100" (4.2 SED fitting)
   - assumption: Scale height exponent p = 1.25 with T proportional to r^-0.5; size distribution n(s) ~ s^-3.5  
  > "For simplicity, we assumed $p=$ 1.25 with a typical midplane temperature profile of $T \propto r^{-0.5}$." (4.2 SED fitting)
   - fitting: Manual iterative grid over four parameters, chi-square over 1-100 um; no sampler or priors stated  
  > "calculated at $\lambda=$1–100 $\mu$m." (4.2 SED fitting)
   - validation: Surface-brightness of MCRT model compared with the visibility-fit profile (step 6); alternative models A-F tested  
  > "The SED can be well reproduced by a cavity in the small dust with a size of $60\pm10$ au." (4.2 SED fitting)
6. **Check consistency of visibility-fit and MCRT surface brightness**: Compare the two band 6 surface-brightness profiles convolved with a 0.2 arcsec beam  
  > "we compare the surface brightness of the two models at band 6 in" (4.2 SED fitting)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Maximum residual about 119 uJy/beam (1.9 sigma), models mutually consistent  
  > "This residual is insignificant and thus the two models are mutually consistent." (4.2 SED fitting)
7. **Upper limit on small-grain mass inside the cavity**: Optically thin emission from small grains with surface density power law, temperature from HOCHUNK3D, scaled to the 10 um IR excess upper limit; sensitivity tested over wavelength, r_min, r_max, alpha  
  > "We assumed that the excess emission at NIR wavelengths is optically thin" (4.3 Upper limit on amount of small grains inside cavity); "We calculated the equilibrium temperature of optically thin dust at each radius inside the cavity, $T(r)$, using the" (4.3 Upper limit on amount of small grains inside cavity)
   - software: HOCHUNK3D
   - parameter: r_min = 0.03 au (sublimation), r_max = 60 au, alpha = -1  
  > "Assuming that $r^{\rm min}$ is the dust sublimation radius at 0.03 at, $r^{\rm max}$ is 60 au, and $\alpha=-1$" (4.3 Upper limit on amount of small grains inside cavity)
   - assumption: Surface density power law with exponent alpha; inclination not needed  
  > "disk inclination is not needed to take into account in the calculation" (4.3 Upper limit on amount of small grains inside cavity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Result varied with wavelength (3.35 um), r_max, r_min and alpha  
  > "We note that the value of $M_{\rm cav}^{\rm small}$ is only weakly affected by the chosen wavelength." (4.3 Upper limit on amount of small grains inside cavity)

## Qualitative findings

- 12CO emission is present inside r<60 au, so the disk is gas rich there  
  > "Gas is also present at $r<$60 au in the $^{12}$CO moment 0 map." (7 Conclusion)
- Large grains dominate at r<60 au, implying efficient growth with less efficient fragmentation  
  > "These results suggest that large dust grains are dominant at $r<$60 au, implying that dust grains efficiently grow with less efficient fragmentation in this region" (abstract)
