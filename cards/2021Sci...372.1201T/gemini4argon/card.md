1. **Calibrate and image dust continuum and [C II] line cube, and generate moment maps**: Calibrate visibilities with CASA pipeline, identify line-free channels with hif_findcont, image continuum and UV-subtracted [C II] cube with tclean, and generate moment maps using the masked moment method.  
  > "We performed data reduction using version 5.1.1 of the Common Astronomy Software Applications (C ASA ) ( _37_ ) pipeline." (Data analysis and imaging); "After continuum subtraction, we imaged two spectral windows of the USB to produce a data cube with the three dimensions of right ascension, declination and velocity using the task tclean in C ASA ." (Data analysis and imaging); "Figures 1B and Figures S1D were made by the masked moment method ( _39_ )." (Data analysis and imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean, hif_findcont, uvcontsub, KinMS
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.0 (continuum) and 0.5 ([C II]); channel width = 20 km s^-1; clean threshold = 1.5x rms (continuum) and 1.0x rms ([C II]); moment mask threshold = 7.5x rms in smoothed cube  
  > "The visibility data were weighted by the Briggs scheme with a robust parameter of 0.0 which provides a trade-off between resolution and sensitivity ( _38_ )" (Data analysis and imaging); "The velocity channel resolution was 20 km s [-1] (=23.4 MHz) after binning by 24 frequency channels of 977 kHz, to improve the signal to noise ratio. The visibility data were weighted by the Briggs scheme with a robust parameter of 0.5" (Data analysis and imaging); "The observed data cube was smoothed by convolving with a uniform filter spatially (1.5 times the beam size) and spectrally (4 times the channel width), then the mask area was determined by the intensity threshold (7.5 times the root mean square noise in the smoothed cube)" (Data analysis and imaging)
   - fitting: Linear function fitted to line-free channels in uvcontsub  
  > "We measured the flux density of the dust continuum emission by fitting a linear function to the line-free channels and subtracted its emission components from the visibility data using the task uvcontsub in C ASA ." (Data analysis and imaging)
   - validation: Synthesized beam checked for negligible sidelobes (< 13%)  
  > "The side lobes of the synthesized beam are < 13%. The influence of this side lobe was removed from the images by the deconvolution process with a task of tclean in C ASA ." (Figure S2 caption)
2. **Measure disk position angle, inclination, and scale radius**: Measure kinematic position angle from central 0.5" of [C II] moment 1 map using PaFit, then fit 2D Sérsic and exponential profiles to dust continuum and [C II] moment 0 images using GALFIT.  
  > "The position angle of the disk was estimated by using P A F IT Package ( _41_ ) from the [C II ] line velocity image (Moment 1), which shows the regular rotation and kinematic position angle." (The size and inclination of the disk); "To estimate the axis ratio of the disk with the estimated position angle, we fitted 2-dimensional (2D) Sérsic functions to the dust continuum image (see Fig 1C) and the [C II ] line intensity image (Fig. 1A and Fig. S1A), using G ALFIT code ( _42_ )." (The size and inclination of the disk); "We therefore fitted a 2D exponential function to the [C II ] line intensity image, resulting in a diskscale radius _R_ d = 1.83 ± 0.04 kpc." (The size and inclination of the disk)
   - software: PaFit, GALFIT
   - parameter: Central 0.5" region used for PaFit  
  > "The central 0.5" region in [C II ] line velocity image was utilized for measuring position angle, as this region features sufficient signal to noise ratio." (The size and inclination of the disk)
   - assumption: Thin disk relation q = cos(i) used to convert dust continuum axis ratio to inclination  
  > "From the estimated axis ratio ( _q_ ) of the dust continuum image, the inclination of the disk _i_ = 37.8 +2.4-3.3 ° was calculated with equation _q_ = cos( _i_ )" (The size and inclination of the disk)
   - fitting: Chi-square minimization in GALFIT (reduced chi^2 = 1.592 with dof = 4090 for continuum Sérsic fit; reduced chi^2 = 0.956 with dof = 4091 for [C II] exponential fit)  
  > "The reduced chi square _χ_ & _υ_ of the fitting is 1.592, where the degree of freedom is 4090. Disk scale radius _R_ d was estimated by fitting exponential profile to the [C II ] line image using the G ALFIT code. The reduced chi square _χ_ & _υ_ is 0.956, where the degree of freedom is 4091." (Table S1)
   - validation: Position angle and inclination compared with independent values derived from 3D gas dynamical modeling  
  > "We also derived the position angle of the disk from the gas dynamical modeling (see below), finding 7.6 +7.-7.13 ° at 95% confidence level." (The size and inclination of the disk)
3. **Decompose [C II] intensity into logarithmic spiral modes via Fourier analysis**: Deproject [C II] moment 0 image to face-on using scikit-image and compute Fourier amplitudes A(p, m) for m = 1, 2, 3, 4 logarithmic spirals.  
  > "We first deprojected the [C II ] line intensity image to be viewed as face-on using the position angle 4.5° and inclination 37.8° measured above (Table S1). The image rotation and stretching for the deprojection were performed using SCIKIT - IMAGE package ( _45_ )" (Spiral structure); "From the deprojected image, we then calculated the Fourier amplitude _A_ ( _3_, _46_ ), which indicates the magnitude of the _m_ -armed logarithmic spirals." (Spiral structure)
   - software: scikit-image
   - parameter: PA = 4.5°, inclination = 37.8°; radial annulus 1.6 kpc to 7.2 kpc; m = 1, 2, 3, 4; bilinear spline interpolation  
  > "We first deprojected the [C II ] line intensity image to be viewed as face-on using the position angle 4.5° and inclination 37.8° measured above (Table S1)." (Spiral structure); "We excluded the central region of 1.6 kpc and the region outside of 7.2 kpc (see Fig. 3B) for the calculation of _A_ ( _p_, _m_ ) to avoid the effects of unresolved components and noise." (Spiral structure)
   - assumption: Polar coordinate center is the peak intensity pixel in the [C II] image  
  > "The center of the polar coordinate was defined as the pixel which has a peak intensity in the [C II ] line image." (Spiral structure)
   - fitting: Peak finding in Fourier spectrum A(p, m); uncertainties from PA and inclination uncertainties  
  > "with a pitch angle of _α_ = 26.7 -1.6 ° at the 95% confidence level. The confidence interval was estimated from the uncertainties of the position angle and inclination of the disk." (Supplementary Materials, Spiral structure)
   - validation: Noise level estimated by applying Fourier transform to 300 noise maps extracted from emission-line region of cube (84th percentile)  
  > "Its underlying noise level was estimated by applying equation (S2) on 300 noise maps extracted from the emission line region of the data cube and computing their 84 [th] percentile." (Spiral structure)
4. **Extract PVD, measure intrinsic velocity dispersion and rotational support**: Extract PVD along kinematic major axis (PA = 4.5°) averaged over one beam width (3 pixels), fit line profiles with Gauss-Hermite h3 and h4 polynomials to measure velocity dispersion, and derive intrinsic velocity dispersion and v_rot from outer disk.  
  > "( **E** ) PVD of the observed [C II ] line emission, extracted along the kinematic major axis shown in panel B, which has a position angle of 4.5° (Table S1). Data were averaging over one beam width (three pixels)." (Figure 1 caption); "(H) shows velocity dispersion, which were measured by fitting a Gaussian with third and fourth order Gauss-Hermite parameters _h_ 3 and _h_ 4 ( _76_ ) to the line profile at each position of PVD (black points and error bars, same as Fig. 1F)." (Figure S1 caption); "We estimate the inclination-corrected rotational velocity _v_ rot is 179 -18 km s at radius of 0.3", calculated using the flat part of the PVD (Fig. 1E)." (main text)
   - parameter: PVD extraction width = 1 beam (3 pixels); SNR > 4 in at least 4 velocity channels required for profile fitting  
  > "This fitting used the pixels where [C II ] line emission has signal to noise ratio SNR > 4 in at least 4 velocity channels." (Figure S1 caption)
   - fitting: Gaussian with third and fourth order Gauss-Hermite parameters h3 and h4 fitted to PVD line profiles  
  > "(H) shows velocity dispersion, which were measured by fitting a Gaussian with third and fourth order Gauss-Hermite parameters _h_ 3 and _h_ 4 ( _76_ ) to the line profile at each position of PVD" (Figure S1 caption)
   - validation: Compared observed PVD and velocity dispersion profile with 3D dynamical model PVD and dispersion profile  
  > "The model PVD is compared to the observed PVD in Fig. S1G, where both PVDs are extracted along the kinematic major axis (Fig. 1B and Fig. S1, D to F). Figure. S1H shows the comparison between the observed velocity dispersion and model velocity dispersion, measured at each position of PVD." (Results of the gas dynamical modeling)
5. **3D gas dynamical modeling of bulge and disk masses**: Generate gas particles from CLEAN components using SkySampler, build 3D dynamical model cubes in KinMS with a spherical de Vaucouleurs bulge + thick pressure-supported exponential disk gravitational potential, and fit directly to the observed cube via MCMC using emcee.  
  > "We used the K IN MS code ( _40_ ) to produce a gas dynamical model cube which has the same beam size and velocity resolution as those in the observed cube (i.e. beam smearing effect was taken into account for the model cube)." (Gas dynamical modeling); "reproduce the [C II ] line intensity profile of BRI 1335-0417 by using the de-convolved CLEAN components that were provided by a task of tclean in C ASA ." (Supplementary Materials, Gas dynamical modeling); "We determined best-fitting model parameters and their associated confidence intervals with a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling using the EMCEE code ( _67_ ), that is an implementation of affine invariant MCMC ensemble sampler." (MCMC fitting)
   - software: KinMS, SkySampler, emcee, CASA
   - parameter: R_d = 1.83 kpc; velocity range -390 to +370 km s^-1; N ~ 4 x 10^4 constraints (dof = 42400); 8 free parameters; 180 walkers, 4000 steps, 600 burn-in steps  
  > "The likelihood was computed using the observed cube over the velocity range from -390 km s [-1] to +370 km s [-1] with the velocity center 0 km s [-1] matched to the frequency of 351.470 GHz in barycentric frame." (MCMC fitting); "We run the MCMC fitting with 180 walkers and 4000 steps. Because the first 600 steps are not converged to the posterior distribution (a burn-in phase) by MCMC sampling, these first steps were excluded from the analysis." (Results of the gas dynamical modeling)
   - assumption: Dark matter halo excluded within 5 kpc; black hole mass included in spherical de Vaucouleurs bulge; axisymmetric exponential disk with R_d = 1.83 kpc; isotropic spatially uniform velocity dispersion; hydrostatic asymmetric drift and disk thickness correction  
  > "In this object, the [C II ] line emission is detected only up to 5 kpc, which is not sufficient to measure the mass distribution of the dark matter using the kinematics. We therefore excluded the dark matter mass distribution from our dynamical model." (Gas dynamical modeling); "Because the velocity dispersion is constant over this galaxy except in the central region [Fig. 1 (E and F) and Fig. S1(G and H)], we adopted an isotropic and spatially uniform velocity dispersion." (Rotation velocity of pressurized galactic disk); "Given the high velocity dispersion, we considered the disk thickness of _h_ ~ (𝜎, / _v_ rot ( _R_ d ))´1.68 _R_ d ( _50_ ), correcting equation (S8) ( _58_ )." (Rotation velocity of pressurized galactic disk)
   - fitting: Bayesian MCMC with emcee; Gaussian prior on inclination (37.8 ± 1.6°), log-uniform priors on M_bulge and M_disk, uniform priors on R_e, PA, dx, dy, dv; chi^2 scaled by sqrt(2N); reduced chi^2 = 1.69 (dof = 42400)  
  > "We used log uniform prior distribution for the mass of the bulge _M_ bulge and disk _M_ disk to cover the multiple orders of magnitude, while uniform prior distribution for the other 5 parameters." (MCMC fitting); "We used a method of scaling the standard Dc [2] by a factor of,(2 _N_ ) ( _68_ )." (MCMC fitting); "The reduced chi square _χ_ & _υ_ in our MCMC fitting is 1.69 under the degree of freedom of 42400." (Table S2)
   - validation: Tested sensitivity of M_bulge and M_disk to varied fixed R_d values and to letting R_d be a free parameter (Fig. S4); checked integrated [C II] spectrum residuals for outflows (Fig. S5)

## Qualitative findings

- BRI 1335-0417 exhibits a two-armed spiral morphology in [C II] extending from 2 to 5 kpc in radius, already formed at z = 4.41 (1.4 Gyr after the Big Bang).  
  > "The [C II ] gas kinematics show a steep velocity rise near the galaxy center and have a two-armed spiral morphology that extends from about 2 to 5 kiloparsecs in radius." (abstract); "These features had been formed within 1.4 billion years after the Big Bang, long before the peak of cosmic star formation." (abstract)
- No evidence of a bipolar outflow is found in [C II] line emission, unlike what was previously reported in CO (J=7->6).  
  > "In addition, in [C II ] line emission, we could not find any evidence of bipolar outflow which is reported in CO ( _J_ =7→6) line ( _14_ )." (main text)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Previous [C II] observations had insufficient spatial resolution to determine the gas distribution and kinematics, whereas the archival Band 7 C43-5 data resolve the galaxy at ~1.3 kpc.  
  > "[C II ] line emission from this object was detected in previous observations at lower spatial resolution, insufficient to determine the gas" (main text)
- Why this method: Direct 3D cube modeling with KinMS accounts for beam smearing, pressure support (asymmetric drift) accounts for the high velocity dispersion, and scaling delta chi^2 by sqrt(2N) prevents unrealistically small MCMC confidence intervals from ~4 x 10^4 constraints.  
  > "We used the K IN MS code ( _40_ ) to produce a gas dynamical model cube which has the same beam size and velocity resolution as those in the observed cube (i.e. beam smearing effect was taken into account for the model cube)." (Gas dynamical modeling); "The pressure in the gas disk is proportional to the square of the velocity dispersions of the gas, which is not negligible for the gas dynamical modeling ( _61_ )." (Rotation velocity of pressurized galactic disk); "In our data ( _N_ ~ 4 ´ 10 [4] and _O_ = 8), it becomes 2 _N_ and yields an unrealistically small confidence interval due to the large number of constraints in fitting the observed cube. We used a method of scaling the standard Dc [2] by a factor of,(2 _N_ ) ( _68_ )." (MCMC fitting)
- Alternative: Tidal-tail origin from a major merger for the two-armed morphology is disfavored (as tidal tails typically occur on >10 kpc scales, whereas these arms are <5 kpc and connected to the rotating disk), though not completely ruled out.  
  > "Tidal-tail features in a galaxy merger are expected occur on larger scales (>10kpc) ( _6_, _31_ ). We therefore disfavor, but cannot completely rule out, a tidal-tail origin for the observed spiral morphology." (main text)
- Alternative: Dark matter halo component in the dynamical model was excluded because [C II] is detected only out to 5 kpc, where baryons dominate.  
  > "In this object, the [C II ] line emission is detected only up to 5 kpc, which is not sufficient to measure the mass distribution of the dark matter using the kinematics. We therefore excluded the dark matter mass distribution from our dynamical model." (Gas dynamical modeling)
- Alternative: Bipolar outflow kinematics were excluded from dynamical modeling because no outflow signature was detected in the [C II] spectrum.  
  > "We could not find any evidence of bipolar outflow in [C II ] line emission, but this does not exclude the bipolar outflow seen in CO ( _J_ =7→6) line emission because the [C II ] line may not be strongly emitted from the outflowing material." (CO (J=7->6) outflow); "Therefore, we did not consider the outflow kinematics in the gas dynamical modeling." (CO (J=7->6) outflow)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Spatial resolution (~0.2") is much larger than the black hole sphere of influence (0.02"), so the black hole mass and stellar bulge mass cannot be separated dynamically (allowing a black hole without a bulge).  
  > "Because estimated _R_ SOI is much smaller than our spatial resolution, we cannot measure the circular velocity of black hole _v_ BH ( _r_ ) and bulge _v_ bulge ( _r_ ) separately. We thus included the black hole mass in the bulge." (Gas dynamical modeling)
- Limitation (authors): Gas inflow into the galaxy center driven by spiral torques cannot be directly detected due to limited signal-to-noise ratio.  
  > "Such gas inflow could not be directly detected in our data due to the limited signal to noise ratio." (main text)
- Limitation (authors): Pixel-to-pixel covariance matrix correction in the data cube was omitted because it is negligible compared to the sqrt(2N) log-likelihood scaling.  
  > "The correction by the inverse covariance matrix for the pixel to pixel correlations ( _71_ ) in the observed data cube is not taken into account, because it is negligible compared to the effect by scaling the logarithmic likelihood." (MCMC fitting)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distinguishing genuine density-wave/bar-driven spiral arms from merger-induced tidal arms or streams at ~1.3 kpc resolution remains morphological and somewhat degenerate.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Disk inclination is inferred from the dust continuum axis ratio assuming an axisymmetric thin disk (q = cos i), even though the dust continuum has a high Sérsic index (n = 2.48) and non-axisymmetric bar-like features.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The sqrt(2N) chi-square scaling is an ad-hoc heuristic to inflate uncertainties in correlated interferometric cubes rather than a full visibility-plane or covariance-matrix treatment.
