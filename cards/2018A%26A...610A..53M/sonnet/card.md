## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA continuum and CO(6-5)**: Manual calibration, interactive imaging with Briggs weighting robust 0.5; continuum from line-free channels, CO cube at 70 km/s  
  > "Imaging was performed in an interactive way to apply a mask, with standard Briggs weighting scheme (robustness parameter set to 0.5) and no tapering." (4.1 SDP 9 high resolution observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Estimate redshift from CO(6-5)**: Peak of spectrum over whole galaxy; error from FWHM of higher-z Gaussian tail  
  > "The peak estimated over a region that includes the whole galaxy corresponds to a redshift 1.5753$\pm0.0003$" (4.1 SDP 9 high resolution observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Wong et al. 2017 (1.5747±0.0002) and Bussmann 2013  
  > "The value of 1.5747$\pm$0.0002 reported by Wong et al. (2017) is well within the line size" (4.1 SDP 9 high resolution observations)
3. **Lens model ALMA 1.3 mm and HST 1.6 um images**: SIE lens with semilinear inversion source reconstruction; parameters explored with MultiNest; properties from 1000 perturbations (median, 16th/84th percentiles)  
  > "Properties such as magnification and sizes are computed perturbing 1000 times the lens model parameters around their best-fit values" (6 Modeling the emission of SDP9)
   - software: multinest
   - parameter: magnification mu = F_LP/F_SP for SNR>=3 pixels  
  > "magnifications are evaluated as $\mu=F^{\rm LP}_{3\sigma}/F^{\rm SP}_{3\sigma}$" (6 Modeling the emission of SDP9)
   - assumption: Singular Isothermal Ellipsoid lens  
  > "The lens is a Singular Isothermal Ellipsoid (SIE, Kormann et al. 1994), described with five parameters" (6 Modeling the emission of SDP9)
   - fitting: MultiNest exploration of lens parameter space; priors not stated  
  > "The lens parameter space is explored using multinest (Feroz et al. 2009)." (6 Modeling the emission of SDP9)
   - validation: Both datasets give consistent lens parameters, similar to literature  
  > "Both data sets lead to the same set of parameters, which are similar to other results showed in literature" (6 Modeling the emission of SDP9)
4. **Fit Chandra X-ray spectra and derive luminosities**: Power-law fits with Cash statistics; Gamma fixed to 1.8 for N_H; compare with SFR-based X-ray expectation (Ranalli 2003, Lehmer 2016)  
  > "using simple models (within xspec v.12.9, Arnaud et al. 1996) and the Cash statistics (Cash 1979), which is appropriate for low-count spectra." (3 Chandra X-ray observations)
   - software: CIAO, XSPEC
   - assumption: Gamma = 1.8 typical of AGN  
  > "If we fix $\Gamma$ to 1.8, as typically observed in AGN and quasars (e.g., Piconcelli et al. 2005)" (3 Chandra X-ray observations)
   - fitting: Cash statistic  
  > "the Cash statistics (Cash 1979), which is appropriate for low-count spectra" (3 Chandra X-ray observations)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- X-ray emission peaks in the nuclear region cospatial with the mm peak, indicating obscured AGN  
  > "The X-ray model, despite its low resolution ($\sim0.5$ arcsec), clearly peaks in the nuclear region, in strong concordance with the mm peaks." (Conclusions)
