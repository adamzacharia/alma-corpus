## Analysis recipe

1. **Map molecular distributions**: Intensity-integrated maps and spectra of each line in both sources, overlaid on continuum  
  > "Intensity integrated maps of each line overlaid on are shown in Fig. [2](#fig:i16293mom)." (3.1 IRAS 16293-2422)
   - parameter: Region within 2" of IRAS 16293 A masked due to line confusion  
  > "Thus the region is masked out in the maps within 2$\ensuremath{''}$ from the source position." (3.1 IRAS 16293-2422)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Model DCO+ peak position with chemical network**: Steady-state analytic H2D+/DCO+ network with CO freeze-out drop abundance, passed through RATRAN to synthetic cubes, temperature/density profiles scaled by arbitrary factors  
  > "a simple steady-state, analytic chemical network that accounts for the basic reactions leading to the production and destruction of is used." (4.1.1)
   - software: RATRAN
   - parameter: T_sub = 20–40 K, n_de = 1e5–1e8 cm-3, X[CO] = 1e-7–1e-4  
  > "we explore the parameter ranges of $T_{\rm
 sub}$ = 20–40 K, $n_{\rm de}$ = 10$^{5}$–10$^{8}$ cm$^{-3}$ and $X[\ce{CO}]$ = 10$^{-7}$ – 10$^{-4}$." (4.1.1)
   - assumption: cosmic-ray ionization rate 1.26e-17 s-1; ortho-to-para H2 lower limit 1e-3; sources centred on and dominated by A  
  > "The ortho-to-para ratio is set to have a lower limit of 10$^{-3}$ at low temperatures" (4.1.1)
   - fitting: By-eye fit of radial profile  
  > "A by eye fit of the chemical model to the observations is used to constrain the decrease in the temperature profile" (4.1.1)
   - validation: Consistency with DCO+ results of VLA 1623 in Murillo et al. 2015 and with line-ratio temperatures  
  > "This temperature is consistent with the chemical modelling of the peak position." (4.1.2)
3. **Derive T and n from line ratios (DCO+, c-C3H2)**: RADEX non-LTE calculations compared to observed ratios, with peak intensities converted from Jy/beam to K  
  > "Using RADEX (cite: vandertak2007), we performed non-LTE excitation and radiative transfer calculations to constrain the temperature and density" (4.1.2)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: IRAS 16293: N=3e12 cm-2, width 1.0 km/s; VLA 1623: N=2e12 cm-2, width 0.7 km/s  
  > "We adopt a line width of 1.0 km s$^{-1}$ and a column density of 3 $\times$ 10$^{12}$ cm$^{-2}$" (4.1.2)
   - assumption: ortho/para ratio of 3 for c-C3H2; molecular data from LAMDA  
  > "To convert to the total (ortho + para) column density, an o/p ratio of 3 was used." (4.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: APEX single-dish ratio used as cross-check for VLA 1623  
  > "The APEX observations are used to double check if this is the kinetic temperature of the bulk of the emission at the disk-envelope interface." (4.1.2)
4. **Test non-detection of N-bearing lines in VLA 1623**: Predict ALMA peak intensities from single-dish detections via RADEX for extended and compact (1") emission  
  > "Two cases are explored, extended and compact emission concentrated in a 1$\ensuremath{''}$ region." (4.4)
   - software: RADEX
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- DCO+ traces cold material at the disk-envelope interface in both sources, shifted inwards by disk shadowing  
  > "consistently traces the cold material at the disk-envelope interface, where gas and dust temperatures are lowered due to disk shadowing." (abstract)
- Hot core molecules abundant toward IRAS 16293 but not VLA 1623  
  > "Finally, hot core molecules are abundantly observed toward IRAS 16293-2422 but not toward VLA 1623-2417." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: SMA data needed for direct comparison of cold chemistry and for two transitions to derive T and n from ratios  
  > "Additionally, two transitions of are needed to derive temperature and density from line ratios." (2.1 IRAS 16293-2422)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Constant abundance profile and density changes failed to reproduce the DCO+ peak position  
  > "For both systems we find that the constant abundance profile produces a peak further out than where it is observed" (4.1.1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Simple chemical model cannot explain inner DCO+ 5-4 emission; single physical profile adopted  
  > "our simple chemical model cannot fully explain the inner part of the 5–4 emission" (4.1.1)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): By-eye model fits with no formal uncertainties or goodness of fit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Arbitrary scaling of literature temperature profiles
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interferometer and single-dish beams differ, so line ratios are subject to beam dilution
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only two sources, so generalisation is limited
