## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract radio and 3 mm compact cores**: DENDROGRAM algorithm plus CASA imfit on the VLA 4.86 GHz and ATOMS 3 mm maps, following Saha2022  
  > "we implement the approach followed by (cite: Saha2022) and use a combination of the `DENDROGRAM` algorithm and the `CASA` imfit task to extract the cores" (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
   - software: astrodendro, CASA
   - parameter: Six radio cores (R1-R6) and ten 3 mm cores (MM1-MM10) identified; procedure parameters in an appendix  
  > "In total, six radio (R1 - R6) and ten 3 mm (MM1 - MM10) cores are identified." (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive ionized-gas parameters of the radio cores**: Emission measure, N_Ly and electron density from Equations 6-8 of Schmiedeke2016  
  > "we calculate the physical parameters such as the emission measure ($EM$), number of Lyman continuum photons emitted per second ($N_{\rm Ly}$), and electron density ($n_{\rm e}$) using Equations 6-8 from (cite: Schmiedeke2016)" (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
   - assumption: 4.86 GHz emission optically thin; electron temperature 6370 K  
  > "we assume the 4.86 GHz emission to be optically thin, and adopt the value of the electron temperature to be 6370 K from (cite: Quireza2006)" (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
   - assumption: Distance 5.82 kpc  
  > "is located at a distance of 5.82 kpc (cite: Urquhart2018)" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CORNISH UCH II classifications and typical UCH II/compact H II properties  
  > "The derived radio properties of these compact VLA cores are consistent with those of UCH II/compact H II regions" (4.2 The inner ring: radio and 3 mm continuum emission)
3. **Estimate free-free contribution to 3 mm cores**: Method of Liu2023 applied to the 3 mm cores; six cores found to be >50% free-free  
  > "We verify this by following the method described in (cite: Liu2023) and find six of the 3 mm cores to have more than 50% contribution from free-free emission." (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cores show no H13CO+ or HCO+ emission, consistent with free-free dominated 3 mm emission  
  > "None of these cores show any emission in the molecular line transitions of H$^{13}$CO$^+$ and HCO$^+$." (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
4. **Infer spectral type of the ionizing star from the 4.86 GHz flux**: Integrate the VLA map within 3 sigma, subtract the six compact radio cores, convert to N_Ly and compare with main-sequence stars of Panagia1973  
  > "the flux density is calculated to be $\sim$0.9 Jy which translates to a Lyman-continuum photon flux, $N_{\rm Ly}$, of $\sim$3.4$\times\,10^{48}\,\rm s^{-1}$" (4.1 H II region G24.47); "we infer the spectral type to be O8.5V - O8V" (4.1 H II region G24.47)
   - assumption: A single star ionizes the H II region; dust absorption of Lyman continuum photons not accounted for, so result is a lower limit  
  > "Assuming a single star to be responsible for the ionization of G24.47" (4.1 H II region G24.47); "this can only be considered as a lower limit, since dust absorption of Lyman continuum photons is not accounted for here" (4.1 H II region G24.47)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: NIR spectral types from the colour-magnitude diagram reasonably consistent with the radio spectral type  
  > "The NIR spectral type inferred from the colour-magnitude diagram is reasonably consistent with the estimated radio spectral type." (4.1 H II region G24.47)
5. **Identify candidate ionizing stars in the NIR**: 2MASS and UKIDSS JHK colour-magnitude and colour-colour plots following Potdar2022  
  > "This is carried out using NIR colour-magnitude and colour-colour plots (cite: Potdar2022)." (4.1 H II region G24.47); "12 candidate massive (with spectral type earlier than B3), Class III stars, namely, E1 - E12, are identified within the VLA radio emission" (4.1 H II region G24.47)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Lyman-continuum flux of the central stars E10-E12 checked against the radio flux; found inconsistent  
  > "So the individual or the sum of their Lyman-continuum photon flux is not consistent with that calculated from the observed 4.86 GHz flux density." (4.1 H II region G24.47)
6. **Extract dense molecular cores and derive masses**: DENDROGRAM extraction on the H13CO+ (1-0) moment zero map (same procedure as the radio and 3 mm cores); two cores from the column density map; masses summed from the H2 column density map  
  > "we utilize the velocity integrated intensity (i.e., moment zero) map of this transition to extract the dense molecular cores" (3.3 Molecular line emission); "The cores are labelled, M1 - M12 of which M1 and M10 are extracted from the column density map." (3.3 Molecular line emission); "Core masses are calculated from the generated hydrogen column density map using" (3.3 Molecular line emission)
   - software: astrodendro, CASA
   - assumption: H13CO+ (1-0) optically thin  
  > "Considering the H$^{13}$CO$^+$ (1-0) line to be optically thin (cite: sanhueza2012; Saha2022)" (3.3 Molecular line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Assess core gravitational stability via virial parameter**: Virial parameter = M_vir / M_core with M_vir from Contreras2016 using Gaussian-fitted H13CO+ line widths, density index p = 1.8 and eccentricity-based shape factor  
  > "we estimate the virial parameter ($\alpha_{\rm vir}$), which represents the ratio of the virial mass ($M_{\rm vir}$) to the mass of the individual cores" (3.3 Molecular line emission); "$\Delta V$ is the line width of the fitted Gaussian profiles to the observed H$^{13}$CO$^+$ spectra" (3.3 Molecular line emission)
   - parameter: p = 1.8; two-component spectra use the average of the two component line widths  
  > "where we adopt $p=1.8$ (cite: Contreras2016)" (3.3 Molecular line emission); "we calculated the average of the line widths obtained from fitting each component individually" (3.3 Molecular line emission)
   - assumption: Cores with alpha_vir < 2 treated as supercritical and collapsing in the absence of other support such as magnetic fields  
  > "we find that 50% of the cores have $\alpha_{\rm vir} < 2$, indicating that these are supercritical and under gravitational collapse in the absence of other supporting mechanisms" (4.3 Expanding molecular ring)
   - fitting: Gaussian line-profile fits to H13CO+ spectra (sampler, priors and likelihood not stated)  
  > "$\Delta V$ is the line width of the fitted Gaussian profiles to the observed H$^{13}$CO$^+$ spectra" (3.3 Molecular line emission)
   - validation: A search for infall signatures in H13CO+ and HCO+ spectra found none  
  > "We searched for infall signature using the H$^{13}$CO$^+$ (1-0) and HCO$^{+}$ spectra extracted toward these cores, but did not find any conclusive evidence." (4.3 Expanding molecular ring)
8. **Measure velocity structure and expansion of the molecular ring**: Moment 0 and 1 maps of HCO+ and H40alpha over full, blue-shifted and red-shifted ranges, an east-west PV diagram of HCO+, and expansion velocity from maximum red- and blue-shifted velocities  
  > "we created separate moment zero maps of HCO$^{+}$ and H40$\alpha$ over three velocity extents" (3.4 Velocity structure of G24.47); "we construct position-velocity (PV) diagram of HCO$^{+}$ towards G24.47 along the east-west direction (offset increases from east to west direction)" (3.4 Velocity structure of G24.47); "we obtain a rough estimate of the expansion velocity to be $\rm \sim9\,km\,s^{-1}$ by considering the maximum red- and blue-shifted velocities observed" (3.4 Velocity structure of G24.47)
   - assumption: Ring inclination not considered  
  > "we have not considered the inclination angle, if any, of the molecular gas ring" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: PV pattern compared with simulations of expanding shells; consistent with moment one map  
  > "that is in very good agreement with the results of simulations of expanding shells discussed in the literature (cite: Arce2011; Wang2016)" (3.4 Velocity structure of G24.47); "Consistent with the moment one map, the PV diagram also displays signatures of expansion." (3.4 Velocity structure of G24.47)
9. **Compute H II region dynamical age**: Dyson and Williams expansion into a homogeneous medium, t_dyn from Stromgren radius R_St, ionized ring radius R_IF and sound speed  
  > "For an H II region expanding into a homogeneous medium, the dynamical age is given by (cite: DysonWilliams1980)" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - parameter: R_IF ~0.8 pc  
  > "$R_{\rm{IF}}$ is the radius of the ionized ring ($\sim$0.8 pc)" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - parameter: n0 ~1e4 cm^-3 from ATLASGAL map  
  > "We estimate $n_{\rm 0}\sim$ $10^4\,\rm cm^{-3}$ from the retrieved ATLASGAL map" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - assumption: Isothermal sound speed 10 km/s  
  > "is the isothermal sound speed (assumed to be 10$\rm\,km\,s^{-1}$)" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - assumption: Ambient density does not change from quiescent to H II region stages  
  > "with the assumption that the physical properties, like density, do not change over evolutionary stages (from quiescent to HII regions) in the star formation process" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with typical lifetimes of UC and compact H II regions  
  > "given the typical lifetimes of these to be $\rm \sim 10^4 - 10^5\,yr$ (cite: Churchwell2002; Davies2011)" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
10. **Energetics of the expanding ring versus ionized gas and stellar wind**: Ring kinetic energy 0.5 M_shell v_exp^2 from column-density mass and 9 km/s; ionized-gas energies from Xu2018 and Li2022 expressions; wind luminosity from Martins2017 parameters integrated over the dynamical age  
  > "the kinetic energy ($0.5 M_{\rm shell} v^{2}_{\rm exp}$) of the expanding ring is calculated to be $\sim 4\times 10^{47} \rm erg$" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation); "This translates to mechanical energy $\sim 3 \times 10^{47} \rm erg$ injected by wind of the ionizing star over the dynamical age of the H II region." (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - assumption: Single ionizing O8.5V-O8V star for the wind  
  > "Considering a single ionizing O8.5V - O8V star for G24.47, as inferred from the radio flux density" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Wind mechanical energy compared with ring kinetic energy; similar energetics reported for RCW 120 and the Orion Veil  
  > "This is comparable to the kinetic energy of the molecular gas ring" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)

## Qualitative findings

- Ionized ring shows secondary collapse; a burst of massive star formation in varied evolutionary phases inside it is the second epoch  
  > "The swept-up ionized ring structure shows evidence of secondary collapse, and within this ring a burst of massive star formation is observed in different evolutionary phases, which constitutes the second epoch." (abstract)
- The 3 mm continuum is predominantly free-free emission with little cold dust contribution  
  > "This suggests that the 3 mm continuum emission is predominantly free-free emission" (3.1 4.86 GHz and 3 mm continuum emission)
- 75% of the VLA and ATOMS cores are in early HC, UC or compact H II phases  
  > "In our case, 75% of the VLA and ATOMS cores are inferred to be in the early phases of HC, UC, or compact H II regions." (4.2 The inner ring: radio and 3 mm continuum emission)
- All twelve molecular cores exceed the 0.05 g cm^-2 surface density threshold  
  > "For all the detected molecular cores, the estimated surface mass densities satisfy the threshold of 0.05$\,\rm g\,cm^{-2}$" (4.3 Expanding molecular ring)
- Molecular cores form six regularly spaced clusters in the ring, consistent with collect and collapse; SO emission (not shown) is co-spatial with the ring  
  > "are observed to be almost *regularly* spaced in the molecular gas ring enveloping the inner ionized ring" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation); "is seen (not presented here) to be co-spatial with the observed H$^{13}$CO$^+$ (1-0) and HCO$^{+}$ (1-0) molecular ring" (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA ATOMS line data chosen to probe kinematics of ionized and dense gas  
  > "To probe the kinematics and dynamics of the associated ionized and dense gas, we use the H40$\alpha$ hydrogen radio recombination line (RRL)" (2.1 ALMA observations)
- Why this method: H13CO+ (1-0) treated as optically thin, so its moment zero map is used to extract dense cores  
  > "Considering the H$^{13}$CO$^+$ (1-0) line to be optically thin (cite: sanhueza2012; Saha2022), we utilize the velocity integrated intensity (i.e., moment zero) map of this transition to extract the dense molecular cores." (3.3 Molecular line emission)
- Alternative: Central NIR stars E10-E12 rejected as the sole ionizing source because their Lyman-continuum flux is inconsistent with the radio flux; off-center ring stars E2, E3, E4, E8 considered potential candidates  
  > "So the individual or the sum of their Lyman-continuum photon flux is not consistent with that calculated from the observed 4.86 GHz flux density." (4.1 H II region G24.47)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Spectral type is only a lower limit because dust absorption of Lyman continuum photons is ignored  
  > "this can only be considered as a lower limit, since dust absorption of Lyman continuum photons is not accounted for here" (4.1 H II region G24.47)
- Limitation (authors): Ionizing star not conclusively identified  
  > "The above analysis, however, restricts any conclusive identification of the ionizing star of G24.47." (4.1 H II region G24.47)
- Limitation (authors): H40alpha resolution and S/N too low to evaluate expansion of the ionized ring  
  > "Given the lower velocity resolution and signal-to-noise ratio of the H40$\alpha$ emission, it was not possible to evaluate the expansion, if any, of the ionized ring." (3.4 Velocity structure of G24.47)
- Limitation (authors): Interferometric data may miss total power, hindering infall absorption detection  
  > "Further, in the interferometric observations, we may be missing the total power, which makes it difficult to identify absorption features." (4.3 Expanding molecular ring)
- Limitation (authors): Ring inclination and winds from newly formed stars not considered in the energetics  
  > "Note that in the above analysis, we have not considered the inclination angle, if any, of the molecular gas ring and the winds from newly formed stars in the ionized ring." (Hierarchical triggering of multi-epoch massive star formation)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No uncertainties are given on core masses, virial parameters, N_Ly, ring mass or energies, so the 50% supercritical fraction is untested against error propagation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Virial parameter threshold of 2 and Gaussian line widths from an optically thin H13CO+ assumption have no stated sensitivity test
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A purely ring-wise expansion velocity of ~9 km/s from extreme velocities in one PV cut is a rough estimate with no cut width or error
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Triggering is inferred from morphology and energetics; a coincidental ring geometry or pre-existing sheet-like cloud is not excluded
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Core mass depends on an unspecified column-density recipe (temperature, abundance) described only in an appendix
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Radio-core radius range (0.1-0.2 pc) is inconsistent with the quoted median of 0.28 pc
