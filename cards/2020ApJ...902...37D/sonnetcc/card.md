## Analysis recipe

1. **Make a deep ALMA dust-continuum map**: Combine line-free channels from all public archival data in Bands 5-7 and image with tclean MTMFS  
  > "The continuum map was obtained from the remaining line-free channels spanning an effective bandwidth of 25 GHz." (2.2.1 Deep Continuum Imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: MTMFS NTERMS=2; natural weighting; multiscale clean to 5 sigma then 1.5 sigma in manual regions; 1500 km/s excluded per line  
  > "The data were imaged with natural weighting to maximize the point source sensitivity." (2.2.1 Deep Continuum Imaging); "A total width of 1500 km s$^{-1}$ centered at each of the emission lines was excluded from the continuum imaging process." (2.2.1 Deep Continuum Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked that STATWT re-weighting did not improve the map; sidelobes below 5 percent  
  > "We have checked that re-weighing of combined visibilities using the STATWT task in CASA does not further improve the map quality." (footnote 4); "The synthesised beam side-lobes of the combined dataset are smaller than 5%." (2.2.1 Deep Continuum Imaging)
2. **Subtract the QSO PSF from the MUSE cube**: Build a PSF image from collapsed QSO continuum layers, scale it to the QSO peak in each wavelength layer, and subtract  
  > "By subtracting this scaled PSF image from each wavelength layer we produce an entire PSF-subtracted datacube." (2.3.2 MUSE PSF Subtraction)
   - parameter: Ellipse masked in every layer with radii equal to the FWHM of a 2D Gaussian fit to the PSF image  
  > "we mask an ellipse on every layer of the datacube of radii equal to the FWHM of a 2-dimensional Gaussian fit to the PSF image" (2.3.2 MUSE PSF Subtraction)
   - assumption: The QSO appears as a point source at MUSE resolution, so the QSO itself is the PSF  
  > "we avoid issues of spatial PSF-variation/interpolation across the field." (2.3.2 MUSE PSF Subtraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure total Lya halo flux and equivalent SFR**: Narrow-band image and PSF-subtracted spectrum; sum diffuse Lya within the SB = 1e-17 contour (QSO and LAE 1 masked), then convert luminosity to SFR  
  > "This amounts to a total of 61Å, or $\approx 2640$ km s$^{-1}$." (3.1 Total Flux of Lya Halo); "For the QSO halo we report the flux summed within the SB $>10^{-17}$ erg s$^{-1}$ cm$^{-2}$ arcsec$^{-2}$ contour across the entire field." (footnote 10)
   - assumption: All halo emission powered by star formation for the SFR conversion  
  > "If the halo emission were powered solely by star formation, we could translate this to an SFR according to Equation" (3.1 Total Flux of Lya Halo)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive moment maps of the Lya halo and compare with [CII]**: Smooth cube spatially, compute non-parametric moments 0, 1, 2 relative to z = 4.6942, apply S/N cut on moment 0; overlay ALMA [CII] channel maps  
  > "we first smooth the datacube in the two spatial directions with a Gaussian kernel of $\sigma = 1.0$ pixel, and calculate the non-parametric moments of the data" (3.3 Kinematic analysis)
   - software: MPDAF
   - parameter: Gaussian smoothing sigma = 1.0 pixel; moment-zero S/N cut of 1.0  
  > "applying a uniform post-processing S/N cut of 1.0 on the moment zero image" (3.3 Kinematic analysis)
   - fitting: none (non-parametric)  
  > "we do not assume any functional form for the Ly$\alpha$ spectral shape" (3.3 Kinematic analysis)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate halo dynamical mass lower limit**: M_dyn = r v^2 / G with r the mean halo radius and v the velocity gradient  
  > "M_{\mathrm{dyn}} = rv^2/G" (3.4 Constraints on the dynamical mass of the halo)
   - parameter: Mean radius 4 arcsec (about 27 pkpc); velocity gradient about +/-300 km/s  
  > "which corresponds to $\approx27$ pkpc at $z=4.69$" (3.4 Constraints on the dynamical mass of the halo); "we see a gradient of approximately $v\approx\pm 300$ km s$^{-1}$ across the halo" (3.4 Constraints on the dynamical mass of the halo)
   - assumption: Rotation causes the velocity gradient; inclination unknown so result is a lower limit  
  > "Then, if one assumes rotation of the gas is responsible for the velocity gradient, we solve for dynamical mass" (3.4 Constraints on the dynamical mass of the halo); "As we have no information on inclination angle, we place a lower limit on the dynamical mass of the halo" (3.4 Constraints on the dynamical mass of the halo)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Measure Lya flux, width and EW0 of companions**: Fit a Gaussian to the 1D MUSE spectrum (LAE 1, LAE 3; peak fixed for LAE 2); sum flux over a 50 A window for QSO, SMG and SRC 6; EW0 limit for LAE 3 from image RMS  
  > "the Ly$\alpha$ flux can be estimated simply by fitting a Gaussian profile to the 1D spectral extraction" (4.1 Lya properties of companions); "we simply sum the flux across a $50$ Å window (i.e. $\approx$ twice the measured FWHM of LAE 1)" (4.1 Lya properties of companions)
   - parameter: Apertures 1.5 arcsec diameter (1 arcsec for SRC 6); LAE 3 continuum limit from RMS of a 700 A wide image redward of the line  
  > "we use the RMS of an image of $\Delta\lambda = 700$ Å, redward of the emission line, to place an upper limit on the continuum" (4.1 Lya properties of companions)
   - assumption: SFR from Lya via the Ouchi et al. calibration; EW0 = (F_Lya/F_cont) x 1/(1+z)  
  > "EW$_0$ = (F$_{{\rm{Ly\alpha}}}$/F$_{{\rm{cont}}}$) $\times$ ($1$/($1$ + z$_{{\rm{best}}}$))" (footnote 8)
   - fitting: 1D Gaussian fit; LAE 2 peak fixed at predicted Lya wavelength from systemic redshift  
  > "we first fix the peak of the Gaussian to the predicted wavelength of Ly$\alpha$ emission corresponding to LAE 2’s systemic redshift" (4.1 Lya properties of companions)
   - validation: Compared with long-slit literature values (Williams et al. 2014)  
  > "can be compared to literature values from long-slit spectroscopy presented in (cite: Williams14)" (4.1 Lya properties of companions)
7. **Search for extended CIV emission**: Extract image and spectrum as for Lya at the predicted CIV wavelength and compare with a composite quasar spectrum  
  > "overlay a composite quasar spectrum (cite: Selsing16)" (3.5 Speculation on Lya powering mechanism)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeated in the 8500-8700 A region for blueshifted metal lines  
  > "but find no evidence for extended emission in this region either" (3.5 Speculation on Lya powering mechanism)

## Qualitative findings

- No Lya halo around the SMG despite similar FIR luminosity and SFR to the QSO  
  > "does not exhibit such a Ly$\alpha$ halo" (abstract)
- Coherent absorption near -400 km/s in QSO, LAE 1 and LAE 2 spectra suggests an expanding neutral shell of at least 24 pkpc  
  > "which could imply the presence of an expanding neutral gas shell with an extent of at least $24$ pkpc" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Deep mm continuum needed, so all public archival ALMA data for the system were combined  
  > "In order to obtain a deep mm continuum map we explored all public data in the ALMA archive available for our system." (2.2.1 Deep Continuum Imaging)
- Why this method: QSO used as its own PSF to avoid spatial PSF variation across the field  
  > "we avoid issues of spatial PSF-variation/interpolation across the field." (2.3.2 MUSE PSF Subtraction)
- Alternative: Additional visibility re-weighting (STATWT) tested and not adopted  
  > "We have checked that re-weighing of combined visibilities using the STATWT task in CASA does not further improve the map quality." (footnote 4)
- How they decided it was enough: Cleaning to 5 sigma globally then 1.5 sigma in manual regions; no extra weighting needed because of good map quality  
  > "first down to $5\sigma$ in the entire map, and then further down to $1.5\sigma$ inside manually defined cleaning regions, which outline the observed emission." (2.2.1 Deep Continuum Imaging)
- Limitation (authors): Inclination unknown, so the halo dynamical mass is only a lower limit  
  > "As we have no information on inclination angle, we place a lower limit on the dynamical mass of the halo" (3.4 Constraints on the dynamical mass of the halo)
- Limitation (authors): LAE 1 Lya flux may be overestimated because it is seen through the QSO halo, or underestimated by the Gaussian fit and aperture  
  > "our flux estimate may be subject to overestimating the Ly$\alpha$ originating within LAE 1" (4.1 Lya properties of companions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ALMA data play a supporting role (dust continuum for orientation, [CII] for comparison); the quantitative results are from MUSE
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SMG Lya flux is 0.04e-16 in the text but 0.12e-16 in the table
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical mass from a velocity gradient of a resonantly scattered Lya halo is not a robust mass
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fluxes for objects embedded in a halo depend on aperture and Gaussian-fit choices that are not tabulated
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Summary quotes a 2 arcsec aperture for LAE 1 while the text uses 1.5 arcsec
