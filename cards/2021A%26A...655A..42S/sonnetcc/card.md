## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image ACA continuum and line data**: CASA pipeline calibration, then tclean with natural weighting; Band 7 continuum as dirty MFS images followed by masked cleaning; Band 4 cubes at native 3.9 MHz channels  
  > "All subsequent imaging was done with CASA task `tclean` and using natural weights to maximize signal-to-noise ratio." (2.2 Atacama Compact Array data); "We then synthesized data-cubes keeping the native channel resolution (3.9 MHz)." (2.2.1 The Sunburst Arc)
   - software: CASA, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: pixel size 1/5 of the beam minor axis  
  > "The resulting cubes and images are sampled at pixel size equal to 1/5 of beam minor axis" (2.2 Atacama Compact Array data)
   - parameter: Sunburst B4 continuum: channels within 250 km/s of CO(4-3) and [CI] excluded  
  > "We then repeated the imaging process excluding channels within 250 km s^−1^ to each side of the expected central frequency" (2.2.1 The Sunburst Arc)
   - assumption: flux calibration accuracy 10%; astrometry accurate to 0.6 arcsec  
  > "We assumed a flux calibration accuracy of 10% throughout." (2.2 Atacama Compact Array data); "the astrometry is expected to be accurate to 0.6″" (2.2 Atacama Compact Array data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **search for emission lines in Band 4 cubes**: Two experiments: integrate expected-line channels at literature redshifts over fixed width, then blind automated search with LINESEEKER; line frequencies from SPLATALOGUE  
  > "we selected and integrated along the velocity axis the channels where lines are expected according to redshift priors from literature" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system); "For the second experiment we proceed with a blind search of emission lines, via the automated LINESEEKER software" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - software: SPLATALOGUE, LINESEEKER
   - parameter: fixed integration width 200 km/s; detection threshold 3 sigma  
  > "using a fixed width of 200 km s^−1^" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system); "This experiment resulted in no detections above $3\sigma$" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - assumption: [CI] detection accepted on strong priors on frequency, position and width set by the CO line  
  > "we consider it a reliable signal based on the strong priors on the frequency, position an width imposed by the" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: [CI] spectrum extracted from the CO region and convolved with the best-fit CO Gaussian, giving a correlation peak ~25 km/s redward of CO  
  > "from the same spatial region as the CO line and convolve it with the best fit Gaussian profile of CO" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
3. **fit line profiles and measure line fluxes and luminosities**: 1D Gaussian fits to CO spectra; zeroth-moment maps integrated over +/-0.85 FWHM; 2D Gaussian imfit on moment maps; 3 sigma upper limits for non-detections  
  > "we fit one dimensional Gaussian profiles to the CO spectra of SGASJ0033-A and SGASJ0033-SMG using non linear least squares minimization" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system); "The resulting moment maps were then fit with a 2D Gaussian profile using CASA task `imfit`" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: integration range -0.85 to +0.85 times best-fit Gaussian FWHM  
  > "we used symmetric integration ranges spanning from $-0.85$ to $+0.85$ times the best-fit Gaussian FWHM" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - parameter: upper limits: 3 sigma point source, fixed 200 km/s range at best available redshift  
  > "we report $3\sigma$ point source upper limits assuming a fixed integration range width of 200 km s^−1^ around the best spectroscopic redshift available" (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - assumption: sources unresolved by the ACA Band 4 beam  
  > "The results of the fits are consistent with the sources being unresolved by the ACA Band 4 beam." (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - fitting: Non-linear least squares Gaussian fits; single, double and triple Gaussians compared with corrected AIC and BIC  
  > "We fit single, double and triple Gaussian models, but only the single Gaussian achieved optimal corrected Akaike Information criterion (cite: Cavanaugh1997CorrectedAkaikeCriterion) and Bayesian Information criterion (cite: Wit2012StatsModelUncertainty) scores." (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
   - validation: Integration range recovers ~99.5% of the flux while keeping S/N >~ 3 per channel; AICc/BIC favour single Gaussian (no broad component)  
  > "In this way we recover $\sim 99.5\%$ of the flux while keeping S/N$\gtrsim 3$ per channel." (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
4. **measure continuum fluxes at 870 um and 2.1 mm**: Single 2D Gaussian imfit on cleaned continuum maps (one Gaussian per image where resolved); 3 sigma upper limits corrected by primary beam attenuation; Band 4 continuum from dirty images with line channels masked  
  > "we fit single 2D Gaussians to each source using `imfit` on the cleaned continuum maps" (3.2 Continuum photometry); "First we built dirty images after masking out the channels with detected and undetected line emission." (3.2 Continuum photometry)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: upper limit = 3 x RMS divided by primary beam attenuation  
  > "we quote the $3\sigma$ upper-limit for point sources based on the observed RMS and corrected by primary beam attenuation" (3.2 Continuum photometry)
   - assumption: all quantities are image plane (not corrected for lensing)  
  > "All quantities are quoted in the image plane, i.e., not corrected for lensing magnification." (Table 1)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fits (imfit)  
  > "we fit single 2D Gaussians to each source using `imfit` on the cleaned continuum maps" (3.2 Continuum photometry)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **extract HST and Spitzer photometry for SED fitting**: HST: tweakreg alignment, astrodrizzle to 0.03 arcsec pixels, hand-defined polygonal apertures, background and noise from 2000 random 10-pixel apertures between BCG isophotes; Spitzer: curve-of-growth circular aperture or annulus segment  
  > "All imaging data were aligned with DRIZZLEPAC routine `tweakreg`, and drizzled to a common pixel size of 0.03″ with `astrodrizzle` using Gaussian kernels with drop size of 0.8." (2.3.1 HST data); "we employed ad-hoc polygonal apertures defined in the filter with the largest PSF for each target" (2.3.1 HST data); "using curve of growth arguments, we determine the optimal sized circular aperture and background annulus for computing the flux of SGASJ1226-A.1" (2.3.2 Spitzer)
   - software: DRIZZLEPAC tweakreg, astrodrizzle, ASTROSCRAPPY
   - parameter: two thousand 10-pixel-wide apertures placed between two BCG isophotes for background mean and variance  
  > "Then we randomly placed two thousand 10 pixel wide apertures between these two isophotes" (2.3.1 HST data)
   - assumption: Sunburst Arc IRAC bands excluded due to blending  
  > "but completely exclude these two bands from the analysis" (2.3.2 Spitzer)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Astrometry vs Gaia DR2 shows at most 0.1 arcsec shift; SGASJ1226-A.1 IRAC fluxes agree with Saintonge et al. 2013  
  > "We find no systematic offset nor rotation, and the observed shift is at most 0.1″." (2.3.1 HST data); "These values agree with the ones reported by (cite: Saintonge2013MolGasLensedGalaxies)" (2.3.2 Spitzer)

## Qualitative findings

- Of four galaxies, only SGASJ0033 was detected in CO(4-3), [CI] and dust; SGASJ1226-A and Sunburst Arc were not detected, SGASJ1226-B was detected only in Band 7 continuum  
  > "The highly magnified SGASJ1226-A was not detected in either CO(5-4) nor dust continuum." (4 Summary and conclusions); "a companion lensed galaxy at the same redshift, SGASJ1226-B, was detected in Band 7 continuum" (4 Summary and conclusions)
- Gas fractions are above z=0 SFGs but 0.5 to 1.0 dex below the PHIBSS-based scaling relations  
  > "The inferred gas fraction in the sample is higher than in $z=0$ SFGs, in agreement with the trend of increasing gas fraction at high redshift." (4 Summary and conclusions); "the gas fraction is roughly $0.5$ to $1.0$ dex lower than predicted by the" (4 Summary and conclusions)
- The apparent gas deficit is attributed to metallicity-dependent tracer-to-gas conversion uncertainties, though a genuine gas deficit is not excluded  
  > "We propose that the apparent gas deficit is rather due to systematic uncertainties in the tracer-to-gas conversion factor dependence on metallicity." (4 Summary and conclusions); "Of course, this does not rule out the possibility of these galaxies actually have less gas than expected" (4 Summary and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ACA chosen for resolution high enough to deblend sources while remaining sensitive to large-scale emission of extended arcs  
  > "ACA offers an optimal combination of an angular resolution that is high enough to deblend the sources while remaining sensible to large scale emission." (2.2 Atacama Compact Array data)
- Why this method: Stellar metallicity used as gas-phase proxy because no homogeneous gas-phase indicator is available for all targets  
  > "Without a homogeneous metallicity indicator for every target, we adopt the stellar metallicities reported by (cite: Chisholm2019MegaSauraMetallicity) as a proxy." (3.5 Molecular gas mass)
- Alternative: Double and triple Gaussian CO line models rejected in favour of a single Gaussian (no broad or high-velocity component)  
  > "Thus, the results indicate no evidence of broad nor high velocity components within current data." (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
- Alternative: Prior-based PSF-fitting IRAC photometry for the Sunburst Arc not attempted; IRAC bands excluded  
  > "we did not attempt a prior-based PSF-fitting approach, but completely exclude these two bands from the analysis" (2.3.2 Spitzer)
- Alternative: CIGALE SED fits compared to MAGPHYS; differences not considered significant  
  > "so we do not consider the differences to be statistically significant" (3.3 SED fitting)
- How they decided it was enough: Number of Gaussian components chosen by information criteria  
  > "According to these criteria, double and triple Gaussian models are not as significant as the single Gaussian model." (3.1 Emission lines in the SGASJ0033 system)
- Limitation (authors): Cannot quantify the differential-lensing bias in magnification  
  > "Unfortunately, we cannot currently provide a quantitative estimation of the bias in the magnification." (4.2 The molecular gas deficit cannot be explained by systematics in M~stars~ or SFR)
- Limitation (authors): Lens-model uncertainties possibly dominated by systematics  
  > "We note, however, that the uncertainties are possibly dominated by systematics" (2.3.3 Lens models and source plane reconstruction)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample and large calibration dispersion at low metallicity make the hypothesis hard to falsify  
  > "Given the large dispersion in tracer-to-gas calibrations in the subsolar metallicity regime and the small size of our sample, it remains difficult to falsify the hypothesis proposed above." (4.3 Low metallicity as the driver of the apparent gas deficit)
- Limitation (authors): No MagE spectrum for SGASJ1226-B; Sunburst IRAC bands excluded  
  > "Unfortunately, a MagE spectrum for SGASJ1226-B is not available." (2.1 Sample selection and prior identification)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low-significance detections (CO 10 sigma, [CI] ~4 sigma, SGASJ1226-B continuum 5.5 sigma) rest on beams of ~10 arcsec that blend lensed images, so image-plane flux splitting and magnification per aperture are uncertain.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar metallicity is used as a proxy for gas-phase metallicity, and SGASJ1226-B was assigned the metallicity of A, so alpha_CO, delta_GDR and X[CI] carry untested systematics.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust-mass method at 870 um is applied to SGASJ1226 where the rest wavelength is 222 um, outside the stated validity range.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fixed Td = 25 K, Tex = 30 K and single-band dust masses are only weakly constrained; MAGPHYS lacks mid-IR and for the Sunburst Arc lacks IRAC.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparison samples were re-calibrated by the authors, so offsets depend on that standardisation; four sources only.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Differential lensing between stars and gas not quantified.
