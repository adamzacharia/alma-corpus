## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate and image ALMA band 6 data**: Pipeline calibration in CASA 6.2.1; tclean on 12-m + 7-m data, three strips combined into one mosaic; line images feathered with total power  
  > "The raw data were calibrated with the data reduction pipeline using *CASA* 6.2.1." (ALMA observations); "We feathered the line images with the TP images to correct for the missing large-scale structures." (ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5; continuum from all line-free channels  
  > "a Briggs weighting scheme with a robust parameter of 0.5" (ALMA observations); "The continuum visibility data were constructed with all line-free channels." (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **estimate clump gas mass and surface density from Herschel**: 2D Gaussian fit to Hi-GAL column density map for FWHM and dust mass; gas mass from dust-to-gas ratio scaled to 0.5 Zsun  
  > "From a 2D gaussian fit to the *Herschel* Hi-GAL FIR dust emission derived column density map (cite: Molinari10)," (Star formation in S284-FIR1); "yields a ratio of 282 and a total gas mass of about 760  $M_\odot$." (Star formation in S284-FIR1)
   - assumption: metallicity -0.3 dex (0.5 Zsun); dust-to-gas ratio 282 from scaling 141  
  > "we adopt a metallicity of $-0.3$ dex (cite: Negueruela15), i.e., $0.5Z_\odot$." (Star formation in S284-FIR1); "Scaling the local Galactic refractory dust-to-gas mass value of 141 (cite: Draine11) for this metallicity yields a ratio of 282" (Star formation in S284-FIR1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **identify dense cores in the 1.3 mm continuum**: astrodendro dendrogram on the uncorrected-for-primary-beam image  
  > "We identify cores using the *astrodendro* algorithm (cite: Rosolowsky08) using parameters of a $4\sigma$ threshold, $1\sigma$ minimum step, and minimum core area of 0.5 beam areas." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: 4 sigma threshold, 1 sigma minimum step, minimum area 0.5 beam areas  
  > "using parameters of a $4\sigma$ threshold, $1\sigma$ minimum step, and minimum core area of 0.5 beam areas." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Parameters chosen as a compromise between completeness and robustness, following earlier CMF studies  
  > "These choices represent a reasonable compromise between completeness and the robustness of source identification (cite: Cheng18)" (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
4. **derive core gas masses**: Optically thin isothermal dust emission: M_gas = R_gd d^2 F_nu / (kappa_nu B_nu(T_d)); fluxes corrected for primary beam  
  > "We estimate core gas masses by assuming their emission arises from optically thin isothermal dust" (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum); "Then final flux and mass estimates are adjusted to account for the primary beam correction" (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
   - parameter: T_d = 20 K; kappa_1.3mm = 0.899 cm^2/g; R_gd = 282  
  > "we assume a uniform dust temperature of 20 K and adopt $\kappa_{\rm 1.3mm}$ = 0.899 $\rm cm^2\:g^{-1}$ (cite: Ossenkopf94)." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum); "We also adopt $R_{\rm gd}=282$, as discussed above." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
   - assumption: Optically thin, isothermal dust at 20 K; distance 4.5 kpc  
  > "we assume a uniform dust temperature of 20 K" (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Temperature sensitivity: 10 K or 30 K change masses by factors of 1.85 and 0.677  
  > "If temperatures of 10 K or 30 K were to be adopted, then the mass estimates would differ by factors of 1.85 and 0.677, respectively." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
5. **split core c1 into c1a and c1b**: Manual division of c1 along the emission minimum ridge, since the two peaks do not meet the astrodendro minimum delta  
  > "we manually divide c1 with the dividing line drawn along the emission minimum ridge" (Location of the protostar and its immediate environment)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **reduce JWST NIRCam images and subtract continuum in F470N**: JWST pipeline v1.12.5 re-reduction; F470N continuum estimated from F356W scaled by a pixel-wise F470N/F356W ratio map smoothed with a Gaussian  
  > "The NIRCAM images were re-reduced using the JWST Calibration Pipeline Version=1.12.5 (cite: jwst)." (JWST observations); "We then multiply the F356W image with this ratio map to estimate the continuum contribution in the F470N band, which is subsequently subtracted from the original F470N image." (The S284p1 outflow)
   - software: JWST Calibration Pipeline
   - parameter: Ratio map smoothed with Gaussian FWHM 15 arcsec; pixel scales 0.03" (SW) and 0.06" (LW)  
  > "smooth it with a gaussian kernel with a FWHM of 15$''$" (The S284p1 outflow); "The three SW filters were reduced using a single pixel scale of 0$''$03" (JWST observations)
   - assumption: F356W may contain H2 lines, so F470N line emission could be over-subtracted  
  > "We note that the F356W image could contain lines from $\rm H_2$ so the 4.70 $\mu$m line emission could potentially be somewhat over-subtracted." (The S284p1 outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Smoothing FWHM tested; 15 arcsec chosen as best compromise  
  > "We have tested different FWHM sizes and found that 15$''$ provide optimal compromise between capturing broad-scale nebulosity and minimizing artifacts." (The S284p1 outflow)
7. **fit the SED of S284p1 with protostar radiative transfer models**: Aperture photometry (15" radius, 5" for NIRCam <5 um in the fiducial case) from NIR to FIR; fit to a grid of RT models with sedcreator; three cases (solar grid, half-solar grid, half-solar plus JWST)  
  > "As input for the SED fitting we perform aperture photometry with a fiducial 15$''$-radius aperture." (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting); "Case 3 (the fiducial case): Same as (2), but including the JWST data." (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
   - software: sedcreator
   - parameter: Case 3 adds 50% systematic uncertainty in quadrature to JWST fluxes; F356W/F405N/F470N measured in 5" aperture  
  > "we add in quadrature an extra 50% systematic uncertainty." (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting); "The flux densities in F356W, F405N and F470N bands are are measured with an aperture of 5$''$ to exclude flux contributions from resolved secondary sources." (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
   - assumption: A single protostar dominates the flux at each wavelength; otherwise derived properties are upper limits  
  > "We note that a major assumption of the SED fitting method is that a single protostar dominates the flux of the SED at each wavelength." (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
   - fitting: Grid fit; 'good' models have chi2 < 2 (or the 10 best if chi2_min > 2); parameters are averages and dispersions of good models  
  > "These are models that satisfy $\chi^2<2$ (or the 10 best physical models if $\chi^2_{\rm min}>2$)" (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
   - validation: Compared with ALMA core c1 mass, Herschel surface density and JWST cone opening angle; checked 50% contamination from c1b  
  > "Finally, the half opening angle of the outflow cavity, $\theta_{\rm w,esc}=29\pm10$$^\circ$, is consistent with the estimate from the IR morphology" (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
8. **measure H2 outflow geometry and propagation speed**: Vectors from S284p1 to bow-shock knots E1 and W1 in the continuum-subtracted F470N image give position angles, lengths and average plane-of-sky speed using the SED age  
  > "Thus the position angles of the NE and SW vectors are 66.3$^\circ$ and 232.8$^\circ$, respectively." (The S284p1 outflow); "In the plane of the sky, this average speed is 3.8 km/s." (The S284p1 outflow)
   - parameter: t_age ~ 3e5 yr; assumed theta_view = 60 deg for 3D speed  
  > "but if we adopt a value of $60^\circ$, then the 3D length and average speed are only boosted by a factor of 1.15" (The S284p1 outflow)
   - assumption: E1 and W1 are the outer bow shocks; speed averaged over the protostar age  
  > "If E1 and W1 represent the location of the outer bowshocks of the outflow, then we can assess the average speed" (The S284p1 outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **derive CO(2-1) outflow mass of the eastern lobe**: Integrated line fluxes in blue (30-40.5 km/s) and red (43.5-67 km/s) velocity ranges converted to mass with the calcu toolkit under LTE, optically thin  
  > "The column densities are derived from the measured line fluxes, using the calcu toolkit (cite: Li20), for which local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) conditions and optically thin line emission have been assumed." (The S284p1 outflow)
   - software: calcu
   - parameter: Systemic velocity 41.2 km/s; innermost +/-1 km/s excluded  
  > "relative to the systemic velocity, $v_{\rm sys}=41.2\:$km s$^{-1}$" (The S284p1 outflow); "since we have excluded the innermost $\pm1\:$km/s about the systemic velocity" (The S284p1 outflow)
   - assumption: Excitation temperature 20 K; 12CO abundance 0.5e-4 relative to H2  
  > "We assume a constant excitation temperature of 20 K and a $^{12}$CO abundance of $\rm 0.5 \times 10^{-4}$ with respect to $\mbox{$\rm H_2$}$" (The S284p1 outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with swept-up core mass expected from best-fit SED model  
  > "the approximate agreement between the observed and theoretical mass estimates of the outflow lobe suggests further support for the core accretion model" (The S284p1 outflow)
10. **compare outflow mass with model swept-up mass and test outflow symmetry**: Swept-up fraction 1 - cos(theta_w,esc) of one hemisphere of the initial core; outflow axis offsets and length asymmetry; acceleration needed to explain offsets  
  > "Thus the fraction of one hemisphere of the initial core mass that is swept-up by one outflow cavity is $1-{\rm cos}\:\theta_{\rm w, esc} \rightarrow 0.15$ (assuming a spherical core)." (The S284p1 outflow)
   - assumption: Spherical core; theta_w,esc = 32 deg for Case 3 in this step  
  > "For case 3, the half opening angle of the outflow cavity is estimated to be $\theta_{\rm w,esc}=32^\circ$." (The S284p1 outflow)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Systematic uncertainties of at least a factor of two acknowledged  
  > "Although the systematic uncertainties are at a level of at least a factor of two" (The S284p1 outflow)

## Qualitative findings

- Most dense cores in S284-FIR1 are associated with molecular outflows, many highly collimated  
  > "we see that most of these cores are associated with molecular outflows, often exhibiting highly collimated morphologies." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
- Core c1 fragments into c1a (host of S284p1 and the main outflow) and c1b (drives a second, near-orthogonal outflow)  
  > "we identify c1a as the host for the driving source for the large-scale bipolar outflow." (Location of the protostar and its immediate environment)
- H2 4.7 um outflow is symmetric and among the longest ever seen around a massive protostar in H2  
  > "This is among the longest outflow structures associated with a massive protostar ever observed via molecular hydrogen (cite: Bally16)." (Location of the protostar and its immediate environment)
- CO emission is weaker in the western lobe, possibly due to stronger FUV and photodissociation  
  > "The relative lack of CO emission on the western side may indicate a stronger FUV radiation field in this location" (The S284p1 outflow)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 6 data designed to detect dust continuum from dense cores and line emission tracing dense gas and outflows  
  > "these observations are designed to detect millimeter continuum dust emission from dense cores, as well as molecular line emission from various species tracing both dense gas and protostellar outflows." (LZ-STAR Survey Overview)
- Why this method: astrodendro parameters chosen as a compromise between completeness and robustness  
  > "These choices represent a reasonable compromise between completeness and the robustness of source identification (cite: Cheng18)" (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
- Alternative: Dust temperatures of 10 K or 30 K for core masses (factors 1.85 and 0.677)  
  > "If temperatures of 10 K or 30 K were to be adopted, then the mass estimates would differ by factors of 1.85 and 0.677, respectively." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
- Alternative: Three SED fitting cases: solar grid, half-solar grid, and half-solar grid with JWST fluxes (fiducial)  
  > "In summary, to investigate the sensitivity of the results to the choice of SED fitting method, we examine three cases:" (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
- Alternative: Competitive accretion expected to give a disordered outflow history; judged less consistent with the symmetric outflow  
  > "In contrast, in competitive accretion models (cite: 2001MNRAS.323..785B; 2022MNRAS.512..216G) the accretion (and thus outflow) history is expected to be much more disordered" (Implications of the S284p1 outflow geometry for massive star formation theories)
- Alternative: Outflow momentum flux not measured because of the near plane-of-sky orientation  
  > "Thus, we do not attempt here to measure the momentum flux of the outflow." (The S284p1 outflow)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Core mass function not well constrained from the limited number of cores  
  > "Due to the limited number of cores, the functional form of the distribution is not well constrained." (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
- Limitation (authors): Dendrogram fluxes and areas may be underestimated  
  > "Also note that the flux densities and areas returned by the dendrogram may be underestimated due to isophotal cutoffs" (ALMA 1.3 mm continuum)
- Limitation (authors): SED fitting degeneracies; single-dominant-source assumption  
  > "SED fitting is prone to significant degeneracies." (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
- Limitation (authors): CO outflow mass possibly underestimated by a few Msun  
  > "The total may be underestimated by a few solar masses" (The S284p1 outflow)
- Limitation (authors): Core c1 mass relies on an uncertain 20 K dust temperature  
  > "(but based on the uncertain assumption of a dust temperature of 20 K)" (Properties of S284p1 from SED fitting)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Theta_w,esc is 29 +/- 10 deg in the text but 32 deg is used for the swept-up mass comparison; the Table 1 value is not checked against either
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 15 arcsec SED aperture includes the whole c1 core and neighbouring sources, so single-source assumption is hard to verify
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CO outflow mass uses a single Tex = 20 K and an assumed half-canonical CO abundance, optically thin; likely a lower limit and uncertain by more than the quoted factor of two
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Agreement between 13 Msun CO mass and 9 Msun swept-up mass is weak support given the stated uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Acknowledgments print ALMA project 2017.1.00552.S while the archive links 2017.1.01552.S, and the methods never describe it
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Core masses depend on one assumed temperature (20 K) for all cores, including protostellar ones
- Caveat (inferred, ours): New RT model grid is unpublished, so the headline protostellar properties cannot be independently reproduced
