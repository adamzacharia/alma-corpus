## Analysis recipe

1. **Prepare stellar-continuum-subtracted PAH maps**: Adopt continuum-subtracted F335M and F770W maps; F335M uses F300M and F360M, while F770W subtracts scaled F200W or F300M  
  > "For F335M, we use continuum-subtracted maps from H. Koziol et al. (in prep.)" (JWST mid-IR imaging of PAH emission); "where the stellar continuum is estimated using F300M and F360M, based on the method of (cite: sandstrom23_33pah)." (JWST mid-IR imaging of PAH emission); "obtained by subtracting the flux in F200W (Cycle 1) or F300M (Cycle 2) scaled by a factor determined using the prescription of (cite: sutter24)." (JWST mid-IR imaging of PAH emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Match spatial resolution and sampling**: Convolve JWST images to the CO resolution with circular Gaussian target PSFs, regrid to CO pixels, then sample CO and continuum-subtracted F770W at one CO-beam spacing  
  > "First, the higher-resolution JWST observations are convolved to match the coarser resolution of the CO maps." (Convolution and homogenization to a common pixel grid); "Then, the convolved images are re-gridded to align with the pixel grid of the CO data." (Convolution and homogenization to a common pixel grid); "We perform outlier detection using CO and stellar continuum subtracted F770W maps that has been resampled on a gird with one CO-beam spacing to reduce inter-sample covariance." (Outlier detection)
   - software: jwst_kernels
   - parameter: Circular Gaussian target PSF

## Qualitative findings

- Outliers are localized along bar lanes and centers without Hα signs of massive star formation; NGC1566 also has spiral-arm outliers  
  > "are spatially localized along bar lanes and centers without signs of massive star formation detected in H$\alpha$." (Outlier detection); "NGC 1566 is the only galaxy where we find such regions at the edges of spiral arms." (Outlier detection)
- CO excess persists across PAH and dust-continuum bands, disfavoring selective 7.7μm suppression  
  > "The significant enhancement across multiple bands indicates that 7.7$\mu$m is not particularly suppressed" (CO-to-mid-IR ratio using other PAH and dust continuum emission); "The outlier regions identified in CO-to-7.7$\mu$m PAH ratio (red) consistently show elevated ratios across all PAH and continuum bands, with the largest excess observed at 3.3$\mu$m and the smallest at 10$\mu$m." (CO-to-mid-IR ratio using other PAH and dust continuum emission)
- No significant outlier–control difference in RPAH  
  > "The fraction of dust mass in PAHs, $R_{\mathrm{PAH}}$, estimated from $(I_{\mathrm{7.7\mu m}}+I_{\mathrm{11.3\mu m}})/I_{\mathrm{21\mu m}}$, shows no significant difference between outlier and null-test regions." (Difference in PAH properties)
- PAH-size and radiation-hardness differences lack a consistent trend  
  > "While some outlier regions show hints of altered PAH sizes or hardness of the radiation field, these trends are not consistent across systems." (Difference in PAH properties)
- Velocity-dependent αCO reduces the outlier–control offset in inferred molecular-gas-to-PAH distributions  
  > "the large offset observed between the outlier and null-test regions in $I_{\mathrm{CO}}/I_{\mathrm{CO}}^{\mathrm{7.7\mu m}}$ distribution decreases when $\Sigma_{\rm{H_{2}}}=I_{\mathrm{CO}}\,\alpha_{\rm {CO}}$ is used." (CO velocity dispersion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Use the same 70-galaxy JWST–ALMA sample with existing CO–7.7μm scaling relations  
  > "We focus on the same 70 galaxies from (cite: chown24) for which scaling relations between CO and 7.7$\mu$m PAH emission were derived using high-resolution JWST imaging with the F770W filter and ALMA CO observations." (Observational Data)
- Why this method: Beam-spaced sampling reduces covariance; nearby controls match dynamical environment and radius  
  > "resampled on a gird with one CO-beam spacing to reduce inter-sample covariance." (Outlier detection); "In practice, we select null-test regions near the outlier regions within the same dynamical structure, which closely matches the local environment and the galactocentric radius." (Outlier detection)
- Alternative: Low CO/PAH outliers with ICO/ICO^7.7μm < 0.1 were sought but not identified  
  > "Using the same procedure, we searched for low CO-to-PAH ratio outliers $I_{\mathrm{CO}}/I_{\mathrm{CO}}^{\mathrm{7.7\mu m}}<0.1$" (Outlier detection); "We did not identify such regions, likely because the CO data is too shallow and noisy to establish a significant deficit relative to the PAH emission." (Outlier detection)
- Alternative: Simultaneous suppression of PAH and dust emission by shielding is considered unlikely  
  > "making simultaneous suppression of PAH and dust emission, for example due to shielding, an unlikely explanation for the elevated CO/PAH ratios." (CO-to-mid-IR ratio using other PAH and dust continuum emission)
- Alternative: Variable αCO produces greater outlier–control ΣH2 overlap than constant Galactic αCO  
  > "when $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ prescription from (cite: teng24) is used, $\Sigma_{\mathrm{H_{2}}}$ between outlier and null-test regions overlaps much more significantly than when a constant Galactic" (CO velocity dispersion)
- Alternative: Metallicity variations are considered an unlikely driver of the most extreme outliers  
  > "making metallicity variations an unlikely driver of the most extreme CO/PAH outliers." (Outlier detection)
- How they decided it was enough: Adopt regional significance ≥10 for significant and obvious outliers; check flux-ratio cuts from 8 to 12  
  > "Throughout the paper, we adopt a more conservative threshold $\bar{\Delta}/\sigma_{\bar{\Delta}}\ge10$ to focus on significant and obvious outliers." (Outlier detection); "As an additional robustness check, we varied the flux ratio criterion applied to beam samples from $I_{\mathrm{CO}}/I_{\mathrm{CO}}^{\mathrm{7.7\mu m}}>8$ to $>12$." (Outlier detection); "Nonetheless, the key qualitative trends and our main conclusions remain unchanged." (Outlier detection)
- Limitation (authors): CO data are too shallow and noisy to establish low CO/PAH deficits  
  > "We did not identify such regions, likely because the CO data is too shallow and noisy to establish a significant deficit relative to the PAH emission." (Outlier detection)
- Limitation (authors): PAH ratios are degenerate with multiple physical effects and require additional spectroscopy  
  > "As the PAH ratios explored here are degenerate with multiple physical effects" (Difference in PAH properties); "additional spectroscopic tracers (e.g., H$_2$ rotational and rovibrational lines, \[Fe II\] for shocks, and a wider suite of PAH features) will be essential to further investigate whether PAH emission is suppressed in these regions." (Difference in PAH properties)
- Limitation (authors): Unresolved central velocity gradients can inflate measured CO dispersions  
  > "We note that the measured $\Delta v_{\mathrm{CO}}$ can be inflated by unresolved velocity gradients in the galaxy centers where large-scale rotational shear is steep." (CO velocity dispersion)
- Limitation (authors): Measured dispersion enhancement does not fully explain the outliers through emissivity alone  
  > "While the outlier regions are associated with higher velocity dispersion, it appears it should be even higher (three times more) to explain these outliers from enhanced emissivity." (CO velocity dispersion)
- Limitation (authors): Available 13CO data do not detect the relevant regions in NGC3627 and NGC4321  
  > "the current available data are not deep enough to detect $^{13}$CO in the regions of interest for NGC 3627 and NGC 4321." (CO excitation from multi-transition lines and CO isotopologues)
- Limitation (authors): The enhanced-emissivity interpretation requires deep CO isotopologue and multi-transition confirmation  
  > "this interpretation requires confirmation through deep observations of CO isotopologues and multi-transitions that can directly constrain variations in optical depth and excitation." (Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Applying a dispersion-based αCO prescription makes the inferred mass comparison dependent on the same dispersion enhancement used to support the emissivity interpretation.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Regional significance may depend on covariance from shared fit parameters; the regional combination does not specify its treatment.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Control-region selection includes qualitative judgments and lacks reproducible masks or quantitative matching tolerances.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Direct isotopologue evidence is limited to NGC3351 and does not establish the mechanism across all 20 hosts.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The narrative area-fraction range of 0.03-7% differs from the displayed Table 1 range of 0.04-7.43%.
