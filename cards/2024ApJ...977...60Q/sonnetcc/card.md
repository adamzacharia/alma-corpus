## Analysis recipe

1. **Build thin and thick VIRaM parametric disk models**: Power-law surface density with exponential taper; hydrostatic vertical density structure from the ideal gas law and a prescribed T(r,z); thin model uses the Dartois+2003 analytic profile, thick model is isothermal to 2H then cos^3 to 6H; CO in a layer bounded by photodissociation and 24 K freeze-out  
  > "the vertical pressure profile is obtained by integrating the hydrostatic equilibrium equation." (Disk Model Prescriptions); "The two models differ only in their prescribed vertical temperature structures" (Disk Model Prescriptions); "we developed two disk models: one with a thick VIRaM layer and another with a thin one." (Disk Model Prescriptions)
   - parameter: M* = 2 Msun; Mgas = 0.04 Msun  
  > "Stellar Mass ($M_*$) | $2 \, M_{\odot}$" (Table 1); "Disk Gas Mass ($M_{\text{gas}}$) | $0.04 \, M_{\odot}$" (Table 1)
   - parameter: r_in = 1 au; r_c = 150 au; gamma = 0.9  
  > "Inner Radius ($r_{\text{in}}$) | 1 au" (Table 1); "Characteristic Radius ($r_c$) | 150 au" (Table 1); "Surface Density Gradient ($\gamma$) | 0.9" (Table 1)
   - parameter: T_mid = 24 (R/80 au)^-0.4; T_atm = 700 (R/1 au)^-0.55  
  > "Midplane Temperature Profile ($T_{\text{mid}}$) | $24 \left( \frac{R}{80\, \text{au}} \right)^{-0.4}$" (Table 1); "Atmosphere Temperature Profile ($T_{\text{atm}}$) | $700 \left( \frac{R}{1\, \text{au}} \right)^{-0.55}$" (Table 1)
   - assumption: CO freeze-out temperature 24 K  
  > "For both models, the CO freeze-out temperature was assumed to be 24 K." (Disk Model Prescriptions)
   - assumption: Constant CO abundance 1e-4 in the emitting layer  
  > "We assume CO emission originates in a vertical layer of the disk with a constant abundance $f_{\mathrm{CO}} = 1 \times 10^{-4}$." (Disk Model Prescriptions)
   - assumption: CO photodissociation above H2 column of ~1e21 cm^-2  
  > "which occurs above an H$_2$ surface column density of approximately $10^{21} \, \text{cm}^{-2}$" (Disk Model Prescriptions)
   - assumption: Depletion factor 320 below 24 K (CO abundance 3.1e-7)  
  > "When the temperature drops below 24 K, the depletion factor" (Disk Model Prescriptions); "is set to 320. In such regions, the CO abundance decreases to $3.1 \times 10^{-7}$." (Disk Model Prescriptions)
   - assumption: Distance 100 pc (snowline at 80 au = 0.8'')  
  > "Adopting a distance of 100 pc, the snowline location lies at" (Disk Model Prescriptions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Simulate ALMA and ngVLA observations of C17O and C18O**: RATRAN non-LTE radiative transfer with ISM isotope ratios, converted to visibilities with simobserve (ALMA cycle 7.5 config for 2-1 lines, ngVLA core config for 1-0 lines), thermal noise added, imaged with tclean, collapsed to integrated intensity maps with bettermoments  
  > "then converted into synthetic visibility datasets using the `simobserve` task in the *Common Astronomy Software Application* package" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "then corrupted the simulated data using the `sm.setnoise` function of the `sm` toolkit" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "Finally, we collapsed the spectral cubes into integrated intensity maps using the Python package `bettermoments` (cite: Teague+2018)." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - software: RATRAN, CASA (simobserve, sm.setnoise, tclean), bettermoments
   - parameter: Briggs weighting, robust = 2  
  > "Imaging was performed using the `tclean` task in CASA with Briggs weighting and a robust parameter of $2$, i.e. close to natural weighting for maximum sensitivity." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - parameter: 60 channels of 0.5 km/s per line  
  > "The simulations were conducted with 60 velocity channels of width 0.5 km $^{-1}$ for each line." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - parameter: Declination -22 deg; inclination 45 deg; position angle 90 deg  
  > "The source declination was assumed to be $-22$ degrees." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "The disk was assumed to be inclined by $45$ degrees oriented at a position angle of $90$ degrees (east from north)." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - parameter: ALMA 2 h with alma.cycle7.5.cfg; ngVLA 4 h with ngvla-revD.core.cfg  
  > "The simulated observations had an on-source integration time of 2 hours, aligning with the observations from the ALMA MAPS Large Program" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "The simulated observations had an on-source integration time of 4 hours for ngVLA simulations." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - assumption: Local ISM ratios CO/C17O = 2005 and CO/C18O = 557  
  > "ratios of CO/C$^\mathrm{17}$O = 2005 and CO/C$^\mathrm{18}$O = 557 (cite: Wilson1999)" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Extract radial profiles and locate the snowline from the derivative**: Deprojected, azimuthally averaged radial profiles of integrated intensity with GoFish; a sharp snowline appears as a local minimum in the radial derivative if the emission is optically thin  
  > "We extracted the deprojected and azimuthally averaged radial intensity profiles using the `radial_profile` function from the Python package GoFish (cite: Teague2019)." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "If the disk has a thick VIRaM layer, then the resulting sharp CO snowline will present a local minimum in the derivative, provided the emission is optically thin." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
   - software: GoFish
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared thick versus thin VIRaM models: clear derivative minimum near the snowline only for the thick model in the J=1-0 lines  
  > "The derivatives for the thick VIRaM layer model show a clear minimum near the CO snowline for the J=1–0 lines" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "The minimum is less prominent in the C$^{17}$O 2-1 profile and is not visible at all in the C$^{18}$O 2-1 profile" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
4. **Apply the derivative method to archival HD 163296 ALMA data**: Radial profiles of integrated intensity and their derivatives for C17O 1-0, C18O 1-0, C18O 2-1 (MAPS) and C17O 2-1 (2016.1.00884.S); look for a local minimum near the known CO snowline  
  > "We examined archival ALMA observations of $\mathrm{C^{17}O}$ and $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 1-0 and $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 2-1 lines in Bands 3 and 6" (ALMA Observations of CO Isotopologues Toward HD 163296); "The $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 2-1 emission, being optically thick, is not suitable for this analysis" (ALMA Observations of CO Isotopologues Toward HD 163296)
   - assumption: Distance of 101 pc  
  > "near $\sim80$ au ($0.8\ensuremath{''}$ at 101 pc)" (abstract)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Derivative minima compared with the CO snowline location determined by Qi+2015  
  > "all display a local minimum near the CO snowline location determined by (cite: Qi+2015)" (ALMA Observations of CO Isotopologues Toward HD 163296)
5. **Test C18O 1-0 derivative profiles in other MAPS disks**: C18O 1-0 radial profiles and derivatives at 0.3'' beam from archival MAPS images for GM Aur, AS 209, IM Lup and MWC 480  
  > "for these disks – GM Aur, AS 209, IM Lup, and MWC 480 – with 0.3$''$ beam size (archival images from the MAPS Large Program project 2018.1.01055.L)" (C18O 1-0 in Other MAPS Disks)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: GM Aur dip in the derivative aligns with the expected snowline radius; no dip for AS 209 and IM Lup  
  > "a clear dip in the derivative of the $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 1-0 profile aligns with the expected snowline radius" (C18O 1-0 in Other MAPS Disks); "no evident dip can be seen in the $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 1-0 derivative profiles" (C18O 1-0 in Other MAPS Disks)
6. **Constrain the CO depletion factor with RATRAN models**: RATRAN non-LTE models following Qi+2015, sampled with vis_sample at the ALMA uv coverage, radial profiles with GoFish radial_profile, flux profiles normalized at 0.65'' and compared with observed profiles for a grid of depletion factors  
  > "We followed the methodology of (cite: Qi+2015), utilizing the RATRAN code to perform non-local thermodynamic equilibrium radiative transfer calculations and to generate synthetic CO data cubes." (CO Depletion Factor); "we normalized the flux profiles at a reference point near the snowline transition, specifically at 0.65$''$" (CO Depletion Factor)
   - software: RATRAN, vis_sample, GoFish
   - parameter: Depletion factor grid: coarse logarithmic grid from 0.5 to 3.0 in steps of 0.5 (df = 5 and df = 10^2.5 = 320 shown in Figure 6)  
  > "we explored models with depletion factors only, spanning a coarse logarithmic grid from 0.5 to 3.0 in steps of 0.5" (CO Depletion Factor)
   - assumption: Snowline location fixed from the derivative minima; only depletion factor varied  
  > "Since the CO snowline location has already been identified through the local minima in the derivatives of the emission profiles" (CO Depletion Factor)
   - fitting: Grid in depletion factor with visual comparison of normalized profiles; no sampler, priors or likelihood stated  
  > "we explored models with depletion factors only, spanning a coarse logarithmic grid from 0.5 to 3.0 in steps of 0.5" (CO Depletion Factor)
   - validation: df = 320 matches the observed normalized profiles better than df = 5  
  > "The model with a depletion factor of 320 matches the data more closely than the model with a depletion factor of 5" (CO Depletion Factor)
7. **Compare CO column density models and optical depth thresholds**: Compare radial CO column density profiles of the df = 5, df = 320 and MAPS models; mark tau = 1 column densities from RADEX; compare normalized C18O 1-0 versus C17O 1-0 profiles  
  > "as calculated using the RADEX code (cite: vanderTak+2007) and assuming interstellar medium ratios of CO/C$^{17}$O = 2005 and CO/C$^{18}$O = 557" (Comparison of HD 163296 CO Distribution Models); "shows the radial profile of the integrated flux of C$^{18}$O 1-0 compared to C$^{17}$O 1-0, normalized at 70 au to more clearly show the difference in profile shape" (Comparison of HD 163296 CO Distribution Models)
   - software: RADEX
   - assumption: ISM ratios CO/C17O = 2005 and CO/C18O = 557  
  > "assuming interstellar medium ratios of CO/C$^{17}$O = 2005 and CO/C$^{18}$O = 557" (Comparison of HD 163296 CO Distribution Models)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Steeper decline of C17O than C18O 1-0 matches the df = 5 and df = 320 models, not the MAPS model  
  > "The steeper decline of emission from C$^{17}$O compared to C$^{18}$O aligns with the predictions of the models with $df=5$ and $df=320$" (Comparison of HD 163296 CO Distribution Models)
8. **Test the effect of thermal structure on disk gas mass estimates**: Two models (thin VIRaM, 0.01 Msun; thick VIRaM, 0.04 Msun); compare CO column density profiles and C17O 1-0 integrated spectra at 1.5'' resolution  
  > "Model 1, with a thin VIRaM layer and a disk mass of 0.01 M$_\odot$, and Model 2, with a thick VIRaM layer and a disk mass of 0.04 M$_\odot$" (Effect on Disk Mass Estimates); "presents the C$^{17}$O 1-0 spectra for these models at $1.5\ensuremath{''}$ resolution, generated using the `integrated_spectrum` function from the Python package `GoFish` (cite: Teague2019)" (Effect on Disk Mass Estimates)
   - software: GoFish
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- C17O 1-0, C18O 1-0 and C17O 2-1 derivative profiles of HD 163296 show a local minimum near the CO snowline; optically thick C18O 2-1 is unsuitable  
  > "The derivatives of $\mathrm{C^{17}O}$ 1-0, $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 1-0, and $\mathrm{C^{17}O}$ 2-1 all display a local minimum near the CO snowline location determined by (cite: Qi+2015)." (ALMA Observations of CO Isotopologues Toward HD 163296)
- In the toy models, the derivative minimum is clearest for the optically thin ground-state lines and absent in C18O 2-1  
  > "The minimum is less prominent in the C$^{17}$O 2-1 profile and is not visible at all in the C$^{18}$O 2-1 profile" (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations)
- A thick VIRaM layer can lower the integrated C17O 1-0 spectrum despite a four times higher disk mass  
  > "Despite Model 2 having a higher CO column density inside the CO snowline, its spectrum is lower than that of Model 1" (Effect on Disk Mass Estimates)
- Gas masses from CO isotopologues can be systematically underestimated because of the sharp snowline thermal structure  
  > "Substantial, systematic, underestimates of the disk gas mass can result from this different thermal structure, even when the depletion due to CO freeze-out is considered." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: C18O is detected toward all MAPS disks, while C17O 1-0 is only marginally detected in some, so C18O 1-0 was used for the other MAPS disks  
  > "is detected toward all of the disks in the MAPS program in addition to HD 163296" (C18O 1-0 in Other MAPS Disks)
- Why this method: Optically thin lines are sensitive to column density changes and high resolution avoids smoothing the profile; the depletion test varied only df because the snowline was already located from the derivatives  
  > "Low optical depth provides intrinsic sensitivity to changes in the CO column density with the radius, while high spatial resolution prevents the shape of the radial profile from being smoothed out." (CO Isotopologue Imaging Simulations); "Since the CO snowline location has already been identified through the local minima in the derivatives of the emission profiles" (CO Depletion Factor)
- Alternative: Optically thick C18O 2-1 rejected as unsuitable for the derivative analysis  
  > "The $\mathrm{C^{18}O}$ 2-1 emission, being optically thick, is not suitable for this analysis" (ALMA Observations of CO Isotopologues Toward HD 163296)
- Alternative: Varying freeze-out temperature and snowline location (as in Qi+2015) was avoided; only the depletion factor was varied, to avoid photodesorption beyond 200 au  
  > "avoiding the need to account for potential CO photodesorption by nonthermal processes beyond 200 au (cite: Salinas+2017)" (CO Depletion Factor)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Disk substructures (rings, gaps) and their temperature effects are not included in the models  
  > "while intentionally omitting the effects of disk substructures, such as rings or gaps, which are not included in our current models." (Disk Model Prescriptions)
- Limitation (authors): Absence of a dip in AS 209 and IM Lup may be due to beam smoothing of small snowlines  
  > "The absence of this feature could result from CO snowlines that are comparable to, or smaller than, the 0.3$''$ beam size" (C18O 1-0 in Other MAPS Disks)
- Limitation (authors): Depletion factor explored only on a coarse logarithmic grid  
  > "spanning a coarse logarithmic grid from 0.5 to 3.0 in steps of 0.5" (CO Depletion Factor)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No quantitative fit, goodness-of-fit statistic or uncertainty on the snowline radius or depletion factor; 'matches more closely' rests on visual comparison of normalized profiles
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Significance of the derivative minima against noise and beam smoothing is not quantified, and the derivative method is not specified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Toy models use one parameter set and a prescribed thermal structure, so the generality of the disk mass bias is not tested
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Depletion-factor conclusion depends on the assumed DIAD thermal structure and fixed snowline; the MAPS model comparison differs in several assumptions simultaneously
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Distance is 100 pc in the models and 101 pc in the abstract; the snowline radius is therefore tied to one distance
