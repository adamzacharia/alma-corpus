## Analysis recipe

1. **Assemble the galaxy and ALMA sample**: Select COSMOS2020 SFGs at 1.2 ≤ z < 1.6; remove AGNs; cross-match A³COSMOS; reject inadequate primary-beam coverage and contaminating close pairs.  
  > "We selected galaxies at 1.2$\leq z$ \<1.6 in `THE FARMER` catalog." (Parent sample and ALMA data); "In addition, we excluded galaxies classified as active galactic nuclei (AGNs) to avoid contamination." (Parent sample and ALMA data); "We cross-matched our parent sample with the A$^{3}$COSMOS database, and excluded galaxies that were not covered by any ALMA observations, i.e., those located in regions with a primary beam response under 0.5." (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - parameter: Rest-frame near-ultraviolet-r/r-J SFG selection  
  > "we classified galaxies into star-forming and quiescent galaxies following the standard rest-frame near-ultraviolet-$r$/$r$-$J$ selection method from (cite: 2013A&A...556A..55I) and selected only the SFGs." (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - parameter: Exclude pairs <2.0 arcsec with SALMA1/SALMA2 > 2 or Mstar1/Mstar2 > 3  
  > "we excluded galaxy pairs ($<2.0\ensuremath{''}$) with $S_{\rm ALMA}^1/S_{\rm ALMA}^2>2$ or $M_{\star}^1/M_{\star}^2>3$" (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - parameter: Final ALMA-covered sample: 1,688 galaxies; science analysis mass-complete above 10^9.5 M⊙  
  > "There are 1,688 galaxies in this final sample." (Parent sample and ALMA data); "We investigated the molecular gas mass properties of a mass-complete sample (\>10$^{9.5}M_{\odot}$) of SFGs at 1.2 $\leq z$ \< 1.6," (Summary)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Determine SFRs and main-sequence offsets**: Use UV+IR SFRs with the best available MIR/FIR luminosity indicator; use COSMOS2020 SFRs without MIR/FIR photometry; calculate ΔMS using the Leslie et al. MS calibration.  
  > "we used the best infrared luminosity indicator available for each galaxy." (Parent sample and ALMA data); "For galaxies without any MIR or FIR photometry, their SFRs were taken directly from the COSMOS2020 catalog." (Parent sample and ALMA data); "using the MS calibration of (cite: 2020ApJ...899...58L)." (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - parameter: SFRUV+IR [M⊙ yr−1] = 1.09 × 10−10 (LIR [L⊙] + 3.3 × LUV [L⊙])  
  > "{\rm SFR}_{\rm UV+IR}[{\rm M_{\odot}\,yr^{-1}}]=1.09 \times 10^{-10} (L_{\rm IR}[{\rm L_{\odot}}]+3.3 \times L_{\rm UV}[{\rm L_{\odot}}])," (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - parameter: LUV at rest-frame 2300 Å; LIR over 8–1000 μm  
  > "the rest-frame $L_{\rm UV}$ at 2300$\AA$ was taken from the COSMOS2020 catalog, and the rest-frame $L_{\rm IR}$ = $L({\rm 8-1000 \,\mu m})$" (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - parameter: ΔMS = log(SFR(z,Mstar)/SFRMS(z,Mstar)); subdivide by stellar mass and ΔMS  
  > "$\Delta$MS = ${\rm log(SFR}(z,M_{\star})/{\rm SFR}_{\rm MS}(z,M_{\star}))$" (Parent sample and ALMA data); "These SFGs were then subdivided into different stellar mass and $\Delta$MS bins." (Introduction)
   - assumption: Chabrier initial mass function for stellar masses and SFRs  
  > "A (cite: 2003PASP..115..763C) initial mass function is assumed for all stellar masses and SFRs." (Introduction)
   - fitting: Fit MIR and/or FIR SEDs with the Chary & Elbaz template; details deferred to Wang et al. Sect. 2.2.  
  > "their $L_{\rm IR}$ was obtained by fitting their MIR and/or FIR SED with the template of (cite: 2001ApJ...556..562C) (see details in Sect. 2.2 of (cite: 2022A&A...660A.142W))." (Parent sample and ALMA data)
   - validation: Compare the galaxy-number-density MS peak to the adopted MS: average offset 0.02 dex; no stellar-mass-bin offset greater than 0.2 dex.  
  > "we then defined the peak of the number density of galaxies at each stellar mass" (Parent sample and ALMA data); "found an average offset of 0.02 dex and no offset greater than 0.2 dex in any of our specific stellar mass bins." (Parent sample and ALMA data)
3. **Scale and stack calibrated visibilities**: Scale visibility amplitudes to rest-frame 850 μm luminosity; shift each galaxy to its coordinates; concatenate galaxies within each stellar-mass–ΔMS bin.  
  > "We first scaled the observed ALMA visibility amplitudes of each galaxy to its rest-frame luminosity at 850 $\mu$m" (The stacking analysis); "For each galaxy, we then shifted the phase center of its visibilities to its coordinates by using a CASA based package: the `STACKER` (cite: 2015MNRAS.446.3502L)." (The stacking analysis); "The visibilities of the galaxies in each $M_{\star}$–$\Delta$MS bin were then stacked together using the CASA task `concat`." (The stacking analysis)
   - software: CASA, gencal, applycal, STACKER, concat
   - parameter: MS template for ΔMS < 0.4; SB template for ΔMS > 0.7; log-space linear interpolation for 0.4 < ΔMS < 0.7  
  > "for a galaxy with $\Delta$MS \<0.4, we kept the scaling obtained with the MS galaxy SED template;" (The stacking analysis); "for a galaxy with $\Delta$MS \> 0.7, we kept that measured with the SB galaxy SED template;" (The stacking analysis); "for a galaxy with 0.4 \< $\Delta$MS \< 0.7, we used a linear interpolation in the log-space between the MS and SB galaxy SED template scalings." (The stacking analysis)
   - assumption: Béthermin et al. MS and SB SED templates  
  > "In practice, this scaling was performed using both the MS and SB galaxy SED templates of (cite: 2012ApJ...757L..23B)." (The stacking analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeat detected-source stacks using A³COSMOS positions instead of COSMOS2020 positions; luminosities and sizes change at most 5%.  
  > "repeating our stacking analysis but limiting it to the source individually detected by ALMA and using their A$^{3}$COSMOS position instead of their COSMOS2020 position." ## Qualitative findings

- Both gas content and star formation efficiency variations contribute to movement across the MS.  
  > "Our findings indicate that the change of gas content and star formation efficiency play again roughly a similar role when SFGs oscillate within the MS." (Molecular gas fraction and depletion time of SFGs)
- Mean molecular gas depletion time does not change with stellar mass.  
  > "their mean molecular gas depletion time does not change with stellar mass." (Molecular gas fraction and depletion time of SFGs)
- The MS bending is consistent with reduced cold gas accretion rather than changing star formation efficiency.  
  > "This finding could be a hint that the bending of the MS at $z\sim$1.4 is primarily driven by variations in cold gas accretion." (abstract)
- SBs tend to have merger-like morphology; MS galaxies have disk-like morphology.  
  > "Starbursts tend to have merger-like morphology, while MS galaxies have disk-like morphology, as revealed by their Gini-M20 coefficient of their optical *HST* $i$-band images." (Summary)
- At Mstar > 10^10.5 M⊙, optical sizes decrease from HST i to UltraVISTA J to Ks.  
  > "The measured optical size decreases with wavelength for $M_{\star}$ \> 10$^{10.5}M_{\odot}$, with *HST* $i$-band \> UltraVISTA $J$-band \> UltraVISTA $K_{\rm s}$-band." (Summary)
- Dust-corrected MS stellar mass sizes are similar to star-forming sizes.  
  > "the size of the stellar component of MS galaxies is similar to that of their star-forming component." (Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The redshift range yielded the highest-significance stacks in earlier work, enabling subdivision by ΔMS; A³COSMOS samples dust RJ-tail luminosity.  
  > "This redshift range was chosen as it yields detection with the highest significance level in (cite: 2022A&A...660A.142W), i.e., we can further dissect the mean molecular gas mass of the SFGs in different $\Delta$MS." (Introduction); "making it ideal for studying the dust RJ-tail luminosity emitted by galaxies, which is a proxy of their molecular gas content." (Parent sample and ALMA data)
- Why this method: Stacking measures population means where individual galaxies are difficult to detect; ALMA frequency/resolution heterogeneity motivates uv-domain processing.  
  > "galaxies at high redshift, low stellar mass, and low $\Delta$MS remain difficult to detect individually with ALMA" (Method); "To statistically measure the mean value of our mass-complete sample of SFGs at $z\sim$1.4, we therefore used a stacking analysis." (Method); "stacking the A$^3$COSMOS dataset is a challenge because the different ALMA projects were observed at different frequencies and spatial resolutions (cite: 2022A&A...660A.142W)." (Method)
- Alternative: Use point-source models where elliptical-Gaussian fits become unsuitable.  
  > "a fit with an elliptical Gaussian component with `uvmodelfit` led to an axis ratio with a value close to 0." (Molecular gas content and star-forming size); "For these bins, we simply fit a point-source model" (Molecular gas content and star-forming size)

(Uncertainties from the mismatch of position and selection of astrometry); "at most 5$\%,$ which is relatively little compared to our uncertainties." (Uncertainties from the mismatch of position and selection of astrometry)

- Limitation (authors): Stacking erases merger irregularities; surviving SB irregularities depend on small-number statistics.  
  > "the stacking analysis naturally erases the irregularity of the merging system and thus decreases their Gini and increases their M20 values." - Limitation (authors): The adopted half-light-to-half-mass correction is not specifically calibrated for SBs.  
  > "using the relation of (cite: 2019ApJ...877..103S) to correct half-light size to half-stellar mass size is probably inappropriate as this relation has not been calibrated specifically for SBs." (Stellar population size and morphology)
- Limitation (authors): Morphology analysis excludes galaxies below 10^10 M⊙ and HST-undetected galaxies.  
  > "we did not measure the morphology of galaxies with $M_{\star}$ \< $10^{10}M_{\odot}$."
