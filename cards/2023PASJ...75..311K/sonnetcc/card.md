## Analysis recipe

1. **Detect and subtract compact millimeter sources**: Fit compact sources in the uv plane with uvmodelfit using only baselines longer than 15 klambda, all channels fitted together excluding line-affected ranges; subtract sources detected above 5 sigma from the whole visibility data  
  > "First, we identified compact sources in the observing field using only the baselines longer than 15k$\lambda$; the position and the flux density were determined in the $uv$ plane by the CASA task *uvmodelfit*." (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3); "were subtracted from the entire visibility data" (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3)
   - software: CASA, uvmodelfit
   - parameter: baselines > 15 klambda  
  > "using only the baselines longer than 15k$\lambda$" (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3)
   - parameter: detection threshold 5 sigma  
  > "All the compact sources detected at $>5\sigma$" (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3)
   - assumption: sources are point-like  
  > "All the detected sources are consistent with being point-like" (3.1.1 RCS J2319+0038)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals at long baselines consistent with noise; fitting channels together vs separately changes residual by less than 1/5 of noise for C1  
  > "the residuals at long baselines are consistent with noise" (3.1.1 RCS J2319+0038); "We checked that this simplification has no apparent effect on the results of the present paper." (Footnote 4)
2. **Image the SZE and estimate noise**: Multi-Scale CLEAN with tclean in joint mosaic imaging of 12-m and 7-m data; noise measured on difference maps from time-split visibilities; images smoothed to 5'' for display  
  > "we performed image deconvolution with the Multi-Scale CLEAN algorithm (cite: Cornwell08; Rich08; Steeb19) using the CASA task *tclean*." (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: mask radius 50'', threshold 0.01 mJy, scales [0,4,8,16,32,64]'', natural weighting, 0.5'' pixels  
  > "we adopted a circular mask region with a radius $50''$ and a flux threshold of 0.01 mJy" (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Noise estimated from difference maps made by splitting the visibilities in time, flipping the sign of one half, concatenating and imaging  
  > "the visibility data was divided into the first and the second half along the time sequence of observations, the sign of the latter was flipped, concatenated with the former, and the resultant was inverse Fourier transformed into the image domain." (2.1 Millimeter: ALMA Band 3)
3. **Fit SZE and X-ray morphology**: Elliptical Gaussian fit to the unsmoothed ALMA image with imfit; elliptical beta model fit to the unsmoothed Chandra image with Sherpa beta2d; integrated SZE flux within 45''  
  > "we fit the unsmoothed ALMA image with an elliptical Gaussian using the CASA task *imfit*" (3.2.1 RCS J2319+0038); "We used the Sherpa modeling package (*beta2d*) in CIAO (cite: Sherpa01; Sherpa07; Sherpa09) for this purpose." (3.2.1 RCS J2319+0038)
   - software: CASA, imfit, CIAO, Sherpa
   - parameter: Gaussian free parameters: center, major and minor axis FWHM, position angle; beta model parameters x0, y0, S0, beta, theta_c, q, psi  
  > "varying its center, major and minor axis FWHMs, and the position angle[^11]" (3.2.1 RCS J2319+0038)
   - assumption: constant noise within 50'' of field centre for error estimates (Condon97)  
  > "assuming a constant noise level within a radius of $50"$ from the field center" (3.2.1 RCS J2319+0038)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SZE and X-ray emission centres and morphology agree within 1.5''  
  > "the emission center also matches within $1.5''$ between two images" (3.2.1 RCS J2319+0038)
4. **Measure mean X-ray temperature and luminosity**: Fit the 0.4-7.0 keV Chandra spectrum within 45'' of the cluster centre with XSPEC; spectral analysis of Chandra data processed with CIAO 4.13  
  > "Spectral fitting was done with XSPEC version 12.12.0 (cite: Arnaud96)" (2.3 X-ray: Chandra ACIS-S); "yielded the average electron temperature of $kT_{\rm e}=5.90^{+0.79}_{-0.62}$ keV" (4.1.2 Input models)
   - software: CIAO, XSPEC, FTOOLS
   - parameter: energy band 0.4-7.0 keV; background from region theta > 3.2'  
  > "The backgrounds were estimated from the off-center region at $\theta > 3.2'$ from the emission peak of this cluster" (2.3 X-ray: Chandra ACIS-S)
   - assumption: collisional ionization equilibrium, metal abundance 0.3 solar (Anders89), z=0.90, N_H=4.2e20 cm^-2 (HI4PI16), helium mass fraction Y=0.25  
  > "We fixed the helium mass fraction at $Y=0.25$" (2.3 X-ray: Chandra ACIS-S); "assuming that the ICM is in collisional ionization equilibrium and the metal abundance $Z$ is 0.3 times the solar value given by (cite: Anders89)" (2.3 X-ray: Chandra ACIS-S)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Galaxy density and weak-lensing mass**: CAMIRA richness and galaxy density from HSC-SSP; weak-lensing shear with re-Gaussianization, background selection, NFW fit to tangential shear  
  > "The galaxy shapes were measured using the re-Gaussianization method (cite: Hirata03) implemented in the HSC pipeline (cite: Mandelbaum18; Li22)." (2.4 Optical: Subaru Hyper Suprime-Cam); "We adopted the NFW density profile (cite: Navarro96) for estimating the deprojected halo mass, from the tangential shear profiles" (2.4 Optical: Subaru Hyper Suprime-Cam)
   - software: HSC pipeline
   - parameter: surface mass density smoothed by a 30'' FWHM Gaussian for S/N peaks  
  > "after being smoothed by a Gaussian with $30''$ FWHM" (3.3 Galaxy density and weak lensing mass)
   - assumption: NFW profile with concentration tied to mass by Diemer15  
  > "the halo concentration is linked to the mass by the relation of (cite: Diemer15)" (2.4 Optical: Subaru Hyper Suprime-Cam)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Lensing mass peak in agreement with main peak of galaxy concentration, though S/N is only 2.8 and 3.2  
  > "While the significance is low, the peak of the surface mass density is in agreement with the main peak of galaxy concentration." (3.3 Galaxy density and weak lensing mass)
6. **Imaging simulations and missing flux correction**: Build model y maps, create SZE input images in four spectral windows, simulate ALMA visibilities with simobserve (10 noise seeds), deconvolve as for real data, and fit I_out = c1 I_in + c0 binned by input intensity; apply c1 to intensity differences relative to a reference radius  
  > "Visibility data were then produced using the CASA task *simobserve* including both instrumental and atmospheric thermal noise in each spectral window; this procedure was repeated 10 times adopting different noise seeds." (4.1.1 Method); "Each bin contains at least 1000 pixel data after accumulating 10 realizations for each adopted value of $y_{\rm peak}$." (4.1.3 Missing flux correction using simulation results)
   - software: CASA, simobserve, tclean
   - parameter: reference radius theta_bar = 35'' from the cluster centre  
  > "the reference points are taken at the mean radius $\bar{\theta}=35''$ from the cluster center" (4.1.3 Missing flux correction using simulation results)
   - parameter: y_peak = 1.1e-4 (RCS J2319+0038) and 1.5e-4 (HSC J0947-0119), with doubled and halved cases  
  > "to obtain $y_{\rm peak}=1.1\times 10^{-4}$" (4.1.2 Input models)
   - assumption: RCS J2319+0038 gas isothermal at 5.90 keV with triaxial elliptical beta density, h70/eta=1; HSC J0947-0119 McDonald14b pressure profile, kT=9.4 keV, h70=1, eta=1  
  > "Assuming that the gas is isothermal[^13]" (4.1.2 Input models); "$M_{500}=5.7 \times 10^{14}$ M$_\odot$ assuming $h_{70}=1$ and $\eta=1$" (4.1.2 Input models)
   - fitting: Linear relation fit with c1 common across y_peak values for each cluster; fitting method not stated  
  > "assuming that $c_1$ is common among different values of $y_{\rm peak}$ for each cluster" (4.1.3 Missing flux correction using simulation results)

## Qualitative findings

- The ICM of both clusters is elongated (axis ratio 0.7 or below), probably linked to asymmetric galaxy distributions  
  > "The elongated morphology is probably associated with highly asymmetrical galaxy distributions found in these clusters." (Conclusions)
- ALMA SZE centre and Chandra X-ray centre of RCS J2319+0038 coincide with the main galaxy density and weak-lensing peaks, with no ICM concentration at the second galaxy peak  
  > "On the other hand, no concentration of the ICM is detected around the second peak of galaxy density" (3.3 Galaxy density and weak lensing mass)
- ALMA SZE morphology and centroid agree with the Chandra X-ray image for RCS J2319+0038  
  > "Both the centroid position and overall morphology of the ALMA SZE map are in agreement with those of the *Chandra* X-ray brightness image." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The SZE surface brightness is free from (1+z)^-4 dimming and directly measures electron thermal pressure; ALMA gives high angular resolution for deep SZE data on these relatively low-mass clusters  
  > "The SZE brightness is free from the aforementioned $(1+z)^{-4}$ dimming and provides a direct measure of electron thermal pressure." (Introduction); "The present paper therefore makes use of one of the deepest ALMA SZE data obtained so far." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Image-domain, non-parametric reconstruction allowing departures from spherical symmetry; simulation-based missing flux correction  
  > "We develop a method to reconstruct electron pressure and temperature profiles of distant clusters non-parametrically, taking into account departures from spherical symmetry." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Model fitting in the visibility (uv) domain is an alternative approach, planned for future work  
  > "An alternative and complementary approach is to perform model fitting in the visibility ($uv$) domain (e.g., (cite: Basu16; Luca19AA)), which we plan to investigate in our future publication." (Conclusions)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Weak-lensing mass errors are very large due to the limited number of background galaxies  
  > "Note that the errors on $M_{500}$ from weak lensing are very large for these distant clusters, owing to the limited number of background galaxies." (3.3 Galaxy density and weak lensing mass)
- Limitation (authors): No deep X-ray data for HSC J0947-0119, so its centre and gas density peak are unconstrained  
  > "As of this writing, deep X-ray observations for this cluster are unavailable." (Introduction)
- Limitation (authors): Redshift of HSC J0947-0119 is photometric  
  > "Given the lack of accurate spectroscopic measurements" (Introduction)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Pressure and temperature profile conclusions depend on h70 and the line-of-sight elongation eta, which are unconstrained; results are shown only for h70=1, eta=1 (eta degeneracy partly explored via h70/eta fit)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Missing flux correction uses a linear relation calibrated on model-dependent simulations (isothermal beta model, McDonald14b profile), so model bias could affect the corrected profiles
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cool-core classification of HSC J0947-0119 rests only on SZE and a photometric redshift with no X-ray data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Weak-lensing masses have S/N of only 2.8-3.2, so they hardly discriminate between pressure models
