## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce Chandra data and align astrometry**: chandra_repro, deflare (3 sigma), offset correction using wavdetect point sources  
  > "Following standard procedures, we reprocessed all raw data using the `chandra_repro` script." (4.1 Reduction and Reprocessing of Chandra Data)
   - software: CIAO 4.9
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Offset vs maser position ≈0.1 arcsec  
  > "The derived spatial offset was $\approx$0.1 arcsec" (4.1 Reduction and Reprocessing of Chandra Data)
2. **Model nuclear point source contamination**: Fit HEG grating spectra with XSPEC then simulate with MARX including pile-up  
  > "We utilized the MARX Version 5.3.3 (cite: Dav12), which performs ray-tracing simulations that take account of the mirror and detector responses." (4.2.2 MARX Simulation)
   - software: XSPEC 12.9.1m, MARX 5.3.3
   - parameter: C-statistic; fit 0.5-8 keV with two power laws plus Gaussians  
  > "We simultaneously fitted the three spectra in the 0.5–8.0 keV band with two power-law components and emission lines identified by (cite: Sam01)." (4.2.1 Grating Spectra)
   - fitting: C-stat/dof = 2670/3130  
  > "C$-statistic/degrees of freedom (d.o.f) = 2670/3130" (4.2.1 Grating Spectra)
   - validation: Compared simulated and observed central 1-arcsec spectra  
  > "we confirmed good agreement between the simulated and observed data by comparing their central 1-arcsec spectra." (4.2.2 MARX Simulation)
3. **Map Fe Kα and ionized iron**: Sub-pixel event repositioning images in 3-6, 6.2-6.5, 6.5-6.8 keV bands, merge_obs, AGN subtraction, band ratios as EW proxies  
  > "A pixel size of 0.0625$\times$0.0625 arcsec$^2$ is adopted, and the images are smoothed via a Gaussian kernel with full width half maximum (FWHM) = 0.495 arcsec." (4.3 Mapping of Iron Emission Lines)
   - software: CIAO
   - parameter: Pile-up fraction >5% contours  
  > "Figure [2](#fig:x_ima) shows contours surrounding areas with pile-up fractions $>$ 5%." (4.3 Mapping of Iron Emission Lines)
   - assumption: alpha = 1 for pile-up  
  > "an assumption of $\alpha = 1$, corresponding to the most conservative case" (4.3 Mapping of Iron Emission Lines)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CIAO pileup_map  
  > "a similar result can be obtained using the CIAO `pileup_map` task" (4.3 Mapping of Iron Emission Lines)
4. **Fit X-ray spectra of CNR-E and CNR-SE**: 3.0-7.5 keV spectra from 1 arcsec radius circles; power law plus four Gaussians, then pexrav, pexmon, xillver  
  > "We uniformly fit a power-law plus four Gaussian functions (`zgauss` in XSPEC terminology)" (4.4 Spatially Resolved X-ray Spectral Analyses)
   - software: XSPEC, CIAO specextract
   - parameter: Line widths fixed at 20 km/s (0.4 eV)  
  > "The line widths are fixed at $\sigma$ = 20 km s$^{-1}$ or 0.4 eV" (4.4 Spatially Resolved X-ray Spectral Analyses)
   - assumption: Incident power law norm 0.7, Gamma 2.31, cutoff 160 keV; inclination 60 deg  
  > "2.31, and 160 keV based on the result of (cite: Are14)." (4.4 Spatially Resolved X-ray Spectral Analyses)
   - fitting: C-statistic  
  > "the best-fits are determined using the $C$-statistic technique on XSPEC" (4.4 Spatially Resolved X-ray Spectral Analyses)
   - validation: Compared EWs and solid angles across models; xillver favours lowest ionization consistent with xi~0.2 estimate  
  > "we obtain the lowest ionization parameter accepted by the model ($\xi_{\rm X} = 1$)." (4.4 Spatially Resolved X-ray Spectral Analyses)
5. **Reprocess ALMA data and make line cubes and moment maps**: uvcontsub, clean with matched uv range 13-250 kλ, impbcor, imsmooth; moments over VLSR 200-650 km/s (150-700 for dataset b), 10 sigma clipping for moments 1 and 2  
  > "The Moment 0 maps were made by calculating the 0th moment over the VLSR range of 200–650 km s$^{-1}$." (5.1 Reduction and Reprocessing of ALMA Data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "We adopted Briggs-weighting with robust = 0.5 and gain = 0.1" (5.1 Reduction and Reprocessing of ALMA Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Line fluxes and brightness temperatures consistent between datasets a and b  
  > "We confirmed that line fluxes and velocity-integrated brightness temperatures used for discussion were however consistent between the (a) and (b) datasets." (5.1 Reduction and Reprocessing of ALMA Data)
6. **Measure line fluxes in three subregions**: Single-beam spectra fitted with Gaussians (two for Nucleus) by least chi-square; HCN(4-3) widths and centres fixed to HCN(3-2) in CNR-E/SE; systematic error 240 mJy/beam added for CO(3-2)  
  > "The best-fits are determined using the least chi-square method." (5.1.1 Basic Molecular Line Properties)
   - fitting: Least chi-square Gaussian fits  
  > "The best-fits are determined using the least chi-square method." (5.1.1 Basic Molecular Line Properties)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Constrain gas physical conditions with non-LTE modelling**: RADEX grid over Tkin 10-600 K, log nH2 2-7, log NH2 20-25, [HCN]/[HCO+] 1-10; chi-square fit to 10 line ratios  
  > "We simulate 10 combinations of line ratios taken from the five lines for various kinetic temperatures ($T_{\rm kin}$), hydrogen densities ($n_{\rm H2}$), and hydrogen column densities ($N_{\rm mol}$)." (5.1.2 RADEX Non-LTE Modeling)
   - software: RADEX, LAMDA
   - parameter: Line widths fixed at average 3 sigma width: 330, 120, 140 km/s for Nucleus, CNR-E, CNR-SE  
  > "330 km s$^{-1}$, 120 km s$^{-1}$, and 140 km s$^{-1}$ for the Nucleus, CNR-E and CNR-SE regions, respectively" (5.1.2 RADEX Non-LTE Modeling)
   - assumption: Spherical homogeneous geometry; Tbg = 2.73 K; [CO]/[H2]=6e-5, [HCO+]/[H2]=8e-9  
  > "A spherically, homogeneous geometry is assumed in the code." (5.1.2 RADEX Non-LTE Modeling)
   - fitting: Chi-square grid  
  > "These parameters are constrained by the chi-square method." (5.1.2 RADEX Non-LTE Modeling)
   - validation: Alternative abundances tested; conclusion not strongly affected  
  > "our conclusion in Section [6.3](#sec:xdr) is not strongly affected" (5.1.2 RADEX Non-LTE Modeling)
8. **Compute XDR effective ionization parameter**: xi_eff ≈ 0.1 L_X,44 / (n_H2,4 R_100^2 N_H,22^alpha) using RADEX densities, 60-62 pc distances, log NH = 23.9  
  > "The distances from the center to the CNR-E and CNR-SE regions were set to 62 pc and 60 pc, respectively." (6.3 XDR Model)
   - parameter: L(1-100 keV)=1.3e43 erg/s, Gamma 2.31, alpha ≈1.1  
  > "The photon index corresponds to $\alpha \approx$1.1." (6.3 XDR Model)
   - assumption: Nucleus distance R > 60 pc; gas-phase C and O abundances 3e-4 and 5e-4  
  > "The gas-phase abundance of C and O with respect to H is assumed to be $3.0\times10^{-4}$ and $5.0\times10^{-4}$, respectively." (6.3 XDR Model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Repeated with luminosities from INTEGRAL, BeppoSAX, Suzaku; same conclusion  
  > "In either case, low ionization parameters of $\log \xi_{\rm eff} \approx -3$ are derived, however." (6.3 XDR Model)

## Qualitative findings

- Fe Kα extends ~3 arcsec (~60 pc) east and southeast, resembling a cone opposite the optical ionization cone  
  > "This configuration looks like a conical structure oriented towards the southeast direction" (4.3 Mapping of Iron Emission Lines)
- HCO+(4-3) anti-correlates spatially with Fe Kα  
  > "The most interesting feature is the spatial anti-correlation between the two emission lines." (6.1 Spatial Comparison between Fe Kα Line and Molecular Emission Line)
- HCN/HCO+ (4-3) ratios are low in Circinus, unlike HCN enhancement in some AGN  
  > "However, Circinus has significantly lower values, implying that it may be difficult to identify all AGN based on HCN emission." (6.2 Diagram of Molecular Gas Line Ratios)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Short 2015 observations used for line ratios to avoid cross-observation flux systematics; long 2016 HCO+(4-3) for detailed distribution  
  > "This allowed us to ignore the systematic uncertainty in the absolute flux as much as possible that can be caused between different observations." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Including 0th-order grating data and SN 1996cr data: result consistent but anti-correlation less clear in CNR-SE  
  > "The result suggests that particularly in the CNR-E region we can still see the anti-correlation, while in the CNR-SE region it seems to be not as clear" (Supplemental Chandra data)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Nucleus distance is hard to determine due to line of sight  
  > "The distance appropriate for the Nucleus region is difficult to determine due to the sightline." (6.3 XDR Model)
- Limitation (authors): CO(3-2) shows negative signals from sparse uv coverage  
  > "some negative signals were found around the line, likely due to the high brightness and sparse $uv$-coverage." (5.1 Reduction and Reprocessing of ALMA Data)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Large RADEX parameter degeneracies and coarse grid; xi_eff ranges span orders of magnitude
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fe Kα EWs have large uncertainties; solid angles imply tiny reflectors
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Short snapshot observations for line ratios; resolution mismatch between Chandra and ALMA
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inclination-based deprojected distance of 60 pc is assumed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SF suppression argument relies on a single literature PAH observation
