(Correcting SFRs with the IRX-$\mathbf{\beta}$ relation)

(Correcting SFRs with dust attenuation curves); "where $R_{V,\text{Calzetti}} = 4.05$ and $\delta$ is the deviation of the attenuation curve slope from the Calzetti-like curve." (Correcting SFRs with dust attenuation curves)

  > "The linear fit to the eight REBELS-IFU galaxies for which H$\alpha$ lies within the NIRSpec wavelength coverage gives a stellar-to-nebular attenuation ratio $f = E(B-V)_{\text{stellar}}/E(B-V)_{\text{gas}}=~0.50\pm0.08$." 3. **Fit IRX-beta relation using ALMA FIR and NIRSpec UV measurements**: Compute IRX = log10(L_IR / L_UV) using ALMA L_IR and NIRSpec L_UV and fit the McLure et al. (2018) IRX-beta relation to dust continuum-detected galaxies.  
  > "The FIR-to-rest-UV luminosity ratio, known as the infrared-excess, ${\rm IRX}$ $= \log_{10}($$L_{\rm IR}$/$L_{\rm UV}$), is a proxy for the obscured-to-unobscured SFR ratio." (Correcting SFRs with the IRX-$\mathbf{\beta}$ relation); "We fix $\beta_{0} = -2.43$; the median intrinsic UV-slope from the SED models of (cite: Fisher2025) and obtain ${dA_{1600}}/{d\beta} = 1.44 \pm 0.14$." (The IRX-$\mathbf{\beta}$ relation)
   - parameter: beta_0 = -2.43  
  > "We fix $\beta_{0} = -2.43$; the median intrinsic UV-slope from the SED models of (cite: Fisher2025) and obtain ${dA_{1600}}/{d\beta} = 1.44 \pm 0.14$." (The IRX-$\mathbf{\beta}$ relation)

  > "We use the rest-frame FIR luminosity values, $L_{\rm IR}$, from (cite: Bowler2023), which assume a dust temperature of $T_{\rm d} = 46$ K and a dust emissivity index of $\beta_{\rm d} = 2.0$, except for REBELS-25 and REBELS-38" (Deriving SFRs from the rest-UV, H$\alpha$, and FIR luminosities); "We fix $\beta_{0} = -2.43$; the median intrinsic UV-slope from the SED models of (cite: Fisher2025) and obtain ${dA_{1600}}/{d\beta} = 1.44 \pm 0.14$." 4. **Derive multi-tracer SFRs and obscured star formation fractions**: Convert L_UV, L_IR, L_Halpha, and [CII] 158 um luminosities to SFRs using standard conversion factors, extract 10 Myr and 100 Myr average SFRs from non-parametric SED fits, and compute f_obs = SFR_IR / SFR_UV+IR.  
  > "The conversion factors, $\kappa_{\text{x}}$, we use in equations of the form SFR$_{\text{x}}~=~\kappa_{\text{x}}~L_{\text{x}}$ to obtain SFRs from the luminosities, $L_{\text{x}}$, of each tracer are given in Table [\[tab:SFREqns\]](#tab:SFREqns)." (Deriving SFRs from the rest-UV, H$\alpha$, and FIR luminosities); "in Fig. [5](#fig:fobs) we show the dust-obscured SFR fractions calculated from $f_{\text{obs}} =$ SFR$_{\text{IR}}$/SFR$_{\text{UV+IR}}$ (where SFR$_{\text{UV+IR}}=$SFR$_{\text{UV}}+$SFR$_{\text{IR}}$)." (Significant dust-obscured star formation fractions)

## Qualitative findings

- Differential dust attenuation remains important at z~7, with an average stellar-to-nebular colour excess ratio of f = 0.50 +/- 0.08, showing no clear evolution with redshift compared to local galaxies.  
  > "We find an average stellar-to-nebular attenuation ratio of $f = E(B-V)_{\text{stellar}}/E(B-V)_{\text{gas}} = 0.50 \pm 0.08$, indicating that differential attenuation remains significant in massive, $z\simeq7$ galaxies." (Conclusions)
- Between 56% and 78% of star formation in the massive REBELS-IFU galaxies is dust-obscured, and the IRX-beta relation implies a dust attenuation curve intermediate between Calzetti and SMC.  
  > "The average attenuation curve inferred from the ${\rm IRX}$–$\beta$ relation lies between the Calzetti-like and SMC relations, consistent with the attenuation curves derived directly from the NIRSpec rest-UV/optical spectra in (cite: Fisher2025)." (Conclusions); "The REBELS-IFU galaxies exhibit high obscured SFR fractions, with $f_{\text{obs}} =$ SFR$_{\text{IR}}$/SFR$_{\text{UV+IR}} = 0.56 - 0.78$" (Conclusions)
- Steeply rising SFHs cause standard rest-UV+FIR SFR calibrations (which assume constant SFHs over 100 Myr) to trace a shorter ~20 Myr timescale and overestimate the 100 Myr average SFR by a factor of ~2.7.  
  > "These discrepancies can be explained with rising SFHs, which causes SFR$_{\text{UV+IR}}$ (calculated using the fiducial luminosity-to-SFR conversion factors that are derived assuming a constant SFH) to match the SFR averaged over a timescale of $\sim20$ Myr, not the assumed $100$ Myrs." (Conclusions)
- The Halpha-to-UV luminosity ratio is an unreliable diagnostic of bursty star formation in galaxies with rising SFHs.  
  > "This suggests the H$\alpha$-to-UV luminosity ratio is not a reliable indicator of bursty SFHs, even with robust dust attenuation corrections." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: REBELS-IFU is the only sample of massive (M_* > 10^9 M_sun) galaxies at z > 6 that combines rest-UV/optical JWST NIRSpec IFU spectroscopy with rest-FIR ALMA observations, enabling direct comparison of unobscured and obscured SFR tracers.  
  > "REBELS-IFU provides a unique dataset for comparing SFR tracers at $z\simeq7$ since multi-wavelength observations extending from the rest-frame UV/optical (*JWST* NIRSpec spectroscopy) to the rest-frame FIR (ALMA) are available (cite: Bouwens2022)." (Introduction); "This is the only sample of massive ($M_{\star}$ $>10^{9}$ ${\rm M}_{\odot}$) galaxies at $z>6$ that has this wealth of SFR tracers, providing a unique opportunity to investigate the consistency between different methods and shed light on the SFHs of these sources." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Combining direct Balmer decrement nebular corrections, flexible-attenuation-curve SED fitting, and ALMA FIR measurements reduces the reliance on untested local calibration assumptions.  
  > "From the NIRSpec spectra, we have measurements of key physical properties of these galaxies, including stellar mass (Stefanon et al. in prep.), dust attenuation curves (cite: Fisher2025), and gas-phase metallicities (cite: Rowland2025), allowing us to build up a comprehensive picture of these galaxies and reduce the number of assumptions required." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Inferring nebular attenuation from stellar continuum attenuation using a fixed average f ratio for the four z > 7 galaxies without Halpha coverage was rejected due to the large galaxy-to-galaxy scatter in f (0.1 to 1).
