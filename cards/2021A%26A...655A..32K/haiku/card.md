## Analysis recipe

1. **measure the continuum components**: Continuum imaged at 224.75 GHz with Briggs weighting; three components (M, B, extended clump) measured with Gaussian fits and flux integration  
  > "A Gaussian fit to this compact source (at Briggs’ $R$=0.5) gives a size" (ALMA results: continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "An example map with Briggs weighting and the robust parameter of" (ALMA results: continuum)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to each continuum component, size corrected for beam  
  > "A Gaussian fit to this compact source (at Briggs’ $R$=0.5) gives a size" (ALMA results: continuum)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **map gas emission and kinematics**: Velocity-integrated (zeroth), first and second moment maps of CO 2-1, SiO 5-4 and SO 5_6-4_5; CO taken as proxy for H2 distribution  
  > "The maps of velocity-integrated emission (zeroth moment) are shown in Fig." (ALMA results: spectral lines); "We assume that CO is the most representative probe of the entire molecular remnant" (ALMA results: spectral lines)
   - parameter: 5-sigma contour radius used as envelope size (natural weighting)  
  > "extends up to a radius of 115" (ALMA results: spectral lines)
   - assumption: CO is a proxy for the H2 distribution  
  > "We assume that CO is the most representative probe of the entire molecular remnant" (ALMA results: spectral lines)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **estimate gas excitation, column density and molecular mass**: LTE simulations of CO line and isotopologue spectra; column densities per beam; H2 mass from CO-based column densities  
  > "by simulating the spectra under LTE, we find a line ratio" (ALMA results: spectral lines); "We find column densities per beam of" (ALMA results: spectral lines)
   - parameter: Beam filling factor of one everywhere  
  > "Assuming a beam filling factor of one everywhere" (ALMA results: spectral lines)
   - assumption: Brightness temperatures taken as rough excitation temperatures at the CO photosphere given moderate optical depth  
  > "this range is a rough measure of excitation temperatures at the CO photosphere." (ALMA results: spectral lines)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Molecular mass consistent with earlier SMA-based estimate  
  > "that is consistent with the old data." (ALMA results: spectral lines)
4. **model the dust continuum (stars, M, B, clumps)**: RADMC-3D radiative transfer with radiative equilibrium dust temperature, ray-traced images and SED comparison with a manually tuned exploratory model  
  > "For a given configuration of a dust density distribution and location of radiation sources, dust temperature is calculated using the radiative equilibrium assumption." (3D reconstruction of the remnant); "the model we attempted to match to the observations was rather exploratory." (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: Black-body B3 V companion, 18 kK, radius 4.5 Rsun, luminosity 1.9e3 Lsun  
  > "The B3 V companion was represented by a black body of a temperature of 18 kK" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - parameter: Mg-rich silicate opacity, single grain size 0.1 um, grain density 3 g cm^-3  
  > "for a single grain size of 0.1 $\mu$m." (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - assumption: Opacity arbitrarily chosen as Mg-rich silicate; dust composition of real remnant not constrained  
  > "We arbitrarily implemented an opacity curve of Mg-rich silicates" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - assumption: B star placed in sky plane at 230 au  
  > "We located the blue star in the sky plane, 230 au away from V838 Mon" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - fitting: Manual trial and error over thousands of simulations matching images and SED; no formal sampler stated  
  > "After running thousands of simulations, we arrived at a model described below" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - validation: Comparison of simulated and observed images and SEDs  
  > "A comparison between observed and simulated images and SEDs are shown in Figs." (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
5. **model the SiO emission structure**: Shape software, optically thin simplified radiative transfer, two shells with linear radial velocity fields, cone-shaped void  
  > "we used the Shape software" (3D reconstruction of the remnant); "The velocity gradient is steeper for the outer structure" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - software: Shape
   - parameter: v = k r with k = 1.9 (outer) and k = 1.7 (inner)  
  > "The velocity gradient is steeper for the outer structure" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
   - assumption: Optically thin limit  
  > "We focused on reproducing the observations in optically thin limit" (3D reconstruction of the remnant)

## Qualitative findings

- The mm continuum identifies the position of the B-type companion, about 225 to 250 au from V838 Mon  
  > "We identify the component as material directly surrounding the B-type companion of V838 Mon" (ALMA results: continuum)
- The molecular envelope is clumpy rather than stratified, similar to red supergiant winds  
  > "we can conclude that the ejecta is not as much stratified as it is clumpy" (Molecular gas in the remnant of V838 Mon)
- Interaction with the B star produces turbulence and altered molecular chemistry (SO2/H2S and AlOH enhanced, SiO depleted)  
  > "Some species appear to have enhanced abundances in the interaction region, including SO$_2$/H$_2$S and AlOH, others are depleted (SiO" (Interaction with the B star)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Radiative transfer calculations are used to reproduce the remnant architecture at the ALMA epoch  
  > "We use radiative transfer calculations to reproduce the remnant" (abstract)
- Alternative: A spherical dusty cloud for component M was rejected because it gives higher extinction toward the central star than observed  
  > "However, such a configuration results in a higher extinction towards the central star than observed." (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
- Alternative: A spherically symmetric merger ejecta was judged unable to produce the observed mm signature of component B without enhanced dust density  
  > "component B requires an enhanced dust density to produce the observed mm signature" (Dusty remnant)
- How they decided it was enough: The model was accepted as exploratory after many trial simulations, given many free parameters  
  > "the model we attempted to match to the observations was rather exploratory." (3D reconstruction of the remnant)
- Limitation (authors): Resolution is insufficient to establish the disk interpretation of component M  
  > "Although this interpretation is not firm, as our data lack the angular resolution to be definitive" (Dusty remnant)
- Limitation (authors): Only the inner part of the gaseous remnant is traced; the fast atomic ejecta are not seen by ALMA  
  > "Our observations therefore show us only the inner" (Unseen atomic gas)
- Limitation (authors): Model degeneracies in the molecular gas fits remain; at least one more CO or SiO transition is needed  
  > "would lift many of the model degeneracies" (Molecular gas)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only one ALMA band-6 setup with a single transition per species, so excitation and temperature gradients cannot be constrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust model uses an arbitrarily chosen opacity and single grain size, so dust masses are very uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas models are non-unique; the CO systemic velocity (70 vs 53/54 km/s) is inconsistent across the paper
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Atomic mass is not constrained, so total remnant mass is a lower limit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Calibration and imaging parameters are largely missing, limiting independent reproduction
