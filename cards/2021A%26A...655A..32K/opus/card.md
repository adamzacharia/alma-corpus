   - validation: Position of M consistent with VLBI maser position

2. **map line emission and kinematics**: Zeroth, first and second moment maps of CO 2-1, SiO 5-4, SO and blends at natural and uniform weighting; panchromatic line map from continuum-subtracted visibilities; SiO channel maps

  > "We focused on reproducing the observations in optically thin limit with very simplified radiative transfer." (Molecular gas)

8. **analytic interaction estimates with B star**: Circular-orbit Kepler estimates, Bondi-Hoyle accretion, ballistic trajectories with gravity and radiation pressure, dust sublimation radius, wind momentum balance

  > "We adopt typical parameters of a B3 V star, i.e. $m_s = 7.0$, $l_s = 1800$" (Effects of radiation pressure of the B star on the ejecta)

## Qualitative findings

- First direct location of the B-type companion, ~225 au projected from V838 Mon; orbit nearly in sky plane  
  > "For the first time, we identify the position of the B-type companion relative to the outbursting component of V838 Mon." (abstract)
- Stellar remnant surrounded by clumpy wind similar to red supergiant winds  
  > "The stellar remnant is surrounded by a clumpy wind with characteristics similar to winds of red supergiants." (abstract)
- Ejecta are clumpy rather than stratified  
  > "we can conclude that the ejecta is not as much stratified as it is clumpy" (Historical view)
- Interaction region near B star shows turbulence and chemical changes (SO2/H2S, AlOH enhanced; SiO depleted), attributed to shocks  
  > "Some species appear to have enhanced abundances in the interaction region, including SO$_2$/H$_2$S and AlOH, others are depleted (SiO" (Interaction with the B star)
- Gas and dust distributions differ; extended dust clump lacks CO counterpart  
  > "The third extended continuum source is most difficult to explain, especially because it does not have a counterpart in CO emission." (Dusty remnant)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High angular resolution needed to spatially resolve gas and dust around the merger site  
  > "With submillimeter observations at 20 mas resolution, for the first time we spatially resolve both gas and dust in the circumstellar environment." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Shape chosen for flexible complex spatial models; RADMC-3D for joint SED and image dust modeling  
  > "which gives more flexibility in constructing complex spatial models than RADMC-3D" (Molecular gas)
- Alternative: Spherical dusty cloud for component M rejected: too much extinction toward central star  
  > "However, such a configuration results in a higher extinction towards the central star than observed." (Dust)
- Alternative: Higher mergeburst shell density to produce component B rejected (extinction too high)  
  > "but then the extinction toward the red star becomes much higher, so it becomes inconsistent with visual observations" (Dust)
- Alternative: Material captured and bound to B star excluded by hydrodynamical considerations  
  > "This scenario can be, however, excluded based on the hydrodynamical considerations outlined below." (Dusty remnant)
- Alternative: Dust model variant with conical void behind B star: requires ~30% more dust near B, separation 250 au, 20% less cloud dust
