## Qualitative findings

- Vibrationally excited HC3N (v7=1) is detected only towards one star-forming clump (the SHC) in the northern SB pseudo-ring and is not detected in the CND despite its high v=0 HC3N column density.  
  > "We have detected emission from vibrationally excited HC$_3$N (HC$_3$N\*) only towards one star-forming region of the SB ring. Remarkably, HC$_3$N\* was not detected towards the CND despite its large HC$_3$N $v=0$ column density." (Abstract)
- The SHC has high dust temperature (~250 K), high density (~6e5 cm^-3), and IR luminosity (~5.8e8 L_sun) typical of internally heated proto-Super Star Clusters (proto-SSCs).  
  > "From LTE and non-LTE modelling of HC$_3$N\*, we obtained a dust temperature of $T_\text{dust} \sim 250$ K and a density of $n_{\text{H}_2}=6\times10^5$ cm$^{-3}$ for this star-forming region. The estimated IR luminosity of $5.8\times10^8$ L$_\odot$ is typical of proto-Super Star Clusters (proto-SSC) observed in the SB galaxy NGC 253." (Abstract)
- The dust in the CND has a low temperature (<= 100 K) because it is heated externally by the AGN in an open geometry where IR photon trapping is negligible, whereas the SHC is heated internally in an IR optically thick closed geometry with strong back-warming.  
  > "This indicates that the dust in the CND not being efficiently heated by the AGN is due to being externally irradiated by the AGN (i.e. geometrical open system), where photon trapping in the IR is negligible and the temperature profile follows effectively the optically thin case." (On the heating of the SHC and the CND)
- The young SSCs in the SB pseudo-ring are concentrated near the contact points connecting the nuclear bar with the spiral arms, supporting gas inflow, and show no systematic age gradient along the ring, supporting the 'popcorn' star formation scenario.  
  > "The SSCs location and derived young ages are closely associated to the region connecting the nuclear bar with the SB pseudo-ring, where most of the gas is being accumulated and an increase of cloud-cloud collisions is expected." (Summary and conclusions); "We do not find any systematic trend of the ages of the SSCs within the ring, supporting the “popcorn” mode of star formation." (Summary and conclusions)
- Detection of HC3N* alone cannot discriminate between AGN and starburst heating in heavily obscured galactic nuclei (closed systems) like NGC 4418 and Arp 220 without spatially resolving the T_vib profile.  
  > "We concluded that just the detection of HC$_3$N\* emission cannot be used as a discriminator because of the greenhouse effect in heavily obscured galactic nuclei (closed systems) makes the AGN and SB dust profiles to be similar at large distances." (Summary and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: NGC 1068 is one of the closest galaxies with spatially resolved AGN and starburst activities and enhanced HC3N abundance in the CND; ALMA archive provides multi-frequency coverage including an 11-field mosaic covering the SB pseudo-ring.  
  > "NGC 1068, one of the closest galaxies hosting spatially resolved AGN and starburst (SB) activities, offers a unique opportunity to study the effects of star formation and AGN activity from its $\sim10^7$ M$_\odot$ SMBH (cite: Davis2014; Combes2019) on the vibrational excitation of HC$_3$N." (Introduction); "Fortunately, project-ID 2011.0.00083.S (cite: SGB2014) is an eleven-field mosaic that fully covers the SB pseudo-ring." (Data reduction)
- Why this method: Data were not continuum subtracted in the uv-plane due to abundant molecular emission and large velocity gradients; MADCUBA SLIM and non-LTE radiative transfer modelling account for line profiles, opacity, blending, and different excitation mechanisms (IR pumping vs collisions).  
  > "Due to the large amount of molecular emission and the large velocity gradients between the CND and the SB pseudo-ring data were not continuum subtracted in the uv-plane." (Data reduction); "To fully account for the line profiles, opacity effects and line blending we have also carried out an LTE analysis of the HC$_3$N\* emission using MADCUBA SLIM tool (cite: Madcuba2019)." (LTE analysis); "To derive the physical properties of the SHC and the CND and to properly account for the different excitation mechanisms of the vibrational and rotational HC$_3$N transitions, we have carried out a non-LTE radiative transfer modelling" (Non-LTE analysis)
- Alternative: Low dust optical depth at 45 um as the cause for inefficient IR pumping of HC3N* in the CND (rejected because N(H2) ~ 10^24 cm^-2 gives tau_45um ~ 16).  
  > "The first possibility can be ruled out since the H$_2$ column densities in the CND are $\sim10^{24}$ cm$^{-2}$ (see Table [\[tab:table_hc3n_lums\]](#tab:table_hc3n_lums)), which translates to dust opacities at $45$ $\mu$m of $\tau \sim16$." (On the heating of the SHC and the CND)
- Alternative: Collisional excitation of HC3N vibrational levels (rejected because derived H2 densities are much lower than the critical density for vibrational levels).  
  > "The derived H$_2$ densities for both the SHC and the CND positions are similar, but much lower than the critical density for collisional excitation of the vibrational levels (cite: Wyrowski1999)." (On the heating of the SHC and the CND)
- Alternative: 'Pearls on a string' scenario for sequential star formation along the SB pseudo-ring (rejected in favor of the 'popcorn' scenario due to lack of ordered age sequence).  
  > "This would be in accordance with the “popcorn” scenario instead of the “pearls on a string” scenario described by (cite: Boker2008)." (Propagation of SSC formation in the ring)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Decreased sensitivity at the edges of single-pointing maps at ~220 GHz where the SB pseudo-ring is located.  
  > "Since most of the NGC 1068 observations are centered on the AGN position, as we move to higher frequencies the observed field of view of the telescope primary beam is reduced, limiting the observations of the SB pseudo-ring to the outer edge of the mapped area at $\sim220$ GHz. As a consequence there is a decrease in sensitivity at the edges of the map where the SB pseudo-ring is observed." (Data reduction)
- Limitation (authors): Lack of sensitivity prevents detecting HC3N* in other SSCs or ruling out the presence of proto-SSCs in SSC 7 and 8.  
  > "The lack of sensitivity prevented us from detecting HC$_3$N\* emission towards any other position in the SB pseudo-ring." (HC$_3$N emission); "From the non-detection of HC$_3$N\* in other star-forming regions of the SB pseudo-ring we cannot completely rule out the presence of SHCs" (LTE analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Strong line blending of HC3N J=38-37 with CO(3-2) and J=39-38 with HCN(4-3) in the CND due to broad line FWHMs.  
  > "^a^ Strongly blended with CO(3-2) due to the higher FWHMs in the CND (see Table [\[tab:table_hc3n_lums\]](#tab:table_hc3n_lums))." (HC$_3$N emission); "^b^ Blended with HCN(4-3) due to the higher FWHMs in the CND (see Table [\[tab:table_hc3n_lums\]](#tab:table_hc3n_lums))." (HC$_3$N emission)

- Caveat (inferred, ours): The multi-transition HC3N analysis combines ALMA datasets with synthesized beams ranging from 0.22''x0.19'' to 1.15''x0.99'', yet the text does not specify whether the cubes were convolved to a matched resolution prior to spectral extraction or how beam dilution across bands was handled.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Units for the integrated HC3N line fluxes in Table 5 are omitted from the table header and caption (likely Jy km s^-1 or mJy km s^-1 or K km s^-1).
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The SSC age calibration (Eq. 11) relies on a single ad-hoc scaling factor (13) anchored to the SHC and assumes a linear gas removal rate over 10 Myr, which is highly simplified.
