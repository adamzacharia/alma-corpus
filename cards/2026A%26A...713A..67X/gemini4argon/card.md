## Science

- Fields: astrochemistry, star_formation
- Question: Whether the purely O-bearing alcohol ratio CH3OH/C2H5OH and the O/S alcohol-thioalcohol analog ratios CH3OH/CH3SH and C2H5OH/C2H5SH remain locally stable across selected positions within G35.2N and how they compare with other chemically rich environments and chemical models.  
  > "First, does the purely O-bearing alcohol ratio CH$_3$OH/C$_2$H$_5$OH remain stable among these positions? Second, does the methyl-level O/S alcohol–thioalcohol analog ratio CH$_3$OH/CH$_3$SH show similar source-internal stability, and how does it compare with ratios measured in other chemically rich environments?" ## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra and identify molecular transitions at target positions**: Extract spectra at common positions MM3-pos, MM4-pos, MM5-pos (plus MM3-peak and MM4-peak for comparison) from C-5 spectral-line cubes and compare with CDMS synthetic spectra to identify transitions  
  > "Following the nomenclature defined in Fig. 1b, the quantitative local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) analysis was performed at MM3-pos, MM4-pos, and MM5-pos." (Molecular identification); "Line identification was based on comparisons between the observed spectra and synthetic spectra of the target species, together with the modeled contributions of other molecules in the same spectral windows." (Molecular identification)
   - software: GILDAS, CLASS/Weeds
   - parameter: Robust identification criterion: >= 3 spectrally independent unblended or marginally blended features with peak intensity > 3 sigma reproduced simultaneously by a single LTE model  
  > "A molecular identification was classified as robust when at least three spectrally independent target features had observed peak intensities above the adopted $3\sigma$ threshold, were unblended or only marginally blended, and were reproduced simultaneously by a single LTE model." (Molecular identification)
   - assumption: Only transitions from the ground vibrational state are considered  
  > "Only transitions from the ground vibrational state were considered." (Molecular identification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison of extracted spectra at MM3-pos and MM4-pos against continuum peaks MM3-peak and MM4-peak  
  > "For MM3 and MM4, where the adopted extraction positions are offset from the corresponding continuum peaks, spectra were also extracted at MM3-peak and MM4-peak for direct comparison." (Molecular identification)
2. **Model spectra under LTE to derive excitation temperatures and column densities**: Fit synthetic LTE spectra to observed unblended/minimally blended transitions using CLASS/Weeds and refine with CLASS/ADJUST, keeping theta, Delta V, and V_LSR fixed while varying T_ex and N_t; derive total CH3OH column density from 13CH3OH  
  > "The observed spectra were modeled under the assumption of LTE using `CLASS/Weeds` (cite: Mar2011) within GILDAS, and the fits were further refined with `CLASS/ADJUST`." (Molecular identification); "To reduce parameter degeneracy, only $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ were treated as free parameters, while $\theta$, $\Delta V$, and $V_{\rm LSR}$ were fixed to the deconvolved continuum size, the average linewidth measured from bright unblended lines, and 32.0 km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Little1985), respectively." (Molecular identification); "The CH$_3$OH column density was derived from $^{13}$CH$_3$OH by adopting $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C = 50, because the main isotopologue is affected by optical-depth effects." (abstract)
   - software: GILDAS, CLASS/Weeds, CLASS/ADJUST
   - parameter: Fixed V_LSR = 32.0 km s^-1; fixed theta = deconvolved continuum size; fixed Delta V = average linewidth from bright unblended lines; free parameters: T_ex and N_t  
  > "To reduce parameter degeneracy, only $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ were treated as free parameters, while $\theta$, $\Delta V$, and $V_{\rm LSR}$ were fixed to the deconvolved continuum size, the average linewidth measured from bright unblended lines, and 32.0 km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Little1985), respectively." (Molecular identification)
   - assumption: Local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)  
  > "The observed spectra were modeled under the assumption of LTE using `CLASS/Weeds` (cite: Mar2011) within GILDAS" (Molecular identification)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 50 to convert 13CH3OH column density to total CH3OH column density  
  > "$^{\rm b}$ Adopted total CH$_3$OH column density derived from $^{13}$CH$_3$OH using $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C = 50 (cite: Sanchez2014; Allen2017)." (Table 1)
   - assumption: Partition functions for C2H5OH and C2H5SH include both trans and gauche conformers  
  > "$^{\rm c}$ Total C$_2$H$_5$OH column density derived from *trans*-C$_2$H$_5$OH transitions, using a partition function that includes both conformers." (Table 1); "$^{\rm d}$ Total C$_2$H$_5$SH column density derived from *gauche*-C$_2$H$_5$SH transitions, using a partition function that includes both conformers." (Table 1)
   - fitting: Refined with CLASS/ADJUST; conservative uncertainties of order 20% adopted to account for formal fitting and systematic effects  
  > "and the fits were further refined with `CLASS/ADJUST`." (Molecular identification); "To account for formal fitting uncertainties and additional systematic effects, including residual line blending, baseline placement, LTE assumptions, and partition-function uncertainties, conservative uncertainties were adopted for the derived $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ values. The resulting relative uncertainties are generally of order 20%." (Column densities and abundance ratios)
   - validation: Simultaneous reproduction of relative intensities, line centers, and profiles across at least three spectrally independent features  
  > "The line centers and profiles of these features had to be compatible with the adopted $V_{\rm LSR}$ and linewidth, and their relative intensities had to be reproduced by the model." (Molecular identification)
3. **Compute local abundance ratios and compare with literature sources and chemical models**: Calculate column density ratios (CH3OH/C2H5OH, CH3OH/CH3SH, C2H5OH/C2H5SH, CH3SH/C2H5SH) from unrounded column densities and compare with recalculated ALMA literature ratios and Garrod (2022) warm-up chemical models  
  > "The ratios and their uncertainties were calculated from the unrounded column densities and uncertainties; the rounded values are reported in Table 1." (Table 2); "We compare the local abundance ratios in G35.2N with ALMA-based measurements toward other chemically rich sources and with the warm-up models of (cite: Garrod2022)." (Discussion)
   - assumption: Independent errors assumed when propagating column density uncertainties into abundance ratios  
  > "When uncertainties were available for both column densities, ratio uncertainties were propagated assuming independent errors and rounded following the convention adopted in Tables 1 and 2." (Table 3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Map spatial distributions and check shock diagnostics toward MM4-pos**: Construct integrated-intensity (moment 0) maps of target species and moment 0 and moment 1 maps of shock tracers H2CO and SO, and check SiO J=5-4 peak intensity at MM4-pos  
  > "Figure 7 shows the integrated-intensity maps of CH$_3$OH, *trans*-C$_2$H$_5$OH, CH$_3$SH, and *gauche*-C$_2$H$_5$SH toward the MM3–MM5 region." (Spatial distributions and the nature of MM4-pos); "The SiO $J = 5$ – 4 transition is covered by the present spectral setup, but its peak intensity toward MM4-pos remains below the adopted $3\sigma$ threshold. The H$_2$CO and SO moment maps presented in Appendix 8 show extended emission and smoothly varying intensity-weighted velocities across the MM3–MM4 region" (Spatial distributions and the nature of MM4-pos)
   - parameter: 3 sigma detection threshold for SiO J = 5 - 4  
  > "The SiO $J = 5$ – 4 transition is covered by the present spectral setup, but its peak intensity toward MM4-pos remains below the adopted $3\sigma$ threshold." (Spatial distributions and the nature of MM4-pos)

## Qualitative findings

- CH3OH, 13CH3OH, CH3SH, and trans-C2H5OH are robustly identified at MM3-pos, MM4-pos, and MM5-pos; gauche-C2H5SH is robustly identified at MM3-pos and MM5-pos but only tentatively (~2 sigma) at MM4-pos; gauche-C2H5OH is not robustly identified.  
  > "On this basis, CH$_3$OH, $^{13}$CH$_3$OH, CH$_3$SH, and *trans*-C$_2$H$_5$OH are robustly identified toward MM3-pos, MM4-pos, and MM5-pos. No robust identification of *gauche*-C$_2$H$_5$OH was obtained." (Molecular identification); "The *gauche*-C$_2$H$_5$SH identification is robust toward MM3-pos and MM5-pos, whereas its emission toward MM4-pos remains tentative at the $\sim2\sigma$ level" (Molecular identification)
- CH3OH/C2H5OH and CH3OH/CH3SH ratios are locally consistent across MM3-pos, MM4-pos, and MM5-pos within uncertainties.  
  > "The CH$_3$OH/C$_2$H$_5$OH ratio is confined to 31–32, and the CH$_3$OH/CH$_3$SH ratio varies only from 100 to 110, indicating locally consistent CH$_3$OH/C$_2$H$_5$OH and CH$_3$OH/CH$_3$SH abundance ratios across the selected positions." (Summary)
- Shock tracers (SiO J=5-4, H2CO, SO) do not provide sufficient evidence to classify MM4-pos as a distinct shock position.  
  > "These observations do not provide sufficient evidence to classify MM4-pos as a separate shock position, although a contribution from shock-affected or dynamically disturbed gas cannot be excluded." (Spatial distributions and the nature of MM4-pos)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-angular-resolution ALMA observations of chemically rich G35.2N allow a source-internal test of O/S alcohol-thioalcohol ratios across multiple compact structures within a common physical and observational environment.  
  > "A source-internal comparison separates abundance variations driven by source-to-source differences from those measured within a common physical and observational environment. High-angular-resolution Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) observations allow source-averaged abundance ratios to be complemented by measurements at selected chemically rich positions within a single source." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Common offset extraction positions (MM3-pos, MM4-pos, MM5-pos) were chosen to examine all four species consistently with good S/N and manageable line blending; 13CH3OH was used because main CH3OH is optically thick; theta, Delta V, and V_LSR were fixed in LTE fits to reduce parameter degeneracy.  
  > "The CH$_3$OH column density was derived from $^{13}$CH$_3$OH by adopting $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C = 50, because the main isotopologue is affected by optical-depth effects." (abstract); "These common spectral-extraction positions were selected so that CH$_3$OH, C$_2$H$_5$OH, CH$_3$SH, and C$_2$H$_5$SH could be examined consistently at the same spatial locations with useful signal-to-noise ratios and manageable line blending." (Molecular identification); "To reduce parameter degeneracy, only $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ were treated as free parameters, while $\theta$, $\Delta V$, and $V_{\rm LSR}$ were fixed to the deconvolved continuum size, the average linewidth measured from bright unblended lines, and 32.0 km s$^{-1}$ (cite: Little1985), respectively." (Molecular identification)
- Alternative: Using the main CH3OH isotopologue column density for abundance ratios (rejected because it is affected by optical-depth effects)  
  > "$^{\rm a}$ Best-fit column density of the main CH$_3$OH isotopologue. This value is affected by optical-depth effects and is not used in the ratio analysis." (Table 1)
- Alternative: Extracting spectra directly at the continuum peaks MM3-peak and MM4-peak for the primary ratio analysis (rejected because CH3SH and C2H5SH are weaker or not robustly identified there compared to MM3-pos and MM4-pos)  
  > "The continuum-peak spectra show that CH$_3$SH and *gauche*-C$_2$H$_5$SH are more clearly identified toward MM3-pos than toward MM3-peak. Toward MM4-peak, CH$_3$SH does not satisfy the robust-identification criterion, and the *trans*-C$_2$H$_5$OH emission is weaker than toward MM4-pos." (Molecular identification)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): C2H5SH identification at MM4-pos is only tentative (~2 sigma), making MM4-pos ratios involving C2H5SH provisional.  
  > "$^{\rm e}$ The MM4-pos value of C$_2$H$_5$SH is tentative because the corresponding *gauche*-C$_2$H$_5$SH features do not meet the robust-identification criterion and are detected only at the $\sim 2\sigma$ level." (Table 1)
- Limitation (authors): Extracted column densities and ratios are local measurements at selected offset positions and do not represent continuum peaks, core-averaged properties, or full spatial variation across G35.2N.  
  > "The column densities and abundance ratios reported below represent local measurements at MM3-pos, MM4-pos, and MM5-pos; they are not intended to represent the corresponding continuum peaks, core-averaged properties, or the complete spatial chemical variation across G35.2N." (Molecular identification)
- Limitation (authors): Cross-source and model comparisons are inhomogeneous because literature C2H5SH values are mostly upper limits and warm-up model ratios use peak gas-phase fractional abundances rather than beam-averaged column densities.  
  > "The comparison is not homogeneous: the observational sample includes detections, lower limits, and a small number of tentative entries, whereas the model ratios are based on peak gas-phase fractional abundances rather than beam-averaged column densities." (Discussion)
- Limitation (authors): Available data cannot distinguish whether ratio variations arise from grain desorption, gas-phase processing, evolutionary state, or sulfur reservoir differences.  
  > "These ratios provide quantitative observational constraints on sulfur-bearing organic chemistry, although the available data do not distinguish between these processes." (Discussion)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Exact coordinates of MM3-pos, MM4-pos, and MM5-pos and the fixed source sizes (theta) and linewidths (Delta V) used in the LTE fits are not tabulated in the paper text, hindering exact independent verification of the column densities.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Adopting a uniform 12C/13C = 50 across all positions directly scales all CH3OH-based ratios; local isotopic fractionation or residual 13CH3OH optical depth could systematically shift the derived ratios.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparing beam-averaged LTE column density ratios directly against peak gas-phase fractional abundances from 0D warm-up models ignores spatial integration and time-evolution effects.
