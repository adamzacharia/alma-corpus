## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum**: clean on calibrated visibilities, line-free channels averaged  
  > "We averaged all line-free channels in the four spectral windows for the construction of the 1.3 mm continuum images." (2. OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Briggs robust = 0.5  
  > "we used a Briggs robustness parameter of 0.5 in the clean task" (2. OBSERVATIONS AND DATA REDUCTION)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure flux and size**: gaussfit on natural-weighted image in 0.2'' x 0.2'' box  
  > "within a 0 . 2” × 0 . 2” box centered on the detection" (3. DISKS WITHIN CARINA'S EGGS)
   - software: CASA gaussfit
   - fitting: Gaussian fit  
  > "Table 1 gives the sizes and integrated fluxes of the disks determined with the gaussfit task of CASA from a natural-weighted image" (3. DISKS WITHIN CARINA'S EGGS)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Spectral index**: compare flux densities between 220 and 237 GHz spectral windows  
  > "By comparing the density fluxes between the spectral windows at 220 GHz and 237 GHz, spectral indices higher than 2 are determined" (3. DISKS WITHIN CARINA'S EGGS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive disk masses**: optically thin isothermal dust: Mdisk = S D^2 /(B(Tdust) kappa)  
  > "The total disk mass ( _M_ disk ) can be derived under the same assumptions, being proportional to the mm flux density _S_ ν" (3. DISKS WITHIN CARINA'S EGGS)
   - assumption: kappa=0.015 cm2/g, 100:1 gas-to-dust, Tdust=40 K (also 20 K), D~2.3 kpc  
  > "We have used a dust mass opacity κ 1300µ _m_ = 0 . 015 cm [2] g [−][1]" (3. DISKS WITHIN CARINA'S EGGS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Masses uncertain within a factor of a few; central parts of 104-593 may be optically thick  
  > "The mass estimates are uncertain within a factor of a few" (3. DISKS WITHIN CARINA'S EGGS)
5. **Estimate photo-evaporation rates and timescales**: analytic EUV/FUV mass-loss estimates scaled with Q_H and distance; compare with Orion proplyds  
  > "the incident photon flux _F_ EUV balances the recombination rate per unit area in the evaporation flow" (4. PHOTO-EVAPORATION OF DISKS AND EGGS)
   - assumption: Rdisk = 60 AU  
  > "The _M_ [˙] EUV alone then gives 2 × 10 [−][7] _M_ ⊙ year [−][1] for _R_ disk = 60 AU" (4. PHOTO-EVAPORATION OF DISKS AND EGGS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Hα surface brightness cross-check within a factor of two for 105-600  
  > "Within a factor of two, the same result is derived from the H α surface brightness of the globule’s bright rim" (4. PHOTO-EVAPORATION OF DISKS AND EGGS)
