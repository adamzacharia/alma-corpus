## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA Bands 3 and 7**: CASA calibration; imaging with robust 0.5; continuum from line-free channels; cleaned to 1σ  
  > "We used casa (McMullin et al. 2007) to calibrate the visibility data and to image them with a robust weighting of 0.5." (2. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND RESULTS)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "image them with a robust weighting of 0.5" (2. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND RESULTS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Characterize the line and continuum source**: imfit for size; single Gaussian fit to the spectrum; flux from 4 arcsec aperture with primary beam correction  
  > "Flux densities are measured with a 4 ′′ aperture and corrected for primary beam attenuation" (Figure 1 caption)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: Single Gaussian line fit  
  > "A single Gaussian fit to the spectrum shows S peak = 12 . 9 ± 1 . 0 mJy" (2. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND RESULTS)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Photometric redshift from SED templates**: Fit submm-to-radio photometry (optionally with IRAC 3.6/4.5 μm) to four templates; chi2 versus redshift with 90% intervals  
  > "Then we fit the submm to radio data to the templates." (3.1. Photometric Redshift Estimates)
   - assumption: Dust T=40 K, β=1.5 for FIR luminosity  
  > "we assume a dust temperature of T dust = 40 K and an emissivity index of β = 1 . 5" (2. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND RESULTS)
   - fitting: chi2 minimization on templates  
  > "Figures 2(a)–(c) show the minimum χ [2] and FIR luminosity as a function of the redshift" (3.1. Photometric Redshift Estimates)
   - validation: Comparison with and without IRAC constraints  
  > "the use of IRAC data should be considered cautiously because IRAC observes the rest-frame optical component" (3.1. Photometric Redshift Estimates)
4. **Line identification via predicted CO intensities**: Predict CO line intensities from 880 μm flux using LFIR-L'CO(3-2) correlation and M82 / BR1202 excitation ladders; compare with observed 3.1 Jy km/s  
  > "we estimate the [12] CO intensities using the 880 μ m continuum intensity and some empirical relations and quantities found in a local starburst galaxy, M82" (3.2. Line Identification)
   - assumption: αCO = 0.8 Msun (K km/s pc2)^-1, thermalized lines; Tdust 40 or 50 K  
  > "derived using a conversion factor of 0.8 M⊙ (K km s [−][1] pc [2] ) [−][1] (Downes & Solomon 1998) and assuming thermally excited lines" (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternatives ruled out: [CI](2-1) non-detection for J=7-6, H2O redshifts outside photo-z range  
  > "The J = 7–6 ( z = 7 . 087) transition is ruled out because no [C i] (2–1) at ν obs = 100 . 08 GHz is found." (2. ALMA OBSERVATIONS AND RESULTS)
5. **X-ray spectral analysis**: 4 arcsec aperture counts; background from 2 arcmin region; absorbed power-law fit in xspec at fixed redshifts  
  > "We use xspec (version 12.8.1; Arnaud 1996) for spectral modeling and assume a power-law spectrum for the intrinsic spectrum." (4. HARD X-RAY DETECTION)
   - software: XSPEC
   - assumption: Absorbed power law with free Γ and NH; no scattered component  
  > "we consider only an absorbed spectrum with the intrinsic photon index (Γ) and obscuring column density ( N H ) as free parameters" (4. HARD X-RAY DETECTION)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Chance-detection rate of CO emitters**: Extract >1 Jy km/s sources from S3-SAX boxes at each CO-transition redshift and integrate counts over bandwidth and primary beam  
  > "we extract sources with an integrated intensity higher than 1 Jy km s [−][1] from the simulated volumes" (5. NUMBER COUNTS OF CO EMITTERS)
   - software: S3-SAX
   - parameter: 62.5×62.5 h^-2 Mpc^2 boxes, Δz=0.50 (0.10 at z=0.156)  
  > "defined by an area of 62 . 5 × 62 . 5 h [−][2] Mpc [2] with a depth of Δ z = 0 . 50" (5. NUMBER COUNTS OF CO EMITTERS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simulation reproduces local CO(1-0) LF; may underpredict z~1.5 counts by a factor of several  
  > "It reproduces the local CO(1–0) luminosity function well (Keres et al. 2003)" (5. NUMBER COUNTS OF CO EMITTERS)

## Qualitative findings

  > "Most of the sources (≃90%) are CO(1–0) at z = 0 . 156." (5. NUMBER COUNTS OF CO EMITTERS)
