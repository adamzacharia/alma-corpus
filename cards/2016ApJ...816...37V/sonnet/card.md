## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce and image ALMA data**: Standard CASA reduction, flagging, removal of long baselines to eliminate striping, cleaning with robust=0.5  
  > "High-frequency striping was present in the data, which was eliminated by removing the long baselines." (2 ALMA OBSERVATIONS)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust=0.5  
  > "The cubes were cleaned using a weighting factor of robust=0.5" (2 ALMA OBSERVATIONS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Self-calibration tested, no improvement  
  > "Self-calibration was attempted with and without the long baselines, but showed no additional improvement" (2 ALMA OBSERVATIONS)
2. **Measure [C II] line and continuum**: Gaussian + constant fit to spectrum of brightest pixel in cube smoothed with 1 arcsec; line maps by averaging over FWHM; sizes with imfit  
  > "We fitted a Gaussian + constant to the spectra to model the [C II] emission and the continuum." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission); "From the maps we measured the sizes of the line and continuum emission using CASA task ‘imfit’." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
   - software: CASA imsmooth, CASA imfit
   - fitting: Gaussian plus constant  
  > "We fitted a Gaussian + constant to the spectra to model the [C II] emission and the continuum." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive FIR/TIR luminosities, SFRs, dust masses**: Scale modified black body (47 K, beta=1.6) and Arp 220/M82 templates to 158 um continuum; SFR from L_TIR (Murphy+11) and L_[CII] (De Looze+14); dust mass with kappa from Dunne+00  
  > "SFR$_\mathrm{[CII]}$/$M_\ensuremath{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$ = $3.0\times10^{-9}$ ($L_\mathrm{[CII]}$/$L_\ensuremath{\odot}$)$^{1.18}$, with an uncertainty of 0.4 dex." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
   - parameter: L_FIR 42.5–122.5 um; L_TIR 8–1000 um rest-frame  
  > "The total infrared luminosity ($L_{\mathrm{TIR}}$) was computed by integrating the continuum from 8 $\mu$m to 1000 $\mu$m in the rest-frame." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
   - assumption: Td=47 K, beta=1.6, tau<<1 at lambda>40 um; Kroupa IMF; emission powered by star formation  
  > "our modified black body model has a dust temperature of $T_d=47$ K and a dust emissivity power-law spectral index of $\beta=1.6$" (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Range across three SED models adopted as uncertainty  
  > "We caution that the range of values of $L_{\mathrm{FIR}}$ and $L_{\mathrm{TIR}}$ for the VIKING quasar hosts presented here strongly depends on our choice of models" (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
4. **Constrain dust temperature of J0305–3150 from continuum slope with CMB**: Fit modified black body (beta=1.6 fixed) to continuum at ~234 and ~250 GHz, with and without CMB heating/contrast; Monte Carlo errors  
  > "We estimated the uncertainty in the temperature by randomly adding noise to the spectrum and remeasuring the best-fitting dust temperature 10,000 times." (4.4.3 Continuum Slope)
   - parameter: beta=1.6, z=6.6145  
  > "With a fixed $\beta=1.6$ and redshift $z=6.6145$ (Table [2](#tab:firprop)), we measure a lower intrinsic dust temperature of $T_d^{z=0}=30$ K" (4.5 Effects of the Cosmic Microwave Background)
   - fitting: Best-fit modified black body, 10,000 noise realizations, 68% range for 1 sigma  
  > "For the 1 $\sigma$ uncertainties we took the range of temperatures of 68% of the values around the median." (4.4.3 Continuum Slope)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Model [C II] kinematics of J0305–3150**: Empirical tilted-ring models with single disk of constant scale height, compared with data in l-v diagrams  
  > "Empirical tilted-ring models were created to match the data using the Tilted Ring Fitting Code (cite: joz07)." (4.4.1 Dynamical Modeling)
   - software: TiRiFiC
   - parameter: Rotation curve rising linearly to 150 km/s at 0.1 arcsec then flat  
  > "a linearly increasing rotation curve (starting at 0 km s$^{-1}$ and peaking at 150 km s$^{-1}$ at 0.1$''$" (4.4.1 Dynamical Modeling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flat rotation curve and constant surface brightness ruled out; inclination unconstrained  
  > "Our data clearly rule out models with a constant surface brightness and a constant rotation velocity" (4.4.1 Dynamical Modeling)
6. **Estimate dynamical masses and compare M_BH to M_dyn**: M_dyn=1.16e5 v_circ^2 D with v_circ=0.75 FWHM/sin(i), i from axis ratio; compare with Kormendy & Ho relation  
  > "$M_\mathrm{dyn}=1.16\times10^5 v_\mathrm{circ}^2 D$ $M_\ensuremath{\odot}$" (4.6 Dynamical mass estimates)
   - parameter: D=1.5x deconvolved size; i=25, 50 deg; 55 deg and 3±1 kpc for J2348–3054  
  > "For the unresolved emission in the host of J2348–3054 we assume an extent of $3\pm1$ kpc." (4.6 Dynamical mass estimates)
   - assumption: Thin inclined disk; gas-to-dust ratio ~100  
  > "If we take the local gas-to-dust mass ratio of $\sim$100 (cite: dra07)" (4.6 Dynamical mass estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Inclination uncertainty not included in M_dyn errors  
  > "The uncertainty in these dynamical masses does not include the uncertainties in the inclination angle." (4.6 Dynamical mass estimates)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Tuned to [C II] using Mg II redshift; short observations in compact configuration  
  > "two overlapping sidebands covering the [C II] line (tuned using the Mg II redshift)" (2 ALMA OBSERVATIONS)
- Alternative: Alternative [C II]-SFR relations (de Looze 2011, Sargsyan 2014, Herrera-Camus 2015) give SFRs lower by factors 2–6  
  > "Using instead the relation between SFR and $L_\mathrm{[CII]}$ derived by (cite: del11) and (cite: sar14) the SFRs would be a factor $\sim$2–2.5 lower." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Inclination and peak rotation velocity cannot be independently determined at current resolution  
  > "with the current data we cannot independently determine the inclination angle and the peak velocity and thus, ultimately, the dynamical mass." (4.4.1 Dynamical Modeling)
- Limitation (authors): FIR/TIR luminosities strongly model dependent; more photometry needed  
  > "Additional far-infrared photometry is required to better constrain the shape of the infrared continuum and thus $L_{\mathrm{FIR}}$ and $L_{\mathrm{TIR}}$." (3.1 [CII] and FIR emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature constraint from 3.1 sigma continuum difference with beta fixed is weak
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical masses rely on assumed inclinations, sizes and disk geometry
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small, heterogeneous literature sample for correlations and Mg II shifts
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Continuum difference could be affected by differing resolution/calibration between sidebands
