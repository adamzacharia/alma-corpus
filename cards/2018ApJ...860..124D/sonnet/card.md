## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum**: tclean Briggs 0.5 with uv taper from pipeline-calibrated data  
  > "We used the Briggs weighting scheme with a robust parameter of 0.5" (2)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Deproject and fit inner ring ellipse**: Deproject with i=21, PA=62 around Gaia position; fit ellipse with one focus on star, compare with offset circle  
  > "The deprojected inner ring is well-approximated by an ellipse with one focus on the star" (3.2)
   - assumption: mm dust settled to midplane so deprojection recovers face-on view  
  > "deprojection roughly recovers the face-on view of the disk" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with off-centered circle and ALMA astrometry uncertainty  
  > "This difference is $\sim$one order of magnitude larger than the expected ALMA astrometry uncertainty" (3.2)
3. **Radial and azimuthal profiles of rings, arm, clumps**: Azimuthally averaged profiles in Ring East/West; FWHM, deconvolved by sqrt(FWHM^2-beam^2); peak-to-trough contrasts  
  > "We estimate their intrinsic (deconvolved) radial FWHM (FWHM$_{\rm intrinsic}$$\approx$$\sqrt{{\rm FWHM}^2-{\rm FWHM_{\rm beam}^2}}$)" (3.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Estimate dust temperature**: HOCHUNK3D radiative transfer models varying scale height and flaring  
  > "We generate representative disk models using the radiative transfer code HOCHUNK3D (cite: whitney13)" (3.4)
   - software: HOCHUNK3D
   - assumption: T*=7580 K, L*=9.7 Lsun, Sigma∝1/r  
  > "The central star is assumed to have $T_\star$=7580 K and $L_\star$=9.7$L_\odot$" (3.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
