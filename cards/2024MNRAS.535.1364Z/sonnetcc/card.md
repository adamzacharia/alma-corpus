## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce and image the ATOMS Band 3 data**: CASA 5.6 tclean on combined main array and 7 m array data with natural weighting  
  > "The main array and 7 m array data were combined and then cleaned using the" (2 Observations and sample selections); "with natural weighting" (2 Observations and sample selections)
   - software: CASA 5.6
   - parameter: Pixel size 0.4 arcsec  
  > "The pixel size was set to 0.4$''$ in the imaging process" (2 Observations and sample selections)
   - parameter: Imaged field to 20% power point, radius 44 arcsec  
  > "The imaged field was reduced to the 20% power point of the primary beam response, equivalent to a circular region with a radius of 44$''$." (2 Observations and sample selections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Select compact H II regions with CCH molecular shells**: Examine CCH and H40alpha distributions in all 146 ATOMS fields and select by two criteria (prominent CCH shell at the edge of H40alpha emission; H40alpha well resolved)  
  > "a thorough examination on the spatial distributions of CCH and H40$\alpha$ emission was performed toward the ALMA images of all 146 ATOMS sources" (2 Observations and sample selections); "H40$\alpha$ emission is well resolved in the FoV of ATOMS data." (2 Observations and sample selections)
   - parameter: Nine regions selected  
  > "A total of nine compact H II regions are selected" (2 Observations and sample selections)
   - assumption: CCH is a sensitive tracer of the photodissociation region  
  > "CCH is thought to be a sensitive tracer for photodissociation region (cite: Cuadrado2015; Kirsanova2021)" (2 Observations and sample selections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive H II region size, ionization rate, spectral type, electron density, pressure**: Size from H40alpha moment 0 mask edges; ionization rate from H40alpha luminosity assuming ionization-recombination equilibrium; ne from the effective volume; Pi from ne and ionized-gas sound speed  
  > "In the H40$\alpha$ moment 0 maps produced from the data cube with a clipped threshold of ${4\rm rms}$, we further mask the pixels with a value below 10% of the image peak." (3 Properties of compact H II regions); "is derived from $A_{\rm H40\alpha}$ by $r_{\rm H40\alpha,eff} = \sqrt{A_{\rm H40\alpha}/\pi}$" (3 Properties of compact H II regions); "we attempt to estimate an averaged $n_{\rm e}$ using" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - parameter: epsilon = 1.99e-32, b_ul = 0.788, f_ul(LTE) = 7.03e-19 cm3, A_ul = 54.7 s-1, alpha_B = 2.54e-13 cm3 s-1  
  > "At the same time, the corresponding $\alpha_{\rm B}= 2.54\times10^{-13}~{\rm cm}^{3}~{\rm s}^{-1}$" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - parameter: Spectral type from Sternberg2003 relations  
  > "The spectral type of ionizing stars is then determined from $\dot{N}_{\rm ion}$ using the relations in (cite: Sternberg2003)." (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - assumption: Te = 1e4 K and ne = 1e4 cm-3 for the efficiency factor; helium ionization ignored  
  > "For an assumed electron temperature $T_{\rm e} = 10^4~{\rm K}$ and an electron number density $n_{\rm e} = 10^4~{\rm cm^{-3}}$" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - assumption: Te from the Galactocentric distance relation when not measured  
  > "We used the Galactocentric distance $R_{\rm GC}$ - $T_{\rm e}$ relation derived by when $T_{\rm e}$ is not measured" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Sensitivity of alpha_B and epsilon checked: about 10% variation for low ne and Te  
  > "there is only a variation of $\simeq10\%$ when $n_{\rm e}$ and $T_{\rm e}$ are as low as 100 cm$^{-3}$ and 5000 K, respectively" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
4. **Estimate initial density and dynamical age of the H II regions**: n_ini from ATLASGAL clump properties in two cases (SExtractor mass/radius; peak column density/GaussClump FWHM); t_dyn from Dyson1997 with Stromgren radius  
  > "We therefore estimate $n_{\rm ini}$ using the present-day properties of natal clumps measured by ATLASGAL observations." (3 Properties of compact H II regions); "The $t_{\rm dyn}$ is estimated using (cite: Dyson1997)" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - software: SExtractor, GaussClump
   - parameter: mu_H2 = 2.8  
  > "$\mu_{\rm H_{2}} = 2.8$" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - assumption: Average clump density does not change much from quiescent to H II region stage (difference ~20%)  
  > "indicating that the average density of a massive clump may not change much during evolution from the quiescent stage to the H II region stage" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Independent test with simulations suggests a typical age around 0.2 Myr  
  > "suggests a typical age around 0.2 Myr with large error for our H II regions, roughly consistent with the estimated lifetime." (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
5. **Extract dense gas fragments from the H13CO+ cubes**: 3D astrodendro dendrogram on cubes without PB correction; leaves are fragments  
  > "that is capable of recognizing intricate and hierarchical structures of molecular clouds" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments); "since they represent the smallest structures without substructures" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: min_value = 3.5 rms  
  > "the minimum intensity for a voxel to be considered $\texttt{min{\_}value} = 3.5~\rm rms$" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments)
   - parameter: min_delta = 2 rms  
  > "the minimum step to differentiate an independent structure $\texttt{min{\_}delta} = 2~\rm rms$" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments)
   - parameter: min_npix equivalent to three channels x half of the beam size  
  > "$\texttt{min{\_}npix}$ equivalent to three channels $\times$ half of the beam size" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments)
   - parameter: pruning: min_peak = 4.5 rms, minimum three channels, minimum spatial size half a beam  
  > "the minimum peak for an indecomposable structure $\texttt{min{\_}peak} = 4.5~\rm rms$" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Extracted fragments compared with moment 0 maps; low-SNR and complicated spectra removed; 164 fragments kept  
  > "where the extracted fragments agree well with the bright emissions in the H$^{13}$CO$^+$ $J=1-0$ moment 0 maps" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments); "a total of 164 dense fragments were extracted and considered in the following analysis." (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments)
6. **Measure fragment properties (sigma, mass, surface density, density, virial ratio)**: Gaussian fit to the average spectrum within the dendrogram area and velocity range; optically thin H13CO+ column density with Tex = Tdust; mass from column density and abundance; Sigma, n_H2 and virial ratio from the dendrogram effective radius  
  > "we extracted in practice the average spectra from the dendrogram spatial area of fragments and then fitted them with Gaussian within the dendrogram velocity range of fragments" (4.2 Fragment properties); "With the assumption that H$^{13}$CO$^+$ $J=1-0$ is optically thin" (4.2 Fragment properties); "$\Sigma_{\rm core} = M_{\rm core}/A_{\rm core} = M_{\rm core}/\left(\pi r^2_{\rm core,eff}\right)$" (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - software: astrodendro, RADEX
   - parameter: A_ul = 10^-4.41416 s-1, f_beam = 1, E_u = 4.16 K, g_u = 3  
  > "Taking $A_{ul} = 10^{-4.41416}~\rm s^{-1}$, $f_{\rm beam} = 1$, $E_u = 4.16~\rm K$, and $g_u = 3$" (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - parameter: chi_H13CO+ = 3.6e-11 uniform for all regions  
  > "a uniform $\chi_{\rm H^{13}CO^+} = 3.6\times10^{-11}$ is taken for our sample, which is the average for UCH II regions in the observation of (cite: Kim2020)" (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - parameter: Virial ratio R_vir = 5 sigma^2 r / (M G)  
  > "$R_{\rm vir} = \frac{M_{\rm vir}}{M_{\rm core}} = \frac{5\sigma^2 r_{\rm core,eff}}{M_{\rm core} G}$" (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - assumption: Tex equals the clump-scale dust temperature  
  > "It is assumed that $T_{\rm ex}$ is equal to the dust temperature $T_{\rm dust}$ on the clump scale measured by (cite: Urquhart2018)." (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - assumption: Constant density profile, no magnetic field, rotation or external pressure for the virial ratio  
  > "Regardless of magnetic fields, rotation, and external pressure and assuming a constant density profile of fragments" (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit to average fragment spectrum for sigma  
  > "and then fitted them with Gaussian within the dendrogram velocity range of fragments" (4.2 Fragment properties)
   - validation: RADEX optical depth check for a typical fragment (tau ~0.46); 16 fragments with complicated profiles excluded from property estimation  
  > "online tool is $\simeq0.46$ (cite: vanderTak2007)" (4.3 Biases); "A total of 16 massive and dense fragments are neglected in property estimation due to their complicated spectral profiles." (4.3 Biases)

## Qualitative findings

- Eight of nine regions show evident CCH molecular shells; I18116-1646 shell not visible, probably a projection effect  
  > "All regions, apart from I18116$-$1646, have evident molecular shells." (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
- Most N fragments are embedded in the CCH shell partly enclosing the H II region  
  > "most of them are embedded in the CCH shell partly enclosing the H II region" (5 Influence of H II region on fragments)
- No difference in virial ratio between N and F fragments; most are near marginally bound (R_vir <~ 2)  
  > "We do not find a difference in $R_{\rm vir}$ between the N and F fragments" (5.1 Nature of fragments)
- Most regions have an ATPCF peaking at or slightly beyond the H II region radius, unlike random distributions  
  > "Most of the regions in our study have an ATPCF that peaks around or slightly farther than $r_{\rm H40\alpha,eff}$" (6.1 Regulated spatial distribution)
- The 3 mm continuum is dominated by free-free emission so dust analysis of fragments is not feasible  
  > "The continuum emission at 3 mm for our ATOMS target regions is dominated by free-free emission" (4 Dense gas fragments)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ATOMS can resolve distant compact H II regions with a wide field of view and provides a statistical sample  
  > "ATOMS observations are capable of resolving distant H II regions at the compact stage ($\gtrsim0.1$ pc) up to around 10 kpc with a wide field of view (FoV)." (2 Observations and sample selections)
- Why this method: H13CO+ is an effective tracer of high-density gas, and dendrograms recognize hierarchical structure  
  > "H$^{13}$CO$^+$ is an effective tracer of high-density molecular gas, especially for the dense gas involved in star formation (cite: Gao2004)" (4 Dense gas fragments)
- Alternative: Simple Gaussian fit to measure H II region size rejected because of intricate H40alpha morphology; used masked moment 0 area instead  
  > "A simple Gaussian fit is not appropriate to measure the size of our H II regions in the H40$\alpha$ moment 0 maps due to the intricate morphology of H40$\alpha$ emission" (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
- Alternative: Dust continuum at 3 mm not used to analyse fragments because it is dominated by free-free emission  
  > "and thus it is very difficult to analyze these fragments with dust emission at 3 mm" (4 Dense gas fragments)
- Alternative: Turbulence-dominated fragmentation rejected: expected mass >~100 Msun and length >~1 pc far exceed observed  
  > "A fragmentation dominated by turbulence is very unlikely" (7.3 Pre-existing fragments)
- Alternative: v_frag - v_HII,local not used for comparison because it reflects intricate local kinematics  
  > "is not appropriate to be used in our comparison because they are dominated by much more intricate kinematics." (6.2 Influence on fragment kinematics)
- How they decided it was enough: Dendrogram parameters and N/F overlap thresholds chosen after tests  
  > "After a deliberate test, the parameters in the calculation of the dendrogram are established as follows" (4.1 Extraction of dense gas fragments); "These thresholds are carefully tested." (5 Influence of H II region on fragments)
- Limitation (authors): Dust absorption of ionizing photons may underestimate the spectral type of ionizing stars  
  > "It should be noted that severe dust absorption of ionizing photons in early H II regions may lead to an underestimation of spectral type of ionizing stars." (3 Properties of compact H II regions)
- Limitation (authors): Tex and H13CO+ abundance variations within the clump are an important uncertainty; effect of the H II region on abundance cannot be determined without chemical modelling  
  > "Variation of $T_{\rm ex}$ and $\chi_{\rm H^{13}CO^+}$ within the clump-scale environment can be another important uncertainty in property calculations." (4.3 Biases)
- Limitation (authors): External ionized gas pressure ignored in the virial ratio  
  > "The external pressure exerted by the ionized gas $P_{\rm i}$ is ignored in our calculation of $R_{\rm vir}$ due to the high inhomogeneity of $P_{\rm i}$." (5.1 Nature of fragments)
- Limitation (authors): Large scatter in the sigma_NT-size relation prevents detailed analysis  
  > "The large scatter hinders us from any further detailed analysis" (5.1 Nature of fragments)
- Limitation (authors): Sample too small and affected by distance bias for the mass growth analysis  
  > "The sample size of this work is probably too small to distinguish the difference." (8.1 Mass growth of fragments)
- Limitation (authors): t_frag and r_frag are likely underestimated because of the classical expansion law and cometary morphology  
  > "We should note that $t_{\rm frag}$ and $r_{\rm frag}$ here are substantially underestimated." (7.2 Comparisons with model of Whitworth1994)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fragment masses depend on a single uniform H13CO+ abundance (3.6e-11) and Tex = clump Tdust, with N/F temperature offsets of +/-5 K assumed, so the N vs F mass difference is not independent of those choices
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical age and n_ini use present-day clump densities and the classical expansion law, so t_frag versus age comparison is order-of-magnitude
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Conclusions rest on nine regions at different distances (1.8-6.2 kpc) with natural-weighted ~2 arcsec beam, so resolution and sensitivity bias fragment extraction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Pre-existing fragment scenario is inferred, not directly tested; no direct kinematic or temperature measurement of the shell
- Caveat (inferred, ours): K-S test details and multiple-comparison handling are not given
