## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and feather 7M+12M and Total Power C18O (2-1) line cubes**: Image 7M+12M calibrated measurement sets with multiscale tclean, smooth to a common beam across channels with imsmooth, subtract continuum with imcontsub, and combine with Total Power cubes using feathering.  
  > "First, we image 7M+12M calibrated measurement (.ms) files and then combine them with the total power (TP) image cube using the CASA task `feathering`." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "After producing the 7m+12m data cube, we first smooth the image cube with a common beam across all channels using the CASA task `imsmooth` where we set `kernel=`$'$`commonbeam`$'$. " (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "Subsequently, we subtract the continuum from the image cube using the CASA task `imcontsub` with `fitorder`=0." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
   - software: CASA, ALMA-IMF data pipeline, tclean, imsmooth, imcontsub, feathering
   - parameter: deconvolver = 'multiscale' with 4 to 5 scales in geometric progression  
  > "We use multiscale cleaning using the `deconvolver = `$'$`multiscale`$'$ to capture all the structures from small to diffuse large scales and use the primary beam limited mask through the parameters `usemask` and `pbmask`." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "We note that, regarding the multiscales, we use four to five scales in geometric progression." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
   - parameter: threshold = 3 sigma, cyclefactor = 3 or 4, pixel size = 1/3 to 1/5 of clean beam minor axis  
  > "For threshold we use 3$\sigma$ value and for `cyclefactor` we use 3 and sometimes if cleaning artifacts arise we use 4." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "Additionally, we set the pixel size between 1/3 and 1/5 of the minor axis of the clean beam of the deconvolved image cube, which is most effective for cleaning." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
   - parameter: fitorder = 0 in imcontsub; kernel = 'commonbeam' in imsmooth  
  > "After producing the 7m+12m data cube, we first smooth the image cube with a common beam across all channels using the CASA task `imsmooth` where we set `kernel=`$'$`commonbeam`$'$. " (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "Subsequently, we subtract the continuum from the image cube using the CASA task `imcontsub` with `fitorder`=0." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked that increasing multiscale scales beyond five gives no flux improvement; manually masked negative bowls in G012.80 after checking 1.3 mm continuum.  
  > "After increasing the number of scales by more than five, we do not observe any flux improvement in the final image cube." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "In one region (G012.80), negative bowls are still present after feathering the 7M+12M and TP data. In such a case, we modify the pbmask after checking the negative bowls in the feathered image cube and the 1.3 mm continuum." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
2. **Extract average C18O (2-1) spectra and measure systemic velocities**: Average C18O (2-1) spectra over the entire field of view and compute intensity-weighted systemic velocity within velocity cuts for channels above 3 sigma_rms.  
  > "In Fig. [1](#fig:fig1), we show the average spectra (over the entire field of view) of C$^{18}$O ($J$=2$-$1) lines for 15 protoclusters." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1)); "We indicate the measured intensity-weighted systemic velocity ($V_{\text{\text{sys}}}$ = $\sum_{i}^{} I_{\text{i}} \text{v}_{\text{i}}/\sum_{i}^{} I_{\text{i}}$, where $i$ is the channel number) for each protocluster with a red dashed line." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1)); "We note that for calculating $V_{\text{\text{sys}}}$, we consider values that are greater than 3 times the RMS noise ($\sigma_{\text{rms}}$)." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1))
   - parameter: Cutoff > 3 sigma_rms; diffuse extended clouds at ~80 and ~115 km s-1 ignored in W43-MM1, W43-MM2, W43-MM3  
  > "We note that for calculating $V_{\text{\text{sys}}}$, we consider values that are greater than 3 times the RMS noise ($\sigma_{\text{rms}}$)." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1)); "For W43-MM1, W43-MM2 and W43-MM3, we observed cloud components at $\sim$ 80 km s$^{-1}$, $\sim$ 115 km s$^{-1}$, which were reported as diffuse extended clouds of the W43 complex (cite: 2011A&A...529A..41N; 2014A&A...571A..32M). For this reason, we ignore these clouds while calculating the systemic velocity ($V_{\text{sys}}$) of the protoclusters." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared measured V_sys values with Motte et al. (2022) and Cunningham et al. (2023) DCN (3-2) core velocities.  
  > "We compare the values with the velocities reported by (cite: 2022A&A...662A...8M) and notice that for most regions the difference is below $\sim$ 2 km s$^{-1}$." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1)); "Earlier, (cite: 2023A&A...678A.194C) examined the $V_{\text{sys}}$ in the 15 protoclusters from the mean line-of-sight values of the DCN (3$-$2) cores. They also observed similar differences in $V_{\text{sys}}$ between W43-MM1, W43-MM2, and W43-MM3 protoclusters residing in the W43 main complex, and W51-E and W51-IRS2 protoclusters residing in the W51 complex." (Average spectra of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1))
3. **Generate moment 0, moment 1, and moment 2 maps**: Calculate integrated intensity (moment 0), peak velocity (moment 1), and intensity-weighted velocity dispersion (moment 2) maps using pixels above 4.5 sigma_rms.  
  > "We show the integrated intensity (moment 0) and integrated intensity-weighted velocity dispersion (moment 2) maps for 15 protoclusters in Figs. [2](#fig:fig2) and [3](#fig:fig3)." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission); "For calculating the moment maps, we take all the values in each pixel that are above 4.5$\sigma_{\text{rms}}$ values." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission)
   - parameter: Amplitude threshold > 4.5 sigma_rms  
  > "For calculating the moment maps, we take all the values in each pixel that are above 4.5$\sigma_{\text{rms}}$ values." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Recognized artificial broadening in moment 2 maps from multiple velocity components along the line of sight, motivating multi-Gaussian decomposition.  
  > "This is possibly due to separate clouds in the same spatial position, which artificially increases the velocity dispersion in the pixels." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission); "For this reason, we calculate the velocity dispersion from the model spectra (obtained after decomposing the spectra into multi-Gaussian components) for analyzing the spatial distribution of $M_{\text{s}}$ in these regions (see Section [4](#section_4))." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission)
4. **Smooth, regrid, and decompose pixel-wise C18O spectra into multi-Gaussian components**: Smooth C18O cubes to 2.5'' resolution and regrid to match Td maps (and smooth W43-MM1 spectral resolution to 0.34 km s-1), mask pixels with SNR < 10, and decompose spectra into multi-Gaussian components using Gausspy+.  
  > "We therefore first smooth the C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) image cubes into the same $T_{\text{d}}$ images, which are at 2.5$''$ resolution, and then regrid to the same $T_{\text{d}}$ cubes, so that both C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) and $T_{\text{d}}$ image cubes become identical." (Estimation of turbulence); "After that, we decompose the pixel-wise C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) spectra using the `Gausspy+` module. To obtain identical image cubes, we use the Python module `radio_beam` and CASA task `imregrid`." (Estimation of turbulence); "Thus, we first mask pixels whose SNR is less than 10." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
   - software: radio_beam, CASA, imregrid, Gausspy+
   - parameter: Angular resolution = 2.5''; spectral resolution = 0.34 km s-1 for W43-MM1  
  > "We therefore first smooth the C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) image cubes into the same $T_{\text{d}}$ images, which are at 2.5$''$ resolution, and then regrid to the same $T_{\text{d}}$ cubes, so that both C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) and $T_{\text{d}}$ image cubes become identical." (Estimation of turbulence); "Therefore, we first smoothed the spectral axis of the W43-MM1 region, so the spectral resolution is 0.34 km s$^{-1}$, which is similar to the other cubes." (Estimation of turbulence)
   - parameter: max_jump_comps = 2, minimum FWHM = 2 channels, pixel SNR >= 10  
  > "This parameter is called `max_jump_comps` and in our case we set it to 2." (Decomposition of spectral profile); "Thus, we first mask pixels whose SNR is less than 10." (Decomposition of spectral profile); "In our analysis, we set the minimum FWHM equal to 2 (in channel number units), which is the minimum criteria for proper sampling of a Gaussian profile." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
   - assumption: Optical depth tau_nu of C18O (2-1) does not significantly impact multi-Gaussian fitting, evaluated via log10(tau_C18O) = 0.6 (log10[N(H2)] - 24.0)  
  > "Optical depth of C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) line is obtained empirically as a function of the density of the molecular hydrogen column density \[$N$(H$_{2}$)\] from the work of (cite: 2022ApJ...936...80S), which is $\text{log}_{10}(\tau_{\text{C}^{18}\text{O}})$ = 0.6 ($\text{log}$$_{10}$ \[$N$(H$_{2}$)\] -24.0)." (Decomposition of spectral profile); "Thus, we assume that the optical depth ($\tau_{\nu}$) does not have a significant impact on the multi-Gaussian fitting." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
   - fitting: Automated Gaussian Decomposition (AGD) based on machine learning in Gausspy+  
  > "`Gausspy+` module is based on the automated Gaussian decomposition method (AGD) based on machine learning (cite: 2019A&A...628A..78R)." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
   - validation: Checked F1 score across SNR bins (<10, 10-20, 20-30), tested variations in significance, snr_noise_spike, and refit_rchi2, and checked percentage of pixels with tau_nu > 0.5 and > 1.0.  
  > "We notice that for spectra whose SNR $<$ 10, the accuracy factor drastically drops below 65%." (Decomposition of spectral profile); "This module contains several other parameters, e.g., `significance`, `snr_noise_spike`, `refit_rchi2`. We have varied these parameters and checked whether these changes affect the final results. However, we did not find any changes in the decomposed spectra." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
5. **Compute non-thermal velocity dispersion and 3D sonic Mach number distributions and compare with DCN cores**: Calculate intensity-weighted effective velocity dispersion sigma_eff per pixel from decomposed Gaussian components, subtract thermal velocity dispersion using Td maps to get sigma_nth, compute 3D sonic Mach number Ms = sqrt(3) * sigma_nth / c_s, correlate with N(H2), and compare with DCN (3-2) cores.  
  > "If multiple Gaussian components are present in a pixel after decomposition of the spectrum, we calculate the effective velocity dispersion ($\sigma_{\text{eff}}$) for that pixel based on the integrated intensity of the components." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "After calculating the $\sigma_{\text{eff}}$ values in each pixel and taking the temperature information from the dust temperature ($T_{\text{d}}$) image, we measure the 1-D non-thermal velocity dispersion ($\sigma_{\text{nth}}$) using the following formula:" (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "Here also, we compare the non-thermal line width and Mach number between C$^{18}$O line and DCN cores to check whether the turbulence inside the protoclusters varies towards the dense cores." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
   - parameter: 12 equal intervals of sigma_nth and N(H2) for correlation analysis  
  > "For studying the correlation, we first grouped the data in 12 equal intervals according to the values of $\sigma_{\text{nth}}$ and $N(\text{H}_2)$." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
   - assumption: Local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) with gas kinetic temperature equal to dust temperature (Tk = Td)  
  > "As we do not have direct information of $T_{\text{k}}$, which is obtained from the analysis of the ammonia spectral line (cite: 2006A&A...447..929P; 2023A&A...674A..46W), we assume that the system is in local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and the gas temperature ($T_{\text{k}}$) and the dust temperature ($T_{\text{d}}$) are almost the same." (Estimation of turbulence)
   - assumption: m_C18O = 30 m_H, m_DCN = 30 m_H, mean molecular weight mu = 2.35 m_H, m_H = 1.67e-24 g, k_B = 1.38e-16 erg K-1  
  > "; $k_{\text{B}}$ is the Boltzmann constant = 1.38 $\times$ 10$^{-16}$ erg K$^{-1}$; $T_{\text{k}}$ is the kinetic temperature; ${m_{c^{18}\text{O}}}$ is the mass of the C$^{18}$O molecule = 30$m_{\text{H}}$, where $m_{\text{H}}$ = 1.67 $\times$ 10$^{-24}$ g." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "where $c_{\text{s}}$ is the isothermal sound speed equal to $\sqrt{k_{\text{B}} T_{\text{d}}/\mu m_{\text{H}}}$; $\mu$ is the mean molecular weight of the gas = 2.35$m_{\text{H}}$ (cite: 2013A&A...550A.135A; 2020A&A...642A..68S; 2020ApJ...896..110L)." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "For calculating $\sigma_{\text{nth, DCN}}$, we take the value of the mass of the DCN molecule as 30 m$_{\text{H}}$, same as for C$^{18}$O molecule." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked impact of large-scale velocity gradients on PV diagrams (only 4-11% in G008.67 and W43-MM1), beam smoothing to 2.5'', spectrometer channel broadening on DCN, and excluding H II and SiO outflow regions.  
  > "Now for the $\sim$ 1.5 km s$^{-1}$ velocity dispersion of the C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) line, it only affects 4 to 11% of the line width." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "We also examined whether the distribution of Mach numbers changes after excluding the H ii emission and SiO (5$-$4) outflow regions. However, we did not find any noticeable changes in Mach numbers." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "However, when we check it for the DCN line, we also observe that the average non-thermal velocity dispersion decreases only by 0.02 km s$^{-1}$ and the average Mach number decreases only by 0.10 from their uncorrected values." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
6. **Extract PPV structures with astrodendro and fit the size-linewidth relation**: Zero channels < 2.5 sigma_rms in original-resolution PPV cubes, extract structures using astrodendro, compute deconvolved radius R and intensity-weighted velocity dispersion sigma_tot, and fit power-law sigma_tot = A (R / pc)^p.  
  > "We first calculate the noise map using the line free channels and then convert each velocity channel for each pixel into zero whose amplitude is $<$ 2.5$\sigma_{\text{rms}}$." (Structure decomposition); "We correct the convolved beam from $R_{\text{obs}}$ to obtain the deconvolved size $R$, which is equal to $\sqrt{R_{\text{obs}}^{2} - \theta_{\text{beam}}^{2}}$." (Structure decomposition); "We fit the size-linewidth relation using the Python module `scipy.optimize.curvefit`." (Structure decomposition)
   - software: astrodendro, scipy.optimize.curvefit
   - parameter: min_value close to zero (on cube zeroed below 2.5 sigma_rms), min_delta = 2.5 sigma_rms, min_npix = minimum 6 velocity channels and 2 beam areas in spatial dimensions  
  > "As we use the modified cube, we set the `min_value` as close to zero. The second parameter is `min_delta`. It determines the threshold beyond which it is considered as a structure. We take this value at 2.5$\sigma_{\text{rms}}$." (Structure decomposition); "We consider minimum 6 channels in the velocity space (v) and for the spatial dimension (x,y), we set the value 2$a$ which is equal to $[2 \pi/4ln2].{[FWHM_{\text{x,beam}}.FWHM_{\text{y,beam}}/(pixel\_size)^{2}}$\]." (Structure decomposition)
   - assumption: Spherical radius R_obs = 1.91 * sqrt(R_maj * R_min); thermal velocity dispersion contribution to sigma_tot is negligible  
  > "After obtaining the $R_{\text{maj}}$ and $R_{\text{min}}$ values, we calculate the spherical radius, $R_{\text{obs}}$ which is equal to 1.91 times the effective RMS size, $R_{\text{rms}}~(\sqrt{R_{\text{maj}} R_{\text{min}} }$) ((cite: 2023ApJ...949...63O), and references therein)." (Structure decomposition); "Thus, the thermal velocity dispersion ($\sigma_{\text{th}}$) does not have any significant impact on the intensity ($A$) and the power-law index ($p$) of the size-linewidth relation." (Structure decomposition)
   - fitting: Power-law fit sigma_tot = A (R / pc)^p using scipy.optimize.curvefit  
  > "We fit the size-linewidth relation using the Python module `scipy.optimize.curvefit`." (Structure decomposition); "| Protocluster | Evolutionary stage | Number of structures | $\sigma_{\text{tot}}$ = $A$ (km s$^{-1}$) ($\frac{R}{\text{pc}})^{p}$ |" (Table 4)
   - validation: Rechecked errors with 10^4 bootstrap samples, tested excluding H II and SiO (5-4) outflow regions, and checked sensitivity to min_value threshold.  
  > "After obtaining the similar value in the power-law index error, we recheck errors especially in the power-law index using the `bootstrapping` method. For that, we run 10$^{4}$ samples in each of these regions and randomly select both the velocity dispersion and the length scale and we recover the same results as reported above." (Structure decomposition); "We also examined the size-linewidth relation after excluding the H ii and SiO (5$-$4) outflow emission regions." (Structure decomposition)

## Software

- **CASA** : Environment for the ALMA-IMF data pipeline and tasks tclean, imsmooth, imcontsub, feathering, and imregrid  
  > "This pipeline has been written in the Common Astronomy Software Applications (CASA) environment [^2]." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "First, we image 7M+12M calibrated measurement (.ms) files and then combine them with the total power (TP) image cube using the CASA task `feathering`." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "To obtain identical image cubes, we use the Python module `radio_beam` and CASA task `imregrid`." (Estimation of turbulence)
- **ALMA-IMF data pipeline** : Producing calibrated and imaged continuum and line cubes via line_imaging.py and imaging_parameters.py  
  > "To produce calibrated and imaged continuum and line cubes for the full spectral line windows, (cite: 2022A&A...662A...9G) developed the ALMA-IMF data pipeline." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "For imaging the line cubes, we use two script files: `line`$\_$`imaging.py` and `imaging`$\_$`parameters.py`, where the necessary parameters for the cleaning task `tclean` are contained." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
- **tclean** : Multiscale cleaning of 7M+12M C18O (2-1) line cubes  
  > "For imaging the line cubes, we use two script files: `line`$\_$`imaging.py` and `imaging`$\_$`parameters.py`, where the necessary parameters for the cleaning task `tclean` are contained." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
- **imsmooth** : Smoothing 7m+12m image cubes to a common beam across all channels  
  > "After producing the 7m+12m data cube, we first smooth the image cube with a common beam across all channels using the CASA task `imsmooth` where we set `kernel=`$'$`commonbeam`$'$. " (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
- **imcontsub** : Subtracting continuum from the 7m+12m image cubes  
  > "Subsequently, we subtract the continuum from the image cube using the CASA task `imcontsub` with `fitorder`=0." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
- **feathering** : Combining 7M+12M continuum-subtracted cubes with Total Power (TP) cubes  
  > "After obtaining the 7M+12M continuum subtracted image cubes, we combine them with the total power (TP) data cubes using the CASA task `feathering`." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
- **imregrid** : Regridding smoothed C18O (2-1) image cubes to match dust temperature cubes  
  > "To obtain identical image cubes, we use the Python module `radio_beam` and CASA task `imregrid`." (Estimation of turbulence)
- **radio_beam** : Smoothing C18O (2-1) image cubes to 2.5'' resolution to match dust temperature cubes  
  > "To obtain identical image cubes, we use the Python module `radio_beam` and CASA task `imregrid`." (Estimation of turbulence)
- **Gausspy+** : Automated pixel-wise multi-Gaussian decomposition of C18O (2-1) spectra  
  > "After that, we decompose the pixel-wise C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) spectra using the `Gausspy+` module." (Estimation of turbulence)
- **astrodendro** : Extracting hierarchical structures from C18O (2-1) position-position-velocity (PPV) cubes  
  > "To accomplish this, we use the Python package `astrodendro` (cite: 2008ApJ...679.1338R; 2012MNRAS.425..720S), which properly decomposes the structures and provides us with various information such as velocity-dispersion ($\sigma_{\text{tot}}$), area ($S$), size ($R_{\text{obs}}$), position angle (PA), major ($R_{\text{maj}}$) and minor ($R_{\text{min}}$) sizes of the structures in the plane of the sky." (Structure decomposition)
- **scipy.optimize.curvefit** : Fitting power-law size-linewidth relations  
  > "We fit the size-linewidth relation using the Python module `scipy.optimize.curvefit`." (Structure decomposition)

## Qualitative findings

- Turbulence traced by C18O (2-1) in all 15 massive protoclusters is highly supersonic (Ms ranging from ~1 to ~25, mean ~5-9) and shows no significant variation with protocluster evolutionary stage.  
  > "We note that the values of $M_{\text{s}}$ for all these protoclusters range between $\sim$ 1 and $\sim$ 25 with median values ranging from $\sim$ 5 to $\sim$ 8 and mean values between $\sim$ 5 and $\sim$ 9." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution); "Although the pattern is quite similar, we, however, did not find any significant increase or decrease in the mean and median values of $M_{\text{s}}$ according to the evolutionary stage of the protoclusters." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
- There is no significant correlation between sonic Mach number Ms and molecular hydrogen column density N(H2) across the protoclusters, except tentatively in G351.77.  
  > "From the figure we see that there is no significant correlation between $M_{\text{s}}$ and $N(\text{H}_2)$ except for the G351.77 region, where a slight correlation is tentatively observed." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
- Mean non-thermal velocity dispersion and sonic Mach number of dense DCN (3-2) cores are approximately half those of the extended gas traced by C18O (2-1), indicating turbulence decreases toward dense cores.  
  > "On average, the mean value of the $\sigma_{\text{nth, DCN}}$ and $M_{\text{s, DCN}}$ is almost half that of the C$^{18}$O values. It indicates that, although turbulence is high in the low density regimes inside the protoclusters, it decreases towards the high density dense cores." (Sonic Mach number ($M_{s}$) distribution)
- The size-linewidth power-law index p across the 15 protoclusters (combined p = 0.51 +/- 0.00) is steeper than incompressible Kolmogorov turbulence (p = 0.33) and consistent with compressible Burgers turbulence (p ~ 0.50).  
  > "We obtain that the value of $p$ is 0.51 $\pm$0.00." (Size-linewidth correlation); "However, for compressible media, the velocity power law is steeper than the Kolmogorov law of turbulence ($p$ = 0.33), closer to the Burger law of turbulence, in which the power-law index is around 0.50 (cite: BURGERS1948171; 1983ApJ...272L..45F; 2007ApJ...666L..69K; 2023PASA...40...46K)." (Size-linewidth correlation)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: C18O (2-1) has a relatively low critical density (9.33e3 cm-3 at 20 K) and low optical depth (generally < 1) compared to 12CO, 13CO, DCN, and N2D+, enabling it to trace extended gas across the protoclusters from ~0.01 pc core scales to >1 pc2 cloud scales.  
  > "The rest frequency and the critical density ($n_{\text{cr}}$) of this line are 219.56035800 GHz [^1] and 9.33 $\times$ 10$^{3}$ cm$^{-3}$ (at 20 K), respectively (cite: 2016A&A...585A..44M)." (Introduction); "Consequently, C$^{18}$O (2$-$1) line traces extended gas compared to the lines mentioned above (cite: 1997ApJ...482..245U; 2015PASP..127..266M; 2022ApJ...936...80S; 2023A&A...678A.194C)." (Introduction); "Compared to other isotopes such as $^{12}$CO and $^{13}$CO, C$^{18}$O’s optical depth ($\tau_{\nu}$) is relatively low (cite: 2016A&A...591A.104H; 2019MNRAS.487.1259L; 2022ApJ...936...80S)." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Multi-Gaussian decomposition with Gausspy+ prevents overlapping velocity components along the line of sight from artificially inflating the velocity dispersion, while astrodendro extracts hierarchical PPV structures to test turbulent eddy scaling.  
  > "For this reason, we calculate the velocity dispersion from the model spectra (obtained after decomposing the spectra into multi-Gaussian components) for analyzing the spatial distribution of $M_{\text{s}}$ in these regions (see Section [4](#section_4))." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission); "To accomplish this, we use the Python package `astrodendro` (cite: 2008ApJ...679.1338R; 2012MNRAS.425..720S), which properly decomposes the structures and provides us with various information such as velocity-dispersion ($\sigma_{\text{tot}}$), area ($S$), size ($R_{\text{obs}}$), position angle (PA), major ($R_{\text{maj}}$) and minor ($R_{\text{min}}$) sizes of the structures in the plane of the sky." (Structure decomposition)
- Alternative: Using direct Moment 2 maps for sonic Mach number analysis was rejected because overlapping velocity components along the line of sight artificially increase the velocity dispersion.  
  > "This is possibly due to separate clouds in the same spatial position, which artificially increases the velocity dispersion in the pixels." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission); "For this reason, we calculate the velocity dispersion from the model spectra (obtained after decomposing the spectra into multi-Gaussian components) for analyzing the spatial distribution of $M_{\text{s}}$ in these regions (see Section [4](#section_4))." (Moment maps of C$^{18}$O ($J$ = 2$-$1) line emission)
- Alternative: Using more than five scales in multiscale tclean was rejected because it yielded no flux improvement and sometimes caused divergence.  
  > "After increasing the number of scales by more than five, we do not observe any flux improvement in the final image cube. In some cases, it has also been observed that processes diverge when too many scales are added during the cleaning process." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data)
- Alternative: Including pixels with SNR < 10 in Gausspy+ decomposition was rejected because the F1 score dropped below 65% and noisy single-component fits artificially increased velocity dispersion at region outskirts.  
  > "We notice that for spectra whose SNR $<$ 10, the accuracy factor drastically drops below 65%. Thus, we first mask pixels whose SNR is less than 10." (Decomposition of spectral profile); "Apart from that in some regions, we also notice that below SNR = 10, the spectra are very noisy and the `Gausspy+` module fits the spectra with one component. When we plot the spatial distribution of the velocity dispersion, we notice that the outskirts of the region have a drastically increase in velocity dispersion." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
- How they decided it was enough: Stopped adding multiscale scales above five in tclean because no flux improvement was observed and processes sometimes diverged; masked pixels with SNR < 10 in Gausspy+ because F1 accuracy dropped below 65%.  
  > "After increasing the number of scales by more than five, we do not observe any flux improvement in the final image cube." (C$^{18}$O (J = 2$-$1) data); "We notice that for spectra whose SNR $<$ 10, the accuracy factor drastically drops below 65%. Thus, we first mask pixels whose SNR is less than 10." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
- Limitation (authors): Astrodendro structure extraction suffers from contamination between small-scale (leaves) and large-scale (branches/trunks) structures along the line of sight, making fitted A an upper limit and fitted p a lower limit.  
  > "We note that `Astrodendro` or any other structure extraction module exhibits a bias with respect to contaminated structures." (Structure decomposition); "Thus, we can say that the fitted $A$ value represents the upper limit and the fitted $p$ value represents the lower limit of the actual value." (Structure decomposition)
- Limitation (authors): The min_value = 2.5 sigma_rms threshold in astrodendro may significantly reduce the measured velocity dispersion of large wing-like structures.  
  > "Apart from the contamination issue, velocity dispersion of the large wing-like structures may reduce significantly due to the value of `min_value` = 2.5$\sigma_{\text{rms}}$. This is also considered as a caveat in the `Astrodendro` module." (Structure decomposition)
- Limitation (authors): Gas kinetic temperature Tk is not measured directly from NH3 and is assumed equal to the column-density-weighted dust temperature Td under LTE.  
  > "As we do not have direct information of $T_{\text{k}}$, which is obtained from the analysis of the ammonia spectral line (cite: 2006A&A...447..929P; 2023A&A...674A..46W), we assume that the system is in local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and the gas temperature ($T_{\text{k}}$) and the dust temperature ($T_{\text{d}}$) are almost the same." (Estimation of turbulence)
- Limitation (authors): A small fraction of pixels (up to 7.8% in G012.80 and 3.0% in W51-E) have estimated C18O (2-1) optical depth tau_nu > 1.0.  
  > "In the case of $\tau_{\nu}$ $>$ 1.0, we notice that for most protoclusters it is below 2%, however, in the cases of G012.80 and W51-E, these are 7.8% and 3.0% respectively." (Decomposition of spectral profile)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparing C18O (2-1) Mach numbers measured at 2.5'' resolution with DCN (3-2) core Mach numbers measured at native (~0.5''-1'') resolution mixes spatial scale effects with density regime effects, as acknowledged in the discussion.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): In Table 4, the number of structures N for W43-MM1 is left blank in the table cell.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The size-linewidth relation is fitted in PPV space using astrodendro on cubes that were NOT Gaussian-decomposed, so overlapping line-of-sight clouds in complex regions like W51-IRS2 can still affect larger astrodendro structures.
