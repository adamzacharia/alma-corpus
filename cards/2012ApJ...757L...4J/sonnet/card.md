- Question: Is glycolaldehyde (and ethylene glycol) present in the warm gas around the solar-type protostellar binary IRAS16293-2422, and what do its abundances imply for formation routes?

## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract spectra and fit line fluxes**: Spectra within one beam toward continuum peaks of A and B; simultaneous Gaussian fits to all lines  
  > "Simultaneous Gaussian fits to all lines in the central beams toward each of the sources were made to determine line intensities." (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with SMA spectra; bright line fluxes agree to 10-20%  
  > "fluxes for bright lines agreeing to better than 10–20%" (Observations)
2. **LTE modelling of methyl formate and glycolaldehyde**: Isothermal LTE model with optical depth fit to all lines in band; column densities averaged over 0.5'' (60 AU)  
  > "The derived column densities averaged over 0.5$''$ (60 AU) regions are given in Table [1]" (Results)
   - assumption: isothermal medium, LTE  
  > "assuming an isothermal medium and LTE excitation" (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Model reproduces line profiles including vibrationally excited lines; predicts band 9 optically thick lines that are observed; alternative identifications ruled out  
  > "The model reproduces the observed line profiles and strengths very well" (Results)
3. **Test Band 9 predictions and ethylene glycol**: Compare 220 GHz-based predictions with 690 GHz spectra at B peak and one beam offset; scale LTE model at 300 K to ethylene glycol line  
  > "Using the LTE model at 300 K, the observed line strength would require an ethylene glycol abundance of 0.3–0.5 with respect to glycolaldehyde." (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Seven optically thick band 9 glycolaldehyde lines show red-shifted absorption toward IRAS16293B, indicating infall, with emission at systemic velocity offset by ~0.2'' (25 AU)  
  > "all show red-shifted absorption profiles toward one of the components in the binary (IRAS16293B) indicative of infall" (abstract)
