## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure multi-band photometry**: SExtractor in dual-image mode on an inverse-variance weighted F277W+F356W+F444W detection stack; circular aperture fluxes on PSF-matched images and Kron total fluxes  
  > "(cite: Bertin1996) in dual image mode with an inverse-variance weighted stack of the longest wavelength NIRCam wide filters, F277W+F356W+F444W, as the detection image." (2.1 HST and JWST NIRCam observations)
   - software: SExtractor
   - parameter: Apertures of 0.16, 0.35, 0.5 and 0.7 arcsec radius, PSF-matched to F444W  
  > "fluxes are measured in 0.16$''$, 0.35$''$, 0.5$''$, and 0.7$''$ radius circular apertures" (2.1 HST and JWST NIRCam observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Photometric redshift**: EAZY with the blue_sfhz_13 template set (redshift-dependent SFHs), 5% error floor, z range 0.01-20  
  > "which imposes redshift-dependent star formation histories (SFHs), excluding SFHs that start earlier than the age of the Universe at a given redshift" (2.3 Zhúlóng with robust photometric redshift); "We apply an error floor of 5% prior to running" (2.3 Zhúlóng with robust photometric redshift)
   - software: EAZY, Bagpipes
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-check with Bagpipes redshift fit (z 1-10); second low-z solution traced to contaminating clump 2  
  > "The resulting $P(z)$ shows a single strong peak, consistent with the `EAZY` results." (Robustness of the redshift solution for Zhúlóng.)
3. **Fit structural morphology**: Single and double Sersic fits to 4x4 arcsec background-subtracted cutouts of F277W, F356W, F444W and their stack, with neighbour masked and spiral arms masked  
  > "We fit our source with `PySersic` (cite: Pasha2023JOSS) using two different strategies: 1) a single Sérsic profile and 2) double Sérsic profiles." (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures); "The bright neighboring foreground galaxy at $z\sim1.59$ is masked during the fitting." (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
   - software: PySersic, WebbPSF
   - parameter: PSFs from WebbPSF, rotated to the observation position angle  
  > "we use PSFs for fitting which are derived from the WebbPSF software (cite: Perrin2014) and rotated to match the position angle of the observations" (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Fits with and without masking spiral arms agree within 1 sigma; individual-band fits give the same results  
  > "Furthermore, we also perform the morphological analysis for individual images, and the results remain the same" (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
4. **Measure light-enclosing size**: Empirical R80 from the azimuthally-averaged light profile of the stacked image, with no parametric shape assumed  
  > "using the azimuthally-averaged light profile from the stacked image without any assumption on the parametric shape of the light distribution" (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Resolved SED fitting in annuli**: Bagpipes fits of UV-to-NIR JWST+HST photometry in annuli with z fixed to the EAZY value, double-power-law and delayed SFHs, Bruzual & Charlot models, Calzetti law  
  > "with the $z_{\rm phot}$ fixed to that measured by `eazy`. We consider two different star formation histories (SFHs): a double-power-law and a delayed SFH" (3.2 A quiescent-like bulge and star-forming disk)
   - software: Bagpipes
   - parameter: Metallicity 0.1-2.5 Zsun; A_V 0-5 mag; log U -4 to -2; DPL: alpha, beta in [0.01, 1000], tau in [0.01, 5] Gyr; delayed: age 10 Myr-1.2 Gyr, log tau 10 Myr-10 Gyr  
  > "We adopt a broad metallicity range of 0.1 to 2.5 $Z_{\odot}$, dust attenuation to the rest-frame V band ($A_{\rm V}$) values from 0 to 5 magnitudes" (3.2 A quiescent-like bulge and star-forming disk)
   - assumption: Bruzual & Charlot (2003) stellar population models; Calzetti et al. (2000) reddening law  
  > "using (cite: Bruzual2003) stellar population models and the (cite: Calzetti2000) reddening law" (3.2 A quiescent-like bulge and star-forming disk)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: The two SFH assumptions give M*, A_V and SFR consistent within 1 sigma  
  > "consistent within $1\sigma$ between the results assuming the two different SFHs" (3.2 A quiescent-like bulge and star-forming disk)
6. **Integrated SED with ALMA upper limit**: Bagpipes UV-to-FIR fit of JWST+HST+ALMA photometry with Draine & Li dust emission (energy balance), double-power-law and delayed SFH, free metallicity  
  > "The total $M_{\star}$, $A_{\rm V}$, and SFR are derived by fitting the UV-to-FIR SED from the JWST+HST+ALMA photometry" (3.3 Ultra-massive spiral galaxy with accelerated formation); "the (cite: Draine2007) dust emission model (with energy balance)" (3.3 Ultra-massive spiral galaxy with accelerated formation)
   - software: Bagpipes, CIGALE
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; Planck cosmology (Om=0.3, OL=0.7, h=0.7, sigma8=0.81)  
  > "we adopt a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: Chabrier2003) to estimate SFR and stellar mass." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CIGALE fit gives consistent values; total mass consistent with sum of annulus masses  
  > "which produces very consistent values within the errors" (3.3 Ultra-massive spiral galaxy with accelerated formation)
7. **Maximum-efficiency argument**: Derive the most massive halo in the PANORAMIC survey volume from the halo mass function and convert to maximum stellar mass M*max = epsilon f_b Mhalo_max, then solve for epsilon  
  > "The maximum stellar mass is inferred from the maximum dark matter halo mass, based on" (3.3 Ultra-massive spiral galaxy with accelerated formation)
   - parameter: Survey area ~432 arcmin^2; Delta z = 1 volume (~1.2e6 Mpc^3 at z=4.7-5.7); f_b = 0.158  
  > "we consider the total PANORAMIC survey area of $\sim$ 432 arcmin$^{2}$" (3.3 Ultra-massive spiral galaxy with accelerated formation)
   - assumption: Galaxy sits in the most massive halo expected in the survey volume  
  > "Assuming it is located in the most massive dark matter halo expected in the whole PANORAMIC survey volume" (Conclusions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Software

- **SExtractor** : Dual-image photometry and Kron fluxes  
  > "(cite: Bertin1996) in dual image mode with an inverse-variance weighted stack of the longest wavelength NIRCam wide filters" (2.1 HST and JWST NIRCam observations)
- **EAZY** : Photometric redshift fitting with blue_sfhz_13 templates  
  > "which imposes redshift-dependent star formation histories (SFHs), excluding SFHs that start earlier than the age of the Universe at a given redshift" (2.3 Zhúlóng with robust photometric redshift)
- **PySersic** : Single and double Sersic morphological fits  
  > "(cite: Pasha2023JOSS) using two different strategies: 1) a single Sérsic profile and 2) double Sérsic profiles" (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
- **WebbPSF** : PSFs for morphological fitting  
  > "derived from the WebbPSF software (cite: Perrin2014) and rotated to match the position angle of the observations" (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
- **Bagpipes** : SED fitting of annuli and integrated photometry; redshift cross-check  
  > "Physical properties are estimated by fitting the UV-to-NIR SED from JWST and HST photometry" (3.2 A quiescent-like bulge and star-forming disk)
- **CIGALE** : Cross-check SED fit of integrated properties  
  > "which produces very consistent values within the errors" (3.3 Ultra-massive spiral galaxy with accelerated formation)

## Qualitative findings

- Spiral arms and clumps visible in residuals of the Sersic fits; arms extend across ~19 kpc  
  > "Zhúlóng shows evidence for a grand-design spiral arm structure, with high contrast primary arms extending from the bulge pointing north and south" (3.1 Appearance of bulge, disk, and spiral structures)
- The core is quiescent-like and the outer regions star-forming, consistent with inside-out growth  
  > "The absence of star formation activity in the core is consistent with the expectations of inside-out galaxy growth and quenching" (3.2 A quiescent-like bulge and star-forming disk)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The 1.2 mm ALMA map is the only long-wavelength data deep enough to constrain the FIR SED  
  > "with the exception of the ALMA 1.2 mm observations, the remaining long-wavelength observations are too shallow to significantly constrain the far-infrared SED shape" (2.2 Longer-wavelength observations)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Low-z (z~1.6) solution rejected: poor fit (chi-square 46) and traced to a probable foreground clump  
  > "has a large chi-square value ($\chi^2 = 46$), indicating a very poor fit." (Robustness of the redshift solution for Zhúlóng.)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Redshift is photometric only; spectroscopic confirmation is needed  
  > "we underline that this is still a photometric redshift." (2.3 Zhúlóng with robust photometric redshift)
- Limitation (authors): Clump 2 is not removed from the outer annulus fit  
  > "we cannot confirm whether it is a low-$z$ clump without spectroscopic observations and thus did not remove it from the analysis." (Robustness of the redshift solution for Zhúlóng.)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA plays a minor role: only a non-detection upper limit at 1.2 mm from an unpublished CHAMPS map
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Photometric-only redshift with possible low-z contamination in the outer annulus
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sersic parameter uncertainties look small and priors/sampler are not given
