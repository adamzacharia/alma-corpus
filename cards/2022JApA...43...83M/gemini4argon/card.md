(Rotational diagram analysis of NH2CN)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: Unblended and optically thin transitions only (excluded optically thick J = 8(7,1)-7(7,0), V = 1 transition)

This transition of NH$_{2}$CN was excluded from the rotation diagram analysis because the rotational diagram method is applicable only for optically thin emission lines." (Rotational diagram analysis of NH2CN)
   - assumption: Optically thin emission and Local Thermal Equilibrium (LTE), justified by inner core density ~ 7x10^7 cm^-3  
  > "Initially, we assumed that the detected NH$_{2}$CN spectra were optically thin and that they populated the Local Thermal Equilibrium (LTE) conditions." (Rotational diagram analysis of NH2CN); "The assumption of LTE condition was reasonable towards the G10 because the density of inner regions of the hot core was $\sim$7$\times$10$^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ (cite: rof11; gor20; man22)." (Rotational diagram analysis of NH2CN)
   - assumption: N(H2) = 1.3x10^23 cm^-2

## Qualitative findings

- The NH2CN emission peaks at the position of the 1.87 mm continuum and originates from the warm inner region of the hot core.  
  > "We noticed the emission map of NH$_{2}$CN has a peak at the position of the continuum." (Spatial distribution of NH2CN); "The integrated emission map indicated that the different transitions of NH$_{2}$CN molecule emit from the different locations of the warm inner region of the hot core due to the different upper state energy (E$_{up}$) of all unblended transitions."
