## Analysis recipe

1. **construct CHD2OH spectroscopic database**: Rotation-torsion lines below 500 GHz computed from the Coudert2021 global analysis for the first three torsional states; partition function tabulated 2.725-300 K; line intensity cutoff applied  
  > "All rotation-torsion lines within and between the first three torsional states" (Methods / Spectroscopy of CHD2OH); "Rotation-torsion lines with a frequency below 500 GHz were selected" (Methods / Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - parameter: Dipole components mu_x and mu_z from Amano1981; J up to 26; cutoff LOGSTR0 = LOGSTR1 = -9  
  > "where F is the frequency in MHz, and LOGSTR0 and LOGSTR1 are two dimensionless constants both set to -9" (Methods / Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - assumption: Vibrational partition contribution negligible (modes above 1000 cm-1)  
  > "the vibrational contribution to the partition function is negligible, because the vibrational modes are above 1000" (Methods / Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with 184 parallel transitions of Mukhopadhyay2016a; 32 transitions with discrepancies above 0.5 MHz reassigned  
  > "Table of the present paper lists the 32 transitions with a frequency discrepancy larger than 0.5 MHz" (Methods / Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
2. **extract single-position spectrum and build reference spectrum**: Single spectrum taken from continuum-subtracted cube at the one-beam SW offset of B; analysed with custom Python; reference LTE spectrum of other PILS molecules overlaid  
  > "Once this single position spectrum is extracted from the continuum-subtracted" (Methods / Spectral analysis); "This spectrum contains computed emission lines from all molecules detected with the ALMA-PILS survey at this position" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - software: Python (custom-made scripts)
   - parameter: v_LSR = 2.7 km/s applied (Jorgensen2011)  
  > "determined for this position is applied (cite: Jorgensen2011)" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: Reference spectrum computed under LTE  
  > "The computation is performed under the local thermal equilibrium (LTE) approximation." (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual overlay of reference spectrum on observed spectrum  
  > "Next, the observed spectrum is investigated at all covered rest frequencies of CHD2OH lines with the reference spectrum overlaid." (Methods / Spectral analysis)
3. **select non-blended optically thin lines**: Visual blending check; removal of blended lines and of optically thick lines (tau > 0.1 at Tex 50 or 300 K and N = 1.3e17 cm-2)  
  > "This check for blending is done through visual inspection" (Methods / Spectral analysis); "these are assumed to be lines that have" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: 554 lines covered; 525 unique rest frequencies; 105 unique frequencies retained  
  > "of which 525 have unique rest frequencies" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: 26 lines with predicted peak >14 mJy/beam for best-fit Tex and N  
  > "Out of 105 lines, 26 are predicted to have a peak intensity" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: Initial rough CHD2OH column density 1.3e17 cm-2 used to flag optical thickness  
  > "initial rough estimate of the column density of CHD2OH" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Non-blended non-detected lines also constrain the fit  
  > "Non-blended non-detected lines are of course also constraining the synthetic spectral fitting." (Methods / Spectral analysis)
4. **fit Tex and N with LTE synthetic spectrum by MCMC**: LTE synthetic spectrum with Sobolev/LVG optical depth correction and beam-filling factor; two emcee runs; likelihood summed over +/- FWHM/2 windows around the 105 lines  
  > "Finally, synthetic spectral fitting for CHD2OH is carried out with a newly developed routine based on the Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Python package EMCEE" (Methods / Spectral analysis); "the model is computed and compared to observations around each line in a range of" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - software: emcee, CASSIS formalism (LTE model), RADEX formalism (Sobolev/LVG)
   - parameter: Beam 0.5 arcsec, source size 0.5 arcsec, FWHM 1 km/s fixed (B); FWHM 2.2 km/s for A  
  > "Several parameters are fixed: beam size of 0.5 arcsec, source size of 0.5 arcsec, and line full width half-maximum (FWHM) of 1 km s" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: Run 1: 300 walkers, 250 steps; run 2: 300 walkers, 1000 steps; initial Tex in [50,400] K and N in [1e13,1e18] cm-2  
  > "The first has 300 walkers for each parameter with each walker taking 250 steps" (Methods / Spectral analysis); "the log-prior function is set to infinity outside of these ranges" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: First 10% of steps discarded as burn-in  
  > "the first 10% of the steps are discarded as this corresponds to the burn-in phase" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: LTE with single excitation temperature for all lines  
  > "the model assumptions (e.g., LTE with a single" (Results / Line profiles)
   - assumption: Gaussian beam and source distributions; partition function log-interpolated between tabulated temperatures  
  > "With both beam and source distributions being Gaussian" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: Model does not properly treat flat-topped profiles of optically thick lines  
  > "it does not properly account for flat-topped line profiles of optically thick lines" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: emcee affine-invariant ensemble sampler with default stretch moves; best-fit values are 50th percentiles of marginalised posteriors; errors are 16th and 84th percentile differences  
  > "The best-fitting values are considered the 50th percentiles of the samples in the marginalized distributions." (Methods / Spectral analysis); "The walkers undertake the default stretch moves of EMCEE" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
   - validation: Mean acceptance fraction 70% and corner plots for both sources; sufficiency of burn-in checked with autocorrelation times; fixed-Tex (150 and 300 K) runs  
  > "For the second MCMC run of the CHD2OH computation, the mean acceptance fraction of the 300 walkers is 70%" (Methods / Spectral analysis); "MCMC models for fixed temperatures of 150 and 300 K were ran while varying the column density" (Results / Line profiles)
5. **derive D/H ratio with statistical correction**: D/H of CH3OH derived from CHD2OH/CH3OH column density ratio with binomial statistical correction; CH3OH columns from literature  
  > "The associated D/H ratio with statistical correction is in the third row" (Results / Table of best-fitting parameters); "the number of indistinguishable combinations with i deuterium atoms at n potential sites" (Appendix / Statistical corrections)
   - parameter: Error of 20% on N gives 30% error on the ratio of two column densities  
  > "which gives an error of 30% on the ratio of two column densities" (Discussion / Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422)
   - assumption: Methanol column density from Jorgensen2018 (B) and Manigand2020 (A)  
  > "The last two rows give the methanol column densities adopted for the calculations of D/H ratios" (Results / Table of best-fitting parameters (table notes))
   - fitting: Error propagation of statistical corrections; no fitting  
  > "statistical error propagation based on the respective statistical corrections" (Discussion / Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422)
   - validation: Comparison of D/H ratios with other protostars and experiments  
  > "Fig. 5 shows the D/H ratio as measured in all the D-bearing molecules detected in the hot corino of I16293B" (Discussion / Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422)
6. **map CHD2OH emission**: Moment 0 maps of the 348.710 and 334.056 GHz lines; VINE maps using the velocity field from the CH3OH 337.519 GHz line, integrated over 25 spectral bins (~5 km/s)  
  > "the rest frequency of this line in every pixel is found by first determining the rest frequency of the bright methanol transition at 337.519 GHz" (Results / Integrated intensity maps); "the integration is performed over 25 spectral bins, corresponding to a" (Results / Integrated intensity maps)
   - parameter: Integration over 25 spectral bins  
  > "the integration is performed over 25 spectral bins" (Results / Integrated intensity maps)
   - assumption: Line is nearly optically thin (tau = 0.18 at B best-fit)  
  > "For the best-fitting parameters of the one-beam offset position from B, this line has" (Results / Integrated intensity maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Classical (non-VINE) map compared with VINE map for both lines  
  > "The morphology of the emission is somewhat more extended in the VINE map." (Appendix / Integrated intensity maps details)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sensitive, unbiased ALMA-PILS survey data at a narrow-line position in a well-characterised nearby source  
  > "The proximity of IRAS 16293-2422 enables spatially resolved observations at high sensitivity to be carried out" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Newly developed MCMC fitting of LTE synthetic spectra to many optically thin lines simultaneously  
  > "This work makes use of newly developed MCMC spectral fitting routines for the derivation of the column density and excitation temperature of a species" (Introduction)
- Alternative: Fixed excitation temperatures of 150 K and 300 K tested; lower D/H (by at most a factor 1.6) but conclusions unchanged  
  > "the conclusions of this paper are not changed even when considering this hypothetical broader range of D/H ratios" (Results / Line profiles)
- Alternative: Classical (non-VINE) moment 0 maps compared with VINE maps; VINE judged better near source A  
  > "Both of these points indicate that the VINE map is doing a better job at recovering the line emission near source A." (Appendix / Integrated intensity maps details)
- How they decided it was enough: Uncertainties inflated to pessimistic 10% and 20% on Tex and N because MCMC errors are very small  
  > "pessimistic errors of 10% and 20% on T_ex and N, respectively, are adopted in order to ensure that the uncertainties are not underestimated" (Results / Line profiles)
- Limitation (authors): MCMC errors are very small and do not capture model and calibration uncertainties  
  > "These errors are very small for well-converged runs and do not take all origins of uncertainty in observations into account" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Fitting model does not properly account for flat-topped line profiles of optically thick lines  
  > "it does not properly account for flat-topped line profiles of optically thick lines" (Methods / Spectral analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Exact uncertainty on parameters is hard to quantify  
  > "It is hard to quantify the exact uncertainty on the parameters" (Results / Line profiles)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Fitted Tex (90 K for B) is lower than the about 300 K from prior CH3OH and CH3OD work; the authors attribute part of this to high-Tex line predictions that are not seen, so the result depends on high-excitation lines.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Statistical MCMC errors (about 1-2%) are far smaller than the adopted 10%/20% pessimistic errors; the quoted D/H uncertainty is set by the assumed errors, not by the fit.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CH3OH column densities from other papers drive the D/H values and are based on different methods for A and B.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Comparisons across sources rely on heterogeneous literature analyses (different isotopic ratios, optical-depth treatments and beams).
