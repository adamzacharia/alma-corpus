## Analysis recipe

1. **Construct spectroscopic database and partition function for CHD2OH**: Calculated rotation-torsion energy levels, line frequencies, line strengths using dipole moment components (mu_x = -1.37 D, mu_z = 0.8956 D), and partition function Q_rot-tors from 2.75 to 300 K up to J=26 and frequencies below 500 GHz with an intensity cutoff.  
  > "The results of the global line position analysis of (cite: Coudert2021) were used to build a spectroscopic database for CHD2OH. All rotation-torsion lines within and between the first three torsional states, 0 <= vt <= 2, were computed up to J=26." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH); "Line strengths were evaluated taking a dipole moment function with two non-vanishing constant components along the x and z axes of the molecule-fixed axis system such that the xz plane remains parallel to the COH group. The values are mu_x=-1.37 and mu_z=0.8956 D" (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - parameter: mu_x = -1.37 D, mu_z = 0.8956 D; frequency < 500 GHz; J <= 26; LOGSTR0 = -9, LOGSTR1 = -9; minimum calculated error = 10 kHz  
  > "The values are mu_x=-1.37 and mu_z=0.8956 D as reported by (cite: Amano1981)." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH); "LOGSTR0 and LOGSTR1 are two dimensionless constants both set to -9." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH); "A minimum value of 10 kHz was selected for the calculated error (ERR)." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - assumption: Vibrational contribution to partition function is negligible (modes > 1000 cm^-1)  
  > "It should be noted that the vibrational contribution to the partition function is negligible, because the vibrational modes are above 1000 cm^-1." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with table 2 of Mukhopadhyay (2016a), identifying 32 transitions with frequency discrepancies > 0.5 MHz caused by past assignment errors.  
  > "A comparison between the present spectroscopic database and a previously available line list is shown in Table [tbl:comp_dtb]." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
2. **Filter CHD2OH lines for synthetic spectral fitting**: Constructed an LTE reference spectrum of all known detected species in ALMA-PILS, visually identified and discarded any CHD2OH lines blended with known species, and removed lines predicted to be optically thick (tau > 0.1 at Tex = 50 K or 300 K with N = 1.3 x 10^17 cm^-2), yielding 105 unique lines.  
  > "This check for blending is done through visual inspection; and all lines that have any level of blending are removed from further synthetic spectrum fitting. Then, optically thick lines of CHD2OH are removed from further synthetic spectrum fitting." (2.3 Spectral analysis); "After the removal of blended and optically thick lines, 105 unique line frequencies remain that are used for synthetic spectral fitting" (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - software: custom Python scripts
   - parameter: tau > 0.1 cutoff checked at Tex = 50 K and 300 K with initial column density N = 1.3 x 10^17 cm^-2  
  > "These are assumed to be lines that have tau>0.1 either at Tex=50 or 300 K at N=1.3x10^17 cm^-2 (initial rough estimate of the column density of CHD2OH)." (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: Local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) for emission modeling  
  > "The computation is performed under the local thermal equilibrium (LTE) approximation." (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Overlay reference spectrum on observed data to ensure no blending.  
  > "Next, the observed spectrum is investigated at all covered rest frequencies of CHD2OH lines with the reference spectrum overlaid." (2.3 Spectral analysis)
3. **Derive excitation temperature and column density with MCMC synthetic spectral fitting**: MCMC sampling using EMCEE minimizing a chi-squared like negative log-likelihood across +/- FWHM/2 around all 105 selected line frequencies simultaneously. Two consecutive runs: Run 1 (300 walkers, 250 steps) exploring wide priors, Run 2 (300 walkers, 1000 steps) initiated around best-fit of Run 1. Burn-in of 10% discarded, thinning by 0.3%.  
  > "Finally, synthetic spectral fitting for CHD2OH is carried out with a newly developed routine based on the Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Python package EMCEE" (2.3 Spectral analysis); "Two runs of MCMC are executed. The first has 300 walkers for each parameter with each walker taking 250 steps... The second has 300 walkers for each parameter with each taking 1000 steps" (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - software: EMCEE, CASSIS formalisms
   - parameter: Source size = 0.5'', beam size = 0.5'', beam-filling factor = 0.5; FWHM = 1.0 km/s (source B) and 2.2 km/s (source A); prior Tex in [50, 400] K, N in [10^13, 10^18] cm^-2  
  > "Several parameters are fixed: beam size of 0.5'', source size of 0.5'', and line full width half-maximum (FWHM) of 1 km s^-1." (2.3 Spectral analysis); "specifically: Tex in [50,400] K and N in [10^13,10^18] cm^-2 (the log-prior function is set to infinity outside of these ranges)." (2.3 Spectral analysis); "All other input parameters are kept the same; only FWHM is changed to 2.2 km s^-1 as deduced from previous analysis of this position" (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: LTE excitation with single Tex; optical depth correction via Sobolev / LVG approximation beta = (1 - e^-tau)/tau  
  > "The code accounts for optical depth by considering the Sobolev or the large velocity gradient (LVG) approximation: N_up; op. thick = N_up; op. thin x beta, where beta = (1-e^-tau)/tau" (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: EMCEE affine-invariant stretch moves; negative log-likelihood sum over line frequencies and +/- 0.5*FWHM velocity windows  
  > "The walkers undertake the default “stretch moves” of EMCEE (cite: GoodmanWeare2010). The negative likelihood function being minimized is: -1/2 x sum_{nu_line} sum (I_observed - I_model)^2" (2.3 Spectral analysis)
   - validation: Checked walker convergence, autocorrelation times, mean acceptance fractions (70% for both sources), and inspected corner plots.  
  > "The output of the second MCMC run is checked for convergence and high values of acceptance ratios. For the second MCMC run of the CHD2OH computation, the mean acceptance fraction of the 300 walkers is 70%" (2.3 Spectral analysis)
4. **Calculate molecular deuteration fraction (D/H ratio)**: Applied combinatorial statistical correction to CHD2OH / CH3OH column density ratio: CHD2OH/CH3OH = 3 * (D/H)^2, yielding (D/H)_CH3OH = sqrt(CHD2OH / (3 * CH3OH)).  
  > "CHD2OH / CH3OH = 3 (D/H)_CH3OH^2" (8 Statistical corrections to abundance ratios for D/H ratio derivations); "D/H of CH3OH (w/ stat. corr.)" (Table 2)
   - parameter: Statistical factor = 3; N(CH3OH) = (1.0 +/- 0.2) x 10^19 cm^-2 for B, (1.3 +/- 0.4) x 10^19 cm^-2 for A  
  > "N(CH3OH) (cm^-2) : (1.0 +/- 0.2) x 10^19; (1.3 +/- 0.4) x 10^19" (Table 2); "CHD2OH / CH3OH = 3 (D/H)_CH3OH^2" (8 Statistical corrections to abundance ratios for D/H ratio derivations)
   - assumption: Statistical independence of H/D substitution across sites in the methyl group  
  > "The probability of a deuterium atom replacing a hydrogen atom at a specific location in a certain functional group (-XHn) is a statistically independent event." (8 Statistical corrections to abundance ratios for D/H ratio derivations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested sensitivity of D/H to Tex by running fixed-temperature models at Tex = 150 K and 300 K.  
  > "To investigate this further, MCMC models for fixed temperatures of 150 and 300 K were ran while varying the column density." (3.1 Line profiles)
5. **Generate integrated intensity (VINE) maps**: Constructed velocity-corrected integrated emission maps by shifting the rest frequency in each pixel according to a velocity map derived from the CH3OH 337.519 GHz line, integrating over 25 spectral bins (~5 km/s).  
  > "Consequently, the rest frequency of this line in every pixel is found by first determining the rest frequency of the bright methanol transition at 337.519 GHz, thereby determining a velocity map of IRAS 16293-2422 (fig. 4 of (cite: Calcutt2018a)). This method was developed by (cite: Calcutt2018a) and produces so-called Velocity-corrected INtegrated emission (VINE) maps." (3.2 Integrated intensity maps)
   - parameter: 25 spectral bins (~5 km s^-1 integration range); reference line CH3OH at 337.519 GHz  
  > "Here, the integration is performed over 25 spectral bins, corresponding to a ~5 km s^-1 range, which is mostly sufficient to encompass the entire line profile near both sources." (3.2 Integrated intensity maps)
   - assumption: Velocity structure traced by CH3OH 337.519 GHz is representative of CHD2OH kinematics  
  > "Consequently, the rest frequency of this line in every pixel is found by first determining the rest frequency of the bright methanol transition at 337.519 GHz, thereby determining a velocity map of IRAS 16293-2422" (3.2 Integrated intensity maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared VINE maps against classical constant-velocity moment 0 maps at 14 spatial positions.  
  > "Fig. 16 displays the “classical” (non-VINE) integrated intensity map, in which a constant vLSR of 2.7 km s^-1 is applied... This panel can be compared to the VINE map of the same line in Fig. 2." (7 Integrated intensity maps details)

## Qualitative findings

- Di-deuterated molecules consistently have higher D/H ratios than their mono-deuterated counterparts in low-mass protostars, indicating successive H-D substitution on grain surfaces rather than changing deuteration environments.  
  > "Di-deuterated molecules consistently have higher D/H ratios than their mono-deuterated counterparts in all low-mass protostars, which may be a natural consequence of H-D substitution reactions as seen in laboratory experiments." (abstract)
- Deuteration levels in IRAS 16293-2422 are comparable to other low-mass protostars (L483, SVS13-A, NGC 1333-IRAS2A, -IRAS4A, -IRAS4B), refuting the notion that IRAS 16293-2422 is anomalously deuterium-rich.  
  > "Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422 is not higher than in other low-mass star-forming regions (L483, SVS13-A, NGC 1333-IRAS2A, -IRAS4A, and -IRAS4B)." (abstract)
- The D/H ratios of CHD2OH and HDS in comet 67P/C-G are more than an order of magnitude lower than in IRAS 16293-2422, indicating a warmer natal cloud, lower initial D abundance, or chemical reprocessing for the early Solar System.  
  > "Fig. 6 shows the cometary D/H ratio of D2O, CHD2OH, NH2D, and HDS are significantly (at least one order of magnitude) lower than that in I16293B." (4.4 Comparison to cometary measurements); "The Solar System’s natal cloud, as traced by comet 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko, may have had a lower initial abundance of D, been warmer than the cloud of IRAS 16293-2422, or been partially reprocessed." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA-PILS provides wide, unbiased Band 7 spectral coverage at high sensitivity and angular resolution (0.5'') resolving the individual hot corinos of A and B while avoiding line confusion limit.  
  > "These data cover the 329-363 GHz frequency range (ALMA Band 7) at a spatial resolution of ~0.5'' (70 au; the applied uniform circular restoring beam). This allows for sources A and B to be studied individually" (2.1 Observations)
- Why this method: Analyzing a one-beam offset position reduces dust attenuation and optical depth while maintaining high molecular column densities; MCMC simultaneously explores Tex and N across all non-blended optically thin lines.  
  > "This position is favorable for spectral analysis as it displays narrow lines, which reduces blending, and probes the hot dense inner hot corino around source B, while avoiding self-absorption and strong dust absorption on source." (2.3 Spectral analysis)
- Alternative: Fitting lines with optical depth tau > 0.1 was rejected because the simple synthetic model does not account for flat-topped profiles of optically thick lines.  
  > "However, it does not properly account for flat-topped line profiles of optically thick lines, therefore being most-suitable for the analysis of optically thin lines." (2.3 Spectral analysis)
- Alternative: Rigid-rotor approximation for CHD2OH was rejected due to large amplitude internal rotation of the methyl group.  
  > "Di-deuterated methanol CHD2OH is a non-rigid species displaying a large amplitude internal rotation of its methyl group." (2.2 Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): 0.5'' angular resolution cannot separate binary components A1 and A2 of IRAS 16293-2422 A.  
  > "This allows for sources A and B to be studied individually; however, this is not enough to spatially separate A1 from A2." (2.1 Observations)
- Limitation (authors): LTE modeling with a single Tex is a simplified model; pessimistic uncertainties of 10% on Tex and 20% on N were adopted.  
  > "However, the model assumptions (e.g., LTE with a single Tex) have much higher uncertainties and so do the data themselves (calibration uncertainty of 5%)... pessimistic errors of 10% and 20% on Tex and N, respectively, are adopted" (3.1 Line profiles)
- Limitation (authors): Moment 0 maps for source A suffer from line blending and severe velocity gradients.  
  > "Around source A it is still hard to compute an accurate integrated intensity and therefore, the shown map should be interpreted with caution" (3.2 Integrated intensity maps)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Excitation temperature of CHD2OH (90 K) is noticeably lower than that found for other methanol isotopologs (~300 K); high-excitation lines overpredicted by the 300 K model may be due to remaining spectroscopic errors.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Derivation of column densities of main isotopologs used as denominators often depends on assumed carbon/oxygen isotope ratios or optically thick corrections.
