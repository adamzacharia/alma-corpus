## Analysis recipe

1. **build CHD2OH spectroscopic database**: Compute rotation-torsion line list and partition function from the global line position analysis of Coudert2021, with JPL-style intensity cutoff; published at CDS  
  > "The results of the global line position analysis of (cite: Coudert2021) were used to build a spectroscopic database for CHD2OH." (Spectroscopy of CHD2OH); "The line list is published as an electronic Table at the Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS) with association to this A&A article." (Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - parameter: Lines below 500 GHz; LOGSTR0 and LOGSTR1 both -9; minimum error 10 kHz  
  > "Rotation-torsion lines with a frequency below 500 GHz were selected" (Spectroscopy of CHD2OH); "A minimum value of 10 kHz was selected for the calculated error (ERR)." (Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - assumption: Vibrational contribution to partition function negligible  
  > "It should be noted that the vibrational contribution to the partition function is negligible, because the vibrational modes are above 1000 cm" (Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with Mukhopadhyay2016a line list revealing assignment errors  
  > "These discrepancies are due to assignment errors and can be as large as 788 MHz." (Spectroscopy of CHD2OH)
2. **extract spectrum at offset positions**: Extract single-position spectrum from continuum-subtracted cube at one-beam offset from B (and A), apply Doppler shift  
  > "Once this single position spectrum is extracted from the continuum-subtracted (based on methodology described in (cite: Jorgensen2016)) data cube, it is analyzed with custom-made Python scripts." (Spectral analysis)
   - software: custom Python scripts
   - parameter: v_LSR = 2.7 km s-1 (B), 0.8 km s-1 (A)  
  > "determined for this position is applied (cite: Jorgensen2011)" (Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: Position probes freshly desorbed hot corino ices and avoids dust absorption  
  > "probes the hot dense inner hot corino around source B, while avoiding self-absorption and strong dust absorption on source" (Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **select non-blended optically thin lines**: Overlay LTE reference spectrum of all known PILS molecules, visually reject any blended CHD2OH line, then reject lines with tau>0.1  
  > "This check for blending is done through visual inspection; and all lines that have any level of blending are removed from further synthetic spectrum fitting." (Spectral analysis); "Then, optically thick lines of CHD2OH are removed from further synthetic spectrum fitting." (Spectral analysis)
   - software: custom Python scripts, CDMS, JPL catalog
   - parameter: tau>0.1 at Tex=50 or 300 K and N=1.3e17 cm-2 rejected; 554 lines covered, 525 unique, 105 retained  
  > "After the removal of blended and optically thick lines, 105 unique line frequencies remain that are used for synthetic spectral fitting" (Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: LTE for reference spectrum  
  > "The computation is performed under the local thermal equilibrium (LTE) approximation." (Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **fit Tex and N with LTE MCMC**: LTE synthetic spectrum fit to all selected lines (detected and non-detected) simultaneously with emcee, two runs  
  > "Finally, synthetic spectral fitting for CHD2OH is carried out with a newly developed routine based on the Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Python package EMCEE" (Spectral analysis); "Two runs of MCMC are executed." (Spectral analysis)
   - software: emcee, custom Python scripts
   - parameter: Fixed beam 0.5'', source size 0.5'', FWHM 1 km s-1 (B) / 2.2 km s-1 (A)  
  > "Several parameters are fixed" (Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: Run 1: 300 walkers, 250 steps; run 2: 300 walkers, 1000 steps; 10% burn-in  
  > "The first has 300 walkers for each parameter with each walker taking 250 steps" (Spectral analysis); "The second has 300 walkers for each parameter with each taking 1000 steps" (Spectral analysis); "In both runs, the first 10% of the steps are discarded as this corresponds to the burn-in phase of the computation" (Spectral analysis)
   - assumption: LTE, single Tex, LVG beta escape factor, Gaussian beam/source with filling factor 0.5  
  > "It is thought that this is a good approximation, because the densities are high and molecules are expected to be thermalized" (Spectral analysis); "the dilution of the observed emission is accounted for by the beam-filling factor" (Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: emcee stretch moves; flat priors Tex in [50,400] K, N in [1e13,1e18] cm-2; chi-square-like likelihood over ±FWHM/2 around each line; 50th percentile best fit, 16th/84th errors  
  > "(the log-prior function is set to infinity outside of these ranges)" (Spectral analysis); "The walkers undertake the default “stretch moves” of EMCEE (cite: GoodmanWeare2010)." (Spectral analysis); "The best-fitting values are considered the 50th percentiles of the samples in the marginalized distributions." (Spectral analysis)
   - validation: Autocorrelation times, acceptance fraction 70%, corner plots; adopted 10%/20% pessimistic errors  
  > "that this is sufficient is subsequently double checked with the autocorrelation times" (Spectral analysis); "the mean acceptance fraction of the 300 walkers is 70%" (Spectral analysis)
5. **test sensitivity of N to Tex**: MCMC runs with Tex fixed at 150 and 300 K varying only N (source B)  
  > "To investigate this further, MCMC models for fixed temperatures of 150 and 300 K were ran while varying the column density." (Line profiles)
   - software: emcee
   - fitting: Same MCMC routine  
  > "The same spectral analysis methodology is applied to this position as for the offset from source B position." (Spectral analysis)
   - validation: Comparison of predicted high-excitation lines with observations  
  > "a couple of high excitation transitions" (Line profiles)
6. **map CHD2OH emission**: VINE integrated intensity maps of 348.710 and 334.056 GHz lines, using per-pixel velocity from the 337.519 GHz methanol line; compared with classical moment 0 maps  
  > "Here, the integration is performed over 25 spectral bins" (Integrated intensity maps); "the rest frequency of this line in every pixel is found by first determining the rest frequency of the bright methanol transition at 337.519 GHz" (Integrated intensity maps)
   - parameter: 25 spectral bins (~5 km s-1)  
  > "Here, the integration is performed over 25 spectral bins" (Integrated intensity maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with classical moment 0 maps and line profiles at 14 positions  
  > "Both of these points indicate that the VINE map is doing a better job at recovering the line emission near source A." (Integrated intensity maps details)
7. **derive D/H ratios and compare**: Compute D/H with statistical corrections using literature CH3OH column densities; compare with other D-bearing species, other protostars, lab experiments, comet 67P  
  > "Fig. 5 shows the D/H ratio as measured in all the D-bearing molecules detected in the hot corino of I16293B, including the respective statistical corrections" (Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422)
   - assumption: 20% error on each N giving 30% on ratios; main-isotopolog N from minor isotopologs with local ISM isotopic ratios  
  > "the error bars on all the values of I16293B are a result of the 20% error on each N, which gives an error of 30% on the ratio of two column densities" (Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422); "For methanol, the column density is based on its minor oxygen isotopolog and an assumed local ISM" (Main isotopolog column densities in I16293)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CHD2OH emission is compact towards both A and B hot corinos  
  > "Fig. 2 illustrates that CHD2OH emission is compact towards A and B" (Integrated intensity maps)
- No trend of D/H with functional group or molecular family; tentative trend for mono-deuterated species with size  
  > "No apparent trend of the D/H ratio with functional group is seen, as was already realized by (cite: Jorgensen2018)." (Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422)
- D/H ratios in A and B very similar molecule by molecule  
  > "The D/H ratios measured in I16293A are very similar to those in I16293B on a molecule by molecule basis" (Deuteration in IRAS 16293-2422)
- Fitted Tex lower than ~300 K of other methanol isotopologs, driven by over-predicted high-excitation lines  
  > "In the end, this set of lines drives the best-fitting T_ex to the lower values" (Line profiles)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Proximity of IRAS 16293-2422 allows spatially resolved sensitive searches; offset position has narrow lines  
  > "The proximity of IRAS 16293-2422 enables spatially resolved observations at high sensitivity to be carried out, making it an optimal target for searches for minor chemical constituents." (Introduction); "This position is favorable for spectral analysis as it displays narrow lines, which reduces blending" (Spectral analysis)
- Why this method: LTE assumed valid due to high densities; model suited to optically thin lines  
  > "It is thought that this is a good approximation, because the densities are high and molecules are expected to be thermalized" (Spectral analysis)
- Alternative: Fixed Tex of 150 and 300 K models tested; would lower D/H by at most a factor 1.6  
  > "the conclusions of this paper are not changed even when considering this hypothetical broader range of D/H ratios" (Line profiles)
- Alternative: Classical constant-vLSR moment 0 maps compared with VINE maps  
  > "Fig. 16 displays the “classical” (non-VINE) integrated intensity map" (Integrated intensity maps details)
- How they decided it was enough: Convergence checks via autocorrelation times and acceptance fractions  
  > "The output of the second MCMC run is checked for convergence and high values of acceptance ratios." (Spectral analysis)
- Limitation (authors): MCMC errors underestimate true uncertainty; pessimistic 10%/20% errors adopted  
  > "These uncertainties are not taken into account in the MCMC computation." (Line profiles)
- Limitation (authors): LTE model does not handle flat-topped optically thick profiles
