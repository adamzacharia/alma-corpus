## Reasoning

- Why this data: Band 7 observations originally targeted the massive protostellar accretion outburst  
  > "The high-mass star-formation region G358.93–0.03 was observed using ALMA band 7 receivers to study the massive protostellar accretion outburst (ID: 2019.1.00768.S., PI: Crystal Brogan)." (Observations and data reduction)
- Why this method: LTE justified by the high warm-inner-region gas density  
  > "The LTE assumption is reasonable in the inner region of the G358.93–0.03 MM1 because the maximum gas density of the warm inner region of the hot core is 1$\times$10$^{8}$ cm$^{-3}$ (cite: ste21)." (Detection of EG towards the G358.93–0.03 MM1)
- Alternative: GA hydrogenation through reactions 2 and 3 considered, with the luminosity-dependent EG/GA variation arguing against a simple direct link  
  > "Similarly, reactions 2 and 3 indicate that the subsequential hydrogenation of GA on the grain surface produces the EG (cite: fed15; ch16; gar13; cou18)." (Possible formation pathways of EG in HMCs); "we observe that the EG/GA ratio varies by more than an order of magnitude (1 to $>$15) with increasing luminosity, which suggests that there is no direct link between EG and GA." (Possible formation pathways of EG in HMCs)
- Alternative: Published two-phase EG/GA models do not match the observed ratios  
  > "the modelled EG/GA ratios in (cite: cou18) cannot match any sources because the modelled EG/GA ratios with respect to luminosities are larger than the observed EG/GA ratios for different high- and low-mass sources." (Comparison of the EG/GA ratio in different star-forming regions)
- Alternative: Both fast and medium three-phase warm-up models provide close matches, depending on the abundance reference
