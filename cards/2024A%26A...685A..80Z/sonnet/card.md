## Science

- Fields: high_z_galaxies
- Question: Are there [C II]-emitting clumps (parent molecular clouds) in a lensed z~3.4 galaxy that hosts UV clumps, and what are their gas masses and depletion times?  
  > "We aimed to observe the [C II] emission of individual clumps that, unlike the UV, is not affected by dust extinction, to probe their nature and cold gas content." (abstract)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Model UV continuum of HST image**: GALFIT Sérsic diffuse component plus PSF/Sérsic clump components; BCG subtracted; fluxes divided by magnification  
  > "We then modeled the 2D surface brightness of our target with a 2D Sérsic profile (the galaxy “diffuse” component) and subtracted the best-fit model" (3.1 Modeling the UV continuum 2D light profile)
   - software: GALFIT, SExtractor
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals consistent with noise; repeated on M2  
  > "We repeated this analysis independently on M2 and reached consistent conclusions." (3.1 Modeling the UV continuum 2D light profile)
2. **Extract [C II] line and integrated map**: Iterative uv-plane 2D Gaussian fit (uv_fit) channel by channel, extract 1D spectrum, average best channels, refit position until convergence  
  > "We modeled our target’s emission in the *uv* plane with the GILDAS task `uv_fit`, adopting a two-dimensional (2D) Gaussian profile in all four sidebands and channel per channel." (3.3 [C II] emission line map)
   - software: GILDAS
   - fitting: Gaussian fit of 1D spectrum for redshift  
  > "we adopt the redshift obtained from the 1D Gaussian fit" (3.3 [C II] emission line map)
   - validation: Redshift agrees with SINFONI within 1 sigma; M2 gives consistent results  
  > "found that they agree within $1 \sigma$, increasing the reliability of the detection" (3.3 [C II] emission line map)
3. **Continuum upper limits**: Averaged line-free channels; 5σ upper limit from uv-plane Gaussian or PSF fit with fixed position  
  > "we report the total flux density upper limit that we estimated as the 5$\sigma$ uncertainty obtained when fitting, in the *uv* plane, the Fourier Transform of a 2D Gaussian model" (3.2 Continuum emission map)
   - software: GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Identify clumps in channel maps**: Natural-weighted channel maps; ≥4σ peaks within 1/3 primary beam with >3σ overlapping emission in ≥2 adjacent 20.3 km/s channels  
  > "identified all the $\geq 4\sigma$ emissions within a radius corresponding to 1/3 of the primary beam" (3.5 Identifying clumps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Symmetry between M1 and M2; mock observations; noise-peak probability  
  > "We calculated the probability of having two noise peaks detected at $> 3.5\, \sigma$, within $0.35\ensuremath{''} - 0.45\ensuremath{''}$ from the galaxy barycenter, and with a symmetric position" (3.7.2 Probability)
5. **Measure clump [C II] fluxes**: uv-plane fit of 2D PSF per channel with free centre (method 3), summed over adjacent channels; compared to image-plane and Gaussian fits; corrected by average magnification  
  > "We decided to adopt the flux measurements obtained by fitting clumps with PSF models" (3.6 Measuring clumps [C II] flux)
   - software: GILDAS
   - assumption: Clumps unresolved  
  > "as they are expect to be unresolved at the resolution of our observations" (3.6 Measuring clumps [C II] flux)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Five flux methods compared; conclusions unchanged  
  > "Even when adopting fluxes obtained with the other reported methods, the conclusions of our work do not change substantially." (3.6 Measuring clumps [C II] flux)
6. **Compute SFR and molecular gas mass**: SFR from UV 1500Å luminosity (Kennicutt98, Chabrier IMF); Mmol = alpha_[CII] × L_[CII] with alpha = 31 Msun/Lsun  
  > "We considered a [C II] luminosity-to-gas mass conversion factor $\mathrm{\alpha_{[C\,{\footnotesize II}]}} = 31\, \mathrm{M_\odot/L_\odot}$" (4.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: No dust correction of UV; [C II] emission mostly from molecular gas  
  > "we assume that the molecular gas is the main contributor to the [C II] emission" (4.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Galaxy SFR agrees with Hβ-based SFR  
  > "Our total, integrated SFR estimate is in agreement with that reported by (cite: Iani2021)" (4.1 Star formation rate)
7. **Kinematics and Jeans length**: PV diagrams and moment maps with immoments (2.5σ threshold); Jeans length from sigma and Sigma_gas  
  > "We adopted a $2.5\sigma$ threshold above the r.m.s. noise level." (4.3 Dynamical properties)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Rotation contribution to dispersion neglected; sigma ~20 km/s  
  > "In the following, the contribution of rotation to the observed velocity dispersion is neglected." (4.3 Dynamical properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Depletion time and SK plane**: t_dep = Sigma_mol/Sigma_SFR compared to literature at ~400 pc radius  
  > "$t\mathrm{_{dep} \sim \Sigma_{mol}/\Sigma_{SFR} \sim M_{H2}/SFR}$" (5.1 Gas depletion time)
   - assumption: constant SFR  
  > "assuming a constant SFR" (5.1 Gas depletion time)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- [C II] emission is ~1.8 times more extended than the UV; peak coincides with the brightest UV clump; SW and C clumps are UV-dark  
  > "The \[C II\] also shows a diffuse component largely overlapping with the UV continuum, despite being $\sim 1.8$ times more extended" (3.4 Qualitative comparison)
- Galaxy is dispersion-dominated, suggesting clumps form via interaction/merger rather than isolated disk instability  
  > "This seems to be a dispersion-dominated galaxy" (4.3 Dynamical properties)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Lensing plus [C II] avoids dust extinction and gives resolution to probe clump gas  
  > "To detect the gas reservoir of young clumps, we targeted a $z \sim 3.4$ galaxy lensed by the foreground cluster Abell 2895, hosting four UV-bright clumps." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Gaussian clump models, image-plane and other uv fits were tested; PSF free-center adopted  
  > "We decided to adopt the flux measurements obtained by fitting clumps with PSF models" (3.6 Measuring clumps [C II] flux)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Clump C is tentative and needs confirmation; dynamical modelling is beyond scope  
  > "More observations with higher S/N are needed to confirm it." (3.5 Identifying clumps)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mmol relies on a single alpha_[CII] with 0.3 dex scatter and an assumption that [C II] is molecular
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Clumps unresolved; sizes are upper limits so surface densities are uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Clump detection significance is low (~3-4 sigma); noise-peak probabilities rely on simulations
- Caveat (inferred, ours): UV SFR uncorrected for dust and traces ~100 Myr, so depletion times are crude
