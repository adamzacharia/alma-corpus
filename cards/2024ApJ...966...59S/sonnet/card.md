## Analysis recipe

1. **Pipeline calibration and continuum imaging**: Pipeline calibration, flag channels, average to 125 MHz, phase-only self-cal where helpful, tclean multiscale imaging with uv-tapered versions  
  > "The continuum images were generated using the `‘tclean’` task with multiscale imaging" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust 0.5/1/2 per source  
  > "the Briggs `‘robust’` weighting parameters being 0.5 for 2M0434, 2M0436, 2M0450 and 2M0508, 1 for 2M0412 and 2 for CIDA 12" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked CO/continuum flux variability over time for 2M0450 and CIDA 12  
  > "After performing time series measurement of the fluxes for 2M0450 and CIDA 12, no significant flux variations are found during our observations." (Footnote 1)
2. **Visibility-plane fitting of dust morphology**: Fit Gaussian profile (2M0450, CIDA 12) or radially asymmetric Gaussian ring(s) (others) with inclination, PA, offsets; galario for model visibilities  
  > "We then use `galario` (cite: tazzari2018) to transform each model image into complex visibilities sampled at the same $(u,v)$ points as those in the observations" (3.1 Visibility Fitting of Dust Morphology)
   - software: galario, emcee
   - parameter: 50 walkers, 10000 steps; model pixel 3 mas  
  > "with 50 walkers and 10000 steps" (3.1 Visibility Fitting of Dust Morphology)
   - assumption: Uniform priors; inclination prior ∝ sin i  
  > "We assumed a uniform prior probability distribution for the parameters above" (3.1 Visibility Fitting of Dust Morphology)
   - fitting: Likelihood L ∝ exp(-chi^2/2); emcee MCMC; medians with 16th/84th percentiles after discarding 10 autocorrelation times  
  > "with the reference values adopted as the median values of posterior samples and their uncertainties estimated from the 16th and 84th percentiles" (3.1 Visibility Fitting of Dust Morphology)
   - validation: Model vs observed in visibility and image planes; no residuals above 5 sigma  
  > "All best-fit model images show reasonable matches with observations, with no residuals above $5\sigma$." (3.1 Visibility Fitting of Dust Morphology)
3. **Test for hidden small cavities (Nuker) and central emission**: Fit Nuker profile to 2M0450 and CIDA 12; compare via BIC; add central point source to 2M0436 ring model  
  > "However, the Nuker profile has more parameters than the Gaussian profile, which results in a larger Bayesian Information Criterion" (3.2.2 Potential cavities)
   - software: galario, emcee
   - parameter: Nuker priors: log10 alpha U(0,2), beta U(0,10), gamma approx uniform (-3,2); point flux U(0,1) mJy  
  > "$p(\log_{10}\alpha)=\mathcal{U}(0,2),\ p(\beta)=\mathcal{U}(0,10)$" (9 Nuker fitting results)
   - fitting: Same MCMC procedure as step 2  
  > "The modeling procedure follows Section [3.1](#subsec:result_uvfit)" (9 Nuker fitting results)
   - validation: BIC preference keeps the Gaussian profile for smooth disks  
  > "Hence, we still treat 2M0450 and CIDA 12 as non-structured disks in this work." (3.2.2 Potential cavities)
4. **Derive flux, dust mass, dust sizes**: Integrate model intensity for flux and R68/R90; optically thin mass formula  
  > "The dust disk sizes are defined to be a radius that encloses 68% and 90% of total fluxes" (3.4 Millimeter Flux, Dust Disk Mass and Size)
   - assumption: Optically thin, T_dust = 20 K, kappa = 2.3 cm2/g (nu/230 GHz)^0.4, Gaia DR3 distances, 10% flux calibration error  
  > "$T_{\rm dust}$ is assumed at $20\ {\rm K}$ (e.g., (cite: ansdell2016))" (3.4 Millimeter Flux, Dust Disk Mass and Size)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CIDA 12 model flux compared with literature 1.16 mJy  
  > "it is consistent with $1.16^{+0.09}_{-0.09}$ mJy reported in (cite: akeson2019)" (3.4 Millimeter Flux, Dust Disk Mass and Size)
5. **CO fluxes and gas disk sizes**: Elliptical extraction regions sized from cumulative flux, with dust inclination and PA; noise from 1000 random regions in line-free channels; gas R90 like dust  
  > "as the standard deviation of integrated fluxes estimated from 1000 randomly distributed elliptical regions" (3.5 CO emission)
   - assumption: Gas radii are lower limits due to cloud absorption and resolved-out emission  
  > "we treat the gas radii $R_{\rm gas, 90\%}$ as lower limits" (3.5 CO emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 3 sigma upper limits for non-detections  
  > "as well as the $3\sigma$ upper limits for the non-detections" (3.5 CO emission)
6. **SED radiative transfer for micron-grain cavity sizes**: RADMC-3D flared disk with BT-Settl star; grid over Rin, beta, H100; p=-1, Rout=R90 mm  
  > "We fix $p$ to -1 and $R_{\rm out}$ to the $R_{90\%}$ in millimeter images" (10 SED Fitting)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: beta in [1.0,1.2], H100 in [5,15], Rin between sublimation radius and mm cavity; grains 0.01-1000 um  
  > "$\beta$ in \[1.0, 1.2\] and $H_{100}$ in \[5,15\]" (10 SED Fitting)
   - assumption: Dust model of Birnstiel et al. 2018; well-mixed gas and dust  
  > "we adopt the model in (cite: birnstiel2018) with grain sizes ranging from 0.01 $\mu \rm m$ to 1000 $\mu \rm m$" (10 SED Fitting)
   - fitting: Grid comparison by eye/best-fit; no formal statistic stated  
  > "We test each combination of the other three parameters with $R_{\rm in}$ between dust sublimation radius and mm cavity size, $\beta$ in \[1.0, 1.2\] and $H_{100}$ in \[5,15\]." (SED Fitting)
   - validation: Cavity size degenerate with other parameters, differs by a few au  
  > "the constrained cavity sizes of small dust grains can differ by a few au under different combinations of other parameters" (4.2 Substructures in disks around mid-to-late M stars)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Visibility fitting avoids biases from image reconstruction  
  > "To quantify the dust emission morphology without biases introduced by the image reconstruction, we fit the brightness profiles in the visibility domain." (3.1)
- Alternative: Dead zone outer edge excluded as origin of dust traps  
  > "We exclude the dead zone outer edge as a possible origin of radial dust traps." (Footnote 5)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Short snapshot observations, lack of short spacings, gas radii as lower limits  
  > "the gas emission at large radii is likely resolved out" (3.5 CO emission)
- Limitation (authors): Dust masses are lower limits if optically thick  
  > "We note that these dust masses serve as lower limits, since part of the disk could be optically thick in reality" (3.4)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low S/N (5-20) limits ring-shape constraints; model choice based on eye identification
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample of six is biased and small for frequency statistics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SED cavity sizes degenerate and grid-based without formal uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Planet masses depend on assumed viscosity and Crida criterion
