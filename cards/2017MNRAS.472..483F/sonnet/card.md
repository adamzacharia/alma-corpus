## Analysis recipe

1. **Derive L_IR from ALMA 240 GHz fluxes**: Fit single-component modified blackbody to the 240 GHz flux and integrate 8-1000 um  
  > "Infrared luminosities ($L_{\rm IR}$) are estimated by fitting a single component modified black body function to the ALMA $240\,{\rm GHz}$ fluxes of our galaxies" (3.1 $L_{\rm IR}$, $L_{\rm UV}$, and $\beta_{\rm UV}$)
   - parameter: Integrated over 8-1000 um; 3 sigma upper limits for 26 non-detections  
  > "For the 26 non-detected targets, we estimated upper limits on their $L_{\rm IR}$ using the $3\sigma$ ALMA flux limits." (3.1 $L_{\rm IR}$, $L_{\rm UV}$, and $\beta_{\rm UV}$)
   - assumption: beta_d=1.5; fiducial T_d=40 K (stack best fit 41.5+-2.5 K); 35 K as alternative  
  > "Hereafter, we will use $T_d=40\,{\rm K}$ as our fiducial value throughout the paper" (3.1 $L_{\rm IR}$, $L_{\rm UV}$, and $\beta_{\rm UV}$)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: CMB effect estimated <5%, negligible  
  > "we estimate that the expected effect on our $z\sim3$ sample is still less than 5%" (3.1 $L_{\rm IR}$, $L_{\rm UV}$, and $\beta_{\rm UV}$)
2. **Measure beta_UV and L_UV**: Power-law fit to COSMOS15 photometry over rest-frame 1500-2500 A; L_UV at 1600 A  
  > "UV spectral slopes ($\beta_{\rm{UV}}$) are calculated by fitting a power law $f_{\rm{\lambda}}\propto \lambda^{\beta_{\rm{UV}}}$ to the broad- and narrow-band photometry" (3.1 $L_{\rm IR}$, $L_{\rm UV}$, and $\beta_{\rm UV}$)
   - parameter: 3 arcsec aperture photometry, typically >10 data points  
  > "Typically more than 10 data points are available covering this waveband for each of our targets." (3.1 $L_{\rm IR}$, $L_{\rm UV}$, and $\beta_{\rm UV}$)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Stellar masses via SED fitting**: MAGPHYS SED fits to optical/NIR, MIPS 24um, PACS 100um and ALMA fluxes  
  > "Stellar masses for our $z\sim3$ sample are calculated based on multi-wavelength spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting using the MAGPHYS code (cite: Dacunha2008)." (2.1 ALMA Observations of $z\sim3.2$ Targets)
   - software: MAGPHYS
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF  
  > "a Chabrier initial mass function (cite: Chabrier2003) where applicable" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Stack ALMA images in beta_UV bins**: Pixel-wise median stack of ALMA maps in three beta_UV bins and for non-detections; weighted by inverse L_UV; peak flux corrected to total flux using mean of detected sources  
  > "stacks can be created by simply taking the median of each pixel in the ALMA maps. During the stacking procedure, the ALMA images are weighted by the inverse of the galaxy’s $L_{\rm UV}$" (3.2 Stacking Analysis)
   - parameter: Bins beta_UV <-1.5, -1.5 to -1.0, >-1.0 (14, 30, 23 galaxies)  
  > "which contain 14, 30, and 23 galaxies, respectively" (3.2 Stacking Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Uncertainties via bootstrap resampling; non-detection stack is most significant signal within central 23"x23"  
  > "Uncertainties on all stacked quantities, including the IRX, are estimated using bootstrap resampling." (3.2 Stacking Analysis)
5. **Compare IRX-beta and fit IRX-M* relation, test evolution to z~4-6**: Compare with M99 and SMC curves; fit normalisation of IRX-M* with slope fixed to Heinis2013; compare with Scoville2016 and Capak2015/Barisic2017 samples  
  > "We quantify the offset by fitting for the normalisation but keeping the slope fixed to the one found in (cite: Heinis2013)." (4.3 The IRX-$M_{\ast}$ Relation)
   - assumption: T_d evolution ∝(1+z)^0.32 tested as caveat  
  > "typically finding an evolution following $T_d\propto(1+z)^{0.32}$" (4.4 The Evolution of IRX–$\beta_{\rm UV}$ Beyond $z=3$)
   - fitting: Normalisation-only fit, slope fixed; method not stated  
  > "We quantify the offset by fitting for the normalisation but keeping the slope fixed to the one found in" (Results and Discussion: The IRX-M* Relation)
   - validation: Mass-matched comparison and T_d sensitivity tests  
  > "before making strong claims about a possible redshift evolution, we have to control for the differences in stellar masses of the different samples." (4.4 The Evolution of IRX–$\beta_{\rm UV}$ Beyond $z=3$)

## Qualitative findings

- SMC-type attenuation is excluded for the general z~3 population, but the non-detection stack is consistent with SMC  
  > "the median stacks show that an SMC-like attenuation is clearly not applicable for the general galaxy sample at $z\sim3.2$" (4.1 IRX–$\beta_{\rm UV}$ Relation at $z\sim3.2$)
