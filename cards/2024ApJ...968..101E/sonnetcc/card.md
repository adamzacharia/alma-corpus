## Analysis recipe

1. **reduce HAWC+ data and build smoothed Stokes maps**: HAWC+ pipeline flat-fields, computes Stokes I, Q, U, corrects pointing and instrumental polarization, applies flux calibration, combines and smooths maps; imaging-block offsets corrected against Herschel PACS 100 um  
  > "The raw images are then processed through the HAWC+ data reduction pipeline, which produces science ready continuum and polarization products." (Observations and Data Reduction); "To correct this, we shifted the fit map Gaussian peak center to align with the archival map from Herschel" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: HAWC+ data reduction pipeline
   - parameter: final beam 11.7 arcsec  
  > "creating a final beam size of 11.7$^{\prime\prime}$" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No individual observation was offset by more than half a beam  
  > "No individual observation was offset by more than half a beam." (Observations and Data Reduction)
2. **inflate pipeline uncertainties**: Per-pixel chi-squared comparison of Stokes I, Q, U across dither sets; pipeline error inflated where chi-squared is larger, using a chi-squared parameterized by Stokes I intensity  
  > "If the $\chi^{2}$ is larger, then we inflate the pipeline error (cite: Chapman2013; Novak2011)." (Observations and Data Reduction); "Following (cite: Erin2022), we perform the inflation by fitting a parameterized $\chi^{2}$ with Stokes I intensity" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: Parameterized chi-squared fit versus Stokes I intensity; details not given  
  > "Following (cite: Erin2022), we perform the inflation by fitting a parameterized $\chi^{2}$ with Stokes I intensity" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **derive polarization fraction and select vectors**: Compute p and its error from Stokes I, Q, U following the HAWC+ handbook, debias, then keep vectors passing signal-to-noise, percentage and intensity cuts; display Nyquist-sampled vectors rotated by 90 deg to show the inferred field  
  > "The polarization fraction is debiased ($p^\prime$), following the HAWC+ handbook (cite: hawcplus_handbook)" (Observations and Data Reduction); "we present only enough vectors to be considered Nyquist sampled, for a total of 45 polarization vectors." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - parameter: P'/sigma_p > 3, debiased polarization percentage < 50%, Stokes I at least 10x the Stokes I RMS  
  > "we selected the debiased polarization vectors that met the criteria of $P^\prime$/$\sigma_{p}$ $>$ 3, the debiased polarization percentage was $<$ 50%, and the Stokes I flux values were at least $\times$10 the Stokes I RMS noise level." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - assumption: Polarization angles rotated by 90 deg to give the plane-of-sky magnetic field, assuming grains aligned by radiative torques  
  > "Many studies therefore rotate the polarization angles by 90$^\circ$ to present the inferred plane-of-sky magnetic field morphology." (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **fit the 89 um continuum envelope**: Gaussian fit to the protostellar envelope continuum emission, giving deconvolved size, position angle, integrated and peak flux; compared with Gaussian fits to Herschel PACS 70 and 100 um  
  > "We fit the protostellar envelope continuum emission using a Gaussian." (Results)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian; fitting routine not stated  
  > "We fit the protostellar envelope continuum emission using a Gaussian." (Results)
   - validation: Compared with Gaussian fits to Herschel PACS 70 and 100 um and the 850 um position angle  
  > "The continuum Gaussian fit of the HAWC+ 89 $\mu$m observation is different than Gaussian fits to the Herschel archival data." (Results)
5. **compare field orientation across wavelengths and with the outflow**: Overplot HAWC+ vectors on POL-2 850 um vectors (normalized), on ALMA 1.3 mm vectors smoothed to 1 arcsec, on the Planck field angle, and on CO (3-2) outflow lobes, and compare average field angles  
  > "At higher spatial scale, Figure [3](#fig:93) compares the 89 $\mu$m HAWC+ data and 850 $\mu$m POL-2 data with high resolution polarization observations at 1.3 mm with ALMA from (cite: Sadavoy2018)" (Results)
   - parameter: ALMA data smoothed to 1 arcsec  
  > "The ALMA data have been smoothed to 1$\ensuremath{''}$ resolution to better compare with our observations." (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative polarization mechanism (dichroic extinction) examined and disfavored on four grounds  
  > "Overall, based on these four reasons, the IRAS 16293-2422 polarization observations presented here are most likely due to magnetic field alignment of dust grains." (Possibility of Polarization by Dichroic Extinction)
6. **track the continuum peak position with wavelength**: Fit Gaussians to the 22 to 850 um emission (WISE, Herschel, SCUBA-2 and others) and plot the fitted peak positions against wavelength  
  > "We fit the different wavelength emission (e.g., WISE, Herschel, and SCUBA2 listed in Section [2](#sec:observations)) to Gaussians." (Continuum Wavelength Dependence)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits; routine not stated  
  > "We fit the different wavelength emission (e.g., WISE, Herschel, and SCUBA2 listed in Section [2](#sec:observations)) to Gaussians." (Continuum Wavelength Dependence)

## Qualitative findings

- The HAWC+ inferred field is nearly uniform and aligned east-west, matching the E-W outflow and unlike the Planck, POL-2 and ALMA fields  
  > "Indeed, one of the most striking features of the HAWC+ map is the uniform field that is nearly completely EW in direction" (Magnetic Fields)
- Polarization fraction is lower toward the center of the HAWC+ map  
  > "Third, the polarization fraction is lower toward the center of our observations in Figure [1](#fig:90)." (Possibility of Polarization by Dichroic Extinction)
- HAWC+, ALMA and POL-2 vectors agree only in parts of the map and are not well aligned overall  
  > "However, in general, the vectors from the three observations are not well-aligned." (Results)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: ALMA data were smoothed to 1 arcsec to compare with the coarser HAWC+ and POL-2 data  
  > "The ALMA data have been smoothed to 1$\ensuremath{''}$ resolution to better compare with our observations." (Results)
- Alternative: Dichroic extinction as the polarization mechanism, disfavored for four reasons but not ruled out  
  > "Although, we can not rule out dichroic extinction as the dominate mechanism for polarization in IRAS 16293-2422 (cite: Zielinski2021)" (Possibility of Polarization by Dichroic Extinction)
- Alternative: Wavelength-dependent peak shift attributed to a difference in evolutionary stage of sources A and B, judged less likely than inclination  
  > "Another explanation of the shift with wavelength could be a difference in evolutionary stage of the binary sources." (Continuum Wavelength Dependence)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The mapped region of the three-way comparison is only slightly larger than the POL-2 and HAWC+ beams, so only broad comparison is possible  
  > "so it is difficult to do more than compare broadly." (Results)
- Limitation (authors): Overall flux calibration uncertainty of 20%, with only statistical uncertainties quoted elsewhere  
  > "We estimate the overall flux calibration uncertainty of the observations at 20%" (Observations and Data Reduction)
- Limitation (authors): Different wavelengths probe different optical depths and dust temperatures, hindering the 3D field picture  
  > "the observations are probing different optical depth and dust temperatures" (Magnetic Fields)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA data are used only as a supporting comparison; no ALMA processing is described, so the ALMA comparison cannot be reproduced from this paper alone
