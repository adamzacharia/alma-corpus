## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and CO(2-1) cubes**: CASA clean with natural weighting after uv continuum subtraction  
  > "reconstructed using the task `clean` with the natural weighting to enhance sensitivities" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: df = 62.5 MHz (quasars), 31.25 MHz (galaxies)  
  > "we set the frequency resolution ($df$) to 62.5 MHz" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure CO line luminosity in 700 pc and 2 kpc apertures**: Convolve cubes to common physical aperture, integrate line flux, convert to L'CO(2-1)  
  > "We first convolved the aperture of individual data cubes to the largest one among our full sample in physical scale, i.e., $\sim 700$ pc (J1151$-$0004)." (3.1 CO(2-1) emission)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: H0=70, OmegaM=0.3, OmegaL=0.7  
  > "we adopt the concordant cosmological parameters $H_0$ = 70 km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$, $\Omega_{\rm M}$ = 0.3, and $\Omega_{\rm \Lambda}$ = 0.7" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: missing flux not an issue since scales << MRS  
  > "that is much smaller than our maximum recoverable scales (MRS), i.e., missing flux should not be an issue" (3.1 CO(2-1) emission)
3. **Convert to molecular gas mass**: M_mol = alpha_CO * L'CO(1-0) with L'CO(1-0)=L'CO(2-1)/R21  
  > "we adopt $R_{21} = 1$ throughout this work." (3.4 Estimates of molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: R21 = 1; alpha_CO = 1.3 Msun pc^-2 (K km/s)^-1 (~0.4 dex uncertainty) for both samples  
  > "we adopt $\alpha_{\rm CO} = 1.3~M_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$ in this work, which would have $\sim 0.4$ dex uncertainty as well." (3.4 Estimates of molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Radial Sigma_mol profiles**: Azimuthally averaged CO integrated intensity at matched physical resolution, rings with step theta_maj/2  
  > "The steps between concentric rings correspond to $\theta_{\rm maj}/2$, where $\theta_{\rm maj}$ is the major axis of each 2D Gaussian beam." (3.5 Molecular gas mass surface density profiles)
   - software: MIRIAD ellint
   - assumption: Sky projection, no inclination correction (cos i ~ factor 2 possible)  
  > "Note that these profiles are sky projections as we have no robust information about the inclination angles of our targets." (3.5 Molecular gas mass surface density profiles)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Robustness checked excluding anomalous J1151-0004  
  > "This result would be robust even if we regard the galaxy J1151$-$0004 to be an anomalous case" (3.5 Molecular gas mass surface density profiles)
5. **Test XDR temperature effect on CO(2-1)**: RADEX non-LTE models at T_kin = 30-500 K  
  > "Here we modeled cases of $T_{\rm kin}$ = 30, 50, 100, 200, 300, and 500 K." (4 Discussion)
   - software: RADEX
   - parameter: n_H2 = 1e5 cm^-3; N_CO/dV = 2.5e16 cm^-2 (km/s)^-1; background 2.73 K  
  > "fixing the H$_2$ volume density ($n_{\rm H2}$) of 10$^5$ cm$^{-3}$ and the CO column density-to-velocity gradient ratio" (4 Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: qualitative only  
  > "In order simply to grasp a qualitative trend" (4 Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: CO(2-1) used as surrogate for CO(1-0) to get higher angular resolution  
  > "while easily acquiring higher angular resolutions than cases of lower frequency CO(1–0) observations" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
- Alternative: One pair (HE0205-2408/J0042-0940) excluded for SFR mismatch; both undetected in CO  
  > "Since HE0205$-$2408 and J0042$-$0940 do not compose a well-matched pair any more, we excluded this pair from our discussion." (Appendix)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Absolute Sigma_mol depends critically on alpha_CO, and inclination is unknown  
  > "the absolute value of $\Sigma_{\rm mol}$ critically depends on the adopted $\alpha_{\rm CO}$" (3.5 Molecular gas mass surface density profiles)
- Limitation (authors): Modest S/N prevents line profile/outflow analysis  
  > "we cannot provide a detailed investigation of the line profiles of the quasar sample (including the search for molecular outflows) given the modest S/N ratios" (Summary)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 4 pairs; small-number statistics
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single alpha_CO and R21=1 applied to both AGN and galaxies
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Matching SFR from different methods (SED-based vs Halpha)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Unequal sensitivity and velocity resolution between samples
- Caveat (inferred, ours): J1151-0004 outlier
