## Analysis recipe

1. **Re-image calibrated visibilities and smooth to common resolution**: tclean with auto-multithresh; hybrid 12m+7m multiscale for 12CO; smooth to 3.5''  
  > "For each spectral line, the visibility data were imaged together using the `tclean` task in CASA 5.0.0." (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 0.5'' pixels, 0.2 km/s channels, 1σ stopping threshold  
  > "Deconvolution proceeded down to a 1$\sigma$ stopping threshold." (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared ALMA 12CO flux spectra with MAGMA single-dish; differences comparable to ~20% MAGMA calibration uncertainty  
  > "Since the differences are comparable to the $\sim$20% calibration uncertainties in the much lower sensitivity MAGMA maps" (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
2. **Build moment maps with dilated signal mask**: Mask from 3.5σ contour expanded to 2σ, extended by 1 channel; sum in velocity  
  > "The mask was obtained by starting at the 3.5$\sigma$ contour and expanding to the surrounding 2$\sigma$ contour" (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 13CO fluxes insensitive to mask choice (12CO-derived vs 13CO-derived mask)  
  > "the measured fluxes are not sensitive to the details of the masking approach." (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
3. **LTE 13CO column density and H2 surface density**: Tex from 12CO peak temperature, 13CO optical depth, column density formula; scaled to H2  
  > "we conduct a simple local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) analysis to infer the $^{13}$CO column density from the observed $^{12}$CO and $^{13}$CO emission." (3.2 LTE Analysis)
   - parameter: T_floor = 6 K  
  > "we impose a minimum value on $T_{\rm ex}$ of $T_{\rm floor}$ = 6 K" (3.2 LTE Analysis)
   - assumption: Beam filling fraction 1; common Tex for both lines; 12CO optically thick; N(H2)/N(13CO)=3e6  
  > "N(\rm H_2)}{N(\rm ^{13}CO)} = 3 \times 10^6\;." (3.2 LTE Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Decompose cubes into structures**: astrodendro on each cube; compute σ_v, σ_r, R, L, M_vir, M_CO  
  > "The algorithm identifies local maxima in the cube above the 3$\sigma_{\rm rms}$ level that are also at least 2.5$\sigma_{\rm rms}$ above the merge level with adjacent structures." (3.3 Structural Decomposition)
   - software: astrodendro
   - parameter: Each local max must span at least two synthesized beams in area  
  > "Each local maximum is required to span at least two synthesized beams in area." (3.3 Structural Decomposition)
   - assumption: d = 50 kpc; X_2=2.4 (CO 1-0), X_2=3 (CO 2-1, line ratio 0.8); M_vir = 5σ_v²R/G  
  > "$X_2=3$ for the CO(2–1) line assuming a CO(2–1)/CO(1–0) line ratio of 0.8." (3.3 Structural Decomposition)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Interactive masking in GMC104 changed flux by ~50% but negligibly affected structure properties  
  > "a negligible impact on the properties of the structures that are measured following decomposition" (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
5. **Fit size-line width relations**: Power law log σ_v = a1 log R + a0, per cloud and aggregate, both-axis errors  
  > "fitting is performed using the `kmpfit` module of the Python package `Kapteyn` (cite: KapteynPackage), which treats errors in both axes using the effective variance method." (3.4 Size vs. Line Width Relations)
   - software: Kapteyn
   - parameter: Points in resolution-limited gray regions excluded; 3σ confidence band plotted  
  > "Points in the gray shaded regions are excluded from fitting due to resolution limitations." (3.4 Size vs. Line Width Relations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Refit without excluding shaded regions; slopes usually within 1σ, sometimes ~3σ discrepant  
  > "The resulting slopes and intercepts are usually within the quoted 1$\sigma$ uncertainties, but are sometimes discrepant by nearly 3$\sigma$." (3.4 Size vs. Line Width Relations)
6. **Compare v0 with 8 micron, 24 micron, dust and surface density**: Colour structures by local/cloud 8um intensity; boundedness plots; cloud-averaged v0 correlations  
  > "We can also examine the relationship between $v_0$, IR emission, and mass surface density on cloud-averaged scales" (3.6 Cloud-averaged correlations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Scatter in line width among clouds exceeds scatter within clouds, allowing a cloud mean v0  
  > "the scatter among clouds is even larger, allowing one to meaningfully assign a mean value of the normalization $v_0$ to each cloud in our sample." (4 Discussion and Conclusions)
- v0 correlates independently with IR brightness and mean surface density  
  > "the characteristic $v_0$ for a cloud correlates independently with both its mean IR brightness and its mean surface density" (4 Discussion and Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Dendrogram chosen as nested structures are needed to compare line widths across sizes  
  > "Any approach that seeks to recover line widths at different size scales must ultimately compare nested rather than disjoint structures" (3.3 Structural Decomposition)
- Alternative: Did not merge interferometer and single-dish data because differences comparable to MAGMA calibration uncertainty  
  > "we have not attempted to merge the interferometer and single-dish data." (2.2 ALMA Cycle 4 Data)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Power law is a poor fit (reduced chi2 >> 1) and fits sensitive to data selection  
  > "a power law is generally a poor fit to the data, as reflected by the reduced $\chi^2$ values all being substantially greater than 1" (3.4 Size vs. Line Width Relations)
- Limitation (authors): Opacity broadening and mass uncertainties (X-factor, LTE) affect interpretation  
  > "*Opacity broadening:* (cite: Hacar:16) have analyzed the overestimate of the intrinsic velocity dispersion that results from high line opacity." (4 Discussion and Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): Assumes J=1-0 and 2-1 trace similar structures; untested  
  > "we defer to a future paper" (2.3 Archival Data for 30 Dor and PCC)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mixing Band 3 J=1-0 and Band 6 J=2-1 data with different uv coverage and total power may bias structure properties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Common-resolution noise injection and differing channel widths for 30 Dor may affect dendrogram results
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dendrogram parameters sensitive; no systematic test of segmentation robustness
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Correlated axes between Σ_LTE and σ_v-derived quantities may create spurious correlations
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only six clouds; trend partly driven by 30 Dor
