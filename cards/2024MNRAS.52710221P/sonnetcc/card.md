## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce MOSFIRE spectra and flux calibrate with the slit star**: MOSFIRE-DRP reduction, 1D extraction with the Horne algorithm, flux calibration from the K1 slit star modeled with a Pickles spectrum scaled to 2MASS  
  > "The spectra were reduced using the MOSFIRE Data Reduction Pipeline (MOSFIRE-DRP, (cite: Steidel14))" (2.1 Keck/MOSFIRE spectroscopy); "By dividing the modeled and observed stellar spectra we obtain the transmission function which includes the conversion from raw units to physical flux" (2.1 Keck/MOSFIRE spectroscopy)
   - software: MOSFIRE-DRP
   - parameter: 180 s exposures in K-band (19540-23970 A), 174 min integration  
  > "individual exposures consisting of 180s each in K-band ($19540-23970$ Å)" (2.1 Keck/MOSFIRE spectroscopy)
   - assumption: The slit-star transmission function is applied to all targets  
  > "Finally, we apply the same transmission function to all our targets." (2.1 Keck/MOSFIRE spectroscopy)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Fit Halpha and [NII] emission lines with Monte Carlo errors**: Local continuum fit and subtraction in sky-line-free windows, then simultaneous Gaussian fit of Halpha and the [NII] doublet (plus a broad component for two AGN-like objects)  
  > "we proceed to fit and subtract a local continuum consisting of several windows free from skyline contamination and encompassing a few hundred angstroms left and right of the detected line" (3.1 Emission line fitting)
   - software: Astropy
   - parameter: 1000 Monte Carlo realizations with Gaussian noise from the 1D noise spectrum; detection if flux/error > 2  
  > "The fitting procedure outlined above is repeated 1000 times allowing for random gaussian variations on every spectral data point with a maximum amplitude equal to the value found in the 1D noise spectra." (3.1 Emission line fitting); "we consider a line to be detected if the ratio between the total flux and the flux error measured in the way described above is higher than two" (3.1 Emission line fitting)
   - fitting: Self-written Astropy-based fitting code with Monte Carlo uncertainties  
  > "We will carry out the emission line fitting by making use of a self-written code written in (Astropy library, (cite: Astropy13; Astropy18))" (3.1 Emission line fitting)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive stellar masses and dust extinction by SED fitting**: Photometry measured with SExtractor in dual-image mode on the NB2315 detection image, then SED fitting with CIGALE  
  > "we use the NB2315 image for source detection while running SEXTRACTOR ((cite: Bertin96)) in dual-image mode" (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction); "We carry out the SED fitting over the multi-band photometric measurements (excluding NB2315) we have gathered for every object with the Code Investigating GALaxy Emission (CIGALE, (cite: Boquien19))." (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction)
   - software: SExtractor, CIGALE
   - parameter: Bruzual & Charlot models with exponentially delayed SFH, Chabrier IMF, Z = 0.004  
  > "we assume the stellar population synthesis models of Bruzual & Charlot ((cite: BC03)) applied to an exponentially delayed star formation history" (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction); "In addition, we assume a Chabrier IMF ((cite: Chabrier03)) and subsolar metallicity (i.e $Z = 0.004$) for the stellar component." (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction)
   - assumption: Calzetti attenuation law  
  > "Calzetti’s attenuation law ((cite: Calzetti2000))" (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction)
   - fitting: Bayesian likelihood-weighted mean of the model PDF; standard deviations as uncertainties  
  > "the physical properties are statistically derived as the likelihood-weighted mean of the PDF and standard deviations represent the uncertainties" (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction)
   - validation: Average stellar-mass accuracy 0.2 dex  
  > "The average accuracy of the derived stellar masses is 0.2 dex." (3.2 Stellar masses and dust extinction)
4. **Compute extinction-corrected Halpha SFRs**: Kennicutt98 Halpha calibration modified for a Chabrier IMF, with extinction from SED Av plus an extra nebular term  
  > "applying the calibration developed by (cite: Kennicutt98) modified for a Chabrier IMF" (3.3 Star-formation activity)
   - assumption: Calzetti extinction law with R_v = 4.05  
  > "assuming a Calzetti’s extinction law ($\mathrm{R_v}$=4.05, (cite: Calzetti2000)) to account for the dust attenuation" (3.3 Star-formation activity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive N2 gas-phase metallicities and stacked metallicities in mass bins**: Pettini04 N2 calibration for individual objects; inverse-variance stacking with bootstrapping for two stellar-mass bins excluding AGN candidates  
  > "we apply the N2 calibration developed by (cite: Pettini04)" (3.4 Gas phase metallicities); "we resort to the stacking analysis (excluding AGN candidates) to trace the mean metal enrichment of our sample in two mass bins" (3.4 Gas phase metallicities)
   - parameter: Bootstrap over a thousand realizations within each stellar-mass bin  
  > "In addition, we use bootstrapping, thus resampling the number of spectra within our stellar mass bins over a thousand realizations" (3.4 Gas phase metallicities)
   - fitting: N2 ratio measured on the mean bootstrapped spectrum with the same fitting procedure as individual spectra  
  > "These two quantities are used to fit and measure the N2 ratio following the same procedure applied to every individual spectrum" (3.4 Gas phase metallicities)
   - validation: Median individual abundance uncertainty about 0.10 dex  
  > "we estimate the median uncertainties for our gas-phase abundances to be of the order of 0.10 dex for the individual measurements" (3.4 Gas phase metallicities)
6. **Convert published ALMA CO(3-2) luminosities to molecular gas masses and gas fractions**: Use CO(3-2) measurements of Tadaki19 for overlapping targets; M_mol = alpha_CO(3-2) x L'CO(3-2) with alpha_CO(3-2) = alpha_CO(1-0) / r31 x f_corr, f_corr a metallicity-dependent term (Genzel15, Tacconi18)  
  > "we will focus solely on the available CO(3-2) measurements published by (cite: Tadaki19)" (3.4 Molecular gas masses); "we compute our molecular gas masses by assuming the metallicity dependent conversion factor, $\mathrm{f_{corr}}$, outlined in (cite: Genzel15) and later revisited by (cite: Tacconi18)" (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
   - parameter: r31 = 0.56 +/- 0.14 (Genzel12)  
  > "However, we will assume $r_\mathrm{31}\approx0.56\pm0.14$ ((cite: Genzel12)) for our own molecular gas computation" (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
   - parameter: alpha_CO(1-0) = 4.36 +/- 0.90 (Bolatto13)  
  > "$\alpha_{\mathrm{CO(1-0)}}=4.36\pm0.90$, (cite: Bolatto13)" (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
   - parameter: Metallicity correction applied only for 12+log(O/H) > 8.44  
  > "we constrain the range of application of this prescription to galaxies with $\mathrm{12+log(O/H)}>8.44$" (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
   - assumption: Metallicity from the Pettini04 N2 calibration is used in f_corr  
  > "where $\mathrm{Z_{gas}=12+log(O/H)}$ assuming the (cite: Pettini04) metallicity calibration" (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Measure local environmental density**: Surface density Sigma_5 from the radius enclosing five neighbouring HAEs in the parent catalog of 107 sources  
  > "This catalog contains 107 sources and represents the parent sample of our spectroscopic campaign." (3.5 Environment quantification); "we will use $\Sigma_5$, i.e. the local density defined by the minimum radius enclosing five neighboring galaxies" (3.5 Environment quantification)
   - assumption: All HAEs without spectroscopic confirmation are assumed to be protocluster members  
  > "which allow us to assume that all remaining HAEs without spectroscopic confirmation have a high chance of belonging to USS1558" (3.5 Environment quantification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Compare with main sequence, field MZR and PKS1138, test significance**: Offsets from the main sequence and mass-metallicity relation compared with Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests against PKS1138 and KMOS3D samples  
  > "we have performed a Kolmogorov-Smirnov test between our samples to investigate if these differences are statistically significant" (4.1 Star formation in the assembling USS1558 protocluster)
   - parameter: SFR > 25 Msun/yr cut applied before comparing offset distributions  
  > "after imposing an $\mathrm{SFR>25 M_\odot/yr}$ cut on them" (4.1 Star formation in the assembling USS1558 protocluster)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- [NII] detections tend to lie in denser regions than non-detections, but the KS test finds the distributions statistically indistinguishable  
  > "However, we should take this result with caution" (4.2 Gas-phase metallicities)
- USS1558 HAEs may be less evolved than those in the Spiderweb protocluster  
  > "These results suggest that HAEs within USS1558–003 may be less evolved than those in the Spiderweb protocluster." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Only CO(3-2) from Tadaki19 is used for the gas masses to limit systematics between molecular gas tracers  
  > "In order to minimize the systematics between different molecular gas tracers we will focus solely on the available CO(3-2) measurements published by (cite: Tadaki19)." (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
- Why this method: Same methods as the PKS1138 study so that relative comparisons between protoclusters are fair  
  > "The aim of this work, however, is to compare the relative metal enrichment of USS1558 protocluster members with those belonging to PKS1138 ((cite: PerezMartinez23)) and the coeval field." (3.4 Gas phase metallicities)
- Alternative: A higher r31 (Riechers20 prescription, about 0.9) agrees better with dust-based gas masses in Aoyama22 but was not adopted, to stay consistent with field comparison calibrations (r31 = 0.56 used)  
  > "Interestingly, (cite: Aoyama22) found that the molecular gas masses computed for USS1558 objects both from CO(3-2) and from dust continuum observations achieve a higher degree of agreement when the (cite: Riechers20) prescription" (3.4 Molecular gas masses); "for our own molecular gas computation in order to be consistent with the calibrations of our field comparison samples (e.g., (cite: Sanders23))" (3.4 Molecular gas masses)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only [NII]-detected objects trace the metal-rich end; metal-poor galaxies may be missed because of the detection limit  
  > "Thus our individual measurements only trace the metal-rich end of the distribution of HAEs" (4.2 Gas-phase metallicities)
- Limitation (authors): Small number of objects limits the gas-fraction comparison  
  > "However, the limited number of objects both in USS1558 and PKS1138 prevent us from drawing firm conclusions at this stage." (4.3 Molecular gas fractions)
- Limitation (authors): Absolute N2 metallicities at high redshift remain uncertain  
  > "the accuracy of its absolute values at high redshift is still under debate" (3.4 Gas phase metallicities)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA CO(3-2) values are used from a published paper without re-analysis, so the CO-derived results are not independently verified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only five galaxies enter the gas-fraction analysis, so mass-loading-factor conclusions are weak
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Narrow-band flux cut and spectroscopic sampling make the SFR comparison with PKS1138 sensitive to selection, handled only with an SFR cut at 25 Msun/yr
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Metallicity offset at low mass is only marginally significant (about 1.75 sigma)
