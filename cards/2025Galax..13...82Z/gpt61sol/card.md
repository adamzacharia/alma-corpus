## Analysis recipe

1. **Compile the molecular inventory**: Summarize existing spectral-line surveys and secure molecular detections in RSG circumstellar gas.  
  > "Currently, several existing spectral-line surveys of RGSs provide a basis for defining the chemical content of these objects." (The Molecular Inventory of the Envelopes of RSGs); "summarizes the current set of molecules identified in circumstellar gas of RSGs, a total of 29 different chemical compounds." (The Molecular Inventory of the Envelopes of RSGs)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Secure detections; all except VO identified through pure rotational spectra at mm/sub-mm wavelengths.  
  > "The listed species are secure detections, and all except VO were identified on the basis of their pure rotational spectrum measured at mm/sub-mm wavelengths." (The Molecular Inventory of the Envelopes of RSGs)
2. **Compare molecular spatial distributions**: Compare same-scale VY CMa ALMA molecular images, including single-dish data, with dust structures and line profiles.  
  > "ALMA images of PN, PO, NS and SiO are shown, also on the same spatial scale." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules); "Note that these maps all contain the single-dish addition." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules); "The HST dust structures Arc 2 and the NW Arc are traced well by SO and SO$_2$, and are visible as spectral features in the observed line profiles of these molecules" (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules)
   - parameter: VY CMa Band 6 resolution: 0.2''–1.5''  
  > "This issue is overcome in new ALMA images of VY CMa, conducted in Band 6 with 0.2$^{''}$–1.5$^{''}$ resolution (cite: 2023ApJ...954L...1S; 2024ApJ...971L..43R; Ravi2025a)." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules)
   - assumption: Similar molecular dipole moments and excitation energies make spatial differences primarily chemical or elemental.  
  > "Because the dipole moments and excitation energies of these molecules are very similar, these variations in spatial location are primarily the result of chemistry and, in certain cases, elemental enrichment." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Single-dish addition is stated to recover all flux.  
  > "These maps include the addition of single-dish data, and therefore, recover all the flux, presenting a “complete picture”." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules)
3. **Compare literature abundances**: Compare compiled H2-relative abundances in VY CMa, NML Cyg, IRC+10420 and O-rich AGB stars.  
  > "A summary of molecular abundances, relative to H$_2$ is given" (An Overview of Abundances); "which lists those for VY CMa, NML Cyg, and IRC+10420, as compiled from the literature." (An Overview of Abundances); "Representative AGB abundances are also listed" (An Overview of Abundances)
   - software: ESCAPADE
   - parameter: Estimated abundance uncertainties: 20–40%  
  > "Uncertainties are estimated at 20–40%." (An Overview of Abundances)
   - parameter: Inherited IRC+10420 source sizes: 11” for CO; 3” for other molecules  
  > "The abundances for IRC+10420 are from a rotational diagram analysis, which assumes LTE and source sizes of 11” for CO and 3” for all other molecules (cite: 2016A&A...592A..51Q)." (An Overview of Abundances)
   - assumption: Inherited IRC+10420 rotational-diagram analysis assumes LTE.  
  > "The abundances for IRC+10420 are from a rotational diagram analysis, which assumes LTE" (An Overview of Abundances)
   - fitting: Inherited ESCAPADE line-profile fits derive peak abundance and spatial extent; spherical and asymmetric outflows.  
  > "a radiative transfer code, ESCAPADE, that considers both spherical and asymmetric outflows, as described in (cite: 2013ApJ...778...22A)." (An Overview of Abundances); "The advantage of this modeling is that the lines profiles are fit to derive both peak abundance and spatial extent." (An Overview of Abundances)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Assess LTE oxide formation**: Compare observed abundances and confined spatial distributions with literature LTE chemistry calculations.  
  > "Although the abundances do not match the model predictions exactly, the spatial extent of these molecules strongly suggests production by LTE chemistry." (The Role of LTE Formation)
   - parameter: Al-bearing species localized within about 50–60 R_star  
  > "all three Al-bearing species are localized near the star within about 50–60 $R_{\star}$, and disappear thereafter" (The Role of LTE Formation)
   - assumption: Tsuji model updated with revised cosmic abundances; oxygen-rich gas, C/O ~0.5  
  > "Here, the model from Tsuji (cite: 1973A&A....23..411T) has been updated with revised cosmic abundances (cite: 2003ApJ...591.1220L) and assumes oxygen-rich gas (C/O$\sim$0.5)." (The Role of LTE Formation)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: PO LTE peak abundance ~10^-7 agrees with observations; aluminum abundances do not match exactly.  
  > "In this case, LTE models predict a peak abundance of $\sim$$10^{-7}$, as is observed (cite: 2024ApJ...971L..43R), in excellent agreement." (The Role of LTE Formation); "Although the abundances do not match the model predictions exactly" (The Role of LTE Formation)
5. **Assess shocks and nitrogen enrichment**: Compare PN, PO, NS and SiO distributions, knot velocities and carbon isotope estimates with shock chemistry.  
  > "Evidence for the shock scenario is the similarity between PN and SiO emission in the SW Fan" (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing); "there is a remarkable correlation between PN and NS in the ALMA images, suggesting that both molecules arise in nitrogen-rich gas." (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing); "The velocities of the PN spectra in the SW Clump region are close to those of Ki present in several of the actual S and SW knots" (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing)
   - parameter: PO confined to <60 R_star; additional PN structures at 80–240 R_star  
  > "PO is solely confined to the inner circumstellar envelope ($<$60 $R_{\star}$)" (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing); "While PN has a similar inner shell distribution, it is prominent in other structures 80–240 $R_{\star}$ from the star" (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing)
   - parameter: Shock-model chemical timescales: ~100–200 years  
  > "The chemical time scales need to be short ($\sim$100–200 years), however, or PO forms." (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing)
   - assumption: Convectively mixed H-burning-shell ejecta may be nitrogen-enriched.  
  > "If the clump ejecta involve material that is convectively mixed from the H-burning shell, they may be enriched in nitrogen." (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: HCN-based SW Fan 12C/13C ~7 is consistent with the proposed scenario.  
  > "Estimates of this ratio in the SW Fan suggest $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C$\sim$7, based on HCN lines (cite: 2023ApJ...954L...1S), consistent with this scenario." (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing)
6. **Interpret isotope ratios**: Compare literature isotope ratios from radiative-transfer abundances and line intensities with solar values and nucleosynthesis expectations.  
  > "The values from (cite: 2009ApJ...690..837M; 2022AJ....164..230S; 2021ApJ...920L..38S; Ravi2025a) were measured from abundances derived from radiative transfer modeling of spectral lines" (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "the others were estimated solely from line intensities (cite: 2023ApJ...954L...1S; 2022MNRAS.510..383A)." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "The carbon ratios measured in VY CMa and NML Cyg thus agree with current models of nucleosynthesis." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis)

   - validation: Check apparent anomalous ratios against optical-depth effects and uncertainties.  
  > "The low ratio is likely a result of high opacity in the H$_2$O lines of the main isotopologue." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "Much lower values were found from comparing line intensities" (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "but are probably due to high opacities in $^{28}$SiO." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis)
7. **Interpret maser wind diagnostics**: Compare maser locations with excitation models and radial density/temperature gradients; discuss polarization-derived magnetic fields.  
  > "Models for pumping and excitation provide constraints on the local number density $n$ and kinetic temperature" (Masers Tracing Wind Conditions); "Most species locations are also consistent with the $T_{\mathrm K} (r)$ gradient in the envelopes" (Masers Tracing Wind Conditions); "The field strength and direction can be retrieved by modeling circular and linear polarization" (Masers Tracing Wind Conditions)

## Qualitative findings

- VY CMa is the most chemically diverse RSG; VO, AlCl and TiO2 are known only there among RSGs.  
  > "VY CMa is in fact the most chemically diverse of all RSGs and is the only one thus far known to contain VO, AlCl and TiO$_2$" (The Molecular Inventory of the Envelopes of RSGs)
- Large species-dependent spatial differences primarily trace chemistry and sometimes elemental enrichment.  
  > "The most remarkable result from these images is the large differences in spatial distributions among chemical species." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules); "Because the dipole moments and excitation energies of these molecules are very similar, these variations in spatial location are primarily the result of chemistry and, in certain cases, elemental enrichment." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules)
- LTE chemistry contributes to metal and phosphorus oxide synthesis near the star.  
  > "LTE chemistry, therefore, plays an important role in the synthesis of metal and phosphorus oxides in RSGs." (The Role of LTE Formation)
- PN and NS spatial correlation suggests nitrogen-rich gas in ejecta; shocks and possible convective mixing influence chemistry.  
  > "there is a remarkable correlation between PN and NS in the ALMA images, suggesting that both molecules arise in nitrogen-rich gas." (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing); "These data show the influence on RSG circumstellar chemistry of the discrete mass loss and associated shocks, as well as that of possible convective mixing in the ejection processes." (Chemical Formation of Phosphorus Nitride: Mass Loss, Shocks, and Convective Mixing)
- RSG carbon isotope ratios agree with nucleosynthesis models involving H-burning-shell mixing.  
  > "The lower values in the RSGs reflect the mixing into the H-burning shell." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "The carbon ratios measured in VY CMa and NML Cyg thus agree with current models of nucleosynthesis." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: High-resolution ALMA images allow close examination of gas-phase chemical processes.  
  > "The high spatial resolution of the ALMA images allows a close examination of the gas-phase chemical processes occurring in VY CMa" (The Role of LTE Formation)
- Why this method: Spatial comparisons probe chemistry because the molecules have similar dipole moments and excitation energies; radiative-transfer line-profile fits constrain abundance and extent.  
  > "Because the dipole moments and excitation energies of these molecules are very similar, these variations in spatial location are primarily the result of chemistry and, in certain cases, elemental enrichment." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules); "The advantage of this modeling is that the lines profiles are fit to derive both peak abundance and spatial extent." (An Overview of Abundances)
- Alternative: Some molecular non-detections may reflect sensitivity rather than absence.  
  > "NML Cyg does not quite have the variety of species that VY CMa has, but this result is likely a simple question of sensitivity." (An Overview of Abundances)
- Alternative: Spatial scatter in carbon isotope ratios may reflect varying mixing or measurement uncertainties.  
  > "This scatter in the ratios may represent different mass loss events with varying degrees of mixing" (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "or they may be more indicative of the uncertainties." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis)
- Alternative: Very low water-based 16O/18O is attributed to opacity rather than extraordinary 18O enrichment.  
  > "It would seem unlikely that these processes would result in such excess $^{18}$O in RSGs." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "The low ratio is likely a result of high opacity in the H$_2$O lines of the main isotopologue." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis)
- Alternative: Age and detached, thinner shells may explain missing inner-shell molecules in YHGs.  
  > "Here, there is likely an age factor at play, as YHGs are more evolved than Red Hypergiants." (Prospectus); "They also appear to have more detached, and therefore, thinner shells." (Prospectus); "This fact may explain the absence of many inner shell molecules such as metal halides and oxides." (Prospectus)

- Limitation (authors): Published spatial information outside VY CMa is limited.  
  > "Outside of the VY CMa images, little else exists in the current literature for RSGs." (The Surprising Variation in Spatial Distributions of Molecules)
- Limitation (authors): Compiled abundances use different methods; rotational diagrams are less accurate than radiative transfer.  
  > "It should be noted that these abundances have not been derived from the same method." (An Overview of Abundances); "Although the latter is not as accurate as a radiative transfer analysis, it nonetheless provides insight into abundances." (An Overview of Abundances)
- Limitation (authors): Non-thermal SiO lines complicate abundance estimates.  
  > "It is more difficult to quantify the abundance of SiO, as the lines are often non-thermal" (An Overview of Abundances)
- Limitation (authors): Isotope measurements are sparse and overlapping regions complicate accurate ratios.  
  > "Unfortunately, the number of values is very limited." (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis); "It is difficult to measure accurate ratios in multiple overlapping regions" (Molecules, Isotopes and Nucleosynthesis)
