## Analysis recipe

1. **Reprocess and combine archival ALMA data into continuum-subtracted cubes**: Archive data downloaded, reprocessed and 12-m and 7-m data combined in CASA with continuum subtraction  
  > "The data were downloaded from the archive, reprocessed" (2.2 ALMA Band 7 data); "in the data reduction package CASA (cite: CASAREF), with a continuum subtraction applied." (2.2 ALMA Band 7 data)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Band 7 12-m mosaic of 39 Nyquist-sampled pointings  
  > "These observations comprise a 39 pointing Nyquist sampled rectangular mosaic of the central region of SDC335" (2.2 ALMA Band 7 data)
   - parameter: 7-m mosaic of 14 pointings  
  > "The 7-m data had a complementary 14 point mosaic pattern" (2.2 ALMA Band 7 data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify outflows and measure lobe velocity ranges, extents and opening angles**: Visual inspection of continuum-subtracted cubes; velocity range ends at the last channel with a 3 sigma detection; integrated intensity maps over those ranges used to measure extent and opening angle  
  > "the velocity ranges of each identified outflow for each molecular species were found by visual inspection" (3 Outflow identification); "(defined as the absolute offset from $V_{lsr}$ to the last channel with a 3$\sigma$ detection)" (3 Outflow identification)
   - parameter: Cubes span -100 to 0 km/s  
  > "These cubes have a velocity range from $-$100 to 0 kms$^{-1}$" (3 Outflow identification)
   - parameter: Emission within +/-5 km/s of Vlsr excluded for all species except CO  
  > "For all species, except CO, we excluded emission $\pm5$kms$^{-1}$from the $V_{lsr}$." (3 Outflow identification)
   - assumption: Vlsr adopted as -46.6 km/s for MM1a and MM1b and -46.5 km/s for MM2, from the mm-core values  
  > "we adopted a $V_{lsr}$ of $-$46.6kms$^{-1}$ for MM1" (3 Outflow identification)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Quantify jet-like collimation and velocity gradient of outflow A blue lobe**: Length-to-width ratio at the 10% integrated-intensity contour and a position-velocity diagram along the outflow axis in CO  
  > "The length-to-width ratio of the ALMA CO and $^{13}$CO contour at the 10% of peak integrated intensity contours" (3.1.1 Jet-like properties in outflow A); "a position velocity (PV) diagram along the outflow axis as observed in CO outward from the driving source" (3.1.1 Jet-like properties in outflow A)
   - parameter: Velocity gradient of 155 km/s/pc from CO  
  > "From the CO data, we calculate a velocity gradient of 155 kms$^{-1}$ pc$^{-1}$." (3.1.1 Jet-like properties in outflow A)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Constrain outflow inclination angles and derive corrected lengths, velocities and dynamical times**: Cabrit and Bertout biconical cases from the 3 sigma opening angle, then a pencil-like morphology and observed length-to-width ratio to narrow the range; lengths, velocities, momentum, energy and flux corrected by trigonometric factors  
  > "we assume each outflow has a symmetric biconical geometry, following (cite: CabritBertout86)" (3.2.1 Inclination angles); "we find inclination angle ranges of between 53 and 76$^{\circ}$ for outflow A and 59 and 79$^{\circ}$ for outflow B which can provide the observed $LWR_O$." (3.2.2 Further consideration of the outflow inclination angles)
   - parameter: Opening angle measured at the 3 sigma outer emission contour  
  > "the outer emission contour at 3$\sigma$" (3.2.1 Inclination angles)
   - parameter: Outflow C uses the statistical average inclination 57.3 deg  
  > "we use, instead, the commonly adopted statistical average value of $i =57.3^{\circ}$." (3.2.1 Inclination angles)
   - parameter: Corrected length l_corr = l_measured / sin(i)  
  > "The corrected length is calculated as $l_{corr}=l_{measured}/\sin(i)$ for inclination angle $i$." (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - parameter: Dynamical time t_dyn = l_corr / |V_max_corr|  
  > "Finally, $t_{dyn}$, the dynamical age of the outflow is calculated as $t_{dyn}=l_{corr}/|V_{max_{corr}}|$." (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - assumption: Symmetric biconical outflow geometry for the initial ranges; pencil-like morphology for the final ranges  
  > "we instead propose an alternate morphology to limit the inclination angle ranges of these outflows." (3.2.2 Further consideration of the outflow inclination angles)
   - fitting: Numerical solution for the inclination and widest-point position that reproduce observed length-to-width ratio and x_O within +/-10%  
  > "we can solve numerically the range of $i$ and $x$ values that will return a $LWR$ matching our observed values" (Appendix B); "we consider values of $i$ and $x$ which return both an $x_O$ and $LWR_O$ within $\pm 10\%$ of our observed values." (Appendix B)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate outflow kinetic temperature from APEX CO SLED**: Regrid APEX CO cubes to the poorest resolution, make 5 km/s integrated-intensity maps, measure intensities at two positions per outflow, and fit a RADEX model grid by chi-squared  
  > "we first regridded the data from each observation to the poorest spatial and velocity resolution" (3.2.3 Temperature estimation); "We then created integrated intensity maps at 5 kms$^{-1}$ intervals from each cube." (3.2.3 Temperature estimation); "we measured the integrated intensity (in K kms$^{-1}$) at two positions along the outflow" (3.2.3 Temperature estimation)
   - software: RADEX
   - fitting: Grid over temperature, number and column density; best fit chi2 ~ 1; average of models with chi2 = 1 +/- 0.05  
  > "The best fit being a $\chi^2\sim1$ following the $\chi^2$ approach of (cite: vanderTak2000)." (3.2.3 Temperature estimation); "We then take all models with $\chi^2=1\pm0.05$ and use the average properties of these models." (3.2.3 Temperature estimation)
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Derive 12CO optical depth correction**: Compare 13CO/12CO line ratios at peak emission along the outflow A blue wing to the optical depth ratio, assuming tau(12CO) = f x tau(13CO)  
  > "we use the $^{13}$CO to $^{12}$CO line ratio observed in the blue wing of outflow A" (3.2.4 Derived properties); "use the assumption that $\tau_{^{12}CO}$ is equal to $f\times \tau_{^{13}CO}$" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - parameter: Isotopic ratio f = 50-100  
  > "Here, $f$ is the isotopic abundance ratio, in the range $f=50-100$ typical of star-forming regions (cite: Pineda10; Szucs14)." (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the literature range of CO optical depths  
  > "These are comparable to typical values seen in CO outflows e.g. (cite: CabritBertout92) of 1 to 8.9 with a median of 3.5." (3.2.4 Derived properties)
7. **Compute outflow mass, momentum, energy and momentum flux from ALMA CO**: Sum of CO emission above 3 x RMS per channel converted to temperature and mass per voxel, then momentum, energy and annular momentum flux, corrected for inclination  
  > "For our calculations with the CO ALMA data, we define mass within the outflow, $M_{out}$ in units of M$_{\odot}$, as:" (3.2.4 Derived properties); "for all pixels with an intensity greater than or equal to three times the RMS per channel" (3.2.4 Derived properties); "Finally, we calculate the momentum flux $F_{CO}$ within annular regions projected out from the central source" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - parameter: Distance 3.25 kpc  
  > "and D is this distance to SDC335 (3.25kpc)" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - parameter: Systemic velocity excluded by +/-2.5 sigma of a Gaussian fit to APEX CO(3-2) for MM1 (sigma = 5.2 km/s); +/-1.5 sigma for MM2 outflow C  
  > "We fit a Gaussian to this spectrum, centred at the $V_{lsr}$ and exclude emission from channels $\pm2.5\times$ the variance, $\sigma$, of this Gaussian fit." (3.2.4 Derived properties); "Instead we use $\pm1.5\sigma$ sufficient to avoid self absorption artefacts in the CO data" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - parameter: Two temperature cases: T = 20 K with tau_corr = 3.5, and RADEX temperatures with tau_corr = 8.2  
  > "We calculate the mass both with a lower bound temperature $T$ of 20K and a literature value for $\tau_{corr}$ of 3.5 (cite: CabritBertout92)" (3.2.4 Derived properties); "with a $\tau_{corr}$ factor of 8.2 based upon our estimated $^{12}$CO optical depth." (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - parameter: Inclination corrections 1/cos(i), 1/cos^2(i), sin(i)/cos^2(i) for P, E, F  
  > "the values of $P_{CO}$, $E_{CO}$, and $F_{CO}$ are corrected by the factors $1/\cos(i)$, $1/\cos ^2(i)$ and $\sin(i)/\cos ^2(i)$, respectively" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - assumption: Lower-bound temperature of 20 K for the first mass estimate  
  > "We calculate the mass both with a lower bound temperature $T$ of 20K and a literature value for $\tau_{corr}$ of 3.5 (cite: CabritBertout92)" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature outflow values from low- to high-mass protostars  
  > "with the SDC335 outflows A and B typically having amongst the highest values compared to published sources" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
8. **Convert momentum flux to protostellar accretion rate and compare with cloud infall**: Accretion rate from F_CO using the Duarte-Cabral et al. relation with fixed entrainment and wind parameters; sum over outflows compared with the Peretto et al. cloud infall rate  
  > "Following this form of the $F_{CO}$ to $\dot M_{acc}$ relation and assuming the same material entrainment efficiency and wind properties as these authors have done" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates); "giving a $\dot M_{tot. acc}$ of 1.4 ($\pm$ 0.1) $\times 10^{-3}$M$_{\odot}$ yr$^{-1}$" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates)
   - parameter: f_ent = 0.5, Mdot_w/Mdot_acc = 0.15, v_w = 40 km/s  
  > "($f_{ent} = 0.5$, $\dot M_{w}/\dot M_{acc} = 0.15$ and $v_w = 40$kms$^{-1}$)" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates)
   - assumption: Same entrainment efficiency and wind properties as Duarte-Cabral et al.  
  > "assuming the same material entrainment efficiency and wind properties as these authors have done" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Total accretion rate compared with the cloud infall rate within quoted errors  
  > "These two values are comparable within the quoted errors" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates)
9. **Place outflows on the F_CO versus L_bol diagram and assess evolutionary stage**: Fit linear relations between log F_CO and log L_bol for literature Class 0 and Class I sources and compare the SDC335 sources, with L_bol of MM1 split between MM1a and MM1b  
  > "we generate best linear fit lines for the $F_{CO}$ as a function of $L_{bol}$ for Class 0 and I sources" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335); "in a 2.5:1 ratio (based on their respective optically thin radio emission flux ratios" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335)
   - parameter: SDC335 points carry +/-50% error on L_bol; A and B F_CO error bars span the inclination range  
  > "To compensate for this source of uncertainty each SDC335 point on Figure" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335)
   - assumption: Each identified outflow is driven by a single protostellar object  
  > "Assuming each of the identified outflows in SDC335 represents a single protostellar object" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335)
   - fitting: Linear fits in log-log space with 1 sigma error margins (sampler and likelihood not stated)  
  > "-4.3(\pm0.15) + 0.60(\pm0.08)\log_{10}" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335); "-5.3(\pm0.12) + 0.63(\pm0.16)\log_{10}" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335)
   - validation: MM1a and MM1b lie within the 1 sigma band of the Class 0 fit  
  > "lie very close to the Class 0 best fit line and within the 1-$\sigma$ error bound of that line of best fit" (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335)

## Qualitative findings

- Red-shifted emission is weaker than blue in every outflow  
  > "This is consistent with the trend seen throughout all the presented data, which shows that red-shifted emission is found to be weaker within each outflow." (3.1.1 A: North-south molecular outflow)
- A biconical morphology cannot reproduce the observed length-to-width ratios of outflows A and B  
  > "we find that it is not possible to recover such observed length-to-width ratios with no external influence on the outflow." (3.2.2 Further consideration of the outflow inclination angles)
- Outflow C has a much lower momentum flux than the literature trend, possibly an artefact of the limited velocity range  
  > "SDC335 outflow C has a significantly lower $F_{CO}$ value away from the proposed observed trend seen within the literature." (4.2 Evolutionary Status of SDC335)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: CO traces the bulk high-velocity entrained gas; ALMA Band 7 CO has the best sensitivity and resolution  
  > "Finally, CO, being ubiquitous in the ISM, provides sufficient molecular abundance to trace the high velocity gas entrained by the outflows." (2 Observations and ancillary data); "Using the ALMA band 7 CO data (the dataset with both the highest sensitivity and spatial resolution)" (3.1.4 Considering other possible outflows)
- Why this method: Outflow momentum flux used as a proxy for protostellar accretion rate  
  > "is a proxy for the protostellar mass accretion rate" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates)
- Alternative: SiO not used for mass/momentum because it traces shocks only  
  > "SiO is a shock-tracing species and therefore cannot reliably trace the bulk outflow properties" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
- Alternative: HNC not used for mass/momentum because its outflow-tracer behaviour is poorly established  
  > "HNC is currently a poorly studied outflow tracer" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
- Alternative: Pencil-like morphology used in place of the biconical morphology for inclination limits  
  > "we instead propose an alternate morphology to limit the inclination angle ranges of these outflows." (3.2.2 Further consideration of the outflow inclination angles)
- How they decided it was enough: Excluded velocity-range factor of 2.5 sigma chosen to remove all self-absorption and missing-flux features in the MM1 outflows  
  > "The factor of 2.5 was chosen to ensure that all self-absorption and missing flux features in the interferometric data were excluded for the MM1 core outflows" (3.2.4 Derived properties)
- Limitation (authors): Excluding near-systemic channels makes derived properties of outflows A and B lower limits  
  > "Excluding these velocity channels means our derived properties are lower limits for outflows A and B." (3.2.4 Derived properties)
- Limitation (authors): No zero-spacing or single-dish data for the ALMA image, causing negative bowling near the systemic velocity  
  > "owing to a lack of zero-spacing or single dish data, the ALMA image suffers from negative" (3.1.4 Considering other possible outflows)
- Limitation (authors): Inclination angles dominate the error budget  
  > "As the inclination angles of the observed outflow are the dominant factor in the error budget of these calculations" (4.1 Cloud infall and protostellar accretion rates)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Accretion rates rest on a literature entrainment and wind calibration plus a pencil-like morphology the authors introduce; the bicone alternative changes the total accretion rate by about a factor of 2.3
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 20 K and RADEX-temperature cases differ, and temperatures come from low-resolution APEX data applied to ALMA-resolved lobes
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No imaging parameters, noise values or uncertainties on masses are given, so error propagation is unclear
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Class 0 and Class I fits use literature samples whose selection and luminosity definitions may not match the SDC335 points
