## Analysis recipe

1. **Self-calibrate DIHCA config 5 and 8 data**: Four iterations of phase-only self-calibration per configuration, first using the config 10 data as model  
  > "The first iteration used the configuration 10 data as model for a better spatial alignment of the three datasets." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center); "Self-calibration was done separately for the configuration 5 and 8 data with four iterations of phase self-calibration for each configuration." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: solint: config 8 inf, 30, 15 s; config 5 30, 20, 10 s  
  > "for the configuration 8 data the length of the scan (solint=’inf’), 30 and 15 sec, for the configuration 5 data 30, 20 and 10sec." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: S/N improved from 145 to 200 (config 8) and 30 to 108 (config 5)  
  > "The signal-to-noise ratio between the first and last self-calibration iterations improved from 145 to 200 and from 30 to 108 for the configuration 8 and 5 data, respectively." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
2. **Jointly deconvolve configs 5, 8 and 10**: Image each configuration separately, then joint deconvolution of all three with Briggs robust 0  
  > "we first imaged all three datasets separately, and then conducted a joint deconvolution of all configurations together with a Briggs robust weighting of 0." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0  
  > "a joint deconvolution of all configurations together with a Briggs robust weighting of 0." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared the individual and joint images to check recovery of extended emission together with high resolution  
  > "To evaluate the scales traced by each configuration, we first imaged all three datasets separately" (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
3. **Large-scale kinematics from MALT90**: Integrated emission and 1st moment maps of HCO+ and N2H+ (1-0); maps shifted west to align with ATLASGAL  
  > "the integrated emission and the 1st moment maps (intensity-weighted peak velocities)" (3.1 Large-scale structure and dynamics); "Therefore, we shifted all MALT90 maps by 3 pixels or $22.9''$ to the west to account for this difference" (2.1 Large-scale and single-dish data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure filament velocity differences and lengths**: Delta v from the DCN 1st moment map between the northern and southern ends of the filaments relative to central v_LSR; lengths along the ellipse long axis  
  > "are measured from the DCN 1st moment map between the northern and southern ends of the filaments" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics); "are also measured along the main long axis of the ellipses" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - parameter: central velocity ~ -3.6 km/s  
  > "with respect to the central velocity of $\sim -3.6$ km s$^{-1}$ ($v_{\rm LSR}$ from (cite: leurini2011))" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Filament masses from 1.3 mm continuum**: Optically thin dust emission from the 1.3 mm continuum  
  > "The masses $M_{\rm fil}$ can be estimated from the 1.3 mm continuum data assuming optically thin dust emission following (cite: hildebrand1983) or (cite: schuller2009)." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - assumption: kappa = 0.9 cm^2/g (MRN dust at 1e6 cm^-3, 1.3 mm)  
  > "we assume a dust opacity $\kappa = 0.9$ cm$^2$g$^{-1}$ ((cite: ossenkopf1994), MRN dust at $10^6$ cm$^{-3}$ and 1.3 mm)" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - assumption: gas-to-dust ratio 150, temperature 30 K, distance 2.0 kpc  
  > "a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 150 (cite: draine2003) and a temperature of 30 K (cite: urquhart2018)" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics); "We use that distance in the following." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Derive filamentary mass inflow rates**: Mdot = M_fil * Delta v / l_fil * 1/tan(i), following Kirk, Henshaw, Wells  
  > "following, for example, (cite: kirk2013), (cite: henshaw2014), and (cite: wells2024):" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics); "the velocity difference from one end of the filament to the central core region" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - assumption: Inclination of filaments unknown, dominant systematic uncertainty (~1 order of magnitude in rate)  
  > "the dominating systematic uncertainty appears to be the unknown inclination angle (see above)." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with literature infall and flow rates of 1e-4 to 1e-3 Msun/yr  
  > "which is consistent with our finding for G351." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
7. **Check supply against free-fall time**: Free-fall time of the region from ATLASGAL mass and size; multiply by steady flow rate  
  > "We can estimate the free-fall time-scale of the region from the single-dish ATLASGAL data." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics); "following (cite: stahler2005), the free-fall time-scale of the region is $\sim1.6\times 10^5$ yrs." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Small-scale kinematics of the central cores**: 1st moment map, velocity-channel integrated intensity maps and an east-west position-velocity diagram of H2CO(3_2,2-2_2,1)  
  > "which is one of the lines the DIHCA and high-resolution data have in common" (3.3 Small-scale kinematics around the central cores); "integrated intensity maps over selected velocity regimes, and a position-velocity (pv) diagram in the east-west direction across this central region" (3.3 Small-scale kinematics around the central cores)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: pv diagram cross-checks the moment analysis, which can be misleading in complex kinematics  
  > "The pv-diagram clearly confirms the complex velocity structure, also showing that moment analysis can partly be misleading in complex kinematic environments." (3.3 Small-scale kinematics around the central cores)

## Qualitative findings

- Filamentary gas streams from north and south converge on the central region; blue-shifted gas connects at the north-east edge and red-shifted gas at the south-west  
  > "More precisely, the blue-shifted gas connects to the central area at its north-eastern edge, whereas the red-shifted gas connects at the south-western side." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
- Innermost kinematics are disordered with several velocity components, attributed to inflow plus protostellar feedback  
  > "We speculate that this is caused by the interplay of the converging infalling gas with feedback processes from the forming central protostars." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Configuration 10 data resolve out extended emission, so they were combined with configs 5 and 8  
  > "To overcome this extreme spatial filtering issue, we now combined the DIHCA configuration 5 and 8 data with the separately observed configuration 10 data." (2.2.2 ALMA zoom toward the center)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Inclination of the filaments is unknown, giving about 1 order of magnitude uncertainty in the flow rates  
  > "Hence, the flow rates are associated with an uncertainty of roughly 1 order of magnitude." (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
- Limitation (authors): Data cannot separate gravity-driven converging streams from externally driven flows  
  > "our data do not allow us to differentiate whether these converging gas streams are caused soley by the gravitational potential of the region" (3.2 Intermediate-scale filamentary structure and kinematics)
- Limitation (authors): Moment analysis can be misleading in the complex central kinematics  
  > "showing that moment analysis can partly be misleading in complex kinematic environments" (3.3 Small-scale kinematics around the central cores)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Filament masses use a single dust temperature (30 K) and opacity with no uncertainty propagation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flow rates assume gas streams along the filaments with no error bars on masses, lengths or velocity differences
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Large-scale link relies on a 38'' single-dish map that was manually shifted by 22.9'' to match ATLASGAL
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only an infall interpretation of the 1st moment gradients is tested; rotation or outflow contamination of DCN and H2CO moments is not excluded
