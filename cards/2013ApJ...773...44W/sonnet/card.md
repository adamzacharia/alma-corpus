## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit source sizes of line and continuum**: 2-D Gaussian fits with deconvolution of the beam  
  > "We fit the sizes of the line or continuum sources using the IMFIT task in CASA, which performs synthesized beam deconvolution and 2-dimensional Gaussian fitting to the images." (3 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure [C II] line parameters and continuum**: Subtract continuum in uv plane, integrate intensity over line-emitting region per channel, fit single Gaussian  
  > "calculate the line spectra by integrating the intensity over the line-emitting region in each channel, and fit the spectra with a Gaussian profile." (3 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: single Gaussian profile  
  > "redshift, flux, and FWHM line width of the [C II] line fitted to a single Gaussian line profile" (Table 2)
   - validation: Continuum flux densities compared with MAMBO values  
  > "which is in very good agreement with the MAMBO value" (3 Results)
3. **Derive [C II] luminosity and FIR luminosity**: L_CII from Solomon & Vanden Bout formula; L_FIR (42.5-122.5 um) from modified blackbody fit to ALMA, MAMBO, PdBI, SCUBA data  
  > "We then estimate their FIR luminosities ($\rm L_{FIR}$) by fitting a modified black body" (3 Results)
   - parameter: Tdust=47 K, beta=1.6 for four sources; J2054-0005 Tdust free with Herschel 350 um  
  > "leave $\rm T_{dust}$ as a free parameter. The fit suggests $\rm T_{dust}=52\pm6$ K" (3 Results)
   - assumption: Tdust=47 K, beta=1.6 (mean of high-z FIR-luminous quasars)  
  > "we adopt a dust temperature of $\rm T_{dust}=47$ K and emissivity index of $\rm \beta=1.6$" (3 Results)
   - fitting: modified blackbody S_nu ~ nu^(3+beta)/(exp(h nu/kT)-1); sampler/priors not stated  
  > "fitting a modified black body (i.e., $\rm S_{\nu}\sim {\nu}^{3+\beta}/(exp(h\nu /kT_{dust})-1)$" (Results)
   - validation: Sensitivity to Tdust=50 K (+20%) and Tdust=41 K, beta=1.95 (-8%)  
  > "could be larger by up to 20% if a higher dust temperature of 50K is assumed" (3 Results)
4. **Estimate dynamical masses**: Rotating-disk Mdyn=1.16e5 vcir^2 D; vcir=0.75 FWHM/sin i; i=arccos(minor/major); D=1.5x FWHM major axis  
  > "$\rm M_{dyn}/M_{\odot}\approx1.16\times10^5{v_{cir}}^2D$" (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission); "$\rm i=cos^{-1}(R)$" (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
   - assumption: Rotating disk geometry; J1044-0125 excluded  
  > "If we assume a rotating disk geometry for the [C II]-emitting gas" (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Uncertainties from FWHM and size errors, 10-40%  
  > "the uncertainties estimated with the measurement errors in $\rm FWHM_{[CII]}$ and [C II] source size are about 10% to 40%" (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
5. **Estimate SMBH masses and MBH/Mdyn**: Lbol=4.2 L1450 then Eddington-limited accretion for MBH; ratio to Mdyn  
  > "We then derive the SMBH masses ($\rm M_{BH}$) from the quasar luminosity assuming Eddington accretion." (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
   - assumption: Negligible host dust extinction at 1450 A; Eddington accretion  
  > "We here assume that the dust extinction from quasar host galaxies at 1450 $\rm \AA$ is negligible." (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Search for serendipitous line emitters**: Bayesian line search in cubes; candidate probabilities  
  > "We do not find any candidate detections of serendipitous line emission in our data with probabilities above $25\%$." (4.3 Search for [CII] line emitters associated with the quasar environments)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA Cycle 0 gives sub-arcsec resolution and sensitivity to detect [C II] in an hour; sources chosen as bright in mm continuum and CO with Dec <= +20.  
  > "Thus, the Cycle 0 phase of ALMA opens a unique opportunity to expand the observation of [C II] line emission to a large sample of quasar-starburst systems at the highest redshift." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Limited resolution gives only constraints on size; morphology uncertain, large errors on minor axis and PA affect inclination and Mdyn.  
  > "there are still large uncertainties in the intrinsic source morphology measurements; the deconvolved minor axis and position angle measurements of some of the objects show large error bars." (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
- Limitation (authors): [C II] may trace only the central starburst, underestimating bulge mass.  
  > "This may underestimate the total masses within the spheroidal stellar bulges." (4.2 Dynamical masses traced by the [C II] line emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dynamical masses depend on a crude inclination from poorly constrained axis ratios and an assumed disk.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): MBH assumes Eddington accretion, which biases MBH/Mdyn.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Velocity gradients could arise from mergers or outflows rather than rotation.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No imaging parameters, so beams and fluxes cannot be reproduced exactly.
