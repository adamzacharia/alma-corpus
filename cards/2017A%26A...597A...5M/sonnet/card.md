## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit UV-to-radio SEDs**: MAGPHYS high-z extension fits to COSMOS2015 plus (sub)mm and radio photometry, with redshift grid for photo-z uncertainty  
  > "The observational data were modelled using the Multiwavelength Analysis of Galaxy Physical Properties code `MAGPHYS`" (3.1.1 Method)
   - software: MAGPHYS
   - parameter: redshift grid Δz = 0.01 over the quoted photo-z range; 16th-84th percentile propagated  
  > "fitting the source SED over the quoted range of redshifts using a fine redshift grid of $\Delta z = 0.01$" (3.1.2 SED results)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; alpha_ff=-0.1, alpha_synch=-0.8; q_FIR centred at 2.34; no AGN  
  > "The thermal free-free emission spectral index in `MAGPHYS` is fixed at $\alpha_{\rm ff}=-0.1$, while that of the non-thermal synchrotron emission is fixed at $\alpha_{\rm synch}=-0.8$." (3.1.1 Method)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared SFR from L_IR (Kennicutt) with MAGPHYS SFR; ratio median 1.42  
  > "the ratio ${\rm SFR}_{\rm IR}/{\rm SFR}_{\rm MAGPHYS}$ was found to range from 0.94 to 3.43 with a median of $1.42^{+0.53}_{-0.22}$" (3.1.2 SED results)
2. **Radio spectral indices and IR-radio q**: Linear least squares on log-log radio SED; survival analysis (dblcens, Kaplan-Meier NADA) for medians; q from L_IR and 1.4 GHz luminosity from 325 MHz  
  > "A linear least squares regression was used to fit the data points on a log–log scale to derive the radio spectral indices." (3.2)
   - software: R dblcens, NADA
   - assumption: single power law across three radio points  
  > "we did not try to fit them with anything more complex than a single power law" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Compare with main sequence and ALESS SMGs**: SFR/SFR_MS using Speagle et al. (2014) main sequence; K-S tests against flux-limited ALESS sample  
  > "we overlay the best fit from Speagle et al. (2014), which is based on a compilation of 25 studies" (3.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Updated MAGPHYS optimised for z>1 star-forming galaxies suits SMGs and allows direct comparison with ALESS

- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Redshift uncertainties propagated only via a redshift grid, so errors are lower limits; small sample statistics  
  > "The uncertainties derived using this approach should be interpreted as lower limits to the true uncertainties." (3.1.2 SED results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Many redshifts are photometric and uncertain; some revised during this work
- Caveat (inferred, ours): AGN contribution ignored in SED fits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Trends from binned data with few points are not significant
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample of 16 is small
