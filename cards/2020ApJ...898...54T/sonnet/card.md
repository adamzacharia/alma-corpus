## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and cubes**: tclean with natural weighting; imcontsub for continuum  
  > "The data cubes were imaged with the TCLEAN task within CASA. Natural weighting was applied." (Data and Reduction Procedure)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify cores and measure positions**: 2D Gaussian fits to moment zero maps of H30alpha (Core A) and CH3OH (Cores B, C)  
  > "Core B and Core C are identified by the 2D Gaussian fitting of the moment zero image of the CH$_{3}$OH ($4_{-2, 3}-3_{-1, 2}$ $E$) line." (Table 2)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian  
  > "Table [2](#tab:coreid) lists general properties of these cores based on 2D Gaussian fittings to the moment zero maps." (Continuum Image and the H$_{2}$ Column Density of Identified Cores)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive H2 column density from dust continuum**: Kauffmann et al. 2008 formula with free-free subtracted for Core A  
  > "We corrected the continuum emission toward Core A by subtracting the free-free continuum emission as we describe in Appendix [6](#sec:Halpha)." (3.1 Continuum Image and the H2 Column Density of Identified Cores)
   - parameter: beam sizes 0.3" Core A, 0.5" others  
  > "These fluxes are beam-averaged values with beam sizes of 0.3$''$ for Core A and 0.5$''$ for the others, respectively." (3.1 Continuum Image and the H2 Column Density of Identified Cores)
   - assumption: Tdust = 100 K, beta = 1.6, kappa = 0.1 (nu/THz)^beta  
  > "The dust temperature ($T$) is assumed to be 100 K, because COMs have been detected toward all of the cores" (3.1 Continuum Image and the H2 Column Density of Identified Cores)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Temperature varied 50-200 K changes N(H2) by factor ~2  
  > "If we change the temperature between 50 and 200 K, the derived $N_{\rm{H}_{2}}$ values change by a factor of $\sim2$." (3.1 Continuum Image and the H2 Column Density of Identified Cores)
4. **CH3CN rotational diagram for Tex and N**: Gaussian fits to K=0-7 lines of J=12-11, rotational diagram, optically thin LTE  
  > "We derive the excitation temperatures and column densities of CH$_{3}$CN using its $K-$ladder lines of the $J=12-11$ transition." (3.4 Analyses)
   - software: CASSIS
   - assumption: optically thin  
  > "The rotational diagram fittings of CH$_{3}$CN do not show any systematic shifts as shown in Figure [8](#fig:CH3CN)." (3.4 Analyses)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile; K=7 omitted for Core A  
  > "We fitted the spectra with a Gaussian profile. We cannot fit the $K=7$ line toward Core A, and we omitted it from the fitting." (Analyses)
   - validation: Rotational diagram shows no systematic shifts; optically thick case not ruled out  
  > "Although we assumed that the CH$_{3}$CN lines are optically thin, we cannot rule out the optically thick case due to the scatter." (Analyses)
5. **Derive column densities of other species**: CASSIS LTE model with MCMC and chi2 minimization, two Tex cases (50-200 K; ranges around CH3CN Tex)  
  > "We derived the column densities and excitation temperatures of other species using the MCMC method and the LTE model in the CASSIS software." (3.4 Analyses)
   - software: CASSIS
   - parameter: source size 0.3" Core A, 0.5" Cores B and C; filling factor 1; FWHM free between 1 and 10 km/s  
  > "The line width (full width half maximum; FWHM) was treated as a free parameter, constrained between 1 and 10 km s$^{-1}$." (3.4 Analyses)
   - assumption: LTE, optically thin  
  > "we have used the local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) model available in CASSIS spectrum analyzer by assuming lines are optically thin." (Analyses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Case 1 vs Case 2 compared  
  > "The “Case 1" and “Case 2" represent the different assumed excitation temperature ranges, as described above." (Analyses)
6. **Fractional abundances and comparison with models**: X = N(mol)/N(H2) at Cores B and C; compared with Nautilus model results  
  > "We derived fractional abundances, $X$(molecules)=$N$(molecules)/$N_{\rm{H}_{2}}$, of each species toward Cores B and C" (4.1 Comparison of the Chemical Composition Between Core B and Core C)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Cores B and C are molecule-rich with oxygen-bearing COMs; CH3CN, HC3N, HNCO detected at all cores  
  > "While oxygen-bearing COMs have been detected toward Cores B and C, CH$_{3}$CN, HC$_{3}$N, and HNCO are located in all of the cores." (Conclusion)

## Claim tuples

- H: Core B is chemically more evolved than Core C | E: Compare fractional abundances N/N(H2) at Cores B and C | R: Abundances ~1 order of magnitude higher at Core B; factor ~100 for CH3OH and H2CO | F: Core B is a more evolved hot core | **supported**
- H: H2CO forms via gas-phase/grain-surface with non-thermal desorption | E: Compare C18O and H2CO moment zero maps | R: Similar arc-like distributions with a peak at Core B | F: Gas-phase and/or grain-surface plus non-thermal desorption likely; thermal evaporation at Core B | **partially**
- H: 33SO ring around Core A is shock-related | E: Overlay 33SO and H30alpha moment zero maps | R: 33SO peaks at the outer edge of H30alpha emission | F: SO enhanced in shock from expanding HC HII region | **partially**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Earlier band 3 resolution of 1.7" could not resolve the central MYSO  
  > "However, an angular resolution of 1.7$''$ was not enough to spatially resolve the central G345.5+1.47 MYSO" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: UV destruction of molecules rejected for the 33SO ring because other molecules show no ring  
  > "This implies that the destruction did not produce the ring-like structure of $^{33}$SO emission by the UV radiation from the central star." (4.3 Spatial distribution of 33SO emission around Core A)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Low velocity resolution (3 km/s) hindered line identification and Gaussian fitting; CH3CN optical thickness cannot be ruled out  
  > "Since the velocity resolution of the final spectra of Figure [6](#fig:line1) is low (3 km s$^{-1}$), some lines could not be identified without ambiguity." (3.3 Spectra toward the three cores)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): N(H2) assumes single Tdust=100 K, uncertain by factor ~2
- Caveat (inferred, ours): LTE optically thin assumption with fixed source sizes
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Case 2 temperatures tied to uncertain CH3CN Tex
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Formation-mechanism conclusions are qualitative from morphology
