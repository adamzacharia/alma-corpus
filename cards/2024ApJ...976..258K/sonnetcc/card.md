## Analysis recipe

1. **calibrate, self-calibrate and image the line cubes and continuum**: CASA pipeline calibration; five rounds of continuum self-phase calibration per execution; recentre with fixvis on an ellipse-fitted disk centre; concat of executions; first-order uvcontsub; tclean with Keplerian masks  
  > "five rounds of continuum self-phase calibration were performed for each execution, selecting line-free channels carefully" (2.1 Data Reduction); "we fitted the continuum structure with an ellipse, and both executions were recentered to the disk center using the `fixvis` task in CASA" (2.1 Data Reduction); "by fitting a first-order polynomial to the continuum channels using `uvcontsub` task in CASA" (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, keplerian_mask
   - parameter: tclean hogbom, briggs robust = 1.0 for N2H+ and DCO+; multiscale scales [0,5,15,25] pixels with natural weighting for weak lines  
  > "using `hogbom` deconvolving algorithm with `briggs` weighting (robust=1.0)" (2.1 Data Reduction); "We adopted the `natural` weighting scheme to enhance the signal-to-noise ratio for the weak lines." (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - assumption: Keplerian mask geometry M* = 1.37 Msun, i = 37 deg, PA = 278 deg rescaled to 150 pc  
  > "with the geometric parameters $M_{*}$ = $1.37 M_{\odot}$, i = 37$^{\circ}$, and PA = 278$^{\circ}$" (2.1 Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Disk-centre offset between the two executions checked  
  > "The offset between the disk centers in the two executions was only" (2.1 Data Reduction)
2. **make integrated-intensity maps and radial intensity profiles**: Moment-0 maps with bettermoments using the Keplerian masks and no intensity threshold; azimuthally averaged radial profiles with GoFish in annuli of 1/4 beam major axis  
  > "The integrated intensity maps were generated by collapsing the image cubes along the velocity axis while applying the Keplerian masks used for cleaning." (2.2 Observational Results); "by dividing the disk radial range into a series of annular rings with a width 1/4th of the beam major axis and averaging the emission for each ring" (2.2 Observational Results)
   - software: bettermoments, GoFish
   - parameter: No intensity threshold for pixel inclusion in moment maps  
  > "We did not use any intensity threshold criteria to decide the inclusion of pixels while generating the moment map." (2.2 Observational Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Radial profiles also made without Keplerian masking for comparison  
  > "We have also shown the radial profiles for all the transitions generated without keplerian masking" (2.2 Observational Results)
3. **measure disk-integrated line flux, width and peak velocity**: Shift-and-stack Keplerian deprojected spectrum with GoFish integrated_spectrum (120-550 au), Gaussian fit with scipy curve_fit, flux from numpy.trapz over a velocity range chosen by eye  
  > "We employed the shifting and stacking technique using the `integrated_spectrum` function in the `GoFish` package (cite: GoFish) to generate a disk-integrated spectrum." (2.2 Observational Results); "We have done a Gaussian fit to the observed spectrum using the `scipy.optimize.curve_fit`" (2.2 Observational Results); "over a velocity range determined by visual inspection" (2.2 Observational Results)
   - software: GoFish, scipy, numpy
   - parameter: Radial range 120 to 550 au; shifted to systemic velocity 6.4 km/s  
  > "The radial range for generating the integrated spectrum is between 120 au and 550 au." (2.2 Observational Results); "Velocities for each pixel were shifted to the systemic one, 6.4 km/s" (2.2 Observational Results)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian fit with scipy.optimize.curve_fit; FWHM = 2 sqrt(2 ln 2) sigma  
  > "We have done a Gaussian fit to the observed spectrum using the `scipy.optimize.curve_fit` to determine the peak intensity and the velocity at which the peak occurs." (2.2 Observational Results)
   - validation: Stacked spectra peak at the systemic velocity, confirming the detections  
  > "are peaking at the systemic velocity, $v_{\text{LSRK}}$ of 6.4 km/s, confirming the detections" (2.2 Observational Results)
4. **verify detections with a matched filter**: Cross-correlate observed visibilities with model visibilities from the Keplerian masks using VISIBLE  
  > "we cross-correlate observed visibility with modelled visibility derived from the Keplerian masks used during cleaning" (2.2 Observational Results); "We utilized a package called `VISIBLE` (cite: loomis2018visible) for this task." (2.2 Observational Results)
   - software: VISIBLE
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Filter response 43 sigma (N2H+) and 20 sigma (DCO+) at 6.4 km/s  
  > "we found a filter response of 43 $\sigma$, and for DCO$^+$ (5-4), it is 20 $\sigma$ at the systemic velocity of 6.4 km/s" (2.2 Observational Results)
5. **fit visibilities with a parametric disk model for geometry, surface density and temperature**: DISKFIT least-squares fit to visibilities of a flared Keplerian disk with two-Gaussian surface density and power-law rotation temperature; stage 1 per-line geometry, stage 2 geometry fixed to the averaged values and 9 remaining parameters fitted, jointly with NOEMA transitions  
  > "we perform a least-square fit to the observed visibilities to adjust an empirical parametric disk model to the data, using the DISKFIT tool from (cite: Pietu2007)" (3.1 DiskFit modelling); "In the first stage, geometric parameters are individually fitted for every observed transition." (3.1 DiskFit modelling); "The model thus has 15 possible parameters, 6 due to geometry and 9 to represent the molecular distribution and excitation conditions" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - software: DISKFIT, IMAGER, CASA
   - parameter: Surface density as the sum of two Gaussians truncated to 150-550 au; temperature a power law defined by T250 and exponent q  
  > "we represented the radial surface density profiles for each molecule by the sum of two Gaussians, truncated to inner and outer radii of 150 and 550 au, respectively" (3.1 DiskFit modelling); "The rotation temperature is assumed to be a simple power law, defined by its value at 250 au, $T_{250}$ and exponent $q$." (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - parameter: H2D+: same adopted geometry, constant T = 15 K, uniform surface density 150-450 au  
  > "a constant temperature of 15 K, and a uniform surface density between 150 and 450 au" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - assumption: Boltzmann level populations, temperature power law in radius  
  > "The model assumes level populations are governed by a Boltzmann law, with the temperature being a power law of radius." (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - assumption: Same vertical distribution for all molecules; constant intrinsic line width with radius  
  > "Molecules are assumed to follow the same vertical distribution for simplicity." (3.1 DiskFit modelling); "we assume that the intrinsic line width remains constant with radius" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - assumption: Distance 150 pc  
  > "using the distance of 150 pc determined from Gaia" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - fitting: Modified Levenberg-Marquardt least squares on visibilities; error bars from the covariance matrix  
  > "A least square minimization is performed on the different visibilities using a modified Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm." (3.1 DiskFit modelling); "Error bars are derived from the covariance matrix." (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
   - validation: Best-fit radial profile imaged from model visibilities and Kepler-deprojected, compared with the data  
  > "where the best fit radial profile (reconstructed by imaging the model visibilities and applying the Kepler deprojection) is compared with the data" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
6. **derive disk-averaged LTE surface densities and upper limits**: Optically thin LTE column density from the integrated flux with Boltzmann partition correction; nested sampling for the posterior; 3 sigma upper limits for undetected molecules over 150-450 au  
  > "We explore the likely ranges of disk-averaged surface density using nested sampling" (9 Simple LTE Approach); "for an integration area between radius 150 au and 450 au and a line width of 0.55 km/s" (2.2 Observational Results)
   - software: dynesty
   - parameter: Trot fixed to 12 K (N2H+), 16 K (DCO+), 15 K (undetected)  
  > "The rotation temperatures for N$_2$H$^+$ and DCO$^+$ were set at 12 K and 16 K, respectively. A value of 15 K was used for the undetected molecules." (9 Simple LTE Approach)
   - assumption: LTE (disk densities high compared with critical densities); optically thin for upper limits; ortho-to-para ratio thermalised for total H2D+  
  > "where we assume local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) considering the disk densities are relatively high compared to critical densities for the studied transitions" (9 Simple LTE Approach); "while assuming optically thin emission" (3.2 Results)
   - fitting: dynesty nested sampling, log-normal priors on NT between 1e5 and 1e18 cm-2, median as best fit with 16th/84th percentile errors  
  > "while assuming log-normal priors for both the molecules in the range $10^5 <$ N$_{\text{T}} < 10^{18}$ cm$^{-2}$" (9 Simple LTE Approach); "We consider the median of the posterior as the best-fit value, while the 16th and 84th percentile values account for the $1 \sigma$ uncertainties." (9 Simple LTE Approach)
   - validation: Peak-opacity estimate shows optically thin approximation costs at most about 22%  
  > "This indicates a peak line opacity of about 0.47, leading to a $\sim 22$% opacity correction at most." (9 Simple LTE Approach)
7. **compare observed surface densities with a grid of astrochemical models**: DNAUTILUS 2.0 1+1D models in a physical ring model (190-290 au) over a grid of C/O, CRI rate, UV flux, settling and initial abundances; best model by lowest reduced chi-square against N2H+ and DCO+ radial surface densities and upper limits  
  > "we explored a grid of models utilizing the least squares method" (4.3 Grid of Models); "Our physical model is constructed for a radial range of 190 au to 290 au" (4.1 Disk Physical Model)
   - software: DNAUTILUS
   - parameter: UV flux at 214 au: 375, 1500, 3000 chi0  
  > "375, 1500, 3000" (Table: Parameter Space Explored)
   - parameter: C/O from 0.5 to 1.2  
  > "different elemental C/O ratios from 0.5 to 1.2 by changing initial O abundances" (5.2 Chemical Trends)
   - parameter: CRI rate 1e-16 to 1e-20 s-1  
  > "$10^{-16}$, $10^{-17}$, $10^{-18}$, $10^{-19}$, $10^{-20}$" (Table: Parameter Space Explored)
   - assumption: Dust temperature equal to gas temperature  
  > "We have kept the dust temperature equal to the gas temperature" (4.1 Disk Physical Model)
   - assumption: Uniform 0.1 micron grains, no grain growth, dust-to-gas 1e-2 below 1 H and 1e-3 above for settling  
  > "we considered a uniform grain size of 0.1$\mu m$" (4.1 Disk Physical Model); "we adjust the $\varepsilon$ to $10^{-3}$ above 1H$_r$, while maintaining the standard $\varepsilon$ of $10^{-2}$ below 1H$_r$" (4.1 Disk Physical Model)
   - assumption: Mean molecular weight 2.37, ring gas mass 0.13 Msun, M* = 1.37 Msun  
  > "the mean molecular weight of the gas per H nuclei $\mu = 2.37$" (4.1 Disk Physical Model); "$M_* = 1.37 M_{\odot}$" (4.1 Disk Physical Model)
   - assumption: Cloud phase: 10 K, density 2e4 cm-3, Av = 15 mag, zeta = 1.3e-17 s-1, evolved 1e6 yr before the disk model  
  > "gas and dust temperature of 10 K, a total gas density of $2\times10^4$ cm$^{-3}$, a visual extinction of 15 mag and cosmic ray ionization rate of $1.3 \times 10^{-17}$ s$^{-1}$" (4.3 Grid of Models)
   - fitting: Reduced chi-square over molecules and radii with 25 degrees of freedom; modelled errors 20%; observed errors from DISKFIT peak surface-density uncertainties  
  > "$\nu$, the number of degrees of freedom is 25" (10 Grids of Astrochemical Models); "while modelled errors were assumed to be 20% to account for uncertainties in the chemical models" (10 Grids of Astrochemical Models)
   - validation: Best-fit solution independent of the assumed error size; modelled values for undetected molecules required to stay below their upper limits  
  > "the best-fit solution was found to be independent of this assumption" (10 Grids of Astrochemical Models); "We ensured that the modelled values for undetected molecules remained below their respective upper limits." (10 Grids of Astrochemical Models)

## Qualitative findings

- N2H+ emission peaks near the ring outer edge while DCO+ shows a double-peaked radial profile, one peak near the ring inner edge and one in the outer disk  
  > "The radial intensity profile of shows most emission near the ring outer edge, while exhibits double peaks, one near the ring inner edge and the other in the outer disk." (abstract)
- The cold-chemistry ions are most sensitive to the cosmic ray ionization rate; stellar UV flux and C/O have minimal impact on the three ions  
  > "while stellar UV flux and C/O ratios have minimal impact on these three ions" (abstract)
- Best model fails to match the low derived temperatures of 12 to 16 K, well below the CO freezing temperature  
  > "However, it fails to match the low temperatures derived from and , 12 to 16 K, which are much lower than the CO freezing temperature." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: GG Tau A is massive, dense and cold, an ideal candidate for cold chemistry  
  > "Given its substantial mass, high density (which should ease the detection of weak molecular lines), and low temperature, GG Tau A emerges as an ideal candidate for unravelling the intricacies of cold molecular chemistry within a protoplanetary disk." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Visibility-plane DISKFIT gives accurate geometry, multi-transition temperatures and consistent errors  
  > "This approach allows an accurate derivation of the geometrical parameters." (3.1 DiskFit modelling); "It also allows for consistent estimates of the errors on the disk parameters" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
- Alternative: N2H+ distribution can be fit nearly as well by a single Gaussian or a truncated power law; temperature unaffected  
  > "the N$_2$H$^+$ distribution can almost equally well be represented by a single Gaussian or a truncated power law" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
- Alternative: Line width as a power law of radius gave a non-significant change and was not adopted  
  > "fitting line width as a power law instead suggests a small but non-significant decrease of intrinsic line width with radius" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
- Alternative: Spin-state chemistry was not included in DNAUTILUS because it would require extensive benchmarking  
  > "we chose not to do so" (4.2 Chemical Model and Network)
- Alternative: Reset (atomic initial abundances) versus inheritance (1e6 yr cloud) scenarios explored; inheritance gave the best fit  
  > "In the reset scenario, we have taken our initial abundances in atomic forms" (Table: Parameter Space Explored notes); "is an inheritance disk model with initial abundances characterized from a starless dense molecular cloud" (4.3 Grid of Models)
- How they decided it was enough: Best chemical model is the one with the lowest reduced chi-square  
  > "The best-fit model is the one with the lowest $\chi^2_{\text{red}}$ value." (10 Grids of Astrochemical Models)
- Limitation (authors): No grain growth and perfectly spherical grains in the chemical model  
  > "One limitation of our model is the absence of a grain growth mechanism and the assumption that grains are perfectly spherical." (5.2 Chemical Trends)
- Limitation (authors): X-ray chemistry not included  
  > "Additionally, our models do not include X-ray chemistry" (5.2 Chemical Trends)
- Limitation (authors): Outer disk not modelled because of its complex structure  
  > "We have not modelled the outer disk due to its complex structure" (5.3 Possible Chemistry in the Outer Disk)
- Limitation (authors): Gaussian ring parameters in DISKFIT are strongly coupled so their errors are unreliable  
  > "so their error bars should be treated with caution" (3.1 DiskFit modelling)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Detection of the weak lines rests on moment maps made with Keplerian masks and a visually chosen velocity range; masking bias is only partly tested with unmasked profiles.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Excitation temperatures from the NOEMA plus ALMA joint fit depend on the cross-calibration of different datasets, which is not discussed.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The chemical grid is coarse (a few discrete values per parameter) and the best model still disagrees with the observed temperatures by about 10 K, so the CRI inference is model dependent.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The ring physical model (power-law structure, fixed 150 pc scaling, sigma = 0.5) is partly assumed, and the ortho-to-para correction for total H2D+ assumes thermalisation.
