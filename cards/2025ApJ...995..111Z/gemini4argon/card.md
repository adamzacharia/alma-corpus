## Qualitative findings

- Emission from CO, SiO, SO, H2CO, and CH3OH separates morphologically into a high-velocity (>12 km s^-1) collimated jet along the outflow axis and a low-velocity (6-12 km s^-1) hollow-shell cavity structure.  
  > "The resulting channel maps reveal that emission from CO 2–1, SiO 5–4, SO 6 5–5 4, H2CO 3 0,3–2 0,2, and CH3OH 4 −2,3–3 −1,2 is morphologically separated into two kinematic components: a high-velocity (> 12 km s−1) component concentrated along the collimated outflow axis, and a low-velocity (6–12 km s−1) component forming a hollow-shell cavity structure." (3.2. Molecular lines detected along the outflow)
- Molecular species in the SDC335 outflow are regulated by three main mechanisms: (1) direct entrainment from the natal core (CO, 13CO, HCO+, H2CO, CS, HC3N), (2) shock-induced grain sputtering and high-temperature shock chemistry (SiO, SO, HCN, H13CN, DCN, HC3N), and (3) thermal desorption and gas-phase chemistry driven by shock heating (CH3OH, HNCO, CH3CHO, CH3CN, CH3OCHO).  
  > "By analyzing the spatial distribution and kinematics of these molecular lines, we find that the molecular inventory in the outflow is regulated by three processes: (i) direct entrainment from the natal molecular core by the outflow; (ii) shock-induced release of molecules or atoms from dust grains; and (iii) thermal desorption and gas-phase reactions driven by shock heating." - Why this method: Normalized molecular intensities to local 13CO intensity because the outflow expands with distance so absolute intensities do not directly trace abundance variations; excluded the inner 2'' around C1 to minimize hot-core contamination; used PCA on PVDs and Kendall correlations to classify species.  
  > "To minimize this contamination, all values are divided by the reference intensity at 2′′ from the core; regions beyond 2′′ are referred to as the “pure” outflow in the following discussion." (B. SUPPLEMENTARY POSITION–VELOCITY ANALYSIS); "Because the outflow expands with distance, absolute integrated intensities are not direct tracers of abundance variations. Fig. B2 therefore shows the ratios of each molecule to the local 13CO intensity, normalized at each position, to reveal abundance gradients." (B. SUPPLEMENTARY POSITION–VELOCITY ANALYSIS)
