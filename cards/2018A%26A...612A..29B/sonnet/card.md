## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure 1.3 mm continuum**: Clean image with Briggs weighting of line-free TDM channels, then Gaussian fit to visibilities  
  > "We then estimated the continuum flux by fitting with a Gaussian function the visibilities of the continuum dataset (via the CASA task `uvmodelfit`)." (2.1 1.3 mm continuum)
   - software: CASA clean, uvmodelfit
   - parameter: Visibility noise 7.5 uJy; flux 142 uJy  
  > "We measured a continuum flux of 142 $\mu$Jy for a detection significance of 19$\sigma$" (2.1 1.3 mm continuum)
   - fitting: Gaussian fit in uv plane  
  > "fitting with a Gaussian function the visibilities of the continuum dataset" (2.1 1.3 mm continuum)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure CO(5-4) flux and size**: Continuum-subtracted cube; spectrum from polygonal region; Gaussian uvmodelfit in -800 to 800 km/s  
  > "We then estimated the line flux by fitting with a Gaussian function the visibilities of the continuum-subtracted dataset (using the CASA task `uvmodelfit`), in the same velocity range as above." (2.2 CO(5-4) emission line)
   - software: CASA, uvmodelfit
   - parameter: velocity range -800 to 800 km/s; 20 km/s binning for spectrum  
  > "integrated over the velocity range \[-800$\div$800 km s$^{-1}$\] around the expected line frequency" (2.2 CO(5-4) emission line)
   - assumption: z=1.5930 for velocity conversion  
  > "The redshift of the narrow component of the rest-frame optical emission lines (z=1.5930) was adopted to convert the frequency to velocity space" (2.2 CO(5-4) emission line)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian line fit, FWHM~500 km/s, reduced chi2 2.27  
  > "the fit with a single Gaussian component returns FWHM$\sim$500 km s$^{-1}$" (2.2 CO(5-4) emission line)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit low-J CO fluxes in PdBI/ALMA Band 3 data with fixed CO(5-4) spatial parameters**: uv-plane model fit using position and FWHM from CO(5-4)  
  > "We performed the measurement in uv space, where the model of the source is convolved with the beam" (2.3 Other CO transitions from PdBI and ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Extrapolate CO(1-0) flux from SLEDs**: Scale CO(5-4) with average SLEDs of ULIRGs, SMGs, BzK; compare with Narayanan and Bournaud models  
  > "the CO(1-0) flux extrapolated from the CO(5-4) measurement varies within a factor of 3, depending on the assumed SLED" (2.4 Estimating the CO(1-0) flux)
   - assumption: Sigma_SFR=25 Msun/yr/kpc^2 for Narayanan model  
  > "adopting $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}$=25 M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$ kpc$^{-2}$" (2.4 Estimating the CO(1-0) flux)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-check with PdBI CO(2-1) upper limit extrapolated to CO(1-0) (<0.18 Jy km/s)  
  > "we obtain an upper limit of I$_{\rm CO(1-0), 4\ensuremath{''}}<0.18$ Jy km s$^{-1}$" (2.4 Estimating the CO(1-0) flux)
5. **Derive kinematics of molecular disc**: Velocity field with immoments, Cresci2009 dynamical fit, and 3D-BAROLO tilted-ring fit  
  > "We then used $^{3D}$BAROLO (3D-Based Analysis of Rotating Object via Line Observations), a tool for fitting 3D tilted-ring models to emission-line data cubes" (4 A fast rotating molecular disc)
   - software: immoments, 3D-BAROLO
   - parameter: inclination 30 deg assumed in BAROLO (20, 40 for spectra)  
  > "Assuming an inclination angle of 30 deg, we retrieved an intrinsic rotation velocity of $\sim$420 km s$^{-1}$." (4 A fast rotating molecular disc)
   - assumption: rotating disc interpretation  
  > "a rotating gas disc remains the most probable interpretation for the detected velocity gradient" (4 A fast rotating molecular disc)
   - fitting: Tilted-ring model  
  > "We then used $^{3D}$BAROLO (3D-Based Analysis of Rotating Object via Line Observations), a tool for fitting 3D tilted-ring models to emission-line data cubes" (A fast rotating molecular disc in XID2028?)
   - validation: Dynamical mass from BAROLO (~8e10) comparable to independent estimate (~6e10)  
  > "The dynamical mass within the observed CO emission is M$_{\rm dyn}\sim8\times10^{10}$ M$_\odot$, which is comparable to our independent estimate." (4 A fast rotating molecular disc)
6. **Derive gas masses, gas fraction, depletion time**: ISM mass from Rayleigh-Jeans continuum (Scoville 2016) with Tdust from SED fit; CO mass with alpha_CO=0.8; mu=Mgas/Mstar; tdepl=Mgas/SFR  
  > "the gas depletion timescale (t$_{\rm depl}$=M$_{\rm gas}$/SFR)" (5 Gas consumption)
   - software: Duras2017 SED code, AGNfitter
   - parameter: Tdust=52+-5 K  
  > "We inferred a dust temperature T$_{\rm dust}$=52$\pm5$ K" (5 Gas consumption)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=0.8 (SB-like)  
  > "we adopted a SB-like luminosity-to-gas-mass conversion factor, $\alpha_{\rm CO}$=0.8" (5 Gas consumption)
   - assumption: fiducial stellar mass 4.5e11 Msun  
  > "we consider as fiducial stellar mass value M$_\star$=$4.5\times10^{11}$ M$_\odot$" (Appendix A.1)
   - fitting: AGNfitter Bayesian MCMC used as a cross-check  
  > "We also ran the publicly available fully Bayesian Markov Chain Monte Carlo SED fitting algorithm *AGNfitter*" (Appendix A.1)
   - validation: Gas fraction stays <10% for lower stellar mass; Band 7 gives consistent ISM mass  
  > "the gas fraction would remain $<10$% if we consider the lower limit of our estimate of the stellar mass" (Appendix A.1)
7. **Detect and quantify high-velocity CO outflow**: Natural-weighted flux maps at |v|>350 km/s; spectrum from region around blue outflow with noise spectra for significance; Gaussian fits; mass with alpha_CO and flux  
  > "We constructed flux maps of the blue tail of the line (\[v$<$ -350 km s$^{-1}$\]; blue tail) and the red tail of the line (\[v$>$ 350 km s$^{-1}$\]; red tail)" (6 High velocity molecular gas)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: outflow scale 10 kpc, velocity 700 km/s  
  > "Assuming a spatial scale of 10 kpc and an outflow velocity of 700 km s$^{-1}$ as inferred from our data" (6 High velocity molecular gas)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=0.13-0.8; uniform density  
  > "in the hypothesis of uniform density in the emitting region" (6 High velocity molecular gas)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits to outflow spectrum  
  > "assuming a Gaussian function with FWHM=280 (260) km s$^{-1}$ (blue curves in Fig. [8](#Xshooter))" (High velocity molecular gas tracing an extended molecular outflow)
   - validation: Compared with three noise spectra from random regions of same shape; extraction avoiding central beam gives same significance  
  > "We checked that the significance of the detection does not change if we extract the outflow spectrum avoiding the central beam." (6 High velocity molecular gas)

## Qualitative findings

- Continuum shows a nucleus plus a NE plume, possibly a minor merger or tidal tail.  
  > "clearly resolved into a central source (‘nucleus’) and a fainter feature extending towards the north-east direction (‘plume’)" (2.1 1.3 mm continuum)
- Blue CO tail is co-spatial with the ionised [OIII] outflow.  
  > "the blue tail of the CO(5-4) is co-spatial with the observed \[O III\] blue emission ascribed to outflowing warm ionised gas" (6 High velocity molecular gas)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Follow-up of PdBI CO(3-2) detection with high-resolution imaging of dust and CO to test feedback and search for molecular outflow  
  > "These two reasons motivated our ALMA follow-up observations for dust continuum and high-resolution imaging of CO transitions." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Minor merger for high-velocity red emission and velocity gradient; rejected for blue emission and disc kinematics  
  > "However, this minor merger scenario would not explain the high velocity blueshifted gas observed in the opposite direction with respect to the nucleus." (6 High velocity molecular gas)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Outflow mass estimates are order of magnitude given chain of assumptions (alpha_CO, excitation).  
  > "We stress that the two values suffer from large uncertainties, given the chain of assumptions employed" (6 High velocity molecular gas)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow detection is only ~2.5-4.5 sigma
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar mass uncertain by factor of ~2-5 affecting gas fraction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): alpha_CO and SLED choice dominate gas mass uncertainty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low-J CO from low-resolution data with noise-boosting issues
