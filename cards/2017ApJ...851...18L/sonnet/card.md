## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA CO(1-0) cubes matched to MaNGA**: CLEAN with Briggs robust 0.5, user-specified image center, pixel size and 2.5'' restoring beam; integrated intensity with 1.5 sigma clip  
  > "We adopted a user-specified image center, pixel size, and restoring beamsize to match the image grid and the spatial resolution of the MaNGA images during the CLEAN process." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: clip 1.5 sigma; varying 2 to 1.3 sigma changes flux -15% to +10%  
  > "Varying the clipping threshold from 2- to 1.3-$\sigma$ results in a change of the CO flux from -15% to +10%" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Results unchanged using original image center and native beam  
  > "We have confirmed that all results remain unchanged if we instead use the original image center and restoring beamsize." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
2. **Derive H2 surface density**: Convert CO surface density with alpha_CO  
  > "computed from the CO surface density by adopting a conversion factor ($\alpha_{\mathrm{CO}}$) of 4.3" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.3 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "conversion factor ($\alpha_{\mathrm{CO}}$) of 4.3 $\rm M_{\odot}$(K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^{2}$)$^{-1}$ (cite: bol13)" (2.2 ALMA Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared total ALMA flux with JCMT (Galaxy 1) and COLD GASS (Galaxy 2)  
  > "We can compare the total CO flux and/or H$_{2}$ mass obtained by integrating the ALMA results with those based on single-dish observations for two of our targets." (2.2 ALMA Observations)
3. **Derive SFR and stellar mass maps from MaNGA**: Pipe3D stellar continuum fit; extinction-corrected Halpha (Balmer decrement) converted to SFR  
  > "SFR is then estimated based on this extinction corrected H$\alpha$ flux using the conversion given by (cite: ken98a) with the Salpeter IMF." (2.1 MaNGA Targets)
   - software: Pipe3D
   - parameter: spaxels with S/N(Halpha) > 3  
  > "we only limit to our analysis to spaxels with S/N (H$\alpha$) \> 3" (2.1 MaNGA Targets)
   - assumption: Calzetti curve Rv=4.05, Halpha/Hbeta = 2.86, case B, Salpeter IMF  
  > "adopting the (cite: cal01) attenuation curve with $Rv = 4.05$ and a theoretical value for the Balmer line ratio (H$\alpha$/H$\beta$= 2.86)" (2.1 MaNGA Targets)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SFR uncertainty less than 33%  
  > "is less than 33% given that we only limit to our analysis to spaxels with S/N (H$\alpha$) \> 3" (2.1 MaNGA Targets)
4. **Define bulge and disk regions**: Two-component GALFIT on SDSS r-band; bulge r < Re_obs, disk r > 2 Re_obs  
  > "We define the "bulge" region to be $r <  R_e^{obs}$, and in order to mitigate contamination from any overlap region we define the "disk" region to be $r >$ 2$\times R_e^{obs}$." (3 Results)
   - software: GALFIT
   - parameter: Sersic n=4 bulge, n=1 disk fixed  
  > "we fix the Sersic index to be $n = 4$ and $n = 1$, respectively" (3 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compute SFE, gas fraction and correlate with sSFR**: SFE = SFR/M_H2, f_gas = Sigma_H2/(Sigma_* + Sigma_H2); Spearman correlations of f_gas and SFE vs sSFR  
  > "we plot $f_{\rm~gas}$  and SFE against sSFR and compute the Spearman’s correlation coefficient $\rho$" (3.2)
   - parameter: Only spaxels with S/N(CO) > 2 shown in KS relation; Galaxy 3 bulge excluded from correlations due to AGN  
  > "Only spaxels with S/N (CO) \> 2 are displayed." (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Bulge gas fractions of Galaxies 2 and 3 are lower than in their disks, supporting inside-out quenching  
  > "the gas fraction in the bulges is significantly lower than that in their disks, supporting an ‘inside-out’ model of galaxy quenching" (abstract)
