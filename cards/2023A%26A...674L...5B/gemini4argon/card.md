## Science

- Fields: high_z_galaxies, agn_and_quasars
- Question: To detect outflowing neutral gas in five strongly lensed z~2-4 QSOs using OH+(1_1-1_0), CO(9-8), and underlying dust continuum observations, and compare their outflow and warm molecular gas properties to high-z star-forming and active galaxies.  
  > "In this letter, we present observations of $\rm OH^+$($1_1$-$1_0$), CO(9-8) and the underlying dust continuum in five strongly lensed $z\sim2-4$ QSOs, using the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) to detect outflowing neutral gas." (abstract); "We compare our sample to high-$z$ far-infrared (FIR) luminous star-forming and active galaxies from the literature." (abstract)

## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA visibility data into sideband spectral cubes**: Reduce raw data in CASA and non-interactively image with tclean without continuum subtraction, separating the two sidebands into two cubes at native channel resolution  
  > "The raw data were reduced using CASA (cite: McMullin2007). The calibrated data were non-interactively imaged using a robust weighting of 0.5 and noise threshold of $1\sigma$ with the `tclean` routine." (Sample and observations); "We did not subtract the continuum and separated the sidebands into two cubes, leaving the frequency resolution the same as that of the receiver channels (15.624 MHz) (Table [1](#tab:obs))." (Sample and observations)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: robust = 0.5, noise threshold = 1 sigma, frequency resolution = 15.624 MHz  
  > "The calibrated data were non-interactively imaged using a robust weighting of 0.5 and noise threshold of $1\sigma$ with the `tclean` routine." (Sample and observations); "We did not subtract the continuum and separated the sidebands into two cubes, leaving the frequency resolution the same as that of the receiver channels (15.624 MHz) (Table [1](#tab:obs))." (Sample and observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spatially integrated spectra and fit spectral lines and continuum**: Iterative two-pass spatial integration and simultaneous multi-Gaussian + linear continuum fitting: first sum spaxels with continuum >= 3 sigma from initial line-free channels, fit spectral components, then include any spaxel with channel value >= 3 sigma within the FWHM of CO(9-8) components and refit  
  > "The spectra were created and fitted twice: first by summing over all spaxels with an underlying dust continuum level $\geq 3\sigma$, estimated from a first guess of the line-free channels." (Spectra and spectral fitting); "We then identified and fit the spectral components and used them to identify any spaxel containing a channel value $\geq 3\sigma$ within the FWHM of one or more of the CO(9-8) components. These spaxels are then included in the spatially integrated spectra and fitted a second time." (Spectra and spectral fitting); "We fit the spectra with a combination of Gaussian spectral components and a linear continuum slope simultaneously, leaving the central frequencies, line widths, intensities, and continuum gradient as free parameters." (Spectra and spectral fitting)
   - software: curve_fit
   - parameter: Spaxel selection threshold >= 3 sigma (continuum in pass 1, CO(9-8) FWHM channel peak in pass 2)  
  > "first by summing over all spaxels with an underlying dust continuum level $\geq 3\sigma$, estimated from a first guess of the line-free channels." (Spectra and spectral fitting); "used them to identify any spaxel containing a channel value $\geq 3\sigma$ within the FWHM of one or more of the CO(9-8) components." (Spectra and spectral fitting)
   - assumption: Same line parameters used to describe the OH+(1_1-1_0) and OH+(2_1-1_0) transitions; CO(9-8) central velocity used as systemic velocity for Doppler shifts  
  > "We used the same line parameters to describe the $\rm OH^+$($1_1$-$1_0$) and $\rm OH^+$($2_1$-$1_0$) transitions." (Spectra and spectral fitting); "The central velocity of the CO(9-8) emission (Table [\[tab:measured\]](#tab:measured)) was used as the systemic velocity when calculating the Doppler–shifted velocities of the OH$^+$ lines." (Results)
   - fitting: Simultaneous fit of Gaussian spectral components and linear continuum slope using curve_fit with central frequencies, line widths, intensities, and continuum gradient as free parameters  
  > "We fit the spectra with a combination of Gaussian spectral components and a linear continuum slope simultaneously, leaving the central frequencies, line widths, intensities, and continuum gradient as free parameters." (Spectra and spectral fitting); "Uncertainties are taken from those given by the fitting procedure in `curve_fit`" (Spectra and spectral fitting)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive integrated optical depths, column densities, and line luminosities**: Compute integrated OH+ absorption optical depths from transmitted and continuum flux, and compute OH+ and CO(9-8) line luminosities using Solomon et al. (1992) expressions  
  > "From the fitted line properties, we derive integrated OH$^+$ absorption optical depths: $$\begin{eqnarray}
        \int\tau\rm dv = -\ln\big(\frac{\rm S_{trans}}{\rm S_{cont}}\big)\rm dv,
        
\end{eqnarray}$$ where $\rm S_{trans}$ is the transmitted flux and $\rm S_{cont}$ is the unobscured continuum flux level fitted at the central velocity of the line." (Derived line properties); "The OH$^+$ emission and CO(9-8) line luminosities were derived using the expressions given by (cite: Solomon1992) (Table [\[tab:derived\]](#tab:derived))." (Derived line properties)
   - assumption: Flat LambdaCDM cosmology with Omega_m = 0.307 and H_0 = 67.7 km s^-1 Mpc^-1; luminosities not corrected for lensing magnification  
  > "Throughout this work, we adopt a flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $\Omega_{\rm m}= 0.307$ and ${\rm H_0 = 67.7\ km\ s^{-1}\ Mpc^{-1}}$ (cite: Planck2016)." (Introduction); "Table notes: a) FIR luminosities(40–120 $\mu$m ) from (cite: Stacey2018), not corrected for lensing magnification." (Derived line properties)

## Qualitative findings

- Blue-shifted OH+(1_1-1_0) absorption is detected in 3/5 QSOs and tentatively in a fourth (RX J0911+0551), with no red-shifted absorption detected in any of the QSOs.  
  > "Blue-shifted $\rm OH^+$($1_1$-$1_0$) absorption is detected in 3/5 QSOs and tentatively detected in a fourth." (abstract); "No red-shifted absorption was found, unlike some of the sources reported in (cite: Riechers2021b) or in the case of HerBS-89a (cite: Berta2021)." (Fitted line properties)
- Active and star-forming galaxies show no significant difference in OH+ absorption line properties and follow the same optical depth-dust temperature relation and OH+ emission-FIR relation.  
  > "We find no difference in OH$^+$ absorption line properties between active and star-forming galaxies with both samples roughly following the same optical depth-dust temperature relation." (abstract); "Similarly, star-forming and active galaxies both follow the same OH$^+$ emission–FIR relation." (abstract)
- Obscured QSOs display significantly broader OH+ emission (>800 km s^-1) and CO(9-8) emission (>500 km s^-1) than unobscured QSOs.  
  > "Obscured QSOs display broader ($>800 \, \mbox{\rm km\ s}^{-1}$) emission than the unobscured QSOs and all but one of the high-z star–forming galaxies (likely caused by the warm molecular gas reservoir obscuring the accreting nucleus)." (abstract); "Broader CO(9-8) emission ($>500 \, \mbox{\rm km\ s}^{-1}
        )$ is found in obscured versus unobscured QSOs, but overall they cover a similar range in line widths as the star-forming galaxies and follow the CO(9-8)–FIR luminosity relation found in low–$z$ galaxies." (abstract)
- Outflows traced by blue-shifted OH+ absorption are only detected in DSFGs with broad CO(9-8) emission (FWHM > 500 km s^-1) and in both obscured and unobscured QSOs.  
  > "However, the eight sources with blue–shifted OH$^+$ absorption all display broad ($\rm FWHM>500\ \mbox{\rm km\ s}^{-1}$) CO(9-8) line widths, similarly to the obscured QSOs and wider than all the unobscured QSOs." (Fitted line properties); "Blue-shifted OH$^+$ absorption is detected in QSOs displaying both broad and narrow CO(9-8) emission but only in DSFGs displaying broad CO(9-8) emission." (CO(9-8) Emission)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: OH+(1_1-1_0) traces the extended turbulent halo of predominantly atomic and diffuse gas and lies close in frequency to CO(9-8), allowing simultaneous observation of warm dense molecular gas and underlying dust continuum in strongly lensed FIR-bright QSOs spanning two decades in dust temperature.  
  > "One molecule of note here is $\rm OH^+$, which traces the extended turbulent halo of predominantly atomic and diffuse gas surrounding galaxies (cite: Indriolo2018)." (Introduction); "Moreover, the proximity of $\rm OH^+$($1_1$-$1_0$) with the CO(9-8) emission line means that we can simultaneously observe the warm molecular gas, providing additional information on the physical properties within the galaxy (cite: Berta2021; Riechers2021b)." (Introduction); "The selected sample covers about two decades in dust temperature, of about order of magnitude in $L_{\rm FIR}$ and including one quasar with a jet-dominated radio emission, MG J01414+0534 (cite: Stacey2018)." (Sample and observations)
- Why this method: At high-z, detecting weak high-velocity emission line wings from CO or [CII] is difficult, making blue-shifted molecular absorption lines against bright dust continuum a reliable way to trace cool gas outflows.  
  > "Low–$z$ studies have commonly made use of high-velocity line wings of bright emission lines to detect outflows (cite: Feruglio2010), however, at high–$z,$ detecting these weak signals from CO or \[CII\] becomes difficult (cite: Fan2018; Ginolfi2020)." (Introduction); "Blue-shifted molecular absorption lines have thus become a popular and reliable way of tracing cool gas outflows both at cosmic noon (e.g. OH$^+$ :(cite: Butler2021; Riechers2021b; Shao2022); CH$^+$ : (cite: Falgarone2017)) and dawn (e.g. OH 119 $\mu m$: (cite: Spilker2018; Spilker2020b; Butler2023a); $\rm H_2O$: (cite: Jones2019))." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Spaxel-by-spaxel deblending of emission and absorption lines was rejected because the S/N of the observations is not sufficient.  
  > "The S/N of our observations are not sufficient to reliably disentangle the blended emission and absorption lines on a spaxel-by-spaxel base, however, the sample’s agreement with unlensed low-z FIR scaling relations suggests that differential lensing is not substantial." (Spatial offsets and differential lensing)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Uncertainties from curve_fit may underestimate OH+ flux errors in MG J0414+0534 and PSS J2322+1944 where the line is broken into multiple components, and deblending of broad OH+ and CO(9-8) emission in MG J0414+0534 may not be unique.  
  > "Uncertainties are taken from those given by the fitting procedure in `curve_fit` and may underestimate the OH$^+$ flux errors in the case of MG J0414+0534 and PSS J2322+1944 where the line has been broken up into multiple components." (Spectra and spectral fitting); "Consequently, the deblending of the broad OH$^+$ and CO(9-8) emission lines in MG J0414+0534 may not be unique." (Spectra and spectral fitting)
- Limitation (authors): OH+ absorption in RX J0911+0551 and NH / NH2 detections in WFI J2026-4536 are tentative and their fitted parameters are uncertain or poorly constrained due to blending.  
  > "We include RX J0911+0551 as a tentative detection as it appears in both the $\rm OH^+$($1_1$-$1_0$) and $\rm OH^+$($2_1$-$1_0$) transitions; however, we stress that these values are uncertain." (Fitted line properties); "The tentaive $\rm NH(1_0-1_1)$ absorption and $\rm NH_2(2_{2,0}-2_{1,1})$ emission lines are blended, resulting in poorly constrained best-fit parameters for these lines." (Additional lines in WFI J2026-4536)
- Limitation (authors): Small sample size (5 QSOs) and selection bias toward Type 1 AGN (4/5 sources) may bias the non-detection of red-shifted absorption; also red-shifted frequencies are not covered in WFI J2026-4536.  
  > "The QSOs show a trend between faster outflow velocity and broader full-width half maximum (FWHM). A larger sample is needed to confirm this finding." (Fitted line properties); "We did not find any occurrences of red-shifted absorption (Fig. [1](#fig:scatter)), however, our selection of mostly Type 1 AGN systems (4/5 sources) may bias our results, as gas is more likely to infall perpendicularly to the opening of the active nucleus, where counter-acting radiation can escape most efficiently from the host galaxy (cite: Shao2022)." (OH$^+$ Absorption); "Furthermore, we cannot rule out red–shifted absorption in WFI J2026-4536, where this frequency range is not covered." (OH$^+$ Absorption)
- Limitation (authors): S/N is insufficient to disentangle blended emission and absorption on a spaxel-by-spaxel basis to directly measure differential lensing, and higher S/N of QSO spectra compared to DSFGs could explain why OH+ absorption is seen in narrow-CO QSOs.  
  > "This may simply be due to the higher S/N of the QSO spectra, thus requiring larger samples at high S/N to be confirmed." (CO(9-8) Emission); "The S/N of our observations are not sufficient to reliably disentangle the blended emission and absorption lines on a spaxel-by-spaxel base, however, the sample’s agreement with unlensed low-z FIR scaling relations suggests that differential lensing is not substantial." (Spatial offsets and differential lensing)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Degeneracy in multi-Gaussian deblending of simultaneously occurring OH+ absorption, OH+ emission, and CO(9-8) emission (especially in MG J0414+0534 where OH+ emission FWHM exceeds 1000 km/s and overlaps CO(9-8)).
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Lack of lensing magnification corrections on L_FIR, L_OH+, and L_CO(9-8) when comparing scaling relations across lensed and unlensed samples.
