## Science

- Fields: protoplanetary_disks, astrochemistry
- Question: What is the role of the asymmetric dust trap in setting the chemical composition of the HD 142527 disk, and how does it compare to IRS 48?  
  > "In this work, we use archival ALMA observations of the HD 142527 disk to obtain an as large as possible molecular inventory, which allows us to investigate the possible influence of the asymmetric dust trap on the disk’s chemistry." (abstract); "To further investigate the role of dust substructures in setting the disk’s chemical compositions, to investigate whether the IRS 48 disk is a special case and to obtain a unique peek into the ice composition of disks, we use ALMA archival data to investigate the chemistry of the HD 142527 disk." (Weak line emission)

   - parameter: Radial range 0.0" to 3.0", bin width = 2x minor axis of beam, step = 1x minor axis of beam  
  > "To also infer the radial location of these transitions, we have acquired integrated spectra between 0.0" and 3.0" using a width of twice the size of the minor axis of the beam and steps of one times the minor axis." (Weak line emission)

## Qualitative findings

- First ALMA detections in HD 142527 of [C I] 3P1-3P0, 13C18O J=3-2, DCO+ J=4-3, and additional transitions of H2CO, HCO+, and CS; non-detections of SO, SO2, and CH3OH.  
  > "We present the first ALMA detections of \[ I\], , , and additional transition of and in this disk. In addition, we have acquired upper limits for non-detected species such as and ." (abstract); "Additionally, we report observations of the HCO$^+$ $J$=8-7 and CS $J$=10-9 transitions in this disk." (Summary)
- Unlike IRS 48, molecular emission in HD 142527 is not co-spatial with the dust trap; instead, most molecules show a decrement at the dust trap and peak on the opposite side of the disk.  
  > "For the majority of the observed molecules, a decrement in the emission at the location of the dust trap is found." (abstract); "in IRS 48 all molecules are more-or-less co-located with the continuum emission, while the molecules in HD 142527 all peak away from the dust trap, hinting that different chemical processes are responsible for molecular emissions in each disk." (Comparison with IRS 48)
- The [C I] emission forms a ring that is smaller than those of C18O and 13C18O and is located almost fully inside the dust cavity, confirming the gas cavity is smaller than the dust cavity.  
  > "As opposed to, for example, the and emission, the \[ I\] ring appears to be smaller and (almost) fully located inside the dust cavity." (Atomic Carbon)
- Thermal sublimation of icy mantles does not play a major role in changing the gas-phase composition of the outer disk in HD 142527.  
  > "As the emission of the observed molecules is not co-spatial with the dust trap and no or are found, thermal sublimation of icy mantles does not appear to play a major role in changing the gas-phase composition of the outer disk in HD 142527 disk." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Archival ALMA datasets were selected to have spatial resolution sufficient to resolve the cavity and asymmetric dust trap, and to cover transitions of molecules detected in IRS 48 with E_up < 150 K and log(A_ij) > -5.  
  > "The analysed datasets were selected based on the highest chance of observing transitions of , , , and , of which the latter four have been detected in the IRS 48 dust trap, allowing for proper comparisons, with upper level energies of $E_{up}<$150 K and Einstein A coefficients of $\log{A_{ij}}>$-5." (Data); "The datasets used throughout this work are summarised in Table [\[table:Datasets\]](#table:Datasets) and have the spatial resolution that is sufficiently high to resolve both the cavity and the asymmetric dust trap." (Data)
- Why this method: Velocity channel width of 0.5 km s^-1 was chosen for consistency across datasets; GoFish spectral stacking was used to boost SNR of weak lines; 13C18O was used for the gas mass because more common CO isotopologues are optically thick.  
  > "The chosen channel width ensures consistency between the different datasets and provides sufficient velocity resolution for the aims of this work, considering that we do not investigate line kinematics." (Imaging process); "*GoFish* uses a spectral stacking technique, first introduced by (cite: YenEA16), which accounts for the Keplerian rotation of the disk and aims to improve the signal-to-noise ratio of weak line emission by aligning and stacking the spectra taken from different (e.g. blue- and redshifted) sides of the disk." (GoFish: Searching for weak line emission); "From this analysis, we confirm that the more common (, and ) must be optically thick and using their observations to determine the gas mass would only yield a lower limit." (Gas mass)
- Alternative: Freeze-out of CO at the dust trap as the sole cause of the CO isotopologue emission decrement was rejected because the CO snowline lies beyond the dust trap.  
  > "As discussed in Section 3.4, the snowline lies beyond the dust trap, indicating that the lack of emission in the other -isotopologues must be the result of a continuum over-subtraction." (Influence of the mm-sized dust)
- Alternative: Freeze-out at the dust trap alone as the explanation for HCN and CS asymmetries was rejected because channel maps show emission enclosing the dust trap.  
  > "As can be seen in their channel maps (Figure 17), both molecules show emission enclosing the dust trap, which means that freeze-out at the dust trap alone can also not explain the observed asymmetry." (Influence of misaligned, warped inner disk)
- Alternative: Thermal desorption of H2CO from icy grains was ruled out because the gas kinematic temperature (~40 K) and dust brightness temperature (~30 K) are below the H2CO sublimation temperature (~66 K) and no CH3OH is detected.  
  > "Furthermore, as we assume the emission to be optically thick, the kinematic temperature can be approximated to be equal to the brightness temperature, $T_\textnormal{kin}\sim$40 K (see Section 3.4, (cite: GargEA21)). As this is lower than the sublimation temperature of ($\sim$66 K (cite: PenteadoEA17)), thermal desorption of can be ruled out." (The formation of )
- Alternative: Simple power-law midplane temperature profile for locating the snowline was rejected because the large dust cavity invalidates the power-law approximation.  
  > "As can be seen the white dashed line is well within the dust trap, showing that a simple power-law for the midplane temperature does not yield the right location of the snowline for the HD 142527 disk." (RADMC-3D Model)
