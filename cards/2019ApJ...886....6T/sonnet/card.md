## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure source positions and flux densities**: Gaussian fits with imfit to continuum images  
  > "We fit Gaussians to these sources using the *imfit* task in CASA to measure their flux densities and positions." (3.1 Protostellar Content)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 3 sigma detection threshold  
  > "at the 3$\sigma$ level (or above)" (3.1 Protostellar Content)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison of flux densities between executions consistent with ~10% accuracy  
  > "comparisons of the observed flux densities for the science targets in the different executions are consistent with this level of accuracy" (2.1 ALMA Observations)
2. **Derive dust/gas masses and radii**: Isothermal optically thin M = D^2 F/(kappa B(T)), times 100 for gas; radius = HWHM x distance  
  > "We make the simplifying assumption that the dust emission is isothermal and optically thin" (3.3 Dust Continuum Mass and Radius Estimates)
   - parameter: T_dust = 43 K (L/Lsun)^0.25; L=1 Lsun if unmeasured  
  > "If a system does not have a measured L$_{bol}$ (e.g., VLA16 and VLA15), then 1 L$_{\odot}$ is assumed." (3.3 Dust Continuum Mass and Radius Estimates)
   - assumption: D=400 pc; kappa0.87mm=1.84 cm2/g; beta=1 extrapolation to 9 mm; dust-to-gas 1:100  
  > "adopting a dust opacity spectral index ($\beta$) of 1" (3.3 Dust Continuum Mass and Radius Estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 9 mm opacity chosen to agree with shorter-wavelength masses  
  > "yields masses in agreement with shorter wavelength studies" (3.3 Dust Continuum Mass and Radius Estimates)
3. **Methanol rotation temperature and column density**: Rotation diagram from three methanol transitions with line fluxes in 0.5'' (HOPS-108) and 0.75'' (HOPS-370) apertures  
  > "we derive rotation temperatures of 140 K and 129 K for HOPS-108 and HOPS-370, respectively" (Molecular Line Emission)
   - software: scipy
   - assumption: Optically thin, lines from same physical structure; mean molecular weight 2.8  
  > "the rotation diagram analysis assumes that the line emission is arising from the same physical structure and that the lines are optically thin" (Molecular Line Emission)
   - fitting: Line centers and widths fit with Gaussians using scipy curve_fit  
  > "The velocity center and linewidth of each line is fit with a Gaussian function using the *curve_fit* function of *scipy*." (Molecular Line Emission)
   - validation: Compared to Herschel HIFI T_kin = 145 K from Kama et al.  
  > "T$_{kin}$ = 145$\pm$12 K, comparable to our rotation temperature" (Molecular Line Emission)
4. **Luminosity required to evaporate methanol**: Thermal equilibrium dust heating at 50 AU radius for 120 K or 160 K; accretion luminosity L=GMMdot/R  
  > "the luminosity required to heat dust to this temperature at a radius of 50 AU is $\sim$86 L$_{\odot}$, calculated assuming thermal equilibrium" (4.3 Origin and Implications of the Compact Methanol Emission)
   - assumption: Evaporation temperature 120 K (160 K with ice mixtures); protostellar radii from Hartmann 1997 and Palla 1993  
  > "Assuming that methanol has an evaporation temperature of 120 K (cite: collings2004)" (4.3 Origin and Implications of the Compact Methanol Emission)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared to SED luminosity range 37-100 Lsun from Furlan et al.  
  > "This is consistent with the range of luminosities favored by (cite: furlan2014)." (4.3 Origin and Implications of the Compact Methanol Emission)

## Qualitative findings

- Six compact continuum sources in OMC2-FIR4, four in FIR3, and one just outside FIR4; one new source (ALMA1) and new binary HOPS-66-A/B  
  > "We identify six compact continuum sources within OMC2-FIR4, four in OMC2-FIR3, and one additional source just outside OMC2-FIR4." (abstract)
- No clear 12CO outflow from HOPS-108; evidence of HOPS-370 jet interaction with FIR4  
  > "We do not identify a clear outflow from HOPS-108 in $^{12}$CO, but find evidence of interaction between the outflow/jet from HOPS-370 and the OMC2-FIR4 region." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Manual reduction needed to correct for flux variation of J0510+1800  
  > "The manual reduction was necessary to better correct for variation of the quasar J0510+1800" (2.1 ALMA Observations)
- Alternative: UCHII region interpretation of cm emission rejected given non-thermal indices and proper motions  
  > "the non-thermal spectral index and proper motions observed by are inconsistent with that interpretation" (4.1 A Young Stellar Group in OMC2-FIR4)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only ~2% of single-dish flux recovered; masses likely lower limits because emission partly optically thick  
  > "The continuum emission from the protostars is likely to be partially optically thick, thus the masses are likely lower limits, especially at 0.87 mm." (3.3 Dust Continuum Mass and Radius Estimates)
- Limitation (authors): CO non-detections of outflows inconclusive owing to spatial filtering  
  > "The strong CO emission from the molecular cloud and the spatial filtering, however, make these non-detections far from conclusive" (3.5 Outflows in 12CO)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Very short on-source time (~0.9 min per field) limits sensitivity and fidelity
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Masses depend on assumed temperature law and ad hoc 9 mm opacity
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotation diagram uses only three lines with possible optical depth and blending
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Burst-luminosity explanation is not independently tested
