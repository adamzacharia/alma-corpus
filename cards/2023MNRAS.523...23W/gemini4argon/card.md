## Analysis recipe

1. **Select parent sample of SDSS DR14Q quasars observable by ALMA and match against the ALMA Science Archive footprint**: Filter SDSS DR14Q quasars to declination <= 40 deg and query the ALMA TAP service (ivoa.obscore) for target observations intersecting a circle of radius 0.0084 deg in selected extragalactic scientific categories  
  > "Setting aside sources with a declination above 40 deg, that are not observable by ALMA, results in a list of 357,392 objects to be considered." (2 SDSS QUASARS IN THE ALMA FOOTPRINT); "These sources were matched against the metadata of the ASA to identify the objects within the ALMA footprint, among observations taken under scientific categories “Active galaxies”, “Cosmology”, “Galaxy evolution” and “Local Universe”, using the script presented in Appendix A." (2 SDSS QUASARS IN THE ALMA FOOTPRINT); "AQ = query_coord(service, CQ.ra.degree, CQ.dec.degree, 0.0084)" (APPENDIX A: QUERYING THE ALMA ARCHIVE USING ASTROQUERY)
   - software: Astropy, pyvo, pandas
   - parameter: Declination cut <= 40 deg  
  > "Setting aside sources with a declination above 40 deg, that are not observable by ALMA, results in a list of 357,392 objects to be considered." (2 SDSS QUASARS IN THE ALMA FOOTPRINT)
   - parameter: TAP search radius = 0.0084 deg; scan_intent like '%TARGET%'  
  > "and scan_intent like ’%TARGET%’"""" (APPENDIX A: QUERYING THE ALMA ARCHIVE USING ASTROQUERY); "AQ = query_coord(service, CQ.ra.degree, CQ.dec.degree, 0.0084)" (APPENDIX A: QUERYING THE ALMA ARCHIVE USING ASTROQUERY)
   - parameter: Scientific categories = 'Active galaxies', 'Cosmology', 'Galaxy evolution', 'Local Universe'  
  > "These sources were matched against the metadata of the ASA to identify the objects within the ALMA footprint, among observations taken under scientific categories “Active galaxies”, “Cosmology”, “Galaxy evolution” and “Local Universe”, using the script presented in Appendix A." (2 SDSS QUASARS IN THE ALMA FOOTPRINT)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Automatically extract submm continuum sources from ALMA archival images**: Run ADMIT task Sfind2D_AT (based on CASA findsources) on full continuum images to detect peaks above 3.5 sigma on primary-beam uncorrected images and fit 2D Gaussians on primary-beam corrected images  
  > "To extract the submm continuum counterparts of SDSS quasars in the ALMA footprint in an automated fashion, we made use of the ALMA Data Mining Toolkit (ADMIT 3 ; Teuben et al. 2015)." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS); "To search for continuum emission we made use of the task Sfind2D_AT, which is based on the CASA findsources task." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS); "In this work we require that the emission peaks have a flux of at least N = 3.5 times the 1 𝜎 noise limit of the data." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS)
   - software: ADMIT, Sfind2D_AT, CASA, findsources
   - parameter: SNR threshold N = 3.5  
  > "In this work we require that the emission peaks have a flux of at least N = 3.5 times the 1 𝜎 noise limit of the data." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS)
   - assumption: Point-like or compact source morphology describable by a 2D Gaussian  
  > "Once an emission peak is identified, the Sfind2D_AT task then fits the region around the brightest pixel value with a two-dimensional Gaussian to obtain integrated flux and shape (major and minor axis size and position angle) information." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS)
   - fitting: Two-dimensional Gaussian fit around brightest pixel value  
  > "Once an emission peak is identified, the Sfind2D_AT task then fits the region around the brightest pixel value with a two-dimensional Gaussian to obtain integrated flux and shape (major and minor axis size and position angle) information." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Cross-match extracted ALMA sources with SDSS DR14Q quasars and visually inspect candidates**: Cross-match ADMIT source list with reduced DR14Q catalogue within a 5 arcsec radius of SDSS coordinates, then visually inspect all candidate images in CARTA to remove artefacts, edge noise, and unassociated nearby sources, and deduplicate multiple extractions on the same source  
  > "To identify the submm source associated with each of the SDSS quasars, we cross-matched the ADMIT list with the reduced DR14Q catalogue, using a matching radius of 5 ′′ centred on the SDSS coordinates." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS); "All ALMA images containing one of these extractions were inspected using CARTA 4, to identify image artefacts, unreliable extractions or extractions that did not correspond to the SDSS quasar." (3.1 Visual inspection); "For some cases (122 in total, corresponding to 34 unique quasars), ADMIT performed multiple extractions on the same source (red spikes in Fig. 4. In these cases, the extraction centred closer to the SDSS coordinates was kept." (3.1 Visual inspection)
   - software: CARTA, TOPCAT
   - parameter: Cross-matching radius = 5 arcsec  
  > "To identify the submm source associated with each of the SDSS quasars, we cross-matched the ADMIT list with the reduced DR14Q catalogue, using a matching radius of 5 ′′ centred on the SDSS coordinates." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS)
   - parameter: Visual confirmation threshold: separation <= 1.5 x beam major axis (or <= 1.0 x beam major axis for full automation)  
  > "Visual inspection indicated that a submm extraction was associated to the SDSS quasar if the ALMA and SDSS coordinates coincide within 1.5× 𝛼 𝑚𝑎𝑥 𝑏 , a value slightly above the minimum of ∼1 seen in the bimodal distribution of this quantity in Fig. 4." (3.1 Visual inspection); "In other words, in order to fully automate the method without further need for visual inspection, a cut at Separation / 𝛼 𝑚𝑎𝑥 𝑏 = 1.0 could be imposed, ensuring minimum contamination and maximum completeness of the resulting catalogue." (3.1 Visual inspection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual inspection of all 1707 extractions in CARTA and analysis of the bimodal distribution of Separation / beam major axis

## Software

- **ADMIT** : Automated extraction of submm continuum sources and 2D Gaussian fitting on ALMA images via the Sfind2D_AT task  
  > "To extract the submm continuum counterparts of SDSS quasars in the ALMA footprint in an automated fashion, we made use of the ALMA Data Mining Toolkit (ADMIT 3 ; Teuben et al. 2015)." ## Reasoning

- Why this data: The ALMA Science Archive contains over 19000 MOUSs of rich interferometric data, enabling cross-project statistical research and high-level data products for SDSS DR14Q quasars; four Galactic/local science categories were excluded to avoid complex overlapping continuum structures.  
  > "After a decade of observations, the ALMA Science Archive (ASA) contains more than 19000 Member Observation Unit Sets (MOUSs)." (1 INTRODUCTION); "Categories “Disks and planet formation”, “ISM and Star Formation”, “Stars and Stellar Evolution” and “Solar System” were left out of this pilot project, as many of these have complex continuum images with overlapping structures." (2 SDSS QUASARS IN THE ALMA FOOTPRINT)
- Why this method: ADMIT Sfind2D_AT was used to automate point-source detection and 2D Gaussian flux extraction across full ALMA images; initial peak finding was done on primary-beam uncorrected images to maintain a coherent noise profile across the field of view.  
  > "To extract the submm continuum counterparts of SDSS quasars in the ALMA footprint in an automated fashion, we made use of the ALMA Data Mining Toolkit (ADMIT 3 ; Teuben et al. 2015)." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS); "The initial search for a bright peak is performed on primary-beam uncorrected images to ensure a coherent noise profile throughout the image." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS)
- Alternative: Including ALMA science categories 'Disks and planet formation', 'ISM and Star Formation', 'Stars and Stellar Evolution', and 'Solar System' was rejected for this pilot project because of complex continuum images with overlapping structures.  
  > "Categories “Disks and planet formation”, “ISM and Star Formation”, “Stars and Stellar Evolution” and “Solar System” were left out of this pilot project, as many of these have complex continuum images with overlapping structures." (2 SDSS QUASARS IN THE ALMA FOOTPRINT)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The Sfind2D_AT task does not recover highly resolved sources with complex structures well (such as lensed arcs, rings, or arms), breaking them into multiple Gaussians.  
  > "In general the Sfind2D_AT task does not recover highly resolved sources with complex structures well (e.g., rings or arms), breaking these down into multiple (sometimes overlapping) Gaussians." (3 EXTRACTION OF SUBMM COUNTERPARTS); "As noted in Sec. 3, ADMIT does not perform well in the case of complex structures. As a result, the automated extraction does not guarantee reliable fluxes for all the images." (5.2 Lensed quasars)
- Limitation (authors): Early-cycle ALMA images lacking primary beam response curves have elevated global noise from outer image edges, causing 4 obvious quasar counterparts to fail the SNR >= 3.5 automatic detection threshold.  
  > "In these cases, the (high) noise level at the outer edges of the image dominated the noise level of the whole image, causing sources that were clearly visible on the images to fail the SNR cut of 3.5." (3.1 Visual inspection)
- Limitation (authors): The sample of ALMA-detected SDSS quasars is neither complete nor statistical, so trends in flux versus redshift are not representative of the overall quasar population.  
  > "The sample is in no way complete or statistical and any trends seen in this figure are not representative of the quasar population." (4 THE ALMA COUNTERPARTS OF SDSS QUASARS)
- Limitation (authors): Interferometric missing flux may affect the reported fluxes of more diffuse counterparts.  
  > "Note that the issue of the missing flux may affect some of the reported fluxes, especially for the more diffuse counterparts." (5.2 Lensed quasars)
- Limitation (authors): With single-band submm measurements for most objects, thermal dust emission cannot be separated from non-thermal synchrotron emission.  
  > "Non-thermal (synchrotron) emission from the nucleus, at least in the case of radio-loud quasars, may pitch in to the mm wavelengths, however with single measurements per object it is not possible to distinguish between the thermal and non-thermal components." (6 DISCUSSION)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Archival FITS images across Cycles 0-8 were produced with heterogeneous pipeline/manual imaging parameters, weighting schemes, and angular resolutions.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Secondary sources within 5 arcsec generally lack spectroscopic redshifts, so physical companions cannot be distinguished from chance projections using continuum images alone.
