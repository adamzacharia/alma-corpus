## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and extract spectra**: Convolve 0.8-mm images to 3-mm beam, apply primary beam correction, extract spectra at CND and SW ring and fit Gaussians  
  > "To obtain the spectra and measure the Gaussian fitted line parameters, we convolved all 0.8-mm band images to the beam size of the 3-mm band data" (3.2)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits  
  > "Table 2 and 3 list the Gaussian fitted line parameters toward the CND and starburst ring, respectively." (3.2)
   - validation: Compared 13CO and CS flux with JCMT; ALMA recovers 32% and ~26%  
  > "Therefore, the ALMA observations recover 32$\pm$5 % of the single dish flux." (3.3)
2. **Rotation diagram Trot and N**: Rotation diagrams combining 3-mm and 0.8-mm lines; Trot and N from slope and intercept; single-transition species use assumed Trot of 5, 10, 15, 30, 45 K; non-detections plotted at 3 sigma  
  > "For the calculation of $N_{\rm{mol}}$ for molecules with only one detected transition, we assumed some temperatures of $T_{\rm{rot}}$ = 5 K, 10 K, 15 K, 30 K and 45 K." (3.4)
   - assumption: Optically thin lines, LTE with single Tex  
  > "under the assumptions that all lines are optically thin, and that a single excitation temperature ($T_{\rm{ex}}$) characterizes all transitions" (3.4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Aladro et al. 2013 single-dish values  
  > "This value of HNCO is consistent with the value of $\sim$30 K obtained by Aladro et al. (2013)." (3.4.2)
3. **Fractional abundances and Galactic comparison**: N_H2 from 13CO column density with [13CO]/[H2]=2e-6; abundances compared to TMC-1, Sgr B2(N), AFGL2591, L1157(B1)  
  > "Here we adopt $N_{\rm H_2}$ values of $7.4 \times 10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$ at the CND and $2.6 \times 10^{22}$ cm$^{-2}$ at the starburst ring" (4.2)
   - assumption: [13CO]/[H2] = 2e-6  
  > "with an assumption of a \[$^{13}$CO\]/\[H$_{2}$\] fractional abundance of $2 \times 10^{-6}$ (Dickman 1978)" (4.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared N_H2 with Tsai et al. 2013: agree at CND, factor ~4 disagreement at ring  
  > "we find a factor of $\sim$4 disagreement in the starburst ring between the $N_{\rm H_2}$ values from Tsai et al. (2013) and our $^{13}$CO multi-transition analysis" (4.2)

## Qualitative findings

- E-knot/W-knot separation seen for 13CO, CN and CS for the first time; CS ratio higher than other lines, suggesting chemical differentiation  
  > "These results suggest that there is a chemical differentiation between the E and W knots in the CND" (4.1)
- CN column density in CND is 20–50 times larger than in the ring  
  > "the column density of CN in the CND is a factor of 20–50 larger than that in the starburst ring" (3.4.5)
