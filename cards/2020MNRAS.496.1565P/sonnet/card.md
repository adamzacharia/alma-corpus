## Qualitative findings

- Hot DOGs are radio-quiet with less cold gas than SMGs, except W2305-0039  
  > "We compare to the FIR-radio correlation, and find that at least half of the Hot DOGs in our sample are radio-quiet with respect to normal galaxies." (abstract)
