## Analysis recipe

1. **prepare and correct the photometry**: Remove sky background and foreground stars (SIMBAD stars, 2D Moffat fits, replaced by random pixel values from annulus), convolve to the 24 micron MIPS resolution, correct Galactic extinction, then correct FUV for dust with k times L(IR)  
  > "we identified the stars using the SIMBAD database (cite: simbad) and fitted a 2D Moffat profile to each star with the MPDAF package (cite: mpdaf1; Piqueras_19) in PYTHON" (2.1 Photometry); "The photometric observations were corrected for the Galactic extinction using the (cite: cardelli_89) dust extinction law" (2.1 Photometry)
   - software: MPDAF, PHOTUTILS, ASTROPY, SIMBAD
   - parameter: Common resolution 6'' (MIPS 24 micron)  
  > "The MIPS 24$\mu$m  data has the lowest resolution of 6$''$, hence, all other data products were convolved to this resolution." (2.1 Photometry)
   - parameter: Sigma clipping at 2 sigma  
  > "The sigma clipping was done using ASTROPY (cite: astropy1; astropy2) with a 2$\sigma$ threshold." (2.1 Photometry)
   - parameter: Constant k = 4.9 used for FUV attenuation  
  > "We used a constant $k=4.9$ from (cite: leroy_19)" (4.1 A(FUV) corrections)
   - assumption: Galactic R_V = 3.1 with E(B-V) from Schlafly  
  > "assuming the Galactic total-to-selective extinction value of $R_\text{V}$ = 3.1" (2.1 Photometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared constant k with the variable-k recipe of Boquien et al.; arm-interarm distribution preserved  
  > "show that the overall distribution of $\langle  SFR_{\textnormal{5}} \rangle \big/  \langle SFR_{\textnormal{200}} \rangle$  in the interarms is preserved regardless of the choice of $k$" (5.2 A(FUV) corrections)
2. **derive dust-corrected H-alpha SFR from MUSE**: Use PHANGS-MUSE convolved-and-optimised maps convolved to 6'', S/N > 5; correct H-alpha for dust per spaxel with the Balmer decrement; convert to SFR  
  > "Those data products were subsequently convolved to 6$''$ resolution to match the photometry. We applied a signal-to-noise (S/N) ratio cut of 5 to all optical observations." (2.2 Optical observations); "We calculated it for each spaxel from the Balmer decrement, F(H$\alpha$)/F(H$\beta$) assuming Case B recombination (cite: osterbrock_06)" (2.2 Optical observations)
   - parameter: k(Hb)/k(Ha) = 1.53 for the Calzetti curve, R_V = 3.1  
  > "is the reddening curve ratio of 1.53 for the (cite: calzetti_00) curve assuming $R_\text{V}$ = 3.1 (cite: cardelli_89)" (2.2 Optical observations)
   - assumption: Kroupa IMF; SFR calibration as in Calzetti  
  > "we were able to calculate the SFR from the H$\alpha$ line emission assuming a (cite: kroupa_01) initial mass function (IMF), as described in (cite: calzetti_summer)" (2.2 Optical observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **convert CO(2-1) intensity to molecular gas surface density**: Sigma_mol = alpha_CO(1-0) / R21 * I_CO(2-1) * cos i, with S/N > 3 on the PHANGS-ALMA broad mom0 map  
  > "To calculate the molecular gas mass surface density" (2.3 PHANGS-ALMA); "We applied an S/N ratio cut of 3 to the ALMA data." (2.3 PHANGS-ALMA)
   - parameter: R21 = 0.65  
  > "We assumed $R_{21}$=0.65, which is the non-weighted mean derived in (cite: den_brok_21)" (2.3 PHANGS-ALMA)
   - parameter: alpha_CO(1-0) = 4.35 Msun pc^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "adopted a constant Galactic value for $\alpha^{1-0}_{\textnormal{CO}}$ of 4.35 M$_\odot$ pc$^{-2}$ (K km s$^{-1})^{-1}$, as in (cite: bolatto_13)" (2.3 PHANGS-ALMA)
   - assumption: Constant Galactic alpha_CO, no variable calibration  
  > "We do not opt for any variable $\alpha_\textnormal{CO}$ calibration here since it would introduce additional scatter to the analysis" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: For NGC 5068 the metallicity-based alpha_CO is compared to the constant value  
  > "Instead, we might actually underestimate" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
4. **compute the H-alpha minus FUV colour and the <SFR5>/<SFR200> map**: Apply the CIGALE-calibrated fit of <SFR5>/<SFR200> versus H-alpha-FUV colour per pixel, after masking quiescent pixels (FUV-IRAC 3.6 micron colour and NUV-J colour)  
  > "fitted a curve to this trend using the LMFIT package in PYTHON" (3.1 diagnostic); "NUV–J \> 3.75 mag were masked out due to no recent SF" (3.1 diagnostic)
   - software: CIGALE, LMFIT
   - parameter: Fit: exp[-10.123*arctan(0.456*(Ha-FUV)) + 0.244*(Ha-FUV) + 7.820]  
  > "\exp [ -10.123 \cdot \arctan (0.456 \cdot" (3.1 diagnostic)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF and Bruzual & Charlot stellar populations in CIGALE; dust-free models  
  > "we assumed a (cite: chabrier_03) IMF and stellar population synthesis models for the stellar emission from (cite: bruzual_03)" (3.1 diagnostic); "Finally, we assumed a dust-free scenario in CIGALE" (3.1 diagnostic)
   - fitting: curve fitted with LMFIT to the CIGALE model trend; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "fitted a curve to this trend using the LMFIT package in PYTHON" (3.1 diagnostic)
   - validation: Compared with log(SFR_Ha/SFR_FUV) for selected galaxies; the paper reports good agreement  
  > "Generally, we see a good agreement between both indices" (5.4 Comparison with other works)
5. **compare arm, interarm, bar and bulge environments**: Split pixels with the Querejeta et al. environmental masks; compare Sigma_mol, Sigma_SFR, SFE and <SFR5>/<SFR200> distributions with K-S tests; flag diffuse ionised gas with a BPT diagram  
  > "we applied a simple environmental mask from (cite: Querejeta_21)" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE); "To identify such pixels we constructed a Baldwin, Phillips, Terlevich (cite: bpt) diagram with the extreme starburst classification line computed by (cite: Kewley_01)" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
   - parameter: Masks built from 1.7'' IRAC 3.6 micron images, convolved and rebinned to 6''  
  > "later convolved and rebinned to 6$''$  resolution and pixel size to match the other observations" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: DIG pixels removed and the distributions rechecked  
  > "The removal of the DIG pixels does not change the SFE distribution in the interarms" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
6. **test artefacts: IMF stochasticity and old-star contamination**: Estimate the SFR below which the IMF is undersampled, and compute the specific SFR per pixel against a threshold  
  > "we calculated the specific SFR (sSFR) in each pixel, setting a threshold of log(sSFR) $\leq$ –10.6 yr$^{-1}$" (5.3 SFR measurements in faint regions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: No pixel exceeded the old-star criterion  
  > "We did not find any pixels where this criterion would indicate that the emission mostly comes from old stars." (5.3 SFR measurements in faint regions)

## Qualitative findings

- NGC 5068 shows the most active recent SF; NGC 3351 shows a consistently decreasing recent SF across the disk  
  > "The most active galaxy in terms of recent SF changes is the low-mass galaxy NGC 5068." (4 Recent changes in the star formation activity); "NGC 3351 is the only galaxy in the sample that has a consistently decreasing recent SF across the entire disk observed." (4 Recent changes in the star formation activity)
- Arm and interarm SFE are statistically different by the K-S test, though medians are very similar  
  > "the SFE distributions in the arms and interarms are intrinsically different, albeit the distribution shapes and median values are rather similar" (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The sample is limited to PHANGS-MUSE galaxies with the needed photometry; the CO map was chosen at 7.5'' because of the MIPS resolution  
  > "The final sample selection in this work was dependent on the availability of the photometric data in these three bands among the PHANGS-MUSE galaxies, which left us with 11 objects." (2.1 Photometry); "Limited by the MIPS 24$\mu$m  resolution, we used the line-integrated CO(2-1) intensity observations (broad mom0 map)" (2.3 PHANGS-ALMA)
- Why this method: Use a calibrated metric of few observables instead of full SED modelling to avoid heavy data and compute needs  
  > "We aimed to use simple observables instead of full SED modeling of SFHs to avoid the strict data requirements and large computational costs associated with that." (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Variable-k FUV dust correction (Boquien et al.), used in the earlier paper, tested against constant k = 4.9; constant k preferred  
  > "However, in , we corrected the FUV images for dust extinction following the recipe from (cite: boquien_16) instead." (2.1 Photometry); "Thus, we deem the use of a constant value for $k$ a more suitable choice in our case" (5.2 A(FUV) corrections)
- Alternative: Variable alpha_CO not adopted  
  > "We do not opt for any variable $\alpha_\textnormal{CO}$ calibration here since it would introduce additional scatter to the analysis" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Small sample size  
  > "We also bear in mind the low sample size in this study." (4.1 AGN, bars, external effects)
- Limitation (authors): Constant alpha_CO probably underestimates Sigma_mol in low-metallicity NGC 5068  
  > "Instead, we might actually underestimate" (4.2 Effects of the internal galaxy environment on SFE)
- Limitation (authors): IMF high-mass end may be undersampled in faint interarm regions  
  > "we might not be able to fully sample the high-mass end of the ISM in the interarms due to the stochastic nature of the O-type SF" (5.3 SFR measurements in faint regions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA enters only through one archive moment-0 map and a constant alpha_CO; the SFE conclusions are sensitive to that choice
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Arm and interarm masks come from 3.6 micron images and are imprecise at 6'' for early-type galaxies
- Caveat (inferred, ours): K-S tests on spatially correlated pixels (6'' beam oversampled) overstate significance
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No uncertainties are propagated into <SFR5>/<SFR200> maps or into group assignments
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The different versus similar A-IA split is not defined quantitatively
