## Qualitative findings

- Blueshifted and redshifted 12CO (J=3-2) emission is detected toward MMS-1, tracing a compact rotating Keplerian disk around a ~0.2 M_odot protostar.  
  > "we have clearly detected blueshifted/redshifted gas in 12CO associated with the protostar. The spatial/velocity distributions of the gas show there is a rotating disk with a size scale of ~10 AU, a disk mass of ~10^-4 M_odot and a central stellar mass of ~0.2 M_odot." (Abstract)
- High-density tracers H13CO+ and C17O are not detected toward MMS-1, and no extended high-density envelope connects to the disk, indicating the disk is detached from the surrounding dense core.  
  > "It is to be noted that we cannot detect high-density tracers, H13CO+ (J = 3–2) and C17O (J = 3–2), toward MMS-1 even with the higher angular resolution compared with Paper II. These results indicate that the disk may be detached from the surrounding environment" (Gas/dust distributions toward MMS-1)
- High-temperature (~50 K) 12CO (J=3-2) gas is detected several hundred au away from MMS-1, suggesting shock heating from turbulent gas interactions.  
  > "The present 12CO (J = 3–2) observations also found very high-temperature (~50 K) gas component at several hundred au away from MMS-1, indicating that there was shock heating possibly caused by interactions among the turbulent gases." (A detached disk at the center of an isolated protostellar core)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Previous ALMA Cycle 0/1 observations (~0.74'' x 0.34'') could not spatially resolve MMS-1 or detect associated gas, requiring higher spatial resolution (~0.18'' x 0.10'' in Cycle 3) to resolve the disk and clarify the evolutionary status of the VeLLO.  
  > "A mm-dust source (MMS-1), which is associated with the protostar, has not been spatially resolved even at the resolution of ~0.74'' x 0.34'' and confirmed no gas association" (Introduction); "In particular, the evolutionally status of the very low-luminosity protostar is still unclear, possibly due to insufficient spatial resolutions." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Because the ALMA beam (~25 x 14 au) only marginally resolves the ~10 au disk, forward modeling with synthetic observations of rotating Keplerian disks is required to remove beam dilution effects; fitting is restricted to high-velocity channels because low velocities are contaminated by the surrounding dense core.  
  > "However, since the present ALMA observations do not have a sufficient resolution to spatially resolve the disk structure, the comparisons with the simulation of rotating disks are needed to remove the effect of the beam dilution." (Physical quantities of disk of MMS-1); "We tuned the parameters to reproduce the 12CO profile at the high-velocity parts because the spectrum is affected by the surrounding dense core toward the source." (Physical quantities of disk of MMS-1)
