   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Absolute flux calibration uncertainty about 10%  
  > "The uncertainty on the absolute flux calibration is about 10%." (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **image the circumbinary disc continuum**: Super-uniform weighting with manual CLEAN boxes on the two central sources to remove the circumstellar discs  
  > "the imaging was performed using super-uniform weighting to give extra weight to the longest baselines" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5); "CLEAN was then manually performed placing clean boxes around the two central sources" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Most extended configuration C43-5, baselines 15 m to 1.4 km  
  > "The continuum data presented here were acquired using the most extended faust configuration (C43-5), with baselines ranging between 15 m and 1.4 km." (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - parameter: Restored beam 0.2 arcsec, rms 0.5 mJy/beam  
  > "The restored beam is 0.2$''$ ($\sim$ 30 au) and the r.m.s. is 0.5 mJy beam$^{-1}$." (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Flux ratio between the two peaks compared with the standard-cleaning ratio of 1.4 from Bianchi et al.  
  > "which is consistent with the measured ratio of 1.4 at the same coordinates in the continuum image using the standard cleaning (cite: Bianchi+2020)." (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
3. **measure the continuum over-density and flux ratio**: Identify the northern brightest area of the CBD and a weaker southern peak, measure their flux ratio  
  > "A flux ratio of 1.2 (0.2) is measured between the two emission peaks" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **image C18O(2-1) and build moment maps**: Combine 12m and 7m data with robust 0.5, then compute moment 0 and moment 1 maps  
  > "was imaged by combining the 12m and 7m data with a robust parameter of 0.5" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5); "the moment 0 (centre) and moment 1 (right) maps reveal gas more extended than the continuum" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)

   - parameter: Beam 0.4 x 0.34 arcsec, rms 2.0 mJy/beam  
  > "yielding a $0.4\ensuremath{''}\times0.34\ensuremath{''}$ beam and 2.0 mJy beam$^{-1}$ rms" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - assumption: C18O(2-1) emission expected to be optically thin at ~50 au  
  > "expected to be optically thin at $\sim$50 au" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **sample plausible binary orbits**: Monte Carlo sampling of orbits with imorbel, restricted to near-coplanar and mildly eccentric orbits consistent with the astrometry  
  > "we ran a Monte Carlo calculations using imorbel" (Hydrodynamical modelling); "we imposed the binary orbit to be close to coplanar with respect to the CBD and only mildly eccentric" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - software: imorbel
   - parameter: a_b = 42.2 au, e_b = 0.175, i_b = 122.3 deg, Omega = 168.9 deg, omega = 176.5 deg, f = 163.5 deg  
  > "semi-major axis $a_{\rm b}=42.2$ au, eccentricity $e_{\rm b}=0.175$, inclination $i_{\rm b}=122.3\deg$" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - assumption: Sink masses M1 = 0.8 Msun, M2 = 0.3 Msun  
  > "The binary is modelled using two sink particles of mass $M_1=0.8\,M_\odot$ and $M_2=0.3\,M_\odot$" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Final orbit chosen as the one most consistent with the observed disc morphology among those allowed by the astrometry  
  > "among the possible orbits allowed by the astrometry in (cite: Hernandez+2024), we selected the most consistent orbit considering the specific disc morphology obtained from observations" (Discussion and conclusions)
6. **run the circumbinary disc hydrodynamics**: 3D one-fluid SPH gas and dust simulation with 10^6 particles, two sink particles, no self-gravity, evolved for 13 kyr  
  > "using $10^6$ SPH particles, a sufficient resolution to vertically resolve the disc" (Hydrodynamical modelling); "we show the gas and dust surface density maps after 13 kyr of evolution" (3.1 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - software: Phantom
   - parameter: Artificial viscosity alpha_AV = 0.25, beta_AV = 2.0, effective alpha_SS ~ 3-5e-3  
  > "adopting $\alpha_{\rm AV}=0.25$ and $\beta_{\rm AV}=2.0$" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - parameter: Disc from 60 au to 140 au, Sigma = Sigma_0 (R/R_0)^-1 with R_0 = 100 au, Sigma_0 = 8.84 g/cm^2, sink radius 5 au  
  > "These particles are distributed between $R_{\rm in}=60$ au and $R_{\rm out}=140$ au" (Hydrodynamical modelling); "with $R_0=100$ au and $\Sigma_0=8.84\,{\rm g\,cm^{-2}}$" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - parameter: Grain size 0.1 mm, density 3 g/cm^3, dust-to-gas ratio 0.01, aspect ratio 0.05 at 100 au  
  > "We set the grain size $s=0.1$ mm with intrinsic density $\rho_d=3\,{\rm g\,cm^{-3}}$ (typical of silicates)." (Hydrodynamical modelling); "we adopt a typical value of $0.01$ for the dust-to-gas ratio" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - assumption: Dust size constant, chosen to match grains emitting at 1.3 mm; envelope neglected  
  > "The dust size is considered to be constant and was selected to correspond to grains emitting at 1.3 mm" (Hydrodynamical modelling); "Here we are mainly interested in the disc so we chose to neglect the envelope." (Hydrodynamical modelling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked Stokes number (~3e-3, tight coupling), Toomre Q (>1), and a run with R_in = 80 au gave no significant differences  
  > "Thus the 0.1 mm particles in our runs are tightly coupled to the gas, fully validating the one–fluid algorithm adopted for the simulations." (Hydrodynamical modelling); "find no significant differences compared to the nominal case" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
7. **produce synthetic continuum and C18O maps**: Post-process Phantom outputs with MCFOST on a Voronoi grid, compute dust thermal emission and LTE C18O line emission  
  > "we map the density, temperature, velocity maps from the hydrodynamical simulations into the radiative transfer grid without any interpolation" (Radiative transfer calculations); "we suppose that the gas is in local thermodynamic equilibrium with $T_{\rm gas} = T_{\rm dust}$" (Radiative transfer calculations)
   - software: MCFOST
   - parameter: 1.28e8 photon packets; astronomical silicates, grain sizes 0.03 micron to 1 mm, slope -3.5, Mie theory; distance 141 pc  
  > "The disc is irradiated using $1.28\times10^8$ photons packets" (Radiative transfer calculations); "with a grain size distribution ranging from 0.03 $\mu$m to 1 mm, and a slope of $-3.5$" (Radiative transfer calculations)
   - assumption: Stellar luminosities 26.4 Lsun (N) and 0.2 Lsun (S); astronomical silicates (Draine 2003)  
  > "have luminosities equal to $26.4\,L_{\odot}$ and $0.2\,L_{\odot}$ (respectively)" (Radiative transfer calculations); "We assume astronomical silicates (cite: Draine2003)" (Radiative transfer calculations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Qualitative visual comparison of synthetic maps with the observations; the best-matching snapshot was selected  
  > "The snapshot with the highest level of resemblance with the observations (across different wavelength and tracers)" (Hydrodynamical modelling)

## Qualitative findings

- The continuum shows an over-density in the northern part of the circumbinary disc with a weaker peak in the south  
  > "An over density is observed in the northern part of the circumbinary disc." (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
- The model forms a precessing over-density near the CBD inner edge, with gas and dust showing the same morphology  
  > "the formation of a precessing over-density near the CBD inner edge" (3.1 Hydrodynamical simulations); "we note that both surface density maps exhibit the same morphology" (3.1 Hydrodynamical simulations)
- The dust-to-gas ratio stays uniform, so the over-density is not a dust trap  
  > "the dust-to-gas remains uniform" (Discussion and conclusions)
- The over-density arises from direct binary-disc interaction and is not part of a large-scale spiral  
  > "we find that the over-density arises from direct binary-disc interaction and is not part of a large-scale spiral" (Discussion and conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Most extended FAUST configuration used for continuum to resolve the CBD  
  > "The continuum data presented here were acquired using the most extended faust configuration (C43-5)" (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
- Why this method: Super-uniform weighting and manual clean boxes used to highlight the CBD emission  
  > "To highlight the emission from the circumbinary disc, the imaging was performed using super-uniform weighting to give extra weight to the longest baselines." (2 FAUST observations of L1551 IRS 5)
- Alternative: A run with the dusty disc inner edge at 80 au instead of 60 au gave no significant differences, so the initial condition does not strongly impact the over-density  
  > "find no significant differences compared to the nominal case" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
- Alternative: Icy aggregates with density 1 g/cm^3 would change the coupling only by a factor of 3  
  > "would not affect the coupling in a significant way (only by a factor of 3)" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
- How they decided it was enough: The simulation snapshot with the highest resemblance to the observations across wavelengths and tracers was selected  
  > "The snapshot with the highest level of resemblance with the observations (across different wavelength and tracers)" (Hydrodynamical modelling)
- Limitation (authors): The model does not reproduce satisfactorily the over-density on the opposite (southern) disc side  
  > "our model does not reproduce satisfactorily the over-density located on the opposite disc side" (3.2 Radiative transfer models)
- Limitation (authors): Simulations omit the envelope, so the extent of C18O emission is underestimated  
  > "This omission results in the simulations underestimating the spatial extent of the C$^{18}$O emission" (Discussion and conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): Grain growth is not included in the modelling  
  > "This physical mechanism is not considered in our modelling though." (Discussion and conclusions)
