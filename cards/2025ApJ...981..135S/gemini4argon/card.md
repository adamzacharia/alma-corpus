## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce JWST NIRCam images and align astrometry**: Reduce NIRCam data using custom pipeline based on JWST pipeline v1.9.4 with snowball/wisp removal and 1/f background homogenization, then align astrometry to three Gaia DR3 stars.  
  > "The data were reduced using our custom pipeline based on the JWST calibration pipeline version 1.9.4 (cite: Bushouse.H:2023a) under CRDS context jwst_1041.pmap." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions); "We aligned the NIRCam images to the three Gaia stars, and then we corrected small offsets between the NIRCam images using faint stars." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions)
   - software: JWST Calibration Pipeline
   - parameter: CRDS context jwst_1041.pmap  
  > "The data were reduced using our custom pipeline based on the JWST calibration pipeline version 1.9.4 (cite: Bushouse.H:2023a) under CRDS context jwst_1041.pmap." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions)
   - parameter: Pixel scale = 0.03 arcsec/pixel  
  > "The pixel scales of the final products for all the filters are set to 0.03 arcsec/pixel." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Checked astrometric uncertainties of NIRCam images are < 0.015''  
  > "The astrometric uncertainties of the NIRCam images are < 0.015\ensuremath{''}." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions)
2. **Homogenize PSFs, resample images, and measure aperture photometry and optical [O III] line map**: Construct effective PSFs using photutils, create convolution kernels with PyPHER to match F444W (or ALMA clean beam), resample with reproject, perform fixed-aperture photometry with local sky annulus subtraction, and derive optical [O III] flux map from F356W-F444W excess.  
  > "Then, we homogenized the PSFs of the NIRCam images using kernels created by PyPHER (cite: Boucaud.A:2016a)." (Image analyses); "Finally, the image pixels were resampled using reproject to match the F444W image pixels." (Image analyses); "Photometry was performed with fixed apertures on the PSF-homogenized images and aperture correction was not applied." (Image analyses)
   - software: photutils, IRAFStarFinder, PyPHER, reproject
   - parameter: Total circular aperture diameter = 1.8''  
  > "We chose a circular aperture of 1.8\ensuremath{''} in diameter to measure the total photometry of B14-65666, which sufficiently recover all the fluxes according to curve-of-growth plots of the flux density." (Image analyses)
   - parameter: Sky background annulus = 0.95'' to 1.95''  
  > "The local sky background computed in an annulus from 0.95\ensuremath{''} to 1.95\ensuremath{''} were subtracted before the photometry measurements." (Image analyses)
   - parameter: Component apertures: E = 1.4x0.80, W = 1.1x0.66, E-core = 0.44x0.44, W-core = 0.44x0.44, N-tail = 0.70x0.40, S-tail = 1.0x0.50 arcsec^2  
  > "| E | 1.4 \times0.80 | 6.6\pm0.7 | 1480\pm260 | 178^{+52}_{-39} | 53^{+26}_{-20} | 178^{+50}_{-39} | -1.18^{+0.15}_{-0.16} |" (Table 3); "| W | 1.1 \times0.66 | 2.6\pm0.4 | 750\pm170 | 37^{+12}_{-8} | 23^{+15}_{-11} | 37^{+12}_{-8} | -2.19^{+0.11}_{-0.09} |" (Table 3); "| E-core | 0.44 \times0.44 | 4.3\pm0.4 | 2050\pm290 | 180^{+113}_{-58} | 506^{+253}_{-182} | 34^{+13}_{-9} | -1.78^{+0.11}_{-0.13} |" (Table 3)
   - assumption: 5% absolute flux uncertainty included in photometric uncertainties  
  > "In addition, the absolute flux uncertainty was included in the photometric uncertainties as a conservative value of 5% (cite: Rigby.J:2023a)." (Image analyses)
   - fitting: Deconvolved effective radius of E-core in F115W fitted assuming an exponential surface brightness profile  
  > "Under an assumption that the core of the galaxy E has an exponential surface brightness profile, the deconvolved effective radius in F115W is r_\text{e} < 0.016 arcsec (85 pc) at the 2\sigma significance limit." (Morphology, Colors, and Optical [O iii] Line Map)
   - validation: Checked PSF homogenization using faint stars in FoVs; compared total photometry with COSMOS2020 catalog  
  > "We have checked that the PSF homogenization works well using faint stars in FoVs." (Image analyses); "Our measurements of the total photometry are consistent with the values on the COSMOS2020 catalog (cite: Weaver.J:2022a)." (Morphology, Colors, and Optical [O iii] Line Map)
3. **Perform UV-to-FIR SED fitting for total system and resolved components**: Fit NIRCam photometry with and without ALMA Band 6, 7, 8 dust continuum and [O III] 88 um line fluxes using Bagpipes with BPASS v2.3 stellar models, CLOUDY nebular emission, and CIGALE Draine et al. (2014) dust emission models.  
  > "We performed SED fitting to the NIRCam photometry using Bayesian Analysis of Galaxies for Physical Inference and Parameter EStimation (cite: Carnall.A:2018a)." (SED fitting); "We tested SED fitting with and without ALMA fluxes to see changes in obtained best-fit parameters." (SED fitting); "Instead of the originally adopted ones, we used the updated version (cite: Draine.B:2014a) implemented in the CIGALE SED fitting code (cite: Boquien.M:2019a), which allows up to \ensuremath{\mathcal{U}_\mathrm{min}}= 50." (SED fitting)
   - software: Bagpipes, CLOUDY, CIGALE, BPASS
   - parameter: z = 7.1520 (fixed)  
  > "The redshift is fixed at z = 7.1520 in the fitting." (SED fitting)
   - parameter: n_H = 100 cm^-3 (fixed in Bagpipes CLOUDY models)  
  > "The hydrogen density in the CLOUDY models is fixed to be n_\text{H} = 100 \mathrm{cm^{-3}} in Bagpipes." (SED fitting)
   - parameter: Old stellar component: constant SF from 600 to 100 Myr ago with Z_star = 0.5 Z_sun  
  > "To constrain the mass of the old stellar components, we added another constant star formation lasting for 500 Myr, from 600 Myr ago to 100 Myr ago with respect to z = 7.15, which does not strongly contribute to UV continuum and emission-line fluxes." (SED fitting); "The stellar metallicity of the old components is fixed to \ensuremath{Z_\text{star}}= 0.5 Z_\text{\(\odot\)} (cite: Asplund.M:2021a) to reduce the number of free parameters, but we checked that results do not vary significantly with the assumed Z_\text{star} value." (SED fitting)
   - parameter: Dust parameters fixed: alpha_U = 2, U_max = 10^7, q_PAH = 2.5%  
  > "The model parameters other than \mathcal{U}_\mathrm{min} and \gamma were fixed: the power-law index of the stellar intensity \alpha_\mathcal{U} = 2, the maximum stellar intensity \mathcal{U}_\text{max} = 10^7, and the PAH mass fraction q_\text{PAH} = 2.5%." (SED fitting)
   - assumption: Calzetti (2000) dust attenuation law  
  > "The dust attenuation curve follows the (cite: Calzetti:2000) law." (SED fitting)
   - assumption: Kroupa (2001) IMF from 0.1 to 300 M_sun and BPASS Delta log(alpha/Fe) = +0.6  
  > "We used the case of \varDelta \log(\alpha/\text{Fe}) = +0.6, where the oxygen abundance is almost the solar value 12 + \log\ensuremath{\text{O}/\text{H}}= 8.69 (cite: Asplund.M:2021a) at Z = 0.02 (cite: Byrne.C:2022a)." (SED fitting); "The adopted initial mass function (IMF) is a (cite: Kroupa:2001) IMF in the mass range from 0.1 to 300 \mathrm{M_\ensuremath{\odot}}." (SED fitting)
   - assumption: Gas-phase metallicity identical to stellar metallicity  
  > "The gas-phase metallicity is assumed to be identical to the stellar metallicity." (SED fitting)
   - fitting: Nested sampling in Bagpipes with uniform priors: t_SF in [1.0, 300.0] Myr, log M* in [1.0, 15.0], A_V in [0.0, 2.0] mag, log U in [-4.0, -0.5], Z* in [0.01, 2.00] Z_sun, log M_old in [1.0, 15.0], U_min in [0.1, 50.0], gamma in [0.001, 1.0]; best-fit from 50th percentile and 1-sigma from 16th/84th percentiles  
  > "The best-fit parameters are computed as the median of the posterior distributions. The 1\sigma fitting uncertainties are evaluated from the 16 and 84 percentiles of the posterior distributions." (SED fitting); "Bagpipes receives these ranges as limits of the uniform prior distributions and performs the nested sampling to generate the posterior distribution of the parameters." (Supplements for the SED fitting)
   - validation: Tested constant, delayed-tau, exponential, and no-old-component SFHs in Appendix and confirmed results are insensitive to SFH choice  
  > "We also tested different star-formation histories, but the fitting results were insensitive to different star-formation histories" (SED fitting)
4. **Analyze the IRX-beta_UV relation**: Compute monochromatic UV luminosity L_UV at 1600 A and UV spectral slope beta_UV via linear fitting to 500 posterior SED models (from NIRCam-only fits) in Calzetti et al. (1994) windows, combine with ALMA L_IR from Hashimoto et al. (2019), and compare against canonical Meurer and SMC relations.  
  > "We computed the monochromatic rest-frame UV luminosity L_\text{UV} and \beta_\text{UV} from the best-fit SED models derived without the ALMA data." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation); "The UV luminosity was computed from the UV continuum as L_\text{UV} = \lambda L_{\lambda} at \lambda = 1600 Å. The UV slope was obtained by linear fitting to the UV continuum within the wavelength windows proposed by (cite: Calzetti.D:1994a)." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation); "The IR luminosity L_\text{IR} was taken from (cite: Hashimoto.T:2019a), who estimated it by the modified blackbody fitting under the assumption of the dust temperature of T_\text{d} = 48 K and the dust emissivity of \beta_\text{d} = 2.0." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation)
   - parameter: beta_UV,0 = -2.62 (fiducial intrinsic UV slope)  
  > "For simplicity we use \ensuremath{\beta_\text{UV,0}}= -2.62 (cite: Reddy.N:2018a) as a fiducial value at high redshift." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation)
   - assumption: Modified blackbody T_d = 48 K and beta_d = 2.0 for L_IR  
  > "The IR luminosity L_\text{IR} was taken from (cite: Hashimoto.T:2019a), who estimated it by the modified blackbody fitting under the assumption of the dust temperature of T_\text{d} = 48 K and the dust emissivity of \beta_\text{d} = 2.0." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation)
   - fitting: Linear fitting to the UV continuum in Calzetti et al. (1994) windows across 500 posterior SED models  
  > "The UV slope was obtained by linear fitting to the UV continuum within the wavelength windows proposed by (cite: Calzetti.D:1994a)." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation); "we measured values from the 500 SED models derived from the parameter posterior distributions and estimated uncertainties from the measurement distribution." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation)
   - validation: Checked intrinsic UV slope beta_UV,0 of best-fit SED curves before dust reddening (-2.6 to -2.55)  
  > "We checked \beta_\text{UV,0} of our best-fit intrinsic SED curves before dust reddening and found that it ranges from \ensuremath{\beta_\text{UV,0}}= -2.6 to -2.55, which is bluer than -2.23 used in (cite: Meurer.G:1999a)." (IRX–\beta_\text{UV} relation)
5. **Construct optical-to-FIR [O III] line ratio map and model gas-phase metallicity**: Correct F356W and F444W images for dust attenuation using best-fit A_V, convolve optical [O III] map to ALMA 0.38'' beam, divide by ALMA [O III] 88 um moment-0 map, and compare ratios with CLOUDY v17.02 photoionization models as a function of Z_gas, U, and n_e (computed with PyNeb).  
  > "We created a line-ratio map by taking a ratio of the optical [O iii]\lambda\lambda4960,5008 emission-line map to the FIR [O iii] 88 \mathrm{\mu m} image." ( [O iii] Line Ratio: Insights into Gas-phase Metallicity); "The created attenuation-corrected optical line map was convolved to match the angular resolution of the ALMA synthesized beam (0.38\ensuremath{''})." ( [O iii] Line Ratio: Insights into Gas-phase Metallicity); "To be more quantitative, we used CLOUDY to explore nebular parameters that reproduce the obtained [O iii] line ratios." ( [O iii] Line Ratio: Insights into Gas-phase Metallicity)

## Qualitative findings

- B14-65666 is confirmed to be a major merger in a starburst phase with two spatially separated rest-frame optical components (E and W), ruling out a scenario of star-forming clumps within a single rotating disk.  
  > "The two galaxies are apparently separated in the rest-frame optical wavelengths, ruling out a scenario that the two bright components are star-forming regions in a rotating stellar disk." (Morphology, Colors, and Optical [O iii] Line Map)
- Galaxy E has an unresolved compact core surrounded by diffuse extended emission (likely tidal tails) that is bright in optical continuum/lines and ALMA [O III] 88 um, while galaxy W is elongated and clumpy.  
  > "The high angular resolution of NIRCam reveals the complex morphology of two galaxy components: galaxy E has a compact core (E-core), surrounded by diffuse, extended, rest-frame optical emission, which is likely to be tidal tails; and galaxy W has a clumpy and elongated morphology with a blue UV slope (\ensuremath{\beta_\text{UV}}=-2.2\pm0.1)." (Abstract); "ALMA archival data show that the bluer galaxy W is brighter in dust continua than the redder galaxy E, while the tails are bright in [O iii]  88 \mathrm{\mu m}." (Abstract)
- Despite having a bluer UV slope, galaxy W is brighter in ALMA dust continuum at 88 and 158 um than the redder galaxy E, which can be explained by a higher dust temperature in galaxy E and/or lower dust temperature or patchy dust geometry in galaxy W.  
  > "The galaxy E is a dusty (A_\text{V}=1.2\pm0.1 mag) starburst with a possible high dust temperature (\ge63\mbox{--}68 K). The galaxy W would have a low dust temperature (\le27\mbox{--}33 K) or patchy stellar-and-dust geometry, as suggested from the infrared excess (IRX) and \beta_\text{UV} diagram." (Abstract)
- The E-core exhibits a higher optical-to-FIR [O III] line ratio and lower gas-phase metallicity than galaxy W, consistent with less-enriched gas inflows triggered by the major merger.  
  > "The high optical-to-FIR [O iii] line ratio of the E-core shows its lower gas-phase metallicity (\simeq0.2\mbox{--}0.4 Z_\text{\(\odot\)}) than the galaxy W. These results agree with a scenario where major mergers disturb morphology and induce nuclear dusty starbursts triggered by less-enriched inflows." (Abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: NIRCam filters were chosen to sample the 1-5 um rest-frame UV/optical continuum and [O III]+Hbeta lines while avoiding line contamination around the Balmer break, and combined with high-resolution (<0.4'') ALMA line and dust continuum maps to resolve stellar, gaseous, and dust properties.  
  > "In the RIOJA project, the NIRCam filters were selected such as to trace the continuum emission over the entire 1–5 \mathrm{\mu m} wavelength range with mostly avoiding the contamination by the main optical emission lines." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions); "The combination of the new NIRCam images with archival high angular-resolution (< 0.4\ensuremath{''}) ALMA data provides us with an unique opportunity to investigate the detailed stellar, ionized, and dust structure in an interacting system at z > 7." (NIRCam and ALMA imaging: Stellar versus dust distribution)
- Why this method: Updated Draine et al. (2014) dust models in CIGALE allow U_min up to 50 needed for warm high-z dust; BPASS v2.3 binary models with alpha-enhancement reproduce strong high-z [O III] lines and hard UV slopes; optical-to-FIR [O III] ratio probes ionized gas temperature, density, and metallicity.  
  > "Instead of the originally adopted ones, we used the updated version (cite: Draine.B:2014a) implemented in the CIGALE SED fitting code (cite: Boquien.M:2019a), which allows up to \ensuremath{\mathcal{U}_\mathrm{min}}= 50." (SED fitting); "This enhancement of \alpha elements is also key to reproducing [O iii] emission line strengths and hard UV spectral slopes at high redshift (cite: Shapley.A:2019a; Sugahara.Y:2022a)." (SED fitting); "Due to the large difference in their excitation energy, the ratio of the optical to FIR [O iii] emission lines is sensitive to the physical conditions (electron density and temperature) of the ionized emitting gas (cite: Osterbrock.D:2006a)." ( [O iii] Line Ratio: Insights into Gas-phase Metallicity)
- Alternative: HST image was not used despite comparable depth to avoid potential systematics between telescopes.  
  > "We note that although the HST image has a comparable depth to the NIRCam ones, we did not use it in this study to avoid a potential systematics among the telescope." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions)
- Alternative: ALMA Band 3 data were not used because neither continuum nor lines were detected and they do not strongly constrain the FIR SED.  
  > "We did not use ALMA Band 3 data (cite: Hashimoto.T:2023b) in this study because neither continuum nor lines were detected and they do not give strong constraints on the FIR SED." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions)
- Alternative: Scenario that the two bright components E and W are star-forming regions in a rotating stellar disk was ruled out because they are separated in rest-frame optical wavelengths.  
  > "The two galaxies are apparently separated in the rest-frame optical wavelengths, ruling out a scenario that the two bright components are star-forming regions in a rotating stellar disk." (Morphology, Colors, and Optical [O iii] Line Map)
- Alternative: Different parametric star-formation histories (delayed-tau, exponential, and constant without old stellar population) were tested and found to yield comparable results to the fiducial constant SFH.  
  > "Table 3 shows results for fitting to the total photometry obtained with different parametric starformation histories: constant (fiducial), delayed \tau, and exponential decreasing histories." (Supplements for the SED fitting); "All the starformation histories give the comparable results because B14-65666 exhibits bursty starformation within a short timescale of \sim10 Myr." (Supplements for the SED fitting)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ALMA fluxes were measured by fitting two Gaussian components whereas NIRCam fluxes were measured with fixed apertures.  
  > "We note that these ALMA fluxes were measured by fitting two Gaussian components to the line and continuum maps, which differs from the aperture photometry for the NIRCam images." (SED fitting)
- Limitation (authors): FIR dust continuum is only constrained at 2-3 bands, which is not very effective at tightening stellar property constraints and hits parameter boundary limits for galaxy E.  
  > "We note that while we added two fitting parameters for the FIR SED, dust continua are constrained at only 2\mbox{--}3 bands." (SED fitting); "In particular, the galaxy E requires so high \mathcal{U}_\mathrm{min} and \gamma that their probability distribution functions reach the maximum values of their permitted parameter ranges." (Resolved components)
- Limitation (authors): The 158 um dust continuum peak is shifted east of galaxy W beyond ideal astrometric uncertainty, possibly due to poorer ALMA astrometric accuracy for extended sources.  
  > "As these estimates for the ALMA data are based on an equation for compact sources, the accuracy may be worse for extended sources like B14-65666." (JWST and ALMA Observations and Data Reductions); "The origin of this shift is unclear, but it might be ALMA astrometric uncertainties poorer than the ideal one." (Footnote, NIRCam and ALMA imaging: Stellar versus dust distribution)
- Limitation (authors): Properties of the diffuse tails are poorly constrained due to non-detections in F115W/F150W/F200W and dust continuum, degeneracies between old stars, dust, and emission lines, and angular resolution differences between NIRCam and ALMA; alternative origins like ionized outflows or offset old stars are not rejected.  
  > "Although we have revealed many properties of the cores of the galaxies, less properties are constrained for the tails. This is mainly because it is difficult to solve the degeneracy in physics that can explain their red SED, dust non-detections, and detection of [O iii] 88 \mathrm{\mu m} emission, with different angular resolutions of the NIRCam and ALMA." (Overall scenario for B14-65666 system); "We expected that the extended diffuse emission originates from the tidal tails produced by the major merger, but we did not reject other possibilities like ionized gas outflows (cite: Yuma.S:2019a) and offset old stellar populations (cite: Colina.L:2023a)." (Overall scenario for B14-65666 system)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mismatch between 2D Gaussian fitting for ALMA photometry (inherited from Hashimoto et al. 2019) and fixed rectangular/circular aperture photometry for JWST NIRCam in the joint UV-to-FIR SED fitting.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Optical [O III] 4960,5008 line fluxes are derived purely from broad-band F356W - F444W color excess rather than spectroscopy, making the optical-to-FIR [O III] line ratio sensitive to assumed continuum slope, Hbeta/Hgamma/Hdelta subtraction, and spatially uniform aperture A_V corrections.
