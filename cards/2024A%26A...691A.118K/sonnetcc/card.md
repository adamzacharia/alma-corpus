## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the CO(2-1) cube**: CASA pipeline calibration, then own scripts with hogbom deconvolution and briggs weighting  
  > "pipeline v5.1.1-5, and our own scripts to perform the imaging and post-processing tasks under" (ALMA mm/sub-mm interferometry)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: briggs weighting, robustness 2.0  
  > "we used the standard **hogbom** deconvolution algorithm with **briggs** weighting and a robustness value of $2.0$" (ALMA mm/sub-mm interferometry)
   - parameter: native velocity resolution 2.4 km/s  
  > "processing it initially at the native velocity resolution of 2.4 km/s" (ALMA mm/sub-mm interferometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Resample, mask, continuum-subtract and primary-beam correct the cube**: Resample to 10 km/s, 2 x rms threshold mask with manual edits, zero-degree polynomial continuum subtraction, primary beam correction  
  > "we resampled the data to a coarser velocity resolution of 10 km/s" (ALMA mm/sub-mm interferometry); "we subtracted the continuum emission using a zero-degree polynomial fit in the spatial frequency domain and the adjacent channels" (ALMA mm/sub-mm interferometry)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: threshold mask at 2 x rms followed by manual refinement  
  > "starting with a threshold mask at $2 \times \text{rms}$ noise level to detect significant emission" (ALMA mm/sub-mm interferometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Make moment maps**: Moment 0 and moment 1 maps from the cube, plus continuum map for comparison with CO contours  
  > "Moment maps were created from the available data cubes" (Molecular gas properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit a tilted-ring disc model to the CO cube**: 3D-BAROLO 3D tilted-ring fit to the 10 km/s cube, run with LOCAL and AZIM normalisations  
  > "This tool fits a 3D tilted-ring model to emission line data cubes" (3D-BAROLO model); "3D-BAROLO provides two distinct normalisation options for the gas column density: pixel-by-pixel (**LOCAL**) and azimuthally averaged (**AZIM**)." (3D-BAROLO model)
   - software: 3D-BAROLO
   - parameter: input cube at 10 km/s spectral resolution  
  > "We used the CO(2-1) data cube with a spectral resolution of 10 $\rm km/s$ as input data for 3D-BAROLO." (3D-BAROLO model)
   - parameter: initial PA and inclination from Hubble and Pan-STARRS images; centre and systemic velocity from moment-0 map  
  > "As inputs to the model, we used *Hubble* and Pan-STARRS images of the galaxy to deduce the initial values for position angle (PA) and inclination (INC)." (3D-BAROLO model); "are instrumental in pinpointing the galaxy’s centre and systemic velocity" (3D-BAROLO model)
   - parameter: disc scale height Z0 = 1''  
  > "disc scale-height (Z0 parameter), set to 1$^{\prime\prime}$ in our study" (DISCFIT model)
   - assumption: thin disc, rotation driven by the galaxy's angular momentum (axisymmetric circular motion)  
  > "the emitting material is encapsulated within a thin disc, and the angular momentum of the galaxy is responsible for the observed rotational pattern" (3D-BAROLO model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-checked with DISCFIT; residual maps and position-velocity diagrams compared with data  
  > "To complement and cross-verify our results obtained from the 3D-BAROLO analysis, we utilised an additional analysis tool, DISCFIT." (DISCFIT model)
5. **Cross-check kinematics with DISCFIT**: DISCFIT applied to the velocity moment map with the same initial conditions as 3D-BAROLO  
  > "using the velocity moment map as an input, and utilising the same initial conditions as used for 3D-BAROLO" (DISCFIT model)
   - software: DISCFIT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals and rotation-curve comparison between the two models  
  > "These deviations, visible in the residuals and the rotation curve comparison, show that while the models capture the general kinematic structure, they struggle to accurately represent the complex dynamics in these regions." (DISCFIT model)
6. **Derive the molecular gas mass**: Integrate moment-0 flux within 12'' radius, compute L'CO(2-1), convert to L'CO(1-0) with a ratio, multiply by alpha_CO  
  > "Focusing on a region with a $12^{\prime \prime}$ radius, we obtained a flux density of $146.3 \pm 1.1$ $\rm Jy \,\rm km/s$" (Estimation of the molecular gas mass)
   - parameter: D = 39 Mpc, z = 0.009, nu_obs = 230 GHz  
  > "$D = 39$ Mpc, $z = 0.009$, and $\nu_{\text{obs}} = 230$ GHz" (Estimation of the molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: L'CO(2-1)/L'CO(1-0) ratio approx 5.0  
  > "$\frac{L^{\prime}_{\text{CO}(2-1)}}{L^{\prime}_{\text{CO}(1-0)}} \approx 5.0$" (Estimation of the molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: alpha_CO between 0.8 and 3.2 Msun  
  > "$\alpha_{\text{CO}}$, which ranges from $0.8 \, M_{\astrosun}$ to $3.2 \, M_{\astrosun}$ (cite: bolatto2013co)" (Estimation of the molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Caveat that interferometric filtering of extended emission may make the mass larger  
  > "Consequently, the total molecular gas mass could be larger if CO(2-1) emission is more extended." (Estimation of the molecular gas mass)
7. **Estimate the SMBH mass from the CO line width**: Fit a double-peaked Gaussian to the integrated CO(2-1) spectrum from the 3D-BAROLO model, derive W50, apply the WISDOM relation  
  > "This approach involves fitting a Gaussian double-peak profile to the emission-line data." (Estimation of SMBH mass); "$W_{50} = 2(w + \sigma \sqrt{2 \ln 2})$" (Estimation of SMBH mass)
   - software: 3D-BAROLO
   - parameter: inclination from 3D-BAROLO, i approx 47.70 deg  
  > "the inclination was derived as $i \simeq 47.70^{\circ} \pm 1.1$" (Estimation of SMBH mass)
   - assumption: relation total scatter 0.6 dex  
  > "This relationship exhibits a total scatter of $0.6$ dex" (Estimation of SMBH mass)
   - fitting: double-peak Gaussian fit to the integrated spectrum; sampler and priors not stated  
  > "we utilised a double-peak Gaussian function to accurately fit the integrated CO(2-1) emission-line spectrum" (Estimation of SMBH mass)
   - validation: Compared with the WISDOM value and a reverberation-mapping mass  
  > "This result was compared with the values obtained from the WISDOM project for NGC 4593 (cite: tiley2016tully)" (Estimation of SMBH mass)
8. **Quantify non-circular motion and the CMZ-like ring**: Inspect residual velocity and dispersion maps and 3D-BAROLO radial profiles; measure flux in the residual region and in the central ring  
  > "This region, visible in the velocity map, contributes approximately 10% of the galaxy’s total flux, with about half of this flux seen in the residuals" (Evidence of non-circular motion and molecular inflow or outflows); "Moreover, the bump in rotation velocity, radial velocity, surface density, and velocity dispersion for the same radius away from the centre, observed in the 3D-BAROLO results" (Evidence of non-circular motion and molecular inflow or outflows)
   - software: 3D-BAROLO
   - assumption: residuals are in-plane non-circular motion rather than significant extraplanar outflow  
  > "The residuals observed in our analysis are relatively small and appear consistent with non-circular motions in the plane, rather than significant extraplanar outflows." (Evidence of non-circular motion and molecular inflow or outflows)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Considered and argued against extraplanar gas  
  > "while we cannot completely rule out the presence of extraplanar gas, it is unlikely to be a significant factor in the observed kinematics of NGC 4593." (Evidence of non-circular motion and molecular inflow or outflows)
9. **Fit the SED with CIGALE**: CIGALE fit to multi-wavelength photometry with delayed SFH, BC03 SSP, Calzetti/Leitherer attenuation, Dale 2014 dust emission and Stalevski 2016 two-phase torus  
  > "We employed a two-phase torus model for the AGN component, as described by (cite: stalevski2016dust)" (The spectral energy distribution); "Dust attenuation was modelled using the attenuation modules from (cite: calzetti2000dust) and (cite: leitherer2002ultraviolet), while the dust emission parameters were based on the models by (cite: dale2014two)." (The spectral energy distribution)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: Chabrier IMF, metallicity 0.02, separation age 10 Myr  
  > "incorporating a Chabrier Initial Mass Function, a metallicity of 0.02, and a separation age of 10 Myr." (The spectral energy distribution)
   - parameter: torus Rout/Rin = 20 (range 10 to 30)  
  > "($R_{\rm out}/R_{\rm in}$) of 20, with possible values ranging from 10 to 30" (The spectral energy distribution)
   - parameter: alpha_ox max deviation 0.2  
  > "$|\Delta \alpha_{\rm ox}|_{\rm max} = 0.2$" (The spectral energy distribution)
   - parameter: dust density gradients 1.0 (polar and radial)  
  > "Dust density gradients were set to $1.0$ for both polar angle and radial direction" (The spectral energy distribution)
   - assumption: face-on type-1 AGN; inclination taken from 3D-BAROLO  
  > "Initial conditions for our object were set as a face-on type-1 AGN" (The spectral energy distribution)
   - fitting: best-fitting CIGALE model; fit statistic, grids and priors not stated  
  > "represents the best-fitting model obtained with CIGALE based on our initial conditions and photometric data" (The spectral energy distribution)
   - validation: SFR compared with PAH 11.25 micron, [CII] and total FIR estimates  
  > "The SFR calculated from the PAH11.25$\mu$m emission (cite: mordini2021calibration) is 0.36" (The spectral energy distribution)

## Qualitative findings

- CO gas shows a one-arm (m=1) structure that the axisymmetric 3D-BAROLO model cannot capture  
  > "the CO gas distribution within NGC 4593 exhibits a striking one-arm structure" (Molecular gas properties); "which indicates lopsidedness that the 3D-BAROLO model, which imposes axisymmetry, struggles to account for" (Challenges in kinematic modelling)
- 3D-BAROLO and DISCFIT differ, with 3D-BAROLO following data bumps and possibly overfitting axisymmetric non-circular motion

## Reasoning

- Why this data: A multi-wavelength SED was added to link AGN and star formation properties to CO kinematics  
  > "we adopted a multi-wavelength approach to construct a well-sampled SED" (Ancillary data)
- Why this method: A second model (DISCFIT) used to cross-verify the 3D-BAROLO kinematics  
  > "To complement and cross-verify our results obtained from the 3D-BAROLO analysis, we utilised an additional analysis tool, DISCFIT." (DISCFIT model)
- Alternative: Extraplanar gas as origin of the residuals considered and judged unlikely to be significant  
  > "while we cannot completely rule out the presence of extraplanar gas, it is unlikely to be a significant factor in the observed kinematics of NGC 4593." (Evidence of non-circular motion and molecular inflow or outflows)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Interferometric filtering of extended emission means missing flux and possibly higher gas mass  
  > "the interferometric observations used in this study filter out extended emissions, resulting in missing flux" (Challenges in kinematic modelling)
- Limitation (authors): CO(2-1) alone does not trace all molecular gas  
  > "A critical limitation in our modelling is that the CO(2-1) line does not represent the entire molecular gas content" (Challenges in kinematic modelling)
- Limitation (authors): Axisymmetric, circular-orbit assumption of 3D-BAROLO omits the single arm and biases inclination  
  > "The assumption of circular orbits by 3D-BAROLO, when the orbits may actually be elongated, introduces further inaccuracies in the modelled inclination" (Challenges in kinematic modelling)
- Limitation (authors): Face-on orientation complicates evaluating model accuracy  
  > "the face-on orientation of NGC 4593 complicates the evaluation of model accuracy" (DISCFIT model)

- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow claim rests on residuals in a face-on galaxy with an axisymmetric model; no independent outflow test (e.g. cube-based line-wing fitting)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Inflow vs outflow is not distinguished, yet the abstract states an outflow
