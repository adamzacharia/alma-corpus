## Analysis recipe

1. **Reduce and image data**: CASA pipeline calibration, CLEAN natural weighting, uvcontsub for line cube, collapse off-line channels for continuum  
  > "To create a pure dust continuum image, we collapsed all off-line channels." (2.2 Observations and Data)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum flux and size**: imfit with 2D Gaussian  
  > "we apply the CASA task `imfit` assuming a 2D Gaussian profile for the specific intensity" (3.1 Dust Continuum)
   - software: CASA imfit
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian  
  > "we apply the CASA task `imfit` assuming a 2D Gaussian profile for the specific intensity." (Results, Dust Continuum)
   - validation: Compared sizes to Wang et al. 2013 Band 6 within 1-2 sigma  
  > "These deconvolved size and PA values are consistent with those obtained by (cite: wang2013) using ALMA Band 6 data within $1-2\sigma$ uncertainties" (3.1 Dust Continuum)
3. **Measure [OIII] flux, size, redshift, FWHM**: Moment image over line range, imfit 2D Gaussian; spectrum extracted from >3 sigma region  
  > "We perform photometry on the image with the CASA task `imfit` assuming a 2D Gaussian profile for the line intensity." (3.2 [Oiii])
   - software: CASA imfit
   - parameter: Integration 481.7-482.6 GHz (J2054), 484.1-485.0 GHz (J2310)  
  > "between $481.7 - 482.6$ GHz ($484.1 - 485.0$ GHz) for J2054-0005 (J2310+1855)" (3.2 [Oiii])
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Searched >1 and >2 sigma spectra for broad component; none found  
  > "We do not find any broad velocity component in the spectra." (3.2 [Oiii])
4. **Fit dust SED for Td, beta, L_TIR, SFR_IR**: Optically thin modified blackbody with CMB correction fit to Herschel, ALMA, MAMBO photometry; chi2; integrate 8-1000 um  
  > "By fitting modified-black body models corrected for the CMB effects to the photometry data, we obtain $T_{\rm d} = 50\pm2$ K and $\beta_{\rm d} = 1.8\pm0.1$ based on the $\chi^{2}$ statistics." (3.4 Dust SED)
   - assumption: Optically thin single-temperature dust; Kroupa IMF 0.1-100 Msun; dust emission star-formation powered (upper limit on SFR)  
  > "Therefore, our values should be treated with upper limits on the dust-obscured SFRs." (3.4 Dust SED)
   - fitting: chi2 minimisation  
  > "By fitting modified-black body models corrected for the CMB effects to the photometry data, we obtain $T_{\rm d} = 50\pm2$ K and $\beta_{\rm d} = 1.8\pm0.1$ based on the $\chi^{2}$ statistics." (Results, Tight Constraints on the Dust Temperature and the Infrared Luminosity)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Astrometry comparison with SDSS**: Recalibrate SDSS z-band with Gaia DR2 via IRAF ccmap/ccsetwcs; positions by imexam  
  > "we have performed `IRAF` tasks `ccmap` and `ccsetwcs` to recalibrate the astrometry of the SDSS image." (3.3 Astrometry)
   - software: IRAF
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Uncertainty from star comparison and imexam repeated cursor positions  
  > "The astrometry uncertainty (i.e., systemic uncertainty) is estimated to be $\approx 120$ and $100$ mas around J2054-0005 and J2310+1855, respectively, based on comparisons of bright star positions in the GAIA catalog" (Results, Astrometry)
6. **Luminosity ratios and SFR comparison**: Compute L[OIII]/L_TIR and L[OIII]/L[CII]; compare SFR_OIII (De Looze ULIRG relation) with SFR_IR  
  > "the empirical relation for ULIRGs would be suitable among the relations in (cite: de.looze2014)" (4 Discussion)
   - assumption: Flat LCDM, Om=0.272, H0=70.4  
  > "$H_{\small 0} = 70.4$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$" (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Bright L_TIR, relatively lower z, avoids atmospheric absorption to secure detection  
  > "To secure the [Oiii] line detection within reasonable ALMA integration times, we selected objects with (i) bright total infrared luminosities" (2.1 Sample)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): SFR_IR and Td uncertain due to possible AGN heating; local SFR relation may not apply to quasars  
  > "a significant fraction of $L_{\rm TIR}$ could be powered by AGN activity" (3.4 Dust SED)
- Limitation (authors): J2310+1855 SPW1 data lost to atmospheric absorption  
  > "In J2310+1855, we could not obtain the data product in SPW1 due to very strong atmospheric absorption" (2.2 Observations and Data)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-temperature SED with few photometric points
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low S/N [OIII] in J2310+1855
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Quasar PA and sizes poorly constrained for [OIII]
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Literature photometry heterogeneous
