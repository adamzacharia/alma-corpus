  > "We then used the line-free channels obtained by the CASA pipeline task $findcont$ as an initial estimate for the continuum-fitting and iteratively exclude channels affected by line emission until no line-emission channels are found over 3$\sigma$." (Data Reduction); "Three iterations of phase self-calibration were performed to improve phase stability, using solution intervals of ‘inf’, ‘30.25s’, and ‘int’, respectively." (Data Reduction); "The continuum emission was then removed in the UV domain using the CASA task uvcontsub, where the line-free channels were fitted with a first-order polynomial." (Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, findcont, uvcontsub
   - parameter: solint = 'inf', '30.25s', 'int'  
  > "Three iterations of phase self-calibration were performed to improve phase stability, using solution intervals of ‘inf’, ‘30.25s’, and ‘int’, respectively." (Data Reduction)
   - parameter: MT-MFS with two Taylor terms; multiscale scales = 0, 5, 15, 50, 150 pixels (continuum) and 0, 5, 15, 50 pixels (line)  
  > "Each iteration employed a multi-term multi-frequency synthesize (MT-MFS) deconvolver with two Taylor terms and multiscale cleaning scales of 0, 5, 15, 50, and 150 pixels." (Data Reduction); "continuum-subtracted visibilities were imaged with the multiscale deconvolver, using the same weighting scheme and scales (0, 5, 15, and 50 pixels)." (Data Reduction)
   - parameter: Briggs weighting robust = 0.5  
  > "Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5 was adopted throughout." (Data Reduction)
   - fitting: First-order polynomial fit to line-free channels in uvcontsub  
  > "The continuum emission was then removed in the UV domain using the CASA task uvcontsub, where the line-free channels were fitted with a first-order polynomial." (Data Reduction)
   - validation: Line-cube sensitivity (~0.5 K per 0.5 km s^-1 channel) checked against expected thermal noise; continuum subtraction compared with STATCONT  
  > "The final line-cube sensitivity achieved was $\sim$0.5 K per 0.5 km s$^{-1}$ channel, consistent with the expected thermal noise after self-calibration." (Data Reduction); "We also assessed the robustness of the continuum subtraction by comparing our CASA-based procedure with STATCONT (cite: Sanchez18)" (Molecular line identifications)
2. **Extract spectra from offset position and perform full-source and amide LTE spectral modeling**: Extract spectra from slightly offset position to reduce Doppler blending; construct a full source LTE model of all detected species in GILDAS CLASS/Weeds; optimize T_ex and N_t for GA, FA, NH2 13CHO, and AA using CLASS/ADJUST with SciPy Powell algorithm.  
  > "The observed spectra were modeled under the local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) assumption using CLASS/Weeds (cite: Mar11) sets of Grenoble Image and Line Data Analysis Software (GILDAS)" (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling); "To refine the LTE parameters, we optimized the initial CLASS/Weeds model by CLASS/ADJUST using the Powell algorithm (cite: Powell64) implemented in the SciPy optimization library." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling); "To mitigate this issue, spectra were extracted from slightly offset positions (Fig. [1](#fig:1) (b)), where most of the GA lines are less affected by blending and display clean Gaussian profiles with a high signal-to-noise ratios." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling)
   - software: GILDAS, CLASS/Weeds, CLASS/ADJUST, SciPy, STATCONT
   - parameter: V_LSR fixed to -16.5 km s^-1; source size theta fixed to deconvolved continuum size  
  > "$\theta$ was set to the deconvolved continuum sizes, $\Delta V$ to the average of Gaussian fits for bright unblended lines, and $V_{\rm LSR}$ to -16.5 km s$^{-1}$ for G358.93 MM1 (cite: 2019ApJ...881L..39B)." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling)
   - parameter: Line widths fixed to 1.4 km s^-1 (GA), 2.8 km s^-1 (FA), 2.0 km s^-1 (NH2 13CHO), and 2.1 km s^-1 (AA)  
  > "Under the LTE assumption, the best-fit model yields a column density of $N_{\rm t}$(GA) = (5.3$\pm$1.1)$\times$10$^{14} \rm cm^{-2}$ and an excitation temperature of $T_{\rm ex}$(GA) = 102$\pm$20 K, with the linewidth of GA set to 1.4 km s$^{-1}$." (Molecular line identifications); "In the analysis, the line widths were fixed to the average values of 2.8 km s$^{-1}$ for the FA lines and 2.0 km s$^{-1}$ for the NH$_{2}$$^{13}$CHO lines to derive the column densities and excitation temperatures." (Molecular line identifications); "For AA, ten unblended lines with $E_{\rm u}$=148-213 K were identified, and their line widths were fixed to the average value of 2.1 km s$^{-1}$ in the analysis" (Molecular line identifications)
   - assumption: Local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)  
  > "The observed spectra were modeled under the local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) assumption using CLASS/Weeds (cite: Mar11) sets of Grenoble Image and Line Data Analysis Software (GILDAS)" (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling)
   - assumption: 12C/13C = 28 +/- 5 to scale NH2 13CHO to total NH2CHO column density  
  > "the optically thin $^{13}$C isotopologue was used to infer the total FA column density $N_{\rm t}$(FA), adopting a scaling factor of $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C = 28$\pm$5 (cite: 2023A&A...670A..98Y)." (Molecular line identifications)
   - assumption: Conservative 20% uncertainty on T_ex and N_t  
  > "we adopted a conservative uncertainty of 20% for the derived $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ values." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling)
   - fitting: Powell optimization algorithm in CLASS/ADJUST (SciPy) with T_ex and N_t as free parameters; manual adjustment for background species in full source model

3. **Verify GA excitation temperature via rotational diagram analysis**: Fit linear relation between ln(N_u/g_up) and E_up using integrated intensities of seven unblended GA transitions.  
  > "To verify the optimality of the fitting results from CLASS/ADJUST and the validity of the LTE assumption, we have made a rotational diagram analysis (Fig. [5](#fig:C1)) using seven unblended transitions of GA." (The rotational diagram analysis for GA)

   - fitting: Rotational diagram linear regression  
  > "To verify the optimality of the fitting results from CLASS/ADJUST and the validity of the LTE assumption, we have made a rotational diagram analysis (Fig. [5](#fig:C1)) using seven unblended transitions of GA." (The rotational diagram analysis for GA)
   - validation: Comparison of derived T_rot (107 +/- 21 K) with CLASS/ADJUST T_ex (102 +/- 20 K)  
  > "The rotational diagram yields a rotational temperature of $107\pm21$ K. Considering the errors, the result agrees reasonably well with the value of $102\pm20$ K derived by the CLASS/ADJUST." (The rotational diagram analysis for GA)

  > "The spatial distributions of FA, AA, and GA were analyzed using the Cube Analysis and Rendering Tool for Astronomy (cite: Com21). Only transitions verified to be free from significant blending were utilized to generate the integrated-intensity (moment 0) maps." (Spatial distribution)

## Qualitative findings

- syn-glycolamide is tentatively detected in the hot molecular core G358.93-0.03 MM1 through seven clean spectral features comprising 17 transitions.  
  > "Within this framework, GA is identified toward G358.93 MM1 through seven clean spectral features (Fig. [2](#fig:2)), six of which are detected above the 3$\sigma$ level and three above 5$\sigma$ level." (Molecular line identifications); "We therefore regard the identification as tentative." (Molecular line identifications)
- The spatial distribution of glycolamide emission is compact and coincident with the formamide, acetamide, and 1 mm continuum peaks.  
  > "The newly obtained GA map is compact and coincident with both the FA and AA emission peaks as well as the continuum maximum." (Spatial distribution)
- The FA/GA and AA/GA abundance ratios in G358.93 MM1 are similar to those in the quiescent molecular cloud G+0.693-0.027, indicating a chemically connected amide network across environments.  
  > "The abundance ratios FA/GA and AA/GA in G358.93-0.03 MM1 closely resemble those measured in the molecular cloud G+0.693-0.027." (Conclusions)
- Current astrochemical models systematically overpredict the abundance of glycolamide by at least an order of magnitude relative to acetamide compared to observations.  
  > "While models (cite: 2020ApJ...899...65S; 2022ApJS..259....1G) tend to produce GA at levels comparable to or exceeding AA, observations of both G358.93 MM1 and G+0.693-0.027 show it to be at least one order of magnitude lower than AA (cite: 2023ApJ...953L..20R)." (Comparison with other sources and chemical models)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: G358.93 MM1 is the brightest submillimetre source in G358.93-0.03 and exhibits exceptionally line-rich spectra and a chemically diverse inventory of prebiotic and amide-related molecules.  
  > "MM1 exhibits exceptionally line-rich spectra and a chemically diverse inventory, including prebiotic or amide-related molecules" (Introduction); "This chemical richness makes G358.93 MM1 an ideal testbed to investigate GA and its potential linkage to other amides." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Spectra were extracted from slightly offset positions to mitigate severe Doppler broadening and line blending at the continuum peak; only T_ex and N_t were left free in LTE fitting to reduce parameter degeneracy; NH2 13CHO was used because parent FA is optically thick.  
  > "To reduce parameter degeneracy, only $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ were treated as free parameters" (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling); "To mitigate this issue, spectra were extracted from slightly offset positions (Fig. [1](#fig:1) (b)), where most of the GA lines are less affected by blending and display clean Gaussian profiles with a high signal-to-noise ratios." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling); "Because the FA lines are substantially optically thick, yielding an unrealistically low apparent $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C ratio of 3.1, the optically thin $^{13}$C isotopologue was used to infer the total FA column density $N_{\rm t}$(FA)" (Molecular line identifications)
- Alternative: Extracting spectra at the continuum peak (rejected due to severe Doppler broadening and line blending)  
  > "Although line intensities peak near the continuum maximum, a detailed line-by-line inspection revealed that most GA transitions in this region suffer from significant blending with neighboring species, making reliable identification and fitting particularly challenging." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling)
- Alternative: Using parent NH2CHO lines directly for total FA column density (rejected because parent lines are optically thick with apparent 12C/13C = 3.1)  
  > "Because the FA lines are substantially optically thick, yielding an unrealistically low apparent $^{12}$C/$^{13}$C ratio of 3.1, the optically thin $^{13}$C isotopologue was used to infer the total FA column density $N_{\rm t}$(FA)" (Molecular line identifications)
- Alternative: Automated image-domain continuum subtraction using STATCONT (tested as an alternative to manual UV-domain CASA uvcontsub; yielded ~30% lower N_t(GA) and slightly higher rms of ~1.65 K)  
  > "Notably, the column density derived from the STATCONT-based analysis is approximately 30% lower than that from the CASA-based analysis ($5.3\times10^{14}$ cm$^{-2}$)." (STATCONT-based continuum subtraction and LTE modeling of GA)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Limited bandwidth (~8 GHz), modest number of securely detected transitions (7 features), and high line density mean coincidental line blending cannot be excluded, making the GA detection tentative.  
  > "However, given the limited bandwidth ($\sim$8 GHz), the modest number of securely detected transitions, and the high line density of this warm HMC, a coincidental assignment cannot be excluded with the confidence required for a firm detection claim." (Molecular line identifications)
- Limitation (authors): Uncertainties are affected by line blending, baseline effects, and simplifying LTE assumptions.  
  > "Because the uncertainties are dominated not only by the fitting procedure, but also by line blending, baseline effects, and the simplifying assumptions of the LTE model, we adopted a conservative uncertainty of 20% for the derived $T_{\rm ex}$ and $N_{\rm t}$ values." (Spectroscopic inputs and local thermodynamic equilibrium modeling)
- Limitation (authors): With only two GA detections to date, conclusions on formation mechanisms remain preliminary.  
  > "Nevertheless, with only two confirmed GA detections to date, any conclusions regarding its precise formation mechanisms remain preliminary." (Chemical linkages of GA with FA and AA)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Internal inconsistency in reported GA abundance relative to H2: the abstract reports (1.7 +/- 0.2) x 10^-10, whereas Table 1 reports (0.7 +/- 0.2) x 10^-10 and the Conclusions report (6.8 +/- 2.0) x 10^-11 (the latter two match 5.3e14 / 7.82e24 = 6.78e-11).
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The exact coordinates and aperture size of the offset extraction position are only indicated visually in Fig. 1b and not given numerically in the text.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The STATCONT continuum subtraction yields a 30% lower GA column density and reduces the number of >3 sigma features from six to five, highlighting sensitivity of weak features to baseline treatment.
