## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate each dataset**: Archive calibration scripts (SB, LB) or ALMA pipeline (OLD), then phase self-calibration of continuum built from line-free channels  
  > "The data were self-calibrated after the line-free channels were combined altogether to create continuum datasets." (2.1 Data calibration, reduction, and combination)
   - software: CASA, gaincal, applycal, tclean
   - parameter: Solint reduced to the integration time: 6.05 s (SB), 2.02 s (LB)  
  > "We performed several phase self-calibration rounds on each continuum dataset until the solution interval was tuned down to the integration time of $6.05$ s for the SB dataset and of $2.02$ s for the LB dataset." (2.1 Data calibration, reduction, and combination)
   - parameter: First round uses combined SPWs, combined polarisations and infinite solint; later rounds decombine SPWs and polarisations  
  > "The first calibration tables were created for combined SPWs, combined polarisation and a infinite solution interval to maximise the S/N." (Appendix: Data combination)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: RMS must decrease between rounds without loss of total flux or peak intensity  
  > "We ensured that the RMS was decreasing between the rounds without any decrease of the total flux and peak intensity." (Appendix: Data combination)
2. **Rescale fluxes and combine the three datasets into a continuum image**: Flux rescaling to OLD, common continuum model with tclean (robust 0.5), second phase self-cal, final tclean with robust -0.5  
  > "We then created a combined continuum model with *tclean* in CASA v6.5.3 from the three datasets, using a Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of $0.5$ (cite: Briggs)." (2.1 Data calibration, reduction, and combination); "The resulting datasets were combined to create a final continuum image with *tclean* using a Briggs weighting with a robust of $-0.5$" (2.1 Data calibration, reduction, and combination)
   - software: CASA, tclean, phaseshift, reduction_utils.py (DSHARP)
   - parameter: Datasets centred on the cavity centre with phaseshift; second-round gaincal solint two times the integration time, combined SPWs, independent polarisations  
  > "Before doing so, the observations were centred together with the *phaseshift* task using the centre of the cavity as a common reference." (Appendix: Data combination); "The *gaincal* parameters were turned down to a solution interval of two times the integration time, combined SPWs and independent polarisations." (Appendix: Data combination)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Calibration stopped before the RMS stopped decreasing; combined data centred consistently  
  > "The calibration were stopped before the RMS stopped decreasing between the rounds." (Appendix: Data combination)
3. **Continuum-subtract and image the 12CO(2-1) cube**: Apply continuum self-cal tables to the gas MS, uvcontsub, scale to common flux, tclean cube with 0.5 km/s channels  
  > "We also applied the resulting self-calibration tables to the gas observations" (2.1 Data calibration, reduction, and combination); "The resulting gas MS were scaled to a common flux reference using the same correction factors that the continuum MS." (Appendix: Data combination)
   - software: CASA, uvcontsub, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust 0.5, channel width 0.5 km/s  
  > "An image cube of the $^{12}$CO (2-1) line emission was then created combining the three datasets using a Briggs weighting with a robust of $0.5$ and a channel width of $0.5$ km s$^{-1}$" (2.1 Data calibration, reduction, and combination)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Measure continuum disc geometry, cavity and extent**: Deprojected radial brightness profile; cavity radius at half peak intensity; R90 from encircled flux; i and PA best-fitted  
  > "that is defined as the radius at which the intensity first reaches half of its peak level with an incertitude taken as the difference with quarter peak radius." (2.2 Dust disc); "The inclination $i$ and the position angle $PA$ of the disc are respectively best-fitted to" (2.2 Dust disc)
   - parameter: Cavity radius defined at half the peak intensity, uncertainty = difference with quarter-peak radius  
  > "that is defined as the radius at which the intensity first reaches half of its peak level with an incertitude taken as the difference with quarter peak radius." (2.2 Dust disc)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with earlier measurements: 18% north-south asymmetry consistent with the reported 20%  
  > "This trend was also found in and is consistent with the reported value of $20\%$." (2.2 Dust disc)
5. **Fit a Keplerian disc model to the 12CO cube with Discminer**: Channel-by-channel image-plane fit of a Keplerian disc model with exponentially tapered emission surfaces, power-law peak intensity and line width, with MCMC  
  > "we used the python package Discminer (cite: DiscminerI; DiscminerII) to fit the $^{12}$CO (2-1) line emission and kinematics channel by channel by a Keplarian disc model." (3.1 Discminer); "Discminer then fits the previous model to the data in the image plane channel by channel thanks to MCMC sampling using emcee (cite: emcee)." (3.1 Discminer)
   - software: Discminer, emcee
   - parameter: 256 walkers, 40000 steps, first 6300 steps burn-in  
  > "Following the need of a precise model, we initialised $256$ walkers with a total of $40000$ steps." (Appendix: Gas emission fitting with Discminer); "The first $6300$ steps are considered as a burn-in phase where the walkers explore the parameter space." (Appendix: Gas emission fitting with Discminer)
   - parameter: Reference radius r0 = 100 au; search ranges per parameter listed in Table 5  
  > "The reference radius $r_0$ was taken as $100$ au." (Appendix: Gas emission fitting with Discminer); "Parameters of the best-fit model found by Discminer to the $^{12}$CO (2-1) emission." (Table 5)
   - parameter: PA defined from north to the blueshifted semi-major axis  
  > "In this work, the PA was defined as the angle from the northern axis to the blueshifted semi-major axis." (3.1 Discminer)
   - assumption: Thin disc with Keplerian rotation, initial emission surfaces set to a thin disc  
  > "The initial values of the parameters defining the emission surfaces were set to initialise a thin disc, in agreement with the observations." (Appendix: Gas emission fitting with Discminer)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC; best-fit = median of posteriors, errors = 16th and 84th percentiles  
  > "The best-fit parameters are taken as the median of the posterior distributions. The errorbars are the $16$ and $84$ percentiles of the posterior distributions." (Appendix: Gas emission fitting with Discminer)
   - validation: Chains converged before end of sampling; autocorrelation length 106 steps  
  > "The chains reached convergence well before the end of the sampling and the resulting auto-correlation length was $106$ steps." (Appendix: Gas emission fitting with Discminer)
6. **Build moment maps and residuals from data and best-fit model**: Moment 0, peak temperature (moment 8), peak velocity (moment 9) and line-width maps from data and model channels, with residual maps by subtraction  
  > "First, a moment 0 map was computed by integrating the intensity in each pixel along the velocity axis." (3.1 Discminer); "Residual maps were built by subtracting the masked model moment maps to the data moment maps." (3.1 Discminer)
   - software: Discminer
   - parameter: 3-sigma clipping for data moment maps; same mask applied to model maps  
  > "We applied a $3\sigma$ clipping in the building of data moment maps." (3.1 Discminer); "The same mask delimiting the detected disc area was applied to the model moment maps." (3.1 Discminer)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Run SPH simulations of the triple system with a circumbinary disc**: 3D SPH with Phantom, central binary + disc + outer companion on five orbits (ref, e05, i30, i60, ei60)  
  > "we performed 3D hydrodynamical simulations using the Phantom (cite: Price+2018-phantom) smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH, e.g. (cite: Monaghan1992)) code." (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations); "From there we modelled the V892 Tau system as a triple system in five numerical setups, with a different orbit for the outer companion each time" (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - software: Phantom
   - parameter: 1e6 SPH particles, Sigma ~ r^-p with p = 1  
  > "The model used of $10^6$ SPH particles distributed in the disc according to a surface density distribution $\Sigma \propto r^{-p}$ with $p=1$" (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - parameter: Disc from 14 au to 200 au, total mass 0.06 Msun  
  > "that extends initially from $14$ au to $200$ au for a total disc mass of $0.06$ M$_{\odot}$" (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - parameter: Sink accretion radii 1 au (inner binary stars) and 10 au (outer star)  
  > "We modelled the stars as sink particles (cite: Bate+1995) with accretion radii of $1$ au for the two components of the inner binary and of $10$ au for the outer star." (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - parameter: Evolved for 50 P_out with P_out = 4375 yr; a_out = 500 au, omega_out = 180 deg  
  > "We evolved the hydrodynamical simulations for $50$ P$_{out}$, with $P_{out}=4375$ yrs the period of the outer binary" (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations); "we assumed the semi-major axis to be $a_{out}=500$ au" (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - assumption: Disc inclination positive (54.6 deg), SE side nearest; inner binary coplanar and prograde with the disc  
  > "We assumed that inclination to be positive and of $54.6\degree$, meaning the closest side to the observer was the SE side." (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations); "The inner binary was initialised coplanar in a prograde configuration with respect to the disc" (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - assumption: Companion mass 0.5 Msun; companion assumed near apoastron  
  > "We accordingly set the argument of the periapsis to $\omega_{out}=180\degree$." (3.2 Hydrodynamical simulations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Simulations considered near equilibrium because the bulk disc size is stable over the last 20 companion orbits  
  > "our simulations reached a state close to this equilibrium, as the size of the bulk of the disc does not significantly change in the last $20$ orbits of the companion." (5.5 Limitations and caveats)
8. **Fit tilt and PA evolution of the simulated discs and search for a warp**: Damped-oscillation model for tilt and linear-plus-sinusoid model for PA, fitted to the evolution of particle-averaged tilt and PA  
  > "For the inclined setups *i30*, *i60,* and *ei60,* we fitted the inclination of the disc with a damped oscillations model" (4.5 Inclination and orientation of the disc); "The PA is fitted by a linear model on the top of which are sinusoidal oscillations" (4.5 Inclination and orientation of the disc)
   - parameter: Tilt and PA from the average over SPH particles between the inner disc border and 200 au  
  > "averaging the tilt and PA of all the SPH particles tilt and PA having a semi-major axis between the inner border of the disc and $200$ au" (4.5 Inclination and orientation of the disc)
   - parameter: First 15 outer-companion orbits excluded from the fit  
  > "We exclude at least the first $15$ orbits of the outer companion of the fitting process to neglect that effect." (4.5 Inclination and orientation of the disc)

## Qualitative findings

  > "no emission is detected from the vicinity of V892 Tau NE in the $1.3$ mm continuum." (2.2 Dust disc)

  > "we present this given geometry of trailing spirals in the disc as a tentative evidence of CBD-companion interactions." (4.4 Spiral arms and kinematics)

- Warp can neither be confirmed nor ruled out  
  > "the combined dataset used in this work can not robustly rule out nor confirm the presence of a warp in the disc." (4.6 Disc warp)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: New observations at twice higher angular and spectral resolution, combined with archival data  
  > "We present new ALMA observations of the V892 Tau circumbinary disc at a twice higher angular and spectral resolution." (abstract); "here we present new ALMA observations of V892 Tau that we combined with archival data." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Forward modelling of disc morphology and kinematics with hydrodynamics gives orbit constraints independent of astrometry  
  > "it provides constrains independent from astrometric measurements, and it can help to discriminate between available orbits." (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Unbound (inner-binary-only) scenario rejected because a coplanar disc does not precess  
  > "It means that the inner binary alone cannot explain the observed disc tilt, which allows us to rule out the unbound scenario." (4.7 The orbit of V892 Tau NE)
- Alternative: Flyby scenario judged unlikely  
  > "the subtle observed spiral patterns and the disc tilt of $\sim 8\deg$ suggest that a flyby event is unlikely to have happened in V892 Tau." (4.7 The orbit of V892 Tau NE)
- Alternative: Gravitational instability rejected as the origin of the spirals (disc mass too low)  
  > "which is not the case of the V892 Tau CBD where $M_{disc} \sim 0.01 M_*$." (4.4 Spiral arms and kinematics)
- Alternative: Negative-inclination Discminer fit tested but gave an almost identical outer-disc model, so orientation remained ambiguous  
  > "The resulting fitted values were similar to the original model apart from the inclination sign." (Appendix: Inclination sign of the disc)
- How they decided it was enough: Self-cal stopped when RMS stopped decreasing; simulations deemed long enough when bulk disc size was stable over the last 20 orbits  
  > "The calibration were stopped before the RMS stopped decreasing between the rounds." (Appendix: Data combination); "as the size of the bulk of the disc does not significantly change in the last $20$ orbits of the companion." (5.5 Limitations and caveats)
- Limitation (authors): Few orbital configurations tested  
  > "The main limitation of our work is the low number of orbital configurations tested." (5.5 Limitations and caveats)
- Limitation (authors): Gas-only simulations; dust dynamics not consistent  
  > "We used gas only simulations for computational reasons." (5.5 Limitations and caveats)
- Limitation (authors): Inner system not reproduced because of numerical resolution  
  > "Due to a lack of numerical resolution, the inner system is not well reproduced in our simulations." (5.5 Limitations and caveats)
- Limitation (authors): Cloud absorption affects central velocities  
  > "The data suffer from absorption by cloud material at the systemic velocity around $8$ km s$^{-1}$." (2.3 CO emission)
- Limitation (authors): Simulated discs manually rotated back to the observed orientation before post-processing  
  > "We are aware of the discrepancy between the observed orientation of the disc and the orientation at the end of the simulations." (Appendix: Manual rotation of the disc before post-processing)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Orbit constraints rest on a qualitative visual comparison with five simulations, with no likelihood or goodness-of-fit metric.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Initial conditions were fixed (coplanar disc, omega_out = 180 deg, a_out = 500 au, M_out = 0.5 Msun, inner-binary orbital phase) so the constraints on e and Delta i are degenerate with these choices.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Synthetic fluxes are ~3x above the data, and the disc is rotated by hand to the observed orientation, weakening quantitative comparison.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The inclination sign of the disc is ambiguous, which changes the inferred geometry and orbit.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas-only simulations may bias the extent and spirals relative to dust-coupled behaviour.
