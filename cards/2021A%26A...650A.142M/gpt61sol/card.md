(Velocity field in the ionized gas); "Taking the pixels of maximum and minimum velocities, we derive a $V_{\rm LSR}$ gradient oriented at PA = 133$^\circ$ and with an amplitude of $\approx$ 12 km s$^{-1}$ across a distance of $\approx$ 850 au." 3. **Compare ionized and molecular rotation and estimate central mass**: Overlay H30α and molecular PV plots at PA = 133°; compare molecular velocities with Keplerian rotation; estimate central mass from the ionized gradient  
  > "we constructed PV plots along the direction, at PA = 133$^\circ$" (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas); "All the tracers show an increase in the velocities going from large ($\lesssim$ 4000 au) to small ($\gtrsim$ 500 au) radii, which is consistent with Keplerian rotation around a 20 $M_{\odot}$ star." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "we derive a central mass $M_{\rm c} = ((\Delta V)^2 \ \Delta S) \ / \ (8 \ G) \approx$ 17 $M_{\odot}$" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
   - parameter: PA = 133°; average three pixels, 21 mas, perpendicular to the cut  
  > "we constructed PV plots along the direction, at PA = 133$^\circ$" (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas); "Before producing the PV plot, the emission was averaged across three pixels (that is, 21 mas) in the direction perpendicular to the positional cut."    - assumption: Rotation in gravito-centrifugal equilibrium; central mass is a lower limit without an inclination correction  
  > "is due to rotation in gravito-centrifugal equilibrium" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "This value is a lower limit because it does not take the disk inclination with respect to the line of sight into account" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
   - assumption: Distance 6.7 kpc

  > "The physical conditions of the molecular gas can be derived by fitting all the unblended lines of a given molecular species simultaneously." (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas)
   - software: XCLASS
   - parameter: Spectrum averaged within a circle of radius 0.1 arcsec around the Hii-region center  
  > "the spectrum obtained by averaging the $^{13}$CH$_3$CN emission over a circle of 0.1$''$ radius around the center of the Hii region." (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas)
   - assumption: One-dimensional radiative transfer; isothermal homogeneous object in LTE  
  > "This tool models the data by solving the radiative transfer equation in one dimension for an isothermal homogeneous object in local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE)." (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas)
   - fitting: Fit column density, rotation temperature, velocity, line width and source size  
  > "The fitted quantities are column density, rotation temperature, velocity, line width, and source size." (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Measure molecular radial extents**: For each listed molecular transition, measure the mean maximum positive and negative PV offsets at 20% of peak; compare size with excitation and optical depth  
  > "determined the radial extent of the velocity profile taking the mean of the maximum positive and negative offset (from the YSO position) for the emission at 20% of the peak." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "Comparing the velocity profiles of the CH$_3$CN and $^{13}$CH$_3$CN isotopic lines of similar excitation, it is also evident that molecules of lower optical depth trace smaller radii." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
   - parameter: PA = 133°; extent threshold 20% of peak  
  > "we constructed the PV plot along the cut at PA = 133$^\circ$" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "for the emission at 20% of the peak." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
   - assumption: Disk gas temperature decreases with radius  
  > "if, as expected, the gas temperature in the disk decreases with radius" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Relative threshold avoids line-intensity dependence and gives ≳3σ even for the weakest lines  
  > "We used a relative level to ensure that the determined size does not depend on the line intensity" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "chose the minimum contour at 20% to get a signal-to-noise ratio $\gtrsim 3\sigma$, even for the weakest lines." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
6. **Convert molecular column to gas density**: Convert 13CH3CN column density to H2 column using the carbon isotopic ratio and CH3CN abundance; estimate number density using a 1500 au disk diameter  
  > "From the above values of $^{12}$C$/^{13}$C and \[CH$_3$CN/H$_2$\], and the $^{13}$CH$_3$CN column density, we obtain an H$_2$ column density of $\log_{10}\, [ N_{\rm col} \, / \, \textrm{cm$^{-2}$}] \sim 26$." (Physical conditions in the molecular disk)
   - parameter: dgal = 3.6 kpc; 12C/13C ≈35  
  > "the value of $d_{\rm gal} = 3.6$ kpc suitable for the G24.78$+$0.08 region, we derive  $^{12}$C$/^{13}$C $\approx$ 35." (Physical conditions in the molecular disk)
   - parameter: 13CH3CN emission mainly at 500–1000 au; adopted disk diameter 1500 au  
  > "we deduce that the $^{13}$CH$_3$CN emission is mainly found at radii between 500 au and 1000 au." (Physical conditions in the molecular disk); "Taking 1500 au as a crude estimate of the disk diameter" (Physical conditions in the molecular disk)

  > "Using the expression for the isotopic ratio $^{12}$C$/^{13}$C$~\approx 7.5 \times d_{\rm gal} + 7.6$ (cite: Wil94)" (Physical conditions in the molecular disk); "an average value of \[CH$_3$CN/H$_2$\] of $\sim$ 10$^{-9}$ (cite: Pol18)." (Physical conditions in the molecular disk)

## Qualitative findings

- The central perpendicular velocity gradients are interpreted as an ionized disk and jet  
  > "the fast motion along the axis at PA = 39$^\circ$  corresponds to the ionized jet; the slower motion corresponds to the rotation of an ionized disk around the same axis." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- Molecular PV profiles are consistent with Keplerian rotation  
  > "All the tracers show an increase in the velocities going from large ($\lesssim$ 4000 au) to small ($\gtrsim$ 500 au) radii, which is consistent with Keplerian rotation around a 20 $M_{\odot}$ star." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- Higher-excitation and lower-optical-depth molecular lines trace smaller disk radii  
  > "the radial extent of the velocity profile decreases regularly with the excitation energy of the line" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "molecules of lower optical depth trace smaller radii." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- The massive ionizing star is interpreted as still actively accreting  
  > "the massive ionizing star is still actively accreting from its parental molecular core." (Conclusions)
- The compact trapped outflow conflicts with models that already excavate a large cavity at Hii-region formation  
  > "the protostellar outflow emerging from the HC Hii region has not yet expanded on a large scale but remains trapped by the surrounding dense material" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas); "This is not consistent with the simulations by (cite: Tan16) and (cite: Kui18)" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Resolve ionized-gas velocities and study surrounding molecular kinematics simultaneously  
  > "The new ALMA data allow us, for the first time, to map the velocity of the ionized gas, and, at the same time, study the kinematics of the surrounding molecular gas." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Robust 0.5 balances resolution and extended-emission sensitivity; 13CH3CN balances signal-to-noise and optical depth  
  > "with the robust parameter of (cite: Bri95) set to 0.5 as a compromise between resolution and sensitivity to extended emission." (ALMA observations); "we used XCLASS to fit the $^{13}$CH$_3$CN J$_K$ = 13$_K$–12$_K$, K = 0–5, lines, which offer a good compromise between high signal-to-noise ratio and low-to-moderate optical depth." (Kinematics and physical conditions of the neutral gas)
- Alternative: A slower, less-collimated disk wind may explain the wide-angle ionized expansion  
  > "the slower and less collimated wind also naturally accounts for the slow, wide-angle expansion of the ionized gas observed at larger scales" (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- Alternative: Large accretion fluctuations may prevent a persistent outflow cavity  
  > "Then, because of the intermittent mass-loss rate, the outflow could never last long enough to excavate a large cavity." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- Alternative: A star at the near edge of the core could explain the asymmetric outflow obstruction  
  > "if the star is found at the near edge of the molecular core and the NE red-shifted lobe is blowing away from us toward the inner and denser region of the core." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- Alternative: Envelope accretion could raise the final stellar mass above the core-only bound  
  > "However, the final stellar mass could be larger than this value if the star gains its mass from the molecular envelope enshrouding A1." (Physical conditions in the molecular disk)
- How they decided it was enough: Use the 20%-of-peak contour to retain ≳3σ even for the weakest molecular lines  
  > "chose the minimum contour at 20% to get a signal-to-noise ratio $\gtrsim 3\sigma$, even for the weakest lines." (Kinematics of ionized and molecular gas)
- Limitation (authors): Continuum noise is limited by dynamic range  
  > "The continuum image of G24.78$+$0.08 has a 1$\sigma$ rms noise level of 0.2 mJy beam$^{-1}$, which is limited by the dynamic range." (ALMA observations)
- Limitation (authors): The higher-resolution ionized velocity map is less sensitive and covers only the central region
