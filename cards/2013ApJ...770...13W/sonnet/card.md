## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure 1.2 mm continuum**: Combine three line-free spectral windows into continuum images and measure flux density at quasar position  
  > "The ALMA 1.2 mm continuum images generated from the three spectral windows that did not include the \[C ii\] line were analyzed to determine their flux-densities." (3.1 Far-infrared luminosity)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive L_FIR and SFR**: Convert continuum flux to L_FIR (42.5-122.5 micron) with a greybody, then SFR via Kennicutt relation  
  > "This continuum flux-density was converted to a far-infrared luminosity assuming a typical SED for high-redshift star-forming galaxies." (3.1 Far-infrared luminosity)
   - parameter: Td=47 K, beta=1.6  
  > "we adopt a greybody spectrum with dust temperature, $T_{\rm d} =47$ K and emissivity index, $\beta=1.6$." (3.1 Far-infrared luminosity)
   - assumption: No contribution from quasar-heated dust; Salpeter IMF; Td 33 K would give 3x lower L_FIR  
  > "the assumption in deriving SFR is that there is no contribution to $L_{\rm FIR}$ from quasar-heated dust." (3.1 Far-infrared luminosity)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure [C II] line properties**: Gaussian plus constant fit to spectrum; integrate line channels after continuum subtraction; 3 sigma upper limit for J2329 assuming unresolved FWHM=300 km/s  
  > "A simple Gaussian plus constant fit was made to the observed spectrum." (3.2 [C ii] luminosity)
   - parameter: 15 channels of 15.625 MHz integrated  
  > "integrated over the 15 channels (each of width 15.625 MHz) that show line emission" (3.2 [C ii] luminosity)
   - fitting: Gaussian plus constant  
  > "A simple Gaussian plus constant fit was made to the observed spectrum." (3.2 [C ii] luminosity)
   - validation: Fitted constant consistent with 120 uJy continuum from line-free basebands  
  > "consistent with the continuum flux-density of $120\,\mu$Jy measured from the three line-free basebands" (3.2 [C ii] luminosity)
4. **Size and kinematics**: IMFIT deconvolved source fit; separate blue and red wing maps (5 channels each); Gaussian-fitted velocity-centroid map  
  > "We used the CASA IMFIT task to fit a deconvolved model image to the data." (3.3 [C ii] dynamics and spatial extent)
   - software: CASA IMFIT
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Compare black hole mass to dynamical mass**: Convert FWHM to sigma and use local M_BH-sigma relation of Gultekin et al. 2009 to predict M_BH  
  > "Based on the local relationship (Gultekin et al. 2009) a galaxy with $\sigma=80$ km s$^{-1}$ would be expected to have $M_{\rm BH} \approx 3 \times 10^6 \,{\rm
  M}_\odot$" (3.3 [C ii] dynamics and spatial extent)
   - assumption: Gaussian, no inclination correction  
  > "equivalent to $\sigma=80 \pm 8$ km s$^{-1}$ for a Gaussian and ignoring any inclination correction" (3.3 [C ii] dynamics and spatial extent)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- J0210-0456 and J2329-0301 have L_FIR well below the stacked L_FIR-L_Bol relation, J2329-0301 by at least a factor of 10  
  > "at least a factor of $10$ lower than the stacked average from the full CFHQS sample (Omont et al. 2013)" (3.1 Far-infrared luminosity)
