## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image CO cubes**: Calibrate with appropriate CASA version, UVCONTSUB, CLEAN with Briggs weighting  
  > "Continuum-subtracted data cubes were generated using the CASA tasks UVCONTSUB and CLEAN." (2 Data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 2 or 0  
  > "Otherwise, Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0 was used" (2 Data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: rms compared to theoretical rms  
  > "The rms in each final datacube was compared with and found to be close to the corresponding theoretical rms" (2 Data reduction)
2. **Map moments and kinematics**: Extract spectra in beam-sized regions per pixel; fit 1-3 Gaussians with MPFIT; 3 sigma detection threshold  
  > "Each extracted spectrum was fitted with a model consisting of one, two and then three Gaussian components using MPFIT." (3 Results)
   - software: MPFIT
   - parameter: >=3 sigma significance  
  > "At least $3\sigma$ significance was required for the detection of an emission line in each region analysed." (3 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: False detections assessed by Monte Carlo with null model  
  > "The significance was assessed by evaluating the number of false detections in Monte Carlo simulations based on a null hypothesis model with no emission line and the observed rms." (3 Results)
3. **CO(3-2)/CO(1-0) line ratios**: Convolve to common beam, extract spectra from same regions, fit single Gaussian  
  > "The integrated intensities (in $\hbox{$\rm\; K\rm\; km\rm\; s^{-1}\,$}$) for key molecular structures were then determined by extracting spectra from the same spatial regions" (3.3 Line ratios)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Molecular gas masses**: Convert CO(1-0) integrated intensity with X_CO  
  > "The molecular gas mass can be estimated from the integrated CO intensity by assuming a CO-to-H$_2$ ($X_{\mathrm{CO}}$) conversion factor" (3.4 Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: Galactic X_CO = 2e20; CO(2-1)/CO(1-0)=0.8, CO(3-2)/CO(1-0)=0.8; Phoenix X_CO = 0.4e20 (ULIRG)  
  > "we therefore assume $X_{\mathrm{CO}}=0.4\times10^{20}\hbox{$\hbox{$\rm\; cm^{-2}\,$}(\rm\; K\hbox{$\rm\; km\rm\; s^{-1}\,$})^{-1}$}$ as appropriate for a ULIRG" (3.4 Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: H0=70, Om=0.3, OL=0.7  
  > "We assume $H_0=70\hbox{$\hbox{$\rm\; km\rm\; s^{-1}\,$}\rm\; Mpc^{-1}\,$}$, $\Omega_m=0.3$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.7$." (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Correlate circumnuclear gas mass with cavity power**: BCES (Y|X) powerlaw fit, Spearman rank, bootstrap with perturbation  
  > "Using the BCES (Y$\vert$X) estimator from (cite: Akritas96), we determine the best-fit powerlaw model" (4.3 AGN fuelling)
   - software: BCES
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Bootstrap shows no significant correlation  
  > "we do not find a significant correlation between the circumnuclear molecular gas mass and the AGN jet power as measured by the X-ray cavities" (4.3 AGN fuelling)

## Qualitative findings
