## Qualitative findings

- No evidence for significant effects of an Active Galactic Nucleus (AGN) within SPT0027-50 based on HCN, HNC, and HCO+ line ratios being consistent with 1.  
  > "The HCN, HNC and HCO$^{+}$ line ratios are in agreement with 1, given the large uncertainties on individual values and the compounding effect of errors within ratios. As such, I find no evidence for significant effects of an Active Galactic Nucleus within the SPT0027-50 system" (ISM conditions from the Band 2 window)
- Band 4 is more efficient for most redshift searches at z < 3, whereas Band 2 becomes significantly more efficient at z > 3 and for large-volume blind surveys due to contiguous spectral window coverage.  
  > "Regardless, this still implies that Band 4 is the better choice for most redshift searches in the $z < 3$ Universe, especially considering Band 4 probes a brighter region of the dust continuum" (Prospects of redshift surveys in the Band 2 and WSU era); "I find that Band 2 becomes significantly more efficient at $z > 3$, where the ability to cover a continuous frequency range without the “ladder”-like tuning overlaps currently required in Bands 3 and 4 outweighs the lower line fluxes." (Prospects of redshift surveys in the Band 2 and WSU era)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: SPT0027-50 was selected as a Band 2 Science Verification target because it is one of the brightest z > 3 sources in the SPT sample and has bright spectral lines (including CO(3-2) and dense gas tracers HCN, HNC, HCO+) in the Band 2 frequency range.  
  > "SPT0027-50 was selected as a verification target because it is one of the brightest $z > 3$ sources in the SPT sample. It has several bright spectral lines, including CO(3–2), at frequencies that before Band 2 were not available to ALMA" (Observations, Data Reduction, and Fluxes)
- Why this method: Used a singly-defined aperture based on the smoothed Band 2 continuum image widened by up to five beams to ensure extended flux is included and to allow direct comparison of line ratios.  
  > "Using the average beam of the data cube, the aperture is widened by up to five beams if the signal-to-noise ratio permitted to ensure extended flux is also included in the flux measurement." (Observations, Data Reduction, and Fluxes); "where I note that the Band 2 data allows us to compare these values directly through a singly-defined aperture." (ISM conditions from the Band 2 window)
- Alternative: Lower CO line luminosity ratios (r_4,1) typically assumed for DSFGs would increase the derived dense gas fraction by a factor of two.  
  > "The subsequent ratio of dense to total molecular gas is thus $17 \pm 9$ per cent, although this value can two-fold increase for lower values of CO line luminosity ratios typically assumed for DSFGs (cite: Harrington2021)." (ISM conditions from the Band 2 window)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): HCN(4-3), HCO+(4-3), 13CO(4-3), and C18O(4-3) were not detected in the Band 2 data.  
  > "The HCN(4–3) and HCO$^+$(4–3) dense gas tracers, as well as the CO isotopologues $^{13}$CO(4-3), and C$^{18}$O(4-3), proved undetected in the Band 2 data." (Observations, Data Reduction, and Fluxes)
- Limitation (authors): Large uncertainties on individual dense gas line fluxes and compounding errors in their ratios limit constraints on HCN, HNC, and HCO+ line ratios.  
  > "The HCN, HNC and HCO$^{+}$ line ratios are in agreement with 1, given the large uncertainties on individual values and the compounding effect of errors within ratios." (ISM conditions from the Band 2 window)
- Limitation (authors): Determining whether the clumps are rotationally supported fragments of a turbulent disc or the product of a recent merger requires future high-resolution CO and [C II] observations.  
  > "Future high-resolution observations targeting CO and [C ii] emission will be essential to determine whether these clumps are rotationally supported fragments of a turbulent disc or the product of a recent merger event." (A clumpy galaxy with Band 2 observations)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Differential lensing is not modelled when comparing extended CO/dust emission to compact dense-gas tracers or clump fluxes.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The Band 2 CO(4-3) flux (21.05 +/- 1.57 Jy km/s) is noticeably higher than the archival Band 3 2015.1.00504.S value (12.84 +/- 1.11 Jy km/s) listed in Table 1, despite the text stating all line fluxes are within one combined standard deviation.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The dense gas fraction relies on ad-hoc conversion factors (alpha_HCN = 10, alpha_CO = 1) and assumed excitation ratios (r_5,1 = 0.25, r_4,1 = 1) that carry significant systematic uncertainty.
