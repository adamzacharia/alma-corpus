## Analysis recipe

1. **Compare existing source selections**: Restrict SCUBA-2 samples to the DUALZ area; cross-match direct and red-prior-selected sources to DUALZ; inspect multiplicity and flux ratios  
  > "Restricting to the DUALZ ALMA area, we first compare the 17 $>5\sigma$ SCUBA-2 sources with the DUALZ catalog." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources); "We cross-match our previously reported direct ($>5\sigma$) SCUBA-2 sample and red JWST NIRCam prior-selected ($>3\sigma$) SCUBA-2 sample to direct ALMA sources from the DUALZ survey." (Abstract); "With the ALMA data, we can check whether multiple DSFGs may be contributing to the 850 $\mu$m flux." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - parameter: Red NIRCam selection: fF444W/fF150W > 3.5; fF444W > 1 μJy; counterparts within 4″  
  > "(cite: barger23) found red NIRCam counterparts (defined as $f_{\rm F444W}$/$f_{\rm F150W}$ $>$ 3.5 and $f_{\rm F444W}$$> 1$ $\mu$Jy) within 4$''$ of 15 of these SCUBA-2 sources" (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - parameter: Multiplicity search within 4.5″ of SCUBA-2 850 μm positions  
  > "multiple ALMA counterparts within $4.5\ensuremath{''}$ of the SCUBA-2 850 $\mu$m position" (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - assumption: Flat ΛCDM: ΩM = 0.3, ΩΛ = 0.7, H0 = 70.0 km s−1 Mpc−1  
  > "We assume a flat concordance $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $\Omega_M= 0.3$, $\Omega_\Lambda = 0.7$, and $H_0 = 70.0$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$." (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: ALMA confirms all but one previously assigned red counterpart; the remaining detection identifies a different similarly red galaxy  
  > "The ALMA data confirm all but one of these; for the remaining source, the ALMA detection matches to a different but similarly red galaxy at a slightly larger separation from the SCUBA-2 centroid." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
2. **Recover faint ALMA counterparts**: For eight prior-selected SCUBA-2 sources absent from the DUALZ catalog, measure the largest local ALMA peak within 1.0″ of each red NIRCam prior  
  > "we measure the ALMA 1.2 mm fluxes at the positions of these eight SCUBA-2 $>3\sigma$ sources by finding the largest local peak within $1.0\ensuremath{''}$ of the red NIRCam prior" (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - parameter: Natural weighted DUALZ Wide map with primary beam correction  
  > "using the natural weighted DUALZ *Wide* map with primary beam correction applied." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - parameter: Local noise: random-pixel dispersion within 12″, masking nearby >4σ sources  
  > "The local noise is estimated by measuring the dispersion in random pixels within 12$''$ of the source, after masking out any $>4\sigma$ sources nearby." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - parameter: Report >2.5σ and >3σ recoveries  
  > "We find that of these eight sources, six are detected at $>2.5\sigma$ (three at $>3\sigma$)." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: One >3σ source lies near a noisy map edge; its estimated error may be too small  
  > "one of the $>3\sigma$ sources falls near the edge where the noise is higher, so the error measured by our method may be an underestimate of the actual uncertainty for this source." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
3. **Measure SCUBA-2 fluxes at ALMA positions**: Measure initial SCUBA-2 fluxes at ALMA positions; work from bright to faint, measure the local peak and error within 4″, and subtract a peak-scaled SCUBA-2 PSF from the residual image  
  > "We first measure the SCUBA-2 flux at the position of each ALMA source;" (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources); "then, working down the catalog from brighter to fainter SCUBA-2 flux, we measure the peak flux and the error within 4$''$ from the ALMA position" (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources); "and subtract from the residual image the SCUBA-2 PSF scaled to the peak flux." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
   - parameter: Iterate until every source is measured; apply to both 450 μm and 850 μm maps  
  > "We then repeat for the next brightest SCUBA-2 flux until all sources have been measured." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources); "Although this process is not strictly necessary for the 450 $\mu$m maps due to the higher angular resolution, we perform the iteration on both SCUBA-2 maps anyway." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
   - assumption: Reported fluxes are not corrected for magnification  
  > "Fluxes have not been corrected for magnification." (Table 2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Recovers all previously matched direct >5σ SCUBA-2 sources and all but three previously matched red-prior >3σ sources  
  > "All of the direct $>5\sigma$ SCUBA-2 sources previously matched to DUALZ sources are recovered by this method" (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources); "as are all but three of the $>3\sigma$ SCUBA-2 sources based on the red galaxy priors that were previously matched to the DUALZ sources." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
4. **Count detections and estimate false positives**: Apply >2.5σ SCUBA-2 thresholds; include three previously identified 850 μm counterparts missed by ALMA-prior extraction when forming final totals  
  > "Combining the 43 ALMA sources detected at $>2.5\sigma$ in the 850 $\mu$m map with the three that were previously identified as SCUBA-2 counterparts but were not selected by this method" (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources); "If we combine the 46 sources detected at 850 $\mu$m with these five sources, then 51/69 (74%) of the ALMA sources have SCUBA-2 $>2.5\sigma$ detections in at least one band." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
   - parameter: 850 μm false-positive test: peak search within 4″ of 5000 random positions  
  > "By searching for peaks within 4$''$ of 5000 random positions in the SCUBA-2 map, we estimate that the false positive rate for this method is $\sim$8% at 2.5$\sigma$" (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
   - parameter: 450 μm false-positive test: fluxes and errors at random positions  
  > "By measuring the fluxes and errors at random positions, we estimate that the false positive rate is 3%." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Expected false positives: at most four 850 μm detections and at most one of 24 detections at 450 μm  
  > "we expect at most four of our $>2.5\sigma$ detections could be false positives." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources); "Thus, we expect that at most one of our 24 detections could be a false positive." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
5. **Assess flux-dependent SCUBA-2 incompleteness**: Compare ALMA fluxes with directly measured SCUBA-2 fluxes for the 59 DUALZ sources with ALMA S/N > 5  
  > "comparing the ALMA fluxes to the SCUBA-2 850 $\mu$m and 450 $\mu$m fluxes directly measured at each ALMA position, limiting the sample to the 59 DUALZ sources with ALMA S/N $>$ 5." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - parameter: SCUBA-2 detections at >2.5σ; investigate the approximately 0.6 mJy ALMA flux boundary  
  > "down to a 1.2 mm flux limit of $\sim$0.6 mJy (shaded region), we detect all the $>5\sigma$ ALMA sources in at least one SCUBA-2 band at $>2.5\sigma$" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Observed flux correlation places faint ALMA sources below the SCUBA-2 850 μm detection limit  
  > "The strong correlation between 1.2 mm and 850 $\mu$m flux implies that fainter ALMA sources will start to fall below the SCUBA-2 850 $\mu$m 2.5$\sigma$ detection limit ($\sim$0.8 mJy in the current images)." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
6. **Compare flux ratios and detection fractions with redshift**: Use published redshifts for >5σ DUALZ sources; compare flux ratios with modified blackbody tracks, fit lines and compare SCUBA-2 detection fractions in redshift bins  
  > "we show the 1.2 mm to 850 $\mu$m flux ratios (left) and 1.2 mm to 450 $\mu$m flux ratios (right) versus redshift for the $>5\sigma$ DUALZ sources with redshifts" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "simple modified blackbody models of the form $f_\nu \propto \nu^\beta B(\nu, T_d)$ with varying emissivity indices, $\beta$, and dust temperatures, $T_d$" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "the SCUBA-2-detected fraction for the $>5\sigma$ ALMA sources with $1<z<3$ is 81%, while for $3<z<5$ it is 60%." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - parameter: Use 2σ lower limits when SCUBA-2 flux significance is <2.5σ  
  > "We show 2$\sigma$ lower limits (upward-pointing arrows) in cases where the SCUBA-2 fluxes have less than $2.5\sigma$ significance." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - parameter: Exclude two >5σ ALMA sources without spectroscopic or photometric redshifts  
  > "two $>5\sigma$ ALMA sources have neither speczs nor photzs and are not shown" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - assumption: Modified blackbody form fν ∝ ν^β B(ν, Td)  
  > "simple modified blackbody models of the form $f_\nu \propto \nu^\beta B(\nu, T_d)$" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - fitting: Best-fit lines reported; sampler, priors, likelihood and weighting not stated  
  > "The best-fit line is $\log(f_{1.2 {\rm mm}}/f_{450 \mu{\rm m}}) = 0.16z-1.50$, with some scatter." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "The 1.2 mm to 850 $\mu$m flux ratio has a slightly shallower best-fit line of $\log(f_{1.2 {\rm mm}}/f_{450 \mu{\rm m}}) = 0.10z-0.70$." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - validation: 1.2 mm/450 μm ratios align with model tracks; 1.2 mm/850 μm ratios show more scatter  
  > "we see good alignment between the measured 1.2 mm to 450 $\mu$m flux ratios and the models" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "the measured 1.2 mm to 850 $\mu$m flux ratios show a larger degree of scatter." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
7. **Check the high-redshift candidate against SCUBA-2 limits**: Assess source #66 non-detections using a simple modified blackbody fit to its ALMA point at z approximately 10  
  > "We find that this source is not detected in either the 450 $\mu$m or 850 $\mu$m data." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "if the source is real and located at $z\sim10$" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "a simple modifed blackbody fit to the ALMA point would suggest the 850 $\mu$m flux should be around 0.20 mJy" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - assumption: Source is real and at z approximately 10; prediction depends on assumed Td and β  
  > "if the source is real and located at $z\sim10$" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections); "with some dependence on the assumed $T_d$ and $\beta$" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - fitting: Simple modified blackbody fit to the ALMA point; numerical Td and β not stated  
  > "a simple modifed blackbody fit to the ALMA point" (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
   - validation: Predicted 850 μm flux lies just below the noise level  
  > "just below the noise level." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)

  > "Thus, we concluded that the lack of detections in SCUBA-2 is primarily an issue of depth." (Summary)

  > "We find that this source is not detected in either the 450 $\mu$m or 850 $\mu$m data." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: DUALZ expands the ALMA comparison sample; SCUBA-2 provides wider coverage and wavelengths closer to the FIR dust peak  
  > "In this paper, we revisit the overlap between the SCUBA-2 and ALMA samples in A2744, incorporating the new DUALZ data." (Introduction); "In addition to SCUBA-2 covering much larger areas than ALMA, its extended wavelength coverage is important for studying the physical properties of DSFGs" (Introduction); "450 $\mu$m and $850~\mu$m lie closer to the far-infrared (FIR) peak of the dust emission and thus can help constrain dust temperatures and redshifts" (Introduction)
- Why this method: Use priors to reach fainter fluxes and iterative PSF subtraction to reduce contamination from brighter sources  
  > "This figure illustrates that the red NIRCam priors enable us to probe to fainter ALMA fluxes than would be possible with a direct search." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources); "using an iterative procedure to avoid contamination of fainter sources by brighter ones" (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
- Alternative: Compare direct >5σ SCUBA-2 selection with red-prior >3σ selection  
  > "We cross-match our previously reported direct ($>5\sigma$) SCUBA-2 sample and red JWST NIRCam prior-selected ($>3\sigma$) SCUBA-2 sample to direct ALMA sources from the DUALZ survey." (Abstract)
- Alternative: Apply iterative deblending to 450 μm data even though its higher resolution makes the procedure less necessary  
  > "Although this process is not strictly necessary for the 450 $\mu$m maps due to the higher angular resolution, we perform the iteration on both SCUBA-2 maps anyway." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
- Alternative: Cannot constrain the 450 μm/850 μm ratio–redshift relation with this sample  
  > "there are not enough sources with both 450 $\mu$m and 850 $\mu$m detections to constrain this relationship for the DUALZ sample." (Properties of ALMA Sources Detected in SCUBA-2 versus Non-detections)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): A faint ALMA source near the map edge may have an underestimated uncertainty  
  > "one of the $>3\sigma$ sources falls near the edge where the noise is higher, so the error measured by our method may be an underestimate of the actual uncertainty for this source." (ALMA properties of SCUBA-2 sources)
- Limitation (authors): Different ALMA and JWST prior samples change SCUBA-2 deblending and detection significance  
  > "the prior sample is different (ALMA versus JWST) and thus our extraction is deblending the sources differently." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
- Limitation (authors): The 46/69 850 μm detection fraction is an upper limit  
  > "we estimate an upper limit on the fraction of ALMA sources detected at 850 $\mu$m of 46/69 (67%)." (SCUBA-2 450 and 850 micron properties of ALMA sources)
- Limitation (authors): Red NIRCam prior selection can miss faint submillimeter/millimeter sources with less red counterparts  
  > "does introduce a potential selection bias against faint submillimeter/millimeter sources with less red NIR counterparts" (Using Red JWST Priors to Identify Counterparts to Faint SCUBA-2 Galaxies)
