## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and line cubes**: clean with Briggs weighting; uvcontsub in the uv plane; continuum from continuum SPWs plus line-free channels  
  > "The continuum image was compiled by concatenating the dedicated continuum spectral windows and line free channels of the other two spectral windows." (3 Observations)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5  
  > "Briggs weighting using a robustness parameter of 0.5" (3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum flux densities and ring structure**: Point-source fit with uvmodelfit for HD 98363 and HD 121191; Gaussian ring fit with uvmultifit for HD 121617 and HD 131488; upper limits from random-position apertures of 100 au radius  
  > "Continuum flux densities of HD 98363 and HD 121191 were determined by fitting a point source to their visibility data using the CASA `uvmodelfit` task." (4 Results); "We determined upper limits for the non-detected sources by measuring the flux at random positions in the images in an aperture radius corresponding to 100 au." (4 Results)
   - software: CASA uvmodelfit, uvmultifit
   - fitting: Gaussian ring model fit to visibilities (sampler/priors not stated)  
  > "To derive the integrated flux density of the bright extended disks around HD 121617 and HD 131488 we used the `uvmultifit` task (cite: marti-vidal2014) to fit a Gaussian ring model to the visibilities." (Results)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive dust masses**: Optically thin M_d = F d^2/(B_nu(T_dust) kappa)  
  > "Assuming optically thin emission, the measured flux densities were converted to dust masses using" (4 Results)
   - assumption: kappa = 2.3 cm2/g at 1.3 mm (scaled with nu^0.7); single SED-based T_dust  
  > "adopting an opacity value of $\kappa_{\nu} = 2.3\left( \frac{\nu}{{\rm 230\,GHz}} \right)^{0.7}$ cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$." (4 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Formal uncertainties 11-30% acknowledged, opacity uncertain by factor of several  
  > "the formal uncertainty of our dust mass estimates are between 11 and 30%." (4 Results)
4. **Measure CO line fluxes and spectra**: Zeroth moment maps over velocity ranges with emission; aperture containing all CO flux; upper limits with ±5 km/s and 100 au aperture  
  > "For the non-detections, we used a velocity range of $\pm$5 km s$^{-1}$ of the stellar radial velocity and an aperture radius corresponding to 100 au to estimate 3$\sigma$ CO flux upper limits." (4 Results)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Estimate CO optical depths from isotopologue line ratios**: Ratio of integrated brightness temperatures (1-e^-tau)/(1-e^-tau') using ISM isotope ratios  
  > "Assuming identical excitation temperatures, emitting regions, and line widths for all isotopologues" (4 Results)
   - assumption: [12C]/[13C]=77, [16O]/[18O]=560; identical Tex, emitting regions and line widths  
  > "\[$^{12}$C\]/\[$^{13}$C\]=77 and \[$^{16}$O\]/\[$^{18}$O\]=560 (cite: wilson1994)" (4 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Discussed possible isotope-selective effects (C18O underabundant up to factor 10)  
  > "the C$^{18}$O molecules are expected to be underabundant by up to a factor of 10 (cite: miotello2014)" (4 Results)
6. **Derive CO gas masses**: M_CO from optically thin isotopologue line with LTE level population, scaled by isotope ratio; lower limits from 12CO for literature targets  
  > "By assuming local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) and adopting 20 K for the gas temperature, we determined $x_2$ from the Boltzmann equation." (4 Results)
   - parameter: Tgas = 20 K (32 K for 49 Cet)  
  > "we adopted 32 K for 49 Cet (cite: hughes2017), and 20 K for the other sources" (4 Results)
   - assumption: LTE, single gas temperature, local ISM isotope ratios  
  > "By assuming local thermodynamical equilibrium (LTE) and adopting 20 K for the gas temperature" (4 Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Mass at most 1.5x higher for Tgas 8-48 K; non-LTE could raise masses  
  > "Between gas temperatures of 8 and 48 K, the mass estimate is at most 1.5$\times$ higher the one derived for 20 K." (4 Results)
7. **Compute CO detection rates and compare A- vs FG-type stars**: Detectability threshold from highest upper limit; binomial uncertainties; Fisher's exact test  
  > "By applying a Fisher’s exact test to compare the occurrence of CO-bearing disks in the two samples, we obtained a $p$ value of 6.4$\times$10$^{-4}$" (6 Molecular gas in bright debris disks)
   - assumption: HR 4796 and HD 181327 excluded due to sensitivity  
  > "we discard this object from the following analysis, reducing the size of our statistical sample to 16." (6 Molecular gas in bright debris disks)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CO gas is likely the rule rather than the exception in bright debris disks around young A-type stars  
  > "This high incidence implies that the presence of CO gas in bright debris disks around young A-type stars is likely more the rule than the exception." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Targets chosen to complete a volume-limited sample of dust-rich debris disks around young A-type stars within 150 pc  
  > "which were selected to complete a volume limited sample of dust-rich debris disks around young A-type stars within 150 pc." (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: All three CO isotopologues targeted because optically thick 12CO alone gives unreliable masses  
  > "We targeted all three main CO isotopologues, since $^{12}$CO alone does not provide reliable gas mass estimates if optically thick." (1 Introduction)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Gas masses may be underestimated due to single temperature, non-LTE and isotope ratio effects; dust opacity uncertain by factor of several  
  > "The combination of all these effects may lead to somewhat higher values than our mass estimates" (4 Results)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas mass relies on ISM isotope ratios and a single assumed 20 K temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample; detection rate depends on a threshold set by one source
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Continuum position offset in HD 121191 may be a background source
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ring radii from Gaussian model fits without a described model degeneracy check
