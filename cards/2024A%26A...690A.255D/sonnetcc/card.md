## Analysis recipe

1. **select major-merger targets**: Apply criteria to the 23 Romano+2021 mergers: delta v <= 500 km/s, r_p > 4 kpc, 1 < mu_K < 4, yielding six systems  
  > "a velocity separation $\Delta v \leq 500 \: \rm km/s$, so that" (2.1 Targets selection); "By applying these criteria to the 23 merging systems selected by (cite: Romano+2021), we end up with a sample of six targets." (2.1 Targets selection)
   - parameter: r_p > 4 kpc  
  > "a projected distance $r_{\rm p} > 4 \:$kpc" (2.1 Targets selection)
   - parameter: 1 < mu_K < 4  
  > "a relative stellar mass ratio of $\rm 1 < \mu _{\rm K} < 4$" (2.1 Targets selection)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **reduce and image the [CII] cubes**: CASA pipeline reduction, uvcontsub, tclean with natural weighting  
  > "Firstly, we reduce the ALMA data for these targets using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (CASA; (cite: McMullin+2007)) pipeline." (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - software: CASA, uvcontsub, tclean
   - parameter: pixel 0.15", channel 25 km/s, natural weighting  
  > "We choose a natural weighting of the visibilities to maximize the sensitivity" (2.2 Observational analysis); "a common pixel size of 0.15"" (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Clean until residual S/N < 2  
  > "using the task `tclean` until we reach a S/N \< 2 for the residuals" (2.2 Observational analysis)
3. **extract preliminary spectra and build moment-0 maps**: 1 arcsec aperture spectrum per component fitted with a 1D Gaussian; 2-sigma frequency range collapsed with immoments; system map uses min/max across components  
  > "we adopt a $1"$ aperture centered on the emitting galaxy to extract a preliminary \[CII\] spectrum, which is then fitted using a single 1D Gaussian model." (2.2 Observational analysis); "Finally, we consider the $2\sigma$ confidence interval of the Gaussian fit to get the min-max frequency range that we collapse" (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - software: CASA immoments
   - fitting: Single 1D Gaussian  
  > "which is then fitted using a single 1D Gaussian model." (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **fit morphology and extract galaxy fluxes in three apertures**: 2D Gaussian fit to each component's moment-0 map; fluxes extracted in 1", 2x2 pixel peak and FWHM-based apertures  
  > "Having the moment-0 maps we then fit a single 2D Gaussian model to each merging component" (2.2 Observational analysis); "we employ a $1"$ aperture, a 2x2 pixel square aperture" (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - parameter: DC_873321 needs a two-component 2D Gaussian fit  
  > "For one system, DC_873321, we had to perform a two 2D Gaussian components fit" (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - assumption: All emission in apertures belongs to galaxies; emission in the 2-sigma contour is total (galaxies + diffuse)  
  > "In this analysis, we assume that all the \[CII\] emission coming from the apertures is associated with the merging galaxies" (2.2 Observational analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **derive diffuse [CII] luminosity and fraction**: Total flux from the 2-sigma region (single Gaussian), converted to L_[CII]; L_diff = L_2sigma - L_aperture; f_[CII] = L_diff / L_2sigma; FWHM-based results corrected by 0.58 for PSF tails  
  > "we extract the emission from the 2$\sigma$ region and fit it using a single Gaussian model." (3 [CII] emission from the CGM); "L^{\rm diff,i} \equiv L^{2\sigma} - L^{i}" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
   - parameter: L_[CII] = 1.04e-3 F D_L^2 nu_obs (L_sun, Mpc, GHz)  
  > "{\rm L_{[CII]}} = 1.04 \times 10^{-3} \; {\rm F_{[CII]}} \; D_{L}^2(z) \; \nu _{obs}(z)" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
   - parameter: Luminosity error = sqrt(N) x rms of moment-0 map, N = beams in region  
  > "we estimate the error associated with this luminosity as $\rm \sqrt N \times rms$ of the moment-0 map" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
   - assumption: Flat LCDM with Planck 2016 parameters  
  > "Throughout the paper, we assume a flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with cosmological parameters from (cite: PLANK+2016)" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: Single Gaussian for the total spectrum  
  > "we extract the emission from the 2$\sigma$ region and fit it using a single Gaussian model." (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
   - validation: Artificial point-source maps with injected ALMA beams and measured noise give a correction factor 0.58 for PSF-tail contamination  
  > "we simulate artificial velocity-integrated maps of point-like sources by injecting the ALMA beams and the measured noise of the ALPINE observations" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM); "our FWHM-based results need to be corrected by a factor of 0.58" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
6. **test trends of diffuse emission with system properties**: Spearman coefficients and linear fits of L_diff and f_[CII] vs L_gal, SFR, M*, r_p, mu_star, mu_[CII]  
  > "we compute the Spearman coefficient, $\rho$, to see how reliable the suggested trend is" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
   - parameter: Conclusions based primarily on FWHM-based aperture  
  > "we primarily base our conclusions on the more appropriate FWHM-based method" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **select synthetic merger analogs in dustyGadget**: M* >= 1e10 Msun galaxies at z~4.5 traced back in merger trees to z~5.1; major merger when ancestor stellar mass ratio 1 < mu_star < 4; 8 unique candidates  
  > "we assume that two ancestors undergo a major merger when their relative stellar mass ratio (defined as the ratio between the most massive and the least massive) is $1 < \mu_\star < 4$." (4.2 Identifying galaxy mergers); "we end up with 8 unique candidates (7 at $z \simeq 4.5$ and one at $z \simeq 5.0$)" (4.2 Identifying galaxy mergers)
   - software: dustyGadget, AHF
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison of synthetic and observed M*, SFR, mass ratios  
  > "the simulated systems appear to span a similar range of properties and can be considered as good synthetic analogs of the systems" (4.2 Identifying galaxy mergers)
8. **compute diffuse carbon fraction and compare with observations**: Cold carbon mass (T < 5e4 K) projected along x, y, z, convolved with ALMA PSF, 2D Gaussian fit, standard FWHM-based aperture; M_diff = M_total - M_aperture; f_C = M_diff / M_total; Spearman correlations and linear fits  
  > "we convolve the image with the ALMA PSF and fit a 2D Gaussian model to it" (4.5 Statistical properties); "M^{\rm diff, i} \equiv M^{\rm total} - M^i" (4.5 Statistical properties)
   - software: dustyGadget
   - parameter: Cold-phase carbon: T < 5e4 K  
  > "we refer to the M$_C$ in the cold phase, $\rm T < 5 \times 10^4$ K" (4.5 Statistical properties)
   - assumption: Cold-phase carbon mass is a proxy for [CII] luminosity; no radiative transfer  
  > "Thus, we do not employ any radiative transfer code to predict the \[CII\] luminosity" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: Linear fit on projection-averaged values; H2 and H6 excluded from fits  
  > "For these reasons, they are considered as not belonging to the fiducial simulated sample and have not been included in the fit." (4.5 Statistical properties)
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALPINE mergers classified by Romano+2021 offer resolved or spectrally separated components at z~4.5  
  > "we selected major merging systems that are spatially and/or spectrally separated in a robust way" (2.1 Targets selection)
- Why this method: Apply earlier morpho-spectral decomposition of a single ALPINE merger to more systems  
  > "we apply a similar morpho-spectral decomposition analysis on additional systems classified as mergers in the ALPINE sample" (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Three apertures (1", peak, FWHM-based) tested; FWHM-based adopted as primary, peak rejected for f_[CII] ~ 0.95 since galaxies unresolved  
  > "we primarily base our conclusions on the more appropriate FWHM-based method" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Comparison of observed [CII] luminosity with simulated carbon mass is not straightforward; no radiative transfer  
  > "it is important to bear in mind that the comparison between the results from observations and those from simulations is not straightforward" (5 Discussion)
- Limitation (authors): FWHM-based aperture needs a PSF-tail correction; correction is conservative  
  > "this method needs to be revised to account for possible contamination of the ALMA beam" (3 [CII] emission from the CGM)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only six systems, so Spearman coefficients (and the p-values, not given) are weak statistical evidence
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Diffuse emission is a residual of aperture-extracted fluxes, dependent on aperture and 2-sigma contour choices and on the single 0.58 PSF correction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Simulated carbon fraction (about 59%) differs from observed [CII] fraction (about 25%), and the sim vs obs trend with distance disagrees until restricted to r_m < 16 kpc
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cold carbon mass is not equivalent to [CII] luminosity (no radiative transfer, no sensitivity effects)
