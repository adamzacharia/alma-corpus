## Analysis recipe

1. **Subtract continuum**: Continuum-subtract each measurement set with a first-order fit excluding clear line detections.  
  > "Each of the measurement sets were continuum subtracted in CASA using a fit order of 1 and all channels with clear line detections were excluded from the fit." (Methods)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: fit order 1  
  > "using a fit order of 1" (Methods)
   - fitting: First-order continuum fit.  
  > "continuum subtracted in CASA using a fit order of 1" (Methods)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify lines and image cubes**: Matched filtering with boolean Keplerian models, followed by masked tCLEAN imaging.  
  > "An initial line identification was performed using matched filtering with Keplerian models (cite: Loomis2018)." (Methods); "These models were boolean masks tracing the expected Keplerian rotation of the disk gas" (Methods); "The data were then imaged with `tCLEAN` using a Briggs robust parameter of 2.0 to maximize line detection sensitivity and a conservative analytical Keplerian mask." (Methods)
   - software: CASA, tCLEAN
   - parameter: Briggs robust 2.0; channel width 0.55 km s−1.  
  > "using a Briggs robust parameter of 2.0" (Methods); "Images were generated with a channel width of 0.55 km s$^{-1}$" (Methods)
   - assumption: Expected Keplerian rotation; previously stated disk position and inclination angles; range of radial extents.  
  > "These models were boolean masks tracing the expected Keplerian rotation of the disk gas based on the aforementioned position and inclination angles of the CO gas disk as traced by ALMA with a range of radial extents." (Methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure line fluxes**: Generate Keplerian-masked integrated intensity maps; extract circular and Keplerian-masked spectra; integrate masked spectra and calculate 3σ upper limits.  
  > "and the python package `BetterMoments`" (Methods); "Spectra were extracted both from a 0.5$''$ radius circular mask centered at the position of HD 100453 A as well as from a Keplerian mask with the same radial extent." (Methods); "The disk integrated flux was then calculated from the masked spectra and for non-detections a 3 $\sigma$ upper limit on the flux was determined" (Methods)
   - software: BetterMoments, keplerian_mask
   - parameter: 1.0 arcsec Keplerian mask for integrated intensity maps.  
  > "The integrated intensity maps were generated from the image cubes using a 1.0$''$ Keplerian mask" (Methods)
   - parameter: Flux uncertainty propagated from line-free rms and mask pixel count.  
  > "where $\sigma$ is propagated from the rms in the line-free channels and the number of pixels included in the mask (cite: 2019A&A...623A.124C)." (Methods)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Mask size assessed to capture all line flux while limiting noisy pixels.  
  > "was found to be sufficent to encapsulate all of line flux whilst minimizing the inclusion of noisy pixels." (Methods)
4. **Locate the emitting gas**: Re-grid and stack eight line measurement sets; image and extract spectra; inspect line wings and images at several robust values.  
  > "This was generated by re-gridding and stacking 8 individual measurement sets that were split out around each of the lines." (Emission morphology and kinematics); "These steps were performed using the CASA tasks `mstransform` and `concat`." (Emission morphology and kinematics); "To investigate the morphology of the emission further we imaged the stacked data at lower robust values of +0.5,-0.5 and -2.0." (Emission morphology and kinematics)
   - software: CASA, mstransform, concat, tCLEAN
   - parameter: Source velocity 5.25 km s−1; line wings approximately -5.5 and +5.0 km s−1 relative to it.  
  > "The line wings drop off sharply at approximately -5.5 km s$^{-1}$ and +5.0 km s$^{-1}$ relative to the source velocity of 5.25 km s$^{-1}$." (Emission morphology and kinematics)
   - assumption: Inclination 35° and stellar mass 1.6 solar masses.  
  > "If we assume an inclination angle of 35$\degree$ and a stellar mass of 1.6 M$_{\odot}$" (Emission morphology and kinematics)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compare with bulk-gas spectra and repeat imaging at higher angular resolution.  
  > "To place this in context with the bulk gas of the disk we also show in Figure 2 the total disk integrated spectra of the J=3-2 line" (Emission morphology and kinematics); "When going to higher resolution the emission becomes more compact and there is a clear offset in the integrated intensity to the North East of the disk." (Emission morphology and kinematics)
5. **Fit rotational temperatures and column densities**: Optical-depth-corrected rotational diagrams, iterating thermal linewidth to agree with the recovered rotational temperature.  
  > "These fluxes are used in a now-standard rotational diagram analysis to determine the column density and rotational temperature of the gas, including an optical depth correction (cite: 2018ApJ...859..131L; 2020ApJ...890..142P; 2023A&A...675A.131T)." (Methods); "We iterate these calculations such that the thermal line width used to determine the optical depth is consistent with the recovered rotational temperature." (Rotational temperatures and column densities)
   - software: emcee-package
   - parameter: For the opacity-affected component, include lines with Einstein A below 10−4 s−1 and/or upper energy above 300 K.  
  > "To mitigate this, we only include lines with Einstein A coefficients lower than 10$^{-4}$ s$^{-1}$ and/or upper energy levels greater than 300 K in the linear fit." (Rotational temperatures and column densities)
   - parameter: Additional 10% flux error when lines occur in both settings.  
  > "In the case that the lines are detected in both of the spectral settings we include an additional 10% error" (Methods)
   - assumption: Same underlying emitting area for all organic molecules; ring from 16 to 30 au.  
  > "Within this framework we require an underlying emitting area of the gas which we assume is the same for all of the organic molecules." (Rotational temperatures and column densities); "This results in a ring of gas from 16 to 30 au" (Rotational temperatures and column densities)
   - assumption: Inner radius at the mm-dust inner edge; outer radius half the major axis of the robust -2.0 stacked beam.  
  > "we set the inner radius of this area to be the inner edge of the mm-dust" (Rotational temperatures and column densities); "the outer radius to be half of the major axis for the beam for the $\mathrm{briggs~robust=-2.0}$ stacked image" (Rotational temperatures and column densities)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC posterior distributions for column density and rotational temperature; priors and likelihood not stated.  
  > "we used the Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) implementation of emcee-package (cite: 2013PASP..125..306F) to obtain posterior distributions of the column density and rotational temperature." (Methods)

## Qualitative findings

- Molecular emission is compact relative to the beam and appears co-spatial.  
  > "All of the molecular emission is compact with respect to the beam and appear to be co-spatial." (Emission morphology and kinematics)
- Higher-resolution stacked imaging shows a North East integrated-intensity excess.  
  > "When going to higher resolution the emission becomes more compact and there is a clear offset in the integrated intensity to the North East of the disk." (Emission morphology and kinematics)
- Line wings trace gas at the mm-dust cavity edge.  
  > "Therefore, these line wings are tracing gas at the mm-dust cavity edge." (Emission morphology and kinematics)
- Results add evidence for at least partial inheritance of icy material.  
  > "this result adds to growing evidence that Class II disks at least partially inherit icy material from the earlier stages of star formation." (Are we tracing inherited ices?)
