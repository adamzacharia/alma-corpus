## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit continuum visibilities**: uvmodelfit with Gaussian (and point source) components to derive positions, fluxes, sizes  
  > "By fitting the visibility data with a 2-dimensional Gaussian function with the CASA task *uvmodelfit*" (3.1 Lupus 3 MMS)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Estimate continuum masses**: M = F d^2 / (kappa B(Tdust))  
  > "M_{\rm 1.3mm} = \frac{F_{\rm 1.3mm}d^{2}} {\kappa_{\rm 1.3 mm} B(T_{\rm dust})}" (3.1 Lupus 3 MMS)
   - parameter: Tdust 30 K (100 AU), 20 K (extended), 100 K (IRAS 15398 point source)  
  > "$M_{\rm 1.3mm}$ of the central point source is estimated with $T_{\rm dust}$ of 100 K" (3.2 IRAS 15398-3559)
   - assumption: kappa=0.009 cm2/g, gas-to-dust 100  
  > "we assume a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100" (3.1 Lupus 3 MMS)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit outflow orientation and inclination**: Compare 5σ 12CO moment 0 and P-V maps to wind-driven-shell model by minimizing residual sum, grid search over c0, PA, i, v0  
  > "we arbitrarily assigned a value one to pixels above 5$\sigma$ in the $^{12}$CO moment 0 maps." (Appendix 7)
   - parameter: PA in steps of 5 deg, i in steps of 5 deg  
  > "searched for the best $c_0$ and $PA$ in steps of 5$\ensuremath{^\circ}$" (Appendix 7)
   - fitting: Residual-minimization grid search; errors from models with <15% larger residuals  
  > "we list the parameter ranges where our outflow models have $<$15% larger residuals compared to the best fits" (Appendix 7)
   - validation: Compared with continuum aspect-ratio inclinations (~50 deg) and previous studies  
  > "Our estimated inclination angle is consistent with theirs" (4.1)
4. **Measure C18O rotational profile**: P-V diagram peak positions per channel fit with power law |V-Vsys|=Vrot(R0)(R/R0)^f with Vsys free  
  > "We included $V_{\rm sys}$ as a free parameter." (Appendix 8)
   - parameter: R0 = 1''; low-velocity channels excluded  
  > "$R_0$ is an arbitrary characteristic radius and is set to be 1$\ensuremath{''}$" (Appendix 8)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo, 1000 repeats varying data within uncertainties  
  > "We repeated this fitting process for a 1000 times, which is sufficient to reach convergence" (Appendix 8)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Fit kinematic envelope+disk models to C18O P-V**: Infall+rotation+Keplerian disk model, LTE C18O images convolved with beam, chi2 fit to P-V; free n0, T0, M*, j; p in {-1.5,-2,-2.5}  
  > "Therefore, there are four free parameters in our kinematic model, $n_0$, $T_0$, $M_*$, and $j$." (Appendix 9)
   - parameter: q fixed -0.4; envelope within 80 deg of polar axis zero density  
  > "$q$ was fixed to be $-0.4$" (Appendix 9)
   - assumption: Free-fall infall, constant angular momentum, LTE  
  > "we computed the model images in C$^{18}$O on the assumption of local thermal equilibrium" (Appendix 9)
   - fitting: chi2 minimization; uncertainties from varying one parameter to reduced chi2 min +1  
  > "we varied one parameter at a time and kept others fixed at the best-fit values to search for the models having a reduced $\chi^2$ of the minimum $+1$" (Appendix 9)
   - validation: Compared with analytical P-V results, Oya et al. 2014 and Hsieh et al. 2016  
  > "The precessing jet model for the CO emission in IRAS 16253$-$2429 (Hsieh et al. 2016) suggests the same central protostellar mass of 0.03 $M_\ensuremath{\odot}$ as our results." (4.3)
6. **Fit Rd-M* and Rd-age relations and accretion rate decline**: Power-law fits to literature+this work sample; Macc=Lbol R*/(G M*); age=M*/Macc iterated with power-law Macc(t)  
  > "We iterated this process until $\dot{M}_{\rm acc}(t_0)$, $a$, and $t_{\rm age}$ were converging, which was with ten iterations." (5.3)
   - parameter: R*=3 Rsun; 30% mass and radius uncertainty for resolved disks  
  > "we adopt 30% uncertainties in both the protostellar masses and the disk radii for all the data points" (5.3)
   - assumption: Masses of unresolved-disk sources are lower limits (factor 1-4 underestimate)  
  > "Each time we randomly increased the protostellar masses of IRAS 15398$-$3559, IRAS 16253$-$2429, and B335 by a factor of one to four" (5.3)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo, 1000 iterations; mean and 1σ adopted  
  > "we adopted the means and 1$\sigma$ widths of them as the best-fit parameters and their uncertainties" (5.3)
   - validation: Compared with collapse models of Terebey 1984 and Basu 1998  
  > "is comparable to that in the collapse model in Basu (1998) but shallower than the one in Terebey et al. (1984)" (5.3)

## Qualitative findings

- A 100 AU Keplerian disk is resolved in Lupus 3 MMS; none in IRAS 15398 and IRAS 16253, which show infall with compact (<30 AU) continuum components  
  > "No 100 AU Keplerian disks are observed in IRAS 15398$-$3559 and IRAS 16253$-$2429." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Targets selected from SMA sample as young protostars with low masses and no clear spin-up at 1000 AU  
  > "They are selected from our SMA sample (Yen et al. 2015a)." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: SO not modelled because abundance and excitation are complex  
  > "We did not construct kinematic models for SO because the SO abundance and its excitation condition are more complex" (Appendix 9)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Only three protostars with M*<0.1 Msun; larger low-mass sample required  
  > "However, the number of the protostars with $M_* < 0.1$ $M_\odot$ in our sample is only three." (5.3)
- Limitation (authors): Masses and disk radii of IRAS 15398 and IRAS 16253 are lower and upper limits  
  > "the protostellar masses of IRAS 15398$-$3559 and IRAS 16253$-$2429 derived from our kinematic models can be considered lower limits" (4.3)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Disk growth trend depends on a heterogeneous literature sample and assumed constant R* and Lbol=accretion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small-disk masses depend on assumed free-fall and flattened envelope geometry
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Counter-rotation interpretation rests on low-resolution SMA data
