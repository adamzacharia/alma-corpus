## Analysis recipe

1. **Image ALMA Band 7 continuum**: Natural-weighted image of all spectral windows combined after non-standard calibration  
  > "We imaged all spectral windows together (343.5 GHz average; approximately 7.5 GHz usable bandwidth), yielding an rms noise of $72\,\mu{\rm Jy}\,{\rm beam^{-1}}$." (3.1 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive stellar parameters via CMFGEN**: Compare X-shooter spectrum with CMFGEN models; Teff from Mg II/Mg I equivalent widths, Mdot from Fe II and Balmer emission, L from absolute flux  
  > "This quantity is constrained based on the equivalent widths of the Mg II $\lambda$4481 and Mg I $\lambda$5183 lines." (4 Stellar parameters)
   - software: CMFGEN
   - parameter: clumping factor fc = 1  
  > "The clumping factor $f_{c}$, which is unknown, is set to unity." (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Measure radio and ALMA flux densities and spectral index**: Integrate emission above 3 sigma in CASA viewer; error sigma*sqrt(N beams); power-law fit to 5.5, 9, 17 GHz; spectral index map  
  > "For the flux density, we integrate the nebular emission of areas above $3\sigma$ level." (6 Ionized matter)
   - software: CASA viewer
   - fitting: weighted power-law fit S proportional to nu^alpha  
  > "The weighted fit of the power-law $S_{\nu}\propto\nu^{\alpha}$ between the flux densities at 5.5, 9, and 17 GHz" (6 Ionized matter)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Model stellar wind free-free emission**: Panagia & Felli formula for ionized spherical wind  
  > "the formulation of the spectrum of free-free radiation from ionized spherical stellar winds by (cite: 1975PF)" (6 Ionized matter)
   - assumption: mu=1.33, Te=5000 K, mean ionic charge 0.9, fc=1  
  > "an electron temperature of $T_{e} = 5\,000\,\rm K$ and an average ionic charge of $\overline{Z}=0.9$" (6 Ionized matter)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive Halpha extinction map**: Pixel-by-pixel comparison of 17 GHz radio with HST Halpha: 2.5 log(F_obs/F_expected)  
  > "The extinction map is derived by comparing pixel-by-pixel the highest-resolution radio image (17 GHz) with the *HST* $\rm H \rm \alpha$ image" (6 Spatial distribution of dust)
   - assumption: Te = 12,200 K  
  > "An electron temperature of $12\,200\,\rm K$ (cite: 1998Smith) is adopted." (6 Spatial distribution of dust)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Fit dust greybody to SED and derive dust mass**: Single-temperature greybody fit to MIPS24, PACS100/160 and ALMA photometry, plus photosphere and free-free components  
  > "we only fit the greybody to the MIPS24, PACS100+160, and ALMA photometry." (8 SED of RMC 143)
   - software: PyAstronomy
   - parameter: free-free contribution to ALMA 0.47-1.01 mJy, uncertainty +/-0.27 mJy added in quadrature  
  > "The uncertainty on the free-free contribution of $\pm0.27\,\rm mJy$ was added in quadrature with the ALMA measurement error in the fit." (8 SED of RMC 143)
   - assumption: kappa_850 = 1.7 cm^2/g, distance 49.97 kpc  
  > "A dust emissivity at $850\,\mu{\rm m}$ of $\kappa_{850}=1.7\rm\,cm^{2}\,g^{-1}$ and LMC distance of $49.97\,\rm kpc$ are assumed." (8 SED of RMC 143)
   - fitting: greybody fit; uncertainty region from twice minimum chi2; reduced chi2 1.44  
  > "The reduced $\chi^2$ of the fit is 1.44." (8 SED of RMC 143)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Derive total nebular mass and Stromgren radius**: Multiply dust mass by gas-to-dust ratio; Stromgren radius from Doran et al. photon fluxes  
  > "we find a total nebular mass $M_{\rm TOT}$ for RMC$\,$143 of $11.0\pm{3.7}~M_{\odot}$, and assuming G/D=100 (more representative of our Galaxy) $M_{\rm TOT}=5.5\pm1.8~M_{\odot}$." (9 Nebular mass)
   - assumption: _not stated_
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- ALMA sub-mm morphology differs from optical and radio, suggesting dust emission partially surrounding the ionized nebula in a photo-dissociation region  
  > "The morphological comparison and the extinction map suggest that the dust is distributed in a photo-dissociation region partially surrounding the ionized nebula." (7 Summary and conclusions)

  > "Given the limitations of the available photometry and lack of a mid-IR spectrum, we fit only a simple single-temperature greybody" (8 SED of RMC 143)
- Alternative: WISE W3 and W4 photometry excluded due to confusion  
  > "We have excluded the *WISE* W3 and W4 band photometry due to confusion in the relatively large effective beams" (8 SED of RMC 143)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Lowest ALMA contour has low significance; only a third of observations completed; deeper data needed  
  > "The lowest contour does not have strong statistical significance, but appears to partially overlap with the H$\alpha$ emission." (3.1 ALMA); "The total nebular mass has an order of magnitude uncertainty, due mostly to the uncertainty of the gas to dust ratio and of the assumed dust emissivity" (8 Nebular mass)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust mass rests on a single-temperature greybody with only four far-IR/sub-mm points, with confusion in Herschel data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Free-free subtraction relies on extrapolating 17 GHz without turnover
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low S/N ALMA detection (2-4 sigma contours)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Total mass depends on assumed G/D and kappa
