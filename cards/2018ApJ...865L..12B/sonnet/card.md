## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image Stokes I, Q, U**: Separate total-intensity calibration, concatenation, polarization calibration, phase-only self-cal, CLEAN with Briggs weighting  
  > "The two datasets were calibrated separately to obtain total intensity, and then concatenated to perform the polarization calibration." (Observations and data reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive polarization maps**: P=sqrt(Q^2+U^2), p=P/I, chi=0.5 arctan(U/Q)  
  > "we obtain the linear polarization intensity, $P=\sqrt{Q^2 + U^2 }$, the linear polarization fraction, $p=P/I$" (Observations and data reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Instrumental error 0.1% on p and >=2 deg on chi  
  > "The ALMA instrumental error is reported to be 0.1% on $p$, and at least $2^{\circ}$ on $\chi$" (Observations and data reduction)
3. **Measure continuum geometry**: 2D Gaussian fit deconvolved from beam to get FWHM, inclination, PA; compare with literature  
  > "The FWHM along the major and minor axis, determined with a 2D Gaussian fit deconvolved from the beam, is 0$\ensuremath{''}$35 and 0$\ensuremath{''}$18 respectively." (Results)
   - assumption: Inclination from axis ratio of the Gaussian  
  > "From these values, we estimate a disk inclination $i_{\rm disk}$ with respect to the line of sight of $\sim 59^{\circ}$" (Results)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Estimate maximum grain size**: Compare mean polarization fraction with diagnostic diagrams of Kataoka et al. 2017  
  > "Using our values of the average polarization fraction and the diagrams in (cite: Kataoka2017) we estimate that the maximum grain size giving rise to the observed polarization is in the range 100 - 150 $\mu$m for CW Tau" (Grain Size Estimates)
   - assumption: Polarization from self-scattering; maximum size ~ lambda/2pi  
  > "the maximum grain size contributing to polarization from self-scattering at a given wavelength, $\lambda$, is comparable to $\lambda/2\pi$" (Grain Size Estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Constrain dust settling**: Compare asymmetry and PA differences along cuts with Yang et al. 2017 models; optical depth from Isella2010 models  
  > "Figure [4](#fig:3cuts) (lower panel) shows PA differences along three cuts parallel to the major axis" (Constraints on Dust Settling)
   - assumption: DG Tau optical depth 1.0-0.4 at 10-30 au, 0.1 at 80 au, 20% uncertainty  
  > "The optical depth varies smoothly from about 1.0 to about 0.4 between 10 and 30 au from the star" (Constraints on Dust Settling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Model DG Tau PA transition**: Geometric model: vectors parallel to minor axis inside R, azimuthal outside; R minimizing std of PA difference  
  > "the standard deviation in PA difference (combining inner and outer disk) as a function of (the free parameter) $R$ reaches its minimum" (Variation of Polarization Orientation in DG Tau)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CW Tau polarized emission is smooth and symmetric; DG Tau shows near-side asymmetry and an outer belt with azimuthal vectors while total intensity is featureless  
  > "In contrast, DG Tau displays a structured polarized emission, with an elongated brighter region in the disk’s near side and a belt-like feature beyond about 0.3$''$ from the source." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Class II jet sources are less embedded; jet identifies the disk near side; sources are nearby, inclined and bright  
  > "the kinematics of the bipolar jet allows identification of the disk near-side" (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Radiative grain alignment with the anisotropic radiation field may contribute to the azimuthal pattern  
  > "alignment of non-spherical grains with the anisotropic radiation field (cite: Tazaki2017) may contribute to the azimuthal polarization pattern" (Variation of Polarization Orientation in DG Tau)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Grain size estimate is rough; higher-resolution multi-wavelength data needed  
  > "Multi-wavelength observations at higher angular resolution are needed to clarify this point." (Variation of Polarization Orientation in DG Tau)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single wavelength cannot separate scattering from grain alignment
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Grain sizes rely on model diagrams not matched to these disks
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Beam is comparable to features so low-SNR polarization (~2-5 sigma) may be biased
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No uncertainty on R or grain size ranges
