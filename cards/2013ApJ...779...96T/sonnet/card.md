## Analysis recipe

1. **Identify N2D+ cores**: Cores defined as 3 sigma N2D+ emission in l-b-v space, deconvolved by the beam  
  > "finding 6 objects (C1-N, C1-S, F1, F2, G2-N, G2-S) that have emission exceeding the 3$\sigma$ noise level within $l-b-v$-space" (3.1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure core velocity dispersion**: Fit hyperfine model (47 components) to integrated spectra; remove thermal part; add sound speed in quadrature  
  > "We fit model spectra, which account for the full blended set of 47 hyperfine components, to these data to derive the centroid velocity" (3.2)
   - assumption: T = 10 +/- 3 K, mu = 2.33 m_p, optically thin  
  > "We assume a gas temperature of $T=10\pm3$ K" (3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: All spectra well modeled as optically thin  
  > "we find all spectra can be well-modeled assuming optically thin line emission" (3.2)
3. **Derive core masses**: Masses from MIREX (max, min by envelope subtraction) and from 1.338 mm continuum  
  > "We will tend to regard the core masses derived from mm dust emission as the most accurate measure" (3.3.2)
   - assumption: kappa_1.338mm = 5.95e-3 cm2/g, gas-to-dust 141, Td = 10+/-3 K, 20% distance error  
  > "our preferred choice of $\kappa_\nu = 5.95\times 10^{-3}\:{\rm cm^2\:g^{-1}}$" (3.3.2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared Sigma_c,mm with Sigma_c,min and Sigma_c,max  
  > "The values of $\Sigma_{\rm c,mm}$ are always greater than $\Sigma_{\rm c,min}$." (3.3.2)
4. **Compare with virial predictions and magnetic field**: Compute sigma_c,vir and R_c,vir from MT03; derive phi_B,vir, m_A,vir, B_c,vir and B_crit  
  > "We then evaluate the ratio of the observed velocity dispersion derived from the $\rm N_2D^+$(3-2) spectrum to the prediction from virial equilibrium" (3.4)
   - assumption: 0.5<m_A<2 range, f_g=1, alpha_vir,cl=1, phi_geom=1  
  > "Finally, we assume that the clump is virialized with $\alpha_{\rm vir,cl}=1$." (1.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Deuterated species trace cold dense gas where CO is frozen out  
  > "We will thus use ${\rm N_2D^+}$to both identify pre-stellar cores" (1.2)
- Alternative: DCO+ not used for kinematics since it traces extended envelopes  
  > "We do not use DCO$^+$ to measure core kinematics" (3.2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Mass uncertain by a factor of about 2; core size definition arbitrary; kinematic distance to F uncertain  
  > "Note, that $M_{\rm c,mm}$ still has uncertainties of about a factor of 2" (3.3.2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass depends on assumed Td and kappa
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cycle 0 compact data only; single pointings
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Ionized tracer may underestimate dispersion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only six cores
