## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract uv-plane spectra and continuum fluxes**: Gildas uv_fit point-source fit to the visibilities at each frequency, at the continuum peak position (or COSMOS2020 position without strong continuum); per-SPW spectra combined in the observed frame  
  > "the spectrum was extracted using Gildas `uv_fit`, in which we fit $uv$ visibility at each frequency using a point source model fixed at the position of continuum peak" (ALMA); "We extracted the 3mm 1D spectrum of each SPW and combined the spectra of all SPWs in the observed frame to enhance the signal-to-noise ratio (S/N) at overlapped frequencies." (ALMA)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - parameter: continuum from all line-free channels with point-source models in uv space  
  > "The continuum fluxes were measured by combining all line-free channels and fitting the sources in $uv$ space with point-source models." (ALMA)
   - parameter: average 1 sigma line sensitivity 0.05 Jy km/s/beam over 500 km/s in Band 3  
  > "This resulted an average $1\sigma$ spectral line sensitivity of 0.05 Jy km s$^{-1}$ beam$^{-1}$ over a 500 km s$^{-1}$ width in Band 3." (ALMA)
   - assumption: sources unresolved in the dust continuum  
  > "The clean residuals indicate the sources are well-fitted and are unresolved in the dust continuum." (ALMA)
   - fitting: point-source model fixed at the continuum peak position  
  > "we fit $uv$ visibility at each frequency using a point source model fixed at the position of continuum peak" (ALMA)
   - validation: clean residuals indicate sources are well fitted  
  > "The clean residuals indicate the sources are well-fitted and are unresolved in the dust continuum." (ALMA)
2. **Fit lines and continuum, determine redshifts**: simultaneous fit of Gaussian emission lines and a power-law continuum with LMfit; common sigma for multiple lines  
  > "We use the `LMfit` package" (Line detections and Redshift determination); "Emission lines were fitted as Gaussians. When multiple emission lines were present in the same spectra, we fixed the sigma value of the Gaussian to be the same when performing the fitting" (Line detections and Redshift determination)
   - software: LMfit
   - parameter: continuum y = A * nu^P with P = 3.7  
  > "the continuum is fitted as a power law using the formula $y=A\times \nu^{P}$, where A is the amplitude and $P=3.7$ following" (Line detections and Redshift determination)
   - assumption: same Gaussian sigma (hence FWHM) for all lines in one spectrum  
  > "we fixed the sigma value of the Gaussian to be the same when performing the fitting" (Line detections and Redshift determination)
   - fitting: LMfit fit; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "We use the `LMfit` package" (Line detections and Redshift determination)
   - validation: using beta~1.9 changes the continuum power-law fit by at most 3.4% and no line fluxes at reported precision  
  > "We note that using a higher $\beta\approx1.9$ for the power law continuum fit changes its value by (at most) $3.4\%$" (Gas mass estimates)
3. **Stack spectra of optical/NIR candidate members**: extract spectra at the candidate member positions and stack at the redshift of CGG-z4.a, then check CO S/N  
  > "we extracted spectra at the positions of all candidate galaxy group members and stacked them at the redshift of CGG-z4.a" (Spectral stacking)
   - software: GILDAS
   - parameter: 2 sigma limits 0.06 Jy km/s (CO 5-4) and 0.04 Jy km/s (CO 4-3)  
  > "we found no definitive detections at the $2\sigma$ level (0.06 Jy km/s for CO(5-4) and 0.04 Jy km/s for CO(4-3))" (Spectral stacking)
   - assumption: all candidate members at the redshift of CGG-z4.a  
  > "stacked them at the redshift of CGG-z4.a" (Spectral stacking)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit the integrated FIR-radio SED for SFR, L_IR and dust mass**: MICHI2 fit of MIPS 24um, Herschel, SCUBA-2, ALMA and radio photometry with AGN, warm PDR, cold and ambient dust and a radio component; individual galaxies obtained by scaling the integrated SED to each ALMA 870 um flux  
  > "we fit the MIPS/24$\rm \mu m$, Herschel, SCUBA2, and ALMA photometry, with a mid-IR AGN, warm photodissociation region (PDR), and cold and ambient dust components" (SED fitting); "we assumed that CGG-z4.a and CGG-z4.b share the same SED shape as the integrated SED. We then scaled the SED to the ALMA 870$\,\rm \mu m$ flux of each source" (Star formation and ISM properties)
   - software: MICHI2
   - parameter: no assumption of energy balance  
  > "without the assumption of energy balance" (SED fitting)
   - parameter: L_IR integrated between 8-1000 um  
  > "from integrating the SED fit between 8-1000$\rm \mu m$" (Star formation and ISM properties)
   - assumption: both galaxies share the integrated SED shape  
  > "we assumed that CGG-z4.a and CGG-z4.b share the same SED shape as the integrated SED" (Star formation and ISM properties)
   - fitting: MICHI2; sampler and priors not stated  
  > "We used the SED fitting code MICHI2" (SED fitting)
   - validation: scaled SED fits align with the data except the VLA radio flux of CGG-z4.a, which is below the IR-radio correlation  
  > "We observe that scaled SED fits align with the data, with the exception of the radio data of CGG-z4.a" (SED fitting)
5. **Fit optical/NIR members for stellar mass and SFR**: Bagpipes fit of COSMOS2020 Classic photometry at z = 4.331 with constant SFH and Salim 2018 attenuation  
  > "we use the SED fitting code Bagpipes" (SED fitting); "We ran the fitting for each COSMOS2020 galaxy at the spectroscopic redshift of CGG-z4.a ($z=4.331$), assuming they are all part of the galaxy group." (SED fitting)
   - software: Bagpipes, EAZY
   - parameter: log(M*) 6-12, Z 0.01-2 Zsun, Av 0-4, log U -4 to -1  
  > "We ran Bagpipes with the following parameters: formation mass $\rm log(M_{\rm \star})\in[6.0:12.0]\, log(M_{\rm \odot})$, metallicity $\rm Z\in[0.01:2.00]\, Z_{\rm \odot}$, reddening $\rm A_{\rm v}\in[0.0:4.0],$" (SED fitting)
   - assumption: constant star formation history and Salim et al. 2018 attenuation curve  
  > "and assumed a constant star formation history (SFH) and the attenuation curve of" (SED fitting)
   - assumption: all candidates placed at z = 4.331  
  > "We ran the fitting for each COSMOS2020 galaxy at the spectroscopic redshift of CGG-z4.a ($z=4.331$), assuming they are all part of the galaxy group." (SED fitting)
   - fitting: Bagpipes; sampler not stated  
  > "we use the SED fitting code Bagpipes" (SED fitting)
   - validation: exponential SFH gives virtually identical results; letting photometric redshift vary changes posteriors by at most 1 sigma  
  > "We note that we tried using an exponential SFH, which resulted in virtually identical results" (SED fitting); "allowing the photometric redshift to vary in Bagpipes results in changes to the posterior fit values that are (at most) at the $1\sigma$ level" (SED fitting)
6. **Estimate stellar masses of the two dark galaxies**: average of (i) IRAC channel 2 flux scaled to COSMOS2020 sources at 4.0<z<4.6 and (ii) dust mass from optically thick blackbody fits times a stellar-to-dust mass ratio of 50-100  
  > "First, we scaled their IRAC channel 2 peak fluxes to the IRAC fluxes of the COSMOS2020 sources with photometric redshifts between $4.0<z<4.6$ and then scaled the average stellar mass to CGG-z4.a and b." (Stellar mass); "we conservatively adopted the average stellar masses with uncertainties compassing both results" (Stellar mass)
   - parameter: M*/Mdust = 50-100  
  > "we adopted a range of stellar-to-dust mass ratios $M_*/M_{\rm dust}=50-100$" (Stellar mass)
   - assumption: M*/Mdust of high-z starbursts applies  
  > "we adopted a range of stellar-to-dust mass ratios $M_*/M_{\rm dust}=50-100$" (Stellar mass)
   - fitting: optically thick modified blackbody to FIR and ALMA photometry  
  > "By fitting optically thick blackbody models to their FIR and ALMA photometry" (Stellar mass)
   - validation: two methods compared; dust-based masses 0.62 and 0.29 dex higher than IRAC-scaled; one-band photometry caveat  
  > "The $\rm M_{\rm dust}$-based stellar masses are 0.62 and 0.29 dex higher than IRAC-scaled ones" (Stellar mass)
7. **Estimate the dark matter halo mass**: five methods following Sillassen et al. 2024: SHMR with central galaxy, two SHMR on summed stellar mass within 105 kpc, overdensity with galaxy bias, NFW fit to radial stellar mass density; final value is their average  
  > "We applied five methods to estimate the dark matter halo mass." (Halo mass); "Given the five results above, we adopted their average of" (Halo mass)
   - parameter: posterior virial radius of 105 kpc for the summed stellar mass  
  > "a sum of stellar masses within a posterior virial radius of 105 kpc" (Halo mass)
   - assumption: galaxy bias factor applied to the overdensity level (value not given)  
  > "based on the overdensity level of CGG-z4 above the whole COSMOS field and adopting a galaxy bias factor" (Halo mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: paper notes halo mass estimates vary greatly between models  
  > "It should be noted that halo mass estimates can vary greatly between different models" (Comparison with simulations)
8. **Derive gas masses, gas fractions and depletion times**: gas mass from 850 um dust luminosity (thin and thick dust), from CO(4-3) converted to CO(1-0) with alpha_CO 0.8 and 3.8, and from [CI](1-0) with alpha_CI; depletion time = Mgas/SFR  
  > "We used an $\alpha_{850}$ value of $7.3\pm0.1\times10^{12}\,\rm W\, Hz^{-1}\, M_{\odot}^{-1}$, such that M$_{\rm gas}=\rm L_{850}/\alpha_{850}$." (Gas mass estimates); "The ratio M$_{\rm gas}$/SFR approximates the gas depletion timescale, which is the inverse of the star formation efficiency (SFE)." (Gas mass estimates)
   - software: Python (Astropy, NumPy, SciPy, uncertainties)
   - parameter: alpha_850 = 7.3 +/- 0.1e12 W/Hz/Msun

## Reasoning

- Why this data: FIR SED suggested z_FIR~6 for CGG-z4.a, so a 3 mm line scan was requested to confirm the redshift  
  > "Our initial FIR SED analysis with deblended FIR photometry suggested a $z_{\rm FIR}\sim6$; this was followed up with an ALMA 3mm line scan" (Selection)
- Why this method: Bagpipes chosen over EAZY for physical properties of individual galaxies  
  > "EAZY primarily excels in determining the redshift probability distribution of large galaxy samples. Other SED fitting codes are more suited when estimating the physical properties of individual galaxies." (SED fitting)
- Alternative: Exponential SFH in Bagpipes gave virtually identical results to constant SFH  
  > "We note that we tried using an exponential SFH, which resulted in virtually identical results" (SED fitting)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Stellar masses of the dark galaxies are uncertain because based on one-band IRAC photometry
