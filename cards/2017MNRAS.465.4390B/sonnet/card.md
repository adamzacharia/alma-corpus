## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and CO line cubes**: Pipeline calibration, MFS continuum clean (natural), uvcontsub, line cube clean, primary beam correction  
  > "These same channels were also used to produce a UV plane model of the continuum emission" (2.2 Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA, clean, uvcontsub
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Dust masses, FIR luminosity and SFR from 3 mm continuum**: Optically thin Rayleigh-Jeans dust mass from single photometric point; greybodies scaled to ALMA flux for L_FIR (40-300 um); Kennicutt relation for SFR  
  > "The FIR luminosity here is defined between 40 and 300$\mu$m" (3.1 Dust Continuum)
   - parameter: average of (T=47K, beta=1.6) and (T=41K, beta=1.95)  
  > "we therefore choose to adopt the average of two commonly used assumptions for the dust SED of high redshift quasars" (3.1 Dust Continuum)
   - assumption: kappa0=0.045 m2/kg at 250 GHz  
  > "We assume $\kappa_0$=0.045 m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$ at $\nu_0$=250 GHz" (3.1 Dust Continuum)
   - fitting: Greybody fit to Herschel+ALMA for ULASJ1234: Td=31+-4 K, beta=2.5+-0.3 (not adopted)  
  > "The best-fit SED has parameters: T$_{\rm{d}}$=31$\pm$4K and $\beta$=2.5$\pm$0.3." (3.1 Dust Continuum)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Gas masses from CO and dust**: CO(3-2) to CO(1-0) with r32/10=0.8, alpha_CO=0.8; dust-based gas mass with gas-to-dust 91; derived alpha_CO  
  > "We assume an excitation ratio between the $^{12}$CO(3-2) and $^{12}$CO(1-0) line of $r_{32/10}$=0.8" (3.2 CO)
   - assumption: alpha_CO=0.8; gas-to-dust ratio 91  
  > "these are converted to gas masses assuming $\alpha_{\rm{CO}}=0.8$ (K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$)$^{-1}$" (3.2 CO)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Line fits, spectroastrometry and dynamical masses**: Single/double Gaussian fits and moments; red/blue spectroastrometric offset; Gnerucci, virial and Neri estimators  
  > "We use either a single or double Gaussian to fit the line profiles in Fig. [3](#fig:spec) to characterise the line shape" (3.3 Kinematics)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: R=4 kpc for sources not resolved  
  > "Assuming a typical R=4 kpc for the other three quasars" (3.3 Kinematics)
   - fitting: Gaussian fits; reduced chi2 compared  
  > "In all cases, apart from ULASJ0123+1525, a single Gaussian gives an acceptable fit to the data with a reduced $\chi^2$ value of $\sim$1." (3.3 $^{12}$CO(3-2) Kinematics and Dynamical Masses)
   - validation: Parametric and non-parametric widths consistent  
  > "The parametric and non-parametric values for the line centroid and line widths are consistent within the error bars" (3.3 Kinematics)
5. **Tilted-ring kinematic model of ULASJ2315+0143**: rotcur in iterative mode on single-Gaussian velocity field, PA fixed, 1-15 rings  
  > "We used the *rotcur* task within GIPSY in iterative mode for the fitting." (3.4.2)
   - software: GIPSY rotcur
   - parameter: PA fixed at 352.3 deg  
  > "This P.A. is measured to be 352.3$^{\circ}$." (3.4.2)
   - fitting: Iterative rotcur; i and vsys from average of successful rings (error on i <20 deg)  
  > "The systemic velocity and $i$ were then determined from the average of all successful rings where a successful ring is defined to be one with errors on $i$ of $<$20$^{\circ}$." (3.4.2 ULASJ2315+0143: Spatially Resolved Studies of the Molecular Gas)
   - validation: Error-weighted average gives little difference  
  > "We have checked that using an error weighted average instead makes very little difference to the model fit parameters." (3.4.2)
6. **Source size measurement**: 2-D Gaussian fits to collapsed CO maps; checked with Briggs robust=0.5 and uvmodelfit  
  > "We used CASA to fit a 2-D Gaussian (in RA, Dec) to the CO maps constructed by summing all the channels covering the full extent of the line." (4.4 CO(3-2) Sizes)
   - software: CASA, uvmodelfit
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with Briggs imaging and uvmodelfit  
  > "We have therefore checked that the sizes quoted here are consistent within the error bars with the deconvolved sizes estimated from images generated using a *Briggs* weighting (robust=0.5)" (4.4 $^{12}$CO(3-2) Sizes)
7. **Population comparisons**: Compare 850um fluxes, LCO-LIR, FWHM-LCO with SMGs, quasars, HotDOGs; KS tests  
  > "using a KS-test to determine the probability that they are drawn from the same parent population" (4.3)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
