## Analysis recipe

1. **measure ALMA 1.3 mm continuum fluxes**: aperture photometry on pipeline-reduced continuum products: 1 arcsec aperture for unresolved sources, custom aperture above 3 times the rms for larger sources  
  > "Most sources were unresolved and fluxes were measured from the pipeline reduced data products using a $1''$ aperture." (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations); "For larger sources, we used a custom aperture that encompassed all the emission above three times the rms noise level." (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations)
   - parameter: 1'' aperture; custom aperture at 3x rms for extended sources  
  > "For larger sources, we used a custom aperture that encompassed all the emission above three times the rms noise level." (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **get V351 Ori inclination and PA from ALMA visibilities**: fit an axisymmetric model to the visibilities with FrankFitter, because the scattered light structure is complex and the disk is resolved in ALMA continuum  
  > "by fitting an axisymmetric model to the visibileties using `FrankFitter`(cite: 2020MNRAS.495.3209J)" (4.2.1 Bright disk of V351 Ori)
   - software: FrankFitter
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **reduce SPHERE polarimetric data to Q_phi images**: IRDAP reduction with PDI, default settings  
  > "(IRDAP, (cite: vanHolstein2020)) with default settings." (2.1 SPHERE polarimetric imaging)
   - software: IRDAP
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **measure disk geometry and radius from Q_phi images**: extract disk edge at 3 sigma along radial profiles, then least-squares ellipse fit  
  > "we determined the point at which the disk flux drops below $3\sigma$ of the noise background of the image and used this as the edge position." (Appendix: Measuring inclination, PA, and radius); "we used the least squares algorithm developed by (cite: oy1998NUMERICALLYSD) to obtain the ellipse solution that best fit the data points." (Appendix: Measuring inclination, PA, and radius)
   - parameter: sliding aperture of 4 pixels diameter; uncertainties from repeating the fit 10^5 times with data points drawn from normal distributions  
  > "we repeated this process $10^5$ times, each time drawing the data points individually from a normal distribution centered on their original position" (Appendix: Measuring inclination, PA, and radius)
   - fitting: least-squares ellipse fit with Monte Carlo resampling of edge points  
  > "we repeated this process $10^5$ times, each time drawing the data points individually from a normal distribution centered on their original position" (Appendix: Measuring inclination, PA, and radius)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **estimate dust and total disk mass of V351 Ori and V1012 Ori**: optically thin isothermal dust mass from the 1.3 mm flux, then gas-to-dust ratio 100, to compare M_disk/M_* with gravitational instability threshold  
  > "We therefore estimated the dust disk mass, assuming that there are optically thin conditions at $\lambda=1.3$ mm ($\nu=230$ Ghz) and that the dust is isothermal" (5.1 Disk asymmetries)
   - parameter: T_dust = 20 K; kappa = 2.3 cm^2 g^-1  
  > "We used the dust temperature, $T_{\rm d}=20K$, and $\kappa_{\nu}=2.3\,\mbox{cm}^{2}\,\mbox{g}^{-1}$ for the absorption coefficient." (5.1 Disk asymmetries)
   - assumption: gas-to-dust ratio of 100  
  > "To obtain the total disk mass we then assumed a gas-to-dust ratio of 100." (5.1 Disk asymmetries)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: described as a crude approximation that ignores temperature effects  
  > "Not taking the temperature effects into account, this crude approximation shows that gravitational instability could be a plausible explanation." (5.1 Disk asymmetries)
6. **test correlation of scattered light contrast with 1.3 mm flux, age and mass**: Kendall tau coefficient between delta_pol and F_1.3mm, age and stellar mass  
  > "We find a correlation between the millimeter flux and $\delta_{\rm pol}$, $\tau_{K}=0.23$, with a probability of $\sim20\%$ that the two quantities are independent of each other." (4.3 Disk brightness versus stellar and disk properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- Three disks (V351 Ori, V599 Ori, V1012 Ori) are exceptionally extended and show scattered light asymmetries  
  > "Of these, three are exceptionally extended (V351 Ori, V599 Ori, and V1012 Ori) and show scattered light asymmetries" (abstract)
- Disk detection is anticorrelated with multiplicity  
  > "We find a strong anticorrelation between disk detection and multiplicity, with only two of our ten disk detections located in stellar multiple systems." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Disks without previous ALMA data were selected for additional ALMA observations to obtain a flux measurement  
  > "The disks in the sample that lacked previous ALMA data were also selected for additional ALMA observations with the aim of obtaining a flux measurement." (3 Sample and stellar properties)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ALMA data are only used for continuum fluxes in this paper; detailed analysis deferred  
  > "A more detailed analysis will be performed in future papers." (2.2 ALMA Band 6 observations)
- Limitation (authors): Sample is far from complete, to be regarded as a pilot study  
  > "We note that our sample is in all likelihood[^4] far from complete and should be regarded in a sense as a pilot study of Orion" (3 Sample and stellar properties)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Kendall tau = 0.23 with ~20% chance of independence is a weak statistical case, from about 10 detected disks
- Caveat (inferred, ours): V351 Ori PA from FrankFitter is 325.8 deg in the text but 320.16 deg in the disk table
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust mass uses fixed T_dust = 20 K and kappa for all sources, ignoring luminosity-dependent temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Some 1.3 mm fluxes come from other ALMA programs with different resolution and sensitivity, mixed with new data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Upper limits and non-detections are not included in the correlation test
