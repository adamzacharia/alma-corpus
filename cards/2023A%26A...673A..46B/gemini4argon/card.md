## Analysis recipe

1. **Image and measure 3 mm continuum emission**: Combine the two low-resolution line-free spectral windows (4 GHz bandwidth) and fit 2D Gaussians on the primary-beam corrected map.  
  > "The map was obtained by combining the data (total bandwidth $\Delta\nu=4$ GHz) from the two low-resolution spectral windows centred at 101.190 GHz and 99.387 GHz, which are not contaminated by line emission." (ALMA map of the 3mm continuum); "A 2D Gaussian fit performed on the PB-corrected map shown in Fig. 18 delivers best-fit peak flux densities of $S_{cont}^{NED01}=0.71\pm0.03$ mJy beam$^{-1}$ and $S_{cont}^{NED02}=2.26\pm0.05$ mJy beam$^{-1}$" (ALMA map of the 3mm continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Spectral windows centred at 101.190 GHz and 99.387 GHz, total bandwidth = 4 GHz  
  > "The map was obtained by combining the data (total bandwidth $\Delta\nu=4$ GHz) from the two low-resolution spectral windows centred at 101.190 GHz and 99.387 GHz, which are not contaminated by line emission." (ALMA map of the 3mm continuum)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit on PB-corrected continuum map  
  > "A 2D Gaussian fit performed on the PB-corrected map shown in Fig. 18 delivers best-fit peak flux densities" (ALMA map of the 3mm continuum)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract total CO(1-0) spectra, fit Gaussian profiles, refine systemic redshifts, and derive total molecular gas masses**: Fit single and double Gaussian functions to continuum-subtracted CO(1-0) spectra to determine central sky frequencies and systemic redshifts, then compute line luminosities and molecular gas masses from redshift-corrected cubes.  
  > "To refine the redshift estimate of NED01 we performed two spectral fits, one with a single-Gaussian function and one with two Gaussians" (4.1.1 NED01); "By using the CO(1-0) line fluxes obtained from the spectral fits, we computed the total CO(1-0) line luminosities of the two galaxies, and from these estimated their molecular line masses" (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: PV diagram slit sizes ~30'' and ~9'' aligned with major and minor axes of NED01  
  > "The CO(1-0) position-velocity (PV) diagrams displayed in Fig. 3 were computed in `CASA` from slit-like apertures with sizes of $\sim30''$ and $\sim9''$, aligned with the major and minor axes of the CO(1-0) disk of NED01 respectively" (4.1.1 NED01)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.3 ± 1.3 M_sun (K km s^-1 pc^2)^-1 for galaxy disks  
  > "by adopting a standard CO-to-H$_2$ conversion factor of $\alpha_{CO}$ = $4.3\pm1.3$ M$_{\odot}$ K km s$^{-1}$ pc$^2$." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - assumption: Systematic flux calibration error of 5% included in integrated flux uncertainties  
  > "Errors on the CO(1-0) integrated fluxes include a systematic calibration error of 5%, typical for ALMA Band 3 observations." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - fitting: 1-Gaussian and 2-Gaussian spectral line fits  
  > "To refine the redshift estimate of NED01 we performed two spectral fits, one with a single-Gaussian function and one with two Gaussians" (4.1.1 NED01); "To refine the redshift estimate of NED01 we performed two spectral fits, one with a single-Gaussian function and one with two Gaussians" (NED01)
   - validation: Verified that the new redshift estimate matches the rotational centre in major and minor axis PV diagrams  
  > "We further checked that the new redshift estimate matches with the kinematic centre of NED01’s host galaxy disk." (4.1.1 NED01)
3. **Construct CO(1-0) moment maps and characterize disk substructures**: Compute zeroth, first, and second moment maps for NED01 (PB-corrected cube) and NED02 (non-PB-corrected cube), and fit spectra of extended components B and C.  
  > "Figure 4 displays the zeroth, first, and second moment maps of the source NED01, computed by applying an intensity threshold of 0.01 Jy beam$^{-1}$ and within a box $30\ensuremath{''}$ in size centred on the AGN." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology); "The companion galaxy NED02 is located close to the edge of the PB, and so the moment maps, reported in Fig. 5, were computed from the datacube not corrected for the PB." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Intensity threshold of 0.01 Jy beam^-1 and box size 30'' for NED01 moment maps  
  > "computed by applying an intensity threshold of 0.01 Jy beam$^{-1}$ and within a box $30\ensuremath{''}$ in size centred on the AGN." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - assumption: alpha_CO = 4.3 ± 1.3 M_sun (K km s^-1 pc^2)^-1 for components B and C  
  > "entrains a CO(1-0) flux of $0.65$ Jy km s$^{-1}$, which, if adopting the same $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ as above, corresponds to $\sim4\times10^7$ M$_\odot$ of molecular hydrogen gas." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - fitting: Single-Gaussian fit to spectra of components B and C  
  > "Component C is only slightly redshifted ($v\sim9$ km s$^{-1}$), and can be modelled with a single-Gaussian with $\sigma_v\simeq30$ km s$^{-1}$ and an integrated CO(1-0) flux of $2$ Jy km s$^{-1}$" (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
   - validation: Checked g-band Pan-STARRS1 and ALMA 3 mm continuum maps to rule out dust-extinguished star-forming companions for components B and C  
  > "We can rule out the hypothesis that the CO components B and C are not distinguishable in the g-band Pan-STARR image because of high dust extinction, since in this case we would expect to detect them in the ALMA 3 mm continuum map, which is not the case" (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)

## Qualitative findings

- The bulk of the CO(1-0) emission in both NED01 and NED02 is dominated by rotating molecular gas disks, with no evidence for very high-velocity wings typical of extreme molecular outflows.  
  > "The velocity map shows clearly that the CO(1-0) gas kinematics in NED01 is dominated by ordered rotation." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology); "This was also evident from the CO(1-0) spectrum of NED01, which presents a clear double peak and no evidence for very high-velocity wings, which are typical of extreme molecular outflows detected in some local (U)LIRGs (cite: 2014Cicone)." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
- Two extended CO(1-0) structures (components B and C) near NED01 follow the rotation pattern of the main disk and are interpreted as ISM substructures, possibly tracing a spiral arm.  
  > "We therefore suggest that the CO(1-0) components B and C are ISM substructures of the main galaxy disk, possibly tracing a spiral arm." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
- Subtracting the best-fit 3D-BAROLO rotating disk models reveals compact (< 3 kpc), blueshifted CO(1-0) residual emission corresponding to ~8% of the total CO(1-0) flux in NED01 and ~7% in NED02.  
  > "The fit that leaves the least residuals in NED01, with flux corresponding to $\sim8$ % of its total CO(1-0) emission, is the one described in Sect. 5.1 and performed on the *Big region*." (Discussion); "For NED02, after subtracting the best-fit BBarolo disk model from the ALMA data, we also detected residual CO(1-0) emission, corresponding to $\sim7$ % of the total line flux from this galaxy." (Discussion)
- In 2021 XMM-Newton and NuSTAR observations, NED01 was caught in an intrinsically faint X-ray state (flux dropped by a factor of ~4) with no detected UFO absorption residuals, though upper limits on wind column density are only marginally below earlier detections.  
  > "The 2021 *XMM* and *NuSTAR* observation caught NED01 in a faint state (see Fig. 13), where the observed 2-10 keV X-ray flux dropped by a factor of $\sim 4$ with respect to the 2012 and 2014 observations." (7.2 2021 simultaneous XMM-Newton and NuSTAR observations); "The upper limits are only marginally below the 90% lower value of $N_{\mbox{\scriptsize H}}$ estimated in the past observations." (7.2 2021 simultaneous XMM-Newton and NuSTAR observations)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: NED01 hosts a powerful X-ray UFO and has a moderate SFR (5-10 M_sun yr^-1), making it ideal to test AGN feedback with reduced contamination from star-formation-driven winds; compact ALMA Band 3 configuration provides high sensitivity to extended CO(1-0) structures and covers the companion NED02 in the same field of view.  
  > "The source shows a moderate SFR of 5-10 M $_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$ (cite: 2014Delooze; 2015Pereira) and hosts a powerful X-ray wind (cite: 2015Ballo), being a suitable candidate to test the AGN feedback scenario reducing the possible contamination from star formation-driven outflows." (Introduction); "Furthermore, the high sensitivity to extended structures enabled by the use of a compact array configuration has revealed important information on the close environment of the target galaxy." (Summary and conclusions)
- Why this method: Because the bulk of the CO(1-0) emission is dominated by rotating disks, modelling the disk rotation with 3D-BAROLO and subtracting the model from the datacube is necessary to isolate any non-rotating residual emission that could trace an outflow; performing the same analysis on NED02 tests the role of galaxy interactions.  
  > "In order to study the presence of CO(1-0) components that are not participating in the rotation and may trace the effect of AGN feedback on the large-scale ISM, we first model the disk kinematics, and then study any residual emission, similar to (cite: 2019Sirressi)." (Modelling of the CO(1-0) kinematics); "A uniform and common analysis of the CO(1-0) gas kinematics of both members of the galaxy pair can give us insights into the role of galaxy interactions in shaping the cold ISM kinematics." (Modelling of the CO(1-0) kinematics)
- Alternative: Components B and C as dust-obscured optical substructures: rejected because they are not detected in the ALMA 3 mm continuum map.  
  > "We can rule out the hypothesis that the CO components B and C are not distinguishable in the g-band Pan-STARR image because of high dust extinction, since in this case we would expect to detect them in the ALMA 3 mm continuum map, which is not the case (see Appendix 11)." (4.2 CO(1-0) morphology)
- Alternative: Small and Medium region 3D-BAROLO fits for NED01: tested in Appendix B, but the Big region fit was preferred for the main analysis because it includes all disk substructures (A, B, C) and leaves the lowest residuals (most conservative).  
  > "We focus on the *Big region* because, once it has been subtracted from the ALMA datacube, this is the fit that produces the lowest residual CO(1-0) emission." (5.1 Modelling of the CO(1-0) disk in NED01)
- Alternative: Hypothesis II (compact rotating structures / circumnuclear disks) for the CO residuals: considered unlikely because the residuals are purely blueshifted with multiple asymmetrical peaks and lack a redshifted counterpart.  
  > "The CO(1-0) residuals in both NED01 and NED02 have a complex, asymmetrical, spectral profile, with multiple peaks at blueshifted velocities, and lack a reliable redshifted counterpart." (Hypothesis II: Compact rotating structures)
- Alternative: Hypothesis III (ISM disk asymmetries not captured by BBarolo): considered as an alternative to the molecular outflow hypothesis.  
  > "Finally, the blueshifted CO residuals detected in both NED01 and NED02 could be due to other types of asymmetries of the molecular disks that are not properly captured by the BBarolo model." (Hypothesis III: ISM disk asymmetries)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The CO-to-H2 conversion factor alpha_CO in molecular outflows is poorly constrained.  
  > "For different ISM environments, such as the massive molecular outflows discovered in local starbursts and AGN host galaxies (cite: 2020Veilleux), the $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ parameter is very poorly constrained." (Introduction)
- Limitation (authors): NED02 is located close to the edge of the ALMA primary beam FWHM, resulting in lower CO(1-0) sensitivity, and its compact size means its morphology and kinematics are more affected by beam smearing.  
  > "For the companion NED02 instead, given its proximity to the edge of the PB’s FWHM, the CO(1-0) line sensitivity is lower, with an average 1$\sigma$ rms value of 4.0 mJy beam$^{-1}$ per 5.2 km s$^{-1}$ channel." (ALMA CO(1-0) observations); "The CO(1-0) emission detected by ALMA in this source is also more compact in size, and hence its morphology and kinematics are more affected by beam smearing effects." (5.2 Modelling of the CO(1-0) disk in NED02)
- Limitation (authors): Negative residuals around the systemic velocity in all 3D-BAROLO fits indicate overfitting of the rotational structure, and 3D-BAROLO may force an average rotation curve that produces artificial residuals when disk perturbations are present.  
  > "There is also a negative feature at the systemic velocity, common to all three BBarolo fits, which is probably an indication of overfitting of the rotational structure." (5.1 Modelling of the CO(1-0) disk in NED01); "it could be that the model is forcing the fit to adapt better to the perturbations than to the rest of the (unperturbed) disk, hence producing artificial residuals with spectral and spatial characteristics that are unrelated to the underlying physical process." (Hypothesis III: ISM disk asymmetries)
- Limitation (authors): Spatial resolution (~1 kpc) is insufficient to definitively distinguish between a compact outflow and interaction-induced disk asymmetries; higher resolution data are needed.  
  > "Therefore, this third scenario can only be tested using higher resolution data." (Hypothesis III: ISM disk asymmetries)
- Limitation (authors): Hard X-ray emission from NED02 cannot be assessed because it falls within the PSF wings of the brighter companion NED01 in XMM-Newton.  
  > "At higher energies, we cannot assess if there is any X-ray emission from NED02 that could be indicative of a weak or highly obscured AGN because it falls within the wings of the PSF of the bright companion." (6.1 Observations and data reduction)
- Limitation (authors): X-ray wind energetics are affected by large uncertainties (~70% on absorption amount, plus uncertainties on wind opening angle, launching radius, and black hole mass).  
  > "We note that these estimates are clearly affected by large errors" (The X-ray disk wind energetics of NED01); "We note that these estimates are clearly affected by large errors; only considering the 90% errors on the column density of the wind, we derive that the kinetic power of the X-ray wind detected in the *Swift* observations could range between $2.5\times 10^{43}$ erg s$^{-1}$ and $1.7\times 10^{44}$ erg s$^{-1}$" (The X-ray disk wind energetics of NED01)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Because NED02 is located near the edge of the primary beam (~29.6'' from phase centre, where Band 3 PB FWHM is ~55''), measuring its CO(1-0) flux and residual masses on a non-PB-corrected cube (if not subsequently corrected for the local PB attenuation factor of ~0.5) would underestimate its true intrinsic CO flux and residual gas mass by a factor of ~2.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Finding nearly identical blueshifted residual fractions (~8% in NED01 and ~7% in NED02) and two-peaked velocity profiles in both galaxies—one with an AGN and one without—strongly suggests that tidal/interaction-induced disk asymmetries or beam-smeared non-circular motions dominate the residuals rather than an AGN-driven wind.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow velocities and radii are not corrected for unknown projection/inclination effects, which can alter dynamical timescales and momentum rates by factors of a few.
