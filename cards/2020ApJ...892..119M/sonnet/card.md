## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure PAH redshifts and luminosities from IRS spectra**: MCMC fit of power-law continuum plus Lorentzian (Drude) PAH profiles at 6.2 and 11.3 um; z posterior gives z_IRS; local continuum for luminosities; errors by 1000 Monte Carlo perturbations  
  > "we adopt a simpler model of Lorentzian profiles plus a power-law continuum to fit only the 6.2$\mu$m and 11.3$\mu$m PAH complexes." (Appendix A); "This process is repeated 1000 times, after which we quote the standard deviation of all iterations as the $1\sigma$ error." (Appendix A)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: uniform priors on N_pl, alpha, tau, z, b_r  
  > "We assume uniform priors on $N_{pl}$, $\alpha$, $\tau_{\nu,pl}$, $z$ and $b_r$" (Appendix A)
   - assumption: Milky Way dust attenuation law; PAH grain properties same as SINGS  
  > "We assume a wavelength-dependent Milky Way dust attenuation law for the optical depth parameter $\tau_{\nu,pl}$" (Appendix A)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC, uniform priors  
  > "We fit our model (Eq. [\[model\]](#model)) to each *IRS* spectrum using the Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) code `emcee`" (Appendix A)
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Image ALMA continuum and measure fluxes and sizes**: tclean Briggs continuum imaging; elliptical apertures from 2D Gaussian fit; R_eff,160 from size  
  > "We extracted peak and integrated continuum flux densities through elliptical apertures which were set by fitting a 2D Gaussian function to the bright continuum emission in each observation." (2.3 ALMA Observations and Data Processing)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian  
  > "fitting a 2D Gaussian function to the bright continuum emission in each observation" (2.3 ALMA Observations and Data Processing)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Detect [C II] and compute line luminosity**: uv continuum subtraction, cube imaging in 30 km/s bins, spectrum from elliptical aperture, integrate line, L_[CII] = 1.04e-3 S dv D_L^2 nu_obs  
  > "We imaged the cube in 30 km s$^{-1}$ bins, and extracted a spectrum through an elliptical aperture" (3.1 [C II] Detection in GS IRS20)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - assumption: Cosmology Om=0.3, OL=0.7, H0=70  
  > "adopt a $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $\Omega_m=0.3$, $\Omega_\Lambda=0.7$, and $H_0=70$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$" (1 Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Search for lines and set upper limits using redshift-coverage probability**: 0.5'' circular aperture 50 km/s spectra incl. offset apertures, search for three channels >2x rms; compute p(l|ALMA,dz) by integrating z posteriors over spectral window coverage; 3 sigma limit with S dv = 3 dv sqrt(6) rms50, dv=300 km/s  
  > "we searched each spectral window for the presence of three channels greater than $2\times$ the local rms." (3.2 [C II] Line Searches and Upper Limits); "$S_{[\mbox{\footnotesize C II}]}\Delta v = 3\Delta v(\sqrt{6}\,$rms$_{50}$), assuming $\Delta v=300$ km s$^{-1}$" (3.2 [C II] Line Searches and Upper Limits)
   - assumption: line width 300 km/s as in GS IRS20  
  > "assuming $\Delta v=300$ km s$^{-1}$ as is observed in GS IRS20" (3.2 [C II] Line Searches and Upper Limits)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Only galaxies with p>90% used for upper limits  
  > "Amongst the non-detections, only GS IRS61 has $p(l|\mathrm{ALMA},\Delta z)>90\%$." (3.2 [C II] Line Searches and Upper Limits)
5. **Fit IR SEDs for L_IR and cold/warm dust**: two-temperature modified blackbody + power law from IRS to far-IR photometry; beta=1.5, T_cold=26.1 K fixed; templates scaled for GS IRS46/52  
  > "For all fits we keep the dust emissivity $\beta$ fixed to a value of $1.5$, and the temperature of the cold dust component fixed at T$_{cold}=26.1$ K" (3.5 SED Fits)
   - assumption: SFR_IR = 1.8e-10 L_IR, Salpeter IMF  
  > "we assume a Salpeter IMF and SFR$_{\tiny\mathrm{IR}} \approx 1.8\times10^{-10}\,$L$_{\mathrm{IR}}$" (Table 1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Kirkpatrick+2015 templates  
  > "we overplot the average SED of $z\sim2$ $\log\mathrm{L}_{\mathrm{IR}}/\mathrm{L}_\odot$$\,=12.5$ star-forming galaxies from (cite: Kirkpatrick2015)" (3.5 SED Fits)
6. **Compare [C II]/PAH with local GOALS galaxies vs IR surface density**: Sigma_IR=(L_IR/2)/(pi R_eff,160^2); linear fit to GOALS L_[CII]/L_6.2 vs Sigma_IR; GOALS PAHFIT luminosities divided by 1.6 (6.2um) and 2.3 (11.3um); slit corrections applied  
  > "we calculate $\Sigma_{\mathrm{IR}}$$\,=($L$_{\mathrm{IR}}$$/2)/\,\pi R^2_{\mathrm{\footnotesize eff},160}$" (4.2 PAH Properties); "divide PAHFIT values by a statistical conversion factor of 1.6 and 2.3 for L$_{6.2\mu\mathrm{\tiny m}}$ and L$_{11.3\mu\mathrm{\tiny m}}$ respectively" (3.4 Comparison Samples)
   - fitting: linear fit to GOALS star-forming galaxies  
  > "We fit a linear relation to star-forming GOALS galaxies on Figure [9](#ciiL62)" (4.3 The Ratio of [C II] to PAH Luminosity)
   - validation: _not stated_

  > "Although high-redshift observations remain limited by small sample statistics" (4.3 The Ratio of [C II] to PAH Luminosity)

  > "our measurements of $R_{\mbox{\footnotesize eff},160}$ may be thought of as upper limits" (3.3 Morphology)
