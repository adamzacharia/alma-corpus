## Qualitative findings

- The SMG hosts an accreting supermassive black hole, making the BR1202-0725 QSO-SMG pair one of the highest-redshift double AGN systems known with a projected separation of 24 kpc.  
  > "Therefore, the QSO-SMG pair of BR1202-0725 represents one of the highest-redshift double AGN to date, with a projected separation of 24 kpc." (abstract)
- Four new companion galaxies (C1, C2, C3, C4) are detected in both [O III] and H-alpha within 1 arcsec (~5-6.5 kpc) of the QSO, and LAE1 is resolved into two merging components (LAE1a and LAE1b), bringing the total number of confirmed galaxies in the system to at least ten within 980 kpc^2.  
  > "Finally, we investigated the environment of this system and found four new galaxies, detected in both \[O iii\] and H$\alpha$ emission, at the same redshift of the QSO and within a projected distance of 5 kpc from it. This overdense system includes at least ten galaxies in a field of view of only 980 kpc$^{2}$." (abstract); "The optical lines of LAE1 exhibit two spectrally and spatially isolated components (LAE1a and LAE1b), which suggest a merger scenario between two galaxies at a projected distance of $\sim$ 2.8 kpc." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: JWST NIRSpec IFS provides high resolution and sensitivity in rest-frame optical emission lines to resolve the obscured SMG, measure AGN/outflow properties, and find faint companion galaxies; ALMA Band 7 [C II] and continuum data provide a comparison for the SMG line profile and dust continuum position; Chandra provides spatial resolution needed to separate the QSO and SMG in X-rays.  
  > "In this work, we also used \[C ii\] 158 $\mu$m and adjacent continuum observations from ALMA in Band 7 to compare the shape of the \[C ii\] spectrum of the SMG with those of \[O iii\] and H$\alpha$ from JWST and to compare the location of the dust continuum with that of H$\alpha$." (Observations and data reduction); "To further probe the AGN nature of the SMG, we investigated its X-ray properties with archival data by *Chandra*, the only X-ray facility that can spatially resolve the QSO and the SMG of the system." (X-ray detection of the SMG)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Fe II templates from Tsuzuki et al. (2006) and Boroson & Green (1992) were tested for the QSO spectrum but rejected because they overpredict flux in the [O III] wavelength range.  
  > "However, we discarded the results obtained with (cite: tsuzuki2006) and (cite: boroson1992) templates because they overpredict the flux emission at the wavelength range of the \[O iii\](cite: park2022)." (QSO)
- Alternative: Two scenarios were considered for the broad H-alpha + [N II] emission in the SMG: Scenario 1 (high-velocity galactic outflow in H-alpha and [N II]) and Scenario 2 (Type 1 AGN BLR emission in H-alpha). Both scenarios yield similar chi-squared values and both support an AGN in the SMG.  
  > "Therefore, we considered the two following possible scenarios: (1) the broad asymmetric profile of the complex line is due to the presence of galactic outflows; (2) the SMG hosts a type 1 AGN and the very broad component is associated with the emission of the broad line region." (SMG)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): The Narrow and Outflow components in the QSO spectral fitting are strongly affected by degeneracies and Fe II multiplet blending.  
  > "We stress that the *Narrow* and *Outflow* components in the fitting are strongly affected by degeneracies, and the Fe ii multiplets present in the spectrum also influence the values obtained for the \[O iii\] emission line." (QSO)
- Limitation (authors): PSF subtraction slightly oversubtracts QSO emission at the center, requiring a 0.2 arcsec radius mask around the QSO centroid and potentially increasing uncertainties on companion Gaussian fit parameters.  
  > "We notice that our procedure slightly over-subtracts the QSO emission at the center, hence the flux measurements in this region are not reliable. Therefore, we masked a region of radius of 0.2$''$ from the centroid of the QSO in the PSF-subtracted data cube" (Point spread function modeling); "We stress that the errors on the Guassian parameters might be larger due to the uncertainties introduced by the PSF subtraction." (LAEs and companions)
- Limitation (authors): Gas metallicities and SFRs derived for the companion galaxies assume star-formation ionization, which may be contaminated by ionizing photons from the bright QSO.  
  > "We stress again, however, that these estimates should be taken with great caution, given that a contribution to the gas ionization from the bright QSO may affect the line emission fluxes and their ratios." (Ionization nature of the sources in BR1202-0725)
- Limitation (authors): The SMG outflow mass and mass outflow rate only probe the ionized gas phase (missing molecular and neutral phases) and are not corrected for dust attenuation, making them lower limits.
