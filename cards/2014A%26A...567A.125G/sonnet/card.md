## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image the data**: Calibrate in CASA, export uv-tables to GILDAS for mapping and cleaning; natural weighting; bin spectral channels; build continuum from line-free channels  
  > "calibrated uv-tables were subsequently exported to `GILDAS`[^3] where the mapping and cleaning were performed as detailed below" (2.1 ALMA data)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Missing flux estimated by comparison with single-dish fluxes (JCMT, HHT, Herschel)  
  > "the ALMA map recovers 80–90$\%$ of the CO(3–2) flux at the CND and over the SB ring." (2.1.1 Band 7 maps)
2. **Fit torus with CLUMPY models**: Fit ALMA Band 9 (and Band 7 upper limit) with NIR/MIR SED and MIR spectroscopy using Bayesian BayesClumpy; derive torus radius and mass  
  > "Using a Bayesian approach to fit the data with the `BayesClumpy` tool" (A CLUMPY torus models)
   - software: BayesClumpy
   - parameter: viewing angle prior i=60-90 deg; torus size Y=5-100  
  > "we allowed the full range for the torus size $Y=5-100$" (A CLUMPY torus models)
   - assumption: N_H2/A_V = 1e21 mol cm^-2 mag^-1 (Bohlin et al. 1978); dust sublimation temperature 1500 K  
  > "$N_{\rm H_2}/A_{\rm V} =
1\times10^{21}\,{\rm mol}\,{\rm cm}^{-2}\,{\rm mag}^{-1}$" (A CLUMPY torus models)
   - fitting: Bayesian fit; sampler details not stated  
  > "Using a Bayesian approach to fit the data with the `BayesClumpy` tool (Asensio Ramos & Ramos Almeida (cite: Ase09)) the torus model parameters of NGC 1068 were well constrained." (CLUMPY torus models (Appendix A))
   - validation: Compared with 12 micron interferometric size and CO-based gas mass  
  > "For comparison the modeled $12\,\mu$m interferometric half radius of the resolved and unresolved components of NGC 1068 is 1.6 pc" (4.1.2 CLUMPY torus models)
3. **Fit modified blackbody to CND dust SED and derive gas mass**: MBB fit to ALMA Bands 7 and 9 (non-thermal corrected) plus PACS 70/160 micron fluxes; gas mass from dust mass via gas-to-dust ratio  
  > "The SED was fit using a modified black-body (MBB) model to derive the dust temperature ($T_{\rm dust}$), dust mass ($M_{\rm dust}$) and emissivity index ($\beta$) in this region." (4.2 The central r<=400 pc region)
   - assumption: kappa_352GHz = 0.09 m^2/kg; gas-to-dust ratio ~60 from Draine et al. 2007 with 12+log(O/H)~8.8  
  > "$\kappa_{{\rm 352~GHz}}=0.09$ m$^2$ kg$^{-1}$" (4.2 The central r<=400 pc region)
   - fitting: Fit method not specified; errors from range of missing flux  
  > "The errorbars on the parameters of the fit reflect the uncertainties due to the estimated range of missing flux in the ALMA bands" (4.2 The central r<=400 pc region)
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Derive X_CO**: Match CO(1-0) flux (~100 Jy km/s in 10" aperture, PdBI recovers ~80%) to the dust-derived gas mass; scale for 3-2 and 6-5 via line ratios  
  > "is $\sim1/(4^{+6}_{-1})\times X_{\rm CO}^{\rm MW}$" (5.4 The CO-to-H2 conversion factor)
   - assumption: X_CO^MW = 2e20 cm^-2 (K km/s)^-1  
  > "$X_{\rm CO}^{\rm MW}=2\times10^{20}$cm$^{-2}$(K km s$^{-1}$)$^{-1}$" (5.4 The CO-to-H2 conversion factor)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with LVG result of Usero et al. 2004  
  > "A similar conclusion, pointing to an $X_{\rm CO}\sim$ a factor of 4–8 lower than $X_{\rm CO}^{\rm MW}$" (5.4 The CO-to-H2 conversion factor)
5. **Fourier decomposition of CO(3-2) velocity field**: kinemetry fit with 28 ellipses (r=0.5" to 24"), iterative three steps (free PA,i; fixed i; fixed PA and i), harmonics to n=3, minimizing v_nonc  
  > "We used in the fit 28 ellipses with semi major axes covering the disk from $r=0.5\ensuremath{''}$ to $r=24\ensuremath{''}$" (6.1.1 Fourier decomposition of the velocity field)
   - software: kinemetry
   - parameter: Dynamical center = AGN; final PA=289±5 deg, i=41±2 deg  
  > "Finally, we derived the Fourier decomposition of the NGC 1068 velocity field fixing $PA=289\pm5^\circ$ and $i=41\pm2^\circ$ at all radii." (6.1.1 Fourier decomposition of the velocity field)
   - assumption: coplanar orbits at all radii; trailing spiral arms (counterclockwise rotation)  
  > "Implicit in this assumption is that the gas kinematics at all radii can be satisfactorily modeled by coplanar orbits" (6.1.1 Fourier decomposition of the velocity field)
   - fitting: kinemetry minimization of v_nonc  
  > "At each step, `kinemetry` finds the best fitting ellipses by minimizing the contribution of noncircular motions ($v_{\rm nonc}$), evaluated as" (6.1.1 Fourier decomposition of the velocity field)
   - validation: PA and i compared with literature; alternatives (warp, lopsided) tested with s1-s3 diagram  
  > "These values of $\langle PA \rangle$ and $\langle i \rangle$ are compatible within the errors with previous determinations of the disk orientation" (6.1.1 Fourier decomposition of the velocity field)
6. **Estimate outflow mass, rate and energetics**: Integrate CO(3-2) outside residual velocity range [-50,+50] km/s; dM/dt = 3 V_out M_mol/R_out tan(alpha); coplanar outflow tan(alpha)=1/tan(i); also L_kin and momentum flux  
  > "The mass ($M_{\rm mol}$) has been calculated from the CO(3–2) data cube, after subtraction of the projected rotation curve" (6.1.2 Mass outflow rate)
   - parameter: R_out~1.5" (100 pc), V_out~100 km/s for CND  
  > "The average projected radial extent of the outflow in the CND is $R_{\rm out}\sim1.5\ensuremath{''}$ (100 pc)" (6.1.2 Mass outflow rate)
   - assumption: multi-conical uniformly filled outflow; coplanar with disk; X_CO ~1/4 of MW  
  > "If we assume that the outflow is coplanar with the main disk, $\tan(\alpha)=1/\tan(i)$" (6.1.2 Mass outflow rate)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Dense tracers (CO 6-5, HCN, HCO+) p-v diagrams confirm outflow; compared with SFR and AGN power  
  > "An inspection of this figure shows that the outflow signatures identified in CO(3–2) out to radii $r \sim 400$ pc are echoed in CO(6–5), HCN(4–3), and HCO$^+$(4–3)" (6.2 The molecular outflow in dense gas tracers)
7. **Line ratio maps and correlation with X-ray illumination**: Ratio maps in Tmb units at common resolution with 3 sigma clipping; compare HCN/CO and HCO+/CO with Xhard/CO at 0.5" resolution  
  > "Line ratios were obtained assuming a common 3$\sigma$ clipping on the integrated intensities to assure image reliability." (7.1 The SB ring versus the CND)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CND is an off-centered ring in dust and all molecular tracers; HCN, HCO+ and CS emission mostly arises in the CND while CO(3-2) is dominated by the SB ring  
  > "Most of the emission in HCO$^+$, HCN, and CS stems from the CND." (abstract)
- Inward radial flows in SB ring and bar region; outward radial motions in CND and bow-shock arc  
  > "rotation is perturbed by an inward radial flow in the SB ring and the bar region" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Eq. for uniformly filled multi-conical outflow adopted as it gives a more conservative lower limit to outflow rate  
  > "we use Eq. [\[out\]](#out), as it provides a more conservative lower limit to the outflow rate estimated globally over the CND" (6.1.2 Mass outflow rate)
- Alternative: Nuclear warp and non-coplanar lopsided instability (minor merger or internal trigger) considered; warp and minor-merger judged unsatisfactory/implausible  
  > "the simplest nuclear warp scenario described above is not a satisfactory explanation for the velocity residuals observed in this region" (6.4.1 A nuclear warp)
- Alternative: Star formation as driver of outflow rejected based on SFR an order of magnitude lower  
  > "this discrepancy suggests that SF is not able to drive the molecular outflow in the CND of NGC 1068" (6.3 The powering source)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): No short-spacing correction; significant missing flux on large scales  
  > "As our observations do not contain short-spacing correction, we expect that a significant amount of flux will be filtered out" (2.1.1 Band 7 maps)
- Limitation (authors): Outflow rate is a lower limit due to limited velocity coverage  
  > "should be taken as a lower limit" (6.2 The molecular outflow in dense gas tracers)
- Limitation (authors): Resolution does not resolve kinematics of inner r~50 pc  
  > "we cannot spatially resolve the kinematics of the dense molecular gas in the inner $r\sim50$ pc" (8 Summary and conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Outflow rate depends strongly on assumed X_CO (1/4 MW), geometry (coplanar) and V_out/R_out choices
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Coplanar-orbit assumption in kinemetry could bias residuals in the off-centered CND
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Non-thermal contamination of Band 7 and ~30% Band 9 calibration uncertainty affect torus and dust results
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Torus size poorly constrained and ALMA fluxes above model
