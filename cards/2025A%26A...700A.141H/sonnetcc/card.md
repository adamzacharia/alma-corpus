## Analysis recipe

1. **Fit hyperfine structure and derive line parameters and column densities at the peak**: Gaussian fits to the hyperfine structure of the lines towards the column density peak, with LTE column densities  
  > "The line parameters and column densities derived from Gaussian fits to the hyperfine structure of the lines observed towards the column density peak of L1544" (Observed spectra and maps)
   - parameter: Spectra converted to the main-beam brightness temperature scale with beam efficiencies provided by the telescope teams  
  > "The observed spectra were converted to the main-beam brightness temperature scale, $T_{\rm MB}$, using the beam efficiencies provided by the telescope teams." (Observed spectra and maps)
   - assumption: Uniform beam filling, line-of-sight homogeneity, constant Tex for all rotational transitions  
  > "In column density estimates, we assumed uniform beam filling, line-of-sight homogeneity, and that the excitation temperature, $T_{\rm ex}$, is constant for all rotational transitions of the molecule." (Observed spectra and maps)
   - assumption: pNHD2 column density in the optically thin approximation with Tex(para)=Tex(ortho)  
  > "we estimated the column density using the optically thin approximation, adopting the $T_{\rm ex}$ derived for the ortho-line" (Observed spectra and maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared o/p from IRAM and APEX spectra separately  
  > "The o/p-$\rm NH_2D$ ratio from the IRAM observations is $2.7\pm0.2$, whereas from the APEX spectra we obtain o/p-$\rm NH_2D=2.3\pm 0.5$." (Observed spectra and maps)
2. **Build a prolate spheroidal core model of L1544**: Deform the spherical density and temperature model into a prolate spheroid on a cubical grid  
  > "However, because the core is clearly elongated, we deformed the originally spherical model to a prolate spheroid." (Core model); "we constructed a cubical grid with an edge length of $\sim 180\ensuremath{''}$ or 30,600 au" (Core model)
   - parameter: Axis ratio 2.0, long axis tilt -36.1 deg from the declination axis (from Spitzer 8 micron Gaussian fit)  
  > "A Gaussian fit to the Spitzer $8\,\mu$m image of the core gave an axis ratio of 2.0 and a long axis tilt angle of $-36.1\ensuremath{^\circ}$ from the declination axis." (Core model)
   - assumption: Isosurface volumes equal those of the spherical model  
  > "The transformation to the spheroidal model was done so that the volume within an isosurface" (Core model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit constant fractional abundances with radiative transfer**: Simulate spectra with LOC on the spheroid assuming constant fractional abundances and fit to observed spectra  
  > "Line simulations were performed using the Line radiative transfer with the OpenCL (LOC) program (cite: 2020A&A...644A.151J)." (Core model); "The fractional abundances were determined by comparing the simulated spectra with the observed ones" (Core model)
   - software: LOC
   - parameter: Positions (0,0) and (-23'',35'') of the APEX LAsMA footprint  
  > "obtained in the centre of the map (0,0) and the second strongest position $(-23\ensuremath{''},35\ensuremath{''})$" (Core model)
   - assumption: LTE between hyperfine components; line overlap not included  
  > "The hyperfine structure of the lines was modelled by assuming local thermodynamic equilibrium between the components" (Core model)
   - fitting: Minimum chi-square estimation (Harju et al. 2024, Sect. 3.2)  
  > "the method that applies minimum chi-square estimation is described in Sect. 3.2 of (cite: 2024A&A...682A...8H)" (Core model)
   - validation: Only statistical uncertainties from spectral noise are included  
  > "Only the statistical uncertainties based on the noise of the observed spectra are taken into account in this method." (Core model)
4. **Predict abundance distributions with a gas-grain chemistry model (PH vs FS)**: pyRate simulations with PH and FS networks; dark cloud phase then dense core phase in concentric shells  
  > "We assumed that the gas has been processed under low-density ‘dark cloud’ conditions ($n(\rm H_2)=10^4\,\rm cm^{-3}$, $T=10$ K, $A_{\rm V}=5^{\rm mag}$) for half a million years before the ‘dense core’ phase." (Chemistry model)
   - software: pyRate
   - parameter: Grain radii a = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 micron  
  > "The simulations were performed using the grain radii $a=0.1$, 0.2, and $0.3\,\mu$m." (Chemistry model)
   - parameter: Cosmic-ray ionisation rate 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 times 1.3e-17 s^-1  
  > "We also varied the cosmic-ray ionisation rate, testing the values 0.5,1,2,4, and 8 times $1.3\times10^{-17}\,\rm s^{-1}$." (Chemistry model)
   - parameter: Chemical desorption efficiency constant, 1% of exothermic surface reactions  
  > "The efficiency of chemical desorption was assumed to be constant, with 1% exothermic surface reactions leading to desorption." (Chemistry model)
   - assumption: Grain material density 2.5 g cm^-3, uniform grain size, dust-to-gas mass ratio 0.01  
  > "The dust grains were assumed to have uniform size characterised by the grain radius $a$ and the density of the material $\rho$, assumed to be $2.5\,{\rm g}\,\rm cm^{-3}$." (Chemistry model)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Static core model neglects contraction while the chemistry evolves  
  > "We think the difference is attributable to the static core model, which neglects the contraction of the core at the same time as the chemistry is evolving." (Simulated spectra)
5. **Compare simulated spectra from chemistry models with APEX and IRAM lines**: Simulate NH2D and NHD2 spectra from predicted abundances and find best-fit simulation time for PH and FS  
  > "For models assuming $a=0.2\,\mu$m and $\zeta_{\rm H_2}=2.6\times10^{-17}\,\rm s^{-1}$ (top panel), the best-fit simulation time is $3.2\times10^5$ yr." (Simulated spectra)
   - software: LOC, pyRate
   - parameter: Best fit at zeta = 1.04e-16 s^-1 reached after 3-4e4 yr  
  > "At high values of $\zeta_{\rm H_2}$ chemical reactions proceed rapidly and the 333 GHz lines reach the observed intensities already after $3-4\times10^4$ yr of simulation time." (Simulated spectra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: PH reproduces both 333 GHz lines simultaneously; FS does not  
  > "Taking a closer look at the spectra one notices that the PH model can better reproduce both 333 GHz lines simultaneously." (Simulated spectra)
6. **Fit ortho/para abundances with an ad hoc density-dependent abundance profile using APEX, IRAM and ALMA**: Fractional abundances constant in n_H2 = 5e4-5e5 cm^-3 and decreasing at lower and higher density, abundances of each spin species varied independently  
  > "We ran another series of simulations assuming that the fractional abundances of ortho- and para-$\rm NH_2D$ and $\rm NHD_2$ are constant in the density range $n_{\rm H_2}=5\times10^4-5\times10^5\,\rm cm^{-3}$" (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile); "The fractional abundances of o$\rm NH_2D$, p$\rm NH_2D$, o$\rm NHD_2$, and p$\rm NHD_2$ (in the flat region) were independently varied" (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)
   - software: LOC
   - parameter: Abundances proportional to n^-2 in the inner parts of the core (steeper than n^-1 needed for ALMA); n in the outer part  
  > "We obtained rather a good agreement with all the observed spectra by assuming that the fractional $\rm NH_2D$ and $\rm NHD_2$ abundances are proportional to $n^{-2}$ in the inner parts of the core." (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile); "We first assumed that fractional abundances are proportional to $n^{-1}$ and $n$ in these two regions." (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)
   - assumption: Spheroidal core model from Core model section used for the radiative transfer  
  > "the simulated spectra of the spheroidal core model were compared with observations" (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)
   - fitting: chi-square statistic as in Harju et al. 2024  
  > "The best-fit abundances and their uncertainties were estimated by evaluating the $\chi^2$ statistic as explained in (cite: 2024A&A...682A...8H)." (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)
   - validation: Simulated spectra compared with observed spectra at seven LAsMA positions and along two ALMA strips  
  > "The simulated spectra from the best-fit model towards the seven positions observed with APEX/LAsMA are shown in Figs." (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)

## Qualitative findings

- PH simulations agree systematically better with the observations than FS simulations  
  > "Simulations using the PH reaction scheme show systematically better agreement with the observations than simulations with the FS model." (abstract)
- Spin ratios of NH2D and NHD2 in L1544 are consistent with nuclear spin statistical weights (3 and 2)  
  > "This means that it is reasonable to assume that the o/p ratios of $\rm NH_2D$ and $\rm NHD_2$ in L1544 are simply determined by the nuclear spin statistical weights." (Discussion: Ortho/para ratios)
- The 0.9 mm ground-state lines observed with the same beam and spectrometer band are the most reliable o/p probes  
  > "the ground-state lines of $\rm NH_2D$ and $\rm NHD_2$ at the wavelength $\lambda=0.9$ mm that are observable with the same beam and in the same spectrometer band are the most reliable probes of the o/p ratios" (abstract)
- Single-dish and ALMA data are consistent with strong pNH2D depletion within 10'' (1700 au) of the centre  
  > "which show that p$\rm NH_2D$ becomes heavily depleted within $10\ensuremath{''}$ (1700 au) from the centre of L1544" (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Repeat the Ophiuchus spin-ratio measurement in a quiescent environment  
  > "Therefore, it seems helpful to repeat the measurement of the spin ratios in a quiescent environment." (Introduction)
- Why this method: The inner density threshold of the ad hoc abundance profile is set from the earlier ALMA observations of L1544  
  > "The high threshold used in L1544 is based on the observations of (cite: 2022ApJ...929...13C)." (Simulations with an ad hoc abundance profile)
- Alternative: A varying grain radius from a coagulation model gave no significant change relative to the standard model  
  > "We also performed simulations with a varying grain radius according to the coagulation model of (cite: 2009A&A...502..845O), but these did not result in significant changes relative to our standard model." (Discussion: Distributions of NH2D and NHD2)
- Alternative: A high cosmic-ray ionisation rate (about 1e-16 s^-1) was judged less trustworthy than the lower value from molecular ions  
  > "the estimate of these authors is probably more trustworthy than the high value obtained here" (Discussion: Distributions of NH2D and NHD2)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Rest frequencies of the NHD2 lines are probably less accurate than those of the NH2D lines
