## Analysis recipe

1. **Find the MIRI extremely red object**: SExtractor hot-mode on the MIRI F1000W image as detection image, MAG_AUTO catalog, cross-matched to JADES DR2 and selected on colour and S/N  
  > "directly using the MIRI/F1000W map as detection image" (Identification of the MERO Virgil); "We cross-match our catalog (using a 0.2$''$ search radius) to the official JADES DR2 catalog published by" (Identification of the MERO Virgil)
   - software: SExtractor
   - parameter: F444W - F1000W >= 1.5 and F1000W S/N >= 5; 0.2'' match radius  
  > "to only consider those sources showing a red F444W - F1000W color ($\rm F444W - F1000W \geq 1.5$) and a robust F1000W detection (S/N$~ \geq 5$)" (Identification of the MERO Virgil)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Spectroscopic redshift from MUSE**: Extract MUSE 1D spectrum in circular apertures at the target position, identify the Lyman-alpha feature, adopt the MUSE catalog redshift  
  > "we extract the MUSE 1D spectrum at the coordinates of our target within circular apertures of different sizes ($r = ~$0.4$''$, 0.8$''$)" (Redshift assessment of Virgil)
   - parameter: Feature at ~9280 A, expected Lyman-alpha at z ~ 6.63  
  > "falls at the expected wavelength of the Ly$\alpha$ at $z \simeq 6.63$ (i.e. $\approx 9280$ Å" (Redshift assessment of Virgil)
   - assumption: Adopted z_spec = 6.6312 +/- 0.0019 for the whole analysis  
  > "Therefore, we assume $z_{spec} = 6.6312\pm 0.0019$ as" (Redshift assessment of Virgil)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement with the public MUSE catalog of detected sources  
  > "is in agreement with the publicly available catalog of MUSE-detected sources by" (Redshift assessment of Virgil)
3. **Model and remove the z=4.77 foreground LAE**: Fit Sersic profiles to the two foreground components with AstroPhot while masking Virgil, adding a third Sersic component where residuals required it, subtract in all filters  
  > "we model and subtract its contribution at all wavelengths" (Removal of Virgil's contaminant); "with single Sérsic profiles while masking" (Removal of Virgil's contaminant)
   - software: AstroPhot
   - parameter: Optional third Sersic component between the two foreground sources  
  > "it is necessary to introduce an optional third Sérsic component between" (Removal of Virgil's contaminant)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Residuals of the best-fit models inspected in several NIRCam and MIRI filters  
  > "we present the residuals of the best-fit models obtained with ASTROPHOT for some of the available NIRCam and MIRI filters." (Removal of Virgil's contaminant)
4. **Measure the morphology**: Radial surface brightness profiles compared with filter PSFs; Sersic fits to F200W, F277W, F444W and F560W profiles  
  > "and other close-by sources, the extracted surface brightness profile shows that" (Virgil's morphology); "to the SB radial profiles of the different broad-band NIRCam and MIRI filters covering" (Virgil's morphology)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with Sersic indices and sizes expected for galaxies at z ~ 6  
  > "Both parameters are in agreement with the expected values of galaxy sizes and Sérsic indices at $z \simeq 6$" (Virgil's morphology)
5. **Extract multi-band photometry**: Kron-aperture photometry with SEP in 31 HST/JWST bands, aperture correction with PSF curves of growth, Galactic extinction correction, errors from random apertures  
  > "photometry in the wavelength range 0.4 – 25 $\mu$m with SEP" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry); "Then, we correct the extracted fluxes to account for the missing flux outside the Kron aperture." (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - software: SEP, WebbPSF
   - parameter: Kron parameter K = 2.5, minimum radius 1.75''  
  > "allowing the Kron parameters to vary from filter to filter and replicating standard SEXTRACTOR settings, i.e. $K = 2.5$ and a minimum radius $r = 1.75\ensuremath{''}$" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - parameter: Errors: 1000 random Kron apertures per filter in a 6''x6'' region; minimum error 0.05 mag  
  > "drawing in each filter 1000 random Kron apertures on the sky region around" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry); "Finally, we impose as a minimum error on the photometry a value of 0.05 mag for all bands." (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - assumption: Upper limits (3 sigma) used above 15 um where Virgil is undetected  
  > "Hence, above 15 $\mu$m we make use of the 3$\sigma$ upper limits on the flux density." (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Photometric redshifts from the photometry compared with the MUSE spectroscopic redshift (step 7)  
  > "Overall, we find a very good agreement with the spectroscopic estimate from MUSE." (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
6. **Check for ALMA continuum emission**: Inspect the downloaded ASPECS 1.2 mm and 3.0 mm maps at the position of Virgil; non-detection gives 3 sigma upper limits  
  > "at (sub-)millimeter wavelengths leveraging on the available deep ASPECS ALMA dust continuum imaging at 1.2 and 3.0 mm" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry); "This finding implies 3$\sigma$ upper limits on the flux density at 1.2 mm and 3.0 mm of 27.9 $\mu$Jy and 4.2 $\mu$Jy, respectively" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Photometric-redshift test of the decontaminated photometry**: EAZY (minimum chi2, no prior, z=0-20, photometry up to 10 um) and CIGALE (stellar templates, HST+NIRCam only)  
  > "We do not impose any prior and work with minimum $\chi^{2}$ photometric redshift estimates in the redshift range $z = 0-20$" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry); "we run it only considering stellar templates and HST + JWST/NIRCam photometry" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - software: EAZY, CIGALE
   - parameter: EAZY v1.3 templates incl. dusty high-EW emission-line galaxy and LRD / AGN+torus models  
  > "For Eazy, we adopt the v1.3 templates, which include a dusty galaxy with a high-equivalent width emission line spectrum" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement with MUSE z_spec  
  > "Overall, we find a very good agreement with the spectroscopic estimate from MUSE." (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
8. **Check for gravitational lensing by the foreground LAE**: Estimate magnification with lenstronomy using the foreground stellar masses, point-mass and extended mass models  
  > "we resort to the usage of the LENSTRONOMY software" (Possible galaxy - galaxy lensing effect on Virgil); "the Einstein radius is $\sim 5$ mas using a point-mass approximation, and $\lesssim 20$ mas when assuming extended mass distributions" (Possible galaxy - galaxy lensing effect on Virgil)
   - software: lenstronomy
   - assumption: Total-mass-to-stellar-mass ratios up to 10 for both foreground galaxies as an extreme case  
  > "even under extreme assumptions for the total-mass-to-stellar-mass ratios of 10 for both foreground galaxies" (Possible galaxy - galaxy lensing effect on Virgil)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **SED fitting with and without an AGN**: Fit the full photometry (including ALMA upper limits) with CIGALE, Bagpipes, prospector and synthesizer-AGN, each with and without an AGN component  
  > "we decide to run the codes both excluding and considering the presence of an AGN" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil); "For the SED fitting, we consider all the photometry available, including the ALMA upper limits at 1.2 and 3mm." (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)
   - software: CIGALE, Bagpipes, prospector, synthesizer-AGN, CLOUDY
   - parameter: CIGALE: delayed-tau SFH with two stellar populations; ne = 10, 100, 1000 cm^-3; log U = -2, -1  
  > "We first employ CIGALE assuming a delayed exponentially declining SFH (delayed-$\tau$ model) modelled with two stellar populations." (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil); "allowing the electron density $n_e$ and ionisation parameter $U$ to assume values of $n_e = 10, 100, 1000\rm~cm^{-3}$ and $\log_{10}(U) = -2,-1$, respectively" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)
   - parameter: Bagpipes: two-component delayed-tau SFH, log U in (-4,-2), AGN component  
  > "The ionisation parameter $\log_{10}(U)$ is set to vary between $(-4,\, -2)$." (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil); "The SFH is set to two components delayed-$\tau$ model" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)
   - parameter: prospector: 5 temporal bins, non-parametric SFH with continuity prior  
  > "we use 5 temporal bins to model the star-formation history non-parametrically while applying the continuity prior from" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)
   - parameter: synthesizer-AGN: two delayed-exponential populations, timescales 1 Myr-1 Gyr, A(V) 0-10 mag each  
  > "each one described by a delayed exponential function with timescales between 1 Myr and 1 Gyr, and with ages from 1 Myr up to the age of the Universe at the redshift of the source" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil); "with A(V) values ranging from 0 to 10 mag for each population" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF in CIGALE and synthesizer-AGN (0.1-100 Msun); Kroupa IMF in Bagpipes; solar and 0.2 Zsun metallicities in CIGALE  
  > "We adopt the stellar populations models with both solar and sub-solar metallicity ($Z = 0.2 Z_\odot$), and the Chabrier IMF." (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil); "assuming a Chabrier IMF with stellar mass limits between 0.1 and 100 M$_\odot$" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared chi2_red between stars-only and stars+AGN fits; all codes prefer AGN  
  > "delivering better quality fits (i.e. lower $\chi^2_{\rm red}$)" (Stellar Populations and AGN models)
10. **Derive photometric indicators and constraints**: UV slope and M(UV) from a power-law fit to the HST+NIRCam fluxes; EW of Hbeta+[OIII] from F356W excess; NIR power-law index over 4-10 um; graybody fit to MIRI residuals; alpha_ox upper limit from Chandra limit  
  > "by fitting a power law to the observed fluxes ($f_\lambda \propto \lambda^{\beta_{\rm UV}+2}$), we estimate a UV continuum slope" (Virgil's properties without MIRI); "By fitting a power law ($f_\lambda \propto \lambda^{\alpha_{\rm NIR}}$) to the observed fluxes in the wavelength range $4 - 10\mu$m (observed)" (Virgil's full SED); "to the residuals of the MIRI datapoints, i.e. after subtracting the best-fit models derived by these three different codes to the observed photometry" (Only Stellar Populations models)
   - parameter: Graybody fit with beta = 1.5; continuum under Hbeta+[OIII] taken as mean of F335M and F410M  
  > "(if we assume $\beta = 1.5$)" (Only Stellar Populations models); "If we assume the continuum below H$\beta$ + \[O III\] as the average value between the F356W adjoining filters F335M and F410M" (Virgil's properties without MIRI)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: EW value compared with literature for z >~ 6 emitters  
  > "This value is in good agreement with recent literature for emitters at $z \gtrsim 6$" (Virgil's properties without MIRI)
11. **Estimate AGN bolometric luminosity and black hole mass**: L_AGN,bol from the dust-corrected AGN L(5100 A) using the best-fit CIGALE and synthesizer-AGN models; M_BH from the Eddington relation  
  > "we derive $L_{\rm AGN,bol}$ from the monochromatic luminosity at 5100Å (corrected for dust extinction) $L$(5100Å) as $L_{\rm AGN, bol} \approx 9 \cdot \lambda \cdot L$(5100Å)" (Stellar Populations and AGN models); "we can infer $M_{\rm BH}$ via the relation $M_{\rm BH} = 7.9 \times 10^{-39} \times (L_{\rm AGN,bol} / \lambda_{\rm Edd})$" (Stellar Populations and AGN models)
   - software: CIGALE, synthesizer-AGN
   - parameter: AGN contributes 40 percent of observed 5100 A emission; A(5100 A) ~ 3.3 mag (CIGALE)  
  > "at 5100Å (rest-frame) the AGN contributes only to 40 percent of the observed emission" (Stellar Populations and AGN models); "dust attenuation is $A(5100{\rm \AA}) \approx 3.3\rm~mag$" (Stellar Populations and AGN models)
   - assumption: Eddington ratio 0.1-1  
  > "Considering typical values for the Eddington ratio $\lambda_{\rm Edd} = 0.1 - 1$" (Stellar Populations and AGN models)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Agreement with bolometric luminosities of z > 4 JWST AGNs  
  > "These estimates are in good agreement with the values of bolometric luminosity of AGNs at $z > 4$ recently studied with JWST" (Stellar Populations and AGN models)
12. **Classify Virgil as a Little Red Dot**: Apply NIRCam colour criteria and compactness criterion, compare with stacked LRD SEDs and LRD spectra  
  > "where the values in parentheses indicate the selection criteria from" (Virgil, an LRD?); "we compare its photometry to empirical SEDs derived from stacked LRDs, including the average SED of 20 MIRI-detected LRDs in the HUDF" (Virgil, an LRD?)
   - parameter: Compactness c = f(r=0.2'')/f(r=0.1'') from F444W after decontamination, criterion c < 1.7  
  > "The estimate of the compactness parameter ($c = f(r=0.2\ensuremath{''})/f(r=0.1\ensuremath{''})$ with fluxes properly corrected for aperture)" (Virgil, an LRD?)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Brown-dwarf contamination rejected by colour criteria and extended morphology  
  > "Additionally, its extended morphology further rules out the brown dwarf hypothesis." (Virgil, an LRD?)

- **SExtractor** : Source detection on the MIRI F1000W image and catalog of MAG_AUTO magnitudes

- **WebbPSF** : JWST PSF models for curve-of-growth aperture corrections  
  > "For the JWST filters, we employ the PSF models by WebbPSF" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
- **lenstronomy** : Magnification of Virgil by the foreground LAE  
  > "we resort to the usage of the LENSTRONOMY software" (Possible galaxy - galaxy lensing effect on Virgil)
- **EAZY** : Photometric redshift of Virgil and Beatrix with v1.3 templates  
  > "For Eazy, we adopt the v1.3 templates, which include a dusty galaxy with a high-equivalent width emission line spectrum" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
- **CIGALE** : Photometric redshift check and SED fitting with/without SKIRTOR AGN

- **synthesizer-AGN** : SED fitting with stellar populations and AGN accretion disk plus torus

  > "The nebular emission is modelled using the CLOUDY" (Spectral energy distribution analysis of Virgil)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Publicly available ancillary photometry and spectroscopy (including ASPECS) complement the MIRI imaging  
  > "We complement our MIRI observations at 10 $\mu$m with a rich set of photometric and spectroscopic data publicly available." (Datasets / Ancillary data)
- Why this method: SExtractor hot mode chosen for faint-source detection  
  > "which is particularly effective for detecting extremely faint sources" (Identification of the MERO Virgil)
- Alternative: Lensing correction not applied because the magnification is minimal  
  > "Due to the minimum value of the retrieved magnification, we do not consider corrections for lensing in the following analysis." (Possible galaxy - galaxy lensing effect on Virgil)
- Alternative: Brown dwarf interpretation rejected on morphology and colour  
  > "Additionally, its extended morphology further rules out the brown dwarf hypothesis." (Virgil, an LRD?)
- Alternative: Beatrix not studied further because its redshift is uncertain  
  > "we do not further investigate this object" (Extraction of Virgil's photometry)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Small size, coarse MIRI resolution and contaminant prevent testing the two-component dusty starburst scenario  
  > "prevent us, however, from further investigating this scenario based on the currently available data" (Only Stellar Populations models)
- Limitation (authors): Shallow MIRI depth above 18 um prevents confirming the flattening  
  > "The shallower depth of the available MIRI filters $\lambda > 18~\mu$m prevents us from robustly confirming the flattening on a longer wavelength range." (The impact of long-wavelengths MIRI filters)
- Limitation (authors): Depth estimates not corrected for aperture  
  > "The depth estimates are not corrected for aperture." (Introduction)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA is used only for two upper limits; how they were derived (aperture, position, primary-beam correction) is not described and they are cited to other papers
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Text and tables disagree in places: synthesizer-AGN stars-only chi2_red is 6.9 in the text and 6.5 in the table; CIGALE AGN-fit stellar mass error is 0.2 dex in the text and 0.02 in the table
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Best-fit chi2_red values of 5-25 indicate poor fits for all codes, so the stars-versus-AGN preference rests on comparing poor fits
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Neither AGN nor starburst is confirmed; no spectroscopy, so the LRD classification relies on photometry and one criterion fails
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Absolute photometric depth and flux scale of the ALMA maps are not discussed
