## Analysis recipe

1. **Assemble multi-facility SED photometry**: Photometry from archival and new observations within four years of the SOFIA epoch, interpolated or averaged where needed, with non-detections as 5 sigma upper limits  
  > "In order to get the best possible wavelength coverage, we select data within four years of the SOFIA observations." (Observations); "Non-detection fluxes are given as 5$\sigma$ upper limits, where $\sigma$ is the root-mean-square noise in a region where BLG-360 should be observed." (Table 1)
   - software: CASA, FORCAST Redux, HAWC DRP
   - parameter: FORCAST aperture photometry with 12-pixel apertures  
  > "Aperture photometry was performed on level 3 pipeline products using large apertures of 12 pixels." (FORCAST)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Alternative PSF-fitting photometry for FORCAST shows fluctuations are consistent with noise  
  > "The PSF photometry shows that the fluctuations are due to noise within the large FORCAST photometry extraction aperture." (Alternative photometry extraction)
2. **Measure ACA Band 7 continuum and CO (3-2) limit**: Integrated continuum flux and deconvolved size from ACA data; CO upper limit from spatial rms of the background in 200 km/s bins  
  > "By measuring the spatial root-mean-square (rms; denoted as $\sigma$) of the background in 200 km s$^{-1}$ bins, we get a 5$\sigma$ upper limit on the circumstellar CO flux of 13.5 mJy" (ALMA/ACA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: natural weighting, clean beam 4.84 x 2.79 arcsec  
  > "deconvolved from a clean beam of 4.84$''$ $\times$ 2.79$''$ in natural weighting" (ALMA/ACA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Revise extinction and compute total flux**: Mean K-band extinction of 100 nearby Miras converted to E(B-V); total extinction-corrected flux integrated over the SED  
  > "A total of 100 Miras sources were used, which show little variation in estimated extinction, with a standard deviation of 0.17 mag." (Extinction and reddening)
   - assumption: A_Ks/A_V=0.118 and R_V=3.1 (CCM89); adopted E(B-V)=1.83 mag  
  > "Therefore, we adopt the $E(B-V)$ value of 1.83 mag, derived from nearby Miras, for our analysis." (Extinction and reddening)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Initial 1-D DUSTY SED fit**: Single spherical dust shell fitted manually, model SED scaled to the maximum observed flux  
  > "Then, we manually adjusted the parameters to better reproduce the observations. As the output SED of DUSTY is dimensionless, we scaled the model SED fluxes to the maximum value of our observed SED." (DUSTY)
   - software: DUSTY
   - parameter: Teff 4500 K, R* 661 Rsun, r_out 20 pc, inner-boundary dust temperature 300 K  
  > "| 4500 | 661 | 208 | 20 | 300 | 0.30 | 1.00 | 2 | –1.1 |" (DUSTY)
   - assumption: Spherically symmetric circumstellar dust  
  > "which was also based on the assumption that the circumstellar dust was spherically symmetric" (DUSTY)
   - fitting: Manual adjustment of parameters  
  > "Then, we manually adjusted the parameters to better reproduce the observations." (DUSTY)
   - validation: Rejected because the shell extends to 20 pc, inconsistent with typical circumstellar sizes  
  > "The dust shell, however, extended out to 20 pc from the central star, which is inconsistent with typical circumstellar sizes of evolved stars (cite: fong2006)." (SED modelling)
5. **Manual RADMC-3D SED fit with single-shell, two-shell and cavity geometries**: Three spherical geometries (S, D, CD) with a point-source blackbody star, grains mixed with Mie theory; silicate type, grain ratios, shell radii and density scaling varied by hand  
  > "We examined three different dust structures: a single shell of continuous dust (S), a two-shell structure (D), and the same D geometry but with a cavity with zero dust density between the inner and outer dust shell (CD)." (SED modelling); "We mix the grains using Mie theory via python routines supplied in the RADMC-3D package." (SED modelling)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - parameter: Box grid -10000 to 10000 AU, 800 points per axis, 25 AU per cell  
  > "We use a grid resolution of 800 points on each axis, giving a spatial resolution of 25 AU per grid cell." (SED modelling)
   - parameter: Dust sublimation temperature 1700 K  
  > "We set the dust sublimation temperature arbitrarily at 1700 K, and remove any dust that exceeds this temperature from the model." (SED modelling)
   - parameter: Density gradient fixed at 2.00  
  > "to 2.00, as larger values increase the optical depth to an extent where the calculation time increases significantly" (SED modelling)
   - assumption: Star treated as a point-source blackbody and sole heat source; spherical geometry  
  > "We treat the central star as a point source, which is the only heat source in the system." (SED modelling)
   - assumption: Only one silicate type per model; same opacity curves for all dust within each model  
  > "For the silicate dust, we only implement one grain type, and do not mix different silicate grains together in any single model." (SED modelling)
   - fitting: Manual fit; no sampler or likelihood stated  
  > "to manually fit the SED" (SED modelling)
   - validation: Models consistent with observed fluxes within 5 sigma; some FIR overprediction  
  > "The models are, however, consistent with the observed fluxes within 5$\sigma$ uncertainties." (SED modelling)
6. **Derive distance and luminosity from the best model**: Equate blackbody model luminosity to 4 pi d^2 F and solve for d  
  > "We then rearrange Equation [\[eq: luminosity\]](#eq: luminosity) for $d$ to calculate the distance." (SED modelling)
   - assumption: Star is a blackbody with model R* and Teff  
  > "where $\sigma_{\rm SB}$ is the Stefan-Boltzmann constant and $d$ is the distance to the star." (SED modelling)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Test iron versus carbon and silicate type**: Swap equal quantity of iron for amorphous carbon (cd1dl vs cd4dl) and compare four silicate types in the 10 micron profile  
  > "The only difference is that we exchange the same quantity of iron dust for amorphous carbon dust" (Mineralogy: iron vs carbon)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Carbon model gives too high opacity below ~17 micron and no 10 micron feature  
  > "The result is that our opacities are too high for wavelengths $\lesssim$17 $\mu$m, resulting in underestimated NIR fluxes and no 10 $\mu$m feature." (Mineralogy: iron vs carbon)
8. **Rule out a disk geometry and test stellar spectrum**: Compare Hyperion disk models (sp-hmi, spu-hmi) from SED Fitter to the observed SED; replace blackbody with BT-Settl synthetic spectra  
  > "We compared SED models calculated using the Hyperion code (cite: robitaille2011hyperion) and different dust geometries." (Spherical vs disk geometry); "The 3500 K spectrum produces an almost identical SED to the `cd1ol` SED, in which the star was modelled as a 3000 K blackbody." (Stellar parameters)
   - software: SED Fitter, Hyperion, RADMC-3D
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Disk models give much larger FIR/sub-mm excess and significant optical emission, unlike the observations  
  > "The shape of the SED, coupled with the fact that BLG-360 is not visible at optical wavelengths, immediately rules out a disk geometry dominating the dust structure of BLG-360." (Spherical vs disk geometry)
9. **Model the 2010 SED to trace evolution**: Refit the T13 2010 SED with RADMC-3D starting from cd1ol, changing only inner-shell density and extent and stellar properties  
  > "We therefore changed only the density distribution, extent of the inner shell, and the properties of the star." (Remnant evolution)
   - software: RADMC-3D
   - assumption: Dust composition and grain size unchanged; outer shell unchanged  
  > "We assume that the dust composition and grain size did not change." (Remnant evolution)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Smooth evolution seen in 2011-2017 NIR/IR photometry  
  > "suggest smooth evolution of the object between the 2010 and 2017–2023 SED epochs" (Remnant evolution)

## Qualitative findings

- No gas lines (CO from ACA/SMA, K-band atomic and molecular) are detected, unlike other red nova remnants  
  > "Another remarkable feature of the BLG-360 remnant is the lack of spectroscopic signatures of gas, atomic or molecular" (The anomaly of BLG-360)
- The 10 micron absorption needs both olivine silicates and alumina; a silicate-only feature is too narrow  
  > "We find that a silicate to alumina ratio of 3:2 is able to reproduce the depth and width of the 10 $\mu$m profile" (Mineralogy: silicate dust)
- The outer shell likely formed from pre-merger mass loss  
  > "Therefore, material that formed the outer shell must have been lost from the star for many years prior to the merger event" (Dust extent)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Sub-mm and far-IR coverage is needed to constrain the Rayleigh-Jeans tail and the extended dust  
  > "Indeed, if we implemented a more compact outer shell, the slope of the Rayleigh-Jeans tail would be steeper and so the sub-mm fluxes, as well as the SOFIA-HAWC+ fluxes, would be underestimated." (Dust extent)
- Why this method: DUSTY gave an unphysical 20 pc shell, so a 3-D code (RADMC-3D) was used for a closer physical representation  
  > "To get a closer physical representation of the SED, we used RADMC-3D" (SED modelling)
- Alternative: FeO, MgS and amorphous carbon grains rejected as not fitting the observations  
  > "We also examined possible contributions from FeO (cite: henning1995), MgS (cite: begemann1994), and amorphous carbon (cite: preibisch1993) grains but find these do not fit our observations." (SED modelling)
- Alternative: Single DUSTY shell rejected because it extends to 20 pc  
  > "The dust shell, however, extended out to 20 pc from the central star, which is inconsistent with typical circumstellar sizes of evolved stars (cite: fong2006)." (SED modelling)
- How they decided it was enough: No better-fitting model found within the simplifying assumptions  
  > "It was very difficult, if not impossible, to find better-fitting models within our simplifying assumptions." (SED modelling)
- Limitation (authors): Models overpredict FIR fluxes and miss SOFIA flux dips  
  > "Our models slightly overpredict the FIR fluxes at 20–220 $\mu$m and do not reproduce the flux variations seen in data gathered with SOFIA" (SED modelling)
- Limitation (authors): ACA deconvolved size is likely only an upper limit  
  > "This deconvolved size is likely just an upper limit." (ALMA/ACA)
- Limitation (authors): Spherical geometry assumed to limit free parameters  
  > "In order to limit the number of free parameters, we assumed in our RADMC-3D models a spherical geometry for the dust shells." (Spherical vs disk geometry)
- Limitation (authors): Stellar and dust constraints carry large uncertainties from both observations and method  
  > "Although we are able to make some constraints on the stellar properties of the merger remnant, these constraints carry some intrinsic uncertainty, both from the observational errors and the methodology." (Caveats)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model fits are manual with no goodness-of-fit statistic, so parameter degeneracies (Teff, distance, silicate type, iron fraction) are not quantified
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SED combines epochs spanning several years for a variable source
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA ACA contributes a single broad-band flux, so the Rayleigh-Jeans slope relies on sparse SOFIA and SMA data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust composition conclusions depend on available opacity tables for metallic iron and alumina
