  > "have the ability to trace the inner hot gas near the central star." (Cyanoacetylene)

  > "We notice that the integrated intensity maps of different COMs have a peak at the position of the continuum." (Spatial distribution of detected COMs); "we observed that the detected emission lines of different COMs arise from the extended envelope of the protostar." (Conclusion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Earlier work reported only a few lines of these molecules toward IRAS 18089 without abundances, so a detailed study was needed; archival ACA data were used  
  > "This indicates that another detailed molecular spectral line study is needed to understand the abundance and prebiotic chemistry of different COMs towards IRAS 18089." (Introduction)
- Why this method: LTE modelling justified by the high gas density of the warm inner region  
  > "The LTE assumption is valid in this case as the gas density of the warm inner region of the target is 1.3$\times$10$^{7}$ cm$^{-3}$ (cite: san21)." (Spectral line analysis of IRAS 18089)
- How they decided it was enough: Lines counted as detections when above 5 sigma and non-blended in the LTE model  
  > "all detected emission lines of exhibit above 5$\sigma$ significance." (Ethyl cyanide)
- Limitation (authors): Higher-resolution observations with the 12-m array are needed to examine the distribution and morphology of the COMs; emission is unresolved here  
  > "Further high-resolution observations of these lines are required, possibly using an ALMA 12-m array, to examine the distribution and chemical morphology of COMs in the IRAS 18089 region." (Spatial distribution of detected COMs)
- Limitation (authors): One methyl formate line is blended and not fitted well; formamide rests on one transition  
  > "is not fitted well because that emission line is blended with" (Methyl formate); "We do not observe any other transition lines of in the entire data." (Formamide)
- Limitation (authors): The paper itself says a single-line detection is not conclusive evidence of a molecule (said of an earlier SMA detection of methyl formate)  
  > "such detection of a single transition line of one complex molecule could not be considered as conclusive evidence of the presence of the molecule in high-mass protostars." (Possible formation mechanism of COMs)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Quoted physical scales of about 29 pc for emitting regions of about 12 arcsec are inconsistent with 2.34 kpc (about 0.14 pc); the table uses cosmological parameters for a Galactic source
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Emitting regions of about 12.1'' equal the beam major axis, yet the text claims both an extended envelope and an unresolved source; deconvolution via Eq. 5 yields sizes at the beam limit
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Formamide column density and abundance rest on one transition while the paper calls single-line detections inconclusive; excitation temperature is then unconstrained
- Caveat (inferred, ours): A single Tex per species and 12'' ACA beam mix hot-core and envelope gas; LTE source size or filling factor is not given, so column densities are beam-averaged and model dependent
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abundances are divided by a beam-averaged N(H2) built on assumed T_d = 30 K, beta = 1.6 and Z = 0.01 with only a measurement error quoted; continuum RMS (26.63 mJy/beam) is larger than the quoted peak uncertainty (6.5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 'Nearly similar' to the Suzuki model abundances is not quantified; stated pairs differ by factors of several (for example 7.5e-11 observed versus 1.1e-11 modelled if matched to cyanoacetylene), and the species-to-model mapping is lost in the text
