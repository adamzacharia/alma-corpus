## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine 12 m, 7 m and TP data into line cubes**: Custom CASA procedure: clean 12m+7m, regrid TP, primary-beam weight, feather clean components with TP, final clean with feathered model  
  > "We use a custom procedure, which combines the 12 m, 7 m, and total power array data, to image all of our spectral lines." (3.1 Spectral line imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: natural weighting, 0.2 arcsec pixel, multiscale [0,1,5,10,15]  
  > "All imaging is done with natural weighting to maximize sensitivity, a pixel size of 0.2 arcsec" (3.1 Spectral line imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Tested direct Fourier-plane feather combination; no clear improvement  
  > "did not find any clear improvements in image quality" (3.1 Spectral line imaging)
2. **Fit modified blackbody to SED for dust properties**: MCMC fit of 22–1110 um spectrum with modified blackbody; ALMA flux excluded  
  > "we fit (using a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm) the broad-band sub-mm through IR (22–1110 $\mu$m; see Table [1](#table:mwemiss)) spectrum" (4.1 Continuum Emission)
   - assumption: kappa=0.1 cm2/g (nu/THz)^beta, includes gas-to-dust 100  
  > "Note that this value of the dust opacity includes a gas-to-dust ratio of 100 by mass." (4.1 Continuum Emission)
   - fitting: MCMC; priors not stated  
  > "we fit (using a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithm) the broad-band sub-mm through IR (22–1110 $\mu$m; see Table [1](#table:mwemiss)) spectrum, with the modified black-body function" (Results, Continuum Emission from IRAS 19132+1035)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit Gaussian line profiles and compute line ratios/PV diagrams**: Gaussian components fit to spectra in regions A-D; uncertainties from median absolute deviation of line-free points; 12CO/13CO ratio map; PV slices through jet feature  
  > "we have estimated the uncertainties on the spectral data points for each line by taking the median absolute deviation of spectral data points well away from the line emission" (4.2 CO line emission)
   - software: spectral-cube, pvextractor
   - fitting: Gaussian fits  
  > "we fit each detected line with one or more Gaussian components" (Results, CO line emission from IRAS 19132+1035)
   - validation: Compared with IRAM 30m results of Chaty et al.  
  > "Our new spectral measurements of the CO emission in the IRAS 19132+1035 region are consistent with those reported in this previous study" (4.2 CO line emission)
4. **Derive H2CO temperature map**: Simultaneous RADEX grid fit of three H2CO lines where 303-202 >0.25 K; density fixed at 1e4 cm-3  
  > "we choose to fix the density at the central value of $n=10^{4}\,{\rm cm}^{-3}$, and refit the spectrum" (4.4 H2CO line emission)
   - software: PYSPECKIT, RADEX
   - assumption: Density fixed due to T-n degeneracy  
  > "This result is likely due to the degeneracies between the temperature and density parameters in the model." (4.4 H2CO line emission)
   - fitting: five-parameter model fit  
  > "to fit a five parameter model to the ${\rm H}_{2}{\rm CO}$ spectrum: line intensity, line width, temperature, density, and column density." (Results, H2CO line emission from IRAS 19132+1035)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **C18O column density and H2 mass**: LTE optically thin C18O with Tex from optically thick 12CO; H2/C18O=1.5e6  
  > "yields a ratio of $\mathrm{H_2/C^{18}O} = 1.5\times 10^{6}$" (4.5 Column densities)
   - assumption: 12CO/H2=2e-4, 16O/18O=300  
  > "Assuming a fractional abundance of $\mathrm{{}^{12}CO/H_2} = 2\times 10^{-4}$ and $\mathrm{^{16}O/{}^{18}O}=300$" (4.5 Column densities)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Jet power from cavity calorimetry**: Kaiser & Alexander self-similar model with Monte Carlo over D, i, opening angle, and v  
  > "We performed Monte Carlo simulations, sampling from the known distributions of distance" (5.4 Constraints on jet properties)
   - parameter: f=0.1, Gamma=5/3, theta uniform 0-4 deg, v=1.01 km/s  
  > "and setting $f=0.1$ (a reasonable assumption given that the jet is displacing molecular gas), and $\Gamma_j=\Gamma_x=\Gamma_c=5/3$" (5.4 Constraints on jet properties)
   - assumption: Constant jet direction, power and external density; sphere of diameter 36.5 arcsec with gas mass 226 Msun  
  > "we estimate the gas mass[^11] in this spherical region to be $226~M_{\rm \odot}$" (5.4 Constraints on jet properties)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo sampling  
  > "We performed Monte Carlo simulations, sampling from the known distributions of distance ($D=8.6^{+2.0}_{-1.6}$ kpc), inclination ($i=60\pm5^\circ$)" (Discussion, Constraints on jet properties)
   - validation: Cross-check with elastic slice model giving ~4e32 erg/s  
  > "This jet power estimate is on par with that estimated from the calorimetric method above." (5.4 Constraints on jet properties)

## Qualitative findings

- CO PV diagrams show a hole in 13CO and C18O at the non-thermal jet feature, consistent with a jet-blown cavity  
  > "we observe a distinct hole in the $^{13}$CO and C$^{18}$O molecular emission, spatially coincident with the location of the non-thermal jet feature" (4.2 CO line emission)
- Jet does not dominantly power the whole IRAS region; star formation does  
  > "does not dominantly power or shape the whole IRAS 19132+1035 region" (5.1 Evidence for a jet-ISM interaction)

## Claim tuples

- H: IRAS 19132+1035 is a jet-ISM interaction site | E: Mapped CO, H2CO, shock tracers and column density with ALMA | R: Cavity in 13CO/C18O PV, 12CO/13CO~1 at jet base, lack of H2CO, compact narrow SiO | F: Conclusive jet-ISM interaction, third in Galactic BHXBs | **supported**
- H: Jet powers the whole IRAS region | E: Compared jet energy with star formation feedback | R: Jet ~1e47 erg vs ~1e50 erg from star formation | F: Jet interaction is small-scale | **refuted**
- H: Cavity can serve as a calorimeter for jet power | E: Kaiser-Alexander model with Monte Carlo | R: 8.4e32 erg/s time-averaged | F: Low average jet power compared to flare-based estimates | **partially**

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Molecular lines diagnose density, temperature and shocks; prior IRAM data lower sensitivity/resolution  
  > "molecular lines provide for excellent diagnostics of shock energetics and ISM excitation" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- Alternative: Direct Fourier-plane feather of single-dish and interferometer data tested; no improvement  
  > "did not find any clear improvements in image quality" (3.1 Spectral line imaging)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Distance mismatch between GRS 1915+105 and IRAS source; jet power assumes constant power and density  
  > "We have also assumed a constant external density." (5.4 Constraints on jet properties)
- Limitation (authors): ALMA continuum lacks single-dish data so flux is underestimated  
  > "we do not recover all the flux from the region in these ALMA continuum observations" (4.1 Continuum Emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Jet power uncertainties as quoted are asymmetric and implausibly large relative to value
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Shock velocity from SiO FWHM is a weak proxy
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single SiO detection underpins shock evidence
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cavity interpretation could be non-unique
