## Analysis recipe

1. **Preprocess input maps**: Repair or drop saturated Herschel maps, build PSFs for all instruments, apply Herschel colour corrections, and set per-map errors; use free-free-subtracted and native Band 6 versions  
  > "we chose to entirely remove these maps from the pool of input data" (6.1.1 Correction of saturated pixels); "This interpolation process was performed using Gaussian process regression, as implemented in the" (2.3 Hi-GAL survey)
   - software: astrofix, astropy.convolution (Gaussian2DKernel)
   - parameter: Maps with 25% or more saturated area removed (11 of 124 images); maps with less than 25% saturated were interpolated  
  > "In severe cases of saturation (11 out of 124 images in our study)" (6.1.1 Correction of saturated pixels); "In cases where the saturation level was lower (less than 25% of the map is saturated), we applied interpolation to estimate the missing values." (6.1.1 Correction of saturated pixels)
   - parameter: 25 of 124 images interpolated, median 6 pixels per image  
  > "25 out of 124 images underwent interpolation for the present study, with a median of 6 interpolated pixels per image" (6.1.1 Correction of saturated pixels)
   - parameter: Herschel PSFs replaced by versions on 2048 x 2048 frames  
  > "We replaced these files with new ones projected on a larger $2048 \times 2048$ frame." (6.1.2 Preparation of point spread functions)
   - parameter: Per-map error = MAD of empty areas plus flux-calibration error times sky background  
  > "Therefore, we incorporated the median absolute deviation measurement, along with the systematic error on the absolute flux calibration of the sky background" (6.2.1 Determination of measurement errors)
   - parameter: Gaussian synthetic beams for SABOCA, LABOCA and ALMA  
  > "We produced our own synthetic beam profile for SABOCA, LABOCA, and ALMA observations using the" (6.1.2 Preparation of point spread functions)
   - assumption: Herschel colour corrections assume a modified blackbody with beta = 2  
  > "assuming a MBB profile with $\beta = 2$" (6.2.2 Herschel color correction)
   - assumption: Free-free subtraction assumes electron temperature of 7000 K and region-dependent helium abundance  
  > "This was achieved by assuming an electron temperature of $7000$ K and variable helium abundances" (6.1.3 Free-free emission in the ALMA 1.3 mm emission map)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Interpolation uncertainty measured by comparing PPMAP runs with and without the interpolated maps  
  > "we performed two iterations of the PPMAP analysis: one with the interpolated maps and the other without any interpolation" (6.1.1 Correction of saturated pixels)
2. **Run PPMAP SED fitting (Run1 and Run2)**: Multi-resolution Bayesian SED fitting of 70 um to 1.3 mm maps with PPMAP; Run1 uses free-free-subtracted Band 6 for column density and temperature, Run2 uses native Band 6 for luminosity  
  > "Through PPMAP, we perform SED fitting for a large set of continuum observations, while preserving the high-angular resolution capabilities of ALMA" (1 Introduction); "we either used the free-free subtracted maps to obtain the dust temperature and column density maps" (6.1.3 Free-free emission in the ALMA 1.3 mm emission map); "or the native ALMA Band 6 maps to estimate the luminosity" (6.1.3 Free-free emission in the ALMA 1.3 mm emission map)
   - software: PPMAP
   - parameter: 8 MBB components, 10-50 K  
  > "we employ 8 MBB components with temperature values ranging from 10 K to 50 K" (3.2 PPMAP analysis)
   - parameter: Opacity index fixed to beta = 1.8  
  > "We therefore fix the opacity index to $\beta = 1.8$" (3.2 PPMAP analysis)
   - parameter: 1.25'' pixels, 2.5'' synthetic resolution  
  > "The pixel sizes of the Nyquist-sampled PPMAP arrays are 1.25$^{\prime \prime}$, corresponding to an angular resolution of 2.5$^{\prime \prime}$." (3.2 PPMAP analysis)
   - parameter: Reference opacity kappa_300 = 0.1 cm2/g  
  > "ref. opacity (cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$) | 0.1 (b)" (Table 2 (PPMAP input parameters))
   - parameter: Maximum 10 000 iterations  
  > "maximum nb. of iterations | 10 000" (Table 2 (PPMAP input parameters))
   - parameter: Dilution eta = 0 (internal default N_prior/N_states)  
  > "causes an internal PPMAP subroutine to adopt a default value, defined as" (6.2.3 PPMAP parameters)
   - parameter: Cells of 60 px, overlap 40 px (50 px in some cases giving 7x7 mosaics)  
  > "cell size (px) | 60 (a)" (Table 2 (PPMAP input parameters)); "cell overlap (px) | 40, 50 (a)" (Table 2 (PPMAP input parameters)); "to 50 in order to enhance the quality of the subfields seaming, thereby using $7 \times 7$ mosaics" (6.2.3 PPMAP parameters)
   - parameter: High-pass option on, subtracting a constant background from model images at ground-based wavelengths  
  > "allowing PPMAP to subtract a constant background from the internal model images" (6.2.3 PPMAP parameters)
   - parameter: Temperature range lowered per region after a first run (example G012.80: 21-39 K)  
  > "we systematically lowered the amplitude of the temperature range" (6.2.4 Temperature and opacity index optimization)
   - assumption: Dust emission optically thin at all wavelengths  
  > "PPMAP operates under the assumption that the radiation emitted by dust across observed wavelengths is optically thin." (3.1 Point process mapping)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100 (kappa_300 defined w.r.t. total mass)  
  > "is in line with a gas-to-dust mass ratio of 100" (3.1 Point process mapping)
   - assumption: X_H = 0.7, X_H2 = 1, Z_D = 0.01 for the H2 column density  
  > "fractional abundance by mass of hydrogen and molecular hydrogen $X_\mathrm{H}=0.7$, $X_\mathrm{H_2}=1$, and fractional abundance by mass of dust $Z_\mathrm{D}=0.01$" (3.1 Point process mapping)
   - assumption: Measurement noise is Gaussian  
  > "The term $\mu$ represents the measurement noise, assumed to be a Gaussian random process." (3.1 Point process mapping)
   - fitting: Iterative Bayesian algorithm with a Gaussian-process prior, minimising the reduced chi-square per pixel; posterior expectation gives column density and temperature  
  > "This is achieved by minimizing the mean square error, ensuring that the best estimate is the a posteriori expectation value of" (3.1 Point process mapping); "In each pixel of the maps, PPMAP minimizes the reduced-" (3.1 Point process mapping)
   - validation: A posteriori chi-square test and SED comparison; iteration number tweaked to avoid over-fitting; 86% of observations within 1 sigma of the model  
  > "The quality of PPMAP outputs underwent a systematic validation process through an a posteriori chi-square test" (6.2.5 Criteria to stop PPMAP iterations); "We systematically controlled for and prevented over-fitting through minor tweaks of the PPMAP iteration number" (6.2.5 Criteria to stop PPMAP iterations); "All of the observations align within $\pm 2$ standard deviation ($\sigma$) of the PPMAP MBB model, and 86% of the observations maintain agreement within $\pm 1 \sigma$." (3.3 Bolometric luminosity measurements)
3. **Correct dust temperature for optical depth**: Build a correction table from PPMAP runs on synthetic uniform-slab observations, extrapolate it with cubic splines and apply it a posteriori to each pixel

## Qualitative findings

- Free-free subtraction is needed to estimate column density in evolved regions  
  > "The disparity between Run1 and Run2 underscores that the free-free subtraction is necessary to precisely estimate the column density in evolved regions." (6.1.3 Free-free emission in the ALMA 1.3 mm emission map)
- A hot core shows a local temperature increase in PPMAP of up to 37 K  
  > "the PPMAP-derived temperature does register a local increase in this specific area, up to 37 K" (5.1.2 Comparison with N2H+ line)
- Massive catalog sources above the 50 Msun track are interpreted as unresolved clusters  
  > "We interpret these massive sources as clusters of unresolved cores and/or protostars." (5.2.2 Results of the getsf extraction)
- Region-averaged L/M is not a robust indicator of one protocluster's global evolutionary stage  
  > "may not systematically serve as a robust indicator of the global evolutionary stage of one protocluster" (5.2 Luminosity and mass of PPMAP luminosity peaks)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Core masses depend on temperature, and millimetre data alone cannot give bolometric luminosity, so far-infrared to submillimetre data are added  
  > "Measuring the mass and thus the temperature of these structures is essential to understand the conditions in which stars form" (1 Introduction); "millimeter observations, on their own, are insufficient to measure the bolometric luminosity" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: PPMAP keeps ALMA resolution while fitting Herschel and single-dish data, avoiding smoothing to 35.2''  
  > "Applying this smoothing procedure would entirely defeat the purpose of high-angular ALMA observations" (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: Smoothing all maps to the coarsest resolution (35.2'') before SED fitting, rejected  
  > "Applying this smoothing procedure would entirely defeat the purpose of high-angular ALMA observations" (1 Introduction)
- Alternative: ALMA Band 3 excluded from PPMAP to avoid free-free contamination  
  > "Band 3 observations were not used in the PPMAP analysis, in order to prevent contamination by free-free emission" (Table 1 footnote 3)
- Alternative: Letting beta vary was tried but the chi-square change was negligible, so beta fixed to 1.8  
  > "we decided to fix $\beta = 1.8$ for all regions" (6.2.4 Temperature and opacity index optimization); "the degeneracy between the temperature and opacity index makes our attempt at simultaneously constraining them uncertain" (6.2.4 Temperature and opacity index optimization)
- Alternative: More than 8 temperature components not used  
  > "Adopting a number of temperature components higher than 8 would result in larger uncertainties without improving the temperature sampling significantly." (3.2 PPMAP analysis)
- Alternative: MSX data not added to fix saturated MIPS 24 um because of its coarse resolution  
  > "Incorporating MSX observations into our study was not feasible" (4.2 Comparison to results from ATLASGAL)
- How they decided it was enough: Iterations monitored with an a posteriori chi-square test to stop before over-fitting artifacts grow  
  > "we monitored the convergence of the PPMAP iterative process to prevent over-fitting" (6.2.5 Criteria to stop PPMAP iterations)
- Limitation (authors): 2.5'' beam dilutes core emission, so core temperatures can be under- or over-estimated  
  > "This dilution of the signal may result in underestimating the temperature of protostellar cores and overestimating that of prestellar cores" (3.4 Temperature correction and final products)
- Limitation (authors): Optically thin assumption may bias temperature and mass at high column density  
  > "the bias induced by the PPMAP assumption of optical thinness, that may significantly affect the temperature and mass estimates, in particular toward high column density pixels" (4.3.2 Quantification of errors)
- Limitation (authors): Reference opacity is poorly known and may vary across the protoclusters  
  > "The reference opacity is in fact not well known, and may vary across the protoclusters." (3.1 Point process mapping)
- Limitation (authors): G333.60 is the least constrained region because of saturation and missing SOFIA data  
  > "This makes G333 the least constrained region within our study." (2.3 Hi-GAL survey)
- Limitation (authors): Ring-like artifacts may bias core temperatures  
  > "Most importantly, the presence of ring-like artifacts may introduce a bias in determining the temperatures of cores." (6.4 PPMAP ring-like artifacts and post-processing)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass and temperature rest on a fixed beta = 1.8 and kappa_300 = 0.1 cm2/g with no free-parameter test of their joint effect; kappa uncertainty is excluded from the quoted error budget
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Total error of 29% for N_H2 is a quadrature sum of correlated, run-to-run differences rather than a posterior
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Run1 relies on unpublished free-free-subtracted maps, so the key temperature and column density products cannot be fully traced
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The Koenig et al. comparison says 10 regions but lists 11, and the distance rescaling is not shown
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Optical-depth temperature correction is tuned to a uniform slab and extrapolated beyond the original table
- Caveat (inferred, ours): L/M of 2.5'' sources blends many cores, so comparison with accretion tracks is qualitative
