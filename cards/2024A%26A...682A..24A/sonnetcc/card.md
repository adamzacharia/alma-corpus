## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image ALMA cubes and continuum**: Pipeline calibration in CASA, tclean cubes with natural weighting, continuum from collapsing line-free ranges  
  > "Data calibration was done using the Common Astronomy Software Application (CASA) software following the observatory-provided pipeline." (Observations and sample); "inverting the calibrated visibilities with the `tclean` task and using a natural weighting scheme to maximize the sensitivity" (Observations and sample)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: natural weighting; continuum excludes [CII] within 2xFWHM when detected  
  > "excluding the frequency ranges that contained the \[C ii\] lines (when detected) within $2\times$FWHM$_{\rm [CII]}$" (Observations and sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Detect [CII] and continuum emission and define the sample**: Search cube and continuum at the rest-frame UV source position; keep [CII] detections, exclude non-detections  
  > "The \[C ii\] line datacube and the continuum image were searched for emission at the location of the rest-frame UV source" (Observations and sample); "We deliberately exclude \[CII\] non-detections from the analysis" (Observations and sample)
   - parameter: 23 [CII] and 16 continuum detections from first-year data, plus 5 pilot [CII] detections, giving 28 galaxies  
  > "We obtained 23 \[C ii\] line and 16 dust continuum detections, respectively, from the first year data." (Observations and sample)
   - assumption: Non-detections may be [CII]-faint or have redshifts outside the scan range, so are excluded  
  > "as it is unclear whether they are \[CII\] faint galaxies or they are sources with redshifts falling outside the line scan covered ranges" (Observations and sample)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive stellar masses and SFRs**: PROSPECTOR SED fitting with non-parametric SFH adopted; constant-SFH PROSPECTOR and BEAGLE as alternatives; SFR from UV (HST) plus IR from 158 micron continuum  
  > "Spectral Energy Distribution (SED) fitting for the REBELS sample was conducted using the PROSPECTOR code (cite: leja19)." (Ancillary data); "we adopt the results from PROSPECTOR with non-parametric SFHs for this work" (Ancillary data)
   - software: PROSPECTOR, BEAGLE
   - parameter: metallicity 0.2 Zsun  
  > "including sub-solar metallicities ($0.2\ Z_\odot$)" (Ancillary data)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF; SMC dust extinction curve  
  > "and a Small Magellanic Cloud (SMC) dust extinction curve (cite: topping22)" (Ancillary data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Constant-SFH PROSPECTOR gives results similar to BEAGLE; effect of non-parametric vs constant SFH masses tested  
  > "the use of PROSPECTOR with a constant SFH yields results similar to those obtained by BEAGLE" (Ancillary data)
4. **Convert L_[CII] to molecular gas mass**: Zanella et al. (2018) calibration, alpha_[CII] = 30 Msun/Lsun, M_mol = alpha_[CII] x L_[CII]  
  > "we measured molecular gas masses for the REBELS galaxies in our sample using Equation (2) from (cite: zanella18)" (Measurements of molecular gas); "We thus adopt $\alpha_{\rm [CII]}\simeq30 (M_\ensuremath{\odot}/L_\ensuremath{\odot})$, with $M_{\rm{mol}}=\alpha_{\rm [CII]} L_{\rm [CII]}$." (Measurements of molecular gas)
   - parameter: log(L_[CII]) = (-1.28+-0.21) + (0.98+-0.02) log(M_mol); alpha_[CII] ~ 30  
  > "{\rm log} (L_{\rm [CII]}) = (-1.28\pm0.21) + (0.98\pm0.02)  {\rm log}(M_{\rm{mol}})" (Measurements of molecular gas)
   - assumption: [CII] calibration holds for massive main-sequence galaxies at z~7; molecular gas dominates the baryon budget at z>6  
  > "suggest that the calibrations to obtain $M_{\rm mol}$ from $L_{\rm [CII]}$ apply for massive main-sequence galaxies at $z\sim7$ and $z\sim4-6$" (Conclusions)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Cross-checked against IR luminosity, dust masses and dynamical masses (steps 5-7)  
  > "we focus on checking the \[C ii\]-based molecular gas mass estimates against other derivations provided by the available data." (Measurements of molecular gas)
5. **Check gas masses against the L_IR - L'_CO relation**: Convert M_mol,[CII] to L'_CO = M_mol/alpha_CO and compare with the L_IR vs L'_CO relation of Dessauges-Zavadsky et al. (2015)  
  > "we convert the \[C ii\]-derived molecular gas masses ($M_{\rm mol, [CII]}$) into the expected CO(1-0) luminosities under the assumption of typical $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ values" (IR luminosity as a proxy of molecular gas mass)
   - parameter: alpha_CO range 1.0 to 4.36 Msun (K km/s pc^2)^-1  
  > "a range of typical values is considered between 1.0 to 4.36" (IR luminosity as a proxy of molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: Dust temperature 46 K for L_IR from a single continuum measurement  
  > "realistic assumptions of the dust temperature (46 K)" (IR luminosity as a proxy of molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: With alpha_CO = 4.36 the REBELS galaxies lie on the Dessauges-Zavadsky et al. 2015 curve  
  > "the REBELS galaxies fall right on top of the (cite: dessauges15) curve." (IR luminosity as a proxy of molecular gas mass)
6. **Check gas masses against dust masses**: Compare literature dust masses (Ferrara et al. 2022; Sommovigo et al. 2022) with M_mol,[CII] and infer the gas-to-dust ratio  
  > "It is thus interesting to check how well the dust masses derived by these studies relate to the \[C ii\]-based molecular gas measurements in the REBELS sample." (Dust masses)
   - parameter: delta_GDR = 1000-3000 expected from MZ and Remy-Ruyer relation  
  > "we find typical values of $\delta_{\rm GDR}=1000-3000$ for the stellar masses and redshifts obtained for the REBELS galaxies" (Dust masses)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Both dust mass estimates consistent and linearly related to M_mol,[CII] within ~30% scatter, suggesting delta_GDR ~1500  
  > "We find that both dust mass estimates are consistent and linearly related to the \[C ii\]-based molecular gas mass estimates within the scatter ($\sim30\%$), suggesting $\delta_{\rm GDR}\sim1500$." (Dust masses)
7. **Check gas masses against dynamical masses**: Dynamical masses from virialized-sphere and disk estimators; M_mol,dyn from M_dyn and M_stars  
  > "we estimate the dynamical masses of the REBELS sources using equations 10 and 11 in (cite: bothwell13)" (Estimates from dynamical mass)
   - parameter: unresolved sources: radius fixed to 2 kpc; disk case mean sin(i) = pi/4 ~ 0.79; R from circularized semi-axes  
  > "simply fix their radius to 2 kpc" (Estimates from dynamical mass); "In the disk case, we adopt a mean inclination angle sin$(i)=\pi/4\approx0.79$." (Estimates from dynamical mass)
   - assumption: Dark matter fraction negligible and ISM dominated by molecular gas within r_e  
  > "thus the dark matter fraction is negligible and that the ISM is dominated by the molecular gas" (Estimates from dynamical mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Disk geometry agrees within +-0.3 dex of a linear relation; virialized geometry gives systematic offset and large scatter  
  > "we find overall agreement between both molecular gas estimates in the case of the disk geometry, with most of the galaxies falling within $\pm0.3$ dex from a linear relation" (Estimates from dynamical mass)
8. **Derive depletion times, gas fractions and Delta_MS and compare with scaling relations**: t_dep = M_mol/SFR, f_mol = M_mol/(M_mol+M_stars), mu_mol = M_mol/M_stars, plotted versus Delta_MS and compared with Tacconi18, Liu19, Scoville17 relations computed at the median stellar mass  
  > "the molecular gas depletion timescale, $t_{\rm dep}=M_{\rm mol}/$SFR" (Analysis and discussion); "computed at the median stellar mass of the REBELS sample, $10^{9.5} M_\ensuremath{\odot}$ at $z=7$" (Scaling relations)
   - parameter: MS prescription of Schreiber et al. (2015); comparison samples restricted to the stellar mass range of REBELS  
  > "For the MS, we use the prescription from (cite: schreiber15)." (Scaling relations); "in the following analysis were restricted to the stellar mass range of the REBELS sample." (Analysis and discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with the ALPINE sample and with the effect of using constant-SFH stellar masses  
  > "This selection has only a minor effect on the main conclusions drawn in this and in the previous section." (Potential effects of our adoption of stellar masses)
9. **Measure redshift evolution of t_dep and mu_mol in bins**: Median per redshift bin with quartile uncertainties, for ALPINE, REBELS and lower-z samples  
  > "With this, we computed the median among all galaxies in each bin." (Evolution of $t_{\rm dep}$ and $\mu_{\rm mol}$); "Uncertainties in each median point taken are obtained by computing the quartile values for the subsamples in each bin." (Evolution of $t_{\rm dep}$ and $\mu_{\rm mol}$)
   - parameter: REBELS bins 6.4<z<7.0 and 7.0<z<7.8; ALPINE bins 4.3<z<4.7 and 5.0<z<6.0; six bins at lower z; MS galaxies at 10^9.75 Msun  
  > "($4.3<z<4.7$ and $5.0<z<6.0$ for ALPINE; $6.4<z<7.0$ and $7.0<z<7.8$ for REBELS)" (Evolution of $t_{\rm dep}$ and $\mu_{\rm mol}$)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Medians agree with weighted averages and previously reported values  
  > "These values are found to be in excellent agreement with weighted average values for the subsamples in each bin" (Evolution of $t_{\rm dep}$ and $\mu_{\rm mol}$)
10. **Derive the cosmic molecular gas density at z~7**: Integrate the [CII] luminosity function (from UV LF and L_[CII]-L_UV relation) above a luminosity limit, then convert with alpha_[CII]  
  > "the \[C ii\] luminosity density is found by integrating the \[C ii\] luminosity function down to a limit of log($L_{\rm [CII], lim})=7.5$" (Cosmic density of molecular gas)
   - parameter: log(L_[CII],lim) = 7.5  
  > "down to a limit of log($L_{\rm [CII], lim})=7.5$" (Cosmic density of molecular gas)
   - assumption: alpha_[CII] ~ 30 Msun/Lsun; uncertainties include the calibration uncertainty  
  > "This result includes the uncertainties associated with the $\alpha_{\rm [CII]}$ calibration (cite: zanella18)." (Cosmic density of molecular gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CO, dust and [CII] based rho_mol measurements at other redshifts  
  > "The REBELS measurements agree with the trend of $\rho_{\rm mol}$ declining at higher redshifts." (Cosmic density of molecular gas)

## Qualitative findings

- The [CII]-based gas masses are consistent with the IR luminosity, dust mass and dynamical mass checks  
  > "we find that all the independent checks obtained by comparing the \[C ii\]-based molecular gas mass estimates with the IR luminosity, the dust masses, and the dynamical masses are consistent" (Estimates from dynamical mass)
- REBELS galaxies are mostly consistent with the star-forming main sequence  
  > "we find that most REBELS galaxies are consistent with being in the MS and thus represent examples of massive yet “typical” star-forming galaxies at high redshift." (Comparison samples)
- mu_mol increases with distance from the MS while t_dep stays relatively constant  
  > "While $\mu_{\rm mol}$ increases toward larger $\Delta_{\rm MS}$, $t_{\rm dep}$ is observed to stay relatively constant." (Conclusions)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Extend molecular gas studies of ALPINE (z~4-6) to higher redshift using REBELS  
  > "Here, we aim to extend such exploration to even higher redshifts, by using data from the Reionization Era Bright Emission Line Survey" (Introduction)
- Why this method: CO and dust continuum are hard to detect at z>6 (CMB contrast, low metallicity), while [CII] is bright and accessible from the ground  
  > "The radiation from these tracers thus becomes almost impossible to detect against the CMB at these redshifts." (Measurements of molecular gas); "Since the \[C ii\] line is among the brightest coolant lines in galaxies" (Introduction)
- Alternative: Other alpha_[CII] values (130 Msun/Lsun from Madden et al. 2020; 10-18 from simulations) not adopted  
  > "(cite: madden20) find $\alpha_{\rm [CII]}\simeq 130" (Measurements of molecular gas)
- Alternative: Constant-SFH stellar masses rejected in favour of non-parametric SFH masses; effect tested  
  > "For consistency with a related parallel study of the HI mass content of REBELS galaxies (cite: heintz22), we adopt the results from PROSPECTOR with non-parametric SFHs for this work." (Ancillary data)
- Alternative: alpha_CO = 1.0 or >6.4 disfavoured by the L_IR-L'_CO relation  
  > "would lead to significantly larger CO luminosities being inconsistent with the $L_{\rm IR}$ vs. $L'_{\rm CO}$ relations." (IR luminosity as a proxy of molecular gas mass)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Unknown fraction of [CII] emission arising from PDRs  
  > "The main caveat to using \[C ii\] as a molecular gas tracer is the unknown fraction of \[C ii\] emission that arise from PDRs." (Measurements of molecular gas)
- Limitation (authors): [CII] only mildly resolved, so no resolved kinematics for dynamical masses  
  > "Unfortunately, our \[C ii\] observations can spatially resolve only mildly some of the sources, and therefore we do not have information about their resolved \[C ii\] kinematics." (Estimates from dynamical mass)
- Limitation (authors): L_IR from a single continuum point and assumed dust temperature; stellar masses poorly constrained without rest-frame optical  
  > "we caution that the REBELS $L_{\rm IR}$ values were computed using a single dust continuum measurements" (IR luminosity as a proxy of molecular gas mass); "is the lack of sensitive constraints on the rest-frame optical SED" (Scaling relations)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): M_mol is a fixed multiple (alpha_[CII] = 30) of L_[CII], so t_dep and mu_mol results are largely set by L_[CII], SFR and stellar mass rather than independent gas measurements
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Agreement with the L_IR-L'_CO relation and dust masses is partly circular, since both use [CII] or SFR-related information
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar mass choice changes mu_mol by a factor 2.4 and the number of starburst-like sources from 2 to 9
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Excluding [CII] non-detections biases the sample toward bright [CII] emitters
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Key inputs (L_[CII], line widths, cosmic density) rely on papers in preparation
