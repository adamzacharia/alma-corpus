## Analysis recipe

1. **Continuum imaging and flux recovery check**: Clean over full ~7.5 GHz with several weightings; compare integrated flux to single-dish 450 µm  
  > "we repeated the imaging process using several different weighting schemes" (3.1 Continuum Imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Integrated flux (~69 mJy) agrees with previous single dish 450 µm  
  > "the integrated flux-density (~69 mJy) agrees with previous single dish observations at 450 µm" (3.1 Continuum Imaging)
2. **Extract [NII] cubes and spectra**: UV-plane continuum subtraction, clean to 3σ, extract spectra from regions, integrate over FWZF  
  > "We then produced a cleaned spectral cube by cleaning down to a 3-sigma RMS level." (3.2 Spectral Imaging)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Cloverleaf natural weighting; SMMJ02399 UV-taper ~0.5" beam  
  > "for SMMJ02399 we imaged with a UV-taper to produce ~0.5” beam" (3.2 Spectral Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with ZEUS-1 spectra; resolved-out flux argued via beam counting  
  > "The significantly weaker [NII] line flux as observed with ALMA can be explained if the line emission has been resolved out." (3.2 Spectral Imaging)
3. **Check ZEUS-1 calibration via free-free radio continuum**: Predict [NII] flux from 32 GHz free-free emission using Eq. 4  
  > "The specific emissivity for free-free emission is (see Rybicki & Lightman 1986)" (4.1 SMMJ02399)
   - parameter: T = 8000 K; q02 = 2.365e-8 cm3/s  
  > "Assuming T = 8000 K, we have 𝑞 02 = 2.365 × 10 [−8] cm [3 ] s [-1]" (4.1 SMMJ02399)
   - assumption: Milky Way N/H = 9.3e-5; ~74% N singly ionized  
  > "we expect ~74% of the nitrogen to be singly ionized" (4.1 SMMJ02399)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Free-free estimate agrees with ZEUS-1 flux  
  > "which is excellent agreement with the ZEUS-1 value" (4.1 SMMJ02399)
4. **Derive ionizing photon rate, SFR from [NII]**: Q0 from [NII] luminosity (Eq. 6), SFR from Calzetti 2007 (Eq. 7); lens-demagnified  
  > "We assume the star-formation rate indicator for Q 0 from Calzetti (2007)" (5.1 [NII] Line Diagnostics: N/H Abundance, Ionized Gas Mass, Ionizing Flux, Mass of Ionizing Stars and Star formation Rate.)
   - parameter: Cloverleaf star-formation [NII] = ZEUS-1 minus ALMA flux (~80 Jy km/s), magnification 11  
  > "We use this line flux (~80 Jy km s [-1 ], i.e. the missing ALMA flux) combined with a magnification factor of 11" (5. Analysis)
   - assumption: _not stated_
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **FIR luminosity, dust mass and FIR SFR**: Scale total LFIR by component 122 µm flux ratio; dust mass from Li & Draine model at Td=45 K; Kennicutt 1998 SFR  
  > "using the component-to-total flux-density ratio for each component, as observed in our ALMA continuum observations, to scale the total FIR luminosity" (5.2 FIR Luminosity, Dust Mass, and Obscured Star-Formation Rate.)
   - assumption: Td = 45 ± 5 K; Milky Way-like dust opacities  
  > "assuming a dust temperature of 45 ± 5 K" (5.2 FIR Luminosity, Dust Mass, and Obscured Star-Formation Rate.)
   - fitting: Two-temperature SED fit with Li & Draine model for Cloverleaf (details not stated)  
  > "we determine dust temperatures of 130 K and 33 K respectively for the warm and cold dust components" (5.2 FIR Luminosity, Dust Mass, and Obscured Star-Formation Rate.)
   - validation: L1 scaled LFIR agrees with AGN contribution (~50%) from Lutz et al. 2005  
  > "our scaled FIR luminosity for L1 agrees with previous estimates of the AGN contribution to the total FIR luminosity (~50%)" (5.2 FIR Luminosity, Dust Mass, and Obscured Star-Formation Rate.)

## Qualitative findings

- Cloverleaf continuum resolved into partial Einstein ring; SMMJ02399 split into AGN point source L1 and extended L2SW  
  > "The Cloverleaf is resolved into a partial Einstein ring, while the SMMJ02399-0136 is unambiguously separated into two components" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: High-resolution mapping to separate NLR from star formation [NII] emission  
  > "high spatial-resolution mapping can also elucidate the nature of the [NII] emission" (1.2 Sources: SMMJ02399 and the Cloverleaf)
- Alternative: Poor calibration of ALMA or ZEUS-1 considered; ALMA calibration trusted, ZEUS-1 checked via free-free  
  > "It could also be that either ALMA or the ZEUS-1 observations are poorly calibrated." (4.1 SMMJ02399)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ALMA [NII] low S/N and missing significant flux; derived values are lower limits  
  > "Since the ALMA observations resolved out significant flux, the derived values should be considered lower limits." (Table 4)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Attribution of resolved-out flux to extended star formation is inferred, not directly measured
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Cloverleaf star formation line flux relies on difference of two instruments with 20% recalibration
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Component FIR luminosity scaling assumes same dust temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Marginal 4-5σ line detections
