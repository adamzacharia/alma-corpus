## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract filaments and hub-filament systems from Herschel 160 um**: Run the getsf algorithm on the Herschel 160 um image to extract filamentary skeletons and identify sub-filaments around the hubs  
  > "we utilized a newly developed algorithm, *getsf* (cite: getsf_2022) on the *Herschel* 160 $\mu$m image (resolution $\sim$12$''$)" (3.1.2 Hub-filament systems)
   - software: getsf
   - parameter: Maximum source size 20 arcsec, maximum filament size 105 arcsec, distance 1.5 kpc  
  > "are extracted with the maximum source and filament sizes of 20$''$ and 105$''$, respectively, and a distance of 1.5 kpc" (3.1.2 Hub-filament systems)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Derive Herschel column density and dust temperature maps and identify clumps**: Pixel-by-pixel modified blackbody SED fit of Herschel images (70 um excluded) with hires to get 12 arcsec N(H2) and Td maps; clumpfind on the column density map; clump mass from summed column density  
  > "A pixel-by-pixel spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting with a modified blackbody spectra provides the desired physical parameters" (3.4 Herschel column density and dust temperature maps); "we employed the *clumpfind* algorithm (cite: Williams_1994)" (3.4.1 Study of Herschel clumps and their physical parameters)
   - software: hires, clumpfind
   - parameter: Herschel 70 um excluded from the SED fit  
  > "this has been excluded from the SED fitting because it generally contains contamination from ultraviolet (UV) heated dust of much higher temperatures" (3.4 Herschel column density and dust temperature maps)
   - parameter: Mean molecular weight mu_H2 = 2.8 for clump masses  
  > "The value of $\mu_{\mathrm H_\mathrm{2}}$ is adopted to be 2.8." (3.4.1 Study of Herschel clumps and their physical parameters)
   - fitting: Modified blackbody SED fit per pixel; priors and likelihood not stated  
  > "A pixel-by-pixel spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting with a modified blackbody spectra provides the desired physical parameters" (3.4 Herschel column density and dust temperature maps)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Map CO gas kinematics and identify two cloud components**: Moment-0, moment-1 and linewidth maps of 12CO, 13CO and C18O; average spectra and a b-v diagram of 13CO to separate blue and red cloud components; derive sound speed, non-thermal dispersion, Mach number along filament F2  
  > "we have presented the integrated intensity (moment-0) maps, the intensity-weighted velocity (moment-1) maps" (3.5.1 Distribution and kinematics of molecular gas)
   - software: GILDAS
   - assumption: Kinetic gas temperature set equal to the average dust temperature  
  > "we have assumed $T_{\mathrm {kin}}$ to be equal to the average dust temperatures." (3.5.1 Distribution and kinematics of molecular gas)
   - assumption: Mean molecular weight per particle mu = 2.37  
  > "The mean molecular weight per particle $\mu$ is adopted to be 2.37." (3.5.1 Distribution and kinematics of molecular gas)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Estimate cloud-cloud collision time scale**: Shift red cloud component to test complementary spatial distribution; combine observed velocity separation and spatial shift with an assumed collision-axis inclination alpha to get t_collision = l_loc / V_loc  
  > "Finally, the collision time scale can be obtained by, $t_{\mathrm{collision}} = \frac{l_{\mathrm{loc}}}{V_{\mathrm{loc}}}$." (4.3 Cloud-cloud collision scenario)
   - parameter: V_obs = 3.7 km/s and l_obs = 2.3 pc  
  > "the observed velocity and spatial shift are $V_{\mathrm{obs}} = 3.7$ km s$^{-1}$ and $l_{\mathrm{obs}} = 2.3$ pc, respectively." (4.3 Cloud-cloud collision scenario)
   - assumption: Angle alpha between collision axis and line of sight taken as 30, 45 and 60 degrees  
  > "Therefore, it is logical to use the $\alpha$ value in between" (4.3 Cloud-cloud collision scenario)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with an earlier proposal of cloud-cloud collision toward the same sites and with the NIR cluster age of ~2.5 Myr  
  > "previous studies determined the age of the NIR cluster to be $\sim$2.5 Myr" (4.3 Cloud-cloud collision scenario)
5. **Measure ALMA 1.3 mm core masses toward T1**: Dust-mass formula applied to the ALMA 1.3 mm continuum; clumpfind at a 6 sigma contour to get total dust emission of core-1, core-2 and an envelope  
  > "We have chosen 6$\sigma$ as the contour value to evaluate the total dust emissions using the *clumpfind*." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)
   - software: clumpfind
   - parameter: Contour level 6 sigma  
  > "We have chosen 6$\sigma$ as the contour value to evaluate the total dust emissions using the *clumpfind*." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)
   - assumption: Distance 1.5 kpc and temperature 25 K  
  > "The distance and the temperature of the source are taken to be 1.5 kpc and 25 K, respectively." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)
   - assumption: Gas-to-dust ratio 100 and opacity 0.33 cm2 g-1  
  > "The gas-to-dust ratio and the opacity value are adopted to be 100 and 0.33 cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$, respectively (cite: Weingartner_2001; Mutie_2021)." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with earlier masses of 8.2 and 4.8 Msun; difference attributed to different distance and temperature  
  > "The difference with our estimated mass values mainly arises due to the use of the different values of distance and temperature." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)

## Qualitative findings

- Both T1 and T2 sit at the opposite edges of an elongated filament and each hosts a hub-filament system; ALMA 1.3 mm shows a dumbbell of two cores within the T1 hub  
  > "Such configuration is traced within the central hub of the HFS in AFGL 5180." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)
- The 6.7 GHz maser and the 8.46 GHz radio continuum coincide with core-1; no radio continuum toward core-2  
  > "we find that the core-1 is associated with the 6.7 GHz MME and the radio continuum emission." (Appendix 7: A zoomed-in view of massive star-forming region T1)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Used 12 arcsec Herschel column density and temperature maps because the 6 arcsec versions were noisy  
  > "However, the resulting column density and dust temperature maps of 6$''$ resolution are noisy" (3.4 Herschel column density and dust temperature maps)
- Alternative: Triggered star formation (collect and collapse, radiatively driven implosion) judged not feasible for T1 and T2  
  > "Therefore, we do not find triggered star formation scenarios feasible in these star-forming regions." (4.1 Impact of the massive star on its environment)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Coarse beam of the molecular line data limits study of fragmentation and sub-filaments  
  > "The coarse beam sizes of the molecular line data are one of the key obstacles in studying such aspects." (4.2 Role of filaments toward our target sites)
- Limitation (authors): Filaments and the massive star assumed to lie in the plane of the sky, so pressures are upper limits  
  > "It is important to note that the filaments and the massive star are assumed to lie on the same plane as of sky." (3.1.3 Feedback of the O-type star)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA core masses depend on assumed T = 25 K and a single opacity; the paper itself shows a factor of about 1.5 to 4 difference from earlier masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Collision timescale depends on an assumed inclination range of 30 to 60 degrees with no independent constraint
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Complementary-distribution evidence relies on an ad hoc 2.3 pc shift of the red cloud and 55 arcsec CO beam
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abstract gives filament length ~5 pc while the text gives ~4 pc
- Caveat (inferred, ours): ALMA data are used only in an appendix and no calibration or imaging details are given
