## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate visibilities and polarization**: CASA QA2 calibration for VLBI-mode data; 3C 273 as flux and bandpass calibrator; 4C 01.28 (J1058+0133) as polarization calibrator giving the source polarization model and the XY cross-phase at the reference antenna; conversion from linear to circular basis  
  > "J1058+0133 (4C 01.28) was chosen as the polarization calibrator due to its adequate parallactic angle coverage" (2.3 Data calibration); "This step enables the conversion of ALMA’s linearly polarized data into a circular polarization basis" (2.3 Data calibration)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Polarization calibrator model Stokes IQUV = [1.50, 0.033, -0.126, 0.0] Jy  
  > "The Stokes parameters derived from the polarization model were determined as *IQUV* = \[1.50, 0.033, $-$0.126, 0.0\] Jy." (2.3 Data calibration)
   - assumption: Stokes V assumed zero during polarization calibration, so no circular polarization claimed  
  > "Consequently, we do not claim CP detections for the observed sources." (Appendix: Stokes parameters per ALMA frequency band (SPW))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with the AMAPOLA Grid Survey (see step 8)  
  > "This consistency validates the accuracy of flux density and polarization calibration in VLBI mode for Band 7" (Appendix: Comparison of Stokes parameters with the AMAPOLA polarimetric Grid Survey)
2. **Make full-Stokes images**: tclean in I, Q, U, V per SPW, per sideband (hogbom) and with all four SPWs (mtmfs nterms=2); automatic cleaning followed by interactive cleaning  
  > "Maps were produced for individual SPWs and by combining SPWs in each sideband (SPW=0,1 and SPW=2,3) using" (2.4 Full-Stokes imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean
   - parameter: Briggs robust 0.5, gain 0.1  
  > "A Briggs weighting scheme (cite: Briggs1995) was adopted with a robust parameter of 0.5 and a cleaning gain of 0.1." (2.4 Full-Stokes imaging)
   - parameter: 576 x 576 pixels of 0.06 arcsec  
  > "We produced 576$\times$576 pixel maps with a pixel size of 0$\rlap{.}^{\prime\prime}$06" (2.4 Full-Stokes imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: mtmfs gave 30-40% better sensitivity than hogbom when combining four SPWs, as expected for rms proportional to 1/sqrt(bandwidth)  
  > "yielding 30–40% better sensitivity on average" (Footnote 3)
3. **Extract Stokes I, Q, U, V of the compact cores**: Two methods: point-source (delta function) fit to the visibilities with uvmultifit per SPW, and summing the central 5x5 pixels of the CLEAN model component map  
  > "To extract flux values for Stokes $I$, $Q$, $U$, and $V$, we employed two alternative methods, one that utilizes the visibility data and the other full-Stokes images." (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources); "Summing only these central pixels isolates core emission from the surroundings" (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
   - software: uvmultifit, CASA
   - parameter: All 240 channels averaged to eight-channel, four-SPW visibility files before fitting  
  > "we first averaged all 240 frequency channels to produce eight-channel, four-SPW visibility $uv$ files." (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
   - assumption: Emission dominated by a central point source at the phase center  
  > "Assuming the emission is dominated by a central point source at the phase center" (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
   - fitting: delta-function model fit to visibilities, per SPW (details not given)  
  > "extracting Stokes $I$, $Q$, $U$, and $V$ parameters for each SPW individually." (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
   - validation: Agreement of the two methods within 1% (median absolute deviation) found in the earlier Band 3 and 6 study is stated to hold for Band 7  
  > "This agreement holds for the current Band 7 observations as well." (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
4. **Derive LP fraction and EVPA with uncertainties**: Compute fractional LP and EVPA from Stokes I, Q, U per SPW and averaged over the four SPWs; uncertainties combine thermal error and a 0.03% Stokes I leakage systematic in quadrature  
  > "we determined polarization properties for all targets, including the fractional LP" (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources); "a 1$\sigma$ systematic error (added in quadrature) associated with Stokes $I$ leakage into Stokes $Q$ and $U$ (0.03% of Stokes $I$)" (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
   - parameter: Leakage systematic 0.03% of Stokes I for Q,U (0.6% for V)  
  > "as recommended by the ALMA observatory (cite: Remijan2019)" (Appendix: Stokes parameters per ALMA frequency band (SPW))
   - assumption: No LP bias correction applied because no sample source has LP much below 1%  
  > "we did not apply an LP bias correction because our sample does not include low-polarization sources with LP$<<1$%" (Appendix: Stokes parameters per ALMA frequency band (SPW))
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: LP uncertainties below 0.1% are stated to be consistent with previous studies  
  > "consistent with previous studies (cite: Nagai2016; Bower2018; Goddi2021)" (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
5. **Fit rotation measure**: Linear fit of EVPA versus lambda squared across the four SPWs (334.6-350.6 GHz) to get RM, mean-wavelength EVPA and EVPA at zero wavelength  
  > "Since we measured the EVPA at four distinct frequencies (one for each SPW) spanning a 16 GHz frequency range (334.6–350.6 GHz), we could estimate the Faraday rotation measure (RM) in the 0.87 mm band." (3.1.2 Rotation measure)
   - parameter: EVPA uncertainties 0.06 to 1 degree map to RM errors of 0.08 to 3 x 10^5 rad m^-2  
  > "corresponding to RM propagated errors between 0.08 and $3 \times 10^5$ rad m$^{-2}$" (3.1.2 Rotation measure)
   - assumption: Single, external, homogeneous Faraday screen so EVPA is linear in lambda squared  
  > "Assuming that the Faraday rotation arises from a single, external, homogeneous Faraday screen (i.e., the rotation occurs outside the plasma responsible for the polarized emission), a linear dependence between the EVPA and the wavelength squared is expected." (3.1.2 Rotation measure)
   - fitting: Weighted least squares of EVPA vs lambda squared  
  > "The RM fitting is performed using a weighted least-squares method applied to $\chi$ as a function of $\lambda^2$." (3.1.2 Rotation measure)
   - validation: 3 sigma RM detections only in 3C 273 and 3C 279; robustness of narrow-band fits evaluated in Goddi et al. 2021  
  > "we achieve $3\sigma$ RM detections in 3C273 and 3C279" (3.1.2 Rotation measure)
6. **Measure in-band spectral index**: Weighted least-squares fit of flux density across the four SPWs, I proportional to nu^alpha  
  > "The spectral index was computed “in-band” using a weighted least-squares fit across the four flux-density measurements in each SPW." (4.2 AGN spectral indices)
   - fitting: Weighted least squares on four flux points  
  > "using a weighted least-squares fit across the four flux-density measurements in each SPW" (4.2 AGN spectral indices)
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Make polarized images of the sources**: Custom Python scripts in CASA turn Stokes I, Q, U images into polarized intensity, fractional LP, EVPA and RM maps, pixel by pixel after convolving SPW images to a common beam  
  > "output images of the linear polarized intensity, the fractional LP, the EVPA, and the Faraday RM." (3.2 Polarization images); "after convolving all SPW images to the same synthesized beam" (3.2 Polarization images)
   - software: CASA, custom Python scripts
   - parameter: Pixels kept above 3 sigma in Stokes I and 2 sigma in polarized flux density  
  > "we applied a threshold defined as $\rm 3 \times \sigma$ (rms noise level) for Stokes $I$ and $\rm 2 \times \sigma$ for the polarized flux density" (3.2 Polarization images)
   - assumption: EVPAs shown are not corrected for Faraday rotation; magnetic field vectors are rotated by 90 degrees  
  > "It should be noted that these EVPAs are not corrected for Faraday rotation and that the magnetic field vectors should be rotated by" (3.2 Polarization images)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Check calibration against AMAPOLA Grid Survey**: Compare Stokes I, Q, U, LP and EVPA per SPW with the AMAPOLA time series (shaded plus/minus 1 sigma) over April 9-29, 2021  
  > "The figures show that most Band 7 measurements derived from our current study fall within the red-shaded regions, demonstrating general consistency with the AMAPOLA trends." (Appendix: Comparison of Stokes parameters with the AMAPOLA polarimetric Grid Survey)
   - software: AMAPOLA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistency with AMAPOLA taken as validation of the Band 7 VLBI-mode calibration  
  > "We conclude that, despite differences in array configurations and data reduction methods, the ALMA-VLBI results are consistent with AMAPOLA." (Appendix: Comparison of Stokes parameters with the AMAPOLA polarimetric Grid Survey)

## Qualitative findings

- LP fractions of the AGN cores range from about 1% to 17% and RMs exceed 10^5 rad m^-2, 1-2 orders of magnitude above those at wavelengths longer than 3 mm  
  > "which are 1–2 orders of magnitude higher than typically observed at longer wavelengths" (abstract)
- AGN cores have negative in-band spectral indices between about -1.3 and -0.4  
  > "The AGN cores systematically show negative spectral indices in the range $\alpha = -1.3$ to $-0.4$" (4.2 AGN spectral indices)
- The 0.3 arcsec resolution resolves HST-1 from the M87 core  
  > "allowing us to resolve HST-1 from the core" (4.3 Polarization properties of the M87 jet at 345 GHz)
- M87 jet EVPAs are generally perpendicular to the jet axis except at HST-1 and knot A, matching 1.3 mm and 3 mm results  
  > "The EVPA distribution is generally perpendicular to the jet axis, except in the regions HST-1 and Knot A." (4.3 Polarization properties of the M87 jet at 345 GHz)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The interferometric by-product of ALMA VLBI commissioning has standalone value for submillimeter polarization of VLBI targets on arcsecond scales  
  > "However, beyond their role in VLBI, these datasets have significant standalone scientific value" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: A single external Faraday screen gives EVPA linear in lambda squared, allowing an RM fit from four SPWs  
  > "a linear dependence between the EVPA and the wavelength squared is expected." (3.1.2 Rotation measure)
- Alternative: hogbom deconvolution of all four SPWs combined was rejected in favor of mtmfs because it gave up to 50% worse rms for steep-spectrum sources  
  > "performed poorly when combining all four SPWs, especially for sources with steep spectral indices" (Footnote 3)
- Alternative: Non-VLBI scans of 3C 273 excluded from polarization analysis because the short observation lacked sufficient quality  
  > "the short observation of 3C 273 did not yield data of sufficient quality for meaningful polarization analysis" (3.1 Polarization analysis of point sources)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Resolution, sensitivity and frequency coverage prevent distinguishing internal from external Faraday rotation and characterizing the helical field  
  > "Our submillimeter observations, however, are limited by angular resolution, sensitivity, and frequency coverage" (4.3 Polarization properties of the M87 jet at 345 GHz)
- Limitation (authors): RM time variability precludes combining non-simultaneous datasets to confirm RM-wavelength dependence  
  > "precludes using nonsimultaneous datasets to reliably confirm RM-wavelength dependences" (4.3 Polarization properties of the M87 jet at 345 GHz)
- Limitation (authors): No circular polarization claimed because Stokes V was assumed zero in calibration  
  > "Consequently, we do not claim CP detections for the observed sources." (Appendix: Stokes parameters per ALMA frequency band (SPW))
- Caveat (inferred, ours): RM rests on four SPWs over 16 GHz with errors up to about 3e5 rad m^-2, so only 3C 273 and 3C 279 are 3 sigma detections; n-pi ambiguity at such high RM is not discussed
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stokes I uncertainty looks like a flat 10% (Table 3) but its origin is not explained; the absolute flux scale rests on one calibrator
- Caveat (inferred, ours): 3C 273 RM error is 1.6 in text and 1.7 in Table 3, and M87 alpha is -1.25 in text and -1.24 in Table 3
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Interactive manual cleaning masks and custom polarization-map scripts make the images non-reproducible without the authors' files
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Single-epoch, non-simultaneous comparison with other bands and sources known to be variable limits the wavelength-dependence claim
