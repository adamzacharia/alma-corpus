## Analysis recipe

1. **Combine tunings and image continuum and line cube**: Concatenate tuning measurement sets, clean with tclean (primary-beam corrected), then smooth cube channels to a common beam  
  > "`concat` for joining different tunings’ calibrated measurement sets together" (Imaging); "to smooth the resolution of image cubes to the smallest common resolution of the joined tunings" (Imaging)
   - software: CASA, tclean, concat, imsmooth
   - parameter: close to natural Briggs weighting  
  > "Using a close to natural Briggs weighting scheme" (Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Identify and measure 100-GHz continuum sources**: Sources found by eye at S/N > 3 and characterised with CASA imfit  
  > "At 100-GHz we identify three significant continuum sources by eye, which are at least the size of the synthesised beam, at S/N $>3$." (Continuum); "The characteristics for continuum sources are obtained with CASA’s `imfit`" (Continuum)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: imfit Gaussian source fits (details not stated)  
  > "The characteristics for continuum sources are obtained with CASA’s `imfit`" (Continuum)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Find emission lines in the cube**: Manual spectrum extraction at the bright continuum source, then SoFiA 2 search, then manual search for lower S/N features coincident in space or frequency with detected lines  
  > "we opted to use an application to automate spatial and spectral searches and to more robustly detect features that would otherwise be missed" (3-D emission line finding); "Hence any additional detections were obtained manually by searching for lower S/N features" (Spectra and line analysis)
   - software: SoFiA 2
   - parameter: scfind.threshold = 6.0  
  > "using SoFiA parameter `scfind.threshold` $=6.0$" (Spectra and line analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: SoFiA gave no detections below S/N ~6 that the authors trusted; manual review of all candidates  
  > "SoFiA did not appear to produce any detections below S/N $\sim6\sigma$ that we were confident in" (Spectra and line analysis)
4. **Characterise the line emitters**: Moment-0 maps over the FWZI, photutils 3-sigma segmentation, spectrum extraction, Gaussian fit with lmfit after first-order polynomial continuum subtraction; S/N from the binned local RMS  
  > "we employ `photutils` (cite: photutils_1.6.0_Bradley_2022) to create a 2-D segmentation map of the line emission at the $3\sigma$ level" (Spectra and line analysis); "We fit a model Gaussian to each line using `lmfit` (cite: lmfit_1.2.2_Newville_2023)" (Spectra and line analysis)
   - software: photutils, lmfit
   - parameter: S/N = peak flux / binned local RMS, binned over the line FWHM  
  > "we bin the spectrum by the number of channels contained within the fitted line’s full-width half-maximum (FWHM)" (Spectra and line analysis)
   - fitting: Gaussian least-squares fit per line (lmfit); priors not stated  
  > "We fit a model Gaussian to each line using `lmfit` (cite: lmfit_1.2.2_Newville_2023)" (Spectra and line analysis)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Test reliability of weak [CI] and single-line CO detections**: Monte Carlo of noise-only spectra for [CI] false positives; photutils-based purity of CO linemaps; CO luminosity-function estimate of interloper counts for CO(2-1) to CO(6-5)  
  > "Simulating a large number ($N=10^4$) of these randomly generated noise spectra" (Statistical analysis concerning the weak detection of the carbon line); "where the purity is $P(>S/N)=1-N_{\rm positive}/N_{\rm negative}$" (Robustness and purity of single-line CO emitters)
   - software: photutils
   - parameter: assumed line FWHM 449 km/s; cube binned by a factor of 22; binned RMS ~98 uJy/beam/channel  
  > "we assume a line with a FWHM of $449\;$km s$^{-1}$" (Robustness and purity of single-line CO emitters)
   - assumption: Non-evolving CO luminosity function and fully thermalised CO SLED (over-estimates interlopers)  
  > "We also assume a fully thermalised CO spectral line energy distribution (SLED)" (Robustness and purity of single-line CO emitters)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: False-positive rate of S/N >= 3 line ~2.5%; expected interlopers <0.01 per transition in the protocluster frequency range  
  > "the false-positive rate of detecting a S/N $\ge3$ Gaussian line is $\approx2.5$%" (Statistical analysis concerning the weak detection of the carbon line); "The expected number of blindly detected CO lines in this narrow range is $<0.01$ for each transition." (Robustness and purity of single-line CO emitters)
6. **Fit host-galaxy SEDs to optical/NIR aperture photometry**: 2-arcsec aperture photometry on DES g,r,i,z,Y and HAWK-I Ks, then BEAGLE SED fitting with uniform priors  
  > "We inspect the region around each candidate protocluster member in all of the aforementioned bands to choose a location to place a 2$^{\prime\prime}$ aperture to measure the flux" (Photometry); "Setup of parameters and priors for the BEAGLE runs used to fit candidate protocluster members." (BEAGLE)
   - software: BEAGLE
   - parameter: Redshift fixed at 3.879; constant SFH; log M*/Msun prior 9-13; tau_V prior -1 to 7; log psi prior 1-3.5; log tau/yr prior 8-9.15  
  > "$\log M_*/{\rm M}_\odot$" (Table 1); "In order for BEAGLE to derive a plausible value for the star formation timescale" (BEAGLE)
   - assumption: Chabrier IMF, solar metallicity, Calzetti attenuation, Inoue IGM, nebular xi default  
  > "the nebular dust-to-metal mass ratio ($\xi$) is kept at its default value and we fix the metallicity to solar metallicity" (BEAGLE); "a fixed (cite: IMF_Chabrier_2003) Galactic-disc initial mass function (IMF)" (BEAGLE)
   - fitting: BEAGLE Bayesian fit; mean and 68% credible intervals quoted; sampler not stated  
  > "quoting the mean and 68% credible intervals as uncertainties" (BEAGLE fitting)
   - validation: J0053d mass checked against a Pegase dusty-starburst mass-to-light ratio (within 0.5 dex)  
  > "This is within $0.5\,$dex of BEAGLE’s estimate" (Fitted galaxy properties)
7. **Estimate SFRs from ALESS templates**: Scale observed-frame 3 mm flux densities to redshifted average ALESS templates  
  > "we can scale our observed-frame 3$\,$mm flux densities to the equivalent redshifted average ALESS templates and infer a crude estimate of the SFR" (Star formation rate estimate from ALESS templates)
   - assumption: 100-GHz flux of J0053a/b dominated by cold dust like SMGs  
  > "Assuming the 100-GHz continuum-detected J0053a/b have their flux dominated by cold dust like SMGs" (Star formation rate estimate from ALESS templates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with BEAGLE 68% upper SFR limits (~871 and ~640 Msun/yr)  
  > "The ALESS-scaled SFRs for J0053a and J0053b agree well with the 68% upper credible intervals from BEAGLE" (Comparison to radio galaxies in clusters and SMG clusters at z~4)
8. **Derive molecular gas masses from CO(4-3)**: Gaussian line flux to L'CO(4-3), then M_H2 = (alpha_CO / r_4-1) L'CO(4-3)  
  > "This yields the H$_2$ gas mass as" (Molecular gas mass estimates from CO(4-3))
   - parameter: alpha_CO / r_4-1 = 0.8  
  > "$\alpha_\mathrm{CO}/r_{4-1}=0.8$" (Estimating protocluster properties)
   - assumption: Fully thermalised CO SLED (r_4-1 = 1), low alpha_CO = 0.8; masses are lower limits  
  > "we make the conservative assumption, of a fully thermalised CO SLED (i.e. $r_{4-1}=1$) and low $\alpha_{\rm CO}=0.8$" (Molecular gas mass estimates from CO(4-3)); "Our H$_{\rm 2}$ gas masses are therefore lower limits." (Molecular gas mass estimates from CO(4-3))
   - assumption: Flat LCDM, Omega_M = 0.3, Omega_Lambda = 0.7, H0 = 70  
  > "$\Omega_M=0.3$, $\Omega_\Lambda=0.7$ and $H_0=70\;{\rm km\,s}^{-1}\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$" (Introduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **Compare protocluster density and halo with CANDELS and TNG300**: Source density per arcmin^2 per 0.05 redshift bin compared with CANDELS GOODS-S; most massive z=4 galaxy and its halo searched in TNG300  
  > "this field’s source density is 20 times that of the Cosmic Assembly Near-infrared Deep Extragalactic Legacy Survey" (Interpretation of sources); "utilise Illustris TNG300 (cite: IllustrisTNG_galaxy_groups_clusters_Pilepich_2018) to search for equivalent objects" (Massive, extremely red objects at z~4)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Software

- **CASA** : Concatenation, tclean imaging, imsmooth smoothing, imfit source fitting  
  > "Imaging was carried out using the Common Astronomy Software Applications (cite: CASA_McMullin_2007) package" (Imaging)
- **SoFiA 2** 2: Automated 3-D emission line search  
  > "we used version 2 of the HI Source Finding Application" (3-D emission line finding)
- **photutils** 1.6.0: 2-D segmentation of line maps and purity estimates (detect_sources)  
  > "we employ `photutils` (cite: photutils_1.6.0_Bradley_2022) to create a 2-D segmentation map of the line emission at the $3\sigma$ level" (Spectra and line analysis)
- **lmfit** 1.2.2: Gaussian fits to emission lines  
  > "We fit a model Gaussian to each line using `lmfit` (cite: lmfit_1.2.2_Newville_2023)" (Spectra and line analysis)
- **BEAGLE** : SED fitting of aperture photometry to derive stellar mass, attenuation and SFR  
  > "We utilise the BayEsian Analysis of GaLaxy sEds (cite: Beagle_Chevallard_2016) code to fit SEDs and galaxy properties" (SED modelling)
- **Data Central Data Aggregation Service** : Producing DES DR2 image cutouts  
  > "via cutouts produced by the Data Central Data Aggregation Service" (SED modelling)
- **astropy, numpy, scipy, matplotlib, TOPCAT** : General analysis and plotting  
  > "This research also made use of numpy (cite: Numpy_Harris_2020), matplotlib (cite: Matplotlib_Hunter_2007), scipy (cite: SciPy_Virtanen_2020), lmfit (cite: lmfit_1.2.2_Newville_2023) and topcat (cite: TOPCAT_Taylor_2005)." (Acknowledgements)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA scans Band 3 at 100 GHz to catch redshifted CO and carbon lines for redshift confirmation of radio-selected, NIR-faint UHzRG candidates  
  > "were included in 100-GHz spectroscopic follow-up with the Atacama Large Millimetre/sub-millimetre Array" (Introduction)
- Why this method: SoFiA 2 used to automate and make the line search more robust than by eye  
  > "we opted to use an application to automate spatial and spectral searches and to more robustly detect features that would otherwise be missed" (3-D emission line finding)
- Alternative: Top-heavy IMFs not used in BEAGLE (they would lower stellar masses)  
  > "We do not use any top-heavy IMFs as current discussions revolve around higher redshifts than we consider here" (BEAGLE)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): SED fits are limited by the few photometric points available  
  > "The Bayesian fits produced by BEAGLE are limited by the number of photometric data points available." (Fitted galaxy properties)
- Limitation (authors): Gas masses are only lower limits because of the assumed thermalised SLED and low alpha_CO  
  > "Our H$_{\rm 2}$ gas masses are therefore lower limits." (Molecular gas mass estimates from CO(4-3))
- Limitation (authors): SFR constraints weak without rest-frame UV detections  
  > "Constraints on star formation rate suffer from the lack of rest-frame UV detections." (Fitted galaxy properties)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Five of the CO(4-3) members rest on single, low S/N lines with a purity argument and no independent redshift
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar mass of J0053d depends on six-band photometry in a 2-arcsec aperture and an assumed star formation history
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Molecular gas masses and depletion times depend on a single CO transition and assumed r_4-1 and alpha_CO
- Caveat (inferred, ours): CANDELS overdensity factor compares different volumes and selection functions
