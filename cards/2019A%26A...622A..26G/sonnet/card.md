## Analysis recipe

1. **Extract absorption spectra**: Spectra extracted at continuum peak or weighted average of bright pixels, divided by continuum, smoothed to 1 km/s  
  > "The absorption spectra have been obtained by dividing the observed spectra by the continuum intensity in the same band." (ALMA observations)
   - software: CASA, GILDAS
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Remove contaminating lines**: Gaussian fits to H66epsilon recombination line and narrow CH3OH/ethanol absorption; line ID with Splatalogue, CDMS, JPL  
  > "We have fit the narrow absorption lines with Gaussian profiles, and used the residual to estimate the absorption due to C$_3$H$^+$ along the G10.62-0.4 line of sight." (ALMA observations)
   - software: Splatalogue, CDMS, JPL
   - fitting: Gaussian profiles  
  > "We have fit the narrow absorption lines with Gaussian profiles" (ALMA observations)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Derive column densities**: Integrate opacity over velocity intervals using N/∫τdv coefficients  
  > "The column densities have been derived by integrating the line opacity over selected velocity intervals" (ALMA observations)
   - assumption: Level populations in equilibrium with CMB (2.725 K)  
  > "we have assumed that the level population is in equilibrium with the Cosmic Microwave Background" (ALMA observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Channel-by-channel correlations with Herschel species**: Least-squares fits assuming proportionality, restricted to SNR>2.5 sigma and opacity<3.5, Galactic disk sightlines only  
  > "assuming the column densities are proportional to each other and restraining the fit to points with SNR greater than 2.5$\sigma$ and opacity lower than 3.5" (Results)
   - fitting: least squares, proportional  
  > "We obtained the following relationships from least square fits, assuming the column densities are proportional to each other and restraining the fit to points with SNR greater than 2.5$\sigma$ and opacity lower than 3.5" (Results, HOC$^+$ and HCO$^+$)
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Derive abundances relative to H2**: HCO+ abundance from CH-derived N(H2), then other ions from ratios to HCO+  
  > "Using the HCO$^+$ abundance as reference, with \[HCO$^+$\] = $3\times 10^{-9}$, we list in Table [\[tab:abundances\]](#tab:abundances) the abundances relative to H$_2$" (Molecular ion abundances)
   - assumption: [CH]=3.6e-8 relative to H2  
  > "\[CH\] = $3.6 \times 10^{-8}$ (cite: sheffer:08)" (Molecular ion abundances)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Steady-state chemical model comparison**: Analytic steady-state formulae for HOC+/H2O and CF+/HF with Langevin or enhanced capture rates  
  > "the ratios of the HOC$^+$ and H$_2$O abundances at steady state can be written" (Chemistry)
   - assumption: C+ main carbon carrier and electron source  
  > "We further assume that C$^+$ is the main carrier of carbon and all electrons are provided by C$^+$" (Chemistry)

## Qualitative findings

- CF+ abundance lower than predicted; formation rate of C+ + HF should be lowered by factor ~3  
  > "the rate coefficient of reaction R5 used by (cite: nw:09) is too high and should be revised downward by a factor of about $\sim 5.3/1.7 \sim 3$" (Chemistry)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Ground-state lines of these ions are at mm wavelengths accessible from ground, unlike HF and H2O  
  > "The ground state transitions of the three molecular ions discussed here are located at millimeter wavelengths where the atmospheric transmission is excellent." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): CF+ and C3H+ lines are weak and contaminated; Galactic center abundances cannot be determined absolutely  
  > "Absolute abundances cannot be determined in the central molecular zone because the relative abundances of the usual H$_2$-tracers (CH, HF, etc) have not been established in this region" (Summary and conclusions)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Abundances rest on a single CH/H2 ratio and Herschel CH data
- Caveat (inferred, ours): No stated treatment of non-CMB excitation or uncertainty propagation
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Chemical modeling is analytic steady state only
