## Analysis recipe

1. **Obtain calibrated data and image cubes**: ALMA-delivered calibrated data; channel maps at maximal spectral resolution  
  > "The calibrated data were produced by the ALMA observatory and following their delivery, where needed we made channel maps at maximal spectral resolution." (4 Data processing)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: CO(1-0) rest frequency 115.271208 GHz  
  > "we use a rest frequency of $f_{\textnormal{CO(1-0)}} = 115.271208$ GHz" (4 Data processing)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract spectra at continuum position**: Spectra extracted from a region centred on continuum source with size equal to synthesized beam FWHM  
  > "each extracted from a region centred on the continuum source with a size equal to the synthesized beam’s FHWM" (5 Molecular absorption)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Rest frequencies from intensity-weighted mean of CN hyperfine components: CN-A 113.49485, CN-B 113.16883 GHz  
  > "We use the intensity weighted mean of the component hyperfine structure lines to calculate the rest frequencies" (4 Data processing)
   - assumption: Redshifts from MUSE/VIMOS/FORS/SDSS give systemic velocity  
  > "We use a range of sources to determine the velocity of the emission and absorption features in each galaxy relative to its recession velocity." (4 Data processing)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Fit Gaussians to lines with Monte Carlo errors**: Gaussian fits to absorption and emission features; 10 000 noise-resimulated spectra; 1 sigma from 15.865 and 84.135 percentiles; emission fitted and subtracted where clear  
  > "Following this, 10 000 simulated spectra are produced." (5 Molecular absorption); "This is done by fitting and subtracting a Gaussian line to the CO(1-0) emission after excluding the velocity channels in which the absorption regions lie." (5 Molecular absorption)
   - parameter: Noise from rms of the continuum source's spectrum after excluding the visible line region  
  > "the noise level is estimated from the root mean square (rms) of the continuum source’s emission" (5 Molecular absorption)
   - fitting: Gaussian line fits; Gaussian noise per channel; no priors  
  > "For each of the 10 000 simulated spectra, Gaussian lines are fitted to the absorption and emission line features." (5 Molecular absorption)
   - validation: Residuals of fits shown in appendix; detection threshold 3 sigma  
  > "show the residuals for the spectra shown in Fig. 2, 3 and 4" (Residuals of Absorption and Emission Line Fits)
4. **Derive column densities and CO/CN ratio**: Optically thin total column density from velocity-integrated optical depth at assumed Tex of 20, 40, 80 K  
  > "Using an estimated excitation temperature and treating the absorption as optically thin, it is possible to calculate the total column density" (7 Column Density Estimates)
   - parameter: Tex = 20, 40, 80 K  
  > "Source | Temperature (K) | CO column density (cm$^{-2}$) | CN column density (cm$^{-2}$) | CO/CN ratio" (Table 6)
   - assumption: Optically thin absorption  
  > "treating the absorption as optically thin" (7 Column Density Estimates)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CO/CN of nearby galaxies (Wilson2018)  
  > "This is similar to the values found by (cite: Wilson2018) for nearby galaxies from ALMA observations of CO and CN emission" (7 Column Density Estimates)
5. **Compare with chaotic cold accretion**: Pencil-beam log sigma_v vs log |v_shift| diagram compared with Gaspari et al. 2018  
  > "to compute the pencil-beam points in the main diagnostic plot of $\log \sigma_v$ versus $\log |v_{\rm shift}|$" (8.3 Comparison with Chaotic Cold Accretion Simulations)
   - fitting: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- CN N=0-1 absorption is typically ~10 times stronger than CO(0-1) in integrated optical depth, owing to CN's higher dipole moment  
  > "We find that the CN N = 0 - 1 absorption lines are typically 10 times stronger than those of CO J = 0 - 1." (abstract)
- Absorption biased towards redshifted velocities (motion toward the black hole)  
  > "have a bias for motion towards the host supermassive black hole" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Bright compact mm cores act as backlights so absorption traces small-scale gas and unambiguous inflow/outflow  
  > "redshifted absorption unambiguously indicates inflow while blueshifted lines indicate outflow." (Introduction)
- Why this method: _not stated_
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Optical depths are often lower limits because emission may fill in absorption  
  > "In many cases, it should be noted that our calculations of the optical depths are simply lower limits." (5 Molecular absorption)
- Limitation (authors): CN spectral resolution is too low to reveal all but the widest strongest lines  
  > "they are likely to lack sufficient spectral resolution to reveal all but the widest and strongest absorption lines." (footnote 5)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Column densities depend on assumed Tex with no constraint
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Low-resolution CN hyperfine blending
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample for inflow bias conclusion
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Continuum variability and time offset between CO(2-1) and CO(1-0) epochs
