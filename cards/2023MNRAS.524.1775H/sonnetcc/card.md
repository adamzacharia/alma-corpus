## Analysis recipe

1. **Image the dust continuum and [CII] cube**: tclean continuum image excluding line region; continuum-subtracted cube imaged with tclean, natural weighting and robust 0.5  
  > "An image of the dust continuum was produced with the CASA command tclean, excluding the region with identified \[Cii\] line emission." (2.1 ALMA Data); "The \[Cii\] line was then imaged using the tclean task" (2.1 ALMA Data)
   - software: CASA 5.6.1, tclean
   - parameter: Continuum subtraction: zeroth-order polynomial, excluding twice the line FWHM  
  > "continuum subtracted with a zeroth order polynomial fitted to the continuum, excluding a spectral region with a width of twice the FWHM of the identified line emission" (2.1 ALMA Data)
   - parameter: Clean to 2 sigma with automasking; natural weighting; Briggs robust 0.5 for morphology  
  > "by cleaning down to a 2$\sigma$ threshold using a clean mask generated with tclean’s automasking feature" (2.1 ALMA Data); "We also reimage the data with a Briggs robust weighting of 0.5 to obtain higher resolution imaging with which to investigate the morphology of REBELS-25." (2.1 ALMA Data)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract the [CII] spectrum and make moment-0 maps**: Circular aperture spectrum (radius 1.75 arcsec, ~9 kpc, where recovered flux plateaus); immoments zeroth-moment maps of main and secondary components  
  > "we extract a spectrum by placing a circular aperture centred at the position of the galaxy of radius 1.75$''$ ($\sim 9$ kpc)" (3.1 Spectral analysis); "We use the CASA command immoments to collapse the cube over the spectral range of these lines and create a zeroth moment map." (3.1 Spectral analysis)
   - software: CASA immoments
   - parameter: Aperture radius 1.75 arcsec (~9 kpc), chosen where flux recovered plateaus  
  > "this radius being that at which the flux recovered from the galaxy plateaus" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
   - parameter: Main component roughly -250 to +250 km/s; secondary roughly +250 to +650 km/s  
  > "(roughly in the range +250 ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$ to +650 ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$)" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Data split into two halves and reimaged; secondary component remains visible (S/N 4.0 and 3.1)  
  > "the secondary \[Cii\] component remains visible in both halves of the data" (Appendix: Significance of the secondary [CII] emission component); "We determine a peak signal-to-noise ratio of 4.0 and 3.1 for the secondary component in first and second halves of the data." (Appendix: Significance of the secondary [CII] emission component)
3. **Fit Gaussian models to the [CII] spectrum and derive L_[CII]**: Fit six models (one or two Gaussians for the main component; broad Gaussian for outflow, narrow Gaussian for merger, or none for the secondary) and compare by reduced chi-squared  
  > "We model the primary emission component with either one or two Gaussians." (3.1 Spectral analysis); "We fit the secondary component with either a broad Gaussian to model an outflow or a narrow Gaussian to model a merging galaxy" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
   - software: scipy optimize.curve_fit
   - parameter: Main Gaussians: c > 0 mJy, -350 < mu < 250 km/s; merger Gaussian: c > 0, 250 < mu < 750 km/s; outflow Gaussian: c > 0, -350 < mu < 750 km/s, sigma > 150 km/s  
  > "For the Gaussian(s) representing the main component we require $c > 0$ mJy and $-350 \mbox{${\rm km~s^{-1}}$}{} < \mu < 250 \mbox{${\rm km~s^{-1}}$}{}$" (Appendix: Models fitted to the spectrum); "for the Gaussian representing an outflow we require $c > 0$ mJy, $-350 \mbox{${\rm km~s^{-1}}$}{} < \mu < 750 \mbox{${\rm km~s^{-1}}$}{}$ and $\sigma > 150$ ${\rm km~s^{-1}}$" (Appendix: Models fitted to the spectrum)
   - assumption: Double-peaked main component likely an unresolved merger or rotating disc  
  > "The observed double peak is likely indicative of unresolved velocity structure, most likely as a result of an unresolved major merger or disc rotation." (3.1 Spectral analysis)
   - fitting: Least-squares optimize.curve_fit with positive-amplitude and centre-range bounds (no sampler or priors)  
  > "We perform fits of our models using the optimize.curve_fit task in the scipy python package" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
   - validation: Reduced chi-squared per model; double-Gaussian merger model best (1.51), statistically indistinguishable from outflow model (1.64)  
  > "We assess the quality of fit for each of the models by calculating the reduced $\chi^2$ parameter." (3.1 Spectral analysis); "is our best-fitting model with reduced $\chi^2 = 1.51$, but is statistically indistinguishable" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
4. **Measure morphology, offsets, sizes and inclination**: imfit Gaussian fits to the robust 0.5 images for the main [CII], secondary [CII] and continuum; inclination from axis ratio of a thin circular disc; ALMA astrometric uncertainty from beam and S/N  
  > "using the task imfit in CASA" (3.2 Morphology); "We perform these fits for the main \[Cii\] emission component, the secondary \[Cii\] emission component and the continuum emission." (3.2 Morphology)
   - software: CASA imfit, CASA immoments
   - parameter: Inclination i = arccos(theta_min / theta_max)  
  > "Under the assumption of a thin circular disc, we then make a rough estimate of the inclination of the source using the ratio of the major and minor axes $i = \cos^{-1}{\theta_{min} / \theta_{max}}$" (3.2 Morphology)
   - parameter: Astrometric uncertainty = FWHM_beam / SNR / 0.9, up to a factor 2 worse in practice; S/N above 20 gives no improvement  
  > "The nominal uncertainty on astrometric positions of ALMA data is given by $\Delta\theta = {\rm FWHM}_{\rm beam} / SNR / 0.9$ (cite: Cortes2022)" (3.2 Morphology)
   - assumption: Thin circular disc for inclination  
  > "Under the assumption of a thin circular disc" (3.2 Morphology)
   - fitting: imfit single Gaussian per component (CASA)  
  > "we fit a Gaussian model to our images produced with a robust weighting of 0.5" (3.2 Morphology)
   - validation: Natural-weighted images give consistent values; offsets flagged as needing higher resolution because imfit errors exclude the image-plane position uncertainty  
  > "Thus higher resolution observations are required to confirm these offsets." (3.2 Morphology)
5. **Characterise the velocity field and line width**: First-moment map above 3 sigma (details in Schouws et al. in prep.); FWHM from single-Gaussian fit to main component  
  > "the map is made by taking the first moment of the image, with a threshold that only includes individual pixels that are above three times the noise level" (3.3 Kinematics); "estimating the FWHM of the \[Cii\] line from a fit to the main \[Cii\] emission component with a single Gaussian" (3.3 Kinematics)
   - software: CASA immoments
   - fitting: Thin rotating disc and unresolved merger models fitted by Schouws et al. (in prep.), not by this paper  
  > "and a separate model of an unresolved merger to REBELS-25" (3.3 Kinematics)
   - validation: Disc and merger models statistically indistinguishable; resolution criterion of three elements across the major axis not met  
  > "found them to be statistically indistinguishable from one another" (3.3 Kinematics); "this criterion is not met by our low-resolution data" (3.3 Kinematics)
6. **Estimate the dynamical mass**: M_dyn = 2.35e9 Msun / (gamma^2 sin^2 i) * (FWHM/100 km/s)^2 * R/kpc, evaluated for three gamma values  
  > "We employ the method outlined in (cite: Kohandel2019) to calculate it as" (3.3.1 Dynamical mass); "we thus evaluate $M_{\rm dyn}$ for all three values and report $M_{\rm dyn}$ as a range" (3.3.1 Dynamical mass)
   - parameter: R = major axis of the main [CII] component; i = 56 +/- 29 deg; FWHM = 316 +/- 15 km/s; gamma = 1.78, 2.03, 1.52  
  > "where we take $R$ to be the major axis of the main \[Cii\] component" (3.3.1 Dynamical mass); "with $\gamma$ = 1.78, 2.03, 1.52, when the galaxy is in a spiral disc, disturbed disc, and merger stage, respectively" (3.3.1 Dynamical mass)
   - assumption: gamma not chosen because morphology is uncertain; disc or merger  
  > "we do not select one value of $\gamma$" (3.3.1 Dynamical mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **Infer the molecular gas mass from L_[CII]**: M_H2 = alpha_[CII] * L_[CII] with alpha_[CII] = 31 (+31/-15) Msun/Lsun  
  > "to estimate the molecular gas mass, $M_{{\rm H}_{2}}$ in solar masses, of REBELS-25 from the empirical relation calibrated by (cite: Zanella2018)" (3.4 ISM properties); "is an empirically calibrated conversion factor" (3.4 ISM properties)
   - parameter: alpha_[CII] = 31 (+31/-15) Msun/Lsun  
  > "We have adopted $\alpha_{\mbox{[C{\sc ii}]}{}} = 31^{+31}_{-15}$, following (cite: Zanella2018)" (3.4 ISM properties)
   - assumption: Relation calibrated at lower redshift; ISM conditions at very high z probably differ  
  > "this relation was calibrated for galaxies at significantly lower redshift than REBELS-25" (3.4 ISM properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Zanella2018 relation agreed with dynamical-mass and dust-based gas masses for ALPINE galaxies (cited, not tested here)  
  > "found that the (cite: Zanella2018) relation agreed with those derived from dynamical masses and dust continuum luminosities" (3.4 ISM properties)
8. **Derive SFR (UV+IR and [CII]) and gas depletion time**: SFR_tot = k_IR L_IR + k_UV L_nu,UV; second SFR from De Looze [CII] relation; t_depl = M_H2 / SFR_tot  
  > "We calculate the total star formation rate for the galaxy using UV to trace unobscured star formation and IR to trace obscured star formation" (3.5 Star formation); "Under these assumptions, $t_{\rm depl, {\rm H}_{2}} = M_{{\rm H}_{2},\mbox{[C{\sc ii}]}{}} / {\rm SFR}_{\rm tot}" (3.5 Star formation)
   - parameter: k_IR = 1.2e-10 Msun/yr/Lsun; k_UV = 7.1e-10 Msun/yr/(erg/s/Hz); C_Kroupa = 1.08  
  > "where $k_{\rm IR} = 1.2\times 10^{-10} \mbox{M$_\odot$}\mbox{${\rm yr}$}^{-1} \mbox{L$_\odot$}^{-1}$" (3.5 Star formation); "where $C_{\rm Kroupa} = 1.08$ is a factor to convert from a (cite: KroupaWeidner2003) IMF to a (cite: Chabrier2003) IMF" (3.5 Star formation)
   - assumption: Dust temperature 46 K for L_IR

## Qualitative findings

- REBELS-25 shows a [CII] velocity gradient consistent with disc rotation but equally consistent with beam-smeared merging galaxies  
  > "However, due to the large beam of our current \[Cii\] data, this could equally be the result of merging galaxies whose kinematics are smeared by the beam." (5 Conclusions)
- Dust continuum is offset from the UV clumps, by more than the astrometric uncertainty  
  > "we conclude that the the offsets that are observed between the UV components and both the dust and main \[Cii\] component centre are larger than the uncertainties" (3.2 Morphology)
- Most of the star formation is obscured (IR-traced)  
  > "From this it can be seen that the obscured star formation, as traced by IR emission, dominates the star formation in REBELS-25." (3.5 Star formation)
- Secondary [CII] component could be a minor merger or an outflow; an outflow consistent with star-formation driving is possible  
  > "which we interpret as either a merging companion or an outflow" (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: REBELS-25 is the strongest ~158 um continuum emitter and the only robust ULIRG in REBELS, with complex morphology  
  > "REBELS-25, which is the strongest $\sim158$ μm continuum emitter in the sample" (1 Introduction); "we find that REBELS-25 is the galaxy most robustly defined as a ULIRG" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Two Gaussians motivated by the double-peaked profile, with a single Gaussian as a conservative alternative; natural weighting for sensitivity  
  > "We are motivated to consider a model with two Gaussians by the observed double-peaked profile" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
- Alternative: [CII]-based SFR rejected in favour of UV+IR SFR because of increased scatter at high redshift  
  > "the increased scatter at higher redshifts disfavours its usage compared to the UV + IR SFR" (3.5 Star formation)
- Alternative: Non-parametric stellar mass (19e9 Msun) considered as alternative to the adopted 8e9 Msun; conservative lower mass kept  
  > "We note in our analysis how this would affect our results." (2.3 Source identification and properties)
- Alternative: Alternative dust temperatures and L_IR from other works (Sommovigo2022 55 K; Algera2023 34-43 K) discussed but 46 K adopted  
  > "we have adopted the median dust temperature (46K) determined from a sample of the REBELS galaxies" (4.1.1 ULIRG)
- How they decided it was enough: Aperture radius set where recovered [CII] flux plateaus  
  > "this radius being that at which the flux recovered from the galaxy plateaus" (3.1 Spectral analysis)
- Limitation (authors): Sources only marginally resolved at current resolution  
  > "However, we caution that the sources are only marginally resolved at the current angular resolution of the data." (3.2 Morphology)
- Limitation (authors): Resolution precludes distinguishing disc rotation from other scenarios  
  > "the resolution of the data preclude us from distinguishing between this and other scenarios" (3.3 Kinematics)
- Limitation (authors): Outflow size and gas conditions are uncertain, so outflow rates are quite uncertain  
  > "due to the difficulty of determining the size of the outflow these numbers are quite uncertain" (5 Conclusions)
- Limitation (authors): alpha_[CII] calibrated at lower redshift; large uncertainty  
  > "there remains significant uncertainty as to the value of $\alpha_{\mbox{[C{\sc ii}]}{}}$" (3.4 ISM properties)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Several key inputs (redshift, L_IR, M*, L_UV, moment-1 map, kinematic fits) come from in-preparation papers and cannot be checked from this paper
- Caveat (inferred, ours): L_IR rests on a single 158 um point with an assumed 46 K dust temperature; the cited alternatives give L_IR from 10^11.85 to 10^12.45 Lsun, so ULIRG status is uncertain
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Model selection among Gaussian spectral models uses only reduced chi-squared with unspecified noise and no information-criterion penalty
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Secondary component significance is S/N 4.7 and 3.1 in the weaker data half; baseline and continuum-subtraction choices could affect broad wings
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Quiescent-progenitor conclusion rests on a toy model with constant SFE and Gaussian-sampled inputs
