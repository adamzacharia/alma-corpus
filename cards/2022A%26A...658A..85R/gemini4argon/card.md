## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure and fit the laboratory rotational spectrum of thioacetamide and compute partition functions**: Record absorption spectrum from 150 to 650 GHz with the Lille fast-scanning THz spectrometer, fit transitions together with literature 59.6-110 GHz lines using the rho-axis-method (RAM) Hamiltonian in RAM36, and compute rotational-torsional and vibrational partition functions using ab initio harmonic frequencies from Gaussian 09.  
  > "The absorption measurements of the rotational spectrum between 150 and 650 GHz were conducted using the fast-scanning Terahertz spectrometer in Lille." (Experimental details); "For the analysis, we employed the RAM36 (rho-axis-method for 3- and 6-fold barriers) code that uses the RAM approach" (Analysis of the spectra); "The normal modes were obtained from the harmonic force field ab initio calculation at the B3LYP/6-311G++(3dp, 2pd) level of theory and basis set using `Gaussian 09` (cite: g09)." (Analysis of the spectra)
   - software: RAM36, Gaussian 09
   - parameter: Frequency range 150 to 650 GHz, sample pressure ~15 Pa at room temperature, 40 RAM Hamiltonian parameters, 2035 lines fitted (v_t=0 and v_t=1)  
  > "The sample pressure during measurements was $\sim15$ Pa at room temperature." (Experimental details); "The total of 2035 lines for $v_t =0$ and $v_t =1$ states of $A$ and $E$ symmetries were fitted with root-mean-square deviation of 43.4 kHz (wrms=0.90). The final fit is based on the RAM Hamiltonian model that includes 40 parameters." (Analysis of the spectra)
   - assumption: Dipole moment components mu_a = 4.1 D and mu_b = -1.1 D; harmonic approximation for lowest 7 vibrational modes (modes 2 to 8) below 1000 cm^-1  
  > "It has nonzero dipole components along the $a$- ($\mu_a$ = 4.1 D) and $b$-axes ($\mu_b$ = -1.1 D) as determined by (cite: Sundaresa1957)." (Analysis of the spectra); "Only the seven lowest vibrational excited state levels were considered (mode 2 to 8 in the Table 2)." (Analysis of the spectra)
   - fitting: Iterative least-squares fit of 40 RAM Hamiltonian parameters to 2035 transitions, achieving rms = 43.4 kHz and wrms = 0.90  
  > "The total of 2035 lines for $v_t =0$ and $v_t =1$ states of $A$ and $E$ symmetries were fitted with root-mean-square deviation of 43.4 kHz (wrms=0.90)." (Analysis of the spectra)
   - validation: Inclusion of v_t = 1 state stabilized the fit and removed strong correlation between F and rho parameters  
  > "In particular, the inclusion of the $v_{t}=1$ state permitted the removal of the strong correlation between $F$ and $\rho$ parameters of the Hamiltonian, and to accurately determine the V$_6$ parameter." (Analysis of the spectra)
2. **Search for thioacetamide and derive column density upper limit toward Sgr B2(N2) using ALMA EMoCA data**: Model the observed ALMA EMoCA spectrum at the peak of Sgr B2(N2) under LTE using WEEDS, adding contributions of previously modeled molecules and deriving a column density upper limit for CH3CSNH2.  
  > "We used the software `WEEDS` (cite: 2011A&A...526A..47M) to model the observed spectrum under the assumption of local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and produce a synthetic spectrum." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA); "As described in (cite: 2016A&A...587A..91B), we modeled the emission of each molecule separately and then added their contributions together." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA)
   - software: WEEDS
   - parameter: Source size = 0.9'', T_rot = 180 K, line width Delta V = 5.0 km s^-1, velocity offset V_off = 1.5 km s^-1 (with respect to v_sys = 74 km s^-1), F_vib = 1.20  
  > "We modeled the spectrum of each species with five parameters: size of the emitting region, column density, temperature, line width, and velocity offset with respect to the assumed systemic velocity of the source ($74\ \mathrm{km\ s}^{-1}$)." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA); "| , $\varv=0$ | n | 0 | 0.9 | 180 | $<$ 1.6 (16) | 1.20 | 5.0 | $1.5$ |" (Table 4)
   - assumption: LTE assumption justified by high density (>1x10^7 cm^-3); CH3CSNH2 assumed to trace the same region with fixed size, temperature, line width, and velocity offset as derived for CH3CONH2  
  > "This assumption is justified by the high densities that characterize the region where the hot core emission is detected ($>1 \times 10^7\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, see (cite: 2019A&A...628A..27B))." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA); "In order to derive an upper limit to its column density, we assumed that its emission should trace a similar region as and we fixed the size of the emitting region, temperature, line width, and velocity offset to the values derived for in Sgr B2(N2) by (cite: 2017A&A...601A..49B)." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA)
   - fitting: Single free parameter (column density of CH3CSNH2) adjusted to derive the upper limit against the observed spectrum  
  > "The only free parameter was then the column density of . Figure 1 shows the synthetic spectrum of that we used to derive the upper limit to its column density." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA)
   - validation: Visual comparison of synthetic LTE spectrum overlaid on the observed EMoCA spectrum and full multi-species model (Figure 1)  
  > "Figure 1 shows the synthetic spectrum of that we used to derive the upper limit to its column density." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA)
3. **Model acetamide transitions and search for thioacetamide toward Sgr B2(N) LMH using GBT PRIMOS data**: Model A- and E-state transitions of CH3CONH2 (including HFS below 18 GHz) and simulate CH3CSNH2 spectra between 8 and 24 GHz in the PRIMOS survey using MOLSIM with a single excitation temperature LTE model and non-thermal background.  
  > "We used the software `MOLSIM` (cite: Lee_molsim_2021) to perform the modeling and to estimate the upper limit for the spectra analysis of the PRIMOS and ASAI surveys." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT); "As was done when first characterizing the astronomical spectra of , we used an LTE-model[^4] assuming a non-thermal background source of emission (cite: 2007ApJ...660L.125H; 2016Sci...352.1449M) and simulated the spectrum of assuming a temperature of 5.8 K, a source size of $\sim20\ensuremath{''}$ and a line width of $20\ \mathrm{km\ s}^{-1}$." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT)
   - software: MOLSIM
   - parameter: T_ex = 5.8 K, source size = 20'', velocity components at +62, +71, and +81 km s^-1 (for CH3CONH2); line width = 20 km s^-1 (for CH3CSNH2)  
  > "What also differs from the initial detection paper by (cite: 2006ApJ...643L..25H) is that the best fit velocity components of occur at +62, +71 and $+81 \ \mathrm{km\ s}^{-1}$" (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT); "In total, the best fit model to the PRIMOS data was found for an excitation temperature of 5.8 K and a source size of $20\ensuremath{''}$ for both the A- and E-state transitions" (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT)
   - assumption: Single excitation temperature (5.8 K), thermalized transitions, optical depth correction, and non-thermal background continuum emission  
  > "Synthetic spectra are produced with a single excitation temperature assumption, that all transitions are thermalized, and corrected for optical depth as discussed in (cite: 1991ApJS...76..617T) and (cite: 2015PASP..127..266M)." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT); "we used an LTE-model[^4] assuming a non-thermal background source of emission (cite: 2007ApJ...660L.125H; 2016Sci...352.1449M)" (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT)
   - fitting: LTE modeling of multi-component absorption/emission profiles for CH3CONH2; upper limit for CH3CSNH2 derived from the lowest noise level in the passbands  
  > "To set an upper limit of , a rotational temperature of 5.8 K was used and based on the lowest noise level from the passbands, an upper limit of $2.2\times10^{13} \ \mathrm{cm}^{-2}$ was found" (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT)
   - validation: Consistency of derived CH3CONH2 column densities with earlier GBT studies by Hollis et al. (2006) and Halfen et al. (2011)

## Qualitative findings

- No transitions of thioacetamide (CH3CSNH2) were detected toward Sgr B2(N2), Sgr B2(N) LMH, NGC 6334I, or any of the eight ASAI sources.  
  > "No transitions of were detected toward any of the sources contained in our survey." (abstract)
- Thioacetamide is at least ~9 times less abundant than acetamide in Sgr B2(N2), at least an order of magnitude less abundant in the extended gas of Sgr B2(N), and at least 25 times less abundant (ratio < 0.04) in NGC 6334I.  
  > "indicates that is at least $\sim9$ times less abundant than in Sgr B2(N2)." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA); "Given the measured column density of $4.7\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{cm}^{-2}$, the upper limit of the / abundance ratio is $0.04$" (Search for toward NGC 6334I with ALMA)
- New A- and E-state transitions of acetamide (CH3CONH2) were detected in GBT PRIMOS data toward Sgr B2(N) LMH with velocity components at +62, +71, and +81 km s^-1, showing resolved HFS and maser emission at 6358 MHz.  
  > "The lowest frequency transition detected near 6358 MHz (Figure 2a) shows resolved HFS, multiple velocity components at +62, +71 and $+81\ \mathrm{km\ s}^{-1}$ and maser activity." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT)
- Proposed neutral-neutral gas-phase formation reactions for thioacetamide are exothermic but possess large activation energy barriers (>110 kJ/mole), making them infeasible in cold interstellar conditions.  
  > "Though all proposed reactions are exothermic, the associated energy barriers are rather large for feasible reaction paths in cold interstellar conditions." (Discussion)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Selected surveys (ALMA EMoCA, ALMA NGC 6334I, GBT PRIMOS, IRAM 30m ASAI) cover sources with confirmed detections of the O-analog acetamide (Sgr B2(N), NGC 6334I) as well as both hot compact cores (ALMA) and cold extended gas (GBT, IRAM 30m).  
  > "Thioacetamide () is the sulfur analog to and is a viable candidate to search for in astronomical environments - specifically toward regions where other S-bearing molecules have been found and, if possible, that also contain a detection of ." (Introduction); "As such, from these two complementary data sets, it is possible to investigate the hot compact regions as well as the colder more extended molecular gas in searching for transitions of toward the Sgr B2(N) region." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N) with the GBT)
- Why this method: The rho-axis-method (RAM) Hamiltonian simultaneously treats A- and E-symmetry species and inter-torsional interactions for low internal rotation barriers; LTE spectral modeling is justified in hot cores by high gas densities (>10^7 cm^-3).  
  > "The main advantage of the RAM Hamiltonian is its general approach that simultaneously takes into account the $A$- and $E$-symmetry species and all torsional levels, intrinsically taking the inter-torsional interactions into account within the rotation-torsion manifold of energy levels." (Analysis of the spectra); "This assumption is justified by the high densities that characterize the region where the hot core emission is detected ($>1 \times 10^7\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, see (cite: 2019A&A...628A..27B))." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA)
- Alternative: Grain-surface radical recombination routes analogous to acetamide formation were considered unlikely because key sulfur precursors (such as H2S and HS) have not been unambiguously identified on interstellar grain mantles.  
  > "The grain surface reactions to the formation of larger S-bearing molecules require that a radical containing S (such as, ) or neutral species (such as, ) be available in large abundances on grain surfaces. Since these precursor species have yet to be directly detected on grain surfaces, it is unlikely that analogous routes with S-bearing species in reactions (6-8) would lead to the formation of larger S-bearing molecules." (Discussion)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Hyperfine splittings of thioacetamide could not be resolved in the laboratory spectrum due to Doppler-limited resolution.  
  > "However, it was not possible to observe the hyperfine splittings due to our Doppler-limited resolution." (Analysis of the spectra)
- Limitation (authors): b-type (^bR and ^bQ) transitions of thioacetamide could not be assigned unambiguously in the laboratory spectrum due to spectral density and small relative intensities.  
  > "Next, we searched for the $^bR$ and $^bQ$ lines. However, these transitions could not be assigned unambiguously due to the dense spectra and their much smaller relative intensities." (Analysis of the spectra)
- Limitation (authors): Expected thioacetamide line intensities in Sgr B2(N2) may be an order of magnitude below the spectral confusion limit in the 3 mm band.  
  > "If these two pairs of molecules have similar abundance ratios, then the emission lines of may be one order of magnitude weaker than the upper limits we obtained with the EMoCA survey, and thus most likely well below the spectral confusion limit toward this source in this frequency range." (Search for toward Sgr B2(N2) with ALMA)
- Limitation (authors): Non-LTE maser activity in low-frequency GBT PRIMOS transitions is not captured by the single-temperature LTE model.  
  > "The lowest frequency transition detected near 6358 MHz (Figure 2a) shows resolved HFS, multiple velocity components at +62, +71 and $+81\ \mathrm{km\ s}^{-1}$ and maser activity. - Caveat (inferred, ours): Using a single-temperature LTE model for GBT PRIMOS cm-wave transitions is an approximation since several transitions exhibit non-LTE maser emission or weak absorption/emission flips.
