## Analysis recipe

1. **measure the 150 GHz continuum flux density**: Two-term Taylor multi-frequency synthesis image from continuum basebands with Uniform weighting; integrated flux density measured  
  > "we generated a two-term Taylor multi-frequency synthesis image with Uniform weighting" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: Uniform weighting; resolution 0.33 x 0.76 arcsec at 152 GHz  
  > "giving a resolution of 0 _._ 33 _×_ 0 _._ 76 arcsec at 152 GHz" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - assumption: Continuum is unresolved by ALMA  
  > "The continuum emission from PKS B1740 _−_ 517 is unresolved by our ALMA" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **extract the CO(2-1) absorption spectrum**: Cube imaged at 2 km/s with natural weighting; spectrum taken at the peak continuum position and normalised by a 1st-order polynomial fit to the continuum to give percentage absorption  
  > "extracted the spectrum from the cube at the position of peak continuum flux density" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 2 km/s velocity resolution; natural weighting  
  > "we imaged the visibilities at the full 2 km s _[−]_ [1] velocity resolution" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - assumption: Molecular gas optically thick (tau>1) for the absorbed fraction interpretation  
  > "If the molecular gas is optically thick to [12] CO (21) then the seemingly low absorbed fraction" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: 1st-order polynomial to the continuum  
  > "We fitted a 1st-order polynomial to the continuum" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - validation: Compared with ASKAP HI absorption spectrum on the same velocity frame  
  > "The velocity axis is given in the rest frame defined by the broader H I and CO absorption" (Figure 2 caption)
3. **search for CO(2-1) emission in the host galaxy**: Continuum subtracted in visibilities with UVSUB, then a 1st-order polynomial with UVCONTSUB; cube binned to 10 km/s with natural weighting; spectrum summed in a 1.75 arcsec aperture  
  > "We then formed a data cube in 10 km s _[−]_ [1] velocity bins" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array); "within 1.75 arcsec (a projected distance of 10 kpc) of PKS B1740 _−_ 517" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - software: UVSUB, UVCONTSUB
   - parameter: 10 km/s bins; 1.75 arcsec radius aperture  
  > "within 1.75 arcsec (a projected distance of 10 kpc) of PKS B1740 _−_ 517" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - assumption: Emission line width 400 km/s assumed for the undetected line  
  > "We assume a line width of 400 km s _[−]_ [1] for the unde" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Non-detection; 5-sigma limit computed from RMS  
  > "giving a 5- _σ_ detection limit for the line integrated flux density of 279 mJy km s" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
4. **average the ASKAP HI absorption spectra**: Continuum fitted and subtracted per observation with UVLIN; epochs averaged with inverse-variance weights  
  > "weighting each observation by its inverse variance" (3.2 The Australian SKA Pathfinder)
   - software: MIRIAD, UVLIN
   - parameter: 5.6 km/s channels in averaged spectrum; RMS 0.07 per cent per 5.6 km/s  
  > "with a resulting RMS fractional continuum absorption of 0 _._ 07 per cent" (3.2 The Australian SKA Pathfinder)
   - assumption: No astrophysical variation in the HI absorption over a few years  
  > "Assuming no astrophysical variation in the absorption line over the timescale" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - fitting: Continuum fitted with UVLIN, subtracted from target spectrum  
  > "we can simply fit and subtract any residual continuum signal in the target spectrum using" (3.2 The Australian SKA Pathfinder)
   - validation: Per-epoch spectra and difference spectra shown to check artefacts  
  > "The difference spectra (blue filled) are shown in the panels below each epoch" (Figure 6 caption)
5. **measure the width of the narrow HI component**: Jointly model the narrow component as two Gaussians across all epochs, convolved with the telescope spectral response  
  > "jointly modeling the narrower H I component as the superposition of two Gaussians" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - parameter: Convolution with fine filter bank response  
  > "by convolving with the fine filter bank response (Tuthill et al. 2012)" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - assumption: Narrow component is a single cold diffuse structure  
  > "we are likely seeing absorption through a single diffuse structure of cold neutral medium" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - fitting: Two-Gaussian joint model fit with asymmetric width error  
  > "we obtain a FHWM line width for the deep narrow component of" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - validation: Line peak crossing channels as Earth moves shows finite width  
  > "thus indicating that it has a finite width on scales comparable to the resolution" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
6. **estimate line-of-sight extent of the absorbing disc**: Circular orbit geometry: velocity separation of absorption peaks related to source separation to give line-of-sight extent of gas  
  > "we can then obtain an approximate estimate" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - parameter: v_circ about 200 km/s; source separation about 300 pc; velocity separation about 20 km/s; inclination 0 to 90 deg; phi 0 to 90 deg  
  > "and assuming _v_ circ _∼_ 200 km s _[−]_ [1], we estimate" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: Gas in circular orbit about the galactic centre  
  > "For gas moving in a circular orbit with velocity _v_ circ with respect to the galactic centre" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
7. **derive HI and H2 column densities and enclosed gas masses**: Optical depth integrated over velocity converted to column density (HI with spin temperature 100 to 2000 K; CO with optically thin-to-thick assumption and T_ul >= 10 K); H2 from CO with Milky Way X_CO; disc model with scale height 150 pc and radius 2 kpc  
  > "We therefore consider spin temperatures in the range 100 to 2000 K, giving" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir); "Assuming that the atomic gas has" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - parameter: Spin temperature 100 to 2000 K  
  > "We therefore consider spin temperatures in the range 100 to 2000 K, giving" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - parameter: Scale height 150 pc; radial extent 2 kpc  
  > "Assuming that the atomic gas has a scale height _h_ _∼_ 150 pc" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - parameter: Milky Way X_CO for the upper H2 limit; Z_CO about 1e-4 for the lower limit  
  > "Without further information we use _X_ CO for molecular gas in the Milky Way" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: Covering factor close to unity for HI  
  > "implying that the covering factor may well be close to unity" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: Lower limit on CO-to-H2 ratio applies to dense clouds; diffuse ISM ratio could be two orders of magnitude lower  
  > "the [12] CO-to-H 2 abundance ratio can be two orders of magnitude lower in the diffuse interstellar medium" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared H2 lower limit with CO emission non-detection  
  > "We can compare this lower limit with the non-detection of" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
8. **estimate star formation rate**: SFR from [OII] 3727 luminosity using Kewley et al. (2004) relation; SFR surface density from Kennicutt-Schmidt law applied to gas surface density  
  > "The KennicuttSchmidt law (Kennicutt 1998) then gives" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - parameter: [OII] luminosity corrected for Galactic extinction; extraction region 0.7 arcsec  
  > "corrected for Galactic extinction, is" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: SFR is an upper limit because AGN may contribute to the [OII] emission  
  > "This is an upper limit since there is likely to be significant" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: Gas mass correction of 37 per cent for helium and metals  
  > "(with a 37 per cent correction for helium and metals)" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **fit the radio SED and estimate the source age**: Least squares fit of the Moffet-type model with optically thick and thin power laws and a high-frequency continuous-injection break; spectral age from equipartition magnetic field  
  > "Least squares fitting gives _v_ p = 786 MHz" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - parameter: Break frequency 16 GHz; continuous-injection break; equipartition field; component size 10 mas  
  > "Assuming equipartition of the cosmic ray and magnetic field energy densities" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - assumption: Magnetic field uniformity, equipartition and electron mixing; age is an order-of-magnitude estimate  
  > "this estimate of the source age involves several assumptions, including magnetic field uniformity, equipartition, and electron mixing" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - fitting: Least squares fit of the parameterised SED  
  > "Least squares fitting gives _v_ p = 786 MHz" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - validation: Source age compared with hot spot advancement estimates; consistent within an order of magnitude  
  > "typically consistent to within an order of magnitude with estimates" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
10. **test whether flux variability could explain the ASKAP flux changes**: Standard deviation of flux for point sources in the same field compared to the target  
  > "We test for this by measuring the standard deviation in flux density for other point sources within the same field." (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - parameter: Sources brighter than 30 times local RMS and within the PAF beam FWHM  
  > "We choose sources that have a flux density greater than 30 times their local image RMS" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - assumption: Systematic errors detectable at about 10 per cent  
  > "so that we can detect any systematic behaviour in flux density at the level of" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Comparison with control sources in the same field  
  > "we obtain a median standard deviation of" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA was used to search for CO emission and absorption tracing the cold molecular gas, and to constrain the high-frequency continuum for spectral age  
  > "Here we have used ALMA to search for [12] CO (2-1) emission and absorption" (1 INTRODUCTION)
- Why this method: Natural weighting used for the line cubes to maximise detected signal-to-noise; Uniform weighting for the continuum  
  > "using natural weighting (with a resolution of 0 _._ 5 _×_ 1 _._ 1 arcsec) to maximise detected signal-to-noise" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
- Alternative: Gas too faint at mm wavelengths to detect CO behind the weaker component, rejected as the sole explanation for the non-detection  
  > "Before discussing a physical interpretation for the absence of" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
- Alternative: Narrow HI line is a jet-driven outflow, rejected because the line width is too narrow  
  > "the line width (∆ _v_ FHWM _<_ 5 km s _[−]_ [1] ) is inconsistent with jet-driven gas outflows" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
- Alternative: Narrow HI structure in the outskirts of the host, rejected as it would be non-circular and still implies tidal interaction  
  > "indicating a tidal interaction either way" (4.1.4 A low-metallicity cloud in the tidal stream?)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Continuum source is unresolved by ALMA so CO covering fraction and kinematics are not determined  
  > "The continuum emission from PKS B1740 _−_ 517 is unresolved by our ALMA" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
- Limitation (authors): Mass estimates apply only to line-of-sight gas in absorption and may underestimate total host masses  
  > "we stress that our estimates are only for line-of-sight gas detected in absorption" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
- Limitation (authors): Spin temperature and CO-to-H2 conversion are uncertain and set the mass range  
  > "Without further information we use _X_ CO for molecular gas in the Milky Way" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass ranges depend heavily on assumed spin temperature (100-2000 K), X_CO and the covering factor; the paper gives no sensitivity analysis for these inputs.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The CO covering fraction and filling factor used in the upper limit are not measured and rely on assumed cloud sizes.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The age estimate depends on equipartition and component size assumptions; authors call it order-of-magnitude.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Companion association with the absorber is based on velocity similarity, not a confirmed stream.
