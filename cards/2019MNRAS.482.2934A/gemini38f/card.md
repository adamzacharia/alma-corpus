## Qualitative findings

- 12CO (2-1) absorption is detected in PKS B1740-517, coincident with the broad HI absorption component, tracing cold gas in a circumnuclear disc or ring within ~2 kpc of the AGN.  
  > "The coincident H I 21-cm and molecular absorption have very similar line profiles and reveal a reservoir of cold gas (M gas ∼ 10^7 − 10^8 M ⊙ ), likely distributed in a disc or ring within a few kiloparsecs of the nucleus." (abstract)
- The kinematically distinct deep narrow HI absorption component has no detected 12CO counterpart, consistent with a small cold atomic cloud (<100 pc) or low-metallicity gas tidally stripped from a companion galaxy.  
  > "The 12CO (2-1) absorption is not associated with this component, which suggests that the cloud is either much smaller than 100 pc along our sight-line and/or located in lowmetallicity gas that was possibly tidally stripped from the companion." (abstract)
- The radio SED shows a continuous injection spectral break at 16 GHz, indicating a young source age (~1600 yr) triggered well after the tidal interaction began (~50 Myr).  
  > "We argue that the gas reservoir in PKS B1740 − 517 may have accreted onto the host galaxy ∼ 50 Myr before the young radio AGN was triggered, but has only recently reached the nucleus." (abstract)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: PKS B1740-517 is a bright, young GPS radio source at z = 0.44 with known HI absorption; ALMA Band 4 offers the sensitivity to detect redshifted 12CO (2-1) absorption and high-frequency continuum.  
  > "Here we have used ALMA to search for 12CO (2-1) emission and absorption, tracing the cold molecular component of the neutral gas, and to provide a stronger constraint on the high frequency continuum and hence spectral age of the source." (1 INTRODUCTION)
- Why this method: Natural weighting was chosen to maximize signal-to-noise on absorption and emission, while uniform weighting was used on continuum to optimize spatial resolution.  
  > "Since the continuum source is not resolved with uniform weighting we used natural weighting (with a resolution of 0.5 × 1.1 arcsec) to maximise detected signal-to-noise" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
- Alternative: Jet-driven gas outflow rejected for the narrow HI component because the line width (∆v FWHM < 5 km/s) is much too narrow to be accelerated by a jet to >100 km/s.  
  > "However, the line width (∆v FHWM < 5 km s−1) is inconsistent with jet-driven gas outflows that are typically associated with young radio sources... It is unlikely that the gas could be accelerated by the jet to more than 100 km s−1 and retain such a low dispersion." (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
- Alternative: Considered whether the lack of CO detection in the narrow HI component is merely an illumination effect (narrow line only in front of weaker radio component), but noted low-metallicity or small cloud size remain highly plausible alternative physical explanations.  
  > "The ALMA data are therefore consistent with not detecting 12CO (2-1) absorption, if the sight-line giving rise to the narrow H I line is seen only toward the weaker source component." (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption); "It is therefore possible that the absence of CO absorption associated with the narrow H I absorption in PKS B1740 − 517 is evidence for low metallicity gas that is being tidally stripped from a companion satellite galaxy." (4.1.4 A low-metallicity cloud in the tidal stream?)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): ALMA observations do not spatially resolve the radio continuum source or the absorbing gas structures (which are separated by 52 mas / 300 pc).  
  > "The continuum emission from PKS B1740 − 517 is unresolved by our ALMA observations" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array); "Since information about the gas kinematics encoded in the spectrum is limited by the spatial extent of the continuum source, it is difficult to determine the three dimensional motion of the gas from the unresolved absorption spectrum alone." (4.1 Constraints on the line-of-sight cold gas)
- Limitation (authors): Mass estimates are based on pencil-beam absorption measurements and simple disc geometry assumptions, and source age estimate assumes equipartition and uniform magnetic fields.  
  > "However, we stress that our estimates are only for line-of-sight gas detected in absorption towards the radio source, and may underestimate the total masses in the host galaxy." (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir); "We note this estimate of the source age involves several assumptions, including magnetic field uniformity, equipartition, and electron mixing... and should therefore be considered a useful order of magnitude measurement of the source age." (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Covering factor of the molecular clouds across the two VLBI components is uncertain and could explain the absence of CO in the narrow HI component without requiring low metallicity.
