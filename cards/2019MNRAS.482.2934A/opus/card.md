## Analysis recipe

1. **Continuum imaging and flux**: Two-term Taylor MFS image of continuum basebands with uniform weighting; source unresolved, total flux measured  
  > "The continuum emission from PKS B1740 _−_ 517 is unresolved by our ALMA observations" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: nterms=2, Uniform weighting  
  > "we generated a two-term Taylor multi-frequency synthesis image with Uniform weighting" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Extract CO absorption spectrum**: Natural-weighted cube at full velocity resolution; spectrum at peak continuum pixel normalised by 1st-order polynomial continuum fit  
  > "extracted the spectrum from the cube at the position of peak continuum flux density" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: natural weighting, 0.5 x 1.1 arcsec  
  > "we used natural weighting (with a resolution of 0.5 × 1.1 arcsec) to maximise detected signal-to-noise" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - fitting: 1st-order polynomial continuum  
  > "We fitted a 1st-order polynomial to the continuum" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Search for CO emission**: uv-plane continuum subtraction (UVSUB + UVCONTSUB3), natural-weighted 10 km/s cube, spectrum summed within 1.75 arcsec  
  > "We extracted the flux density spectrum by summing pixels per spatial resolution element within 1.75 arcsec (a projected distance of 10 kpc)" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: 10 km/s bins  
  > "We then formed a data cube in 10 km s−1 velocity bins" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Build averaged ASKAP HI spectrum**: Inverse-variance weighted average of 11 ASKAP epochs  
  > "was constructed to have optimal S/N by weighting each observation by its inverse variance" (3.2 The Australian SKA Pathfinder)
   - software: MIRIAD
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
5. **Geometric extent of absorbing gas**: Circular-orbit model relating HI peak separation (~20 km/s) and source separation (~300 pc) to line-of-sight extent  
  > "we estimate that the illuminated absorbing gas lies radially within" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: v_circ ~ 200 km/s, 0<=i,phi<=90 deg  
  > "and assuming _v_ circ _∼_ 200 km s _[−]_ [1]" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Column densities and gas masses**: Integrated optical depths converted to N_HI and N_CO (single excitation temperature); N_H2 from CO abundance; upper limit from CO emission non-detection via X_CO; masses for disc model  
  > "We can estimate the column density of neutral atomic and molecular gas from the integrated absorption line strength in the ASKAP and ALMA spectra." (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - parameter: assumed line width 400 km/s for undetected emission, 5-sigma limit  
  > "We assume a line width of 400 km s" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: T_spin 100-2000 K  
  > "We therefore consider spin temperatures in the range 100 to 2000 K" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: T_ex >~ 10 K; CO/H2 = 1e-4; Milky Way X_CO = 2e20; L_CO(2-1)=L_CO(1-0)  
  > "Without further information we use _X_ CO for molecular gas in the Milky Way" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - assumption: Disc scale height ~150 pc, radial extent ~2 kpc; 37 per cent helium/metals correction  
  > "Assuming that the atomic gas has a scale height _h_ _∼_ 150 pc" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Absorption lower limit compared with emission-derived upper limit for consistency  
  > "We can compare this lower limit with the non-detection of" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
7. **Star formation constraints**: Kennicutt-Schmidt law from gas surface density; [OII] SFR via Kewley et al. (2004)  
  > "The KennicuttSchmidt law (Kennicutt 1998) then gives a star formation rate surface density" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
8. **Narrow HI line width and CO non-detection test**: Joint two-Gaussian model of narrow HI component across epochs convolved with filter bank response; detection limits for CO toward weaker component; covering fraction model  
  > "jointly modeling the narrower H I component as the superposition of two Gaussians" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - assumption: No astrophysical variation over a few years  
  > "Assuming no astrophysical variation in the absorption line over the timescale of a few years" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
9. **Radio SED fit and spectral age**: Least squares fit of peaked SED with continuous-injection break; equipartition B field; synchrotron age  
  > "We estimate the age of the source using the following relationship" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - assumption: Equipartition, equal electron/proton energy, component size ~10 mas  
  > "Assuming equipartition of the cosmic ray and magnetic field energy densities, equal energy densities of electrons and protons" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - fitting: Least squares  
  > "Least squares fitting gives" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
   - validation: Variability check using other ASKAP field sources  
  > "We test for this by measuring the standard deviation in flux density for other point sources within the same field." (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Follow-up of known ASKAP HI absorber to trace molecular gas and constrain high-frequency continuum  
  > "A15 discovered H I absorption in the host galaxy of this source" (1 Introduction)
- Why this method: Natural weighting since continuum unresolved, to maximise S/N  
  > "Since the continuum source is not resolved with uniform weighting we used natural weighting" (3.1 The Atacama Large Millimetre Array)
- Alternative: Narrow HI as jet-driven outflow rejected due to narrow width  
  > "is inconsistent with jet-driven gas outflows that are typically associated with young radio sources" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
- Alternative: CO non-detection due to faint subtended continuum (weaker component) considered and found consistent  
  > "The ALMA data are therefore consistent with not detecting" (4.1.2 The narrow HI absorption)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Unresolved continuum limits kinematic interpretation  
  > "it is difficult to determine the three dimensional motion of the gas from the unresolved absorption spectrum alone" (4.1 Constraints on the line-of-sight cold gas)
- Limitation (authors): Masses only line-of-sight; may underestimate host totals  
  > "may underestimate the total masses in the host galaxy" (4.1.1 The broad absorption - a circumnuclear reservoir)
- Limitation (authors): Spectral age is order-of-magnitude  
  > "should therefore be considered a useful order of magnitude measurement of the source age" (4.2 Evidence for a young radio AGN)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only 23 min on source; CO sub-structure at low S/N
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Mass estimates rest on assumed T_ex, T_spin, CO abundance, X_CO and disc geometry spanning orders of magnitude
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SED combines non-simultaneous data over 30 yr with no fit uncertainties quoted
