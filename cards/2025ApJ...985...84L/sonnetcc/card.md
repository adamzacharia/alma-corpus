## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and self-calibrate archival visibilities**: Pipeline calibration by ALMA staff; per-program self-calibration after aligning execution blocks with fixvis/fixplanets; SMA data calibrated with MIR then exported to CASA; some measurement sets obtained pre-calibrated from other authors  
  > "Each archival project was initially calibrated by the ALMA staff using the ALMA calibration pipeline and the required version of CASA" (3.1 Archival Data and Observational Details); "The calibrated visibilities were then exported into CASA `v6.3`" (SMA Data Calibration)
   - software: CASA, MIR
   - parameter: CS 3-2: two phase-only rounds (900s, 360s) on SB, then one phase-only round (inf) after concatenating LB; about 2x peak continuum SNR gain  
  > "performed two rounds of phase-only self-calibration (`solint`=‘900s’, ‘360s’) on the SB data" (3.1 Archival Data and Observational Details)
   - parameter: CS 4-3 and H2CS: three phase rounds (inf, 120s, 60s) and one amplitude round (inf)  
  > "Three rounds of phase (`solint`=‘inf’, ‘120s’, ‘60s’) and one round of amplitude (`solint`=‘inf’) self-calibration were performed" (3.1 Archival Data and Observational Details)
   - parameter: CS 8-7: two phase rounds (900s, 360s) and one amplitude round (inf) after flagging strong-line channels  
  > "We performed two rounds of phase (`solint`=‘900s’, ‘360s’) and one round of amplitude (`solint`=‘inf’) self-calibration after flagged channels containing strong lines" (3.1 Archival Data and Observational Details)
   - parameter: CS 10-9: one phase-only round (inf)  
  > "We performed one round of phase-only self-calibration (`solint`=‘inf’)" (3.1 Archival Data and Observational Details)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Peak continuum SNR improvement reported per program (about 2x, 4x, 600%, 350%)  
  > "This resulted in an improvement of ${\approx}2\times$ in the peak continuum SNR." (3.1 Archival Data and Observational Details)
2. **Subtract continuum and image line cubes**: uvcontsub (first-order polynomial) then tclean with Briggs weighting, multiscale deconvolver and Keplerian masks, with per-line robust and uv-taper  
  > "we first subtracted the continuum using the `uvcontsub` task in CASA with a first-order polynomial" (3.2 Imaging); "All lines were then imaged using the `tclean` task with Briggs weighting" (3.2 Imaging)
   - software: CASA, keplerian_mask
   - parameter: multiscale pixel scales [0,5,15,25]; CLEAN to 4 sigma, sigma from five line-free channels of the dirty image  
  > "All images were made using the ‘multi-scale’ deconvolver with pixel scales of \[0,5,15,25\] and were CLEANed down to a 4$\sigma$ level" (3.2 Imaging)
   - parameter: velocity binning to channel spacings 0.13-1.20 km/s depending on the line  
  > "we also binned the image cubes in velocity with channel spacings ultimately ranging from 0.13-1.20 km s$^{-1}$ depending on the line" (3.2 Imaging)
   - parameter: robust chosen manually to maximize detection SNR  
  > "We manually chose Briggs `robust` parameters to prioritize high SNR line detections." (3.2 Imaging)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Make moment maps, radial profiles and integrated fluxes**: Zeroth-moment and peak-intensity maps with bettermoments; deprojected radial profiles and spectra with GoFish using the Keplerian masks; flux uncertainties by bootstrapping  
  > "We also produced peak intensity maps using the ‘quadratic’ method of `bettermoments`" (3.3 Moment Maps, Radial Profiles, and Integrated Fluxes); "We computed radial line intensity profiles using the `radial_profile` function in the `GoFish` python package (cite: Teague19JOSS) to deproject the zeroth moment maps." (3.3 Moment Maps, Radial Profiles, and Integrated Fluxes)
   - software: bettermoments, GoFish, keplerian_mask
   - parameter: no flux threshold for pixel inclusion; same Keplerian masks as CLEANing  
  > "All maps were generated using the same Keplerian masks employed during CLEANing." (3.3 Moment Maps, Radial Profiles, and Integrated Fluxes)
   - parameter: uncertainty = standard deviation of fluxes in 500 random Keplerian masks over line-free channels  
  > "We estimated uncertainties via a bootstrapping approach as the standard deviation of the integrated fluxes within 500 randomly-generated Keplerian masks spanning only line-free channels." (3.3 Moment Maps, Radial Profiles, and Integrated Fluxes)
   - assumption: flat (midplane) emitting surface for deprojected radial profiles  
  > "All radial profiles assume a flat, i.e., midplane, emitting surface." (3.3 Moment Maps, Radial Profiles, and Integrated Fluxes)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **Fit disk-integrated rotational diagrams**: LTE rotational diagram with optical depth correction, disk-integrated fluxes from Table 1; H2CS and H2C34S Trot fixed to CS-derived value; ortho-to-para ratio 3 for total column  
  > "Following (cite: Legal19; Legal21), we used a rotational diagram analysis (cite: Goldsmith99)." (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation); "we instead fixed the rotational temperature to that derived from CS for both species" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: 256 walkers, 2000 burn-in steps plus 1000 sampling steps; median and 16th/84th percentiles  
  > "We used 256 walkers with 2000 burn-in steps and an additional 1000 steps to sample the posterior distribution." (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - parameter: uniform priors Trot 3-300 K, NT 1e7-1e20 cm^-2  
  > "of T$_{\rm{rot}} (\rm{K}) = \mathcal{U}(3, 300)$ and N$_{\rm{T}} (\rm{cm}^{-2}) = \mathcal{U}(10^7, 10^{20})$" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - parameter: CS and C34S partition function Q(T) = kT/(hB0) + 1/3 with B0 = 24495.56 MHz (CS) and 24103.55 MHz (C34S)  
  > "For the diatomic molecules CS and C$^{34}$S, we adopt the following partition function:" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - parameter: 10% flux calibration uncertainty added in quadrature  
  > "We also included a systematic flux calibration uncertainty of 10% added in quadrature to the statistical uncertainty on all measured line fluxes." (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - assumption: LTE for all lines  
  > "We assume all lines are in local thermal equilibrium (LTE)" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - assumption: statistical ortho-to-para ratio of 3 for H2CS and H2C34S  
  > "we adopt the statistical ortho-to-para ratio of 3" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - assumption: thermal line widths, FWHM about 1-2 km/s measured from resolved lines  
  > "All fits used thermal line widths, with FWHMs of" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - fitting: emcee affine-invariant MCMC with chi-squared likelihood, uniform priors, median and 16th/84th percentile uncertainties  
  > "used the affine-invariant Markov Chain Monte Carlo code `emcee` (cite: Foreman_Mackey13) to generate posterior probability distributions" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation); "We adopt the median as the best-fitting value with uncertainties given by the 16th and 84th percentiles of the posterior distributions" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
   - validation: Optical depths all small; Trot for H2CS varied 15-60 K changes NT by at most 2x (H2CS) and 10x (H2C34S); thermal vs 0.3 km/s intrinsic widths gives tau up to 0.2-0.3  
  > "found that the derived N$_{\rm{T}}$ values of H$_2$CS and H$_2$C$^{34}$S varied by no more than 2$\times$ and 10$\times$, respectively" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation); "Values of $\tau$ range from 0.0007 (H$_2$C$^{34}$S) to 0.08 (CS)" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
5. **Fit radially-resolved rotational diagrams**: Beam-matched images (0.35 arcsec for CS/H2CS, 0.5 arcsec for C34S) and the same rotational diagram fit per radius; H2CS and H2C34S use the CS Trot profile  
  > "We then used these beam-matched images for the radially-resolved analysis as in the previous Section." (4.3 Radially-resolved Analysis); "we adopted the CS T$_{\rm{rot}}$ profile for both species" (4.3 Radially-resolved Analysis)
   - software: emcee, CASA
   - parameter: Lines used: CS J=2-1, 3-2, 4-3, 8-7, C34S J=5-4, 6-5, H2CS J=6-5, H2C34S J=9-8; CS J=7-6 excluded for small MRS  
  > "We excluded the CS J=7–6 line due to its small MRS" (4.3 Radially-resolved Analysis)
   - parameter: C34S priors Trot 10-35 K, NT 1e9-1e17 cm^-2  
  > "T$_{\rm{rot}} (\rm{K}) = \mathcal{U}(10, 35)$ and N$_{\rm{T}} (\rm{cm}^{-2}) = \mathcal{U}(10^9, 10^{17})$" (4.3 Radially-resolved Analysis)
   - assumption: all lines remain optically thin  
  > "all lines remain optically-thin in the spatially-resolved analysis" (4.3 Radially-resolved Analysis)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Ratios compared with disk-averaged values; Trot assumption for H2CS tested with constant 15, 30, 45, 60 K  
  > "The spatially-resolved ratios are approximately constant with radius and are broadly consistent with the disk-averaged values." (4.3 Radially-resolved Analysis); "We tested a set of constant temperatures from 15 K, 30 K, 45 K, and 60 K" (9 The Impact of Rotational Temperature on the N(H2CS) / N(H2C34S) Ratio)
6. **Constrain CS emitting height from channel maps**: Overlay isovelocity contours at z/r = 0 to 0.2 in steps of 0.05 on CS J=4-3 channel maps and compare by eye; direct surface extraction failed for low SNR  
  > "we can still provide an approximate constraint on the CS emitting surface by overlaying a set of isovelocity contours at different heights and visually inspecting how well they match the observed emission." (4.4.1 Inferred From Channel Maps); "labeled emission surfaces from the midplane ($z/r=0$) to a height of $z/r=0.2$ in increments of 0.05" (4.4.1 Inferred From Channel Maps)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual comparison only; cannot exclude a near-midplane origin  
  > "we are unable to robustly exclude a midplane or near-midplane origin of CS with the available angular resolution and SNR" (4.4.1 Inferred From Channel Maps)
7. **Infer emitting regions from Trot and 2D gas temperature**: Map radially-resolved Trot onto the renormalized 2D T(r,z) of HD 163296 to find the (r,z) region where gas temperature equals Trot  
  > "by assuming that T$_{\rm{rot}}$ is equal to the gas temperature for each molecule, we can determine the emitting region in $(r,z)$-space" (4.4.2 Inferred from 2D Gas Structure)
   - assumption: Trot equals gas kinetic temperature  
  > "by assuming that T$_{\rm{rot}}$ is equal to the gas temperature for each molecule" (4.4.2 Inferred from 2D Gas Structure)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Consistent with the channel-map visual height determination  
  > "which is consistent with the visual height determination from the CS J=4–3 channel maps in the previous subsection" (4.4.2 Inferred from 2D Gas Structure)
8. **Test sub-thermal excitation and estimate gap gas density**: RADEX calculations at typical CS gap conditions compared Tex and Tkin; sub-thermal excitation assumed significant within a factor 10 of n_crit of CS J=8-7 to estimate gap surface density  
  > "We confirmed this interpretation via RADEX (cite: vanderTak07) calculations" (5.2 Local Sub-thermal CS Line Excitation in a Planet-Carved Gap); "If we assume that sub-thermal excitation becomes significant when n$_{\rm{H}}$ is within a factor of 10 from the critical density of the CS J=8–7 line" (5.2 Local Sub-thermal CS Line Excitation in a Planet-Carved Gap)
   - software: RADEX

## Qualitative findings

- CS, H2CS and isotopologues show a central cavity and multi-ringed structure; CS ring contrast grows with line excitation  
  > "which reveal a central cavity and multi-ringed emission structure" (abstract)
- No significant azimuthal asymmetries in CS  
  > "However, we do not observe any significant asymmetries" (5.1 Dust and Molecular Gas Structure)
- H2C34S ring is offset inward from the H2CS ring; origin (fractionation vs excitation) unresolved  
  > "The origin and robustness of this shift in H$_2$C$^{34}$S, while intriguing, cannot be definitely confirmed" (5.3.3 Spatial Offset of H2C34S Ring)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Wealth of archival data and well-constrained disk structure make HD 163296 suited to multi-line sulfur study  
  > "Given the wealth of existing archival data and its well-constrained physical and chemical structure" (2 The HD 163296 Disk)
- Why this method: Rotational diagrams following earlier MWC 480 work so results are comparable  
  > "Following (cite: Legal19; Legal21), we used a rotational diagram analysis (cite: Goldsmith99)." (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
- Alternative: Direct extraction of CS emitting heights from channel maps (disksurf-type techniques) failed for low SNR; visual contour matching used instead  
  > "but the data were of insufficient SNR to enable direct extraction of line emitting heights" (4.4.1 Inferred From Channel Maps)
- Alternative: H2C34S ring offset could be fractionation at the cavity wall or an excitation effect; not decided  
  > "this could instead be an excitation effect" (5.3.3 Spatial Offset of H2C34S Ring)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): CS J=7-6 flux is a lower limit because short baselines are missing  
  > "Since the CS J=7–6 observations lack short-baseline data, we consider this flux a lower limit." (Table 1)
- Limitation (authors): Only one transition each for H2CS and H2C34S, so Trot was assumed from CS  
  > "we instead fixed the rotational temperature to that derived from CS for both species" (4.2 Disk-Integrated Excitation)
- Limitation (authors): Emitting height cannot robustly exclude a near-midplane CS origin  
  > "we are unable to robustly exclude a midplane or near-midplane origin of CS with the available angular resolution and SNR" (4.4.1 Inferred From Channel Maps)
- Limitation (authors): H2C34S outer ring is tentative due to low SNR  
  > "due to its low SNR, we consider this ring to be tentative" (4.1 Spatial Distribution of Emission)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotational temperature for H2CS and H2C34S is borrowed from CS even though the lines may trace different layers; ratios inherit this assumption.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Disk-integrated and radial fits mix lines with very different beams and MRS; only the radial analysis is beam-matched.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Several measurement sets were self-calibrated by other authors, so flux scale consistency across programs is not independently checked.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The 10% flux uncertainty is applied uniformly although SMA and ALMA bands differ.
- Caveat (inferred, ours): The gas density estimate from n_crit is order-of-magnitude and not a non-LTE fit.
