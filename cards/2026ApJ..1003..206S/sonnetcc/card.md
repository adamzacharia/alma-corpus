## Analysis recipe

1. **Make the ALMA 1.2 mm continuum mosaics**: Calibrated data reduced with a standard CASA pipeline per Sun et al. 2025a; channels near the quasar [CII] line flagged; mosaics made at native and uv-tapered resolution  
  > "The ALMA data reduction has been described in detail by (cite: sunf25a) through a standard CASA `v6.4.1.12` pipeline (cite: casa22)." (2.2 ALMA); "we first flagged the spectral channels that have a velocity offset smaller than 500" (2.2 ALMA)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: native: Briggs robust=0.5, no uv taper; tapered: robust=2.0 with Gaussian taper FWHM=1.0 arcsec  
  > "we produced ALMA mosaics at both native resolution (Briggs weighting `robust=0.5`, no *uv* tapering) and tapered resolution (`robust=2.0`, uv tapered with a Gaussian kernel of FWHM=1$''$0)" (2.2 ALMA)
   - parameter: channels within 500 km/s of the quasar [CII] line centre flagged to avoid boosting the continuum  
  > "To avoid the artificial boost of continuum flux densities from \[C II\] emitters at quasar redshifts, we first flagged the spectral channels" (2.2 ALMA)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Photometry of the two sources in ALMA and NIRCam**: ALMA Band 6/7 flux densities from the peak of the uv-tapered images; NIRCam photometry in a 0.5 arcsec radius circular aperture after PSF subtraction of the quasar for J1526m2050.C02; uncertainties from random apertures  
  > "ALMA Band-6/7 flux densities are measured from the peak of the *uv*-tapered images as described by (cite: sunf25a)." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies); "to avoid substantial flux loss of diffuse emission, we obtain photometry using a circular aperture of radius $r=0.5\ensuremath{''}$." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies); "Because J1526m2050.C02 is close to the luminous quasar, we subtract a point spread function (PSF) model from the quasar image." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies)
   - software: CASA, Photutils
   - parameter: photometric uncertainties from random apertures in source-free regions within 10 arcsec of the targets  
  > "The photometric uncertainties are estimated using random apertures in source-free regions within 10$''$ from the targets." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies)
   - parameter: PSF model built from a PSF star library in the ASPIRE fields  
  > "The PSF model is constructed using a PSF star library in the ASPIRE fields." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: detections in multiple ALMA datasets at various frequencies and in [CII] confirm the sources  
  > "The detections with multiple ALMA datasets at various frequencies validate the fidelity of these sources." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies)
3. **Measure the 1.17 mm continuum sizes**: image-plane fit with synthesized beam deconvolved  
  > "We also measure the ALMA 1.17 mm continuum sizes using CASA IMFIT function in the image plane with synthesized beam deconvolved." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies)
   - software: CASA
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
4. **SED modelling for stellar mass, SFR, A_V and L_IR**: energy-balance SED fitting with CIGALE of NIRCam and ALMA photometry; delayed-tau SFH, BC03 stellar populations, modified Calzetti attenuation, Casey 2012 dust emission  
  > "We model the spectral energy distributions (SED) of the two NIRCam-dark galaxies with CIGALE, an energy-balance parametric SED-fitting software (cite: cigale09; cigale19)." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - software: CIGALE
   - parameter: age 20-500 Myr, tau 20-2000 Myr; optional late starburst in last 20 Myr producing up to 10% of stellar mass  
  > "We allow the age of main stellar population to be 20–500 Myr and $\tau$ to be 20–2000 Myr." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties); "An optional late starburst is allowed in the last 20 Myr of SFH, which could produce up to 10% of the total stellar mass." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - parameter: modified Calzetti attenuation: slope varied by +-0.2, A_V 0-15  
  > "We adopt a modified (cite: calzetti00) attenuation curve, and allow the variation of the power-law slope by $\pm$0.2 and $A_V$ effectively at 0–15." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - parameter: nebular emission with n_e = 100 cm^-3, log U = -4 to -2  
  > "Nebular emission is included assuming an electron density of $n_e = 100$ cm^−3^ and ionization parameter $\log U=-4 \sim -2$." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - parameter: Casey 2012 dust: T_dust 30-60 K, beta_em 1.6-2.0, alpha_MIR fixed at 2.0  
  > "parameterized by the dust temperature ($T_\mathrm{dust}$; 30–60 K), dust emissivity ($\beta_\mathrm{em}$; 1.6–2.0) and mid-IR power-law slope ($\alpha_\mathrm{MIR}$, fixed at 2.0)." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - assumption: solar metallicity  
  > "and assume a solar metallicity ($Z_{\odot}$) given the strong presence of dust." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - assumption: Chabrier initial mass function; flat LCDM with H0 = 70, Omega_M = 0.3  
  > "We also assume a (cite: chabrier03) initial mass function." (Introduction); "Throughout this work, we assume a flat $\Lambda$CDM cosmology with $H_0= 70$ km s^−1^ Mpc^−1^ and $\Omega_\mathrm{M} = 0.3$." (Introduction)
   - assumption: no AGN component in the SED model  
  > "We note that an active galactic nucleus (AGN) component is not included in our SED modeling." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - assumption: L_IR integrated over rest-frame 8-1000 um; SFR from L_IR following Kennicutt & Evans 2012  
  > "We define the IR luminosity ($L_\mathrm{IR}$) as the integrated luminosity over a rest-frame wavelength range from 8 to 1000 $\mu$m." (Introduction); "for the two sources (cite: ke12)" (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - fitting: Bayesian inference within CIGALE; sampler and priors beyond the listed ranges not stated  
  > "the large errors are natural consequence of poor constraints in the rest-frame UV/optical through Bayesian inference." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - validation: SFR compared with the [CII]-based SFR from earlier papers; best-fit SEDs compared with templates; no X-ray detections  
  > "These are consistent with the SFR inferred from the \[C II\] luminosities (cite: pensabene21; lij22)." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
5. **Derive dust mass and temperature with a modified blackbody**: far-IR modified blackbody fit with CMB heating included  
  > "We further model the far-IR SEDs of the two galaxies with a modified blackbody model assuming a dust absorption coefficient as" (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties); "We also consider the CMB heating effect following (cite: dacunha13), which is found to be small at the derived $T_\mathrm{dust}$." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - parameter: kappa = 0.40 (nu/250)^beta_em cm^2/g with nu in GHz (rest frame); beta_em fixed at 1.8  
  > "where $\nu$ is the rest-frame frequency in GHz and $\beta_\mathrm{em}$ is fixed at 1.8 following previous works (cite: ds17; sunf22a)." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - assumption: gas masses taken from [CI] and CO studies in the literature, used for gas-to-dust ratio  
  > "have been studied through \[C I\] and CO observations by (cite: pensabene21) and (cite: lij22)." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: gas-to-dust ratio and T_dust found normal; J0305m3150.C05 poorly constrained because of limited ALMA coverage  
  > "both sources exhibit normal dust temperature and gas-to-dust ratio $\delta_\mathrm{GDR}$ around 100." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
6. **Halo occupation modelling of the NIRCam-dark number density**: Gaussian halo occupation P(Mhalo) from the Pizzati et al. quasar population model, calibrated to the observed quasar density; heavy model (same Mc and sigma_m, larger P_max) and light model (constant occupation above a halo mass threshold)  
  > "the probability distribution function that a halo could host a detectable quasar (or galaxy), $P(M_\mathrm{halo})$, can be approximated as a Gaussian function" (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts); "We also test an alternative model, dubbed “light model”, in which the occupation fraction of NIRCam-dark galaxies is constant above certain $M_\mathrm{halo}$ threshold." (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
   - parameter: quasar calibration P_max = 0.02, log(Mc/Msun) = 12.73, sigma_m = 0.22  
  > "we reproduce the observed quasar number density at $z\sim6.6$ with the parameter set $P_\mathrm{max} = 0.02$, $\log (M_c / M_\odot) = 12.73$, $\sigma_\mathrm{m} = 0.22$" (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
   - parameter: occurrence rate f = 2/25 with Poisson errors  
  > "($f = 2/25=0.08_{-0.05}^{+0.10}$; error from Poisson statistics)" (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
   - parameter: light-model threshold log(Mhalo/Msun) >= 11.4 from TNG100  
  > "$\log(M_\mathrm{halo}/M_\odot) \geq {11.4}$, and thus we adopt the threshold." (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
   - assumption: independent halo occupations for quasars and NIRCam-dark galaxies  
  > "We also emphasize that our models assume independent halo occupations for quasars and NIRCam-dark galaxies." (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
   - fitting: Monte Carlo draw of Mhalo from the quasar host HMF, apply P(Mhalo) and match the observed occurrence rate to solve for P_max

## Qualitative findings

- Both galaxies are more obscured in the rest-frame optical than Arp 220 and z~3 ALESS optically faint DSFGs  
  > "We find that the two NIRCam-dark galaxies appear to be more obscured in rest-frame optical even compared with Arp 220 or $z\sim3$ optically faint DSFGs." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
- Comparable gas and stellar masses imply high gas fractions  
  > "We find comparable $M_\mathrm{gas}$ and $M_\mathrm{star}$ for the two galaxies, indicating high gas fractions associated with the starbursts." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
- Stellar mass and SFR match starburst progenitors of z~4 massive quiescent galaxies  
  > "We find that the stellar mass and SFR of NIRCam-dark DSFGs at $z\sim7$ exactly match the starburst progenitors of massive quiescent galaxies at $z\sim4$" (4.1 Placing NIRCam-dark starbursts in the context of massive galaxy evolution)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: The galaxies were selected from the ASPIRE JWST and ALMA survey of quasar environments  
  > "These two dusty starburst galaxies are selected through the ASPIRE JWST and ALMA survey (cite: wangf23; wangfprep)." (Introduction)
- Why this method: Native and tapered mosaics made to detect both compact and extended sources; 0.5 arcsec aperture used to avoid losing diffuse flux  
  > "To optimize the detection of sources with both compact and extended dust continuum or \[C II\] emissions, we produced ALMA mosaics at both native resolution" (2.2 ALMA); "to avoid substantial flux loss of diffuse emission, we obtain photometry using a circular aperture of radius $r=0.5\ensuremath{''}$." (3.1 The identification of NIRCam-dark galaxies)
- Alternative: Two halo occupation models (heavy and light) were explored for the number density  
  > "We also test an alternative model, dubbed “light model”, in which the occupation fraction of NIRCam-dark galaxies is constant above certain $M_\mathrm{halo}$ threshold." (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
- Alternative: AGN contribution to the SED was not modelled because of X-ray non-detections and expected insignificance  
  > "The AGN contributions to the ALMA flux densities are also likely insignificant (see the discussion in (cite: sunf25a))." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): Stellar masses are poorly constrained  
  > "In contrast, the constraints on their stellar mass are rather poor." (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
- Limitation (authors): Number density is hard to constrain from a quasar-selected survey  
  > "However, it is not easy to constrain the number density of NIRCam-dark galaxies because they are selected as companions of luminous quasars." (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
- Limitation (authors): Dust properties of J0305m3150.C05 are poorly constrained because of limited ALMA coverage  
  > "Although the constraints are poor for J0305m3150.C05 because of limited ALMA coverage" (3.2 SED modeling and physical properties)
- Limitation (authors): Halo occupation models assume independent occupations for quasars and NIRCam-dark galaxies  
  > "If the two populations are correlated or anti-correlated, the number density estimates could be even more uncertain." (4.2 The number density of NIRCam-dark starbursts)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Only two sources, selected from a quasar-companion sample, so the abundance estimates rest on a 2/25 occurrence rate and a halo occupation model with no direct constraint on NIRCam-dark halo masses
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Stellar mass uncertainty of 0.5 dex weakens the match to quiescent-galaxy progenitor SFHs
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SED model excludes AGN and uses fixed alpha_MIR and a limited dust-temperature range (30-60 K), which affects L_IR and dust temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Flux scale, beam and calibration details are deferred to another paper, so photometric systematics cannot be checked from this paper alone
- Caveat (inferred, ours): NIRCam non-detection of J0305m3150.C05 relies on a 0.5 arcsec aperture and PSF treatment described in a forthcoming paper
