## Analysis recipe

1. **Measure the global X-ray temperature**: Reprocess and merge Chandra observations; detect and mask point sources; fit the remaining cluster spectrum.  
  > "We employed the `chandra_repro` script to produce level 2 event files." (Chandra); "The `CIAO` task `merge_obs` was then used to merge all the available observations and obtain the broadband (0.5-7 keV) exposure-corrected image" (Chandra); "using approximately 800 source counts after background subtraction and masking of all point sources identified using `wavdetect`." (Chandra)
   - software: CIAO, CALDB, chandra_repro, merge_obs, wavdetect
   - parameter: Broadband image: 0.5-7 keV  
  > "obtain the broadband (0.5-7 keV) exposure-corrected image" (Chandra)
   - parameter: Exclude the X-ray core containing the radio-loud AGN  
  > "We note that the X-ray core (also identified as a point source by `wavdetect`) includes the radio-loud AGN and thus was excluded in our spectral analysis." (Chandra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visually inspect point-source detections; compare temperature with previous estimates.  
  > "we visually inspected the results to identify spurious detections." (Chandra); "The inferred temperature is consistent at $1\sigma$ with previous estimates (e.g., (cite: Foley2011), (cite: Bartalucci2017))." (Chandra)
2. **Map X-ray hardness and its significance**: Compute (hard−soft)/(hard+soft) from exposure-corrected images; bootstrap noise realizations.  
  > "We compute the map as the ratio of the hard (1.5–7.5 keV) minus the soft (0.3–1.5 keV) image, divided by the sum of the two." (Chandra); "We estimate the significance of the observed features by bootstrapping over noise realizations for the two energy bands." (Chandra)
   - parameter: Soft: 0.3–1.5 keV; hard: 1.5–7.5 keV  
  > "We compute the map as the ratio of the hard (1.5–7.5 keV) minus the soft (0.3–1.5 keV) image, divided by the sum of the two." (Chandra)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Use bootstrap uncertainties to construct a significance map following Strickland2002 Appendix C2.  
  > "The inferred uncertainties for the hardness ratio map were then employed to compute the significance map" (Chandra); "as detailed in the Appendix C2 of (cite: Strickland2002)." (Chandra)
3. **Separate compact and diffuse radio emission**: Image ASKAP and ATCA data; subtract an unresolved central source from EMU and compact CLEAN models from ATCA visibilities.  
  > "We estimate the flux density of the compact core by subtracting an unresolved source from the image and determining when there were no significant positive or negative residuals on top of the extended emission." (Evolutionary Map of the Universe Pilot Survey); "by performing a first imaging run assuming a uniform weighting, and then employing the reconstructed CLEAN components as input models to be subtracted from the each of the raw interferometric data sets." (Australia Telescope Compact Array)
   - software: ASKAPsoft, CASA
   - parameter: ASKAP: two Taylor terms; convolve 36 single-beam images to 18″ FWHM; linear mosaic; spectral index T1/T0  
  > "utilizing a two Taylor-term (T0 and T1) multi-scale CLEAN algorithm (cite: Rau2011)." (Evolutionary Map of the Universe Pilot Survey); "The resulting 36 single-beam images were convolved to a resolution of $18\ensuremath{''}$ full-width-half-maximum and combined with a linear mosaic" (Evolutionary Map of the Universe Pilot Survey); "Spectral indices could then be recovered using the ratio T1/T0 of the convolved zero- and first-order Taylor coefficient maps." (Evolutionary Map of the Universe Pilot Survey)
   - parameter: ATCA: eight spectral windows for calibration; primary calibrator 1934-638; interleaved calibrator amplitude/phase solutions  
  > "In all cases, we split the 2048 MHz bandwidth into eight spectral windows for calibration." (Australia Telescope Compact Array); "We use the primary calibrator source 1934-638 to solve for the bandpass in amplitude and phase." (Australia Telescope Compact Array); "We then solve for additional amplitude and phase bandpass solutions using the bright point-like sources observed as interleaved calibrators, bootstrapping the flux densities of these sources." (Australia Telescope Compact Array)
   - parameter: ATCA 2.1 GHz: natural weighting and three phase self-calibration rounds; whole-band multifrequency synthesis  
  > "For the 2.1 GHz data, we image using natural weighting and then perform three rounds of self-calibration in phase." (Australia Telescope Compact Array); "The noise levels of the naturally weighted maps, imaged using multifrequency synthesis over the whole bandwidth, are 40, 20 and 15" (Australia Telescope Compact Array)
   - assumption: EMU subtraction includes only the central radio source  
  > "We emphasize that we subtracted from the EMU data only the model corresponding to the central radio source." (Compact sources)
   - fitting: Adjust the unresolved EMU source until no significant positive or negative residuals remain over the extended emission.  
  > "determining when there were no significant positive or negative residuals on top of the extended emission." (Evolutionary Map of the Universe Pilot Survey)
   - validation: Compare high-resolution ATCA observations with the EMU radio distribution.  
  > "The comparison of high-resolution ATCA observations of SPT-CL J2106-5844 with the distribution of the radio signal in the EMU map provides key information on the population of unresolved sources contaminating the diffuse radio emission." (Compact sources)
4. **Measure the diffuse radio spectrum**: Fit integrated diffuse fluxes using two selections: tapered 2.1 GHz sub-bands, and matched-baseline 2.1/5 GHz images.  
  > "First, we split the 2.1 GHz data in four spectral bands and image them separately from the others using natural weighting and a $u\varv$-taper of $10~\mathrm{k\lambda}$." (Diffuse emission); "by imaging the four 2.1 GHz sub-bands and the 5 GHz data after selecting only the baselines between 0.4 and $2~\mathrm{k\lambda}$." (Diffuse emission)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: First method: integrate flux within the >2.5σ region  
  > "is then obtained by measuring the flux within the region with significance larger than $2.5\sigma$." (Diffuse emission)
   - parameter: Second method: integrate fitted Gaussian models; exclude 9 GHz flux from the spectral fit  
  > "we fit its signal in each maps with a Gaussian profile and integrate over the reconstructed model to compute the diffuse flux" (Diffuse emission); "We perform a similar analysis on the 9 GHz measurements, but exclude the corresponding flux estimate from the fit." (Diffuse emission)
   - assumption: Power-law integrated radio spectrum  
  > "both the analyses show that the integrated spectrum seems to follow a well-behaved power law over the entire range of frequencies probed by the ATCA and EMU radio data." (Diffuse emission)
   - fitting: Spectral fits to integrated flux densities; Gaussian spatial fits for the matched-baseline method  
  > "The spectral fit provides an estimate of the integrated spectral index of $-0.96\substack{+0.06\\-0.06}$." (Diffuse emission); "we fit its signal in each maps with a Gaussian profile and integrate over the reconstructed model to compute the diffuse flux" (Diffuse emission)
   - validation: Compare the two methods and extrapolate the spectrum to the EMU diffuse flux.  
  > "Notably, the extrapolation of the resulting spectrum is consistent with the estimate of the diffuse flux from the EMU image." (Diffuse emission); "both the analyses show that the integrated spectrum seems to follow a well-behaved power law" (Diffuse emission)
5. **Search for SZ pressure peaks and compact contamination**: Jointly forward-model Band 3/4 ALMA+ACA visibilities; project a gNFW pressure model and sample its Fourier transform at measured uv coordinates; search for point sources.  
  > "the reconstruction method is devised to sample the Fourier transform of a map of the SZ effect from a model galaxy cluster to the $u\varv$-plane coordinates of the sampled visibilities." (SZ modeling); "is obtained by numerical integration of a generalized Navarro-Frenk-White (cite: Nagai2007) pressure profile along the line-of-sight direction" (Free modeling run); "we further perform a free search across the entire ALMA+ACA field of view for compact sources that may contaminate the SZ signal." (Free modeling run)
   - parameter: Free SZ centroid, mass parameter, and inner slope  
  > "We perform a first modeling run assuming the universal parametrization by (cite: Arnaud2010) but allow the model centroid, mass parameter, and slope at small radii to vary." (Free modeling run)
   - parameter: Unresolved sources: point models with power-law spectra  
  > "we use point source models to describe the contamination from unresolved compact sources with flux characterized by a simple power-law spectral dependence." (Free modeling run)
   - assumption: Isothermal ICM; thermal SZ relativistic correction from Itoh2004  
  > "We compute the average relativistic corrections to the thermal SZ spectrum by means of the approximation by (cite: Itoh2004)." (SZ modeling); "Given the large uncertainties on the temperature distribution, we assume this to be described by an isothermal model" (SZ modeling)
   - assumption: Flat Λ Cold Dark Matter: ΩM=0.30, ΩΛ=0.70, H0=70.0 km s−1 Mpc−1  
  > "Throughout this work, we assume a spatially flat $\Lambda$ Cold Dark Matter cosmological model, with $\Omega_{\mathrm{M}}=0.30$, $\Omega_{\Lambda}=0.70$, and $H_0=70.0~\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$." (Introduction)
   - assumption: Do not model the extended radio source; extrapolate it with α=−1 to check contamination

## Qualitative findings

- Two SZ components support a major merger.  
  > "Given the clear identification of two individual SZ components with comparable strength, we interpret SPT-CL J2106-5844 as a major merger." (Discussion)
- No thermal SZ detection toward the western weak-lensing extension.  
  > "No evidence for thermal SZ effect is found in the direction of the western mass extension reported by (cite: Kim2019)." (Free modeling run)
- The kinetic SZ contribution remains practically unconstrained.  
  > "the limitations in the available ALMA+ACA data leave the kinetic SZ contribution practically unconstrained." (Constraining the kinetic SZ signal)
- The extended radio emission may arise from the central AGN or multiple radio sources.  
  > "whether the extended feature belongs in its entirety to the radio galaxy coinciding with the BCG, or if it is a blend of several extended radio sources and radio-loud galaxies." (Connection with the central AGN); "The EMU and ATCA data do not allow us to narrow down the hypotheses regarding the nature of the diffuse radio emission" (Connection with the central AGN)
- Both pre-first-core-passage and off-axis post-first-core-passage scenarios remain viable.  
  > "We are observing the merger before first core passage." (Discussion); "The merger is observed after the first core passage and has a relatively large impact parameter" (Discussion); "At present, the data appears consistent with both cases." (Discussion)
- A merger-driven shock is a tentative interpretation of the southern arc-like structure.  
  > "An exciting possibility is that this feature is a merger-driven shock." (The dynamics of the ICM substructures); "further observations, as well as a joint SZ and X-ray analysis, will be required to confirm (or reject) them." (The dynamics of the ICM substructures)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: ALMA+ACA resolve thermal SZ structure and compact contamination; ATCA resolves radio structure; Chandra constrains thermodynamics.  
  > "high spatial (i.e., subarcminute) resolution, redshift-independent surface brightness, a large collecting area that yields $\sim 10-100 \mu$Jy-level sensitivity, and the spatial dynamic range required to model contamination by compact sources" (Conclusions and future work); "To better resolve any potential radio structures and compact sources in the cluster field, we complemented the EMU data with observations from ATCA." (Australia Telescope Compact Array); "to better constrain the thermodynamic structure of the cluster, we complement our analysis with reprocessed archival *Chandra* observations." (abstract)
- Why this method: Visibility-space modeling avoids deconvolution problems and uses the exact visibility sampling function.  
  > "This avoids issues related to the deconvolution of interferometric data (e.g., heavily correlated image-space noise), as well as taking full advantage of the knowledge of the exact visibility sampling function." (SZ modeling)
- Alternative: A single SZ component is disfavored relative to two components.  
  > "Also, two components are found to be preferred over one SZ model at the $\sim9.9\sigma$ level." (Multicomponent model)
- Alternative: Extended radio contamination is omitted from the SZ model after extrapolation gives fluxes well below ACA noise.  
  > "We do not consider any model component to describe the extended radio source, which should not significantly contaminate the measured SZ signal." (Free modeling run); "we obtain values of $16~\mathrm{\mu Jy}$ and $9~\mathrm{\mu Jy}$ in Band 3 and Band 4, respectively." (Free modeling run); "These are roughly an order of magnitude lower than the noise RMS of the ACA data" (Free modeling run)
- Alternative: Exclude EMU compact-source flux from central-source fitting because of extended contamination.  
  > "We do not consider here any information from the EMU observations as, at the EMU frequency, the extended radio structure may significantly contaminate the flux measured for the central source." (Unresolved source)
- Alternative: Exclude ATCA 18/24 GHz data from the main analysis because sources are faint.  
  > "We also examined the 18 and 24 GHz ATCA data, observed as part of Project C3217, but do not include them here due to the faintness of the sources at these frequencies." (Australia Telescope Compact Array)
- Alternative: A broad shallow pressure core filtered by the interferometer could produce apparent SZ bimodality.  
  > "it may happen that the bimodal structure in the SZ signal is an artifact of the interferometric large-scale filtering" (The dynamics of the ICM substructures)
- Alternative: A diffuse radio halo contribution cannot be excluded.
