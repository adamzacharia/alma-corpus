## Analysis recipe

1. **Calibrate and image**: Pipeline calibration, visibility continuum subtraction, CLEAN with Briggs weighting  
  > "We then generated both the 0.84 mm continuum and spectral visibilities by fitting and subtracting continuum emission in the visibility domain." (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: robust = 0.5; 0.15 km/s smoothing  
  > "By using Briggs weighting with a robust parameter of 0.5" (Observations and Data Reduction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared pre- and post-CLEAN continuum subtraction; profiles almost identical  
  > "the shapes of the spectral profiles toward 4A1 and 4A2 made with these two CLEAN approaches are almost identical." (Observations and Data Reduction)
2. **Fit line profiles and derive systemic velocities**: Gaussian fits to spectra at continuum peaks; average fitted peak velocity of six H2CO lines gives systemic velocity  
  > "By using the average fitted peak velocity of the six observed H$_2$CO transitions (see Table [2](#tb-line-fit) as well as §[\[res_4a1\]](#res_4a1) and §[\[res_4a2\]](#res_4a2) also), we adopt systemic velocities of 6.86 km s$^{-1}$ and 7.00 km s$^{-1}$" (H2CO and HNC (4-3) Features)
   - parameter: For 4A2, low-velocity redshifted absorption channels excluded from fit  
  > "The observed line profile can be fit with a Gaussian shape if the low-velocity redshifted absorption channels are excluded." (The Line Feature towards 4A2)
   - fitting: Gaussian profile fitting  
  > "We performed Gaussian profile fitting to extract the line parameters." (The Line Feature toward 4A1)
   - validation: _not stated_
3. **Rotation diagram for 4A1 gas temperature and column density**: Rotation diagram of H2CO assuming optically thin, continuum as background, uniform foreground layer  
  > "using the rotation diagram analysis (cite: tur91) by assuming the observed H$_2$CO transitions in 4A1 are optically thin." (H2CO and HNC (4-3) Features)
   - assumption: H2CO abundance 2e-8; CH3OH abundance 1e-6  
  > "With a fractional abundance of H$_2$CO of 2$\times$ 10$^{-8}$ (cite: mar04)" (H2CO and HNC (4-3) Features)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with CH3OH column density from companion paper  
  > "in agreement with that estimated from the CH$_3$OH transitions by (cite: dip19)" (H2CO and HNC (4-3) Features)
4. **Non-LTE RT modelling of 4A1 infall**: 1D spherical collapsing core model, mock ALMA observations, density varied 1x/3x/10x, with and without infall; compare to observed spectra and continuum brightness  
  > "We tried two other high density cases by increasing the original density profile to 3$\times$ and 10$\times$ values." (Discussion)
   - software: SPARX
   - parameter: n = 3.05e9 cm^-3 (r/33.5 au)^-1.8; v1000 = 1.1 km/s; turbulence 0.4 km/s; rin 33.2 au, rout 11200 au; X(H2CO) jump at 100 K  
  > "infall velocity *v*$_{inf}$ = *v*$_{1000}$ $\times$ (*r*/1000 au)$^{-0.5}$, where *v*$_{1000}$ = 1.1 km s$^{-1}$; a turbulence velocity of 0.4 km s$^{-1}$" (Discussion)
   - assumption: 1D spherically symmetric collapse  
  > "only considered the IRAS 4A region as a single one-dimensional spherically symmetric collapsing core." (Discussion)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: 3x density reproduces observed continuum brightness  
  > "The simulated spectra of the 3$\times$ density case, which reproduces well the observed continuum brightness" (The physical structures of 4A1)
5. **Mass infall rate limit for 4A1**: Infall at 75 au at least 8x slower than model extrapolation; rate constrained using model parameters at 75 au  
  > "Using the parameters described above at the layer of 75 au, we further constrain the mass infall rate in 4A1 to be (at most) around 3$\times$10$^{-5}$ *M*$_{\odot}$ year$^{-1}$" (The physical structures of 4A1)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
6. **Absorption opacity and infall rate for 4A2**: Fitted Gaussian taken as unabsorbed model; optically thin opacity tau(v) from model minus observed intensity; adopt T~100 K, layer 100 au, density 4.3e8 cm^-3, infall 0.4-0.8 km/s  
  > "Together with an infall velocity of 0.4 $-$ 0.8 km s$^{-1}$, we anticipate a mass infall rate of 3.1$-$6.2 $\times$ 10$^{-5}$ *M*$_\ensuremath{\odot}$ year$^{-1}$ at the layer of 100 au in 4A2." (Gas Kinematics in 4A2)
   - assumption: Gas temperature ~100 K; density and temperature structure same as earlier low-resolution model  
  > "we adopt a gas temperature of $\sim$100 K for the observed infalling material in 4A2." (Gas Kinematics in 4A2)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_

## Qualitative findings

- HNC (4-3) absorption between 7.15 and 7.60 km/s traces an extended redshifted foreground layer covering both sources  
  > "our HNC (4$-$3) results confirm the existence of a previously inferred redshifted foreground layer." (Summary)
- Both outflows are well collimated along N-S; 4A2 outflow is S-shaped  
  > "4A1 and 4A2 each display a well-collimated outflow along the N-S direction, and an S-shaped morphology can be discerned in the outflow powered by 4A2." (Summary)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: _not stated_
- Why this method: Absorption against continuum (inverse P-Cygni) is a robust probe of infall, and ALMA gives high resolution  
  > "The “inverse P-Cygni” line profile, i.e., the redshifted self-absorption of spectral lines, is considered as a more robust probe of infall motions." (Introduction)
- Alternative: Disk-face-on hot corino atmosphere (Scenario I) vs temperature-stratified envelope (Scenario II) for 4A1 absorption; undecided  
  > "High-resolution observations of disk tracers at low frequency may help to discriminate the two scenarios" (The physical structures of 4A1)
- How they decided it was enough: _not stated_
- Limitation (authors): 1D collapse model likely breaks down; no full RT modelling for 4A2 possible due to complex structure  
  > "The lack of a proper description for the complex mixture of disk, outflow, and envelope components around 4A2 prevents us from formulating a full RT modeling experiment." (Gas Kinematics in 4A2)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Infall rates rest on adopted density/temperature from single-dish model and an assumed 100 K gas temperature
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Optically thin opacity assumption for 4A2 despite optically thick lines
- Caveat (inferred, ours): RT comparison is qualitative with no fit or uncertainties
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Rotation diagram assumes optically thin lines which may conflict with Scenario II
