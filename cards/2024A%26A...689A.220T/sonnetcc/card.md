## Analysis recipe

1. **Image continuum and line cubes from archive-calibrated data**: Archive calibration; CASA tclean natural weighting, 3 sigma threshold; continuum via MFS in line-free channels; uvcontsub for line cubes  
  > "We imaged the continuum using the multifrequency synthesis (MFS) mode in all line-free channels." (3 Observations); "The visibility calibration of the observations was executed by the ALMA science archive." (3 Observations)
   - software: CASA 5.1.1-5, tclean, uvcontsub
   - parameter: natural weighting, 3 sigma cleaning threshold  
  > "We applied `tclean` using natural weighting and a $3\sigma$ cleaning threshold." (3 Observations)
   - parameter: uvcontsub polynomial order 1 for J0224-4711, 0 for others  
  > "and zeroth-order polynomial for the other QSOs" (3 Observations)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: _not stated_
2. **Measure continuum flux densities and sizes, with tapering for extended emission**: 2D Gaussian fit in CASA; peak flux taken as total for unresolved sources; tapered maps for higher-resolution data to recover extended emission  
  > "Neither emission is spatially resolved, and therefore, we considered the peak flux as the total flux of the source" (4.1.1 QSO J036+03); "The sources in the tapered maps were fitted with the CASA 2D Gaussian fit" (Appendix B QSO J0224-4711)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: uvtaper 0.9 arcsec (J036+03) or 0.7 arcsec (J0224-4711, J2054-0005)  
  > "Also for this object, we tapered the higher resolution observations at 245.67 GHz and 260.51 GHz" (Appendix B QSO J0224-4711)
   - fitting: 2D Gaussian fit (details not stated)  
  > "The sources in the tapered maps were fitted with the CASA 2D Gaussian fit" (Appendix B QSO J0224-4711)
   - validation: Checked that lower taper resolution gives no further flux gain; missed flux ~20% at 243.1 GHz for J036+03  
  > "We checked that there was no further flux gain if tapering at even lower resolution." (Appendix B QSO J036+03)
3. **Correct J231-20 Band 8 flux for the close companion**: Subtract companion contribution using Pensabene2021 SED: companion 0.6 dex fainter than QSO at ~400 GHz, highest-temperature model; result 6.74 +/- 0.31 mJy used as detection in the SED fit  
  > "Conservatively considering the highest-temperature model for the companion, that is, that the flux of the companion is 0.6 dex lower than that of the QSO, we can correct our flux in band 8." (4.1.4 QSO J231-20)
   - software: CASA
   - assumption: Companion flux is 0.6 dex below QSO flux at ~400 GHz  
  > "the flux of the companion at $\sim 400$ GHz is more than 0.6 dex lower than that of the QSO" (4.1.4 QSO J231-20)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Refit with the uncorrected B8 flux: T_dust=61 K and SFR=1913 Msun/yr (factor ~2 higher SFR)  
  > "we indeed derived $T_{\rm dust}=61$ K and SFR$=1913 \ \rm M_\odot ~yr^{-1}$" (5.1 Dust properties)
4. **Extract line properties (CO(7-6), [CI], CO(6-5))**: Moment maps from thresholded continuum-subtracted cubes; spectrum from S/N>2 region; single-Gaussian fit of line profile; 2D Gaussian fit of line maps; luminosities from Solomon et al. formulae  
  > "From the fit with a single Gaussian, the FWHM of the line is" (4.2.1 CO(7-6) and [CI]); "obtained by applying 3$\sigma$ threshold to the continuum-subtracted cube in the line channels" (4.2.2 CO(6-5) in J1319+0950)
   - software: CASA
   - parameter: moment-map threshold 2.5 sigma (J0224-4711) or 3 sigma (J1319+0950, J2054-0005)  
  > "obtained by applying a 2.5$\sigma$ threshold to the continuum-subtracted cube in the line channels" (4.2.1 CO(7-6) and [CI])
   - fitting: single Gaussian fit to line profile  
  > "Performing a single-Gaussian fit to the line spectral profile" (4.2.2 CO(6-5) in J1319+0950)
   - validation: CO redshift compared with earlier determinations  
  > "consistent with previous determinations (cite: reed2017)" (4.2.1 CO(7-6) and [CI])
5. **Fit the cold-dust SED with an optically thick modified black body**: MBB with CMB correction; free T_dust, M_dust, beta; MCMC with uniform priors  
  > "We modeled the dust continuum in an optically thick regime with a modified black body (MBB) function given by" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - software: emcee
   - parameter: k0 = 0.45 cm2/g at nu0 = 250 GHz  
  > "$k_0 = 0.45\  \rm cm^{2}\ g^{-1}$ is the mass absorption coefficient" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - parameter: T0 = 2.73 K for CMB  
  > "with $T_0 = 2.73$ K" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - parameter: 40 chains, 6000 trials, burn-in ~100  
  > "obtained from a MCMC with 40 chains, 6000 trials and a burn-in phase of" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - parameter: Solid angle from continuum mean size of resolved observations  
  > "The solid angle for each source was estimated using the continuum mean size from resolved observations" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - assumption: Optically thick regime; opacity law from beelen+2006/carniani2019  
  > "The latter two terms define the opacity model adopted here (cite: beelen+2006; carniani2019)." (5.1 Dust properties)
   - fitting: emcee MCMC, uniform priors 10 K<T_dust<300 K, 1e5<M_dust<1e9 Msun, 1.0<beta<3.0; errors from 16th and 84th percentiles  
  > "We assumed uniform priors for the fitting parameters:" (5.1 Dust properties); "The errors on the best-fitting parameters were computed considering the 16th and 84th percentiles of the posterior distribution of each parameter." (5.1 Dust properties)
   - validation: Agreement with previous papers within uncertainties; refit without B8/B9 data shows T_dust uncertainty ~70%  
  > "Within the uncertainties, our results agree well overall with those in the previous papers." (5.1 Dust properties); "We found that in this case, the uncertainties on $T_{\rm dust}$ become $\sim 70\%$ on average" (5.1 Dust properties)
6. **Derive L_TIR and star formation rate**: Integrate best-fit model over 8-1000 micron rest frame; SFR proportional to L_TIR with Kroupa IMF; SFR reduced for 50% AGN contribution to dust heating  
  > "We estimated the total infrared (TIR) luminosity for the best-fit model for each source by integrating from" (5.1 Dust properties); "We adopted a contribution of the AGN emission to the dust heating of 50%" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - parameter: SFR = 1.496e-10 x L_IR(8-1000 micron)  
  > "1.496 \times 10^{-10}" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - assumption: Kroupa IMF  
  > "We adopted a Kroupa IMF (cite: kroupa2003)." (5.1 Dust properties)
   - assumption: AGN contributes 50% of dust heating  
  > "We adopted a contribution of the AGN emission to the dust heating of 50%" (5.1 Dust properties)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: IMF sensitivity: Salpeter x1.16, Chabrier x0.67  
  > "would imply an SFR that is higher by a factor of 1.16 or lower by factor of 0.67, respectively" (5.1 Dust properties)
7. **Derive molecular gas masses from CO**: M_H2 = alpha_CO / r_J-(J-1) x L'_CO; r65 and r76 from APM0879+5255 and Cloverleaf  
  > "The molecular gas mass was therefore derived as" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - parameter: alpha_CO = 0.8 Msun (K km/s pc2)^-1  
  > "assuming a conversion factor $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ = 0.8" (Table 3 notes)
   - parameter: r65 = 1.23 +/- 0.66 (J1319+0950, J2054-0005); r76 = 0.76 +/- 0.41 (J0224-4711)  
  > "we assumed $r_{65}=$CO(6-5)/CO(1-0)=$1.23\pm 0.66$, considering the average between the maximum and minimum values of $r_{65}$ in APM0879+5255 and Cloverleaf" (5.2 Molecular gas mass); "and $r_{76}=0.76\pm 0.41$, considering the average between APM0879+5255 and Cloverleaf" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - assumption: CO(7-6) ~ CO(6-5) for J0224-4711  
  > "we assumed that CO(7-6)$\sim$CO(6-5), given the averaged CO SLED of high-$z$ QSO" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Compared with gas masses from neeleman2021 ([CII] continuum with GDR=100: agree; Zanella [CII] conversion: ~10x higher)  
  > "Our results agree well on average with the gas masses found in (cite: neeleman2021)" (5.2 Molecular gas mass)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Selected QSOs with high-quality optical-UV spectra plus ALMA/NOEMA continuum covering the Rayleigh-Jeans and peak of the dust SED and CO/[CII] lines; L_bol>1e47 erg/s  
  > "In other words, all these QSOs have high-quality rest-frame optical-UV spectra and ALMA and/or NOEMA observations targeting the continuum emission from low to high frequencies" (2 Sample)
- Why this method: Same MBB method as Carniani2019, and refitting literature SEDs of J1342+0928 and J183+05 for consistency  
  > "For consistency, we modeled the observed SEDs of the other two QSOs using the procedure described in the previous paragraph" (5.1 Dust properties)
- Alternative: Refit J231-20 with the uncorrected B8 flux (upper limit); corrected flux retained  
  > "Hereafter, we conservatively use the results of the SED fitting with the B8 flux corrected for the companion emission" (5.1 Dust properties)
- Alternative: Salpeter or Chabrier IMF instead of Kroupa  
  > "An Salpeter or Chabrier IMF (cite: salpeter1955; chabrier2003) would imply an SFR that is higher by a factor of 1.16 or lower by factor of 0.67, respectively." (5.1 Dust properties)
- Alternative: Radiative transfer or spatially resolved two-component SED fits for AGN heating deemed not feasible or beyond scope  
  > "The former is not yet feasible because of the lack of high-resolution data, and the latter is beyond the scope of this project" (6.1.6 AGN effect on dust heating)
- How they decided it was enough: For J2054-0005, chose per band the observation with best continuum sensitivity and resolution closest to B9; tapering tested until no flux gain  
  > "we considered the observation with the highest continuum sensitivity and with a resolution that allowed us to spatially resolve the source" (3 Observations); "We checked that there was no further flux gain if tapering at even lower resolution." (Appendix B QSO J036+03)
- Limitation (authors): J231-20 B8 flux is effectively a lower limit, so T_dust and SFR can be underestimated  
  > "we adopted a lower limit for the flux in B8 of J231-20, and thus, $T_{\rm dust}$ and SFR can be underestimated in principle" (5.1 Dust properties)
- Limitation (authors): Dust opacity model carries large systematics  
  > "In this model, $k_\nu$ carries huge systematics because the actual composition of the dust is currently unknown." (5.1 Dust properties)
- Limitation (authors): Single-temperature fit mixes AGN-heated and stellar-heated dust; resolution prevents spatially resolved study  
  > "The resolution of our observations prevents us from performing a spatially resolved study of the dust emission" (6.1.6 AGN effect on dust heating)
- Limitation (authors): CO excitation assumptions give >=50% systematic uncertainty on gas mass; quoted gas-mass errors are statistical only  
  > "The uncertainties reported on the gas mass are only statistical." (5.2 Molecular gas mass)
- Limitation (authors): T_dust is poorly constrained for J0224-4711 (Band 9 does not reach the SED peak), J1319+0950 and J1342+0928 (no Band 8/9)  
  > "In the latter case, an ALMA B10 observation is required." (5.1 Dust properties)
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Dust temperature and mass depend on the fixed opacity law and the single-temperature optically thick assumption
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Poor frequency coverage on several sources makes beta, T_dust and M_dust degenerate
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Gas masses rely on assumed CO excitation ratios from two lensed or unusual QSOs and a fixed alpha_CO
- Caveat (inferred, ours): GDR comparisons mix dust masses derived with different regimes and opacities
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Evolutionary-regime classification rests on literature M_dyn and M_BH with 0.1-0.3 dex systematics and an assumed uniform 50% AGN dust-heating fraction
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Sample of ten luminous QSOs is biased toward high luminosity, limiting generality
- Caveat (inferred, ours): SFR depends on assumed AGN fraction, applied uniformly
- Caveat (inferred, ours): Small sample for HYPERION versus non-HYPERION contrasts
