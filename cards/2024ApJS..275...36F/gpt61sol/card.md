## Analysis recipe

1. **Build reliable continuum catalogs**: Extract positive and negative peaks from uncorrected natural and tapered maps; estimate purity; combine blind selection with IRAC-prior selection.  
  > "We conduct a blind two-dimensional source extraction with sextractor version 2.5.0 (cite: bertin1996)." (Source Extraction); "We create inverted maps by multiplying $-1$ to both the natural and tapered maps, run sextractor again, and extract sources with negative peak counts above 2.5$\sigma$ level." (Source Extraction); "we perform source extraction in *Spitzer*/IRAC channel 2 maps and retrieve real sources from the source candidates with SNR$_{\rm nat}$ down to 4.0 by cross-matching them with the IRAC sources." (Catalog)
   - software: sextractor
   - parameter: Peak catalog > 2.5σ; relative mosaic sensitivity > 30%.  
  > "We extract sources with positive peak counts above 2.5$\sigma$ confidence, referred to as a peak catalog." (Source Extraction); "In both original and inverted maps, we select sources that are identified in high-sensitivity regions, where the relative sensitivity to the deepest part of the mosaic is greater than 30%." (Source Extraction)
   - parameter: Blind: SNR_nat ≥ 5.0 or SNR_tap ≥ 4.5; purity > 0.99.  
  > "we adopt thresholds of SNR$_{\rm nat}$ $\geq$ 5.0 or SNR$_{\rm tap}$ $\geq$ 4.5 in the source candidate catalog, yielding 141 ALMA sources that achieve a purity of $p>0.99$." (Catalog)
   - parameter: IRAC SNR ≥ 5; default cross-match radius 1.0″.  
  > "We use secure IRAC sources with SNR$_{\rm IRAC}$ $\geq5$" (Catalog); "We adopt a search radius of $1.0\ensuremath{''}$ in cross-matching catalogs unless otherwise specified" (Introduction)
   - assumption: Noise Gaussian and centered at zero for negative-peak purity analysis.  
  > "after confirming that the noise remains Gaussian and centered at zero flux, we also conduct negative peak analysis" (Source Extraction)
   - fitting: _not stated_
   - validation: Visual inspection removes one chance projection; 180 reliable sources agree with the positive-minus-negative expectation.

2. **Measure continuum fluxes**: Compare natural/tapered peak photometry, 2.0″-radius apertures, and 2D elliptical Gaussian integrated fluxes; apply primary beam correction.  
  > "In the first method, we measure the peak count in the tapered map ($S_{\rm tap}$)." (Flux Measurement); "In the second method, we measure an enclosed flux density with an aperture radius of $2.0\ensuremath{''}$ with the natural map ($S_{\rm ap}$)." (Flux Measurement); "In the third method, we measure a spatially integrated flux density with the 2D elliptical Gaussian fitting with the CASA task imfit ($S_{\rm imf}$)." (Flux Measurement)

   - parameter: Use S_imf at SNR_nat ≥ 10 with no fitting errors; otherwise S_ap, with peak-photometry exceptions.  
  > "we adopt $S_{\rm imf}$ for the source with SNR$_{\rm nat}\geq10$, but with no fitting errors in imfit (e.g., fitting not converged, significant residual)." (Flux Measurement)
   - parameter: Tapered counterpart peak > 3σ within 1.0″; otherwise sample the natural-map peak position.  
  > "we regard a peak above 3$\sigma$ level in the $uv$-tapered map identified within a radius of $1.0\ensuremath{''}$ from the peak in the natural map as the corresponding peak count" (Flux Measurement); "If we do not identify any peaks above 3$\sigma$ level within the $1.0\ensuremath{''}$ radius, we use the count in the tapered map at the peak pixel position in the natural map." (Flux Measurement)
   - assumption: For unresolved sources, peak count in Jy/beam equals total flux density.  
  > "When the source is unresolved, the peak count (Jy/beam) should equal the total flux density." (Flux Measurement)
   - fitting: imfit initialized with natural-map peak count, position, and beam; all parameters free; fitting area 5″ × 5″.  
  > "In the imfit fitting, we use the peak count, position, and beam size in the natural map for the initial values, but all parameters are ultimately fitted as free parameters." (Flux Measurement); "We limit the fitting area to $5''\times5''$." (Flux Measurement)
   - validation: Aperture growth curves and comparison among flux estimators.

## Qualitative findings

- Number counts disfavor flattening beginning around 0.1 mJy.  
  > "Overall, even accounting for possible lensing magnification uncertainties, our results disfavor the scenario whereby the 1.2-mm number counts start flattening at $S_{\rm 1.2mm}\sim$ 0.1 mJy." (Comparison with previous ALMA observations)
- IR LFs show positive luminosity and negative density evolution.  
  > "We confirm that our measurements are consistent with the general trends of the evolution of IR LFs characterized by positive $L_{\star}$ and negative $\Phi_{\star}$ towards high redshifts reported in previous studies." (Redshift Evolution of IR LF)
- Characteristic IR luminosity may flatten in its evolution around z ∼ 4.  
  > "Our measurements suggest a flattening in the correlation between the redshift and $L_{\star}$ at $z\sim$ 4." (Redshift Evolution of IR LF)
- An additional faint-mm/NIR-dark SFRD component is possible, while previous SFRD estimates remain consistent within uncertainties.  
  > "there may be an additional ($\approx 60$%) SFRD component contributed by the faint-mm population, including NIR dark objects." (Cosmic SFRD); "Therefore, we emphasize that our SFRD measurements are still consistent with previous measurements within the $1-2\sigma$ errors." (Cosmic SFRD)

## Reasoning

- Why this data: Gravitational lensing and 33 cluster sightlines expand faint-source coverage; rich HST/Spitzer ancillary data support redshifts and lens models.  
  > "To enlarge the survey area at the faintest regime and overcome cosmic variance, making full use of gravitational lensing is one of the most effective approaches." (Introduction); "the larger survey area and the 33 independent lines of sight in our ALCS survey help to mitigate the cosmic variance compared to the previous surveys" (Comparison with previous ALMA observations); "The target clusters were selected from the best-studied 33 massive galaxy clusters drawn from *HST* treasury programs of HFF, CLASH, and RELICS, where the deep *HST* and *Spitzer* maps are available." (Summary)
- Why this method: Direct detection avoids stacking systematics; tapered maps recover elongated emission; a fiducial lens model preserves highly magnified regions.  
  > "direct individual detection is essential to avoid potential systematics inherent in the stacking approach." (Introduction); "we produce natural-weighted and $uv$-tapered maps ($2.0\ensuremath{''}\times2.0\ensuremath{''}$) for each cluster not to miss strongly elongated mm sources due to the lensing effect." (Summary); "To retain the full range of the lensing magnification, we thus use the fiducial lens model and include the systematic uncertainties from the different models in the following analysis." (Lens Model and Magnification Correction)
- Alternative: Median magnification maps rejected because they smear high-magnification regions and underestimate effective area.  
  > "if we create the median magnification map from these 7 different lens models, the survey area is underestimated at high magnifications ($\mu\gtrsim10$)." (Lens Models Uncertainty)
- Alternative: Schechter and DPL both fit counts; Schechter chosen as fiducial.  
  > "the $\chi^{2}$/dof values indicate that the number counts are well represented by Schechter and DPL." (Number counts at 1.2 mm); "we adopt the best-fit Schechter function for the entire 33 ALCS clusters without any weights among the clusters as our fiducial measurement." (Number counts at 1.2 mm)
- Alternative: Constant, positive, and negative FIR size–luminosity relations tested.  
  > "the size assumptions of constant, negative, and positive correlations, respectively." (Impact from the dust continuum size distribution)
- Alternative: Arp220 and AS2UDS redshift templates compared; AS2UDS adopted.  
  > "We find that the AS2UDS template suggests a lower redshift solution than Arp220 in general." ($z_{\rm phot}$ estimate by template fitting); "We thus use $P(z)$ obtained with the AS2UDS template for the 23 ALCS sources." ($z_{\rm phot}$ estimate by template fitting)

  > "we do not include the measurements from (cite: barrufet2023b) due to its targeted observation aspect." (IR Luminosity Function at $z\simeq$ 1–8)
