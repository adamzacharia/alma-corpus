## Findings
- SR-01 | high | attribution | “Low-velocity exclusion … 1.2–1.6 km/s … 3” | The pack explicitly says 2019ApJ...871..100H **fits C18O below 1.6 km/s** and CO above it; 1.6 is a tracer boundary, not an exclusion. 2017ApJ...834..178Y supplies no numerical cutoff. | Remove 1.6 from the exclusion range; distinguish excluded channels from tracer selection.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “C18O abundance … 4e-8 to 5e-7” | The cited 2019ApJ...871..100H assumes X_CO=5e-4 and X_CO/X_C18O=560, implying X_C18O≈8.9e-7. | Include that value and distinguish absolute abundances from isotope ratios.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Data-point threshold for V(R) … 4” | For 2018ApJ...859..111H, the pack says **channels with SNR>5 for astrometry**, not pixels above 5σ for PV mean positions. | Separate channel detection, pixel clipping and ridge/edge thresholds; recount each category.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “SLAM … then average the masses … [both papers]”; “rejected edge-method radii … ridge-method intersections” | The pack supports mean ridge/edge mass only for 2025ApJ...993..120H. Its supplied entries do not establish SLAM attribution, rejected edge radii or profile intersections. 2024A&A...690A..46S supplies only Vsys and Keplerian-inside-break assumptions. | Retain the supported mass averaging for the first paper; remove or separately substantiate the remaining claims.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “pvextractor … 2025SciA...11.4512O”; “FERIA … reduced-chi2 grid”; “emcee for outflow inclination” | Aggregate tool counts do not establish these paper-level assignments. The supplied entries do not name those tools for these papers, including 2019ApJ...887..209A. | Keep aggregate counts separate; retain paper-level software assignments only where explicitly supported.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “RADMC-3D … [three papers]”; “MOLLIE and Hyperion … 2015ApJ...813L..19J” | RADMC-3D is explicitly supported for 2020ApJ...893...51S; its assignment to 2021ApJ...908..108F and 2019ApJ...871..100H, and the two codes assigned to 2015ApJ...813L..19J, are absent from their supplied pack entries. LIME for 2018A&A...614A.113H is supported. | Remove unsupported software-to-paper mappings.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “i 0–40 … step 10”; “r_CB … step 0.05 arcsec”; “line width … 1 km/s” | The pack does not supply these grid settings for 2019ApJ...873...73Z or the 1 km/s width for 2016ApJ...824...88O. It also does not supply the streamline grid or central 10 Msun claimed for 2025SciA...11.4512O. | Delete unsupported settings from both files; recompute dependent ranges and counts.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “+5 deg … −5 deg”; “six cuts”; “PA 20 deg”; “2D Gaussian” | These details are not supplied for 2015ApJ...812...59O, 2019ApJ...873...73Z or 2015ApJ...814...22E, respectively. | Remove these paper-specific details or provide supporting evidence.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “XCLASS … non-subtracted cube”; “deepest absorption channel”; “r_inf 0.012 pc … c_s,eff 0.233” | For 2018A&A...615A.141B the pack supplies radius and infall velocity, but neither extraction rule nor XCLASS/continuum treatment. The numerical collapse setup and off-source validation attributed to 2015ApJ...814...22E are also absent. | Remove unsupported attributions and parameters; label independently justified recommendations as general practice.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “r^0 and r^-2.5 … Keplerian-only … failed”; “23 Msun rejected”; “SED-fit mass” | These checks are absent from the supplied entries for 2016ApJ...824...88O and 2019ApJ...873...73Z. The claimed velocity-gradient mass cross-check for 2024ApJ...967...15K is likewise absent. | Remove these claimed validations and the unsupported tested-density range.
- SR-11 | medium | attribution | “Break / Keplerian radius … 50–130 AU … 54–624 au” | 2020ApJ...893...51S reports an **outer-disk** profile break in a warped-disk analysis; the pack does not establish that 624 au is an envelope-to-Keplerian transition. | Separate measured profile breaks, corrected disk radii and physical transition interpretations; remove the inferred common default.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “roughly 50 papers”; “most envelope papers … after step 4”; “aggregate is dominated” | No reproducible envelope subset or denominator is supplied. The fitting aggregate lists common phrasings rather than a complete distribution establishing dominance or workflow order. | Supply explicit selection/counting rules or remove these prevalence claims; describe the demonstrated workflow alternatives.
- SR-13 | high | science | “V_rot = V_obs / sin i”; “M* scales as 1/sin² i” | These require i=0 face-on. The pack explicitly defines i=0 **edge-on** for 2019ApJ...873...73Z, which the skill also uses. Mixing conventions can produce severely wrong masses. | Define one internal convention, convert every imported inclination, and document the original convention and near-side/sign information.
- SR-14 | high | science | “major axis … and … minor axis separates rotation from infall” | This is not a unique diagnostic. Radial expansion, projection and other structures can mimic signatures. The pack records indistinguishable infall+Keplerian/pure-Keplerian models in 2013A&A...560A.103M and a successful warped-disk alternative in 2020ApJ...893...51S. | State the thin, axisymmetric geometry assumptions; require near-side information and competing rotation, infall, expansion and warp models before identifying infall.
- SR-15 | high | science | “Mdot = Omega r² rho v_in … rho ~2.3e9 cm−3” | The formula requires **mass density**; cm−3 denotes number density. The pack’s notation for 2017A&A...603A..10B must not be inserted unchanged. | Write rho_mass=mu m_H n, define the particle species and mu, and use positive inward speed and explicitly defined solid angle.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “M = r_CB v_CB²/(2G)”; “barrier … l²/(2GM)” | These barrier relations require a zero-energy ballistic orbit, with v_CB the deprojected tangential speed where radial speed vanishes. They are not general relations for slowed infall or finite-energy streamers. | State those assumptions and distinguish the barrier from the circularization radius j²/(GM).
- SR-17 | medium | science | “Specific angular momentum j = R V_rot … [streamer example]” | For arbitrary streamer trajectories, total specific angular momentum is **r×v**, as explicitly used by TIPSY in 2024A&A...683A.133G. R V_phi gives its axial component in cylindrical coordinates. | Separate total angular momentum from its axial component and define the radius and velocity used.
- SR-18 | medium | science | “Infall velocity = velocity of the deepest absorption channel relative to V_LSR” | An absorption-channel offset is a line-of-sight diagnostic, not generally the physical radial speed at a known radius; opacity and foreground structure can move the minimum. The cited pack entry gives a result, not this universal estimator. | Report the observed offset first; infer radial speed and location through an explicit geometry and line-transfer model.
- SR-19 | medium | science | “Optically thin isotopologues (C18O, 13CO)” | Isotopologue identity does not establish optical thinness. The pack explicitly flags optical-depth uncertainty in 2015ApJ...812...27A. | Call these candidate lower-opacity tracers and require an opacity assessment before adopting thin-line inference.
- SR-20 | medium | science | “a continuum mass is an upper limit under the optically thin assumption” | The pack makes this statement specifically about **ALMA1** in 2025SciA...11.4512O. Optical thinness alone does not make continuum masses upper limits; neglected opacity can instead underestimate mass. | Preserve the source-specific qualification and identify what establishes each limit.
- SR-21 | medium | attribution | “Measured infall is ~0.3 V_ff”; “alpha … plus … A=0.42” | For 2017ApJ...849...56A, alpha≈0.3 is inferred through an assumed shock condition. The pack calls A=0.42 an infall ratio for 2025A&A...693A..76C without defining its normalization as V_ff. | Describe alpha as model-dependent; keep A separate until its velocity normalization is established.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “Self-absorption and optical depth” | The skill omits the distinct dust-opacity limitation: 2017ApJ...843...27L states that optically thick disk dust prevents probing molecules near the midplane. | Add continuum obscuration to the interpretation checks; absence of line emission need not mean absent gas.
- SR-23 | medium | omission | “Streamers … trajectory fits … Mdot” | The pack explicitly assumes line intensity traces density in TIPSY and treats its masses/rates as order-of-magnitude lower limits. The skill mentions mass limits but omits this emissivity assumption and weakens the qualification on rates. | State the intensity–density assumption, excitation/abundance dependence, and lower-limit/order-of-magnitude status of the derived rates.
- SR-24 | medium | omission | “Unstated settings … Record these” | The pack flags flux-scale uncertainty in 13 cards, but the reporting checklist omits it. It also omits the relevant model limitations: fixed vertical temperature structure in 2017ApJ...849...56A and the proof-of-concept status of 2025A&A...693A..76C. | Record flux uncertainty and model-specific fixed structure; distinguish statistical errors from these systematic limitations.
- SR-25 | low | usability | Table cells containing “|dV|” and “|V−Vlsr|” | Unescaped pipes split Markdown table cells in references/parameters.md, corrupting column alignment. | Escape literal pipes or write abs(dV) and abs(V−Vlsr).
