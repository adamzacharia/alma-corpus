## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “take the opacities from the LTE model … (2014Sci...345.1584B)” | That card supports contaminant subtraction and optical thinness verified a posteriori, not model-derived opacity corrections. | Attribute the opacity correction to 2016A&A...587A..91B; describe the 2014 paper separately.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “RADEX … side by side … 2024A&A...689A..64B” | This paper’s non-LTE step explicitly names **grelvg**, not RADEX. | Replace this example with an actual RADEX example or describe it as an LVG cross-check.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “emcee”; “XCLASS / MAGIX”; “Levenberg-Marquardt” | The supplied entries do not establish emcee for 2024A&A...689A..65R or 2024A&A...687A.220I, XCLASS/MAGIX for 2019ApJ...885...82L, or Levenberg-Marquardt for 2021A&A...656A..46M. They provide priors, walkers, algorithm names, or least-squares fitting, respectively. | Remove or qualify these paper-specific software/algorithm attributions.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “size, N, T, v offset, FWHM (by eye)” under “Free” | The 2016A&A...587A..91B excerpt lists five model parameters but explicitly says “The other four parameters were optimized by eye.” | List five model parameters without asserting all five were free and optimized by eye.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “16 … beam filling factor of 1 or emission filling the beam”; “eta 0.384 … to 1” | The Assumptions list contains at least 17 explicit unity/fills-beam bibcodes, plus an approximately-unity case. The parameter range also misses filling factor 0.1 in 2017ApJ...848..101V. | Recount distinct bibcodes using an explicit inclusion rule; extend the range and distinguish assumed from fitted filling factors.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Detection threshold … Papers (count): 5” | At least seven pack entries specify relevant thresholds: 2024A&A...689A..64B, 2021A&A...656A..46M, 2024ApJ...962...48J, 2020ApJ...890...76N, 2025A&A...694A.166C, 2019ApJ...872..196S and 2019A&A...623A..68T. | Recount, separating integrated-intensity, peak, model-prediction and absorption thresholds.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “12C/13C … 50 to 70 … 7”; “Free Tex bounds … 10 to 500 K” | LTE isotopic scaling also uses 76 in 2015ApJ...798...85P and 100 in 2017A&A...608A..38O. The LTE priors in 2024A&A...689A..65R extend over open intervals starting at 0 K. | Correct the ranges and isotope count, or explicitly restrict the table to a named subset.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “most papers set it to 1 and admit that makes N a lower limit” | The pack does not establish either majority claim. The factor 2–3 lower-limit statement belongs specifically to 2025ApJS..276...49J and its adopted filling factor. | Say that several papers assume unity; distinguish beam-averaged columns from intrinsic source columns and retain the lower-limit claim with its specific attribution.
- SR-09 | high | science | “N_u = 8 pi k nu^2 W / (h c^3 A_ul) with W … in K km/s” | The expression requires conversion of the velocity integral into units consistent with the constants. Direct substitution of K km/s with cgs constants misses a factor of 10^5. | Give an explicit unit-safe form: N_u[cm^-2] = 1.94×10^3 nu[GHz]^2 W[K km/s]/A_ul[s^-1].
- SR-10 | high | science | “13CO/12CO line ratio R = 2.0” in parameters §4 | With the pack’s same-Tex assumption and tau_12≈56, tau_13≈0.7, the brightness ratio **12CO/13CO** is approximately 2; its inverse is approximately 0.5. The main skill already uses the correct orientation. | Change the parameter-table label to 12CO/13CO and state the excitation/filling assumptions.
- SR-11 | high | science | “18O/16O = 100” in parameters §8 | The pack is internally inconsistent: ALCHEMI step 8 prints this orientation, but its Oxygen result explicitly reports **16O/18O = 100±20**. Copying the inverted ratio gives incorrect abundance scaling. | Flag the pack inconsistency and use 16O/18O=100; write N(H2)=100 N(C18O)/(8×10^-5) under the stated assumptions.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “add the absolute flux uncertainty … 20% on ln(Eu/gu)” | Although copied from the 2019ApJ...872..200H card, this is not an actionable flux-error prescription. Eu/gu is not the population-diagram ordinate, and a percentage of a logarithm depends on the chosen units. | Mark the card wording as anomalous; propagate intensity errors to ln(Nu/gu), with calibration correlations handled explicitly.
- SR-13 | high | science | “For thick CO, Tex is set to the peak brightness temperature” | The attribution to 2026A&A...705A.198M is supported, but the instruction omits beam filling, background and the Rayleigh–Jeans approximation. These matter for cold ALMA lines. | Use Tb=eta[J_nu(Tex)−J_nu(Tbg)](1−e^-tau) and invert it. Label Tex≈Tb as a conditional approximation.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “Where dust is optically thick, correct … by dividing by exp(-tau_dust)” | This generalizes a source-specific treatment. The 2025ApJS..276...49J card explicitly assumes coupled dust and gas and fits their continuum/filling model; attenuation depends on geometry and temperatures. | Require a justified line–dust transfer model before applying this correction; also distinguish continuum brightness from a physical blackbody background temperature.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “When not to use … the colliders are electrons” | Electron collisions do not inherently invalidate LTE. The 2016MNRAS.460.2933M excerpt explicitly scans electron densities “from the radiation-dominated regime to LTE”; the comet card also uses a rotational diagram alongside non-LTE modelling. | Base the exclusion on demonstrated excitation imbalance, including the actual colliders and pumping processes.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Ortho/para or A/E” appears only as a parameter choice | There is no instruction to distinguish spin-specific from total columns or keep Q and g_u conventions consistent. The pack explicitly uses o/p=3 for total abundance in 2018ApJ...864..170G and para-only analysis without an OPR in 2024ApJ...969..143I. | Require the reported column’s state convention, matching spectroscopy, and any conversion to a total column; apply corrections once.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Convert Jy/beam to K”; one region with plausible conditions | The workflow lacks an explicit common spatial-response check. The pack assumes all lines share an environment (2020ApJ...890..142P), assumes a common distribution for beam coupling (2025A&A...696A.100G), and warns about comparisons across physical scales (2024A&A...689A..64B). | Match beams, apertures and recoverable spatial scales, or model their differences. Distinguish aperture-integrated Jy from Jy/beam; qualify the dilution formula as circular-Gaussian coupling.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “Upper limits: 3 sigma integrated intensity” | No linewidth, integration window or channel-correlation prescription is required. The pack explicitly supplies a linewidth for limits in 2015ApJ...798..124R and 2021ApJ...908..163M, and a width-dependent integrated-opacity limit in 2018A&A...620A.119D. | Require linewidth, channel width, noise and covariance assumptions; show how the integrated limit was calculated.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | MCWeeds absorption fitting is included in an emission-centred workflow | No absorption branch prevents applying the positive-emission W-to-N_u formula to absorption. The pack uses Gaussian opacity components in 2019A&A...623A..68T and a covering factor plus unsubtracted-continuum validation in 2019ApJ...883..193N. | Add continuum normalization, covering factor, source-function treatment and integrated-opacity fitting, or explicitly exclude absorption.
- SR-20 | medium | usability | “Use … an LTE gas mass from line fluxes” | The steps stop at columns and abundance ratios; they never provide the promised column-to-mass calculation. The pack gives explicit mass formulas in 2015A&A...581A..85P and 2017ApJ...848..101V. | Add conversion through tracer abundance and physical area, define the helium convention, and require the column and area to use consistent filling conventions.
