## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “Injections per image per run … 1–50 … 6” | The pack includes 500 objects per run (2025A&A...704A.158C), 350 clouds per injection setup (2017MNRAS.468.1769F), and at least eight explicitly documented papers. | Recount distinct bibcodes, distinguish images from cubes, and include the larger batches.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “Total injections … Papers … 11” | At least 12 distinct papers give injection totals, including 30,000 in 2020PASJ...72...69Y and 1400 in 2023A&A...674A..75N. | Recount using an explicit bibcode-to-total ledger; distinguish totals from repetitions per bin.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “MC realisations … 50–100,000”; “1000 (9 papers)” | The pack gives 10^6 realisations per case for 2024A&A...682A..55M and at least 12 bibcodes specifying 1000 realisations. | Correct the maximum and recount; separate uncertainty propagation from other Monte Carlo applications.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Completeness level quoted … 50–90% … 4” | 2020ApJS..251...14H explicitly reports 95% completeness, making both the range and four-paper tally incomplete. | Include 95% and recount clearly defined completeness thresholds.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “`simobserve`/`simanalyze` (2020ApJS..251...14H …)” | That entry names `simobserve` and `tclean`; `simanalyze` is supported by 2023MNRAS.522...56V. | Attribute each imaging route separately.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “50 realisations … after removing sources above S/N ~50” | For 2025A&A...695A.204V, removal is methodological advice; “Settings cards flag…” explicitly says whether removal occurred was unstated. | Say bright-source removal is required advice, while its execution in the application is unreported.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “1.33 at S/N 3”; “applied to upper limits” | The supplied 2022MNRAS.515.3126I entry supports a boosting factor of 1.33 but supplies neither that S/N nor application to upper limits. | Remove those details or obtain supporting evidence.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “error function … 2016ApJ...833...68A” | Its pack entry provides fitted constants but does not identify an error-function form. Explicit error-function support is supplied for 2025PASJ...77.1163H. | Remove the unsupported attribution or provide the function’s verified evidence.
- SR-09 | medium | unsupported | “2D Gaussians are the norm”; “Image-plane injection is the common default” | The cited examples establish usage, not prevalence across the matched corpus. | Present these as available approaches or labelled recommendations; reserve frequency claims for counted evidence.
- SR-10 | high | science | “blank-mock purity Q” | 2025PASJ...77.1163H defines Q = N_mock/N_real as contamination, not purity. The pack also restricts its domain to N_mock ≤ N_real and N_real > 0. | Rename Q as contamination; distinguish purity 1−Q and state the domain restriction.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “Inject … after removing detected sources”; “Keep injections at least about one beam from real sources” | These universal instructions condition away crowding and blending. The cited exclusion radii are study-specific; the pack explicitly identifies blending and clustering limitations (2017MNRAS.468.1769F; 2020MNRAS.495.3409S). | Define whether completeness is conditional on isolated backgrounds or includes confusion; choose exclusions accordingly and document the excluded population.
- SR-12 | medium | usability | “Run the exact same imaging … on injected … data” | This cannot literally apply to injections added to an already reconstructed image, an approach the skill recommends. | Separate image-plane tests of downstream extraction from visibility-plane tests that rerun imaging and calibration.
- SR-13 | high | omission | “VF = 2 as the threshold” | The omitted limitation in 2023MNRAS.522...56V says noise extends apparent VF to ~3–4 for 1-hour and ~5–7 for 20-minute faint-source measurements. The ~1.6 systematic maximum also applies only to its restricted hour-angle range. | Explain that VF = 2 is a study-specific systematic cutoff, not sufficient detection significance; calibrate the combined noise/systematics distribution by cadence, S/N and hour angle.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “Inverted map … negative peaks as the false-positive proxy” | The skill omits the explicit 2021ApJ...923..121Y warning that non-negative reconstruction creates artificial positive off-source emission. Symmetric input noise does not guarantee symmetric reconstructed artefacts. | Warn against negative-peak fidelity estimates for positivity-constrained reconstruction without dedicated validation.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “Jackknife of visibilities: flip the sign…” | The applicability assumptions in 2025A&A...695A.204V—well-calibrated, additive, Gaussian, uncorrelated, zero-mean visibility noise—are absent. | State these assumptions and require validation when calibration residuals or correlated visibility errors are material.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Perturb the data by their errors and refit” | No explicit instruction preserves covariance. The pack flags residual noise correlation in 2021ApJ...908...19B and documents spatially filtered synthetic noise in 2026MNRAS.548ag325W. | Require noise realisations to reproduce relevant spatial, spectral and temporal correlations, rather than drawing independent errors by default.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Always report…”; “same finder and parameters” | The reporting list omits versions and numerical imaging/finder configurations. The card aggregate flags missing `tclean` settings, FindClump settings, peak-PDF binning/integration, getsf scales and astrodendro parameters. | Add a configuration manifest covering these settings; label missing-for-reproduction assessments as card-author findings.
- SR-18 | medium | usability | “22% … more than 30 arcsec away”; “5000 random positions, 4 arcsec search” | The first test is on an ACT map (2023PASJ...75..311K); the second is the 850 μm SCUBA-2 test (2024ApJ...962..128M). Their instrumental contexts are missing from an ALMA-facing skill. | Name the instruments and prohibit transferring these angular scales or recovery fractions directly to ALMA.
