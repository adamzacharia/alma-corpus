## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “Band 3 … 7114–7400 K … 9”; “Band 6 … 7” | These corpus-wide ranges/counts omit 2022FrASS...9.1118D, which gives QS references of 7110 ± 90 K and 6210 ± 110 K. The table also mixes measurements, adopted references and model-derived estimates. | Enumerate counted papers, correct the ranges/counts, and label each value’s status and frequency.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “6 excerpt papers feather, 3 add a constant” | The excerpts explicitly identify five feathering papers: 2017SoPh..292...87S, 2018A&A...619L...6N, 2019ApJ...881...99M, 2020A&A...635A..71W and 2020A&A...644A.152E. Only two explicitly identify constant offsets; 2017ApJ...841L...5S says “scale with single-dish background.” | Supply bibcode membership and distinguish explicit methods from inferred classifications; otherwise remove the counts.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “linear detrend, Tukey … 0.1, Morlet” attributed jointly to the 2023 and 2025 papers | 2023A&A...671A..69G supplies Tukey 0.1, but its card flags the mother wavelet as unstated. 2025A&A...693A..19S supplies Morlet, but its card flags the Tukey taper parameter as unstated. | Separate the paper-specific recipes; label any combined recommendation as a synthesis.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | Attenuator changes “move the phase by only 0.3 deg … and 0.6 deg” | 2017SoPh..292...87S reports the **standard deviation across the spectral window**, not a phase-shift magnitude or upper bound. | Preserve the measured statistic and its scope.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Snapshot noise … 3.7–21.2 K” | The 3.7 K result in 2017SoPh..292...87S is explicitly for six seconds and 8 GHz, rather than the skill’s usual two-second snapshots. | Attach integration time, bandwidth and image type to each noise example.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Flux-scale uncertainty … Range … 20% systematic for AIA DEM” | 2020A&A...643A..41D assigns 20% to atomic-data uncertainties in the DEM analysis. It is not an ALMA flux-scale uncertainty. The 5% at 3σ in 2019ApJ...875..163R is an adopted error model. | Split the uncertainty sources and identify the 5% distribution as paper-specific.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “region masks from HMI … plage Tb >= 6500 K” | 2021ApJ...906...83C uses ±0.1 kG magnetograms as a guide and an **ALMA Band 6** threshold to exclude the pore. The temperature threshold is neither an HMI measurement nor a universal plage definition. | Describe the magnetogram guide and pore exclusion separately.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “radius near 963–968 arcsec” as a verification check | The pack also reports 961.1 ± 2.5 arcsec at 239 GHz in 2017A&A...605A..78A and 961.6 ± 2.1 arcsec at 230 GHz in 2019ApJ...871...45S. | Use frequency-, method- and distance-matched comparisons with uncertainties instead of this acceptance range.
- SR-09 | medium | unsupported | “chance coincidence with AIA pairing within 15 s” without its source | The pairing and chance-coincidence methods belong to 2020A&A...638A..62N, steps 3–4; pairing also appears in 2021A&A...652A..92N. The adjacent 2023 citation supports the toy tracking experiment. | Cite the pairing method directly and specify its common-pixel requirement.
- SR-10 | high | science | “If the paper is silent, normalise disk-centre quiet Sun to 7300 K … [or] 5900–6350 K” | This turns selected literature references into an automatic calibration. The pack stresses uncertain TP calibration and that cross-normalization cannot replace absolute calibration (2022A&A...661L...4A). Silence also does not establish that delivered products need rescaling. | Establish existing calibration first. Preserve relative measurements when necessary; label any externally imposed zero level as an assumption and propagate its uncertainty.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “feather or add the TP offset” as interchangeable choices | A constant restores a zero level but cannot restore spatially varying missing short-spacing emission. The pack documents both approaches without establishing their equivalence. | Explain the distinction and require justification and validation of a constant-background approximation.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “Measure noise from the XX minus YY image difference … or from the time series” | 2017SoPh..292...87S assumes negligible linear polarization. Raw temporal scatter includes solar variability. An unnormalised XX−YY difference also does not equal the noise of their average. | State the polarization assumption and noise estimator. For independent equal-noise XX/YY images and I=(XX+YY)/2, use σI=std(XX−YY)/2; distinguish instrumental noise from temporal variability.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “zero-fill and apply gap corrections of 0.6 … and 0.7” | These are results for the sampling in 2021A&A...652A..92N, not transferable band constants. The instruction also leaves the corrected quantity and multiplication/division unspecified. | Reproduce and document the paper’s correction for that dataset; derive sampling corrections for new data and test their effect.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “feathered Tb a few percent above TP” as a verification target | The 5–10% excess and factor 1.066 are reported discrepancies in particular datasets, not required successful-combination outcomes. | Compare matched regions and resolutions, investigate discrepancies, and avoid enforcing a positive excess.
- SR-15 | medium | science | Beam-scale widths “need a filling-factor correction (0.25 …)” | In 2020ApJ...888L..28S, 0.25 follows an assumed 0.5-arcsec spicule width versus an apparent 2-arcsec width. Beam-scale size alone does not determine a filling factor. | Retain unresolved-size limits and present filling-factor corrections as geometry-dependent assumptions with sensitivity tests.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | Radius extraction omits distance correction and map rejection criteria | Both radius-method cards require Earth-orbit eccentricity correction. 2021ApJ...910...77M requires at least 35% of limb points; 2022MNRAS.511..877M requires at least ten, with scatter below 20 arcsec. | Include distance normalization, paper-specific point retention and scatter cuts; record the radius definition.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Energy E = 3 N_e k V … with FAL C density” | 2020A&A...638A..62N additionally assumes optical thickness, unity filling factor, constant electron density and V=A^(3/2); event areas are corrected by μ. Merely calling energies upper limits does not specify the calculation. | Define N_e as number density and state the volume, projection, opacity and filling-factor assumptions.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “invert with STiC jointly with IRIS” | 2020A&A...634A..56D identifies hydrostatic, one-dimensional assumptions, inability to treat time-dependent hydrogen ionization and resolution mismatch. It also uses ad hoc ALMA weighting and Monte Carlo/node-count validation. | Add these limitations and require recorded weighting, resolution/time matching and inversion validation.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “drop +/-180 deg phases from phase speeds” | The 2025 card also identifies 0° as indicative of standing waves; its missing-settings list specifically flags zero and near-zero phase treatment. These can yield undefined or unstable speeds. | Specify phase units, lag calculation and uncertainty-based treatment of near-zero lags; distinguish standing-wave interpretations from propagation.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | Feature-tracking pitfalls mention only “~20%” size underestimation | 2023A&A...671A..69G also reports roughly 60% detection success under overlap and termination of individual tracks at mergers. These affect counts and lifetimes. | Add completeness and merger conventions; require detector validation under representative crowding.
- SR-21 | low | omission | Band 5 / Band 7 values presented without their evidential limits | The pack labels Band 5’s 6532 K as an estimate; Band 7’s result depends on commissioning calibration and observations from one day. | Label the estimate and conditional calibration, and retain the single-day validation caveat.
