# Codex review: analysis-software-map
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “median robust in every major field”; “3498 cards”; “unstated in 96% … 61%” | The supplied pack covers 3422 papers and contains no stats README, robust distributions or version-reporting percentages. | Remove these statistics and the literature-derived robust default, or supply the supporting aggregate and its denominator.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “`noisethreshold` 5, `sidelobethreshold` 1.25” | The auto-multithresh entries for 2024AJ....168..151J and 2024ApJ...966...32E say only “standard” or “default parameters”; 2024RAA....24f5011X has no supplied entry. | Retain the documented automasking uses; remove the numerical settings unless explicitly evidenced.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “0 (constant) on small image subcubes” | The imcontsub entry for 2021ApJ...909..181L specifies subcube dimensions, not polynomial order. Additionally, 2021A&A...653A..77P explicitly uses **image-domain** first-order subtraction, despite its placement among evidence for the uv-plane default. | Separate task/domain examples and remove the unsupported constant-order attribution.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “inf then int in two rounds” | 2019ApJ...879L..25I supports phase gain solutions only; 2024A&A...686A.274V has no supplied entry. Neither supports these intervals or round counts here. | Remove the numerical schedule and require a justified, validated solution interval.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “`min_value` 3 sigma, `min_delta` 1 sigma … `min_npix` 60” | No entry for 2017MNRAS.464L..31H or these settings appears in the evidence pack. | Remove the numbers throughout, including “When to use,” or provide the corresponding evidence.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “48 walkers x 3500 steps … 200 x 5000 + 5000 … 8 x parameters” | The supplied entries for 2020ApJ...891...48H concern opacities and Gaussian fits; 2021A&A...648A..19W concerns masks. 2024A&A...688A.149D has no entry. | Delete these configurations; retain model-specific sampler settings and convergence checks as general practice.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Recompute weights with statwt before combining” | 2017ApJ...835..174S supports this sequence, but 2021A&A...649A.122P says only “from observed scatter”; 2021A&A...648A..33M explicitly places reweighting **after** final self-calibration and combination. | Preserve each paper’s sequence and replace the universal weighting default with a conditional, justified choice.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “2022MNRAS.516.4066L 3DBarolo vs KinMS” | This bibcode’s supplied entry documents MgeFit fitting of HST surface brightness, not a comparison between kinematic packages. | Remove this cross-check example from the checklist, parameter table, verification section and source-card description.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “evidence … with … dynesty (2019A&A...628A.100D)” | The dynesty entry says “Dynamic nested sampling of posterior,” not evidence-based model selection. The MultiNest entry for 2013MNRAS.430.2454L explicitly supports evidence and model selection. | Cite MultiNest for the documented example; label dynesty’s evidence capability as general software background.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “SCIMES (S 3)” under GMC decomposition | The pack assigns `scimes 3` to **core-extraction-cmf**, not gmc-decomposition-virial. | State the actual technique associated with this count or omit the count from that row.
- SR-11 | medium | attribution | “CASA `imfit` 96 P” | The aggregate counts the generic name `imfit`, including a Sérsic-modelling use in 2018A&A...616A.110E. It does not establish that all 96 inventories refer to CASA’s Gaussian-fitting task. | Keep the count attached to the inventory label `imfit`; distinguish CASA task examples from other or ambiguous implementations.
- SR-12 | medium | attribution | “add them when you need a total” | Distinct-paper counts for separate names cannot be summed into a distinct-paper total without checking overlap. The pack supplies no intersections. `FAST++` is also presented alongside FAST as though it were merely a spelling variant. | Report separate counts or explicitly labelled sums of name counts; compute deduplicated paper unions before claiming package totals, and distinguish related implementations.
- SR-13 | medium | unsupported | “ALMA papers almost never write their own fitter”; “use the most-named package for that job” | Inventory frequencies do not measure the prevalence of custom implementations or establish job-specific suitability. They include other-facility work, inherited outputs and mentions without execution; the pack explicitly lists self-made scripts and custom continuum subtraction under Python/IDL. | Recommend established tools without claiming an unsupported near-universal practice; rank candidates using demonstrated role, data domain and scientific requirements.
- SR-14 | medium | unsupported | “Few beams across a source, few parameters: KinMS or GalPak3D” | The cited GalPak3D entries document cube fitting and source-plane fitting, without this resolution-dependent preference. Referring to another skill does not supply evidence here. | Label this as a separate methodological recommendation with stated assumptions, or remove the preference.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “PV rotation-curve fitting: SLAM … impv” | CASA `impv` extracts position–velocity diagrams; it does not fit rotation curves. The pack’s `impv 5` matched-step count supplies no fitting attribution. | Move `impv` to PV extraction and separate extraction tools from fitting tools.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “Spatial structure or optically thick lines: RATRAN … or LIME” | Optical thickness alone does not rule out RADEX, which treats opacity through an escape-probability approximation. The cited pack entries establish RATRAN/LIME uses, not this switching rule. | Base the alternative on required geometry, spatial structure and limitations of the adopted radiative-transfer approximation.
- SR-17 | medium | science | “Closes when recovery is within the quoted uncertainty” | A single noisy recovery outside an uncertainty interval need not indicate failure; one inside it does not validate uncertainty calibration. The simobserve entries establish simulation uses, not this acceptance criterion. | Predefine recovery tolerances; use repeated noise realizations to assess bias and coverage when validating uncertainties.
- SR-18 | medium | usability | “recalibrate only when the paper did” | This appears in a router covering novel and archival analyses as well as reproduction. The software pack supplies no such restriction, and it prevents responding to calibration problems in the current data. | Restrict matching the paper’s calibration path to reproduction; otherwise route the decision through data suitability and calibration validation.

## Notes
Most individual inventory counts and package-role examples match the pack.
The main weakness is turning software inventories and isolated examples into numerical defaults or universal selection rules.
No identifiable held-out-target leakage appears; the CosmicCoder anecdotes remain labelled and general.
