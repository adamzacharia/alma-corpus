- SR-17 | medium | science | “Sample, do not grid-guess”; “compare models by evidence … not by eye” | The pack supports legitimate parameter grids and qualitative comparisons: 2017ApJ...850L..21K, 2017ApJ...848L..21A, 2019Natur.575..459M and 2024ApJ...974..279D. - SR-18 | high | science | “The SED peak is nu_m for a relativistic jet” | Peak identity depends on cooling and absorption ordering, not relativistic status. - SR-19 | high | science | “Reproduce those with afterglowpy or boxfit” | The 2019Natur.575..459M model includes SSC, electron evolution, pairs, gamma-gamma absorption and self-absorption. - SR-20 | medium | science | “Handle limits … penalty against a zero-flux point” | This reproduces a particular surrogate in 2025ApJ...985..124L, but is not a general censored-data likelihood.
