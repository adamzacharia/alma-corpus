# Codex review: smbh-gas-dynamics
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | high | attribution | “Papers compare a virial M_BH (Mg II or C IV)” | The pack identifies Eddington-limited BH estimates for two quasars in 2018PASJ...70...36I and minimum BH masses from Eddington luminosity in 2018ApJ...854...97D, not virial measurements. | Distinguish virial estimates, Eddington-based estimates and lower limits for each cited sample.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “Briggs robust 0.5 in most papers”; “6 of 8 cards checked” | The supplied pack does not provide those robust values or that tally. Its 2020ApJ...892...68N card explicitly lists weighting/robust among unstated imaging parameters. | Remove the prevalence claim and unsupported weighting entries; describe weighting selection as general guidance.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Channel width … 2 to 20 km/s”; beam dimensions and range | The cited excerpts do not supply the listed channel widths or most beam dimensions. The channel range also excludes its own 20.1 km/s example. | Retain only dimensions and widths explicitly recoverable from this pack; mark remaining values unavailable and recompute ranges.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “R_eq / R_beam … 1.6”; “R_min / R_eq = 0.37” | Neither ratio appears in the 2025MNRAS.541.2540Z pack entry. The 0.30″ × 0.16″ beam attributed to 2024ApJ...975..179C is likewise absent; only its 0.12″ sphere-of-influence radius is supplied. | Remove unsupported ratios and beam dimensions while retaining supported qualitative resolution statements.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “power law … CASA 5.1.1”; “continuum self-calibration applied to the line SPW” | The pack does not establish that CASA version or the self-calibration transfer procedure. The 2020ApJ...892...68N card flags the exact imaging CASA version and continuum-fitting details as unstated; 2025ApJ...992..211N does not specify transfer to the line SPW. | Remove these paper-specific assertions or label the procedures as general recommendations requiring dataset-specific verification.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “central-image non-detection … gives a lower limit … 2017ApJ...843L..35W” | That pack entry describes lens fitting and priors but supplies no BH lower-limit result. | Cite only the two pack entries that actually report limits; describe the 2017 paper as lens modelling without asserting an established limit.
- SR-07 | high | science | “not resolving the sphere of influence … turns M_BH into a quantity set by … M/L rather than by the data” | This makes prior domination automatic. The 2020MNRAS.496.4061D pack reports an unresolved Keplerian rise and mass-model dependence, but also states that no-BH and overly massive-BH models fail. | State that insufficient resolution increases degeneracy and model dependence; assess information gain and prior sensitivity before classifying a result as prior-dominated or an upper limit.
- SR-08 | high | science | “error bars … too small unless the noise is rescaled” | Scalar inflation is presented as the necessary remedy for correlated pixels. The pack instead includes analytic covariance treatment in 2018MNRAS.473.3818D and explicitly notes neglected correlations in 2020MNRAS.496.4061D. Inflation does not reconstruct covariance. | Present covariance modelling and empirical inflation as distinct approaches; explain their limitations and avoid applying both corrections automatically.
- SR-09 | medium | science | “68% and 99% (or 3 sigma)” | A 99% interval is not a Gaussian 3σ interval. The pack distinguishes 68/99% in 2018MNRAS.473.3818D and 2020MNRAS.496.4061D from 68/99.7% in 2024ApJ...975..179C. | Preserve each paper’s stated probability level; specify credible versus confidence intervals and avoid equating 99% with 3σ.
- SR-10 | medium | omission | “M_BH > 3e8 Msun”; “log M_BH >~ 8.5 preferred” | The lens limits omit decisive model conditions: 2015PASJ...67...72T fixes the core radius to 150 mas; 2015ApJ...811..115W associates the preferred bound with a 0.15″ core. The former explicitly flags BH–core-radius degeneracy. | Attach core-model assumptions to each limit and warn that central-image non-detection does not yield a model-independent BH bound.
- SR-11 | medium | omission | “Many galaxies, need a fast estimate … CNNs” | The pack’s 2025A&A...693A.311P limitation is “One network per galaxy”; the skill retains simulation limitations but omits this restriction. | State that each galaxy requires its own training setup; do not present the demonstrated method as a transferable estimator for arbitrary galaxies.
- SR-12 | medium | omission | “estimate time for S/N = 5 … with the ALMA Observing Tool” | The cited planning calculation assumes homogeneous gas within the central beam and a target beam approximately equal to θ_SoI (2024MNRAS.527.9343L, step 7). Neither condition accompanies the recommendation. | Include both assumptions and require a central surface-brightness estimate at the proposed resolution.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “Gas mass … include or test” | The skill omits the explicit warning that gas-mass systematic uncertainty is likely underestimated in 2024ApJ...975..179C. A single included/tested gas mass does not establish a sufficient uncertainty budget. | Require sensitivity tests over plausible gas-mass distributions and conversion uncertainties; retain the paper’s warning.
- SR-14 | medium | usability | “Noise rescaling … (2N)^(1/4)” | The table leaves N undefined. The pack uses N_dof in 2019ApJ...883..193N and detected-emission pixel counts in 2025MNRAS.541.2540Z, despite fitting the full unmasked cube. | Define the counting convention for each example and require an explicit selection mask/count; do not substitute total cube size automatically.
- SR-15 | medium | usability | “Fit region … 0.25″ radius to 1.55″ box” | This mixes radii and full widths and excludes the 1.5″ semi-major-axis ellipse in 2024ApJ...975..179C. The pack also includes a preliminary 4″ × 4″ fit in 2020ApJ...892...68N. | Separate final and preliminary regions; specify shape and whether each dimension is a radius, semi-axis or full width.
- SR-16 | medium | usability | “theta_SoI = G M_BH / (sigma_*^2 D)”; “M_dyn = 1.16e5 v_circ^2 D” | The angular expression returns radians with consistent length units, whereas the threshold is in arcseconds. The mass expression requires velocity in km/s and physical diameter in kpc; D elsewhere denotes galaxy distance. | Give explicit units and the radians-to-arcseconds conversion; rename the dynamical-mass diameter variable.

## Notes
The nine full-cube-fit papers and named software aggregate counts are supported.
Much of the modelling workflow and several systematic checks are accurately reproduced.
Unsupported observing settings should not become literature-derived defaults.
Absence from this pack means unverified here, not necessarily false in the full paper.
