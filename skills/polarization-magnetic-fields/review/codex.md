- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “robust 0.5 … 16 papers”; “PI … range … 3–5 sigma”; “DCF correction … 2” | These counts and ranges do not represent the current pack. - SR-05 | medium | attribution | “2018ApJ...864...81O … fraction only above 3 sigma_PI … V … not characterized” | The pack supports robust 0.5, σ_PI and ignoring V for this paper, but does not supply those additional explanations. The explicit >3σ_PI fraction recipe and “not … well characterized” quotation occur in **2019ApJ...886..103O**. Similarly, the pack gives only an unspecified “3 sigma cutoff” for 2025ApJ...992..103Z, not the asserted separate Q/U cut. | Correct the O-paper attribution; qualify or remove the unsupported Z-paper threshold interpretation.

- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “repeat with 3C 279 agrees within 0.5%”; “absolute … 0.4 deg” | For 2016ApJ...824..132N, the pack supports the EVPA accuracy but does not provide the repeated-calibrator agreement or full calibration sequence. - SR-07 | medium | attribution | “27–1310 klambda”; “7.9, 5.3, 3.5%”; “5 ± 4 deg … 1.7x” | These values are absent from the supplied entries for 2018A&A...616A..56A and 2020ApJ...893...54Y. - SR-08 | medium | attribution | “card … xi=0.5”; “beam three times larger”; “B_t/B_0 = 0.062 ± 0.025” | The supplied pack does not contain the cited 2019A&A...626A..36D section-4.5.1 card details or 2019ApJ...879...25K smoothing/results card. - SR-09 | medium | attribution | “joint fit V_j = …”; “Zeeman interpretation rejected … ~10 G” | The supplied 2021ApJ...908..141H entries give channelization, blending selections, fixed geometry and confidence conventions, but not the stated fitting equation, shared nuisance coefficients or Keplerian-shifting procedure. - SR-10 | medium | attribution | “a_max ~150 um … ~40–300”; “Stokes V rms 25.2 uJy”; “0.27 ± 0.04% … detection” | These numbers are not supplied for 2016ApJ...831L..12K or 2019ApJ...884L..58U. - SR-11 | high | science | “The deciding test is wavelength … often to azimuthal at longer wavelength” | Wavelength dependence is not decisive by itself, and the wording suggests that longer-wavelength azimuthal polarization identifies scattering. The pack’s 2024MNRAS.528..843L instead describes mixed scattering and aligned-grain emission, weak scattering-spectrum dependence and grain-regime-dependent flips. | Describe wavelength dependence as one constraint in joint mechanism modelling; allow mixtures and optical-depth, grain-property and resolution degeneracies.

- SR-12 | medium | science | “A 1% disk signal … is well above it; off-axis or <0.3% signals are not” | This turns approximate instrumental performance into a universal acceptance/rejection boundary. 2020PASP..132i4501H reports position-dependent errors and mosaic comparisons; 2026ApJ...996L..27L treats a 0.2% measurement cautiously rather than automatically rejecting it. | Require dataset-specific thermal and systematic error budgets and spatial validation. Cite the mosaic study and define the inner-third boundary explicitly relative to primary-beam FWHM.

- SR-13 | medium | science | “debias by Monte Carlo over Q, U instead”; “none if >3 sigma” | Drawing noisy Q/U values and computing their magnitude does not itself specify a debiasing estimator. The blanket >3σ exemption is also unsupported. 2024MNRAS.528..843L explicitly uses a PDF estimator below 5σ and the square-root approximation above it. | Specify the low-S/N likelihood or PDF estimator and its reported interval; distinguish Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation from bias correction. Remove the blanket exemption.

- SR-14 | medium | usability | “PI detection threshold (on PI or P/sigma_P)” | The skill defines P as fractional polarization, whereas several source cards use P for polarized intensity. Fractional-polarization S/N includes I uncertainty and is not interchangeable with PI/σ_PI. The XX/YY lower-limit expression also omits the absolute value on its right-hand side and does not identify the feed-frame projection. | Use PI for polarized intensity and p for fraction consistently; state each cut’s actual variable. Write the dual-hand lower limit with an absolute value and define the polarization frame and correlation normalization.

- SR-15 | medium | omission | “derive … fraction … PI/I” | The filtering warning covers inflated fractions but misses the opposite bias from line-contaminated Stokes I. 2019A&A...630A..54B explicitly reports contaminated I as an upper limit and polarization fractions as lower limits; 2023Natur.623..705S requires consistent spatial sampling of I and polarization. | Add continuum-channel validation and require matched beam, registration and spatial transfer for I/Q/U before forming fractions.

- SR-16 | high | omission | “Goldreich-Kylafis … ties the angle to the field” | This does not explain the **parallel/perpendicular 90° ambiguity**. 2026ApJ...996L..27L explicitly requires radiative-transfer analysis to resolve it and identifies cospatial dust/line tracing as an assumption. | State the ambiguity explicitly; require modelling or justified independent orientation evidence before selecting a line-derived B direction or combining dust and line angles.

- SR-17 | medium | omission | “DCF … optional, order of magnitude” | Missing applicability checks remain consequential despite this qualification. 2026A&A...713A.149V states a small-dispersion reliability regime of σθ <25°; 2021ApJ...907...94C flags finite-resolution and line-of-sight smoothing; 2024ApJ...974..257Z removes angle-measurement variance and channel broadening. | Add checks for intrinsic angle dispersion, beam/LOS averaging, resolved independent samples, instrumental linewidth and non-turbulent motions. Nyquist samples must not be counted as independent beams.

- SR-18 | medium | omission | “Rotate … when … magnetic field … and … optically thin” | Thinness and magnetic alignment do not establish the emission’s polarization orientation without grain-shape and optical-regime assumptions. 2024MNRAS.528..843L explicitly describes a 90° thermal-polarization flip in the Mie regime. | Require the adopted grain model to establish the E-to-B relationship; flag possible Mie-regime flips before applying the standard rotation.

- SR-19 | medium | omission | “Self-scattering a_max … ~100–150 um” | The modelling guidance omits a central limitation of 2023Natur.623..705S: size, composition, albedo, porosity, alignment efficiency and shape are degenerate, and ~100 μm grains are not unique and underpredict longer-wavelength intensity. | Label these as conditional model examples and require joint Stokes I/Q/U and wavelength validation before interpreting a fitted size physically.

- SR-20 | medium | omission | “For rotation measure … fit EVPA versus lambda²” | The RM guidance omits frame conversion, limited wavelength leverage and the inability to infer B uniquely from RM. 2015Sci...348..311M gives RM_rest=(1+z)²RM_obs and the B–electron-density–path-length degeneracy; 2023A&A...672A..67A shows poor RM precision from only ~2° rotation across a band. | State the RM frame and wavelength units, assess rotation leverage against systematic EVPA errors, and require independent density/path-length constraints for field-strength inference.
