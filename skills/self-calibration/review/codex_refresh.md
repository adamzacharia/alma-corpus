- RR-04 | medium | attribution | “ALMAGAL … `minsnr=2.5`”; “rms … >7.5%”; “81% … 66% … 45%” | The supplied rebuilt pack lists 2025A&A...696A.150S bibliographically but supplies no settings or excerpt supporting these values, its cleaning ladder, or its `findcont` limitation. - RR-05 | medium | attribution | “17% to 0.6%”; “components >500 mJy”; “>250 klambda” | The displayed 2015ApJ...808L...1A entry supports approximately tenfold rms improvement, not the sidelobe percentages. - RR-06 | medium | broken-edit | “phase-only only to 3 phase + 2 amp+phase” | This purported mode-sequence range conflicts with the skill’s own seven-phase-round example and the pack’s 2026A&A...705A..96M extended-data sequence: seven phase iterations followed by amplitude trials. | Replace the apparent upper bound with illustrative sequences, or define and recompute separate phase and amplitude round ranges.
- RR-07 | medium | broken-edit | 2026A&A...712A.244H: “p (`int`) x2, ap” | The pack describes “two iterations of one round of phase-only … followed by one round of phase and amplitude,” suggesting repeated phase/amplitude pairs rather than two phase rounds followed by one amplitude round. | Preserve the repeated-pair wording; mark the sequence ambiguous if the truncated card cannot establish its exact ordering.
- RR-08 | medium | unsupported | “a whole-observation solution needs … `'field'` for mosaics” | This makes field combination an unconditional mosaic instruction. The pack also documents separate per-pointing self-calibration in 2020PASP..132i4501H; mosaic status alone does not establish that fields should share a solution. | Make field combination conditional on the model, field selection and justification for shared gains; retain per-pointing solving as an alternative.
- RR-09 | high | science | “solved amplitude gains per polarization with `gaintype='T'`” | CASA `T` solves a polarization-independent antenna gain, whereas separate receptor gains require a different solve. The supplied 2020ApJ...895...64L entries establish “gain mode T,” fixed cross-hand phase and a reported solution count, not this interpretation. | Explain `T` correctly and remove “per polarization” as a solver description. Distinguish the paper’s solution accounting from CASA gain semantics.
- RR-10 | medium | science | “`combine='spws,scans'`” | The reference table reproduces non-executable plural tokens from 2026MNRAS.552g1700S. The pack’s explicit CASA example, 2023PSJ.....4..206R, uses `combine='scan,spw'`. | Normalize executable examples to `combine='scan,spw'`; preserve the paper/card spelling only as a clearly labelled quotation.
- RR-11 | medium | usability | “`tclean` with `savemodel='modelcolumn'` … or `startmodel`” | These parameters serve different purposes. The 2025PSJ.....6..183C excerpt identifies a disk as a `startmodel` for `tclean`; supplying that image does not itself establish that model visibilities were saved for `gaincal`. | Specify the starting image separately, then require saving and checking the model visibilities consumed by the gain solver.
- RR-12 | medium | science | “phase-only self-cal should raise the peak modestly” | “Modestly” is an unjustified acceptance expectation: the pack’s 2014ApJ...791L...6W example reports peak S/N increasing from 150 to 1320. Improvement magnitude alone cannot distinguish successful calibration from noise fitting. | Remove the magnitude expectation; assess flux consistency, model robustness, gain quality and comparable imaging/noise measurements.
- RR-13 | medium | omission | “Successful recovery … injected synthetic transient” | The skill retains the positive validation from 2022A&A...666A..39M but omits its explicit warning that snapshot residuals are dynamic-range limited and non-Gaussian, invalidating a simple Gaussian false-positive interpretation. | Add that limitation beside the injection check and require empirical residual-noise or false-positive validation for transient searches.
