# Codex review: alma-evolved-star-envelopes
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “papers repeatedly lose 25% or more” | Only 2017A&A...597L...5B quantifies approximately 25%; the other cited entries report qualitative filtering. Only IW Car and the Red Rectangle explicitly report model overprediction of extended emission. | Attribute the percentage to IW Car and describe the other losses qualitatively.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “first-order polynomial”; “two rounds … solint ‘inf’ then ‘int’” | The pack does not supply the first-order fit for 2017A&A...597A..99W. The 2024A&A...686A.274V “not stated” entry explicitly lists exact self-calibration intervals and `uvcontsub` fit order as missing. | Remove these asserted paper settings; retain them as unknown.
- SR-03 | high | attribution | “1D … RT on CO, grid + chi2 or manual fit | 3” | For 2018A&A...612L..11S, the pack describes chemistry-derived carbon abundances supplied to CI radiative transfer, with Mdot adopted; CO lines provide validation. It does not describe deriving Mdot through a CO fitting grid. | Count the two documented CO mass-loss fits separately and give the CI chemistry/RT workflow its own row.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Wright & Barlow / Panagia & Felli | 3” | The 2017MNRAS.464L..75F entry attributes its calculation to the Dougherty emission model, without identifying either listed formula. | Count two explicitly documented Wright–Barlow applications and identify Wd1-9 separately.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Mass-loss rates found or adopted (AGB/YHG) … 8” | At least nine cool-wind papers supply inferred or adopted gas Mdot values when 2024A&A...691A..57M’s adopted 1.1e-6 is included; 2015MNRAS.453.4324M adds expected values if those are included. | Enumerate the counted bibcodes and define whether simulation inputs and evolutionary expectations qualify.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Mass-loss rates found (hot massive stars) … about 2e-5 to 6.4e-5” | The 2019A&A...632A..38A validation excerpt reports WR S at Mdot√f_cl = 6.3 ± 1.2e-6 Msun/yr, below this purported range. | Label these as selected means/spherical examples, or include the lower individual result with its clumping convention.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “every named tool appears in exactly one paper” | The aggregate counts distinct software strings. Its two Schöier–Olofsson/Bernes descriptions identify the Monte Carlo code family used by 2016A&A...586A...5M and 2017A&A...597A..99W. | Distinguish string counts from counts of canonical software packages.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “natural”; “Briggs robust 0.5”; “iterative masks”; “Steer-Dewdney-Ito CLEAN” | These paper-specific imaging prescriptions are absent from the supplied pack. The 2018A&A...615A..28D excerpt mentions a natural beam for stellar subtraction, which does not establish all the claimed cube weighting choices. | Remove the unsupported attributions or supply verified excerpts; label independent imaging advice as general practice.
- SR-09 | high | unsupported | “TP map as the initial clean model”; “UVSHORT … after continuum subtraction”; “CASA imsmooth” | The pack mentions feather scaling and UVSHORT merging scaling, but does not document these complete workflows. For 2019A&A...629A.146V it explicitly leaves the continuum-subtraction method unstated. R Scl’s documented convolution concerns model spectra, not an `imsmooth` cube comparison. | Separate documented operations from proposed workflows and specify the latter as general recommendations.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “SHAPE + SHAPEMOL (public)”; “CASA (5.7.2)”; “used simobserve with the real configurations” | These exact software/version/use claims are not supplied. The 2020A&A...633A..13D missing-settings entry names SIMALMA, not the asserted `simobserve` recipe; 2017A&A...605A..28D supplies no simulator recipe. | Remove unsupported versions, release status and simulator attributions; retain instrument simulation as an explicitly added recommendation.
- SR-11 | high | unsupported | “19.5 arcsec shell”; “shell mass 2.5e-3 to 4.75e-3”; “f(12CO) = 4.3e-4 (card)” | None of the detailed shell extraction, smoothing, grid, FWHM, observational-error or CO-abundance values claimed for 2016A&A...586A...5M appears in this pack. Its excerpt supports shell-model subtraction and subsequent degenerate SD fitting. | Delete these numerical recipes and the unsupported abundance entry unless their evidence is added.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “shells at about 150, 315, 420 and 560 R* … coincide with dust shells” | The 2019A&A...629A.146V pack entry provides abundance profiles and molecular disappearance radii, but not these shell peaks, dust coincidences or recurrence times. | Remove these claims or supply their supporting excerpts.
- SR-13 | high | science | “n_H2 = Mdot / (4 pi r² v_exp m_H2)” | This converts total gas mass into H2 number density only for pure H2. It also becomes a strict 1/r² law only at constant velocity; the cited R Scl entry explicitly includes acceleration from 3 to 14.3 km/s. | Use n_H2(r) = Mdot/[4πr²v(r)μ_H2 m_H], define the helium convention, and distinguish accelerating from constant-speed models.
- SR-14 | high | science | “Compare outflow momentum with L/c” | Momentum and L/c have different dimensions. The 2017A&A...605A..28D pack uses this shorthand, but the operational instruction needs a timescale. | Compare P/t with L/c, compare P with Lt/c, or calculate the driving time Pc/L.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “CO-based Mdot scales roughly as d²” | This is not a general scaling for CO radiative-transfer mass-loss estimates. Even an optically thin mass scaling as d² gives an age-averaged Mdot scaling as d when the measured angular extent sets an age proportional to d. | State the distance dependence for the actual estimator or refit at changed distance.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “Dust temperature | single T”; “valid only if optically thin” | Optical thinness alone does not justify a single-temperature mass conversion. The 2018A&A...615A..28D entry supplies temperature ranges and explicitly subtracts stellar emission. The formula also requires an explicit opacity mass convention. | Define S_nu as dust-only flux, κ_nu per unit dust mass, and the assumed temperature distribution or effective temperature.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Magnetic field morphology from line polarisation” | The skill omits 2024A&A...686A.274V’s explicit warning that extended-line circular polarisation is dominated by beam effects and cannot test anisotropic resonant scattering. | Add this limitation and require instrumental-effect checks before interpreting Stokes V.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “radio versus ALMA flux for clumping” | The 2019A&A...632A..38A method assumes constant Mdot when deriving clumping ratios. The pack also identifies differing beams/nebular contamination for RMC 127 and an assumption of no binary contribution for the Westerlund 1 census. | Require those assumptions and flux-comparability checks before interpreting ratios or deriving wind Mdot.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “Kinematic age t = r / v_exp” | The skill omits the explicit age qualifications: uncertain uniform radial velocities for blobs in 2017ApJ...835L..13S and poorly determined inclination in 2017A&A...597L...5B. | Require physical, deprojected radii and velocities; distinguish ballistic ages from acceleration-dependent travel times and propagate geometric uncertainty.
- SR-20 | medium | usability | “Mdot 8 ± 1.5e-5”; “mean WR Mdot 3.6 ± 0.4e-5” | The parameter table leaves the power of ten attached only to the uncertainty. Similar ambiguity affects the WC and RMC 127 entries. The Westerlund 1 mean also omits the pack’s exclusions of WR S and Wd1-13. | Write `(8 ± 1.5) × 10^-5`, apply equivalent formatting throughout, and identify the excluded objects.

## Notes
Many central results and limitations are represented accurately.
The principal problem is treating unprovided “card” details as verified pack evidence.
An absent setting is unverified here; that does not establish that the paper used a different setting.
