# Codex review: source-extraction-counts
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “factors 2.4 and 3.1 from 870 um to 1.2 and 1.3 mm” | The direction is reversed: 2015ApJ...807..128S, step 6, converts faint millimetre counts into its 870-um comparison. | State S870 = 2.4 S1.2mm and S870 = 3.1 S1.3mm; divide for the reverse conversions.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “Peak fluxes of marginally resolved sources may be low by about 20%” | The limitation in 2020A&A...643A..53F explicitly concerns **unresolved** sources. Its validation reports comparing injected and recovered fluxes, not the claimed agreement within errors. | Preserve “unresolved”; restrict the agreement claim to 2016ApJ...833...68A and describe the separate bias found in 2020A&A...643A..53F.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Errors: Poisson with Gehrels … (…2025ApJS..278...45F)” | In that card, Gehrels errors belong to step 8, the **infrared luminosity function**, not continuum number counts. | Remove this citation from count-error practice or explicitly label it as an IRLF example.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “both a natural-weight and a tapered map, with separate thresholds … 2018A&A...620A.152F” | That card documents BLOBCAT extraction on one 0.60-arcsec mosaic with one peak/flood threshold pair. Separate natural/tapered detection thresholds are not established there. | Cite only 2020ApJ...897...91G and 2025ApJS..278...45F for this claim.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “SEAC seed 5 sigma, flood 3 sigma” | 2019A&A...632A..38A describes extraction from **ATCA** observations compared with earlier ALMA data; its detection-image table incorrectly suggests an ATCA/ALMA extraction setup. | Label this as an ATCA methodological example, not demonstrated ALMA extraction practice.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | Injection-recovery flux range “0.03–2 mJy (P(D) grid)” | For 2020ApJ...897...91G, this range is recorded under P(D) forward modelling, separately from the 20,000-source completeness experiment. | Mark the completeness injection flux range as not stated; retain 0.03–2 mJy only in the P(D) settings.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Injected sources … 100 to 2 × 10^4” | The table mixes source totals with numbers of simulated maps. Moreover, 2018A&A...620A.152F injects 300 sources × 100 runs = 30,000 sources **per flux**, exceeding the stated maximum. | Separate sources per realization, realizations per flux/map, and total injections; recalculate ranges within each category.
- SR-08 | low | attribution | “CASA (tclean, simobserve, imfit, uvmodelfit) \| 11, 2, 4, 2” | The aggregate assigns 11 to generic `casa`; `tclean` and `casa tclean` each have separate counts of 1. The presentation can assign 11 to tclean. | Preserve the aggregate’s exact software labels and counts.
- SR-09 | low | attribution | Purity range “0.5 to 0.97” | The pack reports **>0.97** for 2025ApJS..278...45F and **98%** for 2018ApJ...860..161S. | Include 98% and preserve the inequality; distinguish per-S/N fidelity from catalog purity.
- SR-10 | high | science | “(1 − f_contam) / (C(S) A_eff(S)) … A_survey = sum C_i A_i” | Completeness is counted twice if the completeness-weighted area from 2015A&A...584A..78C is substituted for A_eff. That card explicitly uses (1 − f_c)/A_survey. | Define geometric detection area and completeness-weighted area separately; use either P/(C A_geom) under its stated assumptions or P/A_survey.
- SR-11 | high | science | “differential n(S) and cumulative N(>S) in deg^-2”; source weights contributed “to its bin” | Differential counts require a flux-bin normalization. 2014ApJ...795....5O, step 3, explicitly divides by ΔlogS and reports counts per logarithmic interval. | Define dN/dS in flux^-1 deg^-2 and dN/dlog10S in deg^-2 dex^-1; give the appropriate bin-width division. Cumulative counts require neither division.
- SR-12 | high | science | “N(>S) = (N0/S0) […]^-1” | Although reproduced from the aggregate, this cannot serve as a shared cumulative model with the supplied normalizations. The pack gives differential N0 in mJy^-1 deg^-2 for 2020ApJ...897...91G, but N0 in deg^-2 for 2015ApJ...807..128S. | Specify each paper’s differential or cumulative function and normalization units. Obtain cumulative counts by integrating a differential model; do not transfer parameters between conventions.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “Noise estimates seen: the 68th percentile of pixel values” | The quoted 2018ApJ...869...71Z wording is insufficient for implementation: for signed zero-mean Gaussian pixels, the 68th percentile is approximately 0.47σ, not σ. | Flag the original estimator’s definition as needing clarification. Give a clearly labelled general alternative, such as (q84 − q16)/2, with source masking.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “map NOT corrected for the primary beam, where noise is flat” | Flat noise is not guaranteed for mosaics, heterogeneous archival coverage or imaging artifacts. The pack itself includes local-rms estimation and sensitivity-dependent survey regions. | Build and validate a local noise map for the actual detection product; treat flat noise as a checked special case.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “Search radius \| PB FWHM”; “PB response 0.1–0.2” | FWHM is a full width, so using it directly as a radius is ambiguous. Also, 2025ApJS..278...45F specifies **20% relative sensitivity**, not explicitly PB response 0.2. | Define radius, diameter and response/sensitivity quantities separately; retain the paper’s original quantity and provide an explicit search mask.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “choosing a detection threshold without … the inverted map” is a principal mistake | The pack also supports simulation-based calibration, independent-beam estimates and SPFA. Negative-map calibration additionally requires an appropriate noise-symmetry assumption; 2016ApJ...833...67W explicitly assumes no genuine negative absorption. | Require validated false-positive calibration, with inversion as one method. State and check the assumptions before treating negative peaks as noise.
- SR-17 | medium | science | “Convert other surveys … S proportional to nu^(2+beta)” | The 2018ApJ...869...71Z scaling is presented without its optically thin, Rayleigh–Jeans applicability conditions. Applied indiscriminately across wavelengths and redshifts, it can bias comparisons. | Check rest-frame frequencies and dust-temperature/opacity assumptions; use a full SED where the approximation fails.
- SR-18 | medium | science | “Targeted fields are not blank fields … Exclude the target and its surroundings” | Masking a central region does not remove the targeting-selection bias identified for counts below the parent LESS limit in 2013MNRAS.432....2K. The pack’s 2-arcsec exclusion example does not establish such a correction. | Require a selection-function treatment or restrict the claimed counts to a demonstrably valid selection regime; describe masking as an additional measure.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “Overdensity tests” | The skill omits decisive interpretation limits: 2018ApJ...867..153C reports incompleteness, unknown redshifts and foreground contamination; 2018PASJ...70...65U says protocluster association is unconfirmed. | Distinguish angular excess from physical association, state redshift information, and compare against a null model passed through the actual spatial selection and extraction procedure.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “Are Poisson … flux-resampling and cosmic-variance terms all in the error bars?” | Reporting flux-scale uncertainty elsewhere does not ensure it enters the analysis. The pack explicitly flags an omitted 20% absolute-flux uncertainty in 2017ApJ...835...98U. | Include or separately quantify correlated flux-calibration systematics in fluxes, count bins and fitted parameters.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “EBL: integrate the corrected counts and compare with the … background” | The skill omits the explicit limitation in 2015A&A...584A..78C that CIB uncertainties prevent a precise resolved fraction. | Propagate background-reference uncertainty and state integration limits and extrapolated contributions separately.
- SR-22 | medium | usability | “both a natural-weight and a tapered map, with separate thresholds” | No rule combines overlapping detections into one catalog. Independent catalogs can double-count sources, while selecting either detection changes the joint completeness and contamination. The pack’s DUALZ result counts 65 Wide sources plus only four additional Deep sources. | Define deduplication, flux-selection and catalog-union rules; measure recovery and false positives for that complete procedure.

## Notes

The extraction examples are useful, but heterogeneous survey conventions have become interchangeable defaults.
The most consequential fixes are wavelength-conversion direction, completeness/area bookkeeping, and differential versus cumulative count definitions.
Some ambiguities originate in the pack itself; flag them explicitly instead of turning them into executable prescriptions.
