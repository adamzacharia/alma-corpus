# Evidence pack (workflow): Finding, choosing and combining ALMA archive data (`archival-data-reuse`)

Scope: reusing archive data: products used as delivered versus recalibrated, older cycles, several projects and arrays combined, calibration provenance, flux-scale consistency, data volume

Built from 1764 non-held-out ALMA papers whose methods cards passed mechanical evidence verification (quoted strings are verbatim from the paper). Years 2012-2026.
Aggregates come from card fields the technique packs do not show. Lines marked (card) are the card author's inference about the paper, not the paper's own words.
