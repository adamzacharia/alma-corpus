## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “Several papers do not run a simulation … (2016PASJ...68...43K)” | That paper’s pack excerpt explicitly includes new 2D FARGO simulations over a parameter grid; parameters.md also incorrectly classifies it under “no new simulation.” | Distinguish its simulation-calibration step from its subsequent scaling-relation application.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “tested only seven orbits” | The pack’s 2020MNRAS.499.3837G summary is ambiguous, but its quoted caveat concerns the “small number of orbital configurations tested”; the validation quote describes evolution over 10 orbits. | Say seven orbital configurations were tested; do not interpret this as simulation duration.
- SR-03 | high | attribution | “gap width … 4 R_Hill (2018ApJ...869...17L)” | The cited excerpt says “gap radius corresponds to 4 R_Hill,” not full gap width. Interchanging radius and full width changes the inferred Hill-based mass cubically. | Preserve the paper’s gap-radius convention and explicitly distinguish it from full-width prescriptions in Step 1.
- SR-04 | high | attribution | “no magnetic fields or non-ideal MHD … (2025ApJ...984L..21B)” | The pack includes MRI models and identifies the omitted physics specifically as Hall effect and ambipolar diffusion. | Attribute absence of magnetic fields to 2020ApJ...898...10T; list the two omitted non-ideal effects separately for 2025ApJ...984L..21B.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Papers that skipped the simulator … (2017ApJ...839L..24M)” | That entry states “different uv coverage used,” which does not establish that no simulator was used. The corresponding Pitfalls sentence repeats this overstatement. | Describe mismatched uv coverage for this paper; reserve “no ALMA simulator” for 2024A&A...691A..57M.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “alpha_SS in SPH disks … 3e-3 to 5e-3” | The pack explicitly gives alpha_SS approximately 1e-3 for the PHANTOM simulation in 2019NatAs...3.1109P. | Extend the reported range to approximately 1e-3–5e-3.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “SPH particles … 2.5e5 to 5e6” | 2025ApJ...984L..15P starts with 1e6 particles, then splits each into 13 before the final two orbits. | Label the range as initial particle counts or include the final 1.3e7-particle stage.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “Viscosity alpha … 1e-3 in 11” | Ten distinct bibcodes explicitly give this value in the supplied pack. Repeated key-parameter, assumption and excerpt entries do not establish eleven papers; “count of pack lines” also conflicts with the table’s “Papers” heading. | Recount using distinct bibcodes, provide the contributing list, and distinguish paper counts from repeated line occurrences.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “Most papers judge by eye … 17 of the 40 excerpts” | Seventeen of forty is not a majority, and selected excerpts cannot establish the proportion across all 52 retained papers. | Say qualitative or visual comparison is frequent in the excerpted sample; remove “most.”
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “Planet accretion / sink radius … Common choice: 0.125 Hill radius … 2” | Only 2025ApJ...984L..15P supplies that planetary value. The other cited check concerns a stellar accretion radius of 1 au in 2020ApJ...888L...4T. | Separate stellar and planetary sink radii and present 0.125 Hill radius as one paper’s setup, without a shared default.
- SR-11 | high | science | “CO abundance … 12CO/H2 = 1e-4” | The table merges incompatible abundance conventions: 2025ApJ...984L..15P specifies 1e-4 relative to H, whereas 2019A&A...625A.118K and 2025ApJ...984L..21B specify relative to H2. | Record the denominator per paper and convert explicitly before configuring RT; preserve this distinction in the main table as parameters.md already does.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “These need alpha, h/r, temperature and an opacity” | This blanket requirement incorrectly applies gas-disk inputs to every listed estimator. The quoted Hill prescription uses gap size, orbital radius, stellar mass and a coefficient; the debris-disk chaotic-zone route is a different physical regime. | Give method-specific inputs and applicability conditions rather than one universal input list.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “velocity residuals (2024MNRAS.534.2904C)” | The skill recommends residual comparisons but omits that paper’s explicit warning that residuals and Doppler-flips depend on the assumed Keplerian background. | Require the background model to be documented and its uncertainty or plausible alternatives tested before interpreting a residual as a companion signature.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “disk misalignment … (2020ApJ...895L..18B)” | The pack explicitly warns that dust alone cannot determine mutual angular-momentum inclinations because orbital directions are unknown. | Add this ambiguity and require gas-kinematic or orbital-direction constraints before claiming a unique mutual inclination.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “channel-map kinks … (2025ApJ...984L..15P)” | The skill retains subjectivity but drops the paper’s explicit inclination bias in kinematic-structure classification. | State that classification and detectability depend on viewing inclination; do not treat absence or class frequencies as directly comparable across geometries.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Report … synthetic-observation settings” | The pack specifically flags missing rest frequency/systemic velocity, wake projection, extraction settings, and imaging masks/thresholds. Broad reporting language does not make these actionable, especially for kink amplitudes. | Add a concrete checklist covering spectral reference/frame, geometry and emission surface, extraction/masking, imaging parameters, and noise inputs.
