# Codex review: sed-fitting
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “100 walkers x 2500 iterations (2020A&A...633A.160C)” for dust MCMC | That paper’s step 2 explicitly assigns these settings to the **UV-continuum slope**; step 4 scales a dust SED with fixed emissivity. | Remove this example from both dust-MCMC tables and the emcee dust-fitting attribution.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “assume a fixed AGN share (25% in SCUBA bands, 2020MNRAS.493.3744F)” | Step 2 apportions blended SCUBA emission between the QSO and companion; its assumption explicitly says dust is heated by star formation. This is not a 25% AGN-heating correction. | Move this example to source deblending and distinguish source allocation from AGN decomposition.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “bounds beta<3, T<100 K … (2014A&A...568A..92M)” | The pack specifies “T<100 K observed-frame.” Dropping the frame changes the prior. | Preserve the observed-frame qualifier and explicitly transform it when fitting rest-frame temperature.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “scipy curve_fit with covariance errors” as an MBB example | 2021A&A...648A..41V explicitly fits a **simple blackbody rather than greybody**, excluding high-resolution ALMA fluxes. | Label the model and excluded data accurately; retain it only as a least-squares software example.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | MCMC examples introduced by “when T and beta are both free” | The pack fixes beta=1.8 for 2017ApJ...839...58S. Its sampler settings do not establish a joint free-T/free-beta fit. | Separate sampler examples by actual free parameters.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | Fixed-pair table: “z > 5 quasar hosts and galaxies” | The listed 2024ApJ...977..198L studies systems at z≈2; the “alternative for the same” citation 2017MNRAS.465.4390B concerns quasars at z≈2.5, per the pack’s paper titles. | Broaden the context to the supported populations or move these examples to separate rows.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | Opacity table: “0.077 … at 850 um” and “0.15 … at 850 um” | The pack gives **870 um** for 2015ApJ...811..124S and 2014MNRAS.438.1267S, respectively. Consequently, “7 use 0.077” at 850 um includes a mismatched reference wavelength; six listed papers support 850 um explicitly. | Preserve each reference wavelength or convert using a stated beta; recount the exact-wavelength category.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “kappa … range 0.048–0.15 m2/kg at 850 um”; “Gas-to-dust … 60–200” | The supplied table itself includes 0.383 cm2/g = **0.0383 m2/kg** at 850 um (2017ApJ...851...98T). The pack also includes gas-to-dust ≈30 (2020A&A...641A.155V; 2019ApJ...877L..38R). | Correct the observed ranges or explicitly define narrower selection criteria.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | Software counts: “hyperz … 3,” “MCFOST … 5,” “curve_fit, MPFIT … 2, 2” | The software aggregate gives **4, 6, 3, 3**, respectively. Moreover, 2024A&A...690A..55S uses MPFIT for Gaussian lines, so aggregate mentions are not counts of MBB fits. | Correct counts and distinguish software mentions from technique-specific uses.
- SR-10 | low | attribution | Flux-scale variants “sum to 33” | The listed variants sum to **12+9+4+4+2=31**. CASA’s exact “casa version” label is 33; combining its variants produces a different total and does not establish unique-paper counts. | Report 31 listed occurrences and retain explicit counting definitions.
- SR-11 | medium | attribution | “optically thick fits give L_IR about 0.1 dex higher” | 2017ApJ...844..123F’s validation supports a ≈0.1 dex difference between alternatives; the supplied excerpt does not establish the claimed thick-minus-thin direction. | Report the difference without assigning a direction unsupported by the pack.
- SR-12 | medium | attribution | “Error floors … 0.1 dex on MAGPHYS outputs … or 35% on templates” | 2020ApJ...901...79A adds uncertainty to **derived outputs**. In 2016A&A...596A..75S, 35% belongs to the lens-model contribution used during deblending. Neither is a generic photometric calibration floor. | Separate measurement calibration, deblending/model uncertainty, and output systematics.
- SR-13 | medium | unsupported | “Most ALMA papers have one to three far-IR points”; optically thin MBB “Most common at high z” | Neither prevalence claim is quantified in the pack; its regex-selected sample also includes unrelated SED techniques. | Remove the frequency claims or present them as unquantified context.
- SR-14 | medium | unsupported | “Prior-based deblending can bias against sources faint in the prior band (2014MNRAS.438.1267S)” | No supplied entry for this bibcode supports that limitation. Likewise, the claimed beta bias from a small beam is not supplied for 2016ApJ...833...68A. | Label these as general cautions without these citations, or supply supporting evidence.
- SR-15 | medium | unsupported | “differential magnification is usually ignored”; several attributed limitations | The supplied entries do not support this prevalence claim or its cited admissions (2014ApJ...785..149S; 2016ApJ...822...80S). They also do not supply the claimed warm-component admission (2016MNRAS.463...10M), factor-two opacity uncertainty (2014ApJ...791..108F), or excluded-temperature errors (2013ApJ...770...13W). | Remove unsupported paper-specific attributions; retain scientifically sound cautions explicitly as general practice.
- SR-16 | medium | unsupported | “Bethermin 2015/2017 (2016MNRAS.461.2944A …)” | That bibcode’s supplied entries establish IMF, redshift and ≈0.3 dex luminosity uncertainty, not a Bethermin template attribution. | Remove this example; retain the explicitly supported Bethermin2017 examples.

- SR-17 | high | science | “SFR = 1.40e-10 L_IR” attributed to 2024ApJ...962....1S | The excerpt uses **L′_IR**, with AGN removed. Its total L_IR=1.34e13 Lsun and f_AGN=0.59 yield ≈770 Msun/yr only after multiplying by 1−f_AGN; the written equation applied to total luminosity gives ≈1876. | Define L_IR,SF explicitly in every calibration and require the appropriate component luminosity; state luminosity and SFR units.
- SR-18 | high | science | “M_dust … from the MBB normalization” using the opacity table | The table mixes dust-mass opacities with an opacity explicitly including gas-to-dust=100 (2018MNRAS.475..448T). Inserting the latter into the stated dust-mass formula changes the mass interpretation by roughly 100. | Add a mass-basis column and require consistency between opacity denominator and fitted mass; prevent a second gas-to-dust multiplication.
- SR-19 | medium | science | “One or two points cannot constrain T_dust” | The cited 2015ApJ...807..180W limitation concerns **one** point. Two suitably placed bands can constrain temperature with beta fixed; indeed 2017ApJ...840..101C fixes temperature only for fewer than two FIR points. | Make identifiability depend on wavelength coverage, free parameters and degeneracy, rather than a categorical two-point prohibition.
- SR-20 | high | science | CMB correction and universal “T_d = 25–50 K” sensitivity grid | The flux equation requires the actual dust temperature at z, but heating versus contrast treatment is unspecified. At z≳8.2, 25 K is below T_CMB≈2.73(1+z), producing negative contrast if used directly. The pack distinguishes contrast-only treatment (2018MNRAS.481.1631B) from heating plus contrast (2020MNRAS.493.4294B). | Define T_CMB(z), intrinsic versus heated temperature, and consistent heating/contrast treatment; enforce physical temperatures and avoid double correction.
- SR-21 | medium | science | “include a mid-IR power law or drop rest <50 um points” as the remedy for missing warm dust | Excluding those points can protect a cold-component fit, but does not restore missing warm emission when integrating 8–1000 um. The pack explicitly records an MBB luminosity underestimate (2017ApJ...844L..10S). | Distinguish cold-component fitting from total-IR inference; require an appropriate warm/template component or explicit extrapolation uncertainty.
- SR-22 | medium | science | Single-band validation restricted to “25–50 K and beta=1.5–2.0” | This is inadequate for the skill’s quasar scope: the pack includes 50–100 K for W2246 (2018Sci...362.1034D), a 60–90 K prior (2017ApJ...847...21F), and 80 K (2025ApJ...990...29H). | Choose sensitivity ranges by source population and redshift; describe them as conditional tests, not exhaustive uncertainty bounds.

- SR-23 | medium | omission | Photometry construction lacks a spatial-consistency check | The pack flags differing source/beam sizes without correction (2015ApJ...801...82H), requires common solid angle (2017ApJ...840...72L), and excludes ALMA flux where spatial filtering matters (2018MNRAS.475..448T; 2021A&A...648A..41V). Total flux alone does not ensure comparable emission. | Require matched physical apertures or justified component models, compatible spatial recovery, and documented exclusions.
- SR-24 | medium | omission | No explicit line-contamination or primary-beam validation | Numerous “not stated” entries flag line-free channel selection and primary-beam correction; 2024ApJ...962....1S has almost no line-free USB channels, and 2015ApJ...801...82H warns about unreliable off-primary-beam fluxes. | Record channel selection and continuum estimation, test line contamination, and verify primary-beam correction and local uncertainty before fitting.
- SR-25 | medium | omission | Removing non-star-formation heating discusses AGN but omits old stars | 2019MNRAS.486..560S explicitly neglects old stellar heating and therefore treats inferred SFRs as conservative upper values. | Include cirrus/old stellar heating in the luminosity-to-SFR assessment, especially for quiescent or weakly star-forming systems.
- SR-26 | medium | omission | AGN/template subtraction lacks its own uncertainty check | 2017ApJ...844..123F gives at least **0.13 dex** uncertainty from QSO-template dispersion; 2018ApJ...859...35G warns that obscuration can hide AGN and bias decomposition. | Propagate subtraction uncertainty and test plausible buried-AGN alternatives; do not treat a fitted AGN fraction as complete removal.
- SR-27 | medium | omission | MCMC verification requests settings and contours but no convergence assessment | Fixed walker/iteration counts do not establish convergence. The pack explicitly includes convergence testing and visual chain inspection in 2024ApJ...962..116E, although that application is outside dust fitting. | Add convergence, effective-sample and prior-boundary checks as general statistical practice, without attributing that paper’s sampler setup to dust fitting.

- SR-28 | medium | usability | “L_IR in both 8–1000 um and 42.5–122.5 um” | Earlier definitions correctly call the second integral **L_FIR**. Reusing L_IR invites exactly the convention mismatch the skill warns against; the pack also labels 8–1000 um as L_FIR in 2016MNRAS.463...10M. | Report separately named integrals with explicit rest-frame limits; preserve original paper terminology only alongside those limits.

## Notes
The workflow is useful, but several citations transfer settings between different fitted components or physical meanings.
Treat aggregate mentions as mentions, and distinguish published assumptions from recommended defaults.
The optically thin flux equation and 0.077 m2/kg = 0.77 cm2/g conversion are correct.
