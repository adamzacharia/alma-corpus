# Codex review: archival-data-reuse
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | unsupported | “across the whole corpus about 38%…1,346 of 3,537” | The pack supplies 1,764 member papers; neither 3,537 nor the whole-corpus count is supplied. “Most ALMA papers” also generalises from this selected subset. | Remove the whole-corpus statistic and restrict prevalence claims to pack members.
- SR-02 | low | attribution | “66 archive maps from 18 projects” | 2016ApJS..222....1F states 4 authors’ maps, 62 archival field maps, and an archival cluster map; its band counts total 67 maps. | Say “66 field maps, including 62 archival maps, plus one archival cluster map.”
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “37 ALMA and VLA datasets over 11 years” | 2026MNRAS.551g1411H gives 2012.27–2023.62 for ALMA, but 1989.15–2023.62 overall. Eleven years describes the ALMA subset. | Distinguish the ALMA time span from the full ALMA/VLA baseline.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “5% for Bands 3–6 (2021A&A...648A..33M)” | That entry specifies Bands **3, 4 and 6**, not Band 5. The checklist also gives competing 5% and 10% Band 6 fallbacks without a decision rule. | List the supported bands explicitly; separate paper-specific assumptions from one consistent fallback.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Use a larger value (15–20%) for combined early-cycle data” | 2023A&A...674A.125B supports 15% for multi-configuration data observed in 2018–2021, not early-cycle data. 2014ApJ...796L..15S supports one Cycle 0 adoption of 20%. | Present these as distinct examples, not a general early-cycle prescription.
- SR-06 | medium | unsupported | “the rest used observatory calibration” | A negative regex classification does not establish this. The delivered-products list includes unknown calibration routes (2024MNRAS.532.4645H), unspecified details (2016A&A...589A..20V), and non-ALMA products (2024A&A...682L...3P). | Remove the remainder inference; distinguish explicit calibration routes, image reuse and unknowns.
- SR-07 | medium | science | “Recalibrate…[for] a weighting or imaging choice” | 2020MNRAS.496.1834L says under-cleaned images were **re-imaged**; 2018ApJ...854...83T reports reprocessing with natural weighting. Neither establishes a need to redo calibration. | Route these cases to imaging calibrated visibilities; reserve recalibration for calibration defects.
- SR-08 | high | science | “Before combining anything…recompute weights (`statwt`)” | This mandates a modification where inspection may justify retaining weights: 2020A&A...634L..12D kept defaults after checking them. Scatter-based estimates can also include line emission or source structure rather than noise. | Inspect first; reweight only when justified, using suitable noise selections and documented settings, then validate the resulting weights and sensitivity.
- SR-09 | medium | science | “put all data on one frequency frame and common bandwidth” | This makes a particular line-combination procedure universal. 2025A&A...699A..80H retains separate SPWs and lets `tclean` handle combination; 2019A&A...629A..29J describes a specific binning/trimming case. | Require compatible spectral interpretation; regrid or trim only where the intended product requires it. Preserve useful continuum bandwidth.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “Defaults keep separate fields and SPWs” | The pack reports that outcome for 2025A&A...699A..80H, not an unconditional property of `concat` defaults. | State the paper-specific outcome and require checking which fields/SPWs the chosen tolerances actually merge.
- SR-11 | high | science | “align positions (proper motion)” and “Self-calibrate each dataset first” | The skill explicitly includes proper-motion studies, but unrestricted alignment can erase their signal; self-calibration also requires protecting the astrometric reference. 2026MNRAS.551g1411H uses distinct epochs to measure motion. | Add an astrometry branch: preserve phase-referenced epoch positions, document self-calibration’s positional reference, and shift only copies intended for coaddition.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “combined flux within the quadrature sum of the flux-scale uncertainties” | The combination shares calibration errors with its inputs, so these comparisons are correlated. Different spatial filtering can also change recovered flux: 2016A&A...586A..70L reports a 5% change after matching uv coverage. | Compare equivalent spatial/spectral measurements and include statistical noise and calibration covariance in a stated acceptance tolerance.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “pipeline flags remove the lines…absorption channels re-flagged and recalibrated” | The distinction between calibration masks and final science flags is missing. 2024ApJ...969..102N masks absorption channels during calibration alongside genuinely contaminated channels; 2016ApJ...821L...8S unflags science emission. | Specify which channels are excluded from calibration solutions, which valid science channels are restored, and which atmospheric/interference flags remain.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “Never measure from an archive image before checking it” | The checks omit residual-flux scaling and release-mask conventions. The pack identifies JvM-corrected MAPS images (2023A&A...674A.113I), uncorrected CRISTAL products (2025A&A...699A..80H), and strict-mask PHANGS moment maps (2024A&A...691A.351R). | Check and record residual scaling/JvM status and mask definitions before comparing or combining image-derived fluxes.
- SR-15 | low | usability | “Pick the lowest product level that works” | This reverses the checklist’s “highest-level product” instruction and can send an agent toward raw data. | Use “Pick the highest-level product that answers the question.”
