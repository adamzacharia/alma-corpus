## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “H13CN/H13CO+ (> 3 in the NGC 1068 torus)” | 2020ApJ...902...99I reports an absorption-signal ratio >1.8; >3 is the inferred abundance ratio after B-coefficient and excitation corrections. | Distinguish the measured absorption ratio from the corrected abundance constraint.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “12C/13C for opacity … 18-100 … 2014A&A...570A..28V (18, 24)” | That entry gives adopted H12CN/H13CN and H12CO+/H13CO+ molecular ratios, respectively; it does not identify both as elemental 12C/13C values. | Separate molecular isotopologue ratios from elemental isotope abundances.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “column ratio 10 across the CND” | 2014A&A...570A..28V explicitly allows 100 at the E Knot if Set 7 is favoured; the available column-density sets are also non-exhaustive. | Include the exception and identify these as model-dependent results.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “5% B3-5 … 50% on high-z ratios” | 2023ApJ...950...75I supports 5% for Band 5, not Bands 3–4. The 50% in 2023A&A...679A.119R includes calibration uncertainties and potential spatial offsets, rather than a per-band flux-scale error. | Restrict the supported band statement and separate calibration errors from composite ratio uncertainties.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Fiducial N_HCO+, [HCN]/[HCO+] … 1e16 cm^-2, 3 … 3 [papers]” | 2018ApJ...856..143I assumes 1e16 cm^-2 for each species, implying an abundance ratio of 1 in that calculation. It does not support the listed fiducial pair. | Attribute the pair to the two 2023 papers; list the 2018 equal-column assumption separately.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “>= 4 in >= 3 channels to >= 2” | 2023MNRAS.521.3348N requires at least three adjacent channels at both the high and low thresholds. | Restore the three-channel requirement for the low-S/N mask.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Channel width … 6 (my count)”; “T_kin … 5-1000” | At least seven galaxy papers have explicit channel widths in the pack: 2024A&A...691A.121N, 2024A&A...689A.122B, 2024ApJ...967..133S, 2024ApJ...963..115L, 2022MNRAS.510.4485R, 2024A&A...689A.163L and 2026ApJ..1004...39I. The temperature range excludes 10^0.5 K in 2017ApJ...850..170O. | Recount using an explicit inclusion rule; extend the temperature range to approximately 3.16 K or label it a selected subset.

- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “most other work (starbursts, AGN hosts, mergers, high z) uses 10” | Eleven explicit adoptions do not establish prevalence across these populations in the 180-paper corpus. The pack also contains nuclear factors of 6–12 and an assumption tying alpha_HCN to variable alpha_CO. | Say “11 pack entries explicitly adopt 10”; present population-specific choices as examples rather than a demonstrated majority.

- SR-09 | high | science | “alpha_HCN = 14 and alpha_CO = 4.35, f_dense ~ 2.3 HCN/CO for CO(1-0)” | The stated formula gives 14/4.35 ≈ 3.22. The pack itself juxtaposes these constants with 2.3, while separately giving a CO(2-1)/(1-0) ratio of 0.7 for 2018ApJ...868L..38G. | Flag the pack inconsistency. Use 3.22 for HCN(1-0)/CO(1-0); approximately 2.25 applies to HCN(1-0)/CO(2-1) with R21 = 0.7.
- SR-10 | high | science | “close to … 12C/13C … HCN is optically thin”; “main-line ratio understates the column ratio” | These conclusions require excitation, filling-factor and isotopic-abundance assumptions. Differential opacity can suppress either species. 2018ApJ...856..143I estimates relative attenuation for both species; 2020ApJ...902...99I applies spectroscopic and excitation corrections to the rare-species absorption ratio. | State the assumptions, evaluate both main/rare ratios, and derive corrected columns rather than equating raw line ratios with abundance ratios.
- SR-11 | high | science | “I_source = ((theta_s^2 + theta_b^2) / theta_b^2) I_beam” | This faithfully reproduces 2022A&A...667A.131B, but with the stated source/beam definitions it is not the usual Gaussian beam-dilution correction: it fails the extended-source limit. Calling it an assumption does not resolve the formula problem. | Flag the source expression for verification. For Gaussian source and beam FWHMs, use the factor (theta_s² + theta_b²)/theta_s² under the corresponding brightness convention.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “Add these in quadrature to every cross-band ratio” | Quadrature assumes independent calibration errors. Shared observations can have correlated errors, and Step 7 already represents calibration through nuisance factors. 2023ApJ...950...75I uses bounded scaling factors, rather than requiring both treatments. | Propagate covariance where applicable and avoid counting the same calibration uncertainty both in ratio errors and nuisance parameters.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “If low-J and high-J ratios cannot be fitted together, the gas is multi-component” | 2013PASJ...65..100I motivates restricting its fit to high-J ratios; it does not establish this as a unique diagnosis. The pack also identifies beam, filtering, opacity and excitation-model limitations. | Say that multiple components are a possible explanation; check observational and model inconsistencies before concluding.
- SR-14 | high | science | “When reproducing, assume the archive pipeline defaults … check them against reported noise” | The missing-setting cards do not establish which pipeline, version or manual processing was used. For example, 2024A&A...691A.121N leaves combination details unspecified, and 2020ApJ...902...99I leaves calibration reuse unspecified. Matching noise cannot recover these choices. | Retrieve scripts, logs and inherited methods; label unrecovered settings as assumptions and test sensitivity rather than claiming reproduction.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “N(HCO+) = 1.10e12 cm^-2 x integral tau dv” | The cited absorption conversion applies to the J=1-0 line in the limit of negligible excitation above the CMB, as stated in 2018A&A...610A..49L and 2025A&A...699A..17L. The routing row covers absorption more broadly. | Specify J=1-0, dv in km/s and the excitation assumption; route other transitions or warm absorbers to the appropriate column-density calculation.

- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Default mask … a CO-based velocity mask” | The skill omits the prerequisite that dense-tracer emission occupies the CO velocity range, explicitly assumed in 2024A&A...691A.121N and 2023MNRAS.521.3348N. This matters especially for the advertised outflow applications. | Require inspection of tracer spectra and residual emission outside the CO mask; document any excluded wings or displaced components.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Match beam, uv coverage and grid” | 2024A&A...691A.121N also homogenises the spectral axis. The skill does not explicitly require compatible spectral resolution, velocity convention/frame and integration windows when comparing cubes or channel ratios. | Add these checks before masking, stacking or dividing.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “SFR/HCN … IR-based SFR” | Relevant SFR biases are missing: 2023ApJ...942...25F checks multiple tracers and applies a sample-specific TIR correction; 2024A&A...686A..48N removes the AGN contribution from IR-derived SFR. The Halpha calibration in 2024A&A...691A.121N also assumes a specified star formation history and IMF. | Require validation of AGN contamination, stellar-population heating, extinction and calibration assumptions before interpreting SFR/HCN.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “HCN/HNC … X_HCN/X_HNC > 4 flagged regions” | 2024ApJ...977...38B additionally rejects solutions with abundances below 1e-12 and examines temperature-boundary behaviour and detected excitation ladders. A ratio-only check can miss failures affecting both species. | Add individual-abundance, prior-boundary and transition-coverage checks when using that chemical framework.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “HCN-vib detection or limits; CON criterion …” | The table risks treating CON classification as a decisive pumping test. 2025ApJ...985..259J explicitly tests aperture dependence and warns that radii below 5 pc conflict with the criterion definition; 2021A&A...649A.105F also requires a sufficient mid-IR power source. | Separate pumping assessment from CON classification; report aperture, size assumptions and whether the limits meaningfully constrain the proposed pumping scenario.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “Low-metallicity clumps: LTE masses … report the Tex sensitivity” | 2020ApJ...902..140N also warns that small beam filling factors can underestimate optical depth and LTE mass. Temperature sensitivity alone does not bound this error. | Include filling-factor and opacity sensitivity, and identify Tex as assumed because only one transition was available.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “systematic budget (flux scale per band, missing flux, conversion factor)” | Primary-beam treatment is absent despite being flagged in the missing-setting aggregates. 2017ApJ...843..136E reports materially different CO fluxes before and after primary-beam correction. | Require consistent primary-beam correction, an overlap footprint and spatially appropriate noise estimates for ratio maps.

- SR-23 | medium | usability | “beam-spaced grid so each pixel is an independent sightline” | 2024A&A...691A.121N presents independence as an assumption of its sampling scheme. Beam spacing does not automatically eliminate covariance introduced by imaging, smoothing or reprojection. | Describe these as approximately independent samples and assess covariance or effective sample size for uncertainty estimates.
- SR-24 | medium | usability | “r_5,1 = 0.25”; “ratio … differs … by (nu1/nu2)^2” | The conversion direction is not explicit. This is consequential because the 2026MNRAS.549ag931B excerpt itself prints a mass expression multiplying by r, while standard high-J/1-0 notation requires division. | Define r_J1 = L′_J/L′_10 and M = alpha L′_J/r_J1; explicitly write R_K = R_Jy × (nu2/nu1)² for matched beams.
- SR-25 | low | usability | “HCN-vib limits address IR pumping (Step 8)” | In the agent checklist, item 8 concerns upper limits; the intended diagnostic discussion is Steps §8. | Use an unambiguous section reference.

## Notes

The skill usefully emphasises matched filtering, selection bias and competing ratio interpretations.
Several important errors arise from conflating measured ratios, inferred abundances and adopted assumptions.
Most software aggregates and the regrouped missing-setting counts match the pack.
No held-out-target contamination found; the CosmicCoder lesson is appropriately labelled team experience.
