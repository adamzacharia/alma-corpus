## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “all 21 tagged papers (casa 6 … emcee 3)” | The supplied pack contains 14 papers and reports casa 3 and emcee 1. It provides no earlier 21-paper aggregate or matcher history. | Replace these counts with the supplied aggregates; remove the unsupported earlier-pack narrative.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “Relativistic correction … 6”; “Free SZ parameters … 4” | Seven entries specify relativistic treatment: 2021A&A...650A.153D, 2024A&A...689A..41V, 2020A&A...638A..70D, 2019A&A...628A.100D, 2024A&A...682A.186L, 2020ApJ...891...90R and 2020PASJ...72...33K. At least five specify mass-based free SZ parameters, including 2019A&A...629A.104G. The ≥3 detection threshold and uniform Mach 1–10 prior each have only one explicit supporting paper. | Define what each count measures and enumerate its supporting bibcodes; distinguish use of a method from support for a particular default.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “dynamic: 2019A&A...628A.100D; 2024A&A...682A.186L” | The Spiderweb entry specifies nested sampling with dynesty, without identifying static or dynamic sampling. | Attribute dynamic sampling only to the Bullet paper; leave the Spiderweb variant unspecified.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Spherical gNFW pressure … (… 2019A&A...628A.100D)” | The Bullet entry specifies a power-law downstream region, gNFW upstream region and shock geometry, rather than the spherical gNFW model described in this bullet. | Separate the shared projection/uv-sampling procedure from each paper’s pressure geometry.
- SR-05 | medium | unsupported | “compromise between integration time and dust”; “nine known sources … brightest first”; “−190 ± 36 microJy, 5.3 sigma” | These details are absent from the supplied 2019A&A...629A.104G entry. Its missing-settings card explicitly flags source-grouping details as unstated. Calling them “methods card” does not supply evidence. | Remove these details or provide supporting pack material; retain the supported 92 GHz observations and pressure-model comparison.
- SR-06 | medium | unsupported | “uvmodelfit … longer than 30 klambda”; “tclean multi-scale … natural weighting”; “half-split difference maps”; “simobserve mocks” | The supplied Phoenix entry supports division by 0.94 and subtraction at 42 arcsec, but does not establish these other settings or the simulation provenance. Its card mentions uvmodelfit without supplying the baseline cut. | Remove unsupported specifics and mark unavailable settings as requiring recovery from the source methods.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “almost every paper fits a pressure model directly to the visibilities”; “ACA plus ALMA 12-m together is the norm” | The pack includes image-based analysis, stacking, binned uv-profile comparisons, MUSTANG-2 fitting and proposed analyses. It supplies no prevalence calculation supporting these generalizations. Band 4 use is supported, but its stated purpose “to separate SZ from compact sources” is not explicitly established for both cited papers. | Describe visibility forward modelling as a supported approach, name alternatives, and avoid unsubstantiated prevalence or observing-purpose claims.
- SR-08 | medium | science | “sample it only on scales below their largest recoverable scale”; “Total cluster Y or masses … from ALMA alone … cannot constrain these scales” | Recoverability is not a sharp angular cutoff, and whether r500 is constrained depends on target angular size and uv coverage. The cited limitations concern particular datasets: 30–100 arcsec coverage in 2020EPJWC.22800009E and temperature beyond 250 kpc in Phoenix. | Require target-specific transfer-function and recovery tests; distinguish measured scales from model-dependent extrapolation.
- SR-09 | medium | omission | “Mach number from the pressure jump, optionally with an X-ray prior” | The El Gordo pack says upstream temperature is unconstrained and is inferred through a Mach-dependent Rankine–Hugoniot chain using downstream temperature. The Bullet question and evidence comparison explicitly concern electron–ion equilibration. | Require disclosure of temperature-prior construction and electron–ion equilibration assumptions; propagate them into Mach inference.
- SR-10 | medium | omission | “Temperature: kT_e = P_e / n_e”; “pseudo-temperature map” | The pack flags spherical symmetry for 2020ApJ...891...90R, unconstrained central diffuse emission within 2 arcsec and metallicity uncertainty for Spiderweb, and arbitrary X-ray pseudo-pressure normalization for Phoenix. These limits are missing from the thermodynamic instructions. | Require compatible deprojection geometry and annular averaging, identify unconstrained central regions, propagate density/model uncertainties, and distinguish relative pseudo-temperature structure from calibrated temperature.
- SR-11 | medium | omission | “Build the pressure model”; “Settings cards flag … State all of these” | Important flagged items remain absent: frequency integration across spectral windows, line-channel exclusions, external-map beams and transfer functions, and calibrated measurement-set identifiers. The 2024A&A...689A..41V entry also assumes independent ACT and ALMA+ACA likelihoods and warns that gNFW is poorly suited to filaments. | Add these requirements and explicitly test the joint-likelihood independence assumption and pressure-model suitability.
- SR-12 | medium | omission | “break the thermal/kinetic degeneracy with a prior” | The 2021A&A...650A.153D entry says the prior may only reduce the degeneracy and that the best-fit velocity is driven by the posterior’s high-velocity tail. | Say “reduce”; require inspection of posterior tails, prior sensitivity and comparison with thermal-only masses before interpreting velocities.
- SR-13 | high | unsupported | “HE0515-4414”; repeated citations and numerical examples from 2019MNRAS.490.5134B | WRITER CONTEXT explicitly prohibits this held-out target, its paper and its numbers. The skill names it in parameters.md and carries its track/pair counts, correction factor, parameter count and degrees of freedom elsewhere. The pack itself includes this prohibited paper despite claiming a non-held-out corpus. | Remove the target, bibcode and all identifiable examples/results from both files and source lists. Retain only general recommendations independently supported by allowed papers; rebuild affected counts.
