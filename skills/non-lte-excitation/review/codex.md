## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “N/dv (CO) … fixed 1e14 … 2021A&A...645A..45C” | That paper fixes N/dv for HCN, HCO+ and HNC, not CO. The row also mixes column-density and column-per-linewidth examples, making its count of 8 unreproducible. | Separate CO from dense-gas tracers and N from N/dv; recount with explicit bibcode lists.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “H2 ortho/para … 21 mention OPR (some are molecule OPR)” | Molecular spin ratios do not establish collider H2 OPR practice. In 2026A&A...707A.120H, OPR=3 explicitly concerns H2CO and CH2CO. | Give separate entries and counts for collider H2 OPR, molecular OPR and A/E ratios.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Calibration error … 5–20%”; “15% in 2014ApJ...785..149S” | The pack calls the latter an added uncertainty, without identifying it as calibration. For 2019ApJ...872..200H, 20% is a ratio error, not an identified calibration uncertainty. | Preserve those distinctions; remove these values from calibration-specific ranges/counts unless supported.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “K_vir >= 1 … 2014ApJ...785..149S” | Its pack entry says “virialized K_vir,” while its not-stated card explicitly lists the K_vir cutoff as missing. Only 2019MNRAS.486.2366M explicitly supports >=1 here. | Cite the numerical cutoff only to the latter; label the former cutoff unspecified.
- SR-05 | low | attribution | “0.1 dex steps … 2016ApJ...821..112K” | That paper uses 10 K temperature steps; 0.1 dex applies to N_CO. | Identify which axis has each spacing.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “LIME … used to check RADEX-level answers … 1.3 in 2020ApJ...892...99C” | The pack compares an LTE cube model with LIME non-LTE flux, not a RADEX solution. Its emcee fit is also an LTE cube fit. | Describe this as an LTE/non-LTE validation and distinguish it from sampling RADEX grids.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “predicted tau 0.2–0.5 in 2020MNRAS.498L..87B” | These opacities belong to methyl formate and ethanol in the rotational-diagram stage, not the methanol LVG fit. | Name the species and analysis stage so agents cannot infer that the fitted methanol lines are thin.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “Predict a line not used in the fit, e.g. [CI] … 2019MNRAS.486.2366M” | The pack records a predicted-versus-observed [CI] comparison, but does not establish that [CI] was withheld; the method is labelled CO+[CI] SLED modelling. | Retain the flux comparison without calling it a held-out prediction.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “One line … plausible density range … factor 2 to 4 … 2015A&A...581A..85P” | That example uses SO ratios and intensities. The quoted factor 2–4 is at fixed n=3e5 cm-3 as temperature varies from 500 to 50 K. | Present the proposed one-line sensitivity analysis as general practice; describe the cited experiment accurately.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “most papers … log-spaced … about 21 chi2-grid … versus 8 MCMC phrasings” | The fitting aggregate contains seven entries explicitly naming MCMC/emcee, including repeated strategies and an LTE fit. These step-level phrasings establish neither a majority of 154 papers nor predominantly logarithmic grids. | Remove the prevalence claim or supply a defined, deduplicated count and separate evidence for spacing.
- SR-11 | low | attribution | “T_bg … 2.73 to ~11 K”; “MCMC … 20–256 walkers” | The pack includes backgrounds of 10 or 100 K in 2017ApJ...837...60B. The two-free-parameter CH3CN fit in 2026A&A...707A.120H uses eight walkers under its 4× rule. The temperature summary also omits the 6300 K grid in 2018ApJ...868...95M. | Correct the ranges or label them selected examples rather than coverage of values seen.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “For LVG codes set X/(dv/dr) [to] 1e-5” | The pack supports this as an adopted assumption in two cited studies, not a general LVG setup requirement. Likewise, “physically allowed solutions only” overstates the universality of the listed priors. | Require an object-specific choice or fitted range; label abundance-gradient and virial restrictions as conditional modelling assumptions.
- SR-13 | high | science | “collision cross-section 5e-14 cm-2” | Cross-sections have dimensions of area, cm². Both comet entries inherit a dimensional error present in the pack. For 2025ApJ...986L..22R, the pack specifically identifies an H2CO–CO thermalization approximation. | Correct to 5e-14 cm², identify the collision partners, and distinguish the thermalization estimate from transfer-model inputs.
- SR-14 | high | science | “T_bg = 2.73 K locally” | This is insufficient as an unconditional setup for the advertised radiation-dominated cases. The Fomalhaut model explicitly includes CMB, stellar and dust radiation; 2025A&A...696A.100G includes dust continuum excitation. | Specify the CMB contribution, then assess additional frequency-dependent radiation and pumping; use a suitable transfer model when necessary.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “Only one transition … Report an LTE column with a stated T_ex range” | A temperature range does not validate LTE. The pack includes single-transition excitation uncertainty spanning two orders of magnitude in mass (2019AJ....157..117M) and explicit radiation-/collision-dependent mass limits in 2015MNRAS.447.3936M. | Label LTE estimates conditional and provide excitation-dependent bounds when LTE cannot be justified.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Measure intensities on matched data” | Matching beams and recovered scales does not address continuum blocking, oversubtraction or line absorption. These are explicit limitations in 2025A&A...693A.101T and 2015ApJ...801...63M. | Check continuum opacity, subtraction bias and absorption before treating a flux deficit as weak excitation.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “compute chi2”; “Add flux calibration error in quadrature” | The skill omits correlated measurements. 2016MNRAS.460.2933M accounts for independent beams and adjacent-channel correlation; 2018ApJ...864..133T uses correlated-noise modelling and warns about correlated annuli and beam smear. | Specify covariance or an appropriate noise model, shared calibration terms and shared-line ratio correlations; assess beam smear in resolved fits.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “diffuse absorbing gas where T_ex sits near T_CMB” | The workflow provides no absorption-specific treatment. The pack flags assumed covering factor unity and non-simultaneous variability in 2020A&A...637A...7M, and one-sided density/column constraints in 2014ApJ...795...28B. | Add an opacity/absorption branch with covering factor, line-of-sight continuum, variability checks and appropriate bounds.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “alpha_CO(1-0) 0.2 to 0.5”; “Derive the science quantity” | The NGC 1614 result explicitly scales as 3e-4/[CO]/[H2]. The pack identifies x_CO as the largest alpha_CO uncertainty in 2017ApJ...840....8S, but the skill only asks agents to state an abundance. | Preserve the abundance scaling and propagate or bracket abundance uncertainty; state transition, units and helium convention.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “grid ranges and spacing (or priors and sampler settings)” | Bayesian grids also require explicit priors and integration measures. 2020A&A...643A..89P reweights derived quantities; 2024ApJ...969..143I tests linear versus logarithmic filling-factor priors. | Require priors for grids as well as samplers, document marginalization weights, and test influential prior choices.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “MCMC examples … walkers … steps” | Iteration counts alone do not demonstrate convergence. Convergence diagnostics are explicitly missing in the 2025A&A...696A..31G card, with related initialization/convergence gaps in 2020ApJ...892...99C. | Require demonstrated mixing and adequate effective samples, plus stability checks before reporting posterior intervals.
- SR-22 | medium | usability | “mass M = f Omega N d² m” | The expression does not define whether N is source- or beam-averaged, what Ω covers, or whether m is molecular mass or gas mass per tracer molecule. Applying f to an already beam-averaged column double-counts dilution. The cited 2017MNRAS.469.3347M formula derives molecular masses. | Define every quantity and column convention; separately give the abundance conversion needed for total gas mass.
- SR-23 | medium | usability | “two or more transitions (or a ratio)” | This entry condition can suggest that one ratio identifies T, n, N/dv and filling factor. It does not; common filling factors cancel in ratios, and optically thin ratios cannot determine absolute column. The pack repeatedly documents insufficient constraints, including four CO lines in 2017ApJ...835..174S. | Count independent observables before choosing free parameters; require external constraints or absolute intensities and report unidentifiable quantities as bounds.

## Notes

Most individual grid values and software aggregate counts are transcribed accurately.
The main risks are conflating species and collider parameters, turning conditional assumptions into defaults, and losing the context of validation results.
Mechanical verification of quotations does not establish dimensional correctness or support broader claims drawn from them.
