# Codex review: solar-system-atmospheres
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “Papers rescale … by 0.85–1.15 per spectral window” | Only 2024PSJ.....5..125T supports that range per window. 2019AJ....157..219T gives 1.047; 2016ApJ...821L...8S gives an approximately 10% difference from the Horizons model. | Attribute each value and its scope separately.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “first temperature from a CO line at fixed CO abundance”; “troposphere fixed” | This conflates workflows. 2016ApJ...821L...8S simultaneously retrieves CO and temperature; 2019AJ....157..219T adopts a temperature profile; 2018Icar..307..380T describes tropospheric **a priori** profiles, whereas 2024PSJ.....5..125T explicitly fixes tropospheric temperatures. | Separate these retrieval strategies and distinguish fixed quantities from priors.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “uv-plane continuum subtraction … 2014ApJ...795L..30C” | That entry states continuum subtraction using standard CASA routines, without specifying the domain or task. | Mark the subtraction domain as unspecified for this paper; retain the supported uv-plane examples.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Field-of-view / emission-angle points (disk-averaged) … 6” | The six include 2024PSJ.....5..125T, whose 30 angles are calculated “for each individual spectrum extraction location,” including regional extractions. | Count five explicitly disk-averaged examples and list the regional 30-angle example separately.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “12C/13C … Common choice … 89.8–89.9” | In 2016ApJ...821L...8S, 89.9 ± 3.4 is a retrieved result; its terrestrial prior is 89.3. The fixed conversion ratio in 2019Icar..319..417T is 89.8. | Separate retrieved results, retrieval priors and assumed isotope-conversion ratios.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “D/H in CH4 … Range seen … 1.0–1.4e-4” | This excludes the 1.13–1.59e-4 input range explicitly recorded for 2019AJ....157..219T, despite counting both papers. | Give the ranges separately by paper, or report their combined envelope as 1.0–1.59e-4.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “Self-calibrate on the body itself” | The imperative makes self-calibration a universal step. The pack documents particular applications in 2024PSJ.....5..125T, 2016ApJ...821L...8S and 2019AJ....158...47M; it does not establish suitability for every target or dataset. | Make self-calibration conditional on continuum signal, model quality and verified solution quality.
- SR-08 | high | unsupported | “Always derive and report the per-window scale” | The pack supports particular Titan continuum-normalization workflows, not a universal correction. The rings paper checks measured absolute flux; Neptune normalization is an explicit physical assumption (2019AJ....158...47M; 2019AJ....157..251T). Unconditional normalization can erase the continuum signal being studied. | Restrict model-based rescaling to justified workflows; otherwise retain the calibrated flux and propagate its uncertainty.
- SR-09 | medium | unsupported | “derive the main species from optically thin isotopologues” | 2019Icar..319..417T supports fixed isotope-ratio conversions, but its entry does not establish that these isotopologue lines are optically thin. | Describe the documented conversion and require an optical-depth check before using a thin-line approximation.
- SR-10 | medium | science | “CH3D profile … CH4 times D/H” | The parameters table repeats the pack’s shorthand without the methane statistical factor. For low deuteration, CH3D/CH4 ≈ 4(D/H); a literal abundance conversion without this factor is wrong. | State CH4 ≈ CH3D/[4(D/H)] and distinguish profile-shape initialization from abundance conversion.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “Convert Jy to radiance using many line-of-sight emission angles” | This conflates unit conversion with forward-model angular integration. Jy/beam image values, integrated Jy and radiance require different solid-angle handling; the emission-angle count alone defines none of these. | Specify input/output units and solid-angle normalization; account for beam area when summing Jy/beam pixels, then describe model quadrature separately.
- SR-12 | medium | omission | “Error bars reflect only retrieval errors” | The same limitations entry, 2019AJ....157..219T, also warns that disk-averaged ALMA CH4 is not directly comparable with equatorial Huygens GCMS CH4. This matters because GCMS profiles initialize the workflow. | Add the spatial-representativeness warning to comparisons and inferred D/H interpretations.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “Non-simultaneous lines” | The comet pitfalls omit the second explicit limitation in 2025NatAs...9.1476C: low HDO signal leaves the parent scale length L_p unconstrained for the 50% distributed-source case. | Flag this source-geometry degeneracy and require its effect on inferred production ratios to be assessed.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “radial wedges … for latitude and longitude profiles” | The skill omits 2019AJ....158...76C’s warnings that its ALMA limb retrievals are approximate because of coarse resolution and simplified parameterization, and that inferred lifetimes rely on gross assumptions. The stated CIRS reliability interval does not cover these warnings. | Add both limitations and keep ALMA retrieval limitations distinct from CIRS altitude sensitivity.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “two-parameter gradient model” | 2014ApJ...795L..30C assumes profiles do not vary with latitude or longitude. Neither the skill nor its parameters row records this assumption, despite also discussing spatial maps. | State the horizontal-homogeneity assumption and its limits when interpreting mapped asymmetries.
- SR-16 | medium | usability | “a 1.5'' circular aperture” | 2014ApJ...795L..30C explicitly specifies **diameter**. Both supplied tables/prose leave radius versus diameter ambiguous. | Write “1.5 arcsec diameter, centered on Titan.”
- SR-17 | low | usability | “pixels with >= 90% of continuum flux” | This can be read as a per-pixel brightness threshold or a cumulative flux-enclosure criterion. The 2019AJ....157..219T card explicitly flags the pixel-mask definition as unstated. | Mark the mask construction as unresolved; require verification before implementing either interpretation.

## Notes
The paper selection, software aggregates and most numerical transcriptions are sound.
The main risks are turning particular Titan workflows into universal defaults and mixing results with assumed parameters.
Preserve the distinction between documented methods, incomplete card shorthand and additional recommendations.
