# Codex review: paper-reproduction
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “CASA/pipeline version 367” | The pack’s “Settings needed but not stated” rows mentioning versions sum to 360. The separate “pipeline vs manual calibration: 7” row specifies a calibration route, not a version. | Use 360 and document the grouping rule consistently.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “gas-to-dust 0.01” | The inherited-conventions entry for 2014ApJ...784...62A explicitly says “dust-to-gas 0.01”; the skill reverses the ratio. | Write dust-to-gas = 0.01, equivalently gas-to-dust = 100.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Td = 25 (L/Lsun)^0.25 K” | The pack’s inherited-conventions entry for 2014ApJ...784...62A instead gives an exponent of −1/4. | Flag the transcription conflict for source verification; do not silently attribute the positive exponent to this pack or adopt the negative exponent uncritically.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Models the paper ran: … GLAFIC (2024ApJ...965..108U)” | That excerpt says magnifications were extracted from the earlier catalogue, whose fiducial lens model used GLAFIC. It does not establish that this paper ran GLAFIC. | Classify GLAFIC as provenance of an inherited lens model; distinguish software executed here from software behind adopted products.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Single Cycle 0 executions are already ~10–30 GB … cubes are ~2 GB” | These numbers come from explicitly labelled card estimates for 2014ApJ...791...42Z and 2019MNRAS.485.4359S, not reported file measurements. The checklist drops that qualification. The raw-volume table also includes processed single-dish maps. | Label every reuse as a card estimate and separate raw, calibrated-visibility and image-product volumes.
- SR-06 | medium | unsupported | “When the paper is silent, assume observatory pipeline calibration” | The calibration aggregate supports the listed phrasing counts, not calibration provenance for silent papers. Its two literal manual labels total six, while excerpts also identify manual-style reductions, including 2014ApJ...791...42Z and 2019A&A...623A.153O. | Describe the limited aggregate accurately; resolve provenance from delivery scripts or predecessors, otherwise retain an explicit unknown or exploratory assumption.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “Released code is the exception” | The pack provides a truncated, selected “Code released” list. Counts of “not stated” and “on request” among 150 displayed rows do not establish the release rate across 3225 papers. | Remove the prevalence claim; report only what the displayed rows establish.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “CLEAN threshold, masks … pipeline auto-masking thresholds” | 2023PSJ.....4..206R uses particular compact-12-m continuum pipeline heuristics. Its card still flags exact thresholds and mask edits as missing. This does not supply a universal reconstruction default. | Recover the applicable recipe and configuration; label any substitute masking and stopping settings as assumptions requiring validation.
- SR-09 | high | science | “Absolute flux-scale uncertainty … ~3 mJy … to ~20%” | The pack identifies ~3 mJy as accuracy of the calibrator J0854+2006 in 2025A&A...699L..11V. It is neither dimensionally comparable to percentages nor directly a science-target flux-scale uncertainty. | Separate the calibrator’s absolute uncertainty from fractional scale errors; derive a fractional error only with the relevant calibrator flux and transfer assumptions.
- SR-10 | high | science | “Tolerance … combined in quadrature”; “ratios … do not [get the scale term]” | Reprocessing the same observations gives correlated measurements and potentially shared calibration errors. Independent quadrature can overstate tolerance; blanket cancellation for ratios is also wrong when calibration errors are not fully shared. The verification checklist additionally contradicts the cross-dataset ratio rule. | Use uncertainty on the difference with covariance, propagate scale errors through the derived quantity, and state which calibration components cancel. Make all checklist rules consistent.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “the paper’s error … combined in quadrature” | The pack includes formal 3σ intervals in 2019MNRAS.485.4359S, a 2σ interval in 2019A&A...623A.153O, and a factor-four uncertainty in 2015A&A...576L..15O. These cannot all be inserted as Gaussian 1σ errors. Recording the error type alone does not prevent this. | Specify confidence-level conversion where justified, retain asymmetric or non-Gaussian intervals, and define quantity-specific tolerances when errors are unstated.
- SR-12 | high | science | “add phase-only rounds if your rms is above the paper’s”; “1–4 phase rounds, then amplitude” | A noise mismatch alone does not establish that self-calibration is feasible or appropriate. The cited examples do not share an amplitude step: 2017ApJ...847...92H reports phase-only rounds, and 2023PSJ.....4..206R applies amplitude calibration where phase calibration succeeded. | Make self-calibration conditional on signal strength, model quality and solution diagnostics; verify flux, morphology and astrometry, and accept no improvement as a valid outcome.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “uniform priors over the paper’s stated ranges” | The cited 2014ApJ...791...42Z card explicitly lists both priors and parameter bounds as missing. Uniform priors are an additional modelling choice and depend on parameterization. | Treat priors as unresolved; label exploratory choices and test sensitivity rather than presenting a uniform prior as the reproduction default.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “one verdict per answer-key row” | The workflow lacks an explicit upper-limit/nondetection comparison rule. The 2020MNRAS.499.5915M card flags the unknown multiplier behind <0.240 mJy and its treatment in fitting; 2020ApJS..247...15S flags the missing nondetection recipe. | Record limits separately from detections, including confidence level, assumed linewidth/aperture and fitting treatment; do not grade a limit as an ordinary measured value.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “convolve to the common lowest resolution” | Matching restoring beams and tracking centres does not match spatial filtering. The 2021ApJ...907...80O card explicitly flags common spatial-frequency coverage and ALMA–VLA registration as missing requirements for spectral-index reproduction. | Add checks for registration and compatible recoverable scales/uv coverage before comparing maps or forming ratios and spectral indices.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Match the paper’s software” | Package/version matching omits a consequential inherited statistical recipe: 2019MNRAS.485.4359S explicitly inherits pixel-covariance modelling and log-likelihood rescaling. These affect fitted uncertainties even with identical KinMS software. | Inventory and reproduce the likelihood, correlation treatment and uncertainty rescaling alongside priors and sampler settings.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “revised archive scripts in 2021ApJ...907L..27A” | The archive-products entry says those scripts were modified to remove baselines shorter than 33 m. Omitting that modification makes this example an incomplete processing recipe. | Include the baseline cut and require an inventory of author modifications to delivered scripts.
- SR-18 | medium | usability | “Achieved noise versus theoretical … Default: within ~30%” | The pack assigns ~30% to one survey, 2020ApJS..247...15S. The table’s default column promotes it despite the prose disclaimer. Its “range” also mixes theoretical noise with requested sensitivity: 72 versus 40 μJy/beam in 2019A&A...626A.126A. | Remove this default and separate achieved/theoretical noise from achieved/requested sensitivity.

## Notes
The staged workflow and inheritance inventory are useful, but several defaults could manufacture apparent agreement.
Keep paper statements, card inferences and exploratory recommendations visibly distinct.
No held-out target contamination was identified; the team lessons remain recognizably labelled and general.
