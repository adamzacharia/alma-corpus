- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “phase-and-amplitude self-calibration still ‘worked’ … UVSUB” | The supplied 2018A&A...619A..40M entries state continuous jet flow, model parameters and single-epoch limitations; they do not describe either operation. | Delete these attributions or add the relevant evidence.

- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “shift to a common epoch in GILDAS”; “8 yr JCMT … WISE/NEOWISE” | The supplied entries for 2017ApJ...840...32T, 2024ApJ...961..108K and 2024ApJ...964...48T do not establish these software or light-curve details. - SR-07 | medium | attribution | “two-epoch proper motions … unexplored systematics (2018ApJ...866...87B)” | That bibcode’s supplied limitation concerns unmatched Band 10 scales for CS modelling; it does not state this proper-motion limitation. | Present the configuration concern as a general check, without attributing it to this card.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “Flux-scale uncertainty … 2.5% (relative, gencal)” | 2021A&A...656A..46M identifies 2.5% as scatter in residual scaling factors from a continuum-alignment validation, not a general relative flux uncertainty. - SR-09 | medium | unsupported | “35 … off-topic … remaining 52 … 31 … 13 … 5 … 3” | The pack gives no such classification ledger, and detailed excerpts cover only part of the corpus. Titles alone do not establish all these mutually exclusive activity counts. | Label this as an unverified classification and provide a bibcode-level ledger, or omit the numerical breakdown.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “every paper that claims a change shows … stayed constant” | The pack establishes selected checks, not a universal practice. Moreover, 2015A&A...576A..41B explicitly uses check-source **variability** to estimate uncertainty. | Say that several papers check controls, whose residual variability can set a systematic uncertainty.
- SR-11 | high | science | “Use phase-only, or amplitude with a solution interval much longer than the variability” | A long amplitude solution interval does not guarantee preservation of intrinsic variability or its normalization. The instruction also conflicts with the preceding claim that long-interval amplitude calibration absorbs variability. | Prefer phase-only for flux-variability extraction; require a justified variable-source model and a recovery test before using amplitude self-calibration.
- SR-12 | high | science | “Calibrate every epoch … then phase self-calibration” | Applied to the proper-motion branch, target self-calibration can erase absolute positional information or introduce epoch-dependent registration. The pack’s motion examples explicitly use continuum alignment or reference-relative offsets. | Separate flux and astrometric workflows; preserve externally referenced positions or document and validate relative registration before comparing epochs.
- SR-13 | high | science | “|Q/I| = (XX-YY)/2I” | The right side lacks an absolute value and represents a feed-frame Stokes projection, not generally sky-frame Q. Its normalization also requires an explicit correlation convention. | Define the convention; for \(I=(XX+YY)/2\), use \(|XX-YY|/(XX+YY)=|Q'|/I\), explain parallactic-angle dependence and identify it as a lower bound on total linear polarization.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “Power-spectrum frequency resolution … ~3 d” | 2026ApJ..1004..126K itself uses this wording, but days are not frequency units. Mean epoch spacing describes cadence, while Fourier frequency resolution depends principally on the observing baseline. | Preserve the paper wording only with a caveat; distinguish cadence, frequency resolution and period uncertainty, with correct units.
- SR-15 | high | science | “10^5 simulated light curves”; “FAP … <2.9e-7” | Direct empirical exceedance counting with 10^5 simulations cannot resolve a FAP of 2.9e-7. The supplied pack also does not substantiate that threshold for 2025ApJ...994..256Y. | State simulation-limited FAP resolution; require sufficiently many simulations or a justified, validated tail model for smaller probabilities.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “always simulate the red-noise PSD”; “Red-noise PSD slope … f^-2” | The pack supports f^-2 for 2026ApJ..1003L..17Y, while 2026ApJ..1004..126K uses a constant-flux null with normally distributed per-EB errors. Neither establishes a universal noise model or default slope. | Select and justify the null for the source and sampling; test correlated noise where relevant and treat published slopes as examples.
- SR-17 | medium | science | “Non-detections give 3 sigma limits on emitting-region size” | In 2019ApJ...881...33H, 3 sigma describes the flux limit. The size bound depends on assumed gyrosynchrotron physics, including B=600 G and electron index δ=2; its limitation explicitly says non-detections cannot identify the mechanism. | Describe a conditional model-derived size bound and list its assumptions separately from the flux confidence level.
- SR-18 | medium | science | “Offsets smaller than the beam cannot separate proper motion from calibration” | 2019ApJ...871..100H reports ambiguity for one particular offset, not a universal beam-sized astrometric floor. The pack also gives beam/(S/N) positional errors. | State that sub-beam shifts can be measured, but require positional uncertainties and registration/calibration systematics to establish motion.
- SR-19 | high | omission | “Flux comparison across epochs” | The workflow lacks a general check for matched spatial response. The pack explicitly attributes a flux discrepancy to missing ACA data in 2024RMxAA..60..355R and warns about beam changes/interpolation in 2019ApJ...870...59S. | Require compatible uv coverage, recoverable angular scales, frequency selection, beam and extraction aperture before interpreting flux differences.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “Smoothing splines through Q and U before differentiating” | 2025ApJ...987..152R explicitly splits at gaps ≥1.8 min and trims edge points; both safeguards are omitted. Its smoothing values are discrete tested choices, not established continuous ranges. | Include segmentation and edge trimming; report sensitivity to the stated discrete spline settings.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “Structure function for rotation-measure variability” | The supplied 2018ApJ...868..101B limitation says the largest RMs are suspect because of phase wrapping, poor fits and low-polarization calibration errors. | Add RM fit-quality, angle-wrap and polarization/calibration checks before calculating variability statistics.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “hotspot fit … GR ray-tracing check” | 2025ApJ...994..256Y explicitly lists statistical-only uncertainties, neglected finite hotspot size and temporal evolution, and unconstrained spin. These limitations are missing. | Include these interpretation limits and avoid implying that the unsupported <2% check validates the full physical model.
- SR-23 | medium | omission | “Solar oscillations … per-pixel power spectra” | 2021A&A...652A..92N warns that coarse resolution and correlated pixels make observed p-mode RMS a lower limit. The skill mentions gap corrections but omits this separate limitation. | State the resolution-dependent lower bound and account for pixel correlation when interpreting spatial averages and uncertainties.
- SR-24 | medium | usability | “lag -105 +/- 4 d”; “v_t = 4.74 mu …” | The lag lacks an explicit band-order/sign definition. The nearby-source velocity formula appears in a skill also covering cosmological jets, including the pack’s 3C 273 apparent-speed example. | Define lag ordering and which band leads; restrict the pc-based formula to nearby sources and specify the angular-diameter-distance/redshift treatment for cosmological apparent speeds.
