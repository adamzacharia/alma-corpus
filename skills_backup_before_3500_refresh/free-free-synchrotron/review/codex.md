## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “3% at 33 GHz and 5% at 94.5 GHz”; “85/82/70% to 80/76/62%” | The supplied pack supports alpha_NT = −0.85 and a test at −0.6 for 2015ApJ...813..118M, but supplies neither these numbers nor the claimed 94.5-to-33 GHz convolution. | Remove these details or supply supporting evidence; retain the supported index-sensitivity test.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “6.7 of 9.7 mJy”; “index −0.118”; “72% correction” | These details are absent for 2016ApJ...833L...6C. Its pack entries support Te = 13000 K and negligible synchrotron/dust at 3 mm. | Remove the unsupported numbers and describe any extrapolation as conditional on those assumptions.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “30 Dor agreed to 15%, N159E disagreed by about 4x”; “as … did” | No supplied entry documents these ATCA checks for 2019MNRAS.483.1624B. The pack supplies beta = 1.49, alpha_ff = −0.1 and a two-component assumption. | Keep independent cm validation as a recommendation; remove the claimed historical results.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “M* = Q0/4e46”; “with 36 and 350 GHz only … indistinguishable” | The pack for 2018ApJ...869..126L supports Te = 7000 K, all 36 GHz emission assumed free-free and a ZAMS population, but neither this conversion nor the stated two-band analysis. | Remove the numerical conversion; label the identifiability warning as general reasoning.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “case B and fixed n_e = 1e3 cm^-3” for both Bendo papers | These settings are explicitly supported only for 2016MNRAS.463..252B. The supplied 2015MNRAS.450L..80B entry documents the continuum correction, without those emissivity settings. | Restrict this attribution to the 2016 paper.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “fixed indices, free amplitudes” includes 2016MNRAS.463..252B | Its excerpt explicitly gives a **free synchrotron index** and a temperature-dependent free-free Gaunt factor. The source-card summary also incorrectly calls this “four-component”; the pack names three components. | Separate the fixed-index and free-index approaches; change four components to three.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Free-free fraction at Band 3 (measured)”; “Band 3 in starbursts is 70–84%” | These are source-specific decomposition estimates, including the explicitly non-unique, fixed-index fit in 2017ApJ...849...90S. The pack does not establish a population default. | Say “in these three studied regions” and label the fractions model-dependent estimates, unsuitable as defaults.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “Flux uncertainty floor … 10–20% … 3” | The pack identifies a 10% floor for 2018ApJ...862...94L, 10% assigned errors for two datasets in 2016MNRAS.463..252B, and 20% on one 868 GHz point in 2019ApJ...881L...2B. These are different treatments. | Separate uncertainty floors from dataset-specific assigned errors; remove the three-paper floor count.
- SR-09 | low | attribution | “line-free channels … at least 15” | The corresponding entries under “Settings cards flag as needed but not stated” sum to 13: 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1. They also do not establish unique-paper membership. | Report 13 listed occurrences, or provide a deduplicated paper list supporting another count.
- SR-10 | low | attribution | velocities “within 5 km/s” | The 2018ApJ...864L..17G excerpt explicitly says “except F10.37.” | Preserve that exception.
- SR-11 | medium | science | dust “nu^(2+beta) when optically thin” | Optical thinness alone is insufficient; the Rayleigh–Jeans approximation is also required. The 2018ApJ...853..171G excerpt explicitly qualifies alpha = 3.75 this way. | State the Rayleigh–Jeans condition; otherwise use S_nu ∝ kappa_nu B_nu(T), including in the two-band inversion.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “convolve … and regrid … or restrict to a matched uv range” | These are not interchangeable guarantees of comparable emission. The pack documents missing-flux problems, including 2018ApJ...860....9M, and separately reports the matched uv range in 2017MNRAS.467L.120M. | Require compatible spatial filtering and resolution, registered images, consistent apertures and compatible flux units; explain any remaining mismatch.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “Q(H0) …” converted into ionizing photons and SFR | The recipe does not distinguish the recombination-supported photon rate from the intrinsic stellar production rate. The pack explicitly flags photon escape in evolved regions for 2020ApJ...903...29M. | State the ionization-equilibrium assumption and that escape and dust absorption require separately justified corrections.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | HII-region EM, density, mass and Lyman-photon recipes | The skill omits the cylindrical homogeneous geometry of 2015A&A...577A..30A and the excluded distance/density-gradient uncertainties of 2024ApJ...967...15K. Both are explicit pack entries. | State the geometry for each recipe and report sensitivity to distance and density structure alongside formal errors.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | bipolar-cavity and shell fitting recipes | The pack says opening angle/inclination are weakly constrained in 2019ApJ...886L...4Z. For 2014ApJ...791...95A, all tested continuum temperature/width combinations fit, and departures from spherical symmetry introduce degeneracy. | Warn that good continuum fits do not uniquely determine geometry or temperature; report degeneracies and additional line constraints.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | equipartition fields and uv-template subtraction | The skill omits two explicit limitations of 2014ApJ...796...82Z: equipartition estimates may be inaccurate for flat spectra, and double-subtracted images were unusable because of errors. | Add both qualifications and require propagated template/scaling uncertainties before interpreting residual components.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | corona equipartition “gave B of 100–300 G” | The 2025A&A...699A..62J card describes order-of-magnitude estimates and explicitly says detailed SED modelling is needed to constrain B. | Label the values scenario-dependent estimates and preserve the modelling requirement.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | Sgr A*: “n, Te, B in emcee, log priors” | The 2019ApJ...881L...2B excerpt specifies logarithmic priors on **n, Te and plasma beta**, a fixed 40 μas sphere, and neglected relativistic effects and radiative cooling. These qualifications are missing. | State the sampled variables, fixed size and approximations explicitly; do not imply independent log priors on B.
- SR-19 | medium | usability | “turn … into … SFR” | Step 6 supplies a Q conversion but no actionable SFR conversion or selection of IMF and population timescale. The pack contains differing population assumptions, including Chabrier and Kroupa IMFs. | Provide an explicitly sourced SFR calibration with its assumptions, or narrow the output to Q and route SFR estimation elsewhere.
- SR-20 | low | usability | “vary the indices as a test (step 7)” | Step 7 concerns synchrotron physics and supplies no index-sensitivity procedure. | Point to the verification checks and specify which indices to vary and what fraction changes to report.
