# Codex review: redshift-line-search
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “a Lya line was truncated” | The pack names the source “Lya-2”: 2013ApJ...763..120C, limitations, “Lya-2 line truncated by spectral coverage.” It does not identify the truncated transition as Lyα. | Say “the line of source Lyα-2 was truncated.”
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “Velocity window around prior … 500 to 2000 km/s” | These are different selections: 2017A&A...605A..42C searches within ±500 km/s of Lyα; 2020ApJ...904..130V assumes already detected candidates within 2000 km/s are companions. | Separate prior-search windows from companion-association criteria.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “GILDAS … NOEMA/PdBI spectra and uv-plane line fits” | The cited 2024A&A...685A..80Z explicitly uses GILDAS `uv_fit` for ALMA data. The six aggregate mentions also include Galactic GILDAS/CLASS work. | Describe the supported ALMA and NOEMA uses separately; label six as the unfiltered aggregate.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “lmfit … PyMC3 … line-profile fitting” | 2024A&A...691A.133V uses lmfit for 2D Sérsic morphology. The sole PyMC3 entry is the explicitly excluded LMC paper, 2019A&A...632A.106L. | Separate morphology from spectral fitting and remove excluded Galactic software from the scoped recommendations.
- SR-05 | low | attribution | “Channels: 15.625 MHz to 100 km/s in the pack” | 2016ApJ...833...67W explicitly uses 7.8 MHz cubes. The statement also mixes frequency spacing with velocity width. | Give separate frequency and velocity examples, converting with δv ≈ c δν/ν_obs when needed.
- SR-06 | medium | unsupported | “Every row is copied”; “same survey”; “as above” | The pack supports the candidate counts/cut for 2016ApJ...833...69D and fidelity selection for 2019ApJ...882..136A, but does not transfer the 2016 survey’s kernels, completeness simulations and footprint to those rows. | Retain directly supported entries; explicitly label any inferred inheritance and its evidence.
- SR-07 | low | unsupported | “about 124 … on-topic”; “Eight … off-topic” | The pack gives 132 papers and overlapping science tags, without an eight-paper exclusion list or a reproducible relevance classification. | Remove these counts or supply the exclusion list and scope rule.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “Parameter defaults”; “Common choice … 3–9 channels”; “1.2 × FWHM” | These choices are supported as individual examples—2016ApJ...833...67W and 2020ApJ...904..130V respectively—not established common defaults across the pack. | Relabel as observed examples and explain how agents should select settings for their data.
- SR-09 | medium | science | “3 sigma limit from the rms … the limit scales with [width]” | This does not distinguish flux density from integrated flux or specify the scaling. For example, 2014ApJ...792...34O reports a flux-density limit over a 40 km/s channel. | For independent channels give I_lim = 3 σ_ch δv √N, N = W/δv; require aperture noise, channel covariance and any profile/window correction to be stated.
- SR-10 | medium | omission | “derive z” and “Lya-to-systemic velocity offset” | The workflow omits spectral reference frames, although 2015ApJ...807..180W explicitly converts spectra to LSR before comparing centroids. | Record the frequency frame and velocity convention; transform compared measurements consistently and define the offset’s sign.
- SR-11 | medium | omission | “list candidate line peaks anywhere” | 2016ApJ...833...67W explicitly removes duplicates from overlapping kernels. The recipe omits candidate consolidation. | Merge repeated spatial/frequency detections before reporting source counts; define how the retained detection statistic enters fidelity and completeness.
- SR-12 | medium | omission | “a line luminosity function from a blind search” | No LF calculation or handoff follows. The pack requires detectable volumes accounting for field noise and primary beam in 2020ApJ...889...98M; 2020ApJ...905..147Y instead uses a UV-selected, modified 1/Vmax calculation. | Add a volume/selection-correction step or narrow the scope; keep blind-survey and UV-selected estimators distinct.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “Gaussian … fit to the aperture spectrum” | The main measurement recipe lacks actionable flux-correction checks. The pack specifies residual scaling in 2020ApJ...904..130V and a 2.5 aperture correction in 2015MNRAS.452...54M. | Require assessment and documentation of aperture losses, residual scaling and finite spectral-window losses before reporting total flux.
- SR-14 | medium | usability | “Search radius … primary-beam FWHM” | FWHM is a full width, so treating it as a radius can quadruple the search area. The pack gives a 16″ primary-beam FWHM for 2017A&A...605A..42C. | Specify the actual angular radius or beam-response contour; distinguish FWHM diameter from half-power radius.
- SR-15 | medium | usability | “Spectrum aperture … 0.35″ to 4 beams” | This combines radius, diameter and area. The pack specifies 0.35″ radius for 2017ApJ...851..145M, 0.8″ diameter for 2026ApJ..1002L..28Y, and four beam areas for 2019A&A...630A..59B. | Label every aperture’s geometry explicitly, including the 0.4–1.5″ radii in 2020ApJ...904..130V.
- SR-16 | medium | usability | “sigma_z = (1+z) sigma_v/c” | The formula requires uncertainty in the fitted centroid velocity; σ_v is undefined and could be mistaken for the physical line dispersion. | Define σ_v explicitly, or use σ_z = (1+z) σ_νcenter/ν_center.
- SR-17 | low | usability | Table entries containing “|dv|” and “|v|” | Unescaped vertical bars split Markdown table cells, corrupting the companion-association row and the targeted-window row. | Escape the bars or write `abs(dv)` and `abs(v)`.

## Notes

Most numerical examples and the core fidelity/completeness descriptions are supported.
The main risks are turning heterogeneous examples into defaults and mixing search, association and measurement conventions.
Keep aggregate software counts distinct from the skill’s declared scope.
