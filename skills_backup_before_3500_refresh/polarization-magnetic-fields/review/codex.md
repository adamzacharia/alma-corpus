# Codex review: polarization-magnetic-fields

## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “0, 2, -0.5, -2: 1 each” | The pack’s key-parameter lines contain robust 2 in both 2018NatCo...9.4636L and 2020ApJ...892..152H, and -0.5 in both 2017A&A...603A..92V and 2020ApJ...892..152H. | Change the counts for 2 and -0.5 to two papers each; document counting multiple settings within one paper.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “PI detection threshold … 3: 9” | 2025ApJ...992..103Z applies its cutoff to Stokes Q and U, explicitly stated in its excerpt step 2; this is not a 3-sigma PI selection. | Separate Q/U component cuts from PI cuts and recount the PI category.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Self-scattering a_max … Range seen: 61–150 um” | The pack’s 2016ApJ...831L..12K result gives approximately 150 um with a range approximately 40–300 um. | Label 61–150 um as selected best-fit values; include the reported 40–300 um constraint separately.
- SR-04 | medium | unsupported | “3x larger beam … 10 deg, card”; “626A..36D card also uses 0.5”; “outflows and collapse degrade it” | The supplied entries for 2019ApJ...879...25K do not establish these smoothing, dispersion or dynamical claims; the supplied 2019A&A...626A..36D entry does not establish xi = 0.5. The optional table is not independent evidence. | Remove these attributions or mark them unverified against the supplied pack; retain the supported xi = 0.5 example from 2020ApJ...896..157H.
- SR-05 | high | science | “Rotate by 90 deg only when emission is from aligned grains and optically thin” | “Aligned” includes the radiative alignment explicitly listed in step 5. Optical thinness and alignment alone do not establish that the rotated vectors trace B. | Require evidence for alignment with the magnetic field specifically; retain E-vectors when the alignment axis remains uncertain.
- SR-06 | medium | science | “Standard: PI = sqrt(Q² + U² − sigma_PI²)” | This approximation needs noise assumptions and a valid S/N regime. The pack’s 2018ApJ...859..165S explicitly assumes similar Q/U noise; 2019ApJ...878L..26L instead uses Monte Carlo measurements. Negative radicands and poorly detected I are unhandled. | Define the noise term and assumptions, mask invalid estimates, require adequate I for fractions, and use an appropriate low-S/N likelihood or upper-limit procedure.
- SR-07 | medium | science | “sigma_chi = 28.65 deg × sigma_P/P” | The skill defines P as fractional polarization, whereas 2025ApJ...992..103Z gives sigma_p/I_p for polarized intensity. Propagating I uncertainty into fractional-polarization error does not give the EVPA error. | Write the high-S/N expression using polarized-intensity noise explicitly; use Q/U covariance propagation when needed. State that Delta P/(2P) is in radians.
- SR-08 | medium | science | “subtracting a 0.5"-smoothed angle map” | 2020ApJ...896..157H step 4 explicitly convolves Stokes I, Q and U, derives the low-resolution angle, then subtracts. Directly smoothing angles can fail across the 180-degree boundary. | Specify smoothing Q/U first, deriving angles afterward, and wrapping angle residuals modulo 180 degrees.
- SR-09 | high | science | “DCF density … Common choice: 1e6 cm^-3” | In 2020ApJ...896..157H this is an assumed density required by the SiO maser-pumping model, not a transferable density default. The other cited environments have different density constraints. | Remove the default; require a source- and tracer-specific density estimate, geometry, and uncertainty.
- SR-10 | medium | science | “sigma_v … Delta V ~10 km/s” | The DCF formula requires a defined velocity dispersion, but the table mixes sigma_v and Delta V without defining width conventions. This invites insertion of FWHM directly into a dispersion formula. | Define the required one-dimensional non-thermal dispersion, distinguish FWHM from sigma, and state the cgs units for rho, velocity and resulting B.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “Total field 4B/pi” | Although attributed to 2020ApJ...896..157H, this is presented as a direct conversion for an individual source. Such a projection correction requires an orientation-distribution assumption. | Identify B as B_pos and label 4B_pos/pi as a statistical estimate under an explicit orientation prior, not a measured total field.
- SR-12 | high | science | “Fit V = C1 I + C2 dI/dnu + 0.5 Z B dI/dnu” | Without a joint hyperfine fit, C2 and B multiply the same derivative and are degenerate. The pack’s 2021ApJ...908..141H step 3 retains the component index j and component-dependent Z_j. | Restore the indexed joint model, specify shared nuisance coefficients and distinct Z_j, identify B_LOS, and define coefficient units and sign conventions.
- SR-13 | high | science | “SiO masers … or g Omega/2pi = 230 Hz/G” | The main workflow presents 2020ApJ...896..157H as a usable Zeeman recipe, but its step 5 rejects the implied approximately 10 G fields as implausible and reports opposite signs between transitions. | Put this failed interpretation beside the recipe; require instrumental and non-Zeeman checks before accepting a field estimate.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “systematic floor (0.1%)”; “Absolute EVPA accuracy … 0.4 deg” | These are dataset-specific examples. The pack distinguishes 0.03% systematic uncertainty in 2020ApJ...893...33L, 0.09% additional uncertainty in 2020ApJ...895...64L, and a 0.4-degree EVPA assumption derived for 29 antennas in 2016ApJ...824..132N. | Separate minimum detectable fraction, statistical uncertainty and calibration systematics; require dataset-specific estimates instead of universal floors or accuracy defaults.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “Structure function fits … give B_t/B_0” | The pack’s 2024A&A...686A.274V assumption explicitly requires field–gas coupling and isotropic incompressible turbulence from transverse Alfvén waves. The skill retains only the frozen-field lower-limit caveat. | State the physical assumptions and require evidence that the selected line polarization traces B before applying the estimator.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “fit EVPA versus lambda² linearly” | 2024ApJ...975..103P assumes a single screen and common opacity, excludes systematic errors from RMFIT, and flags unspecified n-pi handling. These conditions are absent from the operational RM step. | Require checking the screen/opacity model, testing angle-unwrapping alternatives, and propagating calibration systematics beyond RMFIT’s formal errors.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “scattering-efficiency prior P ~ 0.02 P90 omega” | 2025ApJ...990..183U calls the approximation rough and uses effective albedo. Its limitations flag prior-driven composition preferences, high optical depth, constant filling factor and a smooth gas disk. | Preserve omega_eff, identify the relation as a model-dependent prior, and require prior-sensitivity and optical-depth checks before interpreting grain properties.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “non-ideal MHD … scored by the fraction of segments within 30 deg” | The 2020ApJ...893...54Y limitation states that the misaligned run also changes the cosmic-ray ionization rate and fails to reproduce intensity morphology and N–S asymmetry. | Explicitly identify the comparison as confounded; angle-score improvement alone cannot isolate the effect of misalignment.
- SR-19 | medium | usability | “multiscale 0, 0.3, 0.9 arcsec” | These angular scales are supported by 2016ApJ...831L..12K, but CASA’s multiscale `scales` parameter takes pixels. An agent could copy the quoted values directly. | Label them as angular equivalents and require conversion to pixel scales using the chosen cell size.

## Notes
The skill captures many important filtering, calibration and mechanism caveats.
Its main risks are turning individual-paper settings into defaults and omitting conditions needed to execute estimators safely.
The supplied pack does not verify claims attributed to additional, unseen card content.
