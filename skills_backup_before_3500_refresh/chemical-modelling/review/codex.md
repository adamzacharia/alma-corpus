- SR-01 | high | attribution | “peak gas-phase abundance … (2024ESC.....8..665M)” | That excerpt places the quoted NH2CN maximum, 5.12e-10 at approximately 3.8e5 yr, **“on the grain.”** The parameters table omits its phase. | Label this result as grain-phase; require a separately documented gas-phase prediction before comparing it with observed gas-phase abundance.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “nH 5e8” for 2017MNRAS.469.2230M | Although the pack’s parameter summary says nH, its verbatim excerpt explicitly says **“constant H2 gas density (5×10^8 cm−3).”** The skill propagates the summary’s normalization error into its density range. | Record n(H2)=5e8 cm^-3; distinguish hydrogen nuclei from H2 throughout densities and abundances, converting explicitly where needed.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Disk model age … Papers (count): 8” | The supplied parameters table contains nine disk bibcodes with ages. Only six have explicit age support in the supplied pack: 2016ApJ...831..101B, 2015ApJ...810..112O, 2018A&A...617A..28S, 2019ApJ...876...72L, 2019ApJ...883...98Z and 2024RAA....24g5017M. | Reconcile the count against a visible bibcode list; remove unsupported age assignments or supply their evidence.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “CO, N2 E_b 855, 800 K and sticking 0.9, 0.85” | The pack’s 2017A&A...599A.101V entries supply cosmic-ray rates and validation/limitation statements, but none of these binding energies or sticking probabilities. Consequently, the corresponding aggregate ranges/counts are not fully supported. | Remove these values and recalculate affected rows, or provide the missing verified evidence.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | Additional per-paper software and setup assignments | The supplied pack does not establish 2016ApJ...825...79S’s NAUTILUS/A–D/Garrod-fast setup; 2017ApJ...835..228T’s ALCHEMIC–LIME–simobserve chain and fiducial structure; 2018ApJ...859..131L’s 5970 reactions/600 species and SED/HD setup; or 2017A&A...608A..96L’s NAUTILUS attribution and density/temperature ranges. - SR-06 | medium | attribution | “RADMC-3D … line radiative transfer … Count … 2” | The aggregate combines `radmc3d` and `radmc-3d`, but 2024RAA....24g5017M explicitly uses RADMC3D for **dust temperature**. Two software mentions do not establish two line-transfer applications. | Separate dust-temperature calculations from line-transfer uses; retain the aggregate count only as a software-mention count.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | DALI-grid “Fixed inputs … Tdust 20 K” | For 2025ApJ...989....1Z, the pack distinguishes thermo-chemical gas-mass inference from the optically thin continuum dust-mass estimator. - SR-08 | medium | attribution | “photodissociation radii and daughter-species shells … 2016A&A...592A..76D” | That card instead describes an analytic NaCl accretion, thermal-desorption and photodesorption model, varying binding energies. | Present NaCl gas–grain partitioning as a separate envelope application.
- SR-09 | medium | unsupported | DG Tau “only borrows a thermo-chemical temperature structure … on-topic counts … 45” | The supplied 2018A&A...620L...1G entry provides its title and an unknown-flaring-index limitation; it does not establish the asserted borrowed-temperature-only method. | Identify exclusion as the reviewer’s scope decision, remove the unsupported methodological explanation, and distinguish selected scope from verified counts.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “No paper in the pack fits the chemical-network inputs with chi2 or MCMC” | The pack explicitly documents this exclusion for 2016A&A...585A..58V, but its incomplete method summaries cannot establish a universal negative about all 46 papers. | Say that no such chemical-input fit is documented in the supplied evidence; retain the specific 2016 citation.
- SR-11 | high | science | “Ratios cancel … beam-filling errors … most COM papers compare ratios for that reason” | A common H2 denominator cancels algebraically; differential source sizes, excitation and opacity do not. The pack itself flags uncertain filling factors in 2019ApJ...876...72L and emitting-area dependence in 2021A&A...651L...6B. The stated literature motivation is unsupported. | Require compatible emitting regions, apertures and column conventions; propagate differential filling/excitation errors and remove the unsupported causal generalization.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “Scale main-isotopologue output by ISM isotope ratios when the network has no fractionation” | Omitting fractionation from a network does not establish that the source lacks it. The 2019ApJ...871..107F result attributes 10–20% of gap C18O intensity to isotope-selective photodissociation. | Mark fixed-ratio scaling as an approximation, write X(rare)=X(main)/R explicitly, and test or bound selective chemistry before interpreting depletion or gas structure.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “Chemical desorption probability … 4%” and generic photodesorption defaults | The 2024ApJ...968L..19F excerpt describes reaction-specific chemical desorption and explicitly assigns its photodesorption yield to **P-bearing species**; 2015ApJ...810..112O supplies a CO yield. These are not universal surface-process defaults. | State the species/reaction scope beside each value and prevent application to an entire network without justification.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “Gas and dust temperature … equal” | The supporting 2025A&A...696A..31G assumption is restricted to static models; 2024RAA....24g5017M instead uses a two-layer gas temperature. An unqualified default can erase the relevant thermal structure. | Specify where equality is assumed and require justification for thermal coupling in the emitting region.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “synthetic images convolved to the observed beam” | Beam matching alone does not reproduce spatial filtering or beam dilution, both explicitly flagged for 2025A&A...696A..31G. | Require matching spatial/spectral extraction and interferometric response where material; assess missing flux and differential beam dilution before attributing intensity differences to chemistry.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | Radiative-transfer validation | The skill omits uncertain/partial IR pumping in 2017A&A...601A...4A and 2015ApJ...814..143A, and unavailable PH3–H2 collisional coefficients in 2024ApJ...968L..19F. | Add checks for pumping and collisional-data availability; disclose resulting excitation assumptions and limits on abundance inference.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | C/O inference and model ranking | The pack explicitly says 2024RAA....24g5017M cannot determine a best-fitting C/O. The 2021A&A...655A..80D fitting method also requires avoiding overprediction of non-detections, which the skill drops. | Preserve upper limits as inequality constraints and report allowed ranges or non-identifiability instead of forcing a best value.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “N2H+ as CO snowline tracer” | The 2017A&A...599A.101V limitation states that gas-phase CO/N2 formation is ignored and emission beyond approximately 60 au is underpredicted. | Carry this limitation into snowline interpretation and require checking whether omitted formation chemistry affects the inferred location.

## Notes
The workflow usefully separates literature examples from referee recommendations in several places.
The main risks are phase/normalization errors and presenting incompletely evidenced settings as defaults.
Unsupported details may be correct elsewhere; they are not established by this supplied pack.
