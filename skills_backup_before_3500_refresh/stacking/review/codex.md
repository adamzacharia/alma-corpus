# Codex review: alma-stacking
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “Faint isotopologues … 2025ApJ...988..162D with PyStacker” | That entry measures CO(2–1)/CO(1–0), not isotopologues, and does not specify alignment using a 12CO velocity field. | Describe it as resolved R21 stacking; reserve the stated isotopologue alignment procedure for 2025MNRAS.541.4031D.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “stack non-PB-corrected maps and carry the PB in the weights” | 2014ApJ...780..115D explicitly primary-beam corrects stamps before averaging; 2021ApJ...908...54W specifies non-corrected maps and rms weighting, without establishing this general PB prescription. Weighting alone does not restore attenuated flux. | Separate the methods. Define PB response A; correct amplitudes and propagate noise, or use the response-normalized estimator Σ(A d/σ²)/Σ(A²/σ²).
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Bright-source exclusion … 1″ … from bright sources” | In 2016MNRAS.462.1192L, sources within 1″ of a stacking position are excluded from the subtraction model, protecting target emission. This is not a target-exclusion radius around bright sources. | Label 1″ as a protection radius for subtraction; distinguish it from the 3″ primary-target and 3.5″ bright-source selection cuts.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Stacked spectral channel … 20–125 km/s” | 2020MNRAS.497.2771M uses 20 km/s to measure individual 12CO widths and 125 km/s for cube stacking. The stated range also omits the 500 km/s template grid and 600 km/s line channels in 2014ApJ...785..149S. | Separate input channelization, stack grids and collapsed integration widths; correct the range and cohort count.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Random-position iterations … 50–1000 … 3” | These are different quantities: 50 repeated random-position stacks in 2014ApJ...780..115D; 100 empty positions in 2019A&A...630A..59B; 1000 off-line spectral windows in 2025MNRAS.541.4031D. | Give separate rows for repetitions, positions and spectral windows; remove the pooled iteration range.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Bootstrap over the sample … 5000”; “Bootstrap resamplings … 100–5000” | The pack explicitly assigns 5000 resamplings to PACS in 2018ApJ...853..195W. It does not establish that count for its ALMA stacks. | Label 5000 as a PACS example; do not present it as a measured ALMA bootstrap setting.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Integrate … within the 1 sigma Gaussian width … 2021ApJ...908...46B” | That paper uses this interval for the S/N optimization statistic. 2019ApJ...880...69Y uses matched intervals for line ratios. Neither establishes it as a general total-flux prescription. | Distinguish optimization statistics, matched-window ratios and total flux; specify interval bounds and any Gaussian flux-fraction correction.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “inserted point sources … recovered 2.3±0.2 vs 2.1 mJy” | In 2016MNRAS.462.1192L, those numbers belong to the simulated three-clump morphology, with three 0.7 mJy clumps. The inserted-point-source test is a separate pack entry. | Attribute the numerical recovery to the clumpy-source simulation; describe the point-source test separately.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “Spectral stack weights … 7 … 2019ApJ...871..112X” | That entry inverse-variance stacks moment-8 peak-intensity maps, not spectra. | Move it to transition-map stacking and recompute the spectral-method tally.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “Upper-limit level … 6” | The six papers listed in Step 6 omit 2026A&A...705A.198M, whose pack entry explicitly gives 3σ stacked upper limits. The supplied pack therefore supports at least seven such papers. | Recount with an explicit inclusion rule, or label six as selected examples rather than the pack tally.
- SR-11 | medium | unsupported | “0.5″ diameter … dirty-beam aperture correction”; “stamp 20–30″” | The supplied entry for 2016ApJ...833..195W states an unresolved-source aperture-correction assumption, but gives neither 0.5″ nor a dirty-beam prescription. The pack establishes 30″ stamps for 2020MNRAS.497.2771M, not 20″. | Remove unsupported numerical and procedural details; retain only what the pack establishes.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “cut stamps from the residual map”; “randomized galaxy positions give only non-detections … 2020ApJ...892...66M” | Neither procedure appears in that paper’s supplied aggregate entries. Its supported settings include astrometry, PB coverage, binning and adaptive flux extraction. | Remove these attributions or mark the procedures as recommendations rather than verified actions by that paper.
- SR-13 | medium | unsupported | “Stacked line detection rule … common choice”; “Keplerian grid … common choice” | The full channel-significance rule and numerical grid come from 2018A&A...616A.100Y. 2021ApJ...908...46B instead rejects narrow lines using their width, not three channels above 3σ. The isolated 2σ flag for 2015ApJ...811L...3T does not establish a stacked-line rule. | Present study-specific criteria and grids as examples; require detection thresholds to be validated for the actual search.
- SR-14 | high | science | “The sources are individually detected … stack only the non-detections” | This changes the estimand and biases an intended full-sample mean downward. The pack includes scientific stacks of all targets in 2018ApJ...860..103S and detected-source morphology stacking in 2018ApJ...861....7F. | Include detections when estimating the full-sample mean; identify non-detection-only stacks as conditional measurements.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “put every spectrum in the rest frame” | Alignment uncertainty is not tested. 2019MNRAS.485.2092C explicitly resamples redshift displacements and linewidths; finding negligible effects there does not establish negligible effects elsewhere. | Propagate redshift and linewidth uncertainties through the stack and test their effects on recovered widths, fluxes and wings.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “broad [CII] wings as outflow tracers” | Statistical checks omit physical alternatives. 2019A&A...631A..78S admits interacting-galaxy contamination, an assumed single broad Gaussian and insensitivity to low-velocity outflows; 2026ApJ..1000..312Z cannot distinguish AGN- from star-formation-driven outflows. | Add these interpretation limits and require checks for companions, profile mismatch and velocity coverage.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “template spectrum of lensed DSFGs” | 2014ApJ...785..149S explicitly says differential magnification is unaccounted for. The skill discusses lensing weights and multiple images without this limitation. | State the magnification treatment and warn that differential magnification can distort stacked line ratios and inferred physical conditions.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “Shift each pixel’s spectrum … and sum” | 2019ApJ...880...69Y masks absorption channels and uses only the blueshifted sector for its mass search. Those safeguards are absent from the disk workflow. | Inspect and mask absorption or contaminated channels; test clean spatial sectors before interpreting aligned profiles or fitted masses.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “state whether the significance includes population variance … or only thermal noise” | The uncertainty budget omits explicit propagation of calibration systematics. The pack gives 5% calibration uncertainty in 2025ApJ...988..162D and 20% systematic flux uncertainty in 2018A&A...616A.100Y. | Report calibration systematics separately and propagate correlations; do not assume shared calibration errors average down with source count.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “Convert to physical quantities” | The estimand distinction for ratios is missing. 2020A&A...633A.160C stacks individual IRX values; 2025ApJ...988..162D distinguishes median ratios, ratios of sums and stacked ratios. | Specify whether each output is a mean of individual quantities, a weighted mean, or a ratio of stacked measurements.
- SR-21 | low | usability | “aperture 0.5″ to 10″” | Aperture conventions are mixed or unstated. The pack explicitly gives a 10″ **radius** for 2017MNRAS.466...49J, while other entries only say aperture or extraction region. | Label radius, diameter and shape for every aperture; preserve “not specified” where the pack does not resolve the convention.

## Notes
Science-field and software aggregate counts match the pack.
The main repairs are method attribution, separation of incompatible parameter categories, and explicit definitions of the quantity being estimated.
Unsupported details above are unverified by this pack, not necessarily false in the original papers.
