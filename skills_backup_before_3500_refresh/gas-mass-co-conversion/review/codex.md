## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “CPROPS / cprops / cpropstoo … 3+1+1” | The software aggregate reports `cprops: 4`. It also provides no counts for myRadex, imfit, immoments or GILDAS. | Preserve the aggregate counts and names; remove unsupported additions or supply paper-level evidence.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “r76 … 0.16 (SMG) to 0.48 (QSO)” | The pack’s 2020A&A...636A..37D parameter line assigns these values to CO(7–6)/CO(1–0), hence r71 under the skill’s notation. | Replace r76 with r71 throughout and explicitly define every ratio’s numerator and denominator.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “alpha_CO, z>1 main sequence … 4.6” | 2014ApJ...783...84S specifies 4.6 for **SMGs**; 2020MNRAS.497.2771M concerns galaxies at z≈0.03–0.2 and specifies disk/merger choices. Neither supports this population label. | Move these examples to their actual populations; recount the population-specific total.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Early-type / CO(1-0) with r21 0.7” | The cited 2021ApJ...908..231M assumption line gives r21=0.62. | Annotate that paper separately rather than assigning 0.7 to every listed paper.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “r31 … 0.27 to 1”; “r21 … [maximum] 1.4” | The pack gives brightness ratios r31=2.0–2.9 for 2021A&A...652A..98G and r21=2.2–2.5, r31=2.9 for 2019A&A...632A..61G. The Galactic X_CO range also omits the explicitly Galactic 4.7e20 in 2017ApJ...846...73T. | Include the documented extremes with population labels, or clearly label each range as a restricted subset.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “[CI] Tex … 15 to 40 K”; “Tex=30 K … 2021A&A...645A.120F” | No supplied entry supports 15 K for [CI]. For 2021A&A...645A.120F, 40 K is **dust temperature**, and Q10=0.48 is an adopted median; the pack does not give Tex=30 K. | Separate dust temperature, [CI] excitation temperature and adopted Q10; remove unsupported temperature endpoints and attribution.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Column-density route with Tex and CO/H2 abundance … 2024ApJ...961..228S” | That paper’s step 4 instead uses N=X_CO W_CO with X_CO=2e20, followed by M=μm_HAN. | Remove this citation from the abundance-based route or describe its actual X-factor method.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “flux-calibration error in quadrature … 10% in … 2024ApJ...964..166K” | That card states **assumed 10% observed-flux errors** plus factor-four X_CO variation. It does not identify the 10% as calibration error or specify this quadrature procedure. | Retain 2016ApJ...828...46A for the explicit calibration-in-quadrature example; describe the Antennae uncertainty separately.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “Dynamical ceiling: gas mass plus stars below M_dyn … 2018MNRAS.479.5440L” | Its results give M_bar=3.4±2.0e11 Msun versus M_4kpc=0.9±0.3e11 and M_8kpc=1.8±0.6e11. The card describes a mass-budget comparison, not a demonstrated ceiling satisfied by the masses. | Describe the comparison accurately; require matching enclosed radii before applying a dynamical constraint.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “Galactic alpha_CO … >~8 [overestimate] (2017A&A...606A..17M)” | That pack entry adopts Galactic alpha_CO=6.5 with helium for the dust calibration; it does not state that this overestimates its masses by eight. | Remove that attribution. Label any 6.5/0.8 comparison as a calculated difference between conventions.
- SR-11 | medium | unsupported | “Recovered fractions … 43% … 20% … about half” | The supplied entries for 2016ApJ...832..148V, 2017A&A...608A..98S, 2012A&A...538L...9H and 2017ApJ...843..136E do not contain these recovery measurements. | Remove these percentages or supply supporting pack entries; retain the documented 40–50% missing-flux example.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “interferometric masses sometimes above single-dish”; “SLED-shape uncertainty … left out”; cited differential-lensing examples | The supplied entries do not establish these claims for 2014ApJS..214....1U, 2017ApJ...845..154V, 2017ApJ...846L..14S, 2016A&A...586L...7B or 2016ApJ...822...80S. | Remove unsupported paper-specific assertions. For differential lensing, cite the explicit discussion and limitation in 2017MNRAS.466.2825B.
- SR-13 | high | science | “Using [flux ratios] as r_J1 inflates L'_CO(1-0) by 4 or 9” | The direction is reversed: L'_10=L'_J/r_J1, so substituting the larger flux ratio **underestimates** L'_10 and mass by the frequency-squared factor. | Replace “inflates” with “underestimates”; give the explicit ratio conversion.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “mu 2.37 to 2.8 per particle” | These are different normalizations. The pack’s 2024ApJ...961..228S mass equation uses μ=2.8 per H2 molecule, whereas 2.37 is a mean mass per free particle in molecular gas. | Separate μ_particle from μ_H2 and state which hydrogen mass each multiplies.
- SR-15 | high | science | “3 sigma limit = 3 × rms_channel × sqrt(dv_channel × FWHM)” | This is unsafe for an arbitrary extraction aperture unless rms_channel is already the uncertainty of its integrated spectrum. The pack’s 2020ApJ...897L..19M explicitly uses box/beam scaling. | Define the noise measurement and units; account for spatial and spectral covariance and primary-beam-dependent noise.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “ULIRG value for mergers and compact starbursts” | This reads as an automatic assignment. The pack contains materially different measured or adopted choices, including 2.1±1.2 for the NGC 6240 outflow and Galactic X_CO in Seyfert tori. | Present environment-based values as candidate assumptions; justify the choice and propagate alternatives when ambiguous.
- SR-17 | medium | usability | “X_CO … papers add 1.36 explicitly” alongside the “1.05e4” mass formula | The 1.05e4 coefficient already incorporates the conventional helium-inclusive mass conversion. An agent following the later instruction could add helium twice. | Explicitly label the prefactor helium-inclusive and provide the corresponding H2-only convention.
- SR-18 | medium | usability | “Add H I where needed: H2/HI=0.3 … HI=50% of H2” | These are source/sample assumptions, not interchangeable defaults. The 2014ApJ...783...84S pack entry restricts its 50% assumption to the **local calibration sample**. | State those scopes and require measured HI or a justified population-specific assumption.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “[CI](1-0) or [CI](2-1)” | The workflow only supplies the 1–0 conversion. The pack separately gives Q21 and A21 for 2016A&A...591A..73G and Q21 sensitivity tests for 2024A&A...685A.138V. | Add a transition-specific 2–1 conversion, or explicitly route 2–1 measurements elsewhere.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “13CO LTE … alpha_CO = M_LTE/L'_CO(1-0)” | Missing the explicit limitation in 2017ApJ...848..101V: its LTE analysis traces only a fraction of the total molecular mass. Shared excitation/filling-factor assumptions and finite-opacity validation are also absent. | Require matched components/apertures, test opacity and filling factors, and distinguish traced-component mass from total mass.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “Dust RJ continuum … alpha_850=6.7e19” | The skill omits the calibration-domain limitation: 2016ApJ...820...83S says the calibration is restricted to high stellar mass and does not probe low-metallicity systems. | State the calibrated domain and require explicit validation before extrapolating to low metallicity.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “Cross-check … Dust RJ continuum” | No continuum-contamination check is required. The 2024ApJ...977..198L validation explicitly subtracts synchrotron for one QSO and cannot do so for another. | Check and subtract non-dust continuum; propagate uncertainty or qualify the resulting mass when contamination is unconstrained.
- SR-23 | medium | omission | “Measure a clean line flux” | Listing continuum subtraction among unstated settings does not require validating it. The 2018MNRAS.479.5440L card explicitly finds uv-plane over-subtraction and uses image-plane subtraction. | Require inspection of continuum fits, line-free channels and residual baselines before accepting the line flux.
- SR-24 | medium | attribution | “Independent tracers agree: three tracers … 2024ApJ...977..198L” | Its pack validation states **cross-comparison**; its limitation says comparisons suffer large parameter uncertainties. Agreement is not established by the supplied entry. | Rename this example “three-tracer comparison” and distinguish documented agreement from merely performing a comparison.

Keep paper-specific assumptions distinct from recommended defaults.
