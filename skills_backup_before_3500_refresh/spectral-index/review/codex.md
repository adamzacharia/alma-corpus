- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “10%: 4”; “83 papers state a fixed beta” | The four listed calibration papers omit 2020ApJ...892...99C, whose excerpt also explicitly adds 10% calibration uncertainty. The beta rows sum to 83, but 2015ApJ...810...91H has only a title in this pack, and 2024BSRSL..93..601H lists both beta = 1.5 and 2.0 rather than one fixed value. | Count at least five explicit 10% cases; distinguish supported fixed adoptions, alternatives tested and unverified entries before reporting the beta total.
- SR-04 | high | attribution | “6–150 klambda”; “2.5% B3 and 5% B6”; envelope T(R), beta means and grain-size interpretation | For 2025A&A...700A.188C, the pack supplies only 95/233 GHz and a 0.1 calibration contribution to alpha, plus the paper title. - SR-05 | high | attribution | “1000 rescaled fits”; “50 mas”; “tau 1–10 … at all radii of HL Tau” | For 2019ApJ...883...71C, the pack supplies a jet model of 175 uJy at 32.5 GHz, alpha 0.7 and size 22 mas. - SR-06 | high | attribution | “S/N > 15”; “alpha 2.48 … vs 3.11”; “±5 K changes tau by 25–30%” | 2026ApJ..1001..124M appears only in the paper list. - SR-07 | medium | attribution | “17–1260 klambda, 0.25″”; “2.09 ± 0.03”; “brightness temperature … 12.9 K” | For 2018A&A...616A..88V, the pack supports the 2.5 S/N mask and 10% calibration producing 0.42 uncertainty. - SR-08 | medium | attribution | “mtmfs, nterms=2 … 4”; “Briggs robust … −0.5 to 1”; other imaging counts | The pack gives nterms = 2 and robust = 0 for 2018ApJ...852..122H, and mtmfs maps for 2018ApJ...853..171G, without its nterms value. - SR-09 | medium | attribution | “alpha 2.66, 2.58, 2.40”; “1.3 / 2.9 mm”; “tau 0.1–0.4” | For 2020ApJ...898...36L, the supplied lines establish a temperature assumption, absorption-dominated opacity and resolution/model limitations. - SR-10 | medium | attribution | “53% … lower limits”; “mean ratio 1.03 on 38 sources”; “two … brown-dwarf … lower limits” | Neither these statistics for 2017A&A...602A..54M nor the two limits for 2017ApJ...846...70P occur in the pack. Likewise absent are the specific sub-band result for 2016ApJ...821L..16C, edge gradients for 2017MNRAS.469L.123O, regression comparison for 2014ApJ...796...82Z and 43 GHz refit for 2015A&A...577A..30A. | Keep appropriate general cautions, but delete unsupported numbers and literature attributions.
- SR-11 | medium | attribution | “beta 1.0 vs 2.0 changes dust mass by a factor 2 … T 25 vs 35 K by 1.2” | The 2016ApJ...819...82S pack entry supplies beta = 1.5, T = 30 K and an opacity normalization; it supplies neither sensitivity experiment nor factor. | Remove these factors or provide the supporting calculation as an explicitly labelled illustration.
- SR-12 | medium | attribution | “Extrapolate cm free-free … (2016ApJ...833L...6C)” | Its step 3 explicitly extrapolates **3 mm** emission to 870 um, assuming negligible dust at 3 mm. | Correct the wavelength and retain the negligible-dust assumption as a prerequisite.
- SR-13 | medium | attribution | “−2.73 … (wavelength convention)” | The 2017MNRAS.466.4201W excerpt reports −2.73 but describes fitting F_nu ∝ nu^alpha and supplies positive alpha_pl = 1.84 and q = 3.50. The pack does not establish a wavelength convention and is internally inconsistent on signs. | Flag the unresolved convention; require normalization to the skill’s frequency convention before applying the q relation.
- SR-14 | medium | unsupported | “Recommendation (from the evidence) … propagate at least … spread” | The proposed universal beta spreads are the writer’s recommendation, not a pack result. Counts of adopted assumptions do not establish an uncertainty distribution for another source. | Label this as author guidance; select physically justified opacity/temperature alternatives and propagate their effects together.
- SR-15 | high | science | “thermal dust (alpha > 2) vs free-free or synchrotron (alpha ≤ 2)” | This is not a unique classifier. Cold optically thin dust can have alpha < 2 outside RJ; thick/scattering dust and mixed components also overlap these ranges. The pack itself contains dust/optical-depth caveats and multi-component analyses. | Treat alpha as a diagnostic, requiring temperature, optical-depth, morphology and component constraints before classification; revise the parameters-table reading guide too.
- SR-16 | high | science | “Delta alpha = x e^x/(e^x − 1) − 1” | This is the local differential Planck correction. Applied at an unspecified frequency, it is not the exact correction for a finite two-band index. The pack’s 2019A&A...626A..36D values do not establish that formula. | Use beta = alpha − ln[B_nu1(T)/B_nu2(T)]/ln(nu1/nu2) for optically thin, single-temperature emission; label the differential expression as a local approximation.
- SR-17 | high | science | “tau = −ln(1 − I/B_nu(T))” | The inversion requires compatible specific-intensity units, a beam-filling isothermal absorption model and appropriate background treatment. Direct use of Jy/beam or scattering-affected intensity gives incorrect tau. The pack’s 2020ApJ...891...48H calls this **nominal** optical depth. | Convert units explicitly, state the model assumptions, require 0 < I/B_nu < 1, and report nominal tau or limits when assumptions fail.
- SR-18 | medium | science | “continuum is assumed flat within a spw … <1% change” | The <1% statement is specific to the spectral windows of 2024A&A...689A.317G. For alpha = 2, fractional variation is approximately 2 Delta nu/nu and need not be <1% for other setups. | Calculate the variation for each actual window; use a spectral continuum model when necessary.
- SR-19 | medium | science | “Propagate per-band flux-calibration error in quadrature” | This assumes independent band errors. Shared calibration errors can be correlated; per-band scale errors are also coherent across pixels and annuli. | Include covariance: sigma_alpha² = (f1² + f2² − 2 rho f1 f2)/ln²(nu1/nu2). Draw coherent scale factors per dataset in simulations.
- SR-20 | medium | science | “Keep a source only if abs(alpha) > 5 sigma(alpha) …” | This promotes the particular 2018ApJ...853..171G catalog selection into a universal rule. It preferentially rejects real near-zero indices and biases population distributions; that paper explicitly admits unquantified completeness. | Present it only as that paper’s selection; use task-appropriate uncertainty/detection criteria and model selection effects.
- SR-21 | medium | science | “Self-cal both bands”; “resolve out emission above about 5″” | Self-calibration requires adequate S/N and a reliable model. Recoverable scale depends on the observation; 2016ApJ...833L...6C specifically discusses undersampling above approximately 12″. | Make self-calibration conditional and validate flux/structure preservation; determine filtering from each dataset’s baselines and flux-recovery checks.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “fit T, tau_0 and beta per radius”; grain-size inference | The pack warns that two bands constrain four parameters only with priors (2024ApJ...971..129C), and that temperature/calibration/degeneracies prevent precise grain-size measurements (2020ApJ...891...48H). These limits are not made explicit in the fitting instructions. | Require an identifiability check, stated priors and prior-sensitivity tests; report allowed grain-model regions rather than unique a_max values.
- SR-23 | medium | omission | “Primary-beam correct before measuring fluxes” | Correction alone does not establish reliability near the beam edge. 2017MNRAS.466.4201W explicitly rejects asymmetry/vertical-structure conclusions there. | Require a justified PB cutoff and spatially varying uncertainty treatment; validate apparent spectral structure against beam-related effects.
- SR-24 | low | usability | “State all of these in your own write-up” | The reproducibility checklist omits other pack-flagged settings: uvmodelfit starting parameters/options, CLEAN masks/thresholds/iterations, continuum bandwidth and PB treatment. | Add these applicable settings to the checklist, distinguishing recorded choices from literature defaults.

## Notes

Many detailed claims exceed the evidence actually supplied; a paper appearing in the bibliography does not support its unstated methods.
The pack contains sign inconsistencies; preserve the skill’s declared convention and flag unresolved source conventions.
Rebuild parameter counts after removing unsupported entries and separating measured values from adopted assumptions.
