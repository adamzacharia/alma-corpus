# Codex review: uv-plane-fitting
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | high | attribution | “R_e = 0.826 FWHM” for three papers | The pack is internally inconsistent: 2017ApJ...850...83F explicitly gives this relation, but parameter entries for 2017ApJ...834..135T and 2020ApJ...900....1F give FWHM = 0.826 R_e. | State the discrepancy; retain paper-specific relations pending clarification of size conventions.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “T_mid0 … fixed” for 2018ApJ...856..117F | Its pack parameter entry explicitly lists T_mid0 among the free parameters. | Move T_mid0 to the free list and preserve q_atm notation.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “RADMC-3D + galario/vis_sample (2017MNRAS.465.2595M …)” | That excerpt names RADMC-3D, emcee, CASA and uvsim; it does not attribute sampling to galario or vis_sample. | Give this paper its documented uvsim workflow.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “clumps … at fixed positions (2024A&A...685A..80Z …)” | Step 5 of that excerpt specifies PSF fits with a free centre. Its fixed-position fit concerns continuum upper limits. | Distinguish the free-centre clump fits from fixed-position continuum limits.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “fixed SIE lens (… 2018Sci...361.1016S)” | The pack supports an SIE lens for this paper, but does not state that its parameters were fixed. | Remove “fixed” for this citation.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Walkers per free parameter … 3” | Four pack papers explicitly specify this ratio: 2020ApJ...898..146M, 2025A&A...693A.151M, 2025ApJ...986L..11S and 2025MNRAS.543.2723P. | Change the count to four and add the last example.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “1000 to 1e5 steps”; “1e5 samples” under Steps | The pack includes 300–1000 steps in 2017MNRAS.465.2595M and 10,000–240,000 iterations in 2017A&A...597A..41G. For 2019ApJ...875...87D it states samples, not steps per walker. | Correct the range and separate steps, iterations and total samples.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “Weights … x0.25 to x1/2.667” | The cited 2018MNRAS.475.4924K value is 1/2.5 = 0.4, outside the stated upper endpoint. | Use 0.25–0.4 for these explicit multiplicative examples.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “a missing second null drove a third ring” | 2018ApJ...865...37M describes failure to reproduce the second **minimum in negative visibilities**, near 600 kλ. A minimum is not a zero crossing. | Replace “null” with “negative visibility minimum.”
- SR-10 | high | unsupported | “here lens parameters are usually held fixed” | One fixed-lens example cannot establish “usually.” Moreover, 2015ApJ...811..124S explicitly applies the ALMA-derived fixed lens to **ATCA CO** visibilities. | Identify the instrument and describe fixed-lens modelling as one example; remove the frequency claim.
- SR-11 | medium | unsupported | “fixed h = 0.03 unless resolved” as a common default | The pack supports this choice for two belt studies, not a general default. 2025A&A...693A.151M explicitly warns that constrained heights need not be vertically resolved. | Label h = 0.03 as a study-specific assumption and use “unless vertically constrained.”
- SR-12 | high | science | “Weights are wrong by default”; “Reduced chi2 near 1 after weight rescaling” | Dataset-specific corrections in 2020ApJ...902..141S and 2020ApJ...891...48H do not justify automatic rescaling. The pack also documents high chi2 from model inadequacy in 2025A&A...700A.188C. | Diagnose noise calibration independently; do not inflate errors merely to hide structured residuals or force reduced chi2 to unity.
- SR-13 | high | science | “fit the scaling as a free parameter” with likelihood “exp(-chi2/2)” | When the noise scale varies, the Gaussian likelihood normalization also varies. Omitting it permits arbitrarily inflated variance. The pack supports free scaling but does not supply this implementation detail. | Include the covariance normalization/log-determinant term and specify a proper scale prior.
- SR-14 | high | science | “a uniform disk … whose first null (… for a ring) pins the size” | The uniform-disk formula cannot convert the ring null in 2014ApJ...791L...6W into a disk diameter. Null locations depend on the radial profile and ring width. | Separate disk and ring examples; infer size using the fitted profile. Specify theta in radians.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “Add absolute flux calibration error (10%)” | Ten percent is supported for the named examples, but 2023PSJ.....4..142C explicitly uses 5%. Calibration errors can also be correlated between measurements used for spectral indices. | Adopt the dataset-specific calibration uncertainty and propagate its covariance where relevant.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “delta-chi2 contours (… 1 and 6.63)” | 2025ApJ...986L..22R uses these thresholds for a one-parameter Lp curve, with 68% and 99% confidence. They are not generic joint-parameter contour thresholds. | State the confidence levels and one-parameter scope; select thresholds for the relevant dimensionality.
- SR-17 | medium | science | “free of CLEAN artefacts” | Direct fitting avoids fitting a deconvolved image, but cannot guarantee independence from image-derived subtraction or self-calibration models. The pack documents nearby-source subtraction uncertainty in 2020ApJ...902..141S. | Qualify the benefit and retain calibration, subtraction and model-dependence caveats.
- SR-18 | high | omission | “frank … with galario geometry” | The skill omits the 500-run geometry perturbation used for frank uncertainties in 2025ApJ...986L..11S: 1° scatter in i/PA and one-third beam major axis in offsets. Fixed geometry can understate profile uncertainty. | Require geometry-uncertainty propagation; describe these values as that paper’s example.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “matched-filter detection of weak lines” | 2017A&A...605A..21C explicitly assumes co-spatial lines and treats inferred fluxes as lower limits because of template mismatch. Neither qualification accompanies the recommendation. | Add both assumptions and distinguish detection significance from reliable integrated flux.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “Line emission: Keplerian disk models … turbulence” | The pack warns that parametric temperature structure can inflate turbulence and introduce degeneracies (2015ApJ...813...99F); 2017ApJ...843..150F describes turbulence as disk-averaged and intensity-weighted. | Require temperature/abundance sensitivity checks and state what the fitted turbulence parameter represents.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “Settings papers leave out … Write all of these down” | The checklist omits prominent pack categories: flux calibrator/scale uncertainty, exact line-free channels, continuum-subtraction method/order, pipeline version and reference antenna. Continuum-subtraction systematics are explicitly flagged in 2019ApJ...882..118X. | Add these provenance fields and propagate continuum-subtraction uncertainty when material.
- SR-22 | medium | usability | “Remove unrelated field sources first” | 2025A&A...700A.188C distinguishes subtracting distant secondaries from fitting close secondaries jointly. The unconditional instruction loses that distinction. | Subtract separable contaminants; jointly fit blended components and assess subtraction uncertainty.
- SR-23 | medium | usability | “Common choice” chain lengths and burn-in percentages | These invite mechanical stopping despite widely varying pack settings; 2018ApJ...865...37M retains only the last 5000 of 30000 iterations. | Present historical examples separately from defaults; make convergence and effective posterior sampling determine run length.
## Notes
Software aggregate arithmetic is consistent with the supplied counts and stated case-variant caveat.
The pack’s contradictory size conversions require explicit uncertainty, not silent harmonization.
The workflow is useful, but several study-specific choices currently read as universal instructions.
