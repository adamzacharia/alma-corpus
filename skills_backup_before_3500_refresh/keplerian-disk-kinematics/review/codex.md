# Codex review: keplerian-disk-kinematics
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “emission height from 12CO … 2020ApJ...891...48H, 2018ApJ...864..133T” | The former explicitly fits HCO+; the latter fits CS first-moment geometry with stellar mass fixed, without measuring emission height. The “Emission surface (12CO)” table also mislabels the HCO+ results. | Separate examples by tracer and measured quantity.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | Forward modelling for “Best dynamical masses”; “Free parameters … M*” | The pack fixes M*=2.3 Msun in 2015ApJ...813...99F and M*=1.7 Msun in 2018ApJ...854..130W. These are not examples of measuring stellar mass as a free parameter. The table also treats gamma=1 as common while 2017ApJ...846L..26W and 2018ApJ...854..130W explicitly fit gamma. | Separate structure/turbulence fits with prescribed masses from dynamical-mass fits; preserve each example’s fixed/free parameters.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “Clip the velocity map: 5 sigma (2017ApJ...849...56A …)” | That paper uses intensity pixels above 5 sigma to calculate mean positions in a PV diagram, not to clip a velocity map. | Move this example to PV extraction; distinguish intensity thresholds from velocity uncertainties.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Drop contaminated channels: cloud absorption removed about 30% of 12CO” | 2018ApJ...868..113T reports that the cloud **absorbs approximately 30% of the emission** on the western side. This is not a reported channel-exclusion fraction or procedure. | State the absorption result separately; do not attribute channel removal to this figure.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Thin-disk stacking overestimates mass by 20-30% at i = 80 deg … card” | The supplied 2021ApJ...908...46B entry gives only “scale-height effect small (<3%)”; it contains no 20–30% mass-bias test at 80 degrees. | Remove the numerical claim unless its supporting evidence is supplied; distinguish the quantities and regimes being compared.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Beam smearing … bias reaches up to 8 beams” | In 2025ApJ...994...98F, the eight-beam extent describes **intensity/temperature resolution bias**, with intensity underestimated by up to 50%. It does not establish that stellar-mass or velocity bias extends eight beams. | Label the affected observable; retain the separately supported exclusion of approximately five beams for the mass fit.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Refit with different surface assumptions … consistent within 3 sigma” | 2018ApJ...868..113T explicitly finds agreement between the three **radial rotation-velocity profiles**, not all fitted parameters or stellar masses. | Specify the validated observable and avoid implying three-sigma agreement of masses.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “excludes 23 contaminated channels”; “sin i prior on i, card” | Neither claim appears in the supplied 2016ApJ...818..156C entries, which provide model assumptions, p=1 and a 12-dimensional posterior. An inclination prior also does not support the table’s count of stellar-mass priors. | Remove these unsupported details and recount the M* prior row using actual mass-prior entries.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “13CO and C18O 3-2”; “fixes i, PA, distance and fits only M*, v_sys”; “blank-field … tests” | The supplied 2018A&A...616A.100Y entries do not specify transition 3–2 or those validation tests. The 2021ApJ...908...46B entries do not supply the claimed tracer list or fixed/free fit configuration. | Retain supported stacking-grid values and assumptions; remove or obtain evidence for these additional specifics.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “exoALMA uses DISCMINER … (2026ApJ..1000L..15F, card)” | This bibcode appears only in the supplied paper-title list; DISCMINER is not named anywhere in the pack. Likewise, the pack does not associate DiskJockey specifically with 2019ApJ...883...22C. Aggregate tool counts cannot establish paper-level attribution. | Remove these software attributions or supply the relevant excerpts.
- SR-11 | medium | attribution | “gave a different mass from the visibility fit”; “found [gas and continuum inclinations] consistent” | The 2025ApJ...986L..11S entries report fixed inclination 49.9 degrees, EDDY ignoring beam convolution, and visibility mass inconsistent with Teff. They do not report either claimed comparison result. | Present these comparisons as recommended checks, without asserting unsupported published outcomes.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “Thin-disk fits of 12CO bias M* and PA; MAPS disks need elevated surfaces” | 2024MNRAS.534.2904C records elevated surfaces **assumed** for four MAPS disks, not a demonstrated necessity or measured mass/PA bias. | Describe the reported assumptions accurately; label sensitivity to neglected height as general modelling guidance.
- SR-13 | medium | unsupported | “emcee is the default sampler”; “Common choice” parameter defaults | Eighteen matched steps establish mentions, not a validated default. The same problem affects universal-looking surface, turbulence and sampler settings drawn from heterogeneous, target-specific examples. | Rename the table “Reported settings”; distinguish literature examples from independently justified recommendations.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “conical, psi ~13-15 deg … thin disk to z/r ~0.13” | These bounds are inconsistent: the supplied relation z/r=tan(psi) gives approximately 0.23–0.27 for 13–15 degrees. The HCO+ flaring exponent psi=0.81 is dimensionless, not an opening angle. | Separate conical angle, height ratio and flaring exponent; state the radius at which a flared height ratio applies.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “Turbulence / intrinsic width … 0.01-1 km/s” | The examples mix turbulent xi, intrinsic line width and b without defining profile conventions. The pack does not establish that these are interchangeable dispersions. | Preserve the original parameter definitions; specify thermal/nonthermal contributions and sigma, Doppler-b or FWHM conventions before comparing values.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | Forward-model likelihood and error-budget instructions | The pack explicitly includes independent-beam counts and adjacent-channel correlation in 2016MNRAS.460.2933M, plus Hanning smoothing in 2013ApJ...776...77K. Mentioning correlated annuli alone does not address their treatment in inference. | Require a justified spatial/spectral noise model or effective independent-sample treatment, and matching of the spectral response.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Always report … channel width” | Rest frequency, velocity reference frame and velocity convention are absent from the checklist. The pack flags these settings and documents an incorrect Band 7 sky-frequency transformation in 2013ApJ...774...16R. | Require verification and reporting of rest frequency, frame, convention and spectral-axis alignment before fitting or stacking.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | Massive-disk “Keplerian enclosed mass” workflow | 2025NatAs...9.1833L distinguishes enclosed mass 7.6 Msun from stellar mass 4.89 Msun after subtracting disk and other gas contributions. A general self-gravity warning does not explain this required distinction. | Explicitly distinguish enclosed and stellar mass; document gas contributions and the adopted gravity model before reporting M*.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “Massive protostars … RRLs (2020ApJ...897L..33J)” | This example requires MORELI non-LTE disk-plus-wind modelling; the pack states that a no-wind model fails and flags maser variability and large high-velocity centroid errors. None is carried into the proposed RRL workflow. | Add these requirements and limitations, or route RRL analyses to a separate specialised workflow.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | GoFish “stacking … and Zeeman analysis” | 2021ApJ...908..141H explicitly warns that its red/mirrored-blue stacking destroys vertical-field Stokes V signal; hyperfine blending also restricts usable line portions. | Add a polarity-preservation warning and require component-specific blending masks before applying stacking to polarisation.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “find deviations … pressure bumps, spirals, warps, planets” | 2024MNRAS.534.2904C explicitly says residual morphology, including Doppler-flips, depends on the subtracted model. 2018ApJ...868..113T also warns unresolved velocity features underestimate their true depth. | Require reporting model dependence and resolution limits; avoid treating residual morphology alone as a unique physical identification.
- SR-22 | low | attribution | Distance “59.5 pc to 2.38 kpc”; “Gaia or VLBA parallax” | The pack includes 59 pc for 2018ApJ...862L...2F, below the stated minimum. Gaia DR2 is explicitly supported, but VLBA provenance is not supplied. | Correct the range and retain only evidenced distance-source attributions.
- SR-23 | low | usability | “Papers (count)” | The closing note says these are counts of parameter/assumption lines, not papers. The column heading therefore describes a different denominator. | Rename it “Supporting pack entries” and make counts reproducible without implying paper-level prevalence.

## Notes
The skill captures many important degeneracies and systematic-error warnings well.
Most named-software aggregate counts match the pack; they do not establish methodological prevalence.
The main repairs are paper-level attribution, observable-specific validation claims, and separating reported settings from defaults.
