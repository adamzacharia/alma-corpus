## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “tclean: 24”; “uvcontsub: 10”; “GILDAS: 18 + 1” | The software aggregate gives tclean 25, uvcontsub 11, gildas 18 and gildas mapping 2. - SR-02 | high | attribution | “fitted continuum as a free constant together with the line … 2026ApJ..1002L..28Y … Use those instead” | That excerpt explicitly applies a linear uvcontsub fit **before** Gaussian-plus-constant fitting. 2013ApJ...770...13W likewise subtracts a continuum image and has three line-free windows. Neither demonstrates the stated insufficient-channel fallback. | Describe the former as residual-baseline fitting after subtraction and the latter as an independent continuum consistency check.
- SR-03 | high | attribution | “statistical image-plane methods … instead of a hand-picked channel list (…2018ApJ...866...87B)” | That paper’s parameter entry explicitly says STATCONT c-SCM plus an aggregate deviation spectrum selected channels for **uv-plane** subtraction; its threshold was manually adjusted. | Separate statistical channel selection followed by uv subtraction from direct per-pixel image subtraction. Restrict the 10–20% bandwidth result to this example.
- SR-04 | high | attribution | “Line exclusion, galaxies … 500–2500” | 2019MNRAS.489.1927S lists **continuum fitting intervals** −2500 to −500 and +500 to +2500 km/s, not exclusion widths up to 2500 km/s. 2021A&A...652A..98G specifies mask **widths** of 300–600 km/s, not ±300–600 km/s. | Distinguish full widths, half-widths and fitting intervals explicitly; state the velocity reference for each.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Undetected line … 300–400 km/s … 2020A&A...637A..84P” | Its parameter entry specifies **300 km/s**, not 300–400. The pack’s separate 400 km/s example concerns upper limits in 2020A&A...641A.155V. | Use 300 km/s for the cited subtraction example; keep upper-limit assumptions separate.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Continuum model across spw … <1% change assumed” | The assumptions entry for 2019A&A...632L...7J says flat continuum **despite 1–2% change over a sideband**. | Preserve each paper’s frequency span and variation separately; do not give all three citations a <1% bound.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Line-free fraction … 10–20% … 10 to 144 channels … 2” | The percentage belongs to 2018ApJ...866...87B; the position-dependent channel counts and MHz bandwidths belong to 2018A&A...615A.141B. They are different quantities, and only one cited entry establishes the percentage. | Split these into separate examples with their own denominators and spatial dependence.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “One paper applied a phase self-cal table after the subtraction” | Besides 2020ApJ...898...10T, the parameter entry for 2017ApJ...850L..30Y says subtraction preceded transfer of self-calibration solutions to line SPWs. | Replace “one paper” with a nonexclusive description and include both examples.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “Subtract per data set before combining” | This is supported by some examples but is not a universal sequence: 2014A&A...570L..14R combines arrays before self-calibration and subtraction; 2019PASJ...71..105T subtracts on combined ten-day data. | Present per-dataset subtraction as an option and explain when compatible datasets can instead be combined first.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “first-order baseline fitted simultaneously”; “continuum as a free parameter in a blind search”; “straight-line spectral baseline fits” | The supplied pack gives only bibliography entries for 2018A&A...620A.115B, 2018Natur.556..469M and 2018ApJ...867..166S; it supplies no method statements supporting these attributions. | Remove these paper-specific claims or obtain supporting evidence. General baseline-fitting advice must be labelled accordingly.
- SR-11 | medium | attribution | “subtract a source model … first … then uvcontsub”; “no line-free channels at all” | 2019AJ....157..251T supports limb-darkened model subtraction before deconvolution, without a subsequent uvcontsub step. 2019MNRAS.485.1595P has no supporting method entry here. 2015Natur.522..455C supports line-flux correction, without the claimed absence of line-free channels. | Remove the unsupported prerequisites and mandatory sequences; describe only the operations established by each entry.
- SR-12 | medium | attribution | “<5% residual line contamination”; “N2D+ channels 350–750”; additional cited channel-selection examples | The pack supplies no such settings for 2017ApJ...837L..29H or 2017A&A...606A.125S. - SR-13 | medium | attribution | “GILDAS UV_CONTINUUM + UV_SUBTRACT”; “CLASS baselines”; “MADCUBA … local zeroth/first-order continuum” | The cited 2019A&A...632A..33A, 2016A&A...587A..91B and 2018ApJ...867L...6B appear only in the bibliography here. - SR-14 | medium | attribution | “two uv implementations … agrees to <5%”; cited uv/image comparisons | No supporting method or validation entry is supplied for 2017MNRAS.469.4683C, 2019A&A...629A..75B or 2019ApJ...883L..29L. - SR-16 | medium | attribution | “2902 MHz in 2018ApJ...859...35G”; pipeline example “2019MNRAS.490.5860S”; first-order example “2019ApJ...870...80L” | These papers have no supporting settings or methods entries in the supplied pack. | Remove these specific attributions; use the other supported bandwidth, pipeline and fit-order examples.
- SR-17 | medium | unsupported | “Over 200 cards … sum of the variant labels”; “papers almost never”; “Most cards” | Summed missing-setting labels count occurrences without establishing unique-card membership. - SR-18 | medium | unsupported | “486 of the 656 papers”; “about 115”; “about 230 … 30”; “about 50 … 20” | These denominators and paper counts are not supplied by the aggregates. The parameter section repeats bibcodes and ends with “…222 more,” preventing verification of the claimed census. | Remove the counts or provide an auditable unique-bibcode tally, inclusion rules and treatment of papers using multiple methods.
- SR-19 | medium | unsupported | “Parameter defaults”; “Fit order: 1”; fixed disk and galaxy exclusion choices | The pack establishes heterogeneous, source-specific examples, not transferable numerical defaults. In particular, line richness alone does not establish the appropriate fit order or mask width. | Rename these as reported examples; select masks from the actual line extent and fit complexity from bandwidth, continuum behaviour and validation.
- SR-20 | medium | science | “Calibrate, then self-calibrate … before subtracting” | This unconditional instruction omits feasibility and improvement checks. 2024ApJ...961..228S explicitly reports attempted self-calibration without improvement. | Make self-calibration conditional on adequate constraints and validated improvement; retain calibrated data when it fails.
- SR-21 | medium | science | “Continuum subtraction error: 10%”; “Add a term … (10%)” | The pack supports 10% in one paper, without defining a universal percentage or its applicability to another measurement. A fitted baseline also introduces correlated errors across channels. | Estimate subtraction uncertainty through alternative masks/models or propagated fit uncertainty; define the affected quantity and keep flux-calibration uncertainty separate.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “per-pixel STATCONT”; image-plane alternatives | 2021A&A...656A..46M explicitly convolves cubes to a common beam before per-pixel subtraction. The pack also identifies imcontsub to mitigate the JvM effect in 2024AJ....167...66Y and uvcontsub over-subtraction in 2024ApJ...977..251H. | Add checks for beam consistency and residual/restored-image flux scaling before image-plane fitting or comparing subtraction domains.
- SR-23 | medium | omission | “before combining”; cross-SPW continuum fitting | 2021A&A...648A..19W checks execution flux consistency; 2021A&A...656A..46M reports tuning-dependent line-flux scaling anomalies and excluded calibration uncertainty. These checks are missing from the workflow. | Validate execution/tuning flux scales and cross-SPW model consistency before combining or extrapolating continuum fits.
- SR-24 | medium | omission | “Optical depth needs the continuum level” | The pack also identifies beam-filling assumptions: 2019MNRAS.485..229R gives lower-limit optical depths for filling factor one. 2016ApJ...820L..25Y distinguishes unsubtracted CO from subtracted HCO+ and assumes optically thick CO plus continuum. | Add covering-factor and coupled line/continuum radiative-transfer caveats; subtraction does not recover intrinsic emission hidden by opacity.
- SR-25 | medium | omission | “2019Natur.570...83M: iterative … subtraction per spw” | Its processing entry includes a subsequent SPW-alignment correction by subtracting \(a\nu+b\), omitted from the described procedure. | Include this paper-specific additional operation and check overlap consistency when stitching windows; do not attribute its result solely to uv subtraction.
- SR-26 | medium | omission | “Ask for the exact line-free ranges … fit order” | Cards repeatedly flag CASA/pipeline versions and spectral-frame/rest-frequency settings as missing; these are necessary to interpret channel selections and task behaviour. | Require software versions, input dataset/SPW mapping, channel indexing, spectral frame/rest frequency and the actual subtraction configuration in the reproducibility record.
- SR-27 | high | usability | “flag line peaks”; “Image the continuum” after subtraction | The workflow does not preserve separate continuum and line inputs. Following it on one dataset can leave science line channels flagged or produce a continuum image from nearly zero continuum-subtracted data. | Use selections or a continuum-only copy for line exclusion; retain unflagged calibrated line data and image continuum from the unsubtracted calibrated dataset.
- SR-28 | medium | usability | `fitorder=0|1` | In executable Python, `0|1` evaluates to `1`; it does not express a choice. | Show separate calls or use a variable explicitly assigned the selected integer.

## Notes
The skill captures several useful validation strategies and limitations.
Its main problems are unsupported paper-specific detail and converting heterogeneous examples into defaults.
Bibliography membership alone does not substantiate a methods claim.
The supplied pack does not establish the stated CASA API transition version; verify that separately before release.
