## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “tau = -ln(1 - I/B(T)) (2019ApJ...880...69Y)” | That paper’s pack step 4 uses `B(Td) − B(Tbg)`, with Tbg = 2.73 K; the skill silently drops the background term. | Restore the cited equation; label neglecting the background as an approximation. Require consistent specific-intensity units, a valid logarithm argument, and state the beam-filling and scattering assumptions.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “Ellipse fits also give … eccentricity (2019AJ....158..162F)” | The pack describes a visibility-space constant-surface-density ring fit, followed by eccentricity from the star–ring offset; independent ellipse fits were to HST data. | Attribute the ALMA geometry to visibility modelling and distinguish the HST cross-check.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “7 use 100 (pack lines)” | The key-parameter section contains nine distinct papers explicitly using 100 walkers, plus 2017ApJ...839...86H using about 100. Two listed examples instead use 24 walkers (2025ApJ...984L..19Y) and 1024 (2019ApJ...881...84S). | Correct the count, specify whether approximate values count, and replace the incompatible examples.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Visibility binning … Papers … 4” | At least five papers report binning: the four listed examples plus 2018ApJ...869L..48G, whose pack entry specifies 10 kλ steps. | Count all qualifying papers consistently or label the four as selected examples.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Flux calibration error … 5–10%” | The cited 2018ApJ...869L..41A entry says relative flux scales agree within 5%; it does not establish a 5% absolute calibration uncertainty. | Separate relative scale agreement from absolute calibration errors; retain the supported 10% examples.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Dust temperature (mass) … 10–60 K” | The pack’s 2014ApJ...791L...6W mass assumptions explicitly include 80 K for the inner ring and 40 K for the outer ring. | Include 80 K and identify which temperatures belong to mass estimates.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “alpha = ln(I1/I2)/ln(nu1/nu2) (2016Natur.535..258C)” | The formula is correct for consistently ordered frequencies, but that bibcode’s pack entry reverses the frequency ratio relative to the flux ratio. | Preserve the correct formula and cite 2020ApJ...891...48H, whose excerpt supports it; flag the other pack entry’s sign inconsistency.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “DSHARP definitions … from its card” | The supplied pack does not substantiate the claimed 0.1 au interpolation, 95% flux radius, 1 au annuli, beam-neglect statement, factor-two optical-depth uncertainty, one-degree geometry agreement, or autocorrelation criterion for 2018ApJ...869L..42H. | Supply supporting pack entries or remove these attributions pending verification; do not treat inaccessible cards as supplied evidence.
- SR-09 | medium | unsupported | “recount … over those 214 papers’ tagged analysis steps” | The complete tagged steps are absent. Counts such as emcee 58, CASA 51, Gaussian rings 37, frank mentions 11, and missing-setting totals 165/158/128 cannot be reproduced from the provided aggregates and excerpts. | Supply a per-paper count ledger or retain only supported pack aggregates with their stated counting basis.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “Common choice … 5000 steps, about 2000 burn-in, keep the last 1000” | These settings are supported as examples, principally 2018ApJ...869...17L, but the pack does not establish this combined recipe as a common default. Geometry and ring-location priors are likewise study-specific. | Label these as example configurations; choose run length from convergence diagnostics and priors from the target and model.
- SR-11 | high | science | “Rescale visibility weights before fitting … weights down by about 2–4” | The cited factors are dataset-specific corrections. Moreover, 2018ApJ...869L..46D multiplies profile errors by sqrt(70); it is not a visibility-weight correction. The pack gives f ≈ 2 for 2024ApJ...976..132H without defining its direction here. | Check uncertainty calibration empirically and rescale only when justified. Separate image/profile error inflation from visibility weights and define each factor’s convention.
- SR-12 | high | science | “gap radius = 4 R_Hill” | The pack repeats this shorthand in 2018ApJ...869...17L, but the skill leaves “gap radius” indistinguishable from the stellocentric gap location defined earlier. Substituting that orbital radius produces an invalid planet-mass estimate. | Define the exact gap-size or separation observable used by the prescription and distinguish it from orbital radius; give the Hill-radius equation and conversion convention.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “flag widths below [the beam] as limits” | A width below the image beam is not automatically unresolved in a visibility fit; recoverability also depends on S/N, sampling and model assumptions. This blanket rule conflicts with the proposed injection/recovery validation. | Assign limits when the data fail to constrain the intrinsic width; assess sub-beam claims with recovery tests and model sensitivity.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “rotate by PA, stretch … by 1/cos i” | This prescription requires approximately planar emission and becomes unusable near edge-on. The pack includes edge-on debris models (2015A&A...581A..97S; 2016ApJ...823..108B) and warns that symmetry and moderate resolution limit line-surface geometry (2021A&A...645A.145G). | State the thin-disk applicability condition and route edge-on, vertically extended or warped emission to appropriate forward modelling.
- SR-15 | high | omission | “Kanagawa gap-depth formula for gas gaps” | The skill does not require converting line brightness to gas column/surface density before applying a gas-gap relation. The HL Tau pack requires constant HCO+ abundance between 17–37 au, excitation/opacity modelling and a vertically isothermal disk; it also flags inner-bin biases. | Require those assumptions and checks explicitly; distinguish gas depletion from continuum contrast and define whether depth is a ratio or its reciprocal.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “alpha_r/St = [(w_g/w_d)^2 − 1]^-1” | The cited 2025A&A...697A..64V uses Gaussian widths of model dust surface-density rings and bounds gas width between Hg,temp and the ring–gap separation. These operational requirements are missing from the main workflow. | Define both widths consistently, include the gas-width bounds, require w_g > w_d, and distinguish brightness width from surface-density width.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “stochastic variational inference in pyro” | The pack explicitly says 2024ApJ...976..132H approximates the posterior as multivariate normal and admits SVI is approximate. The skill reports posterior samples without this qualification. | State the approximation and require checks for missed modes, restrictive posterior shape and optimizer/initialization sensitivity.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “radial spectral index, optical depth … physical conversion” | The pack warns that temperature, calibration and degeneracies prevent precise grain-size measurements (2020ApJ...891...48H), and that two bands constrain four parameters only through priors, with porosity unconstrained (2024ApJ...971..129C). | Explicitly prohibit unique grain-size or optical-depth inference from two-band spectral index alone; report prior and thermal dependence.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “fit dirty images with chi2” | The workflow omits the required observational response for these fits. The pack explicitly applies primary-beam attenuation and synthetic-beam convolution in 2017MNRAS.466.4201W, and primary-beam attenuation before Fourier sampling in 2015ApJ...798..124R. | Specify the primary-beam convention and forward-model through the appropriate dirty beam or observed uv sampling before comparison.
- SR-20 | medium | usability | “rms/(2 sqrt(n))”; “innermost bin equal to the beam” | `n` is undefined, making the uncertainty prescription unactionable. For 2019ApJ...880...69Y, the pack specifies the innermost bin’s radius as 0.1 arcsec, while bin width remains 0.05 arcsec. | Define and substantiate `n` before using that estimator; distinguish bin centre/radius from bin width and retain covariance between correlated bins.
