# Codex review: star-formation-laws

## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “ULIRG-like 0.8–1.1 in at least 8” | The pack explicitly documents seven unique bibcodes: 2014ApJ...790..160S, 2017MNRAS.469.4683C, 2017A&A...604A.117C, 2017ApJ...846..108C, 2019MNRAS.489..427I, 2020A&A...641A.151S and 2020MNRAS.497.2771M. Repeated entries are not additional papers. | Report seven explicit numeric adoptions or identify supported evidence for an eighth.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “alpha_CO (Galactic, incl. He) = 4.35 … 2025ApJ...988..162D” | That paper’s pack assumption states “Radially varying alpha_CO”; 4.35 is specifically “for sensitivity estimate.” | Separate the analysis prescription from the sensitivity calculation.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “dust-based vs CO-based gas within 0.2 dex … 2024A&A...692A.215C” | The 0.2 dex statement belongs to 2026ApJ..1001..131C. The 2024 paper’s validation says only “generally consistent” and flags excitation-relation scatter. | Attribute the numerical bound only to the 2026 paper.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “scikit-learn … 2 … GMM split of KS points”; “astropy / reproject … 2024ApJ...976...88L” | Software aggregates establish name frequencies, not procedure-specific counts or paper assignments. Only 2019ApJ...887...88E explicitly documents the KS GMM; the 2024 excerpt names astropy, not reproject. | Distinguish aggregate software mentions from demonstrated uses; remove the unsupported reproject assignment.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Resolution of resolved KS … 34 pc to 1.5 kpc” | The pack identifies 34 pc as an aperture **radius** for 2021A&A...656A..60A, not resolution. The excerpt for 2025ApJ...988..162D explicitly gives 1.7 kpc resolution. | Separate beam resolution, aperture radius/diameter and sampling spacing; correct the stated coverage.
- SR-06 | medium | unsupported | “per its card”; “least squares on binned data … slope fixed to 1”; “double power law … 10,000 bootstraps”; “three scale-height prescriptions” | These details for 2017A&A...602L...9M, 2019ApJ...882....5W and 2018ApJ...861L..18U are absent from the supplied pack. Listing a paper’s title does not establish its method. | Remove these claims pending inclusion of supporting card content.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “Authors call alpha_CO the dominant systematic” | The cited pack entries describe an “additional systematic uncertainty” (2025ApJ...995..151Y), factor ≥2 uncertainty (2021ApJ...908...54W), and approximately 50% uncertainty (2018ApJ...860..103S). They do not establish dominance over other errors. | Say “a substantial systematic” and preserve the paper-specific uncertainty estimates.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “SFR uncertainty floor … factor 2” | 2018ApJ...863..193V adopts a factor-two uncertainty; 2019Natur.569..519K adopts 20%; 2015MNRAS.453L..83M includes a template-derived L_IR uncertainty. The pack does not establish a common minimum uncertainty. | Relabel these as reported uncertainties, and require a tracer-specific error budget.
- SR-09 | high | science | “Divide all extensive quantities by the same mu” | This general instruction conflicts with the pack warning that differential magnification may affect line ratios (2016A&A...586L...7B). Gas and SFR need not share magnification. | Use tracer-specific magnifications where constrained; otherwise propagate differential-magnification uncertainty and label the shared-mu assumption.
- SR-10 | high | science | “CO(2-1)/CO(1-0) = 3” | For 2020A&A...641A.151S, the excerpt explicitly specifies the **flux** ratio, f_CO(2-1)/f_CO(1-0). It is not the brightness-temperature luminosity ratio used elsewhere as R21/r21. | Label it S21Δv/S10Δv = 3; the corresponding L′ ratio is approximately 0.75.
- SR-11 | high | science | “use a method that handles errors on both axes and upper limits (linmix)” | Standard linmix censoring applies to the dependent variable. In the stated KS equation, CO/gas limits are on the independent variable. Moreover, censoring alone does not correct detection-based truncation. The pack distinguishes line-luminosity limits in 2020A&A...641A.155V and CO selection in 2025ApJ...995..151Y. | Specify the censored axis and use an appropriate censoring and selection likelihood; do not imply linmix directly handles every gas/SFR limit configuration.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “Free-free and recombination lines … 8 um PAH … 160 um” | PAH and far-IR dust emission are neither free-free emission nor recombination lines. The pack describes 2019ApJ...887...88E as an 8 µm PAH calibration and 2019ApJ...876..112R as a 160 µm mapping. | Move these to separate PAH/dust tracer categories.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “X_CO … 2e20 with He” | Although inherited from the pack shorthand for 2019A&A...623A..66B, X_CO converts CO intensity to H2 column density. Helium belongs in the mass conversion, not the H2 column definition. | Define X_CO without helium; explicitly mark alpha_CO or the surface-mass coefficient as including helium, and apply 1.36 only once.
- SR-14 | high | omission | “Build the SFR”; “Build the gas”; “Fit the relation” | No safeguard prevents circular KS tests. The pack explicitly derives SFR from an assumed KS law in 2014ApJ...790..160S and warns that gas masses rely on assumed MS SFE in 2019MNRAS.485.2092C. | Record derivation provenance and exclude relation-derived quantities from independent tests of that same relation.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “H-alpha or Pa-alpha … correction” | The skill omits the substantial residual attenuation problem: 2020A&A...635A.119C reports a remaining factor 3±1 discrepancy after the Calzetti correction. For 2020ApJS..247...61F, f = 0.44 gives likely upper-limit H-alpha luminosities and is the largest measurement uncertainty. | Require the nebular attenuation prescription and its uncertainty; cross-check obscured SFR rather than assuming corrected H-alpha recovers the total.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Global compact sources: Sigma = (SFR/2)/(pi R_e^2)” | Matching denominators does not ensure matching enclosed gas and SFR. A continuum half-light radius encloses half the assumed continuum-traced SFR, not necessarily half the gas. The cited formulas establish Sigma_SFR, not a universal gas enclosure fraction. | Define both enclosed fractions: Sigma_SFR = f_SFR SFR/A and Sigma_gas = f_gas M_gas/A; measure or justify each.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Area for global Sigma … R from FWHM/2 or HWHM” | No explicit size-validation check accompanies this recipe. The pack flags uncertain sizes from sparse uv coverage (2014A&A...562A..67G), marginal resolution (2013ApJ...770...13W), and sizes biased high at S/N <10 (2017ApJ...844..123F). | Require beam-deconvolved size validation, propagate area uncertainty, and retain surface-density limits for unresolved sources.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “regrid to about one beam per sample”; “two grids offset by 0.5 beam FWHM” | Regridding does not create independent measurements. The pack’s offset grids and 200 pc apertures on a 100 pc grid (2025A&A...700A.285W) explicitly imply overlapping samples. | Account for spatial covariance or use spatial/block resampling; do not count overlapping apertures as independent constraints.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “Heterogeneous comparison samples … and small samples … can drive slopes” | This misses explicit selection biases: PACS selection favors upper-MS, warm-dust systems in 2020A&A...641A.155V; N2 selection favors high metallicity and gas fraction in 2025ApJ...994....4A. | Document parent-sample selection and restrict comparisons or model selection effects.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “Eddington-limited lower limit of about 650” | The pack derives this under optically thick emission, gas fraction unity, dust-to-gas ratio 1/90 and a global dust temperature (2019ApJ...876..130H). Those assumptions are missing beside the comparison. | Label 650 as a model-dependent result for that analysis and retain its assumptions.
- SR-21 | high | usability | “t_dep = Sigma_mol/Sigma_SFR” | The skill uses gas densities in Msun/pc² and SFR densities in Msun/yr/kpc². Direct numerical division without area-unit conversion introduces a factor of 10^6. | State t_dep[yr] = 10^6 Sigma_mol/Sigma_SFR, or t_dep[Gyr] = 10^-3 Sigma_mol/Sigma_SFR, for those units.
- SR-22 | medium | usability | “1.40e-10 L_IR”; “3.2e-3 I24 + 8.1e-2 I_FUV”; “log Sigma_SFR = 0.844 log Sigma_L8 - 30.923” | Several actionable calibrations omit input/output units. The logarithmic PAH intercept is particularly unit-dependent. | Specify luminosity units, map-intensity units and output surface-density units beside every calibration; leave any unit convention absent from the pack explicitly unverified.

## Notes

The core equations and most quoted numerical results are supported.
The main risks are unsupported card additions, ambiguous conversion conventions, and defaults generalized beyond their evidence.
Mechanical verification of quotations does not establish that a practice is universal or scientifically appropriate.
