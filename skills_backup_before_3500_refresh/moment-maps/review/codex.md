# Codex review: moment-maps
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “High-z: … 2019ApJ...881...10B”; “Nearby galaxies: … 2018PASJ...70...85T” | The pack identifies these as NGC 3258 and the Galactic Center CND, respectively. Their channel widths are correctly copied but assigned to the wrong populations. | Reclassify both examples; retain 2019ApJ...881...63N and 2020MNRAS.493.4294B as high-z examples.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “3D forward modelling of the cube … 2025ApJ...988...72S” | That card’s step 5 explicitly describes “3DBarolo on moment-1,” followed by a tanh rotation curve and moment-1 subtraction. It does not support attributing cube forward modelling to this analysis. | Describe the paper’s actual workflow separately from a general recommendation for cube modelling.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “48-64 walkers, 3000-3500 steps … burn-in 500 to half” | 2024ApJ...974..102S reports 3000 burn-in steps **then another 3000 steps**; 2020ApJ...891...48H reports 3500 total with 500 discarded. The table mixes total and retained lengths. | State walkers, burn-in, total steps and retained steps separately for each example.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Radial profiles … binned with at least 10 samples per bin in 2021A&A...655A..80D” | The quoted parameter concerns binning the **2σ contours**, with angular ranges ≥60°, rather than the azimuthally averaged radial profiles. | Attach the ten-sample requirement to contour/extent measurements; remove it from the radial-profile prescription.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Clip velocity maps on peak S/N: 2.5 … 3.5 … 4 sigma” | Only 2026A&A...705A.198M explicitly identifies a peak-significance cut. The cited 2020MNRAS.493L.108B, 2020ApJ...903..145L and 2020A&A...640A.104A entries state clipping thresholds without establishing this peak-based interpretation. | Distinguish voxel clipping from spatial blanking by peak S/N; mark unspecified semantics explicitly.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “disk papers mostly use … quadratic”; “bettermoments 31” | The aggregate counts software named in matched steps, not disk velocity estimators. It cannot establish a majority; the cited 2024ApJ...963....1O is a galaxy-cluster study. | Say quadratic velocities occur in the named disk examples; remove the majority claim and distinguish software counts from method frequencies.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Upper-limit significance … 1-3 sigma” | For 2024A&A...682A.166F, “1 sigma” describes the **assumed Gaussian peak**, not a demonstrated integrated-flux confidence level. The 2024A&A...691A.178B entry only supplies a non-detection threshold. | Separate peak-amplitude assumptions, detection thresholds and integrated-limit confidence; remove unsupported significance/count assignments.
- SR-08 | low | attribution | “2D Gaussian fits (`imfit` … 2014ApJ...784...78R)” | That card supports a 2D Gaussian PA measurement but does not name its fitting software. | Cite it for the Gaussian method only; use 2020A&A...644A..84M for the explicit CASA `imfit` attribution.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “model velocity field and residual maps … 2018ApJ...869...59W” | That card validates a fit to high-velocity **channel maps**, including residual channel maps and continuum geometry. It does not document the stated moment-1 validation. | Describe channel-map residuals for this citation; retain 2020ApJ...890..167T for velocity-field comparison.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “Smoothing for mask: beam FWHM spatial + Hanning … 4 [papers]” | 2018ApJ...865...13T leaves the spectral kernel width unspecified; 2020A&A...640A.104A states two-channel smoothing without identifying Hanning or beam-scale spatial smoothing. | List the supported kernels paper by paper; reserve explicit Hanning attribution for 2019MNRAS.484.4239R and 2025MNRAS.541.2540Z.
- SR-11 | medium | unsupported | “Moment 8 … for compact or blended species (2019ApJ...871..112X)” | That card describes maps of selected **uncontaminated transitions** and warns that spatial contamination prevents reliable AcA morphology. It does not endorse peak maps as a treatment for blending. | Require uncontaminated velocity intervals or spectral decomposition before assigning a peak map to a species.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “unresolved or barely resolved source … moment 0 only as a cross-check” | 2026A&A...705A..81K and 2024A&A...682A.166F demonstrate aperture spectra and Gaussian fitting, but do not establish this categorical restriction. | Present aperture spectra and moment-0 integration as alternatives whose aperture, window, noise and recovered flux must be validated.
- SR-13 | medium | unsupported | “JvM correction for multi-configuration disk cubes” | The pack documents one application and robustness test in 2024ApJ...974..102S, not a correction required solely by configuration count. | Make application conditional on residual/restoring-beam normalization; derive the correction for the actual imaging product and document its noise implications.
- SR-14 | high | science | “For an aperture … sigma_rms sqrt(npts) times the pixel area” | 2019PASJ...71...85M gives this expression for a **molecular-mass noise map**. Transplanting it to a Jy/beam moment map omits beam normalization and spatial covariance. | Specify map units; convert summed Jy/beam pixels using pixel/beam area, and propagate spatial covariance or measure errors with matching blank apertures.
- SR-15 | high | science | “Upper limits … rms times number of independent channels” | The pack reports this for the specific CO-shifted, CO-masked extraction in 2018ApJ...853..147M. As portable advice, it omits channel-width units and explicit significance and conflicts with the preceding independent-channel √N rule. | Preserve it as a paper-specific report. For general limits use \(k\,\Delta v\sqrt{\sum_{ij}C_{ij}}\), with channel flux-density covariance \(C\), and state the assumed width and extraction.
- SR-16 | high | science | “moment-0 masses from [thick lines] are lower limits” | This is conditional on the mass estimator. The pack explicitly warns that Galactic αCO may **overestimate** mass in 2025ApJ...988...72S. The quoted 2014ApJ...783L..13P lower-limit discussion concerns vortex mass from continuum emission. | Restrict the lower-limit statement to appropriate optically thin conversions applied to opaque emission; discuss empirical αCO uncertainties separately.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Attach uncertainties and limits” | The section omits absolute flux-scale uncertainty, despite the aggregate flags and explicit exclusions in 2021A&A...656A..46M and 2020ApJ...892...99C. | Report statistical and calibration uncertainties separately, with relevant continuum-subtraction and fixed-parameter systematics.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “Upper limits … 2018ApJ...853..147M” | The skill omits the required assumption that other molecules share CO’s spectro-spatial distribution, the detected-CO filtering validation and the agnostic unfiltered limits. It also omits the aperture/binning checks in 2024A&A...682A.166F. | Require those assumptions and validation checks whenever adopting these limit recipes.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “Always report: velocity window …” | Velocity metadata and geometric conventions are missing. The pack includes radio Doppler convention (2018ApJ...866..117C), barycentric velocities (2020ApJ...892...99C), LSR velocities, and an inclination defined with edge-on = 0 (2019ApJ...873...73Z). | Record rest frequency, velocity frame, Doppler convention, systemic reference and geometric conventions; normalize imported mask/model parameters.
- SR-20 | medium | usability | “moment 2 (line width)” | “Line width” leaves σ versus FWHM ambiguous. The pack explicitly distinguishes FWHM = 342 km/s from σ = 145 km/s in 2018ApJ...865...13T and lists instrumental-broadening corrections among fitting practices. | Define moment 2 as intensity-weighted velocity dispersion; distinguish Gaussian FWHM and account for spectral response before interpreting intrinsic dispersion.

## Notes
The masking examples and most explicit parameter values are well grounded.
The main risks are changing the meaning of thresholds, transferring paper-specific uncertainty recipes, and promoting examples into universal defaults.
Mechanical quote verification does not validate those transfers.
