# Codex review: gmc-decomposition-virial
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “Range seen … 4.35–8.4; X 2.0e20–3.0e20” | These are not pack-wide ranges: 2018ApJ...867..165B states X_CO = 4.35e20; 2020MNRAS.499.2534K derives 5.5e20; 2015Natur.525..218R derives alpha_CO = 28 ± 28. | Separate adopted conversions from inferred results, identify the selected sample, and give ranges consistent with it.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “ignore surface pressure and magnetic terms … 2016ApJ...833..209O” | That pack assumption states “uniform density sphere; magnetic fields neglected”; it does not establish omission of surface pressure. | Attribute each omitted term separately; cite this paper only for neglected magnetic fields.
- SR-03 | low | unsupported | “ODR (scipy)” | The software aggregate names ODR, and 2018ApJ...857...19F describes its fitting semantics; neither identifies SciPy. | Remove the implementation attribution or label SciPy as a recommended implementation.
- SR-04 | low | unsupported | “A high threshold was chosen there … in weak lines” | The 2017PASJ...69...18T entry supplies the 8σ threshold and 4σ increment, but gives no such rationale. | Remove the attributed rationale.
- SR-05 | high | science | “CO: M_lum = (alpha_CO / R21) L_CO” | This is specific to CO(2–1) luminosity with a CO(1–0) conversion. The skill also covers other transitions: 2025A&A...699A.369V explicitly uses L_CO(1–0) and R32/10 ≈ 2. | Write M = alpha_CO,10 L′_J/R_J1, define the brightness-temperature luminosity ratio, and supply transition-specific examples and helium conventions.
- SR-06 | high | science | “With a density profile … use … corrections” | This combines incompatible alpha normalizations without explaining their thresholds. In 2020ApJ...894...87C, alpha remains 5σ²R/(GM) = 2aE_K/|E_G|, with alpha_cr = 2a. Applying a profile-corrected coefficient and retaining 2a changes the binding criterion incorrectly. | Distinguish alpha_5 from alpha_E = 2E_K/|E_G| = alpha_5/a. State equilibrium and binding thresholds for each; keep the cited k, a and f definitions separate.
- SR-07 | high | science | “Mean molecular mass … mu = 2.33 … 2.33–2.8” | These are different quantities, not interchangeable choices: 2020ApJ...894...87C specifies mu_p = 2.33, while 2020ApJ...890...76N specifies mu_H2 = 2.8. | Separate mass per free particle for sound speed from mass per H2 molecule for column-to-mass conversion. For thermal support, explicitly subtract tracer thermal variance and add bulk-gas thermal variance.
- SR-08 | medium | omission | “Measure R, sigma_v and L” | The workflow omits component separation before assigning linewidths and masses. The pack describes two-component fits and DCO+ component identification in 2020ApJ...894...87C, and Gaussian treatment of blended clumps in 2025A&A...699A.369V. | Require spectrum inspection, component matching to the mass aperture, and flags for blends before computing alpha.
- SR-09 | medium | omission | “t_ff from the mean density … (2018PASJ...70...73H)” | That paper’s step 3 explicitly includes a factor 1.26 in its free-fall-time prescription; the skill provides neither that convention nor an executable density prescription. | Give the general t_ff formula and adopted volume geometry, and flag the paper-specific factor without guessing its placement from the abbreviated card.
- SR-10 | medium | omission | “Associate HII regions to GMCs” | The M83 entry also splits SFR by mass among multiple associated GMCs, tests IMF sampling and stellar ages, and admits an approximately factor-two SFR underestimate. These are missing despite the skill’s SFE_ff scope. | Specify treatment of multiple associations and require an SFR uncertainty budget covering these effects.
- SR-11 | medium | omission | “Not stated in cards … Write all of them down” | This checklist omits prominent pack gaps: continuum subtraction/line-free channels (11), CASA version (9), flux-scale uncertainty (7), and primary-beam correction details (4), alongside other separately worded entries. | Add continuum treatment, primary-beam/noise handling, calibration uncertainty, software versions, cell size, and spectral response/smoothing to the required provenance.
- SR-12 | medium | usability | “X(1-0) = 2.4, X(2-1) = 3” | These bare values are not usable conversion factors. The 2024ApJ...966...51G pack entry itself leaves their unit scale unspecified. | Mark the scale as unavailable in the pack and require verification before use; do not infer a missing multiplier.
- SR-13 | medium | usability | “Clouds below about one beam … use a fixed-scale … analysis instead” | The 2018ApJ...860..172S entry supplies an assumed depth and profile; it does not establish that a beam-sized analysis recovers an unresolved cloud’s radius or binding state. | Explain that this produces properties of an assumed beam volume, with consistently matched mass and linewidth, and does not measure the compact object’s alpha.

## Notes
Most quoted settings and reported results track the pack closely.
The pressure expression in the skill is dimensionally consistent; the pack’s abbreviated expression containing a multiplicative k should not replace it.
Software aggregate counts should remain distinct from counts of unique papers.
