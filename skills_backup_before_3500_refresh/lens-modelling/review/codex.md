# Codex review: lens-modelling
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “about 35”; “12 on-topic”; “SIE in ~18”; “~7 Sérsic”; “10 [cosmologies]” | These paper-level counts are not reproducible from the supplied aggregates and partial excerpts. The pack establishes 61 papers and software/tag counts, not these methodological censuses. | Remove these counts or supply an explicit bibcode census with supporting pack entries.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “PyAutoLens / AutoLens … uv-plane parametric then Voronoi pixelated sources” | This description incorrectly covers 2019A&A...624A.138Y: its pack entry describes an image-plane sl_fit model cross-checked with PyAutoLens, without establishing a visibility fit or Voronoi grid for that check. | Describe capabilities separately from each cited paper’s demonstrated workflow.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “50 realisations weighted by likelihood” | 2020ApJ...905..152D explicitly says “weighted by the log of the likelihood,” which is a different operation. | Report the stated log-likelihood weighting faithfully; flag its implementation as needing clarification before recommending reproduction.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “emcee run … chain 5×10^5 (Glee) … 2 [papers]” | 2015ApJ...811..115W supplies a Glee MCMC chain length but does not identify emcee; its card explicitly flags missing sampler details. | Separate the emcee configuration of 2019ApJ...873...50D from the sampler-unspecified Glee chain.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “SIE free parameters … 5–12 with shear/multipoles … 4 (stated)” | The four examples mix distinct models: 2020A&A...641L...2M has eight parameters for a softened ellipsoid including source position; 2018A&A...613A..34P has a power-law primary, satellite and shear. These are not comparable SIE-only parameter counts. | List each model’s components and distinguish lens parameters from source parameters.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Image-position error … 0.02–0.03 arcsec” | The table omits documented exceptions: 2015PASJ...67...72T uses 100–200 mas for blended knots and 130–200 mas for HST images; 2016MNRAS.457.2936I uses 6×8 mas for clumps. The asserted 0.02 radial/0.05 tangential values are absent from the supplied pack. | Include the supported exceptions, distinguish clump and deflector errors, and remove the unsupported anisotropic values.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Fix the lens from the continuum … red/blue halves … 2026A&A...705A..81K” | That paper’s pack says “line channels only” and describes parametric lens modelling of each red/blue bin. Also, 2019ApJ...873...50D jointly fits continuum and channels with shared lens parameters. | Present continuum-fixed, joint shared-lens, and independently fitted diagnostic models as separate documented workflows.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “47 SPT sources”; “noise and measurement are well understood”; “amplitude rescaling and astrometric offset per dataset” | These details attributed to 2016ApJ...826..112S do not appear in its supplied pack material, which establishes cosmology and source-parameterisation systematics. | Remove these paper-specific assertions unless supporting evidence is added to the pack.
- SR-09 | medium | attribution | “Glee vs glafic”; “reconstruct beams … 2015ApJ...806L..17S”; gradient regularisation and resolution tests attributed to 2018Sci...361.1016S | The supplied entries do not establish these software comparisons or methods. For example, the 2018Sci entry establishes averaging limits, an SIE assumption and bimodality, but not the claimed regularisation or resolution test. | Retain only supported examples or add the missing method excerpts.
- SR-10 | medium | attribution | “Magnifications reported span … 1.3 … to ~58” | As an unrestricted pack-wide range, this excludes the ~450 HST magnification explicitly reported in 2020ApJ...891...64F. Its ALMA estimate also has a stated 2σ range of 18–117. | Specify that 1.3–58 describes selected ALMA central estimates, and report the optical value and uncertainty range separately.
- SR-11 | medium | unsupported | “SIE, usually with external shear, is the default”; “MCMC almost everywhere”; “gradient is the most common form” | The pack provides examples, but no census supporting these prevalence claims. Several entries use other profiles or optimisation methods, and regularisation forms are often unstated. | Replace prevalence language with “documented choices include” and retain the examples.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “Parameter defaults … Common choice” | Source-specific priors, chain lengths, bin widths and the 80 μJy/beam pixsrc adjustment are presented as transferable defaults. The pack documents individual applications, not generally validated settings. | Rename this column “Reported example”; require dataset-specific justification and convergence or sensitivity checks.
- SR-13 | high | science | “magnification … ratio of source-plane to image-plane flux” | This reverses the flux magnification convention and conflicts with the skill’s subsequent demagnification rule. The 2015MNRAS.451L..40R card repeats this ordering, but a verified quotation does not establish a correct formula. The pack’s 2018A&A...610A..53M setting explicitly gives μ = F_LP/F_SP. | Define positive flux magnification as F_image/F_source; distinguish signed image magnifications used for parity.
- SR-14 | high | science | “intrinsic … size or surface density … divide by mu” | Surface density does not generally receive a blanket 1/μ correction: matched extensive quantities and areas both change under lensing. The cited 2017A&A...597A..41G only documents F/μ and r/√μ. | Restrict division by μ to appropriate extensive quantities; derive surface densities from consistently reconstructed quantities and areas.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “sizes scale as r/sqrt(mu)” | The cited survey documents an effective-radius correction, not a universal correction for arbitrary lengths. Strong lensing stretches axes differently, particularly near caustics. | Limit this approximation to circularised effective radii under appropriate magnification assumptions; reconstruct axes, separations and resolved structure through the lens mapping.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “lens and beam do not rotate polarisation” | The validation in 2023Natur.621..483G concerns a simulated source with constant polarisation orientation and fraction. It does not establish invariance of integrated polarisation for spatially varying sources. | State the simulation’s assumptions and restrict the conclusion to that test.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Unreported settings … Report all of them” | This list covers lens settings but omits recurring pack requirements for line-free channels, continuum-subtraction details, exact velocity bins and zero points, imaging settings, calibration versions and flux-scale uncertainty. | Add a data-provenance checklist covering those inputs alongside the lens configuration.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “Flux recovery … check against single-dish totals” | Spatial flux loss is covered, but spectral truncation is missing. The pack records ~10% missing line flux in 2020ApJ...905..152D and bandwidth-limited emission and vmax in 2020ApJ...905...85S. | Check spectral coverage before interpreting recovered flux, line asymmetry or maximum velocity; distinguish missing channels from lensing effects.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “lens/source redshifts … otherwise assumed” | The pack documents source-redshift uncertainty propagated over z=1–3 in 2017A&A...597A..41G, uncertain lens redshifts in 2025ApJ...991...53C, and distinct deflector redshifts in 2019A&A...624A.138Y. The skill does not require assessing their effect on magnification or the lens geometry. | Record redshift assumptions, test their effect on inferred quantities, and explicitly choose how multiple deflector planes are treated.
- SR-20 | medium | usability | “fixed at ring centre with uniform prior” | This reproduces ambiguous wording in 2026A&A...705A..81K: an exactly fixed coordinate and a sampled coordinate with a nonzero-width uniform prior are different configurations. No prior bounds are supplied. | State that the pack cannot resolve this ambiguity; require explicit fixed coordinates or explicit sampled bounds.

## Notes
The skill captures many important systematic and differential-lensing warnings well.
The reversed magnification formula is the most urgent correction.
Keep reported settings distinct from recommended defaults, and mark missing evidence explicitly.
