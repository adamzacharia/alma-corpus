# Codex review: dust-continuum-mass
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “300 (dust-to-gas 0.0027)” for 2020ApJ...891..164S | The pack’s step 4 uses 0.0027 for continuum column densities, implying gas-to-dust ≈370; step 7 uses 300 for chemical simulations. These are different prescriptions. | Separate the continuum conversion from the chemical-model assumption in both tables.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “43% of single-dish flux recovered in 2016ApJ...832..148V” | The excerpt locates this loss in “Distribution of Molecular Gas” and explicitly discusses undetected molecular gas; it does not establish a continuum recovery fraction. | Label this as a CO missing-flux example; use 2014ApJ...787...48X for a measured continuum recovery fraction.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “kappa and T_d terms are larger than the flux-calibration term (2014ApJ...787...48X)” | The cited budget is 30% opacity, 15% flux calibration and approximately 10% temperature. The temperature term is smaller than calibration. | Quote the actual budget and avoid generalizing its ordering.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “factor five (2013ApJ...764L..14Z)” under “Opacity uncertainty” | The pack’s limitation says the **disk mass** is a lower limit with factor-five uncertainty; it does not attribute that entire uncertainty specifically to opacity. | Describe this as mass uncertainty, or retain the unattributed aggregate opacity check without assigning it to this bibcode.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “upper limits when warm dust dominates (2016MNRAS.457.4536W)” | That pack entry says only “upper limits due to the isothermal assumption.” Conversely, 2016ApJ...833L...6C explicitly associates warm dust dominating the SED peak with **underestimated** mass. | Remove the unsupported causal explanation and state each paper’s limitation separately.
- SR-06 | low | attribution | “High-z: mass-weighted 25 K” citing 2024ApJ...963....1O | Its pack entry states an assumed temperature of 25 K, without establishing a mass-weighted interpretation. That description is explicit for 2020ApJ...892...66M. | Attribute “mass-weighted” only to ASPECS; describe the other value as an assumed 25 K.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “Order of preference seen in the pack” and “Cores in clusters are usually defined with astrodendro or Clumpfind” | The cited cards supply examples, not a ranked temperature-selection policy or prevalence measurements for source-finding algorithms. | Present these as examples; label any recommended hierarchy as the skill author’s guidance and require temperatures appropriate to the emitting component.
- SR-08 | low | unsupported | “almost always custom code; no package dominates” | The software aggregates count named tools in matched steps; they do not establish how unnamed thin-formula calculations were implemented. | Replace with “The pack does not establish a dominant implementation of the thin formula.”
- SR-09 | high | science | “tau = -ln(1 - T_b/T_d)” | This requires the Rayleigh–Jeans approximation, unit filling factor, negligible background and negligible scattering. The skill itself warns that Rayleigh–Jeans is poor for relevant temperatures and frequencies. The 2018A&A...619A.161C excerpt supports a brightness-based check, not this unrestricted formula. | Use `tau = -ln[1 - ΔI_nu/{f(B_nu(T_d)-B_nu(T_bg))}]` under an absorption-only slab model; define the brightness convention and filling factor.
- SR-10 | high | science | “0.9 cm2/g (lambda/1.3 mm)^beta”, beta=1.5 | This reproduces the 2016ApJ...833..209O pack entry, but reverses the wavelength dependence relative to the skill’s `kappa ∝ nu^beta` convention. | Flag the source expression as requiring verification; use `(1.3 mm/lambda)^beta` for the stated positive-frequency-index convention.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “mu (per H2) … range 2.33 to 2.8” | Mean mass per H2 and mean mass per free particle are different quantities. The pack supplies `mu_H2=2.8` in 2019A&A...628A..27B, whereas other entries merely report `mu`; those values cannot automatically populate a per-H2 range. | Keep separate symbols and definitions; use an explicitly justified `mu_H2` in column and H2 number-density equations.
- SR-12 | medium | usability | “use … the (1+z) factor … and correct for the CMB” | The placement of the redshift factor and the treatment of CMB contrast are unspecified, leaving the high-z conversion ambiguous despite the explicit CMB treatment in 2020ApJ...892...66M. | Give `M_d = F_nu,obs D_L² / [(1+z) kappa_nu,rest {B_nu,rest(T_d(z))-B_nu,rest(T_CMB(z))}]`; define `nu_rest=(1+z)nu_obs` and distinguish contrast correction from CMB heating.
- SR-13 | high | omission | “convert an n-sigma flux limit to a mass limit” | These are conditional optically thin mass limits. The 2017ApJ...842....8M excerpt explicitly distinguishes an optically thin mass limit from an optically thick clump-size constraint; 2019MNRAS.488.1005P uses size-dependent RT models. | State that compact optically thick material can evade a thin mass limit; require optical-depth/size assumptions or RT before claiming an upper bound on total mass.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “Measure the flux” / “Convert n-sigma limits” | The workflow does not distinguish integrated Jy from peak Jy/beam or specify spatially varying noise after PB correction. The pack’s Fomalhaut limit is explicitly for a point source, and 2024ApJ...974..102S reports enhanced local gap noise. | Specify integrated flux for mass, beam-solid-angle conversion for surface brightness, local PB-consistent noise, and aperture/model-dependent limits for extended sources.
- SR-15 | high | omission | “Compare the image peak T_b with T_d before accepting ‘optically thin’” | Low observed brightness cannot establish optical thinness when the source is beam diluted or scattering suppresses intensity. The pack flags beam dilution in 2016Sci...353.1519P, excludes scattering-affected pixels in 2020A&A...644A..11L, and includes scattering in 2019MNRAS.488.1005P. | Require resolution/filling-factor and scattering checks; explicitly state that low beam-averaged brightness is inconclusive.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “n(H2) = M_gas / ((4/3) pi r^3 …)” | The spherical geometry is not stated beside the formula. The pack also uses an uncertain thickness profile in 2019ApJ...871..185L and labels density a lower limit for an unresolved core in 2014ApJ...795L..25R. | Label this as a spherical mean density; require a defined radius or alternative volume, propagate geometry uncertainty, and handle unresolved sizes as bounds.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | High-z mass workflow | No lensing correction is specified. The pack’s 2016ApJ...822...80S limitation explicitly identifies a single magnification correction as a simplification. | Require intrinsic flux or divide apparent mass by the applicable magnification, documenting differential-magnification uncertainty.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “SED fit … and use its dust mass” | SED-derived masses need compatible opacity/composition conventions before comparison. The pack explicitly records that 2025A&A...694A..82S divides CIGALE masses by two to match adopted dust composition and opacities. | Record the SED model’s dust conventions and reconcile them with the single-band calculation before comparing masses.

## Notes
The skill captures many useful caveats and reproduces much of the pack accurately.
Some pack entries themselves contain inconsistent conventions; mechanical quotation verification does not establish scientific correctness.
Merged software-label totals should be identified as sums unless distinct-paper overlap has been checked.
Provide bibcode membership lists for the independently counted parameter frequencies so those counts are reproducible.
