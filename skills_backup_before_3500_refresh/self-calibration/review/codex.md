# Codex review: alma-self-calibration
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “combine spws in the solve … 2015ApJ...812..126C” | That pack entry attributes combining both spectral windows to **ATCA Ka**, while ALMA Band 9 used antenna-based phase self-calibration. | Remove this ALMA example; retain 2020ApJ...898..146M.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “If it fails, go back to the pipeline calibration … 2020ApJ...898...10T” | This paper retained its successful 120 s phase round, which reduced noise by ~20%; subsequent rounds failed to improve it. | Say to retain the last validated calibration state; distinguish total failure from an unsuccessful additional round.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “use explicit ranges (2025A&A...696A.150S)” | The ALMAGAL limitation explicitly says “findcont conservative in line-rich sources; **no manual continuum ranges**.” It does not document manually replacing those ranges. | Present manual inspection/selection as an additional recommendation, not ALMAGAL’s reported procedure.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “simulations … did not bias the lens model” | 2013ApJ...767..132H’s validation says simultaneous fitting “does not bias the **antenna phase measurement**”; the pack does not establish unbiased lens parameters. | State the narrower demonstrated result.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “CASA `tclean` + `gaincal` + `applycal` … 2017ApJ...850L...2A” | Its key-parameter entry explicitly names **clean**, gaincal and applycal. | Use `clean`/`tclean` as appropriate, or cite a demonstrated tclean loop such as 2023PSJ.....4..142C.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Typical [phase] ladders … whole observation, 350 s, 60 s”; parameters table “p/ap (10 min)” | 2019AJ....157..251T explicitly lists the self-calibration solution type as unstated. The 2014ApJ...795..156W entry likewise gives 10 min without specifying phase/amplitude mode. Vega’s final round is described as amplitude, without establishing `calmode='ap'`. | Preserve unknown modes; do not infer phase-only or amplitude+phase from a generic self-calibration/amplitude description.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “First phase solint … whole track to 120 s”; “Final … 0.6 s to 2 min”; “Amplitude … 30 s to 1 h” | These bounds conflict with displayed entries: initial phase solutions at ~3–6 s in 2015ApJ...808...36M; final intervals of 240 s in 2016ApJ...829L..35T; amplitude+phase at 2.048 s in 2020ApJ...894L..14L. The 0.6 s MIRIAD interval has no stated phase-only mode. | Recalculate mode-specific ranges, retaining unknown classifications and distinguishing requested solint from integration duration.
- SR-08 | medium | attribution | “S/N to attempt … >3 to >=10 sigma … 3 [papers]” | 2016ApJ...823..160D supplies a **flux-density** selection (~3 mJy), not a sigma threshold. ALMAGAL’s S/N_selfcal and 2017ApJ...851...83C’s detection significance are also different quantities. | Separate flux selection, image detection significance, estimated self-cal S/N and gain-solution `minsnr`; define each before use.
- SR-09 | low | attribution | “63 + 36 + 13 + 12 cards”; “12 missing rounds” | The aggregate’s 12 category is “solution intervals **and** number of rounds.” These are category counts, with no demonstrated disjointness; other interval categories also exist. | Preserve the exact category labels and avoid presenting their sum as a unique-card total or exhaustive count.
- SR-10 | medium | unsupported | “Expect large gains for bright compact sources and modest (10–25%) gains for faint or extended targets” | The pack gives heterogeneous case results, not a validated prediction by brightness/morphology. Its extended HR 4796A example improves peak S/N from 28 to 37, already exceeding that range. | Describe the reported examples without a predictive numerical rule; distinguish rms, peak S/N and dynamic range.
- SR-11 | medium | unsupported | “Band 9 often cannot support self-cal” | Two non-applications do not establish frequency or a band-based exclusion. The pack also reports successful Band 9 self-calibration in 2014A&A...567A.125G, 2015ApJ...812..126C, 2016ApJ...823...91S and 2024PSJ.....5..125T. | Make feasibility depend on available signal and solution quality; present Band 9 successes and failures as cases.
- SR-12 | medium | unsupported | “one [amplitude] round, solint much longer than the last phase round” | This is not a universal literature sequence: 2017A&A...605A..21C uses 30 s for both final phase and amplitude; 2017ApJ...850L..30Y uses integration time for both; 2018ApJ...853..171G uses two amplitude+phase rounds. | Label a longer, single trial as a conservative starting recommendation and explicitly retain the documented alternatives.
- SR-13 | high | science | “Apply continuum solutions to the lines, then subtract continuum”; “applycal … to the full data” | The unconditional instruction overrides documented product-specific exclusions: 2013Natur.493..191C, 2016ApJ...833...41Z, 2018ApJ...853..171G and 2024ApJ...966...59S. Line-based self-calibration can also follow subtraction: 2012A&A...548A..69N explicitly starts with continuum subtraction. | Branch by calibration model and science product; transfer only validated solutions to appropriate data, and state the applicable subtraction order.
- SR-14 | medium | usability | “stop when phase scatter is about ±10%” | 2024PSJ.....5..125T reports this wording, but the pack supplies no definition or normalization for a percentage phase distribution. It is not an executable angular-scatter threshold. | Quote it as an ambiguous paper-specific description; require a defined phase statistic and units for operational stopping.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “missing flux … Phase only (2015ApJ...804...37L)” | Its entry says two phase rounds were performed **but not applied to the 12m data**, as well as no amplitude self-calibration. The table omits the consequential rejection. | State both the attempted phase calibration and its non-application to 12m data.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | Polarization guidance concentrates on solint | The pack additionally reports fixed cross-hand phase in 2020ApJ...895...64L and polarization fractions overestimated by factors 2–3 through spatial filtering in 2024A&A...686A.274V. | Add polarization-specific gain/relative-phase checks and a warning that self-calibration cannot remove filtering-induced polarization bias.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Report every round: mode, solint, combine, minsnr, refant, uv range, model” | Repeated “not stated” entries also identify exact continuum/self-cal channel selections, subtraction fit order, masks and imaging thresholds. These are needed to reproduce the model and assess apparent improvement. | Require those selections/settings, plus `minblperant`, gain type and normalization choices when applicable.
- SR-18 | medium | usability | “applycal the self-cal tables to the full data” | The workflow averages/selects SPWs and iterates gains but does not specify solution-to-data mapping or whether tables are incremental or replacements. The TDM-to-FDM example, 2017A&A...600A..72F, makes this mapping consequential. | Require an explicit calibration chain, input data column/state, SPW mapping and handling of failed solutions before transfer.
- SR-19 | medium | usability | “start long (scan length, `inf` …)” | The distinction between scan-length and whole-observation solutions is operationally important; `solint='inf'` alone does not specify combining scans. The pack separately documents whole-observation and scan-length strategies. | Explain scan boundaries and `combine`; record the actual time span and number of solutions produced.

## Notes

The skill preserves many useful examples, caveats and measured improvements.
The main risks are instrument/mode misattribution and turning individual procedures into universal defaults.
The pack’s truncated listings do not independently establish the claimed custom counts of 41, 43 and 54; retain them only with a reproducible counting definition.
