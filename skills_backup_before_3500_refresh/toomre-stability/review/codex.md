## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “Q = 0.4 to 5 between 20 K and 100 K” | The pack supports T = 20–100 K for 2018ApJ...864L..25O, but supplies neither Q endpoint. Calling them “card” values does not establish support in this evidence pack. | Remove the endpoints or supply their verified evidence; retain the supported temperature range.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | Additional “(card)” values in parameters.md | The pack does not provide Q ≈ 6 at 110 au (2019ApJ...882L..31B), Q ≈ 24 at 80 au (2019MNRAS.485..739M), Q ≈ 1.8 at 85 au (2018ApJ...861...91H), or Q ≈ 0.22 and 0.35 (2016ApJ...827...34O). | Remove these values pending evidence; label unavailable results as not stated in the supplied pack.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “C18O in … 2016MNRAS.461..385B”; “Sigma_g,min from Q plus cooling” | The pack gives H/r and dynamical timescale for 2016MNRAS.461..385B, but does not identify C18O or a Q = 1 criterion. For 2016ApJ...818..158A it supplies an opacity law, but not the asserted Q-plus-cooling method. The Gaia-distance mass attribution for 2019ApJ...882L..31B is also absent. | Remove or explicitly substantiate these paper-specific additions.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “high-z [CII]/CO disks 0.02 to 1.07” | None of the four cited entries supplies 1.07. The parameter table assigns it to 2018A&A...613A..34P, whose pack entry also supplies no Q result. | Use the supported individual results, distinguishing component means, radial ranges and disk averages; remove the unsupported endpoint.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | Gas-to-dust “Range seen … 100 to ~260” | The ~260 value is absent from the 2019ApJ...882L..31B pack entry. The stated range also omits the explicitly reported 70–400 in 2025ApJ...984L..19Y. The limit of 27 in 2024ApJ...971..129C is a different kind of constraint. | Separate assumed ratios, inferred ratios and stability bounds; include supported ranges and retain ~1e3 only as the high-z cross-check.
- SR-06 | medium | unsupported | “CASA images, then custom scripts”; “implicit in most” | The named-software aggregate does not establish what computes Q, and missing CASA settings do not establish that most papers use the asserted workflow. | Describe custom NumPy computation as a recommendation; restrict literature software attributions to what the pack explicitly names.
- SR-07 | high | science | “a = … sqrt(3) solid body, 2 uniform density” | Solid-body rotation has κ = 2Ω, including a uniform-density spherical potential. The pack’s adopted sqrt(3) values do not support the supplied physical classification. | Give κ² = 2(V/R)²[1 + d ln V/d ln R]: a = 1 for Keplerian, sqrt(2) for flat and 2 for solid-body rotation. Preserve paper-specific adopted factors as such.
- SR-08 | high | science | “central mass … bracket it (0 or 30 Msun)” | Following the preceding Keplerian formula with zero central stellar mass would yield κ = 0. The 2019ApJ...871..185L method explicitly uses enclosed gas mass and centrifugal balance; 0/30 Msun brackets the added stellar component. | Include gas self-gravity and derive κ from the adopted rotation model; distinguish stellar mass from total gravitating mass.
- SR-09 | medium | science | “Mean molecular weight … 2.3–2.8” | This combines quantities whose definitions are not established as interchangeable. The pack explicitly uses 2.3 in the sound-speed formula for 2017A&A...603A..10B and in a column-density conversion for 2019A&A...627L...6M; the 2025A&A...693A..76C assumption lists 2.8 without clarifying its role. | Separate mean mass per free particle for sound speed from mass per H2 molecule for column-density conversion; state each convention.
- SR-10 | high | science | “no spirals, so stable” | The 2025ApJ...984L..19Y excerpt says the morphology is “suggesting” stability and explicitly begins the abundance bound with “If we assume Q > 1”. The skill turns a conditional assumption into a demonstrated result. | Say morphology motivates an assumed stability constraint; report the X_CO bound as conditional on Q > 1.
- SR-11 | high | omission | “From gas … a kinematic self-gravity fit” | The proposed Sigma source, 2024ApJ...970..153A, explicitly imposes a gravitational-stability/minimum-Q prior. The computation steps do not warn that subsequently recovering stability from this Sigma is prior-dependent. | Carry the prior into the result and prohibit presenting the resulting Q as independent evidence of stability.
- SR-12 | high | omission | “Opacity per gram of gas” | The pack supplies numerical opacity laws for 2016ApJ...818..158A and 2018ApJ...864L..25O without specifying their mass normalization. Assigning a gas normalization without evidence risks a gas-to-dust-factor error. | Mark the normalization as unverified until established. Require every opacity to specify absorption/extinction, frequency and mass basis, and apply the gas-to-dust conversion exactly once.
- SR-13 | medium | omission | “From CO with alpha_CO” | The workflow omits transition conversion. The pack flags r43/10 uncertainty for 2017A&A...604A.117C and an unstated CO(2–1)-to-CO(1–0) ratio for 2021A&A...655A..25Z. | Require the observed transition, adopted excitation ratio and alpha_CO luminosity/mass convention before deriving Sigma.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “Velocity dispersion … From moment 2” | Beam smearing is covered, but spectral-response broadening is not. The 2019A&A...631A..91G settings explicitly flag instrumental broadening values as needed but unstated. | Require channel width, instrumental response and broadening treatment; distinguish intrinsic dispersion from unresolved or instrumental upper limits.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “spiral shape fits (2024ApJ...966..119L)” | The validation summary omits that only the southern arm fits, the northern arm does not, and the authors find velocity resolution/S/N insufficient for robust arm kinematics. | Preserve those qualifications wherever spiral fitting is offered as corroboration of GI.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | Pitfalls emphasize mass underestimates and Keplerian rotation | For 2018ApJ...864L..25O, the pack explicitly warns that mass may instead be an upper limit or apparent value because of non-Keplerian gravity and unresolved material. This caveat is missing despite repeated use of that paper. | Add this opposite bias and require validation of the rotation model before interpreting its Q estimate.
- SR-17 | medium | usability | “Where stars matter, use a two-component Q_tot” | The branch supplies velocity anisotropies but neither stellar surface density nor the weighting/thickness prescription needed to compute Q_tot. The cited 2025ApJ...995..151Y method is explicitly a thick-disk, H2-only calculation. | Provide the required inputs and explicit approximation, or a precise implementation reference; state the gas-component selection and thickness conventions.
