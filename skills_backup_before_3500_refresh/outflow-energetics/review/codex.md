# Codex review: outflow-energetics
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “Every result is a lower limit” | Contradicted by 2024A&A...686A..46E, whose molecular quantities are explicitly **upper limits** because a bar cannot be excluded; 2014ApJ...797...90S also allows substantially smaller masses under another conversion assumption. | State which quantities are limits, under which assumptions, separately for each source.
- SR-02 | high | attribution | “Correct 12CO with 13CO channel by channel … 2017MNRAS.465..834Z” | That card explicitly says **no optical-depth correction** was applied. For 2018MNRAS.473..879P, the pack supplies isotope abundances but does not establish this correction procedure. | Cite 2020A&A...634L..12D for an applied correction; describe the other entries only as supported.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “3 sigma per channel (2021A&A...648A..41V …)” | The cited card specifies 3-sigma **moment maps**, not a per-channel significance mask. | Distinguish integrated-map thresholds from channel thresholds; retain 2019A&A...628A.118B as the explicit channel example.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “20 deg … from the plane of the sky … 2016A&A...587A.145B” | The supplied pack gives 20 degrees but does not state that convention. The explicit “0 = plane of the sky” entry belongs to 2014ApJ...789L...4T. | Mark the 2016 paper’s convention as unspecified in this pack; do not transfer another paper’s convention.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “Momentum boost Pdot/(L_AGN/c) … ~3 in 2014ApJ...790..160S” | That excerpt reports approximately 3 relative to **L_bol/c**. | Preserve the reported denominator and explain any identification of L_bol with L_AGN.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Tex, protostellar CO … Range seen: 10–100 K” | The pack’s 2019A&A...632A.101T validation explicitly tests 250, 300 and 700 K; the parameters table itself lists 250–700 K. | Separate commonly adopted temperatures from the full range tested in the pack.
- SR-07 | medium | attribution | “Covering factor … 0.3–0.8 … Omega 0.2 2024ApJ...970....9B” | The example lies outside the stated range. Its Ω=0.2 belongs specifically to the **BAL** calculation, whereas the other examples concern OH absorption. | Separate phase-specific covering and solid-angle conventions; remove the pooled default.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “primary-beam response > 0.2 … card of 2019ApJ...873...73Z” | No supplied entry for that bibcode supports this threshold. | Remove the attribution; if retained as general guidance, explain how the cutoff and spatial noise model are chosen.
- SR-09 | low | unsupported | “most papers compute the sums by their own scripts” | The software aggregate records named software; absence of a name does not establish custom scripts. | Say that software is usually unspecified in the matched steps.

- SR-10 | high | science | “F_out = Mdot v_max / R_lobe” | With Mdot defined here as mass per time, this has units of mass/time², not force. The 2019A&A...632A.101T pack reproduces the same problematic expression, so this is an unresolved source-definition issue, not a transcription mismatch. | Use P/t_dyn or Mdot v for the stated definitions; withhold the quoted M7 expression until its numerator and units are resolved.
- SR-11 | high | science | “Mean mass per H2 … 2.34e-24 g per H2”; “mu … 2.3–2.8” | Although faithfully copied from 2016ApJ...832..158Z, 2.34e-24 g is less than the mass of an H2 molecule. Also, 2025A&A...699A.382M describes μ_g=2.3 as a gas mean weight, not an interchangeable mass per H2. | Distinguish weights per H nucleus, H2 molecule and free particle. For N(H2), use an explicitly consistent mass per H2; flag the quoted source inconsistency.
- SR-12 | high | science | “rates scale linearly with alpha_CO, X_C+, OH abundance” | Mass and rate scale with alpha_CO, but **inversely** with tracer abundance at fixed tracer column/luminosity. The pack explicitly converts OH column to H2 through OH/H2. | Write Mdot ∝ alpha_CO and Mdot ∝ X_C+^-1 or X_OH^-1, holding other assumptions fixed.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “No inclination correction … makes … t_dyn lower limits” | Projection does not give a universal bound on age. From the supplied 2013ApJ...775L..31S equations, t_true = (R_obs/v_LOS) cot i, which can increase or decrease the projected estimate. | Give the inclination dependence explicitly; retain individual authors’ lower-limit statements only with their specific observational rationale.
- SR-14 | medium | science | “m(v) proportional to v^-gamma; gamma = -3.43 … -2.78” | This produces increasing mass with speed. The 2016ApJ...832..158Z pack contains an internal sign inconsistency between its fitting expression and reported slopes. | Write m(v) ∝ v^s with s=-3.43/-2.78, or use positive γ=3.43/2.78 in v^-γ; flag the source inconsistency.
- SR-15 | medium | science | “a [J(Tex) - J(Tbg)] correction when T_B is not >> Tbg” | The correction is underspecified, and its relevance depends on excitation and background radiation, not observed line brightness alone. | For optically thin, continuum-subtracted brightness under the stated assumptions, specify the multiplier Jν(Tex)/[Jν(Tex)-Jν(Tbg)], define Jν, and state consistent velocity units.
- SR-16 | medium | science | “Filled sphere or cone, constant velocity … C=3” | Constant velocity and a filled volume do not alone fix C=3; the density distribution matters. The pack explicitly calls 2014A&A...567A.125G uniformly filled. | State the uniform-density assumption. Distinguish lifetime-averaged shell Mv/R from an instantaneous shell-crossing rate involving shell thickness.

- SR-17 | medium | omission | “[CII] … Hailey-Dunsheath … X_C+ … T=200 K” | The 2019A&A...630A..59B assumptions explicitly include a **0.7 PDR fraction**, omitted from the recipe and parameters table. | Include the adopted emitting-phase fraction and distinguish total [CII] luminosity from the luminosity assigned to that phase.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “Ionized phase: M = mu m_H L / (gamma n_e)” | The recipe omits extinction correction and the emissivity/abundance assumptions. The pack specifies extinction-corrected broad [OIII], Cardelli R_V=3.1 and intrinsic Hα/Hβ=3.1 for 2024A&A...686A..46E, and Z=0.5 Zsun for 2025A&A...695A...6P. | Define L and γ for each line; require extinction, temperature, abundance/metallicity and density assumptions.
- SR-19 | medium | omission | “true mass outflow rate about 7 times … momentum rate about 200 times” | The numbers are supported, but their limits are missing: 2024ApJ...966..117X reports wide variation, inclination-dependent ratios, one 60-Msun core sequence and ignored PDR effects. | Label these as model-specific recovery results, not transferable correction factors; distinguish them from the separately applied 10/200 corrections.
- SR-20 | medium | omission | “H2O absorption fitted with a spherical shell model in MultiNest” | For 2019A&A...632L...7J, the pack flags unconstrained shell thickness and poorly constrained loading, and specifies constant mass flow, negligible emission and assumed aperture/radius ratios. | Add these assumptions and degeneracies before presenting the fit as an energetics method.
- SR-21 | medium | omission | “RADEX (Bayesian) … 2018ApJ...868...95M” | Its central warning is missing: four lines constrain five parameters, posterior distributions are broad, and a single-phase model is inappropriate. The pack separately flags excitation degeneracy for 2017A&A...608A..38O. | Require excitation/phase-degeneracy checks and avoid treating a single fitted conversion as uniquely determined.
- SR-22 | medium | omission | “Always report … velocity ranges” | Reporting integration ranges does not establish that the observations cover the wings. The pack explicitly flags limited velocity coverage in 2014A&A...567A.125G, partial coverage in 2021AN....342.1160P and bandwidth preventing broad-component detection in 2019A&A...632A..66O. | Require a spectral-coverage check and label truncated-wing measurements accordingly.

- SR-23 | low | usability | “Counts … are my own counts of these rows” | The supplied parameters tables contain 29 protostellar and 34 galaxy/AGN rows, whereas SKILL.md quotes about 35 and 45 from the paper list. These denominators are not reproducible from the stated table basis. | Label the tables as selected examples and provide an enumerated classification for population counts.
