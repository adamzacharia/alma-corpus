# Codex review: absorption-lines
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings
- SR-01 | low | attribution | “continuum in the same spectral window … 2019A&A...622A..26G” | That card specifies the “same band”; its not-stated entry leaves the continuum choice per window unspecified. | Attribute same-SPW extraction to 2018A&A...617A..54L; label its broader use as a recommendation.
- SR-02 | high | science | “Divide the spectrum by the continuum” followed by “tau = ln(S_cont/S_obs)” | 2018A&A...617A..54L explicitly extracts from **continuum-subtracted** cubes. Dividing that residual by the continuum does not produce the positive transmission required by the logarithm. | Define transmission as R = 1 + S_line/S_cont for continuum-subtracted spectra, or R = S_obs/S_cont for total-flux spectra. Then use −ln(R); handle nonpositive/noise-dominated transmission with limits.
- SR-03 | medium | science | “Linear-regime formula … Assumes optically thin” | The 2017Natur.548..430F card supplies N(CH+) proportional to optical depth times linewidth, reports peak tau up to 1.2, and does not state an optically thin assumption. Proportionality to **true integrated optical depth** does not require tau ≪ 1. | Distinguish the column–opacity relation from the optically thin approximation relating flux decrement to tau. Mark excitation/population assumptions as general background unless supported by the pack.
- SR-04 | medium | science | “line bottom is above zero flux (saturation plus f_c < 1)” | Positive residual flux also occurs for ordinary unsaturated absorption with full coverage. Neither this check nor a flat bottom alone establishes saturation or partial coverage; 2014A&A...566L...6M reports a covering-factor constraint without establishing this diagnostic. | Require independent saturation evidence and consider unresolved structure or emission filling before interpreting residual flux as uncovered continuum.
- SR-05 | medium | science | “absorption sits at the continuum peak and scales with continuum brightness, not with position” | This is not a necessary validation condition for spatially varying optical depth or covering factor. The pack explicitly includes spatial reconstruction of clumpy OH absorption in 2020ApJ...905...85S. | Require absorption to overlap background continuum and survive imaging/extraction checks; allow spatially varying normalized absorption.
- SR-06 | medium | omission | “Constrain the covering factor” | The skill reports 0.3 < f_c < 1 from 2014A&A...566L...6M but supplies no way to apply partial coverage when deriving tau. | Under negligible line emission, give R = 1 − f_c + f_c exp(−tau) and its inversion. State the allowed domain and propagate covering-factor uncertainty into column limits.
- SR-07 | medium | omission | “flag candidates at 5 sigma in two consecutive channels” | The 2019MNRAS.490.1220K card flags **exact noise estimation per spectrum** and **Butterworth filter order and implementation** as unstated. These are absent from the blind-search instructions despite determining what the threshold means. | Require documented filtering and noise estimation after processing for each smoothing scale, including channel correlations.
- SR-08 | medium | omission | “HCO+, HOC+, CF+ against CH, HF and other H2 proxies” | The 2019A&A...622A..26G limitation explicitly says CF+ and C3H+ lines are weak and contaminated; the skill retains the abundance-calibration caveat but omits this measurement caveat. | Require contamination assessment and deblending or appropriately qualified limits before deriving these species’ columns and ratios.
- SR-09 | medium | omission | “dark-gas tracer where CO emission is absent” | 2018A&A...617A..54L warns that Planck CO maps are masked near some sightlines and weak detections may be missed. “Absent” overstates a potentially incomplete nondetection. | Say “CO emission is undetected” and require checking masks, sensitivity and available CO coverage before interpreting dark gas.
- SR-10 | medium | omission | “looked for absorption variability over two years” | The 2020MNRAS.496..364R card specifically flags resolution matching between the 2016 and 2018 data as unstated. The verification section omits this prerequisite for an epoch comparison. | Require compatible angular/spectral resolution and consistent extraction and continuum definitions before interpreting differences as variability.
- SR-11 | low | usability | “Papers use one of four extractions” | The following table lists five extraction methods. | Change “four” to “five.”
