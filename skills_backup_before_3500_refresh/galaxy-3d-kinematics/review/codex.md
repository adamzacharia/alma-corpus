# Codex review: galaxy-3d-kinematics

## Findings

- SR-01 | high | attribution | “adds 30% for the spherical assumption” | 2020Natur.581..269N, step 4, adds **30% uncertainty**, not 30% to the mass. | Say “include a 30% geometry uncertainty”; retain the nominal mass.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “N = number of fitted pixels (2019ApJ...883..193N…)” | That card specifies `(2N_dof)^(1/4)`, whereas 2025ApJ...992..211N and 2020MNRAS.496.4061D use N. The parameter appendix preserves this distinction; Step 5 erases it. | Define the counting convention separately for each prescription and describe the rescaling as an empirical uncertainty treatment.
- SR-03 | high | attribution | “Stage 1: all geometric and kinematic parameters free per ring” | 2019A&A...632A..61G, step 6, explicitly fixes AGN position and V_sys initially; 2021A&A...655A..25Z fixes the centre throughout; 2024A&A...690A.169L fixes centre/V_sys before the initial CO fit. | Make staged fitting conditional: retain independently fixed parameters and specify exactly which parameters each cited implementation releases.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Independent tracer or axis ratio, then fixed” | The table cites 2025NatAs...9.1553F, whose step 2 explicitly lists inclination among the free parameters. Likewise, 2019MNRAS.482.1499M uses photometric constraints while fitting inclination. | Distinguish photometric estimates, priors, starting guesses and fixed values; do not label these examples uniformly fixed.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “8 fix to continuum/AGN” | The pack contains at least nine explicit cases: the seven Step 2 examples, 2020A&A...641A.151S, and 2019A&A...632A..61G, step 6. | Correct the count and provide its complete bibcode membership.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “emcee (9 papers in pack aggregates)” | The aggregate counts software mentions in matched steps, not distinct kinematic papers. It includes emcee in the CO-SLED fit of 2020ApJ...889..162L. | Say “9 matched-step mentions”; separately count distinct, relevant kinematic papers if needed.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “most papers fix [the centre]”; “exponential disk is the default” | The pack supports individual examples, but supplies neither a centre-choice denominator establishing “most” nor a universal surface-brightness default. 2020MNRAS.493.3744F documents defaults for its particular GalPak3D setup. | Use “examples in the pack use…” and label recommended starting models separately from measured literature frequencies or software defaults.
- SR-08 | medium | science | “unless the model is convolved with the beam in 3D”; “2D…does not correct beam smearing” | These make dimensionality decisive. Forward convolution must reproduce the observable; a convolved 3D fit can remain degenerate or biased, while a properly constructed forward model of 2D observables can account for spatial smearing. The pack’s mock tests and unresolved-fit limitations demonstrate that convolution alone is insufficient. | Require response-aware forward modelling and recovery tests; describe ordinary unconvolved velocity-field fitting as vulnerable rather than declaring every 2D approach incapable.
- SR-09 | high | science | “Unresolved or one-beam source…D = 1.5 × the (deconvolved) major-axis FWHM” | A truly unresolved source does not supply a reliable deconvolved diameter. The pack explicitly flags poorly constrained sizes, and 2017MNRAS.465.4390B instead assumes R = 4 kpc for unresolved objects. | Separate marginally resolved sizes, independent size measurements, size limits and assumed radii. Propagate or bracket the size assumption and label the resulting mass accordingly.
- SR-10 | medium | science | “Virial…5…or…C = 3.4”; “virial C = 3.4–5” | These coefficients are not an interchangeable tuning range. 2015ApJ...812L..23S assumes spherical symmetry and applies its estimator to two resolved sources. 2020MNRAS.493.4294B assumes rotation dominance and specifically identifies its deconvolved major axis with r_1/2. | State each estimator’s geometry, radius definition and applicability; preserve these distinctions in both tables and do not infer a generic unresolved-source default.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “correct for pressure support…M = R(V² + 3 sigma²)/G…or…quadrature” | The listed prescriptions encode different assumptions. 2015ApJ...806L..34F uses harmonic coefficient k1 and explicitly attributes HCN broadening to symmetric macroscopic motions; 2015ApJ...806...17R corrects rotation, but not dispersion, for inclination. | Treat these as distinct model-dependent estimators. Define k1/deprojection and dispersion conventions, and require an appropriate pressure-support model rather than offering interchangeable corrections.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “mass estimates not corrected for [non-circular motions] are likely lower limits” | The pack attributes that conclusion specifically to 2013ApJ...770L..27F. Non-circular motions do not establish a universal direction of mass bias. | Restrict the lower-limit statement to that paper; require source-specific modelling or uncertainty brackets elsewhere.
- SR-13 | medium | science | “Mdot = 2 pi R Sigma V_rad, × 1.33 for helium” | 2024A&A...690A.169L, step 3, applies 1.33 to an **H I-only** surface density/mass flow. The generic instruction can double-count helium in a total-gas surface density. | Write `2πRΣ_HI V_rad × 1.33` for that prescription; omit the factor when Σ already includes helium.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “v_rad last”; “|cos theta|² weighting” | The cited 2024A&A...690A.169L switches to **sin² φ weighting** for radial fits, holds other parameters fixed, assumes optically thin H I, and warns that uncertainties preclude definitive flow conclusions. These qualifications are missing from the radial-flow recipe. | Add a separate radial-fit configuration, state the H I assumption, and require uncertainty/significance checks before interpreting inflow or outflow.
- SR-15 | high | omission | “gas from the dynamical mass after subtracting stars and dark matter” | Aperture matching is unspecified. 2015ApJ...812L..23S explicitly subtracts **half** the stellar mass from M_dyn within r_1/2. Subtracting total stellar mass can substantially underestimate the enclosed gas mass. | Require all components within the same radius; distinguish enclosed gas mass from any extrapolation to total gas mass.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Always report…cube…[and] V_sys” | Velocity frame, velocity definition, rest frequency and zero point are absent. Cards repeatedly flag these omissions; the NGC 1068 excerpts explicitly report 1120 km/s LSR versus 1127 km/s heliocentric. | Require these metadata and transform datasets to a common convention before combining tracers or comparing systemic velocities.
- SR-17 | medium | omission | “Always report…”; “Unreported settings” | The pack’s needed-settings list also flags primary-beam correction, flux-scale uncertainty, cleaning thresholds and moment-map clipping. These are not explicitly required by the reporting checklist. | Add these fields, including how primary-beam treatment affects noise and which clipping/selection rules define fitted velocity fields.
- SR-18 | medium | omission | “MCMC burn-in: 20%”; “Chain length: 1e5 iterations” | The table turns heterogeneous run lengths into actionable defaults without a required convergence assessment. The pack repeatedly flags missing convergence settings; 2016ApJ...820L..39N explicitly checks five starting points. | Label lengths and burn-in fractions as examples, require documented convergence and effective sampling checks, and distinguish ensemble steps/walkers from iterations or retained samples.

## Notes
Many individual settings and numerical results are faithfully reproduced.
The main risks are promoting examples into defaults and dropping estimator-specific assumptions.
Keep tracer identity explicit: the 2024A&A...684A..24P ring fit and BIC comparison concern Halpha, not an ALMA cube.
