# Codex review: array-combination
VERDICT: FIX
## Findings

- SR-01 | medium | attribution | “MRS ~11″”; “Missing flux on scales >4.6″”; “~60%”; “within a few percent” | These numbers are absent from the supplied pack entries for 2017MNRAS.468.3266R, 2017ApJ...835..174S, 2017MNRAS.464L..31H and 2020ApJ...893...13M, respectively. | Remove the numerical attributions or supply supporting evidence.
- SR-02 | medium | attribution | “uvcombine … feathering with Herschel/SCUBA”; “SCUBA x~3, tested 0.5–10x” | The 2018ApJ...853..171G excerpt describes combining **Herschel with SCUBA**, including these scaling experiments. It does not establish that this software or scale factor was used to combine ALMA with SCUBA. | Identify the actual dataset pair and remove this example as evidence for an ALMA–single-dish scale correction.
- SR-03 | medium | attribution | “ACA (7m) weight relative to 12m … 4 `statwt`” | One of the four cited papers, 2017ApJ...850...83F, describes combining HUDF projects with recomputed weights; its pack entry does not identify an ACA/12-m combination. | Separate general dataset reweighting examples from verified ACA/12-m weighting examples.
- SR-04 | medium | attribution | “Weighting (combined cubes) … 19 robust 0.5, 7 natural” | The supplied top-level parameter list contains 18 entries mentioning robust 0.5, including continuum settings. In 2020ApJ...889....9M and 2020ApJ...901..151S, the lines use natural weighting and the continuum uses robust 0.5. These are not counts of combined cubes using robust 0.5. | Recompute unique-paper counts for explicitly combined line cubes; separate continuum, line and unspecified products.
- SR-05 | medium | attribution | “self-calibrate … then self-calibrate jointly … 2025ApJ...993..120H” | That entry states only that self-calibrated SB data were combined with LB data. It does not state subsequent joint self-calibration. Likewise, 2025ApJ...986L..11S establishes joint self-calibration without specifying the preceding SB-only stage. | Attribute each stage only to papers explicitly reporting it; remove the unsupported “most common” ranking.
- SR-06 | medium | attribution | “Compact-source extraction … TM1 left out of line cubes” | 2021A&A...656A..60A reports all configurations concatenated, with TM1 excluded for line cubes. This does not support excluding short spacings for compact-source extraction; it excludes a different configuration for a different product. | Move this example to product-specific configuration selection and avoid assigning an unstated motivation.
- SR-07 | medium | unsupported | “12m+7m … often holds 20–60%”; “Two mistakes dominate” | The pack supplies selected missing-flux examples, not a prevalence estimate or ranking of mistakes. The examples also mix ACA-only measurements, combined interferometric measurements and mass comparisons. | Replace with a qualitative warning and retain the individual, correctly scoped examples.
- SR-08 | medium | unsupported | “Common choice … auto-multithresh”; “Clean depth … ~4 sigma, then … ~1 sigma” | The staged cleaning recipe is explicitly supported by 2025ApJ...988..162D, not established as a general default. Generic “automask” descriptions do not identify auto-multithresh, and 2018ApJ...867..166S supplies gain/iteration settings without establishing that mask algorithm. | Label these as paper-specific examples; distinguish `deconvolver` from masking and require dataset-specific thresholds.
- SR-09 | high | science | “feather with TP after multiplying TP by the interferometer primary beam” | This unconditional recipe leaves the high-resolution image’s PB state unspecified. The pack documents PB-weighted single-dish combination in 2019A&A...623A..96Y, but PB correction **before** feathering in 2025ApJ...988..162D. Mixing these conventions introduces position-dependent flux errors. | Specify matched branches: apparent-sky interferometric image plus PB-weighted single dish, followed by PB correction; or PB-corrected inputs with appropriate footprint masking. Prevent double correction.
- SR-10 | medium | science | “manual 0.18–0.3” | These endpoints describe different quantities: 2014A&A...570L..14R applies a **multiplicative factor** of 0.3 to ACA weights; 2020A&A...633A..13D sets an **ACA/12-m weight ratio** of 0.18. They are not interchangeable bounds for one parameter. | Separate correction factors from resulting weight ratios and state that their normalization must be established before reuse.
- SR-11 | medium | science | “Joint deconvolution in the uv domain … `sdintimaging`” | The 2026ApJ..1003...68Y entry puts the **12-m and 7-m** combination in visibility space, then describes joint deconvolution with APEX. It does not describe converting APEX into visibilities. Also, feathering itself combines images in Fourier space, so “Fourier-space” is not a physically separate alternative to feathering. | Separate image-based joint deconvolution, pseudo-visibility methods and Fourier combination of images; describe tally categories as reported terminology.
- SR-12 | medium | science | “Continuum-subtract each dataset before concatenating” | This mandatory ordering conflicts with pack examples: 2021ApJ...908..231M lists CONCAT then UVCONTSUB; 2014A&A...570L..14R combines and self-calibrates before continuum subtraction. The numbered workflow also risks removing the continuum before continuum self-calibration. | Present subtraction before or after concatenation as conditional choices. Preserve continuum data for self-calibration and apply the resulting calibration before line subtraction.
- SR-13 | high | omission | “Herschel, SCUBA, BGPS … as short spacings” | The skill omits the required spectral extrapolation for continuum maps at different frequencies. The pack explicitly describes Herschel 500 μm scaled to 3 mm in 2014ApJ...795L..25R and assumes β=1.8 for the SCUBA comparison in 2020ApJ...895..119T. Regridding alone cannot make these maps compatible. | Require a justified spectral/SED conversion, propagated temperature and spectral uncertainties, and consistent brightness units and beam metadata before combination or recovery comparisons.
- SR-14 | medium | omission | “Treat masses … as lower limits”; “Fit … visibilities jointly” | The pack warns about more than total-flux loss: 2018ApJ...856..147O says the filtered density profile cannot be discussed; 2020ApJ...898..146M and 2021AJ....161..271F identify model-dependent total flux from visibility fitting. | Warn that filtering can invalidate profile slopes and morphology, and that joint visibility fitting does not independently measure unsampled zero-spacing flux.
- SR-15 | medium | omission | “Settings … not stated … Report these yourself” | The reporting checklist emphasizes combination parameters but omits repeatedly flagged essentials: CASA/pipeline versions, line-free channels and subtraction fit order, flux-scale uncertainties, image/cell sizes, masks and self-calibration intervals. These appear explicitly in the pack’s missing-settings aggregate and excerpts. | Add a required provenance/settings checklist; record unavailable values as unknown rather than adopting table examples.
- SR-16 | medium | omission | “Align … epochs” | Coordinate alignment is covered, but temporal compatibility is not. The pack flags non-simultaneous datasets in 2017ApJ...845L..19B and a two-week separation between tracers in 2025NatAs...9.1476C. | Check observing dates and source variability before combining epochs or interpreting cross-dataset differences as missing flux.

## Notes
The skill captures many useful workflows and validation examples.
The 40-paper TP partition and merged CASA count of 67 are consistent with the stated tally conventions.
The main risks are unconditional PB handling, cross-frequency continuum combination, and paper-specific examples becoming defaults.
